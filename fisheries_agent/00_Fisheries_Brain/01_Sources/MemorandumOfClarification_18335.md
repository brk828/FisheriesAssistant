# Memorandum of Clarification: Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program

#aquatic-conservation #freshwater-ecology #endangered-species-management

## Executive Summary

This policy document is a Memorandum of Clarification (MOC) that interprets the [[Memorandum Of Agreement]] (MOA) for developing a Lower Colorado River Species Conservation Program, executed on August 2, 1995. The MOC states that, where the MOA's wording conflicts with it, the MOC controls. Its core purpose is to define the [[Multi-Species Conservation Program]] (MSCP): a program to conserve habitat and promote the recovery of listed "included species" in the 100-year floodplain of the [[Lower Colorado River]] under the [[Endangered Species Act]] (ESA), while accommodating current water diversions and power production. The document contains no species-level biological data. Its significance lies in how it shapes ESA [[Section 7 Consultation]] for federal actions in the floodplain.

## Background & Description

The MOC was prepared by the signatories to the MOA to clarify its intent and move the program forward. It references two attachments: the MOA itself (Attachment 1) and a list of included species (Attachment 2). The attachment contents are not reproduced in the source text, so the species list is not available here.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The program is framed around the 100-year floodplain of the Lower Colorado River. The text notes that federal activities in this zone are or may be subject to ESA section 7 consultation. It places the program's conservation goals alongside existing and future water and power uses "to the extent consistent with law." The document does not describe habitat conditions, water chemistry, or population data for any species.

Key legal framework cited:

- ESA, 16 U.S.C. §1531 et seq., and implementing regulations at 50 CFR §402
- MOA Article II: signatories' statutory responsibilities cannot be delegated, and the MOA does not abrogate them
- MOA Article V: the MOA is subject to all applicable federal and state laws and does not alter existing law

## Key Findings

1. **Program purpose.** The MOA is construed to develop an MSCP that will (a) conserve habitat and work toward recovery of included species in the Lower Colorado River floodplain, and try to reduce the likelihood of further ESA listings; and (b) accommodate current water diversions and power production while optimizing future development, consistent with law.

2. **Section 7 consultation role.** During consultation on federal actions in the floodplain, the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] must review and consider (1) conservation measures developed by MSCP participants and (2) the status of MSCP development when shaping [[Reasonable And Prudent Alternatives]] (RPAs) and [[Reasonable And Prudent Measures]] (RPMs). The Service may consider other measures if items (1) and (2) do not satisfy ESA requirements.

3. **No regulatory guarantee.** Any MOA reference to "regulatory assurance" or "interim regulatory assurances" under ESA section 7 is clarified. These terms do not imply that signatories are exempt from ESA requirements.

4. **Reaffirmed legal compliance.** All signatories reaffirm their commitment to applicable federal and state law, and the MOA does not alter existing statutory responsibilities.

5. **Governing document.** The MOC overrides the MOA wherever the two are inconsistent.
