# Humpback Chub Population Estimate in Black Rocks: FY 2003 Annual Project Report

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This annual report from the [[Colorado River Recovery Program]] (Project 131) covers fiscal year 2003 work toward a second [[Population Estimate]] of [[Humpback Chub]] in the [[Black Rocks]] reach of the [[Colorado River]]. Robust population estimates are needed at regular intervals to track recovery under the program. Four sampling trips in September and October 2003 captured 70 individual humpback chubs. The capture-recapture analysis and the final population estimate were not yet complete when the report was written. The project was on track and fully funded, with expenditures of $20,500 against $20,500 provided.

## Background & Description

- **Project:** Population Estimate of Humpback Chub in Black Rocks, part of the [[Colorado River Action Plan]] (Mainstem, V.C.1).
- **Principal investigator:** Chuck McAda, project leader, [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], Grand Junction, Colorado.
- **Study schedule:** FY 2003 to FY 2005.
- **Prior work:** A population estimate covering 1998–2000 was completed (McAda 2002). This report covers the start of the second estimate.
- **Rationale:** The recovery goals call for population size estimates at regular intervals to measure how the population responds to management actions.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Study species:** [[Humpback Chub]] is the focal endangered fish. [[Roundtail Chub]] was also captured, mainly by electrofishing.
- **Study area:** The entire Black Rocks reach was sampled. Field crews sampled with [[Electrofishing]] during one early-morning and one evening period on each trip.
- **Gear:** [[Trammel Nets]] were set outside the electrofishing periods. Inner mesh sizes were varied. Trammel nets with 0.5-inch inner mesh caught smaller fish, but at a much lower catch rate than 1-inch mesh. Fewer, shorter net sets were used to reduce the time between net checks and limit stress to captured fish.
- **Population structure:** Size structure was similar to previous years (length-frequency figure in the report appendix, total length in cm).
- **Management context:** The report recommends continued operation of the facilities as a primary refuge for endangered fishes of the Upper Colorado River Basin.

## Key Findings

1. **Sampling effort:** Four trips were completed in September and October 2003, with the last trip in the final week of October.
2. **Captures:** 70 individual humpback chubs were captured. Five were recaptured: one within the same sampling rotation and four in different rotations. Twelve of the captured fish had been tagged in earlier years.
3. **Analysis status:** The capture-recapture matrix is complete. The preliminary [[Mark-Recapture]] population estimate and catch-per-effort calculations were still pending.
4. **Gear performance:** Electrofishing caught more roundtail chubs and some humpback chubs than in earlier years. Smaller trammel-net mesh captured smaller fish but at a lower rate.
5. **Data submission:** [[Passive Integrated Transponder Tags]] numbers and data for stocked fish were submitted to the database.
6. **Project status:** Ongoing and on track. Work was 100% complete for FY 2003, and no publication charges were incurred.
7. **Recommendation:** Continue managing the facilities as a primary refuge for endangered fishes of the Upper Colorado River Basin.
