# Desert Fishes Council Letter on Publication Funding for *Battle Against Extinction*

#ichthyology #marine-biology #desert-fishes

## Executive Summary

This is a 1989 letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]], signed by its Executive Secretary, E. P. Pister, to Ms. Barbara Beaty of the [[University of Arizona Press]]. The Council offers a financial commitment toward publication of the book *Battle Against Extinction: Native Fish Management in the American West*. The letter sets a target retail price, a funding ceiling, and a plan to solicit donations, and asks whether donations can be earmarked for the Press and whether they would be tax-exempt. It contains no biological data.

## Background & Description

- **Date and sender:** November 30, 1989, from the Desert Fishes Council, whose stated mission is "Dedicated to the Preservation of America's Desert Fishes." Its address is in Bishop, California.
- **Recipient:** Ms. Barbara Beaty, The University of Arizona Press, Tucson, Arizona.
- **Subject:** *Battle Against Extinction, Native Fish Management in The American West*, a book edited by [[W. L. Minckley]], a professor at [[Arizona State University]]. Minckley is copied on the letter.
- **Contact route:** The Council learned of the Press's request for a commitment statement through Minckley.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not describe any fish species, habitats, populations, or water conditions. Its only ecological link is the Council's conservation mission and the book's theme of native fish management in the American West, a theme framed by the title's reference to extinction. No sampling, species lists, or environmental measurements are reported.

## Key Findings

- **Target price:** The Council wants the book priced at no more than **$45-55**, so that it reaches as many researchers and interested readers as possible.
- **Funding commitment:** The Council would commit **not to exceed $8-10,000** toward publication.
- **Donation handling:** The Council says it is "ill-equipped to handle donations" and asks whether donations can be earmarked and sent directly to the U of A Press, and whether such donations would be [[Tax-Exempt Status]].
- **Fundraising plan:** The Council intends to begin soliciting donations "at an early date."
- **Open questions:** The letter requests information on tax treatment and donation earmarking. The reply is not included in this document.
