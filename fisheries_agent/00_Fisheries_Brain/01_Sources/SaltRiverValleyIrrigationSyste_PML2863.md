## Background & Description

The map is issued by the Salt River Valley Water Users' Association (Phoenix, Arizona) and shows the physical network used to deliver irrigation water across the valley. The legend identifies the following infrastructure types:

- **Canals and conveyance:** main [[Irrigation Canal Network|canals]], [[Lateral Canals|laterals]], and waste and pump ditches.
- **Drainage:** [[Tile Drains]].
- **Pumping and wells:** [[Irrigation Wells]] (including irrigation, contract, and R.A.D. wells) and [[Booster Pumps]].
- **Facilities and yards:** camps and yards, a [[Cross Cut Steam Plant]], [[Cross Cut Shops]], and a [[Dispatching Center]].
- **Administrative divisions:** an Eastern Line Division and a Western Line Division (the OCR is partly illegible here).

Lateral canals are numbered on the map, with roughly LAT. 1 through LAT. 14 legible in the scan. Each is keyed to nearby roads and cross streets, such as [[Hayden Road]], [[Miller Road]], [[Four Mile Road]], [[Greenfield Road]], [[Dobson Road]], [[Gilbert Road]], [[Warner Road]], and [[Williams Field Road]]. Other labels include Central Avenue, Southern Avenue, Baseline Road, Transmission Road, and Highline, Kyrene, and Hunter Drives.
