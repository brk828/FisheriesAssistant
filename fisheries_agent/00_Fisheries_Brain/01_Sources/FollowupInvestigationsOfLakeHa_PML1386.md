## Executive Summary

This is a partially legible 1972 administrative transmittal from the [[Bureau Of Reclamation]] to the [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]]. It forwards a report of data sampled during an earlier investigation and describes planned or completed **follow-up investigations** of the [[Lake Havasu]] biological studies. The letter is dated August 4, 1972. Its main subjects are fish sampling, growth, and the evaluation of sampled coves. The scan is heavily degraded, so most specific numbers, species names and methods cannot be recovered with confidence.

- **Setting:** [[Lake Havasu]] is a reservoir on the [[Colorado River]] along the Arizona-California border. Its management has fish and game, water-resource, and reclamation dimensions.
- **Sampling design:** The follow-up work targeted specific coves within the reservoir, mapped in an enclosure. Cove-based sampling is a common way to compare habitat-specific fish populations in a large impoundment (see [[Cove Sampling]]).
- **Interagency role:** The letter shows coordination between a federal water agency and state wildlife managers. The federal agency was responsible for the reservoir and the state for fish and game.
- **Missing details:** The document does not clearly name target fish species, sample sizes, catch rates, water chemistry values or habitat measurements. Those cannot be reliably recovered from the scan.
