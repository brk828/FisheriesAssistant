## Executive Summary
This document is a 1992 administrative letter from the Arizona State Council of Trout Unlimited to Bob Hirsch, inviting his participation as a panelist in a discussion titled "Management Toward Recovery of [[Apache Trout]]" (*Oncorhynchus apache*) at the 24th Annual Meeting of the [[Desert Fishes Council]] in Mesa, Arizona (November 18–21, 1992). While not a primary scientific research paper, the letter outlines the conservation concerns, management priorities, and stakeholder framework surrounding the recovery of this native salmonid species. It highlights institutional collaboration among the [[White Mountain Apache Tribe]], [[U.S. Forest Service]], [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], and [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] in addressing the species' conservation status.
