# Federal Endangered Species Permit PRT-676811: Scientific Collection and Recovery Authorizations

#ichthyology #marine-biology #endangered-species #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a set of U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) correspondence and permit forms, issued between 1984 and 1986, under Federal Fish and Wildlife Permit PRT-676811. The permit authorizes [[Arizona State University]] researchers, led by Dr. W.L. Minckley, to take, hold, maintain, and propagate listed endangered and threatened freshwater fishes for scientific research and species recovery. The core records are a July 1986 amendment adding four species and a co-permittee, plus annual year-specific authorization letters for 1985 and 1986. The document contains no population data, sample sizes, or ecological measurements.

## Background & Description

The permit is issued under 16 USC 1539(a) (Endangered Species Act) and 50 CFR 17.22. The Regional Director of USFWS Region 2, Michael Spear, is the principal officer. The permit is renewable, expires 5/31/86 on the original form, and is valid only with a concurrent valid State permit. Activities must follow the application submitted, and the permit is void if conducted in violation of federal, state, or local law. Required reporting is an annual activities report to the USFWS Federal Wildlife Permit Office, due 1/31 after each permit year (the year-specific letters set a December 1 deadline).

Named subpermittees and authorized personnel include:
- [[Minckley]] (Dr. W.L. Minckley, Associate Professor, Arizona State University)
- Dr. Paul Marsh
- Dr. David Galat
- Catherine Karp
- Bruce DeMarais
- Dr. Michael Douglas (added as permittee in 1986)

The permit requires that the permit, the original letter, and any amendment be carried during field work.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document lists species under two regulatory categories: endangered and threatened. Authorized activities cover take within USFWS Region 2 (with concurrence from appropriate state authorities), holding, maintenance, propagation, and import through any port of entry. Authorized purposes are limited to:
1. Actions necessary to recover or survive a species whose specimens would otherwise be lost due to natural processes or circumstances beyond the permittee's control.
2. Actions designated under a recovery program or under expert advice.
3. Actions outlined in a recovery plan.

Habitat, geographic site locations, water chemistry, and population status are not described in the document. The species are associated with the desert Southwest and northern Mexico, which are primarily freshwater systems; the document itself does not state this.

Species authorized (names as given in the source):
- Desert pupfish (*Cyprinodon macularius*), spikedace (*Meda fulgida*), Sonora chub (*Gila ditaenia*), loach minnow (*Tiaroga cobitis*): added by the July 1986 amendment.
- Gila topminnow (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*), bonytail chub (*Gila elegans*), humpback chub (*G. cypha*), Yaqui chub (*G. purpurea*), beautiful shiner (*Notropis formosus*), woundfin (*Plagopterus argentissimus*), Yaqui catfish (*Ictalurus pricei*), Apache trout (*Salmo apache*), Colorado squawfish (*Ptychocheilus lucius*): authorized in 1985 and 1986.
- Chihuahua chub (*G. nigrescens*) and Gila trout (*S. gilae*): authorized in 1986 only.

## Key Findings

- **Permit amendment (17 July 1986):** Permit PRT-676811 was amended to add four species (desert pupfish, spikedace, Sonora chub, loach minnow) and co-permittee Dr. Michael Douglas. All other conditions were unchanged.
- **1986 authorization (dated January 1986):** Authorized Minckley, Marsh, Galat, Karp, and DeMarais to take, hold, maintain, and propagate 11 species for research and recovery during calendar year 1986. The letter notes that the authority is functional only with a valid State permit.
- **1985 authorization (dated 15 February 1985):** Authorized Minckley, Marsh, and Galat to handle nine species for calendar year 1985. The 1985 list omits the Chihuahua chub and Gila trout, which appear in 1986.
- **Reporting:** Annual activity reports and renewal requests are required, with deadlines of December 1 (year letters) and 1/31 (permit form).
- **Compliance:** Permit holders must carry the permit and letters while working. Law enforcement offices (Regions 1 and 2) and the Arizona Game and Fish Department were copied on correspondence.
- **Data gaps:** The document contains no sampling results, population counts, or habitat measurements.
