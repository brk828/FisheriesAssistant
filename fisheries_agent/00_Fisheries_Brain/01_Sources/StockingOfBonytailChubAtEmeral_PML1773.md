# Stocking of Bonytail Chub at Emerald Canyon Golf Course

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal memorandum from the Parker [[Fish and Wildlife Office]] (dated 27 October 1994) reports the stocking of [[Bonytail Chub]] into four ponds at the [[Emerald Canyon Golf Course]]. The estimated total stocked was 27,396 fish. One pond received a second stocking in October, and the fish were delivered to the Parker office on 24 October. The memo was addressed to program staff at the [[Arizona Fish and Wildlife Office]] and the [[Recovery Program]] for Region 2.

## Background & Description

The bonytail chub (*Gila elegans*) is a cyprinid fish. The memo does not give its morphology, life history or conservation status. It is referred to only as the stocked species.

The memo was sent from the Project Coordinator at the Parker office to:
- Stuart Leon, Project Leader, Arizona Fish and Wildlife Office
- George Divine, Recovery Coordinator, Region 2
- Lesley (surname illegible in scan), interested persons

Persons involved in the operation were:
- Sara Ray, Fish and Wildlife Service volunteer
- Al Doelker and Tom Pradett, [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM)
- Mitch (surname illegible in scan) and the author
- Steve Benton, manager of Emerald Canyon Golf Course, who coordinated delivery

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The stocking took place in artificial golf course ponds, not in natural river or lake habitat. The memo gives no water chemistry, temperature, habitat condition or monitoring data. It also gives no survival, growth or population information. The purpose of the stocking is not stated beyond the fact that the fish were released.

## Key Findings

**Stocking numbers (estimated, per pond):**

| Pond | Estimated number stocked |
|---|---|
| Pond 1 | 5,732 |
| Upper pond (L2) | 3,156 |
| Lower pond | 8,969 |
| Pond 18 | 9,539 |
| **Total** | **27,396** |

- Pond 1 was stocked again in October, with an additional count partly illegible in the scan. The memo says this brought the total in that pond to an unreadable figure.
- The total of 27,396 matches the sum of the four estimated pond counts.
- The fish were delivered to the Parker office on 24 October 1994, coordinated through Steve Benton.

**Limitations:** The counts are estimates. Several figures and names were distorted by the scan and are marked as illegible above. The memo does not state the source population, the rearing facility, the fish size, or any follow-up monitoring plan.
