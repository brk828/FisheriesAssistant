## Background & Description

The chapter's introduction sets out a three-part classification of plant responses to anoxia:

- **Anoxia tolerant (truly):** the plant adapts metabolically to tolerate low oxygen.
- **Apparently anoxia tolerant:** the plant adapts morphologically and physiologically to avoid anoxia.
- **Anoxia intolerant:** the plant does not adapt and succumbs quickly.

The author notes that a few species that tolerate prolonged soil flooding show both metabolic and avoidance traits. This suggests that [[Flood Tolerance]] is conveyed by a combination of adaptations rather than a single one.

## Key Findings

- Plant responses to anoxia are classed into three categories: truly anoxia tolerant (metabolic adaptation), apparently anoxia tolerant (avoidance), and anoxia intolerant.
- Some species that survive prolonged soil flooding use both metabolic and avoidance strategies.
