## Executive Summary
This paper by R. F. Cressey (1970) describes a new species of parasitic copepod, *Hatschekia pacifica*, discovered infesting the gill filaments of the [[Sand Bass]] (*Paralabrax nebulifer*) along the Eastern Pacific coast.
