# Fish Sampling Effort and Catch Datasheets (Promar Trap Collection Forms)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-survey-records

## Executive Summary

This document is a scanned set of about 21 field pages from a fish sampling program. It contains [[Effort Datasheet]] entries, [[Fish Catch Datasheet]] entries, and [[Promar Trap]] collection forms. The pages record sampling dates, start and end times, gear type, bank sampled, participants, individual fish total lengths, ages, and water-quality readings. Much of the OCR text is corrupted, and the fields for species identity, waterbody name, and location are not reliably legible. The note therefore records only what can be read with reasonable confidence and marks everything else as uncertain or N/A.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Field data sheets, not a research article, proposal, or budget.
- **Sampling units:** Each effort sheet covers a sampling session with a start/end time, gear type, and bank designation. Bank is recorded as Left (L), Right (R), or Both (B), determined by facing downstream (the "bank is determined" note is partly legible).
- **Catch records:** Catch sheets list individual fish by [[Total Length]] (TL, in mm), with comments and counts for each entry.
- **Trap collection forms:** These are split across several pages and list trap set and pull times, species codes, life-stage or age entries, counts, and comments.
- **Gear specifications (Promar trap, as printed):** Length 36 in, width 12 in, mouth width 5 in, mesh 1/2 in. A tag notes that some trap boxes were modified to make data align.
- **Spatial reference:** Trap forms include Easting and Northing fields in NAD 83 (see [[NAD83 Coordinate System]]). The values are illegible in the scan.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Water-quality parameters recorded:** [[Water Temperature]] (°C), [[Dissolved Oxygen]] (DO, mg/L), and [[Water Conductivity]]. Most numeric values are missing or unreadable, so no reliable summary of conditions is possible.
- **Species codes:** Catch entries use short codes, apparently "LICA" and "GUN," with a life-stage or age notation (L, M, J, A). These codes are not expanded in the legible text, and no scientific names can be confirmed.
- **Habitat:** The presence of bank designations and in-water trap deployment suggests a freshwater stream or river, but the waterbody is not named.
- **Participants:** Crew names appear on several sheets but are illegible, so they are not recorded here.

## Key Findings

- **Sampling design:** Repeated effort and catch sheets across multiple dates, indicating a multi-session program. The exact number of sessions cannot be confirmed.
- **Gear use:** [[Promar Trap]] sets were used, with set and pull times logged for each deployment.
- **Size data:** Individual fish were measured for TL in mm. Specific values and sample sizes are not reliably legible, so no statistics are reported.
- **Age data:** [[Fish Age Determination]] entries appear alongside species and length, but the values are not legible.
- **Water-quality data:** Temperature, DO, and conductivity fields were completed on several sheets, but the readings are not legible.
- **Data quality concerns:** Heavy OCR corruption affects dates, times, counts, coordinates, and species codes. Original sheets should be checked before any values are used.
- **Recommended next steps:** Re-scan at higher resolution, transcribe the species codes against the program's key, confirm the waterbody and coordinates, and digitize the catch tables into a structured database.
