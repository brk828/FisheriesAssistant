# Operation of Glen Canyon Dam - Section 7 Consultation Memorandum (1990)

#ichthyology #cyprinidae #endangered-species #conservation-management

## Executive Summary

This February 1990 memorandum from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) to the Bureau of Reclamation addresses Section 7 consultation regarding [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations and their impacts on the endangered [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*) in the [[Grand Canyon]]. The FWS concurs with seven proposed conservation measures but identifies a critical conflict between the timing and implementation approach of Conservation Measure 5 (research on dam operation impacts) and the ongoing [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) program. The document emphasizes the need for coordinated, hypothesis-testing research using controlled flows as experimental variables during the Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) preparation period.

## Background & Description

The [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*), a member of the family [[Cyprinidae]], is an endangered species endemic to the [[Colorado River]] system. This consultation addresses how operations of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] affect the species in both mainstream and tributary habitats, particularly the [[Little Colorado River]], which serves as critical spawning habitat. The consultation process involves multiple federal agencies including the Bureau of Reclamation, Fish and Wildlife Service, National Park Service, and Western Area Power Administration, along with state wildlife agencies.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[Humpback Chub]] population in the [[Grand Canyon]] faces threats from dam operations that alter natural flow regimes, water temperature, and sediment transport. The species requires specific habitat conditions for spawning and recruitment. The [[Little Colorado River]] tributary represents a critical spawning aggregation site. Dam operations create artificial flow patterns that differ substantially from the natural hydrological regime to which the species evolved. The consultation addresses the need to understand these impacts through controlled flow experiments and long-term monitoring programs.

## Key Findings

**Conservation Measures Approved:**

1. **Taxonomic Status**: Research on the taxonomic status of Genus *Gila*
2. **Hatchery Stocks**: Maintenance of hatchery stocks of [[Grand Canyon]] [[Humpback Chub]]
3. **Flood Release Criteria**: Establish criteria and probability assessments for flood releases from [[Glen Canyon Dam]]
4. **Little Colorado River Management**: Development of a management plan for the [[Little Colorado River]]
5. **Impact Research**: Conduct research to identify impacts of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations on [[Humpback Chub]] in mainstream and tributaries (with concerns noted)
6. **Monitoring Program**: Establish long-term monitoring to assess relationship between project operations and [[Humpback Chub]] populations
7. **Second Spawning Aggregation**: Establish, if possible, a second spawning aggregation of [[Humpback Chub]] in the [[Grand Canyon]]

**Critical Conflict Identified:**

The FWS identified a fundamental conflict between Conservation Measure 5 and the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) program approach. The Bureau of Reclamation proposed a 4-5 year research effort, while GCES required information collection within 14 months or less using hypothesis-testing approaches with controlled flows as experimental variables. The existing Request for Proposal for Measure 5 did not provide opportunity for proposals utilizing controlled flows experimentally.

**Consultation Process Guidance:**

The FWS addressed three methods to terminate existing consultation (2-21-87-F-23):
- Issue the biological opinion (draft jeopardy opinion existed)
- Federal agency determines proposed action not likely to occur
- Determination with FWS concurrence that action not likely to adversely affect listed species

The FWS recommended the Bureau of Reclamation could terminate current consultation by replacing the proposed action (existing operation) with the preferred EIS alternative.

**Recommendations:**

- Implement all non-conflicting conservation measures at earliest opportunity
- Require interim or final reports for conservation measures to support both Section 7 consultation and EIS development
- Proceed with alternative development process in association with EIS public scoping
- Coordinate research timing between GCES short-term needs and longer-term conservation measure implementation
- Utilize controlled flows as experimental variables in research design

The FWS committed full resources to assist with study plan development, research proposal evaluation, implementation coordination, results interpretation, and alternative evaluation for future [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations.
