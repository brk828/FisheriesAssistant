# Arizona State University Research Park Lake Research Approval

#limnology #freshwater-ecology #aquatic-ecology

## Executive Summary

This is a one-page letter dated August 6, 1986, from the [[Arizona State University Research Park]] to [[Paul Marsh]], Associate Professor-Research at [[Arizona State University]] in Tempe, Arizona. It accepts Marsh's July 22 proposal for research in the Research Park's lakes and encourages him to begin as soon as possible. The letter also asks for a follow-up meeting on related management activities that could affect the research. The document contains no species, sample data, methods, or results.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Administrative correspondence (approval letter), not a research report.
- **Sender:** Research Park office, Tempe, Arizona. The letterhead describes the organization as a not-for-profit corporation focused on university and industry research and technology transfer.
- **Recipient:** Paul Marsh, Associate Professor-Research, Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ 85287.
- **Subject:** A research proposal dated July 22, 1986, for work in the Research Park lakes.
- **Reference initials:** "DM:kb" (typist/author initials, no further significance).
- **Board of Directors** (listed on letterhead): Rudy E. Campbell, Dr. C.R. Haden, Dr. Jack B. Kinsinger, Russ Lyon, Jr., Jack Marinick, Dr. Henry C. Reeves, G.M. Sollenberger, John R. Welty, and Victor M. Zafra. Executive Director: Reginald W. Owens, AICP.
