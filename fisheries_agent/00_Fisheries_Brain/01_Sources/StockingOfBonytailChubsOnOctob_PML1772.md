# Bonytail Chub Stocking Memorandum (October 1994)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal [[Fish And Wildlife Service]] memorandum, addressed to the Arizona Fishery Resources Office (AZFRO) project leader, records the second shipment of hatchery-reared [[Bonytail Chub]] moved from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] for stocking on 24 October 1994. The memo reports the number of fish transported, an estimated mortality figure, a planned stocking schedule at two sites including [[Lake Havasu]], a set of frozen fish retained for later analysis, and a correction about the origin of the fish. The OCR scan is partly illegible, so some figures and names below are marked as uncertain.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), a cyprinid fish that the memo treats as a federally managed hatchery stocking target.
- **Source stock:** Fish were produced from 50 paired matings by hatchery personnel the previous spring.
- **Transport:** A hatchery truck carried the shipment. The memo gives the load as approximately 73,821 fish, which is the best reading of a partly garbled figure.
- **Mortality:** An estimated 36,672 fish were recorded as mortalities. The scanned text is unclear, so this number should be checked against the original.
- **Preserved samples:** A series of dead fish was frozen for later analysis. The memo says the analyses would be coordinated by Mitch Thorson, with input from a Bureau staff member whose name is partly illegible.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Stocking sites:** Released fish were to go into a canyon reach (name garbled in the scan) on 26–27 October 1994, and into [[Lake Havasu]].
- **Recruitment question:** The memo's correction explains that the fish were not wild recruitment from a pond. They were hatchery-reproduced fish, as the earlier memo of 18 October 1994 had wrongly described. This matters for [[Genetic Provenance]] and for judging whether stocked fish represent a self-sustaining population.
- **Conservation role:** The program is a [[Fish Stocking]] and [[Artificial Propagation]] effort aimed at supplementing wild populations of a native Colorado River fish.

## Key Findings

1. **Shipment logistics:** The second bonytail chub shipment left [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] for stocking on 24 October 1994, with about 73,821 fish reported in transit.
2. **Mortality:** An estimated 36,672 fish were recorded as mortalities. This is a major loss and should be verified.
3. **Planned releases:** Stocking was scheduled for 26–27 October 1994 at a canyon reach and at Lake Havasu.
4. **Sample retention:** Dead fish were frozen for later laboratory analysis, coordinated by Mitch Thorson.
5. **Provenance correction:** The fish came from [[Hatchery Broodstock]] (50 paired matings, spring 1994), not from wild recruitment in a pond. This corrects the 18 October 1994 memorandum.
6. **Data caveat:** Several figures, personnel names and the canyon site name are illegible in the scan and should be confirmed from the original record.
