## Executive Summary
The article reports on Arizona Governor Fife Symington's December 1994 call for Congress to allow the [[Endangered Species Act]] to "sunset" rather than be reauthorized. Symington argued the law harms the state economy and infringes property rights, citing the closure of a sawmill in Fredonia, Arizona, linked to habitat protections for the [[Mexican Spotted Owl]] and [[Northern Goshawk]]. Environmental groups, including the [[Sierra Club]] and [[Maricopa County Audubon Society]], opposed his position, defending the ESA's record in recovering species such as the [[Bald Eagle]], [[Peregrine Falcon]], and [[American Alligator]].
