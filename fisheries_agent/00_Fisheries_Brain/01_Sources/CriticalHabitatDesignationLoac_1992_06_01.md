## Key Findings
- The **critical habitat designation** process for the [[Loach Minnow]] and [[Spikedace]] required "reproposal" due to significant time lapse between the original proposed rule and the draft final rule.
- A **draft economic analysis** must be completed *before* the proposed rule can be submitted for publication in the Federal Register.
- **$20,000** in funding was allocated to the FWS Phoenix field office for critical habitat work covering three taxa: the [[Arizona Willow]], [[Loach Minnow]], and [[Spikedace]].
- The Phoenix Field Supervisor was granted discretion over the timeline needed to collect necessary economic data.
- Additional funding beyond the $20,000 allocation could be requested with proper justification.
