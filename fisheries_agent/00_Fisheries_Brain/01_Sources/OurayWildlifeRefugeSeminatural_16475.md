# Restoration of Pond L-7: Semi-Natural Flood Inflows to Pond

#wetland-ecology #habitat-restoration #hydrological-engineering

## Executive Summary

This document presents engineering plans (Scheme 2) for the restoration of [[Pond L-7]] at [[Ouray Wildlife Refuge]]. The design focuses on implementing semi-natural flood inflows to multiple wetland units (L-2 through L-10) through strategic modifications of existing levees, dikes, canals, and water control structures. Water surface elevations range from 4660 to 4666 feet across the various units.

## Background & Description

The restoration scheme encompasses multiple interconnected wetland units within the Ouray Wildlife Refuge system. The existing infrastructure includes:

- Multiple pond units (L-2 through L-10) with varying water surface elevations
- Interior dikes with elevations ranging from 4661 to 4667 feet
- Main dikes (L-3, L-4, L-5, L-6) at elevations 4663-4666 feet
- Existing gravity feed canal
- Intake and pump structure
- Service roads and drainage systems
- Multiple 24-inch corrugated metal pipe culverts (lengths: L=OR, L=48, L=4c)

The site features existing islands, canals, levees, and culverts that require modification to achieve restoration objectives.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The restoration design aims to restore more natural hydrological processes to [[Pond L-7]] and associated wetland units through semi-natural flood inflows. This approach supports [[Wetland Habitat]] function by:

- Allowing controlled flooding to mimic natural hydrological patterns
- Maintaining varied water depths across units (4660-4666 feet elevation)
- Creating diverse aquatic and riparian habitat conditions
- Supporting wildlife refuge management objectives

The system's design incorporates both existing gravity-fed water delivery and pumped water sources, providing flexibility in water management for optimal habitat conditions.

## Key Findings

**Major Engineering Components:**

1. **Levee Modifications:** Removal of all or portions of Pond L-7 riverside levee
2. **Vegetation Management:** Removal of riverside vegetation and pond vegetation
3. **Grading Operations:** Regrading of pond areas and proposed regrading zones
4. **Dike Adjustments:** Raising of interior dikes as necessary
5. **Water Control Structures:** 
   - Proposed low head weirs with riprap
   - Proposed culverts at strategic locations
   - Proposed jetty structures
6. **Drainage Infrastructure:** Maintenance and modification of existing drainage systems

**Hydrological Design:**
- Water surface elevations maintained between 4660-4666 feet
- Multiple interconnected units allowing water exchange
- Integration of existing gravity feed canal with proposed modifications
- Soil moisture dike incorporated into design
