## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from the Arizona Game & Fish Department (AGFD) to Dr. W. L. Minckley of Arizona State University, dated August 3, 1987. It does not contain primary morphological, ecological, or population data on the species in question. Instead, it serves as a procedural clarification regarding reporting deadlines and funding phases for a joint Arizona-New Mexico research study concerning the [[Spikedace]] and [[Loach Minnow]], two imperiled cyprinid fishes native to the Gila River drainage. The letter references a multi-phase study structure contingent on federal Section 6 funding (under the [[Endangered Species Act]]) and outlines a timeline for progress reports and an annual interstate coordination meeting.
