# Lake Mohave Backwater Razorback Harvest, September 27 to October 2, 2010

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

A trip report from the [[U.S. Bureau of Reclamation]] (USBR) Fisheries group, assisted by the [[National Park Service]] (NPS), documents the harvest of [[Razorback Sucker]] from several [[Lake Mohave]] backwaters between September 27 and October 2, 2010. The backwaters had been stocked earlier in 2010. All adult fish were [[Passive Integrated Transponder Tagging|PIT tagged]] at stocking. Crews harvested fish from North Nine Mile, Willow, Arizona Juvenile, Dandy, and Nevada Larvae coves using [[Trammel Netting]], [[Hoop Netting]], and an overnight Oneida net. The report summarizes harvest counts, length data, year-to-date stocking, and dissolved oxygen (DO) readings, and includes a summary table as of the report date.

## Background & Description

The source refers to the fish as "razorbacks," the common name for [[Razorback Sucker]]. The document does not give a scientific name. The species and family entries above are inferred from the common name.

The trip was a continuation of harvesting in backwaters that had been stocked earlier in the year. The report describes the fish as follows:

- **Adult fish:** All adults were PIT tagged at the time of stocking.
- **Juveniles (YOY):** Young-of-year (YOY) fish were also collected, and some were PIT tagged (see Dandy below).
- **Larval studies:** Some fish from North Nine Mile were part of a larval study by Jim Stolberg, which used eggs from hand-spawned [[Lake Mohave]] adults ([[Hand Spawning]]).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Water quality was recorded in several backwaters, mainly as [[Dissolved Oxygen]] readings at the surface and near the bottom. Reported values are:

- **Willow Cove:** DO ranged from 11.7 to 14.3. The pond bottom depth is garbled in the source and cannot be read reliably.
- **Arizona Juvenile:** At 8:30 am, DO ranged from 1.75 to 1.87 (surface and bottom, 0.5 m). At 2:55 pm, DO ranged from 3.67 to 3.24.
- **Dandy:** DO ranged from 13.8 to 7.94 (surface and bottom, 1.1 m).
- **Nevada Larvae:** DO ranged from 6.84 to 6.28 (surface and bottom, 0.7 m).

The source does not give units for DO. The Arizona Juvenile readings show low early-morning oxygen that rose by afternoon, while the Dandy and Willow readings were much higher. The Nevada Larvae pond yielded no fish after two nights of [[Hoop Netting]].

## Key Findings

**North Nine Mile**
- 334 razorbacks harvested, mean total length 175 mm (range 101 to 218 mm).
- Harvested fish were moved to [[Yuma Cove]] and given a left pectoral fin clip. They were part of the larval study.
- Fish left after the study were stocked back into North Nine Mile in May 2010: 1,723 razorbacks, mean length 20 mm.

**Willow Cove**
- 34 razorbacks harvested to date, mean total length 476 mm (range 434 to 508 mm).
- All were repatriated into [[Lake Mohave]] at Willow Cove.

**Arizona Juvenile**
- 126 adult razorbacks harvested to date, mean total length 432 mm (range 358 to 495 mm).
- 2,676 YOY were produced, mean total length 103 mm. The range is garbled in the source and cannot be read reliably.
- All fish were placed into Lake Mohave at Arizona Juvenile.
- The summary table lists 128 adults for this site, which differs from the 126 in the text.

**Dandy**
- 133 adult razorbacks harvested to date, mean total length 444 mm (range 375 to 485 mm).
- 32 YOY harvested, mean total length 239 mm (range 165 to 265 mm). All Dandy YOY were PIT tagged.
- All razorbacks were placed into Lake Mohave at Dandy Cove.

**Nevada Larvae**
- Hoop-netted for two nights; zero fish were produced to date.

**Summary Table (as of the report date)**
- Total: 500 razorbacks, mean length 451 mm.
- The table is partly unreadable after OCR. Row labels and some values (including the percent of stocked fish) cannot be reliably matched.
- Yuma and Davis coves were harvested previously and were not stocked in 2010.

**Data-quality notes**
- The per-site totals in the text (334 + 34 + 126 + 133 = 627) do not match the table total of 500. The table may reflect a different "as of" date or cutoff.
- Several values (DO depth labels, one YOY size range, and some table cells) are garbled in the source.
