# Museum Records of Cyprinella formosa from Mimbres River, New Mexico

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document comprises museum correspondence and specimen records from May 1995 regarding holdings of [[Cyprinella formosa]] (Beautiful Shiner) from the [[Mimbres River]] in [[New Mexico]]. The records document specimens held at the Museum of Southwestern Biology (MSB) and the University of Michigan Museum of Zoology (UMMZ), collected between 1938 and 1950 from the [[Rio Grande Drainage]] system.

## Background & Description

[[Cyprinella formosa]] is a cyprinid fish species documented in historical collections from the [[Mimbres River]] in [[Grant County]] and [[Luna County]], [[New Mexico]]. The correspondence involves three institutions: [[Arizona State University]] (Dr. W.L. Minckley), [[New Mexico Department of Game and Fish]] (Dr. D.L. Propst), and museum collections at MSB (Lex Snyder) and UMMZ (Doug Nelson).

The specimens represent material collected by W.J. Koster and the Hubbs family during mid-20th century ichthyological surveys of New Mexico waters. All specimens were preserved in 70% ethanol following standard museum protocols.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Habitat Characteristics:**
- Collection locations in the [[Mimbres River]], part of the [[Rio Grande Drainage]] system
- Specific sites: 5 miles below Fayewood (Dwyer) and near Columbus
- UMMZ locality: approximately 14 miles by road north of US 260, near corner of Grant and Luna Counties on State Highway 61, 90 miles northwest of Deming

**Environmental Conditions (UMMZ 124741 collection):**
- Air temperature: 79°F
- Water temperature: 65°F
- Habitat type: pools and riffles
- Stream width: variable
- Water depth: maximum 3-12 inches
- Current present in pools and riffles

## Key Findings

**MSB Holdings:**

1. **MSB 1266** (Cyprinella formosa):
   - Collector: W.J. Koster
   - Date: 30 August 1950
   - Location: Mimbres River, 5 miles below Fayewood (Dwyer), Luna County
   - Township: T20S R10W S7
   - Preservation: 70% ethanol

2. **MSB 1363** (Cyprinella formosa):
   - Collector: W.J. Koster
   - Date: 01 June 1949
   - Sample size: N=2
   - Size range: 24-36 mm SL
   - Location: Near Columbus, Luna County
   - Preservation: 70% ethanol

**UMMZ Holdings:**

3. **UMMZ 124741** (Notropis formosus, synonym of Cyprinella formosa):
   - Designated as topotypes by Hubbs & Greenbank
   - Collector: Hubbs family
   - Date: 30 June 1938, 1700-1830 hours
   - Sample size: 100 specimens
   - Size range: 19-40 mm SL
   - Location: Mimbres River about 14 miles by road north of US 260, near corner of Grant and Luna Counties, T20S R10W
   - Original field number: M38-94
   - Original preservation: 10% formalin
   - Note: Catalog card indicates "many additional remarks" and reference to "Moniana formosa Chernoff"

**Collection Status:**
- MSB reported no specimens of [[Cyprinella lutrensis]] from the Mimbres River in their holdings
- UMMZ confirmed no [[Cyprinella lutrensis]] specimens from the [[Mimbres River]] in their catalogued collections
- This represents the only UMMZ catalogued record for N. formosus from [[New Mexico]]
