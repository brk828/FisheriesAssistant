# Preliminary Data on the Structure of the Sierra Madre Occidental (Yecora-Maicova Sector)

#geology #tectonics #volcanology #Sierra-Madre-Occidental

## Executive Summary

This geological study presents new cartographic data from the Yecora-Maicova sector of the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] in [[Sonora]], [[Mexico]], between parallels 28°15' and 28°30'. The research demonstrates that the Tertiary volcanic sequence must distinguish between basalts and ignimbrites, with ignimbrites poorly represented on the western slope while significant volumes of continental tholeiitic basalts are associated with Mio-Plio-Quaternary extensional movements that shaped the current horst-and-graben morphology. The study modifies previous understanding of the Sierra Madre structure, which was largely extrapolated from the Durango-Mazatlan transect.

## Background & Description

The [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] extends over 1500 km south of the United States border and is classically defined as a vast Cenozoic ignimbritic plateau. Access difficulties have limited geological study since King's initial reconnaissance in 1939. Previous work concentrated on the Durango-Mazatlan sector, the Chihuahua region (uranium deposits in ignimbrites), and the Tomochic-Ocampo transect.

The mapped area lies between the [[Rio Yaqui]] to the west and the Chihuahua state border to the east, on the western margin of the Sierra Madre Occidental. West of Yecora, Laramian granites extensively outcrop at the Sierra's base, cutting through a Triassic-Mesozoic series of sedimentary rocks and andesites (the "Basal Volcanic Complex"). This basement is covered by Tertiary ignimbrites and basalts forming the "Upper Complex."

## Key Findings

**Volcanic Sequence Stratigraphy:**
- The Tertiary volcanic sequence is exceptionally complete in the Yecora-Maicova region
- Basal unit: vitroclastic tuffs and acidic ignimbrites resting in slight unconformity (via indurated red conglomerate) on folded Mesozoic andesitic-sedimentary series
- Upper sequence: alternating acidic-basic units (rhyolitic to rhyodacitic pumice tuffs alternating with basalts and basaltic andesites), with basalts predominating toward the top
- Dismantling of volcanic units during Miocene vertical movements fed the thick continental detrital [[Baucarit Formation]]

**Structural Architecture:**
- General NNW-SSE structural orientation reflecting [[Basin and Range]] extensional phase affecting NW Mexico and SW United States since the Miocene
- Two structure types: (1) horsts and grabens bounded by normal faults; (2) tilted blocks along curved faults characteristic of high-extension regions
- Two NNW-SSE oriented depressions: Yecora depression (west) and Los Rios depression (east), filled with the Baucarit Formation
- Basalt flows horizontal in grabens (thickness exceeding 300 m) but extensively tilted in sierras bordering Yecora Valley
- Between depressions: basement reappears as Laramian granodiorite outcrops (south) and Mesozoic limestones (north)
- [[Maicova]] horst: extensive granodiorite outcrops intrusive into basal complex andesites

**Volcanic Centers and Faulting:**
- Major faults marked by domes of porphyritic rhyolites and dacites with quartz, biotite, and plagioclase
- Most basalt emission points localized along these faults
- Basalt facies vary: doleritic, olivine-rich porphyritic, plagioclase-phyric, and aphanitic (toward top)

**Geochemical Character:**
- Basalts are quartz- or olivine-normative, plotting in the continental tholeiite domain
- This basalt type typically associated with early rift opening phases (comparable to Ethiopian plateau basalts)

**Tectonic-Magmatic Evolution:**
1. **Basal Complex** (Cretaceous): calc-alkaline magmatism (Mesozoic andesites and Laramian granodiorites) related to paleo-subduction of the [[Farallon Plate]] beneath the North American continent; exposed in Maicova horst and uplifted sides of tilted blocks
2. **Oligocene Ignimbrites**: relatively limited outcrop in Sonora, restricting discovery of large caldera structures; emplaced after decreased magmatic activity, possibly linked to Farallon Plate consumption phase
3. **Upper Basalts**: forming upper Cenozoic sequence, linked to Basin and Range extensional phase; characteristics (enormous horizontal accumulations of thin flows, age contemporaneous with graben collapse, emission point localization on NNW-SSE major faults, continental tholeiite nature) comparable to basalts emitted in early stages of major continental rift opening

**Morphological Impact:**
- Basin and Range tectonics exerted predominant influence on current horst-and-graben morphology of the Sierra Madre Occidental in Sonora
