# Where to Find Water Expertise at State Universities in Arizona

#ichthyology #marine-biology #water-resources

Note on scope: this document is a directory of water experts, not a research study. It contains no species-level data. Fish-related and aquatic entries are recorded only where the directory lists them.

## Executive Summary

This is the third revised edition (January 1998) of a directory published by the [[Water Resources Research Center]] at the University of Arizona, College of Agriculture, as Issue Paper Number Thirteen. It lists 201 people with water-related expertise at Arizona's three state universities: the [[University of Arizona]], [[Arizona State University]], and [[Northern Arizona University]]. Information was gathered by surveying university departments, including Cooperative Extension, and is reported as given by respondents. The 1998 edition added 38 new entries and removed ten. The directory is organized into four chapters (alphabetical listing, general areas, specific keywords, and geographic areas and languages) plus a keyword appendix. A searchable version is hosted on the WRRC web site.

## Background & Description

- **Publisher:** [[Water Resources Research Center]], The University of Arizona, College of Agriculture, 350 N. Campbell Avenue, Tucson, Arizona 85721.
- **Author:** [[Barbara Tellman]], Senior Research Specialist, WRRC.
- **Series:** Issue Paper Number Thirteen; the directory is the third in a series. Volume I, *Where to Get Free (Or Almost Free) Information About Water in Arizona*, covers agencies and organizations. A companion guide covers technical information sources such as databases and specialized libraries.
- **Edition:** Third Revised Edition, January 1998. Free copies available on request.
- **Number of experts:** 201 (as stated in the introduction).
- **Structure:**
  - Chapter One: Alphabetical listing with position, campus address, phone, areas of expertise, geographic specialties, and languages.
  - Chapter Two: General areas of expertise (e.g., Agriculture/Ranching, Hydrology, Water Quality).
  - Chapter Three: Specific areas of expertise (keywords such as [[Aquatic Ecosystems]], [[Fish/Fisheries/Ichthyology]], [[Benthos]], [[Constructed Wetlands]]).
  - Chapter Four: Geographic areas and language skills.
  - Appendix: Alphabetical keyword lists.
- **Web resources:** [[WRRC Web Site]] (http://ag.arizona.edu/AZWATER/), with searchable data, complex queries, and direct email links. Main university sites: www.asu.edu, www.nau.edu, www.arizona.edu.

### How to Use the Directory

The directory is designed for a multi-step lookup. The worked example describes finding a hydrologist with expertise in water harvesting and irrigation in Middle Eastern deserts who speaks German. The user checks Chapter Two (Hydrology), then Chapter Three (Water Harvesting, Irrigation), then Chapter Four (Middle East/North Africa, German). The search leads to Sol Resnick, a Director Emeritus at the WRRC, whose details are in Chapter One.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The directory covers water-related fields including ecology, hydrology, agricultural economics, flood control, water law, and water pollution. Relevant aquatic and ecological topics it lists include:

- **Aquatic and riparian systems:** [[Aquatic Ecosystems]], [[Riparian Areas/Wetlands]], [[Lakes/Ponds]], [[Springs]], [[Hyporheic Zone]], [[Phreatophytes]].
- **Aquatic biota:** [[Algae]], [[Benthos]], [[Invertebrates]], [[Insects]], [[Fish/Fisheries/Ichthyology]], [[Shellfish]], [[Birds/Ornithology]], [[Herpetology]].
- **Water quality and chemistry:** [[Eutrophication]], [[Nitrogen]], [[Heavy Metals]], [[Toxic Substances]], [[Water Chemistry]], [[Non-Point Source Pollution]].
- **Climate and hydrologic change:** [[Climate Change/Global Warming]], [[Drought]], [[Flash Floods]], [[Evapotranspiration]].
- **Management:** [[Watershed Management]], [[Ecosystem Management]], [[Instream Flow]], [[Threatened/Endangered Species]], [[Restoration/Revegetation]].

Marine and coastal entries are also present (e.g., [[Marine Biology]], [[Estuaries]], [[Coastal Zone]], [[Seawater]], [[Brackish Water]]), although Arizona is landlocked, so these reflect broader expertise rather than local habitat.

## Key Findings

The directory's main outputs are as follows:

1. **Scale of expertise:** 201 individuals are listed across three universities. The 1998 revision added 38 entries and removed 10.
2. **Fish and aquatic specialists listed:** The directory names experts in [[Fish/Fisheries/Ichthyology]] including [[Thomas Dowling]] (Arizona State University, Zoology), [[James Elser]] (Arizona State University, Zoology; aquatic ecosystems and ecosystem management), [[Kevin Fitzsimmons]] (University of Arizona, Environmental Research Laboratory), [[Philip Hastings]] (University of Arizona, Ecology and Evolutionary Biology), [[John Lundberg]] (University of Arizona, Ecology and Evolutionary Biology), [[Paul C. Marsh]] (Arizona State University, Center for Environmental Studies), [[O. Eugene Maughan]] (University of Arizona, Wildlife and Fisheries Science), [[W. Linn Montgomery]] (Northern Arizona University, Biological Sciences; marine biology), and [[Edella Schlager]] (University of Arizona, Public Administration and Policy).
3. **Most-represented general areas:** Based on the keyword lists in Chapter Two, [[Water Quality]] and [[Agriculture/Ranching]] have the longest lists of names. [[Hydrology]], [[Groundwater]], [[Management/Planning]], and [[Surface Water]] are also heavily represented.
4. **Geographic coverage:** Chapter Four lists experts by region. Southwestern United States has the largest group. Other listed regions include Mexico, Central America, South America, Europe, Middle East/North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, Asia, and Arctic/Antarctic.
5. **Language skills:** Chapter Four also lists language proficiencies, with Spanish, German, and French most frequently named.
6. **Search method:** Users can locate an expert by combining general areas, specific keywords, geographic areas, and languages, then confirming contact details in Chapter One.
7. **Limitations:** Entries are abbreviated and self-reported. Where a category is absent for an individual, the person did not supply that information. The directory states that most experts are assumed to know Arizona matters even when not explicitly listed.
8. **Currency:** The information reflects the 1998 edition. Affiliations and contact details may have changed, and the WRRC web site is the recommended source for updates.
