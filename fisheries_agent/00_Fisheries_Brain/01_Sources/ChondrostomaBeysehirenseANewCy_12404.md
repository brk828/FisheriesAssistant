# Chondrostoma beysehirense: A New Cyprinid Fish from Beysehir Lake, Central Turkey

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This paper describes *Chondrostoma beysehirense* as a new species of cyprinid fish from [[Beysehir Lake]] in [[Central Anatolia]], Turkey. Specimens from the lake had previously been assigned to [[Chondrostoma Regium]], and the author concludes from morphological comparison with 15 *Chondrostoma* species and a review of the literature that the Beysehir material represents a distinct species. The species is diagnosed by an almost straight mouth cleft, a well-developed corneous layer on the lower jaw, a strongly laterally compressed body, and a high gill-raker count. It is known only from its type locality. The description is based on one holotype and three paratypes held in the [[Zoologisches Museum Hamburg]] (ZMH).

## Background & Description

The genus *Chondrostoma* Agassiz, 1835 includes 17–20 species distributed across Europe, the Caucasus, the Middle East and Asia Minor. Six species are recognised in Asia Minor and adjacent regions, following the revisions of Elvira (1987, 1991). Earlier authors (Kosswig, Ladiges, Erdemli, Kuru) treated Beysehir specimens as *C. regium*, and Elvira (1987a) suggested they might merit subspecific rank. The present study shows the material differs from *C. regium* in several morphological characters.

**Type material:**
- Holotype: ZMH 8812, 146.8 mm SL, Beysehir Lake, collected 28 August 1955.
- Paratypes: ZMH 1145 (2 specimens, 98.5–115.2 mm SL, same data as holotype); ZMH 6636 (3 specimens, 102.2–102.8 mm SL, collected by C. Kosswig, August–September 1964).

**Diagnosis (key characters):**
- Mouth cleft almost straight; lower jaw with a prominent corneous layer.
- Dorsal fin rays iv,9; anal fin rays iii–iv,(9)10(11).
- Lateral line scales 65–71; gill rakers 34–40; total vertebrae 45–46.
- Modal pharyngeal tooth formula 7-7.
- Scales with an undulate posterior margin.
- Strongly compressed body with scaled keels in front of the dorsal fin and between the pectoral and pelvic fins.

**Morphology:** The head is narrow and triangular in lateral view, with an elongate, rounded snout. The mouth is inferior. The caudal peduncle is about twice as long as the minimum body depth. Pharyngeal teeth are long, narrow and laterally compressed. Cephalic sensory canal pore counts include 12–16 supraorbital pores, 19–22 infraorbital pores, 15–20 preopercular-mandibular pores, and 10–12 supratemporal pores.

**Coloration:** Alcohol-preserved specimens are light brown dorsally, grading to creamy white ventrally, with small dark dots on the back and upper flanks. The dorsal and caudal fins have dark marginal bands.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Habitat:** [[Beysehir Lake]] is a freshwater lake with a maximum depth of 13 m. It lies within the Central Anatolian endorheic area on the Central Anatolian Plateau at about 1000 m a.s.l., a region dominated by lakes without outlet (including Burdur, Egridir, Aksehir, Beysehir and Tuz).

**Biogeography:** The Central Anatolian region shows comparatively high fish endemism, with several endemic cyprinids, such as *Gobio hettitorum*, *Alburnus akili* (reported only from Beysehir Lake), *Pseudophoxinus* species and *Capoeta pestai*. In Central Anatolia, *Chondrostoma* has been reported only from Beysehir Lake. Pooled literature records from the Beysehir basin cannot confirm whether true *C. regium* occurs there.

**Relationships:** Among *Chondrostoma* species, *C. beysehirense* is most similar to [[Chondrostoma Kinzelbachi]], sharing the highest gill-raker range in the genus (32–40 in *C. kinzelbachi*), a 7-7 tooth formula and undulate scale margins. The two species differ in body shape, head proportions, snout form and vertebral and fin-ray counts. Other comparisons were made with *C. holmwoodii*, *C. nasus*, *C. colchicum*, *C. oxyrhynchum* and *C. regium*.

## Key Findings

- *Chondrostoma beysehirense* is described as new based on one holotype and three paratypes from Beysehir Lake. It is known only from the type locality.
- Meristic values: 9 branched dorsal rays, (9)10(11) branched anal rays, 65–71 lateral line scales, 34–40 gill rakers (the highest in the genus), 45–46 total vertebrae, and a modal 7-7 pharyngeal tooth formula.
- Morphometric comparison (Table 1) gives head length 23.7–24.6% of SL and maximum body depth about equal to head length, and reports that the body is strongly compressed with keels.
- Compared with [[Chondrostoma Regium]] of similar size (99.0–150.5 mm SL), *C. beysehirense* has more gill rakers, a modal 7-7 tooth formula versus 6-6, fewer branched dorsal rays (modal 9 vs. 10), an undulate versus smooth scale margin, fewer total vertebrae (modal 45 or 46 vs. 47), a longer head, a shorter eye and a shallower nape.
- Compared with [[Chondrostoma Kinzelbachi]], the new species has fewer branched dorsal and anal rays, fewer vertebrae, a narrower, more compressed body, a longer head and a more pointed snout.
- Compared with [[Chondrostoma Holmwoodii]], it differs in body shape and in scale radii pattern.
- Osteological features (neurocranium, pharyngeal process, premaxilla, maxilla, dentary) distinguish the species, illustrated in Figure 4.
- Earlier reports of *C. regium* from Beysehir are considered to refer to *C. beysehirense*, as the material was labelled by Kosswig and the Kuru (1981) data cannot be separated from true *C. regium*.
- The species name refers to the type locality, Beysehir Lake.
