# Humpback Chub Population Estimate in Black Rocks, FY 2004 Annual Report

#ichthyology #marine-biology #humpback-chub

## Executive Summary

This Annual Project Report from the [[Colorado River Recovery Program]] (Project 131, FY 2004) describes work toward a second population estimate for [[Humpback Chub]] in the [[Black Rocks]] reach of the [[Colorado River]]. Robust population estimates at regular intervals are needed to judge how the [[Humpback Chub]] population responds to recovery management, as called for by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (2001). The study runs FY 2003 to FY 2005. Sampling spans September and October, so the report is split by calendar year: 2003 sampling was analyzed in FY 2004, and 2004 sampling analysis and the final report are scheduled for FY 2005.

The 2003 season yielded 70 individual humpback chub, with model estimates from 365 to 597 fish. Confidence intervals were wide, and coefficients of variation did not meet [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] population goal recommendations. The 2004 season yielded 77 individuals collected, but data analysis had not started at the time of writing. The project is on track and fully funded for FY 2004.

## Background & Description

**Species.** The report focuses on [[Humpback Chub]], which is the target of the estimate. [[Roundtail Chub]] are also captured, mainly by [[Electrofishing]]. The document does not give scientific names, morphology, or life history details.

**Study purpose.** The [[Colorado River Recovery Program]] needs population size estimates at regular intervals to monitor recovery under the [[Colorado River Action Plan]] (mainstem, item V.C.1, Black Rocks). The study has a schedule of FY 2003 to FY 2005 and builds on a population estimate made for the 1999–2000 period (McAda 2004).

**Project leadership.** The principal investigator is Chuck McAda, project leader at the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] office in Grand Junction, Colorado.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Study site.** All sampling took place in the Black Rocks reach of the mainstem [[Colorado River]]. Electrofishing covered the entire reach during the 2003 season.

**Sampling methods.**
- **[[Trammel Netting]]:** The main capture tool. Nets were set for about 1 to 1.5 hours to limit stress and time between net checks. Fewer nets were run at one time. Trammel nets with 0.5-in inner mesh caught smaller fish, but the catch was much lower than with the 1-in inner mesh (the document's "f-in" is read as 1-in).
- **[[Electrofishing]]:** Used more heavily in 2003, with one early-morning and one evening session per trip. In 2004 it was used at least twice per trip to capture smaller chubs and to sample areas where trammel nets were ineffective. It was most effective for [[Roundtail Chub]], though some humpback chub were also taken.
- **[[PIT Tagging]]:** Fish were tagged, and tag numbers and data for stocked fish were submitted to the project database.

**Population structure.** The 2003 length-frequency distribution (appendix figure, total length about 19 to 39 cm) was similar to earlier years.

## Key Findings

### 2003 Season
- Four sampling trips were held in September and October, with the last in the final week of October.
- A total of **70 individual humpback chub** were captured.
- **Five** fish were recaptured: one within the same sampling rotation and four in different rotations.
- **Twelve** of the humpback chub captured had been tagged in previous years.
- Population estimates were presented at the September workshop. Confidence intervals were wide, and the coefficient of variation (CV) and P-hat values did not meet the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] population goal recommendations.

### 2004 Season
- Four sampling trips were held: September 14–17 and 27–30, and October 12–15 and 26–29.
- Sampling protocol was similar to 2003, with trammel nets set for 1 to 1.5 hours. Most humpback chub were captured in trammel nets.
- A total of **77 individual humpback chub** were collected.
- Only **one** humpback chub was recaptured within the study period.
- **22** humpback chub recaptured in 2004 had been tagged in previous years.
- Data have been computerized, but no analysis had been completed when the report was written.
