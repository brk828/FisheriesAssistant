# Proposed Joint Counterpart Section 7 Consultation Regulations (1995)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #endangered-species-act

## Executive Summary

This document is a 1995 Federal Register proposed rule and a covering memorandum. Together they describe proposed **joint counterpart regulations** under the [[Endangered Species Act]] (ESA) for [[Section 7 Consultation]]. The rule would give the [[Forest Service]] (USFS) and [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) a larger role in consulting on their own actions, while the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] (FWS) and the [[National Marine Fisheries Service]] (NMFS) keep their consultation responsibilities. The memo, sent by the Arizona Ecological Services State Office to the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]], asks members to review the proposal and submit comments. The proposal is regulatory and contains no fish population, ecological or water-quality data.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Proposed rule published in the *Federal Register* (Vol. 60, No. 150, Friday, August 4, 1995, Proposed Rules, beginning at page 39922; the proposed regulatory text starts on page 39923). The rule would amend 50 CFR Part 402.
- **Issuing agencies:** FWS and NMFS (Department of the Interior and Department of Commerce), jointly with USFS and BLM.
- **Subject:** Procedures for Section 7 consultation on federal actions that may affect listed species or [[Critical Habitat]], including a new "non-site-specific" (programmatic) consultation track.
- **Comment deadline:** The comment period closed October 3, 1995. Comments were to go to the Chief, Division of Endangered Species, FWS, in Washington, DC. The memo asks the recovery team to send any group comments to the Regional Director before that date.
- **Memo:** Dated August 24, 1995, from the Arizona Ecological Services State Office. It is addressed to Desert Fishes Recovery Team members and consultants, with the Recovery Coordinator in Albuquerque, New Mexico, copied.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not describe any particular fish species, habitat, water body or ecosystem. Its context is the protection of listed species and their habitat from federal actions. The memo links the proposal to the desert fish recovery effort, so the rule would affect how land-management actions in desert fish habitat are reviewed. The text does not give species names, population counts or habitat measurements.

The proposal is intended to close a gap in the existing Section 7 regulations. Those regulations did not clearly allow ongoing activities to continue while consultation on newly listed species was under way. The rule also sets out procedures for handling "non-site-specific" consultations on land management plans, which do not authorize individual projects.

## Key Findings

**Scope of the proposal**
- The proposed regulations set up a multi-level system. USFS and BLM would handle some of their own consultations, and the rule applies only to some USFS and BLM consultations.
- The rule would not apply to all federal actions. Other agencies stay under the existing framework.

**Non-site-specific (programmatic) consultation**
- At USFS and BLM's discretion, consultation on land management plans could be non-site-specific. The result would be a consultation agreement that sets standards, guidelines and parameters for future actions.
- The consultation agreement could be mandatory or non-mandatory, depending on the proposal. The memo notes that the proposed text does not give criteria for what a "written concurrence" from the Service would cover.
- Site-specific actions that conform to the non-site-specific parameters would not need separate consultation. The rule gives no criteria for how a USFS or BLM finding of conformance would be made.

**Determinations and disagreements**
- Under the proposal, USFS and BLM could make their own "no jeopardy" or "not likely to adversely affect" findings for some actions. The Service's role would shift to review.
- Under the proposed text, the Service would not be able to disagree with USFS and BLM findings in some cases. The memo says the criteria for when the Service may disagree are unclear.

**Interim activities and reinitiation**
- The proposal addresses how ongoing activities may continue while consultation is under way, and sets out when [[Reinitiation of Consultation]] is required.
- The rule identifies a process for revisiting consultation when new species are listed, critical habitat is designated, or land management plans are revised.

**Standards referenced**
- The rule uses the [[Jeopardy Standard]] and the [[Adverse Modification]] standard as the basis for consultation decisions.

**Memo's assessment and recommendations**
- The memo summarizes the proposal as a major change to how Section 7 would operate for USFS and BLM. It suggests recovery team members read the summary and then go directly to the proposed regulatory text.
- Comments may be submitted by individual members or as the recovery team. The memo does not give specific recommendations on the content of the rule.

**Regulatory analysis**
- The document states that the rule was reviewed under Executive Order 12866. It also notes that the rule may affect small entities, so a Regulatory Flexibility Act analysis is included.
