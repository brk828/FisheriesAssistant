# Painted Rock Dam Collecting Trip Report (24 June 1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #mugilidae #cichlidae #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal trip report from the [[Arizona State University]] Center for Environmental Studies documents a fish collecting trip at [[Painted Rock Dam]], Maricopa County, Arizona, on 24 June 1997. Personnel from Arizona State University and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] used trammel nets and a seine to sample fish. [[Striped Mullet]] were the most abundant catch, and juvenile [[Tilapia]] were collected from an area containing nest-like depressions. The report also notes similar nests seen at [[Alamo Lake]] the following day. Specimens are destined for the Arizona State University fish collection.

## Background & Description

- **Purpose:** Field collection of fish specimens for preservation and deposition in the Arizona State University collection of fishes.
- **Personnel:** T. Inman and P. Sowka (Arizona State University); M. Thorson, A. Paddock, and B. Smith (USFWS).
- **Gear:**
  - A 150 ft [[Trammel Net]] set about 3 m from shore in front of a boat launch ramp.
  - A second trammel net set about 15 m from shore in a circular pattern from a boat.
  - A 15 ft [[Seine Net]] used in the area immediately upstream of the boat launch.
- **Preservation:** Mullet were injected with 10% [[Formalin Preservation]] solution.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- Striped mullet were collected in about 1 m of water just downstream of the dam. They were abundant and were observed leaping from the water.
- The seine catch upstream of the boat launch included a mix of species that reflects warm-water, low-elevation Arizona waters: juvenile Tilapia, [[Red Shiner]], [[Mosquitofish]], and [[Sailfin Molly]]. [[Channel Catfish]] and [[Common Carp]] were also captured with the seine and released.
- Six nests, approximately 0.75 m wide and 0.5 m deep, were observed at the dam. The author describes them as similar to nests at Alamo Lake (La Paz County, AZ, 25 June 1997).
- Juvenile Tilapia were collected from the nest area. Nest-building behavior by adult Tilapia was not confirmed because no adult Tilapia were collected.

## Key Findings

- **Striped mullet abundance:** Mullet were abundant downstream of the dam and were captured in both trammel nets. Two were obtained from the first net and immediately preserved. The second net yielded additional mullet, and four more were preserved from it.
- **Mullet size range:** Preserved mullet measured 440 mm to 565 mm total length.
- **Incidental catch:** The second trammel net also captured 3 common carp.
- **Seine catch:** Juvenile Tilapia, red shiner, mosquitofish, and sailfin molly were collected and preserved. Channel catfish and common carp were captured and released.
- **Nests:** Six nests (about 0.75 m wide and 0.5 m deep) were observed at Painted Rock Dam, resembling those at Alamo Lake.
- **Open question:** Adult Tilapia were not collected, so the report cannot confirm that they built the nests.
- **Disposition:** Mullet specimens will be deposited in the [[Arizona State University Fish Collection]].
