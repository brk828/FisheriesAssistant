# Phoenix Zoo and Dexter Hatchery Fish Capture Records

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-capture-records

## Executive Summary

This document is a set of scanned, partly illegible field and hatchery data sheets rather than a research paper. It contains capture records for fish of a single recorded species, "Gilele" (an OCR reading that could not be verified). The records come from two sources: capture data sheets from the [[Phoenix Zoo]] dated December 22, 2000, and a second capture session dated October 9–14 (year unclear in the scan). Several pages are blank hatchery forms from the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery and Technology Center]]. Each record lists a total length, a weight, a sex field (mostly "?"), a [[PIT Tag]] number and a status, which is "recap" (recapture) for the entries that are legible. No analysis, conclusions or methodology text is present.

## Background & Description

The records follow a standard fish-handling format. Each entry includes:

- **Total length (TL)** in the units recorded on the sheet (not clearly stated in the OCR).
- **Weight**, recorded alongside length.
- **Sex**, which is unknown ("?") in every legible entry.
- **PIT tag number**, an individual identifier read by a passive integrated transponder reader.
- **Status**, which marks the animal as a recapture (see [[Mark-Recapture]]).

Pages 1 and 2 hold the bulk of the data. Page 1 contains a short table of about five dated records, and page 2 contains a numbered list running to roughly 49 entries, each with a tag number, a length and a weight. Pages 3 to 5 are mostly blank form headers showing a date and a species field at the Dexter hatchery.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

## Key Findings

- **Recorded species:** the header reads "Gilele." This is most likely an OCR error, and the document does not confirm the species name. No family, habitat or basin can be assigned from the text.
- **Capture sessions:** at least two sessions are present. The Phoenix Zoo sheet is dated December 22, 2000, and the second is dated October 9–14 (year illegible).
- **Page 1 records:** about five individuals are listed, with lengths in the approximate range of 345–415 units and weights in the approximate range of 260–500 units. The values are partly corrupted by the scan.
- **Tagging:** each legible record carries a unique 10-character PIT tag. Recaptures are marked as "recap."
- **Sex:** not determined for any legible individual.
- **Page 2 dataset:** a numbered list of roughly 49 entries with tag numbers, lengths and weights. The values are too degraded to transcribe reliably, so exact summary statistics (means, ranges, sample sizes) cannot be given without manual verification of the original scans.
- **Hatchery forms (pages 3–5):** mostly blank, with date and species fields only, so they add no data.

**Data-quality note:** the scan quality is poor. Dates, species name, units and many numerical values are uncertain. Anyone using this record should check it against the original paper sheets before relying on any figure.
