# Bonytail Stocking Records and Correspondence (1996–2005)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a partly garbled email exchange (OCR scan) between a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service staff member, Chuck McAda, and a colleague, Carol. It concerns compiling [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) stocking records. The sender has tables of stocking data from 1996 to 2004 and is preparing a separate summary for 2005. He notes that the earlier summary contains minor data inconsistencies. The attachment is titled "Bonytail stocking summary 1996-2004" and a second file covers "Bonytail stocking for 2005." The main content is a table of individual stocking events, listing the stocking agency, date, site, number of fish, and length data.

The species name is not stated in the document. It is inferred from the common name "bonytail."

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document contains no habitat, water chemistry, or population ecology data. Its ecological relevance comes from its subject. Bonytail is a native Colorado River fish, and the stocking records document reintroduction efforts for a species of conservation concern. Stocking sites in the table are coded "CO" (apparently Colorado River reaches) and "GR" (apparently Green River reaches). Some entries also list locations such as "Grand Valley" and "Fontenelle." Both appear as site names in the OCR text, but the document does not describe them. Stocking across multiple agencies and river reaches shows coordinated management across state and federal jurisdictions within the [[Colorado River Basin]].

## Key Findings

1. **Stocking history is documented from 1996 onward.** The records cover events from roughly October 1996 through 2004, with the 2005 season handled separately.
2. **Multi-agency stocking.** UDWR, CDOW, and USFWS all appear as stocking sources in the table.
3. **Data quality issues.** McAda states that minor data inconsistencies exist in the earlier summary and that he will send a consolidated version. Users should verify figures against the original summary files.
4. **Stocking events are recorded individually.** Entries include dates such as October 1996, October 1997, March 1998, April 1998, March 1999, and October 2002, with numbers stocked varying widely. Many counts are illegible or incomplete in the scan and are not reproduced here.
5. **Length data at stocking.** Recorded mean lengths range from about 112 mm to about 415 mm. Examples include mean lengths of 131.1 mm, 318.4 mm, 415.3 mm, and 112.3 mm, and a group mean of 163 mm. Size ranges include 72–187 mm, 119–351 mm, 329–495 mm, and 357–421 mm. Some values are OCR-damaged and were checked against the legible text only.
6. **Scope limit.** The document is a correspondence and data-transmittal item. It presents no analysis, survival estimates, or conclusions about stocking success.
