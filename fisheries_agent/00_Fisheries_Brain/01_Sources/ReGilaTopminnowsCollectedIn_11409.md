# Gila Topminnow Captive Stock Maintenance Report (1995)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This short 1995 correspondence, signed by Ruby Jo Sheffer and dated 12/27/95, reports the status of [[Gila Topminnow]] captive stocks. The laboratory made no wild collections in 1995. Stocks collected from four sites in 1994 are being maintained at the Aquatic Facility of the [[Animal Resource Center]] on the Tempe campus of [[Arizona State University]]. All captive stocks are reported as thriving.
