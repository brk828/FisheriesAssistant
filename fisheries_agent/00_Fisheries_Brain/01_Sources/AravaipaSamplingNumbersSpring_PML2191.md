# Aravaipa Creek Fish Survey Records, Spring and Fall 2003

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This document is an email correspondence from [[Peter Reinthal]], Associate Curator at the [[University of Arizona]], transmitting raw fish count data from two sampling events on [[Aravaipa Creek]], Arizona: a spring 2003 survey (April 5–6) and a fall 2003 survey (September 27–28). Counts were recorded at nine numbered sites using [[Seining]] and [[Electrofishing]]. The data were shared with students and collaborators, including Elizabeth Ridder of [[Arizona State University]]. The records document native and non-native fish presence and relative abundance across the creek, with several survey caveats noted by the author.

## Background & Description

- **Study area:** Aravaipa Creek, a stream in Arizona with a series of named survey sites running from the upper end down to a barrier site.
- **Field team:** Spring 2003 sampling was run with students and volunteers, which Reinthal describes as enthusiastic and serious. Robert Ohmart and David Brown were copied on the spring correspondence.
- **Protocol:** Seining followed standard [[Minckley Sampling Protocol]] procedures. Electrofishing used a backpack shocker.
- **Species codes:** The source uses four-letter codes (e.g., AGCH, GIRO, MEFU, TICO) without a full key. The species list above reflects likely identifications from the codes. PACL and CAIN are listed in the records but could not be confidently resolved, so they are not included in the species field. The codes CAIN and PACL appear in the data but their full names are not given in the source.
- **Site list (nine sites):**
  1. TNC House #1 (upper end)
  2. Chimney Rock #2
  3. Turkey Creek Confluence #3
  4. Deer Canyon/Hell Hole #4
  5. Horse Camp #5
  6. Painted Creek #6
  7. Woods Ranch (trail end) #7
  8. White's Ranch #8
  9. Barrier Above #9

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Native fish focus:** Several taxa in the records are native desert fishes of the Gila basin, including [[Roundtail Chub]] (GIRO), [[Spikedace]] (MEFU), [[Loach Minnow]] (TICO), [[Longfin Dace]] (AGCH), [[Sonora Sucker]] (CAIN), and [[Speckled Dace]] (RHOS). Their presence at multiple sites makes the creek relevant to native fish monitoring.
- **Non-native fish:** [[Red Shiner]] (CYLU), [[Green Sunfish]] (LECY), and [[Yellow Bullhead]] (AMNA) appear at selected sites, mainly in the lower and middle reaches.
- **Sampling constraints:** The backpack shocker was not working for the middle three sites during fall 2003, though the team carried it anyway. The Turkey Creek and Above Barrier fall electrofishing data are first-pass depletion results and were not yet analyzed. Painted Creek electrofishing was reported as "Equipment Broken" in the fall data.

## Key Findings

**Spring 2003 (April 5–6):**
- Counts were recorded for the upper sites, with [[Longfin Dace]] (AGCH) the most frequently reported taxon.
- A large AGCH count (476) appears in the Barrier Above #9 entry, though the source's labelling is partly garbled.

**Fall 2003 (September 27–28), seining:**
- Seine catches were recorded at TNC House #1, Chimney Rock #2, and Turkey Creek Confluence #3, with taxa including AGCH, CAIN, GIRO, MEFU, PACL, TICO, and RHOS.
- Peak seine counts reported include values such as 174 and 97 at individual sites, though exact site-to-count alignment is uncertain from the source text.
- Seining at Deer Canyon/Hell Hole #4 through White's Ranch #8 and Barrier Above #9 recorded AGCH, MEFU, GIRO, PACL, CAIN, TICO, CYLU, and AMNA at various sites.

**Fall 2003, electrofishing:**
- Electrofishing was conducted at most sites, with separate first-pass depletion data at Turkey Creek #3 and Barrier Above #9.
- The Turkey Creek first pass reported AGCH 51, CAIN 81, and GIRO 46 among the leading counts, followed by MEFU, PACL, TICO, and RHOS.
- Painted Creek #6 electrofishing was not completed because of broken equipment.

**Data and survey notes:**
- The author asked recipients to contact him if they wished to remain on the survey mailing list and offered to share the full analysis once complete.
- Dates on the fall data sheets are dated 10/8/2003.
- Counts are reported as raw tallies; no statistics, density estimates, or diversity indices are given in the source.
