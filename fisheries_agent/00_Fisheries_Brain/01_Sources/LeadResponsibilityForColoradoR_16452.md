## Executive Summary
This document is an internal U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2, Albuquerque, NM) administrative memorandum from 1990, not a primary scientific research paper. It addresses **organizational lead responsibility** for the recovery coordination of four endangered/imperiled big-river fish species native to the [[Colorado River Basin]]: the [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Razorback Sucker]], and [[Humpback Chub]]. The memo resolves confusion over whether Dexter National Fish Hatchery (NFH) or Parker Fisheries Assistance Office (FAO) holds lead responsibility, ultimately assigning it to Parker FAO under the supervision of Jim Hanson (Pinetop), with budgetary support requested for FY1991. No morphological, ecological, or population data are presented; the content is purely administrative/programmatic.

## Key Findings
- **Administrative reassignment**: Lead responsibility for recovery coordination of the [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Razorback Sucker]], and [[Humpback Chub]] was formally assigned to **Parker FAO**, superseding an earlier (March 23, 1990) reassignment to **Dexter NFH**.
- **Supervisory restructuring**: Temporary supervisory oversight of Parker FAO was assigned to Jim Hanson at Pinetop FAO to strengthen coordination capacity, with a one-year evaluation period proposed for accountability.
- **Staffing change**: The Parker FAO clerical position was converted to full-time status to improve responsiveness to inquiries regarding the four "big river fishes."
- **Budget request**: A $5,000 funding request (originally made July 20, 1990) was reiterated to offset costs of transferring recovery efforts from the Phoenix Ecological Services (ES) office to Parker FAO, pending for FY1991.
- **Institutional context**: The memo underscores early 1990s interagency tension/coordination challenges typical of multi-species recovery programs for endangered [[Colorado River Basin]] fishes, an important precursor to the later formalized [[Colorado River Fishes Recovery Program]].
