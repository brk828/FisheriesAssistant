# Characterization of the Life History and Ecology of the Humpback Chub in the Grand Canyon: Interim Progress Report (April 19, 1991)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #humpback-chub

## Executive Summary

This interim progress report, prepared for the [[Adaptive Management Work Group]] (ACT) meeting of April 19, 1991, summarizes the first seven months of a field study of [[Humpback Chub]] in the [[Grand Canyon]]. The contractor, [[Bio/West Inc]], was awarded the contract on September 1, 1990, and began fieldwork on October 15, 1990. As of March 31, 1991, seven of thirty-nine scheduled field trips were complete. The study has handled 310 humpback chub (69 recaptures), implanted [[Radiotelemetry]] transmitters in 31 adult fish, and begun habitat, movement, drift and light/turbidity studies.

## Background & Description

- **Project:** Characterization of the life history and ecology of the humpback chub in the Grand Canyon.
- **Prepared by:** Richard A. Valdez (Principal Investigator), William J. Masslich, Larry Crist and William Leibfried (Project Leaders), Bio/West, Inc., Logan, UT.
- **Study area:** [[Kwagunt Rapid]] (RM 56) to [[Diamond Creek]] (RM 226), comprising three sample reaches: the [[Little Colorado River]] (LCR) Reach, the [[Granite Gorge]] Reach and the [[Havasu Creek]] Reach.
- **Sampling design:** 10- or 20-day trips using one or two teams, respectively, of six people per team.
- **Gears:** [[Trammel Nets]], [[Gill Nets]], experimental gill nets, [[Hoop Nets]] and [[Electrofishing]].
- **Protocols:** Fish Sampling Protocol, Fish Handling Protocol and Database Management Protocol, submitted January 1, 1991. A 1990 Annual Report was submitted in March 1991, and trip reports were issued for all trips.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Core capture zone:** 98% of humpback chub captured were between [[Malagosa Crest]] (RM 57.0) and [[Carbon Creek]] (RM 64.8).
- **Upstream:** Two humpback chub were captured above [[Shinumo Creek]] (RM 108.3); one was recaptured.
- **Downstream:** Four humpback chub were captured between [[Pumpkin Springs]] (RM 212.5) and 222 Mile Canyon (RM 221.8). Two were recaptured, and one of these was recaptured twice.
- **Movement and home range:** Radiotagged fish were monitored by [[Radiotelemetry]], with net sets and capture locations mapped on the GCES MIPPS system to track movement and home range.
- **Habitat:** Radiotagged fish movement and habitat were mapped; general habitat mapping in the LCR Reach began in March 1991.
- **Water and food-base factors:** A study of ambient light and turbidity addresses the sediment augmentation question. [[Drift Sampling]] is measuring volumes and periodicity of [[Cladophora]] and invertebrates in the water column. A literature review of environmental criteria needed by humpback chub is being compared with Grand Canyon conditions.
- **River stage:** Ten temporary bench marks between RM 60.0 and RM 65.3 monitor river stage change.

## Key Findings

**Project status (as of March 31, 1991)**
- Contract awarded September 1, 1990; first field trip October 15, 1990.
- 7 of 39 scheduled trips completed.
- Trip reports issued for all trips; 1990 Annual Report submitted March 1991.

**Capture and recapture**
- 310 humpback chub handled, of which 69 were recaptures.
- 98% of captures occurred between RM 57.0 and RM 64.8.

**Radiotelemetry**
- Transmitters surgically implanted in 31 adult humpback chub between RM 57.0 and RM 64.6.
- 8 to 16 radiotagged fish recontacted each trip.
- 5 radiotagged fish recaptured (2 by AGF in the LCR).
- One radiotagged fish was an incidental mortality (released 10/17/90, recaptured 1/12/91).
- 28 fish monitored for 2 hours; 25 monitored for 24 hours. Movement and habitat mapped for all 2- and 24-hour monitored fish.
- Two remote telemetry stations established near the LCR in January and February 1991, one upstream and one downstream.

**Other activities and status**
- Stomach pump feasibility study on hold pending availability of test fish.
- Light and turbidity study initiated.
- Literature search on environmental criteria underway.
- General habitat mapping started in LCR Reach (March 1991); additional aerial photos needed to complete the set.
- Drift sampling of Cladophora and invertebrates underway.

**Data limitations**
- Pages 4 to 14 of the source are scanned tables and figures that are largely illegible in OCR (fish length/ID listings and capture records). They were not transcribed and no statistics are drawn from them.
