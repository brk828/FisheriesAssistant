## Executive Summary

This document is not a primary scientific research paper but an **administrative memorandum** transmitting the Final FY 98 Work Plan for the [[Recovery Program for the Endangered Fishes of the Upper Colorado River]], approved by the Implementation Committee on September 17, 1997. The memo is addressed to the Management Committee, Biology Committee, Water Acquisition Committee, and Information and Education Committee, along with associated consultants and interested parties. It outlines administrative and procedural details of the recovery program rather than presenting morphological, ecological, or population data on the target fish species. The Recovery Program itself is a multi-agency, multi-state cooperative effort (Colorado, Utah, Wyoming, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, Western Area Power Administration, and various water and wildlife stakeholder organizations) established to recover endangered fish species native to the [[Upper Colorado River]] system while accommodating water development.
