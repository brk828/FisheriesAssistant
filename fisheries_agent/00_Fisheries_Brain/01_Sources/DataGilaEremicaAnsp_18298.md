# Data: Gila eremica ANSP 165627

## Executive Summary
This record documents a **paratype specimen** of *[[Gila eremica]]* (Sonora Chub), a freshwater cyprinid fish, held in the Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia ([[ANSP]]) ichthyology collection. The specimen was collected by D.A. Hendrickson on 10 June 1978 from the Rio Mátape near San José de Pimas, Sonora, Mexico, at approximately 350 m elevation. The specimen was formally designated with its type status and determined by B.D. DeMarais in 1991. This entry represents catalog/curatorial metadata rather than a full morphological or ecological study, consistent with a museum specimen ledger from the ANSP database (data current as of 3 October 1994).
