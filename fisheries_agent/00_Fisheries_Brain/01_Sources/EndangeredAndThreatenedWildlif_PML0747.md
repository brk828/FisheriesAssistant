# Federal Register Notices: Critical Habitat Corrections for Colorado River Fishes and South Atlantic Snapper-Grouper Management Zones

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This *Federal Register* extract (Vol. 58, No. 48, Monday, March 15, 1993) contains several agency notices. The main item is a correction by the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] to the proposed critical habitat rule for four endangered [[Colorado River Fishes]]: the [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Bonytail Chub]]. The Service corrected three errors in the habitat descriptions for the razorback sucker in Arizona. A second notice, from the [[National Marine Fisheries Service]] (NMFS), proposes eight [[Special Management Zone]]s around [[Artificial Reef]]s in the [[Exclusive Economic Zone]] off South Carolina, under the [[Snapper-Grouper Fishery of the South Atlantic]]. Fragments of other notices (an EPA-related item and a Department of Commerce item) are too garbled to summarize reliably.

## Background & Description

**Colorado River endangered fishes.** The proposed rule, published January 29, 1993 (58 FR 6578), would designate [[Critical Habitat]] for four fishes listed as endangered under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973. The rule proposed amending 50 CFR 17.95(e), the section that describes critical habitat, and included maps and descriptions of the proposed areas for each species. The Service found errors in the razorback sucker description, published at 58 FR 6595, in three separate paragraphs.

**Snapper-grouper fishery.** The South Atlantic snapper-grouper fishery is managed under a [[Fishery Management Plan]] (FMP) prepared by the [[South Atlantic Fishery Management Council]] and implemented at 50 CFR Part 646 under the authority of the [[Magnuson Fishery Conservation and Management Act]]. The NMFS notice is a proposed rule, and the document lists no snapper-grouper species by name.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Colorado River habitat.** The corrected descriptions concern river reaches in the [[Colorado River]] system in Arizona. Three locations are affected:

- The [[Paria River]] to the [[Little Colorado River]] reach in Coconino and Mohave Counties, which had been identified as critical habitat on the map but omitted from the text.
- An unsurveyed reach of the Colorado River in Gila County, redescribed to better match its location.
- A section in Yavapai County, including Sycamore Creek, where the description was clarified.

The Service noted that these errors "could be misleading and cause confusion," which is why it issued the corrections.

**Artificial reef management.** The proposed zones are centred on artificial reefs (ARs) in the EEZ off the South Carolina coast. The NMFS states that the zones would promote orderly use of fishery resources on and around the reefs, reduce potential user-group conflicts, and maintain the socioeconomic benefits of the reefs to the extent practicable. The proposed rule would restrict fishing within these zones to hand-held, bandit-and-line gear (including manual, electric, or hydraulic reels) and to spearfishing without powerheads.

## Key Findings

**Colorado River critical habitat corrections (58 FR 6595):**

1. **Coconino and Mohave Counties, Arizona (second column, full paragraph beginning "Arizona, Coconino and Mohave Counties, and Nevada, Clark County"):** In line 4, "Little Colorado River in T.32N, R5E, section 1" is replaced with "Paria River in T.40N, R.7E, section 24." The Paria River reach had been mapped as critical habitat but was left out of the text.
2. **Gila County, Arizona (third column, paragraph beginning "Arizona, Gila County"):** In line 13, the description "old ranch road crossing in T.R. section (Gila and Salt River Meridian)" is replaced with "Indian Route 1 crossing (about 1 mile upstream of the confluence of Canyon Creek and the Salt River, an unsurveyed area)."
3. **Yavapai County, Arizona (third column, paragraph beginning "Arizona, Yavapai County"):** In line 8, "including" is replaced with "and," so the line reads "elevation and Sycamore Creek and its."

The notice was signed by Bruce Blanchard, Acting Director, Fish and Wildlife Service, on March 8, 1993 (FR Doc. 93-5812).

**Snapper-grouper special management zones (proposed rule, NMFS):**

- **Proposal:** Establish eight special management zones at artificial reefs in the EEZ off South Carolina, with fishing restricted to hand-held gear and spearfishing without powerheads.
- **Objectives:** Promote orderly use of reef fishery resources, reduce user-group conflicts, and maintain the socioeconomic benefits of the reefs to the extent practicable.
- **Comment deadline:** Written comments due on or before April 14, 1993.
- **Contacts:** Documents from the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council (Charleston, SC). Comments and the Environmental Assessment go to Peter J. Eldridge, Southeast Regional Office, NMFS, St. Petersburg, FL (813-893-3161).
