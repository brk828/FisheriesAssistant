# Turtox News Miscellany: Lake Izabal Sharks, Montana Vacation Notice, Conservation Workshop and Lab Notices

#ichthyology #marine-biology #elasmobranchii

## Executive Summary

This is a miscellaneous page from [[Turtox News]], a biological supply house publication, containing a photograph caption, travel and workshop notices, a cover credit, product and slide advertisements, and job openings. Its only substantive aquatic content is a photograph of shark and sawfish specimens from [[Lake Izabal]] in [[Guatemala]], a freshwater lake that [[Robert R. Miller]] describes as one of only two freshwater lakes in the northern hemisphere containing these marine groups. The rest of the material is commercial or promotional and contains no quantitative research data.

## Background & Description

The shark and sawfish photograph was taken by Doctor [[Robert R. Miller]], Associate Curator of Fishes at the [[University of Michigan Museum of Zoology]], Ann Arbor, during a fish and fisheries survey of Guatemala. The specimens came from Lake Izabal, about thirty miles inland from the Atlantic Coast town of [[Livingston Guatemala]]. Miller notes that the only other freshwater lake in the northern hemisphere with marine types of these groups is [[Lake Nicaragua]]. The species are not named in the caption.

The page also includes:

- **A Biologist's Vacation:** A promotional notice for a region west of [[Choteau Montana]], Montana, described as rich in wildlife, bird life, mountain flora and early-summer orchids in coniferous forests. Ranch accommodations, horseback trails, trout fishing in mountain streams and pack trips are advertised. Contact is Mr. and Mrs. J. W. White, Deep Canyon Ranch, Post Office Box 728, Choteau, Montana. The notice refers back to earlier [[Turtox News]] articles, "Buffalo Run" and "Packing Over the Continental Divide."
- **Conservation Workshop:** A session at [[Fresno State College]], Fresno, California, held June 13 to 24, 1949, directed to conservation of natural resources. Most activity is field-based, led by specialists from Federal, State and private agencies. [[Leo F. Hadsall]] is Director in Charge.
- **Cover Picture:** A young specimen of [[Octopus Bimaculatus]] from Southern California, photographed by Mr. Woody Williams of Inverness, California.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The only ecological content concerns two freshwater systems. Lake Izabal supports marine-derived shark and sawfish groups, which is ecologically notable because such taxa are rare in freshwater lakes of the northern hemisphere. The Montana region is described in general terms as biologically diverse, with abundant game, birds, mountain flora and forest orchids, and trout-bearing mountain streams. No measurements, species counts, water chemistry or population data are given for either system.
