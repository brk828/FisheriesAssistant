# Temporary Closure Order for Aravaipa Canyon Primitive Area

#land-management #conservation #habitat-protection #aravaipa-canyon

## Executive Summary

This administrative notice, issued July 6, 1970, by the Bureau of Land Management announces a temporary closure of the [[Aravaipa Canyon]] Primitive Area to all motorized vehicles. The closure was implemented to prevent damage to primitive and environmental resource values caused by increasing vehicular use, with particular concern for extreme fire danger. The restriction applies to approximately 7.5 miles of canyon from the confluence of [[Aravaipa Creek]] and [[Turkey Creek]] eastward, and remains in effect until completion of a comprehensive management plan developed with public input.

## Background & Description

The [[Aravaipa Canyon Primitive Area]] encompasses federal lands in southeastern Arizona, specifically in Township 6 South, Ranges 17-19 East. The affected area extends from the confluence of Aravaipa and Turkey Creeks on the east, downstream approximately 7.5 miles to near the center of Section 13, T.6 S., R.17 E. on the west. The specific sections included are:
- T. 6S., R. 19 E.: Section 19
- T. 6S., R. 18 E.: Sections 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 23, and 24
- T. 6S., R. 17 E.: Section 13

The closure was authorized under Federal Regulations 43 CFR 6251.4 and administered by the [[Bureau of Land Management]] Safford District Office.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document identifies increasing motorized vehicle use as causing damage to "primitive and other environmental resource values" within [[Aravaipa Canyon]]. The area's designation as a Primitive Area indicates recognition of its ecological and wilderness values requiring protection. Extreme fire danger at the time of the order represented an additional critical environmental concern necessitating immediate vehicle restrictions. The canyon environment, though not specifically detailed in this administrative document, was deemed sufficiently sensitive to warrant complete motorized vehicle exclusion pending development of a comprehensive management plan.

## Key Findings

- **Closure Scope**: All motorized vehicles excluded, including those used for mining exploration, resource management, administration, and recreational purposes
- **Effective Date**: July 6, 1970, continuing until completion of the [[Aravaipa Primitive Area Management Plan]]
- **Public Engagement**: Public meeting scheduled for July 22, 1970, at 1:00 p.m. at [[San Manuel High School]] Cafetorium to solicit comments on vehicular use and management considerations
- **Enforcement**: Exterior boundaries at upper and lower canyon ends posted; maps available at Safford District Office and BLM State Office in Phoenix
- **Justification**: Increasing vehicle damage to primitive values and extreme fire danger conditions
- **Management Approach**: Temporary administrative closure pending development of permanent management plan with public input
