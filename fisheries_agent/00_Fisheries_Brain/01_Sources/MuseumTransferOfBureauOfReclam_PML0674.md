# Museum Transfer of Bureau of Reclamation Aquatic Specimens to Arizona State University

#ichthyology #marine-biology #aquatic-specimens

## Executive Summary

This document is a 2006 cover letter from the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] Phoenix Area Office to Dr. Anthony Gill of Arizona State University's School of Life Sciences. It accompanies a separate transfer of 338 lots of preserved aquatic specimens, mainly fish, for accession into the [[Museum of Fishes]]. The specimens were collected by Reclamation or its contractors between roughly 1999 and 2005. Detailed collection information was sent electronically as a spreadsheet.

## Background & Description

- **Sender:** [[Bruce D. Ellis]], Chief, Environmental Resource Management Division, Bureau of Reclamation, Glendale, Arizona.
- **Recipient:** Dr. [[Anthony Gill]], [[Arizona State University]], School of Life Sciences, Tempe, Arizona.
- **Copy to:** Mr. Jerome A. Stefferud, Phoenix, Arizona (without enclosure).
- **Contact for follow-up:** [[Robert Clarkson]], at 623-773-6252.
- **Date:** March 2006 (OCR reading of the date stamp; verify against the original).

The letter is administrative correspondence. It contains no research design, analysis, or new taxonomic results.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter gives no species names, habitat types, water bodies, or water chemistry. The collections were made in the Arizona region under the authority of two permits:

- A [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] threatened and endangered species permit (number partly garbled in the scan, reads approximately TE837751-1).
- An [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] scientific collecting permit (number partly garbled in the scan).

Because the collections involved threatened and endangered species, the permits are the main ecological link. Reclamation's role was as the permit holder's agent, with Mr. Clarkson as the designated contact.

## Key Findings

- **Total transfer:** 338 lots of aquatic specimens.
  - 329 lots of fish.
  - 4 lots of frogs.
  - 1 lot of an unidentified taxon, illegible in the scan.
  - The remaining lots cannot be fully confirmed from the OCR text (the scan shows a further lot description that is not legible).
- **Preservation:** All specimens are preserved in alcohol (the scan reads "preserved in [illegible] alcohol").
- **Collection period:** Approximately 1999 through 2005 (OCR-garbled; verify).
- **Requested action:** Accession the lots into the Museum of Fishes at Arizona State University, assigning catalog numbers. Catalog numbers are to be returned to Reclamation when available, ideally by adding them to the shared spreadsheet.
- **Documentation:** A summary of the collections was sent electronically. The transfer itself was sent under separate cover.
- **Permits:** Collections were made under the federal threatened/endangered species permit and the state scientific collecting permit noted above.
