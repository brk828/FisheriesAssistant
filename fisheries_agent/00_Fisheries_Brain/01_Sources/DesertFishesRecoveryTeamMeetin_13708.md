# Desert Fishes Recovery Team Meeting Minutes December 1985

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

Meeting minutes from the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] (DFRT) held December 10-11, 1985 at [[Arizona State University]]. Team Leader W.L. Minckley convened representatives from USBR, USBLM, Arizona Game & Fish Department, USFWS, New Mexico Game & Fish, and USFS to establish recovery planning priorities for listed southwestern desert fishes. The team outlined a comprehensive work plan including preparation of a [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]], review of existing recovery plans for big river fishes, and future planning for [[Sonoran Chub]] and [[Desert Pupfish]]. Administrative procedures were clarified by Jerry Burton (USFWS Region 2), emphasizing that team members serve as species advocates rather than agency representatives.

## Background & Description

The DFRT was established to draft, review, and revise recovery plans for listed southwestern fishes under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. Team composition included representatives from federal agencies (USFWS, USFS, USBR, USBLM) and state wildlife agencies (Arizona Game & Fish Department, New Mexico Game & Fish). Jerry Burton clarified that recovery team members work as species advocates dedicated to recovery of listed southwest ichthyofauna, not as agency representatives. The team's principle job involves drafting recovery plans with all correspondence directed to the Regional Director, USFWS Region 2.

Team Leader Minckley raised concerns about conflicts of interest regarding recovery team involvement versus contractual agreements for listing, recovery plan preparation, and monitoring studies. Burton assured there was no problem with contracted studies and recovery team involvement, citing established precedent.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The meeting addressed multiple threatened and endangered fish species across southwestern desert aquatic habitats. Discussion focused on localized faunas requiring specialized recovery approaches, particularly in the [[Yaqui River]] system and [[Colorado River]] basin. The team considered taxonomic distinctions relative to [[Notropis formosus]] when discussing [[Yaqui Fishes]].

Habitat considerations included the [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] (SBNWR) as a critical recovery site for Yaqui fishes, and [[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]] (BANWR) with 5-6 ponds of several acres each as potential refugia. Four management options were considered for BANWR ponds: maintaining native unlisted amphibian fauna, establishing refugia for [[Desert Pupfish]], [[Yaqui Topminnow]], and [[Roundtail Chub]], creating grow-out areas for [[Bonytail Chub]], or combinations thereof.

The team emphasized using the closest genetic entity for any reintroduction options to avoid taxonomic conflicts. Big river fishes ([[Bonytail Chub]], [[Humpback Chub]], [[Colorado Squawfish]]) required review of existing recovery plans relative to lower basin recovery actions. [[Bonytail Chub]] was identified as the highest priority species due to its current endangerment status and extinction risk.

## Key Findings

**Immediate Action Items:**

1. **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]**: Multi-species recovery plan to be prepared within 6 months (by June 1986) covering [[Yaqui Chub]], [[Beautiful Shiner]], and [[Yaqui Catfish]]. Plan would work toward recovery and maintenance of localized faunas. [[Yaqui Topminnow]] excluded due to existing plan, though team will review that plan prior to 1989 revision date. Minckley volunteered to prepare plan using data from SBNWR Master Plan.

2. **[[Chihuahua Chub Recovery Plan]]** Review: DFRT committed to 6-week review of Agency Review Draft. Comments from DFRT members due January 15, 1986; team comments to USFWS Region 2 due January 31, 1986.

3. **[[Sonoran Chub Recovery Plan]]**: Work to begin following listing and completion of Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan. Tentative target date for draft plan: December 1986. Field trip to [[Sycamore Canyon]] planned for summer (June) meeting to visit Sonoran chub habitat.

4. **[[Desert Pupfish]]**: DFRT requested USFWS Region 2 assume lead responsibility following listing. Team considers best recovery opportunity within Region 2, especially considering previous efforts between Arizona Game & Fish Department and BLM.

5. **Big River Fishes Review**: Team to review existing plans for [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Colorado Squawfish]] relative to lower basin recovery actions. Additional discussion on listing and recovery of [[Razorback Sucker]].

**Administrative Decisions:**

- Technical Consultants designated: Ben Robertson (SBNWR Manager), Jerry Landye (AzG&FD), Jim Brooks (USFWS), Linda Ulmer (CDFG), and Chuck Kennedy (USFS)
- Species files to be assembled for each DFRT member by Jerry Burton; Tom Burke to photocopy and distribute materials
- USFWS Region 2 to advise DFRT on other recovery team activities involving desert fishes ([[Woundfin]], native trouts, big river fishes) and provide Colorado River Fishes Team meeting minutes

**Future Considerations:**

- Team members to familiarize themselves with [[Gila intermedia]], [[Gila robusta grahami]], [[Catostomus discobolus yarrowi]], and [[Campostoma ornatum]] as potential future ESA listing candidates
- Minckley completing status report for Native Fish Fauna of Arizona and New Mexico (completion December 1986)
- Next DFRT meeting scheduled April 4-6, 1986 in Douglas with tour of San Bernardino NWR
- Summer (June) meeting proposed for Sonoran chub recovery plan discussion with field visit to habitat
