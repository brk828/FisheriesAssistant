# Bonytail Chub Stocking Notification and Recapture, Davis Basin, Nevada (1989)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fisheries-management

## Executive Summary

This is a January 13, 1989 letter from the [[Nevada Department of Wildlife]] to the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] in New Mexico. It requests notification of any native fish stocking carried out within the [[Colorado River System]] bordering Nevada, and reports the recapture of a single [[Bonytail Chub]] during electrofishing. The fish is believed to be from a hatchery stocking of 20,000 five-inch bonytail made on October 12, 1988.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Official correspondence (letter), with a sender block, a hatchery receipt stamp dated January 19, 1989, and typed reference initials.
- **Sender:** [[William A. Molini]], Director, [[Nevada Department of Wildlife]], with the signature of John Donaldson, Regional Manager. The letterhead lists the Governor as Richard H. Bryan.
- **Recipient:** [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], Dexter, New Mexico.
- **Subject:** Coordination of native fish stocking within the Colorado River System, with correspondence to be directed to the Nevada Department of Wildlife Fisheries division in Las Vegas.
- **Species focus:** [[Bonytail Chub]], a native fish of the Colorado River System.

The letter does not give a scientific name, morphological description, or life-history data for the species.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Setting:** The capture occurred in shallow water on the west side of [[Davis Basin]], in the vicinity of [[Two Dollar Cove]] (site #123), within the [[Colorado River System]] bordering Nevada.
- **Capture method:** [[Electrofishing]] (electroshocking) by Nevada Department of Wildlife personnel on the night of October 25, 1988.
- **Source population:** The fish is believed to originate from the October 12, 1988 stocking of 20,000 five-inch bonytail by the Dexter National Fish Hatchery. The letter does not confirm this origin genetically or by marking.
- **Fish size:** The recaptured individual measured 119 mm.
- **Time between stocking and recapture:** About 13 days (October 12 to October 25, 1988).

## Key Findings

1. **Single recapture of a hatchery-released bonytail:** One 119 mm [[Bonytail Chub]] was caught by electrofishing in Davis Basin on October 25, 1988.
2. **Probable hatchery origin:** The fish is believed to be from the October 12, 1988 release of 20,000 five-inch bonytail. The letter presents this as a belief, not a confirmed identification.
3. **Interagency coordination request:** The Nevada Department of Wildlife asked the Dexter hatchery to notify it of any native fish stocking in the Colorado River System bordering Nevada.
4. **Correspondence routing:** Future correspondence should go to the Nevada Department of Wildlife Fisheries office in Las Vegas.
5. **Data limits:** The letter reports one fish and one stocking event. It contains no population estimates, sample sizes beyond the single capture, survival rates, or habitat measurements.
