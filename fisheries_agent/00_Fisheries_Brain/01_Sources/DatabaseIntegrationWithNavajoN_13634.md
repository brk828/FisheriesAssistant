## Key Findings
- The memo (dated November 22, 1993) is authored by **Rich Valdez** (BIO/WEST, Grand Canyon Studies) and addressed to **Dave Wegner** (GCES Program Manager), **Mike Yard** (GCES), and **W.L. Minckley** (ASU) — a noted ichthyologist known for work on Southwestern desert fishes.
- Purpose of the meeting: to coordinate integration of the **GCES Fisheries Database** for the Grand Canyon with NNHP's LCR databases, ensuring compatible formats for **cross-database integration**.
- Planned deliverables outlined by BIO/WEST:
  1. A report documenting database structures, common data fields, and standardized nomenclature.
  2. A report identifying data links and recommending **relational database programs** for the Adaptive Compliance/Coordination Team (ACT).
  3. A report on integrating the BIO/WEST database into a **Geographic Information System (GIS)**, with recommendations for integrating other agency databases (ASU, AGF, FWS) into GIS.
- NNHP was expected to produce parallel reports for ASU, AGF, and FWS databases relevant to the LCR.
- The meeting agenda covered: field data sheets/formats/codes, field data entry and quality control, computerization, data integration, and GIS applications — presented by BIO/WEST staff including **Paul Holden, Tom Twedt, Penny Trinca, Bill Masslich, Todd Hougaard**, and **Rich Valdez**.
- A follow-up **database integration startup meeting** involving BIO/WEST, GCES, and NNHP was recommended pending authorization.
