## Executive Summary

This source is a scanned, supplementary page set from an FAO fisheries publication. Its central content is **Table I, "Principal Species of the Genus Tilapia."** The table lists the main tilapia species by native or regional distribution and by feeding habit. The OCR is very degraded, so most species names, sample sizes and page-level text cannot be recovered. The note therefore reports only the content that is legible: the geographic distribution of the species, their broad dietary categories, and a reference to a previous FAO fisheries article that this text supplements.

- **Subject:** The genus [[Tilapia]], a group of freshwater fishes widely cultured and fished in Africa.

## Key Findings

1. **Table I** covers the principal species of the genus *Tilapia*, with distribution and feeding habits given for each.
2. **Distribution:** The listed species are associated with African localities, including Lake Victoria, the Gold Coast, Nigeria, Angola, Kenya and Southern Africa.
3. **Diet:** The legible diets are omnivorous (algae and aquatic plants) or phytoplankton-based. This supports the view that tilapias are primarily plant-feeding, a trait relevant to [[Aquaculture]] and to pond management.
4. **Pond culture:** At least one entry notes culture in ponds in South Africa.
5. **Data limitations:** Species names, sample sizes, measurements and the full table cannot be reliably recovered from this scan. The table should be checked against the original FAO publication before any species-level use.
