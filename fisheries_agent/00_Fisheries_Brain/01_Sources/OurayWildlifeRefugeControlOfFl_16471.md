# Control of Flood Inflow to the Nursery Habitat

#habitat-management #aquatic-ecology #nursery-habitat

## Executive Summary

This document presents an engineering schematic for controlling flood inflow to nursery habitat through a system of managed ponds, canals, and water control structures. The design incorporates multiple units (L-1 through L-10) with varying water surface elevations (4660-4667 feet), connected by gated culverts and maintained by a network of dikes. The system includes provisions for sediment management, fish passage, and controlled water exchange with the main river channel.

## Background & Description

The nursery habitat system consists of multiple interconnected pond units arranged along a main canal network. Water surface elevations range from 4660 feet (Unit L-7) to 4667 feet (Main Dike L-4). The system includes:

- Ten primary pond units (L-1 through L-10) of varying sizes
- Multiple soil moisture dikes for water retention
- An intake and pump structure for water management
- Existing gravity canal infrastructure
- Network of corrugated metal pipe culverts (24-inch diameter) connecting units
- Main dikes with elevations ranging from 4663 to 4667 feet

The design references "Scheme #7" for possible improvements to the inlet area at the main channel.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The system is designed as [[Nursery Habitat]] for aquatic species, with specific provisions for [[Fish Passage]] through screened structures. The design incorporates:

- Small pond units (10 acres or less, total of 71-207 ponds)
- [[Sediment Deposition]] areas for water quality management
- Sediment settling pond along levee road for removal and return to river
- Fish screens at inlet structures to protect juvenile fish
- Low head weir with riprap for controlled water entry
- Grated culverts to prevent fish entrainment

The floodplain habitat design allows for controlled inundation while maintaining suitable conditions for early life stages of aquatic species.

## Key Findings

**Proposed Infrastructure Improvements:**

1. New inlet and jetty with low head weir, grated culverts, and fish screens
2. Improved outlet structure modifications
3. Clean out of main canal system
4. Connection of ponds to main canal with gated culverts
5. Inter-pond connections via gated culverts
6. Sediment settling pond along levee road for sediment removal and return to river
7. Removal of levee at first connecting pond
8. Removal of existing pond/canal components where construction or regrading is proposed

**Existing Culvert Infrastructure:**
- Multiple 24-inch corrugated metal pipe culverts at various locations (L-1A through L-2C)
- Existing gravity canal system to be maintained

**Water Management Features:**
- Intake and pump structure for active water level control
- Soil moisture dikes for retention
- Gated culverts for controlled water exchange between units
- Water surface elevations maintained between 4660-4667 feet across different units

**Design Considerations:**
- Sediment management through dedicated settling areas
- [[Fish Screens]] to protect juvenile fish during water intake
- Small pond configuration (≤10 acres) optimized for nursery function
