## Executive Summary

This document is a **station profile**, not a primary field study, describing the operational scope of the **[[Hagerman National Fish Hatchery]]** in Hagerman, Gooding County, Idaho, operated by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region I). Established in **1931** under the authority of the **White Act (46 Stat. 371)**, the hatchery's original mission was to produce **trout** for Indian reservations and state-managed federal lands across Idaho, northern Nevada, and eastern Oregon. In **1980**, the facility was reprogrammed under the **[[Lower Snake River Compensation Plan]]** to rear **steelhead** as partial mitigation for the ecological impacts of four federal dams built on the Snake River. The hatchery also continues to produce **rainbow trout** for the **[[Duck Valley Indian Reservation]]**, with rearing costs reimbursed by the Duck Valley Indian Tribe. The station also serves a public outreach function, hosting approximately 12,000 visitors annually.
