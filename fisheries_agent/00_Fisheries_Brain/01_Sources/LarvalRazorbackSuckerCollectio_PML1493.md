# Larval Razorback Sucker Collections in Lake Mohave, 1997

#ichthyology #marine-biology #razorback-sucker

## Executive Summary

This document is a 16 April 1997 transmittal letter from [[Paul C. Marsh]] of the [[Arizona State University]] Center for Environmental Studies to [[Thomas A. Burke]] of the [[U.S. Bureau of Reclamation]]. It reports the results of larval [[Razorback Sucker]] collections in [[Lake Mohave]] (Arizona-Nevada) for two periods in 1997 and gives a season total. Most larvae came from [[Tequila Cove]]. All fish were transferred through a [[National Park Service]] waystation at [[Cottonwood Cove]] to the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] facility at [[Willow Beach]], Arizona. The letter asks the client to confirm that the enclosed summary meets its expectations and the terms of the agreement.

## Background & Description

The letter covers work done under two Bureau of Reclamation purchase orders, cited as 1425-97-PG-30-06780 and 1425-30-97-PG-30-06480 (both OCR-garbled in the source; the numbers are reproduced as read). Together, the records give a complete account of the larval razorback sucker collections made by Arizona State University in 1997.

- **Current reporting period:** 15 March to 15 April 1997, under order 1425-97-PG-30-06780.
- **Earlier periods:** 10 February to 14 March 1997, and 20 January to 6 February 1997 (the second reported under the other order).
- **Purpose of the letter:** to transmit the summary and enclosed documentation and to request acceptance against the agreement's terms.

The collected organism is the larval stage of the [[Razorback Sucker]], a fish whose larvae were the focus of the work. The document does not give the scientific name, family, or morphological details.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The collections took place in [[Lake Mohave]], a reservoir on the Colorado River bordering Arizona and Nevada. Sampling sites named in the letter are:

- [[Tequila Cove]] (NV): the main source of larvae in the reporting period.
- [[Hog Farm Cove]] (NV)
- [[Levi Cove]] (AZ)
- A cove in Arizona whose name is garbled in the scan.

Post-collection handling followed a set path: fish were held at the Cottonwood Cove waystation and then transferred to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility at Willow Beach. The document does not describe water chemistry, habitat conditions, or other environmental variables.
