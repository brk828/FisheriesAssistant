# Wahweap Operation and Maintenance – Utah (FY 2003 Annual Project Report)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This FY 2003 annual report from the [[Colorado River Recovery Program]] covers Project 29c, the operation and maintenance of the [[Wahweap State Fish Hatchery]] at Big Water, Utah. The hatchery rears [[Bonytail]] for stocking in the Upper Colorado River Basin and maintains the Upper Basin's backup [[Razorback Sucker]] brood stock. The project was ongoing, its budget of $205,100 was fully expended, and the principal investigator reports that 100% of FY 2003 work was completed. The report covers stocking, tagging, rearing, fish disposition, inventory and recommendations, with no formal research hypotheses. The principal investigator was [[Quent Bradwisch]] of the [[Utah Division of Wildlife]].

## Background & Description

- **Project:** Wahweap Operation and Maintenance – Utah, Project No. 29c, FY 2003 (ongoing).
- **Facility:** Wahweap State Fish Hatchery, Big Water, Utah. It rears bonytail for Utah and Colorado and holds the Upper Basin's backup razorback sucker brood stock.
- **Bonytail (*Gila elegans*):** A large-bodied, endangered cyprinid native to the [[Colorado River]] system. In 2003 the stocked fish ranged from 130 to 200 mm.
- **Razorback Sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*):** An endangered catostomid of the same system. The hatchery maintained 947 razorback brood fish in fall 2003.
- **Policy framework (2003 RIPRAP):** The work is tied to the General Recovery Program Support Action Plan (secure and manage presumptive stocks in refugia; operate and maintain facilities), the [[Green River]] Action Plan (develop and implement a state stocking plan for the four endangered fishes), and the [[Colorado River Mainstem]] Action Plan (same stocking-plan objective for Utah).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Stocking habitats:** Fish were released into the [[Green River]] at Rainbow Park Boat Ramp and Green River State Park, and into the [[Colorado River]] at Cisco boat ramp. Bottom-land stocking was also carried out via the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Vernal office.
- **Pond rearing:** Growth was improved by [[Pond Fertilization]]. Fish were monitored weekly using minnow traps, seines and cast nets.
- **Spawning:** Two-year-old fish spawned to some extent in hatchery ponds in 2003. Wild-spawned fish were also present, and about 30,000 bonytail chub (20–100 mm) were destroyed because their parentage could not be traced.
- **Fish health:** Lethal sampling for [[Fish Health Inspection]] contributed to losses, along with handling during moves and tagging.

## Key Findings

**1. Stocking output (2003)**
- Wahweap stocked 23,247 bonytail in 2003.
- About 400 untagged bonytail went to the USFWS Flagstaff office for a tagging study.
- Coded-wire-tagged bonytail went to USFWS Vernal for bottom-land stocking.
- Pit-tagged bonytail were stocked at Rainbow Park Boat Ramp and Green River State Park on the Green River, and at Cisco boat ramp on the Colorado River.
- The Utah stocking plan accounted for 9,847 pit-tagged fish. All stocked fish were tagged in 2003, and held excess fish were released after approval by the [[Biology Committee]].

**2. Rearing and intake**
- The station received and reared 10,000 bonytail fry from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]].
- Weekly growth monitoring and pond fertilization were used to increase growth rates over earlier years.

**3. Disposition and losses**
- About 30,000 wild-spawned bonytail chub (20–100 mm) were destroyed under the [[Excess Fish Policy]], with [[Biology Committee]] concurrence.
- Losses in the year were attributed to moving, tagging and lethal fish-health sampling. The report lists losses by size class: 140 mm+ (3), 120 mm+ (4), 70 mm+ (2), 250+ mm (2) and adult (2).

**5. Recommendations**
- Continue rearing bonytail to meet Utah's stocking needs.
- Maintain the brood stock, following the recommendations of the Biology Committee.

**6. Budget and status**
- Funds provided: $205,100. Funds expended: $205,100. Difference: $0.
- Work completed: 100% of FY 2003 tasks. Project status: ongoing fish culture.
- [[Passive Integrated Transponder Tagging]] (PIT tag) data were submitted to the database.
