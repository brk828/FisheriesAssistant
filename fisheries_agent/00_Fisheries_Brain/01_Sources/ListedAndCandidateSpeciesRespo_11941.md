# Listed and Candidate Species Responsibilities - Field Office Personnel Directory

#ichthyology #marine-biology #administrative-records #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This administrative document provides a directory of U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service field office personnel and their responsibilities for listed and candidate species across Arizona, California, and Nevada. The document assigns lead and support roles for various threatened and endangered species, including multiple fish species, mammals, birds, reptiles, and plants. Key fish species mentioned include [[Apache Trout]], [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Yaqui Chub]], [[Yaqui Catfish]], [[Yaqui Topminnow]], [[Little Colorado Spinedace]], and [[Colorado Pikeminnow]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The geographic scope encompasses diverse aquatic habitats across the southwestern United States, including:

- High-elevation cold-water streams (Apache trout habitat)
- Large river systems ([[Colorado River]] basin species)
- Desert aquatic systems (Yaqui basin species)
- Wetland habitats (California black rail)

Species responsibilities reflect the region's unique assemblage of endemic and threatened aquatic fauna, particularly native fish species that have experienced significant population declines due to habitat modification, water diversion, and introduction of non-native species.
