# Descriptions of New Cyprinid Barbs from Yunnan and Guangxi, China

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This is a taxonomic paper, published in a Chinese fisheries journal (Vol. 2, No. 2, apparently October 1982), that describes a [[New Genus]] and several [[New Species]] of cyprinid barbs from southwestern China. The new genus is *Anchicyclocheilus*, and the new species are placed in *Anchicyclocheilus* and [[Sinocyclocheilus]]. The scan is heavily degraded: many species epithets, measurements and counts are illegible, so the summary below reports only what can be read with confidence.

## Background & Description

- **Subject group:** [[Cyprinidae]], the carp and minnow family, represented here by the barbid genera *Anchicyclocheilus* and *Sinocyclocheilus*.
- **Taxonomic action:** The paper proposes one new genus and several new species, with holotypes and paratypes designated in the usual way.
- **Diagnostic characters:** Comparisons with existing relatives rely on features such as the lateral line scale count, the lobe or process on the anterior part of the body, and the body and head proportions. Some values are not legible in the scan.
- **Type material:** Holotype specimens are reported from Yunnan; one type series is from Guangxi, with specimens collected in April. Body lengths of the type series appear to range from roughly 111 to 178 mm, though the OCR is uncertain.
- **Repositories and collectors:** The text mentions specimens kept at a middle school of a machinery or related institute in Yunnan, and authorship credits for the taxonomic names (including "Chu" and "Cui") appear in several places.
