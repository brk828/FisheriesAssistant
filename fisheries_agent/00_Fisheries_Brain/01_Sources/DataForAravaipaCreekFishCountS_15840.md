# Aravaipa Creek Fish Count and Habitat Sampling, April 1996

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This record documents a seine-based fish count and habitat sampling survey of [[Aravaipa Creek]] conducted on April 5-6, 1996. Sampling covered three reaches: the East End, [[Aravaipa Canyon]], and the West End. Catches were dominated by [[Longfin Dace]] (*Agosia chrysogaster*), which was the most abundant species at nearly every site. The assemblage also included several native cyprinids and catostomids, such as [[Spikedace]] (*Meda fulgida*), [[Loach Minnow]] (*Tiaroga cobitis*) and [[Roundtail Chub]] (*Gila robusta*), alongside non-native fishes including [[Green Sunfish]] (*Lepomis cyanellus*), [[Western Mosquitofish]] (*Gambusia affinis*) and [[Red Shiner]] (*Cyprinella lutrensis*). Much of the document is scanned field data sheets, and many entries are illegible or ambiguous after OCR.

## Background & Description

The document is an assembled set of field materials for the survey:

- **Tally sheets** for the East End, Aravaipa Canyon and West End reaches, listing counts by species for each site.
- **Equipment:** 4' x 6' and 4' x 15' seines.
- **Species code list:** native species are marked with asterisks; the list includes 12 species in total.
- **Individual field data forms** (roughly pages 4-12) headed "Aravaipa Creek Fish and Habitat Sampling," with columns for site number, species, size and sex. Most entries are unreadable after OCR. Legible notes include life-stage markings such as "Juv," "Ad" and "Sub Ad," and a few site names.

The survey used [[Seine Netting]] as its main capture method. The tally sheets give catch counts, not densities or lengths.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Study reaches and sites**

- **East End:** TNC Guest House (Pecan Grove), Chimney Rock, and the confluence with [[Turkey Creek]].
- **Aravaipa Canyon:** Hell Hole (34 seine hauls), Horse Camp (42 seine hauls), and Painted Cave Canyon.
- **West End:** West Car Ranch, Jep White (40 seine hauls), and Sycamore Tree.

**Native and non-native composition**

Native taxa recorded were *Pantosteus clarki*, *Meda fulgida*, *Agosia chrysogaster*, *Tiaroga cobitis*, *Gila robusta*, *Catostomus insignis* and *Rhinichthys osculus*. Non-native or introduced taxa recorded were *Lepomis cyanellus*, *Gambusia affinis*, *Ictalurus punctatus*, *Amiurus natalis* and *Cyprinella lutrensis*. The presence of [[Green Sunfish]], [[Western Mosquitofish]] and [[Red Shiner]] at several sites is relevant to the conservation of the native assemblage, although the document does not analyse this.

**Habitat**

The document contains a title for a habitat component ("Fish and Habitat Sampling"), but it provides no habitat measurements, such as water temperature, flow, substrate or cover.

**Aravaipa Canyon**

- **Hell Hole (34 seine hauls):** *P. clarki* = 36. The remaining values (237, 52, 3, 7, 6, 0, 1) are legible but their assignment to species is uncertain after OCR. *Lepomis cyanellus* is listed here.
- **Horse Camp (42 seine hauls):** *P. clarki* = 19; *M. fulgida* = 322; *A. chrysogaster* = 12; *T. cobitis* = 1; *G. robusta* = 29; *C. insignis* = 2; *R. osculus* = 0 (read from OCR; treat with caution).
- **Painted Cave Canyon:** counts are not legible. Species list includes *Lepomis cyanellus*.

**West End**

- **West Car Ranch:** five values (107, 128, 292, 34, 31) are legible for seven species; species assignment is unclear.
- **Jep White (40 seine hauls):** *A. chrysogaster* = 13; other species appear as zero or are illegible.
- **Sycamore Tree:** *P. clarki* = 14; *M. fulgida* = 0; *A. chrysogaster* = 159. *Lepomis cyanellus* and *Ictalurus punctatus* are listed.

**Overall patterns**

- [[Longfin Dace]] was the most abundant species in the legible data, with counts of roughly 250 to 300 at several East End sites and 159 at Sycamore Tree.
- [[Desert Sucker]] was recorded at most sites, with counts from 6 to 36 in the legible data.
- [[Spikedace]] was recorded only sporadically. It appears as 8 at Turkey Creek and as 322 at Horse Camp (the latter should be verified against the original sheet).
- The document provides no summary statistics, sizes, sex ratios or seasonal comparisons.

**Data quality**

Much of the source is scanned and damaged. Many counts, species assignments and field-sheet entries cannot be confirmed. Totals should be checked against original field records before reuse.
