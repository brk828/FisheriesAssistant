# Colorado River Endangered Fishes Critical Habitat Draft Biological Support Document

#ichthyology #marine-biology #freshwater-ecology #cyprinidae #catostomidae #conservation-biology #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This document is a **legal/administrative memorandum** from the Sierra Club Legal Defense Fund (SCLDF), dated September 29, 1993, rather than a primary scientific paper. It reports on the release of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's (FWS) *Colorado River Endangered Fishes Critical Habitat Draft Biological Support Document* (released September 15, 1993). The memo identifies four federally endangered fish species native to the [[Colorado River Basin]] that are subjects of proposed critical habitat designation: the [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Bonytail Chub]]. The memo itself contains **no primary morphological, ecological, or population data** on these species; its purpose is to alert stakeholders to the draft proposal, encourage public comment, and flag procedural gaps (namely, the absence of economic impact analysis at this stage of designation).

## Background & Description

No morphological data (e.g., body plan, coloration, size, meristic counts) are provided in this source document. The memo is procedural/legal in nature and defers all biological detail to the underlying FWS *Draft Biological Support Document*, which is not reproduced here. For species-level morphological data, refer to dedicated taxonomic accounts of [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Bonytail Chub]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat System:** All four species are endemic to the [[Colorado River Basin]], a large arid-region river system in the western United States.
- **Conservation Status:** All four are listed as **endangered** under U.S. federal law, prompting the critical habitat designation process described in this memo.
- **Regulatory Context:** The designation process is being conducted under the U.S. Endangered Species Act (ESA) framework, administered by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]].
- No specific microhabitat, flow regime, spawning habitat, or trophic ecology data for the individual species are given in this source; such details would appear in the full FWS Biological Support Document.

## Key Findings

- **Four Endangered Species Identified:** The FWS draft document addresses critical habitat proposals for the [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Bonytail Chub]] — collectively referred to in later conservation literature as the "Colorado River Endangered Fishes" or "Big River Fishes."
- **Document Availability:** The draft was made available for public inspection through the FWS Salt Lake City office as of September 15, 1993.
- **Public Comment Period:** Stakeholders were given until approximately **November 15, 1993** to submit comments on the draft critical habitat proposals.
- **Procedural Gap Noted:** The draft document explicitly does **not** address:
  - Potential economic impacts of critical habitat designation, or
  - Areas FWS might later exclude from designation due to anticipated economic impacts (an exclusion process under ESA Section 4(b)(2)).
- **Advocacy Recommendation:** SCLDF urged commenters to specifically request that a future public comment period be made available once the economic analysis and exclusion process are completed, ensuring continued public participation in the final habitat designation.
