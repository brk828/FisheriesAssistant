# Animal Genetic Resources Conservation Project (Fisheries) Design Team India - Scope of Work

#ichthyology #marine-biology #conservation-genetics #fisheries-management

## Executive Summary

This fax communication from Winrock International (February 11, 1992) confirms consulting arrangements for Dr. Buddy Jensen to work with India's National Bureau of Fish Genetic Resources (NBFGR) on designing a fish genetic resources conservation project. Dr. F.P. Das, Director of NBFGR, has approved the proposed April 16 - May 9 consultancy dates and provided detailed terms of reference covering inventory methodology, conservation strategies, gene bank establishment, and laboratory infrastructure assessment.

## Background & Description

The consulting assignment is part of the Animal Genetic Resources Conservation Project focused on fisheries in [[India]]. The project involves collaboration between Winrock International and the [[National Bureau of Fish Genetic Resources]] (NBFGR), operating under the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). Government of India clearances are being arranged based on Dr. Jensen's biodata and passport details.

The scope encompasses both wild and domesticated fish varieties, with emphasis on establishing comprehensive conservation infrastructure including ex situ and in situ techniques, gene banking facilities, and research capabilities in modern genetic technologies.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The project addresses conservation of India's fish genetic resources across multiple ecosystems. The terms of reference acknowledge that data on previous abundance of fish populations is not readily available, necessitating development of new methodologies for assessing endangered, vulnerable, and rare species status. The work will examine causative factors leading to endangered status and review existing conservation measures throughout [[India]].

The project recognizes the need to explore genetic diversity both within and between ecosystems, suggesting a broad geographic and ecological scope covering India's diverse aquatic habitats.

## Key Findings

**Primary Consulting Objectives:**

1. **Inventory Development**: Design methodology for comprehensive inventory of existing fish genetic resources in [[India]]

2. **Conservation Prioritization**: Assess types and kinds of fish genetic resources requiring conservation, including domesticated and wild varieties; develop workable methods for identifying endangered, vulnerable, and rare fishes despite limited historical abundance data

3. **Threat Assessment**: Establish methodology for identifying causative factors leading to endangered status

4. **Conservation Strategy**: Review existing conservation measures and suggest pragmatic ex situ and in situ techniques

5. **Infrastructure Development**: 
   - Design blueprint for fish farm-cum-hatchery at proposed NBFGR site for endangered species conservation
   - Plan full-fledged gene bank establishment
   - Assess laboratory infrastructure requirements for optimal research conditions in new building

6. **Research Capabilities**: Create facilities for genetic diversity studies and fundamental research on fish genetics, with special emphasis on:
   - Recombinant DNA technology
   - Restriction Fragment Length Polymorphisms (RFLPs)
   - Gene mapping
   - Cytogenetics
   - Biochemical genetics
   - Biotechnology applications

7. **Genetic Characterization**: Identify requirements for genetic characterization studies covering experimental, laboratory, and field work to strengthen conservation programs

8. **Germplasm Exchange**: Examine prospects for exchanging fish germplasm resources internationally

9. **Network Development**: Determine requirements for network with commodity institutes, universities, and organizations, including infrastructure needs at network sites and Bureau's proposed centers

10. **Institutional Collaboration**: Assign supporting and service roles for different institutes and universities; develop protocols and tie-up programs for interinstitutional collaboration at national, regional, and international levels

11. **Capacity Building**: Address training and manpower development needs; establish educational and training facilities

**Timeline**: April 16 - May 9, 1992 (proposed consultancy period)
