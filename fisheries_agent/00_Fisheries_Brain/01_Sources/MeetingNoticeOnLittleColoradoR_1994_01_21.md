## Executive Summary
This document is a 1994 interagency meeting notice from the Arizona Game and Fish Department (AZGFD), organized by Native Fish Program Manager Kirk L. Young, to initiate planning for the renovation and stocking of **Potato Lake** as a refugium for the **[[Little Colorado River Spinedace]]** (*Lepidomeda vittata*). Rather than presenting original morphological or ecological research data, the source outlines the logistical, regulatory, and biological planning framework required to establish a captive/refuge population of this imperiled cyprinid-lineage fish. The meeting agenda reflects a multi-agency, multi-disciplinary conservation effort involving academic (Northern Arizona University), federal (U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Forest Service), and state (AZGFD) stakeholders.
