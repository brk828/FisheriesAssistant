# Desert Pupfish Annual Permit Report, 1992

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinodontidae

## Executive Summary

This letter is the 1992 annual report from the [[California Department Of Fish And Game]] (Inland Fisheries Division) to the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] (USFWS). It covers fish collections made under Permit No. BOLSBC, dated May 28, 1992, on June 7–8, 1992. The letter is written by Betsy C. Bolster, Associate Fishery Biologist with the Endangered Species Project, and addressed to Allison Banks. The document refers to the species only as "desert pupfish," and the scientific name in the frontmatter is supplied from general knowledge, not from the text. The report lists 255 fish collected from 2 wild and 6 refugium sites, and it notes a pending genetic study and a manuscript in preparation.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Official correspondence (annual permit report), dated December 15, 1992.
- **Sender:** Betsy C. Bolster, Associate Fishery Biologist, Endangered Species Project, [[California Department Of Fish And Game]], Rancho Cordova, California.
- **Recipient:** Allison Banks, [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]], Portland, Oregon. Copies went to Ray Bransfield (USFWS-Ventura) and Sally Stefferud (USFWS-Phoenix).
- **Permit:** No. BOLSBC, dated May 28, 1992, under which the collections were made. The author thanks USFWS for help in obtaining the permit.
- **Target taxon:** [[Desert Pupfish]], a small endangered fish of the arid Southwest. The letter does not describe its morphology, life history or taxonomy.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The collections fall into two groups of sites, which reflects the conservation approach for the species:

**Wild populations (2 sites, 60 fish):**
- [[Salt Creek]], Riverside County: 30 fish
- [[San Felipe Creek]], Imperial County: 30 fish

**Refugium populations (6 sites, 195 fish):**
- [[Thousand Palms Oasis]] / Simone / McCallum Pond, Riverside County: 50 fish
- A pond at The Living Desert, Riverside County: 35 fish
- [[Palm Canyon Pond]], [[Anza Borrego Desert State Park]], San Diego County: 50 fish
- [[Cleveland Street Drain]], Riverside County: 30 fish
- Salton Sea shoreline pool (Cleveland Pool), Riverside County: 30 fish, near the [[Salton Sea]]

Refugium ponds and drains are managed populations kept apart from the wild sites, which is a standard hedge against habitat loss for an endangered species. The letter does not give water chemistry, habitat measurements or population estimates.

## Key Findings

1. **Sampling effort:** Desert pupfish were collected on June 7–8, 1992, from 8 sites in total, for 255 fish.
2. **Wild populations:** 60 fish were taken from two wild sites, 30 each from Salt Creek and San Felipe Creek.
3. **Refugium populations:** 195 fish were taken from six refugium sites. The largest samples came from Thousand Palms Oasis/Simone/McCallum Pond and Palm Canyon Pond (50 each).
4. **Genetic work:** Jason Dunham, a graduate student, is conducting electrophoretic analysis of the collected fish. His report was expected later in December 1992, and a copy was to be sent to USFWS on receipt.
5. **Publication:** Dunham and Dr. W.L. Minckley have submitted a manuscript for publication on this work.
6. **Permit status:** The letter notes that the permit was obtained with USFWS assistance, after a delay.
