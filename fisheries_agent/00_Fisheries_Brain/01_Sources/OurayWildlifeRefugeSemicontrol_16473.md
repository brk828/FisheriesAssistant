# Ouray Wildlife Refuge, semi-control of flood inflow to Pond L-7, One Low Head Weir

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-collection

## Executive Summary

This document is a single engineering drawing sheet, labeled **Scheme 4**, showing a proposed plan to semi-control flood inflow to [[Pond L-7]] using a [[Low Head Weir]]. It was prepared by [[FLO Engineering Inc]] (Breckenridge, CO) and dated 6/24/94. The plan combines weir construction, pond regrading, culvert and gate installation, canal and channel work, dike raising, and a [[Fish Collection Facility]]. The document contains no species data, sampling results, or fish population information. Its fish-related content is limited to the presence of the collection facility on the plan.

The site is a managed impoundment network fed by a main canal and an existing gravity feed canal. Relevant features shown on the plan include:

- **Water surface elevations (W.S. ELEV.):** Most units are at 4661 ft, with others at 4660 ft (lowest), 4663 ft, 4664 ft, 4665 ft, and 4666 ft. Units L-2 and the area near the service road are listed at 4666 ft.

- **Fish collection facility:** Shown as a discrete feature, but no design details, target species, or operational data are provided.

No water quality, habitat, biological, or hydrological data are presented beyond the elevations above.

## Key Findings

The drawing lists six numbered components of Scheme 4, with a seventh-to-eleventh set of related actions. Summarized from the legible labels:

1. **Construct low head weir** to control flood inflow, stabilized with [[Riprap]].
2. **Regrade pond topography** within the pond system.
3. **Remove vegetation and regrade the active channel** leading to the weir.
4. **Install low flow outlet culverts and gates.**
5. **Clean out and improve conveyance** in the main canal.
6. **Install gated culverts joining ponds.**
7. **Remove the existing canal** and **raise the dike** as appropriate.
8. **Install a fish collection facility.** The plan includes a fish collection facility, but no species, capacity, or method details are shown.

Other notes on the plan:

- A **proposed jetty** and **proposed culvert** are shown in the legend.
- The sheet points to Scheme #7 for possible additional options.
