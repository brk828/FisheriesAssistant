## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources (Department of Natural Resources) addressed to the Curator of Ichthyology at [[Arizona State University]]. ## Key Findings
- The Utah Division of Wildlife Resources ([[DWR]]) was building a **locality database** (as of 1997) combining historic and current records of indigenous fish and mollusk occurrences within the state.
- The database was intended to be integrated with a **Geographic Information System (GIS)** for species distribution mapping.
- Data fields specifically requested from museum/university collections included:
  - Scientific name
  - Date collected
  - Locality information (**UTM coordinates preferred**)
  - Sex of specimen
  - Quantity sampled
  - Museum catalogue number
  - Collector name
- The request was directed to the **Curator of Ichthyology** at [[Arizona State University]], indicating that ASU held fish (and possibly mollusk) specimen records relevant to Utah localities.
- The point of contact for the project was **Michael Canning**, working under **[[Native Aquatics Coordinator]] Lentsch**, within the Native Species Section of the Utah DWR.
- This letter exemplifies inter-institutional cooperation between state wildlife agencies and academic natural history collections for **biodiversity data-sharing** and conservation planning purposes.
