## Executive Summary
This document is an emergency Section 7 consultation letter issued by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Arizona Ecological Services State Office) to the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, authorizing emergency repairs to Forest Road 281 along the Blue River in Greenlee County, Arizona. The road was rendered impassable by flooding from rains on December 5–6, 1994 (following earlier damage from mid-to-late November flooding), isolating approximately 12 families. Because the Blue River supports a population of the federally threatened [[Loach Minnow]] (*Tiaroga cobitis*), the letter formalizes emergency consultation under the [[Endangered Species Act]] and imposes conditions to minimize adverse impacts to the species during the road repair work. No morphological, population, or detailed habitat data are presented; the document is regulatory/administrative in nature rather than a primary scientific study.
