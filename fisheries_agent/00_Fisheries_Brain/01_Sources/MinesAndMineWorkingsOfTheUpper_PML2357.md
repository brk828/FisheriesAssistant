# Appendix C: Mines and Mine Workings of the Upper Aravaipa Creek Area

#aquatic-ecology #freshwater-habitat #mining-contamination

## Executive Summary

This appendix is a literature-based inventory of recorded mines, mine workings and exploratory prospects in the upper [[Aravaipa Creek]] area of southeastern Arizona. It draws mainly on [[Simons 1964]], which covers the [[Klondyke Quadrangle]] (USGS). The compiler notes that some localities may lie outside the Aravaipa Creek drainage basin. They were included because they indicate ore bodies from which heavy metals or other contaminants could originate and enter the aquatic system. The document is a reference appendix, not a field study, and it reports no fish, invertebrate, water-chemistry or sampling data.

## Background & Description

The appendix has two parts:

- **Appendix Table C-1** lists recorded mines and mine workings. Scan quality on the table pages is poor, so individual entries cannot be reliably transcribed.
- **Appendix Table C-2** (titled "Miscellaneous Exploratory Prospects of the Upper Aravaipa Creek Area, Southeastern Arizona") lists named and unnamed prospects. Each entry gives a name or location description and a legal description (section, township and range). Entries span pages 5 to 7, and the townships cited run from T5S to T9S with ranges R18E to R20E.

Named examples in Table C-2 include the Green Gem Claim, Silver Reef Claim, Booker T. Washington Claim, Copper Hill Prospect, Mission No. 1 and Mission No. 4 Claims, and the Buena Suerte claim (two prospects). Other entries are referenced to landmarks such as [[Old Deer Creek]], [[Landsman Camp]], [[Tule Spring]], [[Glory Mine]], [[Old Reliable Mine]], [[Copper Creek]] and the [[Galiuro Mountains]] crest. Many entries are described only as "unnamed," and some legal descriptions are marked "Unknown."

The literature cited comprises regional geologic and mining sources, including [[Creasey et al. 1961]] (reconnaissance geologic map of south-central Arizona), [[Davis and Brooks 1930]] (Galiuro Mountains), [[Denton 1947]] (Old Reliable copper mine), [[Galbraith and Brennan 1959]] (Arizona mineral deposits), and [[Ross 1925]] and [[Wilson 1919]] (Aravaipa district ore deposits).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The appendix is relevant to aquatic ecology only through its stated purpose. Mineralized prospects are treated as potential point sources of [[Heavy Metal Contamination]] for the stream system. The compiler's rationale is that the mapped localities show where ore bodies occur, and that mineralized material near drainages could reach the creek. Several entries sit along or near tributaries, including Right Prong Four-Mile Creek, Copper Creek and upper Klondyke Wash / Spring Canyon. The appendix does not measure contaminant levels, document water quality or describe aquatic biota. Any assessment of actual impact would require separate data.

## Key Findings

- The appendix compiles mines and prospects of the upper Aravaipa Creek area from published literature, principally [[Simons 1964]].
- Table C-1 covers recorded mines and workings, but its pages are largely illegible in the scan.
- Table C-2 lists miscellaneous exploratory prospects with section-level legal descriptions. Its entries run from T5S to T9S and from R18E to R20E.
- Some listed localities may lie outside the Aravaipa Creek drainage. They were kept because they indicate ore bodies that could release heavy metals or other contaminants into the aquatic system.
- The appendix contains no measured contaminant concentrations, water-quality results, fish or invertebrate records, or habitat surveys.
