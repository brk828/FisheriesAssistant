## Executive Summary
This document is a **Section 7 Endangered Species Act concurrence letter** from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Arizona Ecological Services Field Office) to the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, addressing three land management projects: the **Bear Canyon Timber Sale**, **Little Timber Sale**, and **KP Trail Reconstruction**.
