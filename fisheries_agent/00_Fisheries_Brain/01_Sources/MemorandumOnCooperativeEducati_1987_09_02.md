# Memorandum on Cooperative Educational Agreement between Arizona State University and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service

## Executive Summary
This document is **not a scientific or ichthyological research paper**. It is an internal administrative memorandum, dated 2 September 1987, from Dr. W. L. Minckley of the Department of Zoology at Arizona State University (ASU) to Dr. Kirkpatrick, Dean of the College of Liberal Arts. The memo requests administrative review and expedited approval of a **Cooperative Education Agreement** between ASU and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS), intended to establish a joint graduate and undergraduate training program. ## Key Findings
Although this document contains no direct fish science data, it provides valuable **institutional and historical context** for research programs that may have supported subsequent ichthyological studies:

- **Origin of Agreement**: Discussions between W. L. Minckley and USFWS personnel began in 1984–85 during an Interagency Personnel Agreement in New Mexico, laying groundwork for a formal partnership.
- **Program Purpose**: Establishes a cooperative graduate/undergraduate education program between [[Arizona State University]] and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], with the incentive that program participants would be offered USFWS employment upon graduation.
- **Institutional Support**: The agreement was supported by ASU Zoology Department leadership, including Dr. Ann E. Kammer (Department Chair) and Dr. Robert McGaughy (Director of Graduate Studies).

- **Liaison Role**: W. L. Minckley agreed to serve as the Zoology Department's liaison for the program, reflecting his active role in bridging academic research and federal fisheries/wildlife management.
