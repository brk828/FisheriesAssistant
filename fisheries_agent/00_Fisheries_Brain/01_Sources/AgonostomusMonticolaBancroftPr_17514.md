# Agonostomus monticola (Mountain Mullet): First Record of Mugilidae in Nuevo León, Mexico

#ichthyology #marine-biology #mugilidae

## Executive Summary

This short 1972 note by [[Salvador Contreras Balderas]], published in the *Cuadernos del Instituto de Investigaciones Científicas* (No. 16, Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León), reports the first record of the family [[Mugilidae]] in the state of [[Nuevo León]], Mexico. Two specimens of [[Agonostomus Monticola]] (Mountain Mullet), collected on 23–24 June 1972 at the [[Presa de Paras]] on the [[Río Alamo]], were measured, counted and compared with the published description by [[Robert D. Suttkus]] (1956). The specimens agree closely with that description, and the author judged the minor differences not significant. The paper also documents the locality's habitat and lists the fish species known from the site.

## Background & Description

**Context of the study.** The note is framed as a further contribution to the knowledge of the freshwater ichthyofauna of Nuevo León, particularly in tributaries of the [[Río Bravo]]. The collection was sponsored by the [[Aquarium Municipal Felipe de J. Benavides]] in Monterrey and led by the author, who was affiliated with the Laboratorio de Vertebrados, Facultad de Ciencias Biológicas (Contribution No. 14).

**Study site.** The collection site lies at 170 m above sea level, at coordinates N 26°30′ and W 99°32′. The source states that it is about 475 km from the sea. The Río Alamo is described as a small subbasin of the Río Bravo, situated between the Salado and San Juan rivers.

**Material examined.** Two specimens were deposited in the UANL collection as catalogue number UANL 1416. Their standard lengths were 104 and 104.5 mm.

**Methods.** Counts and measurements followed the standardized method of [[Hubbs And Lagler Method]] (1947). Morphometric values are expressed in thousandths of standard length.

**Diagnostic remarks.** The specimens lack the yellow coloration at the bases of the dorsal and anal fins that Suttkus described. The upper lip overhangs the lower lip conspicuously, a feature not obvious in Suttkus's material. The eye is small. Some values are slightly lower than those published by Suttkus. The author accepts Suttkus's identification of the rudimentary rays of the second dorsal and anal fins, where scalation extends only to the middle of the anterior fin portion and barely covers the basal quarter of the last rays.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Distribution.** The author reviews the known range of *A. monticola* in Mexico, citing [[Meek]] (1904), [[Álvarez]] (1970), [[Miller]] (1966) and [[Follett]] (1960). The nearest published localities are:
- The [[Río Pánuco]] near Valles, San Luis Potosí (Suttkus, 1956)
- The [[Mississippi River]] at Point à la Hache, [[Louisiana]] (Suttkus, 1956)

[[Hubbs]] (1972) reports the species as coastal in [[Texas]], without locality data. The distribution map (Mapa 1) shows literature localities, the new Río Alamo record, and a visual observation in the [[Río San Fernando]], Nuevo León, reported by Jorge Verduzco M. The San Fernando record is unconfirmed by specimens.

**Habitat at the collection site.**
- The Río Alamo is 6–8 m wide at the collection point, below the dam.
- Depth rarely exceeds 1 m.
- Aquatic vegetation is dominated by [[Nuphar]] and [[Nymphaea]], with relatively abundant algae and sparse [[Utricularia]].
- Shoreline vegetation includes *Eleocharis*, *Scirpus*, *Cyperus* and *Juncus*.

**Associated fish fauna.** The author lists 15 taxa known from the locality:
- Lepisosteus oculatus (recorded there by the author in a separate, in-press paper)
- Dorosoma cepedianum
- Astyanax fasciatus mexicanus
- Cyprinus carpio
- Notropis amabilis
- Carpiodes carpio elongatus
- Ictalurus punctatus
- Ictalurus sp. (indeterminate)
- Pylodictis olivaris
- Gambusia affinis
- Cichlasoma cyanoguttatus
- Lepomis macrochirus
- Lepomis cyanellus
- Micropterus salmoides
- Aplodinotus grunniens

The assemblage is dominated by cyprinids, ictalurids, centrarchids and introduced species such as [[Cyprinus Carpio]] and [[Gambusia Affinis]]. The list contains no other mugilids.

## Key Findings

- **First family record.** This is the first record of Mugilidae in Nuevo León, documented from two specimens (104 and 104.5 mm SL) collected at the Presa de Paras on the Río Alamo, a tributary subbasin of the Río Bravo.
- **Species identification.** The specimens match the redescription by Suttkus (1956) in nearly all characters. The differences noted (absence of yellow fin bases, slightly smaller values for some measurements, and a conspicuously overhanging upper lip) were judged not significant.
- **Range extension.** The record extends the known inland distribution of *A. monticola* northward within Mexico, between the Río Pánuco populations and the Texas coast. The author notes that the San Fernando sighting remains unconfirmed.
- **Habitat.** The species was found in a shallow (<1 m), vegetated river reach of about 6–8 m width at 170 m elevation, with emergent and submerged macrophytes.
- **Fauna inventory.** Fifteen fish taxa are documented from the same site, providing a baseline for the regional ichthyofauna.
- **Collection and deposition.** Voucher specimens are catalogued as UANL 1416 at the Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León, establishing a verifiable reference for the record.
