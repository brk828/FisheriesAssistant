## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary research paper but an administrative letter dated 25 August 1989 from Kevin R. Bestgen (Larval Fish Laboratory, Colorado State University) to Dr. W. L. Minckley (Arizona State University). The letter requests unpublished data, agency reports, stocking records, and expert input to support the preparation of a **[[Razorback Sucker]] Management Plan** for the upper **[[Colorado River Basin]]**, commissioned by the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation on behalf of the **Colorado River Recovery Implementation Committee**. No direct morphological, physiological, or quantitative ecological data on the species are presented in the text; instead, the letter outlines the scope of a forthcoming review document and identifies a network of recognized razorback sucker researchers and agency personnel.

## Key Findings
- The **Colorado River Recovery Implementation Committee** tasked the **U.S. Bureau of Reclamation (BR)** with producing a **Razorback Sucker Management Plan** for the upper Colorado River Basin.
- The **Larval Fish Laboratory, Colorado State University**, with **Kevin R. Bestgen** as principal contact, was contracted to prepare the underlying scientific review document.
- The review was intended to synthesize existing published reviews plus solicited unpublished materials: research in press, unpublished agency reports/memos, stocking records, and ongoing job/project descriptions.
- A distribution list of **20 recipients** was compiled, representing key researchers and agency biologists working on razorback sucker and Colorado River Basin native fish conservation at the time, including:
  - **W. L. Minckley** (Arizona State University) — primary addressee
  - **Paul Marsh**, **Gordon Mueller**, **Harold Tyus**, **Rich Valdez**, **Dean Hendrickson**, **Lynn Kaeding**, **Chuck McAda**, **Doug Osmundson**, **Tom Nesler**, **Pat Martinez**, **Miles Moretti**, **Steve Cranney**, **Ed Wick**, **J. Holt Williamson**, and others (Glenn Black, Jim Brooks, Susan Ellis, Linda Ulmer).
- The resulting document was intended to be incorporated into a **draft management plan** for external review and comment, situating this letter as an early procedural step in what became the broader **[[Upper Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program]]** planning process.
