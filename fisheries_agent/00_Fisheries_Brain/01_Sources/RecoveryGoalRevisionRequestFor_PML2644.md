# Recovery Goal Revision Request for Four Endangered Colorado River Fishes

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

The [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Service) letter, dated March 30, 2007, requests new and substantial data to support a review and revision of the 2002 [[Recovery Goals]] for four endangered fishes of the [[Colorado River Basin]]. The Service plans to use the revised goals as guidance when considering downlisting or delisting these species. The request is addressed to the Department Head at Arizona State University and is also sent to the seven basin states, other federal agencies, [[Native American Tribes]], and nongovernmental organizations. Submissions are accepted through May 31, 2007.

## Background & Description

The four species covered are:

- [[Colorado Pikeminnow]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*)
- [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*)
- [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*)
- [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*)

The recovery goals were developed as an amendment and supplement to each species' recovery plan. They address the requirements of Section 4(f)(1)(B) of the [[Endangered Species Act]] (ESA), which calls for site-specific management actions, objective and measurable criteria, and estimates of time and cost to achieve recovery goals and intermediate steps.

The review is required under Section 4(c)(2)(A) of the ESA, which directs the Secretary to review all listed species at least once every five years. [[Region 6 (Mountain-Prairie)]] of the Service leads recovery efforts, with support from [[Region 2 (Southwest)]] and the California and Nevada Operations Office.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter identifies threats and listing factors as the basis for revisions:

- Chapter 4.0 of the recovery goals describes threats under each of the five ESA listing factors. Changes in threats should be reported against this framework.
- Chapter 5.0 covers site-specific management actions and objective, measurable criteria. Proposed changes should be identified there.
- Relevant data include genetics information affecting genetic effective population size and minimum viable population size, population size estimates, and monitoring results.

The ESA implementing regulations (50 CFR § 402.02) and the Service's 2003 Draft Endangered and Threatened Species Recovery Handbook provide further guidance. The letter does not report any population counts, habitat measurements, or water chemistry data.

## Key Findings

- **Purpose:** Standardize requests for new data (dated since 2002) that may warrant revising the four recovery goals documents.
- **Data requirements:** Submissions should be new and substantial, preferably final reports or peer-reviewed literature. Each must be documented with authorship, publication date, document name, and source, in hard or electronic copy.
- **Cost information:** The Service also requests data on the time and cost of the management actions identified in the recovery goals.
- **Deadline:** New information will be accepted through May 31, 2007.
- **Access:** Current recovery goals documents are available in hard copy on request or for download from the Service's Mountain-Prairie website.
- **Contacts:** Sam Spiller, Lower Colorado River Coordinator, Phoenix, Arizona, signed on behalf of the Assistant Regional Director, Ecological Services.
