# Request for a Ruling to Correct Homonymy: Plethodus (Pisces) vs. Plethodon (Caudata)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #osteoglossiformes #nomenclature #paleoichthyology

## Executive Summary

This is a taxonomic **nomenclature ruling request** submitted to the International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN), addressing a case of **homonymy** between two family-group names derived from similarly spelled but taxonomically unrelated genera: **[[Plethodus]]** (an extinct genus of osteoglossoid fish) and **[[Plethodon]]** (a genus of lungless salamanders). Both genus names independently yield the family-group name **PLETHODONTIDAE** because the suffixes *-odon* and *-odus* share the same Greek genitive stem *odontos* (per Brown, 1954). Since both fish and amphibian workers had used "Plethodontidae" for their respective taxa, direct homonymy existed. The authors (Collette, representing ichthyology, and Wake, representing herpetology) petitioned the Commission to resolve the conflict by reserving **PLETHODONTIDAE** for the salamander family (Caudata) — as it is the larger, better-known group — and formally adopting **PLETHODIDAE** Loomis, 1900, as the correct name for the fossil fish family, rejecting the alternate incorrect spelling "Plethodontidae Hay, 1929" and the substitute name **[[Thryptodontidae]]**.

## Background & Description

Although this paper is chiefly nomenclatural rather than morphological, it references diagnostic taxonomic anchors for both groups:

### Pisces: *Plethodus* Dixon, 1850
- **Order:** [[Osteoglossiformes]] (osteoglossoid fishes)
- **Status:** Extinct (fossil), Upper Cretaceous
- **Type species:** *Plethodus expansus* Dixon, 1850, designated subsequently by Woodward (1899)
- **Family placement:** PLETHODIDAE Loomis, 1900, comprising ~8 genera, including *Plethodus*, *Thryptodus*, and *Pseudothryptodus*
- **Geographic/stratigraphic range:** Upper Cretaceous deposits of Europe, North Africa, and North America (per Bardack, 1965)
- **Gender of genus name:** Masculine

### Caudata: *Plethodon* Tschudi, 1838
- **Order:** Caudata (Amphibia)
- **Common name:** Lungless salamanders
- **Type species:** *Salamandra glutinosa* Green, 1818, designated subsequently by Bibron (in Bonaparte, 1839)
- **Family placement:** PLETHODONTIDAE Gray, 1850
- **Diversity:** 23 genera, ~185 species
- **Gender of genus name:** Masculine

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **[[Plethodus]] (fossil fish):** Marine osteoglossoid fish inhabiting Late Cretaceous seas; known from depositional environments across [[Europe]], [[North Africa]], and [[North America]], indicating a broad Tethyan/Atlantic paleobiogeographic distribution during the Late Cretaceous.
- **[[Plethodon]] (salamanders):** Terrestrial/semi-terrestrial vertebrates of the extant family [[Plethodontidae]], distributed almost exclusively in the **New World**, with two notable exceptions in **southern Europe** (Wake, 1966). This family is ecologically significant as the most speciose group of salamanders, notable for lacking lungs and relying on cutaneous/buccal respiration.

## Key Findings

1. **Homonymy identified:** Both *Plethodus* (Pisces) and *Plethodon* (Caudata) independently generate the family-group name PLETHODONTIDAE due to shared Greek root *odous/odontos* ("tooth"), triggering ICZN Article 55(a) referral.
2. **Authorship confusion:** Loomis (1900) originally proposed **PLETHODIDAE** for the fossil fish family but inadvertently credited "Plethodidae A.S. Woodward" in his introductory text, leading to decades of misattribution (e.g., Hay 1929 credited Woodward, 1912).
3. **Divergent usage in literature:** Fish paleontologists have inconsistently used **PLETHODIDAE** (Berg 1940/1955; Bertin & Arambourg 1957; Bardack 1965), the substitute name **THRYPTODONTIDAE** (Jordan 1923; Romer 1966), or the erroneous **PLETHODONTIDAE** (Romer 1945; Greenwood et al. 1966).
4. **Proposed resolution:**
   - Retain **PLETHODONTIDAE** Gray, 1850, for the salamander family (Caudata), because it is the larger and more widely studied taxon.
   - Formally adopt **PLETHODIDAE** Loomis, 1900, as the valid name for the fossil fish family (Pisces), rejecting Hay's (1929) erroneous "Plethodontidae" for fish as an incorrect spelling under the Commission's plenary powers.
   - Officially designate type-species and type-genera for both *Plethodon* and *Plethodus* to stabilize nomenclature.
5. **Formal actions requested from ICZN:**
   - Rule that PLETHODIDAE is the correct spelling for the family based on *Plethodus*.
   - Place *Plethodon* Tschudi, 1838, and *Plethodus* Dixon, 1850, on the **Official List of Generic Names in Zoology**.
   - Place *Salamandra glutinosa* Green, 1818, and *Plethodus expansus* Dixon, 1850, on the **Official List of Specific Names in Zoology**.
   - Place **PLETHODONTIDAE** Gray, 1850 (Caudata) and **PLETHODIDAE** Loomis, 1900 (Pisces) on the **Official List of Family-Group Names in Zoology**.
   - Place **PLETHODONTIDAE Hay, 1929** (as applied erroneously to Pisces) on the **Official Index of Rejected and Invalid Family-Group Names**.
6. **Broader taxonomic implication:** This ruling exemplifies how the ICZN plenary powers can resolve cross-phylum homonymy arising from convergent Latinized generic name formation, preserving nomenclatural stability across both [[Ichthyology]] and [[Herpetology]] literature.
