## Executive Summary

This document is **not a scientific paper** but a cover letter from the Washington County Water Conservancy District (Utah) transmitting a copy of the *Virgin River Return Flow Study* (conducted by Psomas and Associates, Salt Lake City) to Dr. Wendell Minckley, a noted ichthyologist at Arizona State University's Department of Zoology. The letter itself contains **no direct fish morphological, ecological, or population data**. Its scientific relevance lies entirely in the hydrological context it provides: changes in the flow regime of the [[Virgin River]] following construction of the [[Quail Creek Reservoir]] and associated pipeline. Because flow regime alterations are a primary driver of habitat change for native fish species in the [[Virgin River]] (notably endemic and imperiled taxa historically studied by Minckley), this correspondence is likely included in a broader research archive as supporting hydrological evidence for fish habitat assessments.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

While no direct species-habitat data is given, the letter documents a critical **abiotic driver** of fish ecological niches in the Virgin River system:

- **System:** Mainstem of the [[Virgin River]], downstream from the USGS stream gage at Virgin, Utah.
- **Habitat Alteration Event:** Construction of the [[Quail Creek Reservoir]] and associated pipeline infrastructure.
- **Flow Regime Change:**
  - Increase of **17 cfs (cubic feet per second)** during summer months post-construction.
  - Yearly average gain of over **14 cfs** in the river since the reservoir/pipeline project was completed.
- **Implication for Aquatic Ecosystems:** Return flows (irrigation/reservoir discharge) entering the mainstem can alter thermal regimes, sediment transport, discharge stability, and water chemistry — all of which affect [[Native Fish Habitat]] suitability for Virgin River endemics such as [[Virgin River Chub]] and [[Woundfin]], species historically studied by W.L. Minckley in this basin.

## Key Findings

- The *Virgin River Return Flow Study* quantifies **hydrological changes** in the Virgin River mainstem attributable to the [[Quail Creek Reservoir]] project.
- Documented **summer flow increase: +17 cfs**.
- Documented **average annual flow increase: +14 cfs**.
- The study appears to have been shared with fish biologists (e.g., Wendell Minckley) due to its relevance to **instream flow management** and downstream aquatic habitat conditions in a system supporting federally-listed and endemic fish species.
- No direct fish population, morphology, or behavioral data is contained within this particular document — it functions as **supporting infrastructural/hydrological context** rather than a primary ichthyological study.
