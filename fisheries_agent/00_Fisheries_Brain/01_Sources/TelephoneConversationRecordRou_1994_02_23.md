# Telephone Conversation Record: Roundtail Chub Petition Status (1994)

## Executive Summary
This document is not a scientific/morphological study but an internal U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) telephone conversation record dated February 23, 1994, addressing an administrative discrepancy regarding the listing status of the [[Roundtail Chub]] (*Gila robusta*) under the Endangered Species Act. The call, between Steve Chambers (FWS Albuquerque Regional Office) and Sally Stefferud (FWS Phoenix Arizona Ecological Services Office), clarifies that no petition for listing the roundtail chub was ever formally received by FWS, despite the species being referenced as "warranted but precluded" in the [[Fund for Animals]] lawsuit settlement and appearing in a December 9, 1993 Federal Register notice as "not warranted." The conversation highlights internal disagreement over the significance of this regulatory error and its potential future implications for the species' conservation status.

## Background & Description
No morphological, anatomical, or physical trait data are provided in this source document. The text is purely administrative/regulatory in nature and does not describe body form, coloration, size, or diagnostic features of *Gila robusta*.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- Species discussed: [[Roundtail Chub]] (*Gila robusta*), a member of the family [[Cyprinidae]], native to the [[Gila River]] basin and other desert waterways of the southwestern United States.
- The document references the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]'s recommendation to list the species, implying conservation concern for its wild populations, likely linked to habitat degradation typical of desert fish species (e.g., water diversion, non-native species competition, habitat fragmentation).
- Notably, the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AGFD) is reported to be pursuing efforts to designate the roundtail chub as a **sport fish**, a management action that could preclude federal listing under the Endangered Species Act — reflecting a tension between game-species management and endangered species conservation policy.

## Key Findings
1. **No petition on record**: FWS Regional staff (Steve Chambers) determined that no petition for listing the roundtail chub was ever received, despite it being referenced in three separate official contexts.
2. **Identified errors**: Three specific regulatory errors were acknowledged:
   - The listing of roundtail chub as "warranted but precluded" in the [[Fund for Animals]] lawsuit settlement.
   - An erroneous petition number and date entry in the FWS Notice of Review (NOR) log.
   - A December 9, 1993 Federal Register (FR) notice erroneously declaring a "not warranted" finding for the species.
3. **Resolution proposed**: The Regional Office intended to request removal of the petition reference from the NOR log; however, Chambers declined to provide formal written documentation of the error, considering a verbal phone call sufficient notification to the State and other involved parties.
4. **Interagency disagreement on significance**: Sally Stefferud raised concern that the erroneous "not warranted" FR finding could create future confusion regarding the Service's official position, especially given:
   - The [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]'s recommendation for federal listing.
   - The [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]'s competing effort to classify the roundtail chub as a sport fish, which could preclude ESA listing.
5. **Institutional tension**: Chambers dismissed these concerns as unfounded, while Stefferud maintained that unresolved regulatory ambiguity could complicate future listing and management decisions for *Gila robusta*, foreshadowing later controversy over the species' conservation status.
6. **Distribution list**: The matter was circulated to multiple FWS offices (Reno NV, Grand Junction CO, Albuquerque NM, Phoenix AZ) as well as AGFD and the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] (represented by W.L. Minckley), indicating the multi-jurisdictional interest in the roundtail chub's regulatory status.
