# Cui-ui Recovery Team Invitation and Recovery Plan Review

#ichthyology #marine-biology #chasmistes

## Executive Summary

This two-page correspondence set concerns the reconvening of the [[Cui-ui Recovery Team]] by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (the Service) to guide the recovery of the [[Cui-ui]] (*[[Chasmistes Cujus]]*). Page 1 is a formal invitation from the Service's Regional Director to Dr. [[Minckley]] of [[Arizona State University]] to serve as a team member. Page 2 is a fragment of a reply letter from Arizona State University addressed to [[Marvin L. Plenert]], confirming that the writer had already notified the recipient by telephone of his decision. The invitation requests a response by March 1, 1989. No biological data on the species are presented.

## Background & Description

The Service is reconvening the Cui-ui Recovery Team, a panel of experts from several biological disciplines that will provide broad technical guidance for cui-ui recovery. Candidates were nominated by cooperating governmental agencies and interested organizations. The Service concluded that Dr. Minckley's expertise and professional experience would be especially valuable to the team.

The letter does not describe the species' morphology, taxonomy beyond the scientific name, life history, or distribution.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address habitat, water chemistry, population status, threats, or the wider aquatic ecosystem. Its ecological relevance is limited to establishing the expert team that will help guide recovery of the species.

## Key Findings

- **Team formation:** The Service is reconvening the [[Cui-ui Recovery Team]] and has invited Dr. Minckley to participate as a member.
- **Response deadline:** The invitee was asked to respond by **March 1, 1989**, indicating interest and willingness to serve.
- **First priority:** The team's first task is to review and update the [[Cui-ui Recovery Plan]].
- **Coordination:** Meeting date and location were to be set by later correspondence.
- **Executive Secretary:** [[Chester Buchanan]], Assistant Field Supervisor at the [[Reno Field Station]], is the proposed Executive Secretary. Contact details given are 702-784-5227 (commercial) and FTS 470-5227.
- **Reply from Arizona State University:** The page 2 fragment, dated 4 March (year not legible), is from the university's Department of Zoology to Mr. Plenert.
