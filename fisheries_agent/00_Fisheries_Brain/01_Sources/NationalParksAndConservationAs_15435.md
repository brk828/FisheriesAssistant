# National Park Service Commission on Research and Resource Management Policy Correspondence

#administrative #policy #resource-management #national-parks

## Executive Summary

This document is official correspondence from the [[National Park Service]] (NPS) dated March 24, 1989, addressed to Dr. John C. Gordon, Dean of the [[Yale School of Forestry and Environmental Studies]], regarding the establishment of a Commission on Research and Resource Management Policy in the National Park System. The commission was convened by the [[National Parks and Conservation Association]] (NPCA) to examine policy issues related to research and resource management across the 341 units of the National Park System. The correspondence includes meeting agendas for the commission's first meeting scheduled for April 18-20, 1988, and a roster of commission members representing diverse expertise in natural resources, coastal engineering, history, archaeology, and biological sciences.

## Background & Description

The commission was established to address major policy issues facing the [[National Park Service]] in research and resource management functions. The NPS provided the commission with extensive background materials including draft Management Policies undergoing review, park unit folders, the NPS 12-Point Plan, annual reports, and internal memorandums describing natural and cultural resource management program emphases for the current fiscal year.

The commission membership included experts from multiple disciplines: Dr. Jerry Franklin (Chairman, [[University of Washington]]), Dr. John C. Gordon ([[Yale School of Forestry and Environmental Studies]]), Dr. Ervin Zube ([[University of Arizona]]), Dr. Barbara Howe (West Virginia University), Dr. Tim Clark (Jackson, Wyoming), Dr. George Gumerman (Santa Fe, New Mexico), Dr. Douglas Latimer (Gaia Associates), Dr. Robert Dean ([[University of Florida]], Department of Coastal and Oceanographic Engineering), Dr. Stephen P. Leatherman ([[University of Maryland]], Laboratory for Coastal Research), Dr. Harold Mooney ([[Stanford University]], Department of Biological Sciences), and others representing cultural resources, recreation, and parks management.

Carol F. Aten, Chief of Offices, was designated as the NPS coordination point for the commission, reachable at (202) 343-7468.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The correspondence acknowledges the great diversity in issues facing the 341 units of the National Park System and the diversity of their resources. The commission's scope encompassed both natural and cultural resource management, reflecting the dual mandate of the National Park Service to preserve natural ecosystems and cultural heritage sites.

The inclusion of coastal engineering expertise (Dr. Robert Dean, Dr. Stephen P. Leatherman) and biological sciences (Dr. Harold Mooney) on the commission indicates recognition of environmental challenges including coastal processes, ecosystem management, and biological resource conservation across diverse park units.

## Key Findings

The first commission meeting agenda (April 18-20, 1988) outlined the following key activities and organizational elements:

**Meeting Structure:**
- Welcoming reception with Paul C. Pritchard (NPCA President) and Dr. John Gordon (Commission Chairman)
- Dinner with Commission and NPS Liaison Group
- Evening program featuring NPS/Harpers Ferry video on the National Park System and presentation by Denis Galvin (Deputy Director, NPS)

**Commission Objectives:**
- Establish commission schedule and mission
- Organize working subcommittees
- Develop processes for solicitation of comments, concept papers, and expertise
- Establish liaison protocols with the [[National Park Service]]
- Schedule future meetings at [[Grand Teton National Park]], [[Sequoia-Kings Canyon National Parks]], Tucson, and other locations

**Cultural Resources Focus:**
Panel discussion scheduled with NPS cultural resource chiefs covering:
- History Division (Ed Bearss, Chief)
- Curatorial Services Division (Ann Hitchcock, Chief)
- Division of Archaeology (Doug Scovill, Chief)
- Division of Architecture (Hugh Miller, Chief)

**Field Activities:**
Tour of NPS Greenbelt Preservation Facility with Ann Hitchcock and Pam West (facility manager)

The correspondence indicates that Deputy Director Denis P. Galvin would represent the NPS Director at the April 19 meeting due to a scheduling conflict, demonstrating high-level NPS engagement with the commission's work.
