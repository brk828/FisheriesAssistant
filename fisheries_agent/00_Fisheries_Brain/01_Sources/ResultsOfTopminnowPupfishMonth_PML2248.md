# Results of 2009 Topminnow Pupfish 6-Month Post-Stocking Monitoring at Muleshoe Ecosystem

#ichthyology #marine-biology #gila-topminnow

## Executive Summary

This email from Tony Robinson, CAP Projects Program Manager at the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], reports the spring 2009 post-stocking monitoring of two endangered desert fishes at the [[Muleshoe Ecosystem]] in Arizona. The team sampled four spring sites with minnow traps, dip net sweeps and seine hauls to assess whether [[Gila Topminnow]] and [[Desert Pupfish]] stocked in 2007 and 2008 had established or persisted. The authors concluded that both species persisted at most sites, with [[Gila Topminnow]] considered established at some locations. Final conclusions were deferred to the September monitoring round.

## Background & Description

- **Purpose:** Six-month post-stocking check of [[Gila Topminnow]] and [[Desert Pupfish]] translocated into spring habitats at the [[Muleshoe Ecosystem]].
- **Sender and context:** Email sent 3 April 2009 by Tony (Anthony) Robinson to a distribution list of agency, university and conservation partners, including the [[U.S. Bureau of Reclamation]] and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]. The sender's signature block lists him as CAP Projects Program Manager, [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], Phoenix.
- **Authors credited:** Ross Timmons, David Abbott and a third monitoring staff member (name partly illegible in the scan).
- **Stocking history (as stated):**
  - Upper Swamp Springs Canyon: stocked September 2008; lower canyon stocked October 2007.
  - Cherry Spring Canyon: upper pool stocked in 2007 but not 2008; lower pool stocked September 2008.
  - Headquarters and Secret springs: monitored after the September 2008 stocking.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat type:** Spring-fed pools, hot spring inflow areas and canyon spring reaches. The text describes sites by name (Headquarters Spring, Secret Spring, Swamp Springs Canyon, Cherry Spring Canyon) and does not report water chemistry, temperature or other physical measurements.
- **Sampling gear:** Minnow traps, dip net sweeps and seine hauls. Effort is given per site (for example, 10 minnow traps and 22 dip net sweeps at Secret Spring).
- **Co-occurring fish:** [[Longfin Dace]] were present in Swamp Springs Canyon, which is the only non-target species recorded.
- **Species status note:** The email does not define the legal status of the species. The authors refer to "establishment" and "persistence" of stocked populations, which are the main management endpoints in the message.

## Key Findings

**1. Headquarters Spring**
- Thousands of [[Gila Topminnow]] were seen schooling in the hot spring inflow area.
- Captured counts are partly illegible in the scan (reported as a three-digit number beginning with "212").
- [[Desert Pupfish]] were captured in minnow traps and dip net sweeps.
- **Conclusion:** [[Gila Topminnow]] considered established. [[Desert Pupfish]] described as "persisting", with a firmer assessment deferred to September monitoring.

**2. Secret Spring**
- Over 1,000 [[Gila Topminnow]] observed.
- Captures: 965 [[Gila Topminnow]] and several [[Desert Pupfish]] in 10 minnow traps and 22 dip net sweeps (the scan of this figure is partly degraded).
- **Conclusion:** [[Gila Topminnow]] considered established and [[Desert Pupfish]] persisting, in line with the earlier September 2008 monitoring.

**3. Swamp Springs Canyon (upper reach only)**
- Monitored only the upper canyon, where fish were stocked in September 2008. The lower canyon, stocked October 2007, was not sampled.
- Captures in 10 minnow traps and 16 seine hauls:
  - 14 [[Gila Topminnow]]
  - 6 [[Desert Pupfish]]
  - 876 [[Longfin Dace]]
- Observed but not captured: probably over 1,000 [[Longfin Dace]] and a school of about 10 [[Desert Pupfish]] (described as "blue mates").
- **Conclusion:** Both target species persisting in the upper reach at this time.

**4. Cherry Spring Canyon**
- Lower pool: 28 [[Gila Topminnow]] and 10 [[Desert Pupfish]] captured in 10 minnow traps and 14 dip net sweeps.
- Upper pool: no fish captured. The upper pool was stocked in 2007 but not in 2008.
- Observed: 30 [[Gila Topminnow]] and at least 30 [[Desert Pupfish]] (the second figure is partly illegible).
- **Conclusion:** Both species persisting since stocking in September 2008 (lower pool) and October 2007.

**Overall conclusions and next steps**
- [[Gila Topminnow]] considered established at Headquarters and Secret springs.
- [[Desert Pupfish]] considered persisting at Headquarters, Secret, Swamp Springs and Cherry Spring sites.
- Both species persisting in the sampled upper Swamp Springs reach.
- Authors state that a clearer picture of pupfish persistence will come from the September 2009 monitoring round.
