## Executive Summary

This document is an administrative letter from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Albuquerque, NM Regional Office), dated April 16, 1986, addressed to Mr. Charles Kennedy of the U.S. Forest Service (Coronado National Forest, Tucson, Arizona).
