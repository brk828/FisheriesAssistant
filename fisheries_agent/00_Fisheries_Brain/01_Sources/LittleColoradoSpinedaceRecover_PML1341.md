# Little Colorado Spinedace Recovery Activities in East Clear Creek, 2007

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a partly legible field-activity record and recovery-team agenda for the [[Little Colorado Spinedace]], a threatened Arizona cyprinid. Most of the first four pages is illegible scan text and was not extracted. The readable content covers May to July 2007 monitoring, collection, and translocation work in the [[East Clear Creek]] drainage, plus a January meeting agenda for the [[Little Colorado Spinedace Recovery Team]]. The 2007 work involved trap-based sampling, dip netting and seining, relocation of fish among pools, and stocking into the [[Flagstaff Arboretum]] and a refuge. Pools dried or declined in July 2007, and no spinedace were found on 3 July. Several figures in the field notes do not reconcile with each other, as noted below.

## Background & Description

- **Focal species:** [[Little Colorado Spinedace]] (*Lepidomeda vittata*), a small cyprinid of the Little Colorado River system. The document uses the common name only; the scientific name is added here for identification.
- **Management framework:** Activities followed the *East Clear Creek Watershed Recovery Strategy for the Little Colorado Spinedace and Other Riparian Species* (USDA Forest Service 2002).
- **Agencies and programs:** The [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AZGFD) office in Flagstaff received brood stock. The agenda also refers to a proposal to separate the spinedace team from the [[Apache Trout]] recovery effort.
- **Marking:** Some brood stock still carried visible [[Elastomer Tagging]] marks in June 2007.
- **Co-occurring fauna:** Crayfish were trapped at Dines Tank, and [[Speckled Dace]] were found in pools during July sampling. The OCR reads the second species as "dace," and the identification is inferred from context.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Sampling sites** were identified by UTM coordinates and named pools, including the traditional collection pool, [[West Leonard Canyon]] pools, the Arboretum, Dines Tank, and the 2003–2004 and 2005–2006 stocking locations.

**Water quality** was recorded during the 16 July 2007 stocking at the Refuge:
- [[Dissolved Oxygen]] (D.O.) was 3.30 mg/L at 9:30 am, too low for stocking. The reading at 1600 was 4.01 mg/L.
- pH 7.3; temperature 73°F; conductivity reported as ".222" (units not given).
- The hydrolab D.O. sensor was later found to need replacement. D.O. at the Arboretum was reported as more acceptable.

**Habitat stress:** On 3 July 2007, the document reports that water levels and fish numbers were "down significantly." Pools were drying, and this drove decisions to keep and relocate fish.

**Non-native species:** The agenda includes an update on the review of nonnative control.

## Key Findings

**May–June 2007 monitoring and collection**
- **22 May (Dines Tank):** 12 minnow traps and 6 crayfish traps were checked hourly. The traps caught 7 adult spinedace (released) and 8 crayfish (euthanized). The hour count is garbled in the scan.
- **25–26 June (Arboretum):** Minnow traps yielded 6 spinedace, all brood stock, several with elastomer tags.
- **28 June (West Leonard Canyon):** 160 spinedace were collected by dip netting and seining. Seining was used only at the main site, in 3 hauls. More than 90 fish came from isolated upstream pools, which were kept because of drying risk. 112 fish were released at the traditional pool.

**Stocking and translocation, 28 June**
- The document lists 80, 32, 34, and 16 fish stocked into four pools, plus 80 fish sent to the AZGFD Flagstaff office as Arboretum brood stock.
- **Data discrepancy:** These allocations sum to 242, not the 160 collected. The 112 "released" figure also does not reconcile with the total. These numbers should be checked against the original data sheets.

**July 2007 post-stocking sampling**
- **2 July:** 12 spinedace were found at the 2003–2004 stocking location below the 95 road crossing, and 1 at the 2005–2006 location. Seven fish found downstream in isolated pools were moved to deeper, less drying-prone upstream pool habitat.
- **3 July:** No spinedace were collected. Only speckled dace were taken from pool 5 in Section Dane III. Large fish concentrations occurred in isolated downstream pools, but none were spinedace.
- **Spinedace in West Leonard Canyon:** Fish were found in pools identified as potential habitat during the 28 June survey, downstream of the main stocking pool.

**16 July 2007 stocking**
- 65 spinedace from West Leonard Canyon were stocked into the Flagstaff Arboretum/Refuge. Stocking was delayed until D.O. rose to acceptable levels in the afternoon.

**Recovery team agenda (January 25, 2008; the OCR reads "20Og")**
- Recent population status and on-the-ground accomplishments.
- Renovation of [[Blue Ridge Reservoir]].
- Proposal to formally separate the spinedace team from the Apache trout team.
- Need for a recovery plan update or revision.
- Review of nonnative species control.
- Upcoming recovery actions and planning, plus scheduling of the next meeting.

**Overall:** Spinedace persisted in some pools but declined sharply in July 2007 as water levels fell. Translocation and brood-stock transfers were the main management responses, and water quality, especially D.O., constrained stocking timing.
