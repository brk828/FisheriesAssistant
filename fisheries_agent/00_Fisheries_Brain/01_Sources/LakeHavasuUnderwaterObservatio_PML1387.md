# Lake Havasu Underwater Observation and Fish Sampling Correspondence

#ichthyology #marine-biology #freshwater-fisheries

## Executive Summary

This is a Bureau of Reclamation correspondence packet, dated around July 1970s (year not legible in scan), from the Regional Director to a cooperating agency regional office. It concerns an [[Environmental Impact Study]] of [[Lake Havasu]]. The letter invites representatives to observe planned underwater (scuba) surveys on August 17 and 18, reports that preliminary data from earlier underwater observations and fish sampling were completed, and outlines plans for a progress report, a proposal for future studies, and a joint completion report. The packet also contains roughly a dozen [[Gill Netting and Electrofishing Data]] sheets with species lists that are largely illegible in the scan, plus an unlabeled map sheet.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Interagency letter (two pages of text, one enclosure list) followed by standardized field data forms and a USGS-style topographic map.
- **Sender:** E. A. Lundberg, Regional Director, Bureau of Reclamation, with copies to internal offices.
- **Recipient:** Regional office of a cooperating agency (name partly illegible in scan), contact referred to as Mr. Helens.
- **Purpose:** To build baseline local data for the Lake Havasu impact study and to coordinate joint field work.
- **Field support:** The letter credits personnel of the Division of River Basin Studies in a Phoenix office for helping collect field data.
- **Field forms:** Each form is headed "Bureau of Reclamation, Environmental Office, Gill Netting and Electrofishing Data" and records gear used (gill net and electrofishing), location, date, and species columns. Dates on the forms are scattered and partly unreadable.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- Lake Havasu is the study site for an environmental impact assessment, meaning the work is aimed at documenting existing aquatic conditions before or during a management or development change. The document does not describe the specific project being assessed.
- The study combines two sampling approaches: underwater visual observation by divers and capture-based fish sampling using gill nets and electrofishing gear.
- The forms reference multiple sampling locations and dates across the survey period. Site-level habitat, water chemistry, and invertebrate data are not reported in the readable text.
- Map sheet: a topographic sheet labeled Whipple Mountains quadrangle, probably used to locate sampling stations. Its marks are not interpretable.

## Key Findings

- **Survey timing:** Underwater observations were scheduled for August 17 and 18, and the agency invited regional representatives to observe them.
- **Preliminary results:** Preliminary copies of results from earlier scuba observations and gill netting or electrofishing work were sent to the recipient. The completion of this work is noted as already accomplished at an earlier date (month partly illegible).
- **Planned deliverables:**
  - A progress report and a proposal for further studies over several years on Lake Havasu, to be discussed with all participating agencies.
  - A joint completion report, to be prepared once the impact study is finalized.
- **Data collection records:** About 12 sampling data sheets are included, each listing gear, location, date, and up to roughly 20 species entries. Most species names are not legible in the OCR, so no reliable species checklist can be extracted. Species counts and catch numbers are not readable.
- **Coordination:** The Bureau thanks the recipient's Bureau for cooperative participation and assistance from Phoenix field personnel, and looks forward to continued joint work on Lake Havasu and other cooperative studies.
