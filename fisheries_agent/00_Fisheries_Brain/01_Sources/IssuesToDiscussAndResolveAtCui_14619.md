# Cui-ui Recovery Team Meeting Agenda and Issues (November 1990)

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #Catostomidae #endangered-species #recovery-planning

## Executive Summary

This document is correspondence from Tom Strekal, Team Leader, scheduling a [[Cui-ui]] Recovery Team meeting for November 29, 1990, in Reno, Nevada. The meeting addresses the urgent completion of the draft [[Cui-ui]] Recovery Plan, mandated by Senate Bill 3084 (Truckee-Carson-Pyramid Lake Water Rights Settlement Act). The agenda encompasses critical issues including hybridization studies, population status assessment, stochastic modeling results, water purchase recommendations, water quality standards, and structural improvements to fish passage facilities. Multiple expert presentations are scheduled, including electrophoretic hybridization results by Peter Brussard and stochastic analysis by John Emlen of the National Fishery Research Center.

## Background & Description

The [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) is an endangered sucker species (Family [[Catostomidae]]) endemic to [[Pyramid Lake]] and the [[Truckee River]] system in Nevada. The species requires access to spawning habitat in the lower [[Truckee River]], with spawning success closely tied to river inflow volumes and [[Pyramid Lake]] elevation. The Recovery Team is tasked with revising and updating conservation plans under the Endangered Species Act, with legislative pressure from the Truckee-Carson-Pyramid Lake Water Rights Settlement Act requiring expeditious plan completion.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

[[Cui-ui]] spawning success is directly correlated with [[Truckee River]] inflow and [[Pyramid Lake]] elevation, as documented in Figures 1-4 showing spawning events from 1950, 1978-1979, 1982-1985, and 1987. Historic spawning sites and year-class structure are influenced by water availability. The lower [[Truckee River]] basin faces water quality concerns, with existing conditions compared against established standards for both the river and [[Pyramid Lake]]. Critical infrastructure includes the [[Pyramid Lake Fishway]], [[Truckee River]] delta, [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]], [[Numana Dam]] fish ladder, and lower [[Truckee River]] spawning and riparian habitat. Hatchery operations at [[Lahontan National Fish Hatchery]] impact population dynamics.

## Key Findings

**Meeting Logistics**: Recovery Team meeting scheduled for November 29, 1990, 9:00 AM at Fish & Wildlife Service office in Reno, with potential continuation on Friday due to extensive agenda.

**Hybridization Study**: Peter Brussard completed electrophoretic study of [[Cui-ui]] hybridization; results to be presented to team.

**Population Modeling**: Stochastic computer runs completed by John Emlen (National Fishery Research Center). Analysis compares "Red Book" mortality rates and passage elevation scenarios (Figure 5) with revised Low/High/Very High (L/H/HH) mortality rate scenarios (Figure 6). Figure 5 shows number of model traces reaching zero [[Cui-ui]] adults at year 200 across 200 runs as function of supplemental water (measured in 1,000 acre-feet).

**Critical Issues for Resolution**:
- Current population estimate and year-class structure
- Historic spawning site identification
- Impact of hatchery operations
- Assumptions in [[Cui-ui]] Population Index Model
- Water purchase recommendations based on stochastic modeling
- Water quality standards versus existing conditions
- Trade-offs between water quality/quantity and structural improvements

**Structural Recommendations**: Site-specific requirements under ESA for [[Pyramid Lake Fishway]], [[Truckee River]] delta, [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]], [[Numana Dam]] fish ladder, and lower [[Truckee River]] spawning/riparian habitat. Duane Wainwright (FWS-Lahontan National Fish Hatchery) invited to discuss fish facilities.

**Stakeholder Participation**: Bob Pelcyger (attorney for [[Pyramid Lake Tribe]]) and Ali Shahroody (Tribe's consultant) scheduled to discuss Endangered Species Act provisions at 1:30 PM. Truckee-Carson Irrigation District (TCID) representatives declined participation.

**Deliverables**: Assignments to be made for completing Draft Recovery Plan, with research and monitoring protocols to be established.
