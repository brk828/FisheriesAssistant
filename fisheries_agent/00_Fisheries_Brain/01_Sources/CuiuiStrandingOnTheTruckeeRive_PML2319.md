# Cui-ui Stranding on the Truckee River Delta: 1990 Photograph Transmittal

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This is a one-page letter dated August 7, 1990, from [[G. Gary Scoppettone]], Project Leader at the [[National Fishery Research Center]] of the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] in Seattle, Washington, to [[W.L. Minckley]] of the [[Arizona State University]] Department of Zoology. The letter encloses two photographs (5 x 5 inch and 4 x 6 inch, as OCR-read) of [[Cui-ui]] stranded on the [[Truckee River Delta]] after water had been diverted for irrigation. The photographs were taken in 1940 by [[Tom Trelease]], then Director of the Nevada Department of Fish and Game, and were previously published by [[Ira LaRivers]] in *[[Fishes and Fisheries of Nevada]]* (page 365).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The photographs document a stranding event on the Truckee River Delta, where cui-ui were left on exposed sediment after upstream water was diverted for irrigation. The letter describes this as a diversion-related stranding and does not give the number of fish, their size, the event's duration, or any population data.

The image is therefore historical evidence of a habitat-loss mechanism: reduced river flow into the delta leaving fish stranded. The source does not discuss the cui-ui's life history, distribution, or conservation status beyond this event.

## Key Findings

- The Fish and Wildlife Service's National Fishery Research Center supplied archival photographs of cui-ui stranded on the Truckee River Delta to Dr. Minckley at Arizona State University.
- The stranding was caused by water diversion for irrigation, which left fish on the delta.
- The photographs were taken in 1940 by Tom Trelease, then Director of the Nevada Department of Fish and Game.
- The image was previously published in Ira LaRivers' *Fishes and Fisheries of Nevada* (page 365), so the letter serves to confirm its provenance and availability for reuse.
- The letter contains no quantitative data (counts, sizes, water-flow values, or dates of the stranding event beyond the 1940 photograph date).
