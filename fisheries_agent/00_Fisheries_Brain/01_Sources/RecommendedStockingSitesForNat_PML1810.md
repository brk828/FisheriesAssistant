# Recommended Stocking Sites for Native Fishes, Buenos Aires NWR

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae #cyprinodontidae

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from W.L. Minckley and Paul C. Marsh to the refuge manager recommends sites for stocking three native fishes on the [[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]]. The recommendations rest on field surveys conducted in April and on 17–19 October (the year is not legible in the scanned copy). The sites are mostly small impoundments, or stock tanks, that the authors judged suitable for the species.

## Background & Description

The memo addresses three native species:

- **[[Bonytail Chub]]** (*Gila elegans*), adults only.
- **[[Razorback Sucker]]** (*Xyrauchen texanus*), listed as fry (juveniles).
- **[[Desert Pupfish]]** (*Cyprinodon macularius*), at all sizes.

The memo is a short recommendation list, not a research report. It gives no methods, sample sizes, population counts or water-quality measurements.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The sites are located within or near the refuge's headquarters area and along access roads. Stock tanks are artificial, isolated waters, so the recommended sites are habitat patches separated from one another. Access routes are described relative to landmarks (Arivaca Road, Highway 386, and Refuge Headquarters), not by coordinates. Township and range descriptions are given for each site, but parts of the scan are illegible, so they are not reproduced here.

## Key Findings

**Recommended stocking sites by species** (names as legible in the scan; some may be approximate):

- **Bonytail Chub (adult):**
  - Asua Hito Tank 3 North, reached via a dirt road about 5 miles off Arivaca Road, roughly 4 miles east of Highway 386.
  - Hito Tank, accessed by the same route.
- **Razorback Sucker (fry):**
  - Rock Tank.
  - Sufirido Tank.
  - Teauila Tank (name partly garbled in the scan).
  - Mormon Lake, reached via Headquarters. The scan does not clearly assign this site to a species. It appears to fall in the razorback group, but this is uncertain.
- **Desert Pupfish (all sizes):**
  - Little Hormon Tank, reached via Headquarters.
  - Several unnamed tanks in the immediate vicinity of the Headquarters storage area.

**Summary:** The memo lists 9 site entries in total, covering 3 species. The Bonytail Chub has 2 recommended sites, the Razorback Sucker has 3 to 4, and the Desert Pupfish has at least 2 (one of them a group of unnamed tanks). The memo gives no rationale, no survey counts and no monitoring or follow-up plan.
