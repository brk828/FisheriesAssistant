# Catron County Letter Requesting Water Acquisition for Fish, Wildlife and Recreation (1993)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #freshwater-management

## Executive Summary

This is a 1993 letter from the [[Catron County]] Board of County Commissioners, signed by Chairman [[Carl Livingston]], to the Regional Director of the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] in Albuquerque, [[New Mexico]]. The county proposes negotiations for a tentative water project and asks whether the agency is interested in acquiring water for fish, wildlife and recreational purposes. The document contains no species data, sampling results or ecological measurements. Its relevance to aquatic ecology lies in its water-allocation proposal and its link to fish and wildlife management.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Official correspondence (letter with commissioner signatures and a cc list), dated August 24, 1993.
- **Sender:** Catron County Board of County Commissioners, [[Catron County]], [[New Mexico]]. The chairman is Carl Livingston. The letter lists several commissioners and county staff in its header and cc block.
- **Recipient:** Regional Director, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Albuquerque, New Mexico.
- **Subject:** Possible acquisition of water for fish, wildlife and recreational uses, linked to a tentative county water project.
- **Note on text quality:** The source is an OCR scan with many garbled words. Some names and river-basin references could not be read with certainty and are summarized here rather than quoted.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter describes water-resource planning rather than ecosystem conditions. It states the following points:

- The county's water plan calls for water for fish, wildlife and recreation.
- A quantity of about 1,000 acre-feet is described as available for these uses. The OCR text is partly illegible here.
- The county intends to pursue water acquisition in connection with a [[Bureau of Reclamation]] program, and it refers to a figure of 4,000 acre-feet of water in the same context.
- The water is described as part of a larger river-basin system in the region. The letter's wording on the specific basin is partly unreadable.
- The county states that its water-related interests are compatible with the agency's wildlife and recreation goals and invites the agency's participation.

The document does not describe any specific fish species, habitat condition, water quality, flow regime or population.

## Key Findings

1. **Water offered for fish and wildlife use.** The county identifies about 1,000 acre-feet of water it can make available for fish, wildlife and recreational purposes.
2. **Potential acquisition of 4,000 acre-feet.** The letter refers to acquiring 4,000 acre-feet of water as part of a broader project coordinated with the Bureau of Reclamation.
3. **Request for federal participation.** The county asks the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] whether it is interested in pursuing this water for [[Fish and Wildlife Habitat]] and [[Recreational Use]].
4. **Negotiation timeline.** The county expects negotiations to take about one year to complete. The letter also says it hopes to begin negotiations in October.
5. **Decision context.** The letter states that the county's plans are at an early stage and that no final commitment has been made. The agency is asked whether it wishes to take part in the planning.
6. **Limitations.** The document provides no biological data, sample sizes, species lists, or environmental monitoring results. Its content is limited to water-allocation planning and an invitation to coordinate.
