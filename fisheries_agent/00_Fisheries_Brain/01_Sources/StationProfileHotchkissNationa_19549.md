## Executive Summary
This 1993 station profile documents operations at the **Hotchkiss National Fish Hatchery**, a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility located in Hotchkiss, Colorado. Established in 1967 under the authority of the **Colorado River Storage Project Act (CRSP)** (70 Stat. 110, Section 4), the hatchery's mission is to produce [[Rainbow Trout]] and [[Cutthroat Trout]] to meet mitigation requirements for federal water development projects and to support fishery resource management on Federal Lands. The station distributed over 2 million fish in FY93, primarily into federal waters, with smaller allocations to state and private waters. The report is largely administrative/operational rather than biological, detailing budget, staffing, physical infrastructure, and fish distribution numbers.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat type:** Artificial hatchery environment consisting of raceways (38 total), earthen ponds (6), and a sand settling basin — designed to rear salmonids prior to release.
- **Stocking destinations (FY93 distribution):** 
  - Federal (FWS) waters
  - Other Federal waters
  - State waters
  - Private waters
- **Geographic/watershed context:** Operates under the **[[Colorado River Storage Project]]**, indicating the hatchery's stock is used to mitigate impacts of dam and water development projects within the Colorado River Basin, supplementing wild trout populations affected by habitat alteration.
- **Management purpose:** Functions as a mitigation and put-and-take fishery resource supplier for federal lands in the region, primarily Colorado (with minor distribution noted to other states, likely including New Mexico given regional context).
