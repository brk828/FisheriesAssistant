# Bonytail Update (June 1998 Memo)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-culture

## Executive Summary

This internal memo from Chester R. Figiel, dated 9 June 1998, reports on the husbandry and handling of four [[Bonytail]] (BTCs, as abbreviated in the text) brought to [[Willow Beach]] between 19 and 21 May 1998. The fish were pit-tagged, measured, and moved to an outdoor [[Recirculating Raceway]] on 5 June. The memo covers wound recovery, feeding problems, a chemical treatment, and a request for a timetable to collect muscle plugs and transport the fish to [[Dexter]].

## Background & Description

- **Subject fish:** Four bonytail, identified by pit-tag number and length.
- **Arrival and tagging:** Fish arrived at Willow Beach 19-21 May 1998 and were [[Pit Tagging]] as needed and measured.
- **Transport records:** Transport logs are initialed by four staff members across 19-21 May (MT, CM, TRR, CM), though initials are only partially legible in the scan.
- **Lengths recorded (mm):** Approximately 900 (first fish, OCR-uncertain), 420 (a recapture), 540, and 500. Weights were not obtained.
- **Recipients:** Stewart Jacks, Brian Lubinski, Jerry Landye, and Chuck Mnkley.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Holding system:** Fish were moved from initial holding to an outside [[Recirculating Raceway]] on 5 June 1998.
- **Injury recovery:** Net marks are fading, but white scars remain on the fish.
- **Feeding:** Fish were fed [[Red Shiner]] (a forage fish) but had not yet accepted a prepared trout feed.
- **Disease management:** A [[Formalin Malachite Green Treatment]] was given on 8 June 1998 (described as "yesterday" in the memo).
- **Conservation handling:** Plans call for collecting [[Muscle Plug Sampling]] tissue samples from the fish, which the memo calls "valuable," before transport to Dexter.

## Key Findings

1. Net marks on the four bonytail are healing, but white scars persist.
2. The fish are not yet accepting prepared trout feed and are still being fed red shiners.
3. A formalin/malachite green treatment was administered on 8 June 1998.
4. The four fish range from roughly 420 to 900 mm in length (the first value is OCR-uncertain); one is a recapture (pit-tag 1F777AI25E).
5. Weights were not recorded.
6. Muscle plug collection and transport to Dexter are planned, and the author requests a timetable from the recipients.
