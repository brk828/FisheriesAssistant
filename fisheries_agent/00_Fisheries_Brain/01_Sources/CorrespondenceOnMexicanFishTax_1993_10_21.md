# Correspondence on Mexican Fish Taxonomy (Miller to Minckley, 1993)

#ichthyology #taxonomy #cichlidae

## Executive Summary
This document is a 1993 letter from **Dr. Robert R. Miller** (Emeritus Curator of Fishes, University of Michigan Museum of Zoology) to **Dr. W. L. Minckley** (Arizona State University), responding to Minckley's review of Volume 1 of an *Annotated List of Mexican Fishes*. Rather than presenting primary field or morphological data, the letter documents ongoing **taxonomic revisions** to several North and Central American freshwater fish groups, reflecting Miller's philosophical alignment with a "splitting" approach to classification for conservation purposes. Key groups discussed include [[Tiaroga]] (Cyprinidae), [[Heterophallus]] and [[Pseudoxiphophorus]] (Poeciliidae), several cichlid genera ([[Nandopsis]], [[Thorichthys]], [[Archocentrus]], [[Petenia]], [[Caquetaia]], [[Herichthys]]), and a note on reviving [[Pantosteus]] (Catostomidae). The letter also references a locality record for [[Ictalurus pricei]] in the Rio Sonora basin.

## Background & Description
While no direct morphometric data are given, the letter implies key diagnostic and evolutionary distinctions used to justify generic reassignments:

- **[[Tiaroga cobitis]]**: Recognized as a distinct genus separate from *Rhinichthys*, reinstated based on renewed morphological/systematic assessment.
- **[[Heterophallus]]**: Considered morphologically and phylogenetically distinct from *Gambusia*, not a synonym as previously treated.
- **[[Pseudoxiphophorus]]**: Represents a "vastly different assemblage of species" compared to the monotypic *Heterandria formosa*, indicating substantial morphological divergence within what was formerly lumped taxonomy.
- **Cichlid genera**:
  - *[[Nandopsis]]* — proposed as the valid genus for all West Indian cichlid species (≥4 extant + 1 Miocene fossil).
  - *[[Thorichthys]]* — the genus containing the firemouth cichlid (*Thorichthys meeki*), recognized as valid and distinct.
  - *[[Archocentrus]]* — genus placement for the convict cichlid.
  - *[[Petenia]]* — includes *P. splendida* plus additional species restricted to Panama and northern South America (the South American forms termed *[[Caquetaia]]*).
  - *[[Herichthys]]* — proposed to exclude *pearsei* and *bocourti*; contains more species than previously recognized, all historically misidentified as *Cichlasoma cyanoguttatum*, including *Herichthys geddesi* (Regan).
- **[[Pantosteus]]** (Catostomidae, suckers): Miller considers reinstating this genus, separate from *Catostomus*, based on distinguishing morphological/molecular criteria (previously synonymized, now reconsidered).

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- The taxa discussed occupy **freshwater riverine and lacustrine systems** across Mexico, the Caribbean (West Indies), and Central/northern South America.
- **[[Ictalurus pricei]]** (Yaqui catfish) — documented from the **Rio Sonora basin**, extending known distributional records for this species in northwestern Mexico.
- Cichlid taxa such as *Thorichthys*, *Archocentrus*, *Petenia*, and *Herichthys* inhabit Mesoamerican freshwater systems, with biogeographic patterns constraining *Petenia*'s distribution to as far south as Panama, beyond which related South American lineages (*Caquetaia*) occur — suggesting a biogeographic barrier at the Central American isthmus relevant to speciation and generic boundaries.
- The broader conservation framing of the letter emphasizes that finer taxonomic resolution (splitting) aids identification of **areas of high species diversity**, which is ecologically significant for prioritizing freshwater conservation efforts in Mexico.

## Key Findings
1. **Philosophical stance on taxonomy**: Miller and Minckley concur that a "splitting" approach (elevating subspecies to full species) better reflects true biological diversity and assists conservation biology by highlighting diversity hotspots.
2. **Genus reinstatements**: [[Tiaroga]] and possibly [[Pantosteus]] are being resurrected as valid genera after prior synonymization.
3. **Poeciliid distinctions**: [[Heterophallus]] is reaffirmed as distinct from [[Gambusia]]; [[Pseudoxiphophorus]] is distinguished from [[Heterandria formosa]] due to substantial assemblage-level differences.
4. **Cichlid systematics overhaul**: Using molecular data, Miller was working toward monophyletic groupings within Cichlidae, including:
   - Consolidation of West Indian cichlids under [[Nandopsis]].
   - Validation of [[Thorichthys]] (firemouth cichlid group) and [[Archocentrus]] (convict cichlid).
   - Redefinition of [[Petenia]] and recognition of [[Caquetaia]] for South American relatives.
   - Expansion of [[Herichthys]] to include multiple species previously misidentified as *Cichlasoma cyanoguttatum*, including *H. geddesi*.
5. **Distributional record**: New/confirmed record of [[Ictalurus pricei]] in the **Rio Sonora basin**, enclosed as supporting documentation in the original letter.
6. This correspondence illustrates the collaborative, iterative nature of ichthyological taxonomy in the early 1990s, particularly regarding Mexican and Central American freshwater fish faunas, and foreshadows later molecular-based reclassifications of Middle American cichlids.
