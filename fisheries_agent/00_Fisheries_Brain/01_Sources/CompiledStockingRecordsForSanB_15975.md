# Stocking Records for San Bernardino and Leslie Canyon National Wildlife Refuges (1980-1990)

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #Cyprinidae #Poeciliidae #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This document comprises correspondence and stocking records from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] documenting the translocation and management of [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*) and [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*) at [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] and [[Leslie Canyon NWR]] in Arizona between 1980 and 1990. The records detail fish movements between various ponds, springs, and creeks within the refuge system, primarily involving fish reared at [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] (Dexter NFH) in New Mexico. The correspondence includes discussions of recovery planning and coordination between Professor W.L. Minckley at [[Arizona State University]] and refuge personnel.

## Background & Description

The [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*) is a cyprinid fish native to the Yaqui River basin of Arizona and Mexico. The [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*) is a small poeciliid fish endemic to the Gila River basin. Both species were subjects of intensive conservation efforts during the 1980s involving captive propagation and reintroduction.

[[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] served as the primary propagation facility for these endangered southwestern fishes. The hatchery maintained breeding populations and produced fingerlings for stocking at various refuge sites including [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] and [[Leslie Canyon NWR]] in southeastern Arizona near Douglas.

Key water bodies mentioned in stocking records include: [[North Pond]], [[House Pond]], [[Black Draw]], [[Tule Pond]], [[Upland]], [[Bunting Spring]], [[Border Spring]], [[Robertson Cienega]], [[Twin Pond]], [[Leslie Creek]], and various other springs and ponds within the refuge complex.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The stocking program operated within a complex of managed aquatic habitats at [[San Bernardino NWR]], including natural springs, constructed ponds, and creek systems. Management challenges included:

- **Habitat connectivity**: North Pond and North Pond Swamp were discovered to be connected via a pipe on the west side, affecting stocking outcomes
- **Pond management**: House Pond was drained and treated, destroying all stocked chub
- **Water quality issues**: A partial die-off of [[Yaqui Chub]] occurred at [[Dexter NFH]] on July 13, 1979, when dissolved oxygen levels dropped near zero due to excessive *Chara* (algae) growth. Approximately 1,000 fish died (fry, juveniles, and adults), representing less than 50% of the population. The Dexter Fire Department responded by hosing down the pond to restore oxygen levels
- **Vegetation control**: Ponds required periodic mechanical removal of *Chara* to prevent deoxygenation events

The [[Yaqui Chub]] were also used as forage fish for other endangered species recovery efforts, including [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Roundtail Chub]].

## Key Findings

**Major Stocking Events (1980-1990):**

**October 15, 1980** - Initial large-scale [[Yaqui Chub]] stocking by Gerald Burton:
- 25 adult chub into [[North Pond]] swamp
- 200 adult chub into [[North Pond]]
- 1,000-1,200 juvenile chub into [[North Pond]]
- 9,867 adult chub into [[House Pond]]
- 21,000 juvenile chub into [[House Pond]]
- 50 juvenile chub into [[Black Draw]]
- Note: All chub in [[House Pond]] were subsequently destroyed when the pond was drained and treated; no evidence of successful establishment in North Pond Swamp

**1989** - [[Gila Topminnow]] stocked in: Tule, House, Upland, Blackdraw Source, North, Buntin Middle, Cottonwood, Cienega, and [[Leslie Creek]]. [[Yaqui Chub]] stocked in: North, Black Draw, House, and Leslie Creek

**September 7, 1983** - 200 [[Gila Topminnow]] to [[Black Draw]] (source unknown)

**May 10, 1983** - 86 [[Gila Topminnow]] from [[North Pond]] and 14 from Bunting to [[House Pond]]

**April 9, 1984** - 7 [[Gila Topminnow]] from [[Border Spring]] to Upland

**April 16, 1986** - 200 [[Yaqui Chub]] from [[North Pond]] to [[House Pond]]

**November 4, 1986** - Major stocking from [[Dexter NFH]]:
- 8,645 [[Yaqui Chub]] (2 inches, 247 fish/lb, 35.0 lbs total weight) to University of Arizona Chiricahua Field Station Pond
- This represented the total stock of *Gila purpurea* at Dexter NFH; the facility no longer held this species after this date
- Concurrent [[Bonytail Chub]] stockings to [[Buenos Aires NWR]] tanks

**November 20, 1989** - 200 [[Gila Topminnow]] from [[North Pond]] to Hay Hollow windmill; 200 from [[Tule Pond]] to Twin Pond; 400 from [[House Pond]] to [[Robertson Cienega]]

**March 8, 1990** - [[Gila Topminnow]] from Bunting and [[North Pond]] to Upland

**May 1990** - 300 shiners from [[Dexter NFH]] to Twin Pond

**October 1990** - 30 shiners from [[Dexter NFH]] to 2PHD

**Dexter NFH Management Activities:**

The hatchery maintained detailed records of [[Yaqui Chub]] movements between ponds for various purposes including forage production for other endangered species. Key management actions included:

- Fish moved to Pond 7 as forage for [[Colorado Squawfish]] (multiple dates in 1982)
- Fish moved to Pond 19C as forage for [[Bonytail Chub]]
- Specimens sent to researchers including W.L. Minckley at [[Arizona State University]], Dr. Sublette at Eastern New Mexico University, and Brigham Young University
- Natural reproduction observed in multiple ponds (Pond 5C, Pond 12C)
- Pond treatments with Igran herbicide at various concentrations (0.1-0.25 ppm)
- September 22, 1984: 100 various-sized [[Yaqui Chub]] collected from [[San Bernardino Ranch]] by Buddy Jensen and W.L. Minckley

**Recovery Planning:**

February 19 correspondence indicates coordination between refuge staff and Professor W.L. Minckley regarding recovery plan development, with meetings planned for April. The refuge requested additional stocking information to compile a complete stocking history for both [[San Bernardino NWR]] and [[Leslie Canyon NWR]].

**Program Capacity:**

October 2, 1980 memo indicates [[Dexter NFH]] exceeded production targets, rearing well over 10,000 [[Yaqui Chub]] and approximately 20,000 [[Gila Topminnow]] as committed in the 1981 program. Fall stocking was requested to free rearing ponds for subsequent year's production and allow focus on "harder to rear" species.
