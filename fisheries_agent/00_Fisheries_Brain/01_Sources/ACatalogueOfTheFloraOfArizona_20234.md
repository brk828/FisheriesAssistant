# A Catalogue of the Flora of Arizona

#ichthyology #marine-biology #botany #vascular-plants #flora-checklist

*Note: this document concerns terrestrial and aquatic vascular plants rather than fish; the tags above follow the system format, and the taxon-specific tag is #vascular-plants.*

## Executive Summary

This 1978 catalogue, compiled by [[J. Harry Lehr]] (Curator of the Herbarium, [[Desert Botanical Garden]], Phoenix), is a current checklist of the vascular flora of Arizona. It was produced because the last full state flora, the [[Arizona Flora]] (1960, reprinted 1964), had become outdated after eighteen years of floristic work and taxonomic revisions. The catalogue is explicitly not a critical taxonomic study; it records changes only where they were generally accepted by the taxonomic community and reflected in recent manuals. Families are arranged largely following the Arizona Flora, genera and species alphabetically, and questionable records are flagged with (?). Genera adding new records to the state are marked with an asterisk, and the author planned yearly supplements in the *Journal of the Arizona-Nevada Academy of Science*.

## Background & Description

- **Scope:** Ferns and fern allies (Pteridophyta), gymnosperms, and angiosperms (monocots and dicots) occurring in Arizona.
- **Structure:** An introduction, the annotated catalogue of families, genera and species, a literature cited list of 240 references, and an alphabetical index to families and genera.
- **Nomenclature:** Cactaceae follows [[Lyman Benson]]'s *The Cacti of Arizona*; generic placement in families follows J. C. Willis's *Dictionary of the Flowering Plants and Ferns*. Only the most recent synonym from the Arizona Flora is retained.
- **Common names:** Recorded where they occur in the cited literature or in listed popular works (e.g., [[Weatherby]] for ferns, [[Hitchcock]] for grasses, [[Little]] for trees, [[Parker]] for weeds, [[Jaeger]] for desert wildflowers, and [[Cory and Parks]] for Texas flora).
- **Publication:** Copyright 1978, Desert Botanical Garden, Phoenix; $4.75 per copy; printed by Northland Press, Flagstaff.
- **Acknowledgments:** Reviews and suggestions from Dr. Charles T. Mason, Jr. (University of Arizona), Mr. Timothy Reeves (Arizona State University), and especially Dr. Donald J. Pinkava (Arizona State University) for review of the Compositae. The cover illustration of [[Screwbean Mesquite]] (*Prosopis pubescens*) was drawn by Wendy Hodgson.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The catalogue covers the full range of Arizona habitats, reflected in the taxa listed:

- **Aquatic and wetland plants:** Including [[Water Hyacinth]] (*Eichornia crassipes*), [[Duckweed]] species (*Lemna*, *Spirodela*), [[Cattail]] (*Typha*), bur-reeds (*Sparganium*), pondweeds, and water-lilies (*Nymphaea*, *Nuphar*), as well as sedges, rushes, and spike rushes of marshes and streambanks.
- **Desert vegetation:** Dominant and characteristic genera include [[Saguaro]] (*Carnegiea gigantea*), [[Creosote Bush]] (*Larrea tridentata*), [[Mesquite]] (*Prosopis*), [[Palo Verde]] (*Cercidium*), [[Ocotillo]], numerous cacti (*Opuntia*, *Echinocereus*, *Mammillaria*) and agaves and yuccas.
- **Montane and forest flora:** Conifers such as [[Ponderosa Pine]], [[Pinyon Pine]], [[Douglas Fir]] and firs (*Abies*), along with oaks (*Quercus*), junipers (*Juniperus*), aspens and cottonwoods (*Populus*).
- **Parasitic and epiphytic plants:** Dwarf mistletoes (*Arceuthobium*), mistletoes (*Phoradendron*), and [[Ball Moss]] (*Tillandsia recurvata*).
- **Biogeographic note:** The Arizona flora is shaped by desert, grassland, and mountain ecosystems, with many taxa at range edges (flagged as questionable or new occurrences).

No data on water chemistry, fish, invertebrates, or quantitative field sampling are presented.

## Key Findings

- **Size of the flora:** The catalogue lists a large number of taxa across ferns, gymnosperms, and angiosperms, with grasses (Gramineae), composites (Compositae), legumes (Leguminosae), and sedges (Cyperaceae) among the most richly represented families. Individual families are listed in full in the source; the count is not stated explicitly in the text.
- **New state records:** Genera containing new additions to the Arizona flora are marked with an asterisk. Two species are explicitly identified as first published reports for the state: *Astragalus acutirostris* (Keel Beak) and *Oxalis pes-caprae* (Bermuda Buttercup). Cirsium coloradense is also noted as a first report.
- **Questionable occurrences:** Records of doubtful status are marked (?) throughout.
- **Taxonomic changes:** The Cactaceae were revised to follow Benson's monograph, and several genera (e.g., *Haplopappus*, now split among several genera; *Chrysopsis* merged into *Heterotheca*) are noted with current names.
- **Ongoing updates:** The author planned an annual supplement of additions and corrections in the *Journal of the Arizona-Nevada Academy of Science* until a revised [[Arizona Flora]] or a Southwestern Flora is published.
- **Purpose:** Provides a current, portable checklist for botanists and field workers in Arizona, with common names where available.
