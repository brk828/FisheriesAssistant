## Executive Summary
This document is a **Wild Fish Health Inspection Report** conducted by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Pinetop Fish Health Center) on November 24, 1998, at West Turkey Creek on the El Coronado Ranch property near Pearce, Arizona. The report is a regulatory/diagnostic survey of feral (wild) non-salmonid fish populations inhabiting a stream/impoundment system associated with a water retention structure and floodplain channel. The inspection targeted **Fathead Minnows** and **Bluegill** to screen for parasitic and bacterial pathogens, notably the **Asian Tapeworm** (*Bothriocephalus acheilognathi*, referred to in the source as *Bothriocephalus cuspidatus*) and bacterial agents including *Edwardsiella* and *Escherichia* species. The report explicitly notes it reflects a point-in-time snapshot and is **not evidence of future disease status**.
