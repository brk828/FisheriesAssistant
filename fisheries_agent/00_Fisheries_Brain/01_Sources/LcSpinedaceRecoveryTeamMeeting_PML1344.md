# Little Colorado Spinedace Recovery Team Meeting Materials (July 2009)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #spinedace

## Executive Summary

These materials prepare the [[Little Colorado Spinedace]] Recovery Team for a meeting on July 14, 2009, at the [[Happy Jack Ranger Station]]. They include a meeting agenda, a July 6, 2009 email from Robert W. Clarkson of the [[Bureau of Reclamation]], a 2009 survey site map from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AZGFD), and a partly completed [[Spotlight Species Action Plan]] template from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (FWS). The agenda covers team membership, conservation action updates, habitat renovation at [[Blue Ridge Reservoir]], the FWS action plan, a possible revision of the 1998 [[Recovery Plan]], and a review of a proposed piscicide study. Much of the source text is scanned and partly illegible, so several fields below are incomplete.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** Little Colorado spinedace. The document gives no scientific name. The action plan's "Scientific Name" field is blank, and the survey map header reads "Colorado spinedace," which is presumably the same fish.
- **Governing document:** A 1998 Recovery Plan is the reference for the current review. The team was considering whether to revise it, based on a July 2008 discussion and updates since then.
- **Team structure:** The email was sent to roughly 35 recipients. They included AZGFD regional staff, the [[Coconino National Forest]], the [[Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest]], FWS, the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM), the [[U.S. Geological Survey]] (USGS), and partner organizations. Team membership is still being finalized, and the agenda calls for a recommendation letter to the FWS Regional Director and for identifying an appropriate individual from the Hopi Nation.
- **Logistics:** Participants were asked to bring lunch and clothing for a light hike near Blue Ridge Reservoir.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Survey effort (AZGFD Region 1, as of July 14, 2009).** The map lists about 40 surveyed or planned locations. Sites marked as spinedace collection sites (*) include:
- Large Pool and the Hwy 191 upstream reaches on the Little Colorado River system
- Sipe Wildlife Area lower boundary (and upstream to the fish barrier)
- Becker WA 1, 2, and 3
- Silver Creek WA 1, 2, and 3
- The Steps, Above The Steps, and Below The Steps (Chevelon Creek area)
- Grasslands Pond

Survey coverage extends along the Little Colorado River, Nutrioso Creek, Silver Creek, Chevelon Creek, Benton Creek, and Rudd Creek. Several reaches are described as "scheduled to be surveyed," including Nutrioso Creek, West Chevelon Creek, Richville Valley, Wenima WA reaches, and existing and proposed diversion dam sites. The list also names "spinedace re-introduction sites" as a category, but the individual sites are not clearly legible.

**Threat and habitat concerns mentioned:**
- Diversion structures and fish barriers on several reaches
- Chemical treatment needs at Blue Ridge Reservoir and East Clear Creek, which involve a piscicide (fish-toxicant) renovation
- Weed treatment chemical needs
- Upper Chevelon Creek management, for which the team plans recommendations

The action plan's threat section is only partly legible and mentions threats to be "briefly summarized," but the specific threats are not recoverable from the scan.

## Key Findings

**1. Meeting agenda and outstanding items**
- Recovery Team membership recommendation letter to the FWS Regional Director (in progress).
- Identify an appropriate individual from the Hopi Nation.
- Conservation action updates from AZGFD Regions 1 and 2, the Coconino and Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests, FWS, and BLM.
- Blue Ridge Reservoir/East Clear Creek renovation:
  - Status update (Paradzick)
  - Use of sportfish (Rogers)
  - Reclamation funds for chemical purchase (Clarkson)
  - Set up a renovation planning meeting (Clarkson)
- FWS Action Plan (Hedwall).
- Possible Recovery Plan revision: review the July 2008 discussion, relevant updates since 2008, and make a decision.
- Review the USGS piscicide development proposal, if received.
- Other action items:
  - Team letter to the FWS Regional Director with recommendations for upper Chevelon Creek management (Clarkson)
  - Weed treatment chemical
  - Work crew for the Winslow High School refuge
- Schedule the next meeting, followed by a field trip to the Blue Ridge Reservoir area.

**2. Action plan requirements (from the FWS guidance)**
Each team member was asked to propose actions that could be implemented within five years to improve the species' status. Each action must include:
- A description of the action
- The responsible party
- An estimated cost
- Assurance that it will be completed
- Identification of the threats and listing factors it addresses
- Entry into the FWS tracking system (ROAR)

The template also asks for the role of other agencies, the role of other ESA programs (such as Section 7, Habitat Conservation Plans, safe harbor agreements, and Section 6 grants), the role of other FWS programs, and a funding analysis of additional actions if more money becomes available.

**3. Recovery goal (partly legible)**
The template's conservation goal appears to address the possibility of delisting or downlisting, maintaining the current status, preventing extinction, and reducing the need to elevate the species to species-at-risk status. The scan does not allow the exact wording or the chosen measure to be confirmed.

**4. Key dates and personnel**
- Email and agenda circulated: July 6, 2009
- Meeting: July 14, 2009, 10:00 a.m., at the Happy Jack Ranger Station fire engine bay
- Key contributors named in the agenda: Clarkson (Bureau of Reclamation), Hedwall (FWS), Paradzick (AZGFD), and Rogers
