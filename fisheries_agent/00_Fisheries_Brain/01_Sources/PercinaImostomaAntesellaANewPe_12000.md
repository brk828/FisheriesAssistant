# Percina (Imostoma) antesella

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #Percidae #Imostoma #Etheostomatini

## Executive Summary
This paper (Williams & Etnier, 1977) formally describes **Percina antesella** (the Amber Darter), a new percid fish species from the [[Coosa River System]] in Tennessee and Georgia. First collected in 1948 from Shoal Creek (Etowah River drainage), the species went unrecognized until 1966. The type locality population was subsequently extirpated following inundation by the [[Alatoona Reservoir]]. The species was later rediscovered in the [[Conasauga River]] (1968), which now represents its sole known extant population. *P. antesella* is the fifth described species in the subgenus [[Imostoma]] and is treated as a phylogenetic "relict," distinct from the closely related "saddleback" species-group formed by *P. tanasi*, *P. uranidea*, and *P. ouachitae*.

## Background & Description
*P. antesella* is a small, moderately slender darter, rarely exceeding 60 mm standard length (SL).

**Diagnostic Features (vs. congeners in Imostoma):**
- **Anterior dorsal saddle:** Positioned entirely anterior to the spinous dorsal fin origin (on the nape) — distinct from *[[Percina tanasi]]*, *[[Percina ouachitae]]*, and *[[Percina uranidea]]*, where this saddle sits under the anterior spinous dorsal fin.
- **Breeding male caudal flange:** Lower procurrent caudal rays extend ventrally to form a triangular, tubercle-bearing flange — absent in *P. tanasi*, *P. ouachitae*, and *P. uranidea*.
- **Transverse scale rows:** 15–19 (usually 16–17), notably higher than *P. ouachitae* (11–16, usually 13–15).

**Meristics (Holotype: TU 94031, adult male, 47.0 mm SL, Conasauga River, TN):**

**Head/Sensory features:**
- Head length 3.4–3.6x in SL; snout moderately long, pointed, slightly longer than orbit.
- Eyes placed high on head; interorbital width very narrow (typical of subgenus Imostoma).
- Cephalic sensory canals complete: 3 supratemporal pores, 4 supraorbital pores, 1 coronal pore, ~10 preoperculomandibular pores, 8–9 (typically 8) infraorbital pores.
- 12 branchiostegal rays (6 per side).

**Squamation:**
- Body squamation usually complete; nape/belly variable (naked to embedded scales).
- Midventral modified scale row in males poorly developed (only slightly enlarged vs. adjacent rows) — contrasts with more developed modified scales in relatives.
- Opercles partially scaled; cheeks typically naked.

**Coloration (Live):**
- Golden brown dorsum with **four dark brown dorsal saddles** (diagnostic trait of the "saddleback" clade within Imostoma).
- Prominent dark brown subocular bar (~pupil width), sloping down/forward from eye.
- Iridescent pale blue interopercle/subopercle.
- Spinous dorsal fin largely unpigmented (clear center, vague basal/marginal bands).
- Soft dorsal, caudal, and pectoral fins with clusters of dark chromatophores; caudal spot present at fin base.
- Anal and pelvic fins largely unpigmented.

**Breeding Tubercles (Males):**
- Present on caudal, anal, pelvic, and pectoral fins, plus scales on venter/breast/caudal peduncle.
- **Absent** on branchiostegals, lower jaw rami, cheek/opercle scales (unlike *P. ouachitae*, which bears tubercles in these regions).
- Reduced in size overall compared to *P. ouachitae* and *P. uranidea*.
- Females generally lack tubercles (rare weak development noted in one gravid female); triangular caudal flange present but reduced in females.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat:** Restricted to headwater tributaries of the [[Coosa River]] system — specifically the [[Conasauga River]] and (historically) the [[Etowah River]] (via Shoal Creek).
- **Physical habitat:** Cool, clear water with moderate-to-swift current; substrate of gravel and cobbles with patches of fine gravel/sand.
- **Microhabitat:** Most frequently collected in riffle areas ≤2 feet deep with moderate current, over silt-free sand and cobble.
- **Sympatric/Comparative species:** Co-occurs regionally within the broader distribution of *[[Percina ouachitae]]* (though not syntopic at type locality), which itself overlaps with *[[Percina uranidea]]* west of the Mississippi River.
- **Trophic/phylogenetic context:** Part of the "saddleback" species group within [[Imostoma]] (*P. antesella*, *P. ouachitae*, *P. tanasi*, *P. uranidea*), characterized by 4–5 dark dorsal saddles; distinguished from *[[Percina shumardi]]*, the only saddle-less species in the subgenus.

## Key Findings
1. **New Species Description:** *Percina antesella* is described as distinct based on unique saddle placement (anterior to spinous dorsal) and a diagnostic male breeding-tubercle flange on the caudal fin, absent in all congeners.
2. **Phylogenetic Placement:** Considered a **relict species** not closely aligned with the *P. tanasi*/*P. uranidea* species pair or with the more generalist, adaptable *P. ouachitae*.
3. **Historical Extirpation:** The original 1948 population in Shoal Creek (Etowah drainage, Cherokee Co., GA) was lost following construction of the [[Alatoona Reservoir]] in the 1950s; repeated collection attempts post-impoundment failed.
4. **Current Range:** Now known only from a ~15-mile stretch of the [[Conasauga River]] across Polk and Bradley counties (TN) and Murray County (GA).
5. **Population Status:** ~70 specimens collected over 6 years (up to 32 in a single year) from the Conasauga River without apparent negative impact on population size, suggesting a stable — though narrowly restricted — population as of publication.
6. **Conservation Concern:** Explicitly flagged as highly vulnerable to extirpation due to extremely restricted range; anthropogenic threats identified include impoundments and agricultural runoff.
7. **Taxonomic Context:** Validates *P. ouachitae* as distinct from *P. uranidea* (per unpublished Thompson & Cashner data), clarifying subgenus Imostoma now comprises five species: *P. antesella*, *P. ouachitae*, *P. shumardi*, *P. tanasi*, *P. uranidea*.
