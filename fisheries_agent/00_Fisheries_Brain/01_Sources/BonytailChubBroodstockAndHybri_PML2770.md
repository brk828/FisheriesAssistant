# Bonytail Chub Broodstock and Hybridization Hatchery Records

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a scanned, partly illegible hatchery record kept at [[Willow Beach]] for [[Bonytail Chub]] broodstock. Its readable core describes two controlled hybridization trials, one of [[Bonytail Chub]] with [[Roundtail Chub]] and one with [[Humpback Chub]], carried out after three more bonytails were added to the broodstock, bringing the total to 5. Both trials used [[Carp Pituitary Injection]] to induce spawning and produced fry that were reared in an outdoor [[Recirculating Raceway]] and shipped to the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] in New Mexico. The later pages are mostly monthly feed charts, water chemistry summaries, culture data, holding facility tallies and disease records. Their numbers are largely unreadable after OCR, so they are summarized only in general terms.

## Background & Description

- **Broodstock status:** Three additional bonytails were collected and transferred to Willow Beach, bringing the broodstock to 5 bonytails. All 3 new fish were female, and no males could be obtained.
- **Rationale for hybridization:** Because no males were available, the hatchery attempted two hybridizations so that the females could still contribute eggs.
- **Scope of later pages:** Pages 5 to 65 contain feed charts, feed summaries, [[Water Chemistry Monitoring]] summaries (temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH and related parameters), culture data, holding facility records and a disease and treatment log. Most entries are handwritten or garbled, and no reliable species-level data can be extracted from them.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Rearing water:** Eggs and fry were held in freshwater at controlled temperatures. Warm trays were kept at 20-21°C, and one cold tray at 12-13°C.
- **Flow conditions:** Egg trays received a constant water flow. The OCR-garbled flow value is not reliably recoverable.
- **Outdoor rearing:** Fry were moved to a caged section of an outside recirculating raceway, covered with saran filter cloth to prevent escape. Natural plankton was present in the raceway and supplemented the starter trout diet.
- **Egg characteristics:** Eggs were adhesive and clumped before water hardening. Egg diameter was 1-2 mm, with a volume of 103 eggs/ml in both trials.

## Key Findings

**1. Bonytail x Roundtail Chub hybridization**

- **Broodstock:** 3 bonytail females and 8 roundtail males were used.
- **Hormone induction:** Roundtail males were injected at 24-hour intervals with 4 mg/kg carp pituitary and were all producing milt 48 hours later. Two bonytail females were injected with 4 mg/kg carp pituitary and were gravid 24 hours later. The third female was already gravid at collection, but her eggs were bad (opaque in appearance).
- **Fertilization:** Each female was hand-stripped individually and fertilized with 3 to 4 males.
- **Egg totals:** 43,000 eggs were collected from the 3 females (OCR-read value). About 40,000 eggs were placed on warm screen trays at 20-21°C, and the remainder went on a cold tray at 12-13°C.
- **Outcome:**
  - The first spawn never developed. The bad eggs from the third female were the likely cause.
  - Cold-tray eggs developed very slowly. Embryos were forming at the early checkpoint, but all were dead by 110 hours.
  - Warm-tray eggs hatched starting at 86 hours after fertilization, with a complete hatch at 95 hours. Newly hatched fry measured 5.5-6.5 mm.
  - All fry had absorbed the yolk sac by 72 hours after hatching. By 95 hours they were beginning to swim up, at 6.5-7.5 mm.
  - Fry reached 12-13 mm in 15 days and were air-freighted to the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]].

**2. Bonytail x Humpback Chub hybridization**

- **Broodstock:** 2 bonytail females and 5 humpback males were used.
- **Hormone induction:** Humpback males were injected at 24-hour intervals with 4 mg/kg carp pituitary and were producing milt 48 hours later. Both bonytail females were injected with 4 mg/kg carp pituitary and were gravid 24 hours later.
- **Fertilization:** One female was hand-stripped and fertilized with milt from 4 males. The eggs were mixed with a turkey feather until water hardening occurred, about 1 hour.
- **Egg totals:** 221,660 eggs were collected. All were placed on screen trays at 20-21°C.
- **Outcome:**
  - Eggs developed more slowly than in the bonytail x roundtail trial. Hatching began 101 hours after fertilization, with a complete hatch at about 112 hours. Newly hatched fry measured 5.5-6.5 mm.
  - All fry had absorbed the yolk sac by 95 hours. By 120 hours they were beginning to swim up, at 6.5-7.5 mm.
  - Fry were fed starter trout diet plus natural plankton, reached 13-14 mm in 15 days, and were shipped to the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]].
