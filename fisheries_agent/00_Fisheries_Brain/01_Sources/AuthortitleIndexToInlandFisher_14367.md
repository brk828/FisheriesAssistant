# Author-Title Index to Inland Fisheries Administrative Reports, 1932-1967

#ichthyology #marine-biology #administrative-records #California-fisheries #historical-documentation

## Executive Summary

This document is a comprehensive author-title index compiled by the [[California Department of Fish and Game]] [[Inland Fisheries Branch]] covering administrative reports from March 1932 through December 1967 (Reports 32-1 through 67-16). The index supersedes three earlier versions (55-12, 58-11, and 65-8) and provides an alphabetical listing by author with chronological report numbers. It documents organizational changes in California's fisheries management structure, transitioning from the Bureau of Fish Conservation (1932-1952) to the Inland Fisheries Branch (1953-present). The index catalogs hundreds of reports covering diverse topics including species management, hatchery operations, creel censuses, stream surveys, reservoir studies, pollution investigations, and fish rescue operations across California's inland waters.

## Background & Description

The index was prepared by [[Inland Fisheries Branch]] staff in May 1968 as Administrative Report No. 68-5. It documents three organizational periods:

- **March 1932 - September 21, 1951**: Bureau of Fish Conservation, California Division of Fish and Game
- **September 22, 1951 - December 31, 1952**: Bureau of Fish Conservation, California Department of Fish and Game  
- **January 1, 1953 - Present**: Inland Fisheries Branch, California Department of Fish and Game

The index is organized alphabetically by author surname, with reports listed chronologically under each author. Report numbers use a two-digit year prefix followed by a sequential number (e.g., 32-1, 67-16). The collection encompasses work by dozens of fisheries biologists and managers across California's diverse aquatic ecosystems, from coastal streams to high Sierra lakes, from the [[Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta]] to southern California reservoirs.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The indexed reports document California's inland fisheries across multiple ecological zones and management challenges:

**Geographic Coverage**: Reports span the entire state, including:
- North coastal streams ([[Klamath River]], [[Eel River]], [[Russian River]])
- Sierra Nevada lakes and streams ([[Lake Tahoe]], [[Crowley Lake]], [[Mono County]] waters)
- Central Valley rivers and reservoirs ([[Sacramento River]], [[San Joaquin River]], [[Millerton Lake]])
- Southern California waters ([[Colorado River]], [[Salton Sea]], San Diego County reservoirs)

**Species Focus**: The reports address both native and introduced species:
- Salmonids: [[Rainbow Trout]] (*Salmo gairdnerii*), [[Steelhead]], [[King Salmon]] (*Oncorhynchus tshawytscha*), [[Silver Salmon]], [[Golden Trout]], [[Kokanee Salmon]]
- Warmwater species: [[Largemouth Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*), [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*), [[White Crappie]], [[Black Crappie]], [[Bluegill]]
- Rough fish control and exotic species management

**Management Activities**: The collection documents:
- Hatchery operations and production costs
- Stream improvement and fish rescue operations
- Chemical treatments (rotenone applications)
- Creel censuses and angler surveys
- Pollution investigations and water quality studies
- Dam and diversion impacts on fish populations

## Key Findings

**Comprehensive Historical Record**: The index catalogs 35 years of California inland fisheries research and management, providing an invaluable reference for historical fisheries conditions, management practices, and ecological changes. Reports are numbered from 32-1 (1932) through 67-16 (1967), representing hundreds of individual studies.

**Major Research Themes**: Recurring topics include:
- **Hatchery Management**: Annual reports from [[Nimbus Salmon and Steelhead Hatchery]], [[Trinity River Salmon and Steelhead Hatchery]], [[Iron Gate Hatchery]], and others documenting production, costs, and operational improvements
- **Creel Census Studies**: Systematic angler surveys at major waters including [[Lake Tahoe]], [[Millerton Lake]], [[Clear Lake]], [[Truckee River]], and numerous coastal streams
- **Stream Improvement**: Flow maintenance dams, check dam construction, fish screen development, and barrier removal projects
- **Species Introductions**: Documentation of [[Threadfin Shad]], [[Florida Largemouth Bass]], [[Walleye]], and other species introductions

**Key Contributors**: Prolific authors include:
- **William A. Dill**: 60+ reports covering trout management, reservoir surveys, golden trout, and management systems
- **Leo Shapovalov**: 50+ reports on salmon, steelhead, stream surveys, and fisheries policy
- **J.H. Wales**: 40+ reports on hatchery research, Castle Lake investigations, and king salmon spawning
- **J.B. Kimsey**: 30+ reports on north coast fisheries, kokanee, and warmwater species
- **Elden H. Vestal**: 30+ reports on Mono County fisheries, chemical treatments, and aerial fish planting

**Specialized Studies**: Notable report series include:
- [[Rush Creek Test Stream]] investigations (multiple reports 1947-1959)
- [[Castle Lake]] trout investigations (1941-1959)
- [[Striped Bass]] party boat fishing effort analyses (1946-1951)
- [[Millerton Lake]] fishery development (1943-1965)
- [[Colorado River]] warmwater fisheries (1942-1966)

**Management Innovations**: The reports document development of:
- Catchable trout planting programs and cost analyses
- Electric fish screens and barrier designs
- Aerial fish planting techniques
- Rotenone application methods and detoxification
- Broodstock selection and selective breeding programs
- Oxygen use in fish transport

**Regional Organization**: Reports are organized by district/region, reflecting California's geographic diversity and the decentralized nature of fisheries management across the state's varied aquatic ecosystems.
