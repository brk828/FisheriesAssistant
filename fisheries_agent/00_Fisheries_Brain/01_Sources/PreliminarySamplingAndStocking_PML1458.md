# Stocking of Endangered Native Fishes into Arizona Ponds (25 October 1988)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This OCR-degraded 1988 correspondence reports the stocking of hatchery-reared juvenile native fishes into ponds in Arizona. Juveniles of [[Colorado Pikeminnow]], [[Humpback Chub]] and [[Razorback Sucker]] were moved to ponds in October 1988, and [[Bonytail Chub]] was also supplied from hatchery sources. The letter describes transport and acclimation methods, reports very low transport mortality, and outlines a monitoring plan for the stocked populations and for invasive species. Several passages are illegible, so counts and some species names are uncertain.
