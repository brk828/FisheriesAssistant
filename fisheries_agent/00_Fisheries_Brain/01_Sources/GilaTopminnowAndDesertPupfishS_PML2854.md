# Gila Topminnow and Desert Pupfish Stocking in AD Wash

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinodontidae #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This April 5, 1993 internal memorandum from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] reports the stocking of [[Desert Pupfish]] and [[Gila Topminnow]] into [[AD Wash]] in March 1993. The memo was written by [[David A. Weedman]], Special Projects Assistant. The source is an OCR scan with many illegible passages, so some counts and names below are uncertain.

## Background & Description

- **Author and recipient:** Weedman wrote to the file ("Files") on the subject of Gila topminnow and desert pupfish stocking.
- **Species stocked:**
  - *Cyprinodon macularius macularius* (Desert Pupfish), a cyprinodontid.
  - *Poeciliopsis occidentalis* (Gila Topminnow), a poeciliid.
- **Stocking numbers:** About 500 Gila topminnow were released. The pupfish count is illegible in the scan.
- **Origin:** Both species were acquired from a hatchery or source whose name is illegible in the scan.
- **Personnel:** Weedman was assisted by a colleague (name partly illegible). Rory Aikens, the public information officer, accompanied the release, and a photographer from the *Arizona Republic* was present.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- The release site was a tank in the vicinity of AD Wash, Arizona. The tank name is unclear in the scan.
- Both species are desert fishes stocked into a wash habitat. The memo does not describe water chemistry, habitat conditions, or other ecological parameters.
- The memo does not report population surveys or long-term monitoring beyond the immediate post-release period.

## Key Findings

- Approximately 500 Gila topminnow and an illegible number of Desert Pupfish were stocked into AD Wash on or about March 1993.
- Both species were observed by Game and Fish staff for roughly ten minutes after release. The memo does not give observation results.
- The event was documented for public information purposes, with a press photographer present.
- No quantitative outcomes, survival data, or follow-up plans are recorded in the legible text.
