# Semi-Control of Flood Inflow to Pond L-7 Engineering Scheme

#ichthyology #habitat-engineering #fish-passage #water-management

## Executive Summary

This engineering document presents Scheme 5 for semi-control of flood inflow to [[Pond L-7]], a freshwater pond system consisting of multiple interconnected units (L-1 through L-10). The design incorporates two low head weirs, gated culverts, and a fish collection facility to manage water flow while maintaining fish passage. The scheme involves significant modifications to existing levees, canals, and water control structures across the pond complex, with water surface elevations ranging from 4660 to 4666 feet.

## Background & Description

The pond system consists of multiple management units (L-1 through L-10) separated by dikes with elevations between 4663 and 4667 feet. Water surface elevations vary by unit: [[Unit L-7]] at 4660 feet, Units L-8 and L-9 at 4661 feet, [[Unit L-3]] at 4664 feet, [[Unit L-4]] at 4665 feet, and Units L-1, L-2, and L-6 at 4666 feet. The existing infrastructure includes multiple 24-inch corrugated metal pipe culverts (marked L-1C, L-2C, L-4C, L-5C, L-6C, L-10, L-2C, L-20) connecting various units. The system features an existing main dike L-7, gravity canal, intake and pump structure, and soil moisture dike.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The design explicitly incorporates [[Fish Collection Facility|fish collection facilities]] to maintain ecological connectivity during flood management operations. The scheme addresses fish passage through the modified water control structures, suggesting the pond system supports fish populations requiring movement between units. The presence of active channels, sand bars, and existing islands indicates a dynamic aquatic habitat. Water level management across the interconnected ponds affects habitat availability and quality for aquatic species.

## Key Findings

**Major Infrastructure Components:**
- Remove approximately 30% of pond levee to [[Pond L-7]]
- Construct low head weir with riprap stabilization at elevation 4466 feet
- Install gated culverts (gates not shown in plan) for low flow outlet
- Construct [[Fish Collection Facility|fish collection facilities]] with inlet culverts from main canal
- Install gated culverts joining ponds
- Raise dikes appropriately where needed

**Channel and Topography Modifications:**
- Regrade pond topography around fish collection facility
- Remove vegetation and regrade active channel to weir
- Clean-out and improve conveyance in main canal
- Remove existing canal sections
- Stabilize riverbed with riprap

**Water Management Features:**
- Two low head weirs for semi-controlled flood inflow
- Multiple gated culvert systems for flow regulation
- Maintained connection between Units L-9 (elevation 4661) and L-7 (elevation 4660)
- Cross-reference to Scheme #7 for possible improvements to inlet area at main channel
