# Bonytail Chub Capture, Transport, Treatment and Shipment Record (April 1988)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #bonytail-chub

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Government hatchery record documents the handling of a single [[Bonytail Chub]] captured in [[Lake Mohave]] near [[Katherine's Landing]] in April 1988. The fish was delivered to [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]] in poor condition, was treated with [[Formalin]] and then [[Furacin]], and died within the observation period. A female's ovary was removed and shipped on ice by [[Federal Express]] to [[Dr. W. L. Minckley]] in Tempe, Arizona, for delivery on 04/29/88. The document is a handling and transfer log, not a research study, and it reports no population or ecological data.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Bonytail Chub]] (scientific name not given in the record).
- **Sex:** Female.
- **Total length:** 43.5 cm (OCR reading; the value appears as "43j.5 cm").
- **Weight:** 740.0 g.
- **Capture:** Collected in Lake Mohave near Katherine's Landing and transported to Willow Beach NFH at about 2:30 p.m. by [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] personnel.
- **Condition at arrival:** Fish was in bad shape, with locomotion mostly limited to the anterior portion of the body. The fish could still swim.
- **Agencies and personnel named:** [[Lyle R. Miller]] (Hatchery Manager, Willow Beach NFH), Arizona Game and Fish Department, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Regional Office, Phoenix; Kingman Service office), [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] (Dexter, New Mexico), and [[Dr. W. L. Minckley]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The record refers to the fish's native reservoir habitat only as the capture site, Lake Mohave. It gives no water chemistry, temperature, habitat, population, or other environmental measurements. The [[Bonytail Chub]] is the only organism discussed, and no other aquatic species are recorded.

The treatments referred to in the log were applied in a hatchery setting, not in the natural environment:

- **Raceway A-10:** Fish was initially placed here and treated with [[Formalin]] at 1:40,000.
- **Tank 2-A (Hatchery Building):** Fish was moved here and kept at 10 ppm [[Furacin]].

## Key Findings

1. **Poor initial condition:** The fish was weak and showed impaired swimming at intake, with movement mainly restricted to the front of the body.
2. **Treatment sequence:** Formalin (1:40,000) was used in Raceway A-10, followed by Furacin (10 ppm) in Tank 2-A. The log notes the fish was "belly up" each day, which indicates it did not recover.
3. **Sampling note:** The record includes the instruction "Treated fish not, use tail end of fish," which is ambiguous in the scan. It may concern tissue selection for analysis.
4. **Sample handling and shipment:** The female's ovary was removed, clipped, packed in ice, and sent by Federal Express to Dr. W. L. Minckley in Tempe, Arizona, for delivery on 04/29/88. The purpose of the shipment is not stated.
5. **Log dates:** Entries cover 04/21/88, 04/22/88, 04/25/88, and 04/28/88, with the final shipment on 04/28/88.
6. **Document type:** This is an operational transfer and treatment log from a federal hatchery. It contains no experimental design, sample sizes beyond a single individual, or statistical results.
