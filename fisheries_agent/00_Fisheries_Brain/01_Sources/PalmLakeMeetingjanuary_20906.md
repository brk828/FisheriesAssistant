# Palm Lake Management Meeting Follow-Up Correspondence

#ichthyology #habitat-management #native-fish-conservation

## Executive Summary

This correspondence from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AGFD) to [[The Nature Conservancy]] (TNC) summarizes action items and deadlines from a January 14, 1993 meeting regarding [[Palm Lake]] at the [[Hassayampa River Preserve]] in [[Wickenburg, Arizona]]. The letter outlines collaborative tasks between AGFD, TNC, and [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) for lake management, including development of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), water quality monitoring, and habitat restoration activities.

## Background & Description

[[Palm Lake]] is located at the [[Hassayampa River Preserve]] managed by [[The Nature Conservancy]] in Wickenburg, Arizona. The correspondence was authored by Kirk L. Young, Native Fish Projects Manager at AGFD, and addressed to Val Little at TNC. The meeting involved multiple agencies coordinating management activities for the lake, with particular focus on native fish conservation and habitat quality. An "umbrella" MOU between TNC and AGFD was under consideration by the commission at the time of writing, which could supersede the need for a separate Palm Lake-specific MOU.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The management discussions centered on water quality and habitat conditions at [[Palm Lake]]. Key environmental concerns included [[Dissolved Oxygen]] levels requiring monitoring, water depth management through pumping, and [[Cattail]] encroachment requiring removal. The involvement of the Native Fish Projects Manager indicates the lake supports or is intended to support native fish populations. Water quality monitoring was identified as a priority, with monthly data collection being explored through USFWS. Minimum pool depths were under consideration as part of habitat management planning.

## Key Findings

**Action Items and Deadlines:**
1. **MOU Development Timeline**: Consolidated comments from TNC and USFWS due to AGFD by February 15, 1993; AGFD to incorporate comments and return for review by March 5, 1993; final draft prepared for May or June Commission Meeting
2. **Equipment Loan**: AGFD to loan [[Dissolved Oxygen]] test kit to TNC for [[Palm Lake]] monitoring
3. **Cost Analysis**: TNC to produce cost estimates for water pumping into [[Palm Lake]]
4. **Habitat Management Plan**: AGFD, TNC, and USFWS to collaboratively determine extent, location, and methods of [[Cattail]] removal, and establish minimum pool depths for [[Palm Lake]]
5. **Water Quality Monitoring**: USFWS to investigate possibility of monthly water quality data collection at [[Palm Lake]]
6. **Field Sampling**: AGFD planned to sample [[Palm Lake]] in February 1993

**Administrative Note**: The Palm Lake MOU might be superseded by an umbrella TNC/AGFD MOU under commission review; if approved, the Palm Lake MOU would serve as basis for a Management Agreement instead.
