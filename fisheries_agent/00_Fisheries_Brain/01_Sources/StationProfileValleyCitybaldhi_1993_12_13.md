# 1993 Station Profile: Valley City/Baldhill Dam National Fish Hatchery

## Executive Summary
This document is a 1993 administrative and operational station profile for the [[Valley City National Fish Hatchery]], with its substation [[Baldhill Dam]], located in Valley City, North Dakota. Established in 1938 under the "White Bill" (Public Law 2-40, 71st Congress), the hatchery's primary mission is the production of cool- and warm-water fish species for stocking [[National Wildlife Refuges]], Tribal lands, and to mitigate impacts of Federal water development projects on the [[Missouri River]]. The facility also serves as a research site for fish culture techniques, including dry diet development for cool-water and endangered species in cooperation with the [[Bozeman Technology Center]]. The profile details egg/fry production figures, physical infrastructure, funding/staffing trends (1991–1995), and fiscal year 1993 fish distribution data across five states.

## Background & Description
This is a facility operations report and does not contain morphological data on the species reared. Species produced and distributed include:
- [[Northern Pike]] (*Esox lucius*)
- [[Walleye]] (*Sander vitreus*)
- [[Bluegill]] (*Lepomis macrochirus*)
- [[Tiger Muskie]] (hybrid *Esox lucius* x *Esox masquinongy*)
- [[Largemouth Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*)
- [[Smallmouth Bass]] (*Micropterus dolomieu*)
- [[Yellow Perch]] (*Perca flavescens*)
- [[Channel Catfish]] (*Ictalurus punctatus*)
- [[Black Crappie]] (*Pomoxis nigromaculatus*)

No morphometric, meristic, or physical descriptive data for these species are provided in the source text.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat Type:** Managed freshwater hatchery ponds and associated cool/warm water systems designed to mimic conditions suitable for both cool-water species (e.g., [[Northern Pike]], [[Walleye]]) and warm-water species (e.g., [[Largemouth Bass]], [[Channel Catfish]]).
- **Ecological Role:** The hatchery functions as an artificial propagation and stocking hub supporting recreational and mitigation fisheries rather than a natural ecological study site.
- **Watershed Context:** Fish are produced for stocking in waters connected to the [[Missouri River]] basin, supporting mitigation for Federal water development projects, as well as supplementation of [[National Wildlife Refuge]] waters and Tribal lands.
- **Interagency Cooperation:** Collaborates with the State of North Dakota and the [[Bureau of Indian Affairs]] (BIA) for stocking and species management, and with the [[Bozeman Technology Center]] for applied research on dry diet formulations for cool-water and endangered fish species.
