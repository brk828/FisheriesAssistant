## Executive Summary

This paper describes **[[Nitocrella rhodiensis]]**, a new species of harpacticoid copepod within the genus **[[Nitocrella]]** (family **[[Ameiridae]]**), discovered in phreatic (groundwater) subterranean waters on the island of **[[Rhodes]]**, Greece. This work is highly relevant to aquatic and subterranean ecosystem studies, as it documents a component of the **[[Stygofauna]]** (subterranean aquatic fauna) inhabiting freshwater wells. The species belongs to the "chappuisi-group" as defined by **[[Petkovski 1976]]**'s taxonomic revision of *Nitocrella*, distinguished primarily by the setal armature of leg 2's endopod and the caudal rami morphology. This represents the first record of the genus *Nitocrella* from Rhodes, expanding known distribution of the genus within Greece.

## Background & Description

**General Body Plan:**
- Blind, unpigmented, middle-sized copepod
- Body slender, gradually tapering posteriorly
- Body length (mature females, excluding antennae, antennulae, and furcal setae): **0.40–0.47 mm**
- Rostrum small and poorly defined

**Appendages:**
- **Antennula:** 8-segmented; aesthetasc on segment 4 much longer than following articles combined
- **Antenna:** Exopod subtriangular, 1-segmented, armed with 3 apical stout spines
- **Leg 1 (P1):** Both rami 3-segmented, subequal in size; exopod with subdistal spines and 2 apical long setae; endopod article 1 short, article 2 naked, article 3 with 3 apical setae
- **Leg 2 (P2):** Exopod 3-segmented (longer than endopod); endopod 2-segmented — **diagnostic feature: second segment bears 1 inner seta**
- **Leg 3 (P3):** Similar armature to leg 2, but lacks inner seta on terminal endopod article
- **Leg 4 (P4):** Exopod 3-segmented with **5 spines/setae on terminal segment** (defining trait of the "chappuisi-group"); endopod 2-segmented with subequal articles
- **Leg 5 (P5):** Basiendopod with 3 plumose spines + 1 slender outer seta; exopod subovoidal (~1.2× longer than broad), armed with 4 setae

**Setal Formula (P1–P4):**
| Leg | Exopod | Endopod |
|-----|--------|---------|
| P1  | 0.1.022 | 1.0.120 |
| P2  | 0.1.022 | —.0.120 |
| P3  | 0.1.022 | —.0.020 |
| P4  | 0.1.122 | —.0.020 |

**Abdomen & Furca:**
- Abdominal segments each bear posterior rows of hair-like setules
- Genital segments 1 and 2 not fused dorsally
- **Anal operculum:** slightly convex, 9–11 teeth along medial margin, 5–7 longer lateral spines
- **Caudal rami (furca):** short, subconical, slightly longer than broad (L/W = 1.25–1.30); each ramus with:
  - 1 short inner distal seta
  - 2 long principal terminal setae (innermost longest)
  - 2 setae at lateral distal/subdistal corners
  - 1 long dorsal seta (>2× ramus length)

**Sexual Dimorphism:** Male unknown (only female specimens collected and described).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Habitat Type:** Phreatic (groundwater) subterranean freshwater — specifically man-made freshwater wells.

**Environmental Parameters:**
- Water level from soil surface: 4.5–5.5 m
- Water depth: 4.5 m
- Temperature: 14.9°C–15.1°C
- pH: 7
- Substrate: thin organogenic sand

**Geographic Distribution:** Known only from three well sites on Rhodes:
1. Along the Rhodes–Kamiros road (cross road to Tolos), ~100 m from sea (type locality)
2. Cremasti, near International Airport
3. Along Rhodes–Lindos road, at Ladiko

**Co-occurring Fauna (Stygofaunal Community):**
- Cyclopid copepods: *Eucyclops serrulatus*
- Asellid isopods: *Asellus aquaticus*, *Proasellus coxalis*
- Microparasellid isopods: *Microcharon ullae*
- Amphipods: *Bogidiella* cf. *longiflagellum*, *Niphargus* sp.
- Ostracods
- Gastropods
- Water mites
- Oligochaetes
- Turbellarians
- Nematodes
- Mosquito larvae

This assemblage is characteristic of **[[Groundwater Ecosystems]]** and highlights the specialized, low-light, low-nutrient adaptations (blindness, lack of pigmentation) typical of **[[Stygobites]]**.

## Key Findings

1. **New Species Description:** *Nitocrella rhodiensis* n. sp. is formally described based on holotype and paratype females collected in June 1981.

2. **First Record for Rhodes:** This is the first documented occurrence of the genus **[[Nitocrella]]** on the island of Rhodes, extending the known geographic range of the genus within Greece and suggesting broader distribution than previously recognized.

3. **Taxonomic Placement:** The species is assigned to the **"chappuisi-group"** (sensu Petkovski 1976) based on possessing 5 setae/spines on the terminal segment of the P4 exopod, distinguishing it from the "hirta-group" (3–4 setae) and "vasconica-group" (6 setae).

4. **Diagnostic Differentiation:**
   - Distinguished from **[[Nitocrella stammeri]]** (Italy, Turkey, continental Greece) by: presence of inner seta on P2 endopod terminal segment, longer furcal rami, differing seta lengths, and distinct P5 basiendopod armature.
   - Distinguished from **[[Nitocrella achaiae]]** (Greek Peloponnesus) by: inner seta on P2 endopod, shorter caudal rami, different anal operculum armature, and different spine count on P5 basiendopod.
   - Distinguished from **[[Nitocrella neutra]]** (Yugoslavia) by: markedly different endopod armature across all legs and P1 exopod terminal segment.

5. **Biogeographic Significance:** The discovery reinforces the pattern of endemism and diversification within Mediterranean island groundwater systems, with closely related *Nitocrella* species showing fine-scale morphological divergence across geographically proximate but isolated aquifer systems (Italy, Turkey, mainland Greece, Peloponnesus, and now Rhodes).

6. **Methodological Note:** All specimens were collected via well-sampling techniques, dissected, and mounted in Faure's medium; type material deposited at the Muséum d'Histoire Naturelle de Genève and the Zoological Institute, University of L'Aquila.
