# Ouray Wildlife Refuge, natural flood inflow to Leota Odd-numbered Ponds

#wetland-hydrology #floodplain-restoration #engineering-exhibit

## Executive Summary

This document is a engineering site exhibit (Exhibit 1, "Natural Flood Inflow") for the [[Leota Unit]] of the [[Ouray Wildlife Refuge]]. It maps existing and proposed hydraulic works and shows how [[Natural Flood Inflow]] reaches the odd-numbered management units (L-2 through L-10). The exhibit was prepared by [[FLO Engineering Inc]]. It proposes a set of regrading and structural schemes intended to manage how floodwater enters and drains the units. The sheet covers Scheme 1 in detail and records two revision stages: proposed Schemes 1–4 (10/24/94) and revised Schemes 1–7 (5/1/98). The exhibit contains no biological, fish, or water-quality data.

## Background & Description

The exhibit is a plan-view map at a scale of 1 inch = 600 ft, with a legend and a table of existing culverts. Its main graphic elements are:

- **Management units:** Labeled L-2, L-3, L-4, L-6, L-8, L-9, and L-10, each annotated with a [[Water Surface Elevation]]. Recorded values include 4661, 4663, 4664, 4665, and 4666 ft. One value reads "4860," which is most likely a scan or transcription error for 4660 and should be checked against the original drawing.
- **Existing features:** A [[Riverside Levee]], an [[Existing Canal]], an [[Existing Gravity Feed Canal]], existing islands, and an existing culvert set.
- **Existing culverts:** Eight [[Corrugated Metal Pipe]] culverts, each 24 inches in diameter, marked on the L-series units (for example L-1B, L-1C, L-1D, L-2C, L-4B, L-4C). Culvert IDs in the source are partly garbled by OCR.
- **Legend symbols for proposed works:** [[Low Head Weir]] with [[Riprap]], proposed culverts, proposed regrading, proposed jetty, and a [[Soil Moisture Dike]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The sheet is a hydraulic and civil-engineering document. It concerns water routing, levee and canal modification, and drainage control in a managed refuge wetland. It does not report species, habitat condition, vegetation surveys, water chemistry, or population data. Any ecological interpretation is therefore outside what the source supports. The stated purpose of the schemes (controlling inflow and drainage across impoundment units) is relevant to [[Wetland Impoundment Management]] and [[Floodplain Restoration]], but the document does not state ecological outcomes.

## Key Findings

The exhibit sets out four components of **Scheme 1**:

1. Remove the riverside levee.
2. Remove the existing canal.
3. Remove vegetation and regrade the active channel so that it drains to the pond.
4. Regrade ponds to improve drainage.

Additional proposed elements shown in the legend include the low head weir with riprap, new culverts, regrading areas, a jetty, and a soil moisture dike. These are shown as components of the wider scheme set rather than as separately quantified designs.

The document's revision history indicates the design evolved over time:

- **10/24/94:** Proposed Schemes 1–4.
- **5/1/98:** Revised to Schemes 1–7 (the revision note reads "Remsed Schemes 1–7" in the OCR text).

Quantities, flow rates, volumes, costs, and design criteria are not given in the exhibit. Such values would need to come from the accompanying report or calculations, which are not included here.

**Document limitations:** The sheet is a graphic exhibit without a stated author date beyond the revision dates, no discharge or hydrologic data, and no explicit statement of the refuge's water management objectives. Several labels are partly illegible in the scanned text.
