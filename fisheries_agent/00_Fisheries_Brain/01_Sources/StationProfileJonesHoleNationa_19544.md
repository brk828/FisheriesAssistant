## Executive Summary

This document is a **Station Profile** for the **[[Jones Hole National Fish Hatchery]]**, a U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service facility established in 1968 under the **Colorado River Storage Project Act (70 Stat. 105, Section 8)**. The hatchery's primary mission is to fulfill mitigation requirements for federal water development projects along the Colorado River system by producing **[[Cutthroat Trout]]**, **[[Brook Trout]]**, **[[Brown Trout]]**, and **[[Rainbow Trout]]** for stocking in Utah, Wyoming, and on Tribal lands (Uintah and Ouray Indian Reservations). A separate distribution table also lists large-scale **[[Lake Trout]]** production destined for the Great Lakes region (Michigan, Illinois, Wisconsin), suggesting this facility (or an associated program) contributes to Great Lakes fisheries restoration as well. The document is largely administrative, detailing facility infrastructure, staffing, funding, and annual fish distribution numbers rather than biological or morphological data.

## Background & Description

No morphological, meristic, or anatomical data for the species is provided in this source. The only size-related information given is operational/production-stage sizing:

- Distributed salmonids (Brook, Brown, Cutthroat, Rainbow) are typically released at **3", 6", or 8" in length** — indicating fry, fingerling, and sub-catchable/catchable stocking classes.
- No length/weight data is provided for the **[[Lake Trout]]** distributed under the second program (Michigan/Illinois/Wisconsin), though numbers reported "exclude eggs and fry," implying distributed fish are past the earliest life stages.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Primary Habitat Context:** The hatchery serves as a mitigation facility for the **[[Colorado River Storage Project]]**, replacing salmonid habitat/production lost to federal dam and water development projects. Fish are stocked into:
  - Federal waters (managed by [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] and other federal agencies)
  - State waters (Utah, Wyoming)
  - **[[Flaming Gorge Reservoir]]** — a major recipient, receiving in excess of 450,000 fish each spring.
- **Secondary Program:** A parallel large-volume program stocks **[[Lake Trout]]** into the **[[Great Lakes]]** system (waters relevant to Michigan, Illinois, and Wisconsin), indicating a role in [[Great Lakes Fisheries Restoration]] efforts, likely as part of broader USFWS lake trout rehabilitation programs following historic population collapses (e.g., due to sea lamprey predation and overfishing).
- **Land Management Context:** Stocked fish support recreational and tribal fisheries on Federal lands in Utah and Wyoming and on the **[[Uintah and Ouray Indian Reservations]]**.
