# San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area Biological Resources Committee Meeting Notice

#riparian-ecology #conservation-management #habitat-management

## Executive Summary

This administrative correspondence from the Bureau of Land Management Safford District Office documents a meeting notice dated September 15, 1989, convening the [[San Pedro River]] Biological Resources Committee. The meeting was scheduled to discuss the planned Habitat Management Plan for the [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]], bringing together experts from multiple agencies and academic institutions to coordinate conservation efforts for this riparian ecosystem in Arizona.

## Background & Description

The [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]] represents a significant riparian habitat corridor in southeastern Arizona managed by the Bureau of Land Management. The Biological Resources Technical Committee comprised representatives from:

- Arizona State University Department of Zoology (Dr. W.L. Minckley, Dr. Robert Ohmart)
- University of Arizona (Dr. Bill Mannon)
- Arizona Game & Fish Department (Rich Glinski, Cecil Schwalbe, Rick Gerhardt, John Millican)
- U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service (Sam Spiller)
- Independent consultants (Doug Danforth)

The meeting was scheduled for October 4, 1989, at 7:00 p.m. in Room 311, Biological Sciences East building, University of Arizona campus in Tucson.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[San Pedro River]] riparian corridor represents a critical habitat type in the arid southwestern United States. Riparian zones in this region support disproportionately high biodiversity relative to their limited spatial extent, providing essential habitat for aquatic and terrestrial species. The establishment of the [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]] reflected federal recognition of the ecological importance of protecting and managing this riparian ecosystem through coordinated, science-based planning.

## Key Findings

The meeting agenda outlined the following priority topics for the Biological Resources Technical Committee:

**Management Planning:**
- Summary of final decisions on the Management Plan
- Discussion of the Habitat Management Plan (HMP) process
- Identification of feasible objectives and planned actions

**Current Operations:**
- Review of planned projects for Fiscal Year 1990
- Update on ongoing inventories and monitoring efforts

**Conservation Priorities:**
- Discussion of reintroduction possibilities for species
- Evaluation needs for management effectiveness
- Identification of additional inventory needs

The meeting structure indicated a collaborative, multi-agency approach to riparian conservation management, integrating academic expertise with state and federal agency resources to develop comprehensive habitat management strategies for the [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]].
