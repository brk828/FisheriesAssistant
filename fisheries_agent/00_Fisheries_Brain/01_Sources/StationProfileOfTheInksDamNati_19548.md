# Station Profile: Inks Dam National Fish Hatchery

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fisheries-management #Moronidae #Ictaluridae #Centrarchidae #Polyodontidae

## Executive Summary
The [[Inks Dam National Fish Hatchery]] is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service federal production facility established in 1938 under the Public Works Administration grant that funded [[Buchanan Dam]] construction. Located near Burnet, Texas, its original purpose was to supply fish for stocking the chain of Highland Lakes impoundments on the [[Colorado River (Texas)]]. Historically focused on catchable [[Channel Catfish]] and fingerling [[Largemouth Bass]], the station's mission has shifted toward rearing Phase I [[Striped Bass]] for restoration stocking along the Texas Gulf Coast. The facility also supports mitigation and stocking obligations on Indian Reservation lands and hosts the Regional Distribution Unit (RDU) for USFWS Region II. A cooperative [[Paddlefish]] rearing program with Texas Parks & Wildlife began in FY1991.

## Background & Description
This station profile is an administrative/operational report rather than a biological study, and contains no direct morphological, meristic, or anatomical data on the species reared. Species handled at the facility include:
- **[[Striped Bass]]** (*Morone saxatilis*) — reared to "Phase I" fingerling stage for anadromous restoration.
- **[[Channel Catfish]]** (*Ictalurus punctatus*) — reared to catchable size.
- **[[Largemouth Bass]]** (*Micropterus salmoides*) — reared to fingerling size.
- **[[Rainbow Trout]]** (*Oncorhynchus mykiss*) — hauled/distributed by the RDU, not indicated as reared on-site.
- **[[Paddlefish]]** (*Polyodon spathula*) — rearing program initiated FY1991.

No size, weight, or developmental staging data beyond "fingerling," "Phase I," and "catchable size" classifications are provided in the source text.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat/Infrastructure**: 28 ponds (0.25–1.5 acres each), totaling 25 surface acres, supplied by a gravity-fed water system originating from [[Inks Lake]]. Most production infrastructure was completed by 1940, with subsequent expansions (residences, holding house, shop, service buildings, visitor center).
- **Target Ecosystem**: The hatchery supports the Highland Lakes chain — a series of five impoundments on the [[Colorado River (Texas)]] at and above Austin, TX — functioning as a put-and-take and restoration stocking source.
- **Restoration Role**: Central to the national [[Striped Bass Restoration]] effort, targeting reintroduction to the [[Texas Gulf Coast]] anadromous fish populations.
- **Mitigation Role**: Supplies [[Channel Catfish]] to Indian Reservation waters and other high-priority federal waters (military lands, National Forest lands); [[Largemouth Bass]] supplied to Indian lands and cooperating states.
- **Distribution Network**: The Regional Distribution Unit (RDU), based at Inks Dam NFH, services hatcheries across Region II, including [[Alchesay-Williams Creek National Fish Hatchery]], [[Mescalero National Fish Hatchery]], and [[Uvalde National Fish Hatchery]].
- **Land-use agreement**: A 30-year lease (12.16 acres) with the [[Lower Colorado River Authority]] (Meager Property) for future hatchery expansion, paid via annual fish stocking (catchable Channel Catfish or Striped Bass fingerlings) into the Highland Lakes rather than cash.

## Key Findings
- **Record Striped Bass Production (FY91)**: 221,450 fingerlings produced — a new station record, surpassing the previous record of 177,660. Overall return rate: **37%**.
- **Lake Buchanan Stocking**: 60,000 Striped Bass stocked in 1991 as an in-kind lease payment to the LCRA.
- **Typical Annual Production**: ~300,000 Channel Catfish; up to ~400,000 Largemouth Bass fingerlings in good years.
- **RDU Logistics (FY91)**: 38 hauling trips, 50,233 miles traveled, 1,013,824 fish transported, totaling 99,836 lbs.
- **Distribution Footprint (FY91)**: Fish distributed to Arizona, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Texas, and Utah.
- **Total Fish Distributed by Fiscal Year**:
  - FY89: 825,555 (Federal/Other Waters + State Waters, breakdown partial in source)
  - FY90: 249,530 (Federal + State + Private waters)
  - FY91: 899,499 (Federal + State + Private waters)
- **Funding Trends (Operations, $000)**: $283.0 (1989) → $277.3 (1990) → $359.2 (1991) → est. $340.0 (1992) → est. $355.0 (1993).
- **Staffing**: Stable at 6 FTE (1989–1991), increasing to 7 FTE (1992 est., 1993 est.).
- **Cost Recoverables**: Ranged from $7.1K–$9.5K (1990–1993 est.).
- **New Program**: Paddlefish rearing initiated FY1991 in partnership with [[Texas Parks and Wildlife Department]], diversifying the hatchery's species portfolio beyond traditional catfish/bass/striped bass production.
