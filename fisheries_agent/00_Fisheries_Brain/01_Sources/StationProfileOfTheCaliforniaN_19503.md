# Station Profile: California-Nevada Fish Health Center

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-health

## Executive Summary
The **California-Nevada Fish Health Center** is a diagnostic and disease-monitoring laboratory operated by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Region 1), established in 1986 to provide fish health services across California and Nevada. Headquartered at the converted Residence No. 69 of [[Coleman National Fish Hatchery]] near Anderson, California, the Center supports federal, state, tribal, and private fishery programs. Its work directly underpins hatchery production and wild stock management for anadromous and resident fish populations throughout the region, including salmonid stocks tied to the [[Sacramento River]], [[Klamath River]], and [[Trinity River]] systems, as well as [[Pyramid Lake]] fisheries.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
Rather than describing a species' ecological niche, the source defines the Center's **institutional and geographic niche** within the broader Pacific Northwest/California fisheries management network:

- **Geographic Range Served:** California and Nevada
- **National Fish Hatcheries supported:** [[Coleman National Fish Hatchery]] and [[Lahontan National Fish Hatchery]]
- **Fisheries Resource Offices:** Red Bluff, Arcata, Stockton, Coastal, Klamath, and Trinity River offices
- **Fish and Wildlife Enhancement:** Sacramento Office
- **Research Labs:** Seattle and Leetown Fish and Wildlife Research Labs
- **Cooperative Fishery Research Units:** University of Idaho and Oregon State University
- **Tribal Partners:** [[Pyramid Lake Fisheries]], [[Hoopa Tribal Fisheries]]
- **Private Aquaculture Partner:** Mount Lassen Trout Farms
- **State Agencies:** California Department of Fish and Game, Nevada Department of Wildlife
- **Academic Partner:** University of California, Davis

This network positions the Center as a central diagnostic hub linking hatchery-reared salmonids, tribal fisheries, and wild stock management across two states, with particular relevance to anadromous species migrating through the [[Central Valley]] and [[Klamath-Trinity River Basin]].

## Key Findings
- **Founding & Mission:** Established in 1986 to deliver fish health diagnostic and consultation services to a broad, multi-jurisdictional clientele (federal, state, tribal, and private).
- **Facility:** Operates from a converted residential building at Coleman National Fish Hatchery, repurposed into an office/laboratory in 1989.

- **Scope of Service (FY1991):** The Center's caseload spanned two National Fish Hatcheries, six Fisheries Resource Offices, two federal research labs, two university cooperative units, two tribal fisheries programs, one private aquaculture operation, two state wildlife agencies, and one university — reflecting a comprehensive regional fish health infrastructure role.
- **Congressional Oversight:** Falls under California's 2nd Congressional District (Rep. Wally Herger).
