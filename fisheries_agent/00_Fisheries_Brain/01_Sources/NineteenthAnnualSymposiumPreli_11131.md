# Desert Fishes Council 1987 Annual Symposium: Preliminary Details and Call for Papers

#ichthyology #marine-biology #desert-fishes

## Executive Summary

This is an organizational circular from the [[Desert Fishes Council]]'s Executive Committee, dated May 22, 1987, announcing the council's annual symposium. It contains no research data, species records, or measurements. The meeting will take place November 12–14, 1987, in [[Hermosillo]], [[Sonora]], Mexico, hosted by [[El Centro Ecológico de Sonora]] at the [[Holiday Inn de Hermosillo]]. The letter serves as the official call for papers, sets a submission deadline of October 1, and includes travel, lodging, and banquet logistics, plus a return questionnaire for preliminary planning.

## Background & Description

- **Issuing body:** Executive Committee of the [[Desert Fishes Council]], based in Bishop, California. The letter is signed by K. P. Pister, Executive Secretary.
- **Audience:** All council members and interested parties.
- **Purpose:** Preliminary planning for the 1987 symposium, which the letter describes as one of the council's most popular recurring activities. The ordinal number of the symposium is garbled in the scan and is not reliably recoverable.
- **Format:** Standard paper sessions plus two proposed mini-symposia:
  - "Fishes and Aquatic Habitats of the [[Sonoran Desert]] Region"
  - "[[Fish Community Monitoring]] and Habitat Monitoring: What Do We Need? What Can We Expect to Accomplish?"
- **Prior meetings in Mexico:** The letter recalls earlier Mexican meetings, including a barbecue at [[Cuatrociénegas]] in 1980 and a banquet at [[San Luis Potosí]] in 1984.
- **Enclosures:** A hotel list and a detachable 1987 symposium questionnaire.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document has little direct ecological content. Its only environmental framing is the mini-symposium theme on fishes and aquatic habitats of the Sonoran Desert, and the monitoring symposium's focus on what fish community and habitat monitoring should achieve. No specific water bodies, species, or habitat measurements are reported.

## Key Findings

**Meeting logistics**
- **Dates:** November 12–14, 1987.
- **Venue:** Meetings at the Holiday Inn de Hermosillo, with the host organization El Centro Ecológico de Sonora.
- **Accommodation:** The letter lists roughly eleven alternative hotels and motels, mostly on the same avenue (Bulevar Padre Kino or Bulevar Kino) within walking distance of the Holiday Inn. Prices are quoted in Mexican pesos for single, double, and multi-occupancy rooms and are explicitly subject to change because of inflation. The list is summarized here rather than reproduced in full.

**Transportation**
- [[AeroMéxico]] flies daily from [[Tucson]] to Hermosillo. A group rate of about $75.00 round trip is expected with advance booking, which the letter describes as cheaper than chartering a bus.
- Transportation coordinator: Dean Hendrickson of the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]. Contact number given: (602) 942-3000.
- Proposed group itinerary: fly from Tucson on Wednesday, November 11, and return Monday, November 16. Sunday, November 15 is set aside as a touring day in Hermosillo and its environs. Alternate return dates can be arranged.

**Banquet**
- The first-day banquet will be held at Jardines de Xochimilco and will feature typical Sonoran food.
- The letter estimates a cost not exceeding US$10.00 per person.

**Call for papers**
- Titles are due no later than October 1, 1987.
- Papers are accepted in order of submission, and the program may fill early.
- Some presentations are already arranged, including contributions from Mexican biologists.

**Local coordination**
- A council member in Mexico, Lourdes Juarez Ra, is handling local arrangements. The letter notes she presented papers at the previous year's meeting in St. George, Utah.

**Action items for members**
- Return the questionnaire within two weeks to indicate attendance, party size, flight, banquet and field trip interest, and paper titles for any of the three session types.
- Indicate whether you will book your own hotel or need help.
- The questionnaire is non-binding and serves only for preliminary planning.
- A second questionnaire and the annual dues statement will be mailed in late summer 1987.
