# Wahweap Operation and Maintenance: Colorado River Recovery Program FY 99 Annual Project Report

#ichthyology #marine-biology #razorback-sucker

## Executive Summary

This FY 1999 annual project report (Project Number 29c) from the [[Colorado River Recovery Program]] describes the operation and maintenance of [[Propagation Facilities]] for endangered fishes at the [[Wahweap Fish Hatchery]] on [[Lake Powell]]. The project runs a genetically sound propagation program for high-priority endangered fish species in the [[Upper Colorado River Basin]]. All FY 99 tasks were completed on or near their deadlines, the work was 100% complete, and spending matched the budget exactly at $83,000. The project status is "on track and ongoing."

## Background & Description

- **Project title:** Wahweap Operation and Maintenance (Project 29c).
- **Principal investigators:**
  - [[Wayne Gustaveson]], [[Utah Division of Wildlife Resources]], Lake Powell, Wahweap Installation, Page, Arizona.
  - [[Kevin D. Christopherson]], Utah Division of Wildlife Resources, Northeastern Regional Office, Vernal, Utah.
- **Project summary:** The work covers all tasks needed to run propagation facilities for endangered fishes. These include pond culture, fish marking, and maintenance of pedigreed family lot records.
- **Goal:** Operate a genetically sound propagation program for high-priority endangered fish species for the Recovery Program in the Upper Colorado River Basin, following the Annual Propagation Facilities Operation Plan.
- **Objective:** Operate and maintain propagation facilities that hold, rear, or produce captive-reared endangered fishes for the Recovery Program, under the same operation plan.
- **End products:**
  1. Propagation technology and methodology.
  2. Fish for research.
- **Study schedule:** Initial year 1993; final year ongoing.
- **Recovery Action Plan linkage (RIPRAP):**
  - IV.A. Manage genetic integrity and augment or restore populations.
  - IV.A.4. Secure and manage genetic stocks in refugia, including IV.A.4.a [[Razorback Sucker]].
  - IV.C. Operate and maintain facilities.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The project targets endangered fishes of the Upper Colorado River Basin, a system where wild populations have declined and captive propagation supports recovery. The document names only the [[Razorback Sucker]] specifically, under the genetic stock management action. Other "high priority endangered fish species" are mentioned without being named. The source contains no habitat, water-chemistry, or population data. The propagation work is intended to hold genetic stocks in refugia and to support augmentation or restoration of wild populations under the Recovery Program's genetic management actions.
