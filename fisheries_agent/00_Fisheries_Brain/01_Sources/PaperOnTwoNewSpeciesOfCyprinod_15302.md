## Executive Summary
This document is a piece of institutional correspondence from Robert R. Miller (Curator Emeritus of Fishes) to David Catania at the California Academy of Sciences, dated 8 February 1990. It does not constitute a primary research paper but rather documents a request for specimen data in support of an active taxonomic and zoogeographic study. Miller, along with collaborators Mike Smith and W.L. Minckley, was preparing a manuscript on the fish fauna of northwestern Chihuahua, Mexico, which included the formal description of two new species within the genus [[Cyprinodon]]. The letter specifically requests verification of a historical [[Salmo gairdneri]] specimen record to complete a comprehensive zoogeographic survey of the region.

## Background & Description
No morphological, meristic, or diagnostic character data are presented in this source. The document is administrative/correspondence in nature and pertains to specimen tracking rather than direct taxonomic description. Morphological descriptions of the two new *Cyprinodon* species referenced are noted as being prepared in a forthcoming separate publication by Miller, Smith, and Minckley.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Geographic Focus:** Northwestern Chihuahua, Mexico, specifically the [[Rio Casas Grandes]] drainage system.
- **Specific Locality:** Arroyo Seco, a tributary stream feeding into the Rio Casas Grandes.
- **Habitat Type:** Isolated desert stream/arroyo systems typical of the [[Chihuahuan Desert]] endemic fish fauna, an ecosystem known for high rates of speciation and endemism in genera such as *Cyprinodon* (pupfishes) and *Catostomus* (suckers).
- **Zoogeographic Context:** The correspondence underscores the importance of relict populations (e.g., *Salmo gairdneri*, a trout species) in isolated Mexican highland drainages for reconstructing regional biogeographic history.

## Key Findings
- Confirms active 1990 collaborative research by **Robert R. Miller**, **Mike Smith**, and **W.L. Minckley** on the ichthyofauna of northwestern Chihuahua.
- The study's primary taxonomic contribution involves the description of **two new species of [[Cyprinodon]]** (pupfish), though descriptions are not included in this letter.
- A specific voucher specimen, **Salmo gairdneri, CAS 20701 (30 specimens)**, collected by Needham and Rupp in May 1955 from Arroyo Seco (tributary to Rio Casas Grandes), was identified as a data gap needing verification for the zoogeographic analysis.
- Acknowledges prior correspondence assistance regarding **[[Catostomus plebeius]]** and networking facilitated with ichthyologist George Barlow.
- Illustrates the collaborative, cross-institutional nature of historical museum-based ichthyological research, relying on the California Academy of Sciences' (CAS) Division of Fishes collection records to support regional faunal surveys.
