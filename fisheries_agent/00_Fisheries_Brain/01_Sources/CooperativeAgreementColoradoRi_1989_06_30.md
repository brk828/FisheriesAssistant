## Key Findings
- The memorandum outlines a **fund transfer protocol** between federal agencies undertaking projects affecting Colorado River fishes and the [[National Fish and Wildlife Foundation]], which acts as a financial intermediary for recovery program mitigation funds.
- Payments must be made via certified check or money order, accompanied by a cover letter specifying: the responsible project, the relevant biological opinion, payment amount, check number, remaining balance due, and any special disbursement conditions.
- The Foundation is required to notify the payee, the authorizing federal agency, and the relevant [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] office within **5 working days** of receiving payment, with a copy sent to Ms. Jeanne Fletcher (Chief of Administration and Support Services, Fish and Wildlife Enhancement, Denver, CO).
- The memo references upcoming discussions on the **investment strategy** for funds received under the agreement, proposed for the **July 13–14, 1989 Management Group meeting** in Denver.
- Mention is made of a subsequent **Implementation Committee meeting on August 30, 1989**, indicating ongoing multi-agency governance of the [[Colorado River Fishes Recovery Program]].
