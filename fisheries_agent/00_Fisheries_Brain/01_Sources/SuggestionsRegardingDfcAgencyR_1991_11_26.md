## Key Findings
Although lacking biological data, the memo contains several notable **procedural and archival recommendations** relevant to the history and operation of the [[Desert Fishes Council]]:

- **Agency Reports Reform**: Hendrickson proposes replacing oral Agency Reports at DFC meetings with **written reports** submitted by the same deadline as technical abstracts, to be compiled into a distributed program. This is intended to improve engagement between "Academic" and "Agency" attendees, who he observed were poorly integrated during oral report sessions.
- **Discussion-Based Format**: Suggests a dedicated 2-hour discussion block (with informal food/drink) where Agency representatives are available for direct Q&A, poster displays, and multimedia presentations rather than podium talks.
- **Travel Support Rationale**: Notes that a designated discussion slot would help Agency personnel justify travel funding to their administrators, equivalent to presenting a technical paper.
- **Proceedings Integration**: Argues this format would improve consistency of Agency Report inclusion in the DFC *Proceedings*, historically inconsistent ("hit and miss").
- **Meeting Logistics Reform**: Proposes earlier deadlines for the Call for Papers (May 1) and abstract submission (September 1) to allow time for producing a combined program of abstracts and reports, foreshadowing use of a new computerized abstract-management system also being piloted for the **1993 [[American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists]] (ASIH)** meeting.
- **Proceedings Indexing Project**: Describes an effort (with [[Paul Marsh]] and [[Bill Berg]]) to construct a cumulative index for DFC *Proceedings* Volumes XVI–XXIII (previous index by Cindy and Jack Williams covered only through Volume XV). Proposes a **collaborative, decentralized database approach**:
  - Common fields: *genus & species*, *author names*, *subject keywords*, *volume*, *page*.
  - Contributors could use varied software (dBase, Lotus, Excel, WordPerfect) as long as output is standard ASCII delimited format for merging.
  - Future *Proceedings* volumes should be designed for **author-supplied keywords** to auto-generate indices going forward.
- **Historical Volume Status**: As of this writing, DFC *Proceedings* Volume 21 was the most recently published, Volume 22 was ready for printing, and Volume 23 corresponded to the current year's (1991) meeting.
