## Executive Summary

This document is a **Mineral Resource Circular** from the University of Texas Bureau of Economic Geology, authored by F. B. Plummer (1945). It is a progress report on **peat deposits** in East Texas, covering the geology, mining methods, classification, and industrial uses of peat from five bogs in Lee, Milam, and Leon counties (Patschke, Ender, Atkinson, Weakley, and Lanier/Long Glade bogs). 

**This source contains no fish science, ichthyological, or marine biology data.** It is a terrestrial/wetland economic geology report focused on peat as a soil amendment resource, its chemical properties (pH, water chemistry), and commercial extraction methods. No fish species, fish habitat characterization, aquatic vertebrate ecology, or fisheries data are discussed anywhere in the text.

## Background & Description

**Not applicable.** No organisms (fish or otherwise) are morphologically described in this text. The document instead classifies **peat types** by physical/chemical structure:
- **Fibrous peat** — light brown, high sphagnum content, porous, pH ~4.5
- **Black colloidal peat** — organic ooze, 3–15% fiber, pH 3.3–5.0
- **Black silty/clayey peat (peaty clay)** — high silt/clay content, pH 3.5–5.5
