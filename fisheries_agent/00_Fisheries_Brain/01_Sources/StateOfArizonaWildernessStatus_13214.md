# Grand Wash Cliffs Wilderness Status Map - Arizona BLM September 1992

#conservation #wilderness-management #land-administration #Arizona

## Executive Summary

This document is a wilderness status map produced by the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) for the State of Arizona in September 1992. It depicts various categories of wilderness and protected lands including designated wilderness areas, wilderness study areas (WSAs), and other federally administered lands. The map provides a comprehensive overview of wilderness designations and administrative classifications across Arizona, with particular focus on areas managed by BLM, USFS (U.S. Forest Service), NPS (National Park Service), and FWS (Fish and Wildlife Service).

## Background & Description

The map uses a standardized legend system to classify different types of protected and managed lands in Arizona. Categories include:
- Designated Wilderness or Primitive Area
- Administratively Endorsed as Suitable (prior to May 1983)
- Wilderness Study Area (WSA) or USFS Congressionally Designated Wilderness Study Area (CDWSA)
- Other Lands Under Agency Administration
- [[Riparian National Conservation Area]] (NCA)
- Indian Reservations
- Military Reservations

The base map was produced by the U.S. Geological Survey. For national forests, the map specifically shows only Congressionally designated wilderness study areas and designated wilderness.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document identifies multiple wilderness study areas across Arizona's diverse landscapes, including mountain ranges and riparian zones. The [[Yuma District]] manages WSAs in the western portion of the state, including areas in the [[Chemehuevi Mountains]], [[Whipple Mountain]], [[Big Maria Mountains]], and [[Little Picacho Peak]]. The [[Safford District]] manages areas in southeastern Arizona, including the [[Peloncillo Mountains]] and [[Apache Box]].

These wilderness areas represent important habitat protection zones that would encompass various aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems, though specific ecological details are not provided in this administrative document.
