## Executive Summary
This 1989 letter from the Cui-ui Recovery Team Leader (Thomas A. Strekal) to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Regional Director formally recommends the immediate establishment of a task force to oversee operations at the [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]] during the upcoming 1990 spawning run of the [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*). The core concern driving the letter is suspected **hybridization** between cui-ui and [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]] (or another sympatric catostomid/salmonid), which preliminary findings from fish taxonomists and cui-ui researchers suggest is already occurring. An electrophoretic survey to confirm hybridization was underway but results were not expected until after the critical spawning-season planning window, prompting an urgent, precautionary management response.

## Background & Description
The letter does not provide direct morphometric data on the cui-ui, but it references key population/genetic identity markers relevant to species integrity:
- The **1969 year class** of cui-ui is identified by researchers as "genetically pure," implying that later cohorts may show introgressed or hybridized genetic/morphological characteristics distinguishable via electrophoretic (allozyme) analysis.
- Hybrid individuals within the spawning run are referred to as "putative hybrids," indicating that morphological or genetic screening was expected to be used to visually or biochemically distinguish hybrids from pure-strain [[Cui-ui]] during trapping operations.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat**: [[Pyramid Lake]] (terminal desert lake, Nevada) and its primary tributary, the [[Truckee River]], particularly the river delta where spawning migration occurs.
- **Life history context**: Cui-ui are lake-dwelling, migratory spawners that ascend the Truckee River delta to reproduce; this run is intercepted by the [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]] fishway/dam structure.
- **Sympatric species of concern**: [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]], a co-occurring salmonid in the same river system, implicated in the suspected hybridization event.
- **Threat vector**: Upriver migration through the Truckee River delta creates opportunity for interbreeding at spawning grounds, motivating proposed physical interdiction (e.g., electric fencing) to control access.

## Key Findings
- **Suspected Hybridization Crisis**: Preliminary assessments by cui-ui researchers and fish taxonomists indicate that hybridization has already occurred between [[Cui-ui]] and another species (implied: [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]]), threatening genomic integrity of the species via introgression.
- **Pending Confirmation**: A Reno Office–funded electrophoretic survey was commissioned to scientifically confirm or refute hybridization, with results expected no earlier than December 1, 1989 — after critical 1990 spawning-season planning deadlines.
- **Genetically Pure Reference Cohort**: The **1969 year class** of cui-ui was identified as the reference "genetically pure" population, to be prioritized for upstream passage or hatchery propagation.
- **Recommended Interdiction Strategy**: To halt introgression pending full understanding of hybridization mechanisms and extent, the Recovery Team proposed an aggressive precautionary strategy for the 1990 spawning run, including:
  - Installing an **electric fence** in the Truckee River delta to block upriver migration of potential spawners.
  - **Trapping and selectively passing** only 1969-year-class cui-ui and Lahontan cutthroat trout for spawning (upstream release or hatchery use).
  - **Culling putative hybrids** identified within the spawning run.
- **Institutional Recommendation**: Formation of a dedicated task force (including the full [[Cui-ui Recovery Team]]) to directly oversee and evaluate Marble Bluff Fish Facility operations throughout the 1990 spawning run and subsequent outmigration period.
- **Conservation Urgency**: The letter explicitly frames continued genomic introgression as an existential threat to the species, underscoring the time-sensitive nature of the recommended management intervention.
