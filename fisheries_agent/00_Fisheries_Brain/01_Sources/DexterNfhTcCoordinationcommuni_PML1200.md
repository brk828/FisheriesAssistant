# Dexter NFH Memorandum on F-6 Fish Status and Inter-Office Coordination (1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #hatchery-management

## Executive Summary

This March 31, 1997 memorandum from the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] to the [[Region 2]] Regional Director in [[Albuquerque New Mexico]] addresses communication and coordination problems between hatchery staff and regional office personnel. The main subject is the status of the [[F-6 Fish]] and the handling of fish moved between basins. The scan is heavily degraded (OCR), so many passages are uncertain. The memo is primarily administrative correspondence and contains little quantitative data.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Internal federal fish-hatchery correspondence (memorandum), signed by [[Buddy T. Jensen]].
- **Stated purpose:** To clarify communication channels and responsibilities regarding the F-6 fish, and to record past meetings and phone contacts on the matter.
- **Participants referenced:** Hatchery staff (including a named Dexter staff member), regional office personnel, and an unnamed regional representative. Several individuals are named, but OCR corruption makes most names unreliable.
- **Species and taxonomy:** The memo refers only to "F-6 fish." No scientific name, life stage, or count is legible in the scan.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Basins:** The memo distinguishes between the [[Upper Basin]] and the [[Lower Basin]]. It states that fish were moved between these basins, and that upper-basin facilities may have different resources, including staff and equipment, than the lower basin.
- **Fish health concern:** The author refers to fish from an "infected" area and to the need for disease control. A passage implies that fish were brought into a facility and that a disease or contamination risk was identified. Its exact nature is not legible. This is discussed under [[Fish Health]].
- **Transfers and stocking:** The memo mentions bringing fish into a facility and possibly returning them. The text does not give numbers, locations, or dates for these movements.

## Key Findings

1. **Communication gaps:** Phone contact between the hatchery and the regional office was frequent, but several key facts about the F-6 fish were not shared. The author notes that the regional office was unaware of certain details, which the memo treats as a coordination failure.
2. **Annual coordination meeting:** The author reports that an [[Annual Coordination Meeting]] was held in both regions, with a stated purpose of improving communication. The scan does not show its date or attendees clearly.
3. **Disease-related precautions:** The memo indicates that fish from a suspect or infected source were handled cautiously and that a fish-health concern led to a decision about whether to proceed with transfers.
4. **Resource disparity:** The author notes that upper-basin facilities may lack resources available in other parts of the system, which affects how they can respond to such problems.
5. **Documentation and follow-up:** The author states that a full account of the fish status will be prepared and that the regional office should be informed before further action. This is the memo's main request.
6. **Limitations:** The scan is badly degraded. Quantitative data, species identity, and exact decisions cannot be reliably extracted. Any use of this document should be checked against the original.
