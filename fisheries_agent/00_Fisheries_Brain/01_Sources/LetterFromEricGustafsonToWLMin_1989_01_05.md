## Executive Summary
This document is a personal letter from Eric S. Gustafson to Dr. W.L. Minckley (Arizona State University), dated January 5, 1989. While primarily social in nature, the letter contains substantive references to ongoing conservation research on two endangered Colorado River Basin fishes: the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) and the [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*). Gustafson references his own prior contribution to research on the early life history of *X. texanus*, co-published with Minckley, and expresses interest in participating in future [[Lake Mohave]] recovery operations, including an annual bonytail chub recovery effort conducted over ASU's spring break. The letter also cites two key contemporary publications on razorback sucker status and recovery.
