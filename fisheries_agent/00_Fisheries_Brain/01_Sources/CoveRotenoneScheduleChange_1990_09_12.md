## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from the Arizona Game and Fish Department (Kingman Region) to Dr. Wendell Minckley (Arizona State University) notifying him of a schedule change for a cove rotenone renovation project on [[Lake Mohave]]. The original action, planned as part of conservation efforts for the endangered [[Razorback Sucker]], was postponed due to an unscheduled rise in lake surface elevation. The letter does not contain morphological, physiological, or ecological research data; rather, it documents fisheries management logistics related to habitat preparation for a native fish reintroduction/stocking program.

- **Management context:** The backwater was slated for renovation via piscicide (rotenone) treatment to remove existing fish community (likely nonnative competitors/predators) prior to reintroduction of native [[Razorback Sucker]] stock.

- **Revised schedule:** Due to an unscheduled rise in [[Lake Mohave]] surface elevation, rotenone application was postponed to **Tuesday, October 23, 1990**, with fish removal occurring **October 24–25, 1990** (as needed).
- **Purpose of treatment:** The backwater cove near Yuma Cove was treated with [[Rotenone]] to prepare the site for stocking of adult [[Razorback Sucker]], a federally recognized imperiled catostomid native to the Colorado River system.
