## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter, not a primary research paper, sent from researchers at Arizona State University's Department of Zoology (Thomas E. Dowling and W.L. Minckley) to Mr. Larry Crist of the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation. The letter requests confirmation of a **no-cost extension** to Cooperative Agreement #0-FC-40-09530, originally set to expire January 1, 1993. The underlying research project concerns the analysis of **mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) variation** in populations of the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*), with samples drawn from **Lake Mead**. No morphological, ecological, or population genetic *results* are presented in this text; it solely documents project administration and intent.
