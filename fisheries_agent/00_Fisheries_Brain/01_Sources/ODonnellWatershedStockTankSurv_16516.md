## Executive Summary

This inter-office memo from the Arizona Game and Fish Department documents a 1998 stock tank survey conducted in the [[O'Donnell Creek]] and [[Turkey Creek]] watersheds of Region V, Arizona. Of the stock tanks surveyed, only two contained water at the time of survey, both located in the Turkey Creek drainage. Both water-holding tanks contained established populations of **non-native** [[Largemouth Bass]] and/or [[Bluegill Sunfish]] (family [[Centrarchidae]]), raising concerns about introduced species in a watershed likely relevant to native fish conservation (e.g., desert fish species typical of this region). Several additional tanks were identified but not surveyed due to being reported dry or due to access constraints.

## Background & Description

No detailed morphological data were recorded in this source; the memo functions as a field survey/population log rather than a taxonomic or anatomical study. The only quantitative size-related data reported pertains to length-class thresholds for sampled [[Bluegill Sunfish]]:

- **Bluegill Sunfish (Tank SC35A006):** 38 individuals sampled, all **<100 mm** in length (juvenile/small size class).
- **Bluegill Sunfish (Tank SC35A007):** 90 individuals sampled, all **<100 mm** in length.
- **Largemouth Bass (Tank SC35A006):** 100 individuals sampled, all classified as **young-of-the-year (YOY)**; no adult largemouth bass were captured in either seine haul.

The absence of adult largemouth bass alongside abundant YOY individuals suggests either recent recruitment/spawning events, differential catchability of adults via seine methodology, or adult mortality/emigration between spawning and sampling.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat type:** Small, artificial livestock/stock tanks (impoundments) within semi-arid watershed drainages in southeastern Arizona.
- **Hydrological status:** The majority of surveyed tanks (all but two) were **dry** at time of survey, reflecting the ephemeral nature of these water bodies and likely seasonal/drought-driven water availability — a critical constraint for any fish population persistence in this system.
- **Species co-occurrence:** [[Largemouth Bass]] and [[Bluegill Sunfish]] co-occurred in Tank SC35A006, representing a predator-prey/competitive assemblage typical of introduced warm-water sport fish communities in the southwestern U.S.
- **Isolated occurrence:** Tank SC35A007 contained only [[Bluegill Sunfish]], with no evidence of largemouth bass.
- **Non-native status:** Both species are explicitly noted as **non-native** to the O'Donnell/Turkey Creek watershed, indicating anthropogenic introduction (likely via stocking for recreational fishing in stock tanks) rather than natural range expansion. This is ecologically significant given potential impacts on native aquatic fauna in Arizona desert stream systems.
- **Land ownership/access context:** Several unsurveyed tanks are located on [[USFS]] (U.S. Forest Service) land, with some requiring crossing private property for access, which is relevant to future monitoring logistics.

## Key Findings

1. **Only 2 of the surveyed stock tanks held water** during the summer 1998 survey; all others in the O'Donnell Creek watershed were dry.
2. **Both water-holding tanks were located in the Turkey Creek drainage**, not O'Donnell Creek proper.
3. **Tank SC35A006** contained both [[Largemouth Bass]] (100 YOY, 0 adults via 2 seine hauls) and [[Bluegill Sunfish]] (38 individuals, all <100 mm).
4. **Tank SC35A007** contained only [[Bluegill Sunfish]] (90 individuals, all <100 mm) — no largemouth bass detected.
5. Both fish species present are **non-native introductions**, a notable finding for regional fisheries/conservation management given potential competitive or predatory pressure on native aquatic species.
6. Four additional tanks (**SC35A016 "New Tank"**, **SC35A017**, **SC35A018**, **SC35A019**) were not surveyed:
   - SC35A016 and SC35A017 (T22S R17E, Sections 13 and 12 respectively) reported dry by USFS District Wildlife Manager [[John Millican]].
   - SC35A018 (T22S R18E Section 33) has private-property access but is USFS land.
   - SC35A019 (T22S R18E Section 21) is located in the Turkey Creek drainage.
7. The author recommends **follow-up coordination with USFS personnel** to confirm no unmapped/undocumented tanks exist in the survey area.
8. The survey methodology relied on **seine haul sampling**, with two hauls performed per water-holding tank.
