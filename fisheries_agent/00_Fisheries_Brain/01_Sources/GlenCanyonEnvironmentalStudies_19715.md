# Glen Canyon Environmental Studies - Little Colorado River Scientific Coordination Meeting Memorandum

#administrative #environmental-studies #interagency-coordination

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from the [[Bureau of Reclamation]]'s [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] program addresses coordination issues regarding permits and data acquisition for research on the [[Little Colorado River]]. The document schedules a meeting among principal investigators and agency representatives to resolve technical conflicts impacting the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]'s ability to complete contractual responsibilities for Little Colorado River studies.

## Background & Description

The memorandum, dated May 19, 1992, was issued by Dave Wegner from the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies office in Flagstaff, Arizona. It was addressed to multiple principal investigators and agency representatives including Dr. Stuart Leon and Dr. Owen Gorman ([[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]), Dr. Paul Marsh and Dr. Michael Douglas ([[Arizona State University]]), Dr. Duncan Patten ([[Arizona State University]]), and Mr. Robert Williams ([[Bureau of Reclamation]]).

The document indicates that coordination issues first addressed in a January meeting had not been adequately resolved despite an agreement among principals to coordinate with each other. The memorandum represents an attempt to resolve technical issues at the working level before escalating to Regional Office intervention.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[Little Colorado River]] is a tributary system requiring coordinated scientific study as part of the broader [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] program. Multiple research teams from different institutions were conducting studies in this system, necessitating coordination of permits, data collection, and field activities. The river system's management involves multiple federal and state agencies with overlapping jurisdictions and responsibilities.

## Key Findings

- The [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] identified several significant points impacting their ability to complete contractual responsibilities for [[Little Colorado River]] research
- Previous coordination agreements established in January 1992 had not resolved ongoing conflicts between research teams
- A coordination meeting was scheduled for May 27, 1992, at 3:00-5:00 PM at Sky Harbor Airport (Terminal 4, America West Phoenix Club - Hopi Room)
- The meeting objective was for Principal Investigators of two Little Colorado River studies to develop an agreement to accomplish the work
- The memorandum warned that failure to resolve coordination issues at the technical level could result in none of the work moving forward
- Issues would be escalated to Regional Office level if technical-level resolution proved impossible
- The document emphasized that resolution at the working level was "in the best interest of the resources"
