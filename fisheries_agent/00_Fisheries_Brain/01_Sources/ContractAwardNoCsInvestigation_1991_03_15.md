## Executive Summary
This document is **not a primary scientific research paper** but an administrative contract award letter from the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation (Upper Colorado Regional Office) dated March 15, 1991. It notifies Dr. W. L. Minckley (Arizona State University) that his proposal for "Schedule II, Protein Electrophoresis" under Contract Award No. 1-CS-40-09700 — titled *"Investigations of Genetic and Morphological Variation in the Cyprinid Fish Genus [[Gila]] Throughout the Colorado River"* — was not selected. The contract was instead awarded to **Genetic Analyses, Inc.** (Smithville, Texas) for line items B.1 and B.4, covering protein electrophoresis work and two coordination meetings in Denver, valued at $179,238. No morphological, ecological, or genetic data on *Gila* species are presented in this letter itself; it only documents the administrative and funding context for a planned study.
