# USFWS Endangered Species Research Permit Authorization Letter

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #endangered-species #southwestern-fishes

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service document authorizes Dr. W.L. Minckley and six colleagues at [[Arizona State University]] as subpermittees under blanket permit PRT-676811 to conduct scientific research and species recovery activities with 16 endangered and threatened fish species native to the southwestern United States. The authorization extends through December 1988 and covers activities including taking, holding, maintaining, and propagating listed species within USFWS Region 2 and adjacent states.

## Background & Description

The authorization letter, dated February 22, 1988, designates seven researchers as subpermittees: Dr. W.L. Minckley (Associate Professor of Zoology, Arizona State University), Dr. Paul Marsh, Dr. David Galet, Ms. Caryl M. Williams, Mr. Kirk Young, Mr. Bruce DeMarais, and Dr. Michael Douglas. The permit operates under the authority of 16 USC 1539(a) and 16 USC 1533(d), with regulations specified in 50 CFR 17.22 and 50 CFR 17.32.

The covered species represent multiple families of southwestern native fishes:

**Cyprinidae (Minnows and Chubs):**
- [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*)
- [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*)
- [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*)
- [[Chihuahua Chub]] (*Gila nigrescens*)
- [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*)
- [[Beautiful Shiner]] (*Notropis formosus*)
- [[Woundfin]] (*Plagopterus argentissimus*)
- [[Spikedace]] (*Meda fulgida*)
- [[Little Colorado Spinedace]] (*Lepidomeda vittata*)
- [[Loach Minnow]] (*Tiaroga cobitis*)
- [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*)

**Poeciliidae (Livebearers):**
- [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*)
- [[Sonoran Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*)

**Salmonidae (Trout):**
- [[Apache Trout]] (*Salmo apache*)
- [[Gila Trout]] (*Salmo gilae*)

**Ictaluridae (Catfishes):**
- [[Yaqui Catfish]] (*Ictalurus pricei*)

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The permit addresses species endemic to or characteristic of the southwestern United States aquatic ecosystems, particularly within [[USFWS Region 2]] (covering Arizona, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Texas). These species represent critical components of desert and montane stream ecosystems that have experienced significant habitat degradation and population declines, necessitating federal protection under the [[Endangered Species Act]].

The authorization specifically allows activities within USFWS Region 2 and adjacent states upon concurrence from appropriate state and FWS regional authorities, recognizing the interstate nature of many southwestern watersheds. Import and export activities are permitted through any port listed in 50 CFR 14.12 or 14.16.

## Key Findings

**Authorized Activities:**
- Take, hold, maintain, and propagate listed species for scientific research and species recovery purposes
- Actions necessary to recover specimens that would otherwise be lost due to natural processes or circumstances beyond the permittee's control
- Actions designated in Service Program Advice
- Actions outlined in approved recovery plans

**Permit Conditions:**
- Valid only when used in combination with a valid state permit
- Requires possession of authorization letter and permit copy during field activities
- Subject to general conditions in 50 CFR 13 Subpart D and specific conditions in cited federal regulations
- Must comply with all applicable foreign, state, local, and federal laws
- Any CITES-listed species importation/exportation requires appropriate CITES documentation

**Reporting Requirements:**
- First annual report due December 31, 1988
- Activity reports must be submitted to U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Endangered Species Office, P.O. Box 1306, Albuquerque, NM 87103
- Renewal request (if appropriate) must be submitted to Regional Director by December 1, 1988

**Administrative Details:**
- Permit effective date: December 23, 1987
- Expiration date: December 31, 1989
- Regional Director: Michael Spear
- Permit type: Endangered/Threatened Species
- Copies distributed to Arizona Game and Fish Department, Assistant Regional Directors for Law Enforcement (Regions 1 and 2), Office of Management Authority, and Regional Directors (Regions 1 and 6)
