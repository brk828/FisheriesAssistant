# Aravaipa Creek Monitoring Data, July 1991

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This interagency memorandum, dated 16 July 1991, transmits fish and habitat sampling data from [[Aravaipa Creek]] in Graham and Pinal counties, Arizona. Sampling took place on 12–14 July 1991 at six reaches: three upstream and three downstream of the [[Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness Area]]. The memo was prepared by Arizona State University (Department of Zoology) and circulated to the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], [[The Nature Conservancy]], the [[Bureau of Land Management]], the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]. The memo also schedules a walkthrough for 9–11 August 1991 and asks for additional personnel and equipment. The species catch table is garbled in the source, so per-species counts cannot be recovered.

## Background & Description

The memo sets out the sampling design and the species codes used in the data.

- **Study area:** Six 200-m reaches. The east (upstream) end had Station 1 (gauge station area to the exclosure fence), Station 2 (Chimney Rock) and Station 3 (proposed barrier at the guest house). The west (downstream) end had Station 1 (gauge station), Station 2 (White's Ranch) and Station 3 (the "sycamore tree" reach).
- **Gear:** Mainstream channels were seined for 200 m with a 1/8-inch mesh, 6-ft seine, and a 1/4-inch, 15-ft seine when needed. Side channels and margins were sampled with a 1/16-inch mesh dipnet.
- **Collectors:** Velasco, Reimus, Simms, Elroy Masters (BLM) and Lisa Schmoetzer (ASU), with assistance from Coilaza (west end) and Ed Brunson (TNC, east end).
- **Species codes:** Ten native-range cyprinid, catostomid and related taxa and five further taxa are coded, as listed in the Key Findings.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Habitat was recorded as the percentage of each reach in run, riffle or pool, with substrate composition for each type. Mean width and depth were also recorded.

**East (upstream) end:**
- **Station 1:** 50% run (mean width 7 m, depth 0.25 m); 45% riffle (8 m, 0.20 m); 5% pool (2 m, 0.50 m). Substrates are mostly cobble, rubble, gravel and sand.
- **Station 2:** 55% run (6 m, 0.25 m); 45% riffle (depth 0.20 m). Substrates include rock, cobble, rubble, gravel and sand.
- **Station 3:** 55% riffle (7 m, 0.25 m); 45% run (6 m, 0.20 m). Sand and gravel are the most common substrates.

**West (downstream) end:**
- **Station 1:** 65% riffle (mean width 13 m, depth 0.15 m); 35% run (12 m, 0.20 m). Boulder, rock, cobble and sand are present.
- **Station 2:** 100% riffle (10 m, 0.25 m). Boulder, rock, cobble and rubble dominate; gravel and sand are minor.
- **Station 3:** 75% run (7 m, 0.20 m); 20% riffle (5 m, 0.15 m); 5% pool (7 m, 0.15 m). Sand is dominant in the run and pool; cobble and rock dominate the riffle.

Water was slightly turbid at the west end on the morning of sampling, probably from light overnight showers. Several leopard frogs were collected, photographed and released at two west-end locations.

## Key Findings

- **Survey coverage:** Six reaches were sampled over 12–14 July 1991, three on each side of the wilderness boundary.
- **Habitat contrast:** The east-end reaches are more run-dominated and carry more gravel and sand. The west-end reaches are wider and more riffle-dominated, with coarser substrates (boulder, rock, cobble) at Stations 1 and 2.
- **Species recorded:** The memo codes 15 taxa: [[Longfin Dace]] (AGCH), [[Speckled Dace]] (RHOS), [[Spikedace]] (MEFU), [[Roundtail Chub]] (GIRO), [[Loach Minnow]] (TICO), *Pantosteus clarki* (PACL), [[Sonora Sucker]] (CAIN), [[Common Carp]] (CYCA), [[Red Shiner]] (CYLU), [[Fathead Minnow]] (PIPR), [[Black Bullhead]] (AMME), [[Yellow Bullhead]] (ANNA), [[Western Mosquitofish]] (GAAF), [[Green Sunfish]] (LECY) and [[Largemouth Bass]] (MISA). The results table is corrupted in the source, so per-site counts and percentages are unavailable.
- **Amphibians:** Leopard frogs were collected, photographed and released at two west-end sites. No counts are given.
- **Planned work:** A walkthrough is set for 9–11 August 1991. Sites inside the canyon will be marked inconspicuously then. Outside-canyon reaches were already measured and flagged.
- **Barrier planning:** The guest-house reach was moved slightly upstream in anticipation of a proposed barrier installation.
- **Request:** Additional personnel and equipment, especially backpack electrofishing units (backpack shockers), were requested, with RSVP to Dr. Minckley or Tony Velasco.
