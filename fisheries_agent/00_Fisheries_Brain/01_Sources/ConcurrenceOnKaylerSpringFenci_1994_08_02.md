## Executive Summary
This document is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) concurrence letter, not a primary research study, addressing a Section 7 Endangered Species Act consultation between the FWS and the U.S. Forest Service (Tonto National Forest, Tonto Basin Ranger District). The subject is a proposed fencing project at **Kayler Spring**, Gila County, Arizona, intended to protect habitat for the endangered [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*). The FWS concurred with the Forest Service's determination that the fencing project "is not likely to adversely affect" the species, concluding that all anticipated effects would be beneficial. The letter emphasizes the ecological significance of the Kayler Spring population as one of the longest-persisting reintroduced populations of this endangered fish, underscoring its importance to species recovery efforts.
