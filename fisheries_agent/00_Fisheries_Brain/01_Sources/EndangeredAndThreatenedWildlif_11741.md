# Endangered and Threatened Species Review of Plant and Animal Taxa 1997

#endangered-species #conservation #federal-register #USFWS #candidate-review

## Executive Summary

This Federal Register notice, published September 19, 1997, by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service presents an updated list of plant and animal taxa native to the United States that are candidates or proposed for listing as endangered or threatened under the Endangered Species Act of 1973, as amended. The document identifies 207 candidate taxa and 99 proposed taxa, provides findings on 18 recycled petitions (all receiving continued "warranted but precluded" determinations), and describes listing progress from February 28, 1996 to September 3, 1997. This comprehensive review supersedes all previous animal, plant, and combined notices of review and serves to provide advance notice to environmental planners and resource managers of potential future listings.

## Background & Description

The Service maintains candidate lists to provide advance knowledge of potential listings affecting environmental planning decisions, solicit input on taxa that may or may not require protection, and gather information to prioritize listing actions. Taxa are classified into two main categories: **Candidate taxa** (C) have sufficient information on file to support proposed listing rules, but issuance is precluded by other listing activities; **Proposed taxa** (PE for endangered, PT for threatened) have published proposed rules pending final determination. Taxa marked C(w) are candidates with recycled "warranted but precluded" petition findings requiring annual review.

The notice assigns listing priority numbers based on the September 21, 1983 priority system (48 FR 43098) and December 5, 1996 Fiscal Year 1997 guidelines (61 FR 64475), which consider immediacy and magnitude of threats plus taxonomic status. Regional offices (Regions 1-7) have lead responsibility for specific taxa based on geographic distribution. The document requests additional status information, threat documentation, critical habitat recommendations, and taxonomic updates from the public and scientific community.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The reviewed taxa span diverse ecosystems across U.S. states, territories, and possessions including [[American Samoa]], [[Guam]], [[Northern Mariana Islands]], [[Puerto Rico]], and the [[U.S. Virgin Islands]]. Aquatic taxa include multiple fish species such as [[Catostomus santaanae]] ([[Santa Ana Sucker]]), [[Cyprinodon pecosensis]] ([[Pecos Pupfish]]), [[Salvelinus confluentus]] ([[Bull Trout]]) with distinct Columbia River and Klamath River populations, and [[Oncorhynchus mykiss]] subspecies ([[McCloud River Redband Trout]]). 

Freshwater mollusks include numerous unionid mussels (Family [[Unionidae]]) such as [[Amblema neisleni]], [[Elliptio chipolaensis]], and [[Pleurobema pyriforme]], plus hydrobiid springsnails (Family [[Hydrobiidae]]) including multiple [[Pyrgulopsis]] species. Amphibians feature [[Rana chiricahuensis]] ([[Chiricahua Leopard Frog]]), [[Rana luteiventris]] ([[Columbia Spotted Frog]]) populations, and [[Ambystoma californiense]] ([[California Tiger Salamander]]). The document addresses habitat threats, population declines, and conservation needs across terrestrial, freshwater, and marine environments.

## Key Findings

**Listing Progress (February 28, 1996 - September 3, 1997):**
- 152 taxa received final listing actions
- 23 taxa received proposed listing actions  
- 1 taxon received proposed delisting action
- 13 proposed rules were withdrawn
- The relatively small number of new proposals (23) resulted from priority guidance emphasizing resolution of outstanding proposed listings following the April 26, 1996 lifting of the listing moratorium

**Current Status Summary:**
- **Proposed for Endangered:** 51 plants, 20 animals
- **Proposed for Threatened:** 20 plants, 8 animals  
- **Candidates:** 107 plants, 100 animals
- **Total candidate and proposed taxa:** 306

**Recycled Petitions:** All 18 recycled petitions due during the review period received continued "warranted but precluded" findings, maintaining candidate status for these taxa.

**Taxa Removed from Candidate/Proposed Status:** 181 taxa previously classified as candidates or proposed in the 1996 notice were removed for various reasons including: listing as endangered (E) or threatened (T), insufficient information (I), more abundant than believed (A), taxonomic issues (N), believed extinct (X), mistaken inclusion (M), or range no longer in U.S. territory (F).

**Aquatic Taxa Highlights:**
- Multiple [[Salvelinus confluentus]] populations listed separately by river system
- [[Scaphirhynchus suttkusi]] ([[Alabama Sturgeon]]) as candidate
- Numerous springsnail species ([[Pyrgulopsis]] spp.) in southwestern states
- Freshwater mussels concentrated in southeastern river systems
- [[Macrhybopsis gelida]] and [[Macrhybopsis meeki]] (sturgeon chubs) as candidates

**Priority Guidance:** The Service clarified processing order as: (1) emergency situations (highest), (2) resolving outstanding proposed listings, (3) processing new proposals and petition findings, (4) critical habitat, delistings, and reclassifications (lowest priority).
