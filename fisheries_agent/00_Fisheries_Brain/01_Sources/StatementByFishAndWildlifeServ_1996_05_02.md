## Executive Summary
This document is a press statement issued by the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]], attributed to Director [[Mollie Beattie]], announcing the lifting of a Congressional moratorium on the listing of endangered and threatened species. The moratorium had been in effect since April 1995. President [[Bill Clinton]] exercised executive authority to waive this provision within a recently passed omnibus appropriations law, allowing the [[Endangered Species Act]] listing program to resume. ## Key Findings
- The Congressional moratorium on the listing of endangered and threatened species was first imposed in **April 1995**.
- The [[Department Of The Interior]] "strongly opposed" this moratorium from its inception.
- President Clinton waived the moratorium provision via authority granted under a recently passed omnibus appropriations law.
- The [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] announced plans to:
  - Reassign or rehire staff displaced during the moratorium.
  - Restart listing processes that had been inactive for over a year.
  - Assess potential changes in species status that occurred during the shutdown period.
  - Consider the need for additional scientific and public input before proceeding.
- Detailed operational plans for resuming the listing program were expected to be announced **no later than mid-May 1996**.
