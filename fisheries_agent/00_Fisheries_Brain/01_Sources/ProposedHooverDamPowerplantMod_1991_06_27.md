# Proposed Hoover Dam Powerplant Modification Project

## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative memorandum from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS), Ecological Services Division (Phoenix, Arizona), dated June 27, 1991. It does not contain primary scientific data, morphological descriptions, or ecological findings on fish species. Instead, it serves as a **meeting notice** convening the **Native Fish Work Group** to conduct a technical review of a Biological Assessment (BA) prepared by the Bureau of Reclamation regarding the [[Hoover Dam]] Powerplant Modification Project. The memo is procedurally significant because it documents interagency coordination under likely Endangered Species Act consultation processes concerning native fish conservation in the [[Lower Colorado River]] and [[Lake Mead]] ecosystem.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Geographic scope**: [[Colorado River]] downstream of [[Hoover Dam]], extending through [[Lake Mead National Recreation Area]].
- **Habitat alteration concern**: The proposed powerplant modification project implies potential changes to dam operations (e.g., flow regime, water release patterns), which can affect downstream riverine and reservoir habitat conditions relevant to native fish populations.
- **Institutional ecology**: The Native Fish Work Group functions as a multi-agency body coordinating habitat and species protection efforts across jurisdictions including the [[Bureau of Reclamation]], [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], [[Nevada Department of Wildlife]], [[Lake Mead National Recreation Area]], and academic partners (Arizona State University, Center for Environmental Study).

## Key Findings
- The [[Bureau of Reclamation]] submitted a **Biological Assessment (BA)** to the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] concerning the **Hoover Dam Powerplant Modification Project**, triggering a technical (not full agency) review process.
- A meeting of the **Native Fish Work Group** was scheduled for July 22–23, 1991, at the FWS Ecological Services office in Phoenix, Arizona, with July 23 dedicated exclusively to review of the Hoover Dam BA.
- The Native Fish Work Group was recognized by FWS as the appropriate existing body for this review, given its prior engagement with native fish issues in the project area (Colorado River / Lake Mead system).
- Key individuals and institutions involved include:
  - [[Gilbert D. Metz]] (Acting Field Supervisor, FWS Ecological Services, Phoenix)
  - [[Paul Marsh]] (Center for Environmental Study, Arizona State University)
  - [[Wendell Minckley]] (Department of Zoology, Arizona State University) — a noted expert on southwestern native fishes
  - [[Kent Turner]] (Lake Mead National Recreation Area)
  - [[Jon Sjoberg]] (Nevada Department of Wildlife)
  - [[Gordon Mueller]] (Bureau of Reclamation, Denver)
  - Multiple representatives from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]
