## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative correspondence rather than a primary data source, dated 1 December 1987, from Dr. Mark R. Stromberg (Resident Director, National Audubon Society's Appleton-Whittell Research Ranch Sanctuary) to Dr. W. L. Minckley of Arizona State University's Department of Zoology. The letter requests Dr. Minckley's written support to facilitate collection permits for a small number of [[Desert Pupfish]] individuals, intended for an unspecified research project. The correspondence references coordination with the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]], the National Park Service, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS), indicating an interagency permitting process typical of endangered desert fish conservation efforts in the American Southwest during the late 1980s.
