# Handwritten Fish Survey Field Record (Largely Illegible Scan)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-survey-record

## Executive Summary

This document is a set of scanned handwritten field sheets, apparently from a fish sampling or survey effort. Optical character recognition (OCR) has failed on most of the content, so the species, sample sizes, measurements and conclusions cannot be reliably reconstructed. The only partly legible place name is "Lake Mohave, Az," which suggests a [[Lake Mohave]] (Arizona) reservoir setting. No figures in this note should be treated as verified data.

## Background & Description

The six pages contain columns of numbers, abbreviations and short phrases, most of which are corrupted by the scan. Page 1 has a column of paired values, apparently a count and a decimal measurement, with a total near the bottom. Page 2 is a long tabulated sheet with a three-digit column (possibly lengths in millimetres) and a second column of single- or double-digit decimal values (possibly weights in kilograms or pounds). Page 3 contains handwritten notes, including the place name and a phrase that may read "eyes clear." Pages 4 to 6 contain more tabular entries, date references (possibly November and a "1/26" date), and fragments of what may be a list of sampling sites or capture methods.

Because the handwriting and print are degraded, the layout of the columns, the headers and the units could not be confirmed.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

If the location reading is correct, the material relates to a warm-water reservoir on the lower [[Colorado River]] system, where [[Field Sampling]] for fish populations is commonly carried out. However, the scan does not confirm this. The following points cannot be verified from the text:

- Water chemistry, temperature and habitat conditions
- Sampling gear and methods
- Other aquatic taxa (invertebrates, plankton)
- Season and survey period, beyond possible November dates

## Key Findings

1. **Document type:** A handwritten field or data sheet set, not a published study or formal report.
2. **Legibility:** OCR has failed on most pages. Species names, counts, measurement units and headers cannot be recovered with confidence.
3. **Location:** The only partly legible place reference is "Lake Mohave, Az," which is treated here as a probable, not confirmed, study site.
4. **Numerical content:** There are many numeric columns (apparently counts, lengths and weights). Their values are too corrupted to report exactly, and no statistics are given in this note.
5. **Species:** No species name is legible. The species fields are therefore recorded as N/A.
6. **Recommendation:** Rescan the originals at higher resolution, or transcribe them manually, before any data are extracted. Confirm the headers, units and species codes first.
