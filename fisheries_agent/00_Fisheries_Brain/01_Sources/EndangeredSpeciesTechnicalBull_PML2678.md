# Endangered Species Technical Bulletin: 1991 Index (Vol. XVI)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae #salmonidae #percidae

## Executive Summary

This document is the 1991 annual index (Volume XVI) of the [[Endangered Species Technical Bulletin]], published by the U.S. Department of the Interior, Fish and Wildlife Service. It is not a research paper. It is a dated subject and species index that points to bulletin items on listings, recovery plans, captive propagation, habitat protection, and policy. Entries are alphabetical by common or scientific name, followed by the issue month and day. Most entries cover birds, mammals, and plants. The aquatic material is concentrated in fishes, with additional entries for freshwater mussels, snails, shrimp, and one aquatic mammal. The bulletin's own closing page is a mailing label, so it adds no content.

## Background & Description

The index covers the federal [[Endangered Species Act]] listing process and its implementation. Entries use standard status terms: *final E* (listed as endangered), *final T* (listed as threatened), and *proposed E* or *proposed T* (under review for listing). The index also records the [[Critical Habitat]] concept, the [[Convention On International Trade In Endangered Species]] (CITES) trade controls, and the status of recovery plans. The fish entries below are summarized by taxon. Full species checklists are not reproduced.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Aquatic entries show recurring pressures on freshwater systems:

- **Water management and hydrology:** The Klamath water "jeopardy relief" for two suckers (Lost River and shortnose) on Sep 2, drought-forced relocation of Warner Sucker (May 11), and Sacramento River winter-run protection for [[Chinook Salmon]] (Jun 2; Sep 2-3). Negotiations on the Lower Mokelumne Hydroelectric Project are listed on Sep 3.
- **Contamination and spills:** A herbicide spill in the Sacramento River (Jul 2-3) threatened winter-run Chinook salmon. Pesticide regulation is also covered, including EPA consultation (May 15) and the end of carbofuran production (Jun 10-11).
- **Habitat loss and captive propagation:** Several fishes are managed through captive rearing or stocking. Examples include captive-rearing projects for the [[Smoky Madtom]] and [[Yellowfin Madtom]] (May 13), captive propagation for the [[Pallid Sturgeon]] (Jul 12-15), and breeding and stocking of the [[June Sucker]] in Utah (May 14).

## Key Findings

**Fish entries in the index (condensed):**

- **Sturgeons:** [[Gulf Sturgeon]] reclassified as *final T* (Sep 10). [[Pallid Sturgeon]] captive propagation program with Louisiana capture (Jul 12-15).
- **Suckers:** [[Lost River Sucker]] and [[Shortnose Sucker]] both covered under Klamath jeopardy relief (Sep 2). [[Razorback Sucker]] received adult capture in Colorado (Jul 15) and *final E* status (Sep 10). The [[June Sucker]] was addressed through Utah breeding and stocking (May 14). [[Warner Sucker]] required drought relocation (May 11). The [[Colorado Squawfish]] had increased numbers reported (Jul 15) and a revised recovery plan (Sep 15).
- **Trout and salmon:** [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]] received Utah reservoir breeding and releases (May 14). [[Snake River Sockeye Salmon]] was *proposed E*, with decline factors and conservation measures discussed (May 1, 6-7). [[Chinook Salmon]] entries cover winter-run precautions and hydroelectric negotiations.
- **Smelt:** [[Delta Smelt]] was *proposed T*, with a drawing (Sep 2-5).
- **Minnows and darters:** [[Blue Shiner]] was *proposed T*, with a photo (May 7-8). [[Goldline Darter]] was *proposed T* (May 7-8). [[Longnose Darter]] had taxonomic and population studies (Apr 9). [[Paleback Darter]] had a habitat study with student involvement (Apr 9-10).
- **Madtoms:** [[Smoky Madtom]] and [[Yellowfin Madtom]] had captive-rearing projects and surveys (May 13).

**Other aquatic entries:**

- **Freshwater mussels:** Multiple entries, including the Cumberland pigtoe (*final E*, Jun 8-9), the winged mapleleaf (*final E*, Jul 11), and dwarf wedgemussel studies. Mussel cryopreservation research and a West Virginia freshwater survey (Sep 14) are also listed.
- **Snails:** Several *proposed* and *final* listings, including Tulotoma (*final E*, Feb 7) and the Snake River physa (*proposed E*, Jan 6).
- **Crustaceans:** Alabama cave shrimp monitoring (Jun 10) and California freshwater shrimp, with drought and farming issues (Mar 2, 5).
- **Aquatic mammals:** The Indus River dolphin (*Platanista minor*) was listed as *final E* (Feb 9). Sea otter translocation and the Steller sea lion (*final T*, Jan 9) are also covered.

**Scope note:** This index does not present population counts, sample sizes, or statistical results for fishes. It records only the dates and status of bulletin items. Readers needing quantitative data should consult the original bulletin entries cited by date.
