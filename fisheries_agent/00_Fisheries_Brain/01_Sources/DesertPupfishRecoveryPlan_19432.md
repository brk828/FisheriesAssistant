## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative memorandum (dated 7 October 1989) from Arizona State University's Center for Environmental Studies, circulated to members and consultants of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]. It announces the initiation of a **Desert Pupfish Recovery Plan** for [[Cyprinodon Macularius]] (desert pupfish), including the subspecies [[Cyprinodon Macularius Eremus]], at the request of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. The letter is coordinative rather than data-driven, outlining division of labor (California vs. Arizona/Mexico coverage), a request for agency input, and a submission timeline. It does not itself present primary morphological, physiological, or ecological data on the species but establishes the institutional and geographic framework (Arizona, California, and Sonora, Mexico) within which such data would be compiled for conservation purposes.

## Key Findings
- A **Desert Pupfish Recovery Plan** was being drafted in 1989 at the request of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, covering [[Cyprinodon Macularius]] and [[Cyprinodon Macularius Eremus]].
- Responsibilities were divided geographically: **Don Sada** was assigned the California portion, while the author ("Paul," likely W.L. Minckley or a colleague at Arizona State University) covered **Arizona and Mexico**.
- The plan was intended to integrate "best available biological information" while remaining sensitive to the management goals of responsible agencies (a multi-agency, multi-national coordination effort).
- Recipient agencies/individuals for consultation included representatives from the **U.S. Bureau of Reclamation**, **U.S. Bureau of Land Management**, **U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service** (Albuquerque and Phoenix offices), **Arizona Game and Fish Department**, **Centro Ecológico de Sonora**, **Arizona State University**, **New Mexico Department of Game and Fish**, and the **U.S. Forest Service** — reflecting the broad interagency and binational (U.S.–Mexico) scope of desert pupfish conservation.
- A response deadline of **15 November 1989** was set for agency input, with the initial draft recovery plan scheduled for submittal to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in **early 1990**.
- This document is historically significant as an early procedural record in the development of the formal **Desert Pupfish Recovery Plan**, illustrating the collaborative, cross-jurisdictional process required for recovery planning of an endangered desert fish spanning U.S. state lines and an international border.
