# Capture Locations of Rare Fish in the Upper Colorado River System

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This December 1978 report from the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] compiles capture-location records for six fish species of special concern in the [[Upper Colorado River System]]. It was produced by Ecology Consultants, Inc. under an interagency agreement with the [[Environmental Protection Agency]] and the Fish and Wildlife Service's Western Energy and Land Use Team. The purpose is to support evaluation of how proposed energy and water developments could affect these fish, and to inform mitigation planning. The core product is a set of 20 state-specific capture location maps, keyed to published author reports.

## Background & Description

**Purpose and scope.** The report notes that energy and water projects under consideration for the upper Colorado River basin could affect species of concern. Information on these fish is sparse because the area is relatively inaccessible and field sampling is difficult. The compilation covers six species:

- [[Colorado Cutthroat Trout]] (*Salmo clarki pleuriticus*)
- [[Kendall Warm Springs Dace]] (*Rhinichthys osculus thermalis*)
- [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*; OCR rendered the epithet as "jucius")
- [[Humpback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*)
- [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*)
- [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*)

**Map system.** Capture locations are plotted for Colorado, Utah, Wyoming and New Mexico. Numbered markers on each map correspond to author reports listed in the reference section. For example, in Wyoming, marker 7 on the Colorado cutthroat trout and Kendall Warm Springs dace map denotes trout captures reported by Binns (1977). A page-number key links each species to its maps by state.

**Reference base.** The reference section, of which the first portion is reproduced in the source, lists 12 entries dated 1864 to 1977 (including works by Banks, Baxter and Simon, Behnke, Binns, Cope, Drummond, Evermann and Rutter, and Finnell and Bennett). Several are unpublished or agency reports.

**Caveats.** The authors caution that present distributions should not be assumed identical to historical records, because habitat changes may have occurred.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Setting:** Large-river and tributary habitats of the upper Colorado River system, including the Green, Yampa, Gunnison, Dolores, White and Colorado rivers, plus Wyoming and Utah tributaries such as Muddy Creek and the Snake River area.
- **Landmarks referenced in mapping:** [[Green River]], [[Yampa River]], [[Colorado River]], [[Gunnison River]], [[Lake Powell]], [[Desolation Canyon]] and the Uinta Mountains.
- **Threats and pressures:** The report frames the central concern as potential impacts from energy and water development, with mitigation required for adverse effects. Habitat change is identified as a reason historical distributions may no longer apply.
- **Data limitations:** Sampling is constrained by remoteness and difficult field conditions, and records come from multiple authors with overlapping reports (for example, Author 7 and Author 2 overlapping in south-central Wyoming, and Author 9 overlapping Author 7 in the Muddy Creek drainage).

## Key Findings

- **Six species of special concern** are compiled, with capture locations plotted across four states.
- **20 capture location maps** are provided (Figures 2 to 38, even-numbered plus Figure 17), grouped by species and state:
  - [[Colorado Cutthroat Trout]]: maps in Wyoming, Colorado (headwaters of the Colorado River, White River and Glenwood Springs tributaries) and the Uinta Mountains of Utah.
  - [[Bonytail Chub]]: maps near Evanston and southwest Wyoming, the Colorado near the Green and Yampa confluence, the Green River near Jensen, and the Green and Colorado confluence in Utah.
  - [[Humpback Chub]]: maps in the Yampa River, the Colorado near Grand Junction, the Green River near Jensen and Ouray, and near Lake Powell.
  - [[Colorado Squawfish]]: maps near the Green and Yampa confluence, the Colorado, Gunnison and Dolores rivers, Desolation Canyon, and the Colorado near the Green confluence in Utah.
  - [[Humpback Sucker]]: maps near the Green and Yampa confluence, the Colorado and Gunnison rivers in western Colorado, the Green River in Utah, and the Colorado upstream from Moab.
- **Kendall Warm Springs Dace** is represented in the Wyoming mapping, with habitat-structure work by Binns (1978) cited.
- **Interpretive caution:** Current distributions may differ from historical reports due to habitat change, so the maps document past capture records rather than confirmed present populations.
- **Purpose:** The compilation is intended as baseline information for impact evaluation and mitigation planning for proposed energy and water developments.
