# January 26-27, 1989 Meeting of Desert Fishes Recovery Team

## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative memorandum and agenda from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) announcing and structuring a two-day meeting of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] held at Arizona State University, Tempe, Arizona. Rather than presenting original research data, the agenda outlines the conservation status, recovery planning needs, and management action items for **19 species/subspecies** of imperiled desert fishes native to the American Southwest, primarily within the [[Gila River Basin]] and [[Colorado River Basin]]. Key administrative topics include team reorganization, impacts of the 1988 amendments to the [[Endangered Species Act]], captive breeding/reintroduction status, taxonomic certification issues, and critical habitat consultations.

## Background & Description
The meeting was organized by the FWS Ecological Services office in Phoenix, Arizona, coordinated by Sally Stefferud, with logistical support noted from W.L. Minckley (a prominent desert fish biologist). The two-day agenda (January 26–27, 1989) was divided between:
1. **Programmatic/administrative business** — FWS Endangered Species program reorganization, the historical role and future structure of the Recovery Team, and implications of the 1988 ESA amendments.
2. **Species-specific status reviews** — sequential discussion of recovery plans, captive populations, reintroduction efforts, taxonomic work, and critical habitat designations for each listed or candidate desert fish taxon.

The species discussed span multiple families characteristic of southwestern desert aquatic systems:
- **[[Cyprinodontidae]]**: [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*)
- **[[Poeciliidae]]**: [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis* and *P. o. sonoriensis*)
- **[[Ictaluridae]]**: [[Yaqui Catfish]] (*Ictalurus pricei*)
- **[[Cyprinidae]]**: Multiple *Gila* species (*purpurea*, *ditaenia*, *nigrescens*, *robusta seminuda*, *robusta grahami*, *cypha*, *elegans*, *intermedia*), *Notropis formosus*, [[Lepidomeda vittata]] (Little Colorado Spinedace), *Meda fulgida* ([[Spikedace]]), *Tiaroga cobitis* ([[Loach Minnow]]), *Ptychocheilus lucius* ([[Colorado Pikeminnow]]), and *Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis* (Virgin Spinedace)
- **[[Catostomidae]]**: *Xyrauchen texanus* ([[Razorback Sucker]])

## Ecological & Environmental Context
The species under discussion inhabit fragile, isolated desert aquatic habitats including:
- Spring and stream systems on **BLM and Forest Service (FS) lands** (e.g., Bog Hole, Research Ranch)
- The **Aravaipa Creek** system, noted specifically regarding a fish barrier project
- The **upper Verde River** and **upper Gila River**, subject to federal consultations over critical habitat
- The **Colorado River mainstem and Lake Mohave**, relevant to annual sampling of *Gila elegans* ([[Bonytail Chub]])
- Streams affected by the **Miner Flat Dam** (relevant to *Tiaroga cobitis* habitat)

These systems represent classic desert fish conservation challenges: fragmented habitat, competition/hybridization risk, water diversion and impoundment impacts, and the need for coordinated multi-agency (FWS, BLM, FS) management across jurisdictional boundaries.

## Key Findings
Key action items and status questions raised for each taxon include:

- **[[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*)**: Status of recovery plan, experimental population rule for BLM land, captive population status, and stalled reintroduction efforts.
- **[[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis*)**: Status of down-listing petition and revision of recovery plan/delisting criteria.
- ***P. o. sonoriensis***: Open question on whether this subspecies warrants down-listing.
- **[[Yaqui Catfish]] (*Ictalurus pricei*)**: Need for recovery plan and taxonomic/purity certification work at Dexter National Fish Hatchery.
- ***Gila purpurea*** (Yaqui Chub): Reintroduction efforts ongoing.
- ***Notropis formosus***: Status largely undefined in agenda (open question).
- ***Gila ditaenia*** (Sonora Chub): Recovery plan status pending.
- **[[Lepidomeda vittata]]** (Little Colorado Spinedace): Recovery plan development and FS land habitat surveys needed.
- ***Meda fulgida*** ([[Spikedace]]): Recovery plan needed; upper Verde River consultation and upper Gila critical habitat designation pending.
- ***Tiaroga cobitis*** ([[Loach Minnow]]): Recovery plan needed; Miner Flat Dam consultation ongoing.
- ***Gila robusta seminuda*** (Virgin River Chub): Questions on lead FWS region, chemical treatment results, listing status, and post-treatment plans.
- ***Gila nigrescens*** (Chihuahua Chub): Recovery actions need initiation.
- ***Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis*** (Virgin Spinedace): Status undefined.
- **[[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*)**: Recovery plan revision and ongoing studies.
- **[[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*)**: Recovery plan revision; annual sampling at Lake Mohave.
- **[[Colorado Pikeminnow]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*)**: Recovery plan revision; Arizona stocking programs.
- **[[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*)**: Arizona stocking program status.
- ***Gila intermedia*** ([[Gila Chub]]): Determination of need for formal status assessment.
- ***Gila robusta grahami*** (Bonneville-type or Gila subspecies): Taxonomic clarification and management need questioned.

**Additional administrative/management topics:**
- Discussion of whether additional candidate species should be added to the federal candidate list.
- Aravaipa Creek barrier project status.
- Review of new FWS permit rules and regulations.
- Finalization of team assignments and planning for the next meeting.

This document primarily serves as a **conservation planning and coordination record** rather than a primary data source, reflecting the collaborative, multi-species triage approach used by the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] to manage the Southwest's imperiled native freshwater fish fauna in the late 1980s.
