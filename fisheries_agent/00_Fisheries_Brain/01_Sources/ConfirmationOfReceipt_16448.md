# Colorado Squawfish Trophy Fishery and Experimental Population Letter

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a letter dated July 25, 1986, from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Regional Director, Conrad A. Fjetland, to Bud Bristow, Director of the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]. It responds to a June 20, 1986 request for assistance in establishing a trophy sport fishery for [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*) in the lower [[Colorado River]] between Parker and Yuma, Arizona. The Service welcomed the request as an innovative way to aid recovery of this endangered species.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), a large cyprinid fish of the Colorado River basin.
- **Requesting agency:** [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], led by Director Bud Bristow.
- **Responding agency:** [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], Albuquerque, New Mexico regional office.
- **Legal framework:** [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973, as amended, specifically Section 10(J), which provides for designating experimental populations.
- **Hatchery source:** [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], which the Service agreed to supply with Colorado squawfish fry.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter places the proposed fishery within a recovery framework for an endangered native fish. The stretch of river concerned is the lower Colorado River between Parker and Yuma, Arizona. The document does not report population counts, habitat measurements, water chemistry, or other ecological data.

## Key Findings

- The Arizona Game and Fish Department requested Service help to create a trophy sport fishery for Colorado squawfish in the lower Colorado River between Parker and Yuma.
- The Service described the proposal as an innovative means of supporting species recovery and as "a bold step forward" in endangered species management.
- The Service's staff committed to preparing a separate experimental population designation for this population under Section 10(J) of the [[Endangered Species Act]].
- The Service agreed to provide Colorado squawfish fry from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] for the project.
- The letter was copied to the Fishery Resources office, Dexter National Fish Hatchery, and Dr. W. L. Minckley.
