# Basic Ground-Water Data for Western Salt River Valley, Maricopa County, Arizona

#hydrogeology #groundwater #water-resources

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) report compiles basic groundwater data for the western [[Salt River Valley]] in [[Maricopa County]], [[Arizona]]. It was prepared as a reference for planning and studying water-resource development in an area of roughly 500 square miles, mostly under irrigation. The data cover approximately the period up to 1965, and the report is intended to supplement later USGS publications. The document is not an ichthyological or ecological study. It contains no fish, invertebrate, plankton or water-chemistry data. Its content is hydrogeological: well records, well logs, discharges, drawdowns and water levels.

## Background & Description

**Study origin and partners.** In November 1961 the [[U.S. Geological Survey]] began an investigation of the geology and groundwater resources of western Salt River Valley. The work was done in cooperation with:
- the [[Arizona State Land Department]] (O. M. Lassen, Commissioner)
- [[Maricopa County Municipal Water Conservation District No. 1]] (H. S. Raymond, district engineer)

**Authors.** William Kam, H. H. Schumann, L. R. Kister and F. E. Arteaga. Work was begun under P. E. Dennis, district geologist of the Ground Water Branch.

**Study area.** The area covers about 500 square miles. Its boundaries are:
- South: the [[Gila and Salt River Base Line]]
- North: a line of latitude (OCR-garbled in the scan)
- East: longitude 112° (OCR-garbled)
- West: the [[White Tank Mountains]]

**Data sources.** Well data, logs, discharges and reported drawdowns came mainly from well-owner records and well-registration forms submitted to the Arizona State Land Department. Pump companies and well drillers supplied much of the information. [[Water-Level Monitoring]] by the USGS in the area dates back to 1938.

**Data organization.** Well records are arranged numerically by township, range and section under the [[Well Numbering System]] used in Arizona. That system follows the Bureau of Land Management's land subdivision scheme.

**Contributors.** Acknowledged data providers include:
- Goodyear Farms (C. I. Parkmann)
- Roosevelt Irrigation District (J. P. Vandenburgh)
- Buckeye Irrigation Co. (W. W. Weigold)
- Salt River Valley Water Users' Association (R. L. Juettin)
- W. S. Gookin and Associates, Phoenix (K. J. DeCook and W. A. Samsey), whose well-drilling data for Roosevelt Irrigation Co. were used

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address aquatic ecology, habitat, species or water quality. The only environmental context it gives is the hydrogeological setting: an irrigated agricultural valley dependent on groundwater pumped from wells. Water levels, [[Pumping Lift]] and [[Specific Capacity]] are presented as indicators of aquifer condition and pumping demand. The report does not analyse ecological effects of groundwater withdrawal.
