## Executive Summary

This document is an internal memorandum dated September 8, 1995, from Sally Stefferud, summarizing a conversation with Paul Boucher, Forest Biologist for the [[Gila National Forest]]. The memo reports on an ongoing contracted fish inventory of the [[Gila River]] in New Mexico, targeting two federally recognized sensitive/threatened cyprinid species: [[Spikedace]] (*Meda fulgida*) and [[Loach Minnow]] (*Tiaroga cobitis*). The survey is being conducted under a Federal Fish and Wildlife Permit (referred to as "PWS permit") by contractor Dennis Miller of Western New Mexico State University. The note is primarily administrative, documenting survey logistics, permitting, and intent rather than presenting raw biological, morphological, or ecological data.
