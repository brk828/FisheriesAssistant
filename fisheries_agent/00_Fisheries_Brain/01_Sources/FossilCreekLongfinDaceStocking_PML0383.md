# Fossil Creek Longfin Dace Stocking, February 12, 2008

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

On February 12, 2008, staff of the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] stocked a number of [[Longfin Dace]] (*Agosia chrysogaster*) into [[Fossil Creek]] to supplement an existing small population. The fish were collected from [[Tangle Creek]], a tributary of the [[Verde River]] on the [[Tonto National Forest]]. The report was written by [[Codey Carter]] of the Research Branch and dated February 2009. The stocked population will be monitored and augmented over time.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Longfin Dace]] (*Agosia chrysogaster*), the only fish species encountered in Tangle Creek during this effort.
- **Collection team:** [[Jesse Bahm]], [[Codey Carter]] and [[Tony Robbins]].
- **Source reach:** Tangle Creek, a tributary of the Verde River on the Tonto National Forest (see Figure 1 in the source).
- **Stocking purpose:** Supplementing an existing small population in Fossil Creek, following [[Fish Stocking]] practice.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Population density:** Densities in Tangle Creek were lower than in previous surveys. The authors suggest that recent floods in late 2007 and early 2008 may explain this.
- **Distribution in source stream:** The largest numbers were captured at the confluence of Tangle Creek and [[Roundtree Canyon]].
- **Release habitat:** Fish were released into pool habitat upstream of a low-gradient riffle. The release site lies about 4.5 km downstream of the decommissioned [[Irving Power Plant]].
- **Handling and transport:** Fish were held in a hauling tank with de-chlorinated water, stress coat, and a 0.6% saline solution. They were tempered to the tank water, then to [[Fossil Creek]] water before release.

## Key Findings

- Approximately the number of longfin dace given in the source was stocked into Fossil Creek on February 12, 2008. The exact count is illegible in the OCR text.
- The stocking supplements an existing small population in Fossil Creek.
- Tangle Creek densities were lower than in previous surveys, probably due to recent floods (late 2007 and early 2008).
- The greatest catches came from the confluence of Tangle Creek and Roundtree Canyon.
- Fish were released in pool habitat upstream of a low-gradient riffle, about 4.5 km downstream of the decommissioned Irving Power Plant.
- The stocked population will be monitored and augmented. Future stocking will likely occur both upstream and downstream of the 2008 release site.
