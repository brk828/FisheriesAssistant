# Lahontan Cutthroat Trout Recovery Plan and Federal Register Notices (February 23, 1993)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a Federal Register extract (Vol. 58, No. 34, Tuesday, February 23, 1993) containing several unrelated public notices. The main aquatic item is a notice from the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] (U.S. Department of the Interior) announcing a draft recovery plan for the [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]], a threatened fish native to the Great Basin. Public meetings and written comment periods were set for the draft plan. The other notices cover a [[Missouri National Recreational River]] Advisory Group meeting, a [[Mississippi River Coordinating Commission]] meeting, weekly [[National Register of Historic Places]] listings, a [[Drug Enforcement Administration]] manufacturing application, and [[Job Training Partnership Act]] allotments. The OCR scan is heavily garbled, so some dates and details below are uncertain or omitted.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]] (*Oncorhynchus clarkii henshawi*). The document does not give the scientific name; it is added here from standard taxonomy.
- **Status:** Listed under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973, as amended (16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.). The notice refers to the species as threatened.
- **Historic range and current status:** Historic habitat occurs in the Lahontan Basin, much of it on public lands administered by the U.S. Forest Service, Bureau of Land Management, and other agencies, as well as on state and private lands. Populations are described as reduced in number and range.
- **Management:** Recovery efforts aim to restore self-sustaining populations, and the plan addresses the Carson, Truckee, Walker, Quinn/Black Rock, and Whitehorse basins. Populations also rely on introduction programs.
- **Legal authority:** Section 4(f) of the Endangered Species Act (16 U.S.C. 1533(f)). Under Section 4(f), the agency must make the draft recovery plan available for public review and comment before final approval.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The recovery plan covers stream and lake habitat across Nevada, California, and Oregon. The affected counties include Douglas, Elko, Eureka, Humboldt, Lander, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe (Nevada), plus California and Oregon counties such as El Dorado, Lassen, Mono, and Harney. Recovery depends on restoring suitable habitat and reducing threats from habitat loss and non-native fish. ## Key Findings

1. **Draft Lahontan Cutthroat Trout Recovery Plan released for comment.** The Service solicited written comments and held public meetings on the draft. Comments were due by a date that is illegible in the scan (apparently April 1993). The plan describes actions, authority, and estimated time and cost for implementing recovery.
2. **Public meeting schedule.** Meetings were scheduled for March 12, 1993 (2:00 p.m.) at the Embassy Suites Hotel in Reno, Nevada, and possibly at other sites. Contact was the Field Supervisor, Nevada State Office, Reno. Public notice was given under Section 4(f) of the Endangered Species Act.
3. **Missouri National Recreational River Advisory Group.** The group, established May 24, 1991, advises the Secretary of the Interior on managing the 39-mile free-flowing segment of the Missouri River from Fort Randall Dam to the vicinity of Springfield, South Dakota. The group reports to the Regional Director of the National Park Service in Omaha, Nebraska. Its first meeting was set for March 17, 1993 (1:30–4:30 p.m.), in Lake Andes, South Dakota.
4. **Mississippi River Coordinating Commission.** The commission's meeting was scheduled for April 5, 1993 (9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.) at the University of Minnesota continuing education center in St. Paul, Minnesota. It was to review the draft comprehensive management plan and environmental impact statement for the Mississippi River.
5. **National Register of Historic Places.** Weekly list of properties in multiple states (including Oklahoma, Tennessee, Wisconsin, Ohio, and Iowa). Written comments were due by March 10, 1993. Specific entries are not summarized here.
6. **Drug Enforcement Administration notice.** Orpharm, Inc. (Houston, Texas) applied on November 27, 1992, to register as a bulk manufacturer of controlled substances. Comments or hearing requests were due within 30 days of publication.
7. **Job Training Partnership Act allotments.** The Department of Labor announced PY 1993 (July 1, 1993 to June 30, 1994) allotments for JTPA Titles II-A, II-B, II-C, and III, and preliminary planning estimates for Wagner-Peyser Act employment services.
