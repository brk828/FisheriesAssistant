## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from W. L. Minckley, Professor of Zoology and Leader of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]], addressed to Mike Spear, Regional Director of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region II). Dated 20 February 1986, the letter formally recommends six individuals to serve as **consultants** to the Recovery Team, based on their expertise in native desert fisheries, aquatic habitats, and regional ecological knowledge. ## Key Findings
- The [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] convened in December 1985 to begin deliberations on recovery strategy (minutes referenced but not included in this excerpt).
- Six consultants were nominated to support the Team based on regional expertise:
  - **James E. Brooks** — U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], New Mexico.
  - **Charles Kennedy** — U.S. Forest Service, Coronado National Forest, Arizona.
  - **J. Jerry Landye** — Arizona Game and Fish Department, Phoenix, Arizona.
  - **Dr. Paul C. Marsh** — Center for Environmental Studies, Arizona State University.
  - **Benny Robertson** — U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]], Arizona.
  - **Linda Ulmer** — California Fish and Game Department, Blythe, California.
- Selection criteria emphasized **direct knowledge of native fisheries resources**, familiarity with regional **aquatic habitats**, and demonstrated **expertise and experience** in desert fish conservation.
- The letter illustrates the collaborative, multi-agency governance structure (federal U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Forest Service, state agencies, and academic institutions) underpinning [[Desert Fish Conservation]] efforts in the 1980s.
- W. L. Minckley is identified as the formal Team Leader, situating Arizona State University's Department of Zoology as an institutional anchor for the Recovery Team's operations.
