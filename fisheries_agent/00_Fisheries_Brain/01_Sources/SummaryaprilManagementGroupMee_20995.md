# Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program Management Group Meeting Summary April 1988

#ichthyology #endangered-species #recovery-program #water-management #colorado-river

## Executive Summary

This memorandum and meeting summary documents the April 14, 1988 [[Colorado River Management Group]] meeting held at the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Regional Office in Lakewood, Colorado. The meeting addressed funding, work plans, water rights acquisition, endangered fish propagation, and instream flow determinations for the [[Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program]]. Key discussions centered on budget allocations (including $300K additional funding for FY-89), the [[Yampa River]] water rights negotiations, endangered fish propagation planning, and concerns about instream flow methodology. The meeting involved representatives from federal agencies (FWS, Bureau of Reclamation, WAPA), state agencies (Colorado, Utah, Wyoming), water developers, and [[The Nature Conservancy]].

## Background & Description

The [[Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program]] is a collaborative effort involving federal and state agencies, water developers, and conservation organizations to recover endangered fish species in the [[Colorado River Basin]]. The Management Group serves as a coordinating body overseeing program implementation, budget allocation, and policy decisions. This meeting summary was prepared by John Hamill, Program Director for the Recovery Implementation Program, FWS Region 6.

The program operates under Section 7 of the [[Endangered Species Act]] and involves multiple funding sources including Service base funding, "Windy Gap" Section 7 funds (approximately $500K available), and state contributions. The FY-88 funding situation was described as uncertain and probably inadequate for all ongoing and priority needs, though the Service Director provided verbal assurance of $300K additional base funding for FY-89 to assist in funding the Service share of the Recovery Program ($600K total).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The meeting addressed endangered fish recovery across multiple river reaches in the [[Colorado River Basin]], with particular focus on:

**[[Yampa River]]**: Water rights acquisition efforts focused on the Juniper-Cross Mountain water rights. Colorado state and local officials were receptive to alternative proposals including construction of a fish hatchery at the [[Craig Power Plant]], building a river park and visitor/information center, use of [[Elkhead Reservoir]] for municipal water supply, and constructing a reservoir at alternative sites.

**15-Mile Reach**: Instream flow determinations and water rights protection were under development, with concerns expressed about the biological defensibility of flow recommendations.

**[[San Juan River]]**: Several Management Group members expressed concern about using Recovery Program funds for studies on this river system, with agreement to prepare an issue paper for future discussion.

**[[Flaming Gorge]]**: Tailwater trout study funding related to impacts of reoperating federal dams was approved on a case-by-case basis.

The program also addressed fish passage issues, with plans to construct the [[Redlands Fish Ladder]] put on hold due to concerns about biological merits. The Service was preparing an issue/discussion paper on fish passage for review within 30-45 days.

## Key Findings

**Funding and Budget Decisions:**
- Service FY-88 funding remained uncertain but likely inadequate for all needs
- Director verbally assured $300K addition to Region 6 base funding in FY-89
- Approximately $500K in "Windy Gap" Section 7 funds available, mostly committed to Redlands fish ladder (now on hold)
- Management Group requested Technical Group review research priorities and recommend FY-89 fund allocation by June 30, 1988
- Service and Bureau agreed to resolve FY-89 cost projection differences before May 27-28, 1988 Technical Group meeting

**Endangered Fish Propagation:**
- Technical Group reviewed and supported propagation plan prepared by Lee Mills
- Management Group recommended Service hire fish culture biologist immediately to implement plan
- Authorized use of Windy Gap funds if necessary
- [[Colorado Water Conservation Board]] (CWCB) indicated interest in financing hatchery next year
- Feasibility study for fish hatchery at Craig Power Plant deemed premature unless streamlined approach developed
- Payment credit for hatchery feasibility studies contingent on completion of steps in Mill's plan

**Instream Flow Determinations:**
- Technical Committee distributed questions and comments on Service's draft instream flow reports for [[Yampa River]] and [[15-Mile Reach]]
- Several Management Group members found approach and findings unacceptable
- Ad hoc work group established (Tom Pitts chair, Gene Jencsok, Jay Skinner, Larry Shanks) to develop criteria defining biologically defensible instream flow recommendations
- Work group scheduled to meet May 3, 1988 at CDOW office in north Denver
- Criteria to be provided to Service and Technical Group before May 25-26, 1988 meeting

**Water Rights and Legal Issues:**
- [[Yampa River]] water rights acquisition efforts progressing positively with local and state officials
- [[The Nature Conservancy]] focused on water rights and legal issues
- Regional Solicitor and Department of Justice emphasized legal protection for [[Ruedi Reservoir]]/[[Green Mountain Reservoir]] releases
- Multiple legal questions identified requiring resolution in context of specific problems rather than general manner
- Agreement between Secretary and CWCB (Section 4.1.3.c.1) to be developed in context of specific activities
- Meeting scheduled with Colorado state attorneys to discuss legal issues related to Ruedi/Green Mountain releases

**Section 7 Consultation:**
- Service reviewed standard language for biological opinions addressing depletion impacts to Colorado River fishes
- Language to be sent to Implementation Committee for comment within two weeks
- Concerns expressed that Service was consulting solely with environmental groups; urged to provide language to all Management Group members

**Recovery Goals:**
- Group decided current recovery goals for rare fishes should be reviewed, revised, and quantified
- Activity to be included in Service's FY-89 work plan
- Technical Group to review results

**CWCB Work Plan:**
- Management Group approved CWCB work plan through September 1988 with modifications
- Task 1 (Review Reports) to be incorporated into Tasks 2 and 3
- Credit for Task 4 (Hatchery Program) only granted if Mill's propagation planning identifies need for feasibility study at Craig power plant
- CWCB seeking credit toward Colorado's FY-89 program contribution

**Field Activities:**
- [[The Nature Conservancy]] proposed 4-day [[Yampa River]] field trip for Management Group and consultants, June 11-14 or June 16-19, 1988
- Implementation Committee members to be invited
- Capacity limited to 25 people, subject to good runoff conditions
- Coordination with Service's endangered fish work in [[Yampa Canyon]] to be investigated

**Administrative:**
- Next Management Group meeting rescheduled for Thursday, June 23, 1988, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at FWS Regional Office, 134 Union Boulevard, Lakewood, Colorado (originally scheduled for June 7, 1988)
