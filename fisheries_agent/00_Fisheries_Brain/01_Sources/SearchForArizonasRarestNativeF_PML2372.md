# Search for Arizona's Rarest Native Fish: The Bonytail Chub

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a degraded 1988 newspaper feature (photo credit: [[Dave Nelson]], *The Arizona Republic*) describing a field search for the [[Bonytail Chub]], described as Arizona's rarest native fish. Its work is carried out with the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], and the stated goal is to capture even one individual to add to the broodstock at a federal fish hatchery in New Mexico. The article is largely unreadable due to OCR damage; the main points below rest on the few legible fragments.
