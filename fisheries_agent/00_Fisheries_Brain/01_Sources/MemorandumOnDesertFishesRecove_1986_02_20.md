# Memorandum on Desert Fishes Recovery Team Progress and Meeting Plans

#ichthyology #marine-biology #desert-fishes #Cyprinidae #conservation-biology

## Executive Summary

This 1986 memorandum from W. L. Minckley (Department of Zoology, Arizona State University) to the **[[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]** documents administrative and scientific planning for the recovery of several imperiled southwestern desert fish species. The document is not a primary data report but a coordination memo outlining missed deadlines on a technical review draft for **[[Gila nigrescens]]** (Chihuahua Chub), rescheduling of a team meeting to 17–18 April 1986, and an agenda for addressing listing status, recovery plan drafting, and field survey activities for the **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]**. It provides valuable context on the institutional and logistical processes underlying endangered desert fish conservation in the mid-1980s, including planned field surveys of the upper **[[San Pedro River]]** and **[[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]]**.

## Background & Description

The memo originates from the **[[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]**, a multi-agency body coordinating recovery planning for threatened and endangered fishes of arid-land drainages in the American Southwest, particularly the **[[Yaqui River]]** and **[[Gila River]]** basins. Key personnel referenced include:

- **W. L. Minckley** – team lead/author, Arizona State University
- **Jerry Burton** – responsible for the draft **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]** and upper basin research/programs
- **J. Brooks** – Dexter National Fish Hatchery, responsible for captive propagation and recovery production programs
- **B. Robertson** – San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge manager
- **P. Marsh** – contributor to **[[Xyrauchen texanus]]** (Razorback Sucker) recovery activities
- Arizona Game and Fish Department – cooperating agency

The memo references a 1985 status document on native fishes prepared for the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, intended to serve, alongside the San Bernardino NWR management plan, as the foundation for Recovery Plan preparation.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document identifies several critical desert aquatic habitats slated for survey and management planning:

- **[[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]]** (Douglas, Arizona area) — focal site for **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]** development; refuge management plan under review.
- **[[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]]** — target of a planned multi-day aquatic habitat and fish survey (ponds, muds, and waters) by Minckley's Ichthyology class and Jim Johnson.
- **Upper San Pedro River** (BLM exchange area) — subject of ichthyological class surveys of fish assemblages prior to the team meeting.
- **Dexter National Fish Hatchery** — a captive propagation facility central to recovery production programs for listed desert fishes.

These sites collectively represent isolated desert spring, riverine, and refuge pond ecosystems that support endemic and highly range-restricted fish taxa vulnerable to habitat loss, water diversion, and introduced species — hallmark threats to **[[Desert Fishes]]** conservation.

## Key Findings

- **Recovery planning delays**: The first Team deadline (15 January) for reviewing the **[[Gila nigrescens]]** technical draft recovery plan largely failed — only one reviewer submitted comments, another declined citing insufficient information, and the remainder (including the author) did not respond. Salvage of this review process was to be discussed with Jerry Burton.
- **Meeting rescheduled**: The team meeting, originally planned for March at **[[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]]**, was postponed to **17–18 April 1986**, to be held in Tucson, Arizona, followed by field visits to San Bernardino NWR and Buenos Aires NWR.
- **Species listing status to be addressed**: The agenda explicitly flags review of listing status for:
  - **[[Lepidomeda vittata]]** (Little Colorado Spinedace)
  - **[[Tiaroga cobitis]]** (Loach Minnow)
  - **[[Meda fulgida]]** (Spikedace)
  - **[[Gila robusta seminuda]]** (Virgin River Chub)
  - **[[Cyprinodon macularius]]** (Desert Pupfish)
- **Xyrauchen texanus recovery**: A dedicated agenda item was set for discussing **[[Xyrauchen texanus]]** (Razorback Sucker) recovery activities, involving J. Brooks, P. Marsh, W. Minckley, and the Arizona Game and Fish Department.
- **Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan timeline**: Jerry Burton was tasked with producing a draft of the **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]** by the end of March 1986, to be circulated before the April meeting; this plan, combined with a 1985 FWS status document, would underpin the broader Recovery Plan development process.
- **Planned field surveys**: A multi-day aquatic habitat and fish survey of ponds at **[[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]]** was scheduled (Friday through Sunday/Monday), open to Team members, run in parallel with an Ichthyology class survey of the upper **[[San Pedro River]]**.
- **Institutional concerns**: Minckley expressed particular concern not over technical plan content, but over defining the "step-down" sequence to recovery and setting the required number of self-sustaining populations/communities needed to justify down-listing or delisting — a recurring challenge in desert fish recovery planning.
