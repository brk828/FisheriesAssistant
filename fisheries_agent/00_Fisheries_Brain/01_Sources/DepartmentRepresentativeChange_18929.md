# Request to Change Department Representative on the Desert Fishes Recovery Team

#ichthyology #marine-biology #desert-fishes

## Executive Summary

This one-page letter, dated February 11, 1993, is an administrative request rather than research. [[Duane L Shroufe]], Director of the [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]], asks [[John G Rogers]], Regional Director of [[Fish And Wildlife Service]] Region 2, to replace the Department's representative on the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]. The current representative, [[Francisco Abarca]], would be succeeded by [[Kirk Young]], the Department's Native Fish Program Manager. The letter names no species, sites, or data.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Formal letter (correspondence) on Arizona Game & Fish Department letterhead.
- **Sender:** Duane L. Shroufe, Director (initials DLS, with typist reference dk).
- **Addressee:** John G. Rogers, Regional Director, Region 2, Fish and Wildlife Service, Albuquerque, New Mexico.
- **Copies to:** Sam Spiller (Ecological Services, FWS) and W. L. Minckley ([[Arizona State University]]).
- **Subject:** Change of Department representative on the Desert Fishes Recovery Team.
- **Department leadership named on letterhead:** Governor Fife Symington; Commissioners Gordon K. Whiting (Chairman), Larry Taylor, and Elizabeth T. Woodin; Deputy Director Thomas W. Spalding.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter gives no species list, habitat description, water chemistry, population data, or site information. Its only ecological link is the stated purpose of the role: the Department's program is directed at the "recovery of listed fishes," meaning fish species protected under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. The [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] is the body responsible for that recovery work. The specific fishes involved are not named in the document.

## Key Findings

- **Representative change requested:** Francisco Abarca is to be replaced by Kirk Young on the Desert Fishes Recovery Team.
- **Rationale:** Kirk Young is the Department's Native Fish Program Manager and holds statewide responsibility for Department activities aimed at recovering listed fishes. The Director considers it "appropriate" for him to take the seat.
- **Requested action:** The Regional Director is asked to accept the change. The letter does not say whether approval was granted or what the outcome was.
- **Scope:** The letter contains no quantitative data, statistics, species names, or management plans.
