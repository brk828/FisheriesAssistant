# Federal Fish and Wildlife Permit PRT-676811: Endangered Fish Research and Recovery Authorization (1984–1986)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #poeciliidae #salmonidae #cyprinodontidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a set of administrative correspondence from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) Albuquerque regional office. It covers the federal permit issued under the [[Endangered Species Act]] (16 USC 1539(a), regulations at [[50 CFR 17.22]]) that authorizes [[Arizona State University]] researchers to take, hold, and conduct scientific research and [[Endangered Species Recovery]] on listed freshwater fishes in Arizona and New Mexico. The permit is Federal Fish and Wildlife Permit PRT-676811 (also cited as PRT-57681). It was first issued in 1984, extended through calendar year 1986, and amended to add species and permittees. The document is not a research study, so it reports no field data. Its main content is the scope of authority, the conditions attached, and the reporting obligations.

## Background & Description

The permit is held by Dr. [[W.L. Minckley]], Associate Professor of Zoology at Arizona State University, Tempe. Other authorized subpermittees are:

- [[Paul Marsh]]
- [[David Galat]]
- [[Catherine Karp]]
- [[Bruce DeMarais]]
- [[Michael Douglas]] (added as a permittee by a later amendment)

The authorization covers listed freshwater fishes, most of which are native to the Gila River basin and adjacent desert waters. Based on partly legible OCR text, the species named include:

- [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*)
- [[Gila Chub]] (*Gila intermedia*)
- [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*)
- [[Apache Trout]] (*Oncorhynchus apache*)
- [[Colorado Pikeminnow]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*)
- [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*)

A minnow species and a shiner species are also mentioned, but OCR damage makes their names unreliable. The original permit was valid for activities in calendar year 1986 only. It is valid only when used together with a valid state permit. Permit validity was tied to the Regional Director's permit conditions and the conditions attached to the authorization.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document shows the regulatory setting for research on endangered freshwater fishes in the arid Southwest. The species named are mostly small-bodied desert fishes, several of which are restricted to isolated springs, creeks, and river reaches. Research and recovery work therefore needs federal authorization, because it involves handling, holding, and possibly moving listed animals. The permit ties this work to:

- the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], which must also issue a valid state permit;
- the Law Enforcement offices (Region 2 and Region 1), which receive copies of the permit; and
- the Federal Wildlife Permit Office in Washington, D.C., which receives the correspondence.

The document does not contain water chemistry, habitat measurements, population counts, or other ecological data.

## Key Findings

- **Scope of authority:** The permit authorizes take (capture, hold, and similar actions) of listed fishes for scientific research and species recovery. It applies only within the stated activities and species.
- **Validity conditions:** The permit is valid only when used with a valid state permit. The permittee must follow all stipulations on the attached Regional Director's permit.
- **Expansion by amendment:** A later amendment (dated 17 July 1986) added more species, including the desert pupfish and at least one other listed minnow, and added Michael Douglas as a permittee. The general conditions did not change.
- **Renewal and reporting:** An annual activity report, plus any renewal request, must be submitted to the Regional Director by 1 December (per the 1986 letter). The permit file also specifies a report due by 31 January to USFWS, Federal Wildlife Permit Office, P.O. Box 3654, Arlington, VA 22203.
- **Document-keeping requirement:** The permittee must carry a copy of both the authorization letter and the permit while conducting authorized activities.
- **Permit status:** The file shows the Regional Director's permit with an effective date of 10 May 1984 and a renewal notice in early 1986 (OCR-garbled dates).
