# Xilitloniscus, A New Genus for the Mexican Troglobitic Isopod, Cordioniscus laevis Rioja (Oniscoidea: Trichoniscidae)

#isopod #cave-biology #Trichoniscidae #taxonomy #Mexico

## Executive Summary

This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956. Based on examination of male pleopod morphology unavailable to Rioja, [[Thomas Bowman]] demonstrates that this species belongs to the superfamily [[Trichoniscoidea]] rather than [[Styloniscoidea]], necessitating creation of a new genus. The species is a blind troglobite known from three caves near [[Xilitla]], [[San Luis Potosi]], Mexico, representing the most primitive division of the family [[Trichoniscidae]].

## Background & Description

*Xilitloniscus laevis* was originally described by Rioja from a single female specimen from [[Cueva de la Hoya]] near Xilitla. Rioja's placement in *Cordioniscus* was based on limited characters: the short third segment of antenna 1, armature of the inner ramus of maxilla 1, and number of mandibular setae. However, he could not examine male pleopods 1 and 2, which are fundamental taxonomic characters.

The new genus **Xilitloniscus** resembles *[[Protrichoniscus]]* Arcangeli (1932) in general aspect but differs in several key features:

**Morphological characteristics:**
- Length up to 12 mm
- Antenna 1: 2nd segment nearly as long as 3rd; distal segment with 11 slender sensory filaments
- Antenna 2: reaches posterior margin of pereonite 3; flagellum 8-merous
- Right mandible: 1 seta between incisor and molar, 1 seta on molar
- Left mandible: 2-3 setae between incisor and molar, 1 seta on molar
- Pereonites without tubercles on dorsal posterior margins
- Telson with 2 minute apical setae
- Penis gradually tapering to narrow apex bearing slender seta

**Distinctive features of male pleopods:**
- Male pleopod 1: endopod apparently absent; exopod with plumose terminal seta (not present in *Protrichoniscus*)
- Male pleopod 2: exopod separated from basis by suture

**Specimens examined:**
- [[Cueva de la Selva]], 8 km W of Xilitla: 26 November 1963 (2 females); 25 November 1964 (4 females, 2 males)
- [[Cueva de la Porra]], 10.9 km NE of Xilitla: 3 females, 1 male

## Ecological & Environmental Context

*Xilitloniscus laevis* is a blind troglobite (obligate cave-dwelling organism) restricted to caves in the vicinity of Xilitla, [[San Luis Potosi]], Mexico. The species has been collected from two distinct microhabitats within caves:

1. Cave walls (Rioja's original specimen)
2. Small pools inside caves (specimens collected by Terry Raines, Bill Bell, and Bill Russell)

This represents a typical troglobitic adaptation pattern, with the species showing complete dependence on the cave environment. The presence in pools suggests aquatic or semi-aquatic habits within the cave system.

## Key Findings

**Taxonomic revision:**
- *Cordioniscus laevis* Rioja (1956) is transferred to the new genus **Xilitloniscus**
- The species belongs to superfamily [[Trichoniscoidea]], not [[Styloniscoidea]] as originally thought
- Placement is based on fundamental differences in form and musculature of male pleopod 1
- *Xilitloniscus* belongs to Vandel's first and most primitive division of family [[Trichoniscidae]]

**Phylogenetic relationships:**
- Most closely related to *[[Protrichoniscus]]*, the only other trichoniscid genus with greatly reduced or absent endopod on male pleopod 1
- Distinguished from *Protrichoniscus* by presence of terminal plumose seta on exopod of male pleopod 1
- Of six known *Protrichoniscus* species, five occur only in Mexican caves; type species *P. heroldi* occurs in California

**Systematic placement:**
- Vandel (1953) confirmed *Protrichoniscus heroldi* has male pleopod 1 of "type trichoniscien" rather than "type styloniscien"
- Mulaik (1960) incorrectly moved *Protrichoniscus* to [[Styloniscidae]] based on molar seta presence, ignoring fundamental pleopod differences
- Current revision supports Vandel's placement in [[Trichoniscinae]], most primitive tribe

**Distribution:**
- Known from three caves near [[Xilitla]]: [[Cueva de la Hoya]], [[Cueva de la Selva]], and [[Cueva de la Porra]]
- Represents specialized cave fauna of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] region
- Part of broader Mexican troglobitic isopod fauna

**Morphological details:**
- Detailed descriptions provided for all appendages including antennae, mandibles, maxillae, maxillipeds, pereopods, pleopods, and uropods
- Carpus and propodus of pereopods 1-2 bear strong spines with apical prongs
- Medial surfaces show delicate overlapping triangular scales visible only at high magnification
- Uropods with narrow rami; exopod 1.5 times as long as endopod
