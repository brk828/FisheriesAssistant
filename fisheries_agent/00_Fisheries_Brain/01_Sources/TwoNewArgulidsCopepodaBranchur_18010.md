# Two New Argulids (Crustacea: Branchiura) from the Eastern United States

#ichthyology #marine-biology #parasitology #argulidae #crustacea

## Executive Summary

This taxonomic paper describes two new species of parasitic crustaceans in the genus [[Argulus]] from the eastern United States. [[Argulus meehani]] was collected from [[Florida Gar]] ([[Lepisosteus platyrhynchus]]) in [[Everglades National Park]], Florida, while [[Argulus chesapeakensis]] was collected from [[Oyster Toadfish]] ([[Opsanus tau]]) at [[Chesapeake Biological Laboratory]], Maryland. Both species are distinguished from congeners by morphological characters including respiratory area shape, antennal structure, and sucker sclerotization patterns. The work was conducted as part of a broader effort to produce a key to argulids of the United States.

## Background & Description

**[[Argulus meehani]] new species:**
- **Type material:** Holotype female (USNM 137492), allotype (USNM 137493), and 90 paratypes (USNM 137494)
- **Host:** [[Florida Gar]] ([[Lepisosteus platyrhynchus]])
- **Location:** Royal Palm Pond, [[Everglades National Park]], Florida
- **Collector:** M. C. Kolipinski

**Female morphology:**
- Total body length greater than width at ratio 8:5
- Carapace slightly longer than wide (6:5 ratio)
- Ovigerous specimen measurements: 6.3 mm × 4.1 mm total length and width
- Abdomen slightly longer than wide (3.4 mm × 3.3 mm)
- Caudal rami small, bearing 5 setae
- First antenna: first segment without posterior spine, second segment with prominent anterior spine, medial spine, and terminal claw
- Second antenna: prominent posterior spine and hyaline bubble-like process with several short setae on first segment
- Postantennal spine well developed
- Mouth tube about twice as long as wide, without ornamentation at base
- Respiratory areas distinctive: neck of larger area nearly surrounding smaller within its inner margin
- Legs 1-2 with fringed scales on first 2 segments; scales mixed with unidentate spinules on third segment
- Legs 3-4 with only fringed scales on segments
- All legs bearing plumose setae on flagella
- Natatory lobe with fringed scales plus long setae; slightly more than 3 times as long as wide, shoe-like in appearance
- No pigmentation in preserved specimens

**Male morphology:**
- Body form and appendages as in female except for modifications on basal portions of legs 2, 3, and 4

**[[Argulus chesapeakensis]] new species:**
- **Type material:** Holotype female (USNM 137495), allotype (USNM 137496), and 89 paratypes (USNM 137497)
- **Host:** [[Oyster Toadfish]] ([[Opsanus tau]])
- **Location:** [[Chesapeake Biological Laboratory]], Solomons, Maryland
- **Collector:** F. Schwartz

**Female morphology:**
- Total body length greater than width at ratio 7:5
- Carapace somewhat longer than wide (6:5 ratio)
- Ovigerous specimen measurements: 5.55 mm × 3.90 mm
- Abdomen wider than long (1.1 mm × 0.9 mm), widest across middle
- Caudal rami small, bearing 5 setae
- First antenna: first segment with well-developed posterior spine, second segment with moderately developed anterior spine, well-developed median spine, and terminal claw
- Second antenna 4-segmented; first segment with prominent posterior spine
- Postantennal spine well-developed
- Mouth tube only slightly longer than wide, without ornamentation at base
- Basal plate of second maxilla with remaining segments bearing numerous multidentate spinules
- Legs 1-4 with unidentate spinules on surface plus plumose setae
- Natatory lobe with ventral surface covered with pyriform scales plus plumose setae
- Area between natatory lobes with patch of prominent pyriform scales
- Pigmentation present in transverse band across mid-area of carapace and on dorsal surface of thoracic segments

**Male morphology:**
- As in female except for modified areas at bases of legs 2-4

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**[[Argulus meehani]]:**
- Parasitic on [[Florida Gar]] in freshwater habitat
- Found in [[Everglades National Park]] pond environment
- Kolipinski (1969) published detailed account of parasite effects on host

**[[Argulus chesapeakensis]]:**
- Parasitic on [[Oyster Toadfish]] in marine/estuarine habitat
- Found in [[Chesapeake Bay]] region
- Dutcher and Schwartz (1962) documented host-parasite relationship
- Previously misidentified as [[Argulus laticauda]]

## Key Findings

**Taxonomic distinctions for [[Argulus meehani]]:**
- Separated from all other [[Argulus]] species except [[Argulus lepidostei]] and [[Argulus nobilis]] by respiratory area shape
- Distinguished from [[Argulus lepidostei]] by absence of scales on basal plate of second maxilla (present in lepidostei)
- Distinguished from [[Argulus nobilis]] by number of sclerotized rod elements in suckers: fewer than 10 in meehani versus more than 10 in nobilis
- Further separated from both species by presence of hyaline areas on second antenna
- Sample size: 92 specimens total (holotype, allotype, 90 paratypes)

**Taxonomic distinctions for [[Argulus chesapeakensis]]:**
- Separated from all species except [[Argulus flavescens]] and [[Argulus laticauda]] by respiratory area shape
- Distinguished from [[Argulus laticauda]] by spine morphology: in laticauda the 3 spines on basal plate of second maxilla are cuspidate and antennal spines are spatulate
- Distinguished from [[Argulus flavescens]] by: (1) posterior spine on first segment of first antenna is digitate in flavescens, and (2) the 3 spines on basal plate of second maxilla are longer than wide in flavescens
- Sample size: 91 specimens total (holotype, allotype, 89 paratypes)

**Research context:**
- Both species discovered during work to produce identification key to argulids of the United States
- Specimens deposited in United States National Museum (USNM)
- Previous ecological studies of both species already published under incorrect or provisional identifications
