# Lake Mohave Fish Stocking Records: Correspondence from Dexter Fishery Assistance Office

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a cover letter from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Dexter Fishery Assistance Office to Tom Liles of the Arizona Game and Fish Department. It transmits an attached list of all [[Lake Mohave]] fish stockings made from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] and asks for follow-up on a possible fall stocking of young-of-year (YOY) bonytail. The attachment is a scanned, partly illegible table of stocking dates, locations, species, counts and sizes. The letter is administrative rather than research-based, and most quantitative values in the attachment could not be reliably recovered from the scan.

## Background & Description

- **Sender:** Dexter Fishery Assistance Office, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Dexter, New Mexico. The letter is signed by a Management Biologist.
- **Recipient:** Tom Liles, Arizona Game and Fish Department, Kingman, Arizona.
- **Purpose:** Compile a list of all Lake Mohave stockings sourced from Dexter National Fish Hatchery, at the request of "Buddy" (not further identified in the text).
- **Attachment:** A table of stockings into Lake Mohave, with columns for date, location, species, number, and size. Parts are legible, but most values are corrupted by OCR errors.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- Stockings were made into Lake Mohave, a reservoir on the lower [[Colorado River]].
- Except for the first stocking in 1981, fish were released at [[Cottonwood Landing]]. The letter says to disregard the Arizona and Camp Coves locations listed in the attachment.
- The initial stocking of chubs was made via the [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]] stocking barge. The fish were presumably dispersed through the main lake body.
- No stockings were made in the years listed in the letter (read as 1983, 1984 and 1986; the scan shows "1993" in one place, which is likely an OCR error).
- Stocking is coordinated across agencies. Prior notice was given to the Nongane Branch, and Dexter personnel assumed the information was passed on to Fisheries.

## Key Findings

- **Stocking history:** Dexter NFH has records of Lake Mohave stockings, the first in 1981 and most later ones at Cottonwood Landing. The attachment lists all of them.
- **Species stocked:** The letter identifies the early stocked fish as "chubs." Its reference to "yoy bonytail chub" in the planned stocking points to [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) as the main species of interest. The scan does not clearly show the species for every entry.
- **Planned action:** A stocking of YOY bonytail may take place in the fall, depending on FY 91 funding. The plan was already discussed with the recipient.
- **Data quality:** The attached table is badly degraded. Species counts, sizes (for example 5", 5-9") and totals cannot be fully verified from the scan and should be checked against original hatchery records before use.
- **Follow-up:** The sender offers to provide more information on request.
