## Executive Summary

This document is an **administrative memorandum**, not a primary scientific research report. It was issued on March 7, 1992 by the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) program, operating under the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation's Upper Colorado Region, to coordinate the review of a **DRAFT Interim Flow Monitoring Plan**. The memo announces a stakeholder meeting (March 24, 1992, Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ) intended to consolidate technical comments from an interagency "Interim Flow Monitoring Work Group" before the plan is distributed to Cooperating Agencies.

No direct morphological, taxonomic, or ecological data on fish species are presented in this excerpt. However, the document is contextually significant to [[Ichthyology]] and [[Riverine Ecology]] because it identifies the institutional and personnel framework responsible for monitoring the effects of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations (specifically "interim flows") on the downstream Colorado River ecosystem, including its native and non-native fish communities.
