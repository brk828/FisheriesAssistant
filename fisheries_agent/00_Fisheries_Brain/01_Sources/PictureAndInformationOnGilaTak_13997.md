# Correspondence Regarding Gila Specimen from Shingumu Creek

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #Cyprinidae #species-identification

## Executive Summary

This correspondence between Dr. James E. Deacon ([[University of Nevada Las Vegas]]) and Dr. Robert R. Miller ([[University of Michigan]]) concerns the identification of a [[Gila]] specimen collected from [[Shingumu Creek]] on August 1, 1975. The specimen was tentatively identified as [[Gila cypha]] (humpback chub) but required additional morphological data for confirmation.

## Background & Description

The specimen was collected using a 12' x 6' x 1/8" mesh seine at [[Shingumu Creek]]. Dr. Deacon photographed the fish while it was still in the seine, briefly lifted from the water. Color slides were also available for examination.

Dr. Miller noted that identification of [[Gila cypha]] typically requires critical morphological features including:
- Dorsal ray counts
- Anal ray counts
- Detailed configuration of the characteristic hump in side view

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The specimen was collected from [[Shingumu Creek]], a freshwater habitat. [[Gila cypha]] is a distinctive cyprinid species characterized by a pronounced dorsal hump, making accurate identification dependent on clear morphological assessment.

The collection occurred during summer field work (August 1, 1975), using standard seining techniques appropriate for stream fish sampling.

## Key Findings

- A [[Gila]] specimen was collected from [[Shingumu Creek]] on August 1, 1975
- Initial photographic examination by Dr. Miller and Jerry Smith suggested the specimen may represent [[Gila cypha]], but definitive identification could not be confirmed from the photograph alone
- Critical diagnostic features needed for confirmation included:
  - Dorsal and anal fin ray counts
  - Better side-view imagery showing detailed hump configuration
- The correspondence highlights the importance of detailed morphological data and multiple viewing angles for accurate species identification in the genus [[Gila]]
