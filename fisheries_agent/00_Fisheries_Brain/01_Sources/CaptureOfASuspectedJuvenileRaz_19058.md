# Capture of Suspected Juvenile Razorback Sucker in Colorado River Indian Reservation

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #Catostomidae #razorback-sucker #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This memorandum documents the capture of a juvenile [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) from a lateral canal on the [[Colorado River Indian Reservation]] near [[Parker, Arizona]] on January 12, 1980. The specimen was captured by a reservation resident using a chicken wire seine in a culvert under Burns Road, approximately 6 miles west and south of the Four Corners Area. The fish measured 12.7 inches (323 mm) in length and weighed 0.75 pounds (339.6 grams). After documentation, scale sampling, and temporary holding, the specimen was released into the [[Colorado River]] below Parker Bridge on January 14, 1980.

## Background & Description

The [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) is a member of the family [[Catostomidae]]. The captured specimen was a suspected juvenile based on its size (12.7 inches/323 mm total length, 339.6 grams weight). The fish was captured alive and maintained in good condition throughout the documentation process.

The capture location was within the extensive canal system of the [[Colorado River Indian Reservation]], specifically in a lateral canal at a culvert crossing under Burns Road. The capture method employed was a chicken wire seine, a non-standard but effective technique for the confined culvert environment.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The specimen was found in an artificial habitat—a lateral canal system associated with the [[Colorado River Indian Reservation]]—rather than the main river channel. This occurrence in irrigation infrastructure may reflect the species' use of diverse aquatic habitats or displacement from natural river environments.

Upon release on January 14, 1980, the [[Colorado River]] water temperature was recorded at 52°F (11°C), representing winter conditions in the lower Colorado River system near [[Parker, Arizona]].

## Key Findings

- A juvenile [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) was captured on January 12, 1980, in a lateral canal of the [[Colorado River Indian Reservation]]
- Morphometric data: 12.7 inches (323 mm) total length; 0.75 pounds (339.6 grams) weight
- The specimen was documented photographically and scale samples were collected using a scale press
- The fish was held temporarily in a 50-gallon aquarium at the Parker office over the weekend in good condition
- Documentation included photographs and a newspaper article published in the Parker Pioneer on January 24, 1980
- The specimen was successfully released into the [[Colorado River]] below Parker Bridge on January 14, 1980, into water at 52°F (11°C)
- Interagency notification was provided to Arizona Game and Fish (Yuma) and California Fish and Game (Blythe)
- The capture demonstrates the presence of juvenile [[Razorback Sucker]] in the canal systems associated with the lower [[Colorado River]] during the winter period
