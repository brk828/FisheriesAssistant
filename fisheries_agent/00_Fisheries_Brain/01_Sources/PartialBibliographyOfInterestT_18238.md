# Partial Bibliography on the Primary Productivity of Aquatic Ecosystems

#limnology #marine-biology #primary-productivity

## Executive Summary

This is a class-use bibliography compiled by [[Robert J. Beyers]], Director of the [[Savannah River Ecology Laboratory]], in October 1967. It lists published and unpublished literature on measuring the primary productivity of aquatic ecosystems. Entries are coded with Roman numerals (I–VII) for their main subject area. The document is not a fish study. It covers aquatic primary production, methods, and ecosystem metabolism. It touches fish only indirectly, through fish production and fisheries linked to productivity. Source text is OCR-scanned, with many author names, volumes, and page numbers garbled.

## Background & Description

- **Purpose:** The compiler states the bibliography was prepared for class use, is not considered published, and welcomes corrections and additions.
- **Organization:** Sections cover Papers (pages 3–51) and Books (pages 52–54).
- **Subject codes:**
  - I: Comparisons, Compendia, and Miscellany
  - II: Light and Dark Bottles, Oxygen and Carbon Dioxide
  - III: Light and Dark Bottles, Carbon-14
  - IV: Free-Water Methods, Oxygen and Carbon Dioxide
  - V: Standing Crop and Organic Matter
  - VI: Chlorophyll, Light, and Transparency
  - VII: Various Elements in Primary Production
- **Compiler affiliation:** The laboratory is operated by the [[University of Georgia]] Institute of Ecology for the [[United States Atomic Energy Commission]].
- **Scope of methods:** Entries address the [[Light and Dark Bottle Method]], the [[Carbon-14 Method]], the [[Winkler Method]], [[Chlorophyll Method]] estimation, [[Free-Water Oxygen Method]] techniques, and [[Standing Crop Measurement]].
- **Geographic scope:** Sources include the [[Atlantic Ocean]], [[Pacific Ocean]], [[Indian Ocean]], [[Antarctica]], the [[Arctic Ocean]], [[Lake Balaton]], [[Lake Tanganyika]], [[Lake Erie]], [[Sargasso Sea]], [[Long Island Sound]], [[Texas Bays]], and [[Florida Springs]], among many others.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Core concept:** [[Primary Productivity]] is the rate at which autotrophs (phytoplankton, periphyton, macrophytes, benthic algae) fix carbon. Its measurement underpins studies of energy flow and secondary production.
- **Linked processes:**
  - [[Community Respiration]] and [[Diurnal Oxygen Curves]] for ecosystem metabolism
  - [[Extracellular Organic Products]] (e.g., [[Glycollic Acid]]) released by phytoplankton
  - [[Nutrient Limitation]] by nitrogen, phosphorus, and molybdenum
  - [[Light Penetration]] and [[Photosynthesis-Irradiance Relationships]]
  - [[Eutrophication]] and effluent effects, including [[Oil Refinery Effluent]] and [[Sewage Effluent]]
- **Fish-related links (indirect):**
  - [[Hayes and Anthony 1964]]: productive capacity of North American lakes in relation to fish quantity and trophic level
  - [[McConnell 1963]]: primary productivity and fish harvest in a small desert impoundment
  - [[Hellier 1962]]: fish production and photosynthesis in the [[Laguna Madre]] of Texas
  - [[Cushing 1955]]: production and a pelagic fishery
  - [[Hayne and Ball 1956]]: benthic productivity as influenced by fish predation
  - [[Coker 1925]]: hydrogen-ion concentration and fishes in Catawba River tributaries
  - [[Smith 1966]]: organic matter lost to a lake through eel migration
  - [[Alperin 1965]]: algal epibiosis and parasitism of a blueback herring
- **Environmental settings:** Microcosms, streams, rivers, estuaries, coral reefs, seagrass beds, kelp communities, Antarctic sea ice, and desert impoundments.

## Key Findings

- **Scale of compilation:** The Papers section lists hundreds of citations across roughly 48 pages (pages 3–51). The Books section (pages 52–54) lists about 50 monographs and edited volumes.
- **Compiler's own work:** Beyers is cited for a series on laboratory microecosystems:
  - [[Beyers 1963a]]: "The metabolism of twelve aquatic laboratory microecosystems," *Ecological Monographs* 33: 281–306.
  - [[Beyers 1963b]]: "A characteristic diurnal metabolic pattern in balanced microcosms."
  - [[Beyers 1965]]: pattern of photosynthesis and respiration in laboratory microecosystems.
  - [[Beyers 1966]]: metabolic similarities between symbiotic coelenterates and aquatic ecosystems.
  - [[Beyers and Odum 1959]]: use of carbon dioxide to construct pH curves for productivity measurement.
  - [[Beyers and Gillespie]]: measuring the carbon dioxide metabolism of aquatic organisms.
- **Foundational methods:**
  - [[Steemann-Nielsen 1952]]: use of radioactive carbon (C-14) for measuring organic production in the sea.
  - [[Ryther 1954]]: ratio of photosynthesis to respiration in marine plankton and its effect on productivity measurement.
  - [[Ryther 1956a]]: "The measurement of primary production."
  - [[Odum and Hoskin 1957]]: metabolism of a laboratory stream microcosm.
  - [[Strickland 1960]] (book): *Measuring the Production of Marine Phytoplankton*.
- **Key reviews and compendia:**
  - [[Doty 1962]]: *Proceedings of the Conference on Primary Productivity Measurement, Marine and Freshwater* (TID-7633).
  - [[Vinberg 1960]]: *The Primary Production of Bodies of Water* (AEC-TR-5692).
  - [[Westlake 1963]]: "Comparisons of plant productivity," *Biological Reviews*.
  - [[Strickland and Parsons 1965]]: *A Manual of Sea Water Analysis*.
- **Notable linked works:**
  - [[Odum 1957b]]: trophic structure and productivity of Silver Springs, Florida.
  - [[Odum and Odum 1955]]: trophic structure of a windward coral reef on Eniwetok Atoll.

- **Data quality caveats:** The compiler's note says the bibliography is unpublished and unverified. The OCR text contains many errors in author names, volume numbers, and page ranges. Entries should be checked against originals before citation.
- **Unpublished or incomplete items:** [[Pratt 1966]] "Biological Productivity Project – Final Report" (unpublished). Several entries have no year (marked "nd."). Some entries are duplicated (e.g., [[Steemann-Nielsen 1955b]] appears twice with different titles).
