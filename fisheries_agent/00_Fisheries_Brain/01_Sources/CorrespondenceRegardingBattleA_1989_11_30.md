## Executive Summary
This document is **not a primary scientific research paper** but a piece of institutional correspondence from the [[Desert Fishes Council]], dated November 30, 1989. The letter, authored by Executive Secretary [[E. P. Pister]], is addressed to Barbara Beaty of The University of Arizona Press regarding the publication of the book *[[Battle Against Extinction, Native Fish Management in The American West]]*, co-edited by [[W. L. Minckley]] of Arizona State University. The letter concerns funding commitments, pricing, and tax-exemption logistics for donations to support the book's publication and distribution — it contains no direct morphological, ecological, or taxonomic data on fish species. It is nonetheless a valuable historical artifact documenting the conservation advocacy efforts surrounding [[Desert Fish Conservation]] in the American West during the late 1980s.
