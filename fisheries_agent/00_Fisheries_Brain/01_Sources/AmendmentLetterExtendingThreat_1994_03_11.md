## Key Findings
Although this document contains no scientific findings, it establishes several important **regulatory and procedural facts** relevant to researchers or permit holders working with [[Threatened and Endangered Species]]:

- **Subpermit Extension:** The subpermit's expiration date is extended from December 15, 1993 to **December 31, 1994**.
- **Activity Restrictions:** Permit holders may continue only the same activities authorized in 1993; any new or modified activities require an amended subpermit prior to initiation.
- **Renewal Deadline:** Requests to renew the subpermit for 1995 must be received by the Field Supervisor of [[Ecological Services]] on or before **November 15, 1994** — earlier than in prior years, per the 36-day advance notice requirement in Service regulations.
- **Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Failure to submit a timely renewal request results in automatic invalidation of the subpermit upon its expiration date.
- **Appeal Rights:** Subpermit holders may appeal this permit modification under the appeal procedure specified in **50 CFR § 13.32**.
- **Reporting Requirements:** An annual activity report for 1993 was due December 31, 1993 (with a reminder to submit if outstanding); a 1994 activity report is due **December 31, 1994**, and must include the subpermit number, holder's name, site(s) of work, methods used, time spent, results, recovery action recommendations, recovery plan tasks addressed, and funding source(s).
- **Documentation Requirements:** This amendment letter, the subpermit itself, and the original mailed copy of permit **PRT-704930** must be carried by the permit holder or designated staff during all authorized field activities.

This letter is best understood as a **procedural artifact of the [[Endangered Species Act]] permitting system**, relevant to the administrative history of research or conservation activities conducted under a specific (unnamed in this excerpt) threatened or endangered species subpermit, rather than as a source of primary ichthyological or ecological data.
