# ASU Golf Course Fish Research Plan

#ichthyology #marine-biology #native-fish-stocking

## Executive Summary

This August 18, 1988 letter from [[Henry B. Delozier]], President of [[Sechler-Sollenberger Realty Investment Co.]], responds to a research proposal from [[Paul Marsh]] (letter dated August 4, 1988). Delozier accepts Marsh's proposal in principle, subject to the company's approval, to stock native fish in the golf course lakes at the [[Arizona State University]] (A.S.U.) golf course. The letter states that the native fish will be compatible with [[White Amur]], an algae-eating fish already used in the lakes, and that the project will not harm the [[Irrigation System]] or inconvenience golfers.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Business correspondence (letter), not a research paper.
- **Sender:** Henry B. Delozier, President.
- **Recipient:** Mr. G. M. Sollenberger, [[Sechler-Sollenberger Realty Investment Co.]], 4455 E. Camelback Rd., Suite 180-E, Phoenix, AZ 85018.
- **Copy to:** Prof. Paul Marsh.
- **Subject:** A.S.U. Golf Course Fish Research Plan.
- **Context:** Marsh's proposal was reviewed in a meeting with Delozier before this letter was written. The letter describes the faculty research collaboration as "very gratifying."

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Water bodies:** The [[Golf Course Lakes]] are the stocking sites. They are also linked to the irrigation system.
- **Existing biota:** [[White Amur]] are already used in the lakes as algae-eating fish. This suggests algae management is a concern, although the letter does not describe algae conditions or water quality data.
- **Compatibility:** Marsh reportedly assured the company that the native fish will be compatible with the white amur.
- **Potential impacts assessed:** Marsh stated that the fish will not negatively affect the irrigation system or cause undue inconvenience for golfers.
- **Data:** No quantitative data (species names, population sizes, water chemistry, or monitoring results) are provided.

## Key Findings

1. **Proposal accepted in principle:** The company tentatively approves the [[Fish Research Plan]] proposed by [[Paul Marsh]], subject to final approval.
2. **Compatibility:** Marsh indicates the native fish will be compatible with the white amur currently used in the lakes.
3. **No operational harm expected:** Marsh assures that the fish will neither harm the irrigation system nor inconvenience golfers.
4. **Proposed schedule:** Stocking of the lakes is planned to begin on September 14, with a second stocking on September 22 (the scan reads "September 32nd," which is not a valid date; 22nd is the likely intended date and should be verified against the original).
5. **Academic partnership:** The project creates an opportunity for interaction with [[Arizona State University]] faculty research programs, which the company values.
