# Bonytail Recaptures in Parker Strip: Agency Email Correspondence (2009)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #bonytail

## Executive Summary

This is a short 2009 email exchange between a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service biologist and the [[Native Fish Laboratory]] (fish.dr@nativefishlab.net). The sender asks how many of six [[Bonytail]] recaptured in the [[Parker Strip]] were found in backwaters, with particular interest in the [[La Paz County Park Backwater]]. The question references [[Schooley et al. 2008]], a survival summary for [[Razorback Sucker]] covering 2006–2008. The handwritten reply is largely illegible in the scan, so no answer to the backwater question can be reliably extracted.
