# Aquaculture in Fresh Waters: An Indexed List of Non-FAO Reference Books and Monographs, 1951-1997

#aquaculture #freshwater-fish #ichthyology #bibliography

## Executive Summary

This document is FAO Fisheries Circular No. 926 (FIRI/C926, ISSN 0429-9329), compiled by André G. Coche and published by the [[Food and Agriculture Organization]] in Rome in 1997. It is an indexed bibliography of about 630 selected non-FAO reference books and monographs on [[Freshwater Aquaculture]], covering publications from 1951 to 1997 in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish. Meeting proceedings are excluded. Most listed titles were less than fifteen years old at compilation and were still available from publishers or technical bookstores. The list is arranged chronologically, with alphabetical ordering by author within each year, and is supported by author, geographic, taxonomic and subject indexes. It complements [[Aquaculture in Marine Waters]] (FAO Fish. Circ. 925) and related FAO lists (FAO Fish. Circ. 924 and 808, Rev.1).

## Background & Description

- **Series:** FAO Fisheries Circular No. 926; ISSN 0429-9329; 71 pages.
- **Compiler:** André G. Coche, Consultant.
- **Publisher:** [[Food and Agriculture Organization]], Inland Water Resources and Aquaculture Service (FIRI), Rome, 1997.
- **Intended users:** Students, researchers, field workers and laymen seeking published information on aquaculture.
- **Scope:** About 630 titles; contents span general textbooks, species-specific monographs, diseases, nutrition, genetics, economics, water quality, legal aspects, and regional development.
- **Structure:** Bibliography (entries coded F000 to F534), Author Index, Geographic Index, Taxonomic Index, Subject Index, and a list of publisher addresses. An acronym list (page vi) defines institutions such as [[ICLARM]], [[IDRC]], [[SEAFDEC]], [[ORSTOM]], [[CIFRI]] and [[CMFRI]].
- **Revision plan:** The compiler states that subsequent revisions are planned to keep the lists current, and invites user suggestions to the Senior Adviser (Aquaculture Development), FIRI, FAO, Rome.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The bibliography addresses the freshwater environment as an operational setting for culture. Indexed subject areas relevant to freshwater systems include:

- **Pond and water management:** Pond construction, water quality management, water chemistry, aeration, filtration, fertilization and liming, and plankton culture and control (see [[Pond Aquaculture]] and [[Water Quality]]).
- **Integrated farming:** Integration with agriculture, animal husbandry, rice fields and sugarcane, and waste recycling and wastewater use (see [[Integrated Agriculture-Aquaculture]]).
- **Waste heat:** Aquaculture using heated effluents from power plants.
- **Reservoirs and lakes:** Biological control of reservoirs by fish, reservoir fisheries, and lake management.
- **Public health and environmental impact:** Pollution effects, effluent quality from fish farms, and guidelines for wastewater use in aquaculture.
- **Habitat and ecology:** Production ecology of ponds, habitats of aquatic systems, managed aquatic ecosystems, and floodplain rivers.

Taxonomic coverage of the Taxonomic Index includes salmonids, [[Carps]] (Chinese, common and Indian), [[Eels]] (Anguilla spp.), [[Tilapias]], [[Catfish]] (Clarias and Ictalurus punctatus), [[Sturgeons]] (Acipenser spp.), [[Black Bass]] (Micropterus spp.), [[Striped Bass]] (Morone saxatilis), [[Milkfish]] (Chanos chanos), [[Pike]] (Esox spp.), [[Perch]], [[Crayfish]], [[Freshwater Prawns]] (Macrobrachium rosenbergii), Colossoma spp., Cichlasoma urophthalmus, and Mullets, plus invertebrates such as [[Brine Shrimp]] (Artemia) and [[Freshwater Mollusca]].

## Key Findings

- The circular lists about 630 non-FAO books and monographs on freshwater aquaculture published 1951-1997 (the abstract's count of "about 630" is the only quantity given; the document does not report per-year totals).
- Languages covered are English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, with most titles in English.
- The index structure includes author, geographic, taxonomic and subject indexes, allowing retrieval by region, species, or topic.
- Major thematic clusters in the subject index are fish diseases and pathology, nutrition and feeds, genetics and breeding, hatchery and fry production, pond management, integrated farming, economics and marketing, and legal and regulatory aspects.
- Notable recurring multi-volume series include Fish Physiology (Hoar and Randall, eds), The Biology of Crustacea, The Mollusca, and Diseases of Fishes (Snieszko and Axelrod, eds).
- The circular is one of a set of three FAO bibliographic lists: marine aquaculture books (Fish. Circ. 925, 1997), FAO aquaculture publications (Fish. Circ. 924, 1997), and serials, proceedings and directories (Fish. Circ. 808, Rev.1, 1991).
- Access to the publisher list (pp. 65-71) provides addresses and fax numbers for major publishers, including [[Academic Press]], [[Blackwell Science]], [[CRC Press]], [[Elsevier]], [[Fishing News Books]], [[Pisces Press]] and [[T.F.H. Publications]].
- The document is a reference index and contains no original experimental data, sample sizes or ecological measurements.
