## Executive Summary

This document is a **draft research prospectus** (not a completed study) authored by W. L. Minckley, proposing a one-year, $5,000 investigation into the life history of the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*). The proposal outlines three core research thrusts: (1) embryological analysis using preserved material from a successful 1974 hatchery propagation event at [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]]; (2) morphological and ecological study of ontogenetic (larval-to-juvenile) development in young razorback suckers produced from that propagation; and (3) field study of adult razorback suckers in [[Lake Mohave]] via periodic collections for food habits and reproductive data, supplemented by underwater SCUBA observation and captive adult observation at [[Page Springs Hatchery]]. No experimental results, morphological data, or ecological findings are presented in this text — it is purely an administrative/funding document describing planned research direction, budget structure, and institutional support.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The proposal identifies the intended ecological study scope rather than reporting findings:
- **Habitat range targeted for study**: the mainstream [[Colorado River]] and the impounded reservoir habitat of [[Lake Mohave]], representing both riverine (lotic) and reservoir (lentic) environments occupied by the species.
- **Life stage-habitat linkage**: adults would be sampled from Lake Mohave (via monthly/bi-monthly netting or collection) and observed underwater in situ using SCUBA, while early life stages (embryos, larvae, juveniles) would be studied ex situ at hatchery/laboratory facilities (Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery, Page Springs Hatchery, ASU aquaria).
- **Trophic ecology**: food habits of adult razorback suckers in Lake Mohave were proposed as a subject of investigation, though no dietary data are yet presented.
- **Reproductive ecology**: reproductive data collection from wild adults was proposed, complementing the hatchery-based embryological work, indicating the species' spawning biology was a targeted knowledge gap.

*No empirical findings, data, or results are contained in this document.* As a research prospectus, its substantive content consists of proposed objectives and logistics rather than scientific conclusions. Notable programmatic points include:
