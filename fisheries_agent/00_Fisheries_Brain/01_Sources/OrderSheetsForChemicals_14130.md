# Gila orcutti Diploid/Tetraploid Research Procurement Correspondence (1983)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a 1983 cover letter and six purchase order sheets from [[Don Buth]], Assistant Professor in the Department of Biology at the [[University of California Los Angeles]], to [[William L. Minckley]] at [[Arizona State University]]. It concerns chemical and starch orders for a planned comparative study of *Gila orcutti*, a cyprinid fish. Buth had already used local *G. orcutti* as a control in [[Diploid-Tetraploid Comparisons]] and had buffer systems in place, so he expected to move directly to comparisons. The letter asks that two starch purchases be handled as [[Sole-Source Purchasing]]. The order sheets total about $1,974 by my addition of the line items, just under the roughly $2,000 limit the letter mentions. The document contains no field, habitat, or population data.

## Background & Description

**Correspondence (page 1).** Buth wrote on 24 August 1983. He enclosed six order sheets totalling "close to $2000 but not over that amount." He asked that the starches be treated as sole-source items, because he believed they were available only from their specific suppliers. He grouped the [[Sigma Chemical Company]] orders by storage requirement: frozen, refrigerated (not frozen), and no special treatment. He also asked Minckley to ship frozen specimens by bus, a method that had worked before. Buth would call Minckley to arrange pickup in town.

**Purchase orders (pages 2–7).** Each sheet lists vendor, funding source (Grant), department approval fields, and itemized catalog lines. The six orders are:

- **[[Fisher Scientific Company]]** (Austin, CA): 3 × 2 kg hydrolyzed starch (catalog S-676) at $67.00 each, $201.00.
- **[[Electrostarch Company]]** (Madison, WI): 2 × 8 kg Electrostarch (lot 392) at $95.00 each, $190.00.
- **Sigma Chemical Company, order 1** (St. Louis, MO; marked "Keep refrigerated at all times"): five enzymes and related reagents, about $554.95. Items include nucleoside phosphorylase, glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (12 × 1000 units, $328.20), xanthine oxidase, glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase, and aldolase.
- **Sigma Chemical Company, order 2**: 10 kg TRIZMA base (T 1503) at $203.50.
- **Sigma Chemical Company, order 3** (marked "Keep frozen at all times"): about $799.50 across roughly eleven lines, including Nitro Blue Tetrazolium, NADP, hypoxanthine, glucose-1-phosphate, fructose-6-phosphate, DL-isocitric acid, adenosine 5'-diphosphate, cis-aconitic acid, MTT, and 1 g of snake venom (*Crotalus adamanteus*, $90.00).
- **Sigma Chemical Company, order 4** (marked "Keep frozen at all times"): 1 g 4-methylumbelliferyl-β-D-glucoside, $25.00.

## Key Findings

- **Research aim:** Comparative work on diploid and tetraploid forms of *Gila orcutti*, with the local diploid population serving as the control.
- **Methods readiness:** Buth already had the buffer systems working for *G. orcutti*, so comparisons could begin immediately after reagents and specimens arrived.
- **Specimen logistics:** Frozen fish samples were to be sent from Arizona State by bus, with Buth collecting them downtown.
- **Procurement:** Six orders totalling about $1,974 (my sum of the line items; the letter gives only "close to $2000 but not over"), paid from a grant.
- **Sole-source request:** Two starch products (hydrolyzed starch and Electrostarch) were to be treated as sole-source purchases.
- **Storage control:** Reagents were split into frozen, refrigerated, and room-temperature groups, with explicit "keep frozen" and "keep refrigerated" warnings on the orders.
- **Reagent profile:** The Sigma orders cover enzymes, coenzymes, substrates, buffers, and tetrazolium indicators, consistent with enzyme-based biochemical assays on the specimens.
