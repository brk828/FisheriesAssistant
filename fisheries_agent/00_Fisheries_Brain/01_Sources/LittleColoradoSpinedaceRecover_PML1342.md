# Little Colorado Spinedace Recovery Team: Team Split Request, Agenda, and 2008 Field Updates

#ichthyology #marine-biology #spinedace

## Executive Summary

This document bundles three items from the [[Little Colorado Spinedace]] recovery effort: a January 28, 2009 meeting agenda, a January 26, 2009 letter to the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] requesting formal separation of the spinedace recovery team from the [[Apache Trout]] team, and handwritten 2008 field notes on spinedace status at several creek sites. The letter is the central proposal. The sub-teams, which had operated informally since 2006, unanimously recommended becoming separate teams because quorum problems were delaying business. The field notes show spinedace (coded LEVI in the text) present at most monitored sites in 2008, with no non-native fish collected at several of them. Much of the handwritten material is partly illegible after scanning, so the field data are reported only where legible.

## Background & Description

The species is referred to throughout the document as spinedace, with the code **LEVI** used for the fish in field records. The document does not give a scientific name, family, or detailed morphology. The Little Colorado spinedace is managed under a formal [[Recovery Team]] structure, and the team has historically shared membership with the [[Apache Trout]] recovery effort.

**Meeting agenda (January 28, 2009, [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], Phoenix).** The agenda lists eight items:
1. Status of the letter to the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] requesting a formal split from the Apache trout team (led by [[Rob Clarkson]]).
2. Recommendations for spinedace team membership changes.
3. Update on planning for the [[Blue Ridge Reservoir]] / [[East Clear Creek]] chemical renovation.
4. Update on contact with the [[USGS]] about developing taxon-specific pesticides.
5. Update on on-the-ground monitoring, conservation activities, and upcoming projects.
6. A brief revisit of the upper [[Chevelon Canyon]] native fish restoration project.
7. Other topics and scheduling of the next meeting.

Numbering in the scan is partly garbled, so the order above is a reconstruction.

**Team split letter (January 26, 2009).** The letter, addressed to Regional Director [[Benjamin Tuggle]], states:
- In 2006 the Apache Trout/Little Colorado Spinedace Recovery Team informally split into two sub-teams, each with a co-team lead and separate meetings. The authors believe this improved both sub-teams and recovery prospects.
- Some meetings lacked a quorum of voting members, forcing team business to be deferred.
- Following input from Service liaisons, a quorum vote at the last spinedace sub-team meeting was **unanimous** in favor of forming separate teams.
- The authors request Service concurrence. If granted, [[Larry Riley]] (Apache trout team, Arizona Game and Fish Department) and Rob Clarkson (spinedace team, [[Bureau of Reclamation]]) would become formal team leaders. Current members would be offered continued membership on one or both teams, and membership changes would then go to the Service for approval.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The 2008 field notes cover several creek and pool sites used for spinedace stocking and monitoring. Conditions are described qualitatively and are only partly legible after scanning.

- **[[Bear Canyon]] (7-3-08):** 3 young-of-year (YOY) LEVI were collected below the 95 road crossing, near the 2003 and 2004 stocking location. A long dry stretch lies below the upper stocking site (2005 and 2006 stocking sites), and no LEVI were collected there despite a wet year. No non-native fish were collected.
- **[[Dane Spring]] (7-7-08):** 1 LEVI was collected at the stocking location near the spring. The creek was flowing continuously with high water. No non-native fish were collected.
- **[[Yeager Creek]] stocking pools:** About 30 adults and one YOY spinedace were observed in Stocking Pool #2 in May. On 7-8-08, about 30–40 adults and 25 YOY were observed in Stocking Pool #2. On the same date, **167 LEVI from [[West Leonard Creek]] were introduced into Stocking Pools #1 and #2, split evenly**. Flow looked good.
- **[[West Leonard Creek]] (7-8-08):** Over 300 spinedace were collected during a seining effort ("Honey Hole" area; effort duration partly illegible). All size classes of LEVI were present, up to roughly 6–7 inches. LEVI dominated the catch. Other species coded CEBL, CAIN, and RHOS were also collected, and no non-native fish were collected. The creek and water level were described as excellent.
- **[[Dines Tank]]:** LEVI were observed and collected in May and June.
- **[[Arboretum]] site:** Choked with weeds. No LEVI were collected this year.

The text uses codes for several co-occurring species (CEBL, CAIN, RHOS) without expanding them, so they cannot be identified from this document.

## Key Findings

- **Team restructuring is the main proposal.** The sub-teams unanimously recommend formal separation from the Apache trout team, with Larry Riley and Rob Clarkson as proposed leaders. Approval by the Service is pending.
- **Quorum failures** have repeatedly deferred team business, which is the stated justification for separation.
- **Membership changes** for both teams are planned and will go to the Service for approval.
- **Spinedace were present at most monitored 2008 sites.** Notable counts include 167 LEVI translocated from West Leonard Creek to Yeager Creek stocking pools, over 300 spinedace at West Leonard Creek, and 25 YOY plus 30–40 adults at Yeager Creek Pool #2.
- **Non-native fish were not collected** at Bear Canyon, Dane Spring, and West Leonard Creek. The text does not report non-native status for Yeager Creek or Dines Tank.
- **Habitat problems** are noted at two sites: a dry stretch below the upper Bear Canyon stocking location, and a weed-choked Arboretum site where no spinedace were collected.
- **Ongoing management topics** include the planned chemical renovation of Blue Ridge Reservoir and East Clear Creek, USGS work on taxon-specific pesticides, and a revisit of the upper Chevelon Canyon native fish restoration project.
- **Data limitations:** Field data are incomplete and partly illegible, the meeting outcomes are not recorded, and the letter's approval status is not stated.
