# Black River and Tributaries Fisheries Survey, June 1997

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

A four-day fisheries survey (June 10–13, 1997) of the [[Black River]], [[Nutrioso Creek]], [[Luna Lake]], and associated tributaries in Apache and Greenlee Counties, Arizona, was conducted by Arizona State University personnel (Brian Bagley, Terry Inman, Patti Sowka). The main objective was to define the upstream limits of [[Smallmouth Bass]] and [[Roundtail Chub]] in the Black River. Twelve fish species were collected. Smallmouth bass were observed about 5 miles upstream of the [[Fort Apache Indian Reservation]] boundary, and roundtail chub were found as far upstream as [[Bear Creek]], about 15 miles above the boundary.

## Background & Description

The survey covered a set of sampling localities across the Black River system, including the confluence of the East and West Forks, several points along the river downstream of Bear Creek and Forest Service Road 25, [[Reservation Creek]], [[Beaver Creek]] at Thomas Creek, the North Fork of the East Fork at Three Forks, Nutrioso Creek two miles below Nelson Reservoir, and Luna Lake near the boat launch. Field data are presented in Table 1 and Appendix A, with site maps in Figures 1–5.

**Methods**

- Habitats were sampled with a gas-powered Smith-Root DC [[Backpack Electrofishing]] unit, dip nets, [[Gill Net]]s, [[Trammel Net]]s, and seines.
- Catch was reported as number of fish caught and [[Catch Per Unit Effort]] (fish per minute of shocking) in Table 1.
- Appendix A records site description, date, location (township/range/section), habitat, shocking time, and net set details for each site.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Species collected (12 total):**
- Salmonids: [[Rainbow Trout]], [[Brown Trout]], [[Brook Trout]]
- Cyprinids: [[Roundtail Chub]], [[Little Colorado River Spinedace]], [[Speckled Dace]], [[Loach Minnow]], [[Fathead Minnow]]
- Catostomids: [[Sonora Sucker]], [[Desert Sucker]], [[Bluehead Sucker]]
- Centrarchids: [[Smallmouth Bass]]

**Habitat notes from Appendix A:**
- [[Crayfish]] were present at most sites. The exception was [[Reservation Creek]] immediately below the reservation boundary, where none were recorded.
- Substrates ranged from silt and cobble (Beaver Creek, Luna Lake) to boulder riffles (Black River 1/8 mile below Bear Creek) and cobble/boulder with riffle-pool structure (Reservation Creek).
- The Black River pool at the East/West Fork confluence was about 40 × 10 × 1 m, and the team found no adult fish there, only larval fish.
- Bear Creek had multiple man-made "fallen log" drop structures.
- Snails were abundant at Luna Lake.
- A [[Native Clam]] was found at the Black River confluence and at several downstream sites.

## Key Findings

- **Species richness:** 12 species were collected across all sites.
- **Upstream limits:** Smallmouth bass were observed 5 miles upstream of the reservation boundary. Roundtail chub reached Bear Creek, 15 miles upstream of the boundary.
- **Roundtail chub:** Counts included 8 fish at the Black River ⅜ mile below Bear Creek (5 preserved), 6 at Black River at FS Road 25, and 1 captured plus 10 juveniles observed at the site 1.5 miles below Reservation Creek.
- **Smallmouth bass:** One individual was observed (not captured) at the site 1.5 miles below Reservation Creek.
- **Largest catches:**
  - North Fork of the East Fork Black River at Three Forks: 352 fish total, including 265 speckled dace and 81 desert sucker.
  - Beaver Creek at Thomas Creek: 133 fish total (Appendix A), dominated by 112 speckled dace. The listed components sum to 123, so the reported total may include an unlisted record.
  - Nutrioso Creek 2 miles below Nelson Reservoir: 139 fish total, including 95 fathead minnow, 26 speckled dace, 15 Little Colorado River spinedace, and 3 bluehead sucker.
- **Other sites:**
  - Bear Creek: 18 fish (17 brown trout, 1 rainbow trout) in 456 seconds of shocking.
  - Reservation Creek: 36 fish (26 brown trout, 10 speckled dace) in 960 seconds of shocking.
  - Luna Lake near the boat launch: 15 fathead minnow in 213 seconds of shocking.
- **Trammel and gill netting** produced few captures. The 20 m trammel net at the Black River confluence and several other pools yielded no or very few fish.
- **Native clams** were recorded at Black River sites, indicating mussel-bearing habitat along the river.
