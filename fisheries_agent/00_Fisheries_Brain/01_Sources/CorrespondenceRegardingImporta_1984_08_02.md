## Executive Summary

This document is **not a primary scientific research paper** but rather an official administrative letter from the California Fish and Game Commission (dated August 2, 1984) to Dr. W. L. Minckley, a representative of the [[Desert Fishes Council]] at Arizona State University. The letter acknowledges receipt of Minckley's correspondence expressing concern over a request by **United Energy Corporation** (Foster City, CA) to import and possess exotic fish species for research purposes. The Commission indicates the matter would be reviewed at its August 3rd meeting in Santa Barbara. No taxonomic, morphological, or ecological data on specific fish species are provided in this text — it is purely a procedural/regulatory communication concerning the oversight of [[Exotic Species Introduction|exotic species introductions]] into California waters.
