## Executive Summary

This paper does not concern fish, but rather describes a new species of harpacticoid copepod, **[[Nitocrella morettii]]**, collected from phreatic (groundwater) waters near Gualdo Tadino, Perugia, in Umbria, central Italy. The species belongs to the genus **[[Nitocrella]]** (family [[Ameiridae]], subfamily Ameirinae) as revised by Petkovski (1976), and specifically to the **hirta-group** of species within that genus. The new species is most closely related to **[[Nitocrella psammophila]]** Chappuis, 1955, a species known from interstitial waters of northern Italy. The paper also provides a comprehensive, updated taxonomic key to all recognized species of *Nitocrella* sensu Petkovski (1976), spanning over 40 couplets and encompassing species distributed globally (Europe, Asia, North America, Cuba, etc.).

While outside the scope of ichthyology proper, this work is relevant to aquatic ecosystem science as it documents the invertebrate stygofauna (subterranean groundwater fauna) associated with phreatic aquifers — habitats that can be ecologically linked to fish-bearing spring and karst systems.

## Background & Description

**Female (holotype/paratypes):**
- Body slender, gradually tapering posteriorly.
- Total body length (excluding antennae, antennulae, and furcal setae): **0.48–0.55 mm**.
- Thoracic segments: distal margins without spines or denticulations.
- Abdominal segments: dorsal margin unarmed; ventral margin bears a row of small spinules posteriorly; last abdominal segment with a row of **10–12 spines** at the base of each furcal ramus.
- Anal operculum slightly convex, armed with **9–10 spines**.
- Furcal rami: long, about twice as long as wide (L/W ratio = 1.90–1.95); armed with 4 apical setae (outer seta ~2x longer than inner), 1 long subapical external seta, and a dorsal seta approximately as long as the outer apical seta.
- Antenna 1: 8-segmented; aesthete on the 4th segment overreaching the last segment.
- Antenna 2: exopod 1-segmented, armed with 3 plumose setae.
- Leg 1 (P1): exopod and endopod both 3-segmented.
- Legs 2–4 (P2–P4): endopod 2-segmented, exopod 3-segmented.
- Leg 5: basiendopod short, armed with 3 spines (asymmetric variant: 3 left/4 right in one specimen) plus 1 outer slender seta; exopod ovoidal, 1.75–1.80x longer than wide, armed with 4 setae.

**Male:**
- Body length (excluding furcal setae, antennae, antennulae): **0.53 mm**.
- Antenna 1: transformed (geniculate, typical of copepod males).
- Leg 5: basiendopod with 2 spines and 1 outer seta; exopod ovoidal (as in female), armed with 5 setae.
- Leg 6: a chitinous lamella bearing 2 setae of unequal length, the outer being the longest.

**Setal formula (Legs 1–4):** Exopod/endopod armature recorded as 022/020 pattern variants across P1–P4 (per original tables).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat type:** Phreatic subterranean (groundwater) freshwater — specifically a fresh-water well with a sandstone-and-clay bottom sediment.
- **Type locality conditions:** Water level 8.5 m from soil surface; water depth 5.5 m; temperature 14.9°C; pH 6.9.
- **Associated stygofauna** at the type locality (co-occurring subterranean species), indicating a diverse groundwater invertebrate community:
  - Harpacticoid copepod: **[[Atheyella crassa]]**
  - Cyclopoid copepods: **[[Diacyclops languidoides hypnicola]]**, **[[Eucyclops subterraneus]]**
  - Amphipods: **[[Niphargus longicaudatus]]**, **[[Salentinella angelieri]]**
  - Isopods: **[[Proasellus]]** sp. (Asellidae)
  - Syncarids, ostracods, and water mites (Acari)
- This assemblage is characteristic of **stygobiont** communities inhabiting karstic/phreatic aquifers of central Italy, a system of ecological relevance to groundwater-dependent surface ecosystems, including spring-fed habitats that can support fish populations downstream.

## Key Findings

- **New species described:** *Nitocrella morettii* n.sp., named in honor of Prof. Giampaolo Moretti, Director of the Zoological Institute, University of Perugia, in recognition of his promotion of faunistic research in Umbria.
- **Taxonomic placement:** Assigned to the **hirta-group** of *Nitocrella* based on the setation of the last exopod segment of leg 4 (P4), following Petkovski's (1976) generic revision.
- **Closest relative:** *N. psammophila* Chappuis, 1955 — shared characters include the armature of legs 2–4 endopods and an anal operculum armed with spines.
- **Diagnostic differences from *N. psammophila*:**
  - Structure and armature of the furcal rami (in *N. morettii*, the inner furcal seta is **shorter** than the external one, versus **longer** in *N. psammophila*).
  - Length of setae on endopods of legs 2–4.
  - Setation of male P5 exopod: *N. morettii* has **5 setae** vs. **4 setae** in *N. psammophila*.
- **Comprehensive key provided:** The paper presents a fully revised dichotomous key to all known species of *Nitocrella* sensu Petkovski (1976), covering approximately 30+ species worldwide, including *N. tonsa*, *N. hofmilleri*, *N. calcaripes*, *N. slovenica*, *N. omega*, *N. hirta* (and subspecies *tirolensis*), *N. skvrensis*, *N. gutturna*, *N. maggii*, *N. delayi*, *N. chappuisi*, *N. africana*, *N. kyzylkumica*, *N. petkovskii*, *N. hypogaea*, *N. asiatica*, *N. sterbaii*, *N. japonica*, *N. cubanorum*, *N. rhodiensis*, *N. stammeri*, *N. achaiae*, *N. neurica*, *N. rosswigi*, *N. dussarti*, *N. gracilis*, *N. jankowskajae*, *N. aestuarina*, *N. monchenkoi*, *N. kirgizika*, *N. mara*, *N. negreai*, *N. caroloi*, *N. paceae*, *N. vasconica*, *N. unispinosa*, *N. yokotai*, *N. molasi*, *N. afghanica*, and *N. nana*.
- **Broader significance:** Reinforces the taxonomic diversity and endemism of the genus *Nitocrella* within European and global groundwater systems, and documents a novel stygobiont species from a previously under-sampled phreatic aquifer in central Italy, contributing to the "Contribution to knowledge of the underground water fauna in central and southern Italy" series (installment XXVII).
