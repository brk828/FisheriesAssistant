# Dexter NFH & TC Operations and Maintenance Cost Data, FY1996

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-hatchery

## Executive Summary

This is a one-page-set cost form (Form 3-110, Rev 1/89) for the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery and Technology Center]] (Dexter NFH & TC) for [[Fiscal Year 1996]]. It reports operations and maintenance expenditures by category and funding source. The document contains no species, habitat, or biological data. The total expenditure reported is **$590,905.63**, most of it under Operations (Fisheries). Many cells are blank or garbled by scanning, so only the column totals can be reported with confidence.

## Background & Description

The form is a standard operations and maintenance cost template for a [[Fish Hatchery Operations|fish hatchery]] station. It has four funding columns:

1. **Operations (Fisheries)**
2. **Cyclical Maintenance (Fisheries)**
3. **Quarters Maintenance**
4. **Other Funding**

The cost categories are:

- **Personnel:** permanent and temporary salaries, including benefits.
- **Operating costs:** utilities (telephone, electricity, heating oil, natural gas, water and garbage).
- **Vehicle costs:** maintenance of distribution and non-distribution vehicles, mileage, and fuel.
- **Supplies:** [[Fish Food]], chemicals and drugs, tags and tagging supplies, and office, custodial, fish cultural, and other supplies.
- **Travel and moving expenses.**
- **Miscellaneous items:** listed as contaminants work, cryopreservation and genetics equipment and supplies, contracts, training, equipment repair, and a wetlands project.
- **Totals:** operations, vehicle and equipment purchases over $1,000, cyclical maintenance, quarters maintenance, and total expenditures.

The personnel, mileage, and chemicals/drugs lines are blank or unreadable in the scan.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address ecology, species, water quality, or habitat. The only environmental link is the miscellaneous category, which mentions:

- A **contaminants work** item, which may relate to monitoring of water or fish contaminants.
- **Cryopreservation and genetics** equipment, which supports [[Genetics Management]] and broodstock conservation.
- A **wetlands project**, which implies habitat-related work outside the core hatchery operations.

These are listed only as expense categories. The form gives no detail on what the work involved, where it took place, or what it found.
