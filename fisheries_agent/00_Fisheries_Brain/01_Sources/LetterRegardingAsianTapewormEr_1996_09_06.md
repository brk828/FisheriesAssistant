# Letter Regarding Asian Tapeworm Eradication Strategy at San Bernardino NWR

#ichthyology #marine-biology #parasitology #Bothriocephalidae #conservation

## Executive Summary
This 1996 letter from the [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] Refuge Manager to Dr. W. L. Minckley (Arizona State University) documents the discovery of the exotic [[Asian Tapeworm]] parasite infecting native fish populations on the refuge. The correspondence outlines refuge staff's intent to develop an eradication strategy that simultaneously improves broader native species management, including habitat conditions for the [[Chiricahua Leopard Frog]] and [[Huachuca Water Umbel]]. An information-gathering stakeholder meeting was scheduled for October 9, 1996, at The Nature Conservancy offices in Tucson, Arizona, to solicit expert input before finalizing management action.
