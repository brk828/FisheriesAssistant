# 1994 Fall Fish Count Volunteer Recruitment Letter

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-communities

## Executive Summary

This August 1994 letter from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (Nongame Branch, [[Native Fish Program]]) invites past and prospective volunteers to take part in the 1994 [[Fall Fish Count]]. The count is a long-term monitoring program, started in 1988, that collects data on the fish communities of Arizona's streams. The letter is a volunteer mailing list update and contains no species-level data, counts, or statistics.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Form correspondence (letter to prospective volunteers), not a research report.
- **Issuing agency:** [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], Phoenix, Arizona.
- **Program:** [[Native Fish Program]], Nongame Branch.
- **Signatory:** Mike Lopez, Field Team Leader.
- **Program origin:** Initiated in 1988 "as a means of collecting long-term data on the fish communities of Arizona's streams."
- **Context:** The letter says the mailing list is being updated for the 1994 survey and that some recipients may not have received the original letter because of an agency error.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Focus:** Freshwater stream fish communities in Arizona. The letter names no species, habitats, sites, or water-quality parameters.
- **Monitoring purpose:** The stated goal is long-term tracking of fish community composition across [[Arizona Streams]].
- **Data value:** The letter notes that "much useful data has been collected" through past volunteer efforts.

## Key Findings

1. **Survey recruitment:** The agency is compiling an updated mailing list for the 1994 [[Fall Fish Count]] and asks interested people to respond.
2. **Outreach request:** Recipients are asked to pass the letter to friends, associates, and past volunteers who may have moved or not received it.
3. **Opt-out option:** Anyone who wants removal from the listings is asked to say so.
4. **Contact method:** Responses go by mail to the [[Native Fish Program]] at the Arizona Game and Fish Department office in Phoenix, or by phone at (602) 548-1089.
5. **Program continuity:** The count has run since 1988, and the agency credits past volunteers for the data collected so far.
6. **Data outcomes:** No results, species lists, sample sizes, or statistics are reported, because the letter is a recruitment notice only.
