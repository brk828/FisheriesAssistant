## Key Findings

Since no fish or aquatic ecosystem science is present, the "key findings" below summarize the document's actual (administrative) content for reference purposes:

- The **[[CESU Network]]** was formally established via a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signed by federal agencies on 22–23 June 1999 in Washington, D.C., hosted by then-Secretary of the Interior Bruce Babbitt.
- Founding federal partner agencies included the **Bureau of Land Management**, **Department of Energy**, **National Park Service**, **U.S. Bureau of Reclamation**, **U.S. Forest Service**, and **U.S. Geological Survey**.
- Each CESU is structured around six key elements: (1) multiple federal agencies, (2) a host university, (3) partner institutions, (4) a role and mission statement, (5) a managers committee, and (6) strategic/annual work plans.
- The program explicitly aims to increase **diversity** in the federal science workforce by partnering with Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs), Predominantly Hispanic Serving Institutions (HSIs), and Native American Tribal Colleges (NATCs).
- A second round of CESU competitions, targeting additional biogeographic regions, was announced for FY2000.
- The program was scheduled for independent peer review after five years of operation.
