## Executive Summary

This completion report, authored by James R. Bruce for the Arizona Game and Fish Department, summarizes fishery management activities across District I lakes and streams during the 1960 work period (Project F-7-R-3). The central recurring issue identified is **irrigation-driven water drawdown**, which severely impacts fishery productivity at [[Luna Lake]], [[Rainbow Lake]], [[Concho Lake]], [[Lyman Lake]], and several other reservoirs. The report details stocking outcomes, creel census results, growth rates, and forage species introduction trials (notably [[Threadfin Shad]]) across multiple water bodies, alongside efforts to survey and preserve populations of [[Native Trout|Native Apache Trout]] in headwater streams.

## Background & Description

Quantitative fish size and growth data reported across water bodies:

- **[[Big Lake]]**: Post-winter-kill restocking (12,000 lbs) resulted in fish averaging **9.02–10.78 inches**, with an overall seasonal average of **9.97 inches**.
- **[[Crescent Lake]]**: Gillnet sampling indicated "good" growth during summer months (no specific length data given).
- **[[Nelson Lake]]**: Harvested fish averaged **9.1 inches** in length, contributing to a total biomass harvest of 8,324 lbs (119 lbs/acre).
- **[[Woods Canyon Lake]]**: Resident trout population noted for a "relatively poor growth rate," though showing slight annual improvement attributed to increased harvest pressure (reducing competition/density).
- **[[Luna Lake]] Kokanee**: Stocked Kokanee salmon (spring 1958) exhibited an "excellent growth rate" despite minimal contribution to angler catch.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat Stressors**: The dominant abiotic stressor across District I waters is anthropogenic **water drawdown** for irrigation, which degrades water quality/volume at [[Luna Lake]], [[Rainbow Lake]], [[Concho Lake]], [[River Reservoir]], [[Bunch Reservoir]], [[Tunnel Reservoir]], [[Nelson Lake]], and [[Lyman Lake]].
- **Winter-Kill Dynamics**: [[Big Lake]] and [[Crescent Lake]] are subject to seasonal winter-kill events, prompting proposals to divert additional runoff water into the lake basin to increase depth/oxygenation and mitigate anoxia.
- **Forage Species Introduction**: [[Threadfin Shad]] were introduced at [[Woods Canyon Lake]] as an experimental forage base for trout; the species successfully survived and reproduced, though utilization by trout as prey remained undetermined. A second planting was recommended.
- **Marginal Habitat Classification**: [[Lyman Lake]] is characterized as ecologically "marginal" — insufficient thermal/chemical stability to fully support either a coldwater (trout) or warmwater (rough fish/bass) fishery, compounded by heavy rough fish competition and water fluctuation.
- **Native Species Refugia**: Headwaters of **Bonito Creek** and **Ord Creek** were identified as strongholds for [[Native Trout]] populations, representing critical refugia for the species' historic range.
- **Aquatic Vegetation**: Excessive aquatic vegetation growth (linked to drawdown-related water quality decline) identified as a management concern at [[Big Lake]], [[Luna Lake]], and [[Rainbow Lake]].
- **Stream Habitat Comparison**: [[East Fork Black River]] and [[West Fork Black River]] showed differential fishing pressure/success despite similar stocking-return rates, suggesting habitat carrying capacity differences.

## Key Findings

- **[[Big Lake]]**: Catch-per-hour rose from 0.27 (May) to 1.0 (October) following restocking with 12,000 lbs of fish; ~12,000 lbs harvested over the season. Creel census (3 days/month) recommended for 1961.
- **[[Luna Lake]]**: Fall fingerling stocking (Sept–Oct 1959) significantly boosted 1960 summer catch rates — the highest recorded in 3 years of census. Harvest reached **85 lbs/acre** (vs. historic high of 109 lbs/acre in 1958). Fall stocking recommended for expansion to other lakes.
- **[[Nelson Lake]]**: Sustained excellent fishery performance; average catch of **1.1 fish/man-hour** (range 0.7–1.29); total harvest ~28,703 fish (8,324 lbs / 119 lbs per acre). Current stocking levels recommended to continue.
- **[[Show Low Lake]]**: Post-renovation restocking (1959) led to slow-quality fishing until September, improving to "good to excellent" thereafter. Rough fish resurgence anticipated as a future management concern.
- **[[Woods Canyon Lake]]**: [[Threadfin Shad]] forage trial successful in survival/reproduction; trout utilization inconclusive — second stocking planned.
- **[[Rainbow Lake]]**: Post-1959 renovation stocking produced satisfactory 1960 fishing; increased stocking rate and partial creel census recommended for 1961.
- **[[Fool Hollow Lake]]**: Good fishing achieved despite chronic basin leakage causing water loss of **6–12 inches/day**; leak repair is prerequisite for stable management planning.
- **[[Concho Lake]] & [[Greer Lakes]]**: Chronic irrigation drawdown limits these to "fair to good" fisheries for limited angler numbers; fish kills recorded on [[Concho Lake]] and [[Tunnel Reservoir]] in 1960 due to excessive drawdown.
- **[[Lee Valley Lake]]**: Successfully managed as a dedicated **[[Brook Trout]]** fishery; continued brook trout stocking recommended despite low poundage yield, due to high angler satisfaction.
- **[[Lyman Lake]]**: Multi-factor management challenge (fluctuation, rough fish, marginal thermal regime); Department pursuing **[[Walleye]]** introduction and [[Threadfin Shad]] forage trials; renovation to control rough fish under consideration.
- **[[St. Johns Reservoir]]**: Newly renovated and stocked (August 1960) with **bass** and **channel catfish**; baseline study site with no prior data.
- **Stream Census ([[Black River]])**: East Fork yielded 1.1 fish/man-hour vs. West Fork's 0.4 fish/man-hour, though catch-per-trip was comparable (2.65 vs. 2.1). Recommendation: reduce East Fork stocking by 20%, increase West Fork stocking by 20%.
- **[[Native Trout]] Survey**: Confirmed viable populations in Bonito and Ord Creek headwaters; hatchery propagation attempt from Pinetop Hatchery broodstock largely unsuccessful. Continued range survey recommended for 1961.
- **Aquatic Vegetation Control**: Large-scale control program proposed for 1961 targeting [[Big Lake]], [[Luna Lake]], and [[Rainbow Lake]].
