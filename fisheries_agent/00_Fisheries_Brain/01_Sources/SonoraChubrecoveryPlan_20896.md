# Sonora Chub Recovery Plan Review Memorandum

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Cyprinidae #endangered-species #recovery-planning

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service internal memorandum, dated March 16, 1992, documents the Regional Office review of a draft recovery plan for the [[Sonora Chub]] (Gila ditaenia). The Assistant Regional Director for Fish and Wildlife Enhancement in Region 2 (Albuquerque) provides feedback to the Field Supervisor in Phoenix regarding the draft plan prepared by Jerry Stefferud and submitted July 16, 1991. The memorandum indicates that modifications were being made to better reflect population status in Mexico and desired recovery actions.

## Background & Description

The [[Sonora Chub]] is a cyprinid fish species requiring recovery planning under the Endangered Species Act. The draft recovery plan was prepared by Jerry Stefferud and transmitted to the Phoenix Ecological Services office in July 1991. W.L. Minckley of [[Arizona State University]] served as the Desert Fishes Recovery Team Leader. The document represents internal agency coordination between USFWS Region 2 offices in Albuquerque and Phoenix regarding recovery plan development and review procedures.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The memorandum references [[Sonora Chub]] populations occurring in both the United States (Arizona) and Mexico. The species is part of the broader desert fishes conservation effort coordinated through the Desert Fishes Recovery Team. The document indicates that understanding of population status in Mexico was evolving during the plan development process, requiring modifications to the draft recovery plan to accurately reflect transboundary conservation needs.

## Key Findings

- The Regional Office completed review of the draft [[Sonora Chub]] recovery plan and provided attached comments for consideration by the Phoenix Field Office.
- Some modifications had already been made to the plan to more accurately reflect the status of [[Sonora Chub]] populations in Mexico and desired recovery actions.
- The Regional Office acknowledged that some review comments may no longer be relevant due to these modifications.
- Region 2 implemented enhanced tracking procedures for all recovery plans under its lead responsibility, with commitment to expedited turnaround times for plan reviews.
- The Regional Office anticipated receiving an updated draft recovery plan and Federal Register notice for public review.
- Contact person for questions was George Divine (FTS 474-3972).
