# Hoover Dam Powerplant Uprating and Lower Colorado River Coordination Memorandum (1993)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #endangered-species-management

## Executive Summary

This 1993 interagency memorandum from the [[Bureau Of Reclamation]] (Lower Colorado Regional Office, Boulder City, Nevada) to the Arizona Ecological Services State Office of the [[Fish And Wildlife Service]] confirms a coordination meeting scheduled for November 23, 1993. The meeting was originally framed around the [[Hoover Dam]] powerplant uprating, but Reclamation broadened its scope to the full [[Lower Colorado River]]. The stated aim is to build a joint management plan covering endangered species, proposed critical habitat, and water delivery to customers. The document is administrative correspondence. It names no fish species, reports no field data, and gives no biological measurements.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Regional Director's memorandum, responding to a Fish and Wildlife Service memorandum dated September 21, 1993.
- **Subject line:** Hoover Dam Powerplant Uprating and Other Activities on the Lower Colorado River, marked as an endangered species matter.
- **Sender:** Regional Director, Lower Colorado Regional Office, [[Bureau Of Reclamation]].
- **Primary recipient:** State Supervisor, Arizona Ecological Services State Office, [[Fish And Wildlife Service]], Phoenix.
- **Copied agencies:** [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]] (Director); Fish and Wildlife Service [[Region 2]] (Albuquerque); and the Parker Fisheries Resources Office, Parker, Arizona.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The memorandum treats the lower river as a managed system with competing demands:

- **Endangered species:** The subject line flags endangered species, and the text refers to their management needs.
- **Proposed critical habitat:** The lower river contains habitat proposed for critical designation, which the planned management plan would address.
- **Water customers:** Reclamation identifies its "varied water customers" as a stakeholder group whose needs must be balanced against species management.

No specific species, habitat measurements, water quality data, or population figures appear in the text.

## Key Findings

1. **Meeting logistics:** A coordination meeting is set for November 23, 1993, at 10:00 a.m., in the Colorado Street Building, 1404 Colorado Street, Boulder City.
2. **Expanded scope:** Reclamation invited Fish and Wildlife Service staff to broaden discussion from Hoover Dam powerplant uprating to the entire lower Colorado River.
3. **Invited participants:** Fish and Wildlife Service staff named for the meeting include Tom Gatz and Lesley Fitzpatrick (Arizona Ecological Services), Chuck Minckley (Parker Fisheries Resources Office), and George Divine (Region 2). Reclamation staff host the meeting.
4. **Stated objective:** Develop and implement a management plan for the lower river that addresses endangered species, proposed critical habitat, and water customer needs.
5. **Contact:** Questions about purpose or location go to Tom Shrader at 702-293-8703.
