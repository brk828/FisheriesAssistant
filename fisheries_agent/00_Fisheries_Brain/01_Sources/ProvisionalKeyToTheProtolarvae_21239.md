# Provisional Key to Protolarvae and Mesolarvae of Six Catostomid Species

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #Catostomidae #larval-fish #taxonomic-key

## Executive Summary

This provisional dichotomous key enables identification of protolarvae and mesolarvae of six sucker species ([[Catostomus catostomus]], [[Catostomus commersoni]], [[Catostomus discobolus]], [[Catostomus latipinnis]], [[Catostomus platyrhynchus]], and [[Xyrauchen texanus]]) from the [[Upper Colorado River System]]. Prepared by the [[Larval Fish Laboratory]] at [[Colorado State University]] for the [[Colorado Division of Wildlife]] and [[Ecosystem Research Institute]], the key relies primarily on pigmentation patterns, body length, and developmental features. The document acknowledges significant taxonomic challenges, particularly regarding [[Xyrauchen texanus]] (razorback sucker) misidentification as [[Catostomus latipinnis]] when dorsal ray counts are low (as few as 12 rays in lower basin specimens). Comprehensive morphometric and meristic data are provided for [[Xyrauchen texanus]] protolarvae and mesolarvae.

## Background & Description

This key represents ongoing taxonomic investigations of fish larvae in the [[Upper Colorado River System]], complementing the preliminary key to metalarvae published in Snyder (1981). The document addresses a critical revision: contrary to earlier keys, some [[Xyrauchen texanus]] specimens exhibit dorsal ray counts as low as 12 (based on specimens reared by [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], New Mexico), creating potential for misidentification as [[Catostomus latipinnis]].

The key distinguishes between **protolarvae** (lacking dorsal, anal, or caudal fin rays) and **mesolarvae** (possessing at least one caudal ray but lacking the full complement of principal rays in median fins). Key diagnostic features include:

- **Pigmentation patterns**: dorsal surface pigmentation, midventral pigmentation lines, peritoneal pigmentation, and distinctive "tractor-tread" patterns
- **Body size**: total length (TL) thresholds at 9mm, 11mm, 12mm, 17mm, and 20mm
- **Developmental markers**: presence/absence of yolk, pelvic fin buds, gut loop formation, fin ray development

The [[Catostomus commersoni]] exhibits a unique prominent "tractor-tread" pattern with diagonally paired melanophores along each side of the dorsal midline, a feature not previously illustrated or described in the literature.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Species distribution patterns within the [[Upper Colorado River System]]:

- [[Catostomus platyrhynchus]] and introduced [[Catostomus catostomus]] more commonly inhabit headwater streams and upstream reservoirs, respectively
- [[Catostomus latipinnis]] lacks dorsal surface pigmentation at 11mm TL or larger, a distinctive feature among these species
- [[Xyrauchen texanus]] specimens from lower basin populations show morphological variation requiring intensive study

The document emphasizes that protolarvae and mesolarvae of these species are morphologically very similar, with most useful diagnostic differences occurring in metalarvae and juveniles. Developmental timing varies among species, with [[Xyrauchen texanus]] showing specific patterns of yolk absorption, fin development, and gut loop formation relative to body size.

## Key Findings

**Taxonomic Revision Requirements:**
- [[Xyrauchen texanus]] metalarvae and juveniles require intensive study and key revision due to dorsal ray count variation (as low as 12 rays)
- Specimens under 60mm TL with low dorsal ray counts may have been historically misidentified as [[Catostomus latipinnis]]
- The key requires considerable testing, refinement, and input from users outside the [[Larval Fish Laboratory]]

**Morphometric Data for Xyrauchen texanus:**

*Protolarvae (6.70-11.60mm TL, n=20):*
- Standard length: 9.40 ± 1.27mm (range 6.70-11.10mm)
- Myomere counts: 43 ± 4 total (range 46-49)
- Yolk present in specimens up to 11.10mm TL

*Mesolarvae (10.60-20.15mm TL, n=28):*
- Standard length: 15.83 ± 1.98mm (range 10.60-17.40mm)
- Total myomeres: 47 ± 1 (range 46-49)
- Caudal fin rays (principal): 16 ± 5 (range 4-18, n=23)
- Dorsal fin rays (principal): 6 ± 6 (range 0-15, n=28)
- Anal fin rays (principal): 1 ± 2 (range 0-6, n=23)

**Diagnostic Characters by Species:**

- **[[Catostomus discobolus]]**: Partial midventral pigment line between vent and heart region; short line(s) of midventral melanophores; prominent peritoneal pigmentation
- **[[Catostomus commersoni]]**: Prominent "tractor-tread" pattern with diagonally paired melanophores along dorsal midline (previously undescribed)
- **[[Catostomus latipinnis]]**: No dorsal surface pigmentation at 11mm TL or larger; dorsal pigmentation in lines of single or diagonal pairs along dorsal midline; specimens 17-19mm TL lack pelvic fins/buds with straight gut
- **[[Catostomus platyrhynchus]]**: Continuous midventral pigmentation extending into branchial region; prominent peritoneal pigmentation
- **[[Xyrauchen texanus]]**: Very little midventral pigmentation anterior to vent; peritoneal pigmentation not prominent; less than 12mm TL in protolarval stage

**Key Limitations:**
- Considerable testing and refinement needed
- Developmental study and verification required for species other than [[Xyrauchen texanus]] and [[Catostomus latipinnis]]
- Lack of comparable morphometric tables for remaining species limits data utility
- Most useful differences likely occur in metalarvae and juveniles rather than earlier stages
