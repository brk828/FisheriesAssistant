## Executive Summary
This document is a 1980 interagency memorandum from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2) to the Water and Power Resources Service, addressing regulatory changes relevant to the proposed Hoover Powerplant Modification Project. The core scientific update is the federal listing of the [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*) as an endangered species on May 23, 1980. The memo requests an amended biological assessment to account for this status change and highlights the need for updated survey data to determine the species' actual occurrence in the lower Colorado River reservoir system affected by the dam modifications. Additionally, the document confirms the presence of three federally listed fish species being actively reared at the [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context
* **Primary Habitat:** The species in question inhabit the [[Lower Colorado River]] mainstem and its associated reservoirs, specifically the area to be impacted by proposed operational changes at [[Hoover Dam]].
* **Impact Assessment:** The document notes that the prior biological assessment (dated March 6, 1980) addressed potential impacts to the [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]] in a "superficial manner," specifically regarding the claim that river conditions would remain "essentially the same" post-modification.
* **Hatchery Role:** The [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]] serves as a conservation and rearing facility for three co-occurring endangered [[Cyprinidae]] species, indicating overlapping ecological requirements or conservation strategies for these big-river fishes: the [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]], and [[Humpback Chub]].
* **Co-occurring Taxa Change:** The memo also notes the removal of a non-fish species, Trelease's beavertail prickly pear (*Opuntia basilaris var. treleasei*), from the proposed listing status, indicating a shift in the broader ecological/regulatory landscape of the project area.

## Key Findings
* **Status Change:** The [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*) was officially listed as **Endangered** under the Endangered Species Act as of May 23, 1980, superseding its previous "proposed" status noted in the September 5, 1979 species list.
* **Regulatory Requirement:** Due to this status change, the Regional Director recommended that the [[Water and Power Resources Service]] amend their biological assessment for the [[Hoover Powerplant Modification Project]] to specifically address the *Bonytail Chub*.
* **Data Gap Identified:** There is an explicit request for a "valid survey" of the lower Colorado River and reservoirs to determine the actual presence/absence of the *Bonytail Chub* in the impact zone, suggesting that occurrence data at the time was insufficient or outdated.
* **Hatchery Vulnerability:** The FWS raised concerns regarding insufficient data supporting the claim of unchanged river conditions at the [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]], which is critical infrastructure for the recovery of three listed species (Colorado Squawfish, Bonytail Chub, and Humpback Chub).
* **Concurrent Delisting:** Trelease's beavertail prickly pear was removed from proposed listing on November 10, 1979, with the assessor noting it is unlikely to be reconsidered for listing in the near future.
