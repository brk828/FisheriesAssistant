# Razorback Sucker (Xyrauchen texanus) — Withdrawal of Expired Listing Proposal

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae #freshwater-biology #conservation-status #colorado-river-basin

## Executive Summary
This document is a **regulatory notice** from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS), published in the Federal Register on May 27, 1980, announcing the **withdrawal of a proposed rule** to list the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) as a **Threatened** species under the Endangered Species Act (ESA). The species had originally been proposed for listing on April 24, 1978, without designation of Critical Habitat. Per the 1978 ESA amendments, any listing proposal not finalized within two years (plus a one-year suspension grace period ending November 10, 1979) is subject to mandatory withdrawal. Because the razorback sucker proposal exceeded this statutory deadline, FWS was required to withdraw it on April 24, 1980, with formal notice published shortly thereafter. Notably, a companion species proposed in the same original 1978 notice, the [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), was successfully finalized as **Endangered** on April 23, 1980 — narrowly avoiding the same fate.

## Key Findings
- **Original Proposal:** Razorback sucker proposed for listing as **Threatened** (no Critical Habitat) on **April 24, 1978** (43 FR 17375–77).
- **Statutory Trigger:** The 1978 ESA Amendments (effective Nov. 10, 1978) require that any proposed listing rule be finalized within **two years**, with a one-year moratorium on withdrawals lasting until **November 10, 1979**.
- **Outcome:** Because no final rule was issued for the razorback sucker within the statutory window, FWS was legally obligated under **Section 4(f)(5)** of the ESA to withdraw the proposal.
- **Withdrawal Date:** The proposal was withdrawn on **April 24, 1980**; the official Federal Register notice was dated **May 16, 1980** and published **May 27, 1980**.
- **Contrast with Bonytail Chub:** The [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), proposed in the same original action, was finalized as **Endangered** on **April 23, 1980** (45 FR 27710–13) — one day before the razorback sucker's deadline lapsed, illustrating the narrow procedural margin separating the two species' regulatory outcomes.
- **Future Relisting Constraint:** Per ESA Section 4(f)(5), FWS may not re-propose listing for a withdrawn species unless it determines that **sufficient new information** warrants a new proposal — a legal hurdle that would need to be cleared for any future razorback sucker listing effort.
- **Administrative Contacts:** Listing/status inquiries were directed to Mr. John L. Spinks, Jr., Chief, Office of Endangered Species, FWS, Washington, D.C.
- **Signatory:** Notice signed by Galen Buterbaugh, Acting Director, Fish and Wildlife Service, dated May 16, 1980.

> **Note:** This document reflects a procedural/legal event rather than new biological research. The razorback sucker was **later successfully listed as Endangered** in 1991 (outside the scope of this notice), following accumulation of "sufficient new information" as required by the Act.
