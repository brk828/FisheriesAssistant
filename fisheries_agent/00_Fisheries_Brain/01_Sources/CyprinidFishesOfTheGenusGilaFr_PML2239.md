## Executive Summary

This document is a meristic data table, the only legible content in an otherwise heavily garbled scan. It reports counts of fin rays and lateral-line scales for [[Gila]] chubs from river systems south and east of the lower [[Colorado River Basin]] and from streams tributary to the middle Colorado River. Each row pairs a taxon and locality with museum catalog numbers from [[Arizona State University Collections]] (ASU). Much of the OCR text is corrupted, so many species names, localities and counts are approximate and should be checked against the original publication.

## Background & Description

The table is headed "Meristic variations in chubs, genus Gila." Its columns are:

- **Dorsal fin-rays**
- **Anal fin-rays**
- **Pelvic fin-rays**
- **Scales in the lateral line**, binned in ranges from roughly 50–55 up to 90–95

Meristic counts like these are standard taxonomic characters used to separate closely related chub populations and subspecies. The source's taxon labels appear to include the following, though OCR corruption makes them uncertain:

- *Gila nigrescens* (Chihuahua basin localities)
- *Gila pulchella* (read as "Gila pu?uila"; [[Yaqui Basin]] localities in Arizona and Sonora)
- *Gila* sp. from the [[Rio Grande Basin]] (New Mexico, including Hondo Creek and the [[Jemez River]])
- *Gila robusta* subspecies, including *stahlmi* ([[Little Colorado River]], Arizona), *jordani* (Ash Springs, Nevada) and *seminuda* (Moapa River, Nevada; [[Virgin River]], Arizona)

Localities are given with ASU catalog numbers, for example Rio Casas Grandes (ASU 816), Rio Piedras Verde (ASU 821) and Jemez River (ASU 2362).
