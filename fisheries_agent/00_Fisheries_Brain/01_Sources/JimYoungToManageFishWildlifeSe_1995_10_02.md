## Executive Summary

The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) appointed **Jim Young**, a 32-year federal employee, to oversee agency operations across two newly delineated geographic management units in Arizona: the **[[Lower Colorado River Ecosystem]]** and the **[[Gila-Salt-Verde Ecosystem]]**. This appointment operationalizes a 1994 agency-wide shift from program-based management (e.g., separate refuges, fisheries, wetlands, and endangered species divisions) to a **watershed-based, ecosystem management approach**, restructuring the FWS into 53 geographic units nationwide as defined by U.S. Geological Survey watershed boundaries. The stated goal is to integrate fisheries, wetlands, refuge, and endangered species work under unified geographic managers to improve coordination with state agencies, tribes, and local communities, and to shift emphasis from single-species conservation ("species-by-species") toward proactive, ecosystem-level resource protection.
