# Fish Community Reorganization in a Perturbed Stream: The Virgin River (UT, AZ, NV)

#ichthyology #freshwater-ecology #fish-community

## Executive Summary

This document is an Arizona State University (ASU) proposal routing and approval packet for a new grant proposal titled *Fish Community Reorganization in a Perturbed Stream, the Virgin River of UT, AZ and NV*. The proposal would be submitted to the [[U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service]] (Albuquerque, NM) for a five-year project running from January 1989 to December 1993. The research concerns how [[Fish Community Reorganization]] proceeds in a [[Perturbed Stream]], with the [[Virgin River]] as the study system. The packet contains no scientific data, species lists or methods. Its content is administrative: investigators, budget, cost sharing, indirect cost waiver, and facilities requests.

## Background & Description

- **Project type:** New proposal (not a continuation, renewal or resubmission). Key words: community reorganization, perturbed stream.
- **Award type:** Grant.
- **Project period:** Proposed start January 1989; end December 1993 (five years). The form marks this as Budget Period 2 of 5.
- **Sponsor:** U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, P.O. Box 1306, Albuquerque, NM 87103. No prime sponsor is listed beyond the funding agency. The sponsor ID is n/a.
- **Submission:** Copies required: 2, plus 1 for ORDA. Transmission is marked for express delivery.
- **Principal and co-investigators (all in ASU College of Liberal Arts & Sciences unless noted):**
  - [[W.L. Minckley]], Professor, Department of Zoology (lead PI)
  - [[Michael E. Douglas]], Academic Professor, Department of Zoology
  - [[Thomas E. Dowling]], Assistant Professor, Department of Zoology
  - [[Paul C. Marsh]], Associate Professor (Research), College of Engineering and Applied Sciences (CEAS), with the Vice President for Research listed as the routing office

Each investigator's ASU ID number appears in the source, but these are administrative identifiers and are not reproduced here.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The packet gives only the study's framing: a stream subject to perturbation, and a question about how the fish community reorganizes afterward. It names no fish species, sampling sites, habitat measurements, water chemistry or invertebrate data. Study location is limited to the Virgin River, which spans Utah, Arizona and Nevada. The packet also includes a Biosafety (Chemicals/RDNA) field marked "Yes" on the routing form. The text does not describe what chemicals or hazards are involved.
