# San Pedro Biological Resources Technical Committee Meeting Notice - March 1990

#riparian-ecology #habitat-management #conservation-planning

## Executive Summary

This correspondence from the Bureau of Land Management Safford District Office invites Dr. W.L. Minckley to participate in a meeting of the San Pedro Biological Resources Technical Committee scheduled for April 5, 1990. The meeting's purpose is to discuss and draft objectives and planned actions for the [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]] Habitat Management Plan (HMP), which will guide wildlife management for 5-15 years.

## Background & Description

The [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]] (NCA) is managed by the [[Bureau of Land Management]] Safford District Office in Arizona. The San Pedro Biological Resources Technical Committee provides technical expertise for management planning. Dr. W.L. Minckley from Arizona State University's Department of Zoology serves as a committee member. The committee previously contributed to the San Pedro Monitoring Plan, with input on wildlife and vegetation sections.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[San Pedro River]] riparian corridor represents a critical habitat in Arizona requiring specialized management planning. The Habitat Management Plan (HMP) will detail management actions specific to wildlife and their habitat within the NCA. The monitoring program developed with committee input is designed to assess whether management objectives for the NCA are being met. The riparian ecosystem supports diverse wildlife requiring long-term conservation strategies spanning 5-15 years.

## Key Findings

- Meeting scheduled for April 5, 1990 at 6:30 p.m. at University of Arizona Campus (room 311, Biosciences East Building)
- Primary objective: discuss and draft objectives and planned actions for the San Pedro Habitat Management Plan
- HMP will detail wildlife management actions for the next 5-15 years
- Committee's previous input on the Monitoring Plan was incorporated into wildlife and vegetation sections
- San Pedro Monitoring Plan under review by Safford District Staff
- Committee input credited with improving the monitoring program substantially
- Monitoring program critical for assessing achievement of NCA management objectives
