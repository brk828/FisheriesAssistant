## Executive Summary
This document is a **Bureau of Reclamation administrative memorandum** (Blue Envelope Memo), dated 1990, concerning the coordination of aquatic research programs under **[[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]]** (GCES), Phase II. It is not a primary scientific paper reporting morphological or ecological data on fish species; rather, it addresses the logistical and programmatic coordination of multiple aquatic study efforts occurring within the **[[Glen Canyon]]** and **[[Grand Canyon]]** reaches of the **[[Colorado River]]**. The memo references a recent **Arizona Game & Fish endangered fish river trip** and calls for minimizing impacts to "the fish resource," implying ongoing concern for native and endangered fish populations in the regulated river system below **[[Glen Canyon Dam]]**. ## Key Findings
- The **Aquatic Coordination Team** was convened to integrate multiple, previously uncoordinated aquatic study proposals under the **GCES Phase II** program, following a recommendation from **Dr. Duncan Patten's** review group.
- Six coordination priorities were identified for the June 1990 meeting:
  1. Level of effort of each participating research group
  2. Quality control / quality assurance standards across studies
  3. Ensuring consistent inter-agency interaction
  4. Spatial delineation of research responsibilities ("who will be working where")
  5. Integration of studies to reduce redundant/overlapping efforts
  6. **Minimization of impact to the fish resource** — a direct conservation-management concern
- The meeting also planned to address findings from a recent **Arizona Game & Fish endangered fish river trip**, including what actions were needed to "enhance" the coming year's research program.
- Key personnel/agencies involved span federal, state, tribal, and academic institutions: **Bureau of Reclamation**, **Arizona Game & Fish**, **Grand Canyon National Park**, **Glen Canyon National Recreation Area**, **Navajo Nation**, **U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service**, and **Arizona State University's Center for Environmental Studies**.
- This memo exemplifies the **institutional and logistical framework** underpinning the broader **[[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]]** research effort, which itself was designed to inform dam operation impacts on the Colorado River ecosystem, including its fish community — though the document itself contains no direct biological, morphological, or population-level data.
