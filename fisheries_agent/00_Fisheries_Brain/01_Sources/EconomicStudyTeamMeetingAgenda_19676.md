# Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Economic Study Team Meeting Agenda

#ichthyology #marine-biology #water-resource-economics

## Executive Summary

This is a non-research administrative item: a December 19, 1988 cover letter from the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] (Upper Colorado Regional Office) and the agenda for a two-day [[Economic Study Team]] meeting on January 11-12, 1989, in Phoenix, Arizona. The meeting belongs to the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) Phase II effort. Its goal is consensus on the approach and process for drafting the technical economic study plan. The document contains no fish, aquatic ecological, or habitat data.

## Background & Description

- **Sender:** David L. Wegner, Program Manager, GCES.
- **Recipients:** GCES Economic Study Team members, who were asked to send staff able to discuss issues "on an economic/technical level."
- **Venue:** Salt River Project conference room, Temple, Arizona. The agenda lists Phoenix as the meeting location. Nearby lodging included a Holiday Inn, Embassy Suites, and Doubletree, all on 44th Street.
- **Start time:** 9:00 each day.

The letter states that the study requires consensus on the technical approach before the necessary studies can proceed.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address aquatic species, fish, invertebrates, plankton, water chemistry, or habitat conditions. Its only environmental link is the reference to scenarios posed by the "environmental team," which the economic and hydrology work must be able to evaluate. The agenda therefore concerns the economics of [[Power System Models]] and [[Hydrology Models]] within the broader study, not ecological outcomes.

## Key Findings

The agenda sets three meeting objectives: select a tentative model, outline a work plan, and set a timeline for a draft final report. Its ten agenda items are:

1. **Power rates and repayment issues.** Presentations on wholesale rates (Western), retail rates ([[Central Arizona Water Conservation District]] listed as "CREDA" in the source), and repayment of project costs (BOR).
2. **Review of potential models.** Focus on required inputs, outputs, and cost of effort:
   - [[Elfin Model]] (EDF)
   - [[Egeas Model]] (BOR)
3. **Hydrology needs.** Review of current hydrology models, identification of alternatives, interface with power system models, and the level of detail needed to evaluate environmental scenarios.
4. **Tentative choice of power system model.**
5. **Work statement, documents to be produced, and timeline.**
6. **Budget items** covering time, dollar, and labor commitments.
7. **Definitional problems** carried over from the previous meeting:
   - Whether the [[Western Systems Coordinating Council]] (WSCC) definitions distributed at the last meeting are acceptable.
   - Which other critical definitions need discussion.
   - Whether consensus on study boundaries has improved.
8. **Data needs and sources.**
9. **Research implementation plan.**
10. **Selection of date, site, and agenda items** for the next meeting.

**Bottom line:** The meeting is a planning session. Its outputs are a tentative power system model, a work plan, a timeline, and agreed definitions and boundaries. No quantitative results, sample sizes, or aquatic findings are reported.
