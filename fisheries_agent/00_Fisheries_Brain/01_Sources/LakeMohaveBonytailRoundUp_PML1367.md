# Lake Mohave Bonytail Roundup Memorandum (1996)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal memorandum from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] announces a coordinated field effort on the endangered [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) in [[Lake Mohave]]. The Department's [[Regional Fisheries Program]] plans to trammel net for bonytails and, in conjunction with the scheduled [[Fish Tagging and Tracking]] work, tag and track the fish in the southeast portion of the lake. The memo asks participants of the [[Native Fish Work Group]] to confirm attendance by May 1, 1996, so that logistics can be coordinated.

## Background & Description

- **Author:** [[Thomas A. Liles]], Regional Fisheries Program Manager.
- **Recipients:** Native Fish Work Group participants, including Steve Ferrell, Joe Janisch and Rod Lucas.
- **Date:** March 1996 (the footer also gives "25, 1996").
- **Subject:** "Mohave Bonytail Roundup."

The bonytail is the focal species. The memo does not give its biology, size or population figures.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Setting:** Lake Mohave, a reservoir on the Colorado River system in Arizona. The memo places the work in the lake's southeast portion. The OCR text reads "133–135," which likely refers to river mile designations, but this is not confirmed in the source.
- **Capture method:** Trammel netting, the gear used to capture adult bonytails for this effort.
- **Handling and holding:** The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service may want all adult bonytails transported to the [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]]. [[Chuck Hinckley]] is responsible for the holding and transport of those fish.

The memo does not describe water chemistry, habitat conditions, other aquatic species or the wider ecosystem.

## Key Findings

- The Arizona Game and Fish Department will trammel net for bonytails in Lake Mohave, combined with tagging and tracking work.
- Target area: the southeast portion of Lake Mohave.
- Adult bonytails may be transferred to Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery for holding, under Chuck Hinckley's responsibility.
- Participants must confirm their involvement by May 1, 1996, so logistics and transport can be arranged.
- The memo contains no numerical data, sample sizes, catch results or population estimates.
