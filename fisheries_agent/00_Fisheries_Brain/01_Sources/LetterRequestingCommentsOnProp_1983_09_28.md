## Executive Summary

This document is a 1983 U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) letter soliciting expert comment (from Dr. W.L. Minckley, Arizona State University) on a proposal to list three fish taxa native to the [[Rio Yaqui Drainage]] under the Endangered Species Act of 1973. The proposal, published in the Federal Register (48 FR 32527-32534) on July 15, 1983, recommends [[Endangered Species Act|endangered status]] for the [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*) and [[Threatened Species|threatened status]] for the [[Beautiful Shiner]] (*Notropis formosus*) and the [[Yaqui Catfish]] (*Ictalurus pricei*). All three species are noted as originally occurring in the Rio Yaqui drainage of extreme southeastern Arizona. The letter itself contains no morphological, physiological, or population-density data; it is a procedural/administrative instrument requesting scientific and status information to inform final rulemaking.
