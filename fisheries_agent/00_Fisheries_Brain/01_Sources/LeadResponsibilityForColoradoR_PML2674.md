# Lead Responsibility for Colorado River Fishes

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service memorandum from the Albuquerque regional office settles which office leads the recovery coordination for four endangered big river fishes of the [[Colorado River]]. After confusion over an earlier reassignment, the memo confirms that the [[Parker Fish and Wildlife Office]] holds lead responsibility, not the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]]. It also describes staffing changes meant to support the effort and asks for FY 1991 funding.

## Background & Description

The memo concerns four species of [[Colorado River Fishes]]:

- [[Colorado Squawfish]]
- [[Bonytail Chub]]
- [[Razorback Sucker]]
- [[Humpback Chub]]

A memo from Floyd Nudi dated March 29, 1990 (the OCR reads "2g") reassigned lead responsibility for these species to Dexter NFH. The regional fisheries staff accepted the lead but stated that the appropriate office was Parker FAO. The memo's author believed the regional director had agreed with that choice.

The document is administrative. It contains no biological measurements, population data, sample sizes or habitat descriptions.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The memo does not describe the ecology of these fishes, their habitat, or threats to them. Its only environmental reference is the Colorado River system, which is implied by the subject line. The stated reason for the coordination effort is recovery of the species, but the memo gives no status or population details.

Responsibility for the effort had moved from the [[Phoenix Ecological Services Office]] to Parker FAO. The memo calls the effort "significant."

## Key Findings

- **Lead office confirmed:** Parker FAO has assumed lead responsibility for Colorado River fishes and will keep it until the Regional Director issues instructions otherwise.
- **Temporary supervision:** Supervisory responsibility for Parker FAO has been assigned temporarily to Jim Hanson at [[Pinetop Fish and Wildlife Office]]. The memo presents this as an enhancement to coordination.
- **Staffing:** The Parker clerk has been converted to full-time so that someone is available to answer questions and calls about the big river fishes.
- **Accountability:** The memo sets a one-year evaluation period. If results are unsatisfactory, the authors state they will be held accountable.
- **Funding request:** A prior memo of July 20, 1990 asked for $5,000 to offset costs of the effort. The author asks whether this funding will be provided in [[Fiscal Year 1991 Funding]].
- **Distribution:** The memo was sent to Parker FAO, Pinetop FAO, Dexter NFH and Phoenix ES.
