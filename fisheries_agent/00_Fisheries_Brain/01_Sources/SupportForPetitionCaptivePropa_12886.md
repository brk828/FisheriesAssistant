# Tucson Herpetological Society Letter on Captive Propagation Petition for Arizona Native Reptiles and Amphibians

#herpetology #wildlife-policy #captive-breeding

## Executive Summary

This 1994 letter from the [[Tucson Herpetological Society]] (THS) responds to a petition by [[Michael Brent Peugnet]] to the [[Arizona Game and Fish Commission]]. The petition, titled "Captive Propagation of Arizona Native Species of Reptiles and Amphibians for Commercial Purposes" and co-sponsored by the [[Arizona Herpetological Association]], proposes allowing commercial captive breeding and sale of native Arizona herpetofauna. The THS agrees in principle and considers the petition worthy of serious, open evaluation, while holding reservations about its implementation and about the husbandry of certain species. The letter is a correspondence item and contains no quantitative data.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Personal letter dated 30 July 1994, from the THS President (signature partly garbled in the scan) to Michael Brent Peugnet, Peoria, Arizona.
- **Occasion:** Thanks for a presentation Peugnet gave at the THS membership meeting on 17 July 1994.
- **Subject:** The petition, which Peugnet wrote and circulated to interested parties for comment and support.
- **Species named:** The [[Gila Monster]] (*Heloderma suspectum*) and the green ratsnake (genus *Senticolis*; the species epithet is truncated in the source text).
- **Core concern categories:** Petition implementation, species husbandry (long-term maintenance and breeding), and possible captive stress effects on breeding colonies.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Wild-caught trade:** The letter notes that the rule change would not stop the [[Illicit Wild-Caught Trade]] in Arizona reptiles and amphibians.
- **Market effects:** The THS expects that successful [[Captive Breeding]] and sale could reduce long-term demand for wild-caught animals and gradually lower artificially inflated prices.
- **Husbandry concerns:**
  - Suspected difficulties in long-term maintenance and breeding of the Gila monster.
  - Possible [[Captive Stress Syndrome]] effects on breeding colonies of the green ratsnake.

No habitat, population, or field survey data are presented.

## Key Findings

1. **Conditional support:** The THS agrees in principle with the petition and supports it going forward for serious consideration and open discussion.
2. **Reservations on implementation:** The board's main reservations concern how the petition would be put into practice, which it described as opening a "Pandora's box" of commercialization of Arizona native wildlife.
3. **Species-specific husbandry doubts:** Doubts remain about captive maintenance and breeding of the Gila monster and about stress-related breeding problems in the green ratsnake.
4. **Expected conservation benefit:** Legal captive propagation is expected, over the long term, to reduce pressure on wild populations and to weaken the market for wild-caught animals, even though it will not eliminate illegal collection.
5. **Recognition of the petitioner:** The THS commends Peugnet for drafting the petition and for seeking comment from interested parties.
6. **Call for participation:** The letter invites continued involvement and offers further contact with the THS.
