# Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program: Management Group Meeting Summary, April 6-7, 1989

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Cyprinidae #Catostomidae

## Executive Summary
This is a draft summary, circulated by a US Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) Region 6 program director on May 5, 1989, of a two-day Management Group meeting of the Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program. The Program aims to recover endangered Colorado River fishes: [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Humpback Chub]], [[Razorback Sucker]] (and the [[Bonytail]] by implication of the "three endangered fishes" plans). The meeting covered stocking controversies, a razorback sucker listing petition, FY-90 planning, instream flow reports, water rights acquisition, [[Section 7 Consultation]] policy, sediment impacts, and disagreement over recovery goals. Comments were due May 26, 1989; the next meetings were set for June 14, 1989 and tentatively July 13-14, 1989 (FY-90 work plan and budget).

## Background & Description
- The document is a memorandum plus meeting summary, not a research study. It lists 8 reference items (e.g., Recovery Team letters, a Sierra Club Legal Defense Fund petition, Tom Pitts' memoranda, FY-90 proposal solicitation).
- Attendees: about 21 people representing FWS, Colorado, Utah, Wyoming, Western Area Power Administration, Bureau of Reclamation, The Nature Conservancy, water developers, environmental groups, the Solicitor's office, the National Park Service (NPS), and the Colorado River Water Conservation District.
- Agenda items spanned two days: Recovery Program updates, Technical Group report, FY-89 work plan status, Recovery Team/Plans, water rights acquisition, biological opinion status, Section 7 issues, Windy Gap funds, and [[CRSP Legislation]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- Target systems: the [[Yampa River]], the [[15-Mile Reach]], the [[Little Snake River]], the [[White River]], the [[San Juan River]], [[Cataract Canyon]], the Grand Canyon, [[Flaming Gorge]], [[Ruedi Reservoir]], [[Kenney Reservoir]], and [[Lake Powell]].
- Nonnative stocking concerns: the Colorado Division of Wildlife (CDOW) proposed stocking [[Channel Catfish]] in Kenney Reservoir, and the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources proposed stocking [[Rainbow Smelt]] in Lake Powell. The Group felt both were contrary to the Recovery Program's intent.
- Sediment: a presentation noted that water development alters sediment transport and channel form, with considerable uncertainty about effects on endangered fish habitat, which may not be evident for decades.
- Flow: the Service's instream flow reports for the Yampa and 15-mile reach were to inform flow recommendations.

## Key Findings
- **Stocking:** A Rangely, Colorado public hearing (about 30 attendees) on an experimental Colorado squawfish sport fishery in Kenney Reservoir suggested no opposition to stocking squawfish that year. The catfish and rainbow smelt proposals were referred to the Technical Group. A National Park Service permit for smelt in Lake Powell would trigger Section 7 consultation.
- **Razorback petition:** The Sierra Club Legal Defense Fund petitioned to list the razorback sucker as endangered throughout its range; FWS Region 6 has the lead.
- **Grand Canyon humpback chub studies:** Reclamation and Western withdrew their proposal to use Recovery Program funds; Reclamation will fund the studies from other sources.
- **NPS participation:** NPS is preparing a request to join; participation requires Implementation Committee concurrence and a defined commitment.
- **FY-90 planning:** Proposals were due May 1, 1989, in standard format.
- **Instream flow reports:** Yampa and 15-mile reach reports to be finalized by May 1, 1989, then sent to the Implementation Committee, with a request that the Colorado Water Conservation Board (CWCB) and others develop alternative means of providing flows.
- **Work plan status:** The Flaming Gorge Biological Opinion technical reports were behind schedule, but the Opinion was promised on schedule (May 1, 1990). The brochure was due June 1, 1989, and Reclamation will produce the newsletter. Funds were freed for a razorback management plan draft by fiscal year end.
- **San Juan River:** The Group approved Utah endangered fish surveys, funded by Reclamation at $8,000, with objections to be reported to the program director. Additional Cataract Canyon field work will use non-Program funds.
- **Recovery plans dispute:** FWS maintained that recovery goals are its statutory responsibility and must be objective, quantifiable and measurable, using input from the Recovery Team and Implementation Committee. Tom Pitts argued the Program calls for the Implementation Committee to revise goals and that the Colorado squawfish plan's goals were inconsistent. No resolution; FWS will respond in writing.
- **Water rights:** CWCB aimed to file to protect Ruedi Reservoir releases through the 15-mile reach by December 1989. The River District is preparing a 2-year feasibility study of alternatives to the Juniper and Cross Mountain projects, proposed to be cost shared by the CWCB, the River District and the Recovery Program. Options for donating Little Snake River conditional rights (possibly via FWS to CWCB for federal enforcement) were discussed; an evaluation was assigned to Gene Jencsok.
- **Foundation funding:** Transfer of Section 7 funds to the Fish and Wildlife Foundation was tentatively approved; transfer of congressionally appropriated water rights acquisition funds was considered legally questionable and dropped for now.
- **Section 7:** FWS's position on depletion impacts and "sufficient progress" drew concern over historical depletions needing new federal approval (including the $10/af charge); FWS will issue a policy statement. Status of consultations to be updated each meeting.
- **Sediment:** An ad hoc work group (chair Alan Mauzy) must recommend an approach, evaluating long-term sediment/habitat monitoring, flexible reservoir operations, and man-made habitat (e.g., backwaters).
- **Windy Gap funds:** Tom Pitts proposed using all Section 7 funds exclusively for water rights acquisition to enlarge matching funds; flexibility concerns were raised and he will draft a proposal.
- **Logistics:** The annual river trip was set for August 10-12, 1989 ($100 deposit).
