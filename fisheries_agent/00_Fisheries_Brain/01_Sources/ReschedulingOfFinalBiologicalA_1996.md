## Key Findings

- The **Consultation Agreement** between Reclamation and the Fish and Wildlife Service (dated March 29, 1996) established a stepwise timeline (Step 3) for delivery of a final biological assessment on Lower Colorado River operations and maintenance.
- Reclamation committed to an unusually **open and public consultation process**, atypical for ESA Section 7 consultations, including a 30-day public review period and a public meeting held May 3, 1996.
- Public review generated **29 written comments totaling 189 pages**, covering issues from broad scope to detailed technical concerns.
- Due to the volume and complexity of public comments, Reclamation requested a **schedule extension**, moving the final biological assessment submission date from June 30, 1996 to **July 19, 1996**.
- This delay was expected to cascade into the Fish and Wildlife Service's **draft biological opinion** timeline, but the overall Consultation Agreement schedule retained provision for a **45-day public review period** of the draft opinion and delivery of a **final biological opinion by April 15, 1997**.
