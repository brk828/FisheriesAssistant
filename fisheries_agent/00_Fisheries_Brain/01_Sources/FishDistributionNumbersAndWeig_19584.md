# Fish Distribution by Station, Species, Program and Agency, FY 1993

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) Fisheries table records fish and fish-egg distributions from [[Fish Hatchery]] stations for fiscal year 1993, with data dated 03/31/94. It covers roughly 69 station sections and records each shipment or release by program code, agency code, and species code, with egg numbers, fish numbers, and weight. The document is a data table rather than a research study. It contains no analysis, no environmental measurements, and no conclusions. Questions were directed to Roger Schulz, Division of Fish Hatcheries, (703) 358-1715.

## Background & Description

The report opens with a short cover note and then supplies the coding system used to record data. There are two code sets:

- **Fish and Fish Egg Distribution codes:** agency codes (about 40, from Agriculture and the Army to the Tennessee Valley Authority and state governments), water type codes, and program codes.
- **Broodstock codes:** facility designations (e.g., [[National Fish Hatchery]], [[State Fish Hatchery]], university or research laboratories, foreign hatcheries, Indian reservations) and program categories.

The species code list runs across pages 3 and 4 and includes roughly 140 entries. These cover salmonids such as [[Rainbow Trout]], [[Brown Trout]], [[Brook Trout]], [[Cutthroat Trout]], [[Lake Trout]], and [[Chinook Salmon]]; centrarchids such as [[Largemouth Bass]] and [[Bluegill]]; catfishes such as [[Channel Catfish]]; sturgeons; darters; pupfishes; and minnows and suckers. Hybrid and hatchery strains, such as Blue x Channel Catfish and Rainbow x Steelhead, are also coded.

Program codes include:

- [[Management Assistance Program]] (MA)
- [[Restoration And Mitigation Program]] (RM)
- [[Endangered And Threatened Species Program]] (ES)
- [[Research And Development Program]] (RE)
- [[Egg Shipment Program]] (BS)
- Technology/Health (TH), Farm Pond (FP), and Other (OT)

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document has no environmental or water-quality data. Its ecological relevance comes from the water type codes and the species list:

- **Water types:** aquarium, estuarine, federal and other hatcheries, lake/pond, reservoir, stream/river/canal, research, tailwater, farm pond, and other.
- **Listed or sensitive taxa:** the species list includes several endangered or threatened fishes, such as [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Humpback Chub]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Pallid Sturgeon]], [[Gila Trout]], [[Snail Darter]], and [[Desert Pupfish]]. Their presence in the codes indicates that hatchery output supported restoration and recovery programs.
- **Native and introduced fishes:** the list mixes native western and eastern species with widely stocked game fish and non-native taxa (e.g., [[Tilapia]], [[Grass Carp]], [[Goldfish]]). The table does not say which records are native, introduced, or stocked for which purpose.

The data are organized by hatchery station, so they show where fish were produced and sent. They do not record the receiving waters, their conditions, or post-release outcomes.

## Key Findings

- **Scope:** The table covers about 69 station sections, one of which (Warm Springs) appears twice. Records are sorted within each station by program, agency, and species.
- **Program structure:** Each record links a program (e.g., MA, RM, RE, BS), an agency (federal, state, tribal, municipal, or private), and a species. This allows the same station to supply several programs at once.
- **Verified station totals:**
  - **Carson:** 2,482,600 eggs (465,000 under Egg Shipment and 1,986,600 under Restoration/Mitigation, plus 31,000 under Research and Development). Coded species are Sockeye/Kokanee-type "scs" entries.
  - **Allegheny:** 137,000 eggs, mostly Lake Trout-coded entries (127,000 under Egg Shipment).
  - **Dworshak:** 2,369,900 eggs under Egg Shipment (STT code), plus 700 under another program.
  - **Willard:** 3,063,795 fish and 181,933 weight units (Restoration/Mitigation records dominate, with a single cos-coded entry of 3,063,615 fish).
  - **Winthrop:** 952,824 fish and 54,581 weight units.
  - **Wolf Creek:** 867,705 fish and 229,046 weight units (Management Assistance and Other programs, mainly Brown and Rainbow Trout).
  - **Willow Beach:** 657,673 fish and 215,441 weight units (mainly Rainbow Trout from Bureau of Reclamation and Bureau of Indian Affairs sources).
- **Largest single entries (egg numbers as recorded):** Entiat and Genoa station records include the largest egg counts in the table, with entries in the tens of millions. Egg-shipment records make up most of the highest-volume entries.
- **Dominant species in entries:** Trouts (rainbow, brown, cutthroat, brook, and lake trout) appear most frequently across the stations. Warmwater species (bass, catfish, sunfish, and crappie) occur at many stations, especially in Management Assistance and Farm Pond records.
- **Units:** The report gives counts for egg numbers and fish numbers, and a separate "weight" column. The document does not state the weight unit.
- **Limitations:** The OCR scan contains some garbled codes and missing digits, and some station totals cannot be verified from the text. The table gives no analysis, trend data, or conclusions.
