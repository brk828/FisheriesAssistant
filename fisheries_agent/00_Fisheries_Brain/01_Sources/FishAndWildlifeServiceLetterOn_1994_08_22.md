## Executive Summary

This document is a 1994 regulatory letter from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Arizona Ecological Services State Office) addressed to ManTech Field Engineering Company, responding to an inquiry regarding federally listed, proposed, and candidate species that may occur at [[Fort Huachuca Military Reservation]] in Cochise County, Arizona, in connection with a potential base realignment action. The letter is primarily an administrative species checklist spanning mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, invertebrates, plants, and fish, rather than a dedicated ichthyological study. Six fish taxa are named as Category 1 candidate species for federal listing under the Endangered Species Act: [[Longfin Dace]], [[Desert Sucker]], [[Roundtail Chub]], [[Speckled Dace]], [[Flannelmouth Sucker]], and [[Sonora Sucker]].
