# Flathead Catfish Distribution Record from the San Pedro River, Arizona (1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #pilodictis

## Executive Summary

This 1997 letter from the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (Tucson Field Office) to the curator of the [[Arizona State University]] Department of Zoology forwards photographs of a [[Flathead Catfish]] (*[[Pilodictis Olivaris]]*) caught by hook and line near the [[Charleston Bridge]] on the [[San Pedro River]], east of [[Sierra Vista]], Arizona. The identification is tentative and the agency asks the museum to verify it from the photographs and, if the record is accepted, to supply a catalog number. The fish was consumed before other data were recorded.

## Background & Description

- **Report origin:** A sighting was reported by staff at the Sierra Vista Office the previous year. Photographic evidence was obtained later and sent to supplement the museum's records on the species' distribution.
- **Reported size:** The fish was reported to weigh 30 (the source does not state units) and to measure 40 inches total length, as estimated by a staff wildlife biologist.
- **Identification:** Tentatively identified by the author as *Pilodictis olivaris*. Positive identification depends on the photographs.
- **Capture method:** Hook and line.
- **Location:** Township 21 South, Range 21 East, Section 11, NE1/4 NE1/4, on [[Bureau of Land Management]] land.
- **Documentation status:** No other information was recorded before the specimen was consumed, so no voucher specimen is available.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The record concerns the distribution of a large freshwater catfish in the [[San Pedro River]] system of southeastern Arizona. The letter gives no habitat measurements, water chemistry, population data, or information on abundance, diet, or behavior. Its ecological value lies in adding a locality to the regional distribution record, which is the stated purpose of the museum supplement.

The letter was copied to a group of regional agencies and researchers with interests in Arizona fishes and water resources:

- [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (Region V)
- [[Bureau of Reclamation]] (Arizona Project Office)
- [[Arizona State University]] faculty (W.L. Minckley; P.C. Marsh)
- [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Phoenix)
- Mark Fredlake (AZ 046)

## Key Findings

1. **A single flathead catfish record was documented** on the San Pedro River near Sierra Vista, supported by photographs (not a preserved specimen).
2. **Identification is provisional.** The author tentatively assigned the fish to *Pilodictis olivaris* and asked the museum to confirm it from the photographs.
3. **Size data are estimates.** The fish was reported at 30 (units not given) and 40 inches total length by a staff wildlife biologist.
4. **Data gaps:** The specimen was consumed before other information was recorded, and no museum catalog number had been assigned at the time of writing.
5. **Requested actions:**
   - Verify species identity from the photographs if they allow positive identification.
   - Enter the record in the museum collection and provide a catalog number if accepted.
6. **Correspondence:** The letter is signed by R. Simms, Fishery Biologist, Arizona State Office, and dated February 28, 1997.
