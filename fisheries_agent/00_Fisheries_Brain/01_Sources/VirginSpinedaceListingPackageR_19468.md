# Virgin Spinedace Listing Package Review

#ichthyology #marine-biology #spinedace

## Executive Summary

This document is a 1994 letter from [[Paul C. Marsh]], Research Professor at the [[Arizona State University]] Center for Environmental Studies, to the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] (FWS). It responds to a request for review of the FWS proposal to list the [[Virgin Spinedace]] as a threatened species under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. Marsh judges the scientific information in the proposal to be presented correctly and the proposal to be biologically supportable. He attributes the species' decline mainly to past and future [[Water Development]] in the [[Virgin River Basin]], which he considers sufficient grounds for threatened status.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Formal correspondence (letter), dated 08 September 1994.
- **Addressee:** Robert D. Williams, Assistant Field Supervisor, FWS, Salt Lake City, Utah.
- **Purpose:** A review of the "listing package" requested in the FWS letter of 24 August 1994.
- **Regulatory reference:** The proposal appeared in the [[Federal Register]] as volume 59, pages 25875-25880, dated 18 May 1994. The cited "References Cited" list appears on page 25880.
- **Prior input:** Marsh had already sent comments dated 01 July 1994 that supported the proposal.
- **Documents reviewed:** Marsh read, but did not critically review, two works supplied by FWS: Valdez et al. (1991) and Addley and Hardy (1993). He also drew on other published and unpublished literature.
- **Scope:** The letter answers two questions posed by FWS. It contains no new field data, population counts, or sample sizes.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat:** The species is associated with the Virgin River basin. The letter does not describe habitat conditions in detail.
- **Primary threat:** Past and future water development in the basin is identified as the main factor endangering the species' continued existence.
- **Other factors:** Marsh states that water development is "exacerbated by other factors," but the letter does not name them.
- **Foreseeable future:** Marsh states that the threat is present and expected to persist "into the foreseeable future."
- **Taxonomy and life history:** Not addressed in the document.

## Key Findings

1. **Scientific accuracy:** Marsh considers that FWS correctly presented the available scientific information in the proposal.
2. **Biological support:** Marsh considers the proposal to list the Virgin spinedace as threatened to be biologically supportable.
3. **Threat basis:** Water development in the Virgin River basin, both past and planned, endangers the species. Under the definition used, this places it in the threatened category.
4. **Recommendation:** The state of biological risk is strong justification for federal listing as threatened, which would bring the species under [[Endangered Species Act]] protection.
5. **Consistency of support:** Marsh restates the support he gave in his earlier 01 July 1994 letter.
