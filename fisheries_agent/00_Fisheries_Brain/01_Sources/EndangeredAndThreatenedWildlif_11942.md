## Ecological & Environmental Context

The notice does not describe the ecology, habitat, or distribution of any particular invertebrate. It does state that:

- Data are still being gathered on **[[Critical Habitat]]** (essential habitat) for many candidate taxa.

The notice also explains that candidate species receive no substantive or procedural protection under the Act. Their status is a precursor to possible listing, not a legal designation.

1. **Candidate categories:** The Service sorts invertebrate candidates into categories based on the strength of available evidence:
   - **Category 1:** Taxa for which the Service has substantial information on hand to support the appropriateness of proposing listing as endangered or threatened. Proposed rules are expected once essential habitat data are gathered.
