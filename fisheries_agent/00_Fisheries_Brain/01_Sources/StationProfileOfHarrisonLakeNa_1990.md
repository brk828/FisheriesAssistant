# Station Profile of Harrison Lake National Fish Hatchery

## Executive Summary
The Harrison Lake National Fish Hatchery (HLNFH), located in Charles City, Virginia, is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 5) facility established in 1934 under the Federal Emergency Administration of Public Works Act. Its primary mission is to rear anadromous fish—principally [[Striped Bass]]—to support restoration efforts in the [[Chesapeake Bay]]. In fiscal year 1990/1991, the hatchery produced approximately 248,880 fish, of which 172,800 were striped bass allocated to the [[Chesapeake Bay Striped Bass Restoration Program]]. Total hatchery expenditures for the fiscal year were $225,046, split between operations ($196,700) and cyclical maintenance ($28,346). The station also hosted an estimated 10,000 visitors and launched a large-scale striped bass tagging program during the reporting year, marking a significant operational milestone.
