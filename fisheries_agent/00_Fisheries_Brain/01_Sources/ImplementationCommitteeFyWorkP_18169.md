# Endangered Fish Research Work Plan Cover Letter (FY 1990)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #endangered-fish

## Executive Summary

This is a one-page cover letter dated October 10, 1989, from the [[Colorado Division Of Wildlife]] Technical Group chair, James R. Bennett, to researchers in an endangered fish research program. It reports that 65 proposals were submitted to the [[Technical Group]] and that the projects recommended by the Technical and Management groups were adopted by the [[Implementation Committee]] for the FY 1990 [[Work Plan]]. The letter does not name any fish species, study sites or results.

## Background & Description

The letter is addressed to researchers who submitted proposals as a group. It is signed by the Technical Group chair and is part of the Colorado Department of Natural Resources correspondence, which lists the Division of Wildlife Director and the Wildlife Commission members in its footer. The Division's Denver address is given as 6060 Broadway.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document contains no ecological, habitat, water chemistry or species data. Its only context is that the work supports an endangered fish program with five program elements.

## Key Findings

- **Proposal volume:** 65 proposals were submitted to the Technical Group by the researchers. The chair notes that choosing among them is harder each year because of their high quality.
- **Selection outcome:** A list of projects recommended by the Technical and Management groups and adopted by the Implementation Committee for the FY 1990 Work Plan was enclosed. The list itself is not reproduced in this document.
- **Process acknowledgement:** The selection process is described as imperfect, and the Division states it is working to improve it.
- **Planned guidance:** Future [[Request For Proposals]] will be issued to give researchers a clearer view of which work the program ranks as high priority.
- **Road-mapping plan:** Each of the program's five elements will be "road mapped," showing goals and objectives, sub-elements and relative priority for each.
- **Next steps:** The road-map and priorities are to be discussed at the mid-winter researchers' meeting.
