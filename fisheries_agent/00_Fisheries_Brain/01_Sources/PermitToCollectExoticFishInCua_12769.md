## Executive Summary
This document is **not a primary scientific data paper**, but an official Mexican government authorization (Oficio No. 412) issued by the Dirección General de Conservación Ecológica de los Recursos Naturales (INE/SEDESOL) granting permission for scientific collection in the **Cuatro Ciénegas Basin, Coahuila, México**. The permit authorizes an investigation titled *"Presencia de Peces Exóticos en Cuatro Ciénegas, Coahuila"* and supports a broader phylogenetic study of the genus **[[Cyprinodon]]**, conducted in coordination with **Dr. W.L. Minckley** (University of Arizona) and researchers/students from the Facultad de Ciencias, UNAM (Alfonso Vilches Peluyera, Leticia Huidrobo Campos, Dolores Becerril Martínez), under the supervision of Biól. **Héctor Espinosa Pérez** (Colección Ictiológica, Instituto de Biología, UNAM). While it contains no morphological or ecological datasets itself, it provides valuable taxonomic, locational, and regulatory context for research on endemic pupfishes of this globally significant desert wetland ecosystem.
