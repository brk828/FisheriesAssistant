# Fossil Creek Gila Topminnow Stocking, November 2007

#ichthyology #marine-biology #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This Arizona Game and Fish Department (AZGFD) document reports the stocking of approximately 3,000 [[Gila Topminnow]] (Sharp Springs lineage) into five sites in the upper reach of [[Fossil Creek]] on November 28, 2007. The stream had been isolated by a [[Fish Barrier]] built in 2004 and chemically renovated to remove nonnative fishes. Stocking followed a fall 2007 habitat survey of the reach for five federally listed species. The release was planned as the first step of a longer program of augmentation and monitoring, with later plans for other listed fishes.

## Background & Description

- **Study system:** Fossil Creek is a perennial, travertine-forming tributary of the [[Verde River]] in Central Arizona. A fish barrier was constructed approximately 7.5 km above the confluence in 2004, and the stream was chemically renovated to remove nonnative fishes.
- **Pre-stocking survey:** Fall 2007 habitat surveys covered the length of aquatic habitat from the barrier to the headwater springs. The aim was to find suitable habitat for five federally listed species: [[Spikedace]], [[Loach Minnow]], [[Gila Topminnow]], [[Desert Pupfish]] and [[Razorback Sucker]] (Carter 2007; see [[Carter 2007 Habitat Assessment]]).
- **Fish source and transport:** Fish were obtained from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] on November 27, 2007, flown to Cottonwood Airport in an AZGFD airplane, and taken to the [[Bubbling Ponds Native Fish Hatchery]]. They were then transported to Fossil Creek for release.
- **Personnel:** David Ward, Dave Weedman and Codey Carter (AZGFD) carried out the stocking.
- **Handling:** Fish were collected, transported and stocked following established protocols intended to reduce handling stress (see [[Stocking Protocols]]).
- **Habitat preference:** Gila topminnow prefer backwaters, springs and shallow stream margins with low water velocity.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The release sites were chosen to match this habitat preference. They were grouped around three landscape features:

- **Fossil Springs area (Sites 1 and 2):** Spring habitat above the old [[Diversion Dam]].
- **Irving Powerplant area (Sites 3 and 4):** A small spring-fed tributary about 1.9 km upstream of the decommissioned [[Irving Powerplant]] (Site 3), and backwater created by a decommissioned intake channel (Site 4).
- **Downstream of Irving Powerplant (Site 5):** Extensive backwater formed by travertine dam deposition ([[Travertine]] formation).

A temperature logger was placed in the stream near the Site 5 release point. This suggests that thermal conditions were a concern at the release site, although the document does not report the readings.

## Key Findings

- **Total stocked:** About 3,000 Gila topminnow were released into five upper-reach sites.

| Site | Habitat | Approx. number stocked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Springhead, above diversion dam | 900 |
| 2 | Springhead, above diversion dam | 400 |
| 3 | Small spring-fed tributary, ~1.9 km upstream of Irving Powerplant | 400 |
| 4 | Backwater, decommissioned intake channel near Irving Powerplant | 300 |
| 5 | Travertine-dam backwater below Irving Powerplant | 1,000 |

- **Acclimation:** Fish were tempered for about 30 minutes before release.
- **Condition at release:** Almost all fish appeared healthy and behaved normally. Fourteen apparent mortalities were counted.
- **Monitoring:** The stocked population will be augmented and monitored over time.
- **Future plans:**
  - Stock [[Spikedace]] and [[Loach Minnow]] in habitat downstream of the diversion dam.
  - Stock [[Desert Pupfish]] and [[Razorback Sucker]] into potentially suitable habitat in the renovated portions of Fossil Creek.
- **Scope note:** The document is an operational stocking report. It contains no survey results, population estimates or water-quality data beyond the logger placement.
