## Executive Summary
This 1989 letter from the [[Bureau of Land Management]] Safford District Office solicits renewed input from [[W.L. Minckley]], a prominent ichthyologist, and other members of the [[San Pedro Biological Resources Technical Committee]] to support two planning efforts tied to the newly completed [[San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area]] land use plan: (1) a **Habitat Management Plan (HMP)** intended to maintain and improve habitat, wildlife populations, and species diversity, and (2) a **comprehensive monitoring plan** designed to detect change across hydrological, biological, archaeological, and recreational parameters.
