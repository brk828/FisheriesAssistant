# Bureau of Reclamation Glen Canyon Dam EIS Public Scoping Announcement

#aquatic-ecology #river-management #environmental-assessment

## Executive Summary

This February 1990 announcement from the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] Upper Colorado Regional Office initiates the public scoping process for an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) concerning the operation of [[Glen Canyon Dam]]. The document announces seven public meetings scheduled between March 12 and March 27, 1990, and a written comment period extending from March 12 through April 16, 1990. The EIS will evaluate the effects of dam operations on downstream environmental and ecological resources in [[Glen Canyon National Recreation Area]] and [[Grand Canyon National Park]]. This effort builds upon the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) that began in 1982.

## Background & Description

The [[Glen Canyon Dam]] EIS was directed by Secretary of the Interior Manuel Lujan in summer 1989. The Notice of Intent to prepare the EIS was published in the Federal Register on October 27, 1989. The filing date for the final EIS was established as December 31, 1991.

Cooperating agencies in preparing the EIS include:
- [[Bureau of Reclamation]]
- [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]
- [[National Park Service]]
- [[Western Area Power Administration]] of the Department of Energy

The EIS follows and incorporates technical information from the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES), which began in 1982. The GCES forms the scientific basis for the EIS effort.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The EIS focuses on the downstream environmental and ecological resources of the [[Colorado River]] system affected by [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations. The study area encompasses:
- [[Glen Canyon National Recreation Area]]
- [[Grand Canyon National Park]]

The stated purpose is to "determine specific options to the operation of Glen Canyon Dam that could be implemented to minimize, consistent with law, the impact of the operation of Glen Canyon Dam on the downstream environmental and ecological resources" of these protected areas.

## Key Findings

**Public Scoping Process:**
- Seven public meetings scheduled across multiple states (Utah, Colorado, Arizona, California, and Washington DC)
- Written comment period: March 12 through April 16, 1990
- Comments to be sent to: Glen Canyon Dam Environmental Impact Statement, Bureau of Reclamation, P.O. Box 11568, Salt Lake City, Utah 84147

**Meeting Schedule:**
- Salt Lake City, UT: March 12, 1990, 7:00 p.m.
- Denver, CO: March 13, 1990, 7:00 p.m.
- Phoenix, AZ: March 15, 1990, 7:00 p.m.
- Flagstaff, AZ: March 16, 1990, 7:00 p.m.
- Los Angeles, CA: March 20, 1990, 7:30 p.m.
- San Francisco, CA: March 21, 1990, 7:00 p.m.
- Washington, DC: March 27, 1990, 9:30 a.m.

**Process Objectives:**
- Public input to identify issues for study
- Development of range of operational alternatives
- Evaluation of alternatives in the EIS
- Incorporation of technical information from GCES studies into draft EIS

**Accessibility:**
All meeting locations accessible to persons with disabilities, with special assistance arrangements available through the Regional Public Affairs Officer.
