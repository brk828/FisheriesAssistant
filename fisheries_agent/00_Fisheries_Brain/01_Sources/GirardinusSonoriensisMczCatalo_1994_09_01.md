# Girardinus sonoriensis (MCZ 1310 Catalog Record)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This record documents the **syntype series** of *Girardinus sonoriensis*, catalogued as **MCZ 1310** at the [[Museum of Comparative Zoology]], Harvard University. The specimen(s) were collected by **Dr. C.B. Kennerly** from **San Bernardino Creek**, a tributary of the **Rio Yaqui**, in Sonora, Mexico. This is a curatorial/type catalog entry rather than a morphological or ecological research paper; it establishes taxonomic provenance rather than descriptive biological data. The original species description is referenced as published in *Proceedings of the Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia*, vol. 11, p. 120. Additional syntypes are held under a separate accession, MCZ 1461.
