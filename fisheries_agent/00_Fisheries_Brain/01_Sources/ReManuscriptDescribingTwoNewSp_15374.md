# Correspondence Regarding Two New Species of Catostomus from Northern Mexico

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Catostomidae #taxonomy #new-species

## Executive Summary

This letter from Robert R. Miller, Curator of Fishes at the [[University of Michigan Museum of Zoology]], to the [[American Museum of Natural History]] reviews a manuscript describing two new species of [[Catostomus]] (suckers) from northern Mexico. Miller confirms the validity of both species as distinct taxa and endorses the manuscript for publication, providing editorial comments and noting that holotypes and paratypes will be deposited at UMMZ.

## Background & Description

The document is correspondence dated from [[Ann Arbor]], Michigan, reviewing a manuscript co-authored by the recipient and another researcher describing two new species of [[Catostomus]] from northern Mexico. Miller had previously studied material of these two suckers independently and confirmed they represent clearly valid, undescribed species belonging to the family [[Catostomidae]].

The manuscript includes figures illustrating the two species, adequate descriptions, and explanations of their origin and relationships. Miller particularly notes the value of the zoogeography section of the manuscript.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The new species are from northern Mexico, representing additions to the [[Catostomus]] fauna of this region. No specific habitat or environmental details are provided in this correspondence, though the zoogeographic discussion in the reviewed manuscript is noted as especially interesting and valuable for understanding species origins and relationships.

## Key Findings

- Two new species of [[Catostomus]] from northern Mexico are confirmed as valid, undescribed taxa
- The manuscript provides adequate descriptions and illustrations of both species
- Miller provided editorial comments including: corrections on proper terminology (insertion versus origin), missing Spanish accents, spelling questions, and suggested rephrasing
- One specific editorial recommendation: deletion of pelvic fin position statement (page 19) as not valuable since it applies to all [[Catostomidae]] species involved
- Holotypes and paratype series of both new species to be deposited at [[University of Michigan Museum of Zoology]] (UMMZ)
- UMMZ catalog numbers to be assigned (format: UMMZ 200000 series)
- Specimens to be delivered by Donn Rosen during visit to [[Ann Arbor]]
- Collection Manager Doug Nelson designated to coordinate specimen accessioning and catalog number assignment
