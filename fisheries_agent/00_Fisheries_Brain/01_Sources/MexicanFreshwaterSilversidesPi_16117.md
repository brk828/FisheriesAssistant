# Archomenidia marvelae: A New Freshwater Silverside from the Rio Papaloapan, Mexico

#ichthyology #marine-biology #atherinidae

## Executive Summary

This 1982 taxonomic paper by [[Barry Chernoff]] and [[Robert Rush Miller]] describes *Archomenidia marvelae* as a new species of freshwater silverside from the [[Rio Papaloapan]] basin, Oaxaca and Veracruz, Mexico. The new species raises the genus *Archomenidia* to two species. It is distinguished mainly by a smaller eye, along with differences in body proportions, fin positions, pigmentation, dentition and cephalic pore counts. The authors also synonymize *A. bolivari* with *A. sallei* and provide new descriptive data for *A. sallei*. Morphometric analyses show that the two species differ in allometric growth patterns.

## Background & Description

The fish faunas of the Papaloapan and Coatzacoalcos rivers are biogeographically allied with Central America and form part of the [[Usumacinta Fish Province]]. Three related silverside genera, *Archomenidia*, *Melaniris* and *Xenatherina*, occur in fresh and brackish waters of the region, sometimes sympatrically, and extend into upland areas.

*Archomenidia* belongs to subfamily Menidiinae and is diagnosed by characters including:
- precaudal vertebrae usually 22 or 23;
- two widely spaced prenarial sensory pits on the snout;
- an anteriorly placed anus;
- a divided upper hypural plate;
- gill rakers on the first arch fewer than 19;
- a dark, black-appearing mesovarium.

The type species is *Atherinichthys sallei* (Regan). Chernoff (1981) showed that *A. bolivari* Alvarez and Carranza (1952) is conspecific with *A. sallei*.

**Holotype of *A. marvelae*:** UMMZ 209012, adult female, 80.2 mm SL, collected 19 July 1962 at the Rio Bravo just below Salto de Eyipantla, Veracruz. The paratype series includes 41 specimens (12–36.2 mm SL) from the type locality and 17 specimens (59.1–86.2 mm SL) from the Rio Papaloapan at Papaloapan, Oaxaca.

**Key morphological features of *A. marvelae*:** The species attains 94.7 mm SL, has a blunt, broad snout and strongly falcate anal and second dorsal fins, and its lower caudal lobe is longer than the upper. Its meristics include 18 anal rays (27 of 33 counts) and 38 lateral scale rows (8 of 33 counts). It has 5 supraorbital pores and 4 or 5 anterior infraorbital pores.

The name *marvelae* honors Marvel B. Parrington, who contributed to ichthyological work at the University of Michigan for 17 years.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Distribution:** *A. marvelae* is known from only two localities. The type locality lies in the Rio Bravo, which drains Lake Catemaco. The authors note that no atherinids have been collected above the falls or in the lake (Miller 1975).
- **Habitat:** Both localities have strong currents and clear water, though the Papaloapan may become muddy in the rainy season. The type-locality stream is about 25 m wide with coarse sand, rocks and boulders. At Papaloapan the river is about 500 m wide, with sand, fine mud, gravel bars and occasional large rocks.
- **Climate and setting:** The sites lie in tropical lowlands surrounded by perennial forest, within the 10°C minimum and 40°C maximum isotherms (Rzedowski 1978). Annual rainfall is 160–320 cm.
- **Associated fishes:** Species recorded near the type locality include *Xiphophorus helleri*, *A. sallei*, and three *Cichlasoma* species. The Papaloapan paratype collections also included *Dorosoma anale*, *Astyanax fasciatus*, *Cathorops aguadulce*, *Poecilia mexicana*, *P. sphenops*, *Strongylura hubbsi* and *Agonostomus monticola*.
- **Sympatry:** *A. marvelae* is sympatric with *A. sallei* at both of its known localities.

## Key Findings

1. **New species:** *A. marvelae* is separated from *A. sallei* mainly by its smaller eye. Eye diameter is <9.5% SL (mean 8.6%) versus 29.8% SL (mean 11.1%) in *A. sallei*. Eye diameter for the smallest *A. marvelae* specimens is not conclusively reported, so the value is compared with a range that includes ranges of the two species given in Table 1.
2. **Body and fin differences:** *A. marvelae* has a more slender body (mean greatest depth 19.3% SL versus 22.3%), a shorter preanal distance (57.1% versus 60.2%), shorter pelvic fins (14.1% versus 15.5%), and a shorter distance between the origins of the anal and dorsal fins. Its anal fin usually originates below the 18th vertebra, versus the 19th in *A. sallei*.
3. **Other diagnostic characters:** *A. marvelae* has a ventral caudal-peduncle stripe of two or three rows of large melanophores, fewer dentary teeth in one or two rows, teeth on the second pharyngobranchial with a shoulder, and 4–5 anterior infraorbital pores (versus 2–3 in *A. sallei*).
4. **Allometry:** Eye diameter scales with SL similarly in both species, but the multivariate allometries differ. Head length and eye diameter are negatively allometric in *A. marvelae* and isometric in *A. sallei*. The authors conclude that the discordance cannot be attributed to environmental effects, because two *A. sallei* populations sympatric with *A. marvelae* do not differ significantly from allopatric ones.
5. **Shape discrimination:** Scores on the size-independent shape discriminator do not overlap between species. These scores discriminate even the smallest specimen examined (24.5 mm SL).
6. **Synonymy:** *A. bolivari* is treated as a junior synonym of *A. sallei*, and the authors provide new meristic and morphometric data for *A. sallei* (up to 75 mm SL).
7. **Distribution:** *A. sallei* occurs across the Papaloapan and Coatzacoalcos basins. *A. marvelae* is restricted to two localities in the Papaloapan basin.
