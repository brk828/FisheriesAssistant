# Recovery Team Follow-Up Assignments for Desert Pupfish, Gila Topminnow and Yaqui Topminnow (December 1989)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinodontidae #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This undated-day memorandum, dated December 12, 1989, is a follow-up circular sent to members of the [[Desert Pupfish]] recovery team ("DFRT"). It lists work assignments that arose from a recovery team meeting. The main decisions were that the [[Gila Topminnow]] should not be downlisted at this time, that the [[Yaqui Topminnow]] should move into a draft Yaqui fishes recovery plan, and that the Gila topminnow should receive its own recovery plan. The assignments cover letters to the Regional Director, genetic work, handling of potential hybrid pupfish, archiving of literature, consultation with Mexican biologists, and preparation of draft recovery plans with downlisting and delisting criteria.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Internal correspondence and task list, not a research study. It contains no field data, sample sizes or statistics.
- **Author and purpose:** The memo was written by the team coordinator, Minckley, from meeting notes. It asks assignees to report progress and to flag any omitted items.
- **Species covered:**
  - [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon*-group fish; scientific name not given in the document)
  - [[Gila Topminnow]]
  - [[Yaqui Topminnow]], to be moved from its current recovery plan into a draft [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]
- **Assignees:** Stefferud, Marsh/Sada, Hendrickson, Dahlem and Divine, each with specific tasks.
- **Institutions named:** [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (implied by "FWS files" and the Regional Director), [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AGFD), [[Tohono Chul]], and an unnamed archiving institution.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document says little about the fish's habitat, ecology or population status. It does address three management concerns:

- **Hybridization:** Some pupfish at [[Tohono Chul]] may be potential hybrids. The team recommended removing them and restocking with fish of known genetic lineage.
- **Genetic lineage:** Genetic work on desert pupfish and Gila topminnow is needed to inform [[Downlisting and Delisting Criteria]].
- **Mexican populations:** Pupfish populations in Mexico need plan input, which requires a meeting with Mexican biologists. The document gives no detail on their status or location.

## Key Findings

1. **Gila Topminnow status:** The team recommended that the Gila topminnow *not* be downlisted at this time. The Regional Director letter should state that the team is developing new downlisting and delisting criteria for the next recovery plan revision.
2. **Yaqui Topminnow placement:** The team recommended that the Yaqui topminnow be included in the draft [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]].
3. **Separate Gila Topminnow plan:** The team recommended a separate recovery plan for the Gila topminnow.
4. **Genetic research:**
   - Genetic work on desert pupfish is to be done as soon as possible.
   - Genetic work on Gila topminnow is also needed.
   - Both are intended to support downlisting and delisting criteria.
   - Funding for this work is to be pursued once the letter goes to the Regional Director.
5. **Hybrid management at Tohono Chul:** Potential hybrid pupfish are to be removed and replaced with stock of known genetic lineage. A letter requesting permission from Tohono Chul for AGFD to carry out the removal and restocking is to follow.
6. **Literature archiving:**
   - Desert pupfish literature assembled during the recovery planning process is to be archived at an appropriate institution.
   - Once an institution is chosen, copies are to be made. One copy goes to the archive and one to FWS files, and originals return to contractors.
   - Hendrickson and Dahlem are each responsible for copying half of the literature.
7. **Mexican consultation:** A meeting of desert pupfish recovery plan preparers and Mexican biologists is to be arranged to get plan input on Mexican populations.
8. **Team organization:** Interim team organization and/or reorganization is to be arranged.
9. **Draft recovery plans:**
   - Marsh/Sada: draft desert pupfish recovery plan with draft downlisting and delisting criteria.
   - Hendrickson: draft Gila topminnow recovery plan with draft downlisting and delisting criteria.
   - Divine: coordinate the move of the Yaqui topminnow into the draft Yaqui fishes recovery plan.
10. **Meeting minutes:** Minutes are to be prepared and transmitted to the Regional Director with a summary of recommendations.
