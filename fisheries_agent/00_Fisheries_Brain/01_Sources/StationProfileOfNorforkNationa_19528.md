## Executive Summary

This document is a station profile for the [[Norfork National Fish Hatchery]], a federal salmonid production facility operated by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in Mountain Home, Arkansas. Established in 1955 under Appropriation Acts (60 Stat. 460), the hatchery's mission is to mitigate the loss of warmwater riverine habitat caused by the construction of four multipurpose dams on the Upper [[White River]] system. The facility rears [[Rainbow Trout]], [[Brown Trout]], [[Brook Trout]], [[Lake Trout]], and [[Cutthroat Trout]] for stocking in Arkansas and neighboring states, functioning as a mitigation hatchery for federally altered aquatic habitat rather than a wild population study site.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Historical habitat**: The Upper [[White River]] was originally a warmwater riverine system.
- **Anthropogenic alteration**: Construction of four multipurpose dams (in sequence: [[Beaver Dam and Lake]], [[Table Rock Lake]], [[Lake Taneycomb]], and [[Bull Shoals Reservoir]]) converted the free-flowing warmwater river into a series of lentic (reservoir) impoundments interspersed with cold tailwater reaches below each dam.
- **Resulting niche**: The cold tailwater habitat created below Norfork Dam is unsuitable for native warmwater fish assemblages but supports introduced coldwater [[Salmonidae]] species, which the hatchery is mandated to stock as mitigation for this habitat conversion.
- **Water source**: Hatchery water is drawn directly from [[Norfork Dam]] via a 24-inch multilevel intake line (primary) and a 16-inch auxiliary line from the dam's inhouse generator penstock (enlarging to 24 inches into the aerator building), simulating the cold, hypolimnetic tailwater conditions required by trout species. Average consumption is ~18,000 gallons/minute, with supply/drain infrastructure capable of handling 22,000 gallons/minute (31.68 million gallons/day).

## Key Findings

- **Facility scale**: Located on 43.9 acres of U.S. Army Corps of Engineers land; includes 96 outdoor raceways (100'×8'×3' each), 20 indoor tanks (30'×3'×3' each), and 60 indoor troughs (15'×2'×1' each) within the hatchery building, plus an egg hatching facility.
- **Mitigation mandate**: The hatchery exists specifically to offset ecological damage from Federal water development projects (dam construction) impacting Arkansas and surrounding states.
- **Distribution (stocking) by state**: Arkansas (80%), Missouri (10%), Oklahoma (10%).
