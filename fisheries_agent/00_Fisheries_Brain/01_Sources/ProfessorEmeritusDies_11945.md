## Background & Description

**Obituary of Gerald Ainsworth Cole**
- Born December 25, 1917, in Hartford, Connecticut; died June 22, 1999, in Mesa, Arizona, aged 81.
- Education: B.A. in biology, [[Middlebury College]] (1939); M.S., St. Lawrence University (1941); Ph.D. in zoology (minor botany), University of Minnesota (1949).
- Military service: Captain in the U.S. Army during World War II (European Theatre, 1942–1946).
- Career: joined the University of Louisville, Kentucky, in 1949 and rose to associate professor; joined ASU as lecturer in 1958, became professor in the zoology (now biology) department, and retired as professor emeritus in 1979.
- Teaching style: lectures linked several topics, including current events, English literature, and foreign water problems, to a single point. Students valued his humor and insight.
- Family: survived by his wife, Jean, two daughters, three sons, and three grandchildren.

**Scholarship item**
- Vincent Vernhes, a recent graduate of the [[American English and Culture Program]] from France, received the [[Yucel Yelkin International Student Scholar Award]]. The award carries a $500 scholarship to ASU. It was the first year the program offered this award.
- Vernhes planned to major in history with interest in American history and the Vietnam War.

**Other content**
