Field notes from a fish survey conducted at [[Lake Mjømane]] on November 26-28, 1994. The document records catch data for multiple fish species including [[Coregonus lavaretus]] (whitefish), [[Salmo salar]] (salmon), [[Salvelinus alpinus]] (Arctic char), and other species. The notes appear to be handwritten field records with species identifications, length measurements, and stomach content observations.

## Background & Description

This survey targeted multiple fish species in [[Lake Mjømane]] during late November 1994. The sampling effort documented various salmonid and percid species, with particular attention to:

- **[[Coregonus lavaretus]]**: 23.38% of catch (3.40 specimens recorded)
- **[[Salvelinus alpinus]]**: 1.94% (0.44 specimens)
- **[[Micropterus salmoides]]**: 12.2% (0.15 specimens)
- **[[Perca fluviatilis]]**: 13.94% (0.45 specimens)

Total sample size recorded as N=286 specimens. Length measurements ranged from approximately 185mm to 240-260mm for various specimens.
