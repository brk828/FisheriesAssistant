# Cui-ui Recovery Plan Format — Administrative Correspondence

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Catostomidae #ConservationPolicy #FreshwaterEcology

## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary scientific or morphological study but an internal administrative letter from Tom Strekal, Team Leader of the **[[Cui-ui]] Recovery Team**, addressed to team members regarding the structural format of the forthcoming **Cui-ui Recovery Plan**. The letter summarizes a meeting held on August 17, 1989, in the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) Reno office, where Chet Buchanan (FWS-Region 1) proposed an abbreviated recovery plan format modeled on those used for the **[[Owens Tui Chub]]** and **[[Pahranagat Roundtail Chub]]**. The letter also documents a follow-up conversation with Andy Robinson (FWS-Portland Recovery Coordinator) and raises procedural concerns, notably the unresolved **"hybrid question"** affecting the [[Cui-ui]], a critically endangered catostomid endemic to **[[Pyramid Lake]]**, Nevada.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- The [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) is implicitly referenced as a species endemic to **[[Pyramid Lake]]**, part of the terminal **Lahontan Basin** system in Nevada — an endorheic (closed) freshwater lake lacking oceanic outflow.
- The letter references a **"hybrid question,"** implying documented or suspected hybridization concerns for the [[Cui-ui]], which is ecologically and taxonomically significant for recovery planning and species status determinations under the Endangered Species Act.
- No specific habitat parameters, water chemistry, prey relationships, or spawning ecology are detailed in this correspondence.

## Key Findings
- **Recovery Plan Format**: FWS-Region 1 recommended an abbreviated ("generic") recovery plan format combining elements from the Owens tui chub and Pahranagat roundtail chub plans, consisting of four core sections:
  1. **Introduction** — species distribution, life history, status, and threats, including a "step-back" analysis and a one-page summary figure.
  2. **Recovery** — defines recovery in terms of threat reduction, with narrative strategy (no traditional step-down analysis).
  3. **Task and Implementation Schedule** — table of second-/third-level tasks, total costs, and responsible agencies.
  4. **Management Plan** — site-specific actions to achieve recovery, ideally appended to the main plan.
- **Team Resistance**: The Cui-ui Recovery Team expressed resistance to adopting an abbreviated/generic format, citing caution due to the unresolved **hybrid question** affecting taxonomic/species status.
- **FWS Rationale**: Andy Robinson (FWS-Portland) argued the abbreviated format is more accessible and less prone to unauthorized modification by resource managers, and offered flexibility for the Management Plan (can be completed separately, in installments/"chapters," and updated as new data emerge).
- **Administrative Oversight**: The FWS Regional Director's office retains authority to review and determine the adequacy of the recovery plan format; disagreements must be escalated promptly.
- **Timeline**: A firm deadline of **January 31, 1990**, was set for submission of the first draft of the [[Cui-ui Recovery Plan]], absent alternative arrangements.
- **Missing Expert Input**: Dr. Minckley, a recovery team member, was not present at the format discussion meeting due to short notice and required follow-up briefing.
