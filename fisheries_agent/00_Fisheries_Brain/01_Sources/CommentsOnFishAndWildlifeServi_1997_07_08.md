## Executive Summary
This document is a public comment letter submitted by the Southern California Bass Council (SCBC), a recreational angling and conservation advocacy organization, in response to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's Final Biological and Conference Opinion on Lower Colorado River Operations and Maintenance (Lake Mead to the Southerly International Boundary). The letter is **not a primary scientific study** but rather a stakeholder policy critique. It raises concerns that the Biological Opinion's protections for federally listed [[Threatened and Endangered Species]] on the [[Lower Colorado River]] may come at the expense of habitat quality and management balance for non-listed warm-water sport fish, particularly [[Black Bass]]. The letter argues for an ecosystem-based management approach that considers recreational fisheries alongside endangered species recovery.
