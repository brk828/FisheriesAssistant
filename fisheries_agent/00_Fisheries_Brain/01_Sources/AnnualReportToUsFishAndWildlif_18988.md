# Annual Report on Endangered Species Permit PTR-676811 (1993)

#ichthyology #freshwater-ecology #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) Tucson Resource Area Office to the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Region 2) reports on fish collecting under [[Endangered Species Permit]] PTR-676811 in 1993. Three activities were conducted. The largest was the [[Fall Fish Count]] on [[Cienega Creek]], Pima County, Arizona, run by the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], in which 1,669 [[Gila Topminnow]] and 81 [[Gila Chub]] were captured at eight seine sites. A survey of [[Beaver Dam Wash]] (Mohave County) found no listed species. The memo also describes holding 100 topminnow from Cienega Creek in captivity, with two fish lost to predation by small chub. No formal report had been generated for any dataset at the time of writing.

## Background & Description

- **Author:** Jeffrey R. Simms, Fishery Biologist, BLM Tucson Resource Area Office.
- **Date:** December 7, 1993.
- **Permit:** PTR-676811, an [[Endangered Species Permit]] held under the Endangered Species Act framework, administered by USFWS Region 2.
- **Document type:** Annual permit report with attached field data sheets, habitat monitoring sheets, fish inventory sheets, and USGS topographic maps.

The three permitted activities were:

1. **Beaver Dam Wash, Mohave County (September 1):** The entire BLM-managed reach (about 300 yards) was sampled by [[Backpack Electrofishing]].
2. **Cienega Creek, Pima County (October 12-28):** Eight sites were sampled by [[Seine Sampling]] as part of the Arizona Game and Fish Department's fall count.
3. **Cienega Creek transfer (November 24):** 100 topminnow were collected from two previously sampled sites (FC0311 and FC0313) and held at the BLM Tucson office.

The planned Bonita Creek survey (Graham County) was scheduled for December 8-10, 1993. Its main goal was to locate [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Cienega Creek field conditions.** The attached [[Fall Fish Count]] data sheets record a range of conditions across sites:

- **Air temperatures** were roughly 16-26 °C and **water temperatures** roughly 12.5-22 °C. Values vary by site and date, and some are partly illegible in the scans.
- **Flow** was recorded as none, slow, moderate, or fast at each effort. Some sites were slow-moving pools, and one reach was recorded at about 0.5 ft/s.
- **Aquatic vegetation** included cattail (*Typha*) and beggartick (*Bidens*).
- **Aquatic invertebrates** noted on the sheets included giant water bugs (Belostomatidae), water scorpions (Nepidae), dragonfly and damselfly larvae, mayfly nymphs, beetles, and leeches. Individual site notes mention a mud turtle at one location.
- **Habitat** at several sites was described as turbid water, with some sites near a headwater spring and others below a well or road crossing.

**Captive holding conditions.** Fish were transported in two 5-gallon containers and held in a 50-gallon aquarium at the BLM Tucson office, following conditions in the 1993 permit application. Several topminnow became listless after transport and transfer.

**Predation.** Two topminnow were eaten by small chub (under 100 mm total length), and the chub then became moribund. The memo does not give a cause of death for the chub.

## Key Findings

- **Gila topminnow:** 1,669 captured during the fall count of eight seine sites on Cienega Creek (October 12-28, 1993).
- **Gila chub:** 81 captured incidentally during the same count, which the author notes "may be of interest."
- **Captive holding:** 100 topminnow from two sites (FC0311, FC0313) were moved on November 24. Of these, 96 recovered and appeared healthy after a short adjustment period, and two were lost to predation by small chub.
- **Beaver Dam Wash:** The full BLM-managed reach (about 300 yards) was electrofished on September 1. No listed species were encountered.
- **Bonita Creek:** A survey was planned for December 8-10, 1993, to locate razorback sucker. Results were to be sent as soon as possible.
- **Reporting status:** As of this memo, no formal report had been generated from the Beaver Dam Wash or Cienega Creek data. Raw data sheets and maps with locations were enclosed.

### Attached Data Sheets (summary)

- **Fall Fish Count sheets (roughly 15 pages):** Records for several Cienega Creek sites from October 1993. Each sheet has site ID (FC-series), GPS-style location and township/range, subhabitat type, dimensions, flow, gear type, seine dimensions, and haul counts. Per-species counts are given for topminnow (POOC), Gila chub (GIIN), and the species code AGCH (probably *Agosia chrysogaster*, longfin dace, inferred from the code). Several site totals are partly illegible in the scans.
- **Aquatic habitat monitoring sheet (Beaver Dam Wash):** Substrate, instream cover, pool and riffle measurements, and fish comments. The sheet notes fish collected and recovery of a stressed population after electrofishing.
- **BLM fish inventory sheets (Safford District):** Species, length, weight, effort, and comment fields for fish collected in several streams. Entries are too degraded to transcribe reliably.
- **USGS 7.5' topographic maps:** Spring Water Canyon and related quadrangles (1:24,000 scale, 1985 provisional edition) showing collection sites.
