# Level 1 Contaminant Survey of Green River Bottomland Sites and Selenium Seep Discharge (1994)

#aquatic-ecology #water-quality #selenium-contamination

## Executive Summary

This 1994 correspondence and field packet from the [[U.S. Geological Survey]] (Water Resources Division, Salt Lake City) to the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] (Grand Junction, CO) documents [[Level 1 Survey]] work on potential [[Bottomland Sites]] along the [[Green River]]. The letter reports completed survey sheets for 5 sites and lists 4 sites that were not surveyed. The attached field data focus on seep and spring discharge and [[Selenium]] loading at the Escalante Ranch area. The packet contains no fish or other biological sampling data.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Cover letter dated October 31, 1994, with enclosed survey sheets and a supplemental contaminant checklist (Attachment 1), with pages handwritten and OCR-scanned.
- **Sender:** [[U.S. Geological Survey]], Water Resources Division, Room 1016 Administration Building, 1745 West 1700 South, Salt Lake City, Utah 84104.
- **Recipient:** Kathy Holley, [[Bureau of Reclamation]], 2764 Compass Dr., Grand Junction, CO 81506.
- **Sites completed:** Five potential bottomland sites along the Green River (number not specified by name in the letter).
- **Sites not worked:** Baeser Bend, Brennan Bottom, West Branch, and Tia Juana Bottom, which the author understood to be no longer available.
- **Checklist structure:** The Level 1 Supplemental Contaminant Checklist has a desk audit (consultation with contaminants experts, surficial geology, historical hydrology, mining, energy development, NPDES permits, land use, water quality and contaminant data) and an on-site reconnaissance (land use, water sources, drainage, vegetation, soil, hydrological conditions, excavated pits or subsidence).
- **Data sheet:** A table titled for Escalante Ranch lists collector, estimated time, discharge (L/min), reach length (ft.), discharge per reach length (L/min/ft.), conductivity (µS/cm), selenium concentration (µg/L), and selenium load (µg/day) for sites labeled S-1 through S-15 and SE-series sites.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Setting:** Bottomland and hillside seep sites adjacent to the [[Green River]], with springs and seeps noted in the discharge table and checklist.
- **Contaminant focus:** Selenium concentration and load, with conductivity recorded alongside. Selenium is the main contaminant measured in the packet.
- **Hydrology:** Seep discharge was measured at multiple points along a hillside. The sheet notes a roughly 4,000 ft lineal distance along the hillside.
- **Land use and vegetation:** Checklist entries mention vegetation, drainage, and land use, but many handwritten entries are partly illegible after OCR.
- **Biota:** No fish, invertebrate, or other biological survey data are reported. Biota is only referenced in checklist headings (plant and animal data).

## Key Findings

- **Survey status:** Level 1 survey sheets were completed for 5 potential bottomland sites along the Green River. Baeser Bend, Brennan Bottom, West Branch, and Tia Juana Bottom were not worked because they were understood to be no longer available. The author offered to complete forms for any of them on request.
- **Reporting plan:** The author planned to bring several [[U.S. Geological Survey]] contaminants reports for the Green River area to the next meeting, because it was not known whether the Bureau already had copies.
- **Selenium loading (Escalante Ranch table):**
  - Site S-1: discharge 16.0 L/min, conductivity 21,960 µS/cm (listed as "2100 µg/L" selenium in the OCR; the value 9300 is also printed), selenium load 46.116.
  - Site S-2: discharge 4.0 L/min, selenium load 77.760.
  - Site S-3 (near E15 well): discharge 2.8 L/min, selenium load 136.705.
  - Site S-4: selenium load 1.858.
  - Site S-7: selenium load 0.000 (not sampled for load).
  - Several sites (including S-5, S-6, S-8 through S-10) list selenium load as 0.000 or no sample, and some discharge values are marked "not meas."
- **Total selenium load:** The table reports a total Se of 265 (printed as "g/day" in the OCR, though the column header is µg/day, so the units are ambiguous in the scan).
- **Average discharge per reach length:** 0.252 L/min/ft.
- **Total discharge estimate:** For the 4,000 ft lineal distance along the hillside, the sheet states total discharge (including 2 springs) is 1,469 m³/day, or about 0.6 cfs. A separate total row reads 0.450 CFS, so the two discharge figures should be checked against the original scan.
- **Checklist content:** Desk audit and reconnaissance entries describe soil and parent material (alluvial or gravel-based deposits, partly legible), historical hydrology, drainage, and vegetation, but most handwritten responses are too degraded for reliable transcription.
- **Fish or aquatic biota:** None reported. This document is a contaminant and hydrological survey, not an ichthyological study.
