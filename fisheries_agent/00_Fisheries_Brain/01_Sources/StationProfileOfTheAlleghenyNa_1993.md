# Station Profile: Allegheny National Fish Hatchery

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Salmonidae #fish-hatchery #lake-trout #restoration-ecology

## Executive Summary

The Allegheny National Fish Hatchery, established in 1959 under Public Law 86-205 and located in Warren, Pennsylvania, is a federal propagation facility whose primary mission is the restoration of [[Lake Trout]] populations in [[Lake Erie]] and [[Lake Ontario]]. During Fiscal Year 1993, the station produced approximately 700,000 yearling lake trout and 240,000 lake trout sac fry for stocking, sourced from roughly 3.0 million eggs collected annually from on-station broodfish. All yearling lake trout released were marked with a combined adipose fin clip and binary coded wire tag (CWT) to support later cohort identification in restoration monitoring. The station also maintains a nascent broodstock program for [[Landlocked Atlantic Salmon]], supported by egg transfers from Maine, and distributes surplus eggs to partner state and federal hatcheries as part of a broader interagency and international lake trout rehabilitation effort (likely coordinated under Great Lakes Fishery Commission frameworks).

## Background & Description

Direct morphological data are limited in this administrative station profile, but the following biometric/production figures are notable:

- **Lake Trout spring yearlings (FY93):** 715,300 individuals totaling 74,183 pounds — an average individual weight of approximately **0.104 lb (~47 g)** per yearling.
- **Landlocked Atlantic Salmon stocked (May 25, 1993):** 25,000 individuals weighing a combined 13 pounds — reported as extremely small size class, likely fry/fingerling stage rather than adult stock (weight figure appears anomalously low relative to fish count, possibly a transcription/OCR artifact from the original report).
- **Marking method:** Adipose fin clip combined with binary coded wire tag (CWT), a standard technique allowing non-lethal external recognition (clip) paired with lethal/head-recovery decoding (CWT) for cohort-specific tracking in mixed-stock Great Lakes fisheries.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[Lake Trout]] (*Salvelinus namaycush*) functions as a native apex/top-tier piscivore in Great Lakes deepwater ecosystems, historically extirpated or severely depleted in [[Lake Erie]] and [[Lake Ontario]] due to overfishing, sea lamprey predation, and habitat degradation. The hatchery's stocking program is a direct human intervention supporting **species reintroduction and restoration ecology**, targeting self-sustaining natural reproduction as the ultimate management goal.

The [[Landlocked Atlantic Salmon]] (*Salmo salar*) broodstock program represents a complementary restoration effort for a landlocked salmonid population adapted to Great Lakes and connected river systems (e.g., Oswego and Oneida Rivers), occupying a similar pelagic predatory niche.

Stocking logistics reflect basin-scale ecological management: fish were distributed across **Federal Waters (FWS/International, e.g., U.S. Army Corps of Engineers-associated waters)** and **State waters**, indicating multi-jurisdictional coordination (New York, Pennsylvania, Michigan) typical of Great Lakes fisheries restoration, which require synchronized, cross-border management due to the shared nature of the lake basins.

An experimental stocking of 150,000 sac fry directly into Lake Erie (rather than as reared yearlings) suggests ongoing adaptive management research into optimal life-stage release strategies for maximizing survival and establishment success.

## Key Findings

- **Broodstock & Egg Production:** ~3.0 million lake trout eggs collected annually from on-station broodfish; FY93 total green egg take was 3,257,500 eggs from hatchery and wild broodstock (October–November 1992).
- **Egg Distribution:** 135,000 eyed eggs shipped to National Fish Hatcheries in Tennessee and Vermont plus the Cortland Research Laboratory; 127,000 green eggs provided to New York State for its **splake** program (lake trout × brook trout hybrid).
- **Production Target Adjustment:** Yearling lake trout production was intentionally reduced from 1.2 million to 700,000 tagged fish at the request of interagency and international restoration partners, reflecting coordinated basin-wide stocking quota management.
- **Atlantic Salmon Broodstock Initiative:** 50,000 landlocked Atlantic salmon eggs received from Maine to establish a new broodstock line, anticipating the lake trout production reduction.
- **FY93 Yearling Stocking Campaign:** 26 lots (715,300 lake trout, 74,183 lbs) distributed via 53 trips; stocking ran April 15, 1993 (Lake Erie) through June 4, 1993 (Stony Creek, Lake Ontario). All fish were adipose-clipped and coded-wire tagged.
- **Atlantic Salmon Stocking:** 25,000 fish (13 lbs total) released into the Oswego and Oneida Rivers on May 25, 1993; additionally 150,000 lake trout sac fry released into Lake Erie as an experimental fry plant.
- **Visitor/Outreach:** ~2,400 visitors (mainly group tours) in FY93.
- **Infrastructure Investment:** $31,240 spent in FY93 (1313 funding) for drilling/testing of Well #5; cumulative $147,429 obligated for hookup and testing, with completion expected by late FY94.
- **Volunteer Program:** Two volunteers (Lisa Zumstein, 4 months, earning university biology credit at University of Pittsburgh–Bradford; Vicki Cawthon, June–July 1993) supported hatchery operations.
- **Budget Trends (FY89–93):** Operations budget rose from $434,029 (FY89) to $467,460 (FY93 est.); Maintenance budget fluctuated sharply, from $32,086 (FY89) to a projected $161,000 (FY93), reflecting major infrastructure investment (e.g., Well #5). Staffing held steady at ~7.9–8.0 FTE, aside from a dip to 6.9 FTE in FY91 attributed partly to staff leave-without-pay (LWOP).
- **Fish Distribution Totals (excluding eggs/fry), FY91–93:**
  - FY91: 1,424,936 total (FWS: 44,407; International Waters: 1,355,940; State/Backwaters: 24,589)
  - FY92: 1,180,209 total (FWS: 2,277; International Waters: 1,176,857; State/Backwaters: 1,075)
  - FY93: 1,236,665 total (FWS: 4,134; International Waters: 1,052,300 + 153,000; State/Backwaters: 27,231)
- **Partner States/Agencies:** New York, Pennsylvania, Michigan; multiple partner hatcheries and research stations involved in cost-sharing/recoverables.
