# Draft Recovery Plan for the Endangered and Threatened Fishes of the Rio Yaqui: Comment Period Extension

#ichthyology #marine-biology #ictaluridae #cyprinidae #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a U.S. [[Fish And Wildlife Service]] (Service) cover letter and Federal Register notice announcing an extension of the public review and comment period for a [[Draft Recovery Plan]] covering four fishes of the [[Rio Yaqui]] listed under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. The comment period, originally scheduled to close on August 8, 1994, was extended to October 7, 1994. All comments received from June 7, 1994 through October 7, 1994 will be considered before the plan is finalized. The notice is issued under section 4(f) of the Endangered Species Act (16 U.S.C. 1533(f)).

## Background & Description

The recovery plan covers four species:

- [[Yaqui Catfish]] (*Ictalurus pricei*): listed as threatened on August 31, 1984 (49 FR 34494).
- [[Beautiful Shiner]] (*Cyprinella formosa*): listed as threatened on August 31, 1984 (49 FR 34494).
- [[Yaqui Chub]] (*Gila purpurea*): listed as endangered on August 31, 1984 (49 FR 34494).
- [[Yaqui Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis sonoriensis*): listed as endangered on March 11, 1967 (32 FR 4001).

The Notice of Availability for review and comment was published on June 7, 1994. The 60-day comment period was extended by this notice. Copies of the draft plan could be requested from the Refuge Manager at [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]], 1408 10th Street, Douglas, Arizona 85607. Written comments were to be sent to the same office, and received comments are available for public inspection by appointment during normal business hours.

The cover letter, addressed to the [[Office Of The Federal Register]] at the [[National Archives And Records Administration]], asks that the notice be published as soon as possible. It lists copy recipients including the Refuge Manager, Service regional and field office staff, and Dr. W.L. Minckley, Recovery Team Leader at Arizona State University, Tempe. Contacts named in the letter include George Divine at (505) 766-3972 and Kevin Cobble at (602) 364-2104 (the notice gives Mr. Cobble's number as (602) 823-4251). The letter is signed by a Regional Director, and the signature block carries the name James A. Young as Acting Regional Director. The date is partly illegible in the scan (apparently "1979"), which conflicts with the 1994 comment dates in the notice.

The document does not describe habitat, ecology, life history, population status, water chemistry, or threats for any of the four species. ## Key Findings

- **Comment period extended:** The Service extended the public comment period on the draft recovery plan from August 8, 1994 to October 7, 1994.
- **Scope:** The plan covers four fish species from the Rio Yaqui: two threatened and two endangered.
- **Listing dates:** The threatened species (Yaqui catfish and beautiful shiner) and the endangered Yaqui chub were listed on August 31, 1984. The Yaqui topminnow was listed earlier, on March 11, 1967.
- **Comment handling:** All comments received between June 7 and October 7, 1994 will be considered before the plan is finalized.
- **Access:** The draft plan and comments are available through the [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] Refuge Manager.
- **Legal basis:** The action is authorized under section 4(f) of the [[Endangered Species Act]].
