# Razorback Sucker Induced Spawning Attempts and Genetic Sample Collection, Ouray NFH (May 1991)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This field and hatchery memo summarizes the holding, induced spawning status, and tissue sampling of wild-caught [[Razorback Sucker]] broodstock at [[Ouray National Fish Hatchery]], Utah, during 19–21 May (1991). Fish were taken from [[Lake Powell]] in two cohorts (Spring 1990 and Spring 1991). Most fish were held in a 1/10-acre pond or 8-ft tanks. Induced spawning with [[HCG Injection]] was attempted in only some individuals, since others were judged not sufficiently gravid or too stressed. Tissue and gamete samples were collected for [[Mitochondrial DNA Analysis]] (mtDNA) and [[Allozyme Analysis]]. The memo also notes the possible storage of two mortalities in ultra-cold conditions at the [[Vernal Office]].

## Background & Description

The document is an internal summary of broodstock management for the [[Razorback Sucker]], a large catostomid native to the [[Colorado River Basin]]. The species is a conservation priority, which makes captive propagation and genetic characterization of wild stock important for recovery work.

Broodstock came from two sampling locations in [[Lake Powell]]: San Juan Arm and Hite's Marina. Fish were held at Ouray facilities under the following conditions:

- **1991 cohort:** 7 fish captured Spring 1991 and held in 8-ft circular tanks for about 6 weeks. [[Holt Williamson]] judged them not sufficiently gravid and too recently stressed for HCG injection. Gametes were to be collected for mtDNA analysis in 1992.
- **1990 cohort:** 12 fish captured Spring 1990 and held in the 1/10-acre pond. On 19 May they were seined into an 8-ft tank. A 13th fish could not be recovered from the pond.

Tagging was done with pit tags, and sex was recorded for most individuals. Some tag numbers in the source are partially illegible and are not reproduced here.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The fish were held in artificial conditions: circular tanks and a single 1/10-acre pond at the hatchery. Holding stress was a key concern. Fish judged too recently stressed were deferred from hormone treatment to avoid injury. The memo does not report water quality, temperature, or other habitat parameters.

Some fish were held in a [[Feeding Study]], and a juvenile was recovered from it as a result of imminent mortality. This suggests that feeding trials were part of the broader holding and research program, though the memo gives no further details.

## Key Findings

**1. Cohort 1 (Spring 1991 capture, n = 7)**
- Sex composition: 4 females (one tentatively identified as "female?"), 2 males, and 1 unknown.
- Not injected: judged not gravid enough and too stressed.
- Epaxial muscle cores and fin clips were taken for allozyme analysis.
- Planned action: gamete collection for mtDNA analysis in 1992, followed by transfer to the 1/10-acre pond after recovery.

**2. Cohort 2 (Spring 1990 capture, n = 12 recovered; 13th not captured)**
- Sex composition: 4 females and 8 males.
- Injected: about 7 individuals received the [[HCG Injection]] series.
- Muscle and fin samples were taken from 3 fish judged unsuitable for injection, and from 1 fish whose mtDNA analysis was complete.
- Allozyme tissues (gonad, liver, heart, kidney, eye, and muscle, duplicated) were collected from 1 fish that died overnight in the tank.
- Several fish had mtDNA enzyme-screen completeness recorded as partial (2/8, 4/8, 5/8, 7/8) or complete (8/8). One fish was linked to a previous identifier (Carlin 00,069).
- Fish not injected or sampled were to be returned to the pond.

**3. Possible mortalities in storage (n = 2)**
- Two fish not mentioned in the main account may be stored in ultra-cold conditions at the [[Vernal Office]].
- Both have mtDNA enzyme screening recorded as 4/8 complete.

**4. Gamete, tissue, and juvenile samples**
- Three gamete samples were collected, including milt and egg samples sent to [[Arizona State University]].
- One allozyme tissue sample set was collected.
- A living juvenile was collected from the feeding study due to imminent death. The female parent was not yet identified.

**Overall outcome:** No induced spawning is reported as successful in this memo. The main results are genetic sampling (mtDNA and allozymes), the identification of fish unsuitable for hormone treatment, and planned gamete collection for 1992.
