# The Vertebrates of Arizona: Bass and Sunfish Species Records

#ichthyology #centrarchidae #moronidae #introduced_species

## Species Profile & Ecological Context

**Taxonomic & Life-History Summary**: This document covers multiple introduced bass and sunfish species in Arizona waters. [[Yellow Bass]] (*Roccus mississippiensis*) reached approximately one pound in weight in [[San Carlos Lake]] (1950) and [[Canyon Lake]] (1953), with larger individuals reported from [[Sahuaro Lake]]. Native range extends from upper Mississippi basin south to Tennessee River system in Alabama, and to Louisiana and eastern Texas. [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*), [[White Bass]] (*Roccus chrysops*), [[Smallmouth Bass]] (*Micropterus dolomieui*), [[Spotted Bass]] (*Micropterus punctulatus*), [[Largemouth Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*), [[Rock Bass]] (*Ambloplites rupestris*), and [[Warmouth Bass]] (*Chaenobryttus gulosus*) are all documented as introduced species. Age, maturity, fecundity, and growth parameters: Not reported.

**Habitat Associations**: [[Largemouth Bass]] occurs in virtually all suitable warm-water lakes and reservoirs in Arizona, including [[Colorado River]], [[Gila River]], and [[Verde River]] systems; found in river channels, various streams, and large and small ranch ponds. [[Smallmouth Bass]] documented in [[Beaver Creek]], Verde River drainage. [[Spotted Bass]] established in Verde River drainage, with large numbers noted in West Creek tributary. [[Rock Bass]] occurs in lower [[Oak Creek]] in Verde River drainage. [[Warmouth Bass]] reported from lower Colorado River. [[Yellow Bass]] historically present in [[Roosevelt Lake]], [[San Carlos Lake]], [[Canyon Lake]], and Sahuaro Lake.

## Behavioral & Ecological Findings

**Movement Patterns & Migration**: Not reported.

**Trophic & Community Interactions**: Not reported.

**Environmental Drivers**: Mass mortality of [[Yellow Bass]] in [[Roosevelt Lake]] attributed to heavy silting following flood circa 1934. Roosevelt Lake was emptied in 1940; no yellow bass observed in reservoir from 1940 to at least 1953.

## Conservation Status & Population Dynamics

**Population Trajectory**: [[Yellow Bass]] was very abundant in [[San Carlos Lake]] in spring 1950. Population extirpated from [[Roosevelt Lake]] following 1940 reservoir emptying, with no individuals observed through 1953. [[Smallmouth Bass]] reported abundant in [[Beaver Creek]] in 1956. [[Spotted Bass]] present in large numbers in West Creek by 1949. [[Largemouth Bass]] widely established throughout Arizona warm-water habitats.

**Threat & Risk Assessment**: Reservoir management operations (emptying) and siltation events identified as threats to established populations, as evidenced by [[Yellow Bass]] extirpation from [[Roosevelt Lake]].

## Management Guidance & Policy Takeaways

**Actionable Recommendations**: Not reported.

**Biological Reference Points**: Stocking records documented: [[Striped Bass]] - 938 small individuals planted April 15, 1959, by California Department of Fish and Game immediately below U.S. Highway 60 bridge near Blythe, California, in lower [[Colorado River]]. [[Smallmouth Bass]] planted in southern Arizona circa 1942; first recorded from lower Colorado River in 1953; stocked 4 miles above [[Parker Dam]] in August 1950. [[Spotted Bass]] introduced into [[Verde River]] drainage by 1942. [[White Bass]] first planted in [[Carl Pleasant Lake]], [[Agua Fria River]], in 1960 by Arizona Game and Fish Department (hypothetical status at time of document).
