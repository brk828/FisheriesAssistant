# Fish Stocking and Rearing Records: Lake Mohave and Lake Havasu Locations

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-stocking

## Executive Summary

This document is a multi-page compilation of fish stocking and rearing records, most likely from a reservoir management or hatchery program in the [[Lake Mohave]] and [[Lake Havasu]] area. The OCR text contains monthly stocking logs (1993–2006), stocking totals by location, a set of monitoring counts from [[Cibola National Wildlife Refuge]] (1995–1998), and tables that classify rearing locations by type. The species are not named in the scanned text, so the species-level identity of the stocked fish cannot be confirmed from this source. The grand total of stocked fish is reported as 31,965 in the OCR.

## Background & Description

The records are organized in several linked tables:

- **Annual and monthly stocking logs (1993–2006):** Entries are grouped by year and month, with a monthly total column and a grand total. Stocking events occur irregularly, with some months having no stocking and others having large releases.
- **Stocking by location:** A matrix lists each release site with a yearly and monthly breakdown. Columns include [[Bubbling Ponds Fish Hatchery]] (BPFH), [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] (DNFH), [[Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery]] (WBNFH), [[Bulkhead Cove]] (BULK), Cibola (CIBO), Emerald Canyon Golf Course (ECGC), and [[Office Cove]] (OFFC). Several column totals are illegible in the scan, so they are not reported here.
- **Reservoir reading and location lists:** Lists of reservoir readings, sites, and stocking years (roughly 1991–2005) for coves and points, including [[Davis Cove]], [[Pittsburgh Point Cove]], [[Nine Mile Coves]], [[Yuma Cove]], [[Chemehuevi Cove]], and [[Nevada Egg]].
- **Rearing location tables:** About 43 numbered rearing locations, each labeled by facility type. Types include [[Lakeside Backwater]], [[Off-Site Facility]], on-site facility, unknown, and not applicable. Named sites include [[Arizona Juvenile]], [[Boulder City Golf Course Ponds]], [[Emerald Canyon Golf Course Ponds]], [[Hualapai Tribe Hatchery]], [[Palm Lake]], and [[Ouray National Fish Hatchery]]/Dexter NFH.
- **Monitoring counts (Cibola NWR High Levee Pond):** Numeric entries for 1995–1998 with associated dates (e.g., "1995 February," "1996 January," "1998 December"). The OCR does not label what these values measure, so their meaning is uncertain.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The sites span a reservoir system with several habitat types:

- **Open-water coves and points** on the reservoirs, where many releases were made.
- **Lakeside backwaters**, which are the most common rearing type in the tables.
- **Off-site hatchery and rearing facilities**, including national and tribal hatcheries.
- **Golf course ponds and wetlands** (e.g., Boulder City Golf Course Ponds, Boulder City Wetlands Park), used as stocking or holding sites.

The text does not describe water chemistry, temperature, habitat quality, or the status of the fish populations. Ecological interpretation is limited to the site and facility types listed above.

## Key Findings

- **Stocking volume:** The grand total is reported as **31,965** fish (OCR text). Location-level totals are partly unreadable, so the distribution among facilities cannot be fully verified.
- **Temporal pattern:** Stocking runs from 1993 to 2006. Annual totals are small in the early 1990s, rise in some years, and show large monthly entries in the early 2000s (for example, 2002 and 2004 contain some of the largest monthly figures). Exact per-year sums could not be reliably reconstructed.
- **Facility network:** Fish were reared at multiple hatcheries and at lakeside backwater sites, so stocking relied on both on-site and off-site rearing.
- **Monitoring data:** Cibola NWR High Levee Pond numeric records (1995–1998) are present but unlabeled, so their purpose cannot be determined from this text.
- **Data quality:** The OCR contains many errors, duplicated headers, and misaligned columns. The species, measurement units for monitoring counts, and per-site totals should be verified against the original source before any quantitative analysis.
