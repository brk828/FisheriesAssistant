# Fishes of Alabama: Sunfishes and Black Basses Identification and Natural History

#ichthyology #Centrarchidae #sunfishes #black_basses

## Survey Objectives & Target Population

**Primary Sampling Purpose**: This document provides taxonomic identification keys and natural history information for sunfishes (family [[Centrarchidae]]) in Alabama, with detailed species accounts for [[Redeye Bass]] ([[Micropterus coosae]]) and [[Smallmouth Bass]] ([[Micropterus dolomieu]]). The primary purpose is taxonomic documentation and species identification rather than a formal sampling protocol.

**Target Population & Spatial Boundaries**: The target population encompasses all sunfish species occurring in Alabama freshwater systems, with particular focus on the [[Mobile Basin]] (including [[Coosa River]] and [[Tallapoosa River]] systems) and [[Tennessee River]] drainage. [[Redeye Bass]] is documented as endemic to the Mobile basin above the Fall Line. [[Smallmouth Bass]] is native to the Tennessee River drainage with documented introduction attempts in other Alabama river systems.

## Experimental & Spatial Design Framework

**Stratification & Allocation**: Spatial distribution is documented at the river basin and drainage scale. [[Redeye Bass]] distribution is stratified by river system (Coosa and Tallapoosa above Fall Line). [[Smallmouth Bass]] distribution is documented for Tennessee River drainage with notes on unsuccessful introductions to other systems.

**Temporal Scheduling**: Spawning periods are documented: [[Redeye Bass]] spawns April to June; [[Smallmouth Bass]] spawns April to May when water temperatures reach 59° to 63°F (15° to 18°C). No systematic temporal sampling design is described.

**Detection & Bias Mitigation**: The identification key provides diagnostic characters to minimize misidentification: dorsal spine counts (5-8 for [[Pomoxis]], 10-13 for other genera), anal spine counts (5+ vs. 3 spines), lateral line scale counts (53+ for [[Micropterus]]), fin morphology (notch depth, pectoral fin shape), and coloration patterns (caudal fin white edges diagnostic for [[Redeye Bass]]).

## Field Data Schema & Variable Definitions

**Primary Biological Measurements**: Meristic counts include: dorsal spines and rays ([[Redeye Bass]]: 9-11 spines, usually 10; 11-13 rays, usually 12), anal spines and rays (3 spines, 9-11 rays, usually 10), lateral line scales (63-74 for [[Redeye Bass]]; 68-80 for [[Smallmouth Bass]]), scales above lateral line (12-13 for [[Smallmouth Bass]]; 9-10 for other [[Micropterus]] species), cheek scale rows (4-5 for [[Ambloplites ariommus]]; 7-8 for [[Ambloplites rupestris]]), scales around caudal peduncle (19-22 for [[Enneacanthus obesus]]; 16-18 for [[Enneacanthus gloriosus]]). Morphometric measurements include mouth extension relative to eye position, dorsal fin base length, pectoral fin length and shape, and body proportions (elongate vs. deep-bodied). Adult size ranges documented: [[Redeye Bass]] 14-17 inches (356-432 mm); [[Smallmouth Bass]] 15-20 inches (380-508 mm). Coloration patterns include presence/absence of horizontal spot rows, vertical bands, caudal fin edge coloration (white edges diagnostic for [[Redeye Bass]]), and breeding coloration (light bluish green on lower head and throat in male [[Redeye Bass]]).

**Environmental & Spatial Covariates**: Habitat associations documented include stream size (small to medium-sized upland streams for [[Redeye Bass]]), substrate type (sand and gravel for juveniles), aquatic vegetation presence ([[Justicia]] water willow), structural features (submerged stumps, boulders, undercut banks, rock outcrops, logs, riprap walls), water temperature (spawning thresholds: 59-63°F for [[Smallmouth Bass]]), and water clarity (clear streams preferred by [[Smallmouth Bass]]). Geographic coordinates include river drainage (Mobile basin vs. Tennessee River drainage), position relative to Fall Line (above for [[Redeye Bass]]), and specific collection localities (Turkey Creek, Jefferson County; Spring Creek, Lawrence County; Fisher Creek type locality).

**QA/QC & Data Entry Protocols**: Not reported. The document provides diagnostic characters for field identification but does not describe data recording, voucher specimen protocols, or quality control procedures.
