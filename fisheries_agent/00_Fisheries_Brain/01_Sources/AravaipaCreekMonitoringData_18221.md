# Aravaipa Creek Fish Monitoring, December 1992

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AGFD) reports a seine-based fish and habitat survey of [[Aravaipa Creek]] conducted December 8–10, 1992. Six stations were sampled: three upstream and three downstream of the [[Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness Area]]. A total of 1,848 fish were caught across the six stations. Native cyprinids and catostomids dominated the catches, and the survey recorded breeding coloration in [[Loach Minnow]] and numerous post-larval individuals. The memo was addressed to partners at [[The Nature Conservancy]] (TNC) and the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM).

## Background & Description

The survey was a standardized sampling effort for native fish monitoring along the creek. Key methods:

- **Gear:** Mainstream channels were seined for 200 m using a 1/8-inch mesh, 6-foot [[Seining]] net, with a 1/4-inch mesh, 15-foot seine used when necessary.
- **Sampling units:** Each station was divided into habitat units (runs, riffles, pools, backwaters). Seine hauls were counted, and empty hauls were recorded.
- **Data sheets:** Field sheets recorded site name, weather, participants, flow, habitat unit widths and depths, substrate composition (estimated %), and embeddedness. Fish data were recorded as total catch and [[Catch Per Unit Effort]] (CPUE, fish per 10 m²).
- **Participants:** Staff from AGFD, BLM and TNC took part, including field crews at the east and west ends of the creek.

**Station layout**

| End | Stations | Location |
|---|---|---|
| East (upstream of WA) | 1, 2, 3 | Turkey Creek, Chimney Rock, TNC guest house |
| West (downstream of WA) | 7, 8, 9 | Wagner Ranch, White's Ranch, sycamore tree |

Data tables for habitat and species were partially illegible in the scan, so only the summary-level values are reported here.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Habitat composition (percent of sampled reach by habitat type)**

- **Station 1 (Turkey Creek):** runs 79.8%, riffles 6.6%, connected backwaters 13.6%. Flow 2.96 cms.
- **Station 2 (Chimney Rock):** runs 85.9%, riffles 14.1%. Flow 1.75 cms.
- **Station 3 (Guest House):** runs 83.9%, riffle 10.6%, pools 5.4%. Flow 1.86 cms.
- **Station 7 (Wagner Ranch):** run 87.5%, riffle 12.5%. Flow 5.26 cms.
- **Station 8 (White's Ranch):** run 63.2%, riffle 36.8%. Flow 7.88 cms.
- **Station 9 (Sycamore Tree):** runs 65.0%, riffles 30.2%, pools 4.8%. Flow 4.54 cms.

**Substrate:** The data sheets record a mix of gravel, pebble, cobble, and sand, with varying bedrock and boulder components at some sites.

**Environmental conditions during sampling**

- Weather was partly cloudy to clear, calm and cool.
- The creek was moderately turbid following two days of steady rain before sampling.
- Water temperatures ranged from 9.5°C to 17°C.
- A total lunar eclipse occurred on December 9.

**Wildlife:** Three white-tailed deer and a herd of javelina were observed.

## Key Findings

**Catch totals by station**

| Station | Total fish caught | Seine hauls | Empty hauls |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 187 | 50 | 25 (50.0%) |
| 2 | 274 | 46 | 5 (10.9%) |
| 3 | 436 | 76 | 15 (19.7%) |
| 7 | 105 | 86 | 53 (61.6%) |
| 8 | 344 | 109 | 46 (42.2%) |
| 9 | 502 | 112 | 37 (33.0%) |

- East-end stations (1–3) yielded 897 fish. West-end stations (7–9) yielded 951 fish.
- Station 3 had the highest single-station catch (436). Station 7 had the lowest (105) and the highest proportion of empty hauls.

**Species present**

- Across the survey, the native assemblage included [[Speckled Dace]], [[Longfin Dace]], [[Loach Minnow]], [[Spikedace]], [[Roundtail Chub]], [[Desert Sucker]], and [[Sonora Sucker]].
- [[Western Mosquitofish]] was recorded at Station 8.
- Juvenile individuals of several species were common in the catches.
- Full species-by-station counts and CPUE values were not legible in the source.

**Loach Minnow breeding and recruitment**

- About 11 *Tiaroga cobitis* showed breeding coloration.
- Numerous post-larval *Tiaroga* (<30 mm) were observed, particularly at Station 3.
- 17 post-larval individuals were collected and preserved for verification.
- Post-larvae occurred along the margins of both run and riffle habitats.

**Management implications (as stated in the memo)**

- The paired upstream and downstream station design allows comparison across the wilderness boundary.
- High empty-haul rates at some west-end stations (Station 7 at 61.6%) indicate patchy fish distribution or unsuitable seining conditions, which should be considered in future sampling design.
- The proposed barrier near the guest house (Station 3) is referenced in the sampling layout and may affect fish movement.
