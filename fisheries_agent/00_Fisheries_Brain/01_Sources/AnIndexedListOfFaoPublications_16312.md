# An Indexed List of FAO Publications Related to Aquaculture, 1960-1997

#ichthyology #marine-biology #aquaculture

## Executive Summary

This document is FAO Fisheries Circular No. 924 (FIRI/C924), compiled by André G. Coche and published by the [[Food and Agriculture Organization]] in Rome in 1997. It is a bibliographical index of about 600 selected FAO documents on [[Aquaculture]] published between January 1960 and March 1997. It excludes the numerous technical reports prepared under technical assistance projects. The circular completes companion lists on non-FAO aquaculture literature in marine waters (FAO Fish. Circ. 925) and fresh waters (FAO Fish. Circ. 926), and it also covers the publications of [[Regional Fishery Bodies]] alongside FAO's own series. The list is organised chronologically, with author, geographic, taxonomic and subject indexes. Most of the body is ordering information and reference entries, not original research data.

## Background & Description

- **Compiler:** André G. Coche, Consultant.
- **Publisher:** FAO, 71 pages, ISSN 0429-9329.
- **Scope:** Publications from FAO and its regional bodies, excluding project technical reports.
- **Arrangement:** Titles are ordered by year, then alphabetically by author within each year. Abbreviations follow ASI/UNISIST standards.
- **Front matter:** Ordering instructions in English, French and Spanish, covering mail, fax and e-mail contacts, payment methods (credit card, bank transfer in USD or Italian lire, cheque, local currency), microfiche and photocopy pricing, and a list of [[Sales Agents]] worldwide.
- **Acronyms:** A list of organisations and programmes, such as [[Aquaculture Development and Coordination Programme]] (ADCP), [[Committee for Inland Fisheries of Africa]] (CIFA), [[European Inland Fisheries Advisory Commission]] (EIFAC), [[Commission for Inland Fisheries of Latin America]] (COPESCAL), [[Indo-Pacific Fishery Commission]] (IPFC) and [[Network of Aquaculture Centres in Asia and the Pacific]] (NACA).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The references cover the environmental and ecological side of aquaculture as well as farming techniques. Several entries address the aquatic habitats where culture takes place, including:

- **Water quality and pollution:** [[Dissolved Oxygen]] requirements of freshwater fishes (Doudoroff and Shumway, 1970), water quality criteria for European freshwater fish (chromium, nickel, nitrite, mixtures of toxicants), and the effects of pollution on fisheries and aquaculture in Japan.
- **Coastal and brackish systems:** Mangroves, coastal lagoons, estuaries, acid sulphate soils and [[Brackishwater Aquaculture]] in the Mediterranean and tropics.
- **Inland waters:** Reservoirs, small water bodies, floodplains, irrigation canals, rivers and lakes, with several entries on the management of extensive culture in these systems.
- **Environmental impact:** Carrying capacity models and environmental impact of cage and pen culture, and guidelines for environmental management of coastal aquaculture (Barg, 1992/1994/1995).
- **Genetic resources:** Conservation and utilisation of aquatic genetic resources (FAO/FIRI Expert Consultation, Grottaferrata, 1992).

The taxonomic index lists more than 60 groups, including [[Milkfish]] (*Chanos chanos*), [[Common Carp]] (*Cyprinus carpio*), [[Chinese Carps]], [[Indian Major Carps]], [[Pike]] (*Esox lucius*), [[Penaeid Shrimp]], [[Freshwater Prawns]] (*Macrobrachium*), [[Tilapias]], [[Oysters]], [[Seaweeds]], [[Atlantic Salmon]], [[Rainbow Trout]], [[European Eel]] and sea turtles. Some of these are covered only as synopses of biological data, which were a major FAO series.

## Key Findings

The circular is a reference index, so its "findings" are the scope and main themes of the collection.

- **Size and coverage:** About 600 FAO documents, 1960 to March 1997. The reference list runs from entry K001 to K610 (with some numbers skipped), and the list covers around 71 pages.
- **Synopsis series:** A substantial set of "Synopsis of biological data" reports on species such as milkfish (Schuster, 1960), pike-perch (Deelder and Willemsen, 1964), common carp (Alikunhi, 1966; Sarig, 1966), bream (Backiel and Zawisza, 1968), catla (Jhingran, 1968), northern pike (Raat, 1988), largemouth bass (Heidinger, 1976) and walleye (Colby et al., 1979).
- **Major symposia:** The FAO World Symposium on Warm-Water Pond Fish Culture (1966, published 1967–1968 in five volumes), the World Scientific Conference on the Biology and Culture of Shrimps and Prawns (Mexico City, 1967, published 1968–1970 in four volumes), and the EIFAC symposia on feeding, nutrition and fish diseases.
- **Regional programme output:** The ADCP produced a large body of regional surveys, national reviews and mission reports, including national reviews for aquaculture development in Africa (about 29 countries), regional surveys of aquaculture sectors in 11 regions, and country reports on Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean.
- **Technical manuals:** The Coche "Simple methods for aquaculture" series, the "Better freshwater fish farming" and "La pisciculture en eau douce" series, and the NACA training manuals. Many items were issued in English, French and Spanish, with cross-referenced numbers for each language version.
- **Recurring topics:** Freshwater pond culture, polyculture, carp and tilapia biology, shrimp and prawn farming, cage and pen culture, seaweed culture, feeds and nutrition, legal aspects of live fish transfers, and aquaculture economics and planning.
- **Ordering guidance:** Requests should cite the full bibliographic reference rather than the K index code. Microfiches cost USD 2.00 each. Photocopies cost USD 2.00 per 10 pages or less, up to 40 pages per document.
