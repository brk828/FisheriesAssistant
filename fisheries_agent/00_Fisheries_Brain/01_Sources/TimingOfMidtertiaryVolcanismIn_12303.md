# Mid-Tertiary Volcanism in the Sierra Madre Occidental

#geology #volcanology #geochronology #plate-tectonics

## Executive Summary

This geological study dates the [[Mid-Tertiary Volcanism]] of the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] along a continuous traverse of [[Mexican National Highway 40]] between [[Durango City]] and [[Mazatlan]], Mexico. It contains no fish, invertebrate, or other aquatic biological data. Its marine relevance is indirect: the authors relate the timing of continental-margin magmatism to [[Plate Motion]] changes in the [[Eastern Pacific]], including the formation of the [[Gulf of California]].

Using 40 K-Ar dates, the authors identify two rhyolitic sequences. The Durango sequence was emplaced between about 32.1 and 28.3 m.y. ago, and the Espinazo-El Salto sequence about 23.3 m.y. ago. They also propose that plate-boundary reorganizations occurred near 45, 32, and 23 m.y. ago.

## Background & Description

- **Study region:** The [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] is a linear plateau about 1,200 km long and 200 to 300 km wide, with a maximum elevation of about 3,000 m. It extends from the United States border to near Guadalajara, where it meets the [[Trans-Volcanic Zone]].
- **Volcanic province:** The upper volcanic supergroup, mainly [[Rhyolitic Ash-Flow Tuff]]s, forms the [[Sierra Madre Occidental Volcanic Province]]. Similar rocks occur in [[Trans-Pecos Texas]], southwestern New Mexico, southeastern Arizona, and [[Baja California]].
- **Traverse approach:** The authors chose one continuous traverse, rather than compiling scattered data, to test whether a regional disconformity could mark a hiatus in volcanism. They acknowledge that one traverse may not represent the entire province.
- **Basement:** The upper volcanic supergroup rests on the [[Lower Volcanic Complex]] (about 100 to 45 m.y. old), a batholithic and andesitic assemblage. Older Mesozoic and Paleozoic rocks occur locally.
- **Analytical methods:** Potassium was measured by flame photometry. Argon was extracted by induction heating and analyzed by isotope dilution. Accuracy was estimated at within ±1% for micas. Feldspars required about 1700 ± 50 °C and a fluxing agent (an equal weight of Holocene basalt) for complete argon extraction.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The paper does not address aquatic ecosystems, habitats, species, or water chemistry. Its environmental context is geological:

- The volcanic pile is capped by gently dipping rocks and is incised by west-draining streams into deep canyons along the western escarpment.
- Crustal extension and [[Normal Faulting]] are less prominent in the Sierra than in the western United States.
- Calc-alkalic magmatism is attributed to subduction-related convergence between adjacent plates. Its location and volume are linked to the rate and angle of convergence and to the hydration of the downgoing lithosphere.
- The [[Farallon Plate]] (and the [[Cocos Plate]]) lay between the Pacific and North American plates during the middle Tertiary. Much of its record has been lost to subduction, so models rely on adjacent plates.

## Key Findings

**1. Durango volcanic sequence (about 800 m thick)**
- 23 K-Ar dates range from 28.3 to 32.1 m.y. ago, with an average of 29.8 ± 1.2 m.y.
- The sequence comprises the [[Registro Ash-Flow Tuffs]], the [[Carpintero Group]], and post-Carpintero rhyolites, all emplaced within about 4 m.y.
- Ten Carpintero Group ages range from 28.6 to 31.4 m.y. Eleven post-Carpintero rhyolite samples range from 28.3 to 29.2 m.y., with two ages of 31.2 m.y.
- The source is the [[Chupaderos Caldera]], north of Durango City. Some units came from more distant sources.
- A [[Cacaria Intrusive]] plug gave a 36.5 m.y. age. It was discarded because it was too old for its stratigraphic position and contained xenocrysts.

**2. Espinazo-El Salto sequence (at least 1,000 m thick)**
- Eight of nine flow-unit ages average 23.3 ± 0.3 m.y. The authors consider the eruption so rapid that the age difference between top and bottom cannot be resolved by K-Ar.
- The sequence includes the thick, columnar-jointed unit E, the [[Canyon Tuffs]], and densely welded upper units such as the Plateau units.
- Three rhyolitic flow domes beneath the sequence average 27.7 ± 0.3 m.y.
- No associated volcanic source has been identified.

**3. Chemical and mineralogic contrast**
- The Durango rhyolites are more alkalic, though still calc-alkalic. Quartz, sanidine, and plagioclase phenocrysts are common.
- Espinazo-El Salto rocks are calc-alkalic and dominated by plagioclase phenocrysts.
- The authors note a slight westward trend toward less alkalic chemistry, but they consider crustal thickness a possible controlling factor rather than time.

**4. Younger basalts**
- [[Alkali Basalt]]s of the Metates Formation (three ages) erupted about 12 m.y. ago during normal faulting in the Rio Chico graben. This dates Basin-and-Range-type tectonism near Durango.
- Quaternary [[Tholeiitic Basalt]] flows overlie alluvium in the Guadiana Valley, confirmed by radiocarbon data.

**5. Magmatic hiatus and regional volcanism**
- Little or no magmatism occurred in the Sierra Madre Occidental between 45 and 32 m.y. ago, but the authors note the hiatus needs further confirmation.
- Alkalic rhyolitic volcanism in Trans-Pecos Texas occurred 46 to 27 m.y. ago. [[Barker (1977)]] linked it to rifting (intraplate tectonism), so it is not typical of a continental margin.
- Volcanism in Baja California began in a narrow arc about 20 m.y. ago and persisted into the Pliocene. Its rhyolites are subordinate, possibly because the crust is oceanic.

**6. Plate-motion interpretation**
- The authors infer plate reorganization at about 45, 32, and 23 m.y. ago.
- Convergent motion in the east Pacific continued until about 23 m.y. ago north of 20°N, when the [[Gulf of California]] transform system began to form.
- The 32 to 23 m.y. calc-alkalic interval roughly matches a 29 to 21 m.y. phase of rapid Pacific–Farallon spreading.
- Subduction of young, dehydrated oceanic lithosphere may explain the 45 to 32 m.y. hiatus.
- The Sierra's pattern broadly resembles the western United States sequence, but the timing of transitions may differ.

**7. Limitations**
- The study covers one traverse, so typicality across the range is unknown.
- Feldspar argon extraction may be less complete than for micas, which the authors suggest could bias ages low in some samples.
- Work on a second traverse near [[Chihuahua City]] was in progress at publication.
