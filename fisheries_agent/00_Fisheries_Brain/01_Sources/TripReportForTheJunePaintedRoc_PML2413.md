# Painted Rock Dam Fish Collecting Trip Report (June 24, 1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #mugilidae

## Executive Summary

This memo is a trip report from a fish collecting expedition to [[Painted Rock Dam]] in Maricopa County, Arizona, on 24 June 1997. Personnel from [[Arizona State University]] and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) used [[Trammel Net]] and [[Seine Net]] gear to sample fish. Striped mullet were abundant below the dam and were preserved as voucher specimens for the ASU fish collection. Seine samples upstream yielded several non-native and native species, and six nest-like depressions were observed, with juvenile tilapia present but no adults collected, so nest-building was not confirmed.

## Background & Description

- **Memo details:** Dated 26 June 1997, addressed to interested parties, and written by Patti Sowka of the [[Arizona State University]] Center for Environmental Studies.
- **Sampling personnel:** T. Inman and P. Sowka (ASU); M. Thorson, A. Paddock, and B. Smith (USFWS).
- **Study site:** Waters immediately downstream of [[Painted Rock Dam]] and the boat launch area upstream of the dam.
- **Target species:** [[Striped Mullet]] (*Mugil cephalus*) was the primary focus of collection.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Mullet behavior:** Striped mullet were described as abundant downstream of the dam and were observed leaping from the water near the first trammel net.
- **Water depth:** Mullet were collected in shallow water; the exact depth is garbled in the source scan and is not reported here.
- **Nest observations:** Six nest-like structures, about 0.75 m wide and 0.5 m deep, were found at the dam. The author noted they resembled nests seen at [[Alamo Lake]], La Paz County, Arizona, on 25 June 1997.
- **Juvenile tilapia:** Juvenile [[Tilapia]] were collected from the nest area. Because no adult tilapia were captured, nest-building behavior by tilapia could not be confirmed.
- **Non-native species:** Tilapia, mosquitofish, and sailfin molly are listed among the collected taxa. The text does not classify their status, so this note does not assert it.

## Key Findings

- **Striped mullet sampling:**
  - Trammel net 1 (150 ft) was set 3 m from shore in front of the boat launch; two mullet were captured and immediately injected with formalin. The concentration is garbled in the source scan.
  - Trammel net 2 was set about 15 m from shore in a circular pattern from a boat. It yielded additional mullet and 3 [[Common Carp]] (*Cyprinus carpio*). Four more mullet were preserved from this net.
  - Preserved mullet ranged from 440 mm to 565 mm total length.
  - Mullet specimens were slated for deposit in the [[Arizona State University]] collection of fishes.
- **Seine sampling upstream of the boat launch (15 ft seine):**
  - Preserved: juvenile Tilapia (*Oreochromis* sp.; the source reads "Titapia"), [[Red Shiner]] (*Cyprinella lutrensis*), [[Mosquitofish]] (*Gambusia affinis*), and [[Sailfin Molly]] (*Poecilia latipinna*).
  - Released: [[Channel Catfish]] (*Ictalurus punctatus*) and common carp.
- **Nest observations:** Six nests were observed at the dam. Their similarity to nests at Alamo Lake was noted, but nest-building by tilapia was not confirmed.
- **Limitations:** The report is a field summary. It gives no catch counts for every net set, no abundance estimates, and no water-quality data.
