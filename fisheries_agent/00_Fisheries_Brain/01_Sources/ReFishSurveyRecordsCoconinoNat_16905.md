# Arizona Game and Fish Department Fish Survey Records Response Letter

#ichthyology #fisheries-management #native-fish-conservation #database-systems

## Executive Summary

This correspondence from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] to Dr. W. L. Minckley dated June 26, 1992, responds to a request for fish survey records from [[Coconino National Forest]]. The letter describes the Department's data management systems for fish survey information, which are maintained in three separate locations: the Heritage Data Management System (Habitat Branch), the Native Fish Database (Nongame Branch), and non-computerized game fish survey records (Fisheries Branch). The Department provided computerized database printouts and offered access to additional records at their Phoenix office, with confidentiality requested for special status species location data.

## Background & Description

The [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] maintains fish survey data across multiple organizational branches and database systems. The Heritage Data Management System and Native Fish Database contain computerized records for nongame fish and some game fish, with acknowledged overlap between the two systems. Game fish survey records are maintained in paper format, filed by body of water or stream name. The requested information pertains specifically to surveys conducted within the [[Coconino National Forest]] in Arizona.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter references both game fish and nongame fish populations in Arizona freshwater systems, with particular attention to special status species requiring confidential location data protection. The survey records encompass various bodies of water and streams within the [[Coconino National Forest]], representing a significant geographic area in northern Arizona. The Department's data management approach distinguishes between game species (managed by Fisheries Branch) and native/nongame species (managed by Nongame Branch and Habitat Branch).

## Key Findings

- Fish survey data for [[Coconino National Forest]] are maintained in three separate Department locations with different data management systems
- The Heritage Data Management System (Habitat Branch) and Native Fish Database (Nongame Branch) contain computerized records with some overlapping information
- Game fish survey records are maintained in non-computerized format, organized by water body or stream name
- Database printouts were provided to Dr. Minckley as attachments to this correspondence
- Additional recent survey results may be available that have not yet been entered into the databases
- Site-specific location information for special status species is considered confidential and not for public distribution
- Contact persons were identified for accessing game fish records (Joe Janisch, 789-3258) and nongame fish records (Francisco Abarca, 789-3508)
- The Department offered in-person review of non-computerized records at their Phoenix office location (2221 West Greenway Road)
