# Alamo Lake Tilapia Collecting Trip Report (June 23, 1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #tilapia

## Executive Summary

Personnel from [[Arizona State University]], assisted by staff of the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], conducted a collecting trip on June 23, 1997, at [[Alamo Lake]] in La Paz County, Arizona. The goal was to verify reports of a nest-building [[Tilapia]] population in the reservoir. Nests were observed and juvenile Tilapia were collected, but the species was not confirmed in the field. Adult Tilapia were not captured in nets. The sampling also produced a mixed catch of native and introduced sport and forage fish.

## Background & Description

The trip report, dated June 26, 1997, was written by Patti Sowka of the Center for Environmental Studies and circulated to interested parties. The stated objective was to check a reported nest-building Tilapia population. Three nets were deployed: a [[Trammel Net]], a [[Gill Net]] set in each of two coves, and a [[Seine Net]] for juveniles. Field assistance came from USFWS staff (T. Thorson, A. Paddoch and B. Smith).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Nesting activity:** Nests were about 1 m in diameter and 0.75 m deep, found in the first major cove upstream of [[Spencer's Wash]], which has a boat launch. About 12 nests were observed. Adult fish, species unconfirmed, fled on the team's arrival.
- **Cove at the end of Cholla Road:** Several nests were seen in the shallows, but they appeared to have been vacated for some time. Some were exposed by falling water levels.
- **Unnamed cove upstream of Rawhide Wash:** No nests were observed.
- **Reservoir condition:** The reported lowering of water level exposed some nests, which is relevant to how nesting habitat is available in the reservoir.

## Key Findings

1. **Tilapia presence:** Nests and juvenile Tilapia were found in the Spencer's Wash cove, supporting the reports. The species was not confirmed in the field, and the juveniles were preserved for identification.
2. **Adult capture:** A 150-ft [[Trammel Net]] set across the narrowest part of the Spencer's Wash cove (about 10 m from shore) was checked twice on June 23 and pulled at 0700 hours on June 24. No adult Tilapia were recorded from the nets. One Tilapia was caught on hook and line with worm bait at the Cholla Road cove.
3. **Species tallies by site:**
   - **Spencer's Wash cove (trammel net):** [[Channel Catfish]] (5), [[Bluegill]] (2), [[Flathead Catfish]] (1), [[Largemouth Bass]] (7).
   - **Cholla Road cove (gill net):** [[Redear Sunfish]] (1), Channel Catfish (2), [[Common Carp]] (3).
   - **Rawhide Wash cove (gill net):** Channel Catfish (1), Common Carp (1), [[Threadfin Shad]] (1).
4. **Totals:** Across all sites, 7 Channel Catfish, 2 Bluegill, 1 Flathead Catfish, 7 Largemouth Bass, 1 Redear Sunfish, 4 Common Carp and 1 Threadfin Shad were collected. Juvenile Tilapia were also taken by seine, though the document gives no count.
5. **Specimen disposition:** Preserved juvenile Tilapia will be deposited in the Arizona State University collection of fishes for positive identification.
