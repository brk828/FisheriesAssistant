# First Record of an Adult Oreo, *Allocyttus folletti*, from British Columbian Waters

#ichthyology #marine-biology #oreosomatidae

## Executive Summary

This note documents the **first verifiable record of an adult *Allocyttus folletti*** (a species of [[Oreosomatidae|oreo fish]]) within Canada's recognized fishing territory. A single specimen (383 mm standard length) was captured by otter trawl in **Hecate Strait, British Columbia**, at depths between 47–292 m, aboard the M/V Royal City in September 1979. Prior Canadian records of *Allocyttus* were limited to juvenile specimens caught far offshore in the mid-North Pacific, outside recognized Canadian waters. The specimen is now catalogued at the British Columbia Provincial Museum (BCPM 979-11396) and closely matches the original holotype description by [[Myers 1960]], with only minor meristic and morphometric discrepancies attributed to specimen size or measurement technique.

## Background & Description

- **Taxonomic Family**: [[Oreosomatidae]] comprises four genera: *Pseudocyttus*, *Neocyttus*, *Cyttosoma*, and *Allocyttus* (Myers 1960).
- **Species Diversity**: Only two species of *Allocyttus* — *A. folletti* and *A. verrucosus* — are believed to occur in the North Pacific Ocean. A full taxonomic revision of the family was underway at time of publication (per Dr. W. N. Eschmeyer, pers. comm.).
- **Prior Canadian Records**: Two juvenile *Allocyttus* specimens had previously been recorded off British Columbia ([[Hart 1973]]; [[Welander et al. 1957]]), but these were captured far offshore (~50°N, 150°W) in the mid-North Pacific, outside Canada's recognized fishing territory.
- **California Records**: Several juveniles and adults of *Allocyttus* have been collected off California ([[Fitch and Lavenberg 1968]]; [[Miller and Lea 1972]]).
- **Developmental Biology**: Oreos undergo dramatic ontogenetic changes in body shape, head shape, and scale structure.
  - Juveniles exist as pelagic "**oreosoma**" larvae, characterized by conical body protuberances and often a swollen abdomen.
  - Upon transformation to the adult stage, these protuberances are lost and the abdomen shrinks — a striking example of larval-to-adult metamorphosis in [[Deep-Sea Fish|deep-sea teleosts]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat**: The specimen was captured via otter trawl at depths of **47–292 m (26–160 fm)** in [[Hecate Strait]], a demersal/benthic habitat on the British Columbia continental shelf.
- **Geographic Significance**: This record extends the confirmed range of adult *A. folletti* into Canadian recognized waters, whereas earlier Canadian records were of juveniles captured far outside this territory in the open North Pacific.
- **Collection Circumstances**: Precise location and date of capture within the Sept 2–9, 1979 window are unknown, reflecting the incidental nature of the catch during commercial otter trawl operations.
- **Broader Distribution**: The species' known range spans the North Pacific, with confirmed juvenile and adult specimens off California and now a confirmed adult from British Columbian waters.

## Key Findings

- **First Canadian adult record** of *Allocyttus folletti*, establishing its presence within Canada's recognized fishing territory (previous BC records were juveniles caught far offshore).
- **Specimen size**: 383 mm standard length — a mature adult, taken from Hecate Strait.
- **Meristic characters** recorded for the specimen (compared against the Myers 1960 holotype description):
  - Dorsal rays: VII, 33
  - Anal rays: II, 31
  - Pectoral rays: 21–21
  - Caudal rays: i, 6, 5, i
  - Pelvic fins: I, 6 – I, 7
  - Gill rakers (1st arch, left/right): 6/19 – 6/19
  - Pored lateral line tubes (+ caudal fin tubes, left/right): 95+5 – 97+3
- **Selected morphometric measurements (mm)**:
  - Standard length: 383
  - Greatest body depth: 168
  - Head length (to end bony plate): 113
  - Least caudal peduncle depth: 17
  - Horizontal bony orbit diameter: 48
  - Bony interorbital width: 37
  - Snout tip to anterior orbit rim: 35
  - Maxillary length (from premaxillary symphysis): 56
  - Lower jaw length (to articular angle): 66
  - Anal fin base length: 134
  - Dorsal fin base length: 151
  - Snout tip to dorsal fin origin: 175
  - Snout tip to anal fin origin: 187
  - First dorsal spine length: 13
  - Second dorsal spine length: 27
  - Longest soft dorsal ray: 41
  - Longest soft anal ray: 43
  - Pelvic spine length: 42
  - Pectoral fin length: 50
  - Thoracic edge length (to pelvic spine origin): 55
  - Abdominal edge length (pelvic base end to anal origin): 62
- **Taxonomic agreement**: The specimen's characters agree closely with the *A. folletti* holotype description (Myers 1960), with only minor discrepancies attributable to measurement technique differences or specimen-size variation.
- **Specimen deposition**: Catalogued at the British Columbia Provincial Museum as **BCPM 979-11396**, providing a verifiable voucher for this range record.
