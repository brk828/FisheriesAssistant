# Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Technical Review Meeting Memorandum

#environmental-management #dam-operations #flow-regime #colorado-river

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] program announces a technical review meeting scheduled for June 11, 1993, to evaluate the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] flow scenario for [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations. The meeting was relocated to accommodate increased attendance and was structured to provide technical review of flow recommendations related to a biological opinion, with strict protocols to maintain focus on scientific discussion rather than agency positions.

## Background & Description

The [[Bureau of Reclamation]], [[Upper Colorado Region]], organized this technical review through the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] program. The meeting was directed at the Biological Opinion Technical Review Group and facilitated by Dave Wegner (Glen Canyon Environmental Studies) and [[Duncan Patten]]. The venue change moved the meeting from an unspecified original location to [[Arizona State University]], Memorial Union, Coconino Room (MU-224), maintaining the 9:00 AM to Noon time slot.

The meeting focused specifically on flow scenario recommendations proposed by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] for dam operations, which represented one element of a larger Draft FWS alternative included in a biological opinion. Frank Baucom was designated to present the flow scenario logic and explain how the proposed scenario would benefit fish populations compared to the preferred alternative.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The flow scenario under review addressed operational parameters for [[Glen Canyon Dam]] on the [[Colorado River]], with specific consideration for fish populations downstream of the dam. The technical review process was designed to evaluate whether proposed flow modifications would provide improved conditions for aquatic species compared to existing operational alternatives. The broader biological opinion included multiple elements beyond flow recommendations, though these were explicitly excluded from this particular technical discussion.

## Key Findings

**Meeting Structure and Objectives:**
- Technical review limited to flow scenario evaluation only, not the entire biological opinion
- Strict protocol requiring participants to focus on technical discussion; those presenting agency positions would be asked to leave
- Facilitators (Duncan Patten and Dave Wegner) tasked with containing discussion to pertinent technical points

**Agenda Components:**
- 9:00 AM: Introductions and objectives
- 9:10 AM: Presentation of flow scenario logic by Frank Baucom
- 9:30 AM: Identification of how proposed scenario benefits fish compared to preferred alternative
- 10:00 AM: Group discussion of individual flow elements
- 12:45 AM: Summary
- Noon: Adjournment

**Planned Outcomes:**
- Technical report to be prepared following the review
- Report to undergo review prior to transmittal to [[Bureau of Reclamation]] and [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]]
- Documentation of technical evaluation of flow recommendations for dam operations
