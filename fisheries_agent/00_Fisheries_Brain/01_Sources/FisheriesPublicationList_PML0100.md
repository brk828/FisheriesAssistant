# Arizona Game & Fish Department Fisheries Contact Directory and Publications List (1990)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fisheries-management #Cyprinidae #Catostomidae #Moronidae #Clupeidae #Poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a cover letter and two attachments from the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AZGFD) Fisheries Branch, dated July 13, 1990. The letter, sent to Paul Marsh at the Center of Environmental Studies, Arizona State University, encloses a directory of Arizona fisheries and aquatic contacts and the Department's list of fisheries publications. The material is administrative rather than research: it contains no new field data, sample sizes, or statistics. Its value lies in the index it provides to roughly a decade of Arizona fish management reports (1967–1989) covering reservoirs, rivers, and hatcheries.

## Background & Description

- **Sender:** Janisch, Supervisor, Fisheries Branch, AZGFD (Phoenix). The Department is led by Director Duane L. Shroufe and Deputy Director Thomas W. Spalding, under Governor Rose Mofford and a five-member Game & Fish Commission.
- **Recipient:** Paul Marsh, Center of Environmental Studies, [[Arizona State University]].
- **Enclosures:**
  1. *Arizona Fisheries or Aquatic Related Contacts*: more than 60 named contacts from federal agencies (U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Geological Survey, Bureau of Land Management, U.S. Forest Service, Bureau of Reclamation, Soil Conservation Service), state agencies ([[Arizona Department of Environmental Quality]] and AZGFD), universities ([[Arizona State University]], [[University of Arizona]], [[Northern Arizona University]]), national forests (including [[Tonto National Forest]] and [[Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest]]), and private consultants.
  2. *Publications List*: a numbered series of AZGFD Fisheries reports, coded by year and sequence (from 67-1 through 89-10), spanning 1967 to 1989. The list is dominated by [[Fish Management Report]] series for individual reservoirs and rivers.

Because the list is an index, the document does not state a total number of publications; the entries are not summarized further here.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The publications list documents management across several aquatic systems and species groups:

- **Reservoirs and lakes:** Dozens of individual lake reports, including [[Alamo Lake]], [[Canyon Lake]], [[Lake Mead]], [[Lake Mohave]], [[Lake Powell]], [[Lake Pleasant]], [[Roosevelt Lake]], [[Saguaro Lake]], [[Woods Canyon Lake]], [[Parker Canyon Lake]], [[Patagonia Lake]], and [[Arivaca Lake]]. Many are AZGFD "Aquatic Wildlife Survey" or "Fish Management Report" series.
- **Colorado River system:** Division-level reports for the Yuma, Imperial, Ehrenberg, Parker Strip, and Lake Havasu sections, plus [[Lake Powell Artificial Fish Habitat]] projects and a Glen Canyon to Lake Mead assessment.
- **Rivers and streams:** Creel surveys and management reports for the [[Verde River]], [[Lower Salt River]], [[Tonto Creek]], [[East Verde River]], and [[Canyon Creek]].
- **Endangered and native fishes:** Reintroduction and monitoring reports for the [[Colorado Squawfish]] and the [[Razorback Sucker]], and a report on the [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*, OCR-garbled in the source).
- **Hatcheries and disease:** Annual hatchery reports, a computerized hatchery management system, and a preliminary report on [[Whirling Disease]].
- **Introduced and game species:** Reports on [[Crappie]] growth limitation, [[Striped Bass]] and [[Threadfin Shad]] relationships, [[Tilapia]] in Arizona, walleye fishery surveys at Canyon Lake, and trout stocking programs.
- **Regulation and angler studies:** Bag and possession limit evaluations, a new bass slot-size limit at Alamo Lake, tournament fishing, and urban angler surveys.
- **Lake fertilization and habitat:** Multi-year studies of fertilization at Woods Canyon Lake and fish habitat development projects.

## Key Findings

- **Purpose of the correspondence:** Distribution of an AZGFD contact directory and a publications index to an academic environmental center. No new scientific results are reported.
- **Agency network:** The enclosed directory links state, federal, university, and consulting personnel working on Arizona aquatic resources in 1990.
- **Research breadth:** The publications span 1967–1989 and cover reservoir management, endangered fish recovery, hatchery operations, angler regulation, and habitat development.
- **Recurring themes:** Repeated series for individual lakes (annual or multi-year management reports), a long-running focus on the Colorado River mainstem, and early work on [[Largemouth Bass]], [[Rainbow Trout]], and [[Crappie]] population limits.
- **Notable early reports:** Lake fertilization and crappie growth studies at Woods Canyon Lake (1967–1979), a plankton study of Lynx Lake (1972), and a 1989 review of Arizona's tilapia and exotic fish use.
- **Endangered species work:** Reintroduction and monitoring of the Colorado Squawfish and Razorback Sucker in the Gila and Colorado basins, from 1985 onward.
