#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

The document is a species status summary for the [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*), a fish listed as Endangered. Its numbers cannot be estimated, its present range is sporadic, and its former distribution is unknown. The main threats are habitat change from [[Dam Construction]] on the Colorado River and its tributaries, competition with introduced fishes, and [[Hybridization]] with related native species following the construction of [[Glen Canyon Dam]]. The document lists protective measures that include dam removals, a ban on new dams, and continued research on taxonomy, population size, habitat requirements and life history.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** *Gila cypha*, described by Miller in 1946.
- **Conservation status:** Endangered.
- **Population estimate:** No estimate is possible.
- **Present distribution:** Sporadic occurrence in the [[Grand Canyon]] and the [[Colorado River]] mainstem above [[Lake Powell]]. The species also occurs in the Lower [[Yampa River]] and in Lake Powell.
- **Former distribution:** Unknown.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat change:** Dam building on the Colorado River mainstem and its main tributaries has altered the habitat on which the species depends.
- **Competition:** Introduced fish species have probably had a serious effect on populations.
- **Hybridization and genetic swamping:** Extensive hybridization among *Gila robusta robusta*, *Gila elegans* and *Gila cypha* followed the construction of Glen Canyon Dam. This genetic swamping is probably a factor in the decline of this rare species (Minckley and Rinne, 1970; Minckley, personal communication).
- **Ecosystem restoration:** The protective measures are framed as efforts to restore the ecosystem, with a time frame of 1974–1978 given in the source.

## Key Findings

**Threats identified**
1. Habitat alteration from dam construction on the Colorado River and its main tributaries.
2. Competition with introduced fish species, which has probably had a serious effect.
3. Genetic swamping through hybridization with *Gila robusta robusta* and *Gila elegans*, linked to Glen Canyon Dam.

**Protective measures taken or proposed (10 items)**
1. Preliminary studies on taxonomy and life history (completed).
2. Removal of Glen Canyon Dam.
3. Removal of Flaming Gorge Dam.
4. Removal of Boulder Dam.
5. Removal of Davis Dam.
6. Removal of Parker Dam.
7. Prevention of any new dam building on the Colorado River and its tributaries, especially the Lower Yampa.
8. Continued studies to determine the exact taxonomic relationship of *Gila cypha* to other species in the Colorado River system.
9. Continued studies to determine population size and the habitat requirements of *Gila cypha*.
10. Continued studies on the life history of this species.
