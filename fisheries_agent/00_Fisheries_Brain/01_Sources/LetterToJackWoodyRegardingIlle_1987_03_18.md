## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary research paper but a formal letter from ichthyologist W.L. Minckley (Arizona State University) to Jack Woody of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, dated 18 March 1987. The letter addresses an allegation of illegal, unpermitted scientific collecting of "fishes and reptiles" in the [[Cuatro Cienegas Basin]], Coahuila, Mexico, attributed vaguely to "a researcher from an Arizona institution." Minckley, who had worked in the basin for nearly 30 years under valid Mexican scientific permits and multiple funding agencies (NSF, USFWS), objects to the unspecific attribution, which caused reputational inquiries directed at him and Arizona State University despite his non-involvement. The letter underscores the political sensitivity of illegal collecting activity given ongoing efforts to establish the basin as a protected ecological preserve.
