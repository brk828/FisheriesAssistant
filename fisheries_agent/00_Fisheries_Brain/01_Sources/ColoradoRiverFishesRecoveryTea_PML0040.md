# Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team Meeting Notice, June 21-23, 1983

#ichthyology #marine-biology #endangered-fishes

## Executive Summary

This document is a meeting notice and attached materials for the [[Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team]] meeting, held June 21-23, 1983, in Las Vegas, Nevada. The letter from Team Leader J. A. St. Amant announces the agenda, asks members to review the latest draft of the [[Squawfish]] recovery plan, and notes a shortage of funds for consultant attendance. The attachments are an agenda and a list of team members and consultants with their agencies and contact details. The document contains no field data, sample sizes, or population statistics.

## Background & Description

- **Purpose:** Coordinate recovery planning for endangered Colorado River fishes.
- **Species under review:** [[Bonytail]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Squawfish]].
- **Format:** A two-page cover letter (the "From" and "To" lines are blank in the scan), a meeting agenda, and a roster of about 15 team members and 8 consultants.
- **Membership:** Drawn from state wildlife and fish agencies (Nevada, Colorado, Arizona, Utah, California), the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], the [[U.S. Bureau of Reclamation]], the [[Bureau of Land Management]], and the [[National Park Service]], plus university and museum researchers.
- **Logistics:** The meeting was held in the conference room of the Nevada Department of Wildlife. Travel authorization and plane reservations were handled through the team's budget, which lacked funds for consultants.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document has little direct ecological content. It references:

- Recovery plans for the three named fishes.
- The [[Upper Colorado River Conservation Plan]], which was still unreleased at the time of the letter.
- A Lower Colorado River Subcommittee and a Lower Colorado River Conservation Plan.
- The status of proposed dams, a factor in river flow and habitat management.
- The Upper Colorado River Endangered Fishes Planning Meeting.

No water chemistry, habitat measurements, population counts, or life-history data are reported.

## Key Findings

**Agenda (in order):**
1. Review of recovery plans for Bonytail and Humpback chub (June 21, 1:00 p.m.).
2. Continued review of the Squawfish recovery plan (June 22, 8:30 a.m.).
3. Review of the Upper Colorado River Conservation Plan.
4. Reports on the Lower Colorado River Subcommittee and the Lower Colorado River Conservation Plan.
5. Newsletter, team roles and responsibilities, and the 1983-84 budget.
6. Status of proposed dams and review of the Upper Colorado River Endangered Fishes Planning Meeting.

**Action items and decisions:**
- Members were asked to send comments and corrections on the Squawfish recovery plan by June 13, 1983.
- The Upper Colorado River Conservation Plan was to be sent directly to each member once released, ideally at least a week before the meeting.
- Drafts of Part III (implementation schedule) were to be provided for review at the meeting.
- Funding was insufficient to cover consultants for this meeting; the recovery team budget was on the agenda.

**Roster summary:** About 15 members and 8 consultants, with [[J. A. St. Amant]] (California Department of Fish and Game) serving as team leader and [[Don Archer]] as Recovery Coordinator.
