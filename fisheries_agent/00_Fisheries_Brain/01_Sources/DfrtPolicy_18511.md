# Desert Fishes Recovery Team: Operational Recommendations (1993 Letter)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #desert-fishes

## Executive Summary

This is a 25 February 1993 letter from C.O. Minckley, Fishery Biologist with the [[Fish And Wildlife Service]] at Parker, Arizona, to Dr. W.L. Minckley, Chairman of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] at [[Arizona State University]]. The letter offers the author's views on how the team should function in the past, present and future. It covers meeting focus, subgroup structure, the team's role in [[Section 7 Consultation]] cases, communication with the [[Regional Director]], and an annual meeting. The letter contains no species data, population counts or field results.

## Background & Description

The letter is a short, personal correspondence between two agency and academic participants in desert fish conservation. The author states that he cannot comment on the team's past actions because he has no data on them. His comments therefore concern present and future operations. The author also mentions [[Battle Against Extinction]], a book on the problems faced by native fishes, which he suggests should be provided to the Regional Director.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter does not describe habitats, water chemistry, species distributions or ecological conditions. Its only ecological content is the reference to the taxonomic groups the team serves. The author proposes subgroups for:

- [[Yaqui Fishes]]
- [[Pupfishes]]
- [[Plagopterines]]

These groupings suggest the team manages recovery across several native desert fish lineages. The letter gives no further biological detail for any of them.

## Key Findings

- **Meeting focus:** The author believes the team has been "bogged down" in agency internal matters. He recommends that meetings stick to the agenda and avoid agency-specific problems.
- **Subgroups:** Subgroups should be formed around fish groups, namely Yaqui fishes, pupfishes and plagopterines.
- **Section 7 consultations:** The team should take part only in the most extreme cases. The Service should first make its "best shot," after which team members would be briefed and asked to respond.
- **Decision criterion:** A request to the team should be considered only if the team's involvement would enhance the chance of a successful consultation and thereby benefit the fish.
- **Lead time:** Considerable lead time should be allowed for team decisions.
- **Regional Director engagement:** The Regional Director should receive a letter listing the team's goals and asking for his opinion on team functions and recovery plan implementation. The author notes that the Regional Director had stated during a recent visit to Parker that he was not a "fish person." A copy of [[Battle Against Extinction]] should be made available to him through some Service entity, not necessarily by the recipient.
- **Annual meeting:** An annual meeting between the Regional Director and team members would be beneficial. If the team cannot attend, the author suggests that Dr. Minckley and a colleague named Sally conduct the meeting.
