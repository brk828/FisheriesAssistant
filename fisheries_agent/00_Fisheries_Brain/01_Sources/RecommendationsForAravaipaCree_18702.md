# Recommendations on Red Shiner Effects in Aravaipa Creek (Memorandum, December 1990)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #red-shiner

## Executive Summary

This document is a December 10, 1990 fax transmittal and internal memorandum from the [[New Mexico Department Of Game And Fish]] Laboratory in Santa Fe. David L. Propst sent it to W.L. Minckley. The memo responds to a letter from the Regional Director of the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) about the [[Red Shiner]] in [[Aravaipa Creek]]. It addresses how the species may affect native fishes there. The author describes the goal as a balanced and accurate overview, prepared for discussion at the November 1990 meeting of the [[Desert Fishes Council]]. The document contains no quantitative data, sample sizes, or formal management plan. The text is partly illegible because of scan quality, so the subject matter is summarized only from the legible portions.
