## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2, Albuquerque) dated March 23, 1989, addressed to Dr. David L. Propst of the [[Gila Trout Recovery Team]] (New Mexico Department of Game and Fish). The letter formally approves the transfer of recovery responsibilities for the [[Chihuahua Chub]] (*Gila nigrescens*) from the [[Desert Fishes Team]] (led by W. L. Minckley) to the [[Gila Trout Team]]. The correspondence is not a primary scientific study but a regulatory/administrative record relevant to the conservation management history of two imperiled southwestern fish taxa: the Chihuahua Chub and the [[Gila Trout]] (*Oncorhynchus gilae*, formerly classified as *Salmo gilae*).

## Key Findings
- **Administrative Transfer**: Recovery responsibility for the [[Chihuahua Chub]] was officially transferred from the [[Desert Fishes Team]] to the [[Gila Trout Team]], per a request dated January 31, 1989, from Dr. David L. Propst.
- **Consolidated Management**: As of this letter, the [[Gila Trout Team]] holds recovery responsibility for **both** Gila Trout and Chihuahua Chub, consolidating management of two distinct southwestern native fish conservation efforts under one team.
- **Programmatic Endorsement**: The Acting Regional Director explicitly commends the Gila Trout Team's performance in developing and implementing a [[Recovery Plan]], citing measurable progress in Gila Trout recovery.
- **Down-listing Recommendation**: The letter documents institutional support for down-listing the [[Gila Trout]] (i.e., reclassification from Endangered to Threatened status under the Endangered Species Act), reflecting recovery progress achieved by that time (1989).
- **Future Expectation**: The Service expresses expectation that the Gila Trout Team will achieve "comparable results" for the Chihuahua Chub, framing this transfer as leveraging demonstrated recovery-team effectiveness across taxa.
