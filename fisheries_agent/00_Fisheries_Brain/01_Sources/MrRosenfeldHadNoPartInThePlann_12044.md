# Correspondence Regarding Research Proposal Authorship Dispute

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae #research-ethics #cutthroat-trout

## Executive Summary

This letter from Dr. Richard N. Williams ([[Portland State University]]) to Jim French ([[Nevada Department of Wildlife]]) addresses a dispute over authorship claims for a research proposal concerning native [[Cutthroat Trout]] management in the western United States. Dr. Williams formally refutes claims by Mr. Mark Rosenfeld ([[University of Utah]]) of co-authorship on a proposal written by Williams and Dr. Dennis K. Shiozawa. The correspondence documents the timeline of proposal development and clarifies the actual contributions of all parties involved.

## Background & Description

The research proposal in question was developed by Dr. Williams and Dr. [[Dennis K. Shiozawa]] beginning in late fall 1986. Dr. Williams wrote the proposal during winter 1987 while in a post-doctoral position at the [[University of Hawaii]]. The proposal focused on native [[Cutthroat Trout]] management concerns in the western United States and was submitted to the [[Nevada Department of Wildlife]] for review and possible funding.

The proposal involved establishing laboratory capabilities for specialized analyses. Dr. Williams and Dr. Shiozawa structured their laboratories specifically to conduct the types of analyses outlined in the research proposal.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The research proposal addressed pertinent management concerns for native [[Cutthroat Trout]] populations in the western United States. The work was coordinated with multiple state and federal agencies including the [[US Fish and Wildlife Service]] at [[Yellowstone National Park]], and wildlife agencies in Nevada, California, New Mexico, and Idaho.

Bob Gresswell of the [[US Fish and Wildlife Service]] at [[Yellowstone National Park]] was involved with the research project from an early stage and was familiar with both the proposal content and the parties involved.

## Key Findings

**Authorship Clarification**: Mr. Mark Rosenfeld had no participation in the planning, designing, or writing of the research proposal. He was not consulted during the development process and had never seen any draft or final version of the proposal.

**Actual Contribution**: Rosenfeld's sole contribution to the research effort was teaching Dr. Shiozawa basic tissue culture techniques in winter 1987. Dr. Shiozawa subsequently modified these techniques for work with trout cells and chromosomes without Rosenfeld's involvement.

**Previous Collaboration**: While Rosenfeld and Dr. Shiozawa had collaborated on studies involving suckers and chubs in previous years, this collaboration had ended due to "several other instances of questionable ethics and judgement" by Rosenfeld over the preceding year.

**Verification**: Bob Gresswell ([[US Fish and Wildlife Service]], Yellowstone National Park, phone: 307-344-7381) was designated as a contact for verification and questions regarding the authorship dispute.

**Distribution**: The letter was distributed to multiple agencies and individuals reviewing the proposal, including officials at [[Yellowstone National Park]], [[Brigham Young University]], California Department of Fish and Game, New Mexico Department of Fish and Game, and Idaho Fish and Game.
