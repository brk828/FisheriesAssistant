## Executive Summary

This record is a **museum type-specimen catalog entry** from the Museum of Comparative Zoology (MCZ), Harvard University, rather than a primary research or morphometric study. It documents the syntype series of *Girardinus sonoriensis*, a poeciliid (livebearer) fish originally described from freshwater habitats in Sonora, Mexico. The specimens were collected by Dr. C.B. Kennerly and originally deposited via the USNM (United States National Museum) before being catalogued at MCZ under accession numbers 1461 and 1310. The original taxonomic description appears in *Proceedings of the Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia*, volume 11, page 120. No morphometric, meristic, or ecological data beyond specimen curation metadata are present in the source text.
