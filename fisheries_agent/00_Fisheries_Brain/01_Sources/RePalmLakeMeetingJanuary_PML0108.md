# Palm Lake Management Meeting Follow-Up: Tasks, Deadlines, and MOU Revision

#ichthyology #marine-biology #native-fish-management

## Executive Summary

This is a follow-up letter dated January 15, 1993, from [[Kirk L. Young]], Native Fish Projects Manager at the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (AGFD), to Val, of [[The Nature Conservancy]] (TNC) at the [[Hassayampa River Preserve]]. It summarizes a January 14, 1993 meeting on [[Palm Lake]] and lists tasks, responsible parties, and deadlines. The main subject is revision of a [[Memorandum of Understanding]] (MOU) covering the lake, and the letter also addresses water quality monitoring, water supply, and possible cattail removal. The document names no fish species, so fish-specific data are absent.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Correspondence (follow-up letter to a meeting), not a research report.
- **Sender:** Kirk L. Young, Native Fish Projects Manager, AGFD, Phoenix, Arizona.
- **Recipient:** Val, The Nature Conservancy, Hassayampa River Preserve, Wickenburg, Arizona.
- **Subject:** "Palm Lake Meeting – January 14, 1993."
- **Copied:** Paul Marsh, Arizona State University (ASU) Center for Environmental Studies, among others. The copy list is partly illegible in the scan.
- **Letterhead agency:** AGFD, an Equal Opportunity Agency, West Greenway Road, Phoenix.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The letter gives only brief environmental context for Palm Lake:

- **Water quality:** Monitoring is a stated concern. The letter proposes dissolved oxygen testing and asks whether [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) can arrange regular water quality data collection at the lake.
- **Water supply:** TNC is to estimate costs for pumping water into Palm Lake.
- **Vegetation management:** The parties are to decide the extent, location, and methods of cattail removal, along with minimum pool depths.
- **Native fish programs:** The letter is written by the Native Fish Projects Manager, so the lake is presumably managed for native fish, but no species, population data, or habitat survey results are given.

## Key Findings

**1. MOU revision and deadlines**
- Consolidated comments from TNC and USFWS are due to AGFD by **February 15, 1993**.
- AGFD will incorporate comments and return the revised draft for review by **March 5, 1993**.
- A final draft is to be ready for the **May or June Commission Meeting**, if needed.

**2. Assigned action items**
- **AGFD:** Provide a Dissolved Oxygen Test Kit to TNC for Palm Lake monitoring.
- **TNC:** Produce cost estimates for pumping water into Palm Lake.
- **AGFD, TNC, and USFWS jointly:** Suggest the extent, location, and methods of cattail removal, and the minimum pool depths for Palm Lake.
- **USFWS:** Check whether monthly water quality data can be collected at Palm Lake.

**3. Relationship to an umbrella agreement**
- A separate "umbrella" TNC/AGFD MOU was being submitted to the Commission. If it is signed, the Palm Lake MOU would serve as the basis for a Management Agreement. If it is not, the Palm Lake MOU revision proceeds as planned.
- The letter states that the Palm Lake MOU "may not be necessary" if the umbrella MOU is accepted.

**4. Sampling plan**
- The author plans to sample Palm Lake in **February 1993**. Specific dates were to follow.

**5. Open items**
- The author asks the recipient to flag any points from the meeting that were omitted from the letter.

**Data note:** The letter reports no sample sizes, fish counts, water chemistry values, or statistics. Its substance is administrative and procedural.
