## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary scientific research paper but an **administrative memorandum** dated 25 October 1989 from Arizona State University's Department of Zoology, coordinating a meeting of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]. The memo announces a meeting scheduled for 7–8 December 1989 at USFWS offices in Phoenix, Arizona, with two principal agenda items: (1) evaluating status of downlisting efforts for the [[Gila Topminnow]], and (2) preparing a recovery plan for the [[Desert Pupfish]]. As a scheduling memo, it contains no direct morphological, physiological, or quantitative ecological data on either species, but it is valuable as evidence of the institutional and regulatory conservation context surrounding these two federally-recognized imperiled desert fish taxa.
