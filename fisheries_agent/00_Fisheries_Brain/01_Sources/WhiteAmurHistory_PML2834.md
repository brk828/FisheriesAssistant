# White Amur Stocking History in Arizona Lakes

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This one-page fax, sent by the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] on January 24, 1997, summarizes the stocking history of [[White Amur]] (listed as "Amur") in several Arizona lakes. Fish were stocked from 1989 onward under permits held at three sites in Gilbert and Tempe. Stocking permits were renewed in most years, but no restocking permit was issued at any site. The record does not address ecological effects, population monitoring, or management goals.

## Background & Description

The document is titled "White Amur History" and is dated January 25, 1997. It lists the stocking history of three lake sites, with fish sourced from two commercial aquaculture operations:

- **[[Brown's Fish Farm]]** (Pima, Arizona), a source for two of the three stockings.
- **[[Aqua-Biotech]]** (Tempe, Arizona), a source for one stocking.

The species is identified only as "Amur." Based on common usage, this refers to the White Amur, *Ctenopharyngodon idella*, a herbivorous cyprinid. The document does not give a scientific name, body size, or life-history details.

The fax was sent from the Arizona Game & Fish Department, Fisheries Branch, at 2221 West Greenway Road, Phoenix, Arizona. It includes a standard cover sheet with a fax-number field and a page count, and a standard equal-opportunity notice.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document provides no information on:

The only environmental context is the lake locations: Val Vista Lakes (Gilbert) and sites in Tempe, including the [[Karsten Golf Course]] and the [[ASU Research Park]].

## Key Findings

**Stocking records by site**

- **[[Val Vista Lakes]]** (Gilbert): First stocked October 1989 with 675 Amur from Brown's Fish Farm. The permit was renewed for 1990–1994, 1996, and 1997. It was not renewed in 1995.
- **[[Karsten Golf Course]], [[ASU Research Park]]** (Tempe): First stocked April 1989 with 170 Amur from Aqua-Biotech, distributed among three lakes. The permit was renewed for 1990–1996. As of the fax date, it had not been renewed for 1997.
- **Tempe site** (name not legible in the scan): First stocked October 1989 with 125 Amur from Brown's Fish Farm, distributed among two lakes. The permit was renewed for 1990–1997.

**Summary of totals**

- Total fish stocked across the three records: 970 (675 + 170 + 125), as recorded in the document.
- Fish sources: two commercial operations, Brown's Fish Farm and Aqua-Biotech.
- Initial stockings occurred in 1989 (April and October).
- No [[Restocking Permit]] was issued at any site.

**Permit status**

- The Val Vista permit lapsed in 1995 and was renewed in other years.
- The Karsten/ASU permit had not been renewed for 1997 as of January 1997.
- The Tempe site permit was active through 1997.

**Other notes**

- The Tempe site's name is illegible in the scan, so its location is recorded only as Tempe.
- The document does not state the reason for the 1995 non-renewal.
