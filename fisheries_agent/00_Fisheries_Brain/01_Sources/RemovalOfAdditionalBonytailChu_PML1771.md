# Palm Lake Fish Removal Memo: Bonytail Chub and Razorback Sucker (1994)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This is a 4 April 1994 interagency memorandum from the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] office in Parker, Arizona. It proposes a second removal of additional [[Bonytail Chub]] and [[Razorback Sucker]] from [[Palm Lake]] by [[Electrofishing]] before summer. The fish would be relocated to [[Lake Havasu]] and to the [[Arizona State University Research Park]]. The memo asks recipients to confirm participation by the 16th and proposes the operation for the last week of April, possibly the evening of the 27th. It also asks whether [[Squawfish]] (Colorado pikeminnow) should be placed in the research park.

## Background & Description

The memo is addressed to four recipients:

- Ms. Val Little, [[Hassayampa River Preserve]]
- Mr. Kirk Young, [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]]
- Dr. W.L. Minckley, [[Arizona State University]]
- Dr. Paul Marsh, Center for Environmental Studies

It was written by Chuck Minckley of the Parker office of the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]]. The subject line is "Removal of additional bonytail chubs and razorback suckers from Palm Lake." The memo continues an earlier removal effort; it refers to "last time" and describes the same handling procedures.

The two focal species are native large-bodied fishes of the Colorado River system. The memo does not provide morphological or life-history data for them.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document addresses fish management and translocation only. It does not report water chemistry, habitat conditions, invertebrates, plankton, or population estimates for Palm Lake. Where the memo is silent, these fields are not addressed.

The proposed movements of fish are as follows:

- **Razorback suckers and some bonytail chubs** (the memo labels one group "F2"; the OCR is ambiguous) go to Lake Havasu.
- **Remaining bonytail chubs** go to the Arizona State University Research Park.
- **Squawfish** may also go to the research park, pending agreement.

The memo does not state the source of the transport labels or the reason for the split. It does request that someone transport fish to the research park, which implies a separate transport job.

## Key Findings

1. **Proposed action:** Electrofish Palm Lake to remove additional bonytail chubs and razorback suckers before summer 1994.
2. **Timing:** Target is the last week of April 1994, possibly the evening of 27 April. Responses are requested by the 16th (April).
3. **Destinations:** Razorbacks and some bonytails to Lake Havasu; the remaining bonytails to the Arizona State University Research Park.
4. **Open question:** Whether squawfish should also be placed in the research park.
5. **Data handling:** All captured fish would be [[Pit Tagging]], weighed, and measured, and the data shared with all researchers. This matches the previous removal.
6. **Logistics:** The author can carry out the electrofishing and the Lake Havasu transport. Someone else is needed for the research-park transport.
7. **Action requested:** Recipients must reply to the author, who has agreed to the proposal, by 16 April. The memo does not include a signed agreement or the outcome.
