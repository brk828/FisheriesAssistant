## Executive Summary

This document is **not a primary scientific research paper** but an internal administrative memorandum from the Arizona State University Department of Biology, authored by [[W. L. It outlines compliance requirements for permit holders (students, faculty, staff, and permit "agents") engaged in "take" activities involving [[Federally Listed Species]] under a Departmental USFWS permit and Minckley's [[AZGFD]] (Arizona Game and Fish Department) permit. The memo mandates biannual reporting to USFWS Region II and annual reporting to AZGFD, covering all take activities conducted in calendar years 1996 and 1997, with an internal deadline of 10 November 1997 (ahead of the federal submission deadline of 1 December 1997) and permit renewal application due 31 December 1997. ## Key Findings

- **Permit Structure**: Two separate regulatory tracks require reporting — (1) a Departmental USFWS "take" permit (Region II, Albuquerque) and (2) an individual AZGFD permit held by [[W. L. Minckley]].
- **Reporting Deadlines**:
  - Internal ASU deadline: **10 November 1997** (all data due to Patti Duncan, Biology Office).
  - USFWS take report deadline: **1 December 1997**.
  - USFWS permit renewal application deadline: **31 December 1997**.
- **Defined "Take" Activities**: Includes harassment (capture-and-release, pursuit), manipulation (capture-and-tagging), and removal (dead or alive) of individuals from the natural environment.
- **Required Data Fields** for both USFWS and AZGFD reports:
  - Species identity
  - Number of individuals taken
  - Specific locality (with maps of capture/contact points where needed)
  - Disposition of any preserved or retained specimens, tissues, or other direct evidence of existence
- **Exemptions**: Specimens or tissues obtained from another already-permitted individual or agency do not require separate reporting, but provenance must be demonstrable upon inspection.
- **Scope of Applicability**: Requirement applies strictly to USFWS Region II permitting; holders of other regional or agency permits are independently responsible for their own compliance reporting.
- **Administrative Contact**: All documentation to be submitted to Patti Duncan, ASU Biology Office.
- **New Permit Requests**: Unpermitted individuals seeking inclusion on the following year's permit must submit equivalent data (species, numbers, locality, disposition) as part of their request.
