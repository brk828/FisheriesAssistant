# Rio Grande Silvery Minnow: Proposed Endangered Listing and Comment Period Extension

#ichthyology #marine-biology #hybognathus

## Executive Summary

This document is a regulatory notice and cover correspondence from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Service), issued through the [[Federal Register]]. It extends the comment period on a proposal to list the [[Rio Grande Silvery Minnow]] (*[[Hybognathus Amarus]]*) as an endangered species under the [[Endangered Species Act]], with [[Critical Habitat]] designated. The comment period now closes August 25, 1993, and two public hearings were scheduled for June 2 and June 3, 1993. The notice is administrative rather than research-based; it contains no population data beyond the statement that the species occupies about five percent of its historic range.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** The Rio Grande silvery minnow is named *Hybognathus amarus* in the notice. The OCR text also shows the name as "Hybognathus amarus" in the heading, confirming the binomial.
- **Range:** The species occurs only in the [[Rio Grande]] from [[Cochiti Dam]] downstream to the headwaters of [[Elephant Butte Reservoir]], New Mexico.
- **Current status:** It "occupies approximately five percent of its known historic range."
- **Regulatory history:** The proposed rule was published March 1, 1993 (58 FR 11821). The original comment deadline was April 30, 1993. The notice of extension and hearings was published April 13, 1993 (Vol. 58, No. 69, Proposed Rules).
- **Authority:** The action is authorized under 16 U.S.C. 1531–1544 (the [[Endangered Species Act]]).
- **Regulatory placement:** The proposal falls under 50 CFR Part 17, covering endangered and threatened wildlife and plants.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The notice identifies three main threats to the species:

1. **Habitat loss:** Loss of stream habitat due to [[Dewatering]] and [[Channelization]], plus alteration of river flow to supply water for [[Irrigation]].
2. **Water quality decline:** Diminished water quality from [[Municipal Discharge]], [[Industrial Discharge]], and [[Agricultural Runoff]].
3. **Biological pressure:** Competition with or predation by [[Nonnative Species]] introduced into the river.

The notice gives no quantitative data on population size, trends, sampling effort, or specific habitat measurements.

## Key Findings

- **Proposed action:** Endangered listing of the Rio Grande silvery minnow with critical habitat.
- **Comment deadline:** Extended from April 30, 1993 to **August 25, 1993**. Comments received after this date may not be considered in the final decision.
- **Public hearings:**
  - **June 2, 1993, 7–9 p.m.:** [[Indian Pueblo Cultural Center]], 2401 Twelfth Street NW, [[Albuquerque]], New Mexico.
  - **June 3, 1993, 7–9 p.m.:** [[Macy Center]], [[New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology]] (listed as "New Mexico Technical Institute" in the notice), [[Socorro]], New Mexico.
- **Hearing procedures:** Speakers should bring written copies of their statements. Oral statements may be time-limited if attendance requires it. Written comments and materials have no length limit.
- **Equal consideration:** Oral and written comments receive equal weight.
- **Submission address:** Comments go to the Field Supervisor, [[New Mexico Ecological Services Field Office]], 3530 Pan American Highway NE, Suite D, Albuquerque, NM 87107. Materials are available for public inspection by appointment.
- **Contact:** [[Gerald L. Burton]], telephone (505) 883-7877 or fax (505) 883-7876.
- **Signatory:** The notice is dated April 6, 1993, and signed by the Acting Regional Director.
- **Cover letter:** A form letter dated April 21, 1993 from the Field Supervisor informed interested parties of the revised hearing dates and comment deadline, and enclosed the Federal Register notice.
