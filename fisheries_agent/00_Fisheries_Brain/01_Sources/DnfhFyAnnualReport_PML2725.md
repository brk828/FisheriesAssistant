## Executive Summary

This document is a Form 3-110 operations and maintenance cost summary for the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery and Technology Center]] (Dexter NFH & TC) for [[Fiscal Year 1997]]. It is a financial record, not a biological study. It contains no species, habitat, or population data. Its main content is the expenditure breakdown across four funding columns: Operations (Fisheries), Cyclical Maintenance, Quarters Maintenance (Fisheries), and Other Funding. The recorded grand total is $807,248.84. Scan errors make some line-item values uncertain, and these are flagged below.

## Background & Description

The form is a standard Operations/Maintenance Cost Data template (Form 3-110, Rev 1/89) completed for one station, Dexter NFH & TC. The template has three parts:

- **Operations** (lines 1 to 4): salaries (permanent and temporary, with benefits), operating costs (utilities, vehicle maintenance, fuel, supplies, travel, moving expenses, and miscellaneous items), and their subtotal.
- **Capital and maintenance** (lines 5 to 8): vehicle and equipment purchases over $1,000, [[Cyclical Maintenance]], and [[Quarters Maintenance]].
- **Totals** (lines 9 and 10): column totals and total expenditures.

Operating cost categories listed on the form include:

- Utilities: telephone, electricity, heating oil, natural gas, and water/garbage.
- Vehicle maintenance: distribution and non-distribution vehicles, with mileage fields (no mileage figures are legible in the scan).
- Supplies: [[Fish Food]], chemicals and drugs, fertilizer, tags and tagging supplies, and office/custodial/fish cultural supplies.

The miscellaneous line lists these activities: [[Contaminants Program]] work, [[Cryo and Genetics Program]] equipment and supplies, contracts, training, equipment repair, and the [[Wetlands Project]].
