# Bonytail Chub Critical Habitat GIS Dataset: Metadata Record

#ichthyology #marine-biology #gila-elegans

## Executive Summary

This is an FGDC-standard metadata record for a vector GIS dataset mapping [[Critical Habitat]] for the [[Bonytail Chub]] (*[[Gila Elegans]]*). The dataset was produced by the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] (USFWS), Region 2, with a publication date of 2003. It consists of seven polygons digitized from descriptions in the [[Federal Register]] and distributed as downloadable zipped [[Shapefile]] files. The record is a spatial data product, not a research study. It contains no population, biological, or water-chemistry measurements. Its main content is data provenance, spatial extent, attribute definitions, and use restrictions.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** *Gila elegans*, common name Bonytail Chub. The document names the species and gives no morphology, life history, or taxonomic detail.
- **Dataset type:** Polygon shapefile, geospatial presentation form "vector digital data," with a spatial reference based on geographic (latitude/longitude) coordinates.
- **Purpose stated in the record:** Critical habitat areas are "essential for the conservation of a listed species." Designation gives public and land managers notice of their importance but confers no special protection beyond cases involving federal funding, permits, licenses, authorizations, or actions.
- **Producer and contact:** USFWS Information Technology Specialist Michael Dick, Albuquerque, New Mexico. The record gives a mailing address, phone number, and email, plus service hours of 8:30 am to 4:40 pm.
- **Metadata standard:** [[FGDC Content Standards For Digital Geospatial Metadata]] (FGDC-STD-001-1998). The file was generated with a metadata tool on 8 March 2005.
- **Native software environment:** Microsoft Windows 2000 (Build 2600) and [[ESRI ArcCatalog]] 8.3.0.800.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The record provides only limited environmental context:

- **Geographic extent:** Portions of Arizona, California, Colorado, Nevada, and Utah. Listed counties (9 in total) are [[Mohave County]], [[San Bernardino County]], [[Moffat County]], [[Mesa County]], [[Clark County]], [[Garfield County]], [[Grand County]], [[San Juan County]], and [[Uintah County]].
- **Bounding coordinates (as recovered from OCR):** West about -114.744467, East -108.509417, North 40.546530, South 34.288156.
- **Habitat units:** Attribute fields record river and creek names, "complex" and "reach" designations, and unit names, and they report length in miles. The record does not name specific rivers in the text or describe habitat conditions.
- **Mapping base:** Polygons were digitized from [[USGS 7.5 Minute Quads]] and [[TIGER 2000]] base data, then reviewed by USFWS field staff.
- **Datum:** [[North American Datum Of 1983]] (NAD83), with the GRS 80 ellipsoid and decimal-degree units.

The record does not report population status, threats, water quality, flow regimes, or associated fauna and flora.

## Key Findings

**1. Dataset scope and content**
- The dataset contains **7 polygon features** (stated as the point and vector object count).
- Attribute fields are: FID, Shape, Name, Descrip, State, County, River, Complex, Unit, Length_Mi, Perimeter (feet), Acres, and Hectares.
- Each polygon is described by its state, county, river or creek, complex/reach, length, perimeter, and area.

**2. Provenance and quality**
- Processing date: 2003. Originator: USFWS Region 2.
- Polygons are based on Federal Register descriptions and were checked by field staff.
- The logical consistency report and completeness report are both marked **"Not available."**
- The record notes that inherent limits of accuracy depend on source, scale, and resolution.

**3. Legal status and use restrictions**
- The GIS files and coordinates are **not the legal source** for critical habitat boundaries. The textual unit descriptions in the final rule are the legal source.
- USFWS gives **no warranty** on accuracy, reliability, or completeness, and it is not liable for improper use.
- The data are intended for planning, land management, and [[Section 7 Consultation]] under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. They are **not** for legal survey use.
- Interested parties may ask USFWS for a definitive determination of whether a location lies within critical habitat.

**4. Access and distribution**
- Distributed as downloadable zipped shapefiles, with a reported transfer size of **0.028** (units not stated in the record).
- No access constraints or fees apply.

**5. Metadata information gaps**
- The record does not state a tabulated count of river segments, total habitat length, or total acreage. It provides these as per-feature attributes but not as summary totals.
- The OCR text is partly garbled in places, including the metadata date and some coordinate values. Those values are reported as they appear in the scan.
