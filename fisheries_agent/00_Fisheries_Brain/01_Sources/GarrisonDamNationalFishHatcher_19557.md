# Garrison Dam National Fish Hatchery — 1993 Station Profile

## Executive Summary
This document is a 1993 operational and fisheries management profile for the **[[Garrison Dam National Fish Hatchery]]**, located in Riverdale, North Dakota, and operated by the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Region 6). Established in 1957 under the Coordination Act (48 Stat. 401), the facility's mission is to sustain and enhance fishery resources across the **upper Great Plains**, mitigating habitat impacts from federal water projects (notably the [[Garrison Dam]] itself). The hatchery supplies fish to national wildlife refuges, Indian reservations, and military installations across **North Dakota, South Dakota, and Montana**, with a growing emphasis on **[[Paddlefish]]** and **[[Pallid Sturgeon]]** restoration throughout the upper [[Missouri River]] basin, extending cooperative range into Nebraska, Kansas, Texas, and Louisiana. In FY1993, the station distributed over 10 million fish (excluding eggs/fry) and hosted approximately 60,000 visitors.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat Context:** The hatchery supports mitigation stocking associated with the **[[Garrison Dam]]** and broader **upper Missouri River Basin** hydrologic alterations, addressing habitat loss caused by federal water projects (dams, reservoirs).
- **Restoration Focus:** A new programmatic emphasis on **[[Paddlefish]]** and **sturgeon restoration** reflects concern over declining native large-river fish populations impacted by damming, altered flow regimes, and habitat fragmentation in the Missouri River drainage.
- **Distribution Range:** Fish and eggs/fry were distributed to federal, state, and private waters across 11 states (Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Washington, Wyoming, Kansas, Nebraska, Illinois, Idaho, Arizona, Colorado), indicating a broad, interstate ecological role in stocking programs supporting sport fisheries, refuge management, and tribal/military lands.
- **Facility Infrastructure Supporting Ecological Function:**
  - 11 Raceways, 22 Rearing Tanks, 64 Ponds (100 acres total)
  - 3 Hatchery Buildings, 1 Shop, 4 Storage Buildings, 1 Oil House
  - 3 Residences (staff housing)

## Key Findings
- **Establishment & Authority:** Founded 1957 under the Coordination Act; owned by the Department of the Interior via cooperative agreement with the Department of the Army (likely linked to U.S. Army Corps of Engineers dam construction).
- **Conservation Priority Shift:** FY1993 marks an expanded institutional focus on **[[Pallid Sturgeon]]** and **[[Paddlefish]]** restoration, extending the hatchery's geographic mandate southward to Nebraska, Kansas, Texas, and Louisiana as threatened species are identified in the Missouri River drainage.
