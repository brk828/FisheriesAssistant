# Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Team Meeting Agenda June 1989

#environmental-studies #dam-management #colorado-river #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This administrative document from the [[Bureau of Reclamation]] Upper Colorado Regional Office announces Environmental and Economic Study Team meetings for the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) program scheduled for June 15-16, 1989, in [[Flagstaff, Arizona]]. The meetings were designed to coordinate Phase II research planning, review endangered fish surveys, discuss test flow studies, and integrate scientific and economic analyses of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] operations. The document includes detailed agendas for both technical teams, logistical information for the [[Northern Arizona University]] venue, and accommodation listings for participants.

## Background & Description

The [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] program was conducting integrated environmental and economic assessments of dam operations on the [[Colorado River]] ecosystem. Program Manager David L. Wegner coordinated meetings bringing together technical teams, the GCES Executive Review Committee (ERC), and various stakeholders. The program was transitioning into Phase II, requiring development of an Integrated Research Plan under the direction of newly introduced Senior Scientist Duncan Patten.

The meetings were structured to address both biological/environmental concerns and economic modeling efforts. Key personnel included representatives from [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]], economic modelers using EGEAS, ELFIN, and Western Prototype systems, and federal agency staff coordinating the Fiscal Year 1989 work plan.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The GCES program operated within the context of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] impacts on downstream aquatic ecosystems. The Environmental Study Team agenda specifically addressed:

- **Endangered Fish Status**: Dennis Kubly from [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] was scheduled to review findings from an endangered fish trip conducted May 15-29, 1989
- **Biological Opinion**: Status updates on the [[Glen Canyon Dam]] Biological Opinion were planned, indicating regulatory compliance under endangered species legislation
- **Test Flow Studies**: October 1989 test flows were being planned to assess ecological responses to modified dam operations
- **Habitat Assessment**: Scientific integration trips (July 7-16, 1989) and constituent trips (August 11-20, 1989) were scheduled to evaluate river conditions

The program explicitly linked dam operations, water release patterns, and aquatic ecosystem health, though specific fish species were not detailed in this administrative document.

**Meeting Structure and Objectives:**
- Environmental Study Team meeting June 15, 1989, 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM
- Economic Study Team meeting June 16, 1989, same schedule
- Evening forum with GCES Executive Review Committee June 15, 6:00-8:30 PM to address "direction and level of detail of the GCES Phase II program"
- Venue: "Walnut" meeting room, [[Northern Arizona University]] Union building (second floor)

**Environmental Team Agenda Priorities:**
- Review of endangered fish survey results from May 15-29, 1989 field trip
- Status of [[Glen Canyon Dam]] Biological Opinion
- Development of GCES Integrated Research Plan under Duncan Patten
- Implementation of Fiscal Year 1989 Work Plan
- Planning for October 1989 test flow studies with specific needs and requirements

**Economic Team Agenda Priorities:**
- Status reports on Economic Team activities, meetings held, and work group progress
- Review of methodology input assumptions and data

- Integration with scientific research planning
- Economic component products and timetables for FY 1989

- July 7-16, 1989: Scientific Integration Trip
