# Memorandum: Fish Kill and Failed Relocation of RBS from Blythe Municipal Golf Course Pond

#ichthyology #freshwater-ecology #fish-kill

## Executive Summary

This internal memorandum from the Fisheries Region 5 Management Supervisor, dated June 1, 1999, reports a [[Fish Kill]] at the pond of [[Blythe Municipal Golf Course]] and the failed attempt to relocate the RBS population from that pond. The pond was drawn down too far and was choked with dense aquatic weed growth, and dissolved oxygen levels fell overnight. An estimated 200 to 900 fish died. Staff attempted to seine the remaining population of about 1,000 fish on April 21, 1999, but the net could not be pulled through the weeds. Two individuals were captured in a trap on May 16, 1999, and a later snorkel survey found no live fish. The memo suspects herbicide toxicity as the cause of the extirpation.

## Background & Description

The document concerns the RBS population in the Blythe Municipal Golf Course pond. The expanded species name is not given in the text, so the taxon cannot be confirmed. The memo is addressed to the Fisheries Region 5 file and was prepared by the Associate Fishery Biologist (Region 5), with copies to the Blythe Fisheries file.

Staff measured fish from several year classes. Total lengths were reported as roughly 10 to 82 cm (OCR-uncertain), and weights ranged from about 20 to 80 g (OCR-uncertain). The fish were small enough to be scanned for quality, but the memo gives no further biological detail.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Key environmental conditions described:

- **Water level:** The pond had been drawn down too low before the event.
- **Vegetation:** Dense [[Aquatic Weed Growth]] covered the pond, which impeded netting.
- **Dissolved oxygen:** Levels fell overnight, which the memo links to the die-off.
- **Chemical exposure:** The use of a [[Herbicide Toxicity]] agent is suspected to have contributed to the extirpation. The memo states that this is a suspicion and that the Department needed to demonstrate it.

## Key Findings

1. **Mass mortality:** An estimated 200 to 900 RBS died from low [[Dissolved Oxygen]] after the pond was drawn down and weeded over.
2. **Population size before the event:** About 1,000 fish were estimated in the pond before the die-off.
3. **Failed salvage:** A [[Seining]] attempt on April 21, 1999, was unsuccessful because weeds fouled the net. Fish seen in the bag at the surface escaped as the net was pulled toward shore.
4. **Sampling of survivors:** Length and weight data were taken from several year classes before the net was lost to the weeds.
5. **Trap results:** Two fish were captured in a trap on May 16, 1999, which showed that some individuals survived.
6. **Extirpation indicated:** A later [[Snorkel Survey]] found no live fish, and the memo concludes that the RBS population was extirpated from the pond.
7. **Probable cause:** Herbicide toxicity is suspected as the cause of the extirpation, but the memo says this still needs to be demonstrated.
8. **Management implication:** The incident shows the need for better water-level and vegetation management in ponds that hold fish populations, and for verifying chemical use before treatment.
