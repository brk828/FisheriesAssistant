# Razorback Sucker Non-lethal Tissue Sampling Protocol for Genetic Analysis - FY 1991

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #Catostomidae #genetic-analysis #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This document presents a standardized protocol for non-lethal tissue collection from [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) and related sucker species across the [[Colorado River Basin]] for genetic analysis during FY 1991. Five sampling teams were assigned specific locations and sample number ranges to collect muscle and fin tissue for allozyme analysis, with additional protocols for gamete collection for mitochondrial DNA analysis. The protocol was developed collaboratively by [[Ouray National Fish Hatchery]], U. Texas Cancer Research Center, and Genetics Analysis, Inc. Target sample size was 30 individuals per species per site, though this goal was acknowledged as difficult to achieve without jeopardizing endangered [[Razorback Sucker]] populations.

## Background & Description

The [[Razorback Sucker]] is a threatened/endangered species requiring genetic analysis to support conservation and refugia development efforts in the [[Upper Colorado River Basin]]. The protocol covers five sampling teams with designated locations:

**Team Assignments:**
- Team 1 (BOR/UT/USFWS, samples 001-149): [[Colorado River]] Arm and [[San Juan River]] Arm of [[Lake Powell]], April 1-28
- Team 2 (Grand Junction CRFP, samples 150-299): Upper [[Colorado River]] mainstem (Grand Valley Area), March 18-20 and April
- Team 3 (Vernal CRFP, samples 300-999): [[Green River]] at Echo Park, Razorback Bar, and Escalante Ranch, May/June
- Team 4 (UCLA/ASU): [[Lake Mojave]], March 17-19
- Team 5 (Dexter NFH): [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] broodstock, February 25 - March 1

**Target Species for Sampling:**
- [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*)
- [[Bluehead Sucker]]
- [[Flannelmouth Sucker]]
- [[White Sucker]]
- Hybrid suckers

The protocol specifies PIT-tagging of [[Razorback Sucker]] specimens and transport to refuge facilities at [[Ouray National Fish Hatchery]] or [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Sampling occurred across diverse habitats within the [[Colorado River Basin]], including:
- Reservoir environments ([[Lake Powell]], [[Lake Mojave]])
- Riverine mainstem habitats ([[Colorado River]], [[Green River]])
- Captive broodstock facilities

Field data collection included ecological parameters: depth, velocity, substrate, water temperature, and fish morphometrics (length, weight). The protocol emphasized minimizing stress to endangered populations through non-lethal sampling techniques and proper handling procedures including MS-222 anesthesia, wet towel protection, and 0.5% salt solution for stress reduction.

## Key Findings

**Tissue Collection Protocol:**
- Two tissue types collected per fish: dorsal muscle and caudal fin
- Tissue samples approximately 5mm × 5mm using biopsy punches
- Scales not removed prior to punching
- Tissue removal sites swabbed with Betadine
- All sampling non-lethal to support conservation objectives

**Sample Handling Requirements:**
- Tissues placed in 1.5 ml snap-cap vials
- Samples held on wet ice for maximum 6-7 hours before freezing
- Frozen at -20°C for short-term storage (couple of weeks)
- Long-term storage at -80°C to -90°C (ultracold freezer)
- Shipped on dry ice via overnight mail to prevent thawing

**Labeling System:**
- Three-digit sequential numbers assigned to each team
- Species codes: RB (razorback), BH (bluehead), FM (flannelmouth), W (white), H (hybrid)
- Tissue codes: M (muscle), F (fin)
- Example: 001RBM = sample 001, razorback sucker, muscle
- PIT-tag numbers recorded with corresponding vial numbers for [[Razorback Sucker]]

**Additional Protocols:**
- Gamete collection in 15 ml Falcon sterile conical tubes for mtDNA analysis
- Tissues from deceased individuals prioritized: gonads, heart, liver, spleen, kidney, muscle (in order of preference)
- 1-5 grams of tissue collected from deceased specimens
- All samples shipped to Ms. Catherine Karp at Vernal CRFP, USFWS (except [[Lake Mojave]] samples sent directly to UCLA)

**Target Sample Sizes:**
- Ideal: n=30 per species per sampling site
- Acknowledged as difficult to achieve without jeopardizing [[Razorback Sucker]] populations
- Protocol emphasized flexibility: "Do the best you can without jeopardizing the lives of RBS"

**Planned Fish Collections:**
- Team 1: 21 adult RBS from [[Colorado River]] Arm, 11-12 from [[San Juan River]] Arm
- Team 2: 13-18 RBS from Upper [[Colorado River]] mainstem
- Additional sampling from fish collected in 1990 already in refuge facilities
