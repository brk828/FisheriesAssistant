## Executive Summary

This document is not a primary research paper but a formal **errata sheet** correcting typographical, citation, and taxonomic-authority errors in Bruner (1991), *Bibliography of the Family Catostomidae (Cypriniformes)*, published by the Provincial Museum of Alberta. The original bibliography catalogued literature on [[Catostomidae]] (the sucker family), and this errata corrects reference counts (from ~2000 to 2004 references), author name misspellings, citation formatting errors, misplaced/misordered entries, and — most scientifically relevant — **corrections to taxonomic authorship and nomenclatural dates** for numerous sucker genera and species. No new morphological, physiological, or ecological data are presented; the value of this document lies in refining the bibliographic and nomenclatural record for [[Catostomidae]] research.

## Background & Description

No original morphological data are presented in this errata. However, corrected bibliographic entries reference several morphological/physiological studies relevant to Catostomidae, including:
- Neuroanatomical study of *Octospinifer macilentus* (an acanthocephalan parasite of catostomids), describing praesoma nervous system morphology (Gee 1989).
- Cloning and sequence analysis of vasotocin and isotocin precursor cDNAs in the [[White Sucker]] (*Catostomus commersonii*) (Heierhorst et al. 1989).
- Corrected species epithet spelling: *Catostomus commersoni* → **[[Catostomus commersonii]]** (standardized throughout multiple entries, e.g., pp. 69–70, 152).
- Corrected generic epithet: *Micropterus dolomieui* referenced in association with catostomid literature (p. 11).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

While this errata does not itself report novel ecological findings, corrected citations point to ecological/physiological topics covered in the underlying bibliography:
- Temperature tolerance thresholds in early ontogenesis of the **Big Mouth Buffalo** (*Ictiobus cyprinellus*), per Andryushchenko & Grishcenko (1985), studying the upper thermal survival threshold in larval development.
- Cadmium toxicity and cadmium-binding studies in [[White Sucker]] populations (p. 61).
- Fecundity studies in Catostomidae (Behmer 1969a, 1969b).
- Predation studies referencing catostomid populations (p. 98).
- Baitfish-related pathology/parasitology in suckers (Ostland et al., p. 87).
- Geographic distribution corrections noting records from Québec (Canada) and Moldavia (Eurasia), indicating the bibliography's coverage spans both Nearctic and Palearctic catostomid-adjacent literature.

## Key Findings

**Bibliometric corrections:**
- Total reference count corrected from "2000" to **2004 references** (p. v).
- Number of "thirty-five" new taxa/records corrected to **"fifty-four."**
- Corrected reference count on p. vi from 148 to **164 references**.

**Nomenclatural/taxonomic authority corrections (Index to Scientific Names, Catostomidae):**
- **[[Amblodon]]** Rafinesque — date corrected from 1818 to **1819**.
- **[[Decadactylus]]** — authority added: Eschmeyer and Bailey (1990).
- **[[Deltistes luxatus]]** — authority citation added: Jordan and Evermann (1898).
- **[[Exoglossum lesurianum]]** Rafinesque, 1818 — authority Eschmeyer and Bailey (1990) added.
- **[[Ictiorus]]** — full authority corrected to Rafinesque, 1820.
- **[[Maxillingua]]** Rafinesque, 1818 — authority Eschmeyer and Bailey (1990) added.
- **[[Myostoma]]** — corrected spelling from "Meck" to **Meek, 1904** (Meek, Seth Eugene, 1904).
- **[[Pantosteus]]** Cope, 1875 — clarified as published *IN*: Cope and Yarrow, 1875.
- **[[Quassilabia]]** Jordan and Brayton, 1878 — clarified as *IN*: Jordan, 1878.
- **[[Rhytidostomus]]** Heckel, 1843 — full authority/date restored.
- **[[Sclerognathus]]** — authorship corrected from "Cuvier and Valenciennes" to **Valenciennes, 1844, IN**: Cuvier and Valenciennes, 1844.
- **[[Thoburnia]]** Jordan and Snyder, 1917 — clarified as *IN*: Jordan, 1917.
- **[[Xyrauchen]]** — authority corrected from "Kirsch, 1889" to **Eigenmann and Kirsch, 1889, IN**: Kirsch, 1889.

**Author name and citation corrections (selected):**
- "Etienne La Cepéde" — corrected spelling.
- "M. W. Coleman" → **M. E. Coleman**.
- "MacKay, W. C." → **Mackay, W. C.**
- "Gutenmann" — corrected spelling (multiple instances).
- "Roberto Julis, C." — corrected formatting.
- Multiple undated/ambiguous "Harvey, 1986" and "Harvey, 1987" and "Williams, D. D., 1980" entries disambiguated into lettered sub-references (a, b, c, d, e) to distinguish separate publications by the same author in the same year — a common bibliographic practice for resolving citation ambiguity.
- "Behmer, D. J. 1969" split into **1969a** and **1969b**, with corrected sequencing and spelling of "fecundity."

**Geographic/locality corrections:**
- "Quebec" corrected to **Québec** (with accent) in multiple entries (pp. 80, 98).
- Run-on punctuation and spacing errors corrected throughout the author index (systematic formatting fix, not scientific in nature).

**Significance:** Collectively, these corrections stabilize the taxonomic authority record for [[Catostomidae]] genera — several of which (*Xyrauchen*, *Sclerognathus*, *Pantosteus*, *Thoburnia*, *Quassilabia*) had ambiguous or incomplete original-author citations — bringing them into alignment with Eschmeyer and Bailey's (1990) catalog of fish genera and other primary systematic sources such as Jordan (1878, 1917) and Cope and Yarrow (1875).
