# Biological Opinion Consultation for the Mimbres Resource Management Plan

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cobitidae #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Service) memorandum concerns a draft [[Biological Opinion]] on the [[Mimbres Resource Management Plan]] (RMP) for the [[Mimbres Resource Area]] in southwestern New Mexico. The opinion is prepared under Section 7 of the [[Endangered Species Act]] at the request of the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM). It addresses five federally listed species: the [[Northern Aplomado Falcon]], [[Southwestern Willow Flycatcher]], [[Sneed Pincushion Cactus]], [[Loach Minnow]], and [[Spikedace]]. Only the opening page is provided, so the page covers the consultation framework and history rather than effect determinations.

## Background & Description

The memorandum responds to BLM's August 9, 1996 request for formal consultation on programs implemented under the Mimbres RMP. The Service's Regional Director issued it to the Area Manager in Las Cruces, New Mexico.

The RMP covers a broad range of management activities. Under 50 CFR § 402.12, a [[Biological Assessment]] must evaluate whether listed or proposed species or their habitat are likely to be adversely affected. The memorandum notes that Plan-level consultation does not preclude later [[Section 7 Consultation]] on site-specific projects that may affect listed species.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document names the study area as the Mimbres Resource Area and lists four counties in its species list: Doña Ana, Grant, Luna, and Hidalgo. The two fish species, Loach Minnow and Spikedace, are the only aquatic taxa named. The document does not describe their habitat, water conditions, or ecological requirements. The other three species are a falcon, a flycatcher, and a cactus.

## Key Findings

- **Scope:** The draft opinion covers five species: one falcon, one flycatcher, one cactus, and two freshwater fishes.
- **Legal basis:** Consultation falls under Section 7 of the Endangered Species Act. Site-specific projects may need separate consultation even after Plan-level review.
- **Consultation timeline:**
  - Informal consultation began May 3, 1995, when BLM requested a list of federally listed, proposed, and candidate species.
  - The [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] provided the species list on June 13, 1995.
  - A preliminary amended Biological Assessment was submitted August 6, 1995.
  - Following a Service request for more information, the amended assessment was resubmitted January 25, 1996, seeking Service concurrence.
  - BLM submitted the addendum and requested [[Formal Consultation]] on August 9, 1996. The [[New Mexico Ecological Services Field Office]] acknowledged the request August 26, 1996.
- **Status:** The page does not state any effect determinations, impact measures, or final conclusions. Those are presumably in the remaining opinion text.
