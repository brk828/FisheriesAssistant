# Mississippi River Sturgeons: New Kentucky Records and Comments on Status

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Acipenseridae #freshwater-fish #Sturgeon

## Executive Summary

This note documents new, vouchered Kentucky distribution records for three sturgeon species inhabiting the Mississippi River adjacent to Kentucky: the [[Lake Sturgeon]] (*Acipenser fulvescens*), [[Pallid Sturgeon]] (*Scaphirhynchus albus*), and [[Shovelnose Sturgeon]] (*Scaphirhynchus platorynchus*). Prior to this study, sturgeon records in Kentucky were largely anecdotal, derived from occasional reports by fishermen and biologists, with very few vouchered specimens available for any species. The authors report the **first vouchered Kentucky record of *S. albus*** and the **first confirmed twentieth-century records of *A. fulvescens*** from the Mississippi River in Kentucky, based on specimen collection, museum photographs, and extensive interviews with commercial fishermen between 1979 and 1985. The paper also proposes updated conservation statuses for each species within the Kentucky portion of the Mississippi River.

## Background & Description

- **Scaphirhynchus albus (Pallid Sturgeon)**: Voucher specimen (SIUC 12549) was a **685 mm SL, 1.8 kg female**, collected 5 November 1985. Morphological, meristic, and morphometric features conformed to descriptions of *S. albus* by Bailey and Cross (1954).
- **Possible Hybrid Specimen**: A partial specimen (head and skin only; SIUC 10563), collected 2 August 1984, exhibited **intermediate features of both *S. albus* and *S. platorynchus***, consistent with known hybridization between the two species in other Mississippi River drainage populations (see [[Scaphirhynchus Hybridization]]). Incompleteness of the specimen precluded definitive identification.
- **Acipenser fulvescens (Lake Sturgeon)**: No physical voucher specimens obtained; identifications based on photographic evidence (1977, 1968 catches) and a detailed verbal description from a commercial fisherman, all deemed consistent with the species.
- **Scaphirhynchus platorynchus (Shovelnose Sturgeon)**: 43 specimens collected/obtained from 1979–1985 (all housed at SIUC), representing the most robust sample of any of the three species in this study.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- All three species occupy the main channel habitat of the **Mississippi River** adjacent to Kentucky, particularly near **Columbus, Hickman County**, and near **Hickman, Fulton County**.
- *S. platorynchus* was captured **year-round**, with **peak abundance from late fall through spring**, based on commercial fisherman interviews.
- Sturgeon populations in this reach are difficult to sample using **conventional collecting gear**, contributing to historical data gaps; specimens in this study were primarily obtained via **trotline** (10 lines × 40 hooks, baited with worms, fished overnight) and through commercial fishery bycatch.
- Historical accounts (Forbes and Richardson 1920) indicate *A. fulvescens* was **formerly abundant** throughout the Mississippi River, suggesting significant range-wide decline in this population by the mid-to-late 20th century.
- The [[Mississippi River]] serves as a key connectivity corridor for sturgeon populations spanning Kentucky, Illinois, and Missouri border regions (e.g., Cairo, Illinois; Middle Bar).

## Key Findings

- **First vouchered Kentucky record of *S. albus***: SIUC 12549, collected near Middle Bar (~9 km S of Columbus), Hickman County, 5 November 1985.
- **Possible *S. albus* × *S. platorynchus* hybrid** documented (SIUC 10563) from near Hickman, Fulton County (1984) — supports prior hybridization concerns raised by Phelps and Allendorf (1983).
- **First confirmed 20th-century *A. fulvescens* records** in the Mississippi River of Kentucky: photographic evidence from 1977 (Ballard County) and 1968 (Hickman County), plus one accepted verbal report from the late 1960s.
- Only **2 prior vouchered records** of *S. platorynchus* existed for Kentucky's Mississippi River reach before this study; the authors added **43 new specimens** (1979–1985).
- **Proposed conservation status recommendations**:
  - ***S. albus***: Recommended for **Endangered** status on the Kentucky Academy of Science–Kentucky Nature Preserves Commission list, given its apparent rarity and paucity of abundance data (previously classified as "threatened" range-wide by Deacon et al. 1979).
  - ***A. fulvescens***: Considered **Endangered** in the Mississippi River of Kentucky, based on sporadic occurrence and virtual disappearance from commercial catch (previously listed as "threatened" in Kentucky by Branson et al. 1981).
  - ***S. platorynchus***: Authors conclude Mississippi River populations **do not currently warrant special conservation status**, contrary to the prior "threatened" designation by Branson et al. (1981), though they emphasize the need for further data on abundance, recruitment, and exploitation rate to inform management and sustain the commercial fishery.
- Findings underscore the value of **directly engaging commercial fishermen** as a data source for cryptic, poorly sampled riverine species like [[Sturgeon]].
- Limited historical vouchering (citing [[Bailey and Cross 1954]], [[Burr 1980]], [[Cicerello 1981]], [[Clay 1975]]) had previously precluded accurate assessment of distribution, abundance, and status for all three species in Kentucky.
