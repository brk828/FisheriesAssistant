## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative memorandum from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2) addressed to Dr. W.L. Minckley, Team Leader of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]]. It is not a primary scientific research paper containing morphological or ecological data, but rather a formal acknowledgment of meeting minutes from a recovery team meeting held December 7–8, 1989, in Phoenix, Arizona. The memo emphasizes the FWS's administrative goal to finalize outdated recovery plans for all Federally listed desert fish species within the region by December 31, 1990.

## Background & Description
No morphological, meristic, or anatomical data are provided in this source document. The text is purely administrative correspondence regarding the management and recovery planning process for desert fish species rather than a species account.
