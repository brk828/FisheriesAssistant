# Virgin River Monitoring: Arizona Summary Report (1994)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #freshwater-ecology #Cyprinidae #Catostomidae #Centrarchidae #Ictaluridae

## Executive Summary
This internal memorandum from the Arizona Game & Fish Department (dated October 5, 1994) reports fish catch data from two monitoring stations on the [[Virgin River]] in Arizona: **Cedar Pockets Station** and **Beaver Dam Wash Station**. Sampling was conducted via seine hauls on September 27, 1994, and supplemented with hoop net sets pulled the following morning (September 28, 1994). The report documents native and introduced fish captures, including the sensitive [[Virgin River Chub]] (coded GIROSE, likely *Gila robusta seminuda*), [[Desert Sucker]] (CACL, *Catostomus clarkii*), [[Green Sunfish]] (LECY, *Lepomis cyanellus*), and [[Black Bullhead]] (AMME, *Ameiurus melas*). The data appears to have been shared with a distribution list of regional fisheries scientists and resource managers, indicating a collaborative multi-state monitoring effort for the [[Virgin River Basin]] native fish community.

## Background & Description
Limited direct morphological data are available due to OCR degradation of seine haul tables; however, hoop net catch records provide total length (TL) measurements for individual fish:

- **GIROSE** (probable [[Virgin River Chub]], *Gila robusta seminuda*): 3 adults captured at Beaver Dam Wash Station — TL = 183 mm, 234 mm, 250 mm.
- **CACL** ([[Desert Sucker]], *Catostomus clarkii*):
  - Beaver Dam Wash Station: 1 adult, TL = 187 mm.
  - Cedar Pockets Station: 1 adult (two measurements listed), TL = 140 mm and 301 mm — possibly two individuals.
- **LECY** ([[Green Sunfish]], *Lepomis cyanellus*): 1 adult at Beaver Dam Wash Station, TL = 121 mm.
- **AMME** ([[Black Bullhead]], *Ameiurus melas*): 2 adults at Cedar Pockets Station, TL = 116 mm and 218 mm.

Size ranges suggest a mix of subadult and adult size classes across taxa, consistent with resident, non-migratory riverine fish populations in the [[Virgin River]] system.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Sampling Gear & Placement**: Two hoop nets were deployed at each station, differing in dimensions:
  - **Beaver Dam Wash Station**: 5 ft length, 30" mouth diameter; set in the mainstem Virgin River ~50 ft downstream of the confluence with [[Beaver Dam Wash]] — a lotic/tributary confluence microhabitat.
  - **Cedar Pockets Station**: 10 ft length, 20" mouth diameter; set in the mainstem Virgin River along a cliff wall — indicative of deeper pool or bedrock-margin habitat preferred by larger-bodied fish such as native suckers and chub.
- **Temporal Effort**: Hoop nets were set in the evening (~1230–1600 hrs on Sept. 27) and pulled the following morning (0840–0920 hrs on Sept. 28), representing an overnight passive sampling period (~17 hours).
- **Community Composition**: The co-occurrence of native cyprinids/catostomids (chub, sucker) with introduced centrarchids and ictalurids (green sunfish, black bullhead) reflects the well-documented invasive species pressure facing native [[Virgin River]] fish assemblages, a key conservation concern in this system historically home to endangered and sensitive native fish.
- **Habitat Types Sampled**: Combination of shallow seine-able margins (multiple hauls per station, per the "Catch Data" tables) and deeper mainstem hoop-net habitats, providing a fuller picture of habitat use across the river cross-section.

## Key Findings
- Both Cedar Pockets and Beaver Dam Wash stations were sampled using repeated seine hauls (up to ~12 samples per station), though most tabulated species-level catch values were not recoverable from the OCR source (rendered as zeroes or blanks), limiting quantitative seine-based analysis.
- Total seine catch summary rows suggest substantial total fish captured at both stations (e.g., totals reaching into the hundreds or thousands range per the OCR'd "TOTAL" row), but species-level breakdown for seine hauls is not reliably extractable from this document.
- Hoop net sampling successfully documented presence of four fish taxa across the two stations, providing direct morphometric (TL) data for [[Virgin River Chub]], [[Desert Sucker]], [[Green Sunfish]], and [[Black Bullhead]].
- The presence of adult-sized [[Virgin River Chub]] specimens (GIROSE) at Beaver Dam Wash Station is notable given the historically imperiled status of this taxon in the Virgin River drainage.
- This report exemplifies routine multi-agency native fish monitoring coordination in the Virgin River Basin, with data distributed to researchers at [[Utah State University]], [[Arizona State University]], [[Nevada Division of Wildlife]], [[Utah Division of Wildlife Resources]], and the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]].
- Data quality caveat: Significant OCR corruption affects the seine haul catch tables; numeric values and species codes in those tables should be treated as unreliable without cross-reference to the original source document.
