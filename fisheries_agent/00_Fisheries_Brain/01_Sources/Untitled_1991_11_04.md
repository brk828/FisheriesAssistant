## Executive Summary
This document is an **internal administrative memorandum** (November 4, 1991) from Michael Yard (Glen Canyon Environmental Studies) to Dr. W.L. Minckley (Arizona State University), regarding logistical coordination for the **Endangered Species Research Program** in the Grand Canyon/Glen Canyon reach of the Colorado River. The memo does not contain primary morphological, ecological, or population data on fish species; rather, it catalogs and transmits correspondence, trip reports, and study plans related to ongoing research on the endangered [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*) and other native fishes of the Colorado River. As such, this note primarily documents the **research infrastructure and inter-agency coordination** surrounding the species rather than its biology.
