# Capture of a Suspected Juvenile Razorback Sucker near Parker, Arizona

#ichthyology #marine-biology #xyrauchen-texanus

## Executive Summary

This 1980 interoffice memorandum from the [[Parker Arizona]] Project Leader to the Area Manager (SE) in Phoenix reports the capture, holding, scale sampling, and release of a suspected juvenile [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) taken from the [[Colorado River]]. The fish was photographed, held briefly in the office aquarium, and returned to the river below [[Parker Dam]]. Copies were sent to the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] (Yuma, AZ) and the [[California Department of Fish and Game]] (Blythe, CA). The scanned text is partly illegible, so some details are uncertain.

## Background & Description

- **Subject:** Capture of a suspected juvenile Razorback Sucker, reported as a resident of the [[Colorado River Indian Reservation]] area. The OCR for the subject line is partly garbled.
- **Species:** Razorback Sucker, a large-bodied catostomid native to the [[Colorado River]] system, identified in the memo as the suspected species.
- **Fish size:** The memo gives a total length, but the number is too garbled in the scan to read reliably. It appears to be a few inches, consistent with a juvenile.
- **Handling:** The fish was taken to the local office, where it was photographed and then held in a 50-gallon office aquarium over a weekend.
- **Documentation:** Photographs were taken, and a subsequent newspaper article (published January 24, 1980) used the fish's photos.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Capture site:** The fish was taken with a [[Chicken Wire Seine]] from a culvert where a canal or drainage passes under Burns Road, about 6 miles west and south of the Four Corners area near Parker, Arizona.
- **Water temperature:** Recorded at 52°F (11°C) on January 12, 1980, at the release site.
- **Release:** The fish was returned to the Colorado River below Parker Dam on the same date as the temperature reading. The memo gives no further habitat, water chemistry, or population data.
- **Season:** The January dates indicate a winter capture in cold river water.

## Key Findings

1. A suspected juvenile Razorback Sucker was captured alive from a culvert near Parker, Arizona, and documented with photographs and scale samples.
2. Scale samples were collected with a scale press at the local office, supporting later age or identification work.
3. The fish was held for a short period in a 50-gallon aquarium and then released into the Colorado River below Parker Dam.
4. The release occurred at a recorded water temperature of 52°F (11°C) on January 12, 1980.
5. The capture drew public attention through a January 24, 1980 newspaper article that used the fish's photos.
6. The memo was distributed to agency staff in Arizona and California, reflecting interagency coordination on Colorado River fish.
