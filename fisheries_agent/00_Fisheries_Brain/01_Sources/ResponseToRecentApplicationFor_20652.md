# Endangered Species Permit Application Acknowledgment: Listed Southwestern Fishes

#ichthyology #freshwater-biology #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This is an administrative letter from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Division of Endangered Species/Permits, Ecological Services, Region 2) to Dr. James P. Collins, Chair of the Department of Zoology at [[Arizona State University]]. It acknowledges receipt of an application for an [[Endangered Species Permit]] covering nine listed or protected southwestern fish taxa. The letter confirms the application is being processed under Permit File Number PRT-797129 and returns a $25.00 application fee because federal, state and local government agencies, and institutions under contract to them, are not charged fees for the proposed activities.

## Background & Description

The application concerns the following fishes, as named in the letter:

- [[Humpback Chub]]
- [[Bonytail]]
- [[Yaqui Chub]]
- [[Virgin River Chub]]
- [[Woundfin]]
- [[Colorado Pikeminnow]] (called "Colorado squawfish" in the letter)
- [[Razorback Sucker]]
- [[Desert Pupfish]]
- [[Sonoran Topminnow]] (listed as "Gila (Yaqui) and Sonoran topminnow")

The letter contains no biological descriptions, life-history data, or population figures for these species.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address habitat, range, water chemistry, population status, or threats for any of the listed taxa. Its content is limited to permit processing and fee administration. The species are named as Endangered/Threatened Species Permit subjects, which indicates regulatory protection under federal law, but the letter does not specify the proposed activities (for example, collection, handling, or research methods).

## Key Findings

- **Application status:** The Endangered/Threatened Species Permit application has been received and is under review. Questions should go to the Division of Endangered Species/Permits, P.O. Box 1306, Albuquerque, New Mexico 87103, phone (505) 766-3972, referencing Permit File Number **PRT-797129**.
- **Scope:** Nine fish taxa are covered: humpback, bonytail, Yaqui, and Virgin River chub; woundfin; Colorado squawfish; razorback sucker; desert pupfish; and Gila (Yaqui) and Sonoran topminnow.
- **Fee return:** A check (No. 9401, dated November 16, 1994) for $25.00 was returned under [[50 CFR 13.11]](c)(3). Under this rule, no fee is charged to any federal, state or local government agency, or to any individual or institution under contract to such an agency, for the proposed activities. The fee may be waived or reduced for public institutions under [[50 CFR 10.12]].
- **Signatory:** Janice G. Harp, Legal Instruments Examiner (Permits), signed the letter in December 1994.
