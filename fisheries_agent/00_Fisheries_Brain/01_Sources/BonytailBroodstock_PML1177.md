# Bonytail Chub Broodstock Origin Memorandum

#ichthyology #freshwater-ecology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This internal [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] memorandum, dated May 9, 1985 (OCR reads "IgSs"), from the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] manager to the Regional Director of [[Region 2]] in Albuquerque, New Mexico, explains the wild-source origin of the hatchery's current [[Bonytail Chub]] broodstock. It states that the founding spawn used only a small number of wild fish and that the hatchery now holds 500 broodstock plus 150 excess fish.

## Background & Description

- **Subject:** Bonytail chub broodstock.
- **From:** Buddy L. Jensen, Hatchery Manager, [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], Dexter, New Mexico.
- **To:** Regional Director, Region 2, Albuquerque, New Mexico.
- **Copies:** Divine and [[Minckley]].
- **Attachment:** Referenced but not included in the source text.
- **Reference source:** A 1982 paper by [[Roger Hannan]], "Induced Spawning and Culture of Bonytail Chub," whose Methods section describes the initial spawning.

The memo was written in response to a phone conversation with the Regional Director about how many wild fish produced the hatchery's current stock.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address habitat, wild population status, water chemistry, or ecosystem relationships. Its focus is hatchery stock management and the genetic-origin question of how many wild parents founded the captive population. Relevant framing topics include [[Broodstock Management]] and [[Captive Propagation of Endangered Fishes]], but the memo itself does not discuss them in detail.

## Key Findings

1. **Founding broodstock size:** The initial [[Induced Spawning]] used **six female and five male** wild bonytail chub, as documented in Hannan (1982).
2. **No additional wild fish:** According to the memo, no other fish were available for the initial spawn, so the founding population was limited to these 11 individuals.
3. **Current holdings:** The hatchery was holding **500 broodstock** and **150 excess fish** from that spawning.
4. **Implication for genetic diversity:** A founding group of 11 wild fish means the captive stock has a narrow genetic base. The memo does not state this explicitly, but it is the practical consequence of the stated numbers.
