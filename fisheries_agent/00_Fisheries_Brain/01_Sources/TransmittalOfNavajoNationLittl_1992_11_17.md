## Executive Summary
This document is an internal **memorandum**, not a primary scientific paper. Dated November 17, 1992, it was issued by Dave Wegner of the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES) program, under the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation's Upper Colorado Region. The memo transmits a **draft bibliography** — the *Little Colorado River Bibliography* — compiled by the **Navajo Natural Heritage Program** for GCES. Its intended audience includes endangered fish researchers and sediment researchers working within the [[Little Colorado River]] and [[Glen Canyon]] system. No direct scientific, morphological, or ecological data on fish species are contained within this transmittal itself; rather, it serves as a pointer to a larger bibliographic resource intended to support research on the [[Little Colorado River Basin]].

## Background & Description
Not applicable. This document contains no morphological, anatomical, or taxonomic descriptions of fish or other aquatic organisms. It is administrative in nature, serving only to distribute a bibliography compilation.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
While no direct ecological data is presented, the memo situates its subject matter within the broader ecological and management context of the [[Colorado River Basin]], specifically the [[Little Colorado River]] — a critical tributary supporting populations of federally listed endangered fish species (e.g., [[Humpback Chub]]) and playing a key role in sediment dynamics affecting the [[Glen Canyon]] ecosystem downstream of [[Glen Canyon Dam]]. The referenced bibliography is intended to consolidate existing knowledge relevant to:
- Endangered fish research within the Little Colorado River basin
- Sediment transport and geomorphological studies affecting river habitat quality

## Key Findings
- The **Little Colorado River Bibliography** was developed by the **Navajo Natural Heritage Program** in collaboration with [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] (GCES).
- The bibliography was distributed in **draft form** to assist endangered fish and sediment researchers in understanding the **Little Colorado River basin**.
- This document underscores the **institutional and interagency cooperation** (Bureau of Reclamation, Navajo Nation, GCES) involved in managing and studying the Colorado River ecosystem post-Glen Canyon Dam operations.
- No direct species-level, morphological, or population data are included; the value of this document lies in its role as a **research infrastructure and bibliographic resource** rather than a primary data source.
