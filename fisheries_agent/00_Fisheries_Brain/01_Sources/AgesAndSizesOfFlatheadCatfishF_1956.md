# Ages and Sizes of 29 Flathead Catfish from the Kansas River, Douglas County, Kansas

#ichthyology #marine-biology #ictaluridae #freshwater-fisheries #pylodictis-olivaris

## Executive Summary
This 1956 short paper reports age and length data for 29 [[Flathead Catfish]] (*Pilodictis olivaris*) collected following a mass fish-kill event in the Kansas River near Lawrence, Douglas County, Kansas, in September 1955. The kill was caused by an acute ammonia/nitrate pollution event originating from a fertilizer-manufacturing plant discharge. Researchers salvaged specimens to obtain rare age-and-growth data for this species, using thin cross-sections of pectoral and dorsal fin spines to determine annual growth marks (annuli). The study contributes one of relatively few published growth-rate datasets for Flathead Catfish at the time, spanning ages 1 through 12 and lengths from 8.25 to 44.75 inches.

## Background & Description
- **Total length range:** 8¼ inches to 44¾ inches
- **Estimated weight:** Not directly measured (specimens partially decomposed); several individuals estimated to exceed 20 lbs, with the largest (age 10, 44 in.) estimated at ~45 lbs
- **Aging structures:** Pectoral and dorsal fin spines, thin-sectioned to reveal concentric growth rings (annuli), interpreted as annual year-marks reflecting seasonal growth variation
- **Age range represented in sample:** 1–12 years
- Growth pattern shows general positive correlation between age and length, though the oldest specimen (age 12, 35 in.) was notably shorter than the age-10 individual (44 in.), suggesting individual variation in growth rate or a possible aging/measurement anomaly

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat:** Large, turbid, warm-water river channel (Kansas River)
- **Community context:** Co-occurred with a diverse assemblage of large-river fishes killed in the same pollution event, including:
  - [[Paddlefish]] (*Polyodon spathula*)
  - [[Shovelnose Sturgeon]] (*Scaphirhynchus platorynchus*)
  - [[Longnose Gar]] (*Lepisosteus osseus*)
  - [[Gizzard Shad]] (*Dorosoma cepedianum*)
  - [[Goldeye]] (*Hiodon alosoides*)
  - [[River Carpsucker]] (*Carpiodes carpio*)
  - [[Northern Redhorse]] (*Moxostoma aureolum*)
  - [[Common Carp]] (*Cyprinus carpio*)
  - Native minnow species (Cyprinidae)
  - [[Channel Catfish]] (*Ictalurus punctatus*)
- **Environmental stressor:** Acute pollution event from a fertilizer plant drainage ditch:
  - Ammonia (NH₃): 827 ppm
  - Nitrate (NO₃): 726 ppm
  - pH: 9.4 (highly alkaline)
- This event illustrates the vulnerability of large-river fish communities, including apex benthic predators like [[Flathead Catfish]], to industrial effluent-driven fish kills.

- Aging methodology followed a technique similar to [[Leonard and Sneed 1951]] fin-spine sectioning, cross-validated against contemporaneous work by [[McCoy 1955]] on Flathead Catfish age validation.
- This dataset was noted as a valuable addition to the sparse growth-rate literature on Flathead Catfish compiled by [[Carlander 1953]] (*Handbook of Freshwater Fishery Biology*).
- Companion data on Channel Catfish lengths and fin-spines from the same kill event were slated for separate reporting by W. J. Davis.
- The largest specimen (44¾ in., age 10) represents a notably large, fast-growing individual, underscoring the species' potential for substantial size in Kansas River habitat.
