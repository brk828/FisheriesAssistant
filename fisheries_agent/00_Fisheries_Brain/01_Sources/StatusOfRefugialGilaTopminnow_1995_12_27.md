## Executive Summary

This record documents the 1995 status of captive refugial populations of the **[[Gila Topminnow]]** (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*), a small, imperiled poeciliid fish native to the Gila River drainage of Arizona. No wild collections were conducted by the reporting laboratory in 1995; instead, the note summarizes the maintenance of stocks originally collected in 1994 from four distinct source populations. These stocks are housed and propagated at the **Aquatic Facility of the Animal Resource Center**, Arizona State University (Tempe campus), functioning as a genetic and demographic safeguard against extirpation of wild populations. All four captive lineages were reported as healthy and reproductively thriving, suggesting the ex-situ conservation program was successful at maintaining population viability in captivity through at least this reporting period.
