# Bonytail Chub Backwater Stocking Report, March–August 1995

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This short stocking report tracks [[Bonytail Chub]] released into four lake backwaters during the March 1995 roundup. It gives stocking totals by backwater and compares fish lengths at stocking (8 March 1995) with lengths on 1 August 1995 (status as of 3 August 1995). The total stocked was 1,620 fish. Mean length increased at every backwater between the two sampling dates. The OCR scan is partly garbled, so some figures are uncertain and are flagged below.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), a Cyprinidae fish. The document identifies the species by common name only.
- **Program:** A [[Hatchery Stocking Program]] in which fish were released during a March roundup and then monitored.
- **Monitoring:** Fish were measured at stocking and again in August. Sample sizes (n) are reported for each measurement.
- **Sites:** Four backwaters, listed in the table below.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The report concerns fish released into lake backwaters, which are sheltered, shallow side-areas of a lake. The document does not report water chemistry, habitat conditions, invertebrate prey, or other ecosystem variables. Its ecological relevance lies in release sites and early post-stocking growth.

Several August values are garbled, so the assignment of some lengths to backwaters is tentative. Where legible, the August means are roughly 90–130 mm above the March means at each backwater, which indicates post-stocking growth over about five months.

**Notes on data quality:** The document is an OCR scan with heavy distortion in its headers and tables. The lake name, the removal totals, and several August length values could not be confirmed. The figures above are reported as printed, with uncertainty flagged where the text was illegible.
