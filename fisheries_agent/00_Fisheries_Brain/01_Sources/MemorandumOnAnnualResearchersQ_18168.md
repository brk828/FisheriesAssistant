## Key Findings

- The **[[Technical Group]]** (TG), formerly known as the "Bio Sub-committee," is tasked with compiling a list of current and proposed research within the Upper Colorado River Basin relevant to endangered fish recovery.
- Research prioritization is guided by **existing priority criteria** outlined in **Appendix 6.2** of the "Recovery Implementation Program" document (September 1987).
- The overarching goal is recovery of **endangered fishes** within a **15-year timeframe** established by the **[[Recovery Implementation Program]]**.
- Researchers and consultants were required to submit one questionnaire per project (current or proposed), addressing:
  1. Brief title of project
  2. Issue or problem related to need
  3. Information or service needed (content and format)
  4. Application of the information/service
  5. Recommended activity (tasks)
  6. Other related efforts (proposed or ongoing)
  7. Estimated potential resource benefits
  8. Estimated total costs (internal and external)
- Submission deadline for questionnaires was **May 18th**, to allow summary preparation for a **Technical Group meeting on May 25–26**.
- The memo reflects an administrative/coordination mechanism within the broader **[[Upper Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program]]**, illustrating how multi-agency cooperative research efforts (state Division of Wildlife, consultants, and federal partners) are organized and vetted for funding and prioritization purposes.
