# Non-Game Wildlife and Fisheries Investigations Cooperative Agreement

#ichthyology #marine-biology #administrative-document

## Executive Summary

This document is a cooperative agreement proposal between [[Arizona State University]] (ASU) and the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] for non-game wildlife and fisheries investigations. Proposed by W. L. Minckley, Professor of Zoology at ASU, in collaboration with James E. Brooks, Biologist in the Non-game branch, the agreement establishes a framework for logistic and technical assistance in conducting non-game fish and wildlife research. The agreement requests total funds of $4,000.00 for the period from approval date in 1984 through 30 June 1985.

## Background & Description

The agreement establishes a formal partnership between two Arizona state agencies under the authority of ARS 17-231.87 and the [[Arizona Game and Fish Commission]]. The collaboration leverages ASU's academic resources—including vertebrate collections, laboratory facilities, optical equipment, and student work-study programs—to support the Department's non-game wildlife and fish investigations. The proposal was endorsed by ASU administration including the Vice President for Research, Chairman of the Department of Zoology, and Dean of the College of Liberal Arts on 20 April 1984.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The agreement focuses on non-game wildlife and fishes in [[Arizona]], representing species not traditionally targeted for sport or commercial harvest. The work involves collection, preservation, and study of biological materials resulting from Department investigations and management activities. The collaboration provides access to ASU's vertebrate collections and aquarium facilities for holding non-game fishes, supporting both research and conservation management objectives for Arizona's aquatic and terrestrial fauna.

## Key Findings

**ASU Commitments:**
- Provide use of vertebrate collections, laboratory space, and optical equipment (microscopes) as available
- Provide office space for Department personnel conducting non-game studies
- Provide aquarium space for holding non-game fishes
- Assist in employment of student labor and consider the Department eligible for federal College work-study program (with no guarantee of federal funding)
- Provide liaison and supervision of all ASU participation activities

**Department Commitments:**
- Compensate students on fair and equitable basis for employment
- Reimburse students separately for per diem costs such as travel
- Comply with all College work-study off-campus agreement provisions
- Provide funds for ASU vehicle use at ASU rates for project-related travel
- Provide containers and preservatives for biological materials from Department investigations
- Provide indirect costs at 12.6% of total direct costs (off-campus rates) to defray ASU obligations
- Provide liaison and supervision for all Department activities

**Financial and Administrative Terms:**
- Total reimbursement not to exceed $4,000 for first year unless amended
- Agreement subject to cancellation by Governor pursuant to Section 38-511, ARS
- Agreement automatically renewed annually unless specified otherwise, with monetary amounts negotiated anew each period
- Alterations require written amendment approved by both parties
- Disputes seeking monetary damages of $5,000.00 or less subject to arbitration
- Compliance with Executive Orders 75-5 and 75-11 (non-discrimination orders) required
