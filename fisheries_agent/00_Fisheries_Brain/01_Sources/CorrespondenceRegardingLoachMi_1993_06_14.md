# Correspondence Regarding Loach Minnow Stream Surveys — Fort Apache Reservation

## Executive Summary
This document is an administrative letter from the White Mountain Apache Tribe Chairman, Ronnie Lupe, to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Arizona Fishery Resources Office), approving a proposed stream survey effort aimed at determining the distribution and extent of the threatened [[Loach Minnow]] (*Rhinichthys cobitis*) on tribal lands within the Fort Apache Reservation. The letter is not a primary scientific data source but establishes the institutional and regulatory context for future field surveys, including conditions of tribal oversight and participation.

## Background & Description
No morphological, meristic, or physiological data are provided in this source. The document is purely administrative/correspondence in nature and does not describe body form, coloration, fin structure, or other taxonomic characters of the [[Loach Minnow]].

*Note: The species is listed here under the name "Rhinichthys cobitis," an older/alternative scientific designation; the species is more commonly classified in contemporary literature under the genus **Tiaroga** (i.e., *Tiaroga cobitis*), within family [[Cyprinidae]].*

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat type**: Freshwater stream systems within the [[Fort Apache Reservation]], Arizona, part of the greater [[Colorado River Basin]] watershed.
- **Conservation status**: Referenced as a federally **threatened** species under U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service jurisdiction.
- **Geographic scope**: Streams under tribal jurisdiction, surveyed in coordination with the [[White Mountain Apache Tribe]] and the [[Arizona Fishery Resources Office]].
- No specific data on water chemistry, flow regime, substrate, co-occurring species, or trophic interactions are provided.

## Key Findings
- The [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] proposed a stream survey initiative to assess the distributional extent of the threatened [[Loach Minnow]] population(s) on the [[Fort Apache Reservation]].
- The [[White Mountain Apache Tribe]] formally approved the proposed surveys, contingent on:
  - Inclusion of tribal representatives on all survey teams.
  - Restriction of participating U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service personnel to Fisheries staff only.
  - Advance submission of survey itineraries to the Tribal Chairman's office.
- This correspondence reflects a broader pattern of collaborative, government-to-government management between federal conservation agencies and tribal authorities regarding sensitive/threatened aquatic species such as the [[Loach Minnow]].
- No population counts, abundance estimates, or survey results are included in this letter — it precedes actual field data collection.
