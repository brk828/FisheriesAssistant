# Gila Trout Reintroduction Correspondence in Arizona (1996–1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a set of three letters from late 1996 and early 1997 concerning a proposal to reintroduce [[Gila Trout]] (*Oncorhynchus gilae*) into Arizona waters. The [[Zane Grey Chapter]] of [[Trout Unlimited]] initiated the request to the [[U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service]] (USFWS) Arizona Ecological Services Field Office. The USFWS stated that [[New Mexico Ecological Services]] holds lead responsibility for the species, and the [[Tonto National Forest]] expressed support in concept. No data, sample sizes or biological measurements are reported; the content is administrative and planning-focused.

## Background & Description

The species under discussion is the Gila trout, a native salmonid. The letters identify it as *Oncorhynchus gilae* and describe it as a listed species under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973, as amended. The Tonto letter notes that the fish is listed under the Act, which requires federal coordination for any reintroduction. Its status is tied to a [[Gila Trout Recovery Plan]], which sets the priorities that any reintroduction must follow.

The letters also reference the [[Apache Trout]], another Arizona native trout, as a model for future "down listing" (reclassification from endangered to threatened status).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The correspondence identifies the [[Gila River]] and its drainage as a large area in Arizona suitable for reintroduction. The Tonto National Forest, administered by the [[USDA Forest Service]], was identified as a possible stocking location because of an earlier proposal. The Forest Service stated that several issues must be resolved on its lands before stocking can proceed, but it recognizes its role in providing habitat for listed fish. No habitat measurements, water chemistry, population counts or other environmental data are given.

## Key Findings

- **Initiation of cooperation (November 20, 1996):** The Zane Grey Chapter of Trout Unlimited requested formal, regular meetings with the USFWS to plan the reintroduction. The chapter proposed meetings by year end and a possible standing committee starting January 1997.
- **Stated goal:** An experimental population of Gila trout was targeted for reintroduction in 1997, with long-term aims of recovery and down listing similar to the Apache Trout.
- **Lead agency and guidance (December 5, 1996):** The Arizona USFWS office noted that the Albuquerque (New Mexico) office holds lead responsibility for the species. All reintroductions must follow the priorities of the recovery plan. The request was added to the agenda of a Gila trout recovery team meeting planned for mid-January 1997.
- **Prior proposal:** A May 30, 1996 letter from the [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]] asked the regional USFWS office to form a workgroup to explore reintroduction.
- **Forest Service position (January 3, 1997):** The Tonto National Forest supports the project in concept, wants to join a workgroup with USFWS and the Arizona Game and Fish Department, and identified unresolved issues that must be addressed before stocking on its lands. It named a project contact.
- **Copied parties:** The letters were copied to the Arizona Game and Fish Department, Trout Unlimited, and several USFWS regional and field offices, showing multi-agency involvement.

**Note:** These documents are correspondence only. They contain no population data, sample sizes, or measured environmental parameters.
