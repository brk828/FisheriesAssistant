# Desert Fishes Recovery Team Meeting (February 1993)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #freshwater-conservation #cyprinidae #poeciliidae #catostomidae #desert-fishes

## Executive Summary

This document is an administrative memorandum and agenda for a Desert Fishes Recovery Team (DFRT) meeting held February 24-25, 1993, in Phoenix, Arizona, under the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS). Rather than presenting primary morphological or ecological data, the text outlines the conservation, regulatory, and management agenda for multiple imperiled desert fish species native to the Gila, Yaqui, and Colorado River basins. Key topics include recovery plan revisions, critical habitat proposals, taxonomic uncertainties, candidate species listing discussions, and threats from grazing, water diversion (CAP - Central Arizona Project), and stocking programs. The meeting also addressed a planned follow-up session focused specifically on the [[Gila Topminnow]] recovery plan, incorporating new genetic data from researcher Joe Quattro.

## Background & Description

No direct morphological data (meristics, coloration, body form) is provided in this administrative source. However, the document highlights **taxonomic ambiguity** as a recurring theme relevant to morphology-based classification:

- Ongoing **taxonomic project** to resolve species boundaries within [[Gila (Genus)]], specifically concerning *Gila intermedia* and *Gila robusta* — taxonomic uncertainty is cited as a barrier to listing decisions.
- Debate over correct generic placement of [[Loach Minnow]]: recommendation on FWS acceptance of *Rhinichthys* versus *Tiaroga* as the valid genus.
- Discussion of subspecies nomenclature for [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius macularius*), including how to refer to this subspecies in the recovery plan (common name standardization).
- Pending genetic/taxonomic work on [[Spikedace]] and [[Loach Minnow]] populations.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The species discussed occupy isolated desert aquatic habitats characterized by high endemism and vulnerability to anthropogenic disturbance:

- **[[Gila Topminnow]]** (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*) — subject of an in-progress recovery plan revision; downlisting/delisting criteria to be defined pending new genetic data from Joe Quattro.
- **[[Desert Pupfish]]** (*Cyprinodon macularius*) — recovery plan discussion includes Mexican field populations and subspecies nomenclature issues.
- **[[Senora Chub]]** — habitat threatened by grazing pressure; transplant proposal status and Mexican population updates discussed; plan monitoring implementation questioned.
- **[[Gila Chub]]** (*Gila intermedia*) — status survey ongoing; potential reintroduction sites identified at [[Cave Creek (Maricopa County)]] and streams in the [[Agua Fria River|Agua Fria Drainage]] on BLM lands.
- **[[Spikedace]]** and **[[Loach Minnow]]** — critical habitat reproposal and uplisting under review; population monitoring in [[Aravaipa Creek]], the upper [[Gila River]]/[[San Francisco River]], and [[Verde River]]; potential reintroduction into the San Francisco River discussed.
- **[[Yaqui Catfish]]** — reintroduction efforts planned as part of broader [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]].
- **[[Little Colorado Spinedace]]** (non-Team species) — recovery plan and critical habitat status reviewed.
- **[[Razorback Sucker]]** — noted in context of Fossil Creek population and Irving/Childs hydroelectric relicensing process.
- Candidate species proposed for listing consideration: [[Desert Sucker]], [[Gila Sucker]], [[Speckled Dace]], and [[Longfin Dace]].
- Habitat threats broadly discussed include livestock grazing, water diversion via the [[Central Arizona Project]] (CAP), competing sport-fish stocking programs (Arizona Game and Fish Department and USFWS), and socio-political pressure from the "Wise Use" movement affecting county-assisted recovery efforts.

## Key Findings

- The **Gila Topminnow recovery plan** revision was too preliminary for full discussion at the February meeting; a dedicated one-day follow-up meeting was proposed for spring 1993 (candidate dates: April 29, May 4, or May 11, 1993) to establish downlisting/delisting criteria, informed by geneticist **Joe Quattro's** new data.
- Multiple recovery plans were slated for status updates: [[Desert Pupfish]], [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]], and the [[Gila Topminnow]] plan revision.
- A **critical habitat reproposal** and **uplisting** action for [[Spikedace]] and [[Loach Minnow]] were on the agenda, alongside taxonomic recommendations regarding genus placement (*Rhinichthys* vs. *Tiaroga*).
- The Team discussed expanding its scope: whether **additional species** (e.g., [[Desert Sucker]], [[Gila Sucker]], [[Speckled Dace]], [[Longfin Dace]]) should be covered or added to the federal candidate list.
- Structural/organizational questions were raised regarding the Team's role in **Section 7 consultations**, recovery plan implementation, and whether **implementation subgroups** should be formed.
- Conservation conflicts highlighted include:
  - **Sport fish vs. native fish** management tension ("the Sequel").
  - Status of **CAP (Central Arizona Project) consultation**.
  - Consultations concerning **FWS and Arizona Game and Fish stocking programs**.
  - Political friction from **county-assisted recovery** efforts and the **Wise Use movement**.
- Site-specific monitoring updates were scheduled for **[[Aravaipa Creek]]** (including a fish barrier) and the **[[White River]]** Section 7 consultation.
- The **Fossil Creek** [[Razorback Sucker]] population was linked to the **Irving/Childs hydroelectric relicensing** process, an important regulatory intersection between hydropower and endangered species conservation.
- A **Biennial Report to Congress on Recovery** was scheduled for discussion, indicating formal reporting obligations under the Endangered Species Act framework.
