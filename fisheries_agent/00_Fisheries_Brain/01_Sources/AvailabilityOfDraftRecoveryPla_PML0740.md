# Federal Register Notices: Loach Minnow and Spikedace Recovery Plans, Federal Land Surveys and Alaska OCS Environmental Documents (October 1990)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This Federal Register compilation (Vol. 55, No. 202/207, October 1990) is a set of agency notices rather than a research paper. Its fish-related content concerns two federally listed threatened cyprinids, the [[Loach Minnow]] and the [[Spikedace]], whose draft [[Recovery Plan]]s were made available for public review under the [[Endangered Species Act]] by the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]]. The remaining notices cover a [[Bureau of Land Management]] resource management plan (RMP) for the Green River Resource Area, federal cadastral plat filings and resurveys in several western states, and [[Minerals Management Service]] environmental documents for oil and gas activity on the [[Alaska Outer Continental Shelf]].

## Background & Description

**Loach Minnow and Spikedace (Fish Species)**
- Both species are listed as threatened and have been extirpated from most of their historic range in the [[Gila River Basin]].
- The Loach Minnow is a bottom-dwelling species that inhabits turbulent runs over gravel and cobble bottoms in fast-flowing stretches.
- The Spikedace inhabits riffles and runs in shallow, swift water over gravel, cobble and sand bottoms. Adults use shear zones where fast water meets slow water.

**Legal Framework**
- Recovery plans describe actions needed for species conservation, set criteria for downlisting or delisting, and estimate time and cost for recovery measures.
- The [[Endangered Species Act]] (16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.), as amended in 1988, requires public notice and review during recovery plan development. The Service must consider all comments before approving each new or revised plan.

**Other Notices**
- Green River Resource Area RMP (Rock Springs District, Wyoming), with planning criteria available on request. The planning criteria are intended to make the process efficient and to set the scope of the [[Resource Management Plan]].
- Plats of survey filed in Colorado, Wyoming and Arizona, including surveys for the Bureau of Indian Affairs and the [[Forest Service]] in the Eleventh Guide Meridian and Sixth Principal Meridian areas.
- Environmental documents for Alaska OCS oil and gas proposals, prepared under the [[National Environmental Policy Act]].

## Ecological & Environmental Context

**Current Distribution**
- Loach Minnow: found only in the upper [[Gila River]], [[San Francisco River]] and [[Tularosa River]] (Grant and Catron counties, New Mexico), and in the White, San Francisco, Blue, Aravaipa, Dry Blue and Campbell Blue creeks of Arizona.
- Spikedace: found only in the upper Gila River (Grant and Catron counties, New Mexico), the upper [[Verde River]], and Aravaipa and Eagle creeks in Arizona.
- All existing populations of both species are considered threatened.

**Major Threats (Both Species)**
- Dams
- Water diversion
- Watershed deterioration
- Channelization
- Introduction of non-native predatory and competitive fishes

**Alaska Beaufort Sea Setting**
- The [[Beaufort Sea]] proposals involve shallow leases and a gravel island, with seasonal constraints tied to the [[Bowhead Whale]] migration.

## Key Findings

**1. Recovery Plan Objectives (Loach Minnow and Spikedace)**
- Protect existing populations of both species.
- Restore populations in parts of their historic habitat.
- Allow eventual downlisting, if possible, using defined recovery-progress standards.
- Planned actions include: protection, monitoring, enhancement and study of existing populations and habitats; study of interactions with non-native fishes; quantification of habitat modification effects; reintroduction into historic range; possible captive propagation; and information and education.
- Both plans have already undergone extensive review by federal, state and local agencies, species experts, businesses and conservation organizations. They will be finalized after comments received during this period are incorporated. Public comments were due by the date listed in the notice (OCR reads December 30, 1990).

**2. Green River Resource Area RMP**
- Planning criteria serve to ensure that planning is focused, that the public knows the scope of the planning effort, and that legal requirements are documented.
- Criteria are based on existing resource information and professional judgment, and may be modified based on public comment.

**3. Alaska OCS Environmental Documents (Summary of Two Proposals)**
- **[[Shell Western E&P Inc.]] (Tern A Artificial Island):** Proposal to permanently abandon and dismantle the gravel island in the Beaufort Sea, about 21 miles east of [[Prudhoe Bay]]. Abandonment involves plugging wells, removing surface hardware and gravel bags, and disposing of material at an approved solid-waste site. Holes will be filled and small hummocks created to improve habitat for bird nesting. Operations are planned with heavy equipment between mid-July and early September 1990. A [[Finding of No Significant Impact]] (FONSI) was dated July 11, 1990.
- **[[Chevron]] and [[Conoco]] (Canvasback Prospect):** Proposal to drill one or two wells per year, up to three wells total, on three leases from [[Lease Sale 87]] about 80 miles east of [[Point Barrow]], in 118 to 210 feet of water. The operator requested an exception to Stipulation No. 4, which bars exploratory drilling during bowhead whale migrations, to drill during the fall migration. Drilling would occur during the open-water season, starting as early as June 16.

**4. Survey and Land Records**
- Multiple plat filings and resurveys were executed to meet administrative needs of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management in Colorado, Wyoming and Arizona. These records have no direct ichthyological content.
