# Disposition of Species of Terrestrial Isopod Crustaceans of the Genera Synuropus, Spherarmadillo, Sphaeroniscus and Scleropactes

#isopoda #terrestrial-crustaceans #taxonomy #sphaeroniscidae

## Executive Summary

This taxonomic revision examines terrestrial isopod crustaceans in the family [[Sphaeroniscidae]], resolving the status of several genera originally described by Richardson. The author establishes that [[Synuropus]] is a junior synonym of [[Scleropactes]], transfers multiple species between genera based on morphological examination of type specimens, and corrects previous misidentifications. Key findings include the retention of [[Spherarmadillo]] as a valid genus distinguished primarily by lack of eyes, the transfer of two Colombian species from [[Sphaeroniscus]] to [[Scleropactes]], and the reassignment of one species to [[Armadilloniscus]]. The work is based on examination of specimens from Guatemala, Mexico, Puerto Rico, and Colombia housed at the [[United States National Museum]].

## Background & Description

The genus [[Spherarmadillo]] was established by Richardson (1907) based on *Spherarmadillo schwarzi* from Livingston, Guatemala. The type specimen is a female 18 mm long by 8.5 mm wide (not 50 x 22 mm as erroneously stated in the original description). Additional specimens from Trece Aquas, Guatemala (USNM 57379-80-81) include 11 females (longest 19 mm) and one male (14 mm). One female was gravid.

[[Spherarmadillo]] differs from [[Sphaeroniscus]] as defined by Vandel (1963) primarily in lacking eyes. The coxapodal ridge is not developed along the entire length of the underside of peraeonal segment I, but instead consists of a sulcus or groove on the posterior portion of the margin. An angular border (beveled edge) occurs on the anterior portion, with the notched portion on the posterior part. The maxilla 1 has two pencillate tufts of sensory setae. No pseudotracheae were found on either exopod of the male pleopods, a characteristic of family [[Sphaeroniscidae]].

Two additional blind species were described by Mulaik (1960) from Mexico: *S. huatuscensis* (7 mm long) and *S. cavernicola* (4 mm long, from cave). Specimens of *S. cavernicola* from Rancho de Cielo, Tamaulipas, Mexico (USNM 255216) were examined; none exceeded 4 mm in length. This species resembles *S. schwarzi* but differs in male pleopod configuration and lateral margin structure of peraeonal segment I (more of a groove or "step" rather than beveled/angular).

*Synuropus grandulatus* was originally described by Richardson (1901) from a single female 6.2 mm long from El Yunque, Puerto Rico at 2800 ft altitude. Examination of the type specimen (USNM 23912) revealed it belongs to [[Scleropactes]] rather than [[Oniscidae]]. The body is oval, unable to roll into a ball, with lateral parts of segments expanded and recurved. The dorsum is covered with minute tubercles more apparent anteriorly. The cephalon is deeply set into peraeonal segment I with rounded anterior angulations reaching beyond compound eyes. The flagellum of antenna 2 has three subequal articles. No marginal sulcus or coxal indications are present on segment I. The pleotelson has an obtusely rounded posterior border. The species shares characters with [[Scleropactes]], particularly the cephalon shape with a furrow above each eye and the uropod basis configuration.

Two species described by Pearse (1915) as [[Sphaeroniscus]] from Colombia were reexamined: *S. columbiensis* (USNM 98374, type 16 mm long from Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta at 4800 ft) and *S. gaigei* (USNM 98378, 4.4 mm long from San Lorenzo, Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta at 7800 ft). Both lack the particular ridge and notch on the lateral margin of peraeonal segment I characteristic of [[Sphaeroniscus]] as restricted by Vandel (1963). Both have a pronounced ridge on the cephalon just over the eye. The uropod basis is produced on the lateral posterior margin with the exopod emerging from a notch in the medial posterior margin.

*Scleropactes cedrosensis* described by Mulaik (1960) from a single specimen 1.7 mm long from Isla Cedros, Baja California, Mexico, was reassigned to [[Armadilloniscus]]. The cephalon illustration matches *A. lindahli*, and the maxilla 1 has a mass of "feathery" setae on the apex of the exopod characteristic of [[Armadilloniscus]]. The only discrepancy is the flagellar article count (three illustrated vs. four typical for the genus), likely an illustration error.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

[[Spherarmadillo schwarzi]] specimens were collected from Livingston and Trece Aquas, Guatemala. The genus [[Spherarmadillo]] now contains three species: two from Mexico and one from Guatemala, all blind terrestrial isopods.

*Scleropactes grandulatus* was collected at 2800 ft altitude on El Yunque, Puerto Rico. The original color was described as "brown, mottled with black" (now faded in type specimen).

*Scleropactes columbiensis* was found at 4800 ft in Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Colombia "under leaves and logs" in forest. *Scleropactes gaigei* was collected at 7800 ft on San Lorenzo, Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Colombia "under leaves in ground."

*Armadilloniscus lindahli* (=*Scleropactes cedrosensis*) extends the known range from mid-California to mid-Baja California. Species of [[Armadilloniscus]] are always found on or near marine beaches, suggesting the Isla Cedros specimen was collected on a beach.

## Key Findings

1. **Synonymy established**: [[Synuropus]] Richardson 1901 is a junior synonym of [[Scleropactes]] Budde-Lund.

2. **Genus [[Spherarmadillo]] retained**: Contains three valid species (*S. schwarzi*, *S. huatuscensis*, *S. cavernicola*), distinguished from [[Sphaeroniscus]] primarily by lack of eyes and differences in peraeonal segment I margin structure.

3. **New combinations for Puerto Rican species**: *Synuropus grandulatus* Richardson 1901 transferred to *Scleropactes grandulatus* (Richardson 1901) based on cephalon morphology, uropod configuration, and inability to roll into ball.

4. **New combinations for Colombian species**: *Sphaeroniscus columbiensis* Pearse 1915 and *Sphaeroniscus gaigei* Pearse 1915 transferred to [[Scleropactes]] as *Scleropactes columbiensis* (Pearse 1915) and *Scleropactes gaigei* (Pearse 1915) due to lack of characteristic ridge and notch on peraeonal segment I.

5. **Synonymy and generic transfer**: *Scleropactes cedrosensis* Mulaik 1960 is a junior synonym of *Armadilloniscus lindahli* (Richardson 1905), extending known range to mid-Baja California.

6. **Morphological characters**: Key diagnostic features include presence/absence of eyes, configuration of peraeonal segment I lateral margins (beveled, grooved, or stepped), cephalon ridges, uropod basis structure, and maxilla 1 setation patterns.

7. **Family placement**: All genera except [[Armadilloniscus]] belong to family [[Sphaeroniscidae]] as defined by Vandel (1964), characterized by absence of pseudotracheae on pleopod exopods.
