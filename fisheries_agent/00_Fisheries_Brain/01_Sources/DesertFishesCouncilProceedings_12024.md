# DFC Proceedings: A Proposal

#ichthyology #desert-fishes #organizational-management

## Executive Summary

This memorandum from Jack Williams to Paul Holden, W.L. Minckley, and Phil Pister proposes revisions to the [[Desert Fishes Council]] (DFC) Proceedings publication format and editorial structure. The proposal aims to reduce the burden on Phil Pister, improve timeliness, and enhance the professional stature of the Proceedings by establishing a four-member Editorial Committee and adding a peer-reviewed papers section.

## Background & Description

The [[Desert Fishes Council]] Proceedings had been largely a one-person operation managed by Phil Pister. The memorandum, dated December 3, 1990, acknowledges shortcomings in timeliness and the substantial workload placed on a single individual. The proposal seeks to modernize the publication while maintaining traditional elements.

The proposed Editorial Committee structure includes:
- DFC President
- DFC Past-President
- Two Co-Editors (volunteers from membership)

One Co-Editor position requires bilingual capability in Spanish and English. Co-Editors would serve staggered 4-year terms (with initial terms of 2 and 4 years to establish rotation), ensuring maximum turnover of 2 committee members in any given year.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The [[Desert Fishes Council]] serves as an organization focused on conservation and research of desert aquatic ecosystems and their fish fauna. The Proceedings publication serves as the primary outlet for disseminating agency reports, research findings, management updates, and organizational business related to desert fish conservation efforts across North America.

## Key Findings

The proposed new format for DFC Proceedings includes:

1. **Title Page** - Meeting time, location, volume number, publication date, Editorial Committee names

2. **Forward by Executive Secretary**

3. **Brief Communications Section**:
   - Agency Reports
   - Area Coordinator Reports (with question about continued necessity)
   - Research and Management Abstracts

4. **Research and Management Papers** - New peer-reviewed section for full-length papers from oral or poster presentations, reviewed by Editorial Committee (not external reviewers to maintain timeliness)

5. **Council Business Section**:
   - Minutes of Business Meeting
   - Budget Report
   - Resolutions
   - Award Announcements (Frances Hubbs Miller et al.)
   - Meeting Attendance List
   - Announcement of Next Symposium
   - Membership Recruitment and Proceedings Order Form

The proposal emphasizes maintaining consistency through staggered editorial terms while allowing periodic introduction of new perspectives. Implementation would begin with a mailing to DFC members requesting volunteers for Co-Editor positions.
