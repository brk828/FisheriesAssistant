# Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team Meeting Memorandum (1988)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service memorandum, dated November 2, 1988, announces an informal coordination meeting for the [[Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team]] and consultants. The meeting was to be held from 7 to 10 pm on November 17, 1988, on the sidelines of the [[Desert Fishes Council]] symposium in Death Valley. It was convened by John Hamill, Director of the [[Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program]], because Upper and Lower Basin entities rarely have chances to coordinate. The memo contains no field data, sample sizes, or measured results. Its substance is an agenda of recovery-planning issues for four Colorado River natives: [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Humpback Chub]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], and [[Razorback Sucker]].

## Background & Description

The memo is an administrative communication from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]], Region 6, sent from the Denver Federal Center in Colorado. It is addressed to the recovery team and consultants and copied to ARD-FWE (Regions 6 and 2). It refers to:

- Recovery of the Colorado River fishes since the team's last meeting.
- An August 10, 1988 letter to Jim St. Amant that outlined concerns about habitat fragmentation and the Bonytail Chub protocol.
- The Desert Fishes Council symposium, where the meeting was to be held.

The memo does not describe the species' morphology, life history, or population status.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The fishes named in the agenda are associated with the Colorado River system, which the memo divides into Upper and Lower basins. Two concerns are explicitly tied to habitat:

- **Habitat fragmentation** of [[Colorado Squawfish]] habitat, cited as a topic of concern.
- **[[Lake Mojave]]**, identified as a specific question in Lower Basin concerns about the Bonytail Chub and Humpback Chub recovery plans.

The memo's wording implies that recovery approaches may differ between the Upper and Lower Basins, but it does not give the reasons or any environmental measurements.

## Key Findings

The memo sets out the following agenda items for the November 17, 1988 meeting:

1. **Lower Basin concerns about the Bonytail Chub and Humpback Chub recovery plans**
   - The role of Lake Mojave.
   - Recovery in the Upper Basin versus the Lower Basin.
2. **Fragmentation of Colorado Squawfish habitat and the Bonytail Chub protocol**, as outlined in the August 10, 1988 letter.
3. **Development of a Razorback Sucker Management Plan.**
4. **Upper/Lower Basin coordination.**
5. **Other topics**, to be defined by attendees.

Logistics: attendees were asked to meet briefly at the end of the Wednesday afternoon session, in the conference room lobby, to finalize arrangements. Questions were directed to John Hamill at (303) 236-7398.

The memo reports no data, results, or conclusions. Its main outcome is the coordination of recovery planning across the Upper and Lower Basins.
