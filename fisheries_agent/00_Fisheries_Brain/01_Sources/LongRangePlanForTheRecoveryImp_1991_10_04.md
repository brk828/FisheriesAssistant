## Executive Summary

This document is an administrative memorandum from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Mountain-Prairie Region) transmitting a revised **Long Range Plan** for the **Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program**, targeting rare and endangered fish species of the Upper Colorado River Basin. The memo itself contains no primary morphological, ecological, or population data; rather, it describes the administrative and organizational restructuring of a multi-agency recovery planning process. Key programmatic outputs referenced include a prioritized task list, a "flow chart" format organizing goals and management activities, a glossary of terms, a species-by-subbasin task matrix, and an initial **Species Action Plan** developed specifically for the [[Razorback Sucker]] in the Colorado River subbasin. This note documents the structural and procedural content of the memo, as no direct fish science (morphology, diet, habitat parameters) is present in the extracted text.

## Key Findings

- **Task Prioritization:** At a Road Map Meeting held December 10–12, 1990, participants ranked 67 of 300 total recovery tasks as highest priority.
- **Consolidation:** These 67 high-priority tasks were consolidated into a single generic, all-species/all-rivers plan format applicable across the basin's endangered fish recovery effort.
- **Documentation Enhancements:**
  - Rationale statements were added to each objective and task explaining its importance and linkage to overall recovery goals.
  - Status statements were incorporated to track ongoing work.
  - A glossary was added to standardize terminology used throughout the plan.
  - A matrix was created cross-referencing species with the subbasins in which specific recovery tasks needed to be conducted.
- **Species Action Plan:** A dedicated Species Action Plan was completed only for the [[Razorback Sucker]] in the Colorado River subbasin; action plans for other endangered species were pending format/content review and approval as of this memo's date.
- **Institutional Stakeholders:** The plan involved coordination across numerous subcommittees and advisory bodies, including:
  - Nonnative and Sportfish Subcommittee
  - Habitat/Life History Subcommittee
  - Propagation/Genetics Subcommittee
  - Instream Flow Subcommittee
  - Water Acquisition Committee
  - Information and Education Committee
  - [[Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team]]
  - Biological Research Advisory Group
- **Revision History:** This 1991 version represents a significant revision from an earlier June 29, 1990 draft, based on feedback from the December 1990 Road Map Meeting and the January 1991 Biology Committee Meeting.
- **Feedback Loop:** Comments on the revised plan were solicited from Management and Biology Committees and other interested parties, with a response deadline of November 1, 1991, directed to Pat Nelson.
