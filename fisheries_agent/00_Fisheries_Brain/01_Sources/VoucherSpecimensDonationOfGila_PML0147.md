# Gila elegans Voucher Specimen Record (Adzona State University, 1997)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a custody and handling record for a set of 26 [[Gila Elegans]] (Bonytail) specimens deposited as [[Voucher Specimens]] in the ichthyology collection of the Department of Zoology at [[Adzona State University]]. The specimens were donated in January 1997 by [[Brian Bagley]] of the Center for Environmental Studies, ASU. The record traces their handling through repeated freezing and thawing, formalin preservation, tissue sampling, otolith removal, and transfer between a reservoir and a federal hatchery. The page scans are heavily degraded, so several dates and names are uncertain.

## Background & Description

- **Specimen count:** 26 *Gila elegans*, with data attached to the specimens.
- **Deposit:** Hand-carried to the Department of Zoology, ASU, and logged into the [[Collection Of Ichthyology]].
- **Date:** The day is partly illegible; the month and year read January 1997.
- **Donor:** [[Brian Bagley]], Center for Environmental Studies, ASU.
- **Preservation history:** Fish were frozen and thawed several times for a variety of studies, then preserved in [[Formalin Preservation]].
- **Sampling:** Tissue was removed from the right side of the fish (by [[Tom Dowling]], ASU). Otoliths were removed (by [[W.L. Minckley]], ASU).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Origin and movement:** The record does not give the complete history of the individuals. It states that they were placed in [[Lake Mohave]] by ASU, then transferred to a beach site (the scan reads as "Willow Beach"), and then to the [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]].
- **Hatchery role:** The hatchery was the handling point before the specimens reached the university, and [[Buddy Jensen]] of that hatchery is named as a researcher who worked on them.
- **Habitat:** The record identifies the species' setting only as Lake Mohave, a freshwater reservoir. It contains no water chemistry, habitat, or population data.

## Key Findings

- **Specimen identity:** 26 *Gila elegans* were deposited as voucher material at ASU in January 1997.
- **Custody chain:** Specimens moved from Lake Mohave (placed there by ASU) to a beach site, then to Dexter National Fish Hatchery, then to ASU.
- **Processing history:** Repeated freezing and thawing, followed by formalin preservation, which may limit some later analyses.
- **Specialist work:**
  - [[Tom Dowling]] (ASU, Genetics): tissue sampling from the right side.
  - [[W.L. Minckley]] (ASU, Otoliths): otolith removal.
  - [[Paul Mash]] (Center for Environmental Studies, ASU, Otoliths): listed as a second otolith researcher.
  - [[Buddy Jensen]] (Dexter National Fish Hatchery): listed as a researcher; the scan includes a "????" marker, so the role is uncertain.
- **Data gaps:** The receipt dates are illegible. Page 2 is OCR debris with no usable text, so no further data can be recovered from it.
