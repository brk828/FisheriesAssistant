# Aquatic and Semi-Aquatic Plant Collections from Southern Arizona Native Fish and Amphibian Sites (June 1980)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #aquatic-plants

## Executive Summary

This herbarium record documents aquatic and semi-aquatic plants collected at [[Native Fish Surveys|native fish and amphibian]] sites in southern Arizona between 6 and 13 June 1980. The collection was led by Winckley, Hendrickson, and colleagues and was made by the [[University of Arizona Herbarium]], College of Agriculture. The list covers 12 named sites plus an addendum on stock tanks and Cave Creek. Fish records are sparse: [[Agosia]] was collected at two sites and [[Gambusia]] was observed at one spring. The document is primarily a plant inventory, and its main value is the regional catalogue of aquatic flora tied to fish localities.

## Background & Description

The record is a field plant list organized by site. Each entry gives the county, locality name, USGS quadrangle, township and range or section where available, and collection date. Plants are grouped by habitat type: streambed vegetation, seep vegetation, cienega vegetation, pond and pool vegetation, and aquatic vegetation. Taxa are listed by family, with scientific names and authorities. The source is a scanned typed or handwritten list, and some names and authorities are partly illegible. The text is consistent with a specimen-based survey rather than a quantitative ecological study. It gives no abundance, cover, or sample-size data.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The sites are mostly desert-grassland and mountain-canyon wetlands in the Sonoran and Chihuahuan transition zone. They include:

- **Cienega Creek** (Pima County), where [[Agosia]] was collected on 6 June 1980.
- **Kuper Spring** (Pima County), a seep where [[Gambusia]] was observed.
- **[[San Pedro River]]** at Route 90 (Cochise County), where Agosia was collected on 7 June 1980.
- **Peters Spring cienega** (Cochise County), where no fish or amphibians were collected.
- **[[Leslie Creek Canyon]]**, Swisshelm Mountains, and **[[Rucker Canyon]]** and **[[Turkey Creek Canyon]]**, Chiricahua Mountains (8 and 9 June 1980).
- **[[San Simon Cienega]]** (9 June 1980), with saline-tolerant taxa such as [[Atriplex Gentilis|Atriplex]] and [[Tamarix Pentandra|Tamarix]].
- **San Bernardino Ranch** (including Black Draw; 10 to 11 June 1980), a major wetland complex with the most species-rich list.
- **Bear Creek Canyon** (Huachuca Mountains), **[[Sheehy and Sharpe Springs]]** (San Rafael Valley, Santa Cruz County), and **[[Monkey Spring]]** north of Patagonia. Collection dates are not clear for the first two.

Habitat types range from flowing streams and spring runs to cienegas, pools, and artificial stock tanks. Several taxa recur across sites, indicating shared hydrologic and climatic conditions.

## Key Findings

- **Fish records:** [[Agosia]] was collected at Cienega Creek (6 June 1980) and the San Pedro River (7 June 1980). [[Gambusia]] was observed at Kuper Spring. No fish or amphibians were collected at Peters Spring.
- **Widespread taxa:** [[Mimulus Guttatus]] was recorded at most sites. Other common taxa include [[Rorippa Nasturtium-Aquaticum]], [[Agrostis Semiverticillata]], [[Juncus Saximontanus]], [[Lemna Minor]], and [[Polypogon Monspeliensis]].
- **Dominant emergent and submerged plants:** [[Typha Domingensis]] was recorded at San Simon Cienega, San Bernardino Ranch, and other sites. [[Najas Marina]] and [[Potamogeton Pusillus]] were found in stock tanks and along drainages.
- **Notable taxa:** [[Anemopsis Californica]] and [[Myriophyllum Brasiliense]] were recorded at several localities. [[Chara]] sp. (a macroalga) was present at many sites and in most stock tanks.
- **Stock tank addendum:** Most tanks held [[Potamogeton Pusillus]] and some held [[Najas Marina]]. The tank along the road between Leslie Creek and Rucker Canyon was especially notable. [[Callitriche Heterophylla]] was recorded at Cave Creek.
- **Taxonomic notes:** Several determinations are marked with "cf." or "sp." (for example, *Panicum* cf. *bulbosum*, *Juncus* cf. *interior*, and *Myosurus* sp.). Some authorities are partly illegible in the scan.
- **Research value:** The list provides a baseline for aquatic flora at fish and amphibian localities in southeastern Arizona in 1980. It can be compared with later surveys to assess change in these wetlands.
