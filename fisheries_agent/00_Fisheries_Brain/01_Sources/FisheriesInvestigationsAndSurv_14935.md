# Fish Harvest Regulations - Texas Region 3-B Segment Completion Report

#fisheries-management #regulations #public-policy

## Executive Summary

This segment completion report documents proposed fisheries regulations presented at public hearings across seven Texas counties (Newton, Polk, Jasper, Tyler, Trinity, Houston, and Harrison) during 1964-1965. The primary regulatory change prohibited netting in [[Dam B Reservoir]] except through State rough fish contracts. All proposed regulations were approved by the Texas Parks and Wildlife Commission and enacted by proclamation following public hearings with minimal opposition.

## Background & Description

The report covers Federal Aid Project F-3-R-12, Job B-17 (Segment 5), conducted from February 1, 1964 to January 31, 1965 under the Federal Aid in Fisheries Restoration Act. The project area encompassed Inland Fisheries Region 3-B, which includes counties in two regulatory areas: the Northeast Texas Area (Harrison County only) and the Southeast Texas Area (Polk, Newton, Jasper, Tyler, Trinity, and Houston Counties).

The regulatory process involved preliminary meetings between Parks and Wildlife Department field biologists, wardens, and supervisory personnel to discuss proposed changes, followed by submission through proper channels for approval, public hearings, and final Commission approval.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The regulations focused on two major water bodies: the [[Neches River]] in Tyler and Jasper Counties and the [[Angelina River]] in Jasper County below [[Sam Rayburn Dam]]. The [[Dam B Reservoir]] area specifically included waters from the Jasper-Angelina County line to the Tyler-Jasper-Hardin County line on the Neches River, and waters of the Angelina River in Jasper County below Sam Rayburn Dam.

The prohibition on netting (except through State rough fish contracts) suggests management concerns about commercial fishing impacts on these reservoir and river systems, though specific ecological justifications are not detailed in this administrative document.

## Key Findings

**Northeast Texas Area (Harrison County):**
- Added regulation prohibiting trotline placement near boat docks, piers, bathing beaches, or swimming areas
- Deleted requirement that only non-metallic devices be used for taking fish
- Public hearing held June 8, 1964 with 4 Texas Parks and Wildlife (TPW) staff and 5 sportsmen attending (9 total)
- No opposition recorded

**Southeast Texas Area (Six Counties):**
- Major change: Prohibited use of nets for taking fish from Neches River waters (Tyler and Jasper Counties, from Jasper-Angelina County line to Tyler-Jasper-Hardin County line) and Angelina River waters (Jasper County below Sam Rayburn Dam)
- Exception allowed for State rough fish contracts
- Public hearings held June 9-22, 1964 across six counties
- Total attendance: 6 TPW staff and 94 sportsmen across all hearings (100 total)
- Attendance by county: Polk (31 total), Newton (13 total), Jasper (18 total), Tyler (33 total), Trinity (6 total, 0 sportsmen), Houston (29 total)
- No opposition recorded at any hearing

**Administrative Outcome:**
- All proposed regulations approved by Parks and Wildlife Commission
- Regulations enacted into law by proclamation
- Recommendation made to continue this job for future regulatory hearings and changes
