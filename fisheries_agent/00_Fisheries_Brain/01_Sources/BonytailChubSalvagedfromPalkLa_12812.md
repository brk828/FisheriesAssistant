# Bonytail Chub Transfer from Bubbling Ponds Fish Hatchery to Arizona State University Research Park

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #Cyprinidae #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This memorandum documents the transfer of 18 [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*) from [[Bubbling Ponds Fish Hatchery]] to [[Arizona State University Research Park]] on July 24, 1992. The fish were originally salvaged from [[Palm Lake]] at the [[Hassayampa River Preserve]] near Wickenburg, Arizona in April 1992. All fish were PIT-tagged for identification, transported under controlled conditions, and successfully acclimated and released into a pond at the research facility. The transfer was necessitated when the hatchery could no longer hold the fish.

## Background & Description

Twenty-two [[Bonytail Chub]] were salvaged from [[Palm Lake]] at the [[Hassayampa River Preserve]] during April 20-24, 1992. Two fish were retained at the preserve in a lobby aquarium for educational purposes; these formed a breeding pair that produced approximately 100 larval bonytail. The remaining 20 fish were transferred to [[Bubbling Ponds Fish Hatchery]] for holding. Two fish subsequently died at the hatchery, leaving 18 individuals for transfer.

The transferred fish ranged in size from 260 mm to 495 mm in length and from 140 g to 834 g in weight. Two fish were previously tagged; 16 additional fish were PIT-tagged at the time of loading. Two males were noted as ripe during the tagging process.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

[[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*) is an endangered cyprinid native to the [[Colorado River]] basin. The species requires careful management and monitoring, with PIT tagging enabling individual identification for long-term population studies. The transfer involved careful temperature acclimation to prevent thermal shock, with transport tank temperature at 22°C at loading, rising to 25°C upon arrival, and pond shoreline temperature at 32°C requiring gradual equalization to 28°C before release.

## Key Findings

**Transfer Protocol and Success:**
- 18 [[Bonytail Chub]] successfully transferred from [[Bubbling Ponds Fish Hatchery]] to [[Arizona State University Research Park]] on July 24, 1992
- 16 fish newly tagged with PIT tags; 2 fish already carried tags from previous operations
- Transport conducted by Bruce Denova and Dave Weedman using borrowed PIT tag reader and tags from Rob Clarkson's [[Colorado River]] research crew

**Fish Measurements:**
- Length range: 260-495 mm
- Weight range: 140-834 g
- Two ripe males identified during tagging (PIT tags TF7D173F32 and 7TF7D171809)

**Transport Conditions:**
- Initial temperature (hatchery and transport tank): 22°C
- Transport tank temperature upon arrival: 25°C
- Pond shoreline temperature: 32°C
- Final acclimation temperature: 28°C
- Constant air flow maintained via compressed tank and mechanical agitators
- Tank not crowded during transport

**Release Outcome:**
- Fish released by dirt boat ramp at ASU Research Park pond
- No signs of discomfort observed during release
- Fish swam to deep water normally
- Post-release observation yielded no signs of distressed fish
- Stocking time: 4:30 PM
