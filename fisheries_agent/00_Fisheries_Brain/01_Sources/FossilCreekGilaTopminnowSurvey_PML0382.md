# Fossil Creek Gila Topminnow Post-Stocking Survey (January 3, 2008)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #poeciliidae

## Executive Summary

On January 3, 2008, staff of the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] conducted a one-month post-stocking visual and dip net survey for recently stocked [[Gila Topminnow]] in [[Fossil Creek]]. About 3,000 Gila Topminnow had been stocked at five sites on November 28, 2007. No topminnow were captured or observed. Evidence of high stream flow at all sites suggests the fish were dispersed downstream or had settled in unsurveyed areas. The survey found only four other fish species, in very small numbers. The department plans to restock Gila Topminnow and [[Desert Pupfish]] after spring snowmelt in 2008 and to monitor and augment both species over time.

## Background & Description

- **Target species:** [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*), a small live-bearing fish in the family Poeciliidae.
- **Survey team:** Jesse Bahm and Codey Carter, Research Branch, [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]. Report authored by Codey Carter, February 2008.
- **Survey method:** Visual and [[Dip Net Survey]] sampling at the five stocking sites and nearby slack-water habitat (Figure 1).
- **Governing protocol:** The survey followed the [[Post-Stocking Monitoring Protocol]] for Gila Topminnow and Desert Pupfish. The protocol calls for:
  - Visual/dip net surveys at one month and six months after stocking.
  - A more intensive one-year repeat survey.
  - Annual surveys thereafter.
- **Stocking event:** About 3,000 Gila Topminnow released into five sites in Fossil Creek on November 28, 2007.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Stream conditions:** Evidence of high stream flow was observed at all survey sites on January 3, 2008. This likely dispersed recently stocked fish away from release points.
- **Site features noted in the document:**
  - Site 2 lies above the [[Diversion Dam]].
  - Site 4 lies near the [[Irving Power Plant]].
- **Habitat types surveyed:** Main stream reaches at stocking sites and adjacent slack-water habitat, which may hold fish during high flow.
- **Native and co-occurring fishes:** Four non-target species were captured, indicating a fish community that includes [[Longfin Dace]], [[Speckled Dace]], and [[Desert Sucker]] alongside the target species.

## Key Findings

1. **No target fish detected:** No [[Gila Topminnow]] were captured or observed during the one-month post-stocking survey.
2. **Limited incidental catch:** Only five fish were captured in total, across two sites:
   - **Site 2 (above the Diversion Dam):** one [[Longfin Dace]] (*Agosia chrysogaster*) and one [[Speckled Dace]] (*Rhinichthys osculus*).
   - **Site 4 (near the Irving Power Plant):** one [[Speckled Dace]] and one [[Desert Sucker]] (*Catostomus clarki*).
   - No other fish species were captured.
3. **Likely cause of non-detection:** High stream flow probably carried stocked fish downstream from the release sites. Some topminnow may have settled in other stream sections and remained undetected.
4. **Management response:** The department plans to:
   - Restock additional [[Gila Topminnow]] after spring snowmelt in 2008.
   - Stock [[Desert Pupfish]] at the same time.
   - Monitor and augment both species over time, following the protocol schedule.
5. **Survey limitations:** The report does not give catch-effort data, sampling area, or survey duration, so detection probability cannot be estimated from the document.
