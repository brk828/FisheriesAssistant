# A Creel Census of Lakes in District IV

## Executive Summary
This Arizona Game and Fish Department completion report (Project F-7-R-1) documents a creel census conducted on two District IV lakes: **[[Carl Pleasant Lake]]**, a warm-water reservoir near Phoenix, and **[[Riggs Flat Lake]]**, a newly created 12-acre trout lake atop Mount Graham. The study aimed to quantify angler harvest and evaluate experimental management strategies. At Pleasant Lake, a novel methodology combining manned creel checks with traffic counters was employed to estimate total fishing pressure across two access roads. Findings revealed that despite the lake's reputation as a **[[Largemouth Bass]]** fishery, seasonal **[[White Crappie]]** angling dominated the harvest. At Riggs Flat, a short-term check evaluated the return rate of stocked legal-sized **[[Rainbow Trout]]**, informing recommendations for future stocking schedules.

## Background & Description
Limited morphological data was collected, restricted to mean total lengths of harvested individuals at Carl Pleasant Lake:
- **[[White Crappie]]**: Average length of **9.0 inches**.
- **[[Largemouth Bass]]**: Average length of **12.1 inches**.

No comparative morphological data was provided for [[Bluegill]], [[Common Carp]], [[Channel Catfish]], [[Bullhead]], [[Green Sunfish]], or [[Rainbow Trout]] beyond catch statistics.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Carl Pleasant Lake**: A warm-water reservoir supporting a mixed fish community including [[Largemouth Bass]], [[White Crappie]], [[Bluegill]], [[Common Carp]], [[Channel Catfish]], [[Bullhead]], and [[Green Sunfish]]. The presence of abundant **threadfin shad** forage was noted, prompting a recommendation to introduce **[[White Bass]]** as a new predator species to better utilize this forage base and potentially increase overall game fish production. The lake's fishery exhibits strong seasonality, with crappie fishing being most productive in fall and spring.
- **Riggs Flat Lake**: A small (12-acre), high-elevation, put-and-take cold-water fishery located atop Mount Graham, managed exclusively via periodic stocking of legal-sized [[Rainbow Trout]] rather than natural reproduction.
- **Angler Behavior/Human Ecology**: At Pleasant Lake, fishing pressure was dominated by residents of Maricopa County due to proximity to Phoenix (~30 miles). Anglers were roughly split between shore fishing (54.1%) and boat fishing (39.2%), with "still fishing" being the most common method and trolling the least popular.

## Key Findings

### Carl Pleasant Lake (Aug–Dec 1958)
- **Sampling Effort**: 818 anglers checked; 4,204.75 angler-hours logged; 1,162 fish creeled.
- **Average Trip Duration**: 5.14 hours per angler (higher than typical Arizona warm-water lakes, attributed to poor road access discouraging repeat short trips).
- **Success Rate**: Only 30.8% of anglers successfully caught fish.
- **Catch Composition**:
  | Species | % of Total Catch |
  |---|---|
  | Crappie | 57.1% |
  | Bluegill | 12.0% |
  | Largemouth Bass | 9.4% |
  | Channel Catfish | 2.1% |
  | Bullhead | 1.9% |
  | Green Sunfish | 0.5% |
  | Carp (implied remainder of 28.8% with Bluegill) | — |
- **Catch Rate**: Crappie catch-per-hour consistently exceeded bass catch-per-hour throughout the study period (see original Figure 2).
- **Expanded Population Estimates** (via traffic counter extrapolation): Estimated 4,632 total fishermen, 23,809 total angler-hours, and 6,667 total fish removed, with [[White Crappie]] contributing over 3,807 individuals to this total.
- **Demographics**: Maricopa County residents constituted the primary source of angling pressure due to geographic proximity.

### Riggs Flat Lake
- **Stocking Event**: 1,750 legal-sized rainbow trout planted.
- **Sampling Effort**: 361 fishermen checked over the first six days lake was open.
- **Harvest**: 538 trout harvested (1.48 fish/angler).
- **Return Rate**: 30.7% of stocked trout were recovered by anglers within six days, indicating a rapid depletion rate for this small water body.

### Management Recommendations
1. **Pleasant Lake**: Introduce **[[White Bass]]** to exploit abundant threadfin shad forage and diversify/increase game fish production.
2. **Pleasant Lake**: Investigate improving road access via cooperation between sportsmen's groups, county funding, and department resources to increase angler utilization and reduce trip-length bias.
3. **Pleasant Lake**: Continue creel census through 1961 to specifically evaluate the impact of white bass introduction on bass and crappie fisheries.
4. **Riggs Flat**: Continue stocking program with 2,000 trout planted monthly (4 events/year, May–August) for an annual total of 8,000 fish, based on the high observed depletion rate.
