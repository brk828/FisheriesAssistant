## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary scientific paper but a **cooperative agreement summary letter** outlining a collaborative research program between Donald G. Buth (University of California, Department of Biology) and federal fisheries personnel (referenced as "Holt Williamson"). The agreement, tied to Cooperative Agreement 0-Fe-40-09610, establishes a plan for **population genetics research on the [[Razorback Sucker]]** (*Xyrauchen texanus*), an endangered catostomid fish native to the [[Colorado River Basin]]. The work centers on protein/allozyme electrophoretic screening to assess genetic variation across multiple wild and hatchery populations. No direct morphological, ecological, or population-abundance data are presented; the text instead documents logistical and methodological commitments for a genetic survey.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
The [[Razorback Sucker]] is implicitly identified as a species of concern inhabiting a fragmented network of large-river and reservoir habitats within the [[Colorado River Basin]], including:

- **Green River** — Jensen spawning area; Echo Park (confluence of Green and Yampa Rivers)
- **Lake Powell** — San Juan River inflow area; upper end of lake at Colorado River inflow
- **Colorado River mainstem** — near Grand Junction
- **Dexter National Fish Hatchery** — captive/existing broodstock population
- **Lake Mojave**
- **Lake Mead** — lower end of Grand Canyon

This distribution reflects the species' historic reliance on **large, turbid, warm-water riverine systems** with seasonal flow variation, as well as its persistence in **impounded reservoir habitats** following river regulation. The inclusion of spawning-area sites (e.g., Jensen, Echo Park, San Juan inflow) indicates active reproductive habitat use, while hatchery broodstock (Dexter NFH) represents an ex-situ conservation component supporting the species amid habitat loss and fragmentation — consistent with its status as an [[Endangered Species]] in the Colorado River system.

## Key Findings
- **Item 1 – Preliminary Protein Screening:** Agreement to obtain 52 [[Razorback Sucker]] specimens from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] for non-invasive protein screening (including blood sampling). Sample logistics: 40 frozen and 12 live specimens, transported promptly to allow analysis prior to the spring field collection season.
- **Item 2 – Comparative Phase I Work:** Results from a parallel non-invasive research contract (conducted separately) will be compared against Phase I findings to inform the design of a subsequent **full electrophoretic genetic survey**.
- **Item 3 – Collection Site Design:** Up to 30 fish from **8 possible sites** across the Colorado River Basin will be sampled for **complete allozyme analysis**, spanning wild riverine populations (Green River, Colorado River mainstem), reservoir populations (Lake Powell, Lake Mojave, Lake Mead), and a hatchery broodstock source (Dexter NFH).
- **Tissue Protocol:** Standardized tissue types (muscle, fin clips, blood) were designated for extraction across all sites to ensure methodological consistency in the genetic survey.
- **Research Framing:** The overall project represents an early-stage **conservation genetics initiative** aimed at characterizing genetic diversity and population structure of the [[Razorback Sucker]] across its remaining range, likely to inform recovery planning under endangered species management frameworks.
