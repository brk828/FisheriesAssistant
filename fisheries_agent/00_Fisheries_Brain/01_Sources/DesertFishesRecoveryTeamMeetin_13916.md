# Desert Fishes Recovery Team Meeting Minutes December 1985

#ichthyology #conservation #endangered-species #cyprinidae #catostomidae

## Executive Summary

Meeting minutes from the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] (DFRT) held December 10-11, 1985 at [[Arizona State University]]. Team Leader W.L. Minckley convened representatives from USBR, USBLM, AzG&FD, USFWS, NMG&F, and USFS to establish recovery planning priorities for listed southwestern fishes. The team committed to drafting a [[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]] within 6 months, reviewing the [[Chihuahua Chub]] recovery plan by January 1986, and initiating a [[Sonoran Chub]] recovery plan following listing. Administrative procedures were clarified, with team members serving as species advocates rather than agency representatives. Priority species identified include [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Razorback Sucker]], [[Colorado Squawfish]], and [[Desert Pupfish]].

## Background & Description

The DFRT was established to draft, review, and revise recovery plans for listed southwestern fishes under the Endangered Species Act. Team composition included representatives from multiple federal and state agencies: USBR (Tom Burke), USBLM (Kniffe Hamilton), AzG&FD (Terry Johnson), USFWS (Bill Kepner), NMG&F (David Propst), and USFS (Jerry Stefferud), with W.L. Minckley (ASU) serving as Team Leader. Jerry Burton (USFWS Region 2) provided administrative oversight and clarified that recovery team members serve as species advocates dedicated to southwestern ichthyofauna recovery, not as agency representatives. All correspondence flows to the Regional Director, USFWS Region 2.

The team addressed potential conflicts of interest regarding recovery team involvement versus contractual agreements for listing, recovery plan preparation, and monitoring studies. Burton assured that precedent exists allowing team members to participate in contracted studies without conflict.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The meeting focused on southwestern desert fishes inhabiting extreme environments including the [[Colorado River]] basin, [[Yaqui River]] system, and isolated desert streams and springs. These species face critical endangerment due to habitat loss, water diversion, and introduction of non-native species. The [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]] (SBNWR) serves as a critical refugium for [[Yaqui Fishes]], including [[Yaqui Chub]], [[Beautiful Shiner]], and [[Yaqui Catfish]].

Discussion of [[Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge]] (BANWR) identified 5-6 ponds of several acres each as potential habitat for native listed fishes. Four management options were considered: maintaining ponds for native unlisted amphibian fauna; establishing refugia for [[Desert Pupfish]], [[Yaqui Topminnow]], and [[Roundtail Chub]]; creating grow-out areas for [[Bonytail Chub]]; or implementing combinations of these approaches.

The team emphasized using the closest genetic entity for any reintroduction options to avoid taxonomic conflicts, particularly relevant for [[Notropis formosus]] and related taxa.

## Key Findings

**Priority Recovery Planning Actions:**

1. **[[Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan]]**: Multi-species plan to be drafted within 6 months (by June 1986) covering [[Yaqui Chub]], [[Beautiful Shiner]], and [[Yaqui Catfish]]. Plan will utilize data from SBNWR Master Plan and work toward recovery and maintenance of localized fauna. [[Yaqui Topminnow]] excluded due to existing plan, though team will review that plan prior to 1989 revision date. Minckley volunteered to prepare draft for team review.

2. **[[Chihuahua Chub Recovery Plan]]**: DFRT committed to review Agency Review Draft within 6 weeks. Comments from DFRT members due January 15, 1986; team comments to USFWS Region 2 due January 31, 1986.

3. **[[Sonoran Chub Recovery Plan]]**: Work to begin following listing and completion of Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan. Tentative target date for draft plan: December 1986. Chuck Kennedy (USFS) requested as team consultant.

4. **Big River Fishes Review**: Team to review existing plans for [[Bonytail Chub]], [[Humpback Chub]], and [[Colorado Squawfish]] relative to lower basin recovery actions. [[Bonytail Chub]] identified as highest priority species due to current endangerment status and extinction risk. Additional discussion addressed listing and recovery of [[Razorback Sucker]].

5. **[[Desert Pupfish]]**: DFRT requested USFWS Region 2 assume lead responsibility for desert pupfish recovery following listing. Team considers best recovery opportunity within Region 2, especially considering previous AzG&FD and BLM efforts. Linda Ulmer (CDFG) requested as Technical Consultant for pupfish recovery and reintroduction of [[Colorado Squawfish]], [[Bonytail Chub]], and [[Razorback Sucker]] into lower [[Colorado River]].

**Technical Consultants Designated**: Jim Brooks (USFWS), Ben Robertson (USFWS), Jerry Landye (AzG&FD), Linda Ulmer (CDFG), and Chuck Kennedy (USFS) named as Technical Consultants to DFRT.

**Future Candidate Species**: Team members directed to familiarize themselves with [[Gila intermedia]], [[Gila robusta grahami]], [[Catostomus discobolus yarrowi]], and [[Campostoma ornatum]] as potential future ESA listing candidates.

**Status Report**: Minckley completing status report for Native Fish Fauna of Arizona and New Mexico, with copies to be distributed to DFRT members following December 1986 completion.

**Future Meetings**: 
- April 4-6, 1986 in Douglas, including tour of [[San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge]]
- Summer (June) meeting proposed to discuss [[Sonoran Chub Recovery Plan]] with field trip to [[Sycamore Canyon]]

Meeting adjourned 10:40 a.m., December 11, 1985.
