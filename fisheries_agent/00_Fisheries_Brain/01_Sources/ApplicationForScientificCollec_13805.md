## Executive Summary

This document is **not a primary scientific research paper** but an **administrative permit application and annual report package** submitted by Dr. W.L. Minckley (Professor of Zoology, Arizona State University) to the Arizona Game and Fish Department (AGFD) for a **Scientific Collecting Permit** covering calendar year 1993, along with the associated 1992 annual report. The materials document Minckley's authorization to collect aquatic wildlife (including [[Fish|fish]]), herpetofauna, birds, and mammals — with particular emphasis on **threatened and endangered native aquatic species** in Arizona — for research and conservation management purposes. The permit is linked to a **federal permit (PRT-676811)** issued by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service for work on federally listed endangered species, indicating this research program operated under dual state/federal regulatory oversight. No morphological, taxonomic, or population data on specific fish species are present in this document; its scientific value lies in documenting the **regulatory and institutional framework** supporting Minckley's long-running research program on [[Southwestern US Native Fishes]] conservation.
