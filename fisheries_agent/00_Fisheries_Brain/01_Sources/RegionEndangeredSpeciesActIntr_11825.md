# Region 2 Endangered Species Act Intra-Service Section 7 Consultation Memorandum

#administrative #endangered-species-act #policy

## Executive Summary

This December 26, 1984 memorandum from the Regional Director of [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Region 2 (Southeast) updates internal procedures for [[Endangered Species Act]] (ESA) Section 7 consultations. Effective immediately, Ecological Services field offices will conduct intra-Service consultations instead of the Regional Endangered Species Office, though the Regional Director continues to sign all consultations. The memo outlines responsibilities across [[Arizona]], [[New Mexico]], [[Oklahoma]], and [[Texas]], emphasizing protection requirements for listed, proposed, and Category 1 candidate species.

## Background & Description

The memorandum references the February 24, 1978 intra-Service Section 7 consultation memorandum by Regional Director W.O. Nelson that established internal ESA responsibilities. Section 7 of the ESA has been amended three times since initial implementation, strengthening protections for endangered and threatened species by requiring consideration in early project planning and affording protection to proposed species and their critical habitat.

The document establishes that [[Recovery Plans]] and Regional Resource Plans serve as major sources of proposed conservation measures. The consultation process applies to all Federal actions that may affect listed species or their habitats, whether impacts are positive or negative.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The policy covers Region 2's jurisdiction spanning multiple southwestern states with diverse ecosystems. The consultation framework addresses:

- **Listed species**: Full Section 7 protection against jeopardy and adverse modification of critical habitat
- **Proposed species**: Informal consultation required for any impacts
- **Category 1 candidate species**: Included in intra-Service consultations despite not being legally required, as the Service pursues recommending them as proposed species

The memo emphasizes that no Federal agency may authorize, fund, or carry out actions that may jeopardize listed species or adversely modify their critical habitat without receiving an exemption. Once formal consultation is initiated, irreversible or irretrievable commitment of resources is precluded.

## Key Findings

**Administrative Structure Changes:**
- Ecological Services field offices now conduct consultations (previously Regional Endangered Species Office)
- Regional Director continues to sign all intra-Service consultations
- Five field offices established with designated supervisors and contact information

**Key Section 7 Requirements:**
1. No Federal actions may jeopardize listed species or adversely modify critical habitat without exemption
2. Formal consultation required if Federal action may affect listed species or habitats
3. Resource commitment restrictions during consultation process to preserve modification alternatives
4. Informal consultation required for proposed and Category 1 candidate species
5. Program initiating proposed action bears costs of obtaining adequate information

**Procedural Requirements:**
- Consultation initiators continue using intra-Service Section 7 evaluation form (R2-SE-1, 3/78)
- Ecological Services field supervisors serve as primary consultation contacts
- Regional Office of Endangered Species maintains oversight (505-766-3972)
- Formal consultations initiated by field supervisors must be completed by Regional Director
