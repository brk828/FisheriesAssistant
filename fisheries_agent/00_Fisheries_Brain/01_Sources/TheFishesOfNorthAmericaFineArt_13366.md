# The Fishes of North America: Joseph R. Tomelleri Print Catalog

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae #centrarchidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a sales catalog and promotional brochure for scientific fish illustrations by [[Joseph R Tomelleri]], a Kansas City artist regarded by many ichthyologists as a leading scientific illustrator of fishes. It advertises signed, numbered prints of North American fishes (copyright 1991) sold through the [[Gallery of Fishes]] in Tuscaloosa, Alabama. Its main content is a product list: open edition and limited edition prints, the announced [[North American Fishes Folio]] print series, and a biography of the artist. It contains no original research, population data, or ecological measurements.

## Background & Description

The catalog presents [[Fishes of North America]] as a collection of original art and prints. Tomelleri's work is described as scientific illustration: specimens are drawn with accurate life colors, scale and fin ray counts, diagnostic details, and fully spread fins, which the catalog states cannot be achieved photographically. Most collected specimens were preserved and deposited in museums.

Methods described in the source:
- **Specimen collection:** The artist reportedly traveled more than 60,000 miles to collect live specimens for illustration vouchers.
- **Medium:** Drawings are executed in [[Berol Prismacolor]] and Verithin pencils on 6-ply, 100% cotton rag museum board, highlighted with white acrylic paint.
- **Output:** The artist has produced 260+ drawings for dozens of publications and 163 color illustrations for the book [[Fishes of the Central United States]], co-authored with Mark Eberle and published by the University Press of Kansas.

Print format and sales terms:
- **Open edition (OE) prints:** Signed by the artist, printed on archival, acid-free paper.
- **Limited edition (LE) prints:** Signed and numbered, edition size 995 plus 25 artist's proofs, printed on archival Dulcet paper.
- **Folio prints:** Each print is limited to 600 signed and numbered copies plus 60 artist's proofs reserved as matched sets for subscribers. Each measures 8x10 inches and includes date and locality of collection and scientific and common names.
- **Series release:** A new series is announced roughly every six months. Prices are not stated.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not address habitat, distribution, population status, water chemistry, or other ecological conditions. Some print titles refer to localities or waterbodies, including [[Volcano Creek]] (Golden Trout), the [[Colorado River]] (Cutthroat Trout), the [[McCloud River]] (Rainbow Trout), the [[Kern River]] (Rainbow Trout), the [[North Umpqua River]] (Summer Steelhead), and [[Willow Creek]] (Cutthroat Trout, Oregon). These appear only as print labels. The catalog's references to [[Conservation Status]] are limited to the artist's collaborations with state wildlife agencies and the U.S. Forest Service, and the "Desert Fishes" series includes the [[Razorback Sucker]], which is a federally listed species in the United States.

## Key Findings

- **Product scope:** The catalog lists about 40 prints across open and limited editions, mostly salmonids (trouts and salmon), with sunfishes, darters, minnows, bass, catfish, walleye, and pike also represented.
- **Folio series:** The North American Fishes Folio was released in series:
  - Series I: Sunfishes (Summer 1996), including Bluegill, Redbreast Sunfish, Green Sunfish, Longear Sunfish, and Northern Rock Bass.
  - Series III: Minnows (Winter 1996–1997), including Bluenose Shiner.
  - Series IV: Desert Fishes (Summer 1997), including Razorback Sucker.
- **Planned series:** Killifishes, Trout, Darters, Big River Fishes, Gulf of Mexico fishes, and Catfishes.
- **Edition sizes:** Folio prints are limited to 600 numbered copies with 60 artist's proofs. Limited edition prints have a size of 995 with 25 artist's proofs.
- **Artist credentials:** Tomelleri is described as an award-winning natural history artist and "the finest scientific illustrator of fishes in the world" by many ichthyologists. This is promotional language, not an independent evaluation.
- **Reviews:** Endorsements are quoted from Ralph Cutter (Sierra Trout Guide), Doug Stange (In-Fisherman), Donald Hoffman (Kansas City Star), and Louis Bernatchez (Université de Montpellier II, France).
- **Publication credits:** The work has appeared in outlets including The In-Fisherman, Field & Stream, Sports Afield, Southern Outdoors, and B.A.S.S., and in state agency publications from Nebraska, South Dakota, Arkansas, and Missouri.
- **Purpose:** The document is a commercial marketing piece. Its claims about scientific accuracy are the producer's own and are not supported by data in the text.
