# Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants: Five-Year Review Completion for 1978 Listed Species

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #endangered-species-act #regulatory-review #Percidae #Salmonidae

## Executive Summary

This Federal Register notice documents the completion of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's mandatory five-year review of species listed as endangered or threatened in 1978 under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973. The review examined 39 species total, including three fish species: [[Leopard Darter]] (*Percina pantherina*), [[Greenback Cutthroat Trout]] (*Salmo clarki stomias*), and [[Little Kern Golden Trout]] (*Salmo aquabonita whitei*). After evaluating all available data, the Service found no substantial evidence warranting status changes for any reviewed species. The document also announces initiation of five-year reviews for species listed before 1976 and during 1979-1980, soliciting public comments through November 19, 1985.

## Background & Description

The [[Endangered Species Act]] requires the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] to conduct reviews of all listed species at least once every five years to ensure listing accuracy reflects current species status. On December 8, 1983 (48 FR 55102), the Service requested comments and data on species listed in 1978, with an April 6, 1984 deadline. The Service actively solicited input from recovery teams, state conservation agencies, concerned individuals, and other stakeholders, while also reviewing internal files and consulting Service biologists.

The 1978 review covered 39 species across multiple taxonomic groups. Fish species under review included:
- **[[Leopard Darter]]** (*Percina pantherina*) - Family [[Percidae]]
- **[[Greenback Cutthroat Trout]]** (*Salmo clarki stomias*) - Family [[Salmonidae]]
- **[[Little Kern Golden Trout]]** (*Salmo aquabonita whitei*) - Family [[Salmonidae]]

Additional aquatic taxa reviewed included seven snail species (gastropods) and one crustacean, the [[Socorro Isopod]] (*Thermosphaeroma thermophilus*).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The review process evaluates species status based on five factors specified in §424.11(c) of the Act:
1. Present or threatened destruction, modification, or curtailment of habitat or range
2. Overutilization for commercial, recreational, scientific, or educational purposes
3. Disease or predation
4. Inadequacy of existing regulatory mechanisms
5. Other natural or manmade factors affecting continued existence

Species may be removed from the lists only if data demonstrate they are neither endangered nor threatened due to: (1) extinction, (2) recovery, or (3) original data error. The Service emphasizes that recovery—returning species to a point where Act protection is no longer required—represents the principal conservation goal.

This represents the first published summary of five-year review results. Previous reviews (for species listed in 1976, 1977, etc.) resulted in some reclassifications or delistings, demonstrating the process can lead to status changes when warranted by scientific evidence.

## Key Findings

**Primary Conclusion**: The Service found no substantial data suggesting status changes for any of the 39 species listed in 1978, including the three fish species. All available data support maintaining present endangered or threatened classifications.

**Review Methodology**: The Service examined:
- Comments received in response to the December 1983 Federal Register notice
- Input from recovery teams and state agencies
- Internal Service files and biologist assessments
- Scientific and commercial data available as of the review date

**Future Actions**: While no immediate status changes are proposed, the Service notes that data may become available before the next scheduled five-year review that could warrant proposing reclassification or delisting for one or more species. The Service continuously reviews listed species status and initiates changes whenever appropriate data emerge.

**Concurrent Review Announcement**: The notice simultaneously announces five-year reviews for:
- Species listed before 1976
- Species listed in 1979
- Species listed in 1980

Comments on these additional species were requested by November 19, 1985, directed to appropriate regional offices.

**Regulatory Context**: This review fulfills requirements under 16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq. and 50 CFR 424.21. The Service will continue accepting data on listed species at any time, maintaining ongoing assessment capability beyond formal five-year review cycles.

**Transparency Initiative**: Publication of review summaries represents a new practice to better inform the public of review outcomes, enhancing transparency in the endangered species program.
