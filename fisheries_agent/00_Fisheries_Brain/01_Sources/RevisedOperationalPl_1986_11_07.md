## Executive Summary
This document is not a primary scientific research paper but an **interagency regulatory memorandum** from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2, Albuquerque) to the Bureau of Land Management (BLM), Phoenix, Arizona, dated November 7, 1986. It addresses a "Revised Operational Plan" for reintroducing two federally significant desert fish species — the [[Gila Topminnow]] and the [[Desert Pupfish]] — onto lands administered by the BLM's Safford District. The memo focuses on administrative and regulatory classification issues rather than biological or morphological data, specifically concerning whether introduced populations should be designated as **"nonessential experimental populations"** under the Endangered Species Act (ESA).
