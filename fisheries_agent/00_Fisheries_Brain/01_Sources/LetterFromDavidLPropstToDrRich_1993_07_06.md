## Executive Summary
This document is a formal letter dated July 6, 1993, from David L. Propst (New Mexico Department of Game & Fish) to Dr. Richard L. Mayden (Curator of Fishes, University of Alabama), addressing a taxonomic controversy surrounding the [[Loach Minnow]], previously classified as *Tiaroga cobitis* and reclassified as *[[Rhinichthys cobitis]]* following work by Woodman, Coburn, and Cavender. The letter does not present new morphological, ecological, or population data; rather, it serves as a clarification of professional record regarding the misattribution of Propst's personal opinion as formal scientific "evidence" opposing the taxonomic revision. The correspondence highlights procedural and ethical concerns within the scientific community regarding how taxonomic disputes are documented and cited in official communications to regulatory bodies such as the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS).

## Background & Description
No morphological data, meristic counts, or diagnostic taxonomic characters for *Rhinichthys cobitis* (syn. *Tiaroga cobitis*) are provided in this source. The letter is strictly correspondence regarding the validity of the taxonomic reclassification process rather than the biological basis for the genus-level change (i.e., the shift from the monotypic genus [[Tiaroga]] to [[Rhinichthys]]). Readers seeking morphological justification for this reclassification should consult the original taxonomic work referenced by Woodman, Coburn, and Cavender.

## Key Findings
- **Taxonomic Dispute**: The core issue concerns the reclassification of *Tiaroga cobitis* to *[[Rhinichthys cobitis]]*, a change proposed by researchers Woodman, Coburn, and Cavender.
- **Misattribution of Evidence**: David L. Propst clarifies that his personal, informally expressed opinion questioning the taxonomic change was inappropriately cited as formal "evidence" in correspondence sent to Denise Baker (USFWS, Phoenix) without his knowledge or consent.
- **Scope of Recovery Team Authority**: A notable procedural point was raised at the February 1993 Desert Fishes Recovery Team meeting — whether the Team should recommend that USFWS reject the taxonomic change based on reservations expressed by Propst and W.L. Minckley.
- **Minckley's Position**: According to Propst's recollection, [[W.L. Minckley]] asserted that taxonomic determinations fall outside the purview of the Recovery Team's recommendations to USFWS — a position Propst concurred with.
- **No Formal Data Presented**: Propst explicitly states he "did not personally gather data to propose retention of *Tiaroga*," clarifying that his stance was one of professional caution/reservation, not a data-driven rebuttal.
- **Institutional Implications**: This letter underscores the importance of formal peer-reviewed channels for resolving taxonomic questions, as opposed to using informally expressed opinions in official regulatory correspondence.
