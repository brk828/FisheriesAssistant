## Executive Summary
This document is **not a primary scientific data source** but rather an administrative/organizational letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]], dated May 16, 1977. Authored by Chairman James E. Deacon and addressed to W. L. Minckley of Arizona State University, the letter proposes a re-evaluation of the Council's **Area Coordinator system**, which was established in 1974 to generate status reports on fish populations within the [[Great Basin]] region. The letter requests renewed commitment from Area Coordinators to produce annual written reports — either on specific endangered species or on the broader fish fauna of their designated areas — to support a proposed Council newsletter and inform future conservation actions.
