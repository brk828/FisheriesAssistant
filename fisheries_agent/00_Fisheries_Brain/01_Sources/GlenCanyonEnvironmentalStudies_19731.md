# Lake Powell HYDROLAB Profile Distribution (August 1994)

#limnology #water-quality #reservoir

## Executive Summary

This Bureau of Reclamation memorandum from the [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]] group transmits [[HYDROLAB Profiling]] data collected at [[Lake Powell]] during the August 1 through 6, 1994 field trip. The profiles cover water temperature, [[Dissolved Oxygen]], [[Specific Conductivity]], and [[pH]] at 20 sites. The memo also announces planned distribution of a later (December 1994) set of profiles and lists the recipients, which are mainly federal and state agency staff and university researchers. No fish or invertebrate data are included.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Internal memorandum (administrative correspondence with enclosures), not a research report.
- **Issuing office:** [[Bureau of Reclamation]], Upper Colorado Region, [[Glen Canyon Environmental Studies]], Flagstaff, Arizona.
- **Author:** Dave Wegner, Acting Group Manager, Technical Service Center, Environmental Resources Services.
- **Subject:** Distribution of HYDROLAB profiles from December 1994.
- **Data collected:** August 1 through 6, 1994, Glen Canyon Environmental Studies trip on Lake Powell.

- **Penstock elevation at collection:** Stated in the memo as 59 meters below the surface of Lake Powell.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Study area:** [[Lake Powell]] reservoir, with sampling in the main channel, side canyons, bays, marinas, and river inflow areas.
- **Sampled inflow areas (as shown on the site map):** [[Colorado River Inflow Area]], [[Escalante Inflow Area]], [[San Juan River Inflow Area]].
- **Named sites shown on the site map and graphs:** [[Bullfrog Bay]], [[Good Hope Bay]], [[Lake Canyon]], [[Willow Creek]], [[Dans Gulch]], [[Moki Canyon]], [[Iceberg Canyon]], [[Labyrinth Canyon]], [[Face Canyon]], [[Dark Canyon]], [[Hite Basin]], [[Cha Canyon]], [[Navajo Canyon]], [[Upper Piute Bay]], [[Lower Piute Bay]], [[Midzahn Bay]], [[Crossing of the Fathers]], [[Wahweap Marina]], [[Bullfrog Marina]].
- **Graph site codes (as legible in the enclosures):** SCUCLP0790 (Iceberg Canyon), SCCR0980 (Lake Canyon), SCUCLP17 (Bullfrog Bay), SCCR0180 (Labyrinth Canyon), SCCR0210 (Face Canyon), SCCR1500 (Colorado River Inflow/Dark Canyon), SCUCLP18 (Hite Basin), SCUCLP15 (Cha Canyon), SCNVC050 (Navajo Canyon).
- **Measured parameters:** [[Water Temperature]], [[Dissolved Oxygen]], [[Specific Conductivity]], [[pH]], and turbidity (turbidity appears on several graphs).
- **Vertical profiling:** Depth profiles (depth in metres, roughly 0 to 100 m on the graphs) are plotted against the parameters above.

## Key Findings

- HYDROLAB profiles were collected at 20 sites on [[Lake Powell]] from August 1 through 6, 1994.
- Parameters recorded: temperature, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, and pH.
- The memo states a penstock elevation of 59 meters below the lake surface at the time of collection.
- The enclosures are one set of 10 graphs and a site map, which are not legible in the text, so the numeric values and profile shapes cannot be reported here.
- The memo announces distribution of December 1994 profiles on January 5, 1995, and provides a contact for questions.
- Distribution is to a broad group of federal, state, park, and academic recipients.
- **Data note:** The subject line refers to "December 1994" profiles, while the body describes August 1994 collections. The graphs are labeled August 1994. This likely reflects the distribution date rather than the sampling date.
