## Ecological & Environmental Context
* **Historic vs. Current Range:** The species has experienced a severe contraction in its distribution. It currently occupies approximately **5% of its known historic range**.
* **Current Distribution:** The species is now restricted to the [[Rio Grande]], specifically the reach from **Cochiti Dam** downstream to the headwaters of **Elephant Butte Reservoir**, New Mexico.
* **Habitat Type:** Riverine, freshwater fluvial specialist dependent on flowing water conditions within the Rio Grande channel.
* **Primary Threats (Anthropogenic):**
    * **Habitat Loss:** Caused by dewatering, channelization, and river flow regulation for irrigation purposes.
    * **Water Quality Degradation:** Resulting from municipal, industrial, and agricultural discharge.
    * **Biotic Interactions:** Competition and predation from **nonnative introduced fish species**.

## Key Findings
* **Regulatory Status:** The Fish and Wildlife Service proposed listing the [[Rio Grande Silvery Minnow]] as **Endangered** with **Critical Habitat** (Proposed Rule published March 1, 1993; 58 FR 11821).
* **Comment Period Extension:** The original comment deadline of April 30, 1993, was extended to **August 25, 1993**, to allow for further public and scientific input.
* **Public Hearings:** Two public hearings were scheduled to gather testimony:
    * June 2, 1993 — Indian Pueblo Cultural Center, Albuquerque, NM.
    * June 3, 1993 — Macy Center, New Mexico Technical Institute, Socorro, NM.
* **Range Contraction Metric:** The reduction to 5% of historic range serves as the primary quantitative indicator supporting the endangered status proposal, highlighting the severity of population decline linked to river flow modification (damming/dewatering).
* **Legal Basis:** The action falls under the authority of the [[Endangered Species Act]] (16 U.S.C. 1531-1544).
