# List of Current Fisheries and Wildlife Journals

#ichthyology #marine-biology #wildlife-management #library-science #bibliographic-resources

## Executive Summary

This document is a comprehensive catalog of fisheries and wildlife journals held by the [[William Jasper Kerr Library]] at [[Oregon State University]] as of December 1979. Compiled by Mary J. Lewis, Zoology/Oceanography Librarian, it lists current subscriptions and selected discontinued titles from the previous five years. The catalog includes over 400 serial publications covering ichthyology, marine biology, wildlife management, aquaculture, limnology, oceanography, and related fields. Entries are organized alphabetically by Library of Congress form with call numbers, actual titles, and beginning dates for the OSU collection.

## Background & Description

The catalog represents a specialized research library collection supporting fisheries and wildlife sciences. Publications originate from diverse sources including government agencies (federal, state, provincial, international), research institutions, professional societies, and commercial publishers across multiple countries. Geographic coverage spans North America (United States, Canada, Mexico), Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia, and New Zealand.

The collection encompasses multiple publication types: peer-reviewed journals (e.g., [[Journal of Fish Biology]], [[Copeia]], [[Transactions of the American Fisheries Society]]), government technical reports and bulletins, statistical series, conference proceedings, and extension publications. Subject areas include systematic ichthyology, fisheries management, aquaculture, marine ecology, freshwater biology, wildlife biology, ornithology, mammalogy, toxicology, and water quality.

Major institutional contributors include [[Canada Fisheries Research Board]], [[United States National Marine Fisheries Service]], [[Food and Agriculture Organization]] (FAO), state fish and game departments, and international commissions (e.g., [[International Pacific Halibut Commission]], [[International Commission for the Northwest Atlantic Fisheries]]).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The catalog reflects the breadth of aquatic and terrestrial ecosystem research during the 1970s. Marine and freshwater fisheries publications document commercial and recreational harvest statistics, stock assessments, and management strategies. Aquaculture journals track the emerging development of fish farming for species including catfish, salmon, and trout.

Environmental monitoring is evident through journals covering water pollution ([[Water Pollution Control Federation Journal]]), pesticide impacts ([[Pesticide Monitoring Journal]], [[Residue Reviews]]), and fish disease surveillance ([[Journal of Fish Diseases]], [[Fish Health News]]). Limnological and oceanographic titles address freshwater and marine ecosystem dynamics, while wildlife journals document terrestrial species management including big game, waterfowl, and upland birds.

Regional focus is particularly strong for Pacific Northwest fisheries (Oregon, Washington, British Columbia, Alaska) and Great Lakes systems, reflecting OSU's geographic location and research priorities. International coverage includes tropical fisheries (Southeast Asia, Africa), European marine fisheries, and Antarctic/Southern Ocean resources.

## Key Findings

The catalog documents 400+ serial titles with the following characteristics:

**Geographic Distribution**: North American publications dominate (approximately 60%), with substantial representation from Canada (provincial and federal agencies), European countries (UK, Ireland, Scandinavia, Germany), and international organizations. Pacific Rim coverage includes Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and Philippines.

**Subject Coverage**: Fisheries and ichthyology journals comprise approximately 40% of titles, wildlife and terrestrial ecology 30%, general biology and ecology 20%, and specialized topics (toxicology, aquaculture, limnology) 10%.

**Government Publications**: Federal, state, and provincial agencies contribute extensive technical report series, statistical bulletins, and management reports. Examples include Alaska Department of Fish and Game (multiple series), Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife (12+ series), and Canadian regional fisheries services.

**Professional Societies**: Major organizations represented include [[American Fisheries Society]] (Transactions, Special Publications, Monographs), [[Wildlife Society]] (Wildlife Monographs, Bulletin), and regional groups (Western Association of State Game and Fish Commissioners, Southeastern Association of Game and Fish Commissioners).

**International Commissions**: Specialized bodies managing transboundary resources maintain regular publication series, including Pacific salmon commissions, tuna commissions, and Atlantic fisheries organizations.

**Indexing and Abstracting Services**: The collection includes major indexing tools: [[Aquatic Science and Fisheries Abstracts]], [[Biological Abstracts]], [[Sport Fishery Abstracts]], [[Zoological Record]], and [[Chemical Abstracts]].

**Collection Depth**: Beginning dates range from 19th century (some titles from 1880s-1890s) to contemporary 1970s publications, indicating both historical depth and current awareness. The library maintains complete runs for core titles while selectively retaining recent years for others.

**Format Notes**: The librarian indicates retention policies for certain categories: "latest edition only" for regulatory publications, "latest two years" for newsletters, and complete runs for research journals. Call number prefixes (Q, R, S, T, SK, SH) indicate floor locations within the library system.
