## Executive Summary
This document is a February 20, 1996 interoffice correspondence (fax memo) from the Arizona Game and Fish Department (Glen Canyon Environmental Studies, GCES) to Dr. Paul Marsh at the [[Arizona State University]] Center for Environmental Studies. The memo accompanies a small fish specimen preserved in formalin, collected via boat electrofishing in the [[Lees Ferry]] reach of the [[Colorado River]] on December 6, 1995. The collector, Bill Persons (GCES Coordinator), tentatively field-identified the specimen as a [[Redside Shiner]] (*Richardsonius balteatus*) and requested taxonomic verification from Dr. Marsh, offering the specimen for archival at the university museum if desired. This is a species-occurrence/voucher-verification record rather than a primary research study.
