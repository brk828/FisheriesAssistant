# Bog Hole Renovation Proposal Review - Arizona Game and Fish Department

#ichthyology #conservation-biology #cyprinodontidae #poeciliidae #cyprinidae #endangered-species

## Executive Summary

This document is a formal review letter from the [[Arizona Game And Fish Department]] (AGFD) to the [[Coronado National Forest]] regarding the Bog Hole Renovation Proposal dated July 30, 1992. The letter provides detailed comments on a proposed fish toxicant renovation project at [[Bog Hole]], a 9-acre pond near the headwaters of the [[Santa Cruz River]] in Santa Cruz County, Arizona. The renovation aims to remove non-native fish species and restore habitat for endangered native fishes including [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*), [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*), and [[Gila Chub]] (*Gila intermedia*). The review was conducted by personnel from AGFD's Nongame, Fisheries, and Habitat branches, and Region 5.

## Background & Description

[[Bog Hole]] is a 9-acre pond located at T23S, R17E, Sec. 32 in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, fed by surface waters near the headwaters of the [[Santa Cruz River]]. The site lies within the Bog Hole Wildlife Area, managed cooperatively by the [[U.S. Forest Service]] (USFS) and AGFD under a cooperative agreement. The proposed renovation involves using fish toxicant (initially proposed as Antimycin, though final toxicant selection had not been determined by the working group) to eliminate non-native fish species and restore native fish populations.

The target native species for restoration include three federally listed or sensitive fishes: [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius*), [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*), and [[Gila Chub]] (*Gila intermedia*). The proposal also considers impacts on other native aquatic species including [[Huachuca Tiger Salamander]] (*Ambystoma mavortium stebbinsi*), [[Mexican Garter Snake]] (*Thamnophis eques*), [[Lowland Leopard Frog]] (*Lithobates yavapaiensis*), and [[Chiricahua Leopard Frog]] (*Lithobates chiricahuensis*).

[[Gila Topminnow]] populations are also documented in nearby [[Red Rock]] (tributary of the Sonoita River), Sharp Spring, and probably Sheehy Spring (tributaries of the Santa Cruz River), providing regional context for the species' distribution.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The renovation project addresses habitat management for multiple endangered and threatened aquatic species in the [[Santa Cruz River]] watershed. The [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] (DFRT) had repeatedly recommended similar restoration actions during meetings in 1987, 1988, 1989, and 1992, indicating consistent scientific support for native fish habitat restoration in this region.

The project operates within a complex regulatory and management framework, requiring consistency with multiple planning documents and legal mandates including: the [[Coronado National Forest]] Land Management Plan, the AGFD-USFS Cooperative Agreement, a 1987 Environmental Assessment Checklist, the AGFD Bog Hole Wildlife Area Management Plan, the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973, and recovery plans for the [[Sonoran Topminnow]] and [[Desert Pupfish]].

Environmental considerations identified in the review include potential effects of water drawdown on riparian vegetation and the need to document that the project would have no negative effects on waterfowl populations. The review emphasizes that [[Bog Hole]] was never designated as a fishing spot, clarifying the site's intended management purpose.

## Key Findings

**Technical and Editorial Recommendations:**
- Scientific names should be provided for all species mentioned (Desert Pupfish, Gila Topminnow, Gila Chub, Huachuca Tiger Salamander, Mexican Garter Snake, Lowland Leopard Frog, Chiricahua Leopard Frog)
- Final toxicant selection (Antimycin or alternative) had not been determined by the working group at time of review
- Document should reference consistent recommendations from [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] meetings across multiple years (1987-1992)

**Interagency Coordination Requirements:**
- [[Arizona Department Of Environmental Quality]] should be included as part of the interdisciplinary team
- Additional stakeholder groups recommended for mailing list: [[The Nature Conservancy]], Tucson Herpetological Society, Del Norte Bass Fishing Club, and Tucson Audubon Society

**Additional Environmental Considerations:**
- Possible effects of water drawdown on riparian vegetation should be included as a project issue
- Project expected to have no negative effect on waterfowl populations
- Public comment process should emphasize written submissions over telephone calls to provide complete and accurate record

**Geographic and Taxonomic Corrections:**
- Quitobaquito pupfish spelling correction needed
- Updated distribution information for [[Gila Topminnow]] to include Red Rock, Sharp Spring, and Sheehy Spring locations

The AGFD review found the Bog Hole Renovation Proposal well-structured and reflecting main issues on which the agencies had previously concurred, with the detailed comments provided to strengthen the final document.
