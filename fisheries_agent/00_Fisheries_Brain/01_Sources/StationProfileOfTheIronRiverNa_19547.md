# Station Profile of the Iron River National Fish Hatchery

## Executive Summary
The **Iron River National Fish Hatchery** (Iron River, Wisconsin; USFWS Region 3) was established in 1979 under the Appropriation Act of 1977 to support the [[Great Lakes Lake Trout Rehabilitation]] effort. It functions as the primary broodstock facility for [[Lake Trout]] (*Salvelinus namaycush*) on the upper Great Lakes, producing fingerlings and yearlings for stocking into [[Lake Superior]] and [[Lake Michigan]]. In FY1991, the station distributed over 1.56 million yearling lake trout (5.5 inches average size) and shipped an additional 647,796 fish to other federal hatcheries, while also beginning first-time spawning of broodstock transferred from the [[Charlevoix National Fish Hatchery]] (Michigan) in FY1990, yielding 259,300 eggs incorporated into the yearling program.

## Background & Description
- **Species reared:** Lake Trout (*Salvelinus namaycush*), family [[Salmonidae]]
- **Stocked size class (FY1991):** Yearlings averaging **5.5 inches** in length
- **Life stages produced:** Eggs → fry → fingerlings → yearlings (fall fingerling program eliminated in FY1991 due to inter-hatchery fish transfers)
- **Egg production (FY1991):** 259,300 eggs taken from newly spawned broodstock

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Primary role:** Broodstock and rearing facility supporting restoration of a native top-level [[Coldwater Fish]] predator in the Great Lakes ecosystem
- **Target habitats for stocking:**
  - [[Lake Superior]] (Wisconsin waters)
  - [[Lake Michigan]] (Michigan waters)
- **Broodstock origin:** Relocated from [[Charlevoix National Fish Hatchery]], Michigan, in FY1990; first spawning event occurred at Iron River in FY1991
- **Ecosystem significance:** Central component of the multi-agency [[Great Lakes Lake Trout Rehabilitation]] program, aiming to reestablish self-sustaining lake trout populations following historic declines (attributed to overfishing and sea lamprey predation)

## Key Findings
- **Mission:** Established specifically to support Great Lakes lake trout rehabilitation and serve as the major broodstock facility for the upper Great Lakes.
- **FY1991 Distribution:** 1,563,074 yearling lake trout (5.5 in.) stocked into Lake Superior and Lake Michigan; total distribution (including inter-facility transfers) reached 2,216,970 fish.
- **Inter-Facility Support:** 647,796 fish shipped to other federal lake trout hatcheries to complete their yearling rearing programs, resulting in elimination of the station's own fall fingerling program for that cycle.
- **Broodstock Development:** First successful spawning of broodstock transferred from Charlevoix, MI occurred in FY1991, producing 259,300 eggs.
- **Physical Infrastructure:**
  - 8 broodstock raceways
  - 24 production raceways
  - Office/hatchery building
  - Influent and effluent monitoring buildings
  - Air support structure over broodstock raceways
  - 3 hatchery residences
