# New Species of Cheumatopsyche from the Southeastern United States

#entomology #freshwater-ecology #Hydropsychidae #Trichoptera #caddisfly #taxonomy

## Executive Summary

This taxonomic paper describes two new species of caddisflies in the genus [[Cheumatopsyche]] (family [[Hydropsychidae]]) from the southeastern United States: *Cheumatopsyche enigma* and *Cheumatopsyche petersi*. The species were collected during limnological studies in [[Georgia]], [[South Carolina]], and [[Florida]]. *C. enigma* exhibits unusual morphological variability in male genitalia, possibly indicating a hybrid zone between formerly isolated populations. *C. petersi* is distinguished from the closely related *C. analis* by differences in clasper structure and tenth tergite morphology. These additions demonstrate the diversity of the [[Cheumatopsyche]] genus in southeastern caddisfly fauna.

## Background & Description

### Cheumatopsyche enigma

**Male morphology**: Length 6 mm. Dark brown coloration on head, body, and appendages; antennae, mouthparts, and legs with pale areas; forewings without light spots. Ninth segment annular with paired lateral lobes bearing long setae. Tenth tergite elongate with small lateral setose lobes before apex; apex divided into prominent dorsally projecting lobes that are large, broad, shouldered at base, and truncate at apex in posterior view. Clasper with long narrow basal segment; apical segment narrow, ⅔ length of basal segment. Aedeagus with large bulbous base and paired movable plate-like processes at apex.

**Female morphology**: Length and coloration as in male. Ninth segment with fairly large clasper receptacle; ventral lip extends to midpoint of tenth segment; groove to receptacle sharply delineated antero-ventrally with margin sometimes projecting slightly over groove.

**Variability**: Exhibits extreme variability in apical lobes of male tenth tergite. Some specimens nearly truncate (resembling *C. gracilis*), others with rounded apex (resembling *C. aphanta*), and others with pointed lateral dorsal angles (resembling *C. gyra*). Tenth tergite may have prominent mound-like projections near middle of segment. Variability occurs within single collection localities ([[Wolf Creek]] and [[Wildcat Creek]]), with character states varying randomly and independently.

**Holotype**: [[Georgia]], Union County, 3.5 mi. N [[Neels Gap]], [[Wolf Creek]], 30 August 1945, P. W. Fattig.

**Paratypes**: 47 males, 2 females from [[Georgia]] (Wolf Creek, creek near Dawsonville) and [[South Carolina]] (Wildcat Creek at Clemson, Eastatoe Creek, South Saluda River at Table Rock), collected April-October 1944-1969.

### Cheumatopsyche petersi

**Male morphology**: Length 7 mm. Dark brown head, body, and legs; antennae and mouthparts brown but paler at base. Wings teneral, apparently dark brown with few small light areas. Ninth segment annular with paired lateral lobes bearing setae. Tenth tergite moderately short, sloping evenly from ninth tergite junction, with paired small cushion-like lateral lobes near apex; apex divided into dorsally-projecting lobes that are relatively small and rounded dorsally in lateral view, almost truncate dorsally in posterior view with shouldered dorso-lateral portion. Clasper with elongate apical segment (¾ length of basal segment), slightly recurved laterally, broad at base tapering to narrow apex posterio-ventrally; basal segment unusually narrow in posterio-ventral aspect. Aedeagus with relatively small bulbous base and paired movable plate-like processes at apex.

**Female morphology**: Length, color, and general structure as in male. Clasper receptacle moderately large, extending ventrally to midpoint of tenth segment in lateral view; ventral lip forms almost right angle; inner opening minute and just below apex. In dorsal view, clasper receptacle appears as small ovate projection with inner opening near middle.

**Holotype**: [[Florida]], Okaloosa County, [[Blackwater River]], 4.5 mi. NW Cannon Town, 7 April 1968, W. L. Peters et al.

**Paratypes**: 231 males, 4 females from [[Florida]] (Okaloosa County: Blackwater River at multiple locations; Santa Rosa County), collected April-June 1968-1970.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The specimens were collected during limnological studies in freshwater streams and rivers of the southeastern United States. Collection sites included:

- **Georgia**: [[Wolf Creek]] (Union County), small creek near Dawsonville
- **South Carolina**: [[Wildcat Creek]] at Clemson (Pickens County), [[Eastatoe Creek]] (Pickens County), [[South Saluda River]] at Table Rock
- **Florida**: [[Blackwater River]] system (Okaloosa and Santa Rosa Counties) at multiple bridge locations

Collections spanned April through October, with most specimens collected in spring months (April-June). The genus [[Cheumatopsyche]] forms a diverse and abundant element of the southeastern caddisfly fauna.

## Key Findings

1. **Two new species described**: *Cheumatopsyche enigma* and *Cheumatopsyche petersi* add to the known diversity of [[Hydropsychidae]] in the southeastern United States.

2. **Extreme morphological variability in C. enigma**: The species exhibits unusual variation in male tenth tergite apical lobes, with specimens from single localities showing intergrading series between morphological extremes. Character states vary randomly with respect to each other.

3. **Hybrid zone hypothesis**: The variability pattern in *C. enigma* is "highly suggestive of a situation in which two formerly isolated populations evolved morphological differences but no genetic incompatibility, then dispersed into each others ranges, the result being a highly variable hybrid mixture of character states." This represents an unusual phenomenon not previously documented in the genus.

4. **Diagnostic characters for C. enigma**: Distinguished from congeners by elongate male tenth tergite; differs from *C. gracilis* in slender basal clasper segment, from *C. aphanta* in shorter apical clasper segment and longer tenth tergite, and from *C. gyra* in more obliquely angled and contiguous lateral lobes.

5. **Diagnostic characters for C. petersi**: Close relative of *C. analis* but differs in stouter apical clasper segment and shouldered apical lobes of tenth tergite that do not project dorsally to level of mesal tergite part. Female differs in more perpendicular clasper receptacle with broad angular ventral lip.

6. **Geographic distribution**: *C. enigma* found in [[Georgia]] and [[South Carolina]]; *C. petersi* found in [[Florida]] (Okaloosa and Santa Rosa Counties), particularly associated with [[Blackwater River]] system.

7. **Type specimens deposited**: All type specimens deposited in University of Georgia insect collection.
