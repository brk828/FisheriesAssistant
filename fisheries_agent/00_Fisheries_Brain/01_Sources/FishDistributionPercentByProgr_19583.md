# FY 1992 Fish and Egg Distribution by Station, Program, and Agency

#ichthyology #marine-biology #fish-hatchery

## Executive Summary

This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) Fisheries report, dated 03/30/94, tabulates [[Fish Distribution]] for fiscal year 1992 by hatchery station, species, [[Program Code]], and [[Agency Code]]. For each station it gives egg numbers, fish numbers and weight, and the percentage each program received. The report covers roughly 74 distinct hatchery stations (the Warm Springs name appears twice). Report totals are 82,000,949 eggs, 208,896,734 fish, and 6,510,962 weight units (the unit is not stated in the OCR text). Most stations distribute the bulk of their output under the program coded RM, and the largest single allocations are concentrated at a few large facilities. Questions go to Roger Schulz, [[Division Of Fish Hatcheries]], at (703) 358-1715.

## Background & Description

The document is a set of data-entry instructions and a station-level summary table, not a research paper. It has three parts:

- **Coding key (Page 2):** Defines agency codes (e.g., [[Fish And Wildlife Service]], [[Bureau Of Reclamation]], [[Corps Of Engineers]], [[Forest Service]], [[Tennessee Valley Authority]], [[State Government]]), water-type codes, facility designators, program codes, and broodstock codes. Fish and egg data use the distribution codes; broodstock data use a separate set of program codes.
- **Station summary (Pages 3–11):** One block per station listing program codes with egg numbers, fish numbers and weight, and the percentage of eggs, fish, and weight attributed to each program.
- **Totals:** A single report total at the end.

Water-type codes used in the key are [[Aquarium]], [[Estuarine Waters]], [[Federal Hatchery]], [[Other Hatchery]], [[Lake Or Pond]], [[Reservoir]], [[Stream Or River Or Canal]], [[Research]], and [[Tailwater]].

Program codes defined in the legible text include [[Restoration And Mitigation]], [[Endangered And Threatened Species]], [[Management Assistance]], [[Federal And Indian Land]], [[Egg Shipment]], [[Research And Development]], [[Technology And Health]], and [[Farm Pond Program]]. The OCR text does not clearly define the codes that dominate the table (RM, MA, OT, BS, FP), so their meanings are not assigned here.

The source does not name any fish species, so the species, family, and ocean basin fields are recorded as N/A.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document addresses hatchery production and distribution only. It contains no water chemistry, habitat, invertebrate, plankton, or population data, and no ecological interpretation. Its ecological relevance is in how stocked fish and eggs are spread across water types and program purposes. The stated water types range from lakes and reservoirs to streams, tailwaters, and estuaries. Program allocations show whether output went to restoration, endangered-species recovery, research, or management assistance.

## Key Findings

**Scale of production (FY 1992 totals):**
- Eggs: 82,000,949
- Fish: 208,896,734
- Weight: 6,510,962 (units not stated)

**Largest egg allocations by station:**
- [[Gavins Point]]: 29,474,824 eggs total, with a 22,000,000-egg entry and a 14,000,000-egg entry, and 19,677,594 fish.
- [[Spring Creek]]: 8,796,637 eggs, all under RM; 23,711,359 fish; 278,684 weight.
- [[Saratoga]]: 5,385,821 eggs total, of which 3,750,664 (69.64%) were under BS.
- [[Creston]]: 3,034,686 eggs total, of which 2,717,684 (89.55%) were under BS.
- [[Carson]]: 2,649,700 eggs total, of which 2,258,400 (85.23%) were under BS.

**Largest fish-number allocations by station:**
- [[Coleman]]: 26,552,957 fish total, almost all under RM (26,552,211).
- [[Spring Creek]]: 23,711,359 fish.
- [[Gavins Point]]: 19,677,594 fish total.
- [[Garrison Dam]]: about 21.9 million fish (the OCR text is partly garbled here).
- [[Dworshak]]: 5,273,248 fish, all under RM, with 601,985 weight.

**Stations with a single program:**
- [[Abernathy]]: 600,304 eggs, 2,024,017 fish, 64,778 weight, all RM.
- [[Dexter]]: 215,782 eggs, 145,172 fish, 1,395 weight, all under ES (Endangered And Threatened Species).
- [[Berkshire]]: 91 fish, 146 weight, all under MA.
- [[Quilcene]]: 2,974,586 fish, all RM.
- [[Willard]]: 1,573,088 fish, all RM.

**Program patterns:**
- RM is the dominant program at most stations by fish numbers and weight, though it is absent or minor at some stations (e.g., [[Alchesay Williams Creek]] RM is only 0.91% of fish).
- Several stations have a large BS egg share and very little fish production (e.g., [[Pendills Creek]] with 30,800 eggs and 136 fish). This suggests egg shipment rather than rearing.
- ES (Endangered And Threatened Species) appears at a few stations (e.g., Alchesay/Williams Creek, [[Bo Ginn]], [[Dexter]], [[Valley City Baldhill]]), usually with small shares.

**Limitations:**
- The OCR text has numerous garbled numbers and misaligned columns. Some station blocks are ambiguous, so the totals above are quoted from the report as printed and should be checked against the original.
- The weight unit is not stated.
- The report gives no species names, so it cannot be used for species-level analysis.
