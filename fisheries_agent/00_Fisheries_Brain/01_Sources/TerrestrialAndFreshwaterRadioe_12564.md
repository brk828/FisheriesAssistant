# Terrestrial and Freshwater Radioecology: A Selected Bibliography (Supplement 3)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #radioecology

## Executive Summary

This is the third supplement (February 1965) to a selected bibliography of terrestrial and freshwater radioecology, compiled by [[Alfred W. Klement Jr.]] and [[Vincent Schultz]] for the [[United States Atomic Energy Commission]] (AEC), Division of Biology and Medicine (report TID-3910, Suppl. 3). It follows Supplement 1 (March 1962), Supplement 2 (February 1963) and Supplement 2 of the 1964 series. The document is a reference list rather than a research study. It contains about 1,192 numbered citations, followed by a short list of ten related bibliographies. Most entries concern radiation effects on, or radionuclide cycling in, wild plants, invertebrates, amphibians, birds and mammals. Fish-related entries are a small subset.

## Background & Description

The compilers note growing interest in radioecology, including the biological effects of radioactive fallout and waste disposal. The list draws mainly on [[Nuclear Science Abstracts]] (screened from 1948, continued through 1964), review articles and other publications, with emphasis on United States literature.

Selection criteria:

- **Included:** field or laboratory studies of wild species exposed to radiation or using radionuclide tracers; some marine methods papers; environmental monitoring reports that mention wild organisms.
- **Generally excluded:** laboratory and domestic strains, preliminary and progress reports, and known preprints.
- **Caveat:** some freshwater and marine entries could not be distinguished from titles or abstracts alone.

Symbols and availability notes refer to AEC report TID-4550, Nuclear Science Abstracts numbers, and other document sources (AD, AECL, IAEA, JPRS, RDA). The compilers also acknowledge secretarial assistance and ask authors to list references fully.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The bibliography reflects a research landscape centred on several US and international field sites:

- **[[Nevada Test Site]]:** many entries on desert vegetation, arthropods, small mammals and reptiles, and ecological effects of nuclear detonations.
- **[[Savannah River Plant]]:** studies of old-field ecosystems, ant bioenergetics, mammal populations and radiotracer work.
- **[[Oak Ridge National Laboratory]]:** herpetofauna surveys and chronic-irradiation work.
- **[[Hanford Reservation]] and [[Columbia River]]:** radionuclide uptake by aquatic organisms, including [[Zinc-65]] in fish and euphausiids.
- **European and Soviet sites:** the Elbe River near Geesthacht, Lake Balaton, Stechlinsee, Lake Krasavitsa, and the Black Sea.

Major aquatic themes include radionuclide uptake by fish and algae, the freshwater strontium cycle, and bioaccumulation in molluscs and plankton. Terrestrial themes include [[Cesium-137]] cycling in forests and mammal tissues, and insect sterilisation by gamma radiation as a pest control method.

## Key Findings

The list is not a research study, so its "findings" are its main contents and conclusions about the field.

**Scope and size**

- Roughly 1,192 numbered references, mostly from 1950s to 1964 literature, with some older items (for example, 1903–1915 radium and X-ray studies).
- Entries cover fish, amphibians, insects, plants, algae, protozoa, mammals and birds, together with monitoring and methods reports.

**Fish-related entries (selected)**

- **[[Chronic Irradiation of Fish Embryos]]:** Brown and Templeton (1964, ref. 153) on fish embryo resistance to chronic irradiation; Donaldson and Bonham (1964, ref. 278) on low-level chronic irradiation of chinook and coho salmon eggs and alevins.
- **[[Radionuclide Uptake in Fish]]:** Boroughs et al. (1956, ref. 132) on strontium-89 uptake; Hunn and Fromm (1964, ref. 502) on I-131 uptake, turnover and excretion in rainbow trout; Hoar and Eales (1963, ref. 481) on radioiodine conversion in goldfish, chum salmon and chub; Morgan (1964, ref. 714) on radioactivity uptake by fish and shellfish.
- **[[Cesium-137 in Freshwater Fish]]:** Hänninen and Miettinen (1963, ref. 443) on Finnish freshwater fish.
- **[[Radioactivity in Fish Monitoring]]:** Feldt (1960, ref. 313) on radioactivity in Elbe River fish near the Geesthacht reactor.
- **Fish hazard reviews:** Higgins (1957, ref. 472) on atomic radiation hazards for fish; Hannerz (1958, ref. 430) on atomic waste and fisheries.

**Schultz's own contributions (co-author)**

- Schultz (1965, ref. 933) compares strontium-90 levels between antler and mandible of white-tailed deer.
- Schultz and Flyger (1965, ref. 934) examine sex and age effects on strontium-90 accumulation in deer mandibles.

**Notable general references**

- [[Radioecology and the Study of Environmental Radiation]]: Martin (1964, ref. 670).
- [[Radiation, Radioactivity, and Insects]]: O'Brien and Wolfe (1964, ref. 759).
- [[Low-Level Radioactive Wastes]]: Straub (1964, ref. 1011), with ecological information.
- [[Radioactive Pollutants in the Environment]]: U.S. Public Health Service (1964, ref. 1086).

**Companion bibliographies (ten listed)**

- Include the Goldmann (1963) annotated bibliographies on radiation effects (two volumes), Pierce (1963) on effects of radiation and radioisotopes on life processes (two books, 1,824 pp.), and Schwartz (1961) on external forces in aquatic organisms, which has a radiation section.

**Limitations noted by the compilers**

- Some titles and abstracts were used because originals were unavailable.
- Some citations are incomplete and could not be verified.
- Indexing was not attempted.
