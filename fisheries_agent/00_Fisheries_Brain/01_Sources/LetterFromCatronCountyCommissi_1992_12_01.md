## Key Findings

- The [[Catron County Commission]] adopted a **Comprehensive Land Use and Policy Plan** asserting a legal right to "coordinate" with federal/state agencies (including the [[US Fish and Wildlife Service]]) on management of natural resources, explicitly including **threatened and endangered species** listing and recovery planning.
- The county acknowledges the USFWS's final authority over listing and recovery decisions but demands procedural coordination rights under existing federal law.
