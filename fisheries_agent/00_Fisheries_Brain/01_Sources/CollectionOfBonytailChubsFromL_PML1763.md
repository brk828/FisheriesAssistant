# Bonytail Chub Collection from Lake Mohave (1993)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This U.S. [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] memorandum, dated May 1993 and addressed to the AZFRO project leader and interested persons, reports a field collection of [[Bonytail Chub]] from [[Lake Mohave]]. The text is heavily degraded by OCR, so many figures and sentences cannot be read reliably. The legible content covers netting and handling of bonytail, fin-clipping and tagging, observed spawning behavior, and a small bycatch of other native and non-native species.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Interoffice memorandum from a Fishery Biologist at the Parker Fishery Resources Office (FRO) to the AZFRO project leader and interested persons.
- **Subject:** Collection of bonytail chubs from Lake Mohave during May 1993. The exact day range is illegible in the scan.
- **Personnel:** The memo states that the author was assisted by Paul and Mike Horn, and that a further staff member (initials "AFD," likely a state agency) was involved. Other names in the scan are unclear.
- **Target species:** The [[Bonytail Chub]], a native [[Colorado River]] cyprinid, was the focus of the netting effort.
- **Handling:** Fish were netted and handled, and some were fin-clipped and tagged before release.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Setting:** Lake Mohave is a reservoir on the lower [[Colorado River]]. The collection took place there during the spring spawning period.
- **Spawning:** The memo describes fish with spawning coloration, and reports that some individuals were removed from the net and handled for eggs and sperm (gametes). The OCR is too degraded to confirm the exact procedure or outcome.
- **Bycatch:** Fewer than 20 [[Razorback Sucker]] were taken, along with [[Channel Catfish]] and possibly other species. The memo ends with a reference to "any" additional species, but the text is cut off.
- **Conservation relevance:** Both bonytail and razorback sucker are native Colorado River fishes that have declined in the basin. Their co-occurrence in this collection supports the monitoring and management purpose of the work, though the memo itself does not state this explicitly.

## Key Findings

- A bonytail collection was carried out at Lake Mohave in May 1993 by the Parker FRO, with assistance from additional personnel.
- The total number of bonytail netted is partly legible, appearing in the range of roughly 400 to 480 fish. The exact count, along with the number of fish tagged, should be verified against the original scan.
- Some bonytail were fin-clipped and tagged before release. Some fish appear to have been retained for spawning work. The OCR does not allow these details to be confirmed.
- Fewer than 20 [[Razorback Sucker]] were captured as incidental bycatch. [[Channel Catfish]] were also taken.
- The memo notes spawning condition and gamete handling in some bonytail, though the procedural details are not legible.

**Note on source quality:** The OCR contains many garbled words and numbers, including the collection dates and most of the counts. Values above are given as legible or approximate. Verify them against the original document before citing.
