## Executive Summary

This document is a 1986 administrative letter from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 2, Albuquerque) to the Arizona Game and Fish Department, responding favorably to a request to establish a **trophy sport fishery** for the endangered [[Colorado squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*) in the lower [[Colorado River]] between Parker and Yuma, Arizona. The letter is not a primary scientific study but rather a management/regulatory record documenting an early conservation strategy: using **Section 10(j) of the [[Endangered Species Act of 1973]]** to designate an "experimental population," thereby permitting controlled sport fishing and stocking activities that would otherwise be restricted under standard endangered species protections. The USFWS committed to supplying squawfish fry from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]] to support the stocking effort.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Species**: [[Colorado squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), the largest native North American cyprinid, historically a top predatory fish in the Colorado River Basin.
- **Habitat context**: The proposed project targets the **lower Colorado River** reach between **Parker, Arizona** and **Yuma, Arizona** — a heavily regulated, downstream portion of the river system, notably outside the species' primary historic range in the upper basin.
- **Conservation status**: Referenced implicitly as a federally listed endangered species, subject to protections and recovery planning under the [[Endangered Species Act]].
- **Population management strategy**: Introduction of hatchery-reared fry (sourced from [[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]], New Mexico) into a novel reach of river to establish a self-sustaining or put-and-take population for recreational (trophy) angling, functioning simultaneously as a **species recovery mechanism**.

## Key Findings

- The Arizona Game and Fish Department (via Director Bud Bristow) requested USFWS assistance on **June 20, 1986** to establish a trophy sport fishery for Colorado squawfish in the lower Colorado River (Parker–Yuma reach).
- USFWS Region 2, represented by Acting Regional Director **Conrad A. Fjetland**, approved and endorsed the proposal, characterizing it as an **"innovative means of assisting recovery of the species."**
- USFWS committed to:
  - Preparing a **separate experimental population designation** under **Section 10(j) of the Endangered Species Act (1973, as amended)** for this specific population.
  - Supplying **Colorado squawfish fry** from **[[Dexter National Fish Hatchery]]** for stocking the project.
- The initiative represents an early example of balancing **endangered species recovery** with **recreational fisheries management**, using regulatory flexibility (10(j) designation) to reduce legal liability for anglers/managers while promoting population establishment.
- Distribution list indicates coordination among USFWS **Fishery Resources**, **Dexter NFH**, and noted ichthyologist **Dr. W.L. Minckley**, a leading expert on southwestern U.S. desert fishes, suggesting scientific oversight of the project design.
