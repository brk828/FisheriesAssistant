# A System for Recording Measurements of Fish Scales

#ichthyology #marine-biology #salmonidae

## Executive Summary
This methodological paper by Leo Shapovalov (California Division of Fish and Game) presents a standardized notation system for recording [[Fish Scale Analysis]] measurements used in [[Age and Growth Determination]] of [[Trout]] and [[Salmon]]. Developed from measuring "many thousands" of scales, the system addresses the challenge of distinguishing freshwater, estuarine ("intermediate"), and saltwater growth phases along the anterior radius of the scale, while also encoding degrees of confidence in ambiguous features such as regenerated scales, false checks, and spawning marks. The paper proposes a tabular recording format and two alternative protocols for validating scale interpretations through independent re-examination, aiming to improve reproducibility and inter-investigator agreement in [[Scale Aging Techniques]].

## Background & Description
Although not a taxonomic description, the paper details specific structural features of fish scales relevant to growth interpretation:

- **Anterior radius**: All measurements are standardized to this axis of the scale, measured via microscope with a mechanical stage and calibrated micrometer (recorded in hundredths of a millimeter).
- **Annuli (growth rings)**: Mark the end of a full growing season; used as primary reference points for cumulative growth measurements.
- **Intermediate growth zone**: Growth occurring in fresh or brackish water during the season of seaward migration (encompassing stream and estuary/lagoon growth), representing only a partial growing season rather than a full annual increment.
- **Regenerated scales**: Scales that have regrown after loss, potentially obscuring or misrepresenting early annuli.
- **False checks (incidental/accessory checks)**: Non-annual growth interruptions that can be mistaken for true annuli.
- **Spawning marks**: Distinct marks on the scale associated with spawning-related resorption, which may obliterate or occur separately from a true annulus. Includes recognition of **false spawning marks (FS)**, sometimes accompanied by scale erosion.
- **Spawning absorption**: Resorption of scale material during spawning that can distort final growth measurements.

## Ecological & Environmental Context
The system is designed explicitly for **diadromous or partially diadromous salmonid populations** that transition between habitats:

- **Freshwater growth**: Growth in streams, denoted in Roman type (black pencil in manuscript).
- **Intermediate (estuarine/lagoon) growth**: Growth occurring during the seasonal migration event from freshwater to sea, capturing transitional habitat use in the [[Estuarine Ecology]] context.
- **Saltwater (marine) growth**: Growth in the ocean environment, denoted in italic type (red pencil in manuscript).
- The framework accommodates populations that are exclusively freshwater, exclusively marine, or that exhibit the full anadromous migratory pattern typical of [[Pacific Salmon]] and sea-run [[Trout]], reflecting the paper's origin in California salmonid fisheries management.

## Key Findings
- **Standardized tabular format**: Proposes columns for Fish No., Date, Sex, Fish Length, First Year, Second Year, Third Year, Fourth Year, Intermediate growth, and Remarks, enabling cumulative, chronologically organized scale measurement records.
- **Color/type coding convention**: Roman type (black) = freshwater/intermediate growth; italic type (red) = saltwater growth — allowing rapid visual distinction of habitat-specific growth phases within a single scale record.
- **Symbol system for scale condition and confidence**:
  - `R` — scale regenerated to an indeterminate degree (annuli possibly missing).
  - `(R)` — regenerated scale, but all annuli believed present (first-annulus measurement may still be unreliable).
  - `R–` or `(R)–` — no additional scales remain in the sample book/envelope.
  - `A` — annulus present at the scale edge.
  - `+` — possible spawning absorption affecting the final measurement (excludes scale from growth calculations).
  - `S` — spawning mark (may obscure or occur apart from a coincident annulus).
  - `FC` — false/incidental/accessory check (recorded in Remarks column).
  - `FS` (underscored if accompanied by erosion) — false spawning mark.
  - `?` — feature validity undetermined.
  - `(?)` — feature questionable but believed valid.
  - `(measurement?)` — feature questionable and believed **not** valid.
  - `/` — separates measurements when multiple scales from the same sample are analyzed.
- **Validation protocols**: Two proposed approaches to assess reliability of [[Scale Interpretation]]:
  1. Record all measurements without initially flagging uncertainty; a second independent reading is compared, with matching interpretations classified "certain" and mismatches "doubtful."
  2. Flag doubtful features at the time of initial recording, then have a second reader re-examine only the flagged doubtful scales; consistent reinterpretation upgrades the feature to "certain."
- **Extensibility**: The system is flexible — investigators can add custom columns (e.g., a "Marks" column for marked vs. unmarked fish) or supplementary numbered annotations for special notes, demonstrating adaptability to diverse [[Fisheries Management]] and research contexts beyond the original trout/salmon application.
- **Significance**: By formalizing notation for growth zones and interpretive uncertainty, the system improves reproducibility of back-calculated growth histories, supporting more robust [[Population Dynamics]] and life-history studies in anadromous fishes.
