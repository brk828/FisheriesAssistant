# Razorback Sucker and Bonytail Collection and Release from High Levee Pond

#ichthyology #marine-biology #catostomidae #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

A memorandum from the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] Arizona Fishery Resources Office (Parker, Arizona), dated 19 December 2006, reports the capture of six [[Razorback Sucker]] and one [[Bonytail]] from High Levee pond on the night of 18 December 2006. The fish were released the following day, with the razorback suckers placed in the office cove at [[Bill Williams River National Wildlife Refuge]] (BWRNWR) and the bonytail released into [[Lake Havasu]] near the same cove. Four of the six razorback suckers were recaptures.

## Background & Description

- **Document type:** Interagency memorandum (reference DCN-USFWS-AZFR0-pA-07-004).
- **From:** Chuck Minckley, Project Coordinator, MFRO-Parker.
- **To:** Mark Brouder (MFRO), Paul Marsh, and Carol Pacey (Arizona State University, ASU).
- **Capture crew:** Mitch Thorson and Joseph Barnett.
- **Subject:** Collection and release of endangered native fishes from [[High Levee Pond]].

The memo is a field record of handling and translocation. It does not describe study design, methods, or analysis.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

Both species are native Colorado River fishes that the memo treats as managed, translocated stock. The memo records only fish lengths, weights, sex, and tag numbers. It does not report water chemistry, habitat conditions, or other species present at High Levee pond or the release sites. The document gives no units for length (TL) or weight (WT); the values are presumably millimeters and grams, but this is not stated.
