## Executive Summary
This document is **Resolution 81-7**, issued by the [[Desert Fishes Council]] on December 30, 1981. Rather than presenting primary morphological or ecological research, the text serves as a policy directive urging the establishment of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between the [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM), the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (USFWS), and the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]]. The core objective is to facilitate the reintroduction of specific native desert fish species—the [[Gila Topminnow]] and [[Desert Pupfish]]—into their historic habitats on public lands, citing a successful precedent involving the [[Razorback Sucker]].

## Background & Description
The source document is a legislative resolution and does not provide specific morphological, meristic, or physical descriptions of the species involved. It focuses strictly on taxonomic identification for conservation purposes:
*   **Razorback Sucker:** *Xyrauchen texanus*
*   **Gila Topminnow:** *Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis*
*   **Desert Pupfish:** *Cyprinodon macularius macularius*

## Ecological & Environmental Context
*   **Habitat Type:** The species discussed are endemic to arid desert ecosystems, relying on isolated springs, streams, and remnant aquatic habitats within the [[Sonoran Desert]] region.
*   **Land Management:** The resolution specifically targets **public lands** managed by the Forest Service and the Bureau of Land Management, indicating a reliance on federally protected or managed watersheds for species survival.
*   **Conservation Status:** The document implies these species are considered **threatened or endangered**, as the reintroduction effort is described as "essential to the recovery" of the species within their historic range.
*   **Precedent Case:** The successful reintroduction of the [[Razorback Sucker]] on Forest Service lands serves as the ecological and administrative model for the proposed actions regarding the topminnow and pupfish.

## Key Findings
1.  **Policy Mechanism:** A Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is identified as an effective administrative tool for coordinating multi-agency native fish reintroduction efforts.
2.  **Target Species:** The resolution specifically calls for the reintroduction of the [[Gila Topminnow]] and [[Desert Pupfish]] into suitable public land habitats in Arizona.
3.  **Organizational Consensus:** The resolution was **passed without dissenting vote** by the Desert Fishes Council, representing a consensus among over 300 federal, state, university, and independent scientists/specialists.
4.  **Inter-agency Collaboration:** The proposal requires coordination between three distinct governmental bodies: the Arizona State Office of the [[Bureau of Land Management]], [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Region 2), and the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]].
