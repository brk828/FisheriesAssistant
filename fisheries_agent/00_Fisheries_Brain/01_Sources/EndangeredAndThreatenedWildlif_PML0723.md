# Five-Year Review of Federally Listed Endangered and Threatened Species (1985)

#ichthyology #marine-biology #Percidae #Salmonidae

## Executive Summary

This document is a 1985 U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) package requesting public and agency comment on the five-year review of species listed under the [[Endangered Species Act]]. It consists of a cover letter to regional cooperators, an internal comment routing sheet, and a *Federal Register* notice (50 CFR Part 17, 22 July 1985) covering species listed as endangered or threatened in or before 1978-1980. The notice is a review request, not a research study. It reports that no substantial data suggested a status change for any of the 39 species reviewed. Three fishes are included: [[Leopard Darter]], [[Greenback Cutthroat Trout]], and [[Little Kern Golden Trout]]. Comments were due 31 October 1985 to the Region 2 office and 19 November 1985 to the *Federal Register* contacts.

## Background & Description

- **Legal basis:** The [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973, as amended, requires the [[U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service]] to review each listed species at least once every five years. The purpose is to make sure the list reflects current status.
- **Review outcome options:** Based on the review, the FWS may propose [[Reclassification]] (endangered to threatened, or the reverse) or [[Delisting]] if current evidence no longer supports the present classification.
- **Scope:** The cover letter went to cooperators with information on species in [[Region 2]] (Arizona, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Texas). The notice covers species across all regions.
- **Information gathering:** The Service solicited comments and data from state agencies, organizations, researchers and individuals. It also searched its own files and requested data from Service biologists. The notice states that the Service had reviewed all pertinent material available and found no basis for change for the species considered.
- **Listing factors:** Under the Act, species are listed for (a) present or threatened destruction or modification of habitat or range; (b) overutilization for commercial, recreational, scientific or educational purposes; (c) disease or predation; (d) inadequacy of existing regulatory mechanisms; or (e) other natural or manmade factors.
- **Definitions provided:** The notice defines [[Critical Habitat]], "endangered" (in danger of extinction throughout all or a significant portion of its range), "threatened" (likely to become endangered within the foreseeable future), and "species" (including subspecies and distinct populations capable of interbreeding when mature).
- **Delisting criteria:** Delisting may be based on [[Extinction]] (only after a sufficient period confirms extinction), [[Recovery]] (the species no longer meets listing criteria), or [[Taxonomic Error]] (original data were erroneous).

## Ecological & Environmental Context

The document does not describe the habitat, life history, distribution or population trends of the listed fishes. It does not give water chemistry or ecosystem data. Its environmental content is limited to the general listing factors above, which apply to habitat loss, overharvest, disease, inadequate regulation and other threats.

The review covers a much wider group of taxa than fishes:

- **Mollusks:** About nine snail species, including the Oahu tree snail group and a Virginia freshwater snail. Named examples are the Iowa Pleistocene snail and the Stock Island snail.
- **Crustaceans:** Two isopod species, including the Socorro isopod.
- **Plants:** Many flowering plant species in the families Betulaceae, Brassicaceae, Crassulaceae, Fabaceae, Hydrophyllaceae, Liliaceae, Onagraceae, Poaceae, Ranunculaceae and Scrophulariaceae. Examples include the Antioch Dunes evening-primrose and the Furbish lousewort.
- **Birds and mammals:** Includes the [[Bald Eagle]] (*Haliaeetus leucocephalus*), among other birds and mammals.
- **Reptiles:** Includes the Mona boa, New Mexican ridge-nosed rattlesnake, eastern indigo snake and green sea turtle.

The note's fish content is therefore one small part of a broad national list. Most of the table names are reproduced only as category totals in the source.

## Key Findings

- **Review result:** The Service reports that it completed the five-year review of species listed as endangered or threatened in 1978. No change in status is proposed for any of the 39 species, because all available data supported their present status. The Service noted that new data could still justify a future proposal.
- **Fish species under review:**
  - [[Leopard Darter]] (*Percina pantherina*), family Percidae
  - [[Greenback Cutthroat Trout]] (*Salmo clarkii stomias*), family Salmonidae
  - [[Little Kern Golden Trout]] (*Salmo aguabonita whitei*), family Salmonidae
- **Deadlines and contacts:**
  - Cover letter to Region 2 cooperators: comments requested by 31 October 1985.
  - *Federal Register* notice: comments due by 19 November 1985.
  - Comments went to the Regional Director for the species' region. Questions went to the Office of Endangered Species in Washington, D.C.
- **Process note:** This was the first summary of any five-year review published. The Service said later summaries would be published to better inform the public.
- **Internal routing:** The comment sheet (dated 10 September 1985, due 4 October 1985) asked staff to return comments to the designated coordinator and to forward the document to the next person in line.
