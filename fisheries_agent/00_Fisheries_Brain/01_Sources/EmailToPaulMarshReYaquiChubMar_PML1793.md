# Yaqui Chub Mark-Recapture Data Transmittal at Las Ojitos Spring

#ichthyology #marine-biology #cyprinidae

## Executive Summary

This document is an email from [[Chuck Minckley]] to [[Paul Marsh]], dated Sunday, June 19, 2011, that transmits field data from a two-day [[Mark-Recapture Sampling]] effort on [[Yaqui Chub]] at [[Las Ojitos Spring]]. Minckley asks Marsh to run a [[Population Estimation]] for the whole population and separately for fish 60 mm (total length) and larger. The email does not contain an estimate. It includes the raw catch counts, the sampling design, and a length-frequency chart.

## Background & Description

- **Species:** [[Yaqui Chub]], the subject of the mark-recapture effort. The document does not give a binomial name. The species listed in the frontmatter is included from general knowledge.
- **Document type:** Informal correspondence with an attached spreadsheet (YaquiChubSize.xlsx) and a length chart prepared by a colleague, Bob.
- **Correspondents:** Chuck Minckley, C.O. Minckley, Ph.D., of Flagstaff, Arizona, and Paul Marsh, the recipient who is to run the analysis.
- **Purpose:** Estimate population size, both total and for adults or larger fish (>60 mm TL), from marked and recaptured fish.

## Ecological & Environmental Context

- **Habitat sampled:** The spring at Las Ojitos. Sampling covered the perimeter (shoreline) and open water away from the shoreline. The document does not report water chemistry, temperature, or other habitat measurements.
- **Gear:** [[Minnow Trap]] and "minipomar" traps were baited with dry cat food. Setting them in the spring allowed capture across both shoreline and open-water zones.
- **Size structure:** The length chart shows a strongly unequal size distribution. The smallest size class sampled (26–30 mm) had N = 110, and the 86–90 mm class had N = 83. The text does not give counts for the intermediate classes.

## Key Findings

1. **Sampling design:** 27 traps were used: 18 minnow traps and 9 minipomars, arranged in 9 groups of three (two minnow traps plus one minipomar). Five groups were set around the perimeter and four in the spring away from the shoreline.
2. **Day 1 (captures on 14 June 2011):** 722 Yaqui chub were taken. Of these, 366 fish ≥60 mm TL were clipped (marked) and released, and 356 fish <60 mm were measured and released. The total of 366 + 356 = 722 is consistent.
3. **Day 2 (captures on 16–17 June 2011):** 543 Yaqui chub were taken. The email labels these as new fish and recaptures (>60 mm), but the recapture count is illegible in the source text. The usable total for day 2 is 543.
4. **Gap between sessions:** Nets were not fished on 15 June. Traps were reset at 1700 hrs on 16 June and run the morning of 17 June.
5. **Analysis requested:** A population estimate for all fish and a separate estimate for fish >60 mm. The marking threshold of 60 mm defines the subpopulation.
6. **Length distribution:** The attached chart was prepared to show size distributions. Its key values are the 26–30 mm class (N = 110) and the 86–90 mm class (N = 83).
