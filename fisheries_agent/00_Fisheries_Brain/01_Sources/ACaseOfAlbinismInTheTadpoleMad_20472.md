## Executive Summary
This short paper documents the first recorded case of albinism in the genus *[[Noturus]]*, specifically in the [[Tadpole Madtom]] (*Noturus gyrinus*). A single albino specimen was collected via rotenone sampling from a blackwater slough of the [[Suwannee River]] in Clinch County, Georgia, in May 1970. Out of 2,788 total *N. gyrinus* specimens collected across twelve rotenone samples on the Suwannee River system since 1966, only this one individual exhibited albinism, making it an extremely rare anomaly (roughly 1 in ~2,800 specimens observed). The finding extends prior reports of albinism in other [[Ictaluridae|ictalurid]] catfishes to the genus *Noturus* for the first time, addressing a gap noted in [[Taylor 1969|Taylor's]] comprehensive taxonomic review of the genus.

## Background & Description
**Normal-pigmented *N. gyrinus* (typical phenotype, n=2,070):**
- Dark brown fins
- Yellowish-brown body
- Dark midlateral stripe
- Pale venter
- Dark iris and pupil

**Albino specimen (AU 4213):**
- Standard length: 44 mm
- Weight: 2.7 g
- Body, fins, barbels, and eyes: milky white
- Yellowish band present along:
  - Bases of the caudal fin
  - Bases of the pelvic fin
  - Bases of the dorsal fin
  - Lateralis canals of the head
  - Anterior third of the body
- Complete loss of ocular pigmentation (both iris and pupil white) — distinguishing true [[Albinism]] from partial leucism

## Ecological & Environmental Context
- **Habitat type:** Sluggish, dark-stained (tannin-rich) blackwater slough, approximately 0.3 ha in size
- **Water chemistry:** pH 4.5 (highly acidic, characteristic of [[Okefenokee Swamp]]-influenced drainage)
- **Hydrology:** Site sampled after >180 days of continuous floodplain inundation, indicating the species' tolerance for prolonged flooded, low-flow conditions
- **Population context:** *N. gyrinus* was highly abundant at the sampled site — 718 specimens (2.8 kg) recovered from a total fish sample of 123.8 kg, indicating this species can be a dominant component of blackwater floodplain fish assemblages
- **Regional distribution:** Species is well-established throughout the Georgia portion of the [[Suwannee River]] system, drawing primarily from the [[Okefenokee Swamp]]

## Key Findings
- First documented case of albinism in the genus *[[Noturus]]* (family [[Ictaluridae]])
- Albinism previously reported in other ictalurid genera (*Ictalurus*) by [[Menzel 1944]] and [[McLain 1950]], and even commercially exploited in channel catfish production per [[Prather 1961]]
- [[Taylor 1969]]'s exhaustive literature review of *Noturus* found no prior albinism records, underscoring the rarity of this observation
- Extremely low incidence rate: 1 albino individual among 2,788 total *N. gyrinus* collected in the Suwannee River system across multiple years and sample events (1966–1970)
- Specimen preserved and catalogued in the Auburn University ichthyological collection (AU 4213) for future reference
