## Executive Summary

This document is **not a scientific research paper** but a personal resignation letter dated May 4, 1990, from William G. Kepner to W. L. Minckley, Chairman of the [[Desert Fishes Recovery Team]] at Arizona State University's Department of Zoology. The letter announces Kepner's departure from the team due to his acceptance of an Ecologist position with the U.S. EPA's newly established [[Environmental Monitoring and Assessment Program]] (EMAP), based at the Environmental Monitoring Systems Laboratory, University of Nevada Las Vegas.
