# Santa Ana River

## Summary
The Santa Ana River is a freshwater river system in southern California, USA, that provides habitat for the federally threatened Santa Ana Sucker (Pantosteus santaanae). This study used captive Santa Ana Suckers originating from the Santa Ana River to evaluate PIT tagging protocols, addressing knowledge gaps in population dynamics that result from seasonal sampling limitations and inconsistent survey methodologies in this river system.
