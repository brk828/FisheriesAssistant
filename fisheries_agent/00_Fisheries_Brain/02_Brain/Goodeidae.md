# Goodeidae

## Summary
Goodeidae is a family of small, viviparous (live-bearing) freshwater fishes endemic to the highland drainages and endorheic basins of central Mexico, particularly the Mesa Central region associated with the Lerma-Santiago drainage system. Members of this family are typically confined to specialized habitats such as highland streams, springs, and lake systems, making them ecologically and geographically restricted groups of conservation concern. Taxonomic identification of goodeids relies on detailed examination of meristic characters (fin ray counts, scale series), cranial sensory pore configuration, dentition, and sexually dimorphic features, as demonstrated by dichotomous keys designed for adult specimen classification.
