# Little Colorado Spinedace

## Summary
The Little Colorado Spinedace (*Lepidomeda vittata*) is a small endemic cyprinid fish species native to the Little Colorado River drainage in Arizona that has been afforded federal protection under the Endangered Species Act due to habitat loss, water diversion, and competition from non-native species. As a species of conservation concern, it appears alongside other imperiled southwestern fishes in recovery planning documents and Endangered Species Act consultations, where management strategies address threats to its survival in its limited native range. This species represents part of the broader conservation challenge facing endemic desert fishes in the Colorado River basin and the southwestern United States.
