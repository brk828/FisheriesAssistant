# Population Viability Analysis

## Summary
Population Viability Analysis (PVA) is a quantitative modeling approach used to assess the long-term survival probability of fish populations by integrating demographic data, genetic parameters, and environmental variables to predict extinction risk under various scenarios. In fish conservation, PVA provides a science-based framework for setting recovery goals and management targets by estimating the minimum population sizes and genetic diversity needed to maintain self-sustaining populations over specified time horizons. This analytical tool has proven particularly valuable for endangered aquatic species management, allowing resource managers to move beyond qualitative expert judgment toward data-driven conservation strategies that account for stochastic processes and environmental uncertainty.
