# Terrapene coahuilae

## Summary
Terrapene coahuilae is the Coahuilan box turtle, a species found in the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin of Mexico. During field surveys of aquatic habitats in this region, one large individual was observed at Laguna Churince, indicating the presence of this turtle species in the spring-fed lagoon systems.
