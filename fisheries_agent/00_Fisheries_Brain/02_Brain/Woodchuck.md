# Woodchuck

## Summary
The Woodchuck is a mammal species used as a biogeographic indicator of past climate conditions. This source reports that Woodchuck ranged through northern Arizona within the past few millennia but is now limited in distribution by reduced moisture and higher temperatures, providing evidence of increased aridity and warming.
