# Allie Canyon

## Summary
Allie Canyon is a geographic landmark in the Mimbres River system in Grant County, New Mexico, that marks one end of a river reach occupied by the endangered Chihuahua chub. The recovery plan reviewers note that the distance between Allie Canyon and Mimbres Post Office needs to be specified, and that Figure 1 in the original plan requires demarcation of Allie Canyon's location.
