# Illegal Introduction

## Summary
Illegal introductions by fishermen are suspected as the source of northern pike in California waters. The species was not historically present in California, with first confirmations at Frenchman Reservoir in 1988 and Lake Davis in 1994, and it remains unknown whether pike at Lake Davis survived the 1997 chemical treatment or were reintroduced afterward.
