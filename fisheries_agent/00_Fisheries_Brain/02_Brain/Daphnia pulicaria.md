# Daphnia pulicaria

## Summary
Daphnia pulicaria is a large cladoceran zooplankton species found in carp ponds. This source reports that nitrogen fertilization with ammonium sulphate increased D. pulicaria populations 1.5-2.5 times in experimental ponds, and that carp fry consumed large Daphnia species within 2-3 weeks of stocking.
