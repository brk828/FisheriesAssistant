# Florence Creek

## Summary
Florence Creek is a spring-fed tributary that provides groundwater input to the Gray Canyon spawning area of Colorado squawfish on the Green River (River Mile 145-160). This creek is cited as potential evidence for the Groundwater Seepage Hypothesis, as its distinctive chemical signature from groundwater seepage may serve as an olfactory cue that allows spawning fish to home to this location.
