# Leatherside Chub

## Summary
The Leatherside Chub (Gila copei) is a fish species whose reproductive biology was studied and presented at this meeting. Research found that peak spawning occurs in May, females produce an average of 1,813 eggs, the maximum observed age is 9 years, and reproductive adults range from 2-9 years old with a median age of 6 years.
