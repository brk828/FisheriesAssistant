# Carnegie Institution

## Summary
Carnegie Institution was the institutional affiliation of Dr. C. J. McCoy, a herpetofauna specialist who was part of the expert network assembled to support the Cuatro Ciénegas Biosphere Reserve management planning in 1988. McCoy provided expertise on the reptiles and amphibians of the basin.
