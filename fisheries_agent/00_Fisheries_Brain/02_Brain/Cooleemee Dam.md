# Cooleemee Dam

## Summary
Cooleemee Dam is located at Cooleemee, Davie County, on the South Yadkin River and serves as a barrier to fish migration. This survey confirmed flathead catfish are absent above the dam, with only juvenile flathead catfish found below it, and the habitat above the dam was deemed unsuitable for flathead catfish stocking due to the river's small size and limited structural complexity.
