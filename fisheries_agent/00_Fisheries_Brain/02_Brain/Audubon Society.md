# Audubon Society

## Summary
The Audubon Society is an organization known for conducting volunteer-based bird counts. The 1988 October Fish Count was explicitly modeled after Audubon Society bird counts, adapting their citizen science approach to freshwater fish monitoring in Arizona and New Mexico.
