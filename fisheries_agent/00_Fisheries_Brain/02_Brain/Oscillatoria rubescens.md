# Oscillatoria rubescens

## Summary
Oscillatoria rubescens is a blue-green algae species found in Alpine lakes that produces Type IIIb Oscillatoria maxima similar to O. agardhii in US lakes. This species has been associated with documented eutrophication in Alpine lakes since the early 20th century. It occupies the same ecological role as O. agardhii but in the Alpine lake systems of Switzerland and Austria.
