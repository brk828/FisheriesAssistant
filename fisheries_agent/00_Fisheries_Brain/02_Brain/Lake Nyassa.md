# Lake Nyassa

## Summary
Lake Nyassa (also known as Lake Malawi) is a deep African lake that maintained its status as a large water body even during arid interpluvial periods of the Pleistocene. According to this source, unlike shallower lakes such as Koga, Edward, George, and Victoria that dried completely or reduced to deoxygenated swamps during dry periods, Lake Nyassa's greater depth allowed it to persist and support continuous evolution of its fish fauna throughout the Pleistocene climatic oscillations.
