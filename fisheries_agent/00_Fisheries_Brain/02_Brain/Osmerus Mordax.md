# Osmerus Mordax

## Summary
Osmerus mordax, commonly known as rainbow smelt, is a small euryhaline teleost fish species that inhabits coastal and estuarine waters of the North Atlantic and has been widely studied in fisheries research for its ecological role as both a forage fish and indicator species. In the context of the cited documents, rainbow smelt appears alongside other teleost fishes in comparative analyses of sex ratios, predation dynamics, and responses to hydrological conditions, making it a model organism for understanding population-level variation in fish reproductive biology and community interactions in marine and estuarine ecosystems.
