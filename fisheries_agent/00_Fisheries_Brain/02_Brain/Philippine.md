# Philippine

## Summary
The Philippine region refers to the waters around the central Philippine Islands, which this source establishes as a distinct zoogeographic subregion. The Philippine Subregion is characterized by isolated deep-sea basins (particularly the Sulu Sea complex) with unusually warm bottom temperatures (approximately 10°C at depths greater than 550 meters) that harbor numerous endemic deep-sea fish species, especially macrourids.
