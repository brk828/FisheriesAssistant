# Tusher Diversion

## Summary
Tusher Diversion is a weir equipped with PIT-tag detection equipment located approximately 300 km upstream from Lake Powell in the Colorado River system. Fifty-six razorback suckers were detected at this location, demonstrating the extensive upstream movements made by fish from the reservoir. This detection site is one of eight relatively large diversion structures in the basin with varying fish passage capabilities.
