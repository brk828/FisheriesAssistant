# Central Platte River Recovery Program

## Summary
The Central Platte River Recovery Program is a cooperative recovery effort developed in the 1990s to address endangered species impacts from water development across Colorado, Wyoming, and Nebraska. The program targets three listed bird species (whooping crane, interior least tern, piping plover) and pallid sturgeon, with a first increment proposing 10-13 years of implementation including water flow improvements of 130,000-150,000 acre feet per year and protection of at least 10,000 acres of habitat. The program provides regulatory certainty under Section 7 of the ESA for water-related activities while states meet program milestones, with total contributions of $75 million split 50/50 between federal and state governments.
