# Lyons Reservoir

## Summary
Lyons Reservoir is a water body connected to Strawberry Lake. This source reports that Kokanee migrated from Strawberry Lake to Lyons Reservoir in 1946 as water levels declined, with no documented harm from this dispersal event.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
