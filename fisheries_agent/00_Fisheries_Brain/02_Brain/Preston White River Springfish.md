# Preston White River Springfish

## Summary
The Preston White River Springfish (Crenichthys baileyi albivallis) is a fish subspecies in the family Goodeidae found in White River Valley. It was one of 11 fish species petitioned for endangered species listing, facing threats from habitat modification and introduced species in the White River system.
