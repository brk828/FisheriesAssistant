# Deciduous Riparian Forest Biome

## Summary
The Deciduous Riparian Forest Biome is one of the major biotic communities in the study area, occurring at elevations of 2,600-3,110 feet along Aravaipa Creek floodplains. These communities are dominated by cottonwood, sycamore, and willow, and supported some of the highest nocturnal rodent biomass values (0.75-3.8 kg/hectare) recorded in the survey.
