# Oncorhynchus

## Summary
Oncorhynchus is a genus of anadromous and semelparous fish in the salmon family (Salmonidae) that includes Pacific salmon and trout species such as chinook, coho, pink, chum, and sockeye salmon, as well as steelhead and rainbow trout. These economically and ecologically important species are characterized by their complex life histories involving migration between freshwater and marine environments, making them particularly vulnerable to habitat degradation and requiring careful management and conservation consideration at both population and subspecies levels. Oncorhynchus species have been central to discussions of endangered species protection and subspecies-level conservation distinctions, reflecting the recognition that distinct populations or runs may warrant individual regulatory protections despite being within the same nominal species.
