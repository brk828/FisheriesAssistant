# Game Fish Distribution Patterns

## Summary
Introduced game fish species showed varied distribution patterns across Arizona, with Largemouth Bass achieving the widest distribution in virtually all suitable warm-water habitats including lakes, reservoirs, river channels, streams, and ranch ponds. Other species like Smallmouth Bass, Spotted Bass, and Rock Bass established more localized populations in specific drainages and tributaries.
