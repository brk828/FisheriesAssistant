# Zuni River

## Summary
The Zuni River is a tributary of the Little Colorado River in the southwestern United States. According to the 1874 description of Lepidomeda vittata, the species was first collected from the "Chiquito Colorado" (Little Colorado River) between the mouth of the Zuni River and Sierra Blanca.
