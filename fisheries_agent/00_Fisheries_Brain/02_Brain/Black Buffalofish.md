# Black Buffalofish

## Summary
Ictiobus niger (Black Buffalofish) was represented by 168 specimens ranging from 21 mm juveniles to adults weighing 25 pounds. This species appeared to be more abundant in the Verdigris River than the closely related Bigmouth Buffalofish.

## Context

> This master's thesis documents a comprehensive ichthyological survey of the [[Verdigris River]] drainage in Oklahoma conducted during summer 1956 and spring 1957.
