# Ophiocoma aethiops

## Summary
Ophiocoma aethiops is a common large brittle star found under rocks, with medium-length arm spines and dark gray to black coloration on both arms and disc. It is one of the most frequently encountered brittle star species in the Puerto Peñasco rocky intertidal.
