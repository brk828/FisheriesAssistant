# VR2 receivers

## Summary
VR2 receivers are acoustic telemetry listening stations deployed in an array of nine units throughout Stege Lagoon to detect tagged pike, with detection ranges of 100-500 meters. The receiver array provided full coverage of the lagoon outlet but only partial coverage of interior areas, with data downloaded monthly over the 18-month study period to track pike movements and residence patterns.
