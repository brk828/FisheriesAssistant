# Redfin Shiner

## Summary
The Redfin Shiner (*Notropis umbratilis*) is a small cyprinid minnow species commonly associated with the Arkansas River Basin and other North American stream systems, where it typically inhabits moderate to flowing waters with sandy or rocky substrates. As a component of diverse cyprinid-dominated fish assemblages, this shiner species represents an important member of lotic fish communities and has been documented across multiple stream surveys and locality records spanning the mid-20th century. Its presence and abundance patterns have been useful indicators of stream habitat quality and community composition in ichthyological and ecological surveys of North American freshwater systems.
