# Gonopodium

## Summary
A gonopodium is a modified anal fin found in male poeciliid fishes (family Poeciliidae, including guppies, mollies, and mosquitofishes) that functions as an intromittent organ for internal fertilization during copulation. This specialized reproductive structure exhibits significant morphological variation both within and between species, making it an important diagnostic character for taxonomy and a key factor in reproductive isolation, as differences in gonopodial structure can influence mating success and prevent hybridization between closely related species. The gonopodium's form is particularly relevant in conservation and evolutionary studies of live-bearing fishes, as its structural features reflect both phylogenetic relationships and the mechanics of sperm transfer in these internally fertilizing species.
