# Etheostoma tetrazona

## Summary
Etheostoma tetrazona is the Missouri Saddled Darter, a freshwater darter species in the family Percidae. This study included it among the additional species tested in hybridization experiments to investigate geographic variation in hybrid survival among etheostomatine fishes.
