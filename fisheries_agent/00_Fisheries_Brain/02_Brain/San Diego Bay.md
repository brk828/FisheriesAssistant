# San Diego Bay

## Summary
San Diego Bay is a marine embayment in San Diego County, California that served as a collection site for experimental fish in this study. Specifically, Fundulus parvipinnis (California killifish) were collected from a sandy point and slough in the south portion of San Diego Bay for use in anesthesia and transport experiments.
