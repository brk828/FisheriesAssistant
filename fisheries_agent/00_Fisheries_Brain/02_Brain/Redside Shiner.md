# Redside Shiner

## Summary
The Redside Shiner is a small cyprinid fish native to western North American river systems, particularly in the Colorado River basin and related drainages. As a species sensitive to habitat alterations, it serves as an ecological indicator of river health and has been impacted by dam construction, water impoundment, and irrigation diversions that transform natural free-flowing river conditions into unsuitable reservoir and tailwater environments. Understanding Redside Shiner population dynamics and habitat requirements is important for broader conservation efforts in aquatic ecosystems affected by water management infrastructure.
