# Emerald Shiner

## Summary
The Emerald Shiner (*Notropis atherinoides*) is a small, silvery cyprinid minnow commonly found in freshwater systems across North America, where it serves as an important forage fish in aquatic food webs and frequently co-occurs with other planktivorous and benthic species in rivers, lakes, and backwater habitats. As a pelagic schooling fish with high fecundity and rapid growth rates, the Emerald Shiner plays a significant ecological role as a prey species for larger predatory fish and is often used as an indicator of water quality and habitat conditions in fish community assemblages. Its presence in backwater and riverine environments makes it valuable for understanding ecosystem dynamics and the structure of fish communities in freshwater ecosystems.
