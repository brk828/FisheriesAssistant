# Sacramento-San Joaquin Province

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin Province is one of five major ichthyological provinces in California, encompassing the Central Valley and coastal drainages with similar fish faunas. It includes diverse habitats from vernal pools and alpine lakes to complex valley floor rivers supporting species like Sacramento Squawfish, Sacramento Sucker, Hardhead, California Roach, and anadromous salmonids.
