# Shortnose Sturgeon

## Summary
Shortnose sturgeon (Acipenser brevirostrum) is a benthic anadromous fish species in the family Acipenseridae that inhabits estuarine and coastal river systems, historically rare in North Carolina waters but of significant conservation concern. This species is characterized by its bottom-feeding behavior in brackish tidal environments and has been the subject of management and breeding programs at federal fish hatcheries, including facilities designed specifically for anadromous species propagation. As a long-lived sturgeon with complex life history requirements, shortnose sturgeon populations require careful monitoring and habitat protection to maintain viable populations in Atlantic coastal regions.
