# Peacock Cichlid

## Summary
Peacock Cichlid (Cichla ocellaris) is a predatory fish introduced to Florida's Dade County canals beginning in 1984, marketed as "peacock bass" to attract anglers. The introduction was justified as controlling previously introduced cichlids, but may instead impact native centrarchids including Largemouth Bass; in Gatun Lake, Panama, this species "effectively eliminated six of the eight common fish species and drastically reduced a seventh," potentially increasing mosquito populations.
