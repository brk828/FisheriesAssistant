# Pleasant Lake

## Summary
Pleasant Lake is an 80-foot deep, 20-acre stratified trout lake in Minnesota that was the site of a selective antimycin application. The treatment targeted parasitized minnows (redbelly dace, bluntnose minnows, fathead minnows, and shiners) in the warm upper water layer (epilimnion, approximately 15 feet thick) while preserving rainbow trout in the deeper cold water, using Fintrol-15 and Fintrol-5 formulations to achieve 5 ppb in the upper stratum during late summer stratification.
