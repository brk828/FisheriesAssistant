# Distributional Maps

## Summary
This source documents geographic distributions for all black bass taxa, noting that M. punctulatus occupies streams from southern Ohio to eastern Texas, M. p. henshalli is restricted to the Alabama River system, M. p. wichitae is isolated in the Wichita Mountains, M. coosae inhabits upland streams from Alabama to Savannah rivers, M. d. velox occurs in Ozark swift-water habitats, and recent surveys reveal M. punctulatus (not M. dolomieu) is most abundant in the upper Ohio River. The source emphasizes peripheral differentiation with subspecies occupying geographic extremes and intergrades in intermediate zones.
