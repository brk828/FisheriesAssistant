# Blue Sucker

## Summary
The Blue Sucker (*Cycleptus* genus) is a large-bodied freshwater fish species native to major North American river systems, including the Rio Grande, Ohio, Mississippi, and other large drainage basins, that historically occupied extensive upstream ranges before habitat degradation and dewatering fragmented populations. As a native fish of conservation concern in regions like Kentucky, the Blue Sucker represents one of many native cyprinid species affected by modern riverine alterations and is indicative of the ecological changes in large river ecosystems. This species inhabits flowing river habitats and serves as an indicator of river health and the impacts of water management practices on native fish communities.
