# Fundulus

## Summary
Fundulus is a genus of small topminnows in the family Cyprinodontidae that are widely distributed across North American freshwater and brackish ecosystems, making them ecologically important and commonly used in scientific research. These fish are notably amenable to laboratory hybridization, distinguishing them from more genetically isolated cyprinodont genera, which has made Fundulus valuable for conservation genetics, reproductive biology studies, and investigations into species boundaries and evolutionary relationships. Their broad geographic range, habitat flexibility, and reproductive tractability have established them as model organisms for aquatic ecology and fisheries science research.
