# Carpathian Mountains

## Summary
The Carpathian Mountains are a mountain range where lamprey research was conducted, particularly investigating the relationships between Lampetra danfordi and Lampetra gracilis. Studies showed that only Lampetra planeri was present in the Carpathian Mountains themselves, while both L. planeri and L. mariae occurred between Warsaw and Cracow.
