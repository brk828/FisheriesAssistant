# Chikaskia River

## Summary
The Chikaskia River is an Oklahoma river system mentioned for comparison purposes. As a turbid western stream, it yielded fewer fish species in surveys than clear eastern streams like the Neosho, Poteau, Illinois, and Little Rivers.
