# Garrison Dam

## Summary
Garrison Dam is a major hydroelectric dam on the Missouri River in North Dakota that serves as a critical location for federal fish hatchery operations focused on mitigating the ecological impacts of dam construction on native fish populations. The dam and its associated hatchery complex are central to fish restoration and management programs, particularly for species affected by altered river conditions, habitat fragmentation, and flow regulation caused by the impoundment. Garrison Dam represents a significant example of how large water infrastructure projects necessitate compensatory aquaculture and breeding programs to maintain fisheries and conserve aquatic biodiversity in heavily modified river systems.
