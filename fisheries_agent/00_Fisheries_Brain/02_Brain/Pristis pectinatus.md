# Pristis pectinatus

## Summary
Pristis pectinatus, commonly known as the sawfish, is a marine cartilaginous fish that regularly enters freshwater habitats. This checklist documents it as one of approximately 30 marine species that occur in Texas freshwater systems, reflecting the significant influence of the Gulf of Mexico on the state's aquatic fauna, particularly in estuarine and coastal plain habitats along the Texas Gulf Coast.
