# Asellidae

## Summary
Asellidae is a family of freshwater isopod crustaceans that was formally elevated from subfamily status (Asellinae) to full family rank following taxonomic revisions by Henry and Magniez in 1968-1970. These small benthic crustaceans are important components of freshwater ecosystems and are frequently used as bioindicators of water quality and environmental conditions in aquatic ecology and conservation biology studies. The family includes the genus Asellus and related forms that were previously classified within a polyphyletic assemblage but have since been reorganized to reflect more accurate evolutionary relationships.
