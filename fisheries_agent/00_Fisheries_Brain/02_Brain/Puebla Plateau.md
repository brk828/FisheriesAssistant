# Puebla Plateau

## Summary
The Puebla Plateau is an intermontane region in central Mexico located between the volcanoes Iztaccihuatl and Popocatepetl on the west and Volcan La Malinche on the east. This plateau, from which the new isopod species Asellus puebla takes its name, represents the southernmost known distribution of the genus Asellus at just below 20° N latitude, extending the genus's range approximately 10 degrees south of its previously known limit.
