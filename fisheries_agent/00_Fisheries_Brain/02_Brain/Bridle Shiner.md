# Bridle Shiner

## Summary
Bridle Shiner is a fish species found in Lawrence Brook, Massachusetts prior to the 1955 rotenone reclamation. This source lists it among the warm-water species present in the pre-treatment fish community but provides no additional details about its abundance or role.
