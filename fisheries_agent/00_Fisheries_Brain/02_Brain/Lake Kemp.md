# Lake Kemp

## Summary
Lake Kemp is a highly saline inland reservoir in North Central Texas that served as an experimental introduction site for marine game fish species, particularly Redfish and Speckled Weakfish, during the 1950s Texas Game and Fish Commission fisheries programs. The lake's unusual salinity regime made it unsuitable for native freshwater game fish but presented a unique opportunity to establish novel sport fisheries by transplanting euryhaline marine species from Gulf of Mexico populations. This experimental approach represented an innovative management strategy to create productive game fish populations in otherwise underutilized aquatic habitats.
