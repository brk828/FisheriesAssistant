# Deyoe

## Summary
Deyoe was a researcher who conducted studies on amino acid supplementation and feeding practices at Kansas State University. His 1968 work with Tiemeier showed that low-animal-protein diets (2.5% of ration) performed as well as high-animal-protein diets (17.3%) when supplemented with lysine and methionine, and that splitting the daily ration (50% morning, 50% afternoon) was more efficient than single feeding.
