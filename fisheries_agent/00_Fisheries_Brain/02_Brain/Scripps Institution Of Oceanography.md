# Scripps Institution Of Oceanography

## Summary
Scripps Institution of Oceanography is a leading marine research facility affiliated with the University of California, San Diego, that has played a significant role in ichthyological and aquatic ecological research, as evidenced by its involvement in fish collection documentation and correspondence with prominent ichthyologists studying North American fish fauna. The institution served as an important hub for fish science research and the maintenance of historical ichthyological collections during the mid-twentieth century, contributing to broader understanding of fish distributions, species ranges, and freshwater fish biodiversity in regions such as Mexico and the southwestern United States.
