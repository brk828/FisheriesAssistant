# Yucca Elata

## Summary
Yucca Elata (recorded by Blumer as Y. radicosa) is a yucca species that was a characteristic component of the Upper Sonoran grassland vegetation. Blumer observed this species on the incline descending from the Gleeson foothills toward Sulfur Springs Valley as part of the unusual altitudinal zone inversion in the region.
