# Rafinesque

## Summary
Rafinesque was a 19th-century naturalist who in 1820 coined the phrase "the art of seeing well" in reference to taxonomic observation skills. This source uses Rafinesque's concept to frame the study's findings that qualitative morphological assessment—a trained perceptual ability requiring perseverance and discipline rather than exceptional cognitive faculty—can effectively discriminate closely related fish species under field conditions.
