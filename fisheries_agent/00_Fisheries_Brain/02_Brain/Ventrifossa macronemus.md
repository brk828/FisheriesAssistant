# Ventrifossa macronemus

## Summary
Ventrifossa macronemus is a deep-sea macrourid fish species endemic to the Philippine Subregion. This source notes it is most nearly related to V. misakia found in Japan and eastern Luzon, and it was collected during the Albatross expedition in the Sulu Sea complex.
