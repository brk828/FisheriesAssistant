# Anguillidae

## Summary
Anguillidae is a family of catadromous freshwater eels that exhibit complex diadromous life histories, spawning in marine environments (notably the Sargasso Sea) before their larvae migrate to freshwater systems where they spend most of their adult lives. Members of this family, such as the American eel, are ecologically important in linking marine and freshwater ecosystems and have significant conservation concerns due to declining populations and barriers to migration in many regions. The family's obligate use of both marine and freshwater habitats makes them key indicators of ecosystem connectivity and water resource management practices.
