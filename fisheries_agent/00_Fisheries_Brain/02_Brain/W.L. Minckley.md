# W.L. Minckley

## Summary
W.L. Minckley was a pioneering ichthyologist and conservation biologist who dedicated his career to the study and preservation of desert fish fauna in the American Southwest, particularly in isolated spring and stream systems of the Sonoran Desert region. He was instrumental in establishing the Desert Fishes Recovery Team and co-editing foundational works such as *Battle Against Extinction*, which synthesized scientific, historical, and ethical perspectives on the decline of these vulnerable species. Minckley's work was central to early conservation efforts for federally endangered desert fishes and helped shape modern approaches to managing relict aquatic populations facing habitat loss and extinction risk.
