# Jeffrey

## Summary
Jeffrey was an Auburn University researcher who conducted pond aeration experiments in 1969. Using 0.1-acre ponds stocked with 8,000 fingerlings plus 90 bass fry per acre, he demonstrated that aeration (up to 100 ft³ per minute through submerged perforated PVC pipe) increased yields from 3,321 to 4,311 pounds per acre, allowed feeding rates up to 116.4 pounds per acre per day, and provided benefits including higher dissolved oxygen, higher pH, lower dissolved CO₂, and eliminated stratification.

## Context

> This 1969 review synthesizes the first decade of research on commercial [[Channel Catfish]] nutrition in the United States. Despite only four research stations intensively studying catfish feeding, excellent practical rations have been developed yielding 1 pound of gain per 1.5-2.0 pounds of feed....
