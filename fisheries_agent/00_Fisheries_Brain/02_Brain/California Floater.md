# California Floater

## Summary
The California Floater is a freshwater mussel species native to inland aquatic habitats in the southwestern United States, particularly in Arizona's river basins including the Gila River, Little Colorado River, Colorado River, and Bill Williams River systems. As a benthic macroinvertebrate inhabiting springs, streams, and rivers, it serves as an indicator of freshwater ecosystem health and has become a species of conservation concern due to habitat degradation from dam construction, water regulation, and the introduction of invasive species. The species was considered for federal listing as a candidate species in the early 1990s due to its restricted range and vulnerability to anthropogenic alterations of its aquatic habitats.
