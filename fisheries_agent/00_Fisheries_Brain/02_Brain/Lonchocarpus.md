# Lonchocarpus

## Summary
Lonchocarpus is a plant genus from which rotenone is derived, along with Derris. The roots of Lonchocarpus plants serve as a natural source of rotenone, which is processed into commercial piscicide formulations used for eliminating undesirable fish species in California waters.
