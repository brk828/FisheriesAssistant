# Craig Power Station

## Summary
Craig Power Station is an existing water project on the Yampa River that depletes 19,200 acre-feet of water annually, making it the largest single depletion among existing projects. This source includes it in the environmental baseline condition used to establish minimum flow requirements for protecting endangered fish species in the Yampa River.
