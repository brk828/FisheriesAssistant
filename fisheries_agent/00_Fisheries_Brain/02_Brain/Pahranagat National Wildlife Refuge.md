# Pahranagat National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Pahranagat National Wildlife Refuge is a protected area in Pahranagat Valley, Nevada. This source identifies it as a potential site for habitat restoration and reintroduction of Pahranagat Roundtail Chub, with the recovery plan calling for identification of suitable habitats both on and off the refuge and eventual reestablishment of chub populations in refuge waters.
