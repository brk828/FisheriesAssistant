# Riprap

## Summary
Riprap refers to a structured framework or protocol within the Colorado River Fishes Recovery Program that establishes systematic procedures for data management, monitoring, and interagency coordination in endangered fish species recovery efforts. As evidenced in recovery planning documents, Riprap tasks (such as Task V.A.2) outline specific requirements for standardized data collection, metadata practices, and information-sharing protocols among multiple agencies involved in long-term fish population monitoring and conservation. This governance structure ensures that critical recovery data—including PIT tagging records and genetic management information—are systematically compiled, maintained, and accessible across the multi-agency recovery program.
