# Caudal Fin

## Summary
The caudal fin, commonly known as the tail fin, is the primary locomotor structure of fish located at the posterior end of the body that generates thrust for movement through water. Composed of fin rays supported by complex musculature and skeletal elements, the caudal fin varies in shape and morphology across fish species, influencing swimming performance and ecological behavior. Its anatomical characteristics, including fin ray count and overall morphology, are important taxonomic features used in fish identification and classification across different species and populations.
