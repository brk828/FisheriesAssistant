# Colorado River Endangered Fishes Recovery Implementation Program

## Summary
The Colorado River Endangered Fishes Recovery Implementation Program is a cooperative effort established to manage endangered fish species in the upper Colorado River Basin (excluding the San Juan River). While its management goal is to establish self-sustaining populations of endangered fishes including the razorback sucker, this source notes it is not a regulatory mechanism and may be amended, terminated, or have parties withdraw, making it inadequate as a protective measure under Factor D of the Endangered Species Act analysis.
