# Lake County

## Summary
Lake County was the top-ranked black bass county in 1960 with 2,523 fish reported from 82 reports (having ranked 4th in 1948), and the second-ranked catfish county with 2,798 fish reported from 60 reports. The shift in its black bass ranking reflects the increasing popularity of newer fishing waters and the growing importance of low-elevation reservoirs.
