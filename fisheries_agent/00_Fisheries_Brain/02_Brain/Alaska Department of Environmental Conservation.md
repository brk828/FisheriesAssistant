# Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation

## Summary
The Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation (ADEC) is a state regulatory agency with management jurisdiction over water quality standards in Alaska. This review was funded in part by ADEC and provides recommendations for reconsidering current TDS monitoring standards to focus on specific ion limits rather than total TDS, particularly to protect sensitive salmonid species during spawning periods.
