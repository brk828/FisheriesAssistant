# Animas River

## Summary
The Animas River is a tributary in the Colorado River Basin identified as important habitat for endangered Colorado River fishes. This source recommends extending critical habitat for Colorado Squawfish into the Animas River to the state line and for Razorback Sucker into the Animas River to the state line from its confluence with the San Juan River, based on historic captures and the potential for expanded population occupation.
