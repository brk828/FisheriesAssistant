# Lake Trout

## Summary
Lake Trout (*Salvelinus namaycush*) is a coldwater salmonid species native to deep oligotrophic lakes of North America, characterized by its role as a top piscivorous predator in lacustrine ecosystems and its importance in both recreational and commercial fisheries. In conservation and hatchery management contexts, Lake Trout serves as a key species for mitigation efforts related to habitat alteration and water resource development, being routinely cultured at federal fish hatcheries to support population recovery and fishery restoration programs. Its dietary preferences, habitat requirements, and ecological interactions make it a valuable indicator species for assessing the health and productivity of cold, deep-water lake environments.
