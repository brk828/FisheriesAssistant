# Rucks

## Summary
Rucks conducted a 1984 study of five creek and river systems in the region, finding that only Aravaipa Creek (excluded from cattle since 1973) showed extensive successful regeneration after the 1978-1979 flood. This comparative study provided important context for understanding the role of cattle grazing in preventing riparian regeneration on Bonita Creek.

## Context

> This 1987 baseline report documents vegetation and breeding bird communities along a 3 km cattle exclosure established in 1984 on [[Lower Bonita Creek]], [[Graham County]], [[Arizona]].
