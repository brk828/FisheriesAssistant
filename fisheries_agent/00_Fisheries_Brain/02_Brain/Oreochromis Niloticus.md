# Oreochromis Niloticus

## Summary
Oreochromis niloticus, commonly known as the Nile tilapia, is a cichlid fish species native to Africa that has become one of the most widely introduced and invasive fish species globally due to its rapid growth, hardiness, and aquaculture value. In conservation and ecology contexts, O. niloticus represents a significant threat to native fish communities and ecosystem integrity, as its introduction into non-native waters can lead to competitive displacement of endemic species, predation on native fauna, and trophic restructuring of aquatic ecosystems. This species is frequently studied in conjunction with other introduced tilapiines as a model for understanding the ecological consequences of fish introductions and the mechanisms of biological invasions in freshwater systems.
