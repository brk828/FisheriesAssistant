# Inland Water Resources and Aquaculture Service

## Summary
The Inland Water Resources and Aquaculture Service is an organizational unit within FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations). According to this source, André G. Coche from this service compiled the comprehensive bibliography of freshwater aquaculture literature covering 1951-1984.

## Context
