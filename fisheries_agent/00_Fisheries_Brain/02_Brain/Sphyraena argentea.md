# Sphyraena argentea

## Summary
Sphyraena argentea (Pacific barracuda) is represented at the site by 11 bones, including a vertebra from a fish approximately 34 inches long and 5 pounds. This offshore species ordinarily occurs offshore but is not strictly pelagic, and was probably taken most frequently from boats but may have been caught occasionally from shore.
