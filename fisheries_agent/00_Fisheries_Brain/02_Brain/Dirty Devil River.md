# Dirty Devil River

## Summary
The Dirty Devil River is one of the major tributaries flowing into Lake Powell within Glen Canyon National Recreation Area. According to this source, it is one of the flowing river portions included in the management area, along with the Colorado River, Escalante River, Paria River, and San Juan River, and is part of the watershed draining into the reservoir created by Glen Canyon Dam.
