# El Dorado County

## Summary
El Dorado County is a California county containing Echo Lake. This source identifies El Dorado County as the location of Echo Lake, which received Kokanee plantings in 1944-1945 but showed the most disappointing results with poor growth (fish not exceeding 8-9 inches at maturity).

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
