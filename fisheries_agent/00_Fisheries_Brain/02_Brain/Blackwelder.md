# Blackwelder

## Summary
Blackwelder identified and named four distinct glaciations in the Mono Basin in 1931: McGee, Sherwin, Tahoe, and Tioga. He correlated these with midwestern glacial episodes (Nebraskan, Kansan, Iowan, and late Wisconsin).
