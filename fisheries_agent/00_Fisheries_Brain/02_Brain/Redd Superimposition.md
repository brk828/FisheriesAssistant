# Redd Superimposition

## Summary
Redd superimposition occurs when spawning salmon dig nests (redds) that disturb previously deposited eggs from earlier spawners. In this study, redd superimposition was a major cause of egg loss during the spawning period, particularly in the middle and lower stream areas where finer substrates allowed females to more easily dislodge materials; the upper area's coarse cobbles reduced this effect, resulting in 78% egg retention compared to only 31-38% in other areas.
