# Fish

## Summary
Fish, in the context of fish science and aquatic ecology, are mobile aquatic vertebrates that serve as key indicators of ecosystem health and important subjects for population monitoring, though their high mobility can complicate certain types of environmental assessments. As organisms that respond to water quality changes and environmental disturbances, fish are central to fisheries management and conservation efforts, yet their migratory behavior and active movement patterns can limit their utility for continuous, stationary monitoring compared to more sedentary aquatic organisms. In research and resource management, fish populations are studied through marking and tracking techniques to understand movement patterns, population dynamics, and responses to environmental change across freshwater and marine ecosystems.
