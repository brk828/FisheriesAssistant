# Fontinalis

## Summary
Fontinalis is the dominant genus of bryophytes (aquatic mosses) in the Blue River. These bryophytes formed dense populations on granite substrates and supported extensive aufwuchs (attached algal) communities on their streamers, contributing significantly to the exceptionally high primary productivity observed in granite reaches.
