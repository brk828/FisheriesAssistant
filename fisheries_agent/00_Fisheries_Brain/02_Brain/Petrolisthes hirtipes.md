# Petrolisthes hirtipes

## Summary
Petrolisthes hirtipes is a porcelain crab species found in the Puerto Peñasco region. This field guide documents it among the anomuran crabs of the Gulf of California intertidal zone, identified by Clinton Westervelt consulting with Dr. Janet Haig.
