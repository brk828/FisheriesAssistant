# Zebra-tailed Lizard

## Summary
The Zebra-tailed Lizard (Callisaurus draconoides) is a desert reptile species. This biological assessment confirmed its presence at Papago Park Military Reservation as one of eight reptile species identified during surveys of the Sonoran desertscrub habitat.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
