# Stoneworts

## Summary
Stoneworts are complex algae forms, including the genera Chara and Nitella, that resemble higher plants in their structure but are typically grouped with algae. Despite their plant-like appearance, they are considered part of the diverse assemblage of algae found in freshwater environments.
