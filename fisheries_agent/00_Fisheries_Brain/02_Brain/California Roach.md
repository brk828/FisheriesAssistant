# California Roach

## Summary
The California Roach (*Hesperoleucus symmetricus*) is a small-bodied cyprinid endemic to California streams, originally described in 1854 from specimens collected at Fort Miller on the San Joaquin River. This native fish species has experienced significant range contraction throughout Sierra Nevada foothill streams and associated drainages, as documented in surveys comparing historical distributions with mid-20th century populations. The California Roach is recognized as a species of conservation concern due to its sensitivity to habitat degradation and displacement by introduced species in California's aquatic ecosystems.
