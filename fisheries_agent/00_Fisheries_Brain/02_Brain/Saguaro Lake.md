# Saguaro Lake

## Summary
Saguaro Lake is a warm-water desert reservoir in Arizona characterized by high recreational development and accessibility via paved roads, which distinguishes it from less-developed nearby reservoirs like Bartlett Lake. In the context of fish conservation and aquatic ecology, Saguaro Lake represents a heavily utilized human-impacted aquatic ecosystem that contrasts with low-disturbance reference sites, making it relevant for studying how recreational pressure and development affect fish populations and habitat quality in arid-region water bodies.
