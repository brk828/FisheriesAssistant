# Arizona Trout

## Summary
Arizona Trout (*Salmo apache*) is a native salmonid species endemic to Arizona streams, particularly in the Apache County region, that has become a focal species for conservation and fisheries management efforts aimed at restoring native fish communities. The species has been subject to restoration projects involving the removal of introduced salmonids (such as Rainbow Trout) through piscicide treatments, with subsequent reintroduction programs relying on hatchery-reared populations to rebuild wild populations. As a native trout species, Arizona Trout serves as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health and is vulnerable to parasitic infections, including trematodes and gyrodactylid monogeneans, some of which may be shared with introduced salmonid competitors in sympatric populations.
