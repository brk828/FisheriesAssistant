# Pyramid Lake Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
Pyramid Lake Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii henshawi) is a subspecies of cutthroat trout native to Pyramid Lake, Nevada. During the May 1989 survey, two specimens were collected: one during boat operations and one at the Truckee River mouth.

## Context

> Field survey conducted May 4-5, 1989 at [[Pyramid Lake]], Nevada to assess hybridization between endangered [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) and [[Tahoe Sucker]] (*Catostomus tahoensis*).
