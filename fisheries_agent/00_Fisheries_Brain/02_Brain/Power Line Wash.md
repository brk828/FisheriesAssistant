# Power Line Wash

## Summary
Power Line Wash is a named sampling location along Lake Mohave reservoir on the Colorado River. This 1986 survey identified Power Line Wash as one of several sites targeted for experimental gill netting and electrofishing surveys of Razorback Sucker populations.

## Context

> This document records experimental gill netting and electrofishing surveys conducted on [[Lake Mohave]] in May 1986 targeting [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*).
