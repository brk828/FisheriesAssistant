# Ammocrypta

## Summary
Ammocrypta is a genus of small percid fishes commonly known as sand darters, characterized by their specialization for inhabiting sandy substrates in freshwater streams and rivers where they burrow into sand and fine sediment for concealment and foraging. These benthic darters represent an important component of stream fish diversity and community structure, particularly in North American aquatic ecosystems, and have been subjects of significant taxonomic revision and conservation attention due to their habitat specificity and vulnerability to environmental degradation. Their sand-dwelling behavior and ecological requirements make them valuable indicators of stream health and sandy-bottom habitat conditions in riverine systems.
