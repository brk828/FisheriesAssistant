# Dreissena

## Summary
Dreissena is a genus of freshwater mussels in the family Dreissenidae, including the invasive zebra mussel (D. polymorpha) and quagga mussel (D. rostriformis bugensis) studied here in Lake Erie and the Great Lakes. This study found that both eDNA and eRNA shedding rates from Dreissena increased significantly with mussel abundance, and that nuclear DNA markers (18S, H2B) were released at higher initial concentrations than mitochondrial markers (16S, COI), with eDNA half-lives ranging from 13-21 hours and eRNA degrading approximately 4-5 hours faster, enabling the eRNA:eDNA ratio to serve as a temporal predictor for estimating the age of genetic material and improving early detection of these invasive populations.
