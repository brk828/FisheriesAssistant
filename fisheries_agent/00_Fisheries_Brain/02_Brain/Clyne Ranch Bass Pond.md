# Clyne Ranch Bass Pond

## Summary
Clyne Ranch Bass Pond is a private water body in southern Arizona that was surveyed in 1991 to test experimental gill nets and trammel nets. The pond contained many Largemouth Bass averaging 1.5 pounds and bluegill averaging 0.5 pounds, plus hundreds of small bluegill, though the experimental gill nets proved ineffective for capturing small fish.
