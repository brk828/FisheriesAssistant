# Sagehen Creek

## Summary
Sagehen Creek is a location where Piute sculpin populations have been studied, providing comparative data for Lake Tahoe populations. Growth rates in Lake Tahoe sculpins were similar to Sagehen Creek populations, though Tahoe fish were slightly larger at most ages.

## Context

> This comprehensive life history study of the [[Piute Sculpin]] (*Cottus beldingii*) in [[Lake Tahoe]] examined 6,424 specimens collected from September 1963 through September 1964.
