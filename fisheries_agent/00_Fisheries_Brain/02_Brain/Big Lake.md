# Big Lake

## Summary
Big Lake is a high-elevation Arizona freshwater lake managed as a recreational fishery that experiences seasonal environmental stresses including winter ice-over, snow cover, low water levels, and dense aquatic macrophyte growth that periodically result in fish winter kills. As documented in Arizona Game and Fish Department fisheries surveys from the late 1950s and 1960s, the lake was subject to active management interventions including post-winter-kill restocking with hatchery rainbow trout to maintain sport fishing opportunities and was monitored through creel census programs to assess catch rates and fish population demographics. Big Lake thus represents a typical example of high-elevation lentic fisheries in Arizona's Region I that required adaptive management strategies to sustain viable fish populations against recurring environmental challenges.
