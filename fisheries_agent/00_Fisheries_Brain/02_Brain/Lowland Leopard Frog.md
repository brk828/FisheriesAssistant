# Lowland Leopard Frog

## Summary
The Lowland Leopard Frog is an amphibian species associated with native freshwater stream ecosystems in southeastern Arizona, particularly in the Fort Huachuca and Huachuca Mountains region, where it co-occurs with endemic fish species in areas of high biological diversity. As a candidate species for federal protection, the Lowland Leopard Frog serves as an indicator of aquatic habitat integrity and pristine hydrological conditions in the Gila River Basin, with its presence reflecting the health of relatively unaltered native aquatic communities. Conservation of this species is linked to maintaining the ecological integrity of intact freshwater stream systems that support multiple endemic taxa in this biodiversity hotspot.
