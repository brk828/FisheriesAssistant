# Chicago

## Summary
Chicago is a major Midwest market city where depth interviews with buyers in July-August 1968 revealed overwhelmingly negative perceptions of Montana fish products. Transportation costs from Montana to Chicago (8¢/pound) were significantly higher than from Iowa (2.5¢/pound), placing Montana at a competitive disadvantage, and Montana fishermen reported being cheated by wholesalers in this market.
