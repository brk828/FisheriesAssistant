# Lund Spring

## Summary
Lund Spring is a historic White River Spinedace habitat in White Pine County, Nevada, designated as critical habitat (approximately 1.3 acres of spring and outflow) in 1985. The spinedace population at this spring was extirpated after the 1985 listing when improvements to an irrigation diversion structure eliminated water leakage, causing periodic desiccation of the outflow ditches where the fish lived.
