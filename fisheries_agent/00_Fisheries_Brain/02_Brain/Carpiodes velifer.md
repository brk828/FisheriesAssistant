# Carpiodes velifer

## Summary
Carpiodes velifer is the highfin carpsucker, a species of sucker. This source records it as one of three additional species documented from Kansas waters of the Verdigris River system that was not collected during the 1956-1957 Oklahoma survey, suggesting limited distribution or rarity in the drainage.
