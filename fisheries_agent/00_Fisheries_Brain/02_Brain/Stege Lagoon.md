# Stege Lagoon

## Summary
Stege Lagoon is a shallow brackish lagoon covering 5.69 km² with a mean depth of 1.3 m (maximum 3.7 m) located on Møn Island in the western Baltic Sea, Denmark. The lagoon normally maintains salinity of 8-10 ppt but experiences fluctuations due to wind-driven water exchange, with occasional extreme inflows reaching 17-20 ppt. It connects to Stege Bay through a narrow 60-meter outlet and receives freshwater input from Lendemark Brook, providing critical spawning habitat for Northern Pike living at the edge of their salinity tolerance.
