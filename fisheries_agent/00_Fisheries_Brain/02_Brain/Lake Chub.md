# Lake Chub

## Summary
Lake Chub (*Couesius plumbeus*) is a small cyprinid fish species widely distributed across northern freshwater ecosystems of North America, serving as an important indicator taxon for assessing the health and composition of boreal and subarctic aquatic communities. Characterized by a slender, silvery body with a translucent greenish back and moderately large scales, this species occupies diverse cold-water habitats and plays a significant ecological role in food webs as both a forage fish and predator of aquatic invertebrates. Its abundance and presence in northern water bodies make it valuable for monitoring environmental conditions and understanding the structure of fish communities in high-latitude freshwater systems.
