# Starvation Reservoir

## Summary
Starvation Reservoir is a water storage facility in the Upper Colorado River Basin where fisheries management practices may impact endangered fish species. The recovery plan calls for modifying management of this reservoir's fisheries between 1993-1997 to minimize impacts on endangered fish as part of nonnative species management efforts.
