# Typhlomolge rathbuni

## Summary
Typhlomolge rathbuni (synonym of Eurycea rathbuni) is the Texas Blind Salamander, a subterranean species for which the artesian well site is the type-locality. It is one of several blind, depigmented organisms found in the same Edwards Aquifer habitat as Haideoporus texanus.

## Context

> This study describes the larva of *[[Haideoporus texanus]]*, the first hypogeous (subterranean) dytiscid beetle discovered in North America.
