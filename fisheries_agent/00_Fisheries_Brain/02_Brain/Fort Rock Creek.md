# Fort Rock Creek

## Summary
Fort Rock Creek is a locality in Yavapai County, Arizona where paratype specimens of Pantosteus eremicus (now Catostomus clarki, the desert sucker) were collected. The collection (UMMZ 162830) contained 351 specimens and represents important type material for this sucker species.
