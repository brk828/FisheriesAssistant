# Carp

## Summary
Carp are freshwater cyprinid fishes that have been significant in both ecological and conservation contexts, particularly in North American aquatic systems. In the Colorado River Basin and associated reservoirs, carp represent an important comparative reference point when documenting native fish communities and assessing the status of imperiled endemic species like bonytail and razorback sucker. Carp are frequently encountered in fish sampling surveys and represent part of the broader fish assemblage composition used to characterize aquatic ecosystem health and biodiversity in managed water bodies.
