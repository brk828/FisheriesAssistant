# Otis-Burnham-White-Anderson Method

## Summary
The Otis-Burnham-White-Anderson Method is a statistical technique published in 1978 used to estimate population size from capture data. In this study, this method estimated the Desert Pupfish population at Quitobaquito Pond at approximately 3,533 individuals with a variance of ±281, yielding the same population estimate as the alternative Ricker Method (1975). However, researchers concluded that the actual population likely exceeded this statistical estimate.
