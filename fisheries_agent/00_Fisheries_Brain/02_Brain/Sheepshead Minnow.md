# Sheepshead Minnow

## Summary
The Sheepshead Minnow (*Cyprinodon variegatus*) is a small, hardy, euryhaline fish species native to coastal brackish and saline waters of the Atlantic and Gulf coasts of North America, widely used as a surrogate species in toxicological and environmental impact studies. In conservation and fisheries research, it serves as a model organism for assessing the effects of contaminants and environmental stressors on fish populations, particularly in comparative studies with endangered species like the Pecos Pupfish that are difficult or inappropriate to test directly. Its robustness, ease of culture in laboratory settings, and ecological similarity to threatened endemic fishes make it valuable for regulatory testing and conservation decision-making related to habitat protection and water quality management.
