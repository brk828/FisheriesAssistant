# Department of Soil & Water Science

## Summary
Department of Soil & Water Science is an academic department at the University of Arizona focused on soil and water science research and education. The department was listed in the 1987 directory as one of the educational and research institutions providing critical research and education capacity for water and aquatic resource management.
