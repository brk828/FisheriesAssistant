# Aravaipa Watershed

## Summary
The Aravaipa Watershed is a location in Arizona that has experienced chronic problems with non-native fish introductions from stock tanks, including Green Sunfish and other species. A 2-year eradication program using chlorine as an ichthyotoxin was planned to begin in May 1987, utilizing a Bureau of Land Management fire helicopter for volunteer transport to address these non-native fish populations.
