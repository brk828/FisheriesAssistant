# Sphyraenidae

## Summary
Sphyraenidae is the barracuda family represented at the site by 11 bones from Pacific barracuda. The largest specimen was approximately 34 inches long and 5 pounds, and this offshore species was probably taken most frequently from boats but may have been caught occasionally from shore.
