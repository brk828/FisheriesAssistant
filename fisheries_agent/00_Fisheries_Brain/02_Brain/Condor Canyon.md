# Condor Canyon

## Summary
Condor Canyon is a 5-mile section of Meadow Valley Wash in Lincoln County, Nevada that contains the only known extant population of Big Spring Spinedace. The stream through the canyon averages 9 feet wide and 0.6 feet deep with base flow of approximately 200 gallons per minute from Delmue Springs, supporting habitat with deep pools, shallow marshy areas, and aquatic vegetation including watercress, pondweed, and rushes. Critical habitat designation covers 4 stream miles with 50-foot riparian zones, and the area faces threats from livestock grazing, vehicle use, water diversion, and nonnative species introductions.
