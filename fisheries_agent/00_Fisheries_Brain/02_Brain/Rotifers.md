# Rotifers

## Summary
Rotifers were one of the three dominant zooplankton groups in Lake Mead, represented primarily by the genus Keratella. Keratella is an indicator of eutrophic conditions and showed a spring maximum (February-April at 0-30m depth) and a fall maximum (September at 0-10m depth), confirming the enriched conditions of the reservoir.
