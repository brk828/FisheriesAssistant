# Fish Stocking Programs

## Summary
Fish stocking programs are systematic initiatives to introduce hatchery-reared fish into natural or managed aquatic systems to supplement wild populations, restore depleted fisheries, or establish new populations in historically fishless waters. These programs, typically coordinated through federal and state hatchery systems, serve multiple conservation and resource management objectives including mitigating habitat loss, supporting recreational and commercial fisheries, and restoring anadromous species to their native ranges. Stocking programs represent a central tool in fisheries management policy, though their effectiveness depends on careful consideration of ecological compatibility, genetic impacts on wild populations, and long-term ecosystem consequences.
