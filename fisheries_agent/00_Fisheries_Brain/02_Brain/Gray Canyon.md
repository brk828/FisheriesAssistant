# Gray Canyon

## Summary
Gray Canyon is a section of the Upper Green River in Utah that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly Colorado squawfish and other endemic fishes. Located between the Duchesne River and Yampa River confluence, this flatwater or low-gradient reach contrasts sharply with adjacent whitewater canyon sections and provides essential spawning and rearing habitat for these species. The canyon represents an important conservation area for maintaining populations of threatened native fishes in the Colorado River basin, as fish are notably absent from the more turbulent whitewater canyon reaches upstream and downstream.
