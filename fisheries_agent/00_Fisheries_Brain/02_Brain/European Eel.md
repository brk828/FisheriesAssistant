# European Eel

## Summary
The European Eel (Anguilla anguilla) is a catadromous fish species that undertakes complex long-distance migrations between freshwater habitats and the Sargasso Sea for spawning, making it a significant subject in fisheries management and conservation research. The species faces critical conservation challenges related to migration barriers, habitat loss, and declining populations, as evidenced by historical fisheries management studies examining obstacles to silver eel migration in river systems like the River Bann in Northern Ireland. Understanding European Eel population dynamics, movement patterns, and the environmental factors affecting recruitment and survival is essential for both ecological assessment and sustainable fisheries management in European aquatic ecosystems.
