# Paradise Valley

## Summary
Paradise Valley is a geographic area in the Salt River Valley region where predevelopment groundwater flow direction was from north to south, distinct from the east-to-west flow pattern along the Salt River flood plain. This area was part of the broader study region examining predevelopment hydrologic conditions in east Salt River Valley, Arizona.

## Context

> This U.S. Geological Survey report, prepared in cooperation with the U.S. Bureau of Indian Affairs, documents predevelopment hydrologic conditions in the [[Salt River Indian Reservation]] area prior to non-Indian settlement (pre-1870s).
