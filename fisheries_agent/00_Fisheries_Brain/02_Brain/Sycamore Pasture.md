# Sycamore Pasture

## Summary
Sycamore Pasture is a pasture along Sycamore Creek in the Dos S Unit that would be closed to grazing for the first 10 years under the proposed plan, except for livestock movement between upland pastures (twice yearly, less than one day per crossing, with no holding in the riparian pasture). Exclusion of livestock from this area may improve habitat for native fishes through increased channel stability and habitat diversity.
