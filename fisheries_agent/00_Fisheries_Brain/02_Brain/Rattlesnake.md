# Rattlesnake

## Summary
Rattlesnake is a venomous snake species known to the Northern Utes, who had a specific term for it in their language: /tukWua=p3/. The presence of rattlesnakes in the Northern Utes' fauna documentation suggests their traditional territory included semi-arid to temperate habitat conditions.
