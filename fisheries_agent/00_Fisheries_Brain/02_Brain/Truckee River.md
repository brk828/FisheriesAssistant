# Truckee River

## Summary
The Truckee River is a critical spawning tributary for the endangered cui-ui (*Chasmistes cujus*), an endemic lacustrine fish species that inhabits Pyramid Lake in Nevada and requires seasonal access to riverine habitat for reproduction. As the primary spawning migration corridor between the alkaline Pyramid Lake and suitable gravel spawning grounds, the lower Truckee River has become a focal point for cui-ui recovery efforts, particularly through fish passage structures like the Marble Bluff Fish Facility that enable upstream movement and compensate for historical habitat degradation and flow alterations. The river's hydrology, water quality, and fish passage are therefore essential management considerations for the recovery of this potamodromous species.
