# Garter Snake

## Summary
The Garter Snake (Thamnophis sirtalis) is a common non-venomous snake found throughout Michigan, identifiable by its dark coloration with three pale yellow stripes running the total length of its body. According to this source, it is found in almost any situation but shows a marked preference for moist habitat. Large numbers of young (12-70) are born alive in late summer.
