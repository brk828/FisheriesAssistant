# Colorado River Endangered Fishes Conservation Plan

## Summary
The Colorado River Endangered Fishes Conservation Plan is a Region 6 management strategy that outlines approaches for recovering endangered Colorado River fish species from 1983-1996 and beyond. The Plan calls for using hatchery fish for research and management studies, stocking to reestablish populations and increase Upper Basin stocks after 1987, and potentially developing a sport fishery for Colorado Squawfish, while emphasizing that hatchery production is a management tool rather than an alternative to natural habitat protection.
