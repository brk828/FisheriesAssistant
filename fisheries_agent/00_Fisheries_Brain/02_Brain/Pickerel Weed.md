# Pickerel Weed

## Summary
Pickerel weed is an emergent flowering aquatic plant that grows in freshwater systems. This source identifies it as one of the types of aquatic vegetation found in New York State waters, grouped with water lilies and lotus as examples of flowering plants that may be considered weeds when they interfere with human uses.
