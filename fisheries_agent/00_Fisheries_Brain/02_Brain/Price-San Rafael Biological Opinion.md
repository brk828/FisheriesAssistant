# Price-San Rafael Biological Opinion

## Summary
The Price-San Rafael Biological Opinion is a Section 7 consultation under the Endangered Species Act addressing water development impacts on endangered fish in the Upper Colorado River Basin. As of April 1990, this opinion was pending, with an unresolved question regarding water rights retention for the Desert Lake Waterfowl Management Area.
