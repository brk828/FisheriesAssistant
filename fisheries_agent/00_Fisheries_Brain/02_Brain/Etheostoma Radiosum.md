# Etheostoma Radiosum

## Summary
Etheostoma radiosum, commonly known as the orangebelly darter, is a small benthic percid fish native to North American freshwater systems that inhabits the bottom substrates of streams and rivers. As a bottom-dwelling darter species, it is ecologically important as an indicator of stream health and has been studied in relation to parasitic infections, including gyrodactylid monogeneans, making it relevant to both conservation and disease ecology research. The species' presence in geographically distinct aquatic systems with varying water chemistry makes it a useful model organism for understanding how environmental conditions affect fish populations and their associated parasitic communities.
