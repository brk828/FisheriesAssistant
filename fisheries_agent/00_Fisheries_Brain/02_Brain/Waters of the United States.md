# Waters of the United States

## Summary
Waters of the United States is a regulatory term defining aquatic areas subject to federal jurisdiction under the Clean Water Act and Rivers and Harbors Act. According to this source, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers administers Nationwide Permits that authorize certain activities with minimal environmental impact in these waters, including wetlands, streams, spawning areas, and waterfowl breeding areas. The permits establish standardized conditions and protections for these waters, including requirements to avoid impacts to spawning areas during spawning seasons, protect waterfowl breeding areas, prevent disruption of aquatic species movements, and maintain special aquatic sites including submerged aquatic vegetation areas.
