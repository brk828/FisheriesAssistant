# Cichla

## Summary
Cichla is a genus of large predatory cichlids marketed as "peacock bass" or "butterfly cichlid" to attract anglers. Florida introduced Cichla ocellaris (Peacock Cichlid) and Cichla temensis (Speckled Pavon) into Dade County canals beginning in 1984, ostensibly to control previously introduced cichlids, but these predators may instead impact native centrarchids including Largemouth Bass; in Gatun Lake, Panama, peacock cichlid "effectively eliminated six of the eight common fish species and drastically reduced a seventh." The taxonomy of genus Cichla remains poorly studied with probable undescribed species and at least one known misidentification, raising questions about which species were actually introduced.
