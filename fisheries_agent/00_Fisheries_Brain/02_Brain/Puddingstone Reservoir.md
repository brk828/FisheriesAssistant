# Puddingstone Reservoir

## Summary
Puddingstone Reservoir is a water body in Los Angeles County, California. It was one of the locations where the California Department of Fish and Game introduced Threadfin Shad as part of their forage fish program in the mid-1950s.

## Context

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
