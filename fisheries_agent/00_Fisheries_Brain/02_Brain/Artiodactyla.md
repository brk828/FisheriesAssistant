# Artiodactyla

## Summary
Artiodactyla (ungulates) account for 19% of identified mammals at La Lomita Pequeña, including deer (Odocoileus sp.) and pronghorn (Antilocapra americana). Ungulates contributed 7-21% of identified fauna across Hohokam Sedentary period sites and were notably absent from trash middens, appearing primarily as bone artifacts in pithouses and cremations.
