# Eagle Creek

## Summary
Eagle Creek is a specific aquatic study site or stream system that has been utilized for fish sampling and monitoring efforts related to native species conservation, particularly for threatened and endangered fish populations in the southwestern United States. The creek appears to serve as an important location for documenting populations of species such as Gila trout and other native fishes, as well as for assessing the impacts of unregulated activities on aquatic ecosystems and recovery efforts for imperiled species. Its inclusion in multiple monitoring and recovery documentation efforts suggests Eagle Creek functions as a key reference location for tracking fish community health and evaluating conservation management strategies in its region.
