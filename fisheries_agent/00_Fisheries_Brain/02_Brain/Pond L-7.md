# Pond L-7

## Summary
Pond L-7 is a wetland unit at Ouray Wildlife Refuge that was the focus of a 1994 restoration engineering plan. The restoration design involved removing portions of the pond's riverside levee, removing vegetation, regrading pond areas, and implementing semi-natural flood inflows to restore more natural hydrological processes and support diverse aquatic and riparian habitat conditions.
