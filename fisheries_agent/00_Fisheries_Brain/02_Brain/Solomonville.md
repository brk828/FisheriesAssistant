# Solomonville

## Summary
Solomonville is one of the ten Arizona development projects in Maxwell's 1935 proposal, covering the Upper Gila Valley. Maxwell notes that existing maps and reports by Engineer Harry F. Olmsted were already available for this project area.

## Context

> This 1935 advance print document by George Hebard Maxwell presents an ambitious proposal for water conservation and land reclamation in Arizona and California through comprehensive development of the [[Colorado River]].
