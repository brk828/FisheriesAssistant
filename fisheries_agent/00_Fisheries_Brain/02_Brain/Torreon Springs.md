# Torreon Springs

## Summary
Springs in Socorro County, New Mexico, where a snail similar to the Socorro Hydrobioid was discovered in 1980. This source indicates this discovery raised hope that Amnicola neomexicana survives despite being presumed extirpated from its type locality, and recommends comparing specimens with museum material.
