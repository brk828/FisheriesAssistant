# Arizona Water Commission

## Summary
The Arizona Water Commission is a state agency involved in water resource assessment. In August 1977, it jointly warned with the U.S. Department of Agriculture that groundwater withdrawal was exceeding recharge in the Sierra Vista-Fort Huachuca area of the San Pedro basin.
