# Common Shiner

## Summary
The Common Shiner is a small cyprinid minnow species utilized as a forage and bait fish in freshwater aquatic systems, particularly valued for its appropriate size range (2-6 inches) in supporting recreational fisheries for bass, pickerel, and other predatory fish species. As a component of stream fish communities, it occupies ecological niches alongside other minnow species and serves as an important dietary resource for larger game fish in both natural and managed aquatic environments. Its role in fisheries management reflects its dual significance as both an indicator of stream health and a practical tool for sustaining populations of economically valuable sport fish species.
