# Reclamation Readjustment Act of 1992

## Summary
The Reclamation Readjustment Act of 1992 (P.L. 102-575) allows 50% of operation and maintenance costs for enhancement projects to be funded by the Bureau of Reclamation under certain circumstances. As of the 1993 meeting date, no appropriations had been allocated under this act, though Bob Michaels noted that approximately $15 million might be available nationwide by 1995.
