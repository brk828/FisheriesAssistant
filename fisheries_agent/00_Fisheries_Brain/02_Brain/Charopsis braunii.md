# Charopsis braunii

## Summary
Charopsis braunii is an alternative taxonomic name for Chara braunii used by Gillet (1959), who reported N=12 chromosomes from European material and proposed removing it from tribe Chareae to place it near Nitella in the Nitelleae. However, this proposal's consistent counts of N=14 from American materials contradict Gillet's findings and support keeping ecorticate Chara species as a distinct group within Chareae rather than moving them to Nitelleae.
