# Chateaugay Lake

## Summary
Chateaugay Lake is a New York State lake where landlocked salmon (Atlantic salmon, Salmo salar) were stocked. By 1953, the lake showed evidence of at least some salmon survival, though it was not among the highest abundance waters in the program.
