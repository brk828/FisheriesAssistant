# Cabrulus

## Summary
Cabrulus is a taxonomic group that this source proposes should be treated as a subgenus of Orocastus rather than a separate genus, based on morphological similarities including aedeagal form, style shape, and color patterns. The chief differences (absence of coronal bands and fusion of connective and aedeagus) are considered derived characters with little weight for generic recognition.
