# Guatemala

## Summary
Guatemala is the Central American country where Lake Atitlan is located. The study was conducted in collaboration with the Guatemalan Department of Renewable Natural Resources, which expressed vital interest in developing Lake Atitlan's fisheries, and fish harvested could be sold in Guatemala City (three hours from the lake) for 30 cents per pound.
