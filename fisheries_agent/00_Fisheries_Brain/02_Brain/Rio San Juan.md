# Rio San Juan

## Summary
The Rio San Juan is a river in trans-Andean Colombia where Gymnotus carapo was recently recorded at the time of this study. This record contributed to documenting G. carapo as the gymnotid species reaching farthest north, occurring in rivers and lakes on both slopes of Guatemala.
