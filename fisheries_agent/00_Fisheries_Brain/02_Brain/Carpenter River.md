# Carpenter River

## Summary
The Carpenter River was a pluvial (ancient wet-period) river system, now represented by Meadow Valley Wash, that connected the White River drainage to the Virgin River during late Pleistocene times. This historic connection, which extended approximately 200 miles south through Pahranagat Valley and Moapa Valley, explains the presence of plagopterin fishes (including White River Spinedace) in the White River system as relict populations that became isolated when the pluvial system dried.
