# Dusky Seaside Sparrow

## Summary
The Dusky Seaside Sparrow is an endangered species that was the subject of a 1981 legal analysis regarding hybrid protection under the Endangered Species Act. According to this legal memorandum, hybrids of the Dusky Seaside Sparrow would not receive protection under the ESA, and expending funds to produce such hybrids would be difficult to justify as conservation or recovery under the Act's definitions, unless the hybrid itself could be separately listed as an endangered or threatened species.
