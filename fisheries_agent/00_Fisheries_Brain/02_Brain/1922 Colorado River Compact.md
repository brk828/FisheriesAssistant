# 1922 Colorado River Compact

## Summary
The 1922 Colorado River Compact is an interstate water allocation agreement that allocates water among states in the Colorado River Basin. This compact, along with the 1948 Upper Colorado River Compact, complicates mitigation strategies for endangered species because it establishes legal water allocation frameworks that must be respected while implementing recovery programs.
