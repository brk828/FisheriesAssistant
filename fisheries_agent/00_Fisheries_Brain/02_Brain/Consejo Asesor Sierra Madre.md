# Consejo Asesor Sierra Madre

## Summary
Consejo Asesor Sierra Madre is a Mexican conservation organization that provides technical and legal services to Tarahumara and Tepehuan indigenous communities in the Sierra Madre Occidental of Chihuahua, Mexico. The organization works in cross-border partnership with Forest Guardians to protect threatened species including the Mexican Spotted Owl that exist in both the United States and Mexico, and was one of five petitioners in this 1995 NAAEC complaint.
