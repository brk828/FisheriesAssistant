# Rio Verde

## Summary
The Rio Verde is a river system in San Luis Potosí that includes exceptional spring-fed areas like La Media Luna and environs in its upper reaches. These spring-fed lagunas and marshes show 67% endemism (6 endemic species among cyprinids, cyprinodontoids, and cichlids) and require protection against loss of biodiversity.
