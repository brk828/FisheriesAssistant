# Glen Canyon Dam Environmental Impact Statement

## Summary
The Glen Canyon Dam Environmental Impact Statement is a formal environmental assessment process mandated under the National Environmental Policy Act to evaluate the ecological consequences of Glen Canyon Dam operations on downstream aquatic ecosystems, particularly in the Colorado River and associated reservoirs (Lake Powell and Lake Mead). This assessment synthesized fragmented scientific research on fish populations, habitat conditions, and broader ecosystem effects to inform management decisions regarding dam operations and conservation strategies for native fish species. The statement represents a critical juncture in fisheries management where agency coordination and consolidated ecological data were essential for developing evidence-based policies to mitigate negative impacts on aquatic communities affected by the dam's hydrological regime.
