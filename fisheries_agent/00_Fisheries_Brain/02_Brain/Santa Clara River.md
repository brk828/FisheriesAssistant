# Santa Clara River

## Summary
The Santa Clara River is a major river system in southern California that drains the Los Angeles Basin and serves as a critical study system for understanding freshwater fish ecology and conservation in the region. This river system, bounded by the Santa Susana and San Gabriel Mountains, historically supported native fish populations including the critically endangered Unarmored Threespine Stickleback and other endemic species now largely extirpated from their original ranges. The Santa Clara River and its tributaries (particularly Sespe Creek) represent important remaining habitat for conservation efforts aimed at restoring and protecting native freshwater fish populations in Southern California.
