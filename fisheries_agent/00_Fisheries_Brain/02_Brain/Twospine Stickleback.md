# Twospine Stickleback

## Summary
Twospine Stickleback is the common name for Gasterosteus wheatlandi, a species that was previously confused with G. aculeatus. The checklist documents its occurrence in Quebec (1960) and Newfoundland (1964), representing important distributional records.
