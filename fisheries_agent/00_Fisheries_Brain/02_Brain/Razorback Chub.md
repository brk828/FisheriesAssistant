# Razorback Chub

## Summary
Razorback Chub (Xyrauchen texanus) is an ESA-listed endangered native fish species in the Verde River system of Arizona. This source indicates that the presence of black bass, including the misidentified Redeye Bass, has caused detrimental effects on this species, and large portions of the Verde River and tributaries are Designated Critical Habitat for it.
