# Lahontan System

## Summary
The Lahontan System refers to Pleistocene Lake Lahontan in the Great Basin, which was less than half of Lake Bonneville's area but rose 550-886 feet above its modern successors. This source reports it contains 10 genera (1 endemic) and 13 species (8 endemic) of fish, representing a distinct ichthyofauna shaped by Pleistocene pluvial conditions.
