# Sharp-tailed Grouse

## Summary
Sharp-tailed Grouse were used in both captive and field toxicity studies in 1964. Captive birds showed dieldrin LD₅₀ of 6.4 mg/kg and malathion critical level around 220 mg/kg, while wild birds showed similar dieldrin LD₅₀ of 6.9 mg/kg, and successful artificial propagation produced 148 chicks from 312 eggs for use in toxicity testing.
