# Cortland, New York

## Summary
Cortland, New York was the location of the Cortland Experimental Hatchery where systematic trout nutrition research was established in 1932. This source describes it as the site where decades of cooperative research on trout feeding protocols and nutritional management were conducted, consolidating findings that informed feeding practices across U.S. fish hatcheries.
