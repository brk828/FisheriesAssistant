# Water Pollution

## Summary
Water pollution in aquatic ecosystems refers to the introduction of harmful substances or contaminants into freshwater and marine environments that degrade water quality and negatively impact fish physiology, behavior, and survival. In the context of fish science and conservation, water pollution encompasses various stressors including reduced dissolved oxygen levels, elevated temperatures, and chemical contaminants that compromise fish respiration, metabolism, and overall ecosystem health. Understanding and measuring these pollutants through methods such as polarographic analysis is essential for monitoring aquatic environments and establishing water quality standards necessary for fish conservation and sustainable fisheries management.
