# Southern California Coastal Province

## Summary
The Southern California Coastal Province encompasses coastal drainages from Point Conception south. Notable habitats include vernal pools on the Santa Rosa Plateau and San Diego Mesa, coastal lagoons characterized by Tidewater Goby, and streams supporting Arroyo Chub, Santa Ana Sucker, Speckled Dace, and steelhead with associated species like Prickly Sculpin and Threespine Stickleback.
