# Arizona Revised Statutes

## Summary
Arizona Revised Statutes § 49-263 is the state law that prohibits discharge of pollutants into state waters without a permit. The 1993 assessment found that the tailings discharge at the Klondyke site violated this statute due to evidence of tailings transport into Aravaipa Creek via rainwater runoff.
