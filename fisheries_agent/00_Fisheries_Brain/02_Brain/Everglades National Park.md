# Everglades National Park

## Summary
Everglades National Park is a protected area in southeastern Florida where National Park Service managers are charged with maintaining environments without nonnative species. The Cichla releases in southeastern Florida occurred in areas connected to Everglades National Park, creating interjurisdictional conflicts between state fish introduction programs and federal conservation mandates to exclude exotic species from national parks.
