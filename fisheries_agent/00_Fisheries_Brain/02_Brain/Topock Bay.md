# Topock Bay

## Summary
Topock Bay is a still-water habitat on the Arizona side of the lower Colorado River. Following the channelization project, it remained as one of the few still-water areas in the region, with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service planning marsh rehabilitation there primarily for waterfowl.
