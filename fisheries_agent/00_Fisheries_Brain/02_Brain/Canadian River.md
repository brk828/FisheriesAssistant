# Canadian River

## Summary
The Canadian River is a major tributary of the Arkansas River that originates on the east slope of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains and flows approximately 312 river miles in New Mexico. This source documents that during late Tertiary to middle Pleistocene time, the upper Canadian River drained into the Pecos River near Fort Sumner, but during late Pleistocene time a headward-eroding lower Canadian River captured this ancestral tributary, establishing the present course.
