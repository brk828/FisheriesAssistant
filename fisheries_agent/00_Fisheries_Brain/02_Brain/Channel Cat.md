# Channel Cat

## Summary
Channel Cat is a catfish species (family Ictaluridae) that was successfully propagated at the Pratt hatchery before 1947 and widely distributed throughout Kansas. Many Kansas fishermen considered it the "State Fish" at the time of this publication.
