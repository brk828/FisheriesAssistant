# River Zone

## Summary
River Zone is the upstream, riverine habitat section of Lake Mohave located above Willow Beach, characterized by more lotic (flowing) conditions compared to the main reservoir. This zone serves as critical spawning habitat for razorback suckers, with primary aggregation sites at Black Bar and Boy Scout Canyon, and supports an estimated subpopulation of 2,120 repatriated fish as of 2018, with approximately 64% of fish released in this zone remaining there.
