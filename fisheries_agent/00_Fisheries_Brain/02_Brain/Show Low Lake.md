# Show Low Lake

## Summary
Show Low Lake is a lentic (still-water) fishery in Arizona's Region I that was subject to creel census monitoring by the Arizona Game and Fish Department during the 1960s to assess recreational fishing productivity and population dynamics. The lake supported rainbow trout populations and was evaluated alongside other managed high-elevation lakes using standardized creel survey methods to track catch-per-unit-effort metrics and harvest data, generating baseline fisheries data essential for adaptive management decisions. Show Low Lake represented one of several Arizona coldwater fisheries where environmental manipulation and stocking programs were implemented to optimize fish production and angler success in response to identified ecological constraints.
