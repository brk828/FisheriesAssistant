# Lacey Act

## Summary
The Lacey Act is a foundational federal legislation that grants the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service authority to regulate the importation, transportation, and trade of fish and wildlife species, serving as a key legal instrument for fisheries management and conservation. In the context of aquatic sciences, the act provides the regulatory framework for controlling which fish species can be legally imported for the aquarium trade and other purposes, helping to prevent the introduction of non-native species and protect native fish populations from overexploitation. The Lacey Act works in conjunction with other fisheries legislation to establish federal oversight of fish resources and their utilization by human activities.
