# Jensen

## Summary
Jensen is a geographic reference point in the middle Green River system, associated with the Jensen Reach (RMI 301.7-276.9). The Jensen Reach is where 23% of all adult razorback sucker captures occurred and contains important nursery habitat at Stewart Drain/Lake.
