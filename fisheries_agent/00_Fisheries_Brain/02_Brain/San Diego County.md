# San Diego County

## Summary
San Diego County is a coastal region of California where the first eastern Pacific oilfish specimen was documented. The specimen was caught off Encinitas in this county in 1942, and the authors note that the deep-water basins off southern California, including this area, may harbor commercially valuable deep-sea fish populations.
