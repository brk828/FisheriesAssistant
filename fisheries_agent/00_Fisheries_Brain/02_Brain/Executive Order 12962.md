# Executive Order 12962

## Summary
Executive Order 12962 is a 1995 presidential directive that mandates the Federal government, particularly the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, to facilitate and expand recreational fishing opportunities nationwide by improving access to aquatic systems and coordinating fisheries management across federal, state, and tribal authorities. The order establishes a framework requiring federal agencies to actively support sport fishing through stakeholder engagement, regional planning initiatives, and the removal of barriers to recreational angling while maintaining sustainable fish populations. This executive order represents a formal commitment to balancing recreational fisheries development with conservation responsibilities under existing statutes like the Fish and Wildlife Act of 1956.
