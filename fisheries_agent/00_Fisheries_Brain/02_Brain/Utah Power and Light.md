# Utah Power and Light

## Summary
Utah Power and Light is a power utility company that was engaged in litigation against the Western Area Power Administration regarding NEPA compliance in post-1989 power marketing plans. This litigation was one of the legal complications affecting Glen Canyon Dam operations and the Environmental Studies program at the time of this 1989 meeting.
