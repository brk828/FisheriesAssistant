# Wild Trout

## Summary
Wild trout are self-sustaining populations of trout that reproduce naturally in their native stream habitats, existing within the ecological carrying capacity of those systems. In contrast to hatchery-reared trout used for supplemental stocking, wild trout populations serve as important indicators of ecosystem health and are central to conservation biology efforts that prioritize habitat restoration and ecosystem management approaches. The conservation and management of wild trout populations requires understanding their relationship to stream habitat quality, as natural recruitment and population sustainability depend directly on adequate physical and chemical conditions rather than artificial propagation.
