# Cincinnati, Ohio

## Summary
Cincinnati, Ohio was the location used as a case study example for calculating strontium-90 contamination from multiple environmental sources including air, water (cistern and surface), milk, and food. The analysis showed total daily strontium-90 intake ranged from 17.0 to 60.3 micromicrocuries per day, demonstrating the importance of considering all exposure pathways when establishing permissible discharge levels for radioactive materials.
