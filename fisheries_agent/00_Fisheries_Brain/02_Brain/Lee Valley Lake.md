# Lee Valley Lake

## Summary
Lee Valley Lake is a cold-water fishery in Arizona managed primarily for recreational rainbow trout fishing, where fingerlings were regularly stocked as the principal sport species under state fish and wildlife management protocols. The lake was included in the Arizona Game and Fish Department's cooperative water resources inventory conducted during the 1960s, which monitored recreational use, fishing harvest, and the effectiveness of stocking programs across multiple reservoirs and natural lakes in the state.
