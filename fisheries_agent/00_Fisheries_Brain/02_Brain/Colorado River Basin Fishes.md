# Colorado River Basin Fishes

## Summary
Colorado River Basin Fishes refers to the native and endemic fish assemblages inhabiting the Colorado River system and its tributaries, characterized by species specially adapted to the river's unique hydrological and thermal conditions. These fishes, which include species such as the Colorado pikeminnow, razorback sucker, and humpback chub, have become a focal point for conservation efforts due to their decline from habitat modification, non-native species introduction, and altered flow regimes resulting from dam operations. The study and management of Colorado River Basin fishes involves integrating ichthyological research with fisheries science to understand population dynamics, growth patterns, and ecosystem interactions necessary for recovery and sustainable management of this ecologically distinct fish community.
