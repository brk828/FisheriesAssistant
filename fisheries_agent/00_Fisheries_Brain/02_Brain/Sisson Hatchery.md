# Sisson Hatchery

## Summary
Sisson Hatchery was a California fish hatchery that obtained cutthroat trout spawn from Lake Tahoe tributaries (Taylor Creek and Blackwood Creek) during 1895-1896. The hatchery reared these fish and distributed them throughout California mountain streams as part of state fish stocking programs, including the plantings in the Santa Ana River that established the population later described as Salmo evermanni.
