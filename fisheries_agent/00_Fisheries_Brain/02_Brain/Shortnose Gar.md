# Shortnose Gar

## Summary
The Shortnose Gar (*Lepisosteus platostomus*) is a primitive holostean fish species native to North American freshwater systems such as the Mississippi River, characterized by moderate resistance to ichthyotoxicants like antimycin (up to 20 p.p.b.) and studied extensively in comparative toxicology research due to its ancient lineage and unique gill and respiratory physiology. As a "rough fish" species rather than a food fish, it serves as an important model organism for understanding drug uptake and bioaccumulation across phylogenetically diverse fish groups, particularly for evaluating the efficacy and safety of fish control agents used in aquatic management.
