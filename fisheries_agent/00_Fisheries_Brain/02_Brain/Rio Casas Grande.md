# Rio Casas Grande

## Summary
Rio Casas Grande is a former tributary of the Rio Grande that now reaches the sea independently due to stream capture events. Gila nigrescens from this river shows morphological characters intermediate between G. robusta of the Colorado River and G. pandora of the Rio Grande, and populations have 9,9-9,8 fin ray counts with 68-80 lateral line scales.
