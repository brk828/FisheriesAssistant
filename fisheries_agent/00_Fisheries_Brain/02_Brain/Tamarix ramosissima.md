# Tamarix ramosissima

## Summary
Tamarix ramosissima (tamarisk) is an exotic riparian shrub that continued low-level establishment in the Colorado River corridor under fluctuating Interim Flows. This source identifies it as a species of management concern and lists it among the important riparian species (along with Salix, Populus, and Baccharis) for which foliar area changes were to be monitored and analyzed.
