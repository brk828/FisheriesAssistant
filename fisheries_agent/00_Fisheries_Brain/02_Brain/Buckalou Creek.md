# Buckalou Creek

## Summary
Buckalou Creek is a stream in the vicinity of the Isabelle Timber Sale area. The biological opinion noted that no cutting units were immediately adjacent to Buckalou Creek, suggesting it was considered in the spatial planning of harvest activities to minimize impacts to aquatic habitats.
