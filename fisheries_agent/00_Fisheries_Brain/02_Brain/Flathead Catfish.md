# Flathead Catfish

## Summary
The flathead catfish (Pylodictis olivaris) is a large, predatory ictalurid catfish native to North American river systems, characterized by a flattened head, rounded caudal fin, and ability to reach substantial sizes (30+ pounds and over 40 inches in length), distinguishing it morphologically from forked-tailed catfish species such as channel catfish. In conservation and aquatic ecology contexts, flathead catfish are significant as both invasive species in non-native habitats and as subjects of population monitoring studies in their native and introduced ranges, where their predatory behavior and ecological impacts on native fish communities are of particular concern. Their presence in various freshwater systems documented across multiple regions reflects their role as an important species in fish community assessments and fisheries management decision-making.
