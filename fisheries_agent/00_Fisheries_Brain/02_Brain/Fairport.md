# Fairport

## Summary
Fairport refers to the Iowa location of the U.S. Biological Station where buffalo fish aquaculture research was conducted. In Fairport ponds, buffalo fish reached sexual maturity at 4 years old and demonstrated specific growth rates under controlled feeding and management conditions.
