# Rio Sonoyta

## Summary
The Rio Sonoyta is a river system in the southwestern United States-Mexico border region that serves as an important reference point for understanding fish distribution, endemism, and conservation challenges in the Sonoran Desert. In the context of these documents, the Rio Sonoyta is relevant to discussions of native and non-native fish populations, particularly regarding desert pupfish subspecies and their isolated spring habitats within the broader watershed context. The river system and its associated springs represent critical but fragmented aquatic ecosystems where introduced fish species pose significant threats to endemic species like the endangered Quitobaquito pupfish, making it a focal area for fisheries management and biological conservation efforts in arid southwestern North America.
