# Southwestern Native Fish Conservation

## Summary
Southwestern Native Fish Conservation refers to the coordinated management and protection of imperiled freshwater fish species endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly those listed under the Endangered Species Act or designated as sensitive by regional forest management agencies. This conservation approach involves habitat management, regulatory permitting, and interagency cooperation—such as between the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and the Forest Service—to maintain viable populations of native species threatened by habitat degradation, water resource competition, and other anthropogenic stressors in the region's arid aquatic ecosystems.
