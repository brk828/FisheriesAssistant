# Laguna Chichancanab

## Summary
Laguna Chichancanab is a lake in Yucatan that represents an exceptional center of endemic fish diversity. This source documents it as home to five endemic Cyprinodon (pupfish) species: C. beltrani, C. labiosus, C. maya, C. simus, and C. verecundus, demonstrating remarkable species-level endemism in an isolated water body.
