# Big Sagebrush

## Summary
Big Sagebrush (Artemisia tridentata) is a shrub species found in multiple plant communities in the Mono Basin, including the Jeffrey Pine Forest (7,000-9,500 feet) and Pinyon-Juniper Woodland zones. It is also a dominant component of the Sagebrush Scrub community at the lowest elevations, which covers over 250,000 square miles across western states.
