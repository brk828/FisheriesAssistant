# Speckled Chub

## Summary
Speckled Chub (Macrhybopsis aestivalis) is a native cyprinid species that has been extirpated from the Middle Rio Grande. The last collection of this species occurred in 1964 downstream of present-day Cochiti Dam, representing one of the seven native species (41% of native diversity) eliminated from the system.
