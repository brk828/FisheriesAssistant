# reservoir stratification

## Summary
Reservoir stratification refers to the layering of water by temperature in Lake Powell, occurring from April to December when the reservoir is monomictic (stratified in warm months, mixed in winter). During stratification, the epilimnion (warm upper layer) varied dramatically in depth between years—approximately 24 m in 2022 versus 39 m in 2023—with nonnative fishes concentrating in this warm upper layer, which creates high entrainment risk when dam intakes draw from epilimnetic water.
