# River Colne

## Summary
The River Colne is a polluted English river where Edwards studied the effects of organic enrichment on benthic communities. Chironomid midge emergence from the Colne was estimated at 160 g dry weight/m²/year (five emergences March-October), which exceeded the maximum macrophyte standing crop of 120 g dry weight/m² in the same river.
