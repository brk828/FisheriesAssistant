# Russian River

## Summary
Russian River is one of the California drainages for which this source documents drainage-specific fish subspecies. The authors used geographic distribution patterns, including the Russian River drainage, as important criteria for subspecies identification, particularly within the families Cyprinidae and Catostomidae.
