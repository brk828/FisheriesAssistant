# Q. rugosa

## Summary
Q. rugosa (Quercus rugosa) is one of the dominant oak species in the Sierra San Luis reserve. It is found in the oak forest community at elevations of 1,800-2,100 meters, which is the most extensive vegetation type covering over 70% of the reserve.
