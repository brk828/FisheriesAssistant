# Cui-Ui Recovery Team

## Summary
The Cui-ui Recovery Team is a collaborative group convened by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to coordinate the conservation and management of the cui-ui (Chasmistes cujus), an endangered catostomid fish endemic to Pyramid Lake, Nevada. The team brings together scientists, managers, and stakeholders to develop and implement recovery strategies, monitor population trends, and oversee the execution of recovery plans aimed at restoring this species from critically low numbers to sustainable population levels. Active from at least 1989 through the early 1990s, the team met regularly to review ecological data, genetic analyses, and population dynamics to guide evidence-based conservation decisions.
