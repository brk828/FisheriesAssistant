# Fort Apache Indian Reservation

## Summary
The Fort Apache Indian Reservation in Arizona is a significant geographic and administrative region for aquatic conservation efforts, encompassing critical watershed ecosystems including portions of the Salt River and Gila River drainages that support endemic and threatened fish species such as the Apache trout. Within this reservation territory, tribal management authorities have conducted extensive ichthyological surveys, cryopreservation research, and hatchery operations to monitor fish populations and restore native species in montane stream habitats affected by human disturbance. The reservation represents an important case study in tribal stewardship of aquatic resources and the integration of Native American self-determination with endangered species recovery under the Endangered Species Act.
