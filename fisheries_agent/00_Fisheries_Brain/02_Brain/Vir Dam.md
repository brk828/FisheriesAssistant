# Vir Dam

## Summary
The Vir Dam created the Vir Reservoir on the Svratka River and caused significant downstream ecological effects. Below the dam, organic drift consisted of 76.2% planktonic individuals swept from the reservoir, with only 12.7% rheobiontic organisms.
