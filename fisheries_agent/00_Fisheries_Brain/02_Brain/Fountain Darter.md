# Fountain Darter

## Summary
The Fountain Darter (*Etheostoma fonticola*) is a small, endangered percid fish endemic to the Edwards Aquifer region of Texas that has become a significant case study in conservation aquaculture and recovery planning under the Endangered Species Act. This species has been maintained in captive refugium populations at facilities like the Uvalde National Fish Hatchery and Technology Center as a critical tool for preserving genetic diversity and preventing extinction while habitat restoration and reintroduction efforts proceed. The Fountain Darter exemplifies how imperiled freshwater fishes with highly restricted ranges depend on coordinated hatchery management and species recovery protocols to maintain viable populations in the face of groundwater depletion and habitat degradation.
