# Chilhowee Lake

## Summary
Chilhowee Lake is an impoundment constructed in 1957 on the Little Tennessee River bordering Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Along with Fontana Lake, its construction has affected fishery resources in tributary waters, though the extent of these impacts was unknown as of 1960.
