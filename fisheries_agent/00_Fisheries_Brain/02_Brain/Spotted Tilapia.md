# Spotted Tilapia

## Summary
Spotted Tilapia (Tilapia mariae) is a tropical aquarium species that has been introduced to Nevada and has become established in thermal springs. This source documents it as one of several tropical aquarium species now present in Nevada's warm-water habitats.
