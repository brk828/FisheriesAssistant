# Colorado River Storage Project Act of 1956

## Summary
The Colorado River Storage Project Act of 1956 is federal legislation that is part of the "Law of the River" framework governing Colorado River operations. According to this source, all adaptive management program activities at Glen Canyon Dam must operate within the constraints established by this Act, which relates to water storage and delivery obligations in the Colorado River system.
