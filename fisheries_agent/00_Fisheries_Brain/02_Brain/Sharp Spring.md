# Sharp Spring

## Summary
Sharp Spring is a small, isolated desert spring population in the Gila River basin of Arizona that represents a genetically distinct refuge for native fish species, particularly notable for being the only U.S. population of Gila topminnow surveyed to retain detectable heterozygosity at allozyme loci. This population is significant for conservation efforts as it maintains unique genetic variation that other fragmented Gila basin populations have lost, making it a critically important source for genetic diversity in species recovery and reintroduction programs. Sharp Spring exemplifies the relict aquatic habitats scattered throughout the arid Southwest that serve as refugia for endemic species threatened by desiccation, invasive species, and ongoing habitat degradation.
