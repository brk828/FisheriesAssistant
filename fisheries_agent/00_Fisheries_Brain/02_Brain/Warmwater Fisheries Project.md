# Warmwater Fisheries Project

## Summary
The Warmwater Fisheries Project was a California Department of Fish and Game initiative that by 1955 had transitioned from a purely investigational phase to an active program of controlled introductions of forage fish species. The project aimed to improve warmwater fishing throughout California by introducing non-native forage species like Threadfin Shad, Fathead Minnow, Plains Red Shiner, and Golden Shiner into reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
