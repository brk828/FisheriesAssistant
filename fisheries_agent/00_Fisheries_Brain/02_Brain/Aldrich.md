# Aldrich

## Summary
Aldrich's 1938 observations are cited to support the finding that bass develop distrust of artificial lures based on experience. The study's data showing bass shifting from artificial to live bait preference over the opening week (e.g., from a 3:1 to 1:2 ratio at Marion County Lake) aligns with Aldrich's earlier observations about bass learning capacity.

## Context

> This 1955 study by the [[Kentucky Department of Fish and Wildlife Resources]] examined the impact of intensive fishing pressure during the opening week of three newly stocked state-owned lakes.
