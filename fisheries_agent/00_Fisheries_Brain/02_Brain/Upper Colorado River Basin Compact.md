# Upper Colorado River Basin Compact

## Summary
The Upper Colorado River Basin Compact is one of the legal agreements that comprise the "Law of the River," which governs Colorado River water allocation and management. According to this source, all adaptive management activities and dam operations at Glen Canyon Dam must comply with this compact along with other legal constraints including water delivery obligations.
