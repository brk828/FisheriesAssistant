# Contra Costa County

## Summary
Contra Costa County was the top-ranked striped bass county in 1960 with 1,163 fish reported from 83 reports, and the third-ranked catfish county with 2,421 fish reported from 57 reports. It is located in the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta and San Francisco Bay area, which together contributed 98.6% of the state's striped bass catch.
