# Salt River Project

## Summary
The Salt River Project (SRP) refers to a water management infrastructure and utility system in Arizona that operates dams, reservoirs, and water delivery systems affecting the Lower Colorado River Basin and its aquatic ecosystems. In the context of fish science and conservation, SRP's operations significantly influence river flow regimes, water temperature, and habitat conditions that impact native fish populations, particularly endangered species like razorback suckers and Colorado pikeminnow. SRP facilities and their management practices have been central to discussions regarding habitat restoration, native fish reintroduction programs, and balancing water resource allocation with aquatic ecosystem conservation objectives in the southwestern United States.
