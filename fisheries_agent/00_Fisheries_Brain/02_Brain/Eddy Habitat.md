# Eddy Habitat

## Summary
Eddies are important habitat features for multiple native species in the Colorado River system. Adult humpback chubs specifically occupy deep swirling eddies in turbulent canyon reaches, flannelmouth suckers feed in eddies and pool margins, and bluehead suckers stay in deep pools and eddies during the day before moving to shallow riffles at night.
