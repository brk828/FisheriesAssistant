# Granite Creek

## Summary
Granite Creek is a location on the Verde River system that marks one end of the stretch where Redeye Bass surveys were conducted in 2019. This source reports that in the stretch from Granite Creek to Sycamore Creek, Redeye Bass was the most abundant nonnative fish and at many sites was the most abundant fish overall.
