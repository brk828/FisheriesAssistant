# Ann Arbor

## Summary
Ann Arbor is a city in Michigan that is home to the University of Michigan Museum of Zoology. This correspondence was written from Ann Arbor by Robert R. Miller, who served as Curator of Fishes at the museum, and specimens of the new Catostomus species were to be delivered there by Donn Rosen during a visit.
