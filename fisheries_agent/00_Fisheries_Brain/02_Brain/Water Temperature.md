# Water Temperature

## Summary
Water temperature is a critical abiotic factor that regulates physiological processes in fish, including metabolic rate, growth, reproduction, and behavior, making it essential for understanding fish ecology and population dynamics. Temperature fluctuations, both seasonal and diel (daily), influence fish distribution, feeding efficiency, and development rates, thereby affecting species survival, community composition, and the overall health of aquatic ecosystems. In fisheries management and conservation, monitoring and understanding water temperature patterns is fundamental for predicting fish productivity, managing habitat quality, and assessing responses to environmental changes such as thermal pollution or climate variation.
