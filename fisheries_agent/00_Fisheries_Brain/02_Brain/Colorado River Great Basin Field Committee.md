# Colorado River Great Basin Field Committee

## Summary
The Colorado River Great Basin Field Committee was an inter-agency organization that coordinated fisheries research and management in the Colorado River basin. According to this 1951 report, the Committee's Fisheries Task Group brought together biologists from Nevada, Arizona, and California fish and game departments, plus the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, to conduct comprehensive surveys of the Lower Colorado River fishery from Hoover Dam to the Mexican border.
