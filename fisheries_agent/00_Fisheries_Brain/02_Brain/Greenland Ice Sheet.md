# Greenland Ice Sheet

## Summary
The Greenland Ice Sheet is a major ice mass requiring the equivalent of 31.4 cm of water per year for maintenance. This source notes that snow falls 55% of days and clouds prevail 60% of the time in this region, illustrating the precipitation patterns necessary to sustain large ice sheets.
