# El Paso

## Summary
El Paso is a city where researchers planned to meet on July 27-28, 1988, as part of the logistics for a field expedition to investigate Tilapia introduction in Cuatro Cienegas, Mexico. It served as a gathering point before the team proceeded to Del Rio and then entered Mexico.
