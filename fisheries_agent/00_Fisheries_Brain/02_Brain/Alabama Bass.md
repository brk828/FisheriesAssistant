# Alabama Bass

## Summary
Alabama Bass (Micropterus henshalli) is a non-native black bass species introduced and established in regions of the ACF Basin. This source notes that one hybrid fish captured had alleles from Shoal Bass, Alabama Bass, and Spotted Bass, indicating genetic introgression is occurring.
