# Upper Colorado Region

## Summary
The Upper Colorado Region is an administrative division of the Bureau of Reclamation. In this 1993 document, the Upper Colorado Region organized a technical review meeting through the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies program to evaluate flow scenario recommendations for Glen Canyon Dam operations.
