# Colorado River Storage Project

## Summary
The Colorado River Storage Project (CRSP) is a major federal water development infrastructure program that constructed a series of dams and diversions throughout the Colorado River basin, fundamentally altering aquatic ecosystems and fish habitats through habitat loss, flow modification, and water extraction. From a conservation perspective, the CRSP prompted the establishment of mitigation measures, including fish hatcheries and restoration programs, to compensate for the severe ecological damage inflicted on native fish populations and aquatic communities. The project has been central to ongoing debates in fish science and conservation biology regarding the ecological tradeoffs between water resource development and aquatic ecosystem preservation.
