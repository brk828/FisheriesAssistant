# Blue Springs

## Summary
Blue Springs refers to a significant spring-influenced site located at river mile 13 in the Little Colorado River Gorge, Arizona, that functions as an important ecological and physical feature in the Colorado River Basin's aquatic ecosystem. The springs create distinctive water chemistry conditions characterized by lower turbidity and altered conductivity that influence fish populations and potentially act as a physicochemical barrier affecting gene flow between separated populations of native fishes, particularly speckled dace and endangered humpback chub. Blue Springs represents a critical focal point for understanding habitat use, population dynamics, and the designation of adequate critical habitat for threatened and endangered fish species dependent on the Little Colorado River's spawning grounds.
