# Tryonia quitobaquitae

## Summary
Tryonia quitobaquitae, known as the Quitobaquito Tryonia, is an aquatic snail with global rank G1 and state rank S1, indicating it is critically imperiled. This species was listed as a federal candidate species and identified as a conservation target for aquatic invertebrates in the Sonoran Desert ecoregional planning effort.
