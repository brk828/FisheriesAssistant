# Colorado Chub

## Summary
The Colorado Chub (Gila robusta) is a native cyprinid fish species historically found in the Grand Canyon region. According to this source, it was first collected in Glen Canyon in 1960 prior to dam construction, with subsequent collections by Arizona Game and Fish Department from 1961-1966, but is now considered extinct or exceedingly rare in Grand Canyon National Park and listed as a species of special interest due to limited distribution.
