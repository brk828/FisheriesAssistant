# San Bernardino Ranch

## Summary
San Bernardino Ranch refers to a specific riparian property and aquatic ecosystem in the southwestern United States that served as an important site for fish conservation and research activities, particularly regarding native fish species management and restoration efforts. The ranch's spring-fed waters and associated habitats were utilized for fish stocking programs, taxonomic surveys, and ecological studies focused on endangered or endemic species such as the Yaqui chub. As a managed conservation area, San Bernardino Ranch functioned as a controlled environment for aquatic research and species preservation initiatives during the latter half of the 20th century.
