# Rio Yaqui Basin

## Summary
The Rio Yaqui Basin is a desert aquatic system in the southwestern United States and Mexico that harbors endemic fish species of significant conservation concern, including the Yaqui chub and Yaqui catfish. This basin has become a focal point for conservation efforts due to the vulnerability of its native fish communities to threats from non-native species invasion and habitat degradation, making it a priority area for Desert Fishes Recovery Team management and species recovery planning. The Rio Yaqui Basin represents an important example of isolated desert ichthyofauna requiring active intervention to prevent extinctions of endemic species adapted to this unique lotic environment.
