# Iotichthys Phlegethontis

## Summary
Iotichthys phlegethontis is the scientific name for the Least Chub, a desert fish species inhabiting spring-marsh systems in western Utah. Research presented at the symposium examined its distribution patterns and feeding ecology in these desert aquatic environments.
