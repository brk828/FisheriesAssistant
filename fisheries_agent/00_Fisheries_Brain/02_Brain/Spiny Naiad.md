# Spiny Naiad

## Summary
Spiny Naiad (Najas marina) is a submerged aquatic plant that grows profusely in deeper water during summer and fall in the Lower Colorado River system. Introduced freshwater shrimp were most commonly found near shore in beds of this plant, and at Taylor Lake pond, a fair shrimp population was found only in association with spiny naiad despite cattails being the dominant plants.
