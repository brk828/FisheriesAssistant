# Pantosteus

## Summary
Pantosteus is a genus of suckers (family Catostomidae) that comprises freshwater fish species found primarily in North American river systems, including the Colorado River drainage and other western watersheds. Members of this genus are important components of native fish communities and have become subjects of significant conservation concern due to habitat loss, water management practices, and competition with introduced species. The genus has undergone taxonomic revision and nomenclatural clarification in ichthyological literature, reflecting ongoing systematic reassessment of catostomid fish diversity and classification.
