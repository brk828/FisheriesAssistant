# Auke Bay Fisheries Laboratory

## Summary
Auke Bay Fisheries Laboratory is the research facility whose divers conducted the observations for this study. Divers from this laboratory made 81 separate diving observations spanning all months over the 10-year study period (1965-75).

## Context

> This study documents seasonal behavioral patterns in mixed aggregations of [[Yellowtail Rockfish]] (*Sebastes flavidus*) and [[Dusky Rockfish]] (*S. ciliatus*) observed by divers in [[Lynn Canal]], southeastern [[Alaska]] from 1965-75.
