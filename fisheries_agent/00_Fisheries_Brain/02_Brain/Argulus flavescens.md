# Argulus flavescens

## Summary
Argulus flavescens is a previously described species of parasitic crustacean that shares similar respiratory area shape with the newly described Argulus chesapeakensis. It is distinguished from A. chesapeakensis by having a digitate (finger-like) posterior spine on the first segment of the first antenna and longer spines on the basal plate of the second maxilla (longer than wide, versus shorter in A. chesapeakensis).
