# Sacramento-San Joaquin Basin

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin Basin is a major drainage system in California that is home to Siphateles formosus, one of the channel and lake minnow species to which the Mohave River's S. mohavensis is compared. This basin is separated from the Mohave River by high mountains connecting the southern Sierra Nevada with the Coast Ranges, with no geological evidence of previous connection between the two systems.
