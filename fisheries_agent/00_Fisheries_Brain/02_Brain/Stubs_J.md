## Jacob Corwin Blumer

### Summary
Jacob Corwin Blumer (1872-1947) was a Swiss-born botanist who conducted extensive fieldwork in southeastern Arizona Territory between August 1906 and October 1907. During this 10-month period, he traveled more than 1,600 miles and collected approximately 1,050 plant species (including 25 new to science), making the first extensive plant collections in the Chiricahua Mountains. He was a pioneering plant ecologist who articulated important principles about species distribution being limited by moisture at low elevations and temperature at high elevations, and about how species plasticity enables wider distribution ranges.
