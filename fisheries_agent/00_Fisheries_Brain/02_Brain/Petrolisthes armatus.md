# Petrolisthes armatus

## Summary
Petrolisthes armatus is a porcelain crab species documented at Puerto Peñasco. This source identifies it as one of the anomuran crabs present in the Gulf of California intertidal zone, with identifications provided by Clinton Westervelt in consultation with Dr. Janet Haig.
