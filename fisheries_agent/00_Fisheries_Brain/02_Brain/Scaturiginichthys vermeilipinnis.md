# Scaturiginichthys vermeilipinnis

## Summary
Scaturiginichthys vermeilipinnis is the Redfinned Blue-eye, an endemic fish species from Great Artesian Basin springs. This species was described by Ivantsoff and colleagues in 1991 and represents one of the unique biological discoveries documented in this bibliography's coverage of spring-dependent fauna.
