# Charopsis

## Summary
Charopsis is a genus proposed by Gillet (1959) to accommodate Chara braunii based on a chromosome count of 12 and proposed placement in Nitelleae. However, this study's consistent count of N=14 for Chara braunii contradicts affinity with Nitelleae (which have base numbers of 6 or 9), though cytological evidence regarding sex-ploidy correlation does support generic distinction from corticate Chara species.
