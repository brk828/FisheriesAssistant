# Slimy Sculpin

## Summary
The Slimy Sculpin (*Cottus cognatus*) is a small benthic freshwater fish species distributed across North America and Asia, with populations occurring in cold, clear streams and rivers where it occupies ecological niches as a bottom-dwelling predator of aquatic invertebrates. Its presence across the Bering Strait region documents trans-Beringian faunal connections and paleontological fish dispersal patterns between continents. As a cold-water specialist sensitive to habitat degradation and temperature changes, the Slimy Sculpin serves as an important indicator species for assessing stream ecosystem health and the impacts of climate change and watershed alterations on freshwater biodiversity.
