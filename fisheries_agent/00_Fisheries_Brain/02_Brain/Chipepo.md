# Chipepo

## Summary
Chipepo (located at 28°50'E, 16°45'S) is a location on the lower Zambesi River where specimens of Anguilla nebulosa labiata were collected. One confirmed specimen was a 95.5 cm mottled female with a D value of 12.2.
