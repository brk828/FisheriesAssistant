# Population Establishment

## Summary
Population establishment refers to the successful introduction and self-sustaining reproduction of striped bass in the lower Colorado River. Following stocking of 93,000 fish between 1962-1969, the population showed evidence of limited reproduction from 1966-1969, with the first dominant year class produced in 1970, demonstrating successful establishment with natural reproduction, accelerated growth rates, and early sexual maturity.
