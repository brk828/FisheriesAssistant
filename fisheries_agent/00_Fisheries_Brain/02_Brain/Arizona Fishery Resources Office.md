# Arizona Fishery Resources Office

## Summary
The Arizona Fishery Resources Office is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service division responsible for managing and conserving fish populations and aquatic resources throughout Arizona, operating in coordination with tribal wildlife agencies, federal fish hatcheries, and research programs. As evidenced by its involvement in the Loach Minnow Study Plan and Glen Canyon Environmental Studies, the office conducts and oversees biological research, population monitoring, and habitat management initiatives for native fish species, particularly threatened and endangered species in the Southwest. This office serves as a key institutional partner in collaborative fisheries science and conservation efforts across Arizona's diverse aquatic ecosystems.
