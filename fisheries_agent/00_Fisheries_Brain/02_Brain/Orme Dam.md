# Orme Dam

## Summary
Orme Dam (also known as Maxwell Dam) was a proposed water infrastructure project planned for the confluence of the Salt River and Verde River in Arizona as part of the Central Arizona Project, which prompted extensive fish and wildlife impact assessments by the Arizona Game and Fish Department during the 1960s. The dam represented a significant conservation concern due to its potential effects on aquatic ecosystems and native fish populations in the affected river systems. Environmental reviews documented in the mid-to-late 1960s evaluated the ecological consequences of this proposed impoundment on the region's ichthyofauna and broader aquatic habitat.
