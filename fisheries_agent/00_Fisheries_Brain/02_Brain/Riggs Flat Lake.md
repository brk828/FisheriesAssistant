# Riggs Flat Lake

## Summary
Riggs Flat Lake is a cold-water reservoir in Arizona managed primarily for recreational trout fishing, where rainbow trout fingerlings were stocked and maintained as the principal sport fish species during the mid-20th century. As documented in Arizona Game and Fish Department surveys during the 1960s, the lake was part of a coordinated statewide inventory of recreational fishing resources that tracked license sales, angler use patterns, and species composition across the state's limited aquatic habitats. The lake exemplifies the management approach for Arizona's high-elevation, cold-water fisheries that supported increasing recreational demand during the post-war expansion of sport fishing in the region.
