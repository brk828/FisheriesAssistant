# Chum Salmon

## Summary
Chum salmon (Oncorhynchus keta) are an anadromous Pacific salmonid species that serve as an important component of restoration and hatchery production programs in the Pacific Northwest, particularly as part of cooperative efforts between federal agencies and tribal nations to maintain viable populations. In the context of fisheries management and conservation, chum salmon are distributed through artificial propagation at facilities like the Quinault National Fish Hatchery to support both ecological recovery and subsistence fishing opportunities for Indigenous communities. These fish play a significant ecological role in their native watersheds and represent a key species for regional aquatic resource management and habitat restoration initiatives.
