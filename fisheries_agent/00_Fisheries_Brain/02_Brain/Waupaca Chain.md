# Waupaca Chain

## Summary
Waupaca Chain refers to a group of Wisconsin lakes that exemplify Type IIIa diatom maxima. These lakes maintain permanent climax oxygen maxima throughout summer stratification at temperatures of 15-20°C and light intensities of 5-28% surface intensity, dominated by diatoms such as Fragilaria and Asterionella.
