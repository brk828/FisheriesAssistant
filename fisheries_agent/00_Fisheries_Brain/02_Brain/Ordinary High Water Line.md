# Ordinary High Water Line

## Summary
The Ordinary High Water Line (OHWL) is defined as the water level representing the water surface elevation during a normal annual high water event. It is physically marked where perennial hydrophytic plant life converges with bare substrate, and the procedures use "5 feet above ordinary high water line" as an interim measure to approximate the 50 to 100 year floodplain for determining where certain nonnative fish stocking is permitted.
