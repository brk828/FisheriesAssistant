# Ruffe

## Summary
The Ruffe (Gymnocephalus cernua) is a fish species that appeared in Loch Lomond in 1982, 100 km north of its former range, and has become one of the loch's commonest fish. It potentially threatens the vulnerable Powan by consuming its eggs and has also established in Lake Superior after accidental introduction via ballast water from Europe, demonstrating the threat of non-native species introductions.
