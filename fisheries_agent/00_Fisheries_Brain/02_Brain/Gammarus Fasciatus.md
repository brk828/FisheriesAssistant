# Gammarus Fasciatus

## Summary
Gammarus fasciatus is a freshwater amphipod crustacean species native to North America that has been subject to taxonomic clarification efforts, particularly through comparative morphological studies of abdominal structures used to distinguish it from closely related Gammarus species. As a small benthic macroinvertebrate, it plays an important role in aquatic food webs and serves as both an indicator organism for water quality assessment and a relevant species in thermal tolerance studies for understanding the physiological limits of freshwater fauna. The species has been historically confused with other congeners, necessitating detailed taxonomic revisions to accurately identify populations for ecological and conservation monitoring purposes.
