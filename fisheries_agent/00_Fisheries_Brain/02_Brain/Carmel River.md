# Carmel River

## Summary
The Carmel River is a stream in California that represents part of the southern range of Pacific Lamprey spawning runs in western North America. It marks the northern boundary of the southern California region where Pacific Lamprey maintains spawning runs in several unaltered creeks, with the range extending southward to the Santa Ana River.
