# Bennett

## Summary
Bennett's 1954 work is cited alongside Aldrich to support the study's findings that bass become more selective and develop wariness toward artificial lures based on fishing experience. The dramatic shift in catch rates and bait preferences observed during opening week provides empirical support for Bennett's observations about bass behavior.
