# Asih Environmental Quality Committee

## Summary
The ASIH Environmental Quality Committee is a regulatory and advocacy body of the American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists that evaluates environmental impact assessments and conservation policies affecting aquatic ecosystems and fish populations. Chaired by prominent ichthyologist Walter R. Courtenay Jr., the committee provides expert scientific review and formal recommendations on issues ranging from fishery restoration projects to problematic species introductions that pose threats to native aquatic fauna and biodiversity. Operating from at least the late 1980s through the 1990s, the committee served as an authoritative voice in aquatic conservation, advocating against ecologically damaging proposals and supporting endangered species protections for both fish and associated aquatic vertebrates.
