# Caldwell Hollow

## Summary
Caldwell Hollow is a 2.22 km long intermittent tributary of Salt Creek in Brown County, Indiana, with a watershed of 3.1 km² and steep gradient of 31.7 m/km. During the 1963-64 study period, the stream was connected to Salt Creek for only 46 days and completely dry for 38 days in late summer/autumn, yet maintained permanent populations of aquatic invertebrates that survived in subsurface seepage and moist interstitial spaces within the sandstone substrate. The pristine wooded watershed and non-angular sandstone bed create critical pore spaces extending to 60 cm depth that allow organisms to follow the water table during dry periods.
