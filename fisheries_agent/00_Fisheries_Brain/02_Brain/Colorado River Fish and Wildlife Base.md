# Colorado River Fish and Wildlife Base

## Summary
The Colorado River Fish and Wildlife Base (CFWB) is a facility in California that served as a holding location for fish management activities. In 1980, it housed 80 Humpback Suckers from the 1974 cohort that had been received from Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery, 20 of which were subsequently relocated to West Pond in Imperial County for ongoing fish management work by the California Department of Fish and Game.
