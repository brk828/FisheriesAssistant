# Gambusia affinis holbrooki

## Summary
Gambusia affinis holbrooki is a subspecies of mosquitofish native to the southeastern United States. Like the main species, it is a small livebearer that was widely introduced worldwide for mosquito control but has proven destructive to native fish populations.
