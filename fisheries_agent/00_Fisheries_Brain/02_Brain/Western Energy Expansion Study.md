# Western Energy Expansion Study

## Summary
The Western Energy Expansion Study was a 1977 proposal to expand Glen Canyon Dam's power generation capacity by adding 250 megawatts of new power plant capacity and uprating existing turbines by 104 megawatts. This study was part of the Bureau of Reclamation's shift toward maximizing hydroelectric revenue generation, which contributed to more extreme peaking power operations and accelerated environmental degradation in the Colorado River ecosystem downstream of the dam.
