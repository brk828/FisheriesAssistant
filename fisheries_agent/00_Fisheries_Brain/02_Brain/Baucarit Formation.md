# Baucarit Formation

## Summary
The Baucarit Formation is a thick continental detrital deposit that accumulated during the Miocene in the Sierra Madre Occidental region of Sonora, Mexico. According to this study, it was formed from sediments produced by the dismantling and erosion of volcanic units during Miocene vertical tectonic movements, and it fills two major NNW-SSE oriented depressions (the Yecora and Los Rios depressions) in the study area.
