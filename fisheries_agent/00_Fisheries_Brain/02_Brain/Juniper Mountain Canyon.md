# Juniper Mountain Canyon

## Summary
Juniper Mountain Canyon is a high-gradient canyon reach of the Yampa River in Colorado, located at river kilometers 145.6-141.9. This canyon section is characterized by boulder, cobble, and gravel substrates and represents one of several canyon reaches that dominate portions of the Yampa River's 320-kilometer course.
