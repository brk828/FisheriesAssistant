# Willamette Hatchery

## Summary
Willamette Hatchery is the facility where yearling Chinook salmon from upper-Willamette River populations were reared before being transported to Blind Slough for acclimation and release. The hatchery produced the 527,340 yearlings examined in this study across the 2002-2005 release years.
