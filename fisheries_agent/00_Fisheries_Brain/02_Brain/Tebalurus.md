# Tebalurus

## Summary
Tebalurus is a genus of madtom catfish (family Ictaluridae) found in the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin. Small individuals of Tebalurus sp. were collected during field surveys of the spring-fed aquatic systems in Coahuila, Mexico, and tissue samples were preserved for laboratory analysis.
