# Rio Carmen

## Summary
Rio Carmen is a former tributary of the Rio Grande that now flows independently to the sea due to stream capture. Gila from this river have intermediate morphological characters, with fin ray counts of 9,9-9,8, modal vertebrae count of 42, and 68-80 lateral line scales.
