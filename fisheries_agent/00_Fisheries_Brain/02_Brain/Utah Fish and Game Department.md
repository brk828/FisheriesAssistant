# Utah Fish and Game Department

## Summary
The Utah Fish and Game Department was one of two state agencies (along with Arizona Game and Fish Department) that cooperatively conducted this second-year fisheries investigation at Lake Powell from February 1965 to January 1966. The department participated in creel census operations and plankton studies throughout the reservoir.
