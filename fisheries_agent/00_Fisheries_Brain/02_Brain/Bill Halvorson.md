# Bill Halvorson

## Summary
Bill Halvorson was the unit leader of the National Biological Survey Cooperative Park Studies Unit at The University of Arizona (CPSU/UA) in 1994. He oversaw research activities and field projects across Arizona and California desert regions, including work at national monuments, wildlife refuges, and the Channel Islands.
