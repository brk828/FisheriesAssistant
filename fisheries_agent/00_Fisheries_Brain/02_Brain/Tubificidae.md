# Tubificidae

## Summary
Tubificidae are a family of small, aquatic oligochaete worms (segmented worms) commonly found in freshwater sediments and benthic habitats, particularly in organically enriched or polluted environments. These organisms are ecologically significant as indicators of water quality and pollution, as they proliferate in low-oxygen conditions and heavily contaminated substrates, while also contributing substantially to sediment respiration and oxygen consumption in aquatic ecosystems. In ichthyology and fisheries contexts, Tubificidae are relevant as both potential food sources for fish species and as biological markers for assessing river and sediment health in polluted systems.
