# Ceriodaphnia

## Summary
Ceriodaphnia is a genus of zooplankton (small aquatic crustaceans). This source reports that experimental studies showed Gambusia affinis (mosquitofish) eliminated Ceriodaphnia populations in experimental ponds, demonstrating the predatory impact of introduced poeciliids on zooplankton communities.
