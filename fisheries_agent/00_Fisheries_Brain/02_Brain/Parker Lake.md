# Parker Lake

## Summary
Parker Lake was a 63-acre Wisconsin lake with pH 8.2 and temperature 48°F where antimycin A was tested in alkaline water. An application of 10 ppb successfully eliminated carp, pumpkinseed, bluegill, and yellow perch.
