# Old Dominion

## Summary
Old Dominion is a historical tailings pile that produces extremely acidic runoff with high dissolved toxic metals. Though the contamination is neutralized over short distances during stream flow periods, it represents one of the inactive mine impacts causing localized surface and groundwater pollution.
