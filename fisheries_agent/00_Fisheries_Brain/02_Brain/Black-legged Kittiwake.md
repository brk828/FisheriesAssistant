# Black-legged Kittiwake

## Summary
The Black-legged Kittiwake (Rissa tridactyla) is a seabird species mentioned as an example where multistate models have been applied to study trade-offs and life history strategies. This source indicates that multistate capture-recapture frameworks can be used to test cost-of-reproduction hypotheses in this species by defining states based on reproductive success (breeder versus non-breeder), though specific results for kittiwakes are not detailed in the provided text.
