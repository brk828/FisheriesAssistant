# Yuma Desalination Plant

## Summary
The Yuma Desalination Plant is a facility that the Bureau of Reclamation indicated is not economically viable to operate, with no plans to run it in the foreseeable future. Workshop participants reached consensus on investigating a position paper regarding the plant to address concerns about water supply for the Cienega de Santa Clara while potentially providing better quality water for portions of Mexico.
