# Virgin River Spinedace

## Summary
The Virgin River Spinedace (*Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinus*) is a subspecies-level endemic cyprinid native to the Virgin River system in the southwestern United States that has become increasingly rare due to habitat degradation and competition from invasive fish species. As a focal species for conservation efforts in the Virgin River, this small native fish serves as an indicator of ecosystem health and represents a key component of the desert aquatic community requiring active management and protection measures. Its decline exemplifies broader conservation challenges facing endemic fishes in the Colorado River Basin, where habitat modification and non-native species invasions threaten native biodiversity.
