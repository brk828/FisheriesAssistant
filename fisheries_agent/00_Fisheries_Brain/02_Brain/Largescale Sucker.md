# Largescale Sucker

## Summary
The Largescale Sucker (Catostomus macrocheilus) is a freshwater fish species native to western North America that has been studied extensively in terms of its genetics, morphology, and systematics within the sucker family. This benthic species is of conservation and ecological interest due to its habitat requirements, distribution patterns in riverine and lacustrine systems, and reproductive behaviors that make it an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health. The species is characterized by its large cycloid scales and bottom-feeding habits, and understanding its spawning ecology and population genetics is critical for managing both its conservation status and its interactions with other native fish communities.
