# Arizona Fish Commission

## Summary
The Arizona Fish Commission was a territorial agency established approximately four years before this 1885 report, operating with commissioners for three territorial sections responsible for fish distribution and propagation. Commissioner Richard Rule served the southern territory, focusing primarily on distributing carp from the U.S. Fish Commission to private pond owners, and reported zero territorial expenditure for operations over four years despite handling approximately 400 carp.
