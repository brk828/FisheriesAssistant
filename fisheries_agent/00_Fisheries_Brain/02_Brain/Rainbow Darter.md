# Rainbow Darter

## Summary
The Rainbow Darter (*Etheostoma caeruleum*), also historically referred to as the "soldier darter," is a small benthic cyprinodont fish that inhabits flowing streams and rivers, where it dominates riffle habitats and constitutes a major component of darter communities in freshwater ecosystems. This species is ecologically significant as a bottom-dwelling insectivore and indicator organism for stream health, often comprising nearly half of the total darter population in well-studied lotic environments. As a rheophilic (current-loving) specialist, the Rainbow Darter serves as a key functional component in stream food webs and is widely used in aquatic ecology research to assess habitat quality and community composition in flowing-water systems.
