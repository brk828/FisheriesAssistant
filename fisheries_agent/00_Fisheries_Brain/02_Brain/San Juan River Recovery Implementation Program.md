# San Juan River Recovery Implementation Program

## Summary
The San Juan River Recovery Implementation Program is a coordinated management and scientific initiative designed to restore populations of endemic and endangered fish species in the San Juan River, a major tributary of the Colorado River system. The program encompasses recovery planning, species reintroduction efforts, and adaptive management strategies overseen by scientific workgroups, though it has faced criticism regarding unclear goals, success criteria, and its formal classification as either a comprehensive recovery plan or strategic framework. Addressing the historical collapse of native fish populations like Colorado squawfish through habitat restoration and population monitoring, the program represents a multi-agency effort to meet recovery objectives for fish species affected by dam operations and altered river conditions in the Lower Colorado River Basin.
