# Spawning

## Summary
Spawning in Colorado River native fishes typically occurs on gravel-cobble riffles in current at temperatures above 15-18°C, with humpback chub spawning optimally at 19-22°C. Colorado squawfish spawn in late June through early August after migrating up to 200 km to specific spawning grounds, while post-Glen Canyon Dam hypolimnetic releases (7-10°C) that rarely warm to 16°C prevent reproduction of warmwater native species throughout most of the system.
