# Thunnus alalunga

## Summary
Thunnus alalunga is the albacore, a strictly pelagic tuna species. At the Arroyo Sequit Shellmound, 17 vertebrae from albacore were recovered, providing clear evidence that aboriginal inhabitants used boats for fishing since this species cannot be caught from shore.
