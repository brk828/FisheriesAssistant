# Arivaca Pond

## Summary
Arivaca Pond (site 127) is a conservation site in southern Arizona that was stocked with Gila Topminnow. During the January-February 1991 survey, the pond supported a Gila Topminnow population with abundant juveniles but relatively few adults, though the population had decreased from its 1989 peak. Excessive aquatic vegetation at the site limited fish populations and made sampling difficult.
