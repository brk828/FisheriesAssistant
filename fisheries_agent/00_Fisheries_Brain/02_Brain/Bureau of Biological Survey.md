# Bureau of Biological Survey

## Summary
The Bureau of Biological Survey was a federal agency that conducted extensive ecological surveys across the Southwest and beyond following Merriam's pioneering work, including surveys of Texas, New Mexico, California mountains, and Nevada. This agency later gave rise to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Department of Interior), with some elements transferred to the U.S. Forest Service (Department of Agriculture).
