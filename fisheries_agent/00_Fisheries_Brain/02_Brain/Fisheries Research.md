# Fisheries Research

## Summary
Fisheries Research encompasses the scientific investigation and study of fish populations, aquatic ecosystems, and hatchery operations to support evidence-based management decisions and conservation efforts. This field applies quantitative methods, statistical analysis, and field-based observations to understand fish biology, mortality patterns, productivity, and health in order to inform wildlife management policies and fisheries programs. As a specialized discipline within ichthyology and aquatic ecology, fisheries research bridges basic science with practical applications in fishery sustainability, hatchery management, and the coordination of federal and regional fish conservation initiatives.
