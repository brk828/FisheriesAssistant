# Chareae

## Summary
Chareae is a tribe of mostly corticated Characeae with a single tier of 5 crown cells, containing six genera including the large genus Chara (116 species) plus Protochara, Nitellopsis, Lychnothamnus, and Lamprothamnium. This study documented distinct polyploid series within this tribe, particularly the 14:28 series in corticate Chara species.
