# Black Canyon Lake

## Summary
Black Canyon Lake is an Arizona reservoir managed by the Arizona Game and Fish Department as part of recreational fisheries monitoring and cold-water sport fish management programs during the mid-1960s. The lake served as one of five study sites in a visitor-use and fisheries survey conducted between 1965-1966 under the River Basin Investigations program, with Rainbow Trout stocked as fingerlings and managed as the primary sport species. This reservoir exemplifies the integrated approach to water resource management and recreational fisheries assessment that accompanied major water development projects like the Central Arizona Project.
