# Pike

## Summary
Pike are large predatory freshwater fish that serve as apex or near-apex predators in inland aquatic ecosystems, capable of significantly influencing community structure through predation when present in sufficient abundance. These fish are commonly studied in fisheries science and conservation contexts, particularly regarding their role in regulating populations of smaller fish species and their vulnerability to recreational angling pressure in managed lake systems. Pike populations are often monitored alongside other large predators like largemouth bass and gar to assess ecosystem health and predator-prey dynamics in freshwater communities.
