# Colorado River Basin States

## Summary
The Colorado River Basin States are the seven U.S. states (Arizona, California, Colorado, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, and Wyoming) that share water from the Colorado River system under the Law of the River. In the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies process, these states emphasized they are not fully represented by the Bureau of Reclamation alone and required their own representation at both policy and technical levels; the meeting notes recommended that governor representatives from all seven Basin States should serve on the Executive Review Committee to ensure their interests in water allocation and dam operations were properly considered.
