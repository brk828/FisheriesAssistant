# Lake Mohave Backwater-Rearing Facility Management and Research Plan

## Summary
The Lake Mohave Backwater-Rearing Facility Management and Research Plan is a 1989 planning document (with a 1994 Draft Addendum) that established goals for the razorback sucker recovery program. According to this source, the plan set objectives including stocking 50,000 juvenile razorback suckers into Lake Mohave from various sources, increasing production levels, adopting more effective culturing techniques, and analyzing the effectiveness of on-site rearing methods.
