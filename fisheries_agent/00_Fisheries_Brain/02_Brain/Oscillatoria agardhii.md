# Oscillatoria agardhii

## Summary
Oscillatoria agardhii is a blue-green algae (cyanobacterium) that forms dense, nearly monospecific blooms in the metalimnion of certain lakes. This study found it produces oxygen maxima at 10-12°C and 1-3% surface light intensity, achieving photosynthetic efficiencies of 0.8-1.7% at maximum production depths of 6-8 meters. The species may possess chemosynthetic capabilities similar to Beggiatoa, supplementing photosynthesis with H₂S-based carbon fixation in low-oxygen, sulfide-rich metalimnetic waters.
