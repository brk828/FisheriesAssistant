# Florida Game and Fresh Water Fish Commission

## Summary
The Florida Game and Fresh Water Fish Commission is a state wildlife management agency in Florida. According to this source, they supplied the initial stock of Palaemonetes paludosus shrimp used for the experimental introduction into the Lower Colorado River system beginning in 1958.
