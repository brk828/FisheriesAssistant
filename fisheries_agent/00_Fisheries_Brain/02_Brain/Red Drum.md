# Red Drum

## Summary
Red Drum (*Sciaenops ocellata*), commonly known as redfish, is a sciaenid fish species that inhabits estuarine and coastal waters, particularly in the Gulf of Mexico and Atlantic regions, and serves as an important recreational and commercial fishery resource. As a seasonal immigrant that spawns offshore with juveniles recruiting to estuarine nursery habitats during summer months, red drum plays a significant ecological role in coastal food webs and has been subject to various fisheries management and conservation studies throughout the latter 20th century. The species' ecological importance and economic value have made it a focus of research regarding stock assessment, habitat utilization, and experimental management programs in systems ranging from natural estuaries to inland reservoirs.
