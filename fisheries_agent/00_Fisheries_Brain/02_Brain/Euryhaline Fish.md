# Euryhaline Fish

## Summary
Euryhaline fish are species capable of surviving and reproducing across a wide range of salinity environments, from freshwater to marine conditions, making them well-adapted to estuaries, bays, and other habitats where salinity fluctuates seasonally or regularly. This physiological flexibility allows euryhaline fish to tolerate the osmotic stresses associated with varying water salinity levels and represents a key adaptation for species inhabiting transitional aquatic zones. The ability to regulate internal ion and water balance across different salinity regimes is fundamental to the ecological success of euryhaline species in unstable or mixed-salinity environments.
