# Alpine Lakes

## Summary
Alpine Lakes are permanent fishless standing waters found in high-elevation areas of the Sacramento-San Joaquin Province. Lake Tahoe in the Great Basin Province is described as a large, deep, extraordinarily clear alpine lake, though it contains fish.
