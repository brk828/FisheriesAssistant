# Fish Screens

## Summary
Fish screens are protective barriers that prevent fish from being drawn into water intake structures. This source specifies that fish screens are installed at inlet structures in the nursery habitat system to protect juvenile fish from entrainment during water intake operations. The screens work in conjunction with grated culverts as part of the overall fish protection infrastructure in the managed floodplain pond system.
