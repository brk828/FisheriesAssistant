# Switzerland

## Summary
Switzerland is one of the Alpine regions where 14 lakes were studied for metalimnetic oxygen maxima. Swiss Alpine lakes showed no clear correlation between morphometry and oxygen maxima, with edaphic stability (mountain protection from wind) being more important than lake shape. These lakes featured Oscillatoria rubescens rather than O. agardhii and showed preliminary primary production data of 0.1-1.2 g C/m²/day.
