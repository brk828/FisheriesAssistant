# American Bullfrog

## Summary
The American Bullfrog is an introduced species in the southwestern United States that impacts native wetland ecosystems through predation. CPSU/UA staff co-authored a paper for the NBS Status and Trends report documenting predation by introduced bullfrogs in southwest US wetlands.
