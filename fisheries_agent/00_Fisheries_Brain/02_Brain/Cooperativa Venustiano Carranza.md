# Cooperativa Venustiano Carranza

## Summary
Cooperativa Venustiano Carranza is a fishing cooperative in El Golfo de Santa Clara, Sonora, with 64 members and the only license for shrimp fishing in that area. This source reports their shrimp catches dropped dramatically from 150 metric tons per year to only 60 tons by January 1985, though recovering slightly to 119 metric tons in the 1985-86 season, with summer shark catches also declining 80%.
