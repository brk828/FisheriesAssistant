# Quitobaquito Pupfish

## Summary
The Quitobaquito Pupfish (Cyprinodon maculatus ార subspecies) is a critically endangered cyprinodont endemic to isolated spring systems in the southwestern United States and Mexico, representing a distinct evolutionary lineage of particular conservation concern due to its highly restricted geographic range and vulnerability to habitat degradation. As a small, spring-dependent specialist, this pupfish serves as an indicator species for the health of desert aquatic ecosystems and exemplifies the conservation challenges facing Arizona's freshwater ichthyofauna. Its protection has involved permit management and population monitoring efforts overseen by wildlife agencies working to prevent extinction and maintain genetic viability of this geographically isolated taxon.
