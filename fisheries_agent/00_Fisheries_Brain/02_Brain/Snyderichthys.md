# Snyderichthys

## Summary
Snyderichthys was a monotypic genus originally established for what is now called Gila copei. This study's molecular evidence showed that populations previously identified as Gila copei (formerly Snyderichthys) are nested within Lepidomeda, requiring taxonomic revision to transfer these fish to the genus Lepidomeda.
