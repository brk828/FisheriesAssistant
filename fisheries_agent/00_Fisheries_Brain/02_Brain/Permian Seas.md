# Permian Seas

## Summary
Permian Seas refers to ancient marine waters that once covered areas of Texas and New Mexico, leaving behind marine sediments. This source indicates that all Gammarus-Pecos Complex populations inhabit springs and streams in areas once overlain by these Permian seas, with the amphipods living in sulfatochloride waters derived from these Permian marine sediments. The Permian Basin was connected to open sea through Delaware and Marfa basins via a geosyncline crossing northern Mexico.
