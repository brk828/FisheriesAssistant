# crustacean zooplankton

## Summary
Crustacean zooplankton were a major taxonomic group covered in the Advanced Limnology (Plankton Ecology) course proposed for the Sierra Ancha summer program. Along with phytoplankton, they were emphasized in the five-week course focusing on taxonomy, ecology, and evolutionary biology of freshwater plankton.
