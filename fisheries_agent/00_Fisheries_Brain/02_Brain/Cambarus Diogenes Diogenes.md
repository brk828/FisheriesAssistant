# Cambarus Diogenes Diogenes

## Summary
Cambarus diogenes diogenes is a burrowing crayfish species that inhabits terrestrial burrows in drainage ditches and flood plains. This source reports it serves as the host for two newly described entocytherid ostracod species (Saurocythere rhipis and Ornithocythere gypodes) that live epizootically on the crayfish in Alabama and Mississippi.
