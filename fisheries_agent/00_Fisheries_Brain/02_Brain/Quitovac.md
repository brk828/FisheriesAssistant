# Quitovac

## Summary
Quitovac is a location in Sonora, Mexico, approximately 35 miles southeast of Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. This source mentions it as a site with similar Paleoindian finds including mammoth remains, used as a comparison for potential discoveries at Quitobaquito Basin.
