# Pacific Halibut

## Summary
Pacific Halibut (*Hippoglossus stenolepis*) is a large rightye flounder (Pleuronectidae) species of significant commercial and ecological importance in North Pacific fisheries. As a demersal predator inhabiting various depth zones from shallow coastal waters to deep rocky reef habitats, Pacific Halibut serves as both a key indicator species for evaluating fishing pressure across depth zones and a management subject requiring careful regulatory attention to optimize sustainable yields and population structure. Its presence in archaeological and contemporary fishery records reflects its historical importance as a target species and its utility in understanding human fishing capabilities and marine resource use patterns.
