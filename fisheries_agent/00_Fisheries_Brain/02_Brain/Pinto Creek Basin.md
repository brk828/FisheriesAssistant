# Pinto Creek Basin

## Summary
The Pinto Creek Basin is a drainage basin in eastern Arizona where present-day mining activities have had minimal groundwater and surface water quality impacts. The basin maintains better water quality overall than Pinal Creek Basin, with periodic violations of Arizona Stream Standards for copper attributed to surface runoff from the abandoned Bellview-Gibson Mine site rather than current operations.
