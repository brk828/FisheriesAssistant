# Black Crappie

## Summary
Black Crappie (*Pomoxis nigromaculatus*) is a centrarchid warmwater fish species classified as a panfish and secondary game fish in North American freshwater ecosystems, commonly occurring in lakes and reservoirs where it serves as an important component of recreational fisheries. As a member of the panfish guild alongside species like Yellow Perch and White Crappie, Black Crappie populations are typically monitored and managed at minimum abundance thresholds of approximately 200 individuals per water body to sustain viable fisheries. This species inhabits littoral and near-shore zones of freshwater lakes and reservoirs, where it occupies ecological niches similar to other centrarchids and represents a significant food fish resource in warmwater fishery management and conservation strategies.
