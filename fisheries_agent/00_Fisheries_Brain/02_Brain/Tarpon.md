# Tarpon

## Summary
Tarpon (*Megalops atlanticus*) is a large, euryhaline marine fish species that was experimentally introduced into inland Texas reservoirs, particularly high-salinity impoundments unsuitable for native freshwater game fish, as part of mid-1980s fisheries management trials examining its potential to utilize underexploited aquatic habitats. The species' exceptional salinity tolerance and ability to survive in heated power-plant reservoirs made it a candidate for colonizing marginal inland waters, though its ecological role in these novel environments and long-term establishment success required controlled laboratory and field assessment.
