# Douglas, Arizona

## Summary
Douglas, Arizona is the location of Slaughter Ranch, which contains significant artesian water resources. Residents of Douglas purchased commercially bottled water from the ranch springs during the mid-1920s through early 1930s before the municipal water system was developed.
