# Gila Spikedace

## Summary
The Gila Spikedace (*Meda fulgida*) is a small cyprinid minnow native to the southwestern United States that has experienced significant population declines and habitat loss, resulting in its designation as a threatened species by conservation agencies. This species is characteristic of larger streams and tributaries within the Colorado River system, where it inhabits flowing waters and plays an ecological role within native fish communities alongside other endemic cyprinids such as the Loach Minnow and Gila Chub. Its conservation status reflects the broader environmental pressures affecting southwestern aquatic ecosystems, including water diversion, habitat fragmentation, and competition from non-native fish species.
