# Wading River

## Summary
Wading River is an 11-mile poor-quality trout stream in Foxboro/Mansfield/Norton, Massachusetts, characterized by sluggish flow, dark brown water, summer temperatures in the 70s-80s, and drainage from numerous warm-water ponds and swamps. This source documents that following 1955 rotenone treatment and fingerling stocking, no trout survival was detected after one year, likely due to high water temperatures at stocking and unsuitable habitat conditions, demonstrating that reclamation cannot overcome fundamental habitat limitations.
