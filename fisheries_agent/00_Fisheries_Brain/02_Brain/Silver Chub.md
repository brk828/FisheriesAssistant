# Silver Chub

## Summary
The Silver Chub is a freshwater cyprinid minnow species that serves as an indicator of clean-water conditions in riverine and stream ecosystems, particularly in North American drainage systems including the Missouri River basin. As a "clean-water fish," the Silver Chub typically colonizes or increases in abundance following environmental improvements such as pollution abatement, making it a valuable bioindicator species for assessing water quality recovery and ecosystem restoration in freshwater communities. Its presence and population dynamics are commonly used in ichthyological surveys to monitor changes in fish assemblage composition and the ecological health of lotic habitats.
