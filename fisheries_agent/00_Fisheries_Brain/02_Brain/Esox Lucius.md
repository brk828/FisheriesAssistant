# Esox Lucius

## Summary
Esox lucius, commonly known as the northern pike, is a large, predatory freshwater fish species distributed across the Northern Hemisphere that plays a significant ecological role as an apex predator in lakes and river systems. This species is of considerable importance to both fisheries management and conservation biology, as it serves as both a valued gamefish and an indicator of aquatic ecosystem health, while also presenting management challenges in non-native regions where it can negatively impact native fish populations through predation. Its trophic position, reproductive biology, and population dynamics make it a key subject for understanding predator-prey relationships and the ecological impacts of fish introductions in freshwater ecosystems.
