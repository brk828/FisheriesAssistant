# Las Vegas Wash

## Summary
The Las Vegas Wash is a nutrient-rich effluent stream that enters Lake Mead through Las Vegas Bay in Nevada, serving as a significant source of anthropogenic nutrients and altered water chemistry to the reservoir system. As both a recreational fishing location within the Lake Mead National Recreation Area and a critical zone of ecological study, the Las Vegas Wash represents an important interface between urban water discharge and lacustrine ecosystems, where density current dynamics and nutrient enrichment substantially influence phytoplankton communities and overall aquatic productivity in the receiving waters.
