# Red Shiners

## Summary
Red Shiners are a non-native fish species present in the Colorado River system below Glen Canyon Dam. The report indicates that Red Shiners are expected to have limited impacts on native fish recovery efforts under warmer water release scenarios due to their habitat requirements, current velocity intolerance, or existing distribution patterns.
