# Cashion Citizens for Uncontaminated Water

## Summary
Cashion Citizens for Uncontaminated Water is a citizen organization addressing groundwater contamination from pesticides in the Cashion area of Arizona. The group was mentioned in the 1987 directory as an example of organizations addressing aquatic habitat threats, specifically groundwater contamination issues.
