# A-10 Backwater

## Summary
A-10 Backwater is a 10.1-hectare semi-isolated backwater habitat at river mile 114 in the Lower Colorado River, connected to the main channel via culvert pipes. This source found it accumulated stocked razorback suckers with annually increasing CPUE (22.5 fish/1,000 sec electrofishing by 2008), had population estimates of 2,719 fish (2007) and 6,054 fish (2008), but exhibited the highest avian predation rates (23% of catch showing evidence, significantly more than other locations) with winter peaks exceeding 30% and few large piscine predators.
