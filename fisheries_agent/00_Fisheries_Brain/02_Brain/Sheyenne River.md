# Sheyenne River

## Summary
The Sheyenne River was proposed as an outlet from Devils Lake to lower lake elevations during high water periods in the 1990s. This proposal raised concerns about potential transfer of striped bass to Canadian waters via the Red River system, ultimately flowing into Lake Winnipeg, Manitoba, which prompted this study to assess striped bass survival and reproduction risks.

## Context
