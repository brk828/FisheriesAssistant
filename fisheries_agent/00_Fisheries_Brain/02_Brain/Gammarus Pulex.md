# Gammarus Pulex

## Summary
Gammarus pulex is a freshwater amphipod crustacean widely employed as a model organism and bioindicator in aquatic ecology and conservation studies due to its sensitivity to environmental changes and ease of population monitoring. This species is particularly valuable for assessing the impacts of habitat disturbances such as water level fluctuations, as evidenced by documented population crashes in response to hydrological changes, making it a useful indicator of ecosystem stress and environmental degradation in freshwater systems.
