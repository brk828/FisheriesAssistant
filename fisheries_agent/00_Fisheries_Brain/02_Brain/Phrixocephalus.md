# Phrixocephalus

## Summary
Phrixocephalus is a lernaeocerid genus that most closely resembles Impexus in overall morphology. However, Impexus differs from Phrixocephalus in that its profusely branching holdfast constitutes the most anterior body part (preoral segments) rather than being posterior to cephalic lobes (postoral segments).
