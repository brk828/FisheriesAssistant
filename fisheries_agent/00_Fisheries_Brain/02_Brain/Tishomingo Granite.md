# Tishomingo Granite

## Summary
Tishomingo Granite is a Precambrian coarse-grained pinkish granite formation that created the most productive stream habitat in the Blue River study. The exposed granite scarps, riffles, and low falls provided stable attachment surfaces supporting dense bryophyte and algal communities with exceptionally high productivity (up to 48.0 g O₂/m²/day in summer and 7.8 kg O₂/m²/year annually), making this reach autotrophic with photosynthesis consistently exceeding respiration.
