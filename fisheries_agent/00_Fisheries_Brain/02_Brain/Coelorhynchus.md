# Coelorhynchus

## Summary
Coelorhynchus is a genus of macrourid fishes with numerous species endemic or distinctive to the Philippine Subregion. Species found include C. quincunciatus, C. thompsoni, C. velifer, C. macrolepis, C. carinifer, and C. acutirostris, with C. velifer, C. macrolepis, and C. carinifer also occurring in the northern China Sea off southern Luzon.
