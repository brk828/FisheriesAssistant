# Tiger Salamanders

## Summary
Tiger Salamanders (Ambystoma tigrinum) are amphibians found in the lower Colorado River area. Two individuals were found in striped bass stomachs, but the author attributed their presence to bait use by anglers rather than natural predation, suggesting they are not a typical prey item for striped bass in this system.
