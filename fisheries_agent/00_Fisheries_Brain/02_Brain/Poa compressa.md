# Poa compressa

## Summary
Poa compressa is a grass species that dominated old field communities near East Lansing, Michigan where terrestrial samples were collected. Terrestrial detritus derived primarily from this species showed calorific values of 3906.8±544 to 4246.1±624 cal/g dry weight.
