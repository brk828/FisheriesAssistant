# Trammel Nets

## Summary
Trammel nets are passive fishing gear consisting of multiple layers of netting with different mesh sizes that entangle fish as they attempt to pass through, making them particularly useful for sampling in specific aquatic habitats where active gear like electrofishing may be ineffective or inappropriate. In fisheries science and conservation work, trammel nets are strategically deployed to capture fish populations while minimizing harm compared to some alternative methods, though they require careful monitoring and standardized protocols to ensure consistent and representative sampling. These nets are especially valuable for surveying fish communities in larger water bodies, complex habitats, and situations where electrical or mechanical sampling methods present logistical or ecological constraints.
