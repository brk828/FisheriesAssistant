# Oak Creek Canyon

## Summary
Oak Creek Canyon is a location in Yavapai County, Arizona overlooking Coconino National Forest where the holotype and all paratypes of Latalus draculus were collected on August 24, 1970. This represents the only known collection locality for this species.
