# Tilapias

## Summary
Tilapias are freshwater fish that represent one of the most extensively documented groups in aquaculture literature. This bibliography identifies 29 references specifically focused on tilapia culture, making them the third most-covered taxonomic group after common carp (52 references) and salmonids (48 references), reflecting their commercial importance during the 1951-1984 period.
