# Lees Ferry

## Summary
Lees Ferry is a critical monitoring and study location in the Colorado River ecosystem situated immediately downstream of Glen Canyon Dam, serving as a reference point for assessing how dam operations affect aquatic productivity, macroinvertebrate communities, and fish populations in the tailwater. This site is particularly significant for documenting the "stair-step" decline in benthic algae and invertebrate biomass that occurs below the dam and experiencing predictable shifts in species composition with increasing distance downstream, making it essential for understanding how altered flow regimes and water temperature influence native fish recovery efforts in Grand Canyon.
