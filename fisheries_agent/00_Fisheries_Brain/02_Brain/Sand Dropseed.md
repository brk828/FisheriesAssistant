# Sand Dropseed

## Summary
Sand Dropseed (Sporobolus cryptandrus) is a perennial grass species. At Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge, it has become one of the most common perennial grasses in areas where historical overgrazing has degraded the former Sonoran Savanna Grassland and reduced native perennial grass diversity.
