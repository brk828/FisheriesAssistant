# Virgin River Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Virgin River Roundtail Chub (*Gila robusta seminuda*) is a federally endangered cyprinid endemic to the Virgin River system in the Lower Colorado River Basin, representing a desert fish species adapted to specific riverine conditions. This subspecies is of critical conservation importance due to its extremely limited distribution and apparent rarity, as monitoring surveys have consistently failed to capture individuals at survey sites, indicating severely depleted populations. Its management falls under the Desert Fishes Recovery Team's responsibilities, which coordinates conservation efforts for endemic Lower Colorado River Basin species facing threats from habitat modification, water management practices, and competition from non-native fish species.
