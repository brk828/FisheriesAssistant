# Jensen Reach

## Summary
The Jensen Reach (RMI 301.7-276.9) is a section of the middle Green River where 23% of all adult razorback sucker captures occurred. Stewart Drain/Lake within this reach accounted for 82% of larval captures, making it a priority monitoring location.
