# Iztaccihuatl

## Summary
Iztaccihuatl is a volcano in Mexico from whose eastern slopes the Rio Cosala drains. This volcano forms the western boundary of the Puebla Plateau along with Popocatepetl, and its drainage system provided the habitat where Asellus puebla was discovered.
