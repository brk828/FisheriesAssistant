# Petroleum Refinery Effluent

## Summary
Petroleum refinery effluent is the wastewater discharged from petroleum refining operations, representing a complex mixture of toxic compounds. This study used effluent samples collected from a single refinery's holding pond or discharge pipe at temperatures of 90-100°F, which were then cooled to laboratory temperature (~74°F) before testing. The effluent's toxicity varied between samples, requiring exploratory tests before each bioassay to establish appropriate test concentrations for different fish species.
