# Cottonwood Lakes

## Summary
Cottonwood Lakes is a location in Inyo County, California, that served as a collection site for Golden Trout specimens. Golden Trout from Cottonwood Lakes showed antigenic differences from Laurel Lakes populations, with individual fish displaying detectable antigenic variation when tested with absorbed immune sera.
