# Otter Creek

## Summary
Otter Creek is an unpolluted control stream in Oklahoma used in this study to compare fish populations unaffected by effluents. Fish from Otter Creek showed some differences from the other control stream (Turkey Creek) in morphometric and meristic characteristics, and served as a baseline for evaluating pollution effects on cyprinid populations in Skeleton Creek.

## Context

> This doctoral dissertation (Oklahoma State University, 1966) investigated how domestic sewage and oil refinery effluents affected the morphological characteristics and population structure of three cyprinid fish species in [[Skeleton Creek]], Oklahoma.
