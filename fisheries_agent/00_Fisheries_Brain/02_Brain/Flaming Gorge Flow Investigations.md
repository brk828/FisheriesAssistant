# Flaming Gorge Flow Investigations

## Summary
The Flaming Gorge Flow Investigations (FGFI) is a five-year program that uses electrofishing to monitor and capture adult Razorback Sucker over spawning grounds in the Yampa River and Green River in Colorado-Utah. This research proposal addresses concerns that repeated electrofishing exposure during the spawning season may harm spawning adults, developing embryos, and larvae before they disperse from spawning grounds.
