# Cocopa

## Summary
The Cocopa are a Yuman tribe who were the primary indigenous inhabitants of the Colorado River Delta, living mainly between what are now the Hardy River and Colorado River. Historically numbering as many as 22,000 in 1605, their population declined to only about 776 individuals by 1986, divided between the U.S. and Mexico by the international border, with most now mestizos who depend on tourism, fishing cooperatives, and renewed cultivation of traditional crops like Distichlis grass.
