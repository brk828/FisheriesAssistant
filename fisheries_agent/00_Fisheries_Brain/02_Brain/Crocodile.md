# Crocodile

## Summary
Crocodiles are covered by a dedicated Specialist Group within SSC's reptile and amphibian conservation program. The Crocodile Specialist Group is one of 10 groups focused on reptiles and amphibians within the Commission's approximately 102 total Specialist Groups. This taxonomic group would conduct typical specialist group activities including species assessments and conservation planning.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium.
