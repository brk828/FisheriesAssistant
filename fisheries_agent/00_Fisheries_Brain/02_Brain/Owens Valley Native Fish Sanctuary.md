# Owens Valley Native Fish Sanctuary

## Summary
The Owens Valley Native Fish Sanctuary represents a conservation initiative focused on protecting endemic fish species and their aquatic habitats in the Owens Valley region, which faces threats from water extraction, habitat degradation, and invasive species competition. This sanctuary effort, discussed prominently in Desert Fishes Council meetings during the 1970s-1980s, sought to preserve critically endangered native fishes through habitat restoration, population monitoring, and management of invasive species like Gambusia affinis that outcompete native species such as the Pahrump killifish. The sanctuary exemplifies applied conservation biology strategies aimed at preventing extinctions of desert-endemic ichthyofauna facing severe anthropogenic pressures.
