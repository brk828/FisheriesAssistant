# Water Lilies

## Summary
Water lilies are emergent flowering aquatic plants that grow in freshwater systems. This source classifies them as one type of aquatic vegetation that can be considered problematic where they interfere with human activities, though the article emphasizes that complete removal of such plants is neither practical nor desirable for ecosystem health.
