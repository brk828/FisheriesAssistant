# U.S. Department of Agriculture

## Summary
The U.S. Department of Agriculture is a federal agency that issued warnings about water resource management in the San Pedro basin. In August 1977, along with the Arizona Water Commission, it warned that groundwater withdrawal exceeded recharge in the Sierra Vista-Fort Huachuca area.
