# Biological Research Resources

## Summary
Biological Research Resources (BRR) is an NSF program that supports living organism stock centers and related infrastructure. According to this source, BRR's genetics funding increased from 11.9% to 16.8% of its budget between FY 83-85, totaling $3,687K over three years for 37 awards, representing an important component of NSF's support for genetics research infrastructure.
