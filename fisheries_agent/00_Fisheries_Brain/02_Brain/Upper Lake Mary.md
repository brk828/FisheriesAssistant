# Upper Lake Mary

## Summary
Upper Lake Mary is a lake located near Flagstaff, Arizona. According to this source, it produced the Arizona State record Northern Pike in 1981—a fish measuring 47.5 inches and weighing 24 pounds 3 ounces.
