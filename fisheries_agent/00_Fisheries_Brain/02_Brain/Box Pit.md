# Box Pit

## Summary
Box Pit was a strip-pit lake that produced what was believed to be the largest black crappie on record. In May 1954, Mrs. Dorothy Kirk caught a black crappie measuring 19 inches and weighing 4.5 pounds from this site.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
