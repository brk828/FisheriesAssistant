# Bush Creek

## Summary
Bush Creek is a tributary stream that flows into the Blue River in Greenlee County, Arizona, within the Gila River basin. The existing Forest Road 281 runs between the Blue River and Bush Creek near their confluence, and the road realignment project involves moving the road crossing of Bush Creek to a location further upstream before the road turns southerly along the hillside, with the goal of reducing channelization impacts on both Bush Creek and the Blue River.
