# Eucalia inconstans

## Summary
Eucalia inconstans, commonly known as the brook stickleback, is a freshwater fish in the family Gasterosteidae. In this toxicity study, it ranked as the 6th most resistant species to petroleum refinery effluent with a 24-hour TL₅₀ of 17.70% and a 96-hour TL₅₀ that maintained similar relative resistance. The species fell within the intermediate resistance statistical population (Population III) and was not significantly different from most other species tested.
