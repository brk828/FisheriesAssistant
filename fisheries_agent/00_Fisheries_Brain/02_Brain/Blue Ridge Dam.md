# Blue Ridge Dam

## Summary
Blue Ridge Dam is a double curvature arch dam constructed in 1963 on East Clear Creek in Arizona's Mogollon Rim country as part of a water transfer project by Phelps Dodge Corporation. The dam stands 190 feet high with an unusually thin design (14 feet at base, 6 feet at crest) and creates a 19,500 acre-foot reservoir that was planned to be stocked with trout by the Arizona Game and Fish Department. The structure was designed to move on its base, sliding downstream to absorb water pressure, and was built to transfer water from the Little Colorado River drainage into the Salt River Project system.
