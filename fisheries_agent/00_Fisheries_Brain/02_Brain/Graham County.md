# Graham County

## Summary
Graham County is a county in Arizona that contains part of Aravaipa Creek. The 1974 fish survey was conducted in the sections of Aravaipa Creek that flow through both Graham County and Pinal County.

## Context

> A fish survey of [[Aravaipa Creek]] in [[Arizona]] was conducted November 9-11, 1974, by Arizona State University's Ichthyology class under supervision of Dr. W. L. Minckley.
