# Crystal Reservoir

## Summary
Crystal Reservoir is one of six major reservoirs in the Upper Colorado River Basin that collectively provide approximately 26 million acre-feet of storage capacity. It is part of the water storage and delivery infrastructure managed by the Bureau of Reclamation in the Colorado River system.
