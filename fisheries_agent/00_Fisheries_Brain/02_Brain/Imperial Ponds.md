# Imperial Ponds

## Summary
Imperial Ponds is a complex of 6 artificial ponds totaling 74.2 acres used as off-channel habitat for endangered native fishes in the Lower Colorado River. This source reports that die-offs occurred at this site in 2020 and 2022 due to high temperature, low dissolved oxygen, or human error/pump failure, illustrating the management challenges and stochastic risks facing off-channel habitats.
