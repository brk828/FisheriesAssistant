# General Springs Canyon

## Summary
General Springs Canyon is one of the major tributary waterways draining into the Blue Ridge Dam reservoir on the north side of the Mogollon Rim. When the reservoir is filled, it extends southeast up General Springs Canyon, making it one of the primary arms of the five-mile-long impoundment.
