# Sawatch Range

## Summary
The Sawatch Range is a mountain range in central Colorado where Twin Lakes are located at its eastern foot. The lakes sit at 2802 m elevation in this high-mountain setting, and the range contributes to the watershed dynamics that influence the lakes' productivity patterns.
