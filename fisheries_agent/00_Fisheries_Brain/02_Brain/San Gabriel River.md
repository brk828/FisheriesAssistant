# San Gabriel River

## Summary
The San Gabriel River is a freshwater river system in southern California, USA, that is part of the range of the federally threatened Santa Ana Sucker (Pantosteus santaanae). This study notes that the San Gabriel River is one of three river systems where inconsistent survey methodologies and seasonal sampling limitations have created critical knowledge gaps in Santa Ana Sucker population dynamics.

## Context

> **Primary Sampling Purpose**: This study evaluated the suitability of passive integrated transponder (PIT) tags for marking [[Santa Ana Sucker]] to support long-term population monitoring.
