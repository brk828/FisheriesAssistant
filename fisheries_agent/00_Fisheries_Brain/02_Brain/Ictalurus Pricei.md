# Ictalurus Pricei

## Summary
Ictalurus pricei, commonly known as the Yaqui catfish, is a native North American catfish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui basin in Mexico, belonging to the family Ictaluridae. This species has become a conservation concern due to its severely depleted populations, typically recorded in very low numbers in ecological surveys, and it predominantly inhabits the uppermost tributary streams and effluents rather than the main river channel where exotic species have become increasingly dominant. Its rarity and habitat restriction to specific refugial areas in the Rio Yaqui system make it an important indicator species for assessing the ecological integrity and biodiversity status of this Mexican river ecosystem.
