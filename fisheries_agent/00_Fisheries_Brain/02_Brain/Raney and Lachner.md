# Raney and Lachner

## Summary
Raney and Lachner (1942) conducted a comprehensive study of pikeperch food habits in Oneida Lake that included detailed documentation of prey consumed by young, juvenile, and adult fish along with an extensive literature review. Their summer and fall collections found that 41.5% of pikeperch stomachs (n=65) were empty, which is nearly half the empty-stomach rate observed in this winter study.

## Context

> This study examined winter food habits of [[Pikeperch]] (Stizostedion vitreum) in [[Oneida Lake]], New York during the winters of 1951-52 and 1952-53.
