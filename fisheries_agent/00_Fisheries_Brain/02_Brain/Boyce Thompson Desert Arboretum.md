# Boyce Thompson Desert Arboretum

## Summary
Boyce Thompson Desert Arboretum is a facility that maintains both Desert Pupfish and Gila Topminnow populations together. According to this source, there is concern that pupfish at Fort Huachuca may have hybridized with stock from this arboretum, potentially compromising genetic integrity of recovery efforts.
