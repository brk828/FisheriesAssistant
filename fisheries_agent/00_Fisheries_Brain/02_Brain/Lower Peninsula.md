# Lower Peninsula

## Summary
The Lower Peninsula refers to the southern of Michigan's two main landmasses. This study indicates that Longnose Gar are found throughout the Lower Peninsula and lower Great Lakes, Shortnose Gar are limited to the southernmost third of the Lower Peninsula, and Bullfrogs are common in preferred habitat throughout the Lower Peninsula but less common in the Upper Peninsula.
