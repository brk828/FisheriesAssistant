# Astyanax aeneus

## Summary
Astyanax aeneus is a characin species with a trans-isthmian distribution, occurring on both Pacific and Atlantic slopes in southern Mexico and Central America. This distribution pattern extends into Guatemala, Belize, Honduras, Nicaragua, Costa Rica, Panama, Colombia, Peru, and Brazil.
