# Arizona Riparian Council

## Summary
The Arizona Riparian Council is a conservation organization that was included on the extensive contact list developed during the 1989 Bog Hole management planning process. The organization was identified as a stakeholder group to be involved in future discussions about the wetland management and cooperative agreement.
