# Baird and Girard

## Summary
Baird and Girard were early taxonomists who first described Gila robusta from New Mexico in 1853, marking the beginning of scientific classification of the genus Gila. They described multiple nominal species between 1853-1854, including G. elegans, G. gracilis, G. emoryi, G. grahami, G. gibbosa, and G. pulchella, though their early classification attempts were hampered by gross errors in locality data and inadequate material.
