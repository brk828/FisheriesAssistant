# Black Bass Diversity

## Summary
Black bass diversity in the ACF Basin includes native Shoal Bass, Largemouth Bass, and Redeye Bass, plus non-native Spotted Bass, Alabama Bass, and Smallmouth Bass. This source reports that relative increases in non-native black basses and subsequent declines in Shoal Bass populations suggest non-natives may outcompete Shoal Bass, though mechanisms remain unclear.
