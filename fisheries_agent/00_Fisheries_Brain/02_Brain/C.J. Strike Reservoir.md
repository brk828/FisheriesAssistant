# C.J. Strike Reservoir

## Summary
C.J. Strike Reservoir is a 7,500-acre impoundment in south-central Idaho owned by Idaho Power Company, formed in 1952 by backing water 22 miles up the Snake River and 8 miles up the Bruneau River. The reservoir supports warm-water fisheries but investigations revealed chlorinated hydrocarbon pesticide contamination, with water samples showing aldrin concentrations up to 10,000 ppb during spring runoff and fish tissues containing various pesticides including DDE (up to 260 ppb in carp), DDT, dieldrin, and heptachlor.
