# Flaming Gorge Research Program

## Summary
The Flaming Gorge Research Program conducted larval sampling studies from 1992-1995 that produced the first confirmed captures of razorback sucker larvae in the Upper Colorado River Basin. These studies were part of ongoing research that the razorback sucker monitoring framework builds upon.
