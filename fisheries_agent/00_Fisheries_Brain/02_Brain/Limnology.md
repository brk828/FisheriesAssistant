# Limnology

## Summary
Limnology is the scientific study of freshwater ecosystems, including lakes, rivers, and reservoirs, and encompasses the physical, chemical, and biological characteristics that influence aquatic life. In the context of fish science and conservation biology, limnology provides the foundational knowledge for understanding fish habitat requirements, population dynamics, and ecosystem health in freshwater environments. Limnological research methods and field techniques are essential tools for aquatic ecologists and fisheries scientists monitoring and managing fish communities and their supporting ecosystems.
