# California Development Company

## Summary
The California Development Company was responsible for early irrigation development in the Imperial and Mexicali Valleys beginning in 1900. The company formed a Mexican subsidiary and purchased rights to the Alamo Wash to divert Colorado River water, leading to 10,000 settlers moving onto 100,000 acres irrigated by 400 miles of canals by 1904.
