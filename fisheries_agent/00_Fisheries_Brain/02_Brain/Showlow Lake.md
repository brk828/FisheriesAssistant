# Showlow Lake

## Summary
Showlow Lake is a reservoir in the Little Colorado River basin that is part of an interbasin water transfer system. The source identifies a water transfer between Showlow Lake and Carrizo Creek in the Salt River basin as one of four known interbasin transfers in Arizona. This transfer has been circumstantially implicated in the movement of aquatic fauna between basins, which poses risks to native species through the introduction of non-indigenous organisms.
