# Brewster

## Summary
Brewster is a town in Barnstable County on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, where Cliff Pond is located at 26 feet altitude. The pond attracted anglers from across Massachusetts and beyond, with only 12.6% of fishing pressure coming from local Barnstable County residents.
