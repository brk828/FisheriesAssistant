# Relict Dace

## Summary
Relict Dace (Relictus solitarius) is an endemic fish species found in Steptoe, Goshute, Butte, and Ruby valleys in Nevada. This source identifies it as one of the species directly threatened by the proposed MX Missile System through groundwater removal and habitat destruction, and notes that refuge populations were established in Spring Valley.
