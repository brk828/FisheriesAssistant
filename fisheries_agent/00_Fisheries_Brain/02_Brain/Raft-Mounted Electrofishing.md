# Raft-Mounted Electrofishing

## Summary
Raft-mounted electrofishing is the primary sampling and removal method used in this study, employing two rafts that sample each shoreline of the river. The technique uses pulsed direct current on high range with output current maintained at 4 amperes, and catch per unit effort (CPUE) is calculated as fish per hour.
