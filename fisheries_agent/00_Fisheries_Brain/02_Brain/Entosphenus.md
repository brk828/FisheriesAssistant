# Entosphenus

## Summary
Entosphenus is a genus of lampreys within the family Petromyzontidae that has been subject to taxonomic revision and nomenclatural debate among ichthyologists, particularly regarding species delineation in holarctic (Northern Hemisphere) populations. The genus is characterized partly by morphological features of the velar tentacles and other anatomical structures that systematists use to distinguish species within this parasitic fish group. Understanding Entosphenus taxonomy and distribution is relevant to both basic ichthyological knowledge and conservation efforts for lamprey populations in freshwater ecosystems across North America and Eurasia.
