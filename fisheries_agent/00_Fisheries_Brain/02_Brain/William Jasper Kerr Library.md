# William Jasper Kerr Library

## Summary
The William Jasper Kerr Library is the library at Oregon State University in Corvallis, Oregon. As of December 1979, it maintained a specialized research collection of over 400 serial publications covering fisheries, wildlife, ichthyology, marine biology, aquaculture, and related fields, with holdings dating from the 19th century to contemporary publications.
