# Inspiration Consolidated Copper Company

## Summary
Inspiration Consolidated Copper Company was one of three active mining companies operating during the study period. The company's acidic heap-leach solutions moving down Live Oak Gulch alluvium were identified as a possible contamination source for the Miami town area plume.
