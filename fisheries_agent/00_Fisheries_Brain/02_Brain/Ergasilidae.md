# Ergasilidae

## Summary
Ergasilidae is the family of parasitic copepods to which Ergasilus sieboldi belongs. Members of this family have specialized adaptations including modified antennae for grasping gill filaments and specialized mouthparts for lacerating gill tissues, with only adult females being parasitic while males remain free-swimming.

## Context

> This report documents the first British record of the parasitic copepod *[[Ergasilus sieboldi]]* Nordmann, discovered in July 1967 on [[Brown Trout]] (*Salmo trutta*) in [[Howbrook Reservoir]], Yorkshire.
