# Black Draw-Source

## Summary
Black Draw-Source is a springhead location on San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge where perennial flow originates from a partially thermal spring maintaining temperatures around 22°C. This site served as a reintroduction location for Yaqui topminnow in 1985 and provided refuge from mosquitofish predation, with higher alkalinity (7.18 meq/L) and conductivity (655 μS/cm) than downstream sites. After mosquitofish reinvaded in summer 1986, they remained at relatively low abundance (3.9-21.2%) through October 1987, and the site continued to serve as a source for downstream recolonization of topminnows.
