# Fingernail Clam

## Summary
Fingernail clams are small freshwater bivalve mollusks found in Twin Lakes, Colorado, represented by two species: Pisidium casertanum and P. pauperculum. In this study, they were an important food source for both white and longnose suckers, particularly in the 200-350 mm size class where they comprised 4-7% of diet by both number and mass, with consumption peaking in fall months.
