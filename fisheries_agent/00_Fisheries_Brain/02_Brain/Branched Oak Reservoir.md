# Branched Oak Reservoir

## Summary
Branched Oak Reservoir in Nebraska contains striped bass/white bass hybrids that exhibit rapid growth rates, reaching 442 mm by age 4 (larger than age 6 Devils Lake white bass at 362 mm). The absence of any Morone specimens of comparable hybrid size in Devils Lake sampling provided additional evidence that hybridization had not occurred there.

## Context

> This study assessed the risk of [[Striped Bass]] (Morone saxatilis) transfer from [[Devils Lake]] to Canadian waters via a proposed outlet to the [[Sheyenne River]].
