# Dorosoma Petenense

## Summary
Dorosoma petenense, commonly known as threadfin shad, is a euryhaline forage fish native to Central America and the southeastern United States that has been widely introduced into inland water systems, including Mexican river basins like the Río Yaqui, where it was first documented in 1988. This species is valued in fisheries management as a forage base for sport fish in reservoirs and fluctuating water bodies, though its introduction into non-native systems raises conservation concerns regarding potential ecological impacts on native fish assemblages through competition and habitat partitioning. Its wide salinity and habitat tolerance enables it to thrive in diverse aquatic environments ranging from freshwater rivers and lakes to estuaries and marine settings.
