# Oregon Junco

## Summary
Oregon Junco was monitored for DDT residues following forest application at Knox Mountain, California in 1964. This species showed the second-highest 3-month residues among monitored birds at 6.0 ppm, demonstrating pesticide accumulation in forest songbirds.
