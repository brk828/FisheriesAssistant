# Trichodina

## Summary
Trichodina is a ciliate protozoan parasite that infests the gills of fish, ranging from light to heavy infections depending on environmental and host conditions. As a common gill parasite in aquatic systems, Trichodina infections are frequently documented in fish disease surveys and can indicate poor water quality or stressed host populations, though it is often observed as a non-causative agent alongside other pathogens rather than as a primary mortality factor. Understanding Trichodina prevalence is important in fish conservation and aquaculture management, particularly for sensitive species like the Gila topminnow that inhabit specialized habitats with limited natural disease exposure.
