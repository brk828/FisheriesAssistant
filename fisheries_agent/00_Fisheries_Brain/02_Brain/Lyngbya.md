# Lyngbya

## Summary
Lyngbya is a filamentous blue-green alga that was part of the characteristic waste-treatment community thriving in highly radioactive seepage areas (250-400 mr/hr) from Disposal Pit IV. Along with Oscillatoria strands, Lyngbya occurred at densities of 403,200/ml in these extreme environments, showing resistance to both high radiation and high salt concentrations.
