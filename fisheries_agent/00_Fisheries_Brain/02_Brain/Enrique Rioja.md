# Enrique Rioja

## Summary
Enrique Rioja was the researcher who originally described this Mexican cave isopod species in 1956 as Cordioniscus laevis, based on a single female specimen from Cueva de la Hoya near Xilitla. His placement in the genus Cordioniscus was limited by the lack of male specimens, preventing examination of the male pleopod morphology that is fundamental for accurate taxonomic classification.
