# Fathead Minnow

## Summary
The fathead minnow (*Pimephales promelas*) is a small cyprinid fish native to North America that has become one of the most widely used model organisms in aquatic toxicology, ecotoxicology, and environmental monitoring studies due to its ease of culture, rapid reproduction, and sensitivity to various contaminants and stressors. In conservation and fisheries contexts, fathead minnows serve as indicator species for assessing water quality and ecosystem health, while also being employed as forage fish in aquaculture and as live bait, though they can become invasive when introduced into non-native aquatic systems. Their widespread use in laboratory and field studies makes them essential reference species for understanding fish population dynamics, behavior, and physiological responses to environmental perturbations in both lotic and lentic ecosystems.
