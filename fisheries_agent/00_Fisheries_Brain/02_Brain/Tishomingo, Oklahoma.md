# Tishomingo, Oklahoma

## Summary
Tishomingo, Oklahoma was the location of the U.S. Fish Cultural Station where this channel catfish spawning research took place. The facility conducted work to address rising demand for channel catfish fingerlings in the southern United States during the 1950s.
