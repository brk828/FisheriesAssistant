# Isoperla decepta

## Summary
Isoperla decepta is a stonefly species in Caldwell Hollow that likely survives as eggs with long hatching periods and possibly spends considerable nymphal life in the hyporheic (interstitial) zone. It is part of the winter-spring association of the intermittent stream fauna.
