# Glendale Brook

## Summary
Glendale Brook is a high-quality trout stream in Middlefield, Massachusetts, approximately 5 miles long with elevations from 1,600 to 814 feet and summer temperatures of 45-68°F. This source documents its 1955 rotenone reclamation followed by fingerling stocking, which resulted in exceptional survival (>20%, over 1,000 trout estimated) and growth rates exceeding regional averages one year later, demonstrating that removing competing warm-water species can substantially increase trout carrying capacity in suitable habitat.
