# Tombigbee River

## Summary
The Tombigbee River is a major aquatic system in northeastern Mississippi that serves as an important subject for ichthyological research and conservation, particularly notable for harboring a genetically unique, homozygous walleye population that exhibits distinct genetic characteristics compared to other North American walleye stocks. As a river system with extensive habitat diversity including major river channels, impoundments, and tributaries, the Tombigbee River represents a significant freshwater ecosystem for studying fish populations, genetic structure, and aquatic biodiversity across multiple habitat types. The river's ecological importance has made it a focal point for research examining both the genetic uniqueness of endemic fish populations and broader aquatic conservation issues related to riverine habitat management and species preservation.
