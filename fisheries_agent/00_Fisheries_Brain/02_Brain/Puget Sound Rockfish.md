# Puget Sound Rockfish

## Summary
Puget Sound Rockfish (Sebastes emphaeus) is a Pacific rockfish species that exhibits similar seasonal behavior to the species studied in Lynn Canal. Research by Moulton (1975) found dramatic declines in sightings during fall, with fish huddled in caves by November in Puget Sound.
