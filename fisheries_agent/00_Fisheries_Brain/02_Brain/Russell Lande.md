# Russell Lande

## Summary
Russell Lande is the author of this theoretical paper on captive breeding genetics. He developed analytical models that extend previous work by incorporating mutation, immigration, selection in captivity, and population subdivision into breeding program design for endangered species, with the goal of maintaining genetic diversity in small populations.
