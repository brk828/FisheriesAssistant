# Colorado River Compact

## Summary
The Colorado River Compact is the 1922 interstate water allocation agreement that governs flow distribution in the Colorado River system, mandating a 10-year moving average delivery of 7.5 million acre-feet per year from the Upper Basin to the Lower Basin at Lee Ferry. From an aquatic ecology perspective, this legally binding compact fundamentally constrains river management decisions and environmental flows available for fish populations and ecosystem health, as the rigid allocation requirements often limit flexibility in water management needed to support native fish conservation and restore natural flow regimes in the river system.
