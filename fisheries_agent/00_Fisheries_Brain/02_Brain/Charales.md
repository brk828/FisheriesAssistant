# Charales

## Summary
Charales is the single order within the Characeae family, containing one extant family (Characeae) subdivided into two tribes: Nitelleae (ecorticate species) and Chareae (mostly corticated species). This study examined chromosome numbers across multiple genera within this order to understand taxonomic relationships.
