# Pacific Northwest

## Summary
The Pacific Northwest is the native range of Kokanee salmon. This source identifies the Pacific Northwest as the region where Kokanee naturally occurs, and eggs for California introductions were obtained from Idaho, Montana, and Washington within this region.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
