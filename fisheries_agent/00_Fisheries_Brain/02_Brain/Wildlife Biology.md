# Wildlife Biology

## Summary
Wildlife Biology is an academic field of study focused on the science of wildlife species and their management. According to this 1994 agreement between Arizona State University and the Arizona Game and Fish Department, students pursuing Bachelor's or Master's degrees in Wildlife Biology or related administrative areas were eligible for internships that provided practical experience in wildlife research, wildlife management, and administrative programs at various field sites throughout Arizona.
