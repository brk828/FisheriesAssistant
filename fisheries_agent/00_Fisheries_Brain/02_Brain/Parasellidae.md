# Parasellidae

## Context
> - **Diagnostic criteria used for generic revision**: combination of morphological traits (e.g., degree of coxopodite development on the pereiopods) and structure of the male copulatory organ (used by Steeves to define three evolutionary lineages within *Conasellus*). - **Bragasellus** (nov. gen.)...
