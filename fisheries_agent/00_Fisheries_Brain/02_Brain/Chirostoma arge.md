# Chirostoma arge

## Summary
Chirostoma arge is an atherinid fish that has been revised and recognized as a species complex rather than a single species. Chirostoma is the most prolific genus among vicarious fishes in México with 19 species, and fossil records of the genus extend to the Pliocene.
