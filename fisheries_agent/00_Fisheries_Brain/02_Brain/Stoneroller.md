# Stoneroller

## Summary
Stoneroller refers to cyprinid fishes of the genus Campostoma, particularly common in headwater streams and small tributaries of North American river systems. These benthic-feeding minnows are specialized inhabitants of swift, rocky habitat reaches where they graze on algae and organic matter from stream substrates, making them important indicators of clear, well-oxygenated headwater conditions and contributors to stream ecosystem function through their algal grazing activities. Their presence and abundance in stream surveys reflect habitat stability and quality, as they typically dominate the ichthyofauna of extreme upper reaches alongside other headwater specialists like creek chubs.
