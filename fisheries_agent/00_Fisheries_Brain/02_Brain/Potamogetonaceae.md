# Potamogetonaceae

## Summary
Potamogetonaceae is a family of higher aquatic vegetation (pondweeds) that was present in Holbrook Lake Reservoir. Like Polygonacea, this vegetation was common from 1946-1948 but disappeared by 1952-1953 due to drastic water level fluctuations and rough fish feeding activity, contributing to the acute shortage of organisms associated with aquatic plants.
