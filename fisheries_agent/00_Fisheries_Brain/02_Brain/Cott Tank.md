# Cott Tank

## Summary
Cott Tank is a location in Redrock Canyon where a tributary drainage supports one of three perennial reaches with Gila topminnow populations. The Cott Tank drainage population is described as healthy and relatively stable (38-100% of topminnow/mosquitofish community 1987-1992) despite the presence of largemouth bass and mosquitofish, and will be protected by livestock exclosure under the Redrock Action Plan.
