# Southern California

## Summary
Southern California is a residence area and administrative region (Region 5a) in California's inland fisheries management system. In 1960, anglers from this area comprised 42% of all trout anglers statewide and took 36% of the state's trout catch, with nearly 15% caught within Southern California itself due to extensive stocking programs with catchable-sized trout that began after 1948.
