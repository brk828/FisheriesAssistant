# Backwater Habitat Restoration

## Summary
Backwater habitat restoration involves the rehabilitation and management of low-velocity, off-channel water bodies adjacent to main river channels to enhance ecological conditions for fish and other aquatic organisms. These restoration efforts typically focus on reconnecting isolated backwaters to main stem rivers, improving water quality and flow regimes, and restoring native vegetation to create suitable spawning, rearing, and refuge habitat for fish species, particularly in systems like Lake Mohave where backwater areas have been degraded by dam operations and altered hydrology. Such restoration is critical for maintaining fisheries productivity and supporting the conservation of native fish populations in heavily regulated aquatic ecosystems.
