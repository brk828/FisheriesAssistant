# Pink Boll Worm

## Summary
The Pink Boll Worm is an agricultural pest that infested cotton crops in the Mexicali Valley, entering from the United States. This source indicates that the infestation, combined with salinity issues and collapse of world cotton prices, eliminated three-fourths of the cotton industry in the Mexicali Valley, forcing farmers to diversify to other crops.
