# Stenonema rubrum

## Summary
Stenonema rubrum is a late-summer mayfly species found in Michigan trout streams that emerges between mid-July and mid-August. The nymphs have greatly flattened bodies adapted for fast-water habitats under stones and on submerged planks, while adults are characterized by white coloration with purplish-brown markings, white legs with purple bands, and two white tails with dark cross bands that are nearly twice the body length.
