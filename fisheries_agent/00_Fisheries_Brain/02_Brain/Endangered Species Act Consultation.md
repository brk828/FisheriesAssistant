# Endangered Species Act Consultation

## Summary
Endangered Species Act Consultation is a mandatory legal process under the U.S. Endangered Species Act in which federal agencies must formally engage with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service or National Marine Fisheries Service to assess how proposed actions (such as water management projects, livestock grazing, or other activities) may affect threatened or endangered fish species and their habitats. This consultation mechanism ensures that potential impacts on protected aquatic species are evaluated and mitigation measures are developed before projects proceed, serving as a critical safeguard for fish conservation in aquatic ecosystems subject to human activities.
