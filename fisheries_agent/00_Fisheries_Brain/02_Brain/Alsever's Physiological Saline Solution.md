# Alsever's Physiological Saline Solution

## Summary
Alsever's Physiological Saline Solution was the medium used to wash trout erythrocytes (red blood cells) after collection in this study. The washed cells remained viable for several days under refrigeration, allowing for serological testing.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
