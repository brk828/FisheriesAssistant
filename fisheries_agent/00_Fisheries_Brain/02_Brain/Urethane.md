# Urethane

## Summary
Urethane is a sedative chemical used to anesthetize fish, applied as a 0.5% solution that completely relaxes fish within 3-5 minutes. This study found that while urethane significantly reduced measurement error in lake trout, the practical benefit was negligible (requiring only one additional normal fish per 100 to match drugged fish accuracy), and it is now recognized as a carcinogen with curtailed use.

## Context

> This 1958 study by Wilbur L. Hartman investigated whether sedating fish with [[Urethane]] reduced measurement error and improved accuracy when recording fork and total lengths.
