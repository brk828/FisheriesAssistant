# Texas Game and Fish Commission

## Summary
The Texas Game and Fish Commission was a state agency responsible for aquatic resource management in Texas during the mid-20th century. According to this 1953 checklist, the Commission employed aquatic biologists including Kenneth C. Jurgens and multiple field biologists (Marion Toole, Cecil W. Reid, Edward W. Bonn, and others) who collaborated to document the state's freshwater fish fauna, contributing to the comprehensive inventory of 197 species across Texas waters.
