# Dusky Rockfish

## Summary
Dusky Rockfish (Sebastes ciliatus) is a Pacific rockfish species found in southeastern Alaska. This study documented that Dusky Rockfish form mixed aggregations with Yellowtail Rockfish and exhibit dramatic seasonal behavior changes, being active 1-several meters above substrate during warm months (May-October) but retreating into crevices and becoming sluggish during cold months (November-April).
