# Reservoir Translocation Effects

## Summary
Reservoir translocation effects were studied by moving Shoal Bass into Lake Worth. This source found that 10 of 12 fish returned to the river after an average of 21 days, demonstrating that Shoal Bass do not typically persist in impounded waters and will actively move back to riverine habitats.
