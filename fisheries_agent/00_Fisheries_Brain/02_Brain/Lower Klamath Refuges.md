# Lower Klamath Refuges

## Summary
Lower Klamath Refuges is a wildlife refuge where 307 fish-eating birds died between May 22 and June 2, 1960, from toxaphene poisoning. This source determined the probable source was a 1958 application of 2 pounds per acre toxaphene on 1,500 acres of the refuge, with chemical analyses showing fatal concentrations in bird tissues two years after application.
