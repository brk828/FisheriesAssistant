# Protohistoric Period

## Summary
The Protohistoric Period dates from A.D. 1450-1700 and represents the transition between prehistoric and historic times. This source reports sites from this period at Organ Pipe with ceramics similar to Tucson basin and San Pedro area, associated with O'odham Groups.
