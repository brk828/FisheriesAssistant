# Proteocephalidae

## Summary
Proteocephalidae is a family of tapeworms within the order Proteocephalidea. The cestode Ophiotaenia fragilus recovered from Colorado River Squawfish belongs to this family, which includes various species that parasitize fish and typically have complex life cycles involving crustacean and fish intermediate hosts.

## Context

> This 1972 Master's thesis reports the first identification of the tapeworm *Ophiotaenia fragilus* from the [[Colorado River Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), a native cyprinid that has experienced dramatic population decline over 30 years.
