# Spiny Softshell Turtles

## Summary
Spiny Softshell Turtles were used as a reference comparison for contaminant analysis in this study. Sonoran Mud Turtles at Quitobaquito had significantly elevated concentrations of boron, chromium, selenium, strontium, and zinc compared to Spiny Softshell Turtles from the contaminated Gila River, though direct toxicity from these elements could not be established.
