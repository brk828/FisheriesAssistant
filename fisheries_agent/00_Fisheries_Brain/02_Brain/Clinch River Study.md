# Clinch River Study

## Summary
The Clinch River Study was a research program conducted by Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) to examine the ecological impacts of low-level radioactive waste releases from ORNL operations into White Oak Creek, which flows into the Clinch River in Tennessee. This source's investigation of smallmouth buffalo growth, movement, and radionuclide accumulation was conducted as part of this broader study, using novel autoradiographic techniques to trace fish movements through contaminated zones and assess the distribution and uptake of radionuclides (including strontium-90, cesium-137, cobalt-60, and ruthenium-106) in the aquatic ecosystem of Watts Bar Reservoir and the Clinch River system.
