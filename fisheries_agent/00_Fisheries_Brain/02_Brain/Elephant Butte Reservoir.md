# Elephant Butte Reservoir

## Summary
Elephant Butte Reservoir is a large impoundment on the Rio Grande in New Mexico that represents a critical but ecologically simplified aquatic ecosystem relevant to fish conservation, particularly regarding the endangered Rio Grande Silvery Minnow and other native species. As part of the Rio Grande's connected chain of reservoir and tailwater habitats, it exemplifies how dam construction creates distinct ecological zones with reduced trophic complexity and species diversity, making native fish communities vulnerable to displacement by invasive competitors. The reservoir's role in water management and its impact on downstream critical habitat for endangered species makes it a focal point for conservation biology and fisheries management in the Chihuahuan Desert region.
