# Box Canyon Reservoir

## Summary
Box Canyon Reservoir is a reservoir on the Pend Oreille River in Washington that supports a diverse fish community of 19 species including native salmonids and invasive Northern Pike. The reservoir is divided into management reaches for Northern Pike suppression, including areas north of Riverbend (RM 60-61) and sloughs/mainstem south of Riverbend to Pioneer Park Campground (RM 86-87), with spawning habitat targeted during February-April suppression efforts.
