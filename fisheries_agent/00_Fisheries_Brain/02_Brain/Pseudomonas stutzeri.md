# Pseudomonas stutzeri

## Summary
Pseudomonas stutzeri is a bacterial species that produces primarily nitrogen gas (99%) with trace nitrous oxide during denitrification. An organism isolated during Experiment 3 of this study was identified as P. stutzeri, and it demonstrated the ability to continue denitrification at oxygen levels up to 0.75 ppm once the process was established.
