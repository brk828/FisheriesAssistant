# Taxonomy

## Summary
Taxonomy in fish science refers to the systematic classification and organization of fish species based on their evolutionary relationships, morphological characteristics, and genetic similarities, providing a hierarchical framework for identifying and naming aquatic organisms. This foundational discipline underpins conservation biology and aquatic ecology by enabling researchers to assess biodiversity, track population dynamics, and establish priorities for species protection. Effective taxonomic systems also support the management and retrieval of comparative biological data across fish taxa, facilitating interdisciplinary research and informed fisheries management decisions.
