# Payette Lake

## Summary
Payette Lake is an oligotrophic lake in central Idaho located upstream of Cascade Reservoir on the North Fork Payette River. Despite having similar low mineral content to Cascade Reservoir, Payette Lake produces significantly less fish food due to its steep-sided deep basin morphometry compared to Cascade Reservoir's shallow basin with sloping shorelines.
