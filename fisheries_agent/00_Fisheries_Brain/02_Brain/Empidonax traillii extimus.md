# Empidonax traillii extimus

## Summary
Empidonax traillii extimus is the Southwestern Willow Flycatcher, a federally listed bird species that depends on developing cottonwood/willow riparian habitat in the San Pedro River. This source expresses concern that beaver herbivory on young riparian trees during the early recovery phase could damage critical habitat needed for this endangered species' recovery.
