# Owl Cove

## Summary
Owl Cove area is identified as one of the multiple spawning aggregation sites that razorback suckers visit in Lake Mohave. The study documented that both male and female razorback suckers visited multiple spawning aggregations including Owl Cove during single spawning periods (November-March), and individuals returned to the same aggregations across both the 1994-95 and 1995-96 spawning seasons.
