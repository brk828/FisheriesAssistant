# Grass Valley Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Grass Valley Speckled Dace (Rhinichthys osculus reliquus) is an extinct subspecies of speckled dace that was native to Nevada. This source lists it among at least seven native fishes that are extinct throughout their entire range.
