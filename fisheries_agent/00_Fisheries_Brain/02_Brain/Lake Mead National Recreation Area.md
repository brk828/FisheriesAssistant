# Lake Mead National Recreation Area

## Summary
Lake Mead National Recreation Area is a federally managed landscape encompassing the reservoir created by Hoover Dam on the Colorado River, which has become a critical zone for aquatic ecosystem management and fisheries restoration efforts, particularly through facilities like Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery that compensate for lost riverine habitat by sustaining coldwater fish populations. The area represents a complex case study in dam-altered aquatic ecology, where mitigation strategies such as hatchery-based trout production have been implemented to replace natural spawning and rearing habitat that was eliminated by dam construction. This recreation area illustrates the intersection of hydroelectric infrastructure, fisheries science, and conservation biology in managing aquatic resources within highly modified river systems.
