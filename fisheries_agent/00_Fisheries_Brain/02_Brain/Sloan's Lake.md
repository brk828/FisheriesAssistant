# Sloan's Lake

## Summary
Sloan's Lake is a warm water fishery located in Denver County, Colorado, that was monitored as one of nine representative waters in the 1955 creel census. The lake demonstrated strong fishing success with a catch-per-man-hour of 1.46 (1,069 fishermen caught 2,488 fish), and it received stocking of Drum (freshwater sheepshead) from Kansas along with Two Buttes Reservoir and John Martin Reservoir.
