# San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge (SBNWR) is a protected area in southeastern Arizona's Yaqui River Basin that serves as critical habitat for multiple federally endangered fish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui drainage, including endemic Yaqui fishes that occupy springponds and associated stream habitats within the refuge. The refuge represents a key conservation site for aquatic species management and restoration efforts, with documented populations of threatened and endangered fishes requiring active habitat restoration and multi-agency coordination to address degraded ecological conditions. Research and management activities at SBNWR have focused on population monitoring, habitat rehabilitation, and captive breeding programs to prevent extinction of these Sonoran Desert aquatic species.
