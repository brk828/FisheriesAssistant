# Transformer Pit

## Summary
Transformer Pit was a heavily fished strip-pit lake that attracted anglers from Missouri and demonstrated high fishing pressure. The site received experimental stocking of 3,000 walleye fry in 1953 (with no apparent survival) and produced green sunfish up to 7 ounces.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
