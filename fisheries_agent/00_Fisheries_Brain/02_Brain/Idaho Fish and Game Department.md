# Idaho Fish and Game Department

## Summary
The Idaho Fish and Game Department is a state agency responsible for fisheries and wildlife management in Idaho. During 1964-1965, the department conducted comprehensive water quality investigations across multiple Idaho water bodies, examining nutrient loading, eutrophication, and pesticide contamination in reservoirs and rivers.
