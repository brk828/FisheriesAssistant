# Lepidium latifolium

## Summary
Lepidium latifolium is an invasive exotic plant that expanded in the Colorado River corridor during Interim Flows monitoring (1992-1995). This source reports it was rapidly colonizing riparian habitats along with other invasive species, posing management concerns for native plant diversity and wildlife habitat quality.
