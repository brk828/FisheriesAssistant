# Great Flood

## Summary
The Great Flood refers to the 1993 flooding event in the Midwest United States that affected the Mississippi and Missouri River basins. This source indicates the flood caused over $12 billion in damages and exposed the failures of century-old flood control policies that relied on levees and channelization, leading to proposals for non-structural alternatives utilizing natural floodplain functions.
