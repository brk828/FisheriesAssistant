# D. R. Tindall

## Summary
D. R. Tindall collected environmental data documenting habitat conditions for Characeae species, including measurements of substrate type, water temperature (12.5-35.6°C), total alkalinity (22-310 mg CaCO3/l), chloride content, and pH (7.0-9.8). This ecological data revealed varying degrees of habitat specificity among different Characeae species.
