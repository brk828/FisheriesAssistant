# Devils Hole Warm Spring Riffle Beetle

## Summary
The Devils Hole Warm Spring Riffle Beetle (Stenelmis calida calida) is an aquatic beetle in the family Elmidae that is restricted to a single spring within Ash Meadows National Wildlife Refuge. This extreme endemism makes it particularly vulnerable to extirpation from localized threats.
