# U.S. Soil Conservation Service

## Summary
The U.S. Soil Conservation Service was identified by Maxwell as a key federal agency to coordinate with in implementing his water conservation proposal. Maxwell advocated for the Service's work in addressing severe erosion problems in Arizona valleys, where wheel tracks had become barrancas and plains were honeycombed with deep cuts, and proposed coordination with the Forest Service and Civilian Conservation Corps.
