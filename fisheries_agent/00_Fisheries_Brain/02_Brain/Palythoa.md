# Palythoa

## Summary
Palythoa is a genus of soft corals (zoanthids) found at Puerto Peñasco, with Palythoa ignota being the specific species documented. As a characteristic Zone IV organism, it extends upward into higher tide pools with permanent water bodies, demonstrating upward displacement in the intertidal zone.
