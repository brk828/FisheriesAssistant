# Davis Cove

## Summary
Davis Cove is a managed backwater habitat within Lake Havasu on the Lower Colorado River system that was designated for the continued grow-out and rearing of juvenile razorback suckers (Xyrauchen texanus) as part of the Native Fish Work Group's conservation program. Similar to other cove-based facilities like Yuma Cove, Davis Cove functioned as a controlled nursery environment where fish of specific size classes (approximately 16-17 cm) were transported and maintained to support the recovery of the declining wild population in Lake Mohave. This approach represented a critical element of the 1990s hatchery and habitat manipulation strategy aimed at preventing population collapse of this endangered native fish species.
