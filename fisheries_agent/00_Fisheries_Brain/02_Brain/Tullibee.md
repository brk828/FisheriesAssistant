# Tullibee

## Summary
The tullibee (Coregonus artedi) is a freshwater whitefish species native to North American lakes, particularly in the Great Lakes region and northern states like Minnesota and Michigan, where it inhabits oligotrophic (nutrient-poor) lakes as a commercially and recreationally important species. This coregonid occupies a pelagic to benthic ecological niche and has been extensively surveyed in fisheries population assessments using standardized sampling methods such as gillnetting and hoopnetting to monitor population dynamics and stock health. The tullibee serves as an important indicator species in aquatic ecology studies and fisheries management for understanding fish community structure and the ecological conditions of cold-water lake ecosystems.
