# Alaska

## Summary
Alaska, specifically Southeastern Alaska, is the geographic region where this study took place. The study references water quality conditions in three other Southeastern Alaska streams that showed mean dissolved oxygen levels of 6-8 mg/L during August in most years, providing comparative context for the low oxygen levels (5.1 mg/L, ~50% saturation) observed in Sashin Creek in August 1962.
