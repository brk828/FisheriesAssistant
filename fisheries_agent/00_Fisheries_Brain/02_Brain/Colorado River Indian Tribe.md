# Colorado River Indian Tribe

## Summary
The Colorado River Indian Tribe is a stakeholder and land management entity within the Colorado River Basin with jurisdiction over riparian and aquatic resources, including responsibility for fish and wildlife mitigation efforts in areas such as the Parker Division. As documented in fisheries research and conservation planning, the Tribe's lands and waters support critical habitats for endangered species like the razorback sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*), and their management decisions—including proposed urban development of backwater lakes designated for fish mitigation—directly impact conservation outcomes for native fish populations across the Lower Colorado River system.
