# State University

## Summary
State University refers to an institution where Prof. M.A. Brannon conducted a systematic five-year study of Devils Lake, North Dakota's water chemistry, contents, and plant/animal life. The research aimed to understand why approximately 97% of fish introduced from fresh water to this alkali lake died immediately, though no definitive reason was found.
