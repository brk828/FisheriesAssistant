# Styloniscoidea

## Summary
Styloniscoidea is the superfamily to which this species was originally thought to belong when classified as Cordioniscus laevis by Rioja. Bowman's examination of male pleopod morphology revealed this placement was incorrect, showing the species actually belongs to Trichoniscoidea instead.
