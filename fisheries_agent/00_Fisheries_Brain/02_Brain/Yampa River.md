# Yampa River

## Summary
The Yampa River is a critical tributary of the Upper Colorado River Basin and designated critical habitat for four endangered native fish species, particularly the Colorado pikeminnow (Colorado squawfish), making it a focal system for recovery efforts addressing flow regimes, habitat restoration, and non-native species management. As an unregulated or partially regulated river segment, the Yampa River serves as a key reference ecosystem for understanding natural flow-ecology relationships and evaluating the impacts of introduced species like northern pike on native cyprinid populations. The river has been the subject of intensive technical assessment and recovery planning by the Colorado River Recovery Program to establish flow recommendations and habitat requirements necessary for the persistence of Upper Colorado River Basin endemics.
