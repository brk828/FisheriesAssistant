# Three-Spined Stickleback

## Summary
The three-spined stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) is a small freshwater and euryhaline fish species widely used as a model organism in ecological and evolutionary research due to its rapid adaptation to diverse environmental conditions and its sensitivity to physiological factors such as water temperature, photoperiod, and food availability. This species is particularly valuable in studies examining thyroid function and seasonal metabolic responses, as well as in conservation assessments of temperate aquatic ecosystems where it serves as an indicator of environmental quality and habitat integrity. Its small size, short generation time, and well-characterized behavioral and morphological traits make it an ideal subject for understanding how fish populations respond to cyclical environmental variables and seasonal changes in their aquatic habitats.
