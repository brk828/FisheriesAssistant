# Black Mountains

## Summary
The Black Mountains are a mountain range in Arizona containing desert springs that support endemic mollusk species, including Goldroad Fontelicella at Goldroad Well Spring. These springs represent isolated aquatic habitats in the arid southwestern landscape.
