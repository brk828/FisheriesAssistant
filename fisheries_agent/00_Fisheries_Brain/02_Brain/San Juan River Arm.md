# San Juan River Arm

## Summary
The San Juan River Arm is a section of Lake Powell where upstream fish access is blocked by the Piute Farms Waterfall approximately 30 km upstream of the reservoir transition zone. This study documented that 20-29% of razorback suckers captured in the reservoir moved upstream 30-40 km to the waterfall within one year during spawning periods. Seventeen acoustically tagged fish moved through lacustrine habitat from the Colorado River arm to this arm, traveling at least 170 km across the reservoir.
