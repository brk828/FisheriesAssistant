# ITESM-Guaymas

## Summary
ITESM-Guaymas (Instituto Tecnológico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey, Guaymas campus) is a Mexican research institution that participated in the 1999 Colorado River Delta conservation conference. The institution hosted the delta-colorado list-serve established to facilitate binational dialogue and information sharing about delta conservation, with contact person selenedg@campus.gym.itesm.mx.
