# Raniceps raninus

## Summary
Raniceps raninus (Tadpole Fish) is a Lotinae species with small optic lobes and reduced visual dependence for feeding. Its brain morphology reflects the typical pattern of this subfamily, with limited reliance on visual cues in its ecological niche.
