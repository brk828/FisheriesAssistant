# Fish Slough Milk Vetch

## Summary
Fish Slough Milk Vetch (Astragalus lentiginosus var. piscinensis) is a plant species known only from Fish Slough. Its presence contributes to the ecological uniqueness of the Fish Slough wetland area, which extends beyond just the Owens Pupfish.

## Context

> This recovery plan addresses the [[Owens Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon radiosus*), an endangered species endemic to [[Owens Valley]], California.
