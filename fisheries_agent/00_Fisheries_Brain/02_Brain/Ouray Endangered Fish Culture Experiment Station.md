# Ouray Endangered Fish Culture Experiment Station

## Summary
The Ouray Endangered Fish Culture Experiment Station (OES) is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility that holds wild-caught razorback suckers from Lake Powell for conservation purposes. In 1991, this facility was requested to provide 15 gamete samples (5 ml each) from Lake Powell fish for genetic analysis studies aimed at characterizing genetic diversity in endangered razorback sucker populations.
