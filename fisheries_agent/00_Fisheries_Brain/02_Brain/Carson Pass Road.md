# Carson Pass Road

## Summary
Carson Pass Road is the access route to Salt Springs Reservoir. This source mentions Carson Pass Road as the location reference for Salt Springs Reservoir, the initial Kokanee introduction site in California.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
