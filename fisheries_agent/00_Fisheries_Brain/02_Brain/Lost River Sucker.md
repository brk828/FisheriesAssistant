# Lost River Sucker

## Summary
The Lost River Sucker (*Deltistes luxatus*) is an endangered catostomid fish endemic to the Upper Klamath Lake basin in Oregon that has been the focus of intensive conservation research since its federal listing in 1988. This species, typically studied alongside its co-occurring congener the Shortnose Sucker, faces multiple threats including habitat degradation, invasive species predation (particularly from introduced Fathead Minnows), and hydroelectric development that impacts its larval recruitment in critical nursery habitats like the Williamson River. Understanding the population dynamics, genetics, and ecological interactions of the Lost River Sucker has been central to Klamath basin fisheries management and endangered species recovery efforts.
