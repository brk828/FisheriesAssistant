# Salvador Contreras-Balderas

## Summary
Salvador Contreras-Balderas was a prominent Mexican ichthyologist and conservation biologist who made significant contributions to the study of endemic fish fauna in isolated aquatic systems, particularly in Mexico's arid regions such as Cuatro Ciénegas. His work focused on documenting and understanding the biogeography, speciation, and conservation status of relictual fish populations in desert springs and isolated valleys, contributing foundational knowledge about how geographic isolation drives fish endemism and biodiversity in extreme environments. Through his research and symposium organization, Contreras-Balderas advanced the scientific understanding of these unique ecosystems as critical natural laboratories for studying evolutionary processes and as priority areas for aquatic conservation.
