# South Fork

## Summary
The South Fork refers to the South Fork Salmon River, a tributary in Idaho's Salmon River drainage. This location served as a critical validation site where permit-based harvest estimates (242 steelhead in 1966) closely matched check station counts, while questionnaire estimates (722 fish) were approximately three times higher, proving the superiority of the permit method.
