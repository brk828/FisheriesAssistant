# Plateau Cove

## Summary
Plateau Cove is a location in Nevada being considered as a potential protected habitat site for endangered native fish. The management plan identifies Plateau Cove as the only site under consideration in Nevada for establishing isolated backwater habitats to support bonytail chub and razorback sucker populations as part of the Lower Colorado River native fish restoration effort.
