# Cowtrack Mountain

## Summary
Cowtrack Mountain is a geographic feature near a divide north of which Putnam (1949) identified evidence of overflow from Lake Russell at 7,150 feet elevation during the Tahoe glacial maximum. This overflow route led through Adobe Meadows into Adobe Valley.
