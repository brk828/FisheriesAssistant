# Babacomari River

## Summary
Babacomari River is a waterway connected to the San Pedro River. According to this source, a vandalism incident at the Research Ranch resulted in Findley Tank being partially drained toward this river, creating a potential pathway for alien Desert Pupfish to spread into the San Pedro River system.
