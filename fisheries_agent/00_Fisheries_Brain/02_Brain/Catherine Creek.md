# Catherine Creek

## Summary
Catherine Creek is a tributary stream flowing into Seneca Lake in the Finger Lakes Region, with approximately 11 miles of trout fishing habitat studied. It experienced substantially higher fishing pressure than other streams (365-635 anglers per mile on opening day) and consistently produced the highest catches (580-980 fish per opening week), but had the lowest fishing success rates (5-6%) due to intense angler competition.
