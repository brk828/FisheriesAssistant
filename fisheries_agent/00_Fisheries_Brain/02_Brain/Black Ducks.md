# Black Ducks

## Summary
Black Ducks (Anas rubripes) are waterfowl that utilize beaver impoundments for breeding and resting habitat. This source reports that summer brood censuses conducted by New Hampshire Fish and Game Department biologists consistently demonstrated that beaver flowages were productive breeding areas for this species during the 1940s.

## Context

> This 1948-1949 progress report documents experimental techniques developed by the [[New Hampshire Fish and Game Department]] to control water levels in [[American Beaver]] impoundments using perforated drain pipes.
