# Soulé

## Summary
Soulé and colleagues (1986) proposed the baseline management goal that this paper examines: maintaining 90% of genetic variability present in the original base population for 200 years. Lande's work extends Soulé's previous models by incorporating additional factors like mutation, immigration, selection pressures, and population subdivision strategies to provide more comprehensive breeding plan frameworks.
