# Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana

## Summary
Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana is a Mexican university whose biologists collaborated on developing the first management plan for the proposed Cuatro Ciénegas Biosphere Reserve in 1988. The team from this institution sought expert guidance from Dr. W. L. Minckley, who had 30 years of research experience in the basin.
