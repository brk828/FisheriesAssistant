# Jumbo Reservoir

## Summary
Jumbo Reservoir is a warm water fishery located in Sedgwick County, Colorado, that was one of nine representative waters in the 1955 creel census. The reservoir demonstrated strong fishing success with a catch-per-man-hour of 1.78, the highest among the named waters, with 1,078 fishermen catching 4,332 fish.
