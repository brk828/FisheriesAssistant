# Arizona Agave

## Summary
Arizona Agave (Agave arizonica) is a special-interest plant species evaluated during the Spur Cross Ranch survey. No agaves were observed during field surveys, and the habitat was not typical for this species (lacking oak-juniper woodland or mountain mahogany-oak scrub), making it unlikely to occur or be adversely impacted.
