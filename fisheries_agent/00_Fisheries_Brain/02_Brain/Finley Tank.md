# Finley Tank

## Summary
Finley Tank is a large water storage structure (20 m × 20 m) located at the National Audubon Society Research Ranch that contained abundant Desert Pupfish populations during the January 1991 survey. Plans to stock the tank with additional native fish species were postponed pending completion of San Pedro River headwater surveys to determine if native fish populations already exist in the drainage.
