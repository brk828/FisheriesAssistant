# Critical Habitat

## Summary
Critical habitat refers to specific geographic areas containing physical or biological features essential for the survival, growth, and reproduction of a species, particularly those listed as threatened or endangered under conservation law. These areas function as evolutionary templates where ecological strategies develop, and their designation is based on habitat suitability determined by proximal environmental cues that directly influence organism survival and fitness. Protection of critical habitat is fundamental to fisheries conservation and species recovery, as it ensures the maintenance of the ecological conditions necessary for populations to sustain themselves over time.
