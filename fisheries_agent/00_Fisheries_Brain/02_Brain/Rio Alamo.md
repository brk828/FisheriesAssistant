# Rio Alamo

## Summary
Rio Alamo is one of the Mexican tributaries of the Rio Grande (Rio Bravo) Basin where Dr. Contreras Balderas conducted ichthyofaunal surveys and research. His work in this river system was part of his broader studies on community composition changes and zoogeography across northern Mexican aquatic ecosystems.
