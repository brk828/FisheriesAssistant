# Gila Cliff Dwellings National Monument

## Summary
Gila Cliff Dwellings National Monument is a federally managed area that borders approximately 1 kilometer of the designated critical habitat for spikedace along the Gila River in New Mexico. As a National Monument, any federal actions within this area that may affect the designated critical habitat would require consultation under Section 7 of the Endangered Species Act.
