# Sarotherodon Mossambicus

## Summary
Sarotherodon mossambicus, commonly known as the Mozambique tilapia, is a euryhaline cichlid fish native to southeast Africa that demonstrates remarkable tolerance for variable salinity and temperature conditions, making it well-suited to estuarine environments, closed estuaries, and coastal lakes. This species has become a significant subject of study in conservation biology and fisheries science due to its role as an introduced/exotic species in various aquatic systems outside its native range, where it can substantially alter native fish community composition and ecological dynamics. Its ability to thrive in diverse and fluctuating aquatic habitats has made it both valuable for aquaculture and problematic as an invasive species in non-native regions.
