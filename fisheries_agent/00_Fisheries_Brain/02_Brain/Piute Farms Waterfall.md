# Piute Farms Waterfall

## Summary
The Piute Farms Waterfall is a 6-meter waterfall barrier located approximately 30 km upstream of the reservoir transition zone in the San Juan River arm of Lake Powell. It has presented a complete barrier to upstream fish movement since 2001 (except for a brief 2-week inundation in July-August 2011), preventing razorback suckers from accessing approximately 30 km of upstream spawning habitat. This study found that 80% of razorback suckers translocated upstream of the waterfall returned downstream within one year, necessitating annual pre-spawn translocations to maintain access to spawning areas.
