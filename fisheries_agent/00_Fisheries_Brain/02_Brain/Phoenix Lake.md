# Phoenix Lake

## Summary
Phoenix Lake is located in Marin County, California. It was one of three reservoirs where the California Department of Fish and Game transplanted native freshwater smelt from the lower Sacramento and San Joaquin Rivers as part of experiments with native species of limited distribution.

## Context

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
