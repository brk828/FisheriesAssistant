# Grassy Lake

## Summary
Grassy Lake is a California water body where toxaphene was applied at 0.1 ppm as an alternative piscicide to rotenone. Despite this use, toxaphene was subsequently rejected for future California applications due to its long-lasting residual effects and bioaccumulation through food chains.
