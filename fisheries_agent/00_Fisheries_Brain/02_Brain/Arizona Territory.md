# Arizona Territory

## Summary
Arizona Territory refers to the pre-statehood political entity that existed before Arizona became a state in 1912. Blumer conducted his botanical work in southeastern Arizona Territory in 1906-1907, during a transitional period only 20 years after Geronimo's surrender when the region around the Chiricahua Mountains was still sparsely populated.

## Context

> This historical account documents the botanical work of Swiss-born botanist [[Jacob Corwin Blumer]] (1872-1947) in southeastern [[Arizona Territory]] between August 1906 and October 1907.
