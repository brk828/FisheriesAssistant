# Xilitla

## Summary
Xilitla is a locality in San Luis Potosi, Mexico, near which all three known caves containing Xilitloniscus laevis are located. The caves (Cueva de la Hoya, Cueva de la Selva, and Cueva de la Porra) are situated within approximately 11 km of Xilitla, representing the entire known range of this troglobitic isopod species.
