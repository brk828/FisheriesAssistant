# Spotfin Shiner

## Summary
The Spotfin Shiner (*Notropis spilopterus*) is a small cyprinid fish characterized by its eurythermal nature, meaning it possesses broad thermal tolerance and can thrive in both thermally-loaded and unheated river systems. This species is widely distributed across eastern North American streams and represents a generalist cyprinid that occupies diverse microhabitats, making it a common component of temperate freshwater fish assemblages. Its ecological flexibility and prevalence in various stream conditions make it an important indicator species for assessing fish community composition and environmental stress tolerance in lotic ecosystems.
