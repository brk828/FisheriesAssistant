# Soricidae

## Summary
Soricidae (shrews) is a family of small insectivorous mammals that includes aquatic species found in the study area. Though the reproductive potential of these small insectivores would be sufficient to replace temporary losses from construction, they are among the aquatic mammals potentially affected at crossing points.
