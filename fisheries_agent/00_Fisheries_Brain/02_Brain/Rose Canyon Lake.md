# Rose Canyon Lake

## Context
> This 1969 study by Sewell, Adams, and McConnell (Arizona Cooperative Fishery Unit, University of Arizona) investigates how litter from [[Ponderosa Pine]] watersheds affects the water quality of small recreational fishing impoundments in east-central and southeast Arizona, using [[Rose Canyon Lake...
> -- *[[EffectsOfPineLitterOnQualityOf_11341]]*

> **Habitat**: [[Rose Canyon Lake]] is a small impoundment in Pima County, Arizona, with a granite rubble substrate and a watershed steeper than typical ponderosa pine forest. The lake exhibits seasonal stratification, with a hypolimnion that is typically anaerobic.
