# School Spring

## Summary
School Spring (also called Lovell's Spring) is the type locality and primary habitat for the Warm Springs Pupfish in Ash Meadows, Nevada, where the subspecies was originally described in 1948. Population censuses from 1972-1974 recorded 136-231 individuals in November counts, and the spring maintains an observation facility for public education about this endangered fish.
