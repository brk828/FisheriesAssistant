# Washington County Water Conservancy District

## Summary
The Washington County Water Conservancy District is a regional water management agency in Utah responsible for overseeing water allocation and resource management in the Virgin River basin, an area of significant concern for fish conservation and aquatic habitat protection. In the context of fisheries science and conservation biology, the District plays a key administrative and policy role in habitat management decisions affecting endemic and endangered fish species in the Virgin River system, making it a critical stakeholder in scientific debates regarding water conservation, flow management, and aquatic ecosystem preservation. Communications from ichthyological experts like James E. Deacon to the District represent efforts to ensure that scientific findings on fish habitat requirements inform water management and conservation planning decisions.
