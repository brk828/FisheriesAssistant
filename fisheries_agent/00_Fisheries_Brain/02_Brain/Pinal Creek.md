# Pinal Creek

## Summary
Pinal Creek is a location in Arizona where a breeding pair of Bald Eagles nested upstream of Roosevelt Diversion Dam. Limited data from this pair showed extensive foraging in the Salt River arm of Roosevelt Lake upstream to the diversion dam, with eagles shifting from waterfowl in winter to live fish and fish carrion almost exclusively through summer.
