# Arizona Ecological Services State Office

## Summary
The Arizona Ecological Services State Office is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service field office responsible for implementing the Endangered Species Act in Arizona. In this 1995 memorandum, the office provided detailed technical review and comments on proposed counterpart section 7 consultation regulations for the Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management, drawing on extensive experience with endangered species consultations to identify concerns about clarity, implementation feasibility, and potential legal complications in the proposed regulations.
