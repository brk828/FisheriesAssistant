# Valley of the Ajo

## Summary
The Valley of the Ajo is a location within Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. This source reports a large reservoir-based Hohokam village was identified there, surrounded by smaller villages, with agriculture enabled by water collection from large watershed catchments and canal systems requiring organized labor.
