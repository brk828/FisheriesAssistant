# Semi-desert Scrubland Biome

## Summary
The Semi-desert Scrubland Biome is one of the major biotic communities in the study area, along with Desert Grassland. Semi-desert Scrub with Juniper associations on sloping ridgetops (4,030-4,090 feet) supported high rodent densities (8.5-31.7 individuals/hectare) and biomass (0.49-1.48 kg/hectare), correlating with high plant diversity and structural complexity.
