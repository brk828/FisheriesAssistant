# University of Baja California Museum

## Summary
The University of Baja California Museum is where Anita Williams works and from which she presented documentation of the Cucapa indigenous peoples' history at the workshop. Her presentation covered the population decline from approximately 5,000 in the mid-1800s to fewer than 200 currently living in the delta.
