# San Bernardino Spring Snail

## Summary
The San Bernardino Spring Snail is a candidate species for federal listing that has a very limited distribution, occurring only in shaded hillside seeps at San Bernardino Spring within the San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge. This snail is one of the sensitive species that the refuge was established to protect and conserve.
