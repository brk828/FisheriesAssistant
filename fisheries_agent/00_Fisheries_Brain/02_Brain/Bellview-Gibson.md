# Bellview-Gibson

## Summary
Bellview-Gibson is an area containing abandoned mining facilities whose overland runoff from past heap-leach attempts represents the most significant impact from inactive mines. Surface runoff from this area causes Arizona Water Quality Standard violations in Pinto Creek.
