# Scaled Quail

## Summary
Scaled Quail (Callipepla squamata) is an upland game bird species. It is documented as one of the upland game bird species present on Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge, along with Gambel's Quail, Montezuma Quail, mourning dove, and white-winged dove.
