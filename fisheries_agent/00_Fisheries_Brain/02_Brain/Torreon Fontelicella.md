# Torreon Fontelicella

## Summary
Torreon Fontelicella is a snail discovered at Torreon Springs in 1980 that may represent the Socorro Hydrobioid (Amnicola neomexicana), which was presumed extirpated from its type locality at Sedillo Springs. The report recommends comparing Torreon specimens with museum material to determine if the species survives at this new location.
