# Pronghorn

## Summary
Pronghorn (Antilocapra americana) is an ungulate that historically inhabited grasslands throughout Arizona, though its range has been greatly reduced since the historic period. This source reports one pronghorn atlas (neck vertebra) from La Lomita Pequeña that was described as "very robust," suggesting it came from a large individual, possibly male; the specimen was burned and represented 2% of identified mammalian remains.
