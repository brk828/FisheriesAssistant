# Buffalo-fish

## Summary
Buffalo-fish (genus Ictiobus) are three species of commercially important food fishes in the Catostomidae family found in the Mississippi River region. They spawn between April 15th and May 15th in inundated lowlands during spring floods, producing light amber, highly glutinous eggs that measure 180,000 per fluid quart and hatch in 9-10 days at 62°F.
