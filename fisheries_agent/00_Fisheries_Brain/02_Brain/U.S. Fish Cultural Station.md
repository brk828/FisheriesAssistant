# U.S. Fish Cultural Station

## Summary
The U.S. Fish Cultural Station in Tishomingo, Oklahoma was the research facility where this spawning behavior study was conducted. The station used glass aquaria with flowing water in a holding house to observe pituitary-induced spawning of channel catfish.
