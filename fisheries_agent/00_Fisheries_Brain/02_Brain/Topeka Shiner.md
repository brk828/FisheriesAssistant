# Topeka Shiner

## Summary
The Topeka Shiner (*Notropis topeka*) is a small cyprinid fish historically distributed across clear-water tributary streams in the central Great Plains, particularly in Kansas, where it specialized in cool, clean-water habitats with moderate current. This species is notable for its ecological association with limestone spring-fed creeks and represents a component of Great Plains stream fish communities that indicate high water quality and relatively undisturbed aquatic ecosystems. The Topeka Shiner has become a conservation concern due to habitat degradation and range reduction, making it an important indicator species for assessing the ecological integrity of prairie stream systems.
