# Black Bass

## Summary
Black Bass refers to freshwater fish species in the family Centrarchidae, primarily largemouth bass (Micropterus salmoides) and smallmouth bass (Micropterus dolomieu), that are highly valued for recreational fishing but pose significant ecological threats when introduced to non-native aquatic ecosystems. As aggressive predators and competitors, black bass can devastate native fish populations, particularly in isolated or vulnerable habitats such as desert springs and small ponds, making them a major concern in conservation biology and aquatic invasive species management. Their widespread introduction through intentional stocking and accidental releases has made them one of the most problematic non-native fish species affecting freshwater biodiversity globally.
