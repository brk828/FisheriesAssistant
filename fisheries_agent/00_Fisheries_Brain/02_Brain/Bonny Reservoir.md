# Bonny Reservoir

## Summary
Bonny Reservoir is a warm water fishery in Yuma County, Colorado, that was monitored as one of nine representative waters in the 1955 creel census. The reservoir showed moderate fishing success with a catch-per-man-hour of 0.87, with 2,399 fishermen catching 9,599 fish during the survey period.
