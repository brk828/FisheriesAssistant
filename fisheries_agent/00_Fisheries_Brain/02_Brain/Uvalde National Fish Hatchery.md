# Uvalde National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Uvalde National Fish Hatchery is a federal warmwater fish production facility established in 1935 in Uvalde, Texas, operating under the White Act as a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service installation. The hatchery plays a critical role in fish conservation and aquaculture management by producing warmwater fish species for stocking in public waters and supporting disease certification, fish health diagnostics, and water quality monitoring for multiple state and federal hatcheries across the region. As a key component of the National Fish Hatchery System, it contributes to fisheries restoration, species propagation, and the maintenance of healthy aquatic ecosystems in the southwestern United States.
