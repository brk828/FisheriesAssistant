# Bell Cow Canyon

## Summary
Bell Cow Canyon is a geographic feature on Chevelon Creek in Navajo County, Arizona. The current distribution of Little Colorado Spinedace in Chevelon Creek extends approximately 8 miles from the confluence with the Little Colorado River upstream to Bell Cow Canyon, which also marks the upstream boundary of designated critical habitat in this stream.
