# Pigeon Creek

## Summary
Pigeon Creek is a tributary of the Blue River in Greenlee County, Arizona, proposed as the site for a dam and reservoir project. The feasibility study considered seven alternative dam sites along a 6,000-foot length of the creek, and the area contains riparian vegetation and provides habitat for multiple listed, proposed, and candidate species.
