# Cliff Pond

## Summary
Cliff Pond is a 193-acre, 88-foot-deep two-story pond in Brewster, Massachusetts, that maintains a 29-foot-thick layer of cold, oxygenated water (30-59 feet depth) suitable for trout during summer stratification. This study documented six years (1949-1954) of trout stocking and creel census data, showing that stocking timing dramatically affected harvest rates (0.5-73% first-year recovery) and that the pond supports both warmwater species and carryover trout that grow to trophy size by feeding on abundant smelt.
