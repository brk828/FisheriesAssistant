# Stream Hierarchy Model

## Summary
The Stream Hierarchy Model describes fish populations in dendritic river systems where local populations are only partially isolated with varying degrees of gene flow and connectivity. This source shows it results in hierarchical genetic structure where total diversity is partitioned within colonies, among colonies within rivers, among rivers within drainages, and among major drainages, with higher within-habitat diversity and lower among-habitat differentiation than the Death Valley Model, as exemplified by species like Poeciliopsis occidentalis in the Gila River drainage.
