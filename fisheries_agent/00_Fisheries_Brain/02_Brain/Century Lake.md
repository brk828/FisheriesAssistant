# Century Lake

## Summary
Century Lake is a 555-acre freshwater reservoir in Hopkins County, Texas that serves as the first of three reservoirs supplying water to the city of Sulphur Springs. In 1963, the lake had a fish community dominated by rough fish (72% by number, 87% by weight), primarily Gizzard Shad, and was chemically treated with rotenone to eradicate the existing fish population and allow for restocking with more desirable game fish species.
