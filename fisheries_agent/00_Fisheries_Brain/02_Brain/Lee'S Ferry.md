# Lee'S Ferry

## Summary
Lee's Ferry is a cold-water tailwater reach of the Colorado River located immediately downstream of Glen Canyon Dam in Arizona that serves as a critical study site and management area for aquatic conservation biology and fisheries research. This location is characterized by its clear, cold dam-released waters and has been extensively monitored since at least the 1970s as a focal point for investigating native fish decline, invasive species interactions, and rainbow trout fishery dynamics in the Colorado River ecosystem. Lee's Ferry represents an important reference area for understanding how dam operations affect fish communities, habitat partitioning between native and non-native species, and the ecological sustainability of coldwater fisheries in the region.
