# Schizothorax schumacheri

## Summary
Schizothorax schumacheri is a snow trout species (family Cyprinidae, tribe Schizothoracini) recorded from the Sistan basin of Afghanistan. Like Schizothorax anjac, some authors consider it synonymous with other species, reflecting the need for comprehensive taxonomic revision of the genus.
