# Bigmouth Buffalo

## Summary
The Bigmouth Buffalo (Ictiobus cyprinellus) is a large North American cyprinid fish species that inhabits freshwater systems and is of ecological and fisheries management significance. As a benthic filter-feeder that consumes plankton and detritus, it plays an important role in aquatic food webs and ecosystem dynamics, while also representing a commercial and recreational fishery resource. The species' reproductive behavior and ecological interactions with other fish species, including introduced species, make it relevant to conservation and management considerations in freshwater ecosystems.
