# Incomplete Lineage Sorting

## Summary
Incomplete lineage sorting occurs when ancestral genetic polymorphisms persist through speciation events, preventing reciprocal monophyly. This source identifies incomplete lineage sorting as a challenge for species delimitation in the M. coosae complex, noting that absence of reciprocal monophyly can result from rapid speciation, incomplete lineage sorting, or historical gene flow, yet multiple lines of evidence still supported recognition of distinct evolutionary lineages as separate species.
