# Galaxiiformes

## Summary
Galaxiiformes is a teleost order that shares with Gadidae the placement of olfactory lobes near the nasal capsules rather than at the forebrain. This represents a primitive condition also found in elasmobranchs and some other teleost orders like Cypriniformes and certain Siluriformes.
