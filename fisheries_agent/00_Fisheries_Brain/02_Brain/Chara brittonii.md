# Chara brittonii

## Summary
Chara brittonii is a monoecious, diplostephanous, ecorticate species of stonewort. This study found it has N=14 chromosomes and groups cytologically with other haplostephanous ecorticate species, suggesting that stipulode number is less taxonomically significant than cortication and ploidy-sex correlations.
