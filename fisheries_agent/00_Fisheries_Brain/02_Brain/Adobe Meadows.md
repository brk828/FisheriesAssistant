# Adobe Meadows

## Summary
Adobe Meadows is a location through which Putnam (1949) identified evidence of overflow from Lake Russell during the Tahoe glacial maximum. Water flowed through this area at 7,150 feet elevation, passing through the divide north of Cowtrack Mountain into Adobe Valley.
