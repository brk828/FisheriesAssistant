# Roswell Country Club Spring

## Summary
The single remaining spring in the Roswell area of Chaves County, New Mexico, supporting populations of both Roswell Fontelicella and Roswell Tryonia after other area springs dried from groundwater pumping. This source documents that springhead modification in 1973-74 significantly reduced both populations, and recommends transferring both species to Dexter National Fish Hatchery as a permanent refuge.
