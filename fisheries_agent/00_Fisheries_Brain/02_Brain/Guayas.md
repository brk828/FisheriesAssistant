# Guayas

## Summary
The Guayas is a river system in Ecuador that includes the Chimbo-Chan Chan basin as one of its tributaries. This drainage system occurs at lower elevations than the Esmeraldas system and is home to Rhoadsia altipinna, which contrasts ecologically with the high-elevation R. minor.
