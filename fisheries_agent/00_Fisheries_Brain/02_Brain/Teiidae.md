# Teiidae

## Summary
Teiidae is the family of whiptail lizards documented across the reservoir sites. This source notes that whiptail lizards can penetrate higher elevations than normal on open terraces, and identifies persistent taxonomic problems particularly in the genus Cnemidophorus within this family.
