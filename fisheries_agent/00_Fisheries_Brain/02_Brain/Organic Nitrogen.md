# Organic Nitrogen

## Summary
Organic nitrogen is nitrogen bound in organic compounds, measured as a water chemistry parameter distinct from ammonia. In this study, organic nitrogen concentrations were significantly higher in fertilized pools than unfertilized ones, with nitrate and organic nitrogen fertilizer treatments showing the highest levels.

## Context

> This experimental study by [[George N. Greene]] at [[Auburn University]] (1967-1968) investigated the effects of three nitrogen sources (sodium nitrate, ammonium carbonate, and cottonseed meal) and the presence of [[Cecil Soil]] on fish production and water chemistry in fertilized plastic pools....
