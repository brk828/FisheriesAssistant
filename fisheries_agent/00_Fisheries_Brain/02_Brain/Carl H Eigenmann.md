# Carl H Eigenmann

## Summary
Carl H. Eigenmann was the author of this 1907 taxonomic revision of Poeciliid fishes from Rio Grande do Sul and the La Plata Basin. He described one new species (Ilyodon paraguayense) and proposed three new genera (Ilyodon, Phalloptychus, and Phalloceros) based on detailed examination of teeth morphology and male reproductive organs.
