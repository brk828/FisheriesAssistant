# Acanthocephala

## Summary
Acanthocephala are a phylum of parasitic worms characterized by a distinctive retractable proboscis armed with hooks, which they use to attach to the intestinal walls of fish and other aquatic vertebrates. These endoparasites are significant in fisheries science and aquatic ecology as they can reduce host fitness, affect fish population dynamics, and serve as indicators of ecosystem health and food web structure. Understanding acanthocephalan infections in fish is important for both fish conservation efforts and for assessing the overall condition of aquatic environments.
