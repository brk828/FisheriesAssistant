# Chondrostoma nasus

## Summary
Chondrostoma nasus is a fish species studied in the Rokytná River and affected by the Vir Reservoir. In the Rokytná River, populations showed densities of 4.2-14.2 individuals per 100 m², maturity at 5-7 years, and average fertility of 8,251 eggs; the species decreased markedly in areas affected by the Vir Reservoir.
