# Glyptothorax jalalensis

## Summary
Glyptothorax jalalensis is a sisorid catfish species from Afghanistan. This source notes it is one of only two Siluriformes in the Kabul River basin that are not Oriental elements, indicating distinct zoogeographic affinities from other catfish in that system.
