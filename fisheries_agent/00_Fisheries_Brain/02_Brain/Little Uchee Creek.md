# Little Uchee Creek

## Summary
Little Uchee Creek is a stream in Alabama where ontogenetic habitat shifts in Shoal Bass have been documented. This source reports that larval fish prefer boulder substrates in deep water with low current velocities, then rapidly transition to shallow shoal habitats as juveniles.
