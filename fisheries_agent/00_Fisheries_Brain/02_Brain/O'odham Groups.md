# O'odham Groups

## Summary
O'odham Groups are indigenous peoples of the Sonoran Desert region. This source associates them with Protohistoric Period sites (A.D. 1450-1700) at Organ Pipe and documents historic Hia C'ed O'odham villages, cemeteries, and shrines, including the discovery of previously unknown cremation burial practices.
