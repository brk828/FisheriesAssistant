# Terrapene Coahuila

## Summary
Terrapene coahuila, commonly known as the Coahuila box turtle or "Bisagrita," is a highly endemic and critically endangered aquatic turtle species found exclusively in the Cuatrociénegas Basin of Mexico, representing the only fully aquatic member of the predominantly terrestrial Terrapene genus. This species is of significant conservation concern due to its extremely restricted geographic range (approximately 1,500 km²) and its dependence on isolated spring-fed aquatic ecosystems that are vulnerable to environmental degradation and water resource depletion. Its presence in the Cuatrociénegas Basin, which harbors disproportionately high levels of endemic aquatic fauna, makes it a key indicator species for assessing the ecological health and conservation status of this unique inland aquatic ecosystem.
