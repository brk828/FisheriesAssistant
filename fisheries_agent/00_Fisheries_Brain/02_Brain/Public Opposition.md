# Public Opposition

## Summary
Public opposition influenced management decisions at Lake Davis, with Director Hight assuring the community on October 7, 1999 that no Proposition 65 chemicals (those with carcinogenic or reproductive effects) or persistent harmful chemicals would be used. This assurance effectively removed formulated rotenone as a control option and led to development of a non-chemical management plan emphasizing containment and control measures.
