# Little Colorado River

## Summary
The Little Colorado River is a critical tributary of the Colorado River in the southwestern United States that serves as essential habitat for endemic and endangered fish species, particularly the humpback chub (*Gila cypha*) and other native desert fish adapted to highly variable flow regimes. In the context of conservation biology and aquatic ecology, the Little Colorado River is significant as a refuge and spawning area for native fishes that have been displaced or extirpated from much of the lower Colorado River Basin due to habitat modification, water extraction, and competition from introduced species. Research and management efforts in this system focus on understanding population dynamics, habitat requirements, and the impacts of flow management and exotic species on the persistence of threatened native fish communities.
