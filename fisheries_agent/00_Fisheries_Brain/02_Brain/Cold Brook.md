# Cold Brook

## Summary
Cold Brook is a tributary stream flowing into Keuka Lake in the Finger Lakes Region, with approximately 4.5 miles of trout fishing habitat studied. During the opening week of fishing season (1955-1957), it experienced moderate fishing pressure (seldom exceeding 100 fishermen per mile) and generally yielded 100-300 fish per opening week, with the lowest recorded catch being 140 trout in 1957.
