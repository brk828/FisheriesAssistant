# Boggy Creek

## Summary
Boggy Creek is a stream on the Fort Apache Indian Reservation that was identified in the 1988 survey as a good candidate for barrier installation. This designation suggests it contained a viable Apache Trout population that could benefit from protection against upstream invasion by introduced salmonid species.
