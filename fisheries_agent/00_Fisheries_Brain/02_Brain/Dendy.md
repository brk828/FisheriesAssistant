# Dendy

## Summary
Dendy (1946) studied pikeperch in Norris Reservoir, Tennessee and observed that empty stomachs coincided with fish movement into deeper, cooler water in early summer where forage was less available, resulting in declined growth rates. This work demonstrated that prey availability in preferred thermal strata is a critical factor controlling feeding success in pikeperch.
