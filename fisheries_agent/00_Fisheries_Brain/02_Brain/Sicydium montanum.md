# Sicydium montanum

## Summary
Sicydium montanum is a small freshwater goby species (21-29 mm) described by Carl L. Hubbs in 1920 from mountain brooks near Caracas, Venezuela. It is distinguished from related species by its larger scales (approximately 48 in median series), distinctive coloration pattern featuring six cross bars or blotches on the sides that often fuse to form a longitudinal streak, and deeply sculptured scales with strong hastate spines.
