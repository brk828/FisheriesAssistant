# Stege Bay

## Summary
Stege Bay is a coastal water body in the western Baltic Sea that connects to Stege Lagoon through a narrow outlet approximately 60 meters wide. Pike from Stege Lagoon migrate into Stege Bay and beyond after spawning, with approximately two-thirds of tagged individuals leaving the lagoon for extended periods (57-335 days) before returning.
