# Bonytail Chub Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Bonytail Chub Recovery Plan is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service initiative designed to restore populations of the bonytail chub (Gila elegans), an endangered native cyprinid fish of the Colorado River Basin, through coordinated habitat management, captive breeding, and reintroduction strategies. The plan addresses the species' severe population decline caused by habitat alteration from dam operations, water diversions, and competition/predation from invasive non-native fish species introduced through historical stocking programs. Implementation involves evaluating suitable reintroduction sites, managing backwater and run habitats, and assessing recruitment dynamics to distinguish between environmental and biological factors limiting natural reproduction in the wild.
