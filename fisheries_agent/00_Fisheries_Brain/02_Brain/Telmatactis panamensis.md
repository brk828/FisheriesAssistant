# Telmatactis panamensis

## Summary
Telmatactis panamensis is an orange sea anemone with a leathery column and short club-shaped tentacles that become knobbed in older specimens. The oral disc and tentacles are mottled orange, red, and brown, and the species possesses acontia (defensive thread-like structures).
