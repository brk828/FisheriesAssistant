# Livingston Manor

## Summary
Livingston Manor is a location in New York where the New York State Conservation Department operated a Fish Laboratory. This laboratory housed twelve temperature-controlled aquarium tanks (350-550 gallons each) designed for conducting controlled experiments on the effects of chemicals on fish species.
