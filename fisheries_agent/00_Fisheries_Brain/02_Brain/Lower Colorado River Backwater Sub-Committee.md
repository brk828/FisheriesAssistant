# Lower Colorado River Backwater Sub-Committee

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Backwater Sub-Committee is a specialized working group formed in 1987 under the Lower Colorado River Management Work Group. This source indicates it was established to prioritize restoration needs for the approximately 153 backwater lakes in the Imperial Division, following inventory efforts that began in 1986.
