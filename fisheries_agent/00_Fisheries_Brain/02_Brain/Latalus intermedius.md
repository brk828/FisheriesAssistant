# Latalus intermedius

## Summary
Latalus intermedius is a new species of deltocephaline leafhopper measuring 2.6-2.8 mm (male) to 2.8-3.0 mm (female), with stramineous coloring mottled with brown. It shows intermediate genitalic characters between L. personatus and L. histrionicus, and has a northern range sympatric with these species but extends further south into Colorado.
