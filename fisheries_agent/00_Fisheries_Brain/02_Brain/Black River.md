# Black River

## Summary
The Black River refers to a cold-water montane stream drainage system in Arizona that serves as an important trout fishery managed through creel census monitoring and fish stocking programs. The drainage is divided into distinct managed reaches, particularly the East Fork and West Fork Black River, which function as put-and-take fisheries supporting recreational angling through periodic restocking with hatchery-reared trout. These stream sections represent key study sites for fisheries management research, providing data on catch rates, population dynamics, and the effectiveness of harvest monitoring and stocking protocols in maintaining productive cold-water fisheries.
