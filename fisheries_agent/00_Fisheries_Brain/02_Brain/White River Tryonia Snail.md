# White River Tryonia Snail

## Summary
The White River Tryonia Snail (Tryonia clathrata) is an aquatic snail species in Pahranagat Valley that was formerly proposed as threatened. This source notes that the recovery plan takes an ecosystem management approach, and successful restoration of the Pahranagat Valley aquatic ecosystem may secure this snail species without requiring it to be listed under the Endangered Species Act.
