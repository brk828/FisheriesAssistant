# Electrofishing

## Summary
Electrofishing is a sampling technique used in ichthyology and fisheries research that applies electrical current to water to temporarily stun or immobilize fish, facilitating their capture for population surveys, species inventories, and ecological assessments. The method exploits the principle that larger-bodied fish experience greater voltage-induced effects than smaller individuals, making it effective for capturing fish across multiple size classes. Modern electrofishing equipment ranges from portable, lightweight designs suitable for small-scale two-person surveys to larger rigs for extensive sampling, and remains a standard non-lethal tool in fish population monitoring and aquatic ecology studies.
