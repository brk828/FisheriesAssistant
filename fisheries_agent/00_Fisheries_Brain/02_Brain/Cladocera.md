# Cladocera

## Summary
Cladocera are small crustacean zooplankton organisms that inhabit freshwater aquatic ecosystems and serve as a crucial food source for many fish species. These microscopic animals are important indicators of water quality and ecosystem health, making them valuable subjects for limnological studies examining seasonal distribution patterns and community composition in lakes. In fisheries science, understanding cladoceran populations is essential for assessing fish food availability and nutritional dynamics in aquatic food webs.
