# Size-Structured Populations

## Summary
Size-structured populations are those where individuals of different sizes have different ecological roles, vulnerabilities, or behaviors. This study shows that June sucker vulnerability to white bass predation depends critically on the size distributions of both species, with young June suckers (mean 13.3 mm at swim-up) being most vulnerable immediately after hatching when size differences with juvenile white bass predators are greatest, and vulnerability declining as suckers grow through their first summer.
