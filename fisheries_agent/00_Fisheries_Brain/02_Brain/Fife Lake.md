# Fife Lake

## Summary
Fife Lake is a 575-acre lake in Grand Traverse and Kalkaska counties, Michigan, connected to the Manistee River drainage, and was the only northern Michigan lake included in this study. It was opened to year-round fishing for bluegills and sunfish, and exhibited a mixed warm/cool water fishery with catch composition of 49.6% bluegill, 12.0% walleye, and 11.1% yellow perch over the five-year study period.
