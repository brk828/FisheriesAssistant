# Daruntah

## Summary
Daruntah is a location in Afghanistan where a fish research and breeding center was established in 1967 with Chinese assistance. This source reports that several introduced species including grass carp, silver carp, Rhodeus sinensis, and four varieties of carp were kept in breeding pools there, and mentions a gorge at Daruntah that was dammed.
