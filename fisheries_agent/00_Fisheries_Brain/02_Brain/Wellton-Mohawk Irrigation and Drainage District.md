# Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation and Drainage District

## Summary
The Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation and Drainage District produces highly saline drainage water that is transported via bypass drain to create and maintain the Cienega de Santa Clara wetland in Mexico. The district's drainage water discharge has expanded the wetland from approximately 500 acres of vegetation prior to discharge initiation to over 6,100 acres.
