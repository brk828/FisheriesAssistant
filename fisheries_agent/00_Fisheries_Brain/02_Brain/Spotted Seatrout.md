# Spotted Seatrout

## Summary
Spotted Seatrout (*Cynoscion nebulosus*) is a euryhaline estuarine fish species that spawns in offshore waters and barrier island zones, with juveniles subsequently recruiting to estuarine nursery habitats during summer months. As a seasonal immigrant with high salinity tolerance, the species was experimentally introduced into inland Texas reservoirs and saline impoundments during mid-20th century fisheries management programs to utilize otherwise unproductive aquatic habitats. The species' life history strategy, combining marine spawning grounds with estuarine rearing areas, makes it a valuable indicator of estuary-offshore connectivity in coastal ecological systems.
