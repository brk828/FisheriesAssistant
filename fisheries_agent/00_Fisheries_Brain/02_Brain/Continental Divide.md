# Continental Divide

## Summary
The Continental Divide is a major geographic boundary in North America that separates watersheds draining into different ocean basins. According to this source, the cyprinid genus Gila is distributed across western North America, with most species occurring west of the Continental Divide, from southern Canada to deep into Mexico.
