# Scirpus

## Summary
Scirpus (bulrushes) are obligate wetland species found in wet fluvial marshes of the Colorado River corridor. By 1991, Scirpus species were among the dominant vegetation in wet marshes, which comprised approximately 30% of the total marsh vegetation and occurred primarily between 10,000 and 20,000 cfs stage elevations.
