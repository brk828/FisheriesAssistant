# Phalloceros caudomaculatus

## Summary
Phalloceros caudomaculatus is the type species of the new genus Phalloceros, previously classified as Girardinus caudomaculatus Hensel. Its geographic distribution extends from Rio Grande do Sul to Paraguay, and geographic variation was noted between Paraguay and Rio Grande do Sul specimens in tooth arrangement.
