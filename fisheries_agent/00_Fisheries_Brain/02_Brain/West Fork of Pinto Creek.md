# West Fork of Pinto Creek

## Summary
West Fork of Pinto Creek is a stream in the Brushiest Allotment and Superstition Wilderness that was previously inspected and deemed suitable for Gila Topminnow reintroduction, with formal Section 7 consultation completed in November 1990. This source indicates new consultation was needed for a riparian pasture fence to improve livestock control and enhance riparian and wilderness values in the key introduction area.
