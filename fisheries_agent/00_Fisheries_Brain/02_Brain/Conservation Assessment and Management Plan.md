# Conservation Assessment and Management Plan

## Summary
Conservation Assessment and Management Plan (CAMP) Workshops are one type of meeting and workshop activity conducted by SSC Specialist Groups. CAMP Workshops represent a specific methodology used by the Commission for assessing species conservation status and developing management strategies. These workshops are part of the diverse range of activities that Specialist Groups engage in alongside Action Planning, newsletter production, and formal interventions.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium. The SSC is described as the largest global network of volunteer experts working for species conservation, comprising approximately 5,000 members in 170 countries....
