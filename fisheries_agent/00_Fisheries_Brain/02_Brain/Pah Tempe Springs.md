# Pah Tempe Springs

## Summary
Pah Tempe Springs is a significant spring system along the Virgin River in southwestern Utah that serves as an important hydrological and ecological landmark in the management and monitoring of endangered fish species, particularly the Virgin Spinedace and other endemic fishes of the Virgin River basin. The springs represent a key sampling and reference location for assessing water quality conditions (especially total dissolved solids and clarity) and fish habitat conditions along a longitudinal gradient of the river system. As a major groundwater input to the Virgin River, Pah Tempe Springs is relevant to understanding the geochemical and flow dynamics that influence the distribution and survival of native fish species adapted to specific water chemistry and habitat conditions.
