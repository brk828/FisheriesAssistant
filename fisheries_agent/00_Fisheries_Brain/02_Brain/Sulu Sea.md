# Sulu Sea

## Summary
The Sulu Sea is a deep marine basin in the Western Pacific bounded by Luzon, Samar, Mindanao, the Sulu Archipelago, Borneo, and Palawan, connected to surrounding waters by straits 350-550 meters deep. This source reports that it maintains unusually warm bottom temperatures of approximately 10°C at depths greater than 550 meters (compared to <7°C in surrounding open waters at similar depths), creating hydrographic isolation that has produced a rich, distinctive deep-sea fish fauna with numerous endemic macrourid species.
