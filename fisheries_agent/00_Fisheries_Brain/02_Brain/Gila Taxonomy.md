# Gila Taxonomy

## Summary
Gila Taxonomy refers to the systematic classification and genetic characterization of Gila species (including Gila trout and other Gila-genus fishes) native to southwestern U.S. river systems, with particular relevance to understanding population structure, evolutionary relationships, and management units for conservation purposes. In the context of Glen Canyon and Colorado River studies, Gila taxonomy work involved genetic and morphological analysis to distinguish native Gila populations and inform recovery efforts for endangered southwestern fishes, often conducted in parallel with similar studies on Colorado squawfish and other native species affected by dam operations and habitat alteration. This taxonomic work was essential for establishing baseline genetic data and population identity necessary for effective fish conservation and hatchery management decisions in the region.
