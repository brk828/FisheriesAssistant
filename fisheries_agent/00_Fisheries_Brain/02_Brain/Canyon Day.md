# Canyon Day

## Summary
Canyon Day is a location on the mainstem White River that marks the downstream extent of the currently documented Loach Minnow distribution. The species occupies approximately 14 miles from below Geronimo Cave on the East Fork downstream to below Canyon Day, though the population likely extends further downstream where access limitations prevent adequate sampling.
