# Bass Canyon Creek

## Summary
Bass Canyon Creek is a small first or second order stream in southeastern Arizona that is a tributary of the San Pedro River system. This source found that it contained numerous Desert Suckers and considerable suitable habitat representing a viable population, with suckers observed feeding primarily on larger rocks and boulders, though the stream lacks direct or continuous flow to downstream rivers which may isolate breeding groups.
