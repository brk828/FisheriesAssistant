# Yaqui River Basin

## Summary
The Yaqui River Basin is a transboundary aquatic ecosystem spanning the United States-Mexico border that represents a distinct biogeographic subdivision for desert fish conservation, hosting multiple imperiled endemic and native fish species requiring coordinated recovery efforts. This basin has been a focal point for the Desert Fishes Recovery Team and federal agencies managing endangered species populations, reflecting the complex conservation challenges posed by international water systems in arid regions. The Yaqui River Basin's fish assemblages are characterized by high endemism and vulnerability to habitat degradation, making it a priority area for recovery planning alongside other southwestern river systems such as the Gila and upper Colorado river basins.
