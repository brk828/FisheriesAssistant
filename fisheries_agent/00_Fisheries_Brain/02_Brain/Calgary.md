# Calgary

## Summary
Calgary is a Canadian city where a survey found annual Goldeye sales of only 4,000-4,500 pounds despite favorable consumer attitudes. Sales were limited by high wholesale prices (80¢/pound), "woody" flesh quality, and unreliable supply from Montana sources.
