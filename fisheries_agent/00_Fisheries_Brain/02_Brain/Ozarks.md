# Ozarks

## Summary
The Ozarks is a geographic region that contains significant cave systems with numerous candidate species for endangered species listing. The 1991 review identified this area as one of the regions with concentrations of troglobitic invertebrates being evaluated for potential protection under the Endangered Species Act.
