# Zuni Mountain Sucker

## Summary
The Zuni Mountain Sucker is a freshwater sucker species endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly associated with drainage systems in the Zuni Mountain region of New Mexico and Arizona. As a small native cyprinid dependent on specific stream habitats, this species faces conservation challenges typical of desert aquatic endemics, including habitat degradation from water extraction, impoundment, and competition from non-native fish species. Its listing status as a Federal Candidate Species reflects concerns about population viability and the need for monitoring and potential recovery actions to prevent further decline.
