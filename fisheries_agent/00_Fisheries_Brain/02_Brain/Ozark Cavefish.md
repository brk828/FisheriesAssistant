# Ozark Cavefish

## Summary
The Ozark Cavefish is a rare, endemic freshwater species inhabiting isolated cave systems in the Ozark region, making it a significant subject for conservation biology and recovery efforts under the Endangered Species Act. As a specialized troglobiotic organism adapted to subterranean aquatic environments, it represents a unique ecological niche and serves as a focal species for understanding cave fish ecology and the challenges of preserving species with extremely limited geographic ranges. Conservation programs, including those coordinated through facilities like the Neosho National Fish Hatchery, have employed captive breeding and reintroduction strategies to maintain viable populations and support recovery plans for this highly vulnerable species.
