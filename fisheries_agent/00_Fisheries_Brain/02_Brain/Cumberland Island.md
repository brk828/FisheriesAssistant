# Cumberland Island

## Summary
Cumberland Island is located off southern Georgia where the Dysommina rugosa specimens were collected nearby. The collection site was at coordinates 31°09'00" N, 79°33'30" W, in the Gulf Stream off this island at 352 fathoms depth over gray sand and dead coral bottom.

## Context

> This 1951 publication by Béhlke and Hubbs describes [[Dysommina rugosa]], a deep-sea eel from the [[North Atlantic]] that represents a new family, [[Dysomminidae]].
