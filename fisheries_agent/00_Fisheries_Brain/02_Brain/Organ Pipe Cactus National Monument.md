# Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument

## Summary
Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument is a protected desert landscape in southwestern Arizona that serves as part of the broader Sonoran Desert and Colorado River Delta conservation complex relevant to aquatic ecosystem management in the American Southwest. While primarily known as a terrestrial preserve, the Monument's location within the Colorado River watershed system makes it ecologically connected to regional freshwater and delta aquatic habitats that support native fish populations and require coordinated conservation efforts across the desert ecosystem. Its designation within multi-jurisdictional conservation initiatives highlights the importance of landscape-level planning that integrates terrestrial and aquatic resource protection across the U.S.-Mexico border region.
