# Larval Drift Sampling

## Summary
Larval drift sampling was used to assess Channel Catfish spawning productivity in different river reaches. Data showed larval Channel Catfish densities were 10 times higher at Mexican Hat (near the upper limit of subreach G) compared to Four Corners downstream, indicating middle reaches are the most productive spawning habitats.
