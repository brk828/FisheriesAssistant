# Salix exigua

## Summary
Salix exigua (coyote willow) is a riparian shrub in the Colorado River corridor that showed water stress inversely related to discharge volumes during Interim Flows monitoring. It is identified as one of the important riparian species (along with Tamarix, Populus, and Baccharis) for which foliar area changes were to be analyzed using paired t-tests comparing 1995 and 1996 data.
