# Cyprinodon Nevadensis

## Summary
Cyprinodon nevadensis, commonly known as the Nevada pupfish, is a small, endemic desert fish species native to isolated spring and aquifer systems in the Great Basin region of Nevada and California. This species is significant in conservation biology as an indicator of unique desert aquatic ecosystems and has been extensively studied for its genetic variation, reproductive isolation, and adaptability to extreme environmental conditions, making it valuable for understanding speciation processes and the impacts of habitat fragmentation on small populations. The Nevada pupfish and its subspecies represent important subjects for aquatic ecology research regarding survival in isolated thermal springs and the effects of groundwater management on endemic fish populations.
