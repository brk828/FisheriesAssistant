# Cucapa

## Summary
The Cucapa are an indigenous people of the Colorado River Delta whose population declined from approximately 5,000 in the mid-1800s to fewer than 200 currently living in the delta. According to workshop presentations, the loss of water and associated habitat that these communities depended upon for food is identified as the most significant cause of their decline, and enhancing opportunities for Cucapa fishing was identified as a long-term restoration goal.
