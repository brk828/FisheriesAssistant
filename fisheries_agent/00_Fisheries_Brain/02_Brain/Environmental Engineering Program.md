# Environmental Engineering Program

## Summary
Environmental Engineering Program is an academic program at the University of Arizona focused on environmental engineering education and research. The program was listed in the 1987 directory as one of the educational and research institutions providing critical capacity for environmental and water resource management in Arizona.
