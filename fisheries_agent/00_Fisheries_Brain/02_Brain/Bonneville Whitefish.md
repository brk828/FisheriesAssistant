# Bonneville Whitefish

## Summary
Bonneville Whitefish (Prosopium spilonotus) is a whitefish species endemic to Utah's Bonneville Basin. This source lists it as a native species that evolved in ancient Lake Bonneville during the Pleistocene, representing a relict population from that prehistoric lake system.
