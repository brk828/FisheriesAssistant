# Zebra Fish

## Summary
Zebra Fish (Brachydanio rerio) is a small freshwater cyprinid species used in this study as a bioassay organism for testing pituitary activity. This source reports that 24 females injected with 0.6 mg carp pituitary spawned an average of 57 eggs per female (range 17-148) with 51-96% fertility, though the species' natural readiness to spawn in captivity complicated assessment of the pituitary injection effect, and high mortality in cull fish (11 of 33) suggested the dosage may have been excessive for poorly-developed individuals.
