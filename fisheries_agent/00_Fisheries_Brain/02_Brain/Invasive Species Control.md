# Invasive Species Control

## Summary
Invasive species control at Lake Davis involved multiple strategies to manage non-native northern pike, including chemical treatment with rotenone in 1997 that initially appeared successful but ultimately failed. A 2000 management plan outlined 12 non-chemical containment and control actions including net barriers, electrofishing, spawning barriers, and public education after chemical options were removed due to concerns about Proposition 65 chemicals.
