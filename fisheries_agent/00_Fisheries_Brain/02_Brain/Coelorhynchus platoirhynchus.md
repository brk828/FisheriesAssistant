# Coelorhynchus platoirhynchus

## Summary
Coelorhynchus platoirhynchus is a deep-sea macrourid fish species known from the East Indies. This source lists it among the East Indian species that were not obtained in the Philippine Subregion during the Albatross expedition, despite intensive dredging efforts.
