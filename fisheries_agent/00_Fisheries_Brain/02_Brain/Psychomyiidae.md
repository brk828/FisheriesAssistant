# Psychomyiidae

## Summary
Psychomyiidae is a family of caddisflies (Trichoptera) recorded in the Red Cedar River. This family was present at Stations IV and VI but found at low densities (<1/ft²) at Station IV, with all four caddisfly families showing sensitivity to pollution near sewage outfalls.
