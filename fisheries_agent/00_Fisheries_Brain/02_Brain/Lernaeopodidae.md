# Lernaeopodidae

## Summary
Lernaeopodidae is a family of parasitic copepods of marine ancestry that became established in freshwater during post-glacial times. This source documents four species from this family in the Yorkshire Ouse system: Achtheres percarum, Salmincola gordoni, Salmincola thymalli, and Tracheliastes polycolpus.
