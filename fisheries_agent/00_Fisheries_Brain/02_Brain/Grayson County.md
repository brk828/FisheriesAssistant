# Grayson County

## Summary
Grayson County is a county in Texas that contains Loy Lake near the city of Denison. The watershed feeding Loy Lake includes approximately 20 ponds and a small stream on private lands that could not be treated during the 1963 fish eradication operation, creating potential for reinfestation of the lake.
