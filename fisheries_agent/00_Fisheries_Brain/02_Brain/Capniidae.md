# Capniidae

## Summary
Capniidae is a family of small stoneflies (order Plecoptera) that are commonly found in cold, fast-flowing streams and rivers across temperate regions. These aquatic insects are important components of freshwater ecosystems, serving as indicators of water quality and as food sources for fish species, making them ecologically significant for both conservation assessments and fisheries management. Their presence and abundance in streams are often used as bioindicators of environmental health and stable, oxygen-rich aquatic habitats.
