# Saddle Lake

## Summary
Saddle Lake is a 271-acre warm-water lake in Van Buren County, Michigan, connected to the Kalamazoo River drainage. It was one of two lakes tested with no closed season on pan fish AND no creel limit on pan fish under six inches to test removal of stunted populations, with a catch composition of 74.0% bluegill (legal size) and 12.6% yellow perch, and seining showed increased young-of-year pan fish from 21.6 to 29.9 per 100 feet between 1946 and 1950.
