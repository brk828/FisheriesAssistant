# Arizona Bay

## Summary
Arizona Bay is a shallow nearshore habitat within Lake Mohave (on the Arizona-Nevada border of the lower Colorado River) characterized by cobble substrate in 1-5 meter depths, particularly around areas like Cottonwood Cove. This microhabitat is significant for conservation efforts as it serves as important refuge and feeding grounds for imperiled native fish species, especially adult Bonytail Chub and Razorback Sucker during nocturnal periods, though it faces ecological pressure from invasive carp populations. Arizona Bay and similar shallow cove habitats have been the focus of intensive ichthyological surveys to monitor and recover the last remaining wild populations of these endemic Colorado River Basin fishes.
