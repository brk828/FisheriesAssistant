# Bodega Marine Laboratory

## Summary
Bodega Marine Laboratory (BML) is a facility that maintains captive breeding programs for endangered Sacramento Winter-Run Chinook Salmon and where disease challenge protocols have been developed. This source reports that winter-run salmon have been successfully maintained there as captive broodstock since 1991, that Rosette Agent parasite was first detected in this captive broodstock in 1994, and that the 1996 broodstock was large enough to conduct full-sibling matings and outcrosses for research on parasite resistance and inbreeding effects.
