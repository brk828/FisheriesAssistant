# Orchelimum fidicinum

## Summary
Orchelimum fidicinum is a grasshopper species representing terrestrial macroinvertebrates. This source includes O. fidicinum in its compilation of terrestrial consumer calorific values, with grasshoppers showing values ranging from 5035.3 to 6151.7 cal/g ash-free dry weight.
