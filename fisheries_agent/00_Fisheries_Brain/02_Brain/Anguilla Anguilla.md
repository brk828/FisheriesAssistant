# Anguilla Anguilla

## Summary
Anguilla anguilla, commonly known as the European eel, is a catadromous fish species that migrates between freshwater and marine environments, making it ecologically and economically significant across European aquatic systems. As a bottom-dwelling species with specific habitat requirements and behavioral adaptations, it exhibits variable catchability in sampling programs depending on its life stage and habitat utilization patterns. The species faces significant conservation challenges due to barriers to migration, habitat degradation, and complex life history requirements, making it an important subject in fisheries management and aquatic ecology research.
