# Bluestriped Snapper

## Summary
Bluestriped Snapper (Lutjanus kasmira) is a marine fish deliberately introduced to Hawaii. Of the 13 marine fish species that became established in Hawaii (7 deliberate, 6 unplanned), with 4 rated as mistakes, the Bluestriped Snapper is identified as the only one that may have actual value.
