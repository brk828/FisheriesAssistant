# Cynoscion nebulosus

## Summary
Cynoscion nebulosus is the Spotted Seatrout, an important native food fish in the Gulf of Mexico. Texas managers hybridized this species with the introduced Orangemouth Corvina and released both hybrids and parent stocks into reservoirs, raising serious concerns about genetic dilution of this native species throughout the Gulf of Mexico.
