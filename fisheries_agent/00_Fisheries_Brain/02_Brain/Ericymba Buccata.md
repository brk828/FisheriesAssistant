# Ericymba Buccata

## Summary
Ericymba buccata, commonly known as the silverjaw minnow, is a small cyprinid fish species native to Kentucky and Indiana with a historically restricted distribution that has been subject to reassessment and range expansion studies. This species exhibits specific habitat preferences for small to medium-sized streams with sandy or gravelly substrates and minimal flow, and its age and growth patterns have been documented through scale analysis to understand population dynamics in tributary systems. The silverjaw minnow serves as an important indicator species for evaluating changes in stream distribution and habitat quality, particularly in the Green, Tradewater, and Otter Creek drainage systems.
