# Lerma

## Summary
The Rio Lerma is part of the Lerma-Santiago river system in central Mexico. This source identifies it as a center of Goodeidae (splitfin) diversity with numerous endemic species, and notes that Ameiurus melas has been introduced into this basin beyond its native range.
