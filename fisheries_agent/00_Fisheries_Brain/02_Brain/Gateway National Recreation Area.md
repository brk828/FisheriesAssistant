# Gateway National Recreation Area

## Summary
Gateway National Recreation Area is represented on the NPS coordination group through Robert W. McIntosh, Jr., who serves as the expert for Recreation/Leisure. This representation provides social science perspectives on visitor use and recreational aspects of resource management policy.
