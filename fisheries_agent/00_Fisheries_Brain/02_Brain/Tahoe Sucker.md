# Tahoe Sucker

## Summary
The Tahoe Sucker (*Catostomus tahoe*) is a native cyprinid fish species endemic to the Lake Tahoe basin that serves as an important ecological indicator and forage species in Sierra Nevada aquatic ecosystems. In the context of endangered species management and fish facility operations, the Tahoe Sucker is relevant to recovery efforts for co-occurring threatened species like the cui-ui, as native sucker populations reflect broader watershed health and may be affected by fish passage operations and facility management at facilities like the Marble Bluff Fish Station. Understanding Tahoe Sucker population dynamics and habitat requirements is essential for developing comprehensive, multi-year management plans that protect both the sucker and dependent species within integrated recovery programs.
