# Project Vela Uniform

## Summary
Project Vela Uniform was a series of underwater explosion tests conducted in Lake Mead during 1961-1962. The June 1962 tests involved nine explosions using ammonium nitrate and U.S. Navy Composition B explosives in 200 feet of water, resulting in significant mortality of young-of-year fish, particularly crappie, largemouth bass, and threadfin shad.
