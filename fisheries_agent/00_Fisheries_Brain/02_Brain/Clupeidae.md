# Clupeidae

## Summary
Clupeidae is a large family of ray-finned fishes that includes herrings, sardines, anchovies, and shad, characterized by their streamlined bodies, silvery coloration, and schooling behavior. Members of this family are ecologically and economically significant as they inhabit both freshwater and marine environments across the world, serving as important forage fish in aquatic food webs and supporting major commercial and subsistence fisheries. Their abundance, reproductive capacity, and role as prey for larger predatory fish make Clupeidae a key focus in conservation biology and fisheries management, particularly given their susceptibility to overharvesting and environmental changes.
