# Tryonia

## Summary
Tryonia is a related genus of hydrobiid snails that has been the subject of previous systematic work by Hershler. Studies of Tryonia in the Amargosa River basin helped identify that region as a biotic and geologic composite, providing context for understanding western aquatic endemism patterns.
