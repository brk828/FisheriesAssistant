# Paddlefish

## Summary
Paddlefish (*Polyodon spathula*) is a primitive chondrostean fish species belonging to the family Polyodontidae, characterized by a cartilaginous skeleton and an elongated, flattened rostrum (paddle-like snout) used for sensory detection and feeding. In conservation and fisheries management contexts, paddlefish are significant for restoration efforts and mitigation programs, particularly in river systems managed by agencies such as the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA), as they are indicator species of riverine ecosystem health and have been subject to population declines due to habitat modification and water management practices. These large, migratory fish represent an ancient fish lineage and are culturally and ecologically important in North American freshwater systems, making them priority species for hatchery propagation and restoration initiatives.
