# Oncorhynchus Kisutch

## Summary
Oncorhynchus kisutch, commonly known as coho or silver salmon, is a Pacific anadromous salmonid species of significant importance in fisheries science and conservation biology. This species is widely studied regarding early life history development, egg incubation requirements, and environmental factors affecting survival, including oxygen concentration in spawning habitats. As a keystone species in both marine and freshwater ecosystems, O. kisutch serves as an important indicator for assessing coldwater habitat quality and the ecological integrity of Pacific coastal watersheds.
