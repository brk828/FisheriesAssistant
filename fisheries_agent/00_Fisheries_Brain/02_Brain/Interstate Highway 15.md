# Interstate Highway 15

## Summary
Interstate Highway 15 is a major transportation corridor that passes through the Virgin River gorge in Arizona. The source cites this highway as an example of construction occurring directly above rivers, which represents both short-term high-magnitude impacts during construction (including modified drainage patterns, soil disruption, and altered channel geometry) and chronic long-term effects on stream ecosystems. Transportation corridors like I-15 affect streams through modified drainage, increased erosion, and barriers to organism movement.
