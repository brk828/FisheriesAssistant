# Tilapia Zillii

## Summary
Tilapia zillii, commonly known as the redbelly tilapia, is a cichlid species native to Africa that has been introduced into North American aquatic systems, including the lower Colorado River and artificial pond complexes in the southwestern United States. This omnivorous cichlid is of conservation concern as an invasive species that can compete with native fishes, alter aquatic vegetation through herbivory, and potentially hybridize with other introduced tilapiine fishes in colonized ecosystems. Like other tilapia species, T. zillii demonstrates significant ecological plasticity, allowing it to establish populations in diverse aquatic habitats from natural river systems to artificial impoundments and canal networks.
