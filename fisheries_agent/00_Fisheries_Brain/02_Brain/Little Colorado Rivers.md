# Little Colorado Rivers

## Summary
The Little Colorado Rivers is mentioned as a location in the Lower Basin where the Humpback Chub is most numerous, particularly at its confluence with the Colorado River in Grand Canyon. This source identifies this confluence area as an important habitat for the species in the Lower Basin.
