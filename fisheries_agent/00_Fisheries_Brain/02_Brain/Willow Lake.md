# Willow Lake

## Summary
Willow Lake is a location within Topock Marsh, Arizona, where fish stocking occurred in 1977. Specifically, the Willow Lake dredge channel was one of three areas where approximately 41,000 hybrid fingerling sunfish were planted by dipnet on October 19, 1977, with underwater vegetation sought at the site to provide cover for released fish.
