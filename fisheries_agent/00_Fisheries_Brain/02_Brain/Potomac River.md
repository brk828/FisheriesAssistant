# Potomac River

## Summary
The Potomac River is a major Atlantic Slope drainage system of the eastern United States that serves as an important biogeographic region for ichthyological surveys and fish biodiversity studies. As documented in systematic fish checklists and taxonomic investigations, the Potomac River represents a distinct ichthyofaunal province supporting diverse freshwater fish communities and serves as a key reference drainage for comparative studies of fish species distribution and systematics across Appalachian watersheds. Its designation as a separate major drainage basin reflects its ecological and evolutionary significance as a discrete fish fauna unit distinct from other major systems such as the Ohio River drainage.
