# Reptilia

## Summary
Reptilia (reptiles) are represented by 12 specimens of small reptiles and amphibians at La Lomita Pequeña, all recovered from flotation samples. These remains were found in middens and trash pits and were not burned, demonstrating that flotation recovery is essential for capturing small vertebrate material missed by 1/4-inch mesh screens.
