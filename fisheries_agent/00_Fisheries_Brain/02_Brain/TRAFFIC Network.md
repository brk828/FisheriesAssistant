# TRAFFIC Network

## Summary
The TRAFFIC Network is identified as a key partner organization that SSC collaborates closely with, specifically focused on monitoring wildlife trade. This partnership extends SSC's capacity to address conservation challenges related to the commercial trade in wild species. TRAFFIC works alongside other partner organizations like the World Conservation Monitoring Centre and BirdLife International to support SSC's broader conservation mission.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium. The SSC is described as the largest global network of volunteer experts working for species conservation, comprising approximately 5,000 members in 170 countries....
