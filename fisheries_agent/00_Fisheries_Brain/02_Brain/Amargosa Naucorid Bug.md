# Amargosa Naucorid Bug

## Summary
The Amargosa Naucorid Bug (Pelocoris shoshone) is an aquatic invertebrate in the family Naucoridae that is restricted to a single spring within Ash Meadows National Wildlife Refuge. Like other highly endemic species in the petition, its single-spring distribution makes it especially vulnerable to habitat loss.
