# Anderson and Hendrickson

## Summary
Anderson and Hendrickson (1994) conducted taxonomic and morphological research on Spikedace populations. Their work found that Spikedace from Aravaipa Creek were morphologically distinguishable from Verde River populations, with upper Gila River and Eagle Creek populations showing intermediate characteristics.
