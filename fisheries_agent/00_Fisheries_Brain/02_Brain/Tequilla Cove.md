# Tequilla Cove

## Summary
Tequilla Cove is one of the known spawning aggregation locations for razorback suckers in Lake Mohave. This site was among several spawning areas (including Horseshoe Rapids, Owl Cove, Yuma Cove, and Six and Nine Mile areas) where telemetered razorback suckers demonstrated spawning-site fidelity, visiting the same aggregations during both years of the study.
