# Redrock Ranch

## Summary
Redrock Ranch is a private property in Redrock Canyon where livestock grazing and a house are present, with owners visiting daily. The Falls area, located below Redrock Ranch, supports one of the three perennial reaches where Gila topminnow populations exist, though this population is the least stable and fluctuates from very abundant to undetectable levels.
