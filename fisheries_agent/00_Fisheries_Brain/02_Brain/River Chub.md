# River Chub

## Summary
The River Chub (*Nocomis micropogon*) is a cyprinid fish species inhabiting lotic freshwater systems, particularly tributary creeks and streams in eastern North America. As a benthic-foraging cyprinid closely related to the Hornyhead Chub, the River Chub is an important component of stream fish assemblages and has been studied for its feeding ecology and trophic interactions within aquatic communities. Its presence in regional faunal surveys and historical museum collections makes it a useful indicator species for documenting fish diversity and distributional patterns in river and creek ecosystems.
