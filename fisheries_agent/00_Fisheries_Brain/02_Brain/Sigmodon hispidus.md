# Sigmodon hispidus

## Summary
Sigmodon hispidus (hispid cotton rat) is represented by one burned immature mandible recovered from a hearth flotation sample at La Lomita Pequeña. This species inhabits riparian and grassland areas between 37 and 1524 m elevation, with breeding potentially occurring June through November or year-round.
