# Conservation Plan

## Summary
The Conservation Plan refers to the Region 6 Colorado River Endangered Fishes Conservation Plan, which establishes strategies for recovering endangered fish species including providing hatchery fish for research from 1983-1996, stocking to reestablish populations after 1987, and developing management approaches. The Plan specifically calls for increasing Colorado Squawfish stocks by 500% (young-of-year) and 200% (juveniles and adults) through habitat management and stocking, while emphasizing that stocking should be limited to areas where species have been extirpated and causes of decline have been corrected.
