# Agua Prieta

## Summary
Agua Prieta is a municipality in Sonora, Mexico, that contains part of the proposed Sierra San Luis protection area. The reserve encompasses portions of both Agua Prieta and the adjacent Janos municipality in Chihuahua, with all land privately owned and divided among 22 properties.
