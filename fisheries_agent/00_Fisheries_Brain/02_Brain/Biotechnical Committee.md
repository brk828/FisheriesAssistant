# Biotechnical Committee

## Summary
The Biotechnical Committee was a component of the organizational structure for the Upper Colorado River endangered fish recovery program. This 1989 meeting agenda indicates it was one of the distinct committees, along with the Recovery Implementation Committee, that coordinated the multi-agency recovery efforts for the four endangered fish species.
