# Colorado Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
Colorado Cutthroat Trout is a fish species used as an example in this BLM manual to illustrate quantified habitat management objectives. The manual provides a specific management scenario where protecting 30 miles of riparian vegetation on Indian Creek to reduce turbidity from 400 mg per liter to 50 mg per liter would increase the population from 25 fish per mile to 200 fish per mile by 1983, demonstrating how habitat improvements can be linked to measurable population goals for priority aquatic species.
