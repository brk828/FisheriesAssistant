# Shasta Dam

## Summary
Shasta Dam is a water development structure whose construction severely impacted Sacramento River Chinook salmon populations by restricting spawning habitat. This source reports that the dam's construction forced spring and fall runs of Chinook salmon to share spawning grounds, raising concerns about hybridization between these distinct runs.
