# Crowley Lake

## Summary
Crowley Lake is an artificial reservoir in Mono County, California, created in 1941 by damming the Owens River. At maximum storage it covers 5,272 acres with a mean depth of 34.85 feet and is managed as a highly successful trophy trout fishery, producing exceptional growth rates in planted rainbow trout (reaching about one pound in 8 months) due to high biological productivity from shallow depths, high dissolved solids (187 ppm), and abundant bottom fauna (153 pounds per acre). The fishery is managed through annual planting of approximately 300,000 subcatchable rainbow trout near season's end, producing trout at about $0.15 per pound returned to anglers compared to $0.90 per pound for catchable trout programs in nearby waters.
