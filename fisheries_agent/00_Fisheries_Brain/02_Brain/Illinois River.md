# Illinois River

## Summary
The Illinois River is a freshwater system in Oklahoma that has served as a significant research site for fisheries studies, particularly regarding the ecological impacts of dam operations and reservoir management on fish populations and water conditions. The river's tailwater section below Tenkiller Reservoir has been of particular interest to ichthyologists due to its modified thermal and dissolved oxygen regimes created by hydroelectric dam discharge, which creates distinct conditions suitable for studying introduced species like rainbow trout and native fish ecology. This river system has been instrumental in understanding how anthropogenic alterations to aquatic environments affect fish distribution, survival, and the feasibility of various fishery management strategies.
