# Amphipoda

## Summary
Amphipoda is an order of small crustaceans that serve as important prey organisms and bioindicators in freshwater and marine ecosystems, making them valuable subjects for population studies and ecological monitoring in aquatic research. These chitinous invertebrates, which include organisms like freshwater amphipods, are commonly sampled and marked in field studies to track population dynamics and assess environmental conditions in both lentic and lotic habitats. Their presence and abundance are frequently used to evaluate water quality and ecosystem health in conservation biology and limnological investigations.
