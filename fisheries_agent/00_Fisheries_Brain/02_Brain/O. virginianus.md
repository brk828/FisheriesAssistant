# O. virginianus

## Summary
O. virginianus is the scientific name for white-tailed deer, a species that inhabits grasslands and riparian environments. This source indicates that O. virginianus occurs in grasslands and riparian environments in the study area, though the four deer specimens recovered from La Lomita Pequeña were only identified to genus (Odocoileus sp.) and could represent either this species or mule deer.
