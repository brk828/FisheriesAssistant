# Schizothoracini

## Summary
Schizothoracini, commonly known as snow trout, are cold-adapted cyprinid fish. This source reports they dominate the upper reaches of the Kabul River basin along with Cobitidae species, being adapted to fast mountain streams in Afghanistan.
