# Colorado Basin

## Summary
The Colorado Basin (also referred to as Colorado River Basin) is the endemic range of the Humpback Chub, historically including the mainstem Colorado River and four major tributaries (Green River, Yampa River, White River, Little Colorado River). The basin's native fish fauna has been severely impacted by dam construction (particularly Flaming Gorge Dam, Glen Canyon Dam, and Hoover Dam), altered flow regimes, and the introduction of nonnative species that have caused the number of fish species to triple, creating competition and predation pressures on native species.
