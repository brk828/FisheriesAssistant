# Ohio Valley

## Summary
Ohio Valley is a reference region used for comparative context regarding species diversity. The 435 species of algae and protozoa identified in the Clinch River system were comparable to those found in Ohio Valley streams, but far exceeded the fewer species documented in the Columbia River, highlighting the relatively high biodiversity of the Clinch River system.
