# Obed River

## Summary
The Obed River is a Tennessee stream system where introgression between Redeye Bass and Smallmouth Bass is highly probable. Redeye Bass were found in the Obed River and its tributary Otter Creek, suggesting migration from stocked tributaries or undocumented stocking events beyond the original 1953-1960 introduction sites.
