# Lepidomeda Vittata

## Summary
Lepidomeda vittata, commonly known as the Little Colorado spinedace, is a small-bodied cyprinid fish endemic to Arizona's desert aquatic systems that has become a conservation concern due to its vulnerability to displacement by introduced rainbow trout and other non-native species. This native minnow exemplifies the broader challenge of protecting small, localized fish populations in the Colorado River basin, where competition and predation from introduced salmonids have documented negative impacts on native fish communities. Its conservation status and recovery planning are part of the larger effort to manage threatened and endangered desert fish species in the American Southwest.
