# Athletic Mining Company

## Summary
The Athletic Mining Company operated the milling operations that created the Klondyke Tailings between 1948-1958, processing ore from the Head Center Mine and Iron Cap Mine. This source indicates the company processed ore averaging 11.7% lead and 17.4% zinc from the Head Center Mine and 7.0% lead and 10.6% zinc from the Iron Cap Mine, with approximately 80% of the ore being oxidized oxide ores.
