# Price-Stubb Diversion Dam

## Summary
Price-Stubb Diversion Dam is a water diversion structure in the Upper Colorado River Basin that impedes movement of endangered fish species. The FY 2000 Recovery Program allocated $1.3 million for fish passage restoration at this dam as part of habitat restoration efforts to reconnect fragmented critical habitat.
