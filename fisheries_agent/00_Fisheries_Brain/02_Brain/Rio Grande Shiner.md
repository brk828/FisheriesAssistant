# Rio Grande Shiner

## Summary
Rio Grande Shiner (Notropis jemezanus) is a native mainstream warmwater endemic cyprinid of the Middle Rio Grande. As of 1994, this species was under "notice of review" for federal listing, reflecting concerns about its conservation status in the face of water diversion, habitat degradation, and competition from introduced species.
