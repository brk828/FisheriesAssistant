# East Carson River

## Summary
East Carson River is one of 13 streams included in this study of California's catchable trout fisheries. It served as a representative stream system for evaluating the performance and management of stocked rainbow trout populations.

## Context

> This comprehensive study evaluates California's catchable-sized trout stocking program from 1954-1958, during which approximately seven million 7-to-8-inch [[Rainbow Trout]] (Salmo gairdnerii) were stocked annually at a cost of $1,366,000.
