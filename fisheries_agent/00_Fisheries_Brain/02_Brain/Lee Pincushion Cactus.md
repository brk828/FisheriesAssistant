# Lee Pincushion Cactus

## Summary
Lee Pincushion Cactus is an endangered cactus species that depends on halicitid bee pollinators for reproduction. The Service determined that aerial pesticide spraying would likely jeopardize this species through loss of insect pollinators, requiring a 3-mile no-aerial-spray buffer or alternative use of carbaryl bait or biological control.
