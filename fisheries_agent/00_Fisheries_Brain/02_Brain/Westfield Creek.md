# Westfield Creek

## Summary
Westfield Creek was a 3.5-mile Wisconsin stream with flow of 17.2 cfs, pH 8.8, and temperature 77°F where antimycin A non-repellency was demonstrated. Fish showed no downstream escape attempts when exposed to 10 ppb maintained for 10 hours, with mortality occurring in sequence from darters (2 hours) to carp (8 hours), and post-treatment electrofishing finding only small minnows and lampreys surviving.
