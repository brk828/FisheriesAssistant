# Pseudomonas denitrificans

## Summary
Pseudomonas denitrificans is a bacterial species that produces nitrogen gas as the sole end-product of nitrate reduction. This bacterium represents one of the key denitrifying organisms that convert nitrate to gaseous nitrogen rather than ammonia.
