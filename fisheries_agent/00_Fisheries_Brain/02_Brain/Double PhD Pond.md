# Double PhD Pond

## Summary
Double PhD Pond is a pond system at San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge consisting of northwest and southeast sections, with the northwest pond (20°C, turbid) containing large numbers of Western Mosquitofish (224 collected) and abundant bullfrog tadpoles, while the southeast pond (19-20°C) appeared less productive. The southeast pond contains submerged metal tractor parts buried in the silt bottom that pose a safety hazard.

## Context

> This field report documents fish collecting activities conducted by S.P. Vives and five students across multiple sites in southeastern Arizona during April 1-2, 1989. The survey targeted native and introduced fish species at [[San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge]] and surrounding creek systems....
