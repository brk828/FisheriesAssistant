# Denver Research Center

## Summary
Denver Research Center is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility that conducted fishery investigations as part of the 1964 pesticide research program. The center performed experimental field studies examining effects of various chemicals on fish, including testing paraquat and Casoron effects on warm-water fish species.
