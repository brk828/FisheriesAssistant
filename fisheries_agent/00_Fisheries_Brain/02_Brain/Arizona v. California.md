# Arizona v. California

## Summary
Arizona v. California is a legal decision that forms part of the "Law of the River" framework governing Colorado River water allocation. It was covered as a key topic at the May 1999 Continuing Legal Education seminar on Colorado River law, alongside other foundational legal instruments like the 1922 Colorado River Compact and 1928 Boulder Canyon Project Act.
