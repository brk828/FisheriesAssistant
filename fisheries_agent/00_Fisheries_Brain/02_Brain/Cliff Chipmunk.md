# Cliff Chipmunk

## Summary
The Cliff Chipmunk (Eutamias dorsalis) is a diurnal rodent with distinctive tail-flicking behavior that inhabits cliffs and rocky canyons. This survey found it to be usually observed or common around rocky areas in the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve.
