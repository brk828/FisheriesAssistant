# Belted Kingfisher

## Summary
The Belted Kingfisher (Megaceryle alcyon) is a sensitive bird species addressed by the LCR MSCP. It is one of more than 50 federal- or state-listed, candidate, and sensitive species that the program aims to conserve in the Lower Colorado River basin.
