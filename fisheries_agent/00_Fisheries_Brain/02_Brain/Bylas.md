# Bylas

## Summary
Bylas is a location in Graham County, Arizona, near which eight springs support populations of Bylas Fontelicella and Bylas Tryonia. The land is divided among private ownership, San Carlos Apache Reservation, and Bureau of Land Management, with recommendations to fence springheads, eliminate Gambusia, and coordinate fish-mollusk recovery efforts.
