# Lake Trummen

## Summary
Lake Trummen in Sweden underwent habitat restoration between 1970-1971 when 300,000 m³ of nutrient-rich sediment was removed. This restoration led to improved water quality and fish community recovery, demonstrating the effectiveness of sediment removal as a habitat restoration technique for fish conservation.
