# Eragrostis curvula

## Summary
Eragrostis curvula is an invasive exotic grass species that expanded in the Colorado River corridor during Interim Flows monitoring (1992-1995). It was identified as one of several invasive exotics (along with Erianthus ravennae, Lepidium latifolium, and annual bromes) that were colonizing riparian habitats and raising management concerns.
