# Bullfrogs

## Summary
Bullfrogs are amphibians that were legally harvested by Arizona anglers as part of the recreational fishery. The 1980 survey documented that resident anglers harvested 64,400 bullfrogs statewide, representing 0.6% of the total harvest reported.
