# Woodcock

## Summary
Woodcock are migratory birds that demonstrated progressive bioaccumulation of pesticides on their wintering grounds. This source found that woodcock collected shortly after arrival in Louisiana in 1958 showed only trace heptachlor residues, but by February 1960, 5 of 9 birds contained residues averaging 1.5 mcg/bird (maximum 3.0 mcg/bird), and immature birds from New Brunswick also contained residues, suggesting contamination occurred on both breeding and wintering grounds.
