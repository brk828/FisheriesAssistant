# World Wildlife Fund

## Summary
The World Wildlife Fund is an international conservation organization. In this context, WWF worked jointly with The Nature Conservancy and CONABIO (Mexico's National Biodiversity Institute) to adapt and define the boundaries of the Sonoran Desert ecoregion for conservation planning purposes, extending from uplands to the littoral zone.
