# Pinetop Fishery Assistance Office

## Summary
The Pinetop Fishery Assistance Office is a field office of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. This office submitted a technical proposal in July 1990 to the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation Glen Canyon Environmental Studies outlining a comprehensive multi-year research program to study habitat use by the endangered Humpback Chub in the Little Colorado River and other Colorado River tributaries.
