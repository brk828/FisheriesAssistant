# Pointe Coupee Parish

## Summary
Pointe Coupee Parish is a Louisiana parish where both False River and Old River oxbow lakes are located, situated 15 miles apart in the Mississippi River floodplain. The study area features fertile alluvial soils with high calcium and magnesium content that contribute to the hard water chemistry of both lakes.

## Context

> This 1965 Master's thesis by Jerry Brashier examined age and growth of [[Largemouth Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*) in two Louisiana oxbow lakes: [[False River]] and [[Old River]], located 15 miles apart in [[Pointe Coupee Parish]].
