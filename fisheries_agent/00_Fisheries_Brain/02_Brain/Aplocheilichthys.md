# Aplocheilichthys

## Summary
Aplocheilichthys is an African killifish genus that Jordan and Snyder noted approaches Oryzias in characteristics. However, they concluded the two genera remain sufficiently distinct to warrant separate generic status for the Japanese species.

## Context

> This 1906 taxonomic review by [[Jordan]] and [[Snyder]] documents two species of killifishes (family [[Poeciliidae]]) from Japan: [[Fundulichthys virescens]] and [[Oryzias latipes]]. Both are small fishes inhabiting rice swamps, pools, and ditches in southern Japan, likely feeding on mosquitoes....
