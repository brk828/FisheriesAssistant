# Fisher

## Summary
Fisher conducted a 1953 tagging study at the 5,000-acre Millerton Lake in California, reporting a tag return rate of 19.7%. This work is cited as a comparison point to show that the 27-31% opening week tag return rates in Kentucky's newly opened lakes exceeded typical returns from established waters.

## Context

> This 1955 study by the [[Kentucky Department of Fish and Wildlife Resources]] examined the impact of intensive fishing pressure during the opening week of three newly stocked state-owned lakes.
