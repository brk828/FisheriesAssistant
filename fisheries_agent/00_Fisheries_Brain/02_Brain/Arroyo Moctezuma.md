# Arroyo Moctezuma

## Summary
Arroyo Moctezuma is a stream in the Rio de Bavispe drainage (sites 76-77 in the Hendrickson et al. 1981 survey) located in the Rio Yaqui Basin of Mexico. This source identifies it as the target collection site for Yaqui Chub (Notropis formosus) because it had the largest Notropis population documented in the 1978 survey, with plans to collect approximately 300 specimens there over 2-3 days using seining or electrofishing.
