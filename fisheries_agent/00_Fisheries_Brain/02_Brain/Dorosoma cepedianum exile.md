# Dorosoma cepedianum exile

## Summary
Dorosoma cepedianum exile is a subspecies of gizzard shad from Texas that this source suggests may have taxonomic affinities with a potentially undescribed Dorosoma subspecies collected near Belize. The source notes that the relationship between these forms requires further investigation to clarify their taxonomic status.
