# Powder River

## Summary
Powder River in Oregon showed dramatic differences in fish communities related to turbidity from gold mining operations. Below the dredge operations where turbidity reached 1,700 ppm, only rough fishes were present, while clear water (5 ppm) above the dredge supported trout and whitefish.
