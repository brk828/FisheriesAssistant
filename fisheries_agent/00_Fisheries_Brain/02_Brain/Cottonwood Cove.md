# Cottonwood Cove

## Summary
Cottonwood Cove is a shallow nearshore habitat zone in Lake Mohave characterized by 1-5 meter depths over cobble substrate that serves as important nocturnal foraging and refugial habitat for imperiled native fishes, particularly adult Bonytail Chub and Razorback Sucker. This locality has been a key sampling site for monitoring populations of these endemic Colorado River Basin species, though it is now heavily invaded by non-native carp that create significant competitive pressure on the native fish communities.
