# Woundfin Recovery Team

## Summary
The Woundfin Recovery Team is a collaborative conservation effort established to address the endangerment and potential extinction of the woundfin (*Plagopterus argentissimus*), a small native fish species found in the Virgin River drainage of the southwestern United States. This interdisciplinary team coordinates research, habitat restoration, and management strategies aimed at stabilizing wild populations and developing recovery protocols for this threatened species. The team's work exemplifies the organized, multi-agency approach required for endangered fish conservation in arid western aquatic ecosystems facing threats from habitat degradation, water management practices, and non-native species competition.
