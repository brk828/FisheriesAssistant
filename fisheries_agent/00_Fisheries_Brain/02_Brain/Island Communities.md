# Island Communities

## Summary
Island communities are relatively simple, bounded ecological systems used as natural laboratories for studying colonization and extinction processes. This source uses island communities as the focal system for examining whether lower species-to-genus ratios on islands compared to mainland areas result from competitive exclusion or random colonization, noting that islands may experience more intense competition due to smaller area and lower habitat diversity.
