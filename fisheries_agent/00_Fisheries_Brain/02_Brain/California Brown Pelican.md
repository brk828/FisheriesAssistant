# California Brown Pelican

## Summary
The California Brown Pelican (Pelecanus occidentalis californicus) is a sensitive species addressed by the LCR MSCP. It is one of more than 50 federal- or state-listed, candidate, and sensitive species that the program aims to conserve in the Lower Colorado River basin.
