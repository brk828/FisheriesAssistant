# St. Marys

## Summary
St. Marys is a location in Ohio, USA, where State Fish Farm No. 1 was situated. This facility conducted experimental creek chub propagation studies in 1941-1942, using specially constructed ponds along the east bank of Lake St. Marys to develop simplified methods for raising bait minnows.
