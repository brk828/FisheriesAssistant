# Arizona Sycamore

## Summary
Arizona Sycamore (Platanus wrightii) is one of the canopy layer broadleaf species in Lower Bonita Creek. On the three lowermost transects, it comprised (along with Fremont Cottonwood and Goodding Willow) 65-93% of broadleaf species, but its proportion decreased on upper transects where other species became more prominent.
