# Blackwater River

## Summary
Blackwater River is a freshwater river system in Okaloosa and Santa Rosa Counties, Florida, that served as the primary collection site for the newly described caddisfly species Cheumatopsyche petersi. The holotype was collected 4.5 miles northwest of Cannon Town in Okaloosa County on April 7, 1968, and 231 male and 4 female paratypes were collected from multiple locations along the river system during April-June 1968-1970.
