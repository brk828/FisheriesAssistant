# Chase Creek

## Summary
Chase Creek is a stream near Clifton, Arizona, that has been severely impacted by mining pollution. The source documents that Chase Creek contains springs with naturally high iron and sulfate concentrations, and historical records show that fishes in Chase Creek and the downstream Gila River were decimated by toxic releases from Clifton-Morenci mining operations. This creek exemplifies how mining activities have consistently produced toxic releases affecting Arizona's aquatic ecosystems.
