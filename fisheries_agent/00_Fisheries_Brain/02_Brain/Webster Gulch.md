# Webster Gulch

## Summary
Webster Gulch is a former alluvial channel where subsurface flow has created a severe contamination plume with highly acidic water containing extremely high ferrous sulfate complex and dissolved toxic metals. Copper concentrations exceed 300 mg/L in Gila Conglomerate groundwater underlying tailings deposits, with possible sources including Webster Lake and metal leaching from past tailings.
