# Unnamed Spring Fed Tank

## Summary
Unnamed Spring Fed Tank #498 (Site #39) was a reintroduction site where Gila Topminnow failed, possibly due to low oxygen conditions. The tank was found dry during surveys in both 1989 and 1996, meeting extirpation criteria.
