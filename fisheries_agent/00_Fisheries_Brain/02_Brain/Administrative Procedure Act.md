# Administrative Procedure Act

## Summary
The Administrative Procedure Act (APA) is a federal law (5 U.S.C. Section 557(c)) that establishes procedural requirements for federal agency decision-making, including requirements that agencies provide findings, conclusions, and reasons for their decisions. In this case, Catron County alleges that the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service violated the APA by failing to provide required findings and reasons for decisions to postpone critical habitat designation for Spikedace and Loach Minnow, to propose critical habitat without NEPA compliance, and to adopt recovery plans before designating critical habitat.
