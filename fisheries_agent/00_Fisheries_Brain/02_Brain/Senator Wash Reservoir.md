# Senator Wash Reservoir

## Summary
Senator Wash Reservoir is a lentic (stillwater) impoundment along the Lower Colorado River system that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly in conjunction with the adjacent riverine channel where species demonstrate utilization of both reservoir and flowing water environments. This reservoir ecosystem is significant for monitoring native fish populations and understanding seasonal behavioral patterns related to water temperature fluctuations and habitat preferences of species such as the razorback sucker. The reservoir represents an important component of Colorado River fisheries management, requiring systematic monitoring protocols to track population dynamics and habitat use in this mixed lentic-lotic system.
