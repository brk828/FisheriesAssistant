# Acapulco, Mexico

## Summary
Acapulco, Mexico is a location that was the source of a particular stock of Mollienisia sphenops with a dorsal ray count of 8.5. A male M. sphenops from Acapulco was used in extensive single-pair matings with M. formosa over 5,196 days, producing 67 broods with 1,067 young at a rate of 20.54 young per 100 days.
