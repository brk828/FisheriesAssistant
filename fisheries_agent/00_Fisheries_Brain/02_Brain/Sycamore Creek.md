# Sycamore Creek

## Summary
Sycamore Creek is an intermittent arid-land stream in Central Arizona that has served as a model system for studying hyporheic zone ecology and the role of surface-water/groundwater exchange in supporting aquatic communities. Research at this site has demonstrated how the saturated subsurface sediment layer beneath the streambed provides refuge and habitat stability for benthic invertebrates and specialized hypogean fauna, with hyporheic community composition strongly influenced by patterns of groundwater upwelling and downwelling. The creek exemplifies how hydrological processes in desert streams regulate ecosystem resistance and resilience, making it a key reference ecosystem for understanding aquatic conservation in water-limited environments.
