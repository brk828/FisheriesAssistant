# Ocean Lake

## Summary
Ocean Lake in Wyoming was the subject of mark-recapture studies in 1969-1970 that estimated populations of 1,299,000 Carp and 42,500 White Sucker. Planned studies for 1971-1972 included refining standing crop estimates, evaluating methods and costs for efficient carp removal, and initiating large-scale removal to simulate commercial harvest and evaluate ecosystem effects.
