# Vernal Colorado River Fish Project

## Summary
The Vernal Colorado River Fish Project was one of multiple agencies that collaborated on the 1987 experimental razorback sucker hatchery at Ouray National Wildlife Refuge. The project worked alongside Jones Hole National Fish Hatchery, Dexter National Fish Hatchery, Ouray National Wildlife Refuge, and Arizona State University Tempe.
