# Virgin River Narrows

## Summary
The Virgin River Narrows refers to a geomorphologically distinct reach of the Virgin River in southwestern Utah where the channel becomes confined and incised, characterized by narrower channel widths and increased depth relative to upstream sections. This section serves as a critical ecological boundary and sampling location in conservation studies of endemic Virgin River fish species, particularly the Virgin River chub, as it represents a transition zone between the upper braided, shallow reaches and the more confined downstream habitat. The Narrows functions as an important reference point for fisheries operations and population monitoring, with management activities often delineated by natural or constructed barriers relative to this geomorphic feature.
