# Division of Wildlife Resources

## Summary
The Division of Wildlife Resources (DWR) is Utah's state wildlife management agency. In this case, the Utah DWR proposed introducing rainbow smelt into Lake Powell and argued that this was a state action not requiring federal Endangered Species Act consultation, a position that the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service strongly challenged due to federal involvement in Lake Powell management and funding of DWR activities.
