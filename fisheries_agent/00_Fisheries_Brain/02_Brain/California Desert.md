# California Desert

## Summary
The California Desert is a unique terrestrial ecosystem in California requiring specialized management approaches. This source indicates it encompasses portions of San Bernardino County and Riverside County, and the Bureau of Land Management manages it through a multiple-use classification system that balances conservation with human activities such as recreation and resource extraction.
