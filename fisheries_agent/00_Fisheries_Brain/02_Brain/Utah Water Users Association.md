# Utah Water Users Association

## Summary
The Utah Water Users Association is one of the participating organizations in the Upper Colorado River Recovery Program. This water user group joined the 1988 cooperative agreement alongside federal agencies, state representatives, and environmental organizations to implement recovery efforts for endangered fish species in the Upper Colorado River Basin.
