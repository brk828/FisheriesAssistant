# oxygen minimum zone

## Summary
The oxygen minimum zone is a region of extremely low dissolved oxygen concentration in ocean waters. This source reports that Bathyconchoecia deeveyae was collected from waters with dissolved oxygen of only 0.15 ml/l at 500-520 m depth, indicating the species inhabits the oxygen minimum zone and suggesting adaptation to hypoxic conditions in the Peru-Chile Trench System.
