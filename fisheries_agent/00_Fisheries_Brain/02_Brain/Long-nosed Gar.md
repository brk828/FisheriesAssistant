# Long-nosed Gar

## Summary
Long-nosed Gar (family Lepisosteidae) is the most common gar species in Kansas, with a beak approximately 20 times as long as wide. The guide describes gars as having sharp-edged scales and beaks with sharp teeth, making them "most destructive" to game fishes and fishing gear and an "unqualified nuisance," though possible value for controlling overstocked lakes is suggested.
