# Santa Rosa Formation

## Summary
The Santa Rosa Formation is a late Miocene geological formation that contains fossil records of secondary freshwater fishes. It includes the goodeid species Tapatia occidentalis, providing evidence of the ancient presence of the autochthonous Goodeidae family in México.
