# Still Lake

## Summary
Still Lake is an example of a Type II temporary sub-climax oxygen maximum lake, recording 14.8 mg/L at 7 meters depth on June 2, 1961. This deep, transparent lake showed maximum oxygen in June-July that subsequently declined, with a thick oxygen layer of uniform concentrations that disappeared despite adequate light, possibly due to nutrient depletion.
