# Sicydium punctatum

## Summary
Sicydium punctatum is a congener species described by Perugia that is closely related to Sicydium montanum. The new species S. montanum is distinguished from S. punctatum primarily by having larger scales (approximately 48 in median series versus fewer scales in S. punctatum).
