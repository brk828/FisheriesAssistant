# Glutamate Oxaloacetate Transaminase

## Summary
Glutamate Oxaloacetate Transaminase is an enzyme examined at 1 genetic locus that showed a four-banded pattern. The enzyme displayed identical mobility between Rio Grande and Cuatro Cienegas cichlid populations with no variation detected.
