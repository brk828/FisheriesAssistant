# Bombay Beach

## Summary
Bombay Beach is a location on the Salton Sea where ten-pounders were first documented in this water body, with 10 specimens (18-20 inches) caught in beach seine hauls on May 14, 1942. Additional specimens were collected there during subsequent sampling, demonstrating the species' presence at this site.
