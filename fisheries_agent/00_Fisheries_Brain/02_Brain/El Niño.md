# El Niño

## Summary
El Niño events were shown in workshop presentations to correlate with fishing production in the Upper Gulf of California. Charts presented by Salvador Galindo-Bect demonstrated that the greatest fishing in San Felipe's history occurred during high-water years associated with El Niño events.
