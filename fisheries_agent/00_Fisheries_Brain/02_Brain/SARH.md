# SARH

## Summary
SARH (Secretaria de Agricultura y Recursos Hidraulicos) is Mexico's Secretariat of Agriculture and Water Resources. This source indicates that SARH receives Colorado River water allocation information from CILA and coordinates with regional officials, the Rural Bank, crop insurance agencies, and farmer associations to determine irrigation plans. SARH also estimates that over 15 million metric tons of salt enter the region annually, with a net deposition of approximately 1.6 million metric tons.
