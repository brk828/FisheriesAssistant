# San Juan River Basin

## Summary
The San Juan River Basin is a river basin that is explicitly excluded from the Upper Colorado River Basin as defined in these interim stocking procedures. The procedures developed for regulating nonnative fish stocking apply to waters in Colorado, Utah, and Wyoming within the Upper Basin, but not to the San Juan River Basin.
