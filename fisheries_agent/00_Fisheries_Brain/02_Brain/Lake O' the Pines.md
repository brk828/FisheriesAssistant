# Lake O' the Pines

## Summary
Lake O' the Pines is a reservoir in Texas Region 3-B that experienced excess aquatic vegetation problems due to constant water level maintenance. The dam (Ferrell's Bridge Dam) constructed upstream of Caddo Lake eliminated natural water level fluctuations, contributing to approximately one-third or more of Caddo Lake becoming choked with aquatic moss.
