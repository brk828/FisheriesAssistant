# Groundwater Seepage Hypothesis

## Summary
The Groundwater Seepage Hypothesis is a proposed mechanism to explain homing behavior in Colorado squawfish, suggesting that fish use olfactory imprinting on groundwater chemical cues at spawning sites for orientation and navigation. This hypothesis is supported by the observation that both confirmed spawning areas (lower Yampa Canyon and Gray Canyon) receive input from spring-fed tributaries and sandstone/limestone seeps, which could provide distinctive chemical signatures for fish to recognize and return to in subsequent spawning seasons.
