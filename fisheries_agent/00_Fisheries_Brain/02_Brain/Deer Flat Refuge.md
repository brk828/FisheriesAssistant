# Deer Flat Refuge

## Summary
Deer Flat Refuge showed the highest sediment contamination (40.0 ppm average) among monitored refuges in 1964, correlated with scarcity of invertebrates. Black-crowned night heron mortality in 1963 revealed tissue residues up to 25 ppm DDT, 4 ppm dieldrin, and 5 ppm toxaphene.
