# Region 3-B Texas

## Summary
Region 3-B Texas is a fisheries management area in Texas that includes major reservoirs such as Lake Palestine, Lake O' the Pines, Lake Striker, Dam B Reservoir, Lake Murvaul, and Caddo Lake, as well as the Angelina River. The region's primary fishery problem during 1962-1963 was excess aquatic vegetation in larger reservoirs, largely due to inadequate timber clearing before impoundment and constant water level maintenance.
