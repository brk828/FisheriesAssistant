# Gila Bend

## Summary
Gila Bend is listed as one of the ten Arizona development projects in Maxwell's 1935 proposal for water conservation and land reclamation. It was to be part of the comprehensive system centered on Colorado River water diversion and local watershed development.

## Context

> This 1935 advance print document by George Hebard Maxwell presents an ambitious proposal for water conservation and land reclamation in Arizona and California through comprehensive development of the [[Colorado River]].
