# Arizona State University

## Summary
Arizona State University is a major research institution that has played a significant role in fish science and aquatic ecology research in the southwestern United States, particularly through faculty affiliations such as Dr. Minckley's work on native desert fish species and their conservation. The university has been a key participant in interdisciplinary monitoring and recovery efforts for imperiled freshwater fish species in arid-land aquatic habitats of Arizona and the greater Southwest, collaborating with federal and state agencies including the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Bureau of Reclamation, and Arizona Game and Fish Department. Its contributions to desert fish research have been instrumental in informing conservation and recovery strategies for threatened and candidate species endemic to southwestern aquatic ecosystems.
