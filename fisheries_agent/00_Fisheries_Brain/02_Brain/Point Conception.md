# Point Conception

## Summary
Point Conception is a major biogeographic boundary on the California coast that marks a persistent faunal break between northern and southern marine assemblages. Pleistocene fossil assemblages and oxygen-18 estimates indicate this faunal break and the north-south temperature gradient were maintained throughout the Quaternary, demonstrating remarkable persistence of geographic climate patterns despite changes in temperature magnitude.
