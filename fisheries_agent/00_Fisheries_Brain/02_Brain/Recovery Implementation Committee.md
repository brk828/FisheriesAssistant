# Recovery Implementation Committee

## Summary
The Recovery Implementation Committee is the policy-level body overseeing the Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program for four endangered fish species (Colorado Squawfish, Razorback Sucker, Bonytail Chub, and Humpback Chub). The committee operates through a tiered structure including the Management Group, Technical Group, and various subcommittees, involving federal and state agencies, water users, environmental groups, and power utilities.
