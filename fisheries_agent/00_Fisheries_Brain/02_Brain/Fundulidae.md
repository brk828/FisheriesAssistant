# Fundulidae

## Summary
Fundulidae is a family of small freshwater and brackish-water killifishes native primarily to North America, characterized by their adaptability to diverse aquatic habitats and notable for including several species of conservation concern, such as the Barrens Topminnow (*Fundulus julisia*). Members of this family have been subjects of extensive taxonomic study and conservation efforts due to their restricted geographic ranges and vulnerability to habitat loss, making them important indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and subjects of interest in both ichthyological research and aquatic resource management.
