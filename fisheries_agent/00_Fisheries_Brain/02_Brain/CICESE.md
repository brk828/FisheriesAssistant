# CICESE

## Summary
CICESE (Centro de Investigación Científica y de Educación Superior de Ensenada) is a Mexican research institution that participated in the May 1999 stakeholder workshop on Colorado River Delta conservation. It was among the Mexican organizations represented alongside U.S. environmental groups, tribal communities, and academic researchers working on delta ecosystem issues.
