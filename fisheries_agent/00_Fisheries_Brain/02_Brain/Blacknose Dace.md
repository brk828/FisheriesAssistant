# Blacknose Dace

## Summary
The Blacknose Dace (*Rhinichthys atratulus meleagris*) is a small cyprinid fish species commonly used in aquatic ecology and fisheries research as an indicator organism for stream health and habitat quality. Native to North American freshwater systems, particularly limestone and spring-fed streams, this species typically ranges from 8 mm as fry to approximately 124 mm in total length at maturity, making it an important forage fish in benthic communities. Its presence and population dynamics are frequently studied through scale sampling and age-growth analysis to assess stream conditions, particularly in systems with specific water chemistry and vegetation characteristics.
