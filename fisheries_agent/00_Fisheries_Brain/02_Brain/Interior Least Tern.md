# Interior Least Tern

## Summary
The interior least tern is a federally listed endangered bird species that is one of the primary targets of the Central Platte River Recovery Program. This species uses the Central Platte River and adjacent wet meadow and sand pit habitat seasonally for roosting, nesting, and feeding. Critical habitat has been designated for this species along 59 miles of the Central Platte River.
