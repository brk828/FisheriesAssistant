# Proteocephalidea

## Summary
Proteocephalidea is an order of tapeworms (cestodes) that includes the family Proteocephalidae. The tapeworm Ophiotaenia fragilus found in Colorado River Squawfish belongs to this order, and related species in this order typically require two intermediate hosts (crustaceans and fish) before reaching their definitive fish host.

## Context

> This 1972 Master's thesis reports the first identification of the tapeworm *Ophiotaenia fragilus* from the [[Colorado River Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), a native cyprinid that has experienced dramatic population decline over 30 years.
