# Lower Colorado River National Wildlife Refuges

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River National Wildlife Refuges are a system of federal protected areas along the lower Colorado River that include the Bill Williams River, Cibola, Havasu, and Imperial National Wildlife Refuges. According to this management plan, these refuges are designated as priority sites for developing riverine cove refugia (protected habitats less than 5 hectares) for endangered big-river fish species within 3 years, and for creating larger isolated habitats (80 hectares or more) within 5 years. The refuges provide designated critical habitat where fish exclusion barriers can be installed, resident fishes removed, and target endangered species stocked and grown to approximately 300 mm before being used for population augmentation or maintained as genetic reserves.
