# Upper Ohio River Basin

## Summary
The Upper Ohio River Basin refers to the watershed area above the mouth of the Kanawha River in West Virginia, encompassing parts of Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, and New York. This source documents that the basin originally supported more than 125 fish species across 25 families, but has experienced dramatic declines due to deforestation, pollution, dam construction, and siltation over the preceding century.
