# Yadkin River

## Summary
The Yadkin River is a major waterway in north-central North Carolina supporting important recreational catfish fisheries. This survey found abundant bullhead populations (mean CPUE 2.11 fish/minute) above Idols Dam with favorable habitat including wide channels (>38 m), numerous riffle areas, and gravel bottoms, while flathead catfish were confirmed absent above the dam but present below it.
