# Ann McLuckie

## Summary
Ann McLuckie was a University of Arizona graduate student who conducted desert tortoise research near Kingman, Arizona in 1994. Her studies focused on ecology, genetics, and morphometrics of desert tortoises, using fiber-optic probes to observe hibernating animals, with cooperative funding from multiple agencies including BLM and Arizona Game and Fish.
