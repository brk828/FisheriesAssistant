# Western Least Bittern

## Summary
The Western Least Bittern (Ixobrychus exilis hesperis) is a marsh bird subspecies for which habitat is managed at the Imperial Ponds Conservation Area. The site includes 12 acres of managed marsh specifically designated for this species along with California Black Rail and Yuma Clapper Rail.
