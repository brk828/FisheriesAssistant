# Gila Woodpecker

## Summary
The Gila Woodpecker (Melanerpes uropygialis) is a desert-adapted woodpecker species. This survey identified it as one of 36 native bird species present at Papago Park Military Reservation in the Sonoran desertscrub habitat.
