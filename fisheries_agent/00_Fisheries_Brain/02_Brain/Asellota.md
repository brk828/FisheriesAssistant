# Asellota

## Summary
Asellota is a superorder of aquatic isopods (crustaceans) that includes freshwater taxa such as *Asellus*, *Lirceus*, and related genera, which are important components of freshwater benthic communities and have served as key organisms for systematic and biogeographic studies. The taxonomy of Asellota has undergone significant revision, particularly regarding the reclassification of polyphyletic assemblages into more natural groupings based on morphological and phylogenetic analysis. These organisms are of interest to aquatic ecologists and conservation biologists as indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and as subjects for understanding freshwater invertebrate diversity across different geographic regions.
