# Fantail Darter

## Summary
The Fantail Darter is a small North American freshwater fish species belonging to the family Percidae that was commonly found in riffle habitats of streams and rivers during mid-20th century ecological surveys. As one of the numerically dominant darter species in benthic communities, the Fantail Darter comprised a significant portion of darter assemblages and played an important role in local fish community structure and aquatic food webs. This species has been a subject of scientific study regarding growth, reproduction, feeding ecology, and territorial behavior, making it a relevant indicator organism for understanding stream fish population dynamics and habitat preferences in flowing water ecosystems.
