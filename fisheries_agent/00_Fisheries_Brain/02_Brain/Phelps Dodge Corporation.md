# Phelps Dodge Corporation

## Summary
The owner of Tavasci Spring that was involved in a land trade for the spring to become part of Tuzigoot National Monument. This source identifies the corporation in the context of conservation efforts for the critically endangered Tavasci Spring Fontelicella.
