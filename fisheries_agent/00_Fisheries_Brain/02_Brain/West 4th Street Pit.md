# West 4th Street Pit

## Summary
West 4th Street Pit was a strip-pit lake that contained gizzard shad populations supporting crappie and received experimental walleye stocking. Six walleyes (6-8 inches) stocked in November 1953 showed some success, with a 3-pound walleye caught in March 1956, and the site produced a 6 pound 8 ounce bass in April 1956.
