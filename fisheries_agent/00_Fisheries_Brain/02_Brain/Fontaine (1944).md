# Fontaine (1944)

## Summary
Fontaine (1944) described flathead catfish spawning and noted behavioral similarities with channel catfish and brown bullhead. The authors cite Fontaine's indirect observations of non-injected channel catfish as supporting evidence that pituitary injections did not fundamentally alter the species' innate spawning pattern.
