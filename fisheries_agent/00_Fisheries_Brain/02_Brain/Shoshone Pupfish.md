# Shoshone Pupfish

## Summary
The Shoshone Pupfish is a small, endemic fish species inhabiting isolated desert springs within California's Death Valley/Amargosa drainage system, representing a relict population adapted to extreme environmental conditions including alkaline and geothermal waters. As a state-endemic species with highly restricted distribution, it exemplifies the conservation challenges faced by desert aquatic fauna, where habitat isolation, limited geographic range, and vulnerability to environmental perturbations place it at significant risk of extinction. The species serves as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health in the Basin and Range province and is subject to conservation management and regulatory protection under California's special concern designations and federal endangered species legislation.
