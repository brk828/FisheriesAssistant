# Bonneville System

## Summary
The Bonneville System refers to the Pleistocene Lake Bonneville and its associated waters in the Great Basin, which reached 1,000 feet depth, stretched 346 miles, and covered over 20,000 square miles. This source reports it supports 12 genera (2 endemic) and 22 species of fish, with its modern remnant being the Great Salt Lake at only 50 feet deep.
