# Bathyconchoecia

## Summary
Bathyconchoecia is a genus of deep-sea ostracods in the family Halocyprididae, with type-species B. paulula established by Deevey in 1968. This source describes a new species, B. deeveyae, which is distinguished from all other members of the genus by its extreme spinosity, bearing 7 long hollow spines on its highly ornamented carapace, and was collected from 520 m depth in the Peru-Chile Trench System.
