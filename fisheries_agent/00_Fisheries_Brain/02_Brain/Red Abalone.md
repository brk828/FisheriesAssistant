# Red Abalone

## Summary
Red Abalone (Haliotis rufescens) is a cold-water indicator species used to reconstruct past ocean temperatures. Indian middens on Santa Rosa Island dated to about 7,000 years B.P. contained predominantly Red Abalone, suggesting that cold glacial conditions persisted well into the Postglacial period along the California coast.
