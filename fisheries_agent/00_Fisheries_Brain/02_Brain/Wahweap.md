# Wahweap

## Summary
Wahweap was the most heavily fished area in Lake Powell, receiving 44.7% of all angler trips (2,554 of 5,715 trips with location data) due to its proximity to the launch area at Wahweap Marina. It served as both a marina location and a general fishing area designation in the lower reservoir.
