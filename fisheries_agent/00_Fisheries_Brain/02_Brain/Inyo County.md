# Inyo County

## Summary
Inyo County is part of Region 5b and was the second-ranked trout county in 1960 with 4,773 fish reported from 165 reports. Together with Mono County, Region 5b contributed 19.1% of the state's trout catch (5,420,000 fish), with 75% taken by southern California anglers who comprised 81% of anglers fishing there.
