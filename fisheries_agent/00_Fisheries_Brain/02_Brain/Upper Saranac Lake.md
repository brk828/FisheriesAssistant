# Upper Saranac Lake

## Summary
Upper Saranac Lake is a New York State lake where landlocked salmon (Atlantic salmon, Salmo salar) were stocked as part of the state's salmon program. During 1953 spawning operations, few salmon were captured there, indicating lower population establishment compared to other program waters.
