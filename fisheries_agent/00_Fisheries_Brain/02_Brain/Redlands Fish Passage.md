# Redlands Fish Passage

## Summary
Redlands Fish Passage is a fish passage structure completed in FY 1996 that restored fish access to the Gunnison River. According to the FY 1998 work plan, this structure restored access to 57 miles of Gunnison River habitat and was successfully used by 19 Colorado squawfish and more than 19,000 other native fishes.
