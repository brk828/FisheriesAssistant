# British Honduras

## Summary
British Honduras is the former colonial name for the modern nation of Belize in Central America. This source documents freshwater and brackish water fishes collected from British Honduras and neighboring Guatemala, noting that the region was poorly known ichthyologically at the time and appears to have relatively low characoid fish diversity compared to regions farther south.
