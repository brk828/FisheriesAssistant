# East Fork

## Summary
The East Fork is a tributary of the Gila River in New Mexico that historically supported spikedace populations. According to this source, spikedace were completely extirpated from the East Fork between 1978-1984, with the loss attributed to increased numbers of exotic smallmouth bass and catfish that became abundant in the area by 1983-1984.
