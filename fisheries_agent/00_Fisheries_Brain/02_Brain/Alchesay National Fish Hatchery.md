# Alchesay National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Alchesay National Fish Hatchery is a U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service facility located in Arizona that serves as a federal fish propagation and rearing center for trout and other species in the southwestern United States. The hatchery plays a critical role in fisheries management and conservation by producing fish for stocking in public waters and supporting fish health diagnostics through the co-located Pinetop Fish Health Center, which certifies disease status and conducts health assessments for National and State Fish Hatcheries across the Southwest region. As part of the integrated management of Arizona's aquatic resources, the facility contributes to maintaining viable populations in river systems affected by water development projects and supports broader regional aquaculture operations.
