# Oasis Valley

## Summary
Oasis Valley is a geographic area in Nevada that contains several petitioned aquatic species including the Oasis Valley Speckled Dace, Amargosa Toad, Oasis Valley Springsnail, Death Valley Agabus Diving Beetle, and Amargosa Tryonia Snail. The area is part of the Amargosa River system where habitat has been impacted by channelization and recreational vehicle use.
