# Cholla Bay

## Summary
Cholla Bay is a large intertidal area at Puerto Peñasco approximately four miles wide at zero tide, exposing extensive sandstone reefs up to two miles from settlement at lower tides. The bay features sloughs draining into desert marshland, with prolonged exposure limiting organisms to hardy mud and sand dwellers, and grain size and tidal movement producing zonation patterns not marked by surface bands.
