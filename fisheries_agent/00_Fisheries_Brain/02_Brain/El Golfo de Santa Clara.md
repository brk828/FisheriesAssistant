# El Golfo de Santa Clara

## Summary
El Golfo de Santa Clara is a location in Sonora, Mexico, situated near the Colorado River delta and Gulf of California. During 1967-1968 collections at this marine site, striped mullet averaged 22.4 cm in standard length across 756 specimens, with five distinct size classes ranging from 7-46 cm collected at approximately 2-month intervals.

## Context

> This study documents the distribution patterns of [[Striped Mullet]] (Mugil cephalus) in the [[Colorado River]] and [[Gulf of California]] during 1967-1968, revealing evidence of upstream migration and questioning traditional understanding of salinity requirements for spawning.
