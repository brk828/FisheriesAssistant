# Spencer Canyon

## Summary
Spencer Canyon is a major tributary to the Colorado River in the lower Grand Canyon study area, located in Stratum B (RM 239-252). This source reports that river stage monitoring at Spencer Canyon (RM 246.0) documented a maximum fluctuation of 42 cm over 27.7 hours, and the canyon's lower 1 km contained abundant fish populations dominated by red shiners, requiring further upstream survey.
