# Warpaint Shiner

## Summary
The Warpaint Shiner (Notropis coccogenis) is a native cyprinid fish that is abundant and distributed park-wide in Great Smoky Mountains National Park. It is described as a strikingly-colored fish that is appropriately named for its distinctive appearance.
