# Powan

## Summary
The Powan (Coregonus lavaretus) is a vulnerable fish species with 6 populations in Great Britain that is threatened by the introduced Ruffe which consumes its eggs. Conservation efforts include translocation projects where adults from Loch Lomond are netted, stripped for eggs, hatched indoors, and released into two selected lochs, with 1991 sampling showing excellent growth of translocated populations.
