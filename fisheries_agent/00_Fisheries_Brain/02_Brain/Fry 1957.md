# Fry 1957

## Summary
Fry 1957 established a foundational conceptual framework for categorizing fish oxygen consumption into distinct metabolic levels, most notably distinguishing between standard metabolic rate (the theoretical basal consumption at absolute rest) and other elevated rates of oxygen utilization under various activity and environmental conditions. This classification system became a fundamental tool in fish physiology and respiratory ecology for standardizing measurements and comparing metabolic demands across species and experimental conditions. The framework continues to be widely referenced in studies examining how factors such as temperature, activity level, and environmental stress influence fish metabolism and survival.
