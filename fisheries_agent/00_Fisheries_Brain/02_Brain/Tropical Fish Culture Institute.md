# Tropical Fish Culture Institute

## Summary
The Tropical Fish Culture Institute in Malacca, Malaysia was the source of Tilapia hornorum stock used in early hybridization experiments in Arizona. This source reports that the Arizona Cooperative Fisheries Research Unit obtained T. hornorum from this institute in 1962 for crossing with T. mossambica to produce the "Malacca hybrid" for aquaculture purposes.
