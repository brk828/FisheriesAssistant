# Arizona Queen of the Night

## Summary
Arizona Queen of the Night (Peniocereus greggii) is a cryptic cactus species of special concern that was not observed during the 1994 PPMR survey but was identified as potentially present by consultations with nearby natural areas. This species chiefly occurs under trees and shrubs on flats, along washes, and occasionally on hillsides, with recommendations to closely scrutinize areas under shrubs/trees before removal.
