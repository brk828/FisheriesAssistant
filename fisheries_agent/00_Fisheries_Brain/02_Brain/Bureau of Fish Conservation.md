# Bureau of Fish Conservation

## Summary
The Bureau of Fish Conservation was the California state agency responsible for fisheries management in the 1940s. This source indicates the Bureau initiated experimental plantings of Kokanee in California waters starting in 1941, beginning with Salt Springs Reservoir and expanding to four additional water bodies through 1947.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
