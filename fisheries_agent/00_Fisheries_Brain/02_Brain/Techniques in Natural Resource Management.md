# Techniques in Natural Resource Management

## Summary
Techniques in Natural Resource Management (Zol 410) was a field class proposed as part of the Sierra Ancha summer program curriculum. It was designed as one of the immediately available courses that could accommodate 12 students in field-based instruction.
