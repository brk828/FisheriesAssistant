# Pupfish

## Summary
Pupfish (family Cyprinodontidae, genus Cyprinodon) are small-bodied, highly adaptable freshwater fishes endemic to isolated desert springs, streams, and relict water systems, particularly in the southwestern United States and Mexico. These euryhaline and eurythermal species are of significant conservation concern due to their restricted ranges and vulnerability to habitat loss, making them important subjects for understanding desert aquatic ecology and extinction risk in fragmented ecosystems. Pupfish represent critical focal species for freshwater conservation biology, as their survival often depends on protecting isolated spring habitats and implementing specialized captive breeding and reintroduction programs.
