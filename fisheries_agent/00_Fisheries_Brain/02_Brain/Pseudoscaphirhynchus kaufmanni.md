# Pseudoscaphirhynchus kaufmanni

## Summary
Pseudoscaphirhynchus kaufmanni is one of three Acipenseridae species found in Afghanistan's Amu Darya basin. This source notes that the genus Pseudoscaphirhynchus, to which it belongs, is endemic to the Amu Darya system.
