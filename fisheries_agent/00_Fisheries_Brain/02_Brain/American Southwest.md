# American Southwest

## Summary
The American Southwest, in the context of fish science and aquatic ecology, refers to the arid and semi-arid region of the western United States—particularly Arizona and surrounding states—characterized by unique freshwater and desert aquatic ecosystems including the Colorado River system and its tributaries. This region is of critical importance to ichthyology and conservation biology due to its endemic fish species, extreme environmental constraints, and significant management challenges related to water scarcity, dam operations, and habitat fragmentation. Research in this region often focuses on the conservation genetics and population dynamics of threatened native fishes adapted to these specialized desert aquatic environments.
