# White Perch

## Summary
White Perch (*Morone americana*) is a panfish species that occupies an important ecological and management niche in North American warm-water fisheries, serving as both a prey species for apex predators and a direct harvest target for recreational anglers. In the context of mid-20th century fisheries management, white perch populations were particularly vulnerable to stunting and recruitment overshoot when predator populations were inadequate, requiring active management interventions such as selective removal to maintain sustainable populations with sufficient numbers of harvestable-sized individuals above legal minimum lengths.
