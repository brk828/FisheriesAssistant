# Bureau Of Indian Affairs

## Summary
The Bureau of Indian Affairs, in the context of fish science and aquatic conservation, functions as a federal agency responsible for managing natural resources on Native American tribal lands, including fisheries and aquatic ecosystems. As evidenced in interagency documents addressing endangered species management in the Southwest, the BIA serves as a key stakeholder and implementing partner in collaborative conservation strategies that balance tribal sovereignty with federal environmental regulations and species protection objectives. The agency's involvement in fisheries management reflects its dual mandate to support tribal interests while ensuring sustainable aquatic resource management across reservation watersheds and associated waters.
