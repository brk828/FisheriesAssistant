# Grand Lake

## Summary
Grand Lake is a natural lake connected to Shadow Mountain Reservoir via a channel, maintaining clearer water than the reservoir with Secchi disc readings up to 18 feet and zooplankton counts of 1.9-37.5 organisms per liter. In 1953, it yielded 185 trout in 590 hours (0.31 catch-per-man-hour, down from 0.58 in 1952), with Rainbow Trout comprising 90% of catches averaging 11.1 inches. The declining fishing success was attributed to poor fry survival and growth, leading to recommendations to switch from fry stocking to creel-size fish.
