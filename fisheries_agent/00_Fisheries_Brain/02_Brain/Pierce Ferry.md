# Pierce Ferry

## Summary
Pierce Ferry is a location on the Colorado River between Glen Canyon Dam and Lake Mead where water temperature measurements were taken. The study found that by the time cold water released from Glen Canyon Dam reaches Pierce Ferry in summer, it has warmed to only 15-16°C, which is still nearly 10°C colder than pre-1963 spring and summer temperatures.
