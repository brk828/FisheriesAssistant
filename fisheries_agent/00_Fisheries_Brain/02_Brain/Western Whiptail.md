# Western Whiptail

## Summary
The Western Whiptail (Cnemidophorus tigris) is a lizard species native to desert environments. This biological assessment confirmed its presence at Papago Park Military Reservation in Phoenix, Arizona, as one of eight reptile species identified during surveys conducted in July 1994.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
