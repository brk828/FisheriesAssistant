# Gila River Research Natural Area

## Summary
The Gila River Research Natural Area is a U.S. Forest Service designation that covers a section of the upper Gila River in New Mexico where spikedace populations persist. While this designation provides some use restrictions that help protect habitat, the source notes that the area is still affected by watershed uses and water diversion activities.
