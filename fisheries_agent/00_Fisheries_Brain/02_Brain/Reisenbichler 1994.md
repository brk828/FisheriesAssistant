# Reisenbichler 1994

## Summary
Reisenbichler 1994 is a scientific reference cited regarding the genetic impacts of hatchery fish on wild populations. The work documents how domestication and adaptation to hatchery conditions reduces fitness, and when hatchery fish interbreed with wild fish, the fitness of wild populations may be reduced.
