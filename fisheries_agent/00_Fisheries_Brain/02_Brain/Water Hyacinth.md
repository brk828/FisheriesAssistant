# Water Hyacinth

## Summary
Water hyacinth (*Eichhornia crassipes*) is a prolific floating aquatic macrophyte that plays a complex and often contradictory role in fish production systems, simultaneously providing structural habitat for invertebrate prey organisms while potentially competing with fish for nutrients and light in fertilized aquatic environments. In fisheries management and aquaculture contexts, water hyacinth coverage must be carefully controlled, as unmanaged proliferation can reduce the efficiency of sport fish production despite offering favorable microhabitat structure for forage organisms in nutrient-poor conditions. Its effects on fish productivity are strongly dependent on nutrient status, pond management practices, and target species, making it an important variable in pond ecosystem dynamics and an occasional target for biological control using herbivorous fish like grass carp.
