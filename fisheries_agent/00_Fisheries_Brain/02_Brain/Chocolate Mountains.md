# Chocolate Mountains

## Summary
The Chocolate Mountains are a mountain range in southeastern California whose western bajada (alluvial slope) borders the Coachella Canal study area. The canal runs along the Imperial East Mesa adjacent to these mountains in the Colorado Desert region.
