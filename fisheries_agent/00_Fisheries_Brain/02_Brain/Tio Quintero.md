# Tio Quintero

## Summary
Tio Quintero is a location in the Cuatro Cienegas basin of Mexico where unconfirmed reports (without specimens) indicated potential presence of introduced Tilapia as of 1988. This site was described as an isolated spring system, which would make containment of invasive species more feasible if the reports were confirmed.
