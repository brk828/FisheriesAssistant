# Orange-Spotted Sunfish

## Summary
The Orange-Spotted Sunfish (Lepomis humilis) is a small, colorful North American centrarchid commonly found in clear, vegetated freshwater systems including lakes, impoundments, and creeks. This species is characteristic of habitats with abundant aquatic macrophytes such as Chara and emergent vegetation, which provide essential cover and spawning substrate in shallow water zones. As a forage fish and secondary predator, the Orange-Spotted Sunfish plays an important role in freshwater food webs and serves as an indicator species for assessing the ecological health and vegetation complexity of small impoundments and stream systems.
