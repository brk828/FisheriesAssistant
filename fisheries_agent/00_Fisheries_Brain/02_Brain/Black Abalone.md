# Black Abalone

## Summary
Black Abalone (Haliotis cracherodii) is a warm-water mollusk species that serves as a paleoclimatic indicator in archaeological contexts. Later middens on Santa Rosa Island show a shift from cold-water Red Abalone to warm-water Black Abalone, documenting postglacial warming along the California coast following the persistence of cold conditions approximately 7,000 years ago.
