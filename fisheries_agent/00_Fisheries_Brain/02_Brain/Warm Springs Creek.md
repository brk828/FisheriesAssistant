# Warm Springs Creek

## Summary
Warm Springs Creek is a spring-fed tributary that provides groundwater input to the lower Yampa Canyon spawning area of Colorado squawfish (River Mile 0-20 of the Yampa River). This creek is cited as potential evidence for the Groundwater Seepage Hypothesis, as its distinctive chemical signature from groundwater seepage may serve as an olfactory cue that allows spawning fish to home to this location.
