# Yuma Desalting Plant

## Summary
The Yuma Desalting Plant is a facility authorized under the Colorado River Salinity Control Act of 1974 to help meet water quality obligations to Mexico under Minute 242. The Bureau of Reclamation is considering alternatives and may have discretion regarding the plant's operation if suitable alternatives are identified.
