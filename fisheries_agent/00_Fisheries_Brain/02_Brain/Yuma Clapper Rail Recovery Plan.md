# Yuma Clapper Rail Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Yuma Clapper Rail Recovery Plan was published in February 1983 for the threatened subspecies Rallus longirostris yumanensis, which inhabits the Lower Colorado River Basin. According to this source, the recovery plan directly ties the recovery of the Yuma clapper rail to the operation of Bureau of Reclamation dams and reservoirs, indicating that modifications to these operations are necessary for the species' recovery.
