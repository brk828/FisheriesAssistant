# Blue Ridge Reservoir

## Summary
Blue Ridge Reservoir is an impoundment on East Clear Creek in Coconino County, Arizona. The designated critical habitat for Little Colorado Spinedace includes approximately 18 miles of East Clear Creek from the confluence with Leonard Canyon upstream to the Blue Ridge Reservoir dam, plus approximately 13 miles from the upper end of the reservoir upstream to Polato Lake.
