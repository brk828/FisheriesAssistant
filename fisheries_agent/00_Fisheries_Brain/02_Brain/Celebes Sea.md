# Celebes Sea

## Summary
The Celebes Sea is one of the open waters surrounding the Sulu Sea complex, connected to it by straits 350-550 meters deep. This source indicates that like other surrounding open waters, it shows continued decline in bottom temperature with increasing depth, dropping below 7°C at depths greater than 950 meters, contrasting with the Sulu Sea's constant ~10°C bottom temperature.
