# Embiotocinae

## Summary
Embiotocinae is the primitive subfamily within Embiotocidae containing three-fifths of the family's genera. This source establishes it as the most diverse group, including species with unusual ranges such as the two Japanese genera, species ranging to Puget Sound, one deep-water species, and the only freshwater representative, with most inhabiting bays, beaches, and reefs of California.

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
