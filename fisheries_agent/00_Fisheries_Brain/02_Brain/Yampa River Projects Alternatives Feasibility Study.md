# Yampa River Projects Alternatives Feasibility Study

## Summary
The Yampa River Projects Alternatives Feasibility Study is an examination of reservoir options on the Yampa River to support endangered fish recovery and water management in the Upper Colorado River Basin. As of April 1990, CH2MHill had been selected as the contractor for the study, which was 4-6 weeks behind schedule though summer fieldwork remained on track, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service agreed to provide in-kind technical review services to offset budget shortfalls.
