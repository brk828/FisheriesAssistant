# San Juan de las Boquillas y Nogales

## Summary
San Juan de las Boquillas y Nogales is a historic land grant in Arizona. The lands along the upper San Pedro River that became part of the BLM study area were historically referred to by this name, along with the San Rafael de Valle land grant.

## Context

> The [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) released a Draft San Pedro River Management Plan and Environmental Impact Statement for public review in 1988. The plan addresses 47,668 acres along a 36-mile stretch of the upper [[San Pedro River]] near [[Sierra Vista]] in [[Cochise County]], [[Arizona]]....
