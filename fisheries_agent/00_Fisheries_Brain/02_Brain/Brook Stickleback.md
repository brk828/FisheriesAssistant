# Brook Stickleback

## Summary
The Brook Stickleback (*Culaea inconstans*) is a small freshwater fish native to Canada and the upper Mississippi and Missouri River systems that has become notable in ichthyological and conservation literature primarily as a range-expansion species documented in new geographic areas outside its native distribution. This species is of interest to aquatic ecologists and fisheries scientists because its appearance in previously unrecorded regions—such as Pennsylvania's Susquehanna River Drainage and the Upper Colorado River Basin in Utah—provides evidence of fish dispersal patterns and changing biogeographic distributions, potentially influenced by human activities or environmental factors. The Brook Stickleback serves as an indicator species for monitoring changes in freshwater fish communities and understanding how native and nonnative fish assemblages interact in aquatic ecosystems.
