# Laguna Salada

## Summary
Laguna Salada is a hypersaline lake in the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin of Coahuila, Mexico, that serves as a reference system for studying extreme aquatic environments and their unique limnological properties, including anomalous thermal stratification patterns. As part of a globally significant desert wetland hotspot, Laguna Salada and its associated salt-harvesting pools support specialized aquatic communities adapted to harsh conditions and represent important habitats for endemic fish species. The system has been the focus of ecological research examining how extreme salinity, temperature dynamics, and artificial modifications influence aquatic biodiversity and ecosystem functioning in arid regions.
