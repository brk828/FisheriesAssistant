# Big Portage Lake

## Summary
Big Portage Lake is a 360-acre warm-water lake in Jackson County, Michigan. It was one of four lakes opened to year-round fishing for bluegills and sunfish, with a five-year catch composition of 69.8% bluegill, 9.6% yellow perch, 6.0% rock bass, and 5.0% largemouth bass, and seining surveys showed stable young-of-year abundance between 1946 and 1950.
