# Carnegie Botanical Laboratory

## Summary
The Carnegie Botanical Laboratory was a research institution directed by Dr. D.T. MacDougal at the time of the Lucania browni collection in 1907. The director was present during the field work that documented the extreme environmental conditions of the hot spring habitat.

## Context

> This 1907 publication by [[David Starr Jordan]] and [[Robert Earl Richardson]] describes a new species of killifish, *[[Lucania browni]]*, collected from a hot spring in northeastern [[Lower California]] (Baja California).
