# Sierra Club

## Summary
The Sierra Club is a major environmental advocacy organization that engages in aquatic conservation efforts, including collaborative initiatives to protect and restore native fish species and their habitats in critical ecosystems such as the Colorado River and its delta. Within the context of fish and aquatic ecology, the organization works alongside other conservation groups to develop and promote comprehensive restoration plans aimed at addressing habitat degradation and species decline in freshwater systems. As a prominent voice in environmental policy and litigation, the Sierra Club contributes scientific and legal expertise to protect imperiled aquatic species and their ecosystems from anthropogenic threats.
