# Paguristes anahuacus

## Summary
Paguristes anahuacus is a hermit crab species (anomuran) documented at Puerto Peñasco. The source note indicates that anomuran identifications were made by Clinton Westervelt consulting with Dr. Janet Haig, but detailed species information is cut off.
