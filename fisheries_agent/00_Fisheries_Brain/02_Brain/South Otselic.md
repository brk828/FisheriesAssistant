# South Otselic

## Summary
South Otselic is the location of a bass hatchery in New York State that developed specialized culture systems for raising Smallmouth Bass. The facility featured multiple Daphnia (microscopic crustacean) ponds connected to main rearing ponds via valves, allowing timed release of live food to feed bass fry during their initial feeding stages when they require live prey rather than artificial food.
