# Cladophora Glomerata

## Summary
Cladophora glomerata is a filamentous green alga commonly found in freshwater ecosystems, particularly in clearwater reaches of rivers below dams where it forms dense mats on substrates. In aquatic ecology and fisheries science, this alga is significant as a primary producer that supports complex food webs by providing habitat and food resources for macroinvertebrate grazers such as oligochaetes, chironomids, and amphipods, which in turn support fish populations. Its abundance in regulated river systems serves as an important biological indicator of water clarity and nutrient conditions, making it relevant for understanding lower trophic level dynamics and assessing the ecological effects of dam operations on downstream aquatic communities.
