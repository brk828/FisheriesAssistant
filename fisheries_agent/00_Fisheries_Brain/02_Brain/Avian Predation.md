# Avian Predation

## Summary
Avian predation was identified as a major mortality source for stocked bonytail, with 7 acoustic tags and 14 PIT tags recovered under Double-Crested Cormorant roosting sites, 3 radio tags found on land, and direct observations of Great Blue Heron predation. Trail cameras documented increased bird presence immediately after stocking events, and estimated daily consumption rates suggest birds could consume 30% of stocked fish within one month.

> This multi-year study (2013-2016) documented post-stocking survival, distribution, and habitat use of endangered [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) in [[Lake Havasu]], [[Colorado River]].
