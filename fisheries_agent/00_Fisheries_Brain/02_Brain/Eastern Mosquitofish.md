# Eastern Mosquitofish

## Summary
Eastern Mosquitofish (Gambusia holbrooki) is an introduced species from the family Poeciliidae that was artificially introduced worldwide for mosquito control. This laboratory study found that mosquitofish performed over 90-95% of aggressive acts (chases and nips) toward native Iberian toothcarps, significantly reduced feeding rates in Valencia Toothcarp, disrupted reproductive behaviors, and preyed on small juveniles of Spanish Toothcarp, with effects varying by temperature, fish size, and food availability.
