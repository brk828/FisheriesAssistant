# Elkhorn River

## Summary
The Elkhorn River is a tributary in the Platte River Basin where it meets the Missouri River. According to this source, the pallid sturgeon, one of the federally listed species addressed by the Central Platte River Recovery Program, occupies the Lower Platte River between the Elkhorn River confluence and the Missouri River confluence.
