# Bog Hole

## Summary
A bog hole is a small, isolated water body or pool, typically found in desert or arid riparian ecosystems, that serves as critical habitat for endemic or native fish species and other aquatic organisms. These features function as refugial habitats during periods of low water availability and are particularly important for conservation of threatened or endangered fish populations in fragmented aquatic systems. Bog holes require active management and restoration efforts to maintain suitable conditions for native fish communities and prevent degradation or loss of these ecologically significant microhabitats.
