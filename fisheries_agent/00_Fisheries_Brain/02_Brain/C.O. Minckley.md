# C.O. Minckley

## Summary
C.O. Minckley was a prominent agency biologist who served on the Lower Basin Recovery Implementation Scientific Workgroup, a collaborative group of ichthyologists and resource managers dedicated to fish conservation and recovery efforts in the Colorado River basin. As a member of this influential scientific workgroup alongside academic experts, Minckley contributed to recovery planning and implementation strategies for native fish species in the lower Colorado River system during the 1990s.
