# Abundance Target

## Summary
Abundance targets are quantitative benchmarks used to assess Northern Pike suppression effectiveness in Box Canyon Reservoir, measured as catch per unit effort (CPUE) in pike per net-night. The 2022 targets were ≤0.50 pike/net-night for mainstem north of Riverbend (achieved: 0.10) and ≤1.73 pike/net-night for sloughs and southern mainstem (achieved: 0.26), with both areas meeting management goals.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary:** [[Northern Pike]] (*Esox lucius*) is an invasive predatory fish in [[Box Canyon Reservoir]].
