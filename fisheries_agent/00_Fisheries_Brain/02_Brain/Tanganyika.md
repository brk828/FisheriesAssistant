# Tanganyika

## Summary
Tanganyika is the territory where elvers of Anguilla nebulosa labiata were discovered in Mzinga Creek near Dar-es-Salaam in January-February 1956. This discovery provided evidence that the species exhibits catadromous behavior, with adults descending to the sea to breed.
