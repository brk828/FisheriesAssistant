# Fish And Wildlife Service

## Summary
The Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) is a federal agency responsible for managing and conserving fish species and their aquatic habitats, particularly through implementation of the Endangered Species Act and development of recovery plans for threatened and endangered fish populations. As demonstrated in the source documents, the FWS works collaboratively with other agencies, forest services, and research teams to protect native fish species through biological assessments, regulatory decisions, and coordinated conservation efforts in aquatic ecosystems. The agency plays a critical role in determining whether water management projects and other human activities will jeopardize the survival of listed fish species and in establishing science-based recovery strategies for species conservation.
