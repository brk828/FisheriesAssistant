# Gadiculus argenteus thori

## Summary
Gadiculus argenteus thori (silvery pout) is a small gadid species examined in this study, with specimens measuring 131-149 mm body length and brain lengths of 6.2-7.1 mm. This species represents one of the smaller members of the family included in the comparative neuroanatomical analysis.

## Context

> This 1953 study by A.N. Svetovidov provides a comprehensive comparative analysis of brain structure across the family [[Gadidae]] (codfishes), examining representatives from three subfamilies: [[Lotinae]], [[Merlucciinae]], and [[Gadinae]].
