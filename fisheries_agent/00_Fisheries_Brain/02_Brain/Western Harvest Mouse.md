# Western Harvest Mouse

## Summary
The Western Harvest Mouse (Reithrodontomys megalotis) is a small rodent that was one of seven species newly documented for the Aravaipa Creek watershed during this survey. This discovery helped fill distribution gaps and contributed to the documentation of 60 mammal species in the watershed.

## Context

> This is the fourth annual progress report (1979-1982) on a comprehensive mammal survey of the [[George Whittell Wildlife Preserve]] in the [[Aravaipa Creek]] watershed, Arizona.
