# Salt River Basin

## Summary
The Salt River Basin is a watershed in Arizona that includes the Black River. In 1945, Phelps Dodge Corporation constructed a diversion from the Black River in the Salt River Basin into Willow Creek, a tributary of middle Eagle Creek, which augments flow in Eagle Creek below Willow Creek by approximately 27 percent.
