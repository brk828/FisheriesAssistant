# Allenson and Noble

## Summary
Allenson and Noble were researchers who in 1964 reported the upper lethal temperature for Tilapia mossambica as 36°C (100.8°F). Their findings contributed to understanding the temperature tolerance limits of the species.

## Context

> This 1966 progress report documents the [[California Department of Fish and Game]]'s experimental culture of all-male [[Tilapia mossambica]] hybrids produced by crossing Zanzibari males with Javanese females.
