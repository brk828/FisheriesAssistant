# Bubbling Ponds State Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Bubbling Ponds State Fish Hatchery is a state-operated fish culture facility identified in this 1994 management plan as having potential to assist Dexter National Fish Hatchery as an additional culture facility for endangered Colorado River big-river fishes. The plan recognizes it as a possible partner facility to support captive propagation efforts for Colorado squawfish, razorback sucker, bonytail, and humpback chub, though specific operational details or commitments are not provided in this preliminary draft.
