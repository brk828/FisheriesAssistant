# Black Draw-Border

## Summary
Black Draw-Border is a non-thermal stream reach on San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge where water temperatures fluctuated seasonally from 11°C in winter to 22°C in summer. At this site, the proportion of Yaqui topminnows declined significantly over time (approximately 2% per month) until none were collected in July 1987, but following monsoonal flash flooding in August 1987 (producing ~1.5 m flood depth), topminnow proportions increased dramatically to >50% in September-October 1987. The site had lower alkalinity (4.57 meq/L) and conductivity (579 μS/cm) than the springhead upstream.
