# Federal Aid in Fisheries Restoration Act

## Summary
The Federal Aid in Fisheries Restoration Act was a federal program that funded state fisheries management activities. This report was prepared under Project F-3-R-10 of this Act to document fisheries problems in Texas Region 3-B during 1962-1963.

## Context

> This segment completion report documents fisheries problems identified in Texas Region 3-B during February 1, 1962 - January 31, 1963 under Federal Aid Project F-3-R-10.
