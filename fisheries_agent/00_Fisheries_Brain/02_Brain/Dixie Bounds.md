# Dixie Bounds

## Summary
Dixie Bounds was a graduate student who conducted reptile and small mammal trapping research at Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. During the first two weeks of spring trapping in 1994, she doubled the number of lizards caught, marked, and released compared to previous periods, indicating seasonal increases in activity.
