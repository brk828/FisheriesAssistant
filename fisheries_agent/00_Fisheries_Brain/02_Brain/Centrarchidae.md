# Centrarchidae

## Summary
Centrarchidae is a family of North American freshwater fishes commonly known as sunfishes and black basses, characterized by their small to medium size, compressed body shape, and prominent dorsal spines. Members of this family, including genera such as Lepomis (sunfishes), Micropterus (black basses), and Archoplites (Sacramento perch), play important ecological roles as both predators and prey in freshwater ecosystems, though they have also become invasive species when introduced outside their native ranges. The family is frequently studied in conservation biology and aquatic ecology research due to their ecological impacts, use as indicator species for environmental health, and susceptibility to various physiological anomalies related to water quality and pollution.
