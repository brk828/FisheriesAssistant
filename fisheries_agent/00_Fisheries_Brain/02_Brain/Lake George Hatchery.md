# Lake George Hatchery

## Summary
Lake George Hatchery is one of six New York State fish hatchery facilities that participated in this feeding trial experiment. Notably, when the experiment was extended into May at this facility, the all-pellet lot experienced rapidly rising mortality on May 11, which immediately halted when fish were switched to a meat diet, suggesting a temporal limit for continuous pellet feeding.
