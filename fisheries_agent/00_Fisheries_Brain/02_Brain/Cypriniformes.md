# Cypriniformes

## Summary
Cypriniformes is an order of freshwater fishes that includes cyprinids (minnows, carps, and relatives) and represents one of the most diverse and economically important groups of fishes globally. Members of this order are characterized by specific anatomical features and are frequently subjects of taxonomic study, conservation concern, and aquaculture research due to their ecological significance and susceptibility to environmental changes. Type specimens and morphological analysis remain critical tools for resolving taxonomic questions and clarifying species identity within this diverse group.
