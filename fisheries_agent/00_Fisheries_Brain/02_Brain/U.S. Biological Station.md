# U.S. Biological Station

## Summary
The U.S. Biological Station at Fairport, Iowa was a federal facility where artificial propagation and pond culture operations for buffalo fish were conducted. This station successfully reared both Ictiobus bubalus and Ictiobus cyprinella artificially and documented growth rates and spawning protocols that formed the basis of this 1922 guidance.
