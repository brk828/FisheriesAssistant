# University of California Santa Barbara

## Summary
The University of California Santa Barbara was one of two institutions where this trout immunogenetics research was conducted from July through November 1962. Researcher John E. Cushing was affiliated with this institution during the study.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
