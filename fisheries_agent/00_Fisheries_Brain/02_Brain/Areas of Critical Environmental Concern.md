# Areas of Critical Environmental Concern

## Summary
Areas of Critical Environmental Concern (ACECs) are special management designations within the California Desert Conservation Plan that identify locations with particularly significant ecological, cultural, or scenic values. These areas require special management attention and are shown as distinct zones on the planning map alongside the standard multiple-use classifications.
