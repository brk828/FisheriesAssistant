# Perch

## Summary
In ichthyological research, perch refers to members of the family Percidae, freshwater fish commonly used as model organisms in experimental studies of fish behavior, ecology, and physiology in temperate lake environments. As benthic or demersal inhabitants, perch occupy the lake bottom and lower water column, making them valuable subjects for investigating feeding ecology, activity cycles, and responses to environmental variables such as temperature in controlled field and laboratory settings. Their prevalence in European lakes like Windermere and their ecological importance as both predators and prey in freshwater food webs make perch significant indicators of aquatic ecosystem health and function.
