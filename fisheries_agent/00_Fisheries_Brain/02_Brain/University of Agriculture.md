# University of Agriculture

## Summary
The University of Agriculture in Brno operated multiple research units including the Chair of Fisheries and Hydrobiology and the Biological Station at Lednice. Research focused on pond aquaculture, particularly carp production optimization through nitrogen fertilization and studies of coregonid culture.
