# Deer Mouse

## Summary
Deer Mouse was monitored for DDT residues following forest application at Knox Mountain, California in 1964. This species showed low persistent contamination of only 0.08 ppm at 3 months post-treatment, much lower than insectivorous bird species in the same environment.
