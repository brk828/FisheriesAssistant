# Ivel River

## Summary
The Ivel River in England was used as a comparative system in the Blue River study. It had annual primary production of 3.5 kg/m²/year with a P/R ratio of 1.13, which was substantially lower than the Blue River's granite reach productivity of 7.8 kg O₂/m²/year.
