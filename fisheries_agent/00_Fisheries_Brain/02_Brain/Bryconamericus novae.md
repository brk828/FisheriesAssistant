# Bryconamericus novae

## Summary
Bryconamericus novae is a new species of characid fish described from below Cachoeira da Velha waterfall near Piabana in the Rio Novo (Rio Tocantins basin) of Brazil. It is allied to Bryconamericus hyphessus but differs in being less elongate with a more arched dorsal profile and having an additional scale row below the lateral line, and shows variable premaxillary tooth counts (three to six conically tricuspid teeth in outer series) with possible asymmetry between sides.
