# Tetrazolium Oxidase

## Summary
Tetrazolium Oxidase is an enzyme examined at 1 genetic locus that showed one invariant band across all specimens. The Cuatro Cienegas endemic forms displayed faster mobility than the Rio Grande C. cyanoguttatum population.
