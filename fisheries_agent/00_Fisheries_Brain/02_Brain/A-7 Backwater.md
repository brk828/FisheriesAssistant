# A-7 Backwater

## Summary
A-7 Backwater zone encompasses river miles 124-109 (24.4 km) including four named backwaters with open connections to the main channel in the Lower Colorado River. This source found that razorback suckers stocked in A-7 showed rapid dispersal and consistently low CPUE (0.76 fish/1,000 sec electrofishing), with 50% of sonic-tagged fish dispersing within 50 days, all 12 telemetry fish dying or disappearing within 70 days, and only 15 fish captured after ≥150 days at large representing <0.01% of releases.
