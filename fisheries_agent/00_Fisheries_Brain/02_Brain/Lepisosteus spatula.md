# Lepisosteus spatula

## Summary
Lepisosteus spatula (Alligator Gar) was not collected during the survey but was documented through a photographed specimen of 165 pounds taken in January 1957. A 160-pound specimen was also reported from 1953, confirming the species' presence in the Verdigris River system.

## Context
