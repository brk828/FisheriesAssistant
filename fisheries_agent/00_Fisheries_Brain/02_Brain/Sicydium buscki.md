# Sicydium buscki

## Summary
Sicydium buscki is a congener species described by Evermann and Clark that is closely related to Sicydium montanum. The new species S. montanum is distinguished from S. buscki by its larger scale counts and distinctive morphological and coloration differences.

## Context

> This 1920 taxonomic description by Carl L. Hubbs establishes [[Sicydium montanum]] as a new species of goby collected from a mountain brook in [[Macuto]], [[Caracas]], [[Venezuela]].
