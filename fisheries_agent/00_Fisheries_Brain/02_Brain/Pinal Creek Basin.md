# Pinal Creek Basin

## Summary
The Pinal Creek Basin is a drainage basin in eastern Arizona that has experienced intensive copper mining and milling operations since the early 1900s. This study found significant groundwater degradation over 40+ years, with total dissolved solids at Pringle Well Field increasing from 800 mg/L (1942) to 2,500-2,900 mg/L (1982-1983), and identified multiple contamination plumes originating from process solution losses, heap-leach operations, and historical tailings facilities.
