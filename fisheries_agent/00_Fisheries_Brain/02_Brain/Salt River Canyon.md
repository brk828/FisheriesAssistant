# Salt River Canyon

## Summary
Salt River Canyon refers to canyon-bound reaches of the Salt River system in Arizona that historically offered the largest and most persistent populations of native fishes remaining in the state. This assessment identified these undammed canyon sections as areas where native fish populations were most stable and important, and expressed concern that removal of Roosevelt Diversion Dam could allow large non-native fish from Roosevelt Lake to invade these critical native fish refugia.
