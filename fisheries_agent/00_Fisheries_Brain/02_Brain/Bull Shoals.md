# Bull Shoals

## Summary
Bull Shoals is a reservoir used as a comparative reference for plankton productivity in the Lake Powell smelt introduction proposal. Its April plankton abundance of 24,600 organisms per cubic meter is cited alongside other water bodies to demonstrate that Lake Powell's plankton density of 17,600/m³ is adequate to support a smelt population.

## Context

> This 1987 proposal to the [[Colorado River Wildlife Council]] recommends introducing [[Rainbow Smelt]] (*Osmerus mordax*) into [[Lake Powell]] to address forage deficiencies for game fish populations.
