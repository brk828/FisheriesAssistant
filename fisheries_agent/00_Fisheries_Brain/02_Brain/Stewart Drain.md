# Stewart Drain

## Summary
Stewart Drain (also called Stewart Lake, located at approximately RMI 299.2-299.1) is a key nursery habitat in the Jensen Reach where 82% of razorback sucker larval captures in that reach occurred. It is recommended as one of the primary sites for concentrated larval monitoring efforts using light traps.
