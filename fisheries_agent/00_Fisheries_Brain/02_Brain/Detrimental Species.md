# Detrimental Species

## Summary
Northern pike are listed as a "detrimental animal" under California Code of Regulations Title 14 Section 671 in California. This designation makes it unlawful to import, transport, or possess northern pike alive except under permit, reflecting their status as a non-native predator that poses severe threats to native and established fisheries.
