# Seasonal Movement

## Summary
Seasonal movement refers to changes in fish activity and displacement patterns across different times of year. This source documented that Razorback Sucker total distance moved was highest in spring (median 94 km), intermediate in summer (50 km), and lowest in autumn (2 km) and winter (4 km), with fish detected at the upstream Piute Farms Waterfall in all seasons except winter.
