# Alluvial Valley

## Summary
Alluvial valley reaches historically featured braided channels, sandbars, backwaters, and oxbow lakes that native fishes characterized under pristine conditions. These valley reaches served as important nursery habitats, but many have been inundated by reservoirs, contributing to the loss of critical habitat for species like Colorado squawfish whose juveniles concentrated in valley embayments.
