# Lampreys

## Summary
Lampreys are jawless fish (agnathans) belonging to the class Petromyzontida, characterized by their distinctive circular mouths lined with keratinous teeth and parasitic feeding behavior in many species. In ichthyology and conservation biology, lampreys are significant subjects of study due to their ecological impacts as both predators on commercially important fish species and as indicators of freshwater ecosystem health, with particular attention paid to invasive species like the sea lamprey (Petromyzon marinus) that threaten native fish populations. Taxonomic classification of lampreys has historically been contentious among specialists, reflecting ongoing debates about their systematic relationships and proper categorization within fish phylogeny.
