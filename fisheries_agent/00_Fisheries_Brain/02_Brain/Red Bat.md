# Red Bat

## Summary
The Red Bat (Lasiurus borealis) is a bat species that was newly documented for the Aravaipa Creek watershed during this 1979-1982 survey. It was one of ten bat species documented in the study area, contributing to the overall mammal diversity of 60 species recorded for the watershed.
