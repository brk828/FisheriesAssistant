# Lake Berryessa

## Summary
Lake Berryessa is a reservoir in California that became a popular black bass fishing destination. The source notes it as one of the newer waters that contributed to considerable shifts in county rankings for black bass fishing between 1948 and 1960, reflecting the increasing importance of low-elevation reservoirs.
