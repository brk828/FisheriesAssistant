# Lionurus decimalis

## Summary
Lionurus decimalis is a deep-sea macrourid fish species endemic to the Philippine Subregion. This source identifies it as one of the distinctive macrourid forms found in the Sulu Sea complex that distinguishes this region from the broader East Indian fauna.
