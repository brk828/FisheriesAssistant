# Loachminnow

## Summary
Loachminnow (*Tiaroga cobitis*) is a small native cyprinid fish endemic to the Gila River Basin of Arizona and New Mexico that is federally listed as threatened under the Endangered Species Act. This species is particularly vulnerable to hydrological alterations, habitat fragmentation, and competition from non-native fishes, making it a key focal species for conservation monitoring and management in southwestern aquatic ecosystems. The loachminnow's presence in Arizona streams serves as an indicator of intact riparian habitat conditions and is central to fisheries management planning and biological opinion requirements for water development projects affecting the Gila River Basin.
