# Great Slave Lake

## Summary
Great Slave Lake is a deep lake in Canada (maximum depth 614 meters) where Mysis relicta has been found at all depths, including shallow waters less than 10 meters where low light intensity prevails. Studies by Larkin (1948) on Great Slave Lake documented the distribution patterns and vertical migration behavior of opossum shrimp, which informed the collection and release strategies used in the California and Nevada introduction program.
