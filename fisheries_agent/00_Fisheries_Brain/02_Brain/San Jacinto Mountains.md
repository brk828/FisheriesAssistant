# San Jacinto Mountains

## Summary
The San Jacinto Mountains were the subject of life-zone studies conducted by Joseph Grinnell in 1913 as part of the University of California's work applying and extending Merriam's life-zone concept. These studies were among the important subsequent applications of the life-zone framework developed in the Southwest.
