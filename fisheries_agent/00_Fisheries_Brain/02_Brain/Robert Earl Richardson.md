# Robert Earl Richardson

## Summary
Robert Earl Richardson was a co-author with David Starr Jordan of the 1907 description of Lucania browni. He collaborated in describing this new killifish species from a hot spring in Lower California.

## Context

> This 1907 publication by [[David Starr Jordan]] and [[Robert Earl Richardson]] describes a new species of killifish, *[[Lucania browni]]*, collected from a hot spring in northeastern [[Lower California]] (Baja California).
