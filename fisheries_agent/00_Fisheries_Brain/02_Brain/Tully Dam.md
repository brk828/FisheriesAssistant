# Tully Dam

## Summary
Tully Dam is a U.S. Corps of Engineers flood control reservoir downstream of Lawrence Brook in Massachusetts. This source reports that during the August 1955 rotenone treatment, the Corps closed the gate at this dam to control downstream dispersal of the chemical, though a tropical hurricane caused severe flooding the following day.
