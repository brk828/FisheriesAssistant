# White River Desert Sucker

## Summary
The White River Desert Sucker (Catostomus intermedius) was one of six native fish species historically found in Pahranagat Valley. This source reports that this species went extinct by the 1960s, part of the dramatic declines experienced by all native fishes in the valley.
