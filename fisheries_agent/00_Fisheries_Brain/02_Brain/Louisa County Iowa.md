# Louisa County Iowa

## Summary
Louisa County Iowa is the site of a levee district acquisition project that served as a model for floodplain habitat restoration following the 1993 Great Flood. This source describes it as a prototype approach where farmers owning more than 400,000 acres expressed interest in buyouts or easements as an alternative to traditional structural flood control.
