# Ferruginous Hawk

## Summary
The Ferruginous Hawk (Buteo regalis) is a C2 candidate species that primarily inhabits grassland and prairie environments. The assessment determined it was not expected to occur at Papago Park except perhaps during migration, and no specific recommendations were proposed for this species.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
