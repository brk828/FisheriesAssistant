# Skatutakee Lake

## Summary
Skatutakee Lake in Harrisville, New Hampshire, was included in the survey. Yellow perch from Skatutakee Lake showed a low infection rate of 10.9%, suggesting the unmeasured bass population likely also had light bass tapeworm infections.
