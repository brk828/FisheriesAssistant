# Desolation Canyon

## Summary
Desolation Canyon is a significant reach of the Green River in the upper Colorado River Basin that has served as an important study site for monitoring native fish populations and documenting ecological changes following river impoundment and dam construction. This canyon section, located below Flaming Gorge Dam, represents a critical habitat area where researchers have tracked the distribution and decline of endemic large-river fish species such as Colorado squawfish and bonytail chub in response to altered flow regimes, water temperature changes, and other post-dam environmental shifts. The canyon's fish communities have been central to understanding the conservation needs of threatened and endangered native fishes in the Colorado River system.
