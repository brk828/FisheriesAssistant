# Strongylura Timucu

## Context
> - **Gonad Structure** (used to separate *Strongylura marina* from congeners): - *[[Strongylura marina]]*: Only the **right gonad** is developed (asymmetrical). - *[[Strongylura senegalensis]]* & *[[Strongylura timucu]]*: Possess the **usual paired gonads** (symmetrical).
