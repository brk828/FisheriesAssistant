# Black Drum

## Summary
Black Drum (*Pogonias cromis*) is a large sciaenid fish species commonly found in estuarine and coastal environments, particularly along the Gulf Coast and Atlantic regions. As a member of the drum family, it represents an important component of estuarine fish assemblages and is notable for its ecological role in these productive coastal habitats where it coexists with other commercially and recreationally significant species like red drum and speckled trout. The species' presence in mid-20th century fisheries surveys and ecological studies reflects its established status as both a native estuarine inhabitant and a species of interest for fisheries management and conservation monitoring.
