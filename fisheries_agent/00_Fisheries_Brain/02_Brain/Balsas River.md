# Balsas River

## Summary
The Balsas River is one of several rivers that have cut back into the edges of the Mesa Central plateau. This source notes that Goodeidae occur in the headwaters of the Balsas River, representing part of the broader distribution pattern of these endemic viviparous fishes.
