# Shoshone Falls

## Summary
Shoshone Falls is a 65-meter-high waterfall located on the Snake River in southcentral Idaho. This natural barrier historically isolated Yellowstone Cutthroat Trout above the falls, preventing their dispersal downstream and contributing to their evolutionary isolation in the upper Snake River drainage.
