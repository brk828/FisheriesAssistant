# Laurel Lakes

## Summary
Laurel Lakes is a location in Mono County, California, that served as a collection site for Golden Trout specimens used in this study. Golden Trout from Laurel Lakes showed distinct antigenic characteristics compared to populations from Cottonwood Lakes, and blood from Laurel Lakes fish (specimen Laurel-27) was used to create immune sera for testing.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
