# San Francisco Striped Bass Club

## Summary
The San Francisco Striped Bass Club was an angling organization that participated in the striped bass tagging study by conducting publicity programs to encourage tag returns from recreational fishermen. The club offered prizes including a $100 yearly prize and other awards to anglers who reported tagged fish, demonstrating the importance of angler cooperation in fisheries research.
