# Atlantic Menhaden

## Summary
Atlantic Menhaden (Brevoortia tyrannus) is one of the prey fish species found in striped bass stomachs during the field diet study conducted in mid-Chesapeake Bay and tributaries from January 1990 to March 1992. It was included in the analysis of 788 prey items used to determine that wild striped bass select prey with a modal prey-to-predator ratio of 0.12, matching laboratory-predicted optimal sizes.
