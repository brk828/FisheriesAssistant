# Sciaenidae

## Summary
Sciaenidae is a diverse family of primarily marine and estuarine teleost fishes commonly known as drums and croakers, characterized by the possession of specialized sonic muscles that produce audible sounds for communication and prey detection. This family is ecologically and economically significant in coastal fisheries throughout the Western Hemisphere and beyond, with members ranging from small estuarine residents to commercially important species. In the context of the Eastern United States, Sciaenidae species such as spot, croaker, seatrout, and weakfish are frequently encountered in anomaly surveys as indicators of environmental stress and water quality degradation in marine and estuarine ecosystems.
