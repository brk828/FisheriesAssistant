# Dalapon

## Summary
Dalapon is one of twelve herbicide active ingredients reviewed in this report for aquatic plant control in fishery management. It was evaluated for toxicity data and safety precautions for use in freshwater systems.

## Context

> This 1961 special report by Charles L. Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
