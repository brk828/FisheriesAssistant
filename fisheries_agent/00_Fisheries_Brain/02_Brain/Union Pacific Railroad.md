# Union Pacific Railroad

## Summary
The Union Pacific Railroad formerly operated through Condor Canyon, and its abandoned right-of-way now creates road access to the Big Spring Spinedace habitat. This access increases human impacts and the risk of nonnative species introductions, with recovery actions recommending that vehicle use be limited to the railroad bed to minimize habitat disturbance.
