# Clinocottus

## Summary
Clinocottus analis (wooly sculpin) was a species used to test long-term effects of anesthesia in this study. Twenty-nine individuals collected with methylparafynol showed normal weight gain (9.3% over 21 days) and reproductive function, with fish depositing eggs in the aquarium, demonstrating no adverse effects from anesthetic exposure.
