# Ricinus communis

## Summary
Ricinus communis (castor bean) is a plant species with extremely lipid-rich seeds. This source reports that R. communis seeds have a calorific value of 6834.7 cal/g, representing one of the highest values among plant materials due to their exceptional lipid content.
