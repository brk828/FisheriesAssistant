# Woodrow Canyon

## Summary
Woodrow Canyon is a tributary in the Mogollon Creek drainage that was reclaimed for Gila Trout in 1988 as part of recovery efforts. It served as one of the temporary holding areas for Gila Trout during the 1993 Mogollon Creek antimycin treatment, with the restoration area extending from 5 miles east of Woodrow Canyon westward.
