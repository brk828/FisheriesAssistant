# Fine-scaled Sucker

## Summary
Fine-scaled Sucker is the most widely distributed sucker species throughout Kansas, reaching over 12 inches and considered a favored food fish in some localities. It prefers clear cool water, feeds on small clams and snails with crushing teeth, and is essentially a small clear stream species that has adapted to wide Kansas conditions, with both eastern and western varieties existing.
