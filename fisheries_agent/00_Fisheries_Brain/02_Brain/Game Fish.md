# Game Fish

## Summary
Game fish are fish species of recreational or commercial importance that are actively managed and harvested by humans, typically characterized by their desirability for sport fishing or food value. In fisheries management contexts, game fish serve as primary targets for experimental introductions and management programs, as exemplified by efforts to establish non-native species in inland reservoirs to enhance recreational fishing opportunities. The designation reflects both ecological and socioeconomic considerations, as game fish species influence management priorities, conservation strategies, and the allocation of fisheries resources.
