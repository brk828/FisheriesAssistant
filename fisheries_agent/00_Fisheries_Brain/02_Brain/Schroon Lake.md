# Schroon Lake

## Summary
Schroon Lake is a New York State lake that was selected in 1944 as the initial study area for the state's salmon fishery program. By 1953, it had developed high abundance of landlocked salmon (Atlantic salmon, Salmo salar), with fall netting operations capturing significant numbers of fish for spawning operations.
