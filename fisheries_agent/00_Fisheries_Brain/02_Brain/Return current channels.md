# Return current channels

## Summary
Return current channels (RCCs) are eddy environments in the Colorado River that form in association with sand bars and recirculation zones. According to this source, RCCs and their associated backwaters provide critical rearing habitat for endangered native fish species and serve as potential sources of trophic material for the broader aquatic food web, with nutrient delivery to these habitats depending on subsurface hydrologic connections and groundwater flux through sand bars.
