# Schmittou

## Summary
Schmittou was an Auburn University researcher who conducted extensive cage culture experiments in 1969. Over 2 years he stocked 34,000+ catfish in 98 cages at densities up to 500 fish per cubic meter, achieving an overall feed conversion of 1.25, a maximum standing crop of 421 pounds in a single 1 m³ cage, and maximum daily gain per cage of 4.94 pounds using 40% protein floating pellets.

## Context

> This 1969 review synthesizes the first decade of research on commercial [[Channel Catfish]] nutrition in the United States. Despite only four research stations intensively studying catfish feeding, excellent practical rations have been developed yielding 1 pound of gain per 1.5-2.0 pounds of feed....
