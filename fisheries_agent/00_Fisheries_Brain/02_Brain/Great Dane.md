# Great Dane

## Summary
The Great Dane is a large breed of domestic dog. The lecture notes use the example of domestic dogs ranging from Chihuahua to Great Dane to illustrate how artificial selection creates diverse forms that remain interfertile, making species definition more complex in domesticated organisms than in wild populations.
