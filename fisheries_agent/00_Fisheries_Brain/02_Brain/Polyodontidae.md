# Polyodontidae

## Summary
Polyodontidae is a family of primitive freshwater fishes characterized by cartilaginous skeletons and highly specialized morphologies, including the North American paddlefish (Polyodon spathula) with its distinctive elongate rostrum. Members of this family are of significant conservation concern due to their vulnerability to habitat degradation and overfishing, particularly for caviar, making them important subjects for restoration programs and mitigation efforts by agencies such as the Tennessee Valley Authority. These ancient fishes serve as valuable indicators of riverine ecosystem health and are frequently utilized in aquatic restoration and reintroduction initiatives.
