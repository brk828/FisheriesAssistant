# Chiricahua National Forest

## Summary
Chiricahua National Forest encompasses the heavily timbered portions of both the Chiricahua Mountains (western boundary) and the southern portion of the Peloncillo Mountains (eastern boundary) of San Simon Valley. This source notes these forested areas are characterized by pine, oak, and juniper vegetation.
