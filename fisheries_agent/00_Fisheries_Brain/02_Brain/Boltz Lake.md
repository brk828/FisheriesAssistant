# Boltz Lake

## Summary
Boltz Lake is a 96-acre impoundment in northern Kentucky with a maximum depth of 62 feet and volume of 2,870 acre-feet. This source reports it was used as a test site for artificial destratification experiments in both 1965 (using mechanical pumping) and 1966 (using diffused air systems), with the diffused air method achieving near-uniform temperature in 72 hours and producing significant water quality improvements including 71% reduction in sulfides and 25% reductions in iron and manganese.
