# Usfws

## Summary
USFWS (United States Fish and Wildlife Service) is the primary federal agency responsible for managing and conserving fish and wildlife resources in the United States, including the protection of endangered and threatened aquatic species through enforcement of legislation such as the Endangered Species Act. In the context of ichthyology and fisheries science, USFWS plays a critical role in designating critical habitats for fish species, establishing recovery programs for imperiled populations, and coordinating research and management efforts to sustain both native fish communities and fisheries resources.
