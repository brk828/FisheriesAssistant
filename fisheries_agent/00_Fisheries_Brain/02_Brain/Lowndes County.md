# Lowndes County

## Summary
Lowndes County, Mississippi is the type-locality for the new species Ornithocythere gypodes. The holotype specimen was collected from a burrow in the flood plain of the Tombigbee River, one-fourth mile east of the river and one mile west of the Alabama-Mississippi state line, though repeated collection efforts at this location have been unsuccessful.
