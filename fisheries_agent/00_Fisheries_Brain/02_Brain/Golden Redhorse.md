# Golden Redhorse

## Summary
The Golden Redhorse is a cyprinid fish species that serves as an indicator of water quality and ecosystem health in freshwater systems, typically appearing in communities only after pollution levels have been reduced or abated. As a "clean-water fish," its presence or absence is used by ichthyologists and ecologists to assess the recovery status of aquatic ecosystems and to monitor changes in fish community composition following environmental disturbance or restoration efforts. This species is particularly relevant in limnological studies examining the relationships between dissolved oxygen levels, habitat conditions, and fish assemblage dynamics in streams and rivers.
