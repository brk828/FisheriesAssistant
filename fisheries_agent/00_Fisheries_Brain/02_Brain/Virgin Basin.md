# Virgin Basin

## Summary
Virgin Basin is one of the four major sections of Lake Mead reservoir. According to this study, it exhibited natural eutrophic lake conditions during June with primary productivity rates of 25-75 g C/m²/growing season, and it was generally less enriched than Boulder Basin but showed higher productivity than the upstream sections.
