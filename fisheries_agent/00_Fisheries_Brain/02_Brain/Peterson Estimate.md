# Peterson Estimate

## Summary
The Peterson Estimate is a mark-recapture population estimation method used to calculate fish abundance. This source applied Peterson estimates to razorback sucker populations, finding 50-84 fish in the A-7 zone (February-April 2007), 2,719 fish (95% CI 1,502-5,438) in A-10 (February-March 2007), and 6,054 fish (95% CI 3,145-12,746) in A-10 (January-March 2008).
