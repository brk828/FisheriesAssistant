# Point Terese

## Summary
Point Terese is a study site in Lynn Canal featuring a boulder-strewn slope base at 13-28 m depth where rockfish aggregations were observed. During cold months, fish at Point Terese retreated into crevices between boulders, where they were clearly visible but inactive.
