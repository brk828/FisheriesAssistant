# Tavasci Spring Fontelicella

## Summary
An undescribed species of tiny hydrobiid snail (Fontelicella n. sp.) endemic to Tavasci Spring in Yavapai County, Arizona. This source reports the population declined from viable in 1976 to possibly extinct by 1980, with no living specimens found and habitat reduced to 2 meters due to artificial pooling and canal construction at the spring.
