# Panum Crater

## Summary
Panum Crater is a volcanic feature in the Mono-Inyo Craters complex that extends below the highest beach level but shows no wave marks or lacustrine deposits. This indicates the crater formed after the last pluvial expansion of Lake Russell, representing post-pluvial volcanic activity.
