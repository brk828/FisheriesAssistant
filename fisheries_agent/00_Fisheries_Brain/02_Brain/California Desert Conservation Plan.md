# California Desert Conservation Plan

## Summary
The California Desert Conservation Plan is a comprehensive land management framework established by the Bureau of Land Management for managing public lands in the California Desert. The plan employs a four-tier multiple-use classification system (Classes C, L, M, and I) ranging from controlled to intensive use, and includes special designations such as National Scenic Areas and Areas of Critical Environmental Concern to balance conservation objectives with various land uses.
