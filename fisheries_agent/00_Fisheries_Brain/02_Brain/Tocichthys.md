# Tocichthys

## Summary
Tocichthys is a new subgenus established in this source with Hyperprosopon agassizii as the subgenotype. This represents a taxonomic revision within the Embiotocidae family classification.

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
