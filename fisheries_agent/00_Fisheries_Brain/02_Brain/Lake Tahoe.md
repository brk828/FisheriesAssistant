# Lake Tahoe

## Summary
Lake Tahoe is a large, deep alpine lake located in the Sierra Nevada mountains on the California-Nevada border that has served as a significant site for fish introductions and ecological studies in western North America. Historically, the lake received introductions of non-native species such as lake trout beginning in 1895, which has made it an important system for understanding the ecological consequences of fish stocking and species interactions in oligotrophic (nutrient-poor) freshwater environments. As a well-documented aquatic ecosystem, Lake Tahoe has been the subject of numerous fisheries science and conservation studies examining food habits, species composition, and the long-term effects of human interventions on native fish communities.
