# Phillips Petroleum Company

## Summary
Phillips Petroleum Company held leases on approximately 18,000 acres of Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge for oil exploration, with leases expiring in 1988. This represents part of the complex mineral rights situation on the refuge, where mineral rights were retained by previous owners on purchased private lands.
