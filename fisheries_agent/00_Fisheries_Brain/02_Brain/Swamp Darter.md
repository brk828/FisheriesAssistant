# Swamp Darter

## Summary
The Swamp Darter is a small freshwater fish species inhabiting shallow, vegetation-rich wetland and swamp environments across southeastern North American drainage systems, particularly in regions like Kentucky and the Mississippi River basin. As a member of the darter family (Percidae), it represents an important component of lentic and semi-lentic freshwater ecosystems, though its conservation status and specific ecological role are relevant to broader assessments of aquatic biodiversity and wetland habitat quality. This species is typically included in inventories of regional fish fauna when evaluating freshwater community composition, habitat requirements, and the impacts of environmental stressors on small benthic fishes.
