# Fort Pierce Wash

## Summary
Fort Pierce Wash is a tributary or wash in the Virgin River system that has been invaded by Red Shiner (Cyprinella lutrensis), an invasive cyprinid species. The source documents Red Shiners found above previous treatment drip stations and Virgin River Chubs present up to approximately 1 mile above a golf course in this wash, with barrier construction and chemical treatment proposed for October-November to eradicate the invasive species.
