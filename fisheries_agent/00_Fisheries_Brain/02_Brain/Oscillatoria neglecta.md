# Oscillatoria neglecta

## Summary
Oscillatoria neglecta is a species of blue-green algae (cyanobacteria). According to this source, Nakamura (1938) described CO₂ assimilation in O. neglecta using H₂S as a hydrogen donor, suggesting it may have chemosynthetic capabilities in addition to photosynthesis.
