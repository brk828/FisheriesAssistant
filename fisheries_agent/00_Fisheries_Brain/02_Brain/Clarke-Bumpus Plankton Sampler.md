# Clarke-Bumpus Plankton Sampler

## Summary
The Clarke-Bumpus Plankton Sampler is a piece of equipment used to collect plankton samples from water bodies. In this study of Arizona reservoir fisheries, it was among the equipment provided by the Arizona Game and Fish Department or University for sampling operations across six reservoirs on the Salt River and Verde River systems.
