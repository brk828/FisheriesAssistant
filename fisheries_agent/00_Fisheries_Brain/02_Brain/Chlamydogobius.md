# Chlamydogobius

## Summary
Chlamydogobius is a genus of desert gobies endemic to Australian desert springs. The bibliography includes references to species like C. eremius that show physiological adaptations to the stable thermal conditions and isolated nature of mound spring habitats in the Great Artesian Basin.
