# Williamston, Michigan

## Summary
Williamston, Michigan is a town in the Red Cedar River drainage basin where this 1968 study was conducted. The study area near Williamston received raw sewage and septic tank effluents from six outfalls delivering approximately 100 gallons per minute into the river, creating pollution impacts that were measured at multiple sampling stations above and below the town.
