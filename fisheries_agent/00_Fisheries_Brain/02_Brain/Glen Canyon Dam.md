# Glen Canyon Dam

## Summary
Glen Canyon Dam is a major hydroelectric facility on the Colorado River whose operation profoundly affects downstream fish populations and aquatic ecosystems, making it a central subject in Colorado River fisheries management and conservation biology. The dam's flow regulation, temperature changes, and habitat alterations have necessitated extensive environmental studies and coordination efforts among federal agencies to monitor impacts on native fish species, particularly endangered taxa like Colorado squawfish, and to develop management strategies to mitigate ecological damage. Understanding Glen Canyon Dam's effects on riverine fish communities is fundamental to Colorado River water management and species recovery programs.
