# Professor Valley

## Summary
Professor Valley is a section of the Colorado River located below Westwater Canyon in Utah that was identified as an alternative to Moab Canyon for the initial release of 2,000 pit-tagged, flow-trained juvenile Bonytail Chub. Like Moab Canyon, this site was chosen to minimize hybridization risks with related native chub species during the reintroduction effort.

## Context

> This draft plan outlines a comprehensive strategy for reintroducing hatchery-reared [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*) into the [[Upper Colorado River Basin]] (UCRB).
