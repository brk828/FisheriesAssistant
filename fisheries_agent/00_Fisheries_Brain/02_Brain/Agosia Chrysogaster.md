# Agosia Chrysogaster

## Summary
Agosia chrysogaster, commonly known as the longfin dace, is a small native cyprinid fish endemic to the southwestern United States and northern Mexico, particularly found in the Rio Yaqui basin and associated tributaries of Arizona. This species is of significant conservation concern due to habitat loss, competition from introduced exotic species, and its restricted geographic range, making it an important indicator species for assessing the ecological integrity of native desert aquatic communities. Research on A. chrysogaster has focused on its habitat partitioning preferences, genetic variation, and population dynamics in fragmented stream systems, contributing to understanding of how native cyprinids persist in ecosystems increasingly dominated by exotic fish species.
