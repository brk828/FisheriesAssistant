# Boy Scout Canyon

## Summary
Boy Scout Canyon is one of five fixed remote PIT scanning sites established in the River Zone of Lake Mohave for monitoring razorback sucker populations. This site is part of the network of locations where shore-based and submersible PIT antennas were deployed to track fish movements and aggregations in the riverine habitat upstream of Willow Beach.
