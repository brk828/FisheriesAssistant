# Species Introduction Protocols

## Summary
This source documents multiple bass and sunfish species introductions to Arizona waters, including specific stocking records such as 938 Striped Bass planted in 1959 by California Department of Fish and Game in the lower Colorado River, and Smallmouth Bass planted circa 1942. The Arizona Game and Fish Department planted White Bass in Carl Pleasant Lake in 1960.
