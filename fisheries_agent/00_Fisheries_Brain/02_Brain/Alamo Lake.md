# Alamo Lake

## Summary
Alamo Lake is a reservoir on the lower Colorado River in Arizona that serves as a critical study site for examining fish community dynamics, including interactions between native species like razorback suckers and invasive species such as tilapia and flathead catfish. The lake's diverse habitat structure—comprising main channel and backwater areas—provides opportunities to investigate habitat partitioning and niche segregation among benthic and pelagic fish species, making it an important location for understanding aquatic ecology and informing conservation strategies for native Colorado River fishes.
