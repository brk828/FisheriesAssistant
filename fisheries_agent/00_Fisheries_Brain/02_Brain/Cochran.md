# Cochran

## Summary
Cochran is a locality in the Gila River Basin where intensive aquatic sampling occurred during the 1973 field season. Multiple collections were made at Cochran on different dates, making it one of the most frequently sampled sites in the survey and providing temporal data on water quality and habitat conditions.
