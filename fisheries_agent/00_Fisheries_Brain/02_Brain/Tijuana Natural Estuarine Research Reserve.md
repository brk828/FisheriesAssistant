# Tijuana Natural Estuarine Research Reserve

## Summary
The Tijuana Natural Estuarine Research Reserve is a protected area that receives significant pollutant loading from urban runoff and non-point sources in the Tijuana River Watershed. This proposal identifies it as a sensitive ecosystem affected by decades of contamination from the watershed. The research program aimed to characterize pollutant sources and loading to support management decisions for the reserve.
