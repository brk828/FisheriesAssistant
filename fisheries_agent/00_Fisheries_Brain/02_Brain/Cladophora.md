# Cladophora

## Summary
Cladophora is a filamentous green alga that grows prolifically in the tailwaters below Glen Canyon Dam along the Colorado River in the Grand Canyon, where its abundance and sloughing dynamics are significantly influenced by managed flow regimens from the dam. In the context of aquatic ecology and fish conservation, Cladophora growth and periodic detachment (sloughing) events affect backwater habitat quality and ecosystem productivity, with implications for native fish populations like the endangered humpback chub that depend on stable shoreline and eddy habitats for feeding and reproduction. Understanding and managing Cladophora dynamics has become an important consideration in experimental flow releases designed to balance hydroelectric operations with ecological restoration objectives in this managed river system.
