# Arizona Juvenile

## Summary
Arizona Juvenile is one of the backwater coves used for rearing juvenile razorback suckers at Lake Mohave. This source indicates that improvements to the berm at Arizona Juvenile would be conducted by boat-accessed hand crews using sand and gravel from below operational high water elevation, as part of efforts to enhance predator barriers for native fish protection.
