# Rokytná River

## Summary
The Rokytná River is a stream in Czechoslovakia where Chondrostoma nasus population studies were conducted. The population showed densities of 4.2-14.2 individuals per 100 m², biomass of 112.1 kg/ha, and yield of 11 kg/ha.
