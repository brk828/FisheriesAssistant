# Sierra Club v. Abston Construction Co.

## Summary
Sierra Club v. Abston Construction Co. is a legal case relevant to Clean Water Act jurisdiction and the definition of 'waters of the United States.' This source references this case in the context of explaining how the Clean Water Act has been broadly interpreted to include non-navigable tributaries, intermittent streambeds, normally dry arroyos, gullies, ditches, and wetlands, thereby expanding NPDES permit requirements to mining operations in desert regions far from substantial rivers.

## Context

> This legal analysis examines the comprehensive federal and state environmental regulatory framework governing the domestic copper mining industry in the United States as of the early 1990s.
