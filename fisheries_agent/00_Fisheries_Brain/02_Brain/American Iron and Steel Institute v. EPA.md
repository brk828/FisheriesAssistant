# American Iron and Steel Institute v. EPA

## Summary
American Iron and Steel Institute v. EPA (886 F.2d 309, D.C. Cir. 1989) was a court decision establishing that RCRA corrective action requirements apply broadly to hazardous waste treatment, storage, and disposal facilities. The court ruled that corrective action applies to all releases from any solid waste management unit at TSD facilities regardless of when waste was placed, including units that received only Bevill-excluded mining wastes.
