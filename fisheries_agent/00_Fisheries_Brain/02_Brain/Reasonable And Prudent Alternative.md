# Reasonable And Prudent Alternative

## Summary
A Reasonable and Prudent Alternative (RPA) is a mitigation measure or operational modification proposed within a Biological Opinion under the Endangered Species Act to minimize or avoid adverse effects on threatened or endangered species and their habitats. In the context of fish and aquatic conservation, an RPA represents a practicable course of action that federal agencies can implement alongside their proposed projects—such as water management operations on rivers—to ensure compliance with species protection requirements while still allowing the project to proceed. RPAs typically include specific management actions, monitoring protocols, and habitat improvements designed to protect listed aquatic species and maintain ecosystem integrity.
