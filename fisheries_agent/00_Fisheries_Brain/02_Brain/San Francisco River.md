# San Francisco River

## Summary
The San Francisco River, flowing through both Arizona and New Mexico as a tributary of the Gila River system, represents a critical aquatic habitat for endemic and threatened fish species in the southwestern United States. This river system, particularly its Arizona and New Mexico segments spanning approximately 6 and 15 kilometers respectively in designated recovery areas, serves as designated critical habitat for endangered desert fishes including the Gila Topminnow and Desert Pupfish. The San Francisco River's role in fish conservation efforts reflects its ecological importance as one of the few remaining intact riverine habitats supporting populations of native southwestern fish species facing threats from habitat degradation, water extraction, and invasive species.
