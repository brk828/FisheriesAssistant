# Illinois Natural History Survey

## Summary
The Illinois Natural History Survey is a research institution that conducted electrophoretic analyses to determine the genetic composition and fitness characteristics of the rainbow trout population in the Colorado River below Glen Canyon Dam. According to this source, the genetic makeup of the trout population remained largely unknown until these analyses were performed by this organization.
