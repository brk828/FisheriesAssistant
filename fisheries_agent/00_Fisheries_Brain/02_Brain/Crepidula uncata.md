# Crepidula uncata

## Summary
Crepidula uncata is a slipper shell (gastropod mollusk) found in the Puerto Peñasco intertidal zone. This field guide documents it among the mollusks of the Gulf of California region, identified using Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other references.
