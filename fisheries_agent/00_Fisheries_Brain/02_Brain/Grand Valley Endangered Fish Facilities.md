# Grand Valley Endangered Fish Facilities

## Summary
Grand Valley Endangered Fish Facilities are propagation facilities used for rearing endangered fish species in the Upper Colorado River Basin. The FY 1998 work plan allocated $151,000 for operation and maintenance of these facilities, which were involved in bonytail stocking programs including approximately 2,200 bonytail stocked in Professor Valley in late FY 1997.
