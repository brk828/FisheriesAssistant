# Willow Beach NFH

## Summary
Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery is a federal fish production facility in Arizona participating in endangered species recovery efforts. In the 1987-1988 stocking plan, this hatchery was allocated 200,000 Colorado squawfish fry as part of the intermediate rearing program designed to grow endangered fish to larger, less vulnerable sizes before their release into historic river habitats.
