# Channel A

## Summary
Channel A is a 5-mile intermittent coulee flowing from Dry Lake to Six Mile Bay that serves as one of two major inlets to Devils Lake. With water velocities of 30.5 to 91.5 cm/sec, it provides only 7.3 to 2.4 hours for egg suspension (far below the 28-80 hours required for striped bass egg hatching), and has unsuitable mud substrate with decaying vegetation rather than the clean gravel or rock needed for striped bass spawning.
