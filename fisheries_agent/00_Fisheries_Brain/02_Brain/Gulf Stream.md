# Gulf Stream

## Summary
The Gulf Stream is the ocean current in which European Eel leptocephalus larvae drift during their development. The larvae drift slowly to Europe in the Gulf Stream, requiring three years to reach the elver transformation stage before entering streams for freshwater growth.
