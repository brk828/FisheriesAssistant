# Sicydium

## Summary
Sicydium is a genus of amphidromous gobies in the family Gobiidae that typically inhabit fast-flowing streams and rivers. This paper describes a new species from this genus, S. montanum, collected from mountain brooks in Venezuela, bringing attention to the genus's diversity in South American freshwater habitats.
