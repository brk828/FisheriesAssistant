# American Rivers

## Summary
American Rivers is a conservation organization that has played a significant role in collaborative fisheries and aquatic ecosystem management, particularly through its involvement in binational research initiatives and stakeholder agreements focused on major river systems like the Colorado River. In the context of fish science and conservation biology, American Rivers functions as a convening institution that brings together scientific experts, government agencies, and international partners to address complex issues of river ecology, fish habitat restoration, and water management across entire hydrological continua. The organization's work exemplifies how non-governmental conservation entities contribute to evidence-based management strategies that balance ecological restoration with competing water resource demands in aquatic systems.
