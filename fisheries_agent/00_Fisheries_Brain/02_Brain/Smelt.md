# Smelt

## Summary
Smelt (Osmerus mordax) are an introduced species in Cliff Pond that reproduce abundantly on wind-swept shores and serve as critical forage for carryover trout, enabling rapid growth to trophy size (3-4 pounds). The study identified smelt as creating a management dilemma because while they support trophy trout production, they might compete with fingerling trout for food, leading to the recommendation for partial rather than complete pond reclamation to preserve this valuable forage base.
