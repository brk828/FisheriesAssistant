# Asian Tapeworm

## Summary
The Asian Tapeworm (*Bothriocephalus acheilognathi*) is an invasive endoparasitic cestode that infects freshwater fish species and represents a significant concern in aquaculture and wild fish populations. This pathogen is screened during fish health inspections as part of disease surveillance protocols to prevent its establishment and spread in susceptible fish populations, particularly in aquatic ecosystems where native species lack evolved resistance. The tapeworm's presence can impact fish health, growth rates, and population dynamics, making it an important target for monitoring in both conservation and fisheries management contexts.
