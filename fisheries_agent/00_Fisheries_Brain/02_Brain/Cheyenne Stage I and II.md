# Cheyenne Stage I and II

## Summary
Cheyenne Stage I and II are water projects on the Little Snake River (a Yampa River tributary) that together deplete 23,800 acre-feet annually. This source includes them in the environmental baseline condition with favorable biological opinions, representing the second-largest depletion component in the system after Craig Power Station.
