# Walton Creek

## Summary
Walton Creek is a tributary of the Yampa River where its confluence serves as a known spawning location for invasive Northern Pike. One day of sampling in October 2009 at the Walton Creek confluence captured many times the number of age-0 Northern Pike captured during weeks of downstream sampling, illustrating the high reproduction occurring in upstream reaches and the importance of this site for Northern Pike recruitment.

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary:** [[Northern Pike]] (*Esox lucius*) is a large-bodied, piscivorous species invasive to the upper [[Colorado River Basin]].
