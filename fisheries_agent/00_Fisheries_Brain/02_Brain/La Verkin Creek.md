# La Verkin Creek

## Summary
La Verkin Creek is a tributary of the Virgin River that supports a small population of Virgin spinedace. According to this conservation strategy, the lower portions of the creek have been impacted by agricultural crop production and livestock, and habitat enhancement procedures are planned for occupied reaches.

## Context
