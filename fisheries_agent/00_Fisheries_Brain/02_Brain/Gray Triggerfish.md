# Gray Triggerfish

## Summary
Gray Triggerfish (Balistes capriscus) is a reef-associated species found on natural and artificial reefs of the Gulf of Mexico continental shelf. This study found that males grow significantly larger than females (asymptotic length of 502 mm vs. 447 mm using the new spine protocol), with both sexes reaching similar sizes until age 2-3 years, and that the species has been subject to overfishing with failed recovery despite rebuilding plans implemented in the early 1990s.
