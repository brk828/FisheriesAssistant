# Sierra Nevada Province

## Summary
The Sierra Nevada Province is a relatively humid montane biotic province represented in the Mono Basin by 20 genera in ten families. The province's proximity to the basin allowed valley glaciers to reach the shore of pluvial Lake Russell during the Pleistocene.
