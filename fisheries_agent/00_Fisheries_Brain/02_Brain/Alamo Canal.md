# Alamo Canal

## Summary
The Alamo Canal is an irrigation canal into which the Colorado River was diverted by the Morelos Dam. This source states that the Morelos Dam, completed in 1950 just south of the U.S.-Mexico border, diverted the river into the Alamo Irrigation Canal, effectively drying up the river's natural path to the Gulf of California.
