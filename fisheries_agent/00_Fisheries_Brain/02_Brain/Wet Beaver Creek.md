# Wet Beaver Creek

## Summary
Wet Beaver Creek is a tributary of the Verde River system in Arizona where Redeye Bass have been observed through citizen science reports. This source notes that previous studies of purported Smallmouth Bass (later identified as Redeye Bass) in this creek showed preference for shallower habitat than typical for true Smallmouth Bass.
