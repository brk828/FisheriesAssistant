# Loggerhead Shrike

## Summary
Loggerhead Shrike (Lanius ludovicianus) is a Candidate Category 2 (C2) bird species that was observed and considered locally common at PPMR, with four to six individuals identified including 2 pairs south of McDowell Road and 1 pair north. The species primarily utilized terraced creosote flats and occasionally bajadas/buttes, with recommendations to restrict vegetation disturbance to immediate construction areas and revegetate with native plants at similar densities.
