# Southeastern Alaska

## Summary
Southeastern Alaska is a coastal region of Alaska in the North Pacific. This study examined pink salmon spawning in Sashin Creek on Baranof Island in this region during the 1963 brood year, documenting spawner distribution, egg survival, and fry production across different stream habitats with varying gradients and substrate compositions.
