# Centropomidae

## Summary
Centropomidae is a family of euryhaline and anadromous/catadromous fishes commonly known as snooks that inhabit both marine and freshwater environments, particularly in coastal river systems and drainages of Mexico and similar tropical regions. These fishes are ecologically significant as they form important linkages between marine and freshwater aquatic systems, utilizing different habitats throughout their life cycles. In the context of ichthyological surveys and fish identification keys, Centropomidae represents a notable group of commercially and recreationally important teleosts that demonstrate the complex biogeographic patterns and habitat connectivity characteristic of tropical and subtropical fish faunas.
