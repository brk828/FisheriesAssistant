# FWS

## Summary
FWS (U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service) is a federal agency that was involved in discussions with the Bureau of Reclamation concerning protection measures for endangered fish species in Glen Canyon and Grand Canyon below Glen Canyon Dam. According to this source, FWS reached agreement with Reclamation on acceptable measures to protect endangered fish species and was expected to complete a draft Biological Opinion on the preferred alternative operational criteria for the dam.
