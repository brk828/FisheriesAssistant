# Salix goodingi

## Summary
Salix goodingi is a willow species that is part of the Deciduous Riparian Woodland vegetation in the southwestern United States. This study identifies it as one of the dominant riparian species, along with cottonwood and sycamore, that characterizes intermittent and perennial streams at lower elevations in the Gila River Basin, Arizona.
