# Parade Grounds

## Summary
The Parade Grounds is a sandlot ballfield location in Brooklyn, New York, where these observations of ant-bee interactions occurred during summer 1957 and spring 1958. The sandy footpath habitat at this site provided suitable substrate for both bee nesting and the ants' use of readily available sand grains as tools during their predatory attacks.

## Context

> This observational study documents an unusual predatory behavior in [[Tetramorium caespitum]] (pavement ant) attacking nests of the primitively social bee [[Lasioglossum zephyrum]].
