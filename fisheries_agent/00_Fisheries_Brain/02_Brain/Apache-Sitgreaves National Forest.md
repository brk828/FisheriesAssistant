# Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest

## Summary
Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest is a large forest management area in Arizona that encompasses significant aquatic habitats and watersheds important for fish conservation and fisheries management. The forest contains multiple stream systems and aquatic ecosystems that support native fish populations, making it a key area for fisheries surveys, population monitoring, and conservation efforts related to desert fish species. Management activities and livestock grazing within the forest have direct implications for aquatic habitat quality and fish community composition, necessitating coordination between forest managers, fisheries scientists, and conservation biologists.
