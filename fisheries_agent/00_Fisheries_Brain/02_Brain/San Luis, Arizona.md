# San Luis, Arizona

## Summary
San Luis, Arizona is a location in the Lower Colorado River Basin where a unique Tilapia population exhibiting evidence of hybridization between species has been documented. This source reports that drainage canal populations at San Luis show introgression between Tilapia aurea and the T. mossambica complex, with many specimens combining T. aurea body morphology and pigmentation with the elongate, duck-like jaws characteristic of the T. mossambica group—a mosaic of characters indicating ongoing hybridization.
