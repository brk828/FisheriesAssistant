# Goldroad Fontelicella

## Summary
Goldroad Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species endemic to Goldroad Well Spring in the Black Mountains of Arizona. This tiny hydrobioid snail was assessed in 1981 as rare and of special concern due to its restriction to a single spring system.
