# Desert Pupfish Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Desert Pupfish Recovery Plan is a coordinated conservation and management strategy developed by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team to protect and restore populations of endemic pupfish species (genus Cyprinodon) and other federally protected native fishes in arid southwestern aquatic ecosystems. This plan addresses the critical threats facing these small, highly specialized fishes through habitat restoration, captive breeding programs, and establishment of refuge populations to prevent extinction of species adapted to isolated desert springs and pupfish habitats. The recovery effort represents a multi-institutional approach combining federal protection under the Endangered Species Act with active biological management to reverse population declines caused by water extraction, habitat degradation, and invasive species.
