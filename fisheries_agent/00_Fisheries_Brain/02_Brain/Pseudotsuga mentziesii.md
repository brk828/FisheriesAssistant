# Pseudotsuga mentziesii

## Summary
Pseudotsuga mentziesii, commonly known as Douglas fir, is a conifer species found in the Sierra San Luis reserve. It occurs in the mixed conifer forests at elevations above 2,100 meters, representing one of the characteristic tree species of the higher elevation forests.
