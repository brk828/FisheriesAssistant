# U.S. Geological Survey

## Summary
The U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) is a federal scientific agency that conducts research and monitoring on fish populations, aquatic ecosystems, and conservation biology to inform management decisions and protect vulnerable species, particularly in sensitive habitats like desert springs and streams in the American West. In the context of fish science and conservation, the USGS works collaboratively with other federal agencies such as the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and the Bureau of Reclamation to study endangered fish species, assess watershed health, and develop strategies for preserving endemic and threatened aquatic fauna facing extinction risks from habitat loss and environmental change.
