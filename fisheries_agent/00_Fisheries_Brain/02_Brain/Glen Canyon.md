# Glen Canyon

## Summary
Glen Canyon refers to a critical reach of the Colorado River ecosystem in Arizona that encompasses the area immediately downstream of Glen Canyon Dam, extending into the Grand Canyon. This region is central to aquatic conservation efforts, particularly regarding management of dam-release flows and their effects on native fish populations, especially the endangered Humpback Chub and its critical spawning habitat in tributaries like the Little Colorado River. Glen Canyon Dam operations and the resulting flow regimes represent a primary management tool and concern for fisheries scientists and conservation biologists seeking to balance water resource demands with the restoration and recovery of native aquatic communities in the Colorado River system.
