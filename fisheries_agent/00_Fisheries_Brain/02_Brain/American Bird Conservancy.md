# American Bird Conservancy

## Summary
The American Bird Conservancy is a bird conservation organization that designates areas of global importance for bird populations. In 1996, the American Bird Conservancy designated the San Pedro River as a "Globally Important Bird Area," recognizing its significance as habitat for nearly half of North America's 800 bird species that frequent the area at some point in their lives.
