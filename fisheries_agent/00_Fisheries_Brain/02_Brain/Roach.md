# Roach

## Summary
Roach (Rutilus rutilus) is a common cyprinid fish species inhabiting the benthic zones of temperate freshwater lakes, serving as an important component of aquatic food webs and frequently used as a model organism in ecological and behavioral studies. In the context of the cited research on brown trout activity cycles, roach represent a key prey species and ecological associate studied within experimental confinement systems in lacustrine environments. As a generalist benthic feeder and eurythermal species, roach are significant indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and have been extensively used in laboratory and field experiments investigating predator-prey interactions and fish behavior in controlled conditions.
