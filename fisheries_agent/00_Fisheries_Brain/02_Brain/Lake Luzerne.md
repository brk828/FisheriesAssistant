# Lake Luzerne

## Summary
Lake Luzerne is a New York State lake where red salmon (sockeye salmon, Oncorhynchus nerka) were experimentally stocked. By 1953, adult red salmon spawners were successfully collected there during fall netting operations, indicating successful establishment of this Pacific salmon species.
