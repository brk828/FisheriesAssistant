# Scuba Equipment

## Summary
Scuba equipment refers to underwater breathing apparatus used for fisheries surveys, with equipment selection varying by depth and application. Snorkels were preferred in shallow waters where scuba air bubbles disturbed fish behavior, while scuba gear was reserved for deep water studies where direct surface breathing was impractical.
