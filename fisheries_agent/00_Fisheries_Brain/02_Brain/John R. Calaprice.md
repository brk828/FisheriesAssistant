# John R. Calaprice

## Summary
John R. Calaprice was a researcher who co-authored this 1964 study on erythrocyte antigens in California trout species. He worked at the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Convict Creek Experiment Station in Mono County, conducting immunogenetic research from July through November 1962.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
