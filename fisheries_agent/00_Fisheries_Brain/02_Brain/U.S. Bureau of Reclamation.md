# U.S. Bureau Of Reclamation

## Summary
The U.S. Bureau of Reclamation is a federal agency responsible for managing water resources and dam operations in the western United States, and in the context of fish science and aquatic ecology, it plays a critical role in regulating river flows and water conditions that directly impact native fish populations and their habitats. As the operator of major dam systems, particularly along the Colorado River, the Bureau coordinates with conservation efforts and research programs aimed at protecting and recovering endangered fish species, such as the humpback chub, through collaborative management strategies that balance water allocation with environmental flows necessary for fish survival and reproduction.
