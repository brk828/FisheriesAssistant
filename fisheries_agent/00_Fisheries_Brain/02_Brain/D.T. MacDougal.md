# D.T. MacDougal

## Summary
D.T. MacDougal was the director of the Carnegie Botanical Laboratory who was present during the collection of Lucania browni specimens and the measurement of the hot spring's water temperature. He witnessed the temperature recording of 128°F (53°C) along with Herbert Brown and engineer Godfrey Sykes.

## Context

> This 1907 publication by [[David Starr Jordan]] and [[Robert Earl Richardson]] describes a new species of killifish, *[[Lucania browni]]*, collected from a hot spring in northeastern [[Lower California]] (Baja California).
