# Usda Forest Service

## Summary
The USDA Forest Service is a federal agency responsible for managing over 190 million acres of public lands, including numerous watersheds, streams, and aquatic habitats that are critical for fish conservation and ecosystem health. In the context of fish science and aquatic ecology, the agency plays a key role in implementing habitat restoration projects, managing water resources, and coordinating with other organizations on species recovery efforts, particularly for threatened and endangered fish populations in western watersheds. The Forest Service's land management decisions directly influence water quality, stream flow, riparian vegetation, and overall aquatic ecosystem integrity across the regions it oversees.
