# Oncorhynchus mykiss nelsoni

## Summary
Oncorhynchus mykiss nelsoni is the scientific name for the San Pedro Martir Trout, a subspecies of rainbow trout. This fish inhabits the Rio San Rafael and was the focus of conservation attention and upcoming research as documented in 1990 Desert Fishes Council correspondence.
