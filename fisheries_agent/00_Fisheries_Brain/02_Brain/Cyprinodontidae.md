# Cyprinodontidae

## Summary
Cyprinodontidae is a family of small, typically desert-dwelling freshwater fishes commonly known as pupfishes, characterized by their adaptability to extreme aquatic environments including isolated springs, salt lakes, and other marginal habitats with fluctuating salinity and temperature. Members of this family, such as the genus Cyprinodon, are of particular importance in conservation biology and aquatic ecology due to their restricted distributions, vulnerability to habitat loss and competition from non-native species, and their role as indicator species for groundwater-dependent ecosystems in arid regions like the Chihuahuan Desert. These fishes have become focal species for desert aquatic conservation efforts and are frequently featured in recovery programs and ecological monitoring studies across western North America and Mexico.
