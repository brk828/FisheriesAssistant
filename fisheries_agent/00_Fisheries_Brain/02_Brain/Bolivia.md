# Bolivia

## Summary
Bolivia is a South American country that contains Lake Titicaca (shared with Peru). This source uses Lake Titicaca in Bolivia and Peru as the only comparable example to the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin in terms of the concentration of unique genera and species of aquatic snails, indicating that the Mexican basin's biodiversity is unmatched elsewhere in North America.
