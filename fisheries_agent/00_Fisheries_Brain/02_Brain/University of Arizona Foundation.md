# University of Arizona Foundation

## Summary
The University of Arizona Foundation served as the tax-deductible donation recipient for contributions supporting the publication of 'Battle Against Extinction: Native Fish Management in the American West.' The Desert Fishes Council sought $10,000 in donations through this foundation to subsidize the book's retail price from $100 to $50, with all royalties accruing to the Council.
