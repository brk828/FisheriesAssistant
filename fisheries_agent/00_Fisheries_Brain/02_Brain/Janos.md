# Janos

## Summary
Janos is a municipality in Chihuahua, Mexico, that contains part of the proposed Sierra San Luis protection area. The reserve spans both Janos and the adjacent Agua Prieta municipality in Sonora, with the area supporting cattle ranching and beargrass harvesting as primary economic activities.
