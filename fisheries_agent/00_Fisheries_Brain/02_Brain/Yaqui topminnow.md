# Yaqui Topminnow

## Summary
The Yaqui Topminnow is a small-bodied livebearing fish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui basin in Arizona that is federally listed as endangered due to severe habitat loss, water extraction, and competition from invasive species, resulting in its restriction to isolated spring and stream refugia. As a focus of multi-agency native fish conservation efforts coordinated through groups like the Southeast Arizona Native Fish Team, the species represents a critical case study in desert aquatic ecosystem management and the challenges of recovering populations of endemic fishes in arid regions. Conservation strategies for the Yaqui Topminnow typically involve habitat restoration, captive propagation, and translocation to protected refuge populations to prevent extinction.
