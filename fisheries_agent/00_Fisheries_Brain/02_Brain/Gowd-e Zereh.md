# Gowd-e Zereh

## Summary
Gowd-e Zereh is a salt flat in the Sistan basin. This source reports it receives overflow water from the Hamun-e Helmand via the Shelah River, with this flushing action helping maintain freshwater conditions in the upstream lakes.
