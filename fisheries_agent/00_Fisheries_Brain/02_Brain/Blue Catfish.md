# Blue Catfish

## Summary
Blue Catfish (*Ictalurus furcatus*) is a large, opportunistic ictalurid catfish native to the Mississippi River basin that has become an invasive species in various aquatic ecosystems, particularly in the southeastern United States and southwestern drainages. As a generalist predator with high fecundity and rapid growth rates, blue catfish can establish populations in diverse freshwater habitats and pose significant ecological threats through predation on native fish species and competition with endemic fishes such as the Yaqui Catfish. Their capacity to hybridize with congeners like Channel Catfish and their ability to thrive in altered riverine and reservoir environments make them a conservation concern in regions where they have been introduced outside their native range.
