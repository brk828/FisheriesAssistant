# Spatially Balanced Sampling

## Summary
Spatially balanced sampling is the survey design used for the Spring Pike Index Netting (SPIN) to provide unbiased abundance estimates of Northern Pike populations. This standardized approach allows for statistically valid population assessment, with the 2022 survey producing a catch rate estimate of 0.26 pike/net-night (95% CI: ±0.16) for sloughs and southern mainstem areas.
