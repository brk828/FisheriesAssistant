# Fisheries Management

## Summary
Fisheries Management encompasses the science and practice of sustainably regulating fish populations and aquatic resources through monitoring, assessment, and adaptive strategies that balance harvest with conservation objectives. It integrates biological data on fish stocks, population dynamics, and ecosystem health with policy implementation to maintain viable populations while supporting economic and social benefits from fishing. Effective fisheries management requires defining appropriate management units—such as distinct population segments or stocks—and establishing evidence-based regulations that account for taxonomic complexity and population structure to prevent overexploitation and ensure long-term resource sustainability.
