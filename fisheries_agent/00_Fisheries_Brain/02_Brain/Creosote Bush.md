# Creosote Bush

## Summary
Creosote Bush (Larrea tridentata) is a predominant shrub species in the Sonoran Desertscrub vegetation community at PPMR. The terraced creosote flats were identified as important habitat utilized by birds like the Loggerhead Shrike and ranked as having relatively low local impact but supporting wildlife use.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
