# Coregonidae

## Summary
Coregonidae is a family of coldwater freshwater fish commonly known as whitefishes and ciscoes, characterized by their adaptation to deep, oxygenated lakes and their role as key components of North American aquatic ecosystems, particularly in the Great Lakes and northern temperate regions. Members of this family are typically fall spawners with complex life history strategies and represent a group of significant ecological and commercial importance that has been extensively studied for both conservation and fisheries management purposes. The family exhibits notable species-level diversity among coldwater specialists and serves as an important indicator group for monitoring the health and productivity of deep oligotrophic lake environments.
