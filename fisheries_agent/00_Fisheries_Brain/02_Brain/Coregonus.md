# Coregonus

## Summary
Coregonus is a genus of freshwater fish within the Coregonidae family, commonly known as whitefish, which are distributed across the Holarctic region in North America and Eurasia. These coldwater salmonids inhabit lakes and rivers and are ecologically significant as both keystone species in their aquatic ecosystems and important resources for commercial and subsistence fisheries, though many populations face conservation challenges. The genus has been extensively studied across multiple disciplines to understand its biology, population dynamics, and environmental responses to human impacts and climate change.
