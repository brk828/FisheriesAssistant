# Cocopa Indians

## Summary
The Cocopa Indians are traditional fishers and flood-recession farmers who historically inhabited the Colorado River Delta region, numbering approximately 5,000 four centuries ago. According to this source, they have been devastated by the loss of freshwater flows in the delta, last harvesting their staple grain nipa (Distichlis palmeri) in the early 1950s, with only 40-50 Cocopa families remaining south of the border today.
