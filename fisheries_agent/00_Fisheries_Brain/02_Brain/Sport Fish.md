# Sport Fish

## Summary
Sport fish are fish species of recreational and economic importance that are actively pursued by anglers and managed for sustained populations in natural waters, distinguishing them from non-game native species in conservation and permitting contexts. In fisheries management, sport fish typically include both native and introduced species maintained through stocking programs and habitat management, and their scientific collection often requires specialized permits due to their regulated status and the need to monitor populations subject to fishing pressure. The designation reflects a management category that balances human recreational use with conservation objectives, particularly relevant in arid regions like Arizona where native sport fish populations may overlap with endangered species reintroduction efforts.
