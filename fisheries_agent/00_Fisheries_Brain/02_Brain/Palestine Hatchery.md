# Palestine Hatchery

## Summary
Palestine Hatchery refers to the same facility as Palestine State Fish Hatchery, which served as the project headquarters. The hatchery's 2.2-acre reservoir was used as an experimental pond, stocked with a balanced population in November 1954, and demonstrated that heavy fishing pressure (556 hours) could throw a balanced population out of balance through excessive harvest of large bass.
