# Elastomer Tagging

## Summary
Elastomer tagging is a marking technique using visible implant elastomer injected under the skin to create unique individual identification codes. This study used four colors and 10 anatomical positions to create unique marks for up to 760 individual fish, allowing mark-recapture tracking of 237 individuals (478 recaptures) over five months to study movement patterns in stream fish populations.
