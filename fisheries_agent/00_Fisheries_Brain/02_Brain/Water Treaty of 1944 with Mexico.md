# Water Treaty of 1944 with Mexico

## Summary
The Water Treaty of 1944 with Mexico is an international agreement that forms part of the "Law of the River" governing Colorado River management. This source identifies it as one of the critical legal constraints that all Glen Canyon Dam operations and adaptive management activities must comply with, alongside domestic compacts and legislation.
