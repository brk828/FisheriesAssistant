# Taxonomic Revision

## Summary
Taxonomic revision is the process of re-evaluating and updating species classifications based on new evidence. This source presents a comprehensive taxonomic revision of the Redeye Bass (M. coosae) complex using morphological variation and mitochondrial DNA data, restricting M. coosae sensu stricto to the Coosa River system and describing four new species (M. cahabae, M. tallapoosae, M. warriorensis, and M. chattahoochae), demonstrating the value of integrating morphological and molecular approaches for species delimitation.
