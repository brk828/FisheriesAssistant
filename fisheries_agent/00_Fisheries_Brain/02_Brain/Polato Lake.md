# Polato Lake

## Summary
Polato Lake (also referred to as Potato Lake in the document) is located near the headwaters of East Clear Creek in Arizona. The critical habitat designation for Little Colorado Spinedace includes approximately 13 stream miles from the upper end of Blue Ridge Reservoir upstream to Polato Lake.

## Context

> The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service issued a final rule on September 16, 1987, determining [[Lepidomeda vittata]] ([[Little Colorado Spinedace]]) to be a threatened species under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973.
