# Littlefield Springs Fontelicella

## Summary
Littlefield Springs Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species found in five springs near Littlefield, Mohave County, Arizona. Three springs were modified during Interstate Highway construction in 1972-73, with one population nearly extirpated but recovered post-construction, and all populations were "normal" by 1980, though the springs have been suggested for desalinization which would cause extinction.
