# Talitridae

## Summary
Talitridae is a family of crustaceans represented in the Red Cedar River by Hyallela azteca. This family was present at all sampling stations with greatest numbers at Station VI, indicating tolerance across the pollution gradient from upstream controls through recovery zones.
