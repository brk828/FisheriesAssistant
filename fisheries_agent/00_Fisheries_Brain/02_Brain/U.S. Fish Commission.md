# U.S. Fish Commission

## Summary
The U.S. Fish Commission was the federal agency that supplied carp to the Arizona Fish Commission for distribution throughout the territory. According to this report, the agency experienced operational challenges including delayed and unreliable shipments, with an expected 1884 carp shipment not arriving by December 15 due to frozen eastern carp ponds, and no prospect of delivery until late spring.
