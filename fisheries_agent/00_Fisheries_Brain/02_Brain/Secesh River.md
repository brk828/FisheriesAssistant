# Secesh River

## Summary
Secesh River is a tributary in the Salmon River drainage where summer-run Chinook salmon spawn, including in its tributary Lake Creek. In 1966, the Secesh River drainage had 41% females in the spawning escapement, and Summit Creek (a Secesh tributary) was used for an experimental fish transfer.
