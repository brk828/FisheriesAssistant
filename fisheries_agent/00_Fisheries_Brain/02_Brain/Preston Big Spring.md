# Preston Big Spring

## Summary
Preston Big Spring is one of seven historic habitats for White River Spinedace in White Pine County, Nevada, characterized by cool (65°-71°F) clear water with gravel and sand substrate and emergent vegetation. The spring and its outflow (approximately 4.0 acres) were designated as critical habitat in 1985, support diverse algal flora (74 species) and invertebrate fauna (over 40 taxa), and historically contained White River Spinedace along with other native species, though the spinedace population was extirpated by 1985.
