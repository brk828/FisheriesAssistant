# Buffalofish

## Summary
Buffalofish are large, bottom-feeding cyprinids (family Cyprinidae) native to North American freshwater systems, commonly including species such as bigmouth buffalo (Ictiobus cyprinellus) and smallmouth buffalo (Ictiobus bubalus) that inhabit rivers, lakes, and reservoirs. In conservation and fisheries contexts, buffalofish are significant both as native fish populations affected by habitat degradation and as species relevant to discussions of native versus non-native fish management in freshwater ecosystems. Their ecology and population dynamics have been subjects of study regarding how native fish communities respond to environmental changes and the impacts of invasive species introductions in aquatic habitats.
