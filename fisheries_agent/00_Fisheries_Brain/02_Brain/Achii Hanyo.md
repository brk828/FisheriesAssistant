# Achii Hanyo

## Summary
Achii Hanyo (located in Parker, Arizona) is a hatchery facility that propagated some of the bonytail used in this study. Study fish from Achii Hanyo were surgically implanted with acoustic or radio transmitters before being released into Lake Havasu for post-stocking survival monitoring.

## Context

> This multi-year study (2013-2016) documented post-stocking survival, distribution, and habitat use of endangered [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) in [[Lake Havasu]], [[Colorado River]].
