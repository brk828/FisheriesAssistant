# Elephant Mountain

## Summary
Elephant Mountain is a prominent topographic feature within Spur Cross Ranch, reaching approximately 3,926 feet elevation at its peak. The mountain's eastern and southern slopes were surveyed with binoculars during the 1996 biological evaluation, though direct access to much of the mountain was limited.
