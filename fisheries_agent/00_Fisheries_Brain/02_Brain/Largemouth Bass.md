# Largemouth Bass

## Summary
Largemouth bass (*Micropterus salmoides*) is a warmwater centrarchid fish species native to North America that has become one of the most abundant and economically important sport fish in managed aquatic ecosystems, particularly in reservoirs and lakes throughout the western United States. As an apex predator in these systems, largemouth bass play a significant ecological role in structuring fish communities and regulating prey populations, though their introduction into non-native waters has raised conservation concerns regarding impacts on endemic and threatened species. The species' prevalence in catch composition data and fisheries management documents reflects both its value as a recreational resource and its status as a non-native invasive species in some aquatic habitats where it may compete with or prey upon native fishes.
