# San Francisco

## Summary
San Francisco is a major city in California used as a geographic reference point in this study, with Tomales Bay located 40 miles to its northwest. San Francisco Bay is part of the larger migratory circuit for striped bass, with historical substantial fisheries existing there from 1938-1943 and increased migrations into the bay during the late 1950s and early 1960s.

## Context

> This tagging study (1963-1966) investigated the migration patterns of [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*) in [[Tomales Bay]], California, located 40 miles northwest of [[San Francisco]]. A total of 207 striped bass were tagged using disk-dangler tags, with 65 tag returns received by July 1968....
