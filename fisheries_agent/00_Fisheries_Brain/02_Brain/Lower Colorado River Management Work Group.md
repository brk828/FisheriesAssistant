# Lower Colorado River Management Work Group

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Management Work Group is an interagency coordination body with shared management responsibility for the Imperial Division of the Lower Colorado River. According to this source, the Work Group initiated inventory efforts in 1986 to assess backwater lake complexes and associated wetland-riparian ecosystems, leading to the formation of the Lower Colorado River Backwater Sub-Committee in 1987.
