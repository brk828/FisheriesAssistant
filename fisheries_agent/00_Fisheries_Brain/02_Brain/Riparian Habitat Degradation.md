# Riparian Habitat Degradation

## Summary
Riparian habitat degradation refers to the decline in quality and ecological function of vegetation and streamside ecosystems along riverbanks and floodplains, typically resulting from human activities such as cattle grazing, logging, or development. This degradation directly impacts fish populations by reducing shade cover, destabilizing stream banks, increasing water temperature, and diminishing the supply of woody debris and organic matter essential for aquatic food webs and fish shelter. Restoring riparian zones is therefore a critical conservation strategy for maintaining healthy fish populations and overall stream ecosystem integrity.
