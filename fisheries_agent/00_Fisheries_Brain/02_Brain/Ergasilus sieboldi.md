# Ergasilus sieboldi

## Summary
Ergasilus sieboldi is a parasitic copepod that attaches to the gill filaments of freshwater fish. This source documents its first British record in 1967 at Howbrook Reservoir, Yorkshire, where it heavily infested Brown Trout with over 130 parasites found on a single small fish, causing mortality through gill tissue destruction, blood withdrawal, and secondary infections.
