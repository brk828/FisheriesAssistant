# Plankton

## Summary
Plankton comprises the microscopic and small organisms suspended in aquatic environments that form the foundation of aquatic food webs, serving as the primary food source for larval and juvenile fish during their early developmental stages. As obligate planktivores, young fish depend critically on abundant and stable plankton populations for survival and growth, making plankton availability a key ecological factor in fish recruitment and overall aquatic productivity. In conservation and fisheries management contexts, plankton abundance and community composition are important indicators of water quality and ecosystem health, directly influencing fish population dynamics and the sustainability of aquatic ecosystems.
