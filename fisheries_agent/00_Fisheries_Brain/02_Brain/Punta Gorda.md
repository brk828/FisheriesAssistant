# Punta Gorda

## Summary
Punta Gorda is a locality in British Honduras (Belize) where fish specimens were collected for this study. It yielded 13 juvenile specimens of Brycon guatemalensis (representing the first record of this species from British Honduras) and one specimen of Astyanax fasciatus aeneus.

## Context
