# Chlamydogobius eremius

## Summary
Chlamydogobius eremius is a desert goby species endemic to Great Artesian Basin springs. The bibliography documents this species as demonstrating physiological adaptations to stable thermal conditions and representing the isolated evolutionary history of fish fauna in mound spring habitats.
