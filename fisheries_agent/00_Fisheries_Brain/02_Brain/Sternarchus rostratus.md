# Sternarchus rostratus

## Summary
Sternarchus rostratus is a gymnotid species collected from both the Magdalena Basin (Atlantic drainage) and Tuyra Basin of Pacific slope Panama. Twenty-four specimens were collected from localities including Girardot, Apulo, Cartago, and Cali, ranging from 94-304 mm in length, with morphometric characteristics including head length 5.3-6.5 in length to anal end and anal rays 151-175.
