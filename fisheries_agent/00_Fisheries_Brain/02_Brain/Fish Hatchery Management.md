# Fish Hatchery Management

## Summary
Fish Hatchery Management encompasses the practical operational and technical procedures required to maintain controlled breeding environments and rear fish populations in artificial settings, including the design and maintenance of filtration systems, water quality management, and facility infrastructure necessary for successful fish culture. This discipline integrates engineering, biology, and animal husbandry to optimize survival rates and growth of cultured fish species, whether for conservation purposes, commercial aquaculture, or research applications. Effective hatchery management is essential for producing healthy fish for stock enhancement, species recovery programs, and supporting both recreational and commercial fisheries.
