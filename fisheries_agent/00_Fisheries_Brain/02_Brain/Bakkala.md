# Bakkala

## Summary
Bakkala is a researcher who conducted environmental measurements at the Abernathy Channel during the 1960-63 seasons. This source cites Bakkala's 1966 findings on dissolved oxygen levels (11.8 to 8.1 ppm) and seepage velocities (2.0 to 2.6 ft/hr) at different developmental stages.
