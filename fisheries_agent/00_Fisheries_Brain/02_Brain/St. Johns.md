# St. Johns

## Summary
St. Johns is a town in Apache County, Arizona, located along the Little Colorado River. The current distribution of Little Colorado Spinedace in the mainstem river includes approximately 40 miles of permanent stream from St. Johns upstream to the permanent headwaters in the White Mountains near Greer.
