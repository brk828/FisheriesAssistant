# Turner And Tafanelli 1983

## Summary
Turner and Tafanelli (1983) appears to be a foundational reference for understanding the habitat requirements and ecological characteristics of endemic cyprinid fishes in southwestern desert streams, particularly spiked dace and other threatened species of the Gila River basin. The work likely documents critical habitat associations with specific stream microhabitats such as boulder fields and aquatic vegetation, as well as the hydrological and geomorphological conditions necessary for persistence of these species in low-gradient, perennial desert streams. This reference has become essential to fisheries management and conservation policy, informing instream flow requirements and habitat protection strategies under water codes and federal resource management frameworks.
