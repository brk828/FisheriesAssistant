# Winchester Mountains

## Summary
The Winchester Mountains are one of the mountain ranges surrounding the Aravaipa Creek watershed in the study area. This source lists the Winchester Mountains as part of the geographical context for the tailings site and creek system, along with other ranges including the Pinaleno Mountains, Galiuro Mountains, Santa Teresa Mountains, and Turnbull Mountains.
