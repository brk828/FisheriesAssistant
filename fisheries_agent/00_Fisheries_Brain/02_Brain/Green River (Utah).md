# Green River (Utah)

## Summary
The Green River in Utah is a major tributary of the Colorado River system that serves as critical habitat for several endangered fish species endemic to the Colorado River basin, including razorback suckers and Colorado squawfish. The river's flow regimes, water quality, and thermal characteristics significantly influence the survival, reproduction, and genetic diversity of these native fish populations, making it a focal point for conservation efforts and fisheries management in the southwestern United States. Understanding the Green River's ecological dynamics and implementing appropriate flow recommendations are essential components of recovery programs for these threatened aquatic species.
