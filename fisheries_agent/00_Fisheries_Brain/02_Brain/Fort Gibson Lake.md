# Fort Gibson Lake

## Summary
Fort Gibson Lake is a 19,100 surface acre reservoir in Oklahoma that was one of the most productive commercial fishing waters in the state. This source reports that Fort Gibson Lake was fished continuously for three years (1958-1960) and contributed 13.3% of the statewide catch in 1958, 14.7% in 1959, and 20.6% in 1960, making it one of the top three producing waters along with Lake Texoma and Grand Lake.
