# Southwest Willow Flycatcher

## Summary
The Southwest Willow Flycatcher is a rare bird species requiring federal and/or state protection. This source identifies it as one of 24 protected species among the 489 total species supported by the San Pedro River riparian ecosystem in Arizona.
