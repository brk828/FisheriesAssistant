# Bear Canyon

## Summary
Bear Canyon is a stream site in southern Arizona where Gila Topminnow were stocked in 1982 as part of conservation efforts. The 1991 survey found no topminnow above or below the original stocking site, marking the second reported extirpation at this location, though Longfin Dace were abundant; the site was recommended for restocking 1-2 miles above the previous stocking location.
