# Alaska Science and Technology Foundation

## Summary
The Alaska Science and Technology Foundation is a funding organization that supported this research. The foundation provided financial support for this literature review and analysis examining the effects of total dissolved solids on aquatic organisms, with particular focus on developing water quality recommendations for salmonid species in Alaska.
