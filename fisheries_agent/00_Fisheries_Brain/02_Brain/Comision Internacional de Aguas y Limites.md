# Comision Internacional de Aguas y Limites

## Summary
The Comision Internacional de Aguas y Limites (CILA) is the Mexican component of the International Boundary and Water Commission (IBWC). According to this source, CILA receives annual water allocation information from the U.S. and communicates it to the state of Baja California and SARH to coordinate irrigation planning in the Mexican delta region.
