# Sharp and Birman

## Summary
Sharp and Birman added evidence in 1963 for two additional glaciations in the Mono Basin: Mono Basin glaciation (possibly Illinoian equivalent) and Tenaya glaciation (intermediate Wisconsin). Their work expanded the understanding of the region's glacial chronology beyond Blackwelder's four-stage model.
