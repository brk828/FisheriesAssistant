# Amargosa Speckled Dace

## Summary
Amargosa Speckled Dace is an endemic fish species found in the Death Valley System. This source identifies it as one of the species that would be secondarily influenced by the MX Missile System through impacts to groundwater systems, despite being more distantly removed from direct construction zones.
