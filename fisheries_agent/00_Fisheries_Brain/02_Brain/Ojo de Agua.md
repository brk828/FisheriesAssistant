# Ojo de Agua

## Summary
Ojo de Agua is a spring system southeast of Cananea, Sonora, Mexico where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva occurs at mid-elevation (around 4,700-4,740 feet). The plant is found uncommonly among sedges and rushes along pool banks, though the spring system supplies water to Cananea, representing a threat to the habitat.
