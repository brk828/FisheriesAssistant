# Population Biology and Physiological Ecology

## Summary
Population Biology and Physiological Ecology (PBPE) is an NSF program that significantly supports genetics research across diverse organisms. This source reports that genetics components appeared in 37.3% of PBPE awards in FY 83, increasing to 42.6% in FY 85 and 71-72% in preliminary FY 86 data, with total genetics funding of $10,387K over FY 83-85 for 197 awards spanning theory, plants, invertebrates, and vertebrates.
