# Baetidae

## Summary
Baetidae is a major family of mayflies (order Ephemeroptera) that comprises small, agile aquatic nymphs widely distributed across freshwater ecosystems and commonly used as bioindicators of water quality due to their sensitivity to pollution and habitat disturbance. These mayflies are ecologically important as primary consumers in aquatic food webs and represent a significant food source for fish populations, making them valuable subjects for both fisheries and conservation biology research. The family's diverse sensory structures and developmental complexity across multiple instar stages make Baetidae nymphs particularly useful organisms for studying aquatic insect biology and assessing the health of lotic and lentic environments.
