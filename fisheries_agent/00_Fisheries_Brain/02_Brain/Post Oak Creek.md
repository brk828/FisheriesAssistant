# Post Oak Creek

## Summary
Post Oak Creek is a stream in White County, Tennessee that served as a control site in this study. This source collected 40 control redeye bass specimens from Post Oak Creek to establish baseline meristic and biochemical genetic characteristics for comparison with the potentially hybridized Roaring River population.
