# Shasta Lake

## Summary
Shasta Lake, a major California reservoir system, served as an important site for kokanee salmon (*Oncorhynchus nerka*) introduction and management during the mid-20th century as part of efforts to establish self-sustaining landlocked salmon populations in California freshwater systems. As a large lacustrine ecosystem, Shasta Lake provided suitable habitat for kokanee life-history completion, including spawning in tributary streams and rearing in open-water environments, making it a critical location for both fisheries stockings and subsequent biological studies on introduced salmonid populations.
