# Colorado Environmental Coalition

## Summary
The Colorado Environmental Coalition is an environmental advocacy organization that, along with the Southern Utah Wilderness Alliance, was represented by the Sierra Club Legal Defense Fund in this 1991 legal notice. The organization was one of the parties formally notifying the U.S. Secretary of the Interior and Bureau of Reclamation of violations of the Endangered Species Act concerning the failure to designate critical habitat for the razorback sucker.
