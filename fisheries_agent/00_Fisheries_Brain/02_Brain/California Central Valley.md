# California Central Valley

## Summary
California Central Valley is a region where water development for hydroelectric production, irrigation, domestic water supplies, and flood control has severely impacted Chinook salmon habitat. This source reports that these water development activities have restricted or eliminated much of the spawning habitat for Sacramento River Chinook salmon in this valley.
