# University Of Nevada Las Vegas

## Summary
The University of Nevada Las Vegas serves as an important academic institution supporting research and recovery efforts for endangered native fish species in the Colorado River Basin, particularly through its involvement with the Lower Basin Recovery Implementation Scientific Workgroup. UNLV ichthyologists and researchers have contributed critical field studies and distributional documentation for threatened species like razorback suckers in Lake Mead, helping to inform conservation strategies and management decisions for native fish recovery programs in the Lower Colorado River.
