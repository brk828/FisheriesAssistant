# Sunfish

## Summary
Sunfish (family Centrarchidae) are small to medium-sized freshwater fishes native to North America that have become a significant conservation concern in aquatic ecology due to their role as invasive species in sensitive ecosystems. These opportunistic predators and competitors pose substantial threats to endangered native fish species, particularly in isolated habitats like spring-fed ponds and restricted ranges, where their introduction can cause severe population declines through predation on eggs, larvae, and juveniles. Their widespread use in recreational fishing and pond stocking has facilitated their establishment in non-native waters, making them a key species of management concern in freshwater conservation efforts.
