# Colorado Desert Fringe-Toed Lizard

## Summary
The Colorado Desert Fringe-Toed Lizard (Uma notata) is a desert-dune specialist found in extremely arid areas south and east of Yuma, Arizona, mostly on military lands including the Yuma Dunes and Mohawk Dunes. This species is a federal candidate species and Arizona state candidate for listing, and is also considered a "species of special concern" in California; monitoring procedures are being developed to track population trends in its isolated populations.
