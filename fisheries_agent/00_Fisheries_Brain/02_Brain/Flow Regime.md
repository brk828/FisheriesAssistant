# Flow Regime

## Summary
Flow regime refers to the pattern and timing of water flow in streams and rivers. This source notes that Arizona's streams exhibit high natural variability in flow, with only about 8,000 km of the state's 240,300 total stream-km supporting perennial flow, while the remainder are ephemeral or intermittent either naturally or due to human intervention. The source emphasizes that dams and hydroelectric power demands have dramatically altered downstream flow regimes through modified water release schedules, causing large daily discharge fluctuations and major modifications in seasonal hydrologic patterns.
