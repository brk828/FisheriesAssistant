# Papigochic

## Summary
Papigochic is a Mexican river system where Gila populations have 8,9-9,8 fin ray counts, modal vertebrae counts of either 41 or 42, 60-70 lateral line scales (intermediate scale size), and faint or weak basal radii on scales. These populations are part of the Rio Grande fauna group.
