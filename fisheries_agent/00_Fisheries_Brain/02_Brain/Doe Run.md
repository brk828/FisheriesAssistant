# Doe Run

## Summary
Doe Run is a spring-fed tributary stream of the Ohio River located in Meade County, Kentucky, that has served as an important study site for ichthyological research on fish population dynamics and community structure. Rising as a large torrent spring near Ekron, the stream flows approximately 3.5 miles before entering Doe Valley Lake, an impoundment on its course to the Ohio River, providing researchers with a defined aquatic ecosystem suitable for investigating age, growth, and population characteristics of cyprinid fishes such as the Western Blacknose Dace. The stream's stable spring-fed hydrology and accessibility have made it a valuable reference site for understanding fish assemblage composition and life history patterns in temperate North American freshwater systems.
