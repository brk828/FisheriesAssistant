# Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge is a critical conservation area located in the Pecos River system near Roswell, New Mexico, that represents part of the historic range of endemic and endangered fish species, particularly those adapted to the refuge's unique gypsum sinkhole and saline aquatic habitats. The refuge serves as a protected refuge for native ichthyofauna within a 650+ kilometer reach of the Pecos River extending into Texas, making it essential for the preservation of regionally distinct fish populations and their specialized ecological niches. As a designated wildlife refuge, it functions as a foundational site for implementing conservation agreements and recovery programs aimed at protecting threatened and endangered aquatic species within the Pecos River basin.
