# Lepomis Cyanellus

## Summary
Lepomis cyanellus, commonly known as the green sunfish, is a native centrarchid (sunfish family) species that serves as an important model organism in aquatic ecology research for studying predator-prey interactions and chemical communication in freshwater systems. This predatory fish species produces waterborne mucus cues that elicit strong chemically-mediated predator avoidance responses in aquatic invertebrates such as isopods, making it a valuable subject for understanding non-contact predation risk assessment in freshwater ecosystems. As both a native species and effective predator, L. cyanellus plays a significant ecological role in fish assemblages and represents an important component of freshwater fish communities, particularly in systems where it may interact with other native and non-native fish species.
