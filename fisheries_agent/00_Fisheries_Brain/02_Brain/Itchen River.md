# Itchen River

## Summary
The Itchen River in England served as a comparative system in the Blue River study. It exhibited annual primary production of 2.0 kg/m²/year with a P/R ratio of 0.54 (heterotrophic), considerably lower than the Blue River's granite reach productivity.
