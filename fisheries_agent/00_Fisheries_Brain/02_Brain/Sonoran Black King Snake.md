# Sonoran Black King Snake

## Summary
The Sonoran Black King Snake is a color variety cited in the petition as available for $35-$50, representing one of the more affordable captive-bred king snake morphs. It was used to illustrate how captive propagation had made Arizona-region king snake varieties commercially available at moderate prices.
