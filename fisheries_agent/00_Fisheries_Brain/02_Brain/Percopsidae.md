# Percopsidae

## Summary
Percopsidae is a family of small, primarily freshwater fishes native to North America, classified as a primary freshwater fish lineage with limited salt tolerance. Members of this family, commonly known as trout-perches and pirate perches, are characterized by their primitive spiny-rayed fin structures and are considered biogeographically significant indicators of freshwater fish fauna composition and historical continental fish distribution patterns. The family's presence and distribution have been used in phylogeographic studies to understand freshwater ecosystem history and the relationships between isolated drainage systems in North America.
