# Stream Habitat Classification

## Summary
Stream habitat classification involves categorizing stream sections into distinct structural units based on flow and depth characteristics. This study classified Durant Creek habitat into alternating pools, runs, and riffles, with discrete patches (pools and runs) of 10-15 m length serving as the spatial units for tracking fish movement in the one-dimensional diffusion model.
