# Coho Salmon

## Summary
Coho Salmon (*Oncorhynchus kisutch*) are anadromous salmonids native to North Pacific coastal waters that migrate between ocean and freshwater environments to complete their life cycle. As a keystone species in Pacific Northwest aquatic ecosystems, coho salmon are significant both ecologically and culturally, serving as indicators of watershed health and supporting indigenous subsistence and recreational fisheries. In fisheries management and conservation contexts, coho populations are actively monitored and managed through hatchery programs and habitat restoration efforts to sustain stocks impacted by dam construction, habitat degradation, and other anthropogenic stressors in major river systems like the Columbia River.
