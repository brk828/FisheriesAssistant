# Cincinnati Water Research Laboratory

## Summary
The Cincinnati Water Research Laboratory is a facility of the Federal Water Pollution Control Administration that initiated this destratification research in 1962. This source indicates the laboratory conducted the study to develop engineering methods for maintaining water quality in impoundments used as sources for downstream dilution water, as part of streamflow augmentation programs authorized under the 1961 Federal Water Pollution Control Act amendments.
