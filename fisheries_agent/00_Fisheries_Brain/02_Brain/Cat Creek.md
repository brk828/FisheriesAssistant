# Cat Creek

## Summary
Cat Creek is a tributary of Campbell Blue Creek that marks the approximate upstream limit of Loach Minnow distribution in the Campbell Blue Creek system. Based on records from 1978-1992, Loach Minnow distribution in Campbell Blue Creek extended from near Cat Creek downstream to the mouth of the creek.
