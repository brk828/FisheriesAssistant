# Stone and Pasko

## Summary
Stone and Pasko (1946) conducted summer and fall food habits research on pikeperch that found 46% of stomachs empty (n=64). This empty-stomach rate is comparable to other warm-season studies but substantially lower than the 78.3% empty rate observed during winter in Oneida Lake.

## Context

> This study examined winter food habits of [[Pikeperch]] (Stizostedion vitreum) in [[Oneida Lake]], New York during the winters of 1951-52 and 1952-53.
