# Riffle Sculpin

## Summary
The Riffle Sculpin (*Cottus gulosus*) is a benthic freshwater fish species commonly found in fast-flowing riverine habitats, particularly in riffle zones of Pacific coastal streams. As a small, bottom-dwelling resident species, it serves as an important component of lotic fish assemblages and is frequently used as an indicator organism in studies examining microhabitat utilization and fish community responses to environmental changes in California stream systems. Its presence and population dynamics in studies like those of the Santa Ynez River drainage provide valuable insights into the ecological integrity and habitat quality of running-water ecosystems.
