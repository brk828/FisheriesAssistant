# San Solomon Springs

## Summary
San Solomon Springs in Toyah Creek, Texas, is a spring system habitat where a healthy population of Ictalurus lupus was located during the 1982 field surveys. Thousands of individuals were found at this location, representing one of the robust populations identified in the study.
