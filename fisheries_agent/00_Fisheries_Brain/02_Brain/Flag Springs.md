# Flag Springs

## Summary
Flag Springs is a spring complex in Nye County, Nevada, consisting of multiple spring pools and outflows that historically supported White River Spinedace and other native fishes. As of 1991, only the northern spring pool of this complex retained the last remaining population of White River Spinedace (estimated under 50 individuals), making it critical for species survival; the springs are located on the Wayne E. Kirch Wildlife Management Area owned by the State of Nevada, and habitat improvements were undertaken in 1992 to remove excess vegetation and install structures, though largemouth bass presence above fish barriers poses an ongoing threat.
