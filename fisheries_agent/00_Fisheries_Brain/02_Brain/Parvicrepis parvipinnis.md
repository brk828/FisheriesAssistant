# Parvicrepis parvipinnis

## Summary
Parvicrepis parvipinnis is a small fish species inhabiting the surf zone at low water mark that serves as the host for the parasitic copepod Impexus hamondi. The parasite was found deeply embedded in the visceral cavity of a 19.4 mm specimen collected at Long Reef, with the parasite's trunk protruding from the body wall.
