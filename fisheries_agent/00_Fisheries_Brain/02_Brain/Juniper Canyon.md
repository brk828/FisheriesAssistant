# Juniper Canyon

## Summary
Juniper Canyon is another name used in this source for Juniper Springs Canyon, an upper canyon area of the Yampa River. The source reports that the largest Colorado squawfish specimen captured (820mm) came from Juniper Canyon and represented one of the largest documented captures from the Yampa River in years.
