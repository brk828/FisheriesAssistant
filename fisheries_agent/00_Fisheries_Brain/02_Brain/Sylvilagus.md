# Sylvilagus

## Summary
Sylvilagus is the genus of cottontail rabbits, with 10 specimens identified at La Lomita Pequeña representing 24% of identified mammalian remains. Sylvilagus remains were found in cremations and various site contexts, with species-level identification not attempted due to small sample size and fragmentary condition.
