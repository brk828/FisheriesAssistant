# Sullivan Lake

## Summary
Sullivan Lake is located at the headwaters of the Verde River. The proposed Prescott/Yavapai-Prescott water diversion would extract water from approximately 2.5 miles downstream from Sullivan Lake in Yavapai County, Arizona.
