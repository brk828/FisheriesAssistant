# Fortune Cove

## Summary
Fortune Cove is a stocking location in Lake Mohave where razorback suckers were released during telemetry studies. Thirty-five percent of telemetered adult razorback suckers released at Fortune Cove dispersed upstream to shallow gravel bars between Willow Beach and Hoover Dam, where large aggregations were observed during spawning season.
