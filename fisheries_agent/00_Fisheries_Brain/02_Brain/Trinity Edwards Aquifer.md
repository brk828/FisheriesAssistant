# Trinity Edwards Aquifer

## Summary
The Trinity Edwards Aquifer is a shared groundwater resource that provides 50% of river flow during low-flow periods in the 100 miles upstream of Amistad Reservoir in the Rio Grande/Rio Bravo basin. This aquifer represents one of the already limited water resources in the border region that is threatened by increased consumption and pollution.
