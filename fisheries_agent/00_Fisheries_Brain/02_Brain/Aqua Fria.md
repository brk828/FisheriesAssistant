# Aqua Fria

## Summary
The Aqua Fria is a river drainage within the Gila River system in Arizona where the Spikedace historically occurred. According to this source, the Spikedace was last found in the Aqua Fria drainage in 1943 and has since been extirpated from this system, representing part of the species' catastrophic 94% reduction in historic range.
