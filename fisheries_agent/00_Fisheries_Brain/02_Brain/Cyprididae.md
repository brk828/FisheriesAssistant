# Cyprididae

## Summary
Cyprididae is the family of ostracod crustaceans to which Cypretta kawatai belongs. The family was established by Baird in 1845 and includes the genus Cypretta.

## Context

> This paper formally validates the new species **Cypretta kawatai** Sohn and Kornicker, 1972, a freshwater ostracod (Crustacea) from Brazil.
