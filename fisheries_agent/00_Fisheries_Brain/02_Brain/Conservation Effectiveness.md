# Conservation Effectiveness

## Summary
Conservation Effectiveness refers to the ability of an area to maintain biodiversity values over time through intact ecological processes. The BIOTA Project used this concept as a key criterion for selecting biodiversity areas, considering factors such as population viability, habitat quality, functioning natural processes, manageable boundaries, feasibility of maintaining natural processes like fire and flooding, and current or projected land use threats, with watersheds and larger units generally having greater conservation effectiveness.
