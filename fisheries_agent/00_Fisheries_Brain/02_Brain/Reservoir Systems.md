# Reservoir Systems

## Summary
Reservoir systems like Box Canyon Reservoir require specialized management approaches for invasive species control that account for habitat stratification and spawning locations. The Northern Pike suppression program demonstrates long-term commitment to reservoir management, with 19,430 pike removed since 2012 and declining CPUE trends indicating effectiveness of targeted mechanical removal at spawning aggregations.
