# Don drainage area

## Summary
The Don drainage area is a river catchment system in Yorkshire, England, between Sheffield and Barnsley. Howbrook Reservoir, where the first British record of Ergasilus sieboldi was documented, is located within this drainage area.

## Context

> This report documents the first British record of the parasitic copepod *[[Ergasilus sieboldi]]* Nordmann, discovered in July 1967 on [[Brown Trout]] (*Salmo trutta*) in [[Howbrook Reservoir]], Yorkshire.
