# Clay Phacelia

## Summary
Clay Phacelia is a threatened or endangered plant species dependent on insect pollination. The opinion concluded that the grasshopper control program would likely jeopardize this species due to pesticide-caused mortality of its pollinators, requiring a 3-mile buffer zone around populations to exclude aerial ultra-low-volume spraying or use of alternative control methods like carbaryl bait.
