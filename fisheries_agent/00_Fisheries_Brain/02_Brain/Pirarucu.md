# Pirarucu

## Summary
Pirarucu is a large freshwater fish species for which mark-recapture methods are used in fisheries assessment. This study cites pirarucu fisheries assessment as one of the contemporary applications where closed population mark-recapture estimators are employed, and where the bias-adjusted methods evaluated in this study (particularly Chapman for two-visit and adjusted Schumacher-Eschmeyer for multi-visit studies) would provide improved population size estimates.
