# Basin And Range Province

## Summary
The Basin and Range Province is a physiographic and tectonic region characterized by Cenozoic crustal extension that creates a distinctive landscape of alternating mountain ranges and sedimentary basins, relevant to fish science because its geological heterogeneity produces spatially varied groundwater geochemistry with distinct ionic signatures from different bedrock sources (limestone, granite, volcanic rock). This geochemical variability directly influences aquatic habitat conditions and water chemistry in isolated aquatic systems, making the province's complex geological structure fundamental to understanding fish dispersal patterns, population isolation, and biogeographic distribution in arid and semi-arid aquatic ecosystems.
