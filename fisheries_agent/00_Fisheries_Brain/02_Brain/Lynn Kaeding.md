# Lynn Kaeding

## Summary
Lynn Kaeding was a fisheries biologist involved in endangered fish research and identification methods for Colorado River species. This source indicates he presented preliminary work on using fin ray counts to distinguish young-of-year humpback chub from other chub species (based on 1982-1985 collections) as a field tool, and he suggested that frozen milt from male Colorado Pikeminnow could be provided to support hatchery spawning efforts.
