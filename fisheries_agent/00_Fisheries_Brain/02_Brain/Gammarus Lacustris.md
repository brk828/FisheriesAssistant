# Gammarus Lacustris

## Summary
Gammarus lacustris is a freshwater amphipod crustacean that serves as a critical component of aquatic food webs in coldwater lake and stream ecosystems. As a detritivorous macroinvertebrate, it plays an important ecological role in organic matter decomposition and nutrient cycling while simultaneously functioning as a key food resource for fish species such as trout. The abundance and quality of G. lacustris populations can directly influence fish nutrition and growth, making it an important indicator species for monitoring ecosystem health and food web productivity in freshwater systems.
