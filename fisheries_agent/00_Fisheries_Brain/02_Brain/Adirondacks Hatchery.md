# Adirondacks Hatchery

## Summary
Adirondacks Hatchery is one of six New York State fish hatchery facilities that participated in this feeding trial experiment. The facility provided paired ponds for comparing all-pellet versus meat-supplemented diets for Brook Trout and Brown Trout during the November 1957 through April 1958 study period.
