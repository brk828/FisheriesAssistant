# Rio Grande Silvery Minnow

## Summary
The Rio Grande Silvery Minnow (Notropis simus simus) is a small native cyprinid fish endemic to the Rio Grande drainage of New Mexico that has experienced severe population decline and is considered functionally extinct in its native range, with the last verified record dating to 1964 despite intensive recent surveys. This species is listed as State Endangered in New Mexico and serves as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health in the Rio Grande system, where it historically inhabited clear, flowing riverine habitats that have been dramatically altered by water management, dam construction, and changes in dissolved solids concentration. The silvery minnow's conservation status reflects broader challenges in Southwestern river ecology related to water scarcity, habitat fragmentation, and water quality degradation in the Rio Grande basin.
