# Crotalus willardi obscurus

## Summary
Crotalus willardi obscurus is a subspecies of ridgenosed rattlesnake with restricted mountain distribution. This source notes its discovery in the Sierra San Luis area and lists it among the 22 protected reptile species (categorized as threatened, special protection, or rare) found in the reserve.
