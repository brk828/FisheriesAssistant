# Ecology Program

## Summary
The Ecology Program is one of four NSF programs within the Division of Biotic Systems and Resources that funds genetics research. According to this source, its genetics funding increased from 1.0% to 2.1% of its budget between FY 83-85, totaling $352K over three years for 7 awards, with substantial increases expected in FY 86 for microbial ecology research.
