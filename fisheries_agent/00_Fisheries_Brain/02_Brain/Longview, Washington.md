# Longview, Washington

## Summary
Longview, Washington is a location in the Pacific Northwest where the Abernathy Channel facility is situated, approximately 16 miles to the east of the Salmon-Cultural Laboratory. This source describes it as the geographic reference point for the experimental spawning channel operated from 1959-1966.

## Context

> This technical paper reports on seven seasons (1959-1966) of experimental operation of the [[Abernathy Channel]], a 2,000-foot artificial spawning and incubation channel near [[Longview, Washington]].
