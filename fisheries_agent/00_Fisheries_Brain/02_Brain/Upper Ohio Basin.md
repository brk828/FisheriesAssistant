# Upper Ohio Basin

## Summary
The Upper Ohio Basin is defined in this source as waters above the mouth of the Kanawha River in West Virginia. The basin's fish fauna shows closest zoogeographic affinity to Eurasia and includes several families endemic to North America, with origins tracing to ancient continental connections more than 100 million years ago.
