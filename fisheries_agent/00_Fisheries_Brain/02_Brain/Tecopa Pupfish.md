# Tecopa Pupfish

## Summary
The Tecopa Pupfish (*Cyprinodon nevadensis calidae*) is a spring-endemic subspecies from the Ash Meadows/Death Valley region of Nevada-California that is likely extinct or extremely rare in its native habitat, with no confirmed sightings in recent surveys despite occasional unconfirmed local reports. As a representative of the highly specialized and geographically isolated pupfish fauna of the Great Basin thermal springs, it exemplifies the conservation challenges facing desert aquatic endemics threatened by habitat alteration from geothermal development and other anthropogenic disturbances. Its presumed loss underscores the critical importance of establishing refuge populations and conducting targeted surveys for surviving populations of these irreplaceable spring-endemic fishes.
