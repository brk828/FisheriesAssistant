# Tres Alamos Falls

## Summary
Tres Alamos Falls is a site located in the Phoenix District of Arizona (T10N R0W NE NW Sec.13) on Bureau of Land Management public lands. According to this source, it was one of two Phoenix District sites where desert pupfish were successfully introduced in August 1982 as part of the recovery effort for this endangered species.
