# Bear Canyon Reservoir

## Summary
Bear Canyon Reservoir is a privately-owned water body in the Mimbres River drainage system in Grant County, New Mexico, where the endangered Chihuahua chub occurs. A key question raised in this recovery plan review is whether this reservoir is a mainstem impoundment or an off-river reservoir, and whether chubs occur in associated discharge streams or tail-race areas.
