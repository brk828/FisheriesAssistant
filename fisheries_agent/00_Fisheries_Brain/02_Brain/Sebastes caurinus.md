# Sebastes caurinus

## Summary
Sebastes caurinus (Copper Rockfish) is a Pacific rockfish species mentioned for comparative context. Patten (1973) found that Copper Rockfish moved into rock piles in winter, with only 1-2 of the largest fish visible outside the interstices, demonstrating similar seasonal shelter-seeking behavior.
