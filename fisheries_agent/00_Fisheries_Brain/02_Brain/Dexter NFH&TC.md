# Dexter NFH&TC

## Summary
Dexter National Fish Hatchery & Technology Center is a federal facility that maintains broodstock of endangered Bonytail Chub. According to this source, Dexter has received all Bonytail Chub captured from Lake Mohave since 1987 to increase genetic variation of their broodstock, which originally came from 10 fish, though none of the fish captured since 1987 had successfully spawned at the facility. In the 1993 incident documented here, 7 of 15 transported fish died after arrival at Dexter, with pathology showing sloughing of necrotic tissue from the caudal peduncle area.
