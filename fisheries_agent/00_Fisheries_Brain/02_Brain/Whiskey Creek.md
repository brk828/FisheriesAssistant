# Whiskey Creek

## Summary
Whiskey Creek is a headwater stream in the West Fork Gila River drainage that supports one of only four pure Gila Trout populations confirmed by this genetic study. The population appears extremely small and has never been replicated through translocation efforts, making it a high priority for conservation; the study recommends establishing a broodstock from at least 25 males and 25 females over multiple years before attempting translocation to avoid genetic bottlenecks.
