# Apalachicola-Chattahoochee-Flint Basin

## Summary
The Apalachicola-Chattahoochee-Flint (ACF) Basin spans Alabama, Florida, and Georgia and is the native range of Shoal Bass. This source notes it is the second-most impounded watershed east of the Mississippi River with over 1,400 individual impoundments, and that Shoal Bass populations have declined throughout the basin due to dams, non-native black basses, and angling mortality.
