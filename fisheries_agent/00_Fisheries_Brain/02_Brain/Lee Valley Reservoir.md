# Lee Valley Reservoir

## Summary
Lee Valley Reservoir is a stocked coldwater lake within the historic native range of Apache Trout in Arizona's White Mountains that serves as critical habitat for conservation breeding populations of this threatened species. The reservoir was managed as part of Arizona's Apache Trout recovery program, with populations derived from the genetically pure East Fork strain produced at Williams Creek National Fish Hatchery. As a high-elevation impoundment, it functioned as both a refuge population and a recreational fishery resource within the broader Apache Trout restoration strategy in the White Mountains ecosystem.
