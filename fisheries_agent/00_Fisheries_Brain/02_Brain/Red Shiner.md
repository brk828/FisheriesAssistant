# Red Shiner

## Summary
The Red Shiner is a small, invasive cyprinid fish native to the central United States that has become established in western river systems, particularly the Colorado River Basin, where it represents a significant threat to native fish species through predation and competition for resources. As a generalist species highly adapted to disturbed habitats, the Red Shiner has proliferated in reservoirs and degraded stream reaches, contributing to the decline of endemic species such as the Colorado Pikeminnow and Humpback Chub. Its presence in critical habitat areas is considered a major biotic constraint to the recovery of endangered native fishes in the region.
