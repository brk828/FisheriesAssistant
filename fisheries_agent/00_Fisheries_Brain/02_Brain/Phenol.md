# Phenol

## Summary
Phenol is a toxic organic compound that represents a significant water pollutant of concern in aquatic toxicology research, particularly regarding its acute lethal effects on freshwater fish species such as rainbow trout. In the context of fish science and aquatic ecology, phenol has been studied extensively to determine its toxicity thresholds and lethal concentrations, often in combination with other heavy metals and pollutants, to understand how industrial and chemical contamination impacts fish survival and ecosystem health. Understanding phenol toxicity is critical for water quality standards, pollution monitoring, and the protection of aquatic organisms in contaminated freshwater environments.
