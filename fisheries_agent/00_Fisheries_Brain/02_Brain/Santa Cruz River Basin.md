# Santa Cruz River Basin

## Summary
The Santa Cruz River Basin is a hydrological and ecological system in Arizona that encompasses critical freshwater habitat for federally listed aquatic species and has been subject to extensive water development projects including diversions and reclamation schemes that require environmental assessment under the Endangered Species Act and Fish and Wildlife Coordination Act. As a region of significant concern for ichthyological conservation, the basin's management involves coordination between federal agencies (such as the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and Bureau of Reclamation) and state authorities (particularly Arizona Game and Fish Department) to evaluate how water operations and infrastructure projects affect native fish populations and aquatic ecosystems. The basin represents a case study in the challenges of balancing water resource development with the protection of endangered aquatic species in arid southwestern United States watersheds.
