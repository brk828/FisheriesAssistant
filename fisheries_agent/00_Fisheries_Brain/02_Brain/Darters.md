# Darters

## Summary
Darters are a diverse group of small, benthic freshwater fish belonging primarily to the family Percidae, characterized by their bottom-dwelling habits and preference for swift-flowing stream and riffle habitats. These fish are important subjects of ichthyological study and conservation concern due to their high species diversity (600+ species recognized), ecological significance as forage organisms in stream food webs, and sensitivity to habitat degradation, making them valuable indicators of stream health and water quality in North American freshwater ecosystems.
