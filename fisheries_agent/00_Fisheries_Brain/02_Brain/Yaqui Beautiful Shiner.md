# Yaqui Beautiful Shiner

## Summary
The Yaqui beautiful shiner (Cyprinella formosa) is a small, silvery cyprinid fish with orange to yellow-orange fins, listed as threatened in 1984. Historically occurring in southeastern Arizona and the Mimbres River Basin in southern New Mexico, it was re-introduced to the USA in 1990 when fish collected from Arroyo Moctezuma, Chihuahua, Mexico were stocked into Twin Pond on San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge, where they are now reproducing and thriving in pond habitats. Full delisting cannot be determined until viable self-sustaining populations are demonstrably secure within the historic range in Mexico and populations are established and secure in both Arizona and New Mexico.
