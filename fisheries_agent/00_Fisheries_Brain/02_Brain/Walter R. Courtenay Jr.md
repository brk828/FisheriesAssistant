# Walter R. Courtenay Jr

## Summary
Walter R. Courtenay Jr. was a prominent ichthyologist and conservation biologist who served as chair of the ASIH (American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists) Environmental Quality Committee and played a significant role in documenting range expansions of fish species, particularly non-native and invasive fish populations in Florida during the 1970s-1990s. His work bridged scientific research and policy advocacy, contributing to understanding how introduced fish species colonize new habitats and informing regulatory responses to biological invasions in aquatic ecosystems. Through administrative leadership and collaborative research with other fish scientists, Courtenay helped establish frameworks for monitoring and addressing environmental quality issues related to fish communities and aquatic conservation.
