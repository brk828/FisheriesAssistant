# Colorado River Fish Conservation

## Summary
Colorado River Fish Conservation refers to the integrated management and restoration efforts aimed at protecting and recovering the native fish species and their aquatic ecosystems within the Colorado River basin, which has been severely altered by damming, water diversions, and other anthropogenic impacts. This conservation approach encompasses habitat restoration, species recovery programs (particularly for endangered endemic species like the razorback sucker), water management strategies, and scientific research to maintain ecological integrity and biodiversity in one of North America's most heavily modified river systems. The concept reflects the broader challenge of balancing competing water demands with the ecological needs of native fish populations that have evolved specifically to thrive in this unique riverine environment.
