# Tucson Resource Area Office

## Summary
The Tucson Resource Area Office is a field office of the Bureau of Land Management located in Arizona. In this 1993 correspondence, Jeffrey R. Simms from this office provided input to the Desert Fishes Recovery Team regarding the team's functions and effectiveness, noting communication challenges with the Fish and Wildlife Service and recommending specific duties the Recovery Team should undertake to improve conservation of endangered desert fishes.
