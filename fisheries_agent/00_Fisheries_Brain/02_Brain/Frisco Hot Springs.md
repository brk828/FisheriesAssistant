# Frisco Hot Springs

## Summary
Frisco Hot Springs is a thermal spring system on the San Francisco River in New Mexico where water temperatures reach 105°F, 32°F above the ambient river temperature. The area near Frisco Hot Springs (Station 1B, approximately 1 km downstream) exhibited the highest fish species diversity in the survey with ten species including five native and five introduced species, with native species clearly dominant at this upstream location.
