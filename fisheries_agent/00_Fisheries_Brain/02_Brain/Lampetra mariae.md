# Lampetra mariae

## Summary
Lampetra mariae is a lamprey species studied in relation to its distribution north of the Carpathian Mountains. This source reports that evidence suggests the species spread through Baltic drainage from the northeast, and that both L. mariae and L. planeri are present between Warsaw and Cracow, though only L. planeri occurs in the Carpathian Mountains themselves.
