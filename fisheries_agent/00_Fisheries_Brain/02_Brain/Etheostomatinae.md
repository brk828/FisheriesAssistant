# Etheostomatinae

## Summary
Etheostomatinae is a subfamily of small benthic percid fishes commonly known as darters, comprising bottom-inhabiting species native to North American freshwater systems that have become important subjects for ichthyological research and conservation due to their ecological roles and sensitivity to habitat degradation. These fishes are frequently used in laboratory studies examining reproductive biology, artificial hybridization, and population genetics, as well as in assessments of water quality and ecosystem health in freshwater aquatic systems. Their diversity and restricted geographic ranges make many darter species significant indicators of conservation status in North American rivers and streams.
