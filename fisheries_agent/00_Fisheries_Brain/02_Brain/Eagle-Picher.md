# Eagle-Picher

## Summary
Eagle-Picher refers to the Eagle-Picher Industries v. EPA court decision (759 F.2d 905, D.C. Cir. 1985) that significantly expanded CERCLA liability for mining operations. The decision ruled that mining wastes containing any substances listed under referenced environmental statutes qualified as 'hazardous substances' under CERCLA, exposing mining operations to liability for releases of naturally occurring inorganics and radionuclides present in virtually all mining wastes, despite the RCRA Bevill Amendment exclusion.
