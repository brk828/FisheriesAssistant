# Coptodon zillii

## Summary
Coptodon zillii (formerly Tilapia zillii), commonly known as Redbelly Tilapia, was one of the species requested by UEC and was listed as prohibited under California regulations. The Department used it as an example of regulatory gaps, noting that while T. zillii was prohibited, other tilapia species with comparable threats (T. aurea and T. nilotica) were not prohibited.
