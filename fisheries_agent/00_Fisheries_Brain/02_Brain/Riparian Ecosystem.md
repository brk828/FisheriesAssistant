# Riparian Ecosystem

## Summary
A riparian ecosystem is the dynamic interface between aquatic and terrestrial environments that encompasses the river or stream channel itself, adjacent floodplain wetlands, and riparian vegetation, functioning as an integrated ecological unit. In fish science and conservation, riparian ecosystems are critical because they provide essential habitat functions including water temperature regulation, food resources, shelter, and spawning grounds for fish populations, while also maintaining water quality and connectivity between fragmented aquatic habitats. The health and integrity of riparian systems directly influence fish community composition, diversity, and resilience, making riparian restoration and protection central priorities in freshwater conservation and fisheries management.
