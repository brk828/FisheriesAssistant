# Colorado River Province

## Summary
The Colorado River Province includes the Colorado River and its tributaries in California. The main river originally supported a fauna of endemic big-river fishes and euryhaline invaders from the Gulf of California, with backwaters and marshes serving as important nursery areas and the presumed home of Desert Pupfish.
