# IFIM

## Summary
IFIM (Instream Flow Incremental Methodology) is a modeling approach used to quantify flow requirements for fish habitat. In the Upper Colorado River Basin recovery program, IFIM is being used along with PHABSIM models to identify physical habitat variables important to endangered fishes and to develop biologically defensible flow recommendations for the Yampa River and Colorado River that support spawning, recruitment, and adult maintenance of species like Colorado Squawfish.
