# Etheostoma Nigrum

## Summary
Etheostoma nigrum, commonly known as the Johnny darter, is a small benthic cyprinodontid fish species widely distributed across North American freshwater streams and rivers. This species serves as an important indicator organism in aquatic ecology and stream health assessments, particularly in studies examining the effects of habitat disturbance such as strip mining and siltation on benthic fish communities. As a bottom-dwelling insectivore inhabiting rocky and sandy substrates, E. nigrum is frequently encountered in biodiversity surveys and is valued for its sensitivity to water quality changes and substrate degradation.
