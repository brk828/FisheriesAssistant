# Dagua River

## Summary
The Dagua River is a Pacific slope drainage in Colombia where gymnotid specimens were collected, including one specimen of Sternarchus leptorhynchus from Cordova. This river system was among the Pacific drainage localities sampled during the 1912-1913 collections.
