# Clark

## Summary
Clark conducted a 1984 study on Bonita Creek that recorded 46 breeding bird species and found average densities of 244 individuals per 40 ha. This earlier work provided baseline data for comparison with the 1985-1986 study, which found 62 species and 452 individuals per 40 ha, with a combined total of 64 summering species between the two studies.

## Context

> This 1987 baseline report documents vegetation and breeding bird communities along a 3 km cattle exclosure established in 1984 on [[Lower Bonita Creek]], [[Graham County]], [[Arizona]].
