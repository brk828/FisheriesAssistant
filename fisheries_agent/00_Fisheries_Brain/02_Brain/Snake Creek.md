# Snake Creek

## Summary
Snake Creek is a stream in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that required hiking to access for the 1988 Apache Trout survey. The population exhibited a male-biased sex ratio of 3.8:1 (19 males to 5 females), with 24 total specimens collected for genetic and morphometric analysis.
