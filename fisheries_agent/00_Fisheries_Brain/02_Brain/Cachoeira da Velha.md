# Cachoeira da Velha

## Summary
Cachoeira da Velha is a waterfall on the Rio Novo in Brazil below which Bryconamericus novae was collected. The type specimen (56mm) and 9 paratypes (30-57mm) were found in this habitat near Piabana by J.D. Haseman.
