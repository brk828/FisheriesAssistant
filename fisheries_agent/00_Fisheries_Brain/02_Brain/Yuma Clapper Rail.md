# Yuma Clapper Rail

## Summary
The Yuma Clapper Rail is a federally endangered bird species that inhabits wetland and riparian habitats associated with the Lower Colorado River system, which represents an important focal point for endangered species conservation efforts in the region. While not a fish species itself, the Yuma Clapper Rail is documented in ichthyology and aquatic ecology literature as a key indicator species for wetland habitat quality and health in river systems affected by dam operations, water management, and nonnative species introductions. Its conservation status and habitat requirements make it relevant to comprehensive aquatic ecosystem management and recovery planning efforts that address both fish and wildlife species in altered riverine systems.
