# Globe Ranger District

## Summary
Globe Ranger District is an administrative unit where three alternate sites (Seven Mile Wash, Hess Canyon, and West Fork of Pinto Creek) were identified for Gila Topminnow reintroduction as mitigation for the extirpation at Sycamore Spring. Pre-stocking evaluations found these sites suitable for topminnow populations.
