# Uncompahgre River

## Summary
The Uncompahgre River is a tributary in Colorado within the Colorado River Basin. According to this source, it is the location of the earliest documented record of razorback × flannelmouth sucker hybridization, described by Jordan in 1891 as *Xyrauchen uncompahgre*.
