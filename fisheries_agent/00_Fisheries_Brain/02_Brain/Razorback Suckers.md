# Razorback Suckers

## Summary
The Razorback Sucker (Xyrauchen texanus) is an endangered fish species that was the focus of multiple conservation studies and stocking efforts. The Lake Mohave population was estimated at fewer than 55,000 individuals with most fish exceeding 40 years of age and no detectable natural recruitment since the early 1950s; conservation actions included stocking 853 individuals into Lake Havasu and over 1,200 fish exceeding 300 mm into the Verde River.
