# Chevelon Creek Wildlife Area

## Summary
Chevelon Creek Wildlife Area is a property managed by the Arizona Game and Fish Department along Chevelon Creek. A small portion of the approximately 8-mile reach of Chevelon Creek inhabited by Little Colorado Spinedace is located within this wildlife area, though most of the occupied habitat is on private lands.

## Context

> The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service issued a final rule on September 16, 1987, determining [[Lepidomeda vittata]] ([[Little Colorado Spinedace]]) to be a threatened species under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973.
