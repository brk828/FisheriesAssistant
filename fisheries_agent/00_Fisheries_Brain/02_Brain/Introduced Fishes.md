# Introduced Fishes

## Summary
Introduced fishes are non-native fish species that have been deliberately or accidentally transported and established in aquatic systems outside their native range, often with significant ecological consequences for native fish communities and ecosystem function. The study of introduced fishes encompasses understanding their invasion dynamics, habitat preferences, movement patterns, and impacts on stream fish community assembly and invasion resistance in recipient ecosystems. Management of introduced fish populations is a critical concern in conservation biology, particularly in river systems where non-native species may compete with or prey upon endemic fishes.
