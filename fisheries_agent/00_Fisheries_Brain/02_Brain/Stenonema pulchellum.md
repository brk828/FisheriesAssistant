# Stenonema pulchellum

## Summary
Stenonema pulchellum is a mayfly species whose nymphs are aquatic macroinvertebrates. This source reports calorific values for different size classes of S. pulchellum nymphs ranging from 5295 to 5975 cal/g ash-free dry weight, demonstrating the energy content variation across developmental stages.
