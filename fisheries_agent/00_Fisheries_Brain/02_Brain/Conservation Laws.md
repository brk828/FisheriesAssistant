# Conservation Laws

## Summary
Conservation Laws in New York State established size limits for salmonid species, with a seven-inch minimum for most protected species. Proper identification of salmonids was essential for legal compliance, particularly to distinguish legal-sized Brook Trout from undersized Lake Trout and to avoid mistaking short salmon for legal-sized Brown Trout, with violations subject to fines.
