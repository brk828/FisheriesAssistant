# Pinyon Pine

## Summary
Pinyon Pine is a small pine species characteristic of southwestern woodlands. The lecture notes describe Pinyon Pine-Juniper woodlands as very extensive in Arizona, occurring in the woodland zone with open canopy under semi-arid to semi-humid conditions, though largely modified by grazing.
