# Reservoir Inflow Zones

## Summary
Reservoir inflow zones are transitional areas where rivers enter reservoirs, encompassing a continuum from riverine through transition to lacustrine habitats. This source describes these zones as spatially dynamic areas where Razorback Sucker aggregate, possibly to access turbid, productive water and submerged vegetation, with fish preferentially using shallow water (<2 m depth) and depth use decreasing significantly toward the inflow area.
