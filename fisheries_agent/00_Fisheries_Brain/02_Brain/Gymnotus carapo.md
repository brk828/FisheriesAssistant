# Gymnotus carapo

## Summary
Gymnotus carapo is one of the four most widely distributed gymnotid species, occurring from Central America through the Amazon Basin, Orinoco River, Guiana, Uruguay, southeastern Brazil, and the La Plata Basin. This study collected 8 specimens (75-306 mm) from the Calima River and Truando, noting it reaches the farthest north of any gymnotid, occurring in rivers and lakes on both slopes of Guatemala and recently recorded from the Rio San Juan of trans-Andean Colombia.
