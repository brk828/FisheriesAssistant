# Smith and Harron (1904)

## Summary
Smith and Harron (1904) documented spawning behavior in the Brown Bullhead (Ameiurus nebulosus), a catfish species related to the channel catfish. This source cites their work as part of the existing literature on ictalurid spawning behavior that provided context for understanding channel catfish reproduction.
