# Arctogadus borisovi

## Summary
Arctogadus borisovi (East Siberian Cod) is a mobile coastal Arctic species characterized by elevated cerebellum that does not rest on the medulla oblongata. Its brain structure reflects adaptation to active swimming, consistent with the pattern seen in other mobile coastal gadids.
