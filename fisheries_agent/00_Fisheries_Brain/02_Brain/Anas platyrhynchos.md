# Anas platyrhynchos

## Summary
Anas platyrhynchos is the scientific name for the Mallard duck. This source reports comprehensive ecological studies including age structure, movements, mortality, migrations, wintering quarters, body weight variations throughout the year for both sexes, breeding density in pollarded willows, and artificial breeding experiments.
