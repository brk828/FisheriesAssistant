# Etheostoma

## Summary
Etheostoma is a genus of small freshwater darters within the family Percidae, comprising numerous species distributed primarily throughout eastern North American river drainages. These benthic and semi-benthic fishes are ecologically important indicators of stream health and habitat quality, occupying specific microhabitats such as riffles, pools, and sand substrates where they feed on small invertebrates. The genus has been extensively studied in systematic and taxonomic research, making it a focal point for understanding darter diversity, evolution, and conservation in freshwater ecosystems, particularly regarding the impacts of siltation and habitat degradation on population dynamics.
