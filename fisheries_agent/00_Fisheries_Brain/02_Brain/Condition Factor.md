# Condition Factor

## Summary
Condition factor (K factor) is a quantitative measure of fish body robustness or overall health that describes the relationship between a fish's weight and its length, indicating whether an individual is relatively heavy or light for its given size. This metric is widely used in fisheries science and conservation biology to assess nutritional status, environmental quality, and population health, as it serves as a strong predictor of fish body composition including dry weight and energy reserves. A higher condition factor generally indicates better feeding conditions and physiological condition, making it a valuable indicator for evaluating fish populations across different habitats and time periods.
