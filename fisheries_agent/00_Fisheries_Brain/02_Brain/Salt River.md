# Salt River

## Summary
The Salt River is a major tributary system of the Lower Colorado River Basin in Arizona that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly the endangered razorback sucker and other endemic fishes. As part of an integrated river system modified by dams and water management infrastructure, the Salt River and its associated reservoirs represent important areas for fish conservation efforts, including large-scale stocking programs and habitat management studies aimed at recovering imperiled fish populations. The river's main channel reaches, headwater tributaries, and impoundments provide diverse ecological conditions that influence predation dynamics, fish recruitment, and species interactions relevant to recovery and management objectives in the Colorado River Basin.
