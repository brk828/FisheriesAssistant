# Twin Lakes Tunnel

## Summary
The Twin Lakes Tunnel is an infrastructure feature that augments water flows into Twin Lakes by diverting water from the Western Slope under the Continental Divide. This tunnel supplements the natural inflow from Lake Creek and affects the hydrological regime of the lake system.
