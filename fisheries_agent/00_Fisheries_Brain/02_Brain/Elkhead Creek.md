# Elkhead Creek

## Summary
Elkhead Creek is a tributary of the Yampa River in the Colorado River Basin. This source recommends extending critical habitat for Colorado Squawfish upstream in the Yampa River to the confluence with Elkhead Creek (approximately river mile 96) and into Elkhead Creek upstream to a bridge at Moffat County Road 29 (about 10 miles), based on historic and recent captures and its importance as an area for conditioning during spawning and migration periods.
