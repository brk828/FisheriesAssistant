# Arizona Interagency Natural Areas Coordinating Council

## Summary
The Arizona Interagency Natural Areas Coordinating Council is a multi-agency coordinating body to which the BIOTA Project proposal was presented for evaluation and implementation consideration. It served as the formal recipient and evaluator of the demonstration model proposal developed between November 1992 and August 1993.
