# Creopelates

## Summary
Creopelates is a lernaeocerid genus that closely resembles Impexus in morphology. Impexus is distinguished from Creopelates by its holdfast distribution: Creopelates has short bushy outgrowths around the neck, while Impexus has irregular branching processes at the anterior cephalothorax.
