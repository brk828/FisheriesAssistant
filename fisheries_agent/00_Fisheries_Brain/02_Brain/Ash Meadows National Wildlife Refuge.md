# Ash Meadows National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Ash Meadows National Wildlife Refuge is a protected desert aquatic ecosystem in Nevada that serves as the critical conservation site for the endangered Devils Hole pupfish, an endemic species with a naturally restricted population of fewer than 500 individuals confined to geothermal limestone caverns and springs. The refuge encompasses unique isolated aquatic habitats including Devils Hole, where a shallow rock shelf provides essential feeding and spawning substrate for this species of extreme conservation concern. Establishment of the refuge represented a crucial intervention in conservation biology to protect one of the world's most range-restricted fish populations and its highly specialized geothermal aquatic environment from anthropogenic threats.
