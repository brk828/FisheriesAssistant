# Lerma-Santiago

## Summary
The Lerma-Santiago is the principal westward-flowing river system draining the Mesa Central of Mexico, designated as a natural fish province of Middle America. This source identifies it as the center of a highly endemic fish fauna including approximately 30 species of Goodeidae and multiple Algansea species, though habitat is threatened by water diversion, pollution, and introduced Largemouth Bass.
