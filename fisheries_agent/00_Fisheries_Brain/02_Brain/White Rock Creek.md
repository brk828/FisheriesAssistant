# White Rock Creek

## Summary
White Rock Creek is one of the major tributaries entering the Navarro Mills Reservoir basin in Navarro County, Texas. It was among the tributaries treated with rotenone on March 18, 1963, as part of the preimpoundment fish control operation.

## Context
