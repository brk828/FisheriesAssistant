# Leptophlebiidae

## Summary
Leptophlebiidae is a family of mayflies (order Ephemeroptera) that represents one of the major taxonomic groups within mayfly diversity, as documented in systematic surveys and inventories of aquatic insect fauna. Members of this family are important components of freshwater benthic communities and serve as key bioindicators of water quality and ecosystem health in aquatic ecology. Their nymphs, which are the primary life stage encountered in field sampling, are used extensively in taxonomic identification and bioassessment studies, though adult collection and rearing can present significant challenges in certain geographic regions or environmental conditions.
