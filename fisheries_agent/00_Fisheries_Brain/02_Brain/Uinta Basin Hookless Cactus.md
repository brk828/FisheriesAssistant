# Uinta Basin Hookless Cactus

## Summary
Uinta Basin Hookless Cactus is an endangered cactus species that depends on insect pollinators for reproduction. The Service concluded that aerial pesticide spraying would likely jeopardize this species through loss of halicitid bee pollinators, requiring a 3-mile no-aerial-spray buffer or use of alternative control methods.
