# Delaware Creek

## Summary
Delaware Creek in Texas and New Mexico is a location where a healthy population of Ictalurus lupus was found during the 1982 field surveys. This spring-fed tributary represents one of the diverse habitat types occupied by the species, contrary to expectations based on its "headwater catfish" common name.
