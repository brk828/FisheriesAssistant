# Clark County

## Summary
Clark County, Nevada is the location of two of the three current refugia for the Pahrump Killifish: Corn Creek Springs on the Desert Game Range and Spring Mountain Ranch State Park. These managed populations were established in 1971 and 1983 respectively after the species' natural habitats were destroyed.
