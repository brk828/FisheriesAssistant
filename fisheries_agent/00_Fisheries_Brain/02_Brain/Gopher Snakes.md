# Gopher Snakes

## Summary
Gopher Snakes refer to Pituophis melanoleucus, a native Arizona species proposed for captive propagation with an 18-inch minimum size limit for sale of offspring. This was the largest minimum size among the proposed species in the regulatory framework.
