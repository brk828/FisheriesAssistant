# Watermilfoil

## Summary
Watermilfoil (Myriophyllum spp.) is a genus of aquatic plants that requires management in fish and wildlife habitats. This manual indicates it can be controlled using iso-octyl ester of 2,4-D at 20 lbs. acid equivalent per acre applied during vigorous growth periods.
