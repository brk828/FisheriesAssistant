# U.S. National Park Service

## Summary
The U.S. National Park Service is a federal agency with management jurisdiction over portions of the Colorado River system. In this study, the National Park Service is identified as one of the multi-agency partners involved in coordinating management and conservation efforts for the endangered Razorback Sucker population in Lake Mohave, working alongside the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, Arizona Game and Fish Department, Nevada Department of Wildlife, and U.S. Geological Survey.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary**: The [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) is an endemic big-river fish of the [[Colorado River Basin]].
