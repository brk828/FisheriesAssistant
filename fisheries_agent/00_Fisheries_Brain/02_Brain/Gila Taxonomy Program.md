# Gila Taxonomy Program

## Summary
The Gila Taxonomy Program is a scientific research initiative focused on studying four endangered cyprinid chub species in the Colorado River Basin and associated waterways: Humpback Chub (Gila cypha), Bonytail Chub (Gila elegans), Virgin River Chub (Gila seminuda), and Pahranagat Roundtail Chub (Gila robusta jordani). According to this 1991 subpermit, the program involves authorized capture, measurement, PIT tagging, and tissue sampling of these species across multiple river systems in Utah, Colorado, Arizona, and Nevada, with strict protocols to minimize mortality and coordinate with federal and state agencies.
