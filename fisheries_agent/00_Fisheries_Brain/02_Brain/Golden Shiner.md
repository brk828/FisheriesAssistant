# Golden Shiner

## Summary
The Golden Shiner is a small freshwater minnow species commonly used in ichthyological research, particularly as a sentinel organism for assessing pesticide toxicity and environmental contamination in aquatic systems. This species has been employed in toxicological studies examining the effects of various chemical stressors on fish populations, with populations from contaminated and uncontaminated sources serving as comparative baselines for understanding pesticide susceptibility. In the context of aquatic ecology, Golden Shiners represent an important component of panfish communities and are frequently collected during fish surveys to monitor ecosystem health and species distribution patterns in freshwater environments.
