# Ventrifossa lucifer

## Summary
Ventrifossa lucifer is a deep-sea macrourid fish species found in the Philippine Subregion. This source lists it among the endemic or distinctive macrourid forms collected from the Sulu Sea and associated waters during the Albatross expedition.
