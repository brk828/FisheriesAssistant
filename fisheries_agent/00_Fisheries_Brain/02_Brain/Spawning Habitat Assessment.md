# Spawning Habitat Assessment

## Summary
Spawning habitat assessment identified two primary aggregation areas and characterized specific microhabitat features used by razorback suckers. Spawning occurred on submerged shoreline riffles with cobble-gravel-sand substrates at average depth 0.67 m and velocity 0.64 m/s, during increasing spring flows at temperatures averaging 14°C (range 9-17°C), with specific sites documented in the lower Yampa River and Green River near the Jensen area.
