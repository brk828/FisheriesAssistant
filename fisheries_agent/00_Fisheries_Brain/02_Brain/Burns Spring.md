# Burns Spring

## Summary
A spring in Mohave County, Arizona, that was historically modified during pig farming operations and is the sole habitat for Burns Spring Tryonia. This source documents that by 1980 the critically endangered snail population of approximately 30 individuals was found only in a pool inside an old tunnel rather than the previous upper habitat area.
