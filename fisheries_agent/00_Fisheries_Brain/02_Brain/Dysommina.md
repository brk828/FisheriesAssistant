# Dysommina

## Summary
Dysommina is the type genus of the family Dysomminidae, established because it cannot be properly placed in any previously described eel family. The genus exhibits a unique combination of characters including reduced palatopterygoid bones, ankylosed vomer-ethmoid, paired frontals, and enlarged otic bulla that distinguish it from related eel groups.
