# Lasioglossum zephyrum

## Summary
Lasioglossum zephyrum is a primitively social halictine bee measuring approximately 7.5 mm in length that nests in sandy substrates with burrow entrances about 2 millimeters in diameter. The bees defend their nests by plugging entrances with their heads or abdomens and can dislodge attacking materials, though they are targets of coordinated predatory attacks by pavement ants as documented in this study.
