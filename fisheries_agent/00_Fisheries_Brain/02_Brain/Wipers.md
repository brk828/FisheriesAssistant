# Wipers

## Summary
Wipers (Morone chrysops x Morone saxatilis) are a hybrid fish species that require case-by-case evaluation for stocking in the Upper Colorado River Basin. They are among the additional species listed that cannot be routinely stocked and must undergo detailed review including assessment of potential impacts to threatened and endangered species.
