# Gerald A. Cole

## Summary
Gerald A. Cole was a retired Arizona State University researcher recognized as a world authority on Cuatro Ciénegas crustaceans and desert limnology. He was part of the expert network assembled in 1988 to support the Biosphere Reserve management planning effort.
