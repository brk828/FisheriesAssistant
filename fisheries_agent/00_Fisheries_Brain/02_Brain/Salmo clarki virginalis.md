# Salmo clarki virginalis

## Summary
Salmo clarki virginalis is the Rio Grande Cutthroat Trout, a native salmonid species documented in this study. This source lists it among the 51 native fish species inhabiting drainages east of the Continental Divide in New Mexico as part of the family Salmonidae, representing coldwater stream fauna in the region.
