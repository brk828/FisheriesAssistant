# Ash Fork

## Summary
Ash Fork is one of the ten Arizona development projects in Maxwell's 1935 proposal. The document notes that Ash Fork sits at an elevation of 5,143 feet, which would provide climate moderation similar to other elevated Arizona locations.

## Context
