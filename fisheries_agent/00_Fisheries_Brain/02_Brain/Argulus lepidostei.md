# Argulus lepidostei

## Summary
Argulus lepidostei is a previously described species of parasitic crustacean that shares similar respiratory area shape with the newly described Argulus meehani. It is distinguished from A. meehani by the presence of scales on the basal plate of the second maxilla, which are absent in A. meehani.
