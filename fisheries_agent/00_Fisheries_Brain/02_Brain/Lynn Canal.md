# Lynn Canal

## Summary
Lynn Canal is a waterway in southeastern Alaska where this study was conducted from 1965-75. Three observation sites in lower Lynn Canal (21-43 km north and west of Juneau) at depths of 13-28 m were used to document seasonal rockfish behavior, with water temperatures ranging from 3°C in February to 9°C in August at 20 m depth.
