# Bear Valley Creek

## Summary
Bear Valley Creek is a spring-run Chinook salmon spawning stream in the Salmon River drainage, Idaho. In 1966, an experimental underwater diving survey counted 428-442 live chinook in a four-mile section, and the stream had a spawning escapement with 44% females, the lowest female percentage among spring chinook spawning areas surveyed.
