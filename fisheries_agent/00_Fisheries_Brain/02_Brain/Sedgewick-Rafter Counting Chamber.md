# Sedgewick-Rafter Counting Chamber

## Summary
The Sedgewick-Rafter Counting Chamber is a laboratory device used for quantitative analysis of plankton samples. In this study, it was used at 3.5x to 20x magnification to conduct actual counts of zooplankton in triplicate subsamples collected from Lake Powell.
