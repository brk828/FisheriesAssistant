# Ambloplites ariommus

## Summary
Ambloplites ariommus is a sunfish species in the family Centrarchidae found in Alabama. This source indicates it can be distinguished from the related Ambloplites rupestris by having 4-5 cheek scale rows, compared to 7-8 rows in A. rupestris.
