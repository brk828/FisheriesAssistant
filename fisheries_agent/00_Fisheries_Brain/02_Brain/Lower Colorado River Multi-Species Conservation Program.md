# Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program (MSCP) is a collaborative management framework established in 1995 to coordinate conservation efforts for multiple fish species and aquatic resources throughout the Lower Colorado River Basin. The program operates through institutional oversight structures, including a Biology Subcommittee responsible for implementing and reviewing Interim Conservation Measures, managing research priorities, and allocating resources to address critical biological needs of native fish species in the region. This multi-stakeholder initiative integrates legal agreements, scientific assessment, and adaptive management strategies to balance water resource development with the protection and recovery of endangered and native fish populations.
