# pinnipenis

## Summary
Pinnipenis refers to the species name of Orocastus (Cabrulus) pinnipenis, a new deltocephaline leafhopper measuring 2.7-2.8 mm (male) to 2.7-2.9 mm (female). It is distinguished by finlike apical processes on the male aedeagus and was collected from Vernon, British Columbia and McDonald Pass, Montana.
