# Yuma Desalinization Plant

## Summary
The Yuma Desalinization Plant was the subject of a Bureau of Reclamation study examining whether its operation would significantly impact existing resources at the Cienega de Santa Clara wetland. Preliminary answers from this study were anticipated by the end of 1993.
