# Susan River

## Summary
The Susan River in Lassen County supported a persistent Pumpkinseed population for at least 12 years between 1942 and 1954. The 1954 survey of the lower Susan River near Honey Lake found several hundred individuals ranging from 0.9 to 6.1 inches fork length, indicating a good population, though the origin of this population remained unknown.
