# Gila Complex

## Summary
The Gila Complex refers to a group of closely related Gila species in the Colorado River basin that present significant taxonomic uncertainty, making it difficult to clearly distinguish between species such as the bonytail chub and humpback chub. This taxonomic ambiguity was a major concern for fish conservation efforts in the 1980s, prompting resource managers to seek expert scientific review to resolve systematic questions and clarify the evolutionary relationships within the complex. Understanding and properly classifying these species was considered essential for developing accurate recovery strategies for endangered Colorado River fishes.
