# Idaho Power Company

## Summary
Idaho Power Company is the owner of C.J. Strike Reservoir in south-central Idaho. The company operates the 7,500-acre reservoir for power generation, and the reservoir also supports warm-water fisheries including largemouth bass and black crappie.
