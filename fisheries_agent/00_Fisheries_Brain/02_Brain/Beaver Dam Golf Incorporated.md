# Beaver Dam Golf Incorporated

## Summary
Beaver Dam Golf Incorporated is a private entity that owns a golf course on the west/south side of Beaver Dam Wash near the project area. According to this source, the company conducted unpermitted channelization work in Beaver Dam Wash in 1989, which was part of the extensive human modifications to the stream that have occurred since the mid-1980s.
