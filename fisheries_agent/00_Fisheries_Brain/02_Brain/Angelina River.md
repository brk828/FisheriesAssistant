# Angelina River

## Summary
The Angelina River is a Texas river that flows through Jasper County below Sam Rayburn Dam and forms part of the Dam B Reservoir area. In 1964-1965, new regulations prohibited the use of nets for taking fish from the Angelina River waters in Jasper County below Sam Rayburn Dam, with exceptions only for State rough fish contracts.
