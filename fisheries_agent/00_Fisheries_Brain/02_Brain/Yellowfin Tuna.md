# Yellowfin Tuna

## Summary
Yellowfin tuna (*Thunnus albacares*) is a pelagic fish species belonging to the genus *Thunnus* and represents one of the most commercially important tuna species globally. As a highly migratory, fast-growing predator of tropical and subtropical oceans, yellowfin tuna occupy a key position in marine food webs and serve as important indicators of ocean health and radionuclide accumulation in pelagic ecosystems. The species is of significant concern in fisheries management and conservation biology due to its high exploitation rates and vulnerability to overfishing across major fishing regions.
