# Sonoran Desert Conservation Plan

## Summary
The Sonoran Desert Conservation Plan (SDCP) is a regional multi-species habitat conservation plan established by Pima County, Arizona through Resolution No. 1998-250 in December 1998. The plan aims to address 18 federally listed threatened and endangered species while balancing species recovery, habitat conservation throughout the regional ecosystem, and community economic needs through a cooperative process with the U.S. Department of the Interior and other stakeholders.
