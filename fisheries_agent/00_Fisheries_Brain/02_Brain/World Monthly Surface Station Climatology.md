# World Monthly Surface Station Climatology

## Summary
The World Monthly Surface Station Climatology (WMSSC) is a pre-existing climate database that was incorporated into the GHCN. The GHCN represents a significant improvement over WMSSC, containing 80% more temperature stations and 100% more precipitation stations, with many stations having longer periods of record.
