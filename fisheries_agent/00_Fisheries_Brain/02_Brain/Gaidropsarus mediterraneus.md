# Gaidropsarus mediterraneus

## Summary
Gaidropsarus mediterraneus (Shore Rockling) is a member of subfamily Lotinae with relatively small optic lobes that barely exceed forebrain dimensions. This species has small eyes and relies minimally on visual cues for feeding, with brain structure reflecting reduced visual dependence typical of its ecological niche.
