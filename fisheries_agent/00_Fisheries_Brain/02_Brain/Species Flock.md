# Species Flock

## Summary
A species flock is a group of closely related species that have evolved from a common ancestor within a single geographic area. Minckley (1978, 1984) considered the Cuatro Cienegas cichlids to be a species flock rather than a single polymorphic species, a view supported by Williams et al. (1985), based on the presence of at least three distinct endemic morphological types that show pronounced resource and space partitioning in the basin's aquatic habitats.
