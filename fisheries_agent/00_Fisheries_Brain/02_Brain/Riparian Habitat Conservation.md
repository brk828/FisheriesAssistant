# Riparian Habitat Conservation

## Summary
Riparian habitat conservation refers to the protection and restoration of vegetation and physical structures along riverbanks and stream corridors that are critical for maintaining fish populations and aquatic ecosystem health. These riparian zones provide essential functions including stream temperature regulation, bank stability, nutrient input, and complex in-stream habitat structures that support fish spawning, rearing, and migration. Conservation efforts typically focus on preserving native vegetation, managing water flow, and restoring degraded streamside areas to support both aquatic species and broader ecosystem functions in freshwater environments.
