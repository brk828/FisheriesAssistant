# Bohr Effect

## Summary
The Bohr Effect refers to the reduction in blood oxygen affinity caused by high carbon dioxide levels. This study notes that high free carbon dioxide levels (5.0 mg/L) measured in spawning beds with decomposing eggs in August 1962 can reduce blood oxygen affinity through this mechanism, potentially compounding the stress on fish from already low dissolved oxygen conditions.
