# Los Angeles Aqueduct

## Summary
The Los Angeles Aqueduct is a water conveyance system that contributed to the decline of the Owens Pupfish by diverting most Owens River water out of Owens Valley. These diversions, along with upstream impoundments, destroyed the shallow marshy areas adjacent to the Owens River by preventing normal flooding cycles that historically maintained pupfish habitat.

## Context

> This recovery plan addresses the [[Owens Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon radiosus*), an endangered species endemic to [[Owens Valley]], California.
