# Cetacean

## Summary
Cetaceans (whales, dolphins, and porpoises) are covered by a dedicated Specialist Group within SSC's mammal conservation program. The Cetacean Specialist Group is one of 31 mammal-focused groups within the Commission's approximately 102 total Specialist Groups. This taxonomic group would conduct typical specialist group activities including species status assessments and conservation planning.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium.
