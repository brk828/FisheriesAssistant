# Bog Lake

## Summary
A bog lake is a shallow, acidic waterbody with dark-stained water, low alkalinity, and margins covered with floating mats of sedges, mosses, and ericaceous shrubs. Camerton Lake (28.3 ha, maximum depth 3.0 m) exemplifies this habitat type with pH 6.1, total alkalinity of 3 mg CaCO₃/L, and brown-stained water surrounded by bog and deciduous forest.
