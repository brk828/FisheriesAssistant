# Sturgeon Sucker

## Summary
The Sturgeon Sucker (Catostomus catostomus) is one of eighteen species formerly common in the upper Ohio Basin that have not been reported in recent years in Pennsylvania tributaries. This source lists it among the extirpated species from the region.
