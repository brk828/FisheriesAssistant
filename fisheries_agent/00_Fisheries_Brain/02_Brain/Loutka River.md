# Loutka River

## Summary
The Loutka River is a brown trout stream in Czechoslovakia where zoobenthos and fish population studies were conducted. The river showed an average zoobenthos biomass of 13.5 g/m² and brown trout biomass ranging from 26-211 kg/ha across three sections.
