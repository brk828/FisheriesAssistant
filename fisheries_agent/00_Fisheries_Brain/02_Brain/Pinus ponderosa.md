# Pinus ponderosa

## Summary
Pinus ponderosa is a dominant conifer species in the Sierra San Luis reserve found in multiple forest types. It occurs in conifer forests above 2,100 meters elevation and in oak-juniper forests at lower elevations between 1,800-2,100 meters.
