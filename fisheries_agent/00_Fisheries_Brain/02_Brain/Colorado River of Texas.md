# Colorado River of Texas

## Summary
The Colorado River of Texas is a stream that was captured by the Pecos River during late Pleistocene time. This source documents that following the uplift of the Guadalupe and Sacramento Mountains, the beheaded ancestral Pecos River became a headward eroding stream that slowly worked north and eastward, capturing several streams including the Colorado River of Texas during late Pleistocene time.
