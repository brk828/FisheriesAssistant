# Colorado River Water Conservation District

## Summary
The Colorado River Water Conservation District is a state water management entity collaborating with the Colorado Water Conservation Board on water availability studies for the Recovery Program. In April 1990, they were developing a scope of work for a water availability study on the 15-mile reach of the Colorado River, with a proposed budget of $50,000 for FY-91.
