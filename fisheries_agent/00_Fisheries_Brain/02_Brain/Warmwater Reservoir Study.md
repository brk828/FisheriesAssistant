# Warmwater Reservoir Study

## Summary
The Warmwater Reservoir Study was a research program that contributed to larger average trout sizes during 1967-68, with fish reared to approximately 3 per pound for research purposes. This study was part of the factors that increased overall average fish size from 21.5 per pound to 14.5 per pound.
