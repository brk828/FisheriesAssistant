# Copeo Lake

## Summary
Copeo Lake is a water body in Siskiyou County where Pumpkinseed specimens were collected as part of the documentation of the species' distribution in the upper Klamath River system. This location represented one of several sites where the species was found in the early 1950s in northern California.

## Context
