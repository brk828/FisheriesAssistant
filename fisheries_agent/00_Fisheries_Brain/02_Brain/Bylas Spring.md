# Bylas Spring

## Summary
Bylas Spring is one of the four remaining natural refuge populations of the endangered Gila Topminnow in Arizona, serving as a critical conservation site for studying genetic variation and population diversity in this species. This spring has been specifically investigated for Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) gene variation and microsatellite loci to understand the genetic structure and immunological capacity of isolated Gila Topminnow populations, making it an important location for assessing the species' long-term viability and recovery prospects.
