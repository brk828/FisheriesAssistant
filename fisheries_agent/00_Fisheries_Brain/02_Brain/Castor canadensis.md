# Castor canadensis

## Summary
Castor canadensis (beaver) is an aquatic mammal found in the upper reaches of Cibeque Creek, Canyon Creek, and Carizzo Creek in the study area. Beavers in these locations could be especially disturbed by construction activities which might be sufficient to displace them from their current habitats.
