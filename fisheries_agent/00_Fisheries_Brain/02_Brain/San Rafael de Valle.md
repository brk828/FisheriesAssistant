# San Rafael de Valle

## Summary
San Rafael de Valle is a historic land grant in Arizona. Along with the San Juan de las Boquillas y Nogales grant, this name historically referred to the lands along the upper San Pedro River that were obtained by BLM through private exchange.

## Context

> The [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) released a Draft San Pedro River Management Plan and Environmental Impact Statement for public review in 1988. The plan addresses 47,668 acres along a 36-mile stretch of the upper [[San Pedro River]] near [[Sierra Vista]] in [[Cochise County]], [[Arizona]]....
