# Threatened And Endangered Species

## Summary
Threatened and Endangered Species (TES) in fish science refers to fish populations and communities that face significant risk of extinction or severe population decline due to habitat loss, overexploitation, or ecological disruption, and are formally designated under legislation such as the Endangered Species Act. These designations identify aquatic organisms requiring intensive conservation management and protection, including habitat preservation, population monitoring, and potentially captive breeding or hatchery support to prevent irreversible loss of species and genetic diversity. The conservation of TES represents a critical priority in fisheries management and aquatic ecology, as species extinctions are irreversible phenomena with cascading consequences for ecosystem integrity and biodiversity.
