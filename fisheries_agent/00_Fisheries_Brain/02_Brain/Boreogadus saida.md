# Boreogadus saida

## Summary
Boreogadus saida (Polar Cod) exhibits the most elevated cerebellum among all examined gadids, projecting dorsally above the optic lobes. Living among ice floes and requiring extreme maneuverability for this habitat, this species represents the most extreme expression of cerebellar elevation correlated with highly mobile, active swimming behavior.
