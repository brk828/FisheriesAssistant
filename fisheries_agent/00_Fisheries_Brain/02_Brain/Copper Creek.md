# Copper Creek

## Summary
Copper Creek is a tributary of the Clinch River in Virginia where Noturus flavipinnis was rediscovered in 1969 and found to be fairly common in the lower reaches. The creek flows through a narrow U-shaped valley with short swift riffles and shallow pools (mostly less than 3 feet deep), has a gravel and rubble bottom with white water showing no evidence of pollution, and supports a rich fish fauna of 44 species. The source reports that 28 specimens of N. flavipinnis were collected during two night collections in October 1969 from this location.
