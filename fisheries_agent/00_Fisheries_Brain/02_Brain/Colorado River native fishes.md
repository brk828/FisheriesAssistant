# Colorado River Native Fishes

## Summary
Colorado River Native Fishes refers to the suite of indigenous fish species that historically inhabited the Colorado River system and its associated waters, including endemic and regionally endemic species adapted to the river's unique environmental conditions. These species, such as the Colorado pikeminnow, humpback chub, razorback sucker, and bonytail chub, have become the focus of intensive conservation and management efforts due to population declines caused by dam construction, water diversions, habitat alteration, and the introduction of non-native species. The scientific study and preservation of these native fishes represents a critical concern in Colorado River management and Southwestern aquatic ecology, particularly in protected areas like the Grand Canyon.
