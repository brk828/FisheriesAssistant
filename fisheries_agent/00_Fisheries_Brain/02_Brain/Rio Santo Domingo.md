# Rio Santo Domingo

## Summary
Rio Santo Domingo is a coastal stream in Baja California, Mexico, located at approximately 30°43'N, 116°02'W, flowing into Bahia San Ramon near Vicente Guerrero. In 1995, this river yielded the first freshwater record of Pacific Lamprey from Baja California when an ammocoete was collected about 600 meters above the river's mouth in habitat characterized by sand and gravel substrate, representing the southernmost freshwater occurrence of the species in western North America.
