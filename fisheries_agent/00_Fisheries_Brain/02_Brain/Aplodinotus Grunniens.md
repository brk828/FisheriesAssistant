# Aplodinotus Grunniens

## Summary
Aplodinotus grunniens, commonly known as the freshwater drum, is a large catostomid fish species native to North American river systems that serves as an important indicator of large-river ecosystem health and habitat diversity. This generalist species inhabits major drainage systems across multiple regions, including the Ohio River basin and Atlantic Slope drainages, and its presence in fish community assemblages reflects the ecological integrity of medium to large river systems. In conservation and fisheries contexts, freshwater drum populations are monitored as part of broader assessments of freshwater biodiversity and aquatic habitat quality, particularly in systems experiencing anthropogenic pressures or habitat fragmentation.
