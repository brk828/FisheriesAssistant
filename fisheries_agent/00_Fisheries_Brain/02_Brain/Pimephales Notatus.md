# Pimephales Notatus

## Summary
Pimephales notatus, commonly known as the bluntnose minnow, is a small cyprinid fish species frequently documented in North American stream ecosystems, particularly in small to medium-sized streams with sandy, gravelly, or silty substrates and minimal water flow. This species is significant in ichthyological surveys as a tolerant, widespread indicator organism that commonly co-occurs with other cyprinids and serves as a key component of small fish community structure across various aquatic habitats, making it important for understanding stream ecological conditions and assessing impacts of environmental disturbances such as strip mining on freshwater fish assemblages.
