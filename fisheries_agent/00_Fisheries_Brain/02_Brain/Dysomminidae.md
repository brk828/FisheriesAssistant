# Dysomminidae

## Summary
Dysomminidae is a distinct eel family established to accommodate Dysommina, which cannot be placed in any existing eel family. The family is characterized by a unique combination of features including paired frontals, enlarged otic bulla, reduced palatopterygoid bones, ankylosed vomer-ethmoid, and distinctive tooth patterns that distinguish it from related families like Moringuidae, Heterenchelidae, and others.
