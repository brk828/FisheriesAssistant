# Fishable Waters Act

## Summary
The Fishable Waters Act is a proposed legislative initiative to address the failure of 40% of U.S. waters to meet the Clean Water Act's "fishable and swimmable" goals after 26 years. According to this source, the Act proposes a voluntary, incentive-based watershed approach using locally-controlled Watershed Councils with state administration and federal funding through the Department of Agriculture, targeting non-point source pollution, habitat restoration, stream flows, and floodplain management rather than EPA regulatory enforcement. The proposal aims to restore approximately 1 million miles of rivers and streams, with political strategy recognizing that the current Congress is more likely to support voluntary programs than EPA regulations.
