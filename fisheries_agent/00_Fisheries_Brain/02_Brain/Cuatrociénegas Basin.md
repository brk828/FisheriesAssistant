# Cuatrociénegas Basin

## Summary
The Cuatrociénegas Basin is a desert wetland and spring complex located in Coahuila, Mexico, characterized by exceptional levels of endemism among its aquatic fauna, including fish, invertebrates, and chelonians adapted to isolated spring and pool habitats. This unique ecosystem has become a focal point for conservation biology research due to its high concentration of endemic species facing threats from habitat degradation and isolation, as evidenced by dedicated symposia and research initiatives examining both fish and turtle communities within the basin. The basin's hydrogeological isolation in an arid region creates distinct microhabitats that support distinct populations of endemic aquatic organisms, making it an important model system for understanding speciation and conservation challenges in isolated freshwater ecosystems.
