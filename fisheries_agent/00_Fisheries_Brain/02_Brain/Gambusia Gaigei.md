# Gambusia Gaigei

## Summary
Gambusia gaigei, commonly known as the Big Bend gambusia, is a small livebearing fish endemic to the Big Bend region of Texas and recognized as one of the most critically endangered freshwater fish species in North America. This species is restricted to a highly fragmented range limited to tributary waters of the Rio Grande and faces severe conservation challenges due to habitat loss, water diversion, and competition from invasive species. As a geographically isolated and range-restricted taxon, G. gaigei serves as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health and represents a priority for freshwater conservation efforts in the southwestern United States.
