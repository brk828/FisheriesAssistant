# West Turkey Creek

## Summary
West Turkey Creek is a perennial stream in the Rio Yaqui drainage basin of southeastern Arizona that serves as critical habitat for federally endangered Yaqui Chub (Gila purpurea) and other native cyprinids, making it a focal site for interagency conservation efforts coordinated by the Southeast Arizona Native Fish Team. Fish surveys of West Turkey Creek documented significant invasive species pressure, particularly the non-native fathead minnow (Pimephales promelas), which numerically dominated native populations and posed threats to the persistence of endemic species in this isolated aquatic refuge. The creek's ecological conditions and native fish assemblages made it a priority conservation project for regional fisheries management agencies seeking to protect Rio Yaqui basin endemics from extinction during the mid-1990s.
