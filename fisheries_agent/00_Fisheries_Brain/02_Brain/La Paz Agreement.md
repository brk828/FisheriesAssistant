# La Paz Agreement

## Summary
The La Paz Agreement of 1983 provides coordination mechanisms for U.S.-Mexico border environmental issues but lacks mandatory provisions. The agreement defines a 100-km border zone that the Committee considers arbitrary, and its Annexes 4 and 5 regarding air quality are deemed insufficient, requiring a new approach for addressing transboundary environmental problems.
