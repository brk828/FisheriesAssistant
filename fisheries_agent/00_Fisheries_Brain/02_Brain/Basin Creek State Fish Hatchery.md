# Basin Creek State Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Basin Creek State Fish Hatchery is a California fish rearing facility located near Sonora. This source indicates that the initial 100,000 Kokanee eggs obtained from Idaho in late 1940 were hatched at Basin Creek State Fish Hatchery, producing 67,000 fingerlings that were planted in Salt Springs Reservoir in July 1941.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
