# David L. Soltz

## Summary
David L. Soltz was one of three compilers of the 1977 Death Valley System aquatic resources bibliography, working alongside Robert Rush Miller and Peter G. Sanchez for the National Park Service. He contributed to creating a comprehensive catalog of scientific and popular literature on the region's native fishes and aquatic habitats covering publications from 1878 through 1976.
