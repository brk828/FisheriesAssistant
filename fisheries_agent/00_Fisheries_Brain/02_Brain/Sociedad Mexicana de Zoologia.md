# Sociedad Mexicana de Zoologia

## Summary
Sociedad Mexicana de Zoologia (Mexican Society of Zoology) is a professional scientific organization where Dr. Salvador Contreras Balderas served as President from 1981 to 1983. This leadership role was part of his extensive professional service in Mexican zoological and ichthyological organizations.
