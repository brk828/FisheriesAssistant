# Ammonium Sulfamate

## Summary
Ammonium sulfamate is a relatively low-toxicity herbicide used in fishery management for aquatic vegetation control. With an oral LD50 of 3600 mg/kg in rats, it represents one of the least toxic herbicides reviewed in the report.

## Context

Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
