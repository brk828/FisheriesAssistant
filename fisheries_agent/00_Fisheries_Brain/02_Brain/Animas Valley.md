# Animas Valley

## Summary
Animas Valley is a neighboring basin mentioned as a possible alternative source for the artesian water in San Simon Valley. This source discusses but considers improbable the theory that artesian water could be derived from seepage from Animas Valley through the Peloncillo Mountain barrier, favoring instead local precipitation on the San Simon drainage basin as the primary source.
