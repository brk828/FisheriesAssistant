# Seine Surveys

## Summary
Seine surveys refer to annual small-bodied fish surveys conducted from 2003-2012 to monitor age-0 fish populations. These surveys showed that age-0 Channel Catfish densities increased downstream and peaked in lower river portions, providing information on recruitment patterns.
