# California Game and Fish Department

## Summary
The California Game and Fish Department was contacted by the petitioners to inquire about the impacts of California's existing captive propagation allowances for reptiles. The Department reported no available statistical data on whether captive propagation reduced pressure on wild populations, though informal impressions suggested habitat destruction had not accelerated under their program.
