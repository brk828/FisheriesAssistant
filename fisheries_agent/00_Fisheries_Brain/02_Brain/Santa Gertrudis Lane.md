# Santa Gertrudis Lane

## Summary
Santa Gertrudis Lane is a road crossing location on the Santa Cruz River in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, where fish surveys were conducted in May 1993. This site is particularly significant because it supported populations of the federally endangered Gila Topminnow, which were observed utilizing thermal refugia under thick filamentous algae mats where water temperatures were 5.5°C cooler (18.5°C) than adjacent open water (24°C). The discovery of topminnows at this location necessitated Section 7 Consultation under the Endangered Species Act and prompted plans for ongoing monitoring during summer 1993 surveys.
