# Buffalo Pound Lake

## Summary
Buffalo Pound Lake is a shallow eutrophic lake in the Qu'Appelle River system of southern Saskatchewan, Canada, with a mean depth of 3.6 m, maximum depth of 5 m, and surface area of 2,950 ha. This study tracked Northern Pike movements in the lake during winter ice-angling season, finding that pike dispersed widely over the eastern and central portions of the long, narrow lake with little depth heterogeneity. The lake supports populations of Northern Pike, Yellow Perch, and Walleye and has been a main source of brood stock for stocking programs in Saskatchewan since 2013.
