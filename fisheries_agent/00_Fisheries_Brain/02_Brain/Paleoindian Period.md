# Paleoindian Period

## Summary
The Paleoindian Period dates from approximately 10,000-8500 B.C. and represents the earliest documented human occupation at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. This source reports finding a single Clovis projectile point in Growler Valley and Paleoindian tools surrounding an extinct cienega in Quitobaquito Basin, suggesting the environment during this period was substantially different from present conditions.
