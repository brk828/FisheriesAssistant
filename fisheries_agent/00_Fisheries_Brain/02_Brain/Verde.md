# Verde

## Summary
Verde is a Mexican river system inhabited by Gila with 8,9-9,8 fin ray counts, 60-70 lateral line scales (intermediate scale size), and faint or weak basal radii on scales. Populations from this location have deep caudal peduncles with head length into least depth ratio of 2.1-2.8.
