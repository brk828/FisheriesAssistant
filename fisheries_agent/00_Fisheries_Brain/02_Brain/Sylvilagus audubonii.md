# Sylvilagus audubonii

## Summary
Sylvilagus audubonii (desert cottontail) occurs below 1800 m elevation in brushy areas of the region. While cottontails (Sylvilagus sp.) were identified at La Lomita Pequeña, species-level distinction between desert and eastern cottontail was not attempted due to small sample size and fragmentary condition.
