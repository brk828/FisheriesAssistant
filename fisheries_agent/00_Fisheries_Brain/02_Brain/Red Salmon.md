# Red Salmon

## Summary
Red Salmon (Oncorhynchus nerka) is a salmonid species that was being experimentally stocked in multiple New York State waters at the time of this 1952 guide. The species is characterized by black markings that are often faint, helping to distinguish it from other salmonids in the region.
