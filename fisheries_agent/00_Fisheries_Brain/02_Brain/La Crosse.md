# La Crosse

## Summary
La Crosse is the Wisconsin location of the Fish Control Laboratory that conducted extensive testing of antimycin on over 30 freshwater fish species from 1963-1966. This laboratory was central to evaluating antimycin's potential as a fishery management tool.

## Context
