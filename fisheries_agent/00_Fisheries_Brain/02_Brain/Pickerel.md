# Pickerel

## Summary
Pickerel refers to a guild of game fish species within freshwater ecosystems, primarily including Chain Pickerel and Redfin Pickerel, which are classified alongside bass and trout as important recreational and management target species in fisheries science. These predatory fish are commonly monitored in population surveys and conservation assessments, with management protocols typically establishing minimum sampling thresholds of approximately 200 individuals per species per water body to ensure adequate data for stock assessment and ecological monitoring. Pickerel occupy a significant ecological role as mid-level predators in freshwater food webs and are valued both for sport fishing and as indicators of aquatic ecosystem health.
