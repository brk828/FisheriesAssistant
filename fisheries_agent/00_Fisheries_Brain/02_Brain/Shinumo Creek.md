# Shinumo Creek

## Summary
Shinumo Creek is a tributary of the Colorado River within the Grand Canyon that serves as an important sampling location for monitoring native fish populations and habitat conditions in the region. The creek is particularly significant for surveys of small-bodied native cyprinids such as Speckled Dace (*Rhinichthys osculus*), which shows strong habitat association with tributary systems like Shinumo Creek. As part of comprehensive Grand Canyon fish monitoring programs, Shinumo Creek represents a key site for assessing the distribution and abundance of native fishes in relation to main-stem river habitat changes and tributary ecosystem dynamics.
