# Water And Power Resources Service

## Summary
The Water and Power Resources Service (WAPRS) refers to a federal agency or bureau responsible for managing water infrastructure and hydroelectric power generation facilities, which in the context of fish science and conservation involves the stewardship of artificial aquatic environments such as hatcheries and canal systems that may house endangered or listed fish species. As a manager of wholly artificial habitats, WAPRS operations intersect with Endangered Species Act compliance and Section 7 consultation requirements, raising important questions about whether listed species maintained in human-controlled water facilities receive the same regulatory protections as those in natural ecosystems. This concept highlights the regulatory ambiguities and management responsibilities that arise when federal agencies operate critical infrastructure that incidentally or intentionally supports fish populations of conservation concern.
