# Lake Havasu

## Summary
Lake Havasu is a major Colorado River reservoir in the Lower Colorado River Basin that serves as critical habitat for both native endangered fish species (including razorback suckers and bonytail chubs) and non-native sport fish populations, making it a focal point for conservation conflicts and recovery efforts. As one of a chain of ecologically distinct reservoirs along the Colorado River, Lake Havasu and its associated tailwater represent important but threatened habitats where management decisions regarding fish stocking, species introductions, and habitat modification directly impact the survival of federally listed threatened and endangered species. The reservoir's role in native fish recovery programs, particularly for the most critically endangered Colorado River fishes, makes it a key site for assessing the tradeoffs between recreational fisheries management and the preservation of endemic aquatic biodiversity.
