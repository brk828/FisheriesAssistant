# Gulliver Lake Whitefish

## Summary
The Gulliver Lake Whitefish is part of the "common whitefish" group and appears to be the native Adirondack form of whitefish. It is one of the subspecies of Coregonus clupeaformis found in New York waters. The source provides limited specific information about this form beyond noting its apparent native status to the Adirondack region.
