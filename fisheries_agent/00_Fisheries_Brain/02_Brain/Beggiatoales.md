# Beggiatoales

## Summary
Beggiatoales are colorless blue-green algae (cyanobacteria) known for chemosynthetic metabolism. This source suggests that Oscillatoria agardhii may possess metabolic pathways similar to Beggiatoales, potentially supplementing photosynthesis with chemosynthesis in metalimnetic zones where H₂S and low oxygen concentrations coexist.
