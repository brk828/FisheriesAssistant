# Sauna Cave

## Summary
Sauna Cave is one of five fixed remote PIT scanning sites established in the River Zone of Lake Mohave for continuous monitoring of razorback sucker movements. This site, along with Gio's Point, Black Bar, Ringbolt Rapids, and Boy Scout Canyon, was equipped with PIT scanning equipment to detect tagged fish in the riverine habitat section of the reservoir.
