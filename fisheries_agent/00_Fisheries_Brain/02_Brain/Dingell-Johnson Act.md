# Dingell-Johnson Act

## Summary
The Dingell-Johnson Act is a federal legislation that provided funding mechanisms for fish and wildlife conservation projects, enabling state fish and game agencies to conduct scientific research and management studies such as creel surveys and population assessments. As evidenced in mid-20th century fisheries research, the Act supported practical field investigations into fish population dynamics, angler harvest rates, and hatchery stocking effectiveness that informed conservation decisions. The funding helped establish standardized scientific methods for monitoring fish populations and evaluating the outcomes of fisheries management practices across diverse aquatic systems.
