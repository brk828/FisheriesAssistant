# Southern Sea Otter

## Summary
The Southern Sea Otter is a listed subspecies that was examined in this 1981 legal memorandum regarding augmentation strategies under the Endangered Species Act. The memorandum concluded that augmenting the Southern Sea Otter population with an unlisted subspecies or with hybrid offspring would not satisfy legal requirements for recovery, as such additions would not change the biological status of the listed subspecies itself or bring it to the point where ESA protections are no longer necessary.
