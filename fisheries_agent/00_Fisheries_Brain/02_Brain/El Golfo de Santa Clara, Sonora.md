# El Golfo de Santa Clara, Sonora

## Summary
El Golfo de Santa Clara, Sonora is a fishing community on the Gulf of California in the Colorado River Delta region. This source describes it as having three fishing cooperatives (one for shrimp, two for scale fish) and experiencing dramatic declines in shrimp and shark catches in 1985, with scale fish being relatively rare except for seasonal appearances of shark, sierra mackerel, and mullet.
