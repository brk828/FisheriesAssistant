# Finger Lakes Region

## Summary
The Finger Lakes Region is an area in New York containing several large lakes with tributary streams that support spawning runs of Rainbow Trout. This study examined the stream fishery during 1955-1957, finding that fishing pressure and success varied considerably among tributaries, with opening-week catches ranging from 140 to 980 trout depending on the stream and year.

## Context

> A creel census was conducted during the opening week of stream fishing for [[Rainbow Trout]] in the [[Finger Lakes Region]] of New York during 1955, 1956, and 1957.
