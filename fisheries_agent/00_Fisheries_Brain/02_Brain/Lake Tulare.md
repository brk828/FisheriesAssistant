# Lake Tulare

## Summary
Lake Tulare was California's largest lake that was eliminated by lowland drainage projects between 1850-1900. This source describes how its elimination drove the Thicktail Chub to extinction and caused the Sacramento Perch to be extirpated from its native range, representing one of the major historical alterations to California's aquatic ecosystems.
