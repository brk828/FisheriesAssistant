# Aravaipa Creek Wilderness

## Summary
Aravaipa Creek Wilderness is a protected drainage area that was the subject of exotic fish removal efforts. This source indicates that a BLM-AGFD-FWS cooperative survey and removal project for exotic fishes was approved for this area, anticipated to begin in summer 1987 with possible extension to summer 1988.
