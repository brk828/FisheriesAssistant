# Etheostoma Caeruleum

## Summary
Etheostoma caeruleum, commonly known as the rainbow darter, is a small freshwater darter species that plays an important ecological role in North American stream communities as both a predator of larval fish and macroinvertebrates and as prey for larger predatory fishes. This species is frequently referenced in ichthyological studies examining predator-prey interactions, competitive dynamics among darter assemblages, and the taxonomy of Percidae family relationships. E. caeruleum serves as a model organism for understanding darter ecology, reproductive behavior, and the impacts of habitat alteration on benthic fish communities in cool to moderate-temperature stream ecosystems.
