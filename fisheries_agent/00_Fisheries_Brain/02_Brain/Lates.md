# Lates

## Summary
Lates is a predaceous fish genus that was exterminated from certain African lakes during interpluvial periods. This source reports that Lakes Koga, Edward, George, and Victoria became so deoxygenated during dry periods that Lates and other Nilotic genera were eliminated, demonstrating the dramatic impact of Pleistocene climatic oscillations on fish fauna.
