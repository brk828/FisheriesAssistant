# IMADES

## Summary
IMADES (Instituto del Medio Ambiente y el Desarrollo Sustentable del Estado de Sonora) is a Mexican environmental institute focused on sustainable development in Sonora state. According to this source, IMADES co-sponsored the 1998 Sonoran Desert Ecoregion Experts Workshop along with The Nature Conservancy and the Sonoran Institute to identify priority conservation sites across the binational Sonoran Desert region.
