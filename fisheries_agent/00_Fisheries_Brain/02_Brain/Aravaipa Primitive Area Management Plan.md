# Aravaipa Primitive Area Management Plan

## Summary
The Aravaipa Primitive Area Management Plan was a comprehensive management plan being developed by the Bureau of Land Management in 1970 with public input. The temporary vehicle closure order for Aravaipa Canyon would remain in effect until this management plan was completed. Public meetings were scheduled in July 1970 to solicit comments on vehicular use and other management considerations for inclusion in the plan.
