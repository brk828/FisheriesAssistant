# Cryptochiton stelleri

## Summary
Cryptochiton stelleri (Giant Chiton) is a mollusk species currently restricted to waters north of Point Conception that provides evidence for past temperature fluctuations. Indians harvested this species along the Baja California coast approximately 900 years B.P., as documented in middens, indicating a cool period when ocean temperatures were below present levels, supported by oxygen-18 estimates from the same period.
