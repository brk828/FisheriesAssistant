# Roswell Artesian Basin

## Summary
The Roswell Artesian Basin is a region in New Mexico where Gammarus-Pecos Complex populations occur. This source identifies it as one of the areas once covered by Permian seas where amphipod populations were discovered, specifically including North Spring in Roswell (home to G. desperatus) and the now-extinct Lander Spring Brook population.
