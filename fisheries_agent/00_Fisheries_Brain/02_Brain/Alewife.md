# Alewife

## Summary
The alewife (*Alosa pseudoharengus*) is a small, schooling forage fish species that inhabits freshwater lakes and coastal systems in eastern North America. As documented in mid-20th century fisheries research, alewives were experimentally introduced into various inland water bodies as a potentially abundant food source to support populations of predatory game fish such as bass and pickerel, though their ecological impacts on native panfish communities required careful management consideration. This species served as an important subject in early fisheries manipulation studies examining how introducing new prey species could influence predator-prey dynamics and overall fish community structure in managed lacustrine ecosystems.
