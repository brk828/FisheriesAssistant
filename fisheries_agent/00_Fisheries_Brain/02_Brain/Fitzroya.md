# Fitzroya

## Summary
Fitzroya is a genus of Poeciliid fishes characterized by an alimentary canal approximately equal to entire body length. This intestinal length is notably shorter than in related genera like Ilyodon (3 times body length) or Phalloptychus and Phalloceros (twice body length), suggesting different feeding strategies.
