# Black-tailed jackrabbits

## Summary
Black-tailed jackrabbits (Lepus californicus) are the most abundant identified taxon at La Lomita Pequeña, with 18 specimens representing 43% of identified mammals. These animals are distributed throughout Arizona below 1800 m elevation, and 61% of their remains at the site showed burning, suggesting they were a primary food source.
