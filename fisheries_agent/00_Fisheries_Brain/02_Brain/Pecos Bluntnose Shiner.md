# Pecos Bluntnose Shiner

## Summary
The Pecos Bluntnose Shiner is an endangered fish that occurs sporadically in only 175 miles of the Pecos River mainstream, representing severe range contraction from its historic distribution. The Service determined that malathion use would likely jeopardize this species due to the pesticide's extreme aquatic toxicity, requiring a 1-mile exclusion buffer around occupied habitat.
