# Galloway

## Summary
Galloway (1965) controversially suggested that the last glacial period experienced a 20% reduction in both evaporation and precipitation. This implied that the pluvial period actually occurred during the post-pluvial temperature optimum rather than during glacial maxima, contradicting prevailing theories.
