# Cattails

## Summary
Cattails (Typha) are aquatic vegetation found in Owens Pupfish habitat. The pupfish's typical habitat includes shallows with sparse cattails and bulrush with sand-silt-detritus bottom, though dense tule stands exclude pupfish.
