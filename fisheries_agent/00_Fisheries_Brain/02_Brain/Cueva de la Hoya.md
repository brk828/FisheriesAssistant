# Cueva de la Hoya

## Summary
Cueva de la Hoya is the cave near Xilitla from which Rioja collected the original single female specimen used to describe this species in 1956. This cave represents one of only three known locations where Xilitloniscus laevis has been found, with the original specimen collected from cave walls.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
