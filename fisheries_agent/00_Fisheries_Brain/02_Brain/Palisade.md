# Palisade

## Summary
Palisade is a location on the Colorado River in the Upper Colorado River Basin that serves as a geographic reference point for endangered fish recovery efforts. The recovery plan identifies the Colorado River from Palisade to Lake Powell as one of the target areas for establishing naturally self-sustaining populations of Colorado Squawfish, and the Colorado River above Palisade is designated for ongoing population augmentation of both razorback sucker and Colorado squawfish.
