# Veterans Memorial Park

## Summary
Veterans Memorial Park in West Salem, Wisconsin was the site of an October 1965 field trial of antimycin. The park's 5-acre pond was treated with 10 parts per billion antimycin, successfully eliminating 5,994 common carp and thousands of fathead minnows within 24 hours while channel catfish and black bullhead survived.
