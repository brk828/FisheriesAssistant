# Argentina

## Summary
Argentina is a country in the La Plata Basin where Poeciliid species ranges extend. Species distributions documented include Fitzroya lineata occurring from Montevideo and Maldonado to Province Salta, Argentina, and Cnesterodon decemmaculatus extending from Rio Grande do Sul to Maldonado and the Bolivian Chaco.
