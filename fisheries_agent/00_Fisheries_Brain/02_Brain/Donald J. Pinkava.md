# Donald J. Pinkava

## Summary
Donald J. Pinkava was a flora and vegetation authority at Arizona State University who was assembled as part of the expert network to support the Cuatro Ciénegas Biosphere Reserve management planning effort in 1988. He was identified as an expert on the plant life and vegetation of the basin.
