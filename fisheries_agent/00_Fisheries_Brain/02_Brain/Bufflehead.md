# Bufflehead

## Summary
Bufflehead (Bucephala albeola) are waterfowl species that use the wetland habitats created along the Colorado River after Glen Canyon Dam closure in 1963. These limited backwater and wetland habitats are critical components of the Greater Grand Canyon ecosystem for Bufflehead and other migratory waterfowl, particularly in dry western areas.
