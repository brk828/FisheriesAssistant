# Chaparral Biome

## Summary
The Chaparral Biome is one of the major biotic communities in the study area, occurring with pinyon and juniper on level basin bottoms at around 4,350 feet elevation. This habitat type supported the lowest nocturnal rodent biomass values (0.0-0.53 kg/hectare) among the intensively studied vegetation associations.
