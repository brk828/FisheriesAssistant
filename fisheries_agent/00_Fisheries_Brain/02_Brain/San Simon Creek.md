# San Simon Creek

## Summary
San Simon Creek is the main drainage channel that originates at the lower end of San Simon Cienaga and follows the valley axis northward to the Gila River. This source documents that the creek channel has been deeply incised with high vertical banks formed primarily in recent decades since American settlement.
