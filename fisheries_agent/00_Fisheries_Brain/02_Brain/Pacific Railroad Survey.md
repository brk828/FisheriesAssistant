# Pacific Railroad Survey

## Summary
The Pacific Railroad Survey of 1853-57 provides crucial historical biogeographic data documenting a warm period approximately one century before the study. The survey found warm-water fish species at San Diego (including a seahorse) resembling assemblages from Turtle Bay and Magdalena Bay, and southern species at Monterey that no longer occur at those latitudes, indicating significantly warmer conditions in the mid-19th century.
