# Dam B Reservoir

## Summary
Dam B Reservoir refers to a reservoir area in Texas encompassing waters of the Neches River from the Jasper-Angelina County line to the Tyler-Jasper-Hardin County line, and waters of the Angelina River in Jasper County below Sam Rayburn Dam. In 1964-1965, new regulations prohibited the use of nets for taking fish from this reservoir except through State rough fish contracts, a change that was approved with no public opposition.
