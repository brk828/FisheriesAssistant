# Freshwater Drum

## Summary
The freshwater drum (Aplodinotus grunniens) is a benthic-feeding cypriniform fish found throughout freshwater systems in North America, often classified as a rough fish rather than a game or food species despite its potential commercial value. In aquatic ecology and fisheries management contexts, freshwater drum serves as an indicator species for ecosystem conditions and is frequently encountered in surveys of freshwater communities alongside more desirable food fish species like catfish and crappies. The species' abundance, bottom-feeding habitat preference, and role in food webs make it ecologically significant for understanding freshwater fish assemblage dynamics and community composition.
