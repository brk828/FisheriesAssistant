# Piedmont Region

## Summary
The Piedmont Region is an upland physiographic area in Alabama and Georgia where all members of the M. coosae species group are endemic. This source indicates that species occupy rocky pools and upland stream habitats above the fall line in this region, with physiographic restriction to these upland habitats limiting gene flow among populations separated by larger lowland rivers.
