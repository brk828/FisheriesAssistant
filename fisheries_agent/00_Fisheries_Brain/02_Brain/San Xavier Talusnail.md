# San Xavier Talusnail

## Summary
The San Xavier Talusnail is an endemic invertebrate species that appears on the Sonoran Desert Conservation Plan's list of additional species likely to require conservation attention in the future. It represents the endemic invertebrate fauna of Pima County potentially requiring protection beyond the federally listed and candidate species.
