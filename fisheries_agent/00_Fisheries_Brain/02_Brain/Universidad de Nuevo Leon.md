# Universidad de Nuevo Leon

## Summary
Universidad de Nuevo Leon (also referred to as Universidad Autonoma de Nuevo Leon or UANL) is a Mexican university where Dr. Salvador Contreras Balderas earned his biology degree (1957-1961) and later served as a full-time professor starting in 1965. At UANL's Faculty of Biological Sciences, he founded the Division of Graduate Studies (1975), established the Institute of Fisheries Ecology and Wildlife (1984), and founded ichthyological and herpetological collections (1965).
