# Pymatuning Laboratory of Ecology

## Summary
The Pymatuning Laboratory of Ecology is a research facility at the University of Pittsburgh where this calorific equivalents compilation was produced. Kenneth W. Cummins published this comprehensive reference work through the laboratory in October 1967.

## Context
