# Simuliidae

## Summary
Simuliidae is a family of aquatic insects commonly known as black flies, whose larvae are important benthic macroinvertebrates in freshwater ecosystems that serve as indicators of water quality and food sources for fish populations. In fish science and aquatic ecology studies, Simuliidae populations are monitored as part of invertebrate community assessments to evaluate ecosystem health, recovery following disturbances like chemical treatments or dam operations, and the availability of drift-feeding resources for fish species. These midges are typically sensitive to environmental stressors, making their presence or absence valuable for understanding the ecological impacts of management practices on aquatic food webs.
