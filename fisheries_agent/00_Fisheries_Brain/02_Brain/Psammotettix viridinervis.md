# Psammotettix viridinervis

## Summary
Psammotettix viridinervis is a new species of deltocephaline leafhopper measuring 3.2-3.3 mm (male) to 3.3-3.6 mm (female), most closely related to P. asper. It has pale ochre coloring with stramineous tegmina bearing paler yellow to light green veins that give the wings a greenish cast, and was collected from east of Laramie, Wyoming.
