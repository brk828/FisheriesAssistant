# Scorpion Island

## Summary
Scorpion Island is a geographical reference point located on Lake Mead at River Mile 279 on the Colorado River downstream of Glen Canyon Dam, serving as a downstream boundary marker for aquatic ecosystem monitoring and fish sampling studies in the Colorado River corridor. In ichthyological research, the island and its adjacent Scorpion Bay are used as sentinel locations for assessing localized variations in water temperature, turbidity, and habitat conditions that influence fish communities and aquatic life in the transitional zone between riverine and lacustrine environments. The area's distinct thermal and optical water properties make it valuable for understanding how dam operations and downstream flow regimes affect fish habitat quality and ecosystem function in the lower Colorado River.
