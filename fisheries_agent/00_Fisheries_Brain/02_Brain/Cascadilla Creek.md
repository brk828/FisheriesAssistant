# Cascadilla Creek

## Summary
Cascadilla Creek is a rapid-flowing mountain stream in Ithaca, New York, characterized by water flowing over rocks and pebbles with high oxygen content and temperatures ranging from 0°C to 28°C. This study used it as the primary research site for observing Black-nosed Dace from September 1918 to August 1919, documenting their feeding, reproduction, and habitat associations in this clear, cold stream environment.
