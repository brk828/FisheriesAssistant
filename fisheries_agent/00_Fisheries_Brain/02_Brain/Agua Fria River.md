# Agua Fria River

## Summary
The Agua Fria River is a free-flowing desert river system in Arizona that serves as critical habitat for native fish species within the Gila River basin, particularly supporting a standardized core assemblage of native fishes including longfin dace, speckled dace, desert sucker, and Sonora sucker. As a tributary within BLM-managed public lands in the Sonoran Desert, the Agua Fria River represents an important reference ecosystem for understanding historical fish community composition and for recovery efforts of endemic and threatened species such as the Gila topminnow and desert pupfish. The river's designation under the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act recognizes its qualifying ecological values for fish and wildlife conservation, making it central to regional aquatic recovery planning and desert aquatic ecosystem management.
