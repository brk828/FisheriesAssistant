# Astyanax fasciatus aeneus

## Summary
Astyanax fasciatus aeneus is a northern subspecies of characid fish that this source documents from multiple localities in British Honduras and Guatemala. The specimens examined (9 from vicinity of Belize, 1 from Punta Gorda, 1 from arroyo Ixlu) were assessed as close to the aeneus-type but showing some features of the related subspecies mexicanus, supporting a clinal distribution pattern with possible localized reproductive isolation creating sibling species.
