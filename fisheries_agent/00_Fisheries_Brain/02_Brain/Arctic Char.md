# Arctic Char

## Summary
Arctic Char (Salvelinus alpinus) is a cold-water salmonid species distributed across northern regions that exhibits notable phenotypic and behavioral diversity both among and within populations, including distinct reproductive strategies such as differentiated spawning times and habitat preferences. The species has been extensively studied in limnological research to understand how environmental factors, particularly temperature and dissolved oxygen, influence its physiology, survival, and population dynamics in freshwater ecosystems. Its sensitivity to thermal and oxygen conditions, combined with its capacity to form multiple reproductive populations within single lake systems, makes Arctic Char a valuable model organism for investigating fish ecology, intraspecific variation, and the ecological impacts of environmental change in aquatic systems.
