# Aravaipa Canyon Primitive Area

## Summary
The Aravaipa Canyon Primitive Area is a federally-managed area in southeastern Arizona encompassing approximately 7.5 miles of canyon from the confluence of Aravaipa Creek and Turkey Creek eastward. In 1970, it was recognized for its primitive and environmental resource values that required protection from increasing motorized vehicle use and fire danger. The area was designated as a Primitive Area, indicating its ecological and wilderness values, and was subject to a temporary vehicle closure while a comprehensive management plan was developed.
