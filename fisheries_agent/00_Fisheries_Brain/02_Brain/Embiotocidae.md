# Embiotocidae

## Summary
Embiotocidae is a family of viviparous marine and freshwater fishes commonly known as surfperches, characterized by internal fertilization and live-bearing reproduction rather than egg-laying. Members of this family, such as Hysterocarpus traski, are ecologically important in coastal and estuarine environments and serve as hosts for various parasitic organisms, making them significant subjects of study in parasitology, aquatic ecology, and conservation biology. Their reproductive strategy and habitat preferences make them valuable indicators of ecosystem health and hydrological conditions in their native ranges.
