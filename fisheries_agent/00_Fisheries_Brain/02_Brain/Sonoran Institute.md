# Sonoran Institute

## Summary
The Sonoran Institute is a non-governmental organization that volunteered to serve as a lead NGO, along with the International Sonoran Desert Alliance, in the implementation committee established under the Department of Interior and SEMARNAP Letter of Intent. The committee was formed to coordinate management of protected natural areas in the border region, with the Delta-Upper Gulf Biosphere Reserve as a pilot project.
