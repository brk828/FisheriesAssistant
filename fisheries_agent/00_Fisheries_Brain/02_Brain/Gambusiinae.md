# Gambusiinae

## Summary
Gambusiinae is a subfamily within the Poeciliidae family. This source indicates that members of Gambusiinae, including the genus Gambusia, share a primarily carnivorous feeding habit that can shift toward omnivorousness when experiencing food stress.
