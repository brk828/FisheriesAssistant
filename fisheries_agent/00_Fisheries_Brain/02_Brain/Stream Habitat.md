# Stream Habitat

## Summary
Smallmouth bass prefer rocky streams with riffle-pool complexes, clean hard stony bottoms, and stream gradients of 0.5 to 5.0 m per km. Population success is influenced by pool permanence, with bass showing strong site fidelity in streams with stable permanent pools but being more mobile and less abundant in streams where pools are transitory due to flooding and sediment movement.
