# Sanders and Wright

## Summary
Sanders and Wright conducted research in 1962 on salmonid erythrocyte antigens that was cited as part of the foundational work informing this study. Their research contributed to the body of knowledge on using blood antigens as genetic markers in trout species.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
