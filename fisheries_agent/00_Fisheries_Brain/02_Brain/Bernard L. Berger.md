# Bernard L. Berger

## Summary
Bernard L. Berger was a researcher with the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife who co-authored this 1970 report with Robert E. Lennon. He helped document and analyze field applications of antimycin A as a fish control agent across 19 U.S. states, Canada, and Guatemala.
