# Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe

## Summary
The Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe is the sovereign Native American nation with primary governance and stewardship authority over Pyramid Lake in Nevada, and as such holds central importance in the conservation and recovery efforts for the endangered cui-ui (*Chasmistes cujus*), an endemic fish species found exclusively in that lake. The Tribe's collaboration with federal agencies, particularly the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, has been essential to monitoring and managing cui-ui populations and implementing recovery strategies that depend critically on water management and spawning habitat conditions. As both the traditional inhabitants of the region and legal stakeholders in the lake's fishery resources, the Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe represents the intersection of indigenous sovereignty, cultural resources, and modern conservation biology in managing this unique aquatic ecosystem.
