# Spotted Sucker

## Summary
The Spotted Sucker is a freshwater fish species found in eastern North American aquatic systems, particularly in the Great Lakes and St. Lawrence River region, where it serves as an indicator of water quality and ecosystem health. As a component of "clean-water" fish assemblages, this species typically appears or increases in abundance following pollution abatement and environmental recovery, making it relevant to monitoring programs assessing water quality improvements and community recolonization dynamics in impacted freshwater ecosystems. Its presence and population trends are documented in limnological and fisheries surveys examining freshwater fish fauna composition and responses to anthropogenic stressors such as pollution, dam construction, and habitat degradation.
