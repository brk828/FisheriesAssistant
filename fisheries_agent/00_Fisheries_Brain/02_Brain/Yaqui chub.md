# Yaqui Chub

## Summary
The Yaqui Chub is a federally endangered fish endemic to the Yaqui River Basin in southeastern Arizona, representing a key species of conservation concern within the desert fishes recovery program managed by U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and state agencies. As a native cyprinid adapted to the isolated Yaqui River system, this species exemplifies the broader conservation challenges facing desert aquatic ecosystems in the southwestern United States, where habitat loss, water diversions, and introduced species have severely depleted populations of endemic fishes. Recovery efforts for the Yaqui Chub have involved habitat management at protected refuges such as San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge and coordination among multiple agencies to prevent extinction through both in-situ preservation and, when necessary, controlled population management interventions.
