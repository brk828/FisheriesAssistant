# Pánuco River

## Summary
The Pánuco River is one of several rivers that have cut back into the edges of the Mesa Central plateau in Mexico. This source identifies it as one of the drainages where headwater populations of Goodeidae occur, representing part of the biogeographic distribution of endemic Mesa Central fishes.
