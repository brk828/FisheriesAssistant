# Colorado Division Of Wildlife

## Summary
The Colorado Division of Wildlife is the state agency responsible for managing and conserving Colorado's fish and wildlife resources, including oversight of endangered species recovery programs and aquatic habitat management in critical river systems like the Colorado River. In the context of Colorado squawfish conservation, the Division played a central coordinating role in interagency efforts to recover this endangered species through habitat management, population monitoring via tagging studies, and coordination of dam release protocols to maintain suitable flow conditions in critical reaches. The Division's mandate encompasses both the scientific assessment of fish populations and the implementation of management actions necessary to restore and protect aquatic ecosystems within Colorado's jurisdiction.
