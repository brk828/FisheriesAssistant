# Lehner Ranch

## Summary
Lehner Ranch is the location of a famous Clovis archaeological site in the San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area, dating to approximately 11,000 years ago. The Lehner Mammoth Kill Site at this location was designated a National Landmark in 1967 and is one of the area's most significant paleontological and archaeological resources.
