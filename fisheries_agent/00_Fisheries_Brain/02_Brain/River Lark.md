# River Lark

## Summary
The River Lark is one of the organically enriched or polluted English rivers included in Edwards' extended studies. It represented part of the gradient from unpolluted to polluted conditions used to examine how organic enrichment affects plant and invertebrate communities and oxygen dynamics.
