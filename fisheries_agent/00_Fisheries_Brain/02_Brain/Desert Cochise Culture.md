# Desert Cochise Culture

## Summary
The Desert Cochise Culture represents the earliest documented human occupation of the Aravaipa Creek area, dating from 7500-200 B.C. This culture was part of multiple waves of human occupation spanning over 9,000 years that likely influenced the mammal fauna through habitat modification and hunting.
