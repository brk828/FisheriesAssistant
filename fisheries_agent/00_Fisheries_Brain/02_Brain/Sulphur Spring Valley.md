# Sulphur Spring Valley

## Summary
Sulphur Spring Valley was a location in southern Arizona where artesian wells at the Sanderson & Lyle property produced an unknown fish species from depths of 48-58 feet. These wells continuously ejected small fish (1-2 inches) to the surface over a two-year period, with the fish displaying perfect eyes, high reproductive capacity, and color variations from dark brown to golden, though they did not grow materially larger after surface emergence.
