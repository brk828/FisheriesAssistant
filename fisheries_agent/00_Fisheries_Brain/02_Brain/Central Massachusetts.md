# Central Massachusetts

## Summary
Central Massachusetts is a region adjacent to the New Hampshire study area. Data from Central Massachusetts reported 95% bass tapeworm infection in 20 examined smallmouth bass, which was higher than New Hampshire's 80.2% infection rate and provided regional context for comparison.
