# Lundberg

## Summary
Lundberg's 1979 unpublished dissertation was the only substantive morphological work on Ictalurus lupus prior to this 1982 study. However, his work relied on skeletal material from Cuatro Cienegas, a population that may not be typical of the species and shows distinctive morphometric characteristics.
