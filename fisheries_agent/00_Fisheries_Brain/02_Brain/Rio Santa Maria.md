# Rio Santa Maria

## Summary
Rio Santa Maria is one of five major drainage systems in Chihuahua, Mexico inhabited by the Chihuahua Chub (Gila nigrescens). Specimens from this drainage were included in morphometric and meristic analyses that distinguished them from Mimbres River and Rio Casas Grandes populations, particularly by smaller eye size and greater body width and depth.
