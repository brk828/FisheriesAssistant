# University of California Riverside

## Summary
The University of California Riverside maintained experimental ponds used in the tilapia study. All 48 hybrid tilapia held at these ponds died during the week prior to November 16, 1964, when water temperatures dropped to 42-43°F lows.

## Context

> This 1966 progress report documents the [[California Department of Fish and Game]]'s experimental culture of all-male [[Tilapia mossambica]] hybrids produced by crossing Zanzibari males with Javanese females.
