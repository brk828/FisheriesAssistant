# Triangle-leaf Bursage

## Summary
Triangle-leaf Bursage (Ambrosia deltoidea) is a predominant shrub species in the Sonoran Desertscrub - Arizona upland subdivision vegetation community at PPMR. It is part of the characteristic Paloverde-cacti-mixed scrub series found at the site.
