# Fishing Areas

## Summary
Fishing Areas are geographic management units used to organize and analyze fisheries data. In this Arizona survey, the state was divided into 34 Fishing Areas based on similarity of fisheries resources, management approaches, fish species present, access patterns, and angler use patterns to systematically track where anglers fished and what they harvested.

## Context

> This report presents results from the first year (1980) of a three-year mail survey program designed to estimate statewide angling effort and fish harvest by Arizona's licensed resident anglers.
