# Spongilla lacustris

## Summary
Spongilla lacustris is a freshwater sponge species included among the aquatic macroconsumers analyzed for calorific equivalents. This organism contributed to the aquatic macroconsumer data showing a grand mean of 4209 cal/g dry weight.
