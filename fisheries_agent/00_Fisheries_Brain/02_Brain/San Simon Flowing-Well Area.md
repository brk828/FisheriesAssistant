# San Simon Flowing-Well Area

## Summary
The San Simon Flowing-Well Area is the region where artesian wells flow naturally to the surface due to sufficient hydrostatic pressure in the confined aquifer beneath the blue clay layer. This source indicates that in this area the water table in the upper unconfined aquifer is less than 100 feet deep, and the area experienced rapid agricultural development following the discovery of artesian water at San Simon in late 1910.
