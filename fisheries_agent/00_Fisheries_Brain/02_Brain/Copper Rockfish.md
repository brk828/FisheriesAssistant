# Copper Rockfish

## Summary
Copper Rockfish (Sebastes caurinus) is a Pacific rockfish species that exhibits similar seasonal behavior to the species studied in Lynn Canal. Research by Patten (1973) documented that Copper Rockfish moved into rock piles during winter with only the largest individuals remaining visible outside.
