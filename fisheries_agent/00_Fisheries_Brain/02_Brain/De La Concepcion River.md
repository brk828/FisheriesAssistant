# De La Concepcion River

## Summary
The De La Concepcion River is one of three river drainages in Arizona, New Mexico, and Sonora, Mexico where Gila topminnow historically occurred. The species once inhabited the Gila River, Sonora River, and De La Concepcion River drainages before being extirpated throughout most of its range.
