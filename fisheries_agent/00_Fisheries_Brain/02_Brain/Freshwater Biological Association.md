# Freshwater Biological Association

## Summary
The Freshwater Biological Association is a research organization based at Windermere in England that conducted foundational studies on freshwater fish ecology, physiology, and conservation during the mid-20th century. The organization's work, particularly on species such as char and eels in Windermere lake, contributed significantly to understanding thermal preferences, larval development, and population dynamics in freshwater fish through experimental and field-based research. Its research output became influential in establishing methodologies for fish marking, temperature effects on mortality, and broader principles of aquatic ecology that informed freshwater fisheries management and conservation practices.
