# Wilderness Study Areas

## Summary
Wilderness Study Areas are lands where transplanting threatened and endangered species into their historic range is considered appropriate under this BLM policy. The source states that such transplanting should use minimum necessary actions and methods most appropriate for wilderness areas, following guidance in BLM Handbook H-8560-1, treating these areas similarly to designated wilderness for species reintroduction purposes.
