# Salmonids

## Summary
Salmonids refer to fish in the family Salmonidae, which includes salmon and trout species that are ecologically and economically important in freshwater and marine ecosystems, particularly in North America and other temperate regions. These fish are often the focus of conservation and fisheries management efforts due to their commercial value, recreational importance, and sensitivity to environmental changes, making them key indicator species for aquatic ecosystem health. Salmonids serve as central subjects in ecological studies examining predator-prey relationships, competition, and the impacts of other species on their survival and population dynamics in their native habitats.
