# Doane Falls

## Summary
Doane Falls is an impassable natural cascade on Lawrence Brook near its confluence with the East Branch Tully River in Massachusetts. This source identifies it as a barrier feature on the stream but provides no additional details about its characteristics or role in the reclamation study.
