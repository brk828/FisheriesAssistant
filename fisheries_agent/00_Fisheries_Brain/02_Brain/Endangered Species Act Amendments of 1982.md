# Endangered Species Act Amendments of 1982

## Summary
The Endangered Species Act Amendments of 1982 (Public Law 97-304) created Section 10(j) of the ESA, which authorized the Secretary of the Interior to designate specific populations of listed species as 'experimental populations.' This amendment was designed to provide administrative flexibility for reintroducing listed species into suitable habitat outside their current range while addressing local concerns about rigid protective measures that had previously caused significant opposition to translocation efforts.
