# Green River

## Summary
The Green River is a major tributary of the Colorado River Basin that serves as critical habitat for endangered native fish species, particularly the Colorado Squawfish and other large-river endemics. This river system, especially below Flaming Gorge Dam in Utah and its backwater habitats, functions as a vital nursery and rearing area for larval and juvenile fish populations and has been the focus of extensive ecological research and conservation efforts under the Colorado River Recovery Program. The Green River represents a key study site for understanding fish predation dynamics, population monitoring through electrofishing surveys, and the impacts of non-native species invasions on native aquatic communities in the Upper Colorado River Basin.
