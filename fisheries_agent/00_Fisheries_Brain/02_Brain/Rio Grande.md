# Rio Grande

## Summary
The Rio Grande (Río Bravo del Norte) is a major river system in North America that serves as a critical conservation focal point for endemic and native fish species in the southwestern United States and northern Mexico, particularly within the Chihuahuan Desert ecoregion. Its extensive drainage system, including important tributaries such as the Rio Conchos and smaller creeks within Big Bend National Park, supports diverse cyprinid and other freshwater fish communities of high conservation significance. The Rio Grande's transboundary nature has made it a key subject for binational conservation planning and watershed-level management efforts, particularly regarding the recovery of threatened endemic species in this arid region.
