# Eagle-Cherokee Pit

## Summary
Eagle-Cherokee Pit is a reclaimed coal strip-pit lake in southeastern Kansas that was part of the state's fisheries management program in the 1950s. This source reports notable catches from this pit including a 7-pound largemouth bass in March 1954 and a 3-pound crappie in May 1953, indicating successful fish growth following reconditioning and restocking efforts.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
