# Vemco V13-1L

## Summary
Vemco V13-1L transmitters are acoustic telemetry tags weighing 6 grams in water with a 40-80 second burst interval and 376-day battery life that were surgically implanted in 22 Northern Pike in this study. These tags enabled tracking of individual pike movements within Stege Lagoon and detection of exits and returns through the lagoon outlet over an 18-month period from March 2003 to August 2004.
