# Gio's Point

## Summary
Gio's Point is one of five fixed remote PIT scanning sites established in the River Zone of Lake Mohave for continuous monitoring of razorback sucker movements and aggregations. These fixed sites, equipped with shore-based or submersible PIT antennas, collectively accumulated 44,653 scan hours in River Zone and detected 3,398 unique razorback suckers during the 2015-2019 study period.
