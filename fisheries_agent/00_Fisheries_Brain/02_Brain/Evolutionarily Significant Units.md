# Evolutionarily Significant Units

## Summary
Evolutionarily Significant Units (ESUs) are groups designated by the National Marine Fisheries Service that contribute to diversity in populations and have evolutionary significance. ESUs receive protected status under the Endangered Species Act, with examples including Snake River Sockeye Salmon in Redfish Lake and Snake River fall and spring-summer Chinook Salmon.
