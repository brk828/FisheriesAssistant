# Winslow

## Summary
Winslow is a town in northern Arizona along the Little Colorado River. Historically in the 1880s, extensive swamps and marshy areas existed in the Little Colorado River basin above Winslow, but the river now has perennial flow only in its uppermost 10 to 15 percent of its length.
