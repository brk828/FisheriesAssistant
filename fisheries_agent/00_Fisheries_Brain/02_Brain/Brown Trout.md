# Brown Trout

## Summary
Brown Trout (Salmo trutta) is a highly adaptable, invasive salmonid species native to Europe and western Asia that has been widely introduced to freshwater ecosystems across North America and globally for recreational fishing purposes. As both a predatory competitor and habitat modifier, brown trout significantly impacts native fish communities through predation on smaller species and competition for resources, making it a key species of concern in conservation efforts aimed at protecting endemic and threatened freshwater fishes, particularly in western riverine systems. Its establishment in non-native waters exemplifies the ecological challenges posed by salmonid introductions and the complexities of managing invasive species in aquatic ecosystems where native fish populations are already threatened by habitat degradation and other anthropogenic stressors.
