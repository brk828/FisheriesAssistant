# Warm Springs Pupfish

## Summary
The Warm Springs Pupfish is a critically endangered endemic species belonging to the Cyprinodontidae family, confined to geothermal spring systems in the Great Basin region, particularly within or near Death Valley National Monument. Like other pupfish species in this region, it represents an extreme example of range restriction and endemism, having evolved in isolation within specific spring complexes and facing severe conservation challenges from habitat degradation, water extraction, and invasive species. These tiny cyprinodont fishes serve as important indicators of spring ecosystem health and are central to regional conservation efforts documented in Death Valley Area Committee planning documents from the 1980s.
