# Eastern Chain Pickerel

## Summary
Eastern Chain Pickerel (Esox niger) is a fish species that serves as a second intermediate host for bass tapeworm. In this survey, 154 pickerel were examined from 68 lakes, with 39.6% infected; infections typically involved 1-15 larvae in liver, fat, and mesenteries, though one specimen from Upper Suncook Lake contained 106 larvae.
