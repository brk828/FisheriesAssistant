# Desert Sucker

## Summary
The Desert Sucker (*Pantosteus clarki*) is a native cyprinid catostomid fish species endemic to southwestern North American aquatic systems, particularly the Lower Colorado River basin and its tributaries. This algae-grazing sucker possesses the characteristic subterminal, cartilaginous-edged mouth typical of benthic detritivorous suckers and has been considered a conservation candidate due to habitat loss, river flow regulation, dam impacts, and competition from introduced nonnative species. As a small-bodied native fish inhabiting altered riverine ecosystems, the Desert Sucker represents a significant focus for southwestern desert fish recovery efforts and was recommended for federal candidate species listing.
