# U.S. Fish And Wildlife Service

## Summary
The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) is a federal agency responsible for managing and conserving fish, wildlife, and natural resources, playing a central role in implementing the Endangered Species Act and overseeing fish recovery programs, hatchery operations, and interagency coordination for aquatic ecosystem management. In the context of fish science and conservation, the USFWS develops and implements recovery plans for threatened and endangered fish species, conducts ecological monitoring, and works collaboratively with other agencies and institutions to restore aquatic habitats and manage fish populations. Through its regional offices and facilities such as national fish hatcheries, the USFWS provides scientific expertise and regulatory oversight for freshwater and marine fisheries conservation efforts across the United States.
