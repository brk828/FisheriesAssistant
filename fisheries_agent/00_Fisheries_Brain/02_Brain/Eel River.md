# Eel River

## Summary
The Eel River was the location of Cedar Creek Hatchery in California. The catastrophic December 1964 flood on this river completely destroyed the hatchery and all its fish stock, contributing to significant losses in the state's steelhead trout production program.

## Context

> During the 1964-65 fiscal year, California Department of Fish and Game hatcheries planted a record-breaking **60,129,973 fish** totaling **1,879,769 pounds**, though total weight was 207,290 pounds less than the previous year.
