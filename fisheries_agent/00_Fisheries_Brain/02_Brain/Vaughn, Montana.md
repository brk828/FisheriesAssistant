# Vaughn, Montana

## Summary
Vaughn, Montana was the site of a mosquitofish stocking experiment where 200 adult fish were introduced to a pond. Within one week of stocking, mosquito larvae density in the pond decreased dramatically from 60 per square foot to 4 per square foot, demonstrating the effectiveness of the biological control method.

## Context

> This article from Montana Fish & Game Department (August 1969) describes [[Gambusia affinis affinis]] (mosquitofish) and their use in biological mosquito control.
