# Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation Project

## Summary
The Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation Project in Arizona pumped saline water from a fossil aquifer into the Gila River, which flowed into the Colorado River, causing water salinity delivered to Mexico to increase from 800 parts-per-million to 2,700 ppm by 1960. This created a major diplomatic crisis as Mexican farmers in the Mexicali Valley found their fields coated with white salt residue.
