# Prescott

## Summary
Prescott is a city in Arizona that proposed to divert 7,127 acre-feet of water annually from the upper Verde River as part of the Central Arizona Water Exchange Project. The proposed diversion would extract water from approximately 2.5 miles downstream from Sullivan Lake, with peak demand occurring in June and July requiring flows of approximately 15 cfs.
