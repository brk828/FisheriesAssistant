# Leslie Canyon National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Leslie Canyon National Wildlife Refuge is a 1,240-acre protected area in southeastern Arizona established in 1988 when the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service purchased the property from The Nature Conservancy. The refuge serves as critical habitat for endangered Yaqui fish species, particularly after Dr. W.L. Minckley moved approximately 200 Yaqui chub to Leslie Creek in 1969 from rapidly drying Astin Spring, forming the basis for broodstock. Recovery objectives include securing and protecting the Leslie Creek watershed to maintain adequate flows and establishing instream flow rights for Leslie Creek.
