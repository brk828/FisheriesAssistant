# El Golfo

## Summary
El Golfo refers to the Gulf of California collection site near the Colorado River delta where striped mullet were sampled. Fish collected from El Golfo were significantly smaller on average (22.4 cm) compared to Colorado River specimens (33.1 cm), and larger size classes (39-46 cm) were difficult to collect in these Gulf waters.

## Context

> This study documents the distribution patterns of [[Striped Mullet]] (Mugil cephalus) in the [[Colorado River]] and [[Gulf of California]] during 1967-1968, revealing evidence of upstream migration and questioning traditional understanding of salinity requirements for spawning.
