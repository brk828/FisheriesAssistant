# Rio Conchos

## Summary
The Rio Conchos is a major tributary of the Rio Grande located in the Mexico-Texas border region that serves as an important confluence point and aquatic habitat zone relevant to ichthyological surveys and conservation efforts in the Chihuahuan Desert. This river system is particularly significant for understanding the fish fauna composition of the middle Rio Grande and represents a critical area for monitoring endemic and endangered desert fish species, as well as detecting the spread of non-native aquatic organisms. The Rio Conchos and its lower reaches have been identified as priority zones for targeted biological surveys and conservation action to protect native Chihuahuan Desert fish assemblages in the Rio Grande system.
