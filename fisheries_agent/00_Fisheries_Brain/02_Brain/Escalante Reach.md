# Escalante Reach

## Summary
The Escalante Reach (RMI 319.4-301.7) is a section of the middle Green River where 55% of all adult razorback sucker captures occurred, the highest concentration of any reach. It is identified as a key monitoring area, with Cliff Creek accounting for 90% of larval captures within this reach.
