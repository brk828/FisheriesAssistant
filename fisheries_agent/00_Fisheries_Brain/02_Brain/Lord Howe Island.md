# Lord Howe Island

## Summary
Lord Howe Island is the location where Lernaeolophus sultanus, one of only two lernaeocerid copepods previously recorded from Australia, was found parasitizing the lower lip of a Tetraodon species. This was one of the few Australian records of this parasitic copepod family before the discovery of Impexus hamondi.
