# Typha domingensis

## Summary
Typha domingensis (southern cattail) is an obligate wetland species that dominates wet fluvial marshes in the Colorado River corridor. This source reports it was one of the dominant species in wet marshes (which comprised 30% of total marsh vegetation by 1991), and it experienced riverward displacement under the Interim Flows regime along with other wet marsh species.
