# Atrato River

## Summary
The Atrato River is an Atlantic drainage basin in Colombia that was sampled during the 1912-1913 gymnotid collections. The Atrato Basin was one of several major river systems surveyed to document the distribution of electric knifefishes in trans-Andean regions.
