# Arctic Cisco

## Summary
Arctic Cisco (Coregonus autumnalis) is a whitefish species documented in the Arctic Watershed of Canada. The checklist notes taxonomic uncertainty regarding the C. autumnalis/C. laurettae species complex in Alaska, as identified by McPhail in 1966.
