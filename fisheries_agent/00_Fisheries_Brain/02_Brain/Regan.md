# Regan

## Summary
Regan was a previous researcher who noted the presence of nitrate in the upper Thames Estuary reaches and observed its reduction further seaward when dissolved oxygen fell near major sewage outfalls. This earlier work provided context for understanding nitrate dynamics in the estuary.
