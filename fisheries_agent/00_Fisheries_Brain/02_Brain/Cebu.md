# Cebu

## Summary
Cebu is a Philippine island that forms one side of Tanon Strait (the other being Negros). This source notes that Tanon Strait between Negros and Cebu has much higher bottom temperatures (approximately 17°C at depths between 400-700 meters) compared to the deeper Sulu Sea proper.
