# Turks Head

## Summary
Turks Head is a location on the Green River in Utah that served as the downstream boundary of the study area. The research examined early life history of Colorado Squawfish and Razorback Sucker across 520 km of the Green River, from Echo Park in Colorado downstream to Turks Head, Utah.
