# Carlin Tag

## Summary
A Carlin tag is a type of external fish identification marker used in mark-recapture studies to track individual fish movements, growth, and survival over time. Named after its design, this tag is attached to fish and allows researchers to identify previously tagged individuals upon recapture, making it valuable for population studies, migration research, and long-term monitoring of fish populations. Carlin tags were commonly employed in fisheries science alongside other tagging methods like Floy tags to gather data on fish growth rates, population dynamics, and ecological responses to environmental changes.
