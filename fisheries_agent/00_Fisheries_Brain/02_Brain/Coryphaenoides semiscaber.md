# Coryphaenoides semiscaber

## Summary
Coryphaenoides semiscaber is a macrourid fish found in the Philippine Subregion that represents the East Indian species C. tydemani. This species is one of the endemic or distinctive forms found in the isolated Sulu Sea complex.
