# Tellico Dam

## Summary
The Tellico Dam is a hydroelectric dam project on the Tellico River in Tennessee that became a landmark case in conservation biology and fisheries science due to its conflict with the protection of the snail darter (*Percina tanasi*), an endangered endemic fish species. The dam's construction threatened the river habitat critical for the snail darter's survival, making it a pivotal example of how infrastructure development intersects with endangered species protection under the Endangered Species Act. This case fundamentally shaped discussions about balancing economic development with aquatic ecosystem conservation and species preservation in North America.
