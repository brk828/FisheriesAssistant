# Turkey Creek

## Summary
Turkey Creek is a desert stream system in Arizona that serves as a critical conservation focal point for native fish species protection and invasive species management, particularly concerning red shiner invasion barriers and habitat monitoring in the broader Aravaipa Creek watershed. The creek and its associated riparian corridor represent an important aquatic ecosystem on public lands where native fish assemblages, including species like spikedace and loach minnow, require active management to prevent upstream colonization by non-native competitors. Turkey Creek exemplifies the complex conservation challenges in arid southwestern aquatic systems where strategic barrier placement and periodic habitat surveys are necessary to maintain native fish populations against the threat of invasive species expansion.
