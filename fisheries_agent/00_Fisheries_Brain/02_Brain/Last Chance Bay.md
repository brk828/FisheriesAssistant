# Last Chance Bay

## Summary
Last Chance Bay is a side-canyon embayment in Lake Powell where oxygen depletion patterns were studied in detail. During the period from August 5 to September 30, 1974, researchers found that the oxygen deficit was approximately equal at both the bay mouth and bay head, but the depletion was distributed over a greater depth at the bay mouth, demonstrating how oxygen depletion becomes more concentrated and intense toward the heads of side canyons.
