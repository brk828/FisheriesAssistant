# San Jose, California

## Summary
San Jose, California is the location of a garden pond where the author conducted detailed seven-year observations of Gambusia's impact on goldfish populations. The controlled study showed that Gambusia presence reduced goldfish recruitment by approximately 95%, with populations recovering dramatically after Gambusia removal.
