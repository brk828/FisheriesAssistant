# Granite Rapid

## Summary
Granite Rapid is a location on the Colorado River where a significant Humpback Chub specimen was collected in April 1979. This source notes that a yearling fish (70 mm total length) taken at this location represented the first juvenile Humpback Chub captured from the mainstream Colorado River since 1970.
