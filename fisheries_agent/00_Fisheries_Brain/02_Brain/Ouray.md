# Ouray

## Summary
Ouray refers to Ouray National Wildlife Refuge, the location where an experimental razorback sucker hatchery was established in 1987. The facility achieved exceptional growth rates for razorback suckers (0.75-1.05 inches per 30 days), exceeding rates at Dexter National Fish Hatchery by 56-119%.
