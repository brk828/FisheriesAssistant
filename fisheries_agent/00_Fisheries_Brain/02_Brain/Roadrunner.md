# Roadrunner

## Summary
The Roadrunner (Geococcyx californianus) is a ground-dwelling bird characteristic of desert environments. This assessment identified it as one of 36 native bird species observed at Papago Park Military Reservation during surveys conducted in July 1994.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
