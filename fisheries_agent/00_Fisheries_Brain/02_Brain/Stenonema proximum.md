# Stenonema proximum

## Summary
Stenonema proximum is a mayfly species found in Michigan trout streams that is similar in size, color, and habits to Stenonema rubrum. It is one of several related Stenonema species that collectively extend the group's flight period from June 15 through the close of trout season.
