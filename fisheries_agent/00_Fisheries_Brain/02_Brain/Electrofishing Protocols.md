# Electrofishing Protocols

## Summary
Electrofishing was used as a capture method to collect razorback suckers and other catostomid species during this study in the Green River basin. The technique enabled researchers to capture 560 adult razorback suckers between 1981 and 1989, with 55% of captures being fish in breeding condition, allowing documentation of spawning behavior, movements, and population characteristics.
