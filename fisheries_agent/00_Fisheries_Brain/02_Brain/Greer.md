# Greer

## Summary
Greer is a town in the White Mountains of eastern Arizona near the headwaters of the Little Colorado River. The current distribution of Little Colorado Spinedace in the mainstem river extends from St. Johns upstream approximately 40 miles to the permanent headwaters near Greer.
