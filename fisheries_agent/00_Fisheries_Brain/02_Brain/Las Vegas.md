# Las Vegas

## Summary
Las Vegas is a city in the American West that exemplifies unsustainable water use in desert environments. According to this source, Las Vegas is part of urban sprawl that uses water for purposes beyond original development intent, including ornamental fountains and recreational uses that place excessive demands on limited water resources in water-poor desert regions.
