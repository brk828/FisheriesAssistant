# Pacific Coast

## Summary
The Pacific Coast refers to the western seaboard of North America where striped bass were successfully introduced from Atlantic stock in 1879 and 1882. According to this source, striped bass populations now range from San Diego, California, to the Columbia River, Oregon, and exhibit similar growth rates to Atlantic populations, though no fish over 100 pounds has been recorded on the Pacific Coast (compared to 125-pound records from the Atlantic).
