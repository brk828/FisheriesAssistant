# Smith Mountain Lake

## Summary
Smith Mountain Lake is a reservoir in Virginia where alewives (a species occupying nearly identical ecological niches to rainbow smelt) were introduced and rapidly became the dominant food source for striped bass. This example is cited in the proposal as evidence that a similar pelagic forage fish could successfully support striped bass populations in Lake Powell.

## Context
