# Rockport

## Summary
Rockport is a coastal location in Texas that served as the primary collection site for marine fish used in this transplant study. Red drum and other marine species were obtained by seining along the Texas coast near Rockport using 20-foot to 100-foot seines with ¼-inch to ½-inch mesh, then transported inland for stocking in the Pecos River System reservoirs.

## Context

> This Federal Aid completion report documents a 14-year experimental program (1957-1971) introducing marine fish species from the [[Gulf of Mexico]] into the saline waters of the [[Pecos River System]] in western Texas.
