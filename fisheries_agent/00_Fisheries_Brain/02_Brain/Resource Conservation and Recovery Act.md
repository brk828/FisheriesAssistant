# Resource Conservation and Recovery Act

## Summary
The Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) is a 1976 federal law establishing two primary regulatory frameworks for waste management: Subtitle C for hazardous waste with rigorous federal standards and enforcement, and Subtitle D for non-hazardous solid waste emphasizing state implementation with federal guidance. This source examines RCRA's application to mining wastes, which received special consideration due to their distinctive characteristics including generation in vastly larger volumes than other industrial wastes, predominantly on-site land disposal, and typically lower hazards.
