# Carbon Dioxide

## Summary
Carbon dioxide is an atmospheric gas that traps long-wave radiation as part of the greenhouse effect. The lecture notes warn that projected increases in CO₂ from fossil fuel combustion over the next 100 years could raise atmospheric temperature 2-3°C, potentially shifting vegetation zones and reducing wheat production significantly.
