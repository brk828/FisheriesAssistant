# Fir

## Summary
Fir is a coniferous tree genus found in high-elevation forests. The lecture notes describe Spruce-Fir forests as part of Arizona's coniferous forest zone, occurring in closed-canopy stands under semi-humid to humid, temperate to cold climatic conditions.
