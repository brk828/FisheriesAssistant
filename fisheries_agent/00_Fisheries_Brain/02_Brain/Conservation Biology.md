# Conservation Biology

## Summary
Conservation Biology, as applied to fish science and aquatic ecology, is the scientific discipline focused on protecting and preserving fish species and their aquatic habitats from extinction and degradation, particularly for vulnerable populations like desert fishes in threatened ecosystems. This field integrates research data, management strategies, and collaborative archival efforts to ensure that scientific knowledge about fish populations and their environmental needs is accessible to researchers and resource managers working to maintain biodiversity. By centralizing and preserving research on fish conservation priorities, conservation biology enables informed decision-making for protecting species at risk and maintaining the ecological integrity of aquatic systems.
