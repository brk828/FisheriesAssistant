# Gila Robusta Complex

## Summary
The Gila Robusta Complex refers to a group of closely related chub species and populations within the Colorado River basin whose taxonomic status, genetic relationships, and morphological distinctions were not definitively resolved and therefore required scientific clarification and study. Research on this complex, conducted under the Colorado River Recovery Program, aimed to better understand the taxonomy, distribution, and genetic variation among these native fishes to inform conservation and management decisions for federally protected species like the endangered bonytail and other at-risk chubs in the region. The complex encompasses both wild populations and specimens from captive propagation programs, highlighting the integration of field research and hatchery management in addressing the conservation needs of these Colorado River endemic fishes.
