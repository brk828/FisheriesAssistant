# Little Snake River

## Summary
The Little Snake River is a major tributary of the Yampa River in the Upper Colorado River Basin that serves as critical habitat for several endangered fish species, most notably the Colorado Squawfish (*Ptychocheilus lucius*) and Humpback Chub (*Gila cypha*). As a component of the Upper Colorado River system, the Little Snake River represents an important conservation focus area for fisheries management and endangered species recovery efforts, requiring coordinated habitat monitoring and restoration strategies to support these native fish populations. The river's ecological significance within the larger basin context makes it a key study area for understanding species distribution, recovery trajectories, and the effectiveness of conservation interventions in desert aquatic ecosystems.
