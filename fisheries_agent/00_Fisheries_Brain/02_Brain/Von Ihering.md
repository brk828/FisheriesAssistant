# Von Ihering

## Summary
Von Ihering was a previous researcher who noted the general similarity between freshwater faunas of Rio Grande do Sul and the La Plata Basin. This observation was supported by Eigenmann's finding that all Poeciliid species recorded from Rio Grande do Sul also occur in the La Plata basin.
