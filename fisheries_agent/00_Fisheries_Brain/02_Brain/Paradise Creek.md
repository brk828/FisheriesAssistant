# Paradise Creek

## Summary
Paradise Creek is a stream in the White Mountains of Arizona that was surveyed in 1988 for Apache Trout populations. The survey found low numbers of Apache Trout with unexpected upstream advancement of German Brown Trout, requiring two days to collect only 24 specimens, indicating a struggling native population under pressure from introduced species.
