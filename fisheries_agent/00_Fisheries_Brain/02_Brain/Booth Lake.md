# Booth Lake

## Summary
Booth Lake is a freshwater lake located in Charlevoix and Otsego counties, Michigan, that was the site of a 1948 chemical treatment experiment to address stunted fish populations. The south basin was treated to remove part of the existing population, yielding 20,192 fish (including 6,060 panfish, perch, and rock bass), with only 42 fish of legal length, and following this population reduction, fish such as Rock Bass showed dramatically improved growth rates, with some individuals showing post-treatment annual growth nearly equal to their first three years combined.
