# UABC

## Summary
UABC (University of Baja California) is the academic institution that houses the Instituto de Investigaciones Oceanologicas, whose researcher Salvador Galindo-Bect presented at the workshop on the connections between river flows and fisheries. UABC also has a museum where Anita Williams documented the history of the Cucapa indigenous peoples.
