# ESA Recovery

## Summary
ESA (Endangered Species Act) recovery efforts for native salmonids in the Box Canyon Reservoir system were threatened by the Northern Pike invasion, which could undermine four decades of planned conservation work. The Northern Pike suppression program was implemented to protect ongoing recovery efforts including upstream fish passage improvements at Box Canyon and Albeni Falls dams, tributary passage enhancements, habitat restoration, and native salmonid conservation hatchery programs.
