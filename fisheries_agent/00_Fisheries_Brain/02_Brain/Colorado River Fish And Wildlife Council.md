# Colorado River Fish And Wildlife Council

## Summary
The Colorado River Fish and Wildlife Council is a multi-jurisdictional management body responsible for coordinating fisheries and conservation decisions across the Colorado River system, including its major reservoirs such as Lake Powell. Operating in conjunction with scientific advisory committees and government agencies, the Council evaluates proposed fish introductions and management actions to balance recreational fishery development with ecological sustainability. The Council's deliberations, as documented in 1990s correspondence regarding rainbow smelt introductions, reflect the complex policy negotiations required when pursuing sportfish management objectives in heavily regulated aquatic ecosystems shared by multiple states.
