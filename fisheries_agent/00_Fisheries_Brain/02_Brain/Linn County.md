# Linn County

## Summary
Linn County was one of three southeastern Kansas counties (along with Crawford and Cherokee) where the strip-pit fisheries management program operated. The county contained experimental fertilization sites including Ideal Pit, Fertilized Pit, and Pumphouse Pit used to study effects of fertilization on fish production.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
