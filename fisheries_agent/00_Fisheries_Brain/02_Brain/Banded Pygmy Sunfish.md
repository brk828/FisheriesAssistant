# Banded Pygmy Sunfish

## Summary
The Banded Pygmy Sunfish is a small freshwater fish species documented across diverse aquatic habitats throughout the Mississippi River drainage system, including main-stem rivers, tributary creeks, oxbow lakes, sloughs, swamps, and semi-permanent wetlands, with notable populations concentrated in the Cache River and Ohio River drainages. This species exhibits ecological associations with densely vegetated shallow wetland environments, making it an important indicator of intact floodplain and lentic ecosystem health in the southeastern United States. Research on its distribution and habitat requirements has contributed significantly to understanding small fish community structure and the conservation status of endemic species within temperate freshwater systems.
