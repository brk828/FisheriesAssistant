# Advanced Limnology

## Summary
Advanced Limnology was a five-week course proposed for the Sierra Ancha summer program emphasizing taxonomy, ecology, and evolutionary biology of freshwater plankton, particularly phytoplankton and crustacean zooplankton. The course included quantitative and qualitative sampling methods, taxonomic identification, and experimental field approaches.
