# Ideal Free Distribution

## Summary
Ideal Free Distribution is a theoretical framework used to explain the partial migration patterns observed in Stege Lagoon pike, where approximately two-thirds leave the lagoon after spawning while one-third remain resident. The theory suggests that pike movement out of the lagoon after spawning may reflect exploitation of alternative food resources in different habitats, with autumn returns corresponding to decreasing temperatures and lower food demands.
