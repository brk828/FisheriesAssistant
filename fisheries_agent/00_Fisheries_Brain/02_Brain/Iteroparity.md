# Iteroparity

## Summary
The long lifespans documented for Colorado River native fishes (razorback suckers averaging 35 years, Colorado squawfish potentially exceeding 50-75 years, bonytails averaging 40 years) combined with sexual maturity at 3-5 years indicate these species are iteroparous, reproducing multiple times over their lifetimes. Colorado squawfish show unerring site fidelity to spawning grounds across years, demonstrating repeated spawning events.
