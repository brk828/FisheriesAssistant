# Vegas Wash Arm

## Summary
The Vegas Wash Arm is a section of Lake Mead where the Project Vela Uniform test explosions took place in June 1962. All nine explosive charges were detonated at the same location in this arm, approximately 200 feet deep and 1,800 yards from the southwest shore.

## Context

> In June 1962, the [[United States Geological Survey]] conducted nine underwater test explosions in [[Lake Mead]] as part of [[Project Vela Uniform]], a continuation of November 1961 tests.
