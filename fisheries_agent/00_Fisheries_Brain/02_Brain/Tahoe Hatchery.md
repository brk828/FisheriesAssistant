# Tahoe Hatchery

## Summary
Tahoe Hatchery is a fish hatchery facility located in the Lake Tahoe region. According to this source, an accidental escapement of kokanee salmon from Tahoe Hatchery in 1944 led to the first successful natural reproduction of the species in Lake Tahoe, with returning spawners observed in 1946 averaging 14.5 inches in length.
