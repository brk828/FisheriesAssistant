# Cyprinodon salinus

## Summary
Cyprinodon salinus, known as the Salt Creek pupfish, is one of the endemic pupfish species of the Death Valley System documented in the bibliography. This species represents one of the new species descriptions cataloged in the literature, reflecting the taxonomic diversity of pupfishes that evolved in the isolated spring and creek systems of the Death Valley region.
