# Escalante Canyon

## Summary
Escalante Canyon is one of the key sampling locations identified in this Lake Powell monitoring proposal. It is listed among the 14 stations along the reservoir mainstem where water quality monitoring would be conducted to document conditions and identify spatial variation in the reservoir.
