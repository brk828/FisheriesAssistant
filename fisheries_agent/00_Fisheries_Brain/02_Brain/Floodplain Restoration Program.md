# Floodplain Restoration Program

## Summary
The Floodplain Restoration Program is a major habitat restoration initiative that received $2.74 million (the largest single habitat restoration allocation) in FY 1999. The program includes land acquisition from 14 owners covering over 3,000 acres on the Green River, levee removal evaluation, and experimental habitat manipulation to restore floodplain connectivity and nursery habitat for endangered fish species.
