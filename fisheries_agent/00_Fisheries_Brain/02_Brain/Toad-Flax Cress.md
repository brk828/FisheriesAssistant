# Toad-Flax Cress

## Summary
Toad-Flax Cress is a proposed threatened or endangered plant species requiring insect pollinators for reproduction. The conference report mandated a 3-mile exclusion zone for aerial ultra-low-volume pesticide spraying around populations to prevent pollinator loss, or alternatively allowed use of carbaryl bait or Nosema biological control instead of aerial spraying.
