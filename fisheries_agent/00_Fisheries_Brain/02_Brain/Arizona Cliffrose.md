# Arizona Cliffrose

## Summary
Arizona Cliffrose is a threatened or endangered plant species requiring insect pollination. This opinion concluded the grasshopper control program would likely jeopardize the species through pesticide impacts on its pollinators, establishing a 3-mile no-aerial-spray buffer around populations or requiring alternative control methods like carbaryl bait or Nosema biological control.
