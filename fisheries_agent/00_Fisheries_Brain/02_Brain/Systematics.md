# Systematics

## Summary
Systematics is the scientific discipline focused on classifying and understanding the evolutionary relationships and diversity of organisms, including fish species. In the context of fish science and conservation biology, systematics integrates morphological, genetic, and chromosomal evidence to identify distinct species, understand their reproductive isolation, and establish taxonomic frameworks that support biodiversity assessment and conservation prioritization. As an infrastructural discipline within biology, systematics provides the foundational taxonomy and phylogenetic understanding necessary for all downstream ecological, conservation, and management efforts in aquatic sciences.
