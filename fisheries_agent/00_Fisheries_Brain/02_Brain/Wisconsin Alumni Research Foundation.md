# Wisconsin Alumni Research Foundation

## Summary
The Wisconsin Alumni Research Foundation is the organization that developed antimycin as a compound for potential agricultural disease treatment. The foundation was responsible for developing this antibiotic produced by microscopic plants, originally intended as an anti-fungal agent.
