# Lago de Guzman

## Summary
Lago de Guzman is a location where the Mimbres River may have historically joined the Casas Grandes drainage. This historical connection helps explain why Mimbres River Chihuahua Chub specimens show greatest phenetic similarity to Rio Casas Grandes populations in morphometric analyses.
