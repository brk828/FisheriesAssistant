# Cannabis sativa

## Summary
Cannabis sativa (hemp) is a plant species whose seeds are notably lipid-rich. This source reports that C. sativa seeds have a calorific value of 6300 cal/g, which is substantially higher than typical plant vegetative tissue due to their high lipid content.
