# Trail Canyon

## Summary
Trail Canyon is a tributary of Mogollon Creek in the Gila National Forest where a 3-foot waterfall barrier at its junction was intended to protect Gila Trout populations upstream. In 1993, Brown Trout were discovered above this barrier, prompting restoration efforts; Trail Canyon was itself reclaimed for Gila Trout in 1986-1987 and served as one of the temporary holding areas for Gila Trout during the 1993 Mogollon Creek antimycin treatment.
