# Plecoptera

## Summary
Plecoptera is an order of freshwater macroinvertebrates commonly known as stoneflies, whose nymphs are important bioindicators of aquatic ecosystem health and water quality due to their sensitivity to pollution and habitat degradation. These organisms are key components of benthic communities in streams and rivers, serving as crucial food sources for fish populations and reflecting the overall integrity of freshwater ecosystems. In ichthyology and conservation contexts, Plecoptera are frequently studied as part of aquatic macroinvertebrate assemblages to assess habitat quality and monitor the ecological conditions that support fish communities.
