# Ictalurus Punctatus

## Summary
Ictalurus punctatus, commonly known as channel catfish, is a large-river generalist native to North American freshwater systems that has become an important game fish species and is frequently introduced into new aquatic ecosystems. In conservation assessments of southwestern river systems like the Pecos and Verde Rivers, this species is documented as a non-native introduction that coexists with native cyprinids and catostomids, representing one of many introduced species that can alter native fish community composition. Its ecological role as a benthic predator and its competitive interactions with native fishes make it a significant consideration in aquatic ecosystem management and conservation planning for southwestern river systems.
