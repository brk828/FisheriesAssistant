# Copepods

## Summary
Copepods are small crustaceans that serve both as prey for young Colorado River Squawfish (fish <50 mm) and as likely first intermediate hosts for the tapeworm Ophiotaenia fragilis. Based on related proteocephalid species, copepods such as Cyclops species probably ingest tapeworm eggs and develop procercoid larvae in their body cavity.
