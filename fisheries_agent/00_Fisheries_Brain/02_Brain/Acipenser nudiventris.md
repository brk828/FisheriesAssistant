# Acipenser nudiventris

## Summary
Acipenser nudiventris is a sturgeon species found in the Amu Darya basin of Afghanistan. This source reports it is shared between the Amu Darya and Caspian Sea basins, but notes the Aral Sea population is "almost entirely exterminated" in the Amu Darya, indicating severe conservation concerns.
