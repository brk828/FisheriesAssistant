# Federal Columbia River Power System

## Summary
The Federal Columbia River Power System (FCRPS) is a network of eight mainstem hydroelectric projects on the Columbia and Snake Rivers. According to this 1994 court case, the FCRPS operations were found to cause significant mortality to listed salmon species through turbine passage (6-32% mortality), spill (0-3% mortality), and bypass systems, with the court ordering re-initiation of consultation because NMFS used an arbitrary baseline period and failed to adequately consider species needs over system operational capabilities.
