# Colorado River Arm

## Summary
The Colorado River Arm is a section of Lake Powell that offers unimpeded access to approximately 299 km of upstream inundated habitat in the Colorado and Green River systems. Between 2014-2016, 722 razorback suckers were captured in this arm, with 36% of re-encountered fish moving outside the capture area, including extensive long-distance movements upstream (some exceeding 600 km) or across the reservoir to the San Juan River arm. Fish in this arm were primarily stocked from upstream rivers, particularly the Green River (574 fish) and Colorado River (37 fish).
