# Tule Creek Unnamed Spring 1E

## Summary
Tule Creek Unnamed Spring 1E (Site #74) is a reintroduction site with limited perennial habitat that is located close to the successful Tule Creek site. The report recommends maintaining management at the site and restocking with Gila Topminnow as needed.
