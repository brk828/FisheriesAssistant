# Chironomidae

## Summary
Chironomidae, commonly known as midges or non-biting midges, are a family of small dipteran insects whose larvae are among the most abundant and nutritionally important aquatic macroinvertebrates in freshwater ecosystems. As documented in studies of fish and aquatic predator diets, chironomid larvae represent a primary food source for fish species and other aquatic fauna, often comprising the majority of consumed prey items in stomach content analyses. Their ecological significance in aquatic food webs makes them a key indicator organism for assessing ecosystem health and understanding predator-prey dynamics in lentic and lotic environments.
