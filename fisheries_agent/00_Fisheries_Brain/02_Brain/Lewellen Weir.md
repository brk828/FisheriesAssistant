# Lewellen Weir

## Summary
Lewellen Weir on the North Platte River in Nebraska was used to capture upstream spawning migrations of fish species. Carp, Quillback, and River Carpsucker showed the strongest migration tendencies and vulnerability to weir harvest among the species studied.
