# Camp Creek Reservoir

## Summary
Camp Creek Reservoir is one of several lentic refuge habitats in Utah (located in Cache County) where June sucker have been maintained as part of conservation efforts. This source indicates that June sucker successfully spawned and recruited in this reservoir, and some individuals stocked from Camp Creek were later recaptured during spawning runs, though in smaller numbers than those from Red Butte Reservoir.
