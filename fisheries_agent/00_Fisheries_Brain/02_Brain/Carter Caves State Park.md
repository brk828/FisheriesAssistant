# Carter Caves State Park

## Summary
Carter Caves State Park is a state park in northeastern Kentucky that contains Smoky Hollow Lake, one of the three study lakes examined in this research. The 43-acre lake is located in an area characterized by high wooded hills with narrow bottomland farms, a large watershed of 8,400 acres, and very clear water with high alkalinity (124 ppm) and hardness (142 ppm).
