# Fish Creek

## Summary
Fish Creek is a stream in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that required hiking to access during the 1988 survey. The Apache Trout population there showed a relatively moderate male-biased sex ratio of 1.56:1 (14 males to 9 females), with 23 total specimens collected for analysis.
