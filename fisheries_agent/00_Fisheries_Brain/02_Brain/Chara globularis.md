# Chara globularis

## Summary
Chara globularis is a monoecious species of stonewort in the corticate group of the genus Chara. This study found it has a diploid chromosome number of N=28, consistent with the strong correlation observed where monoecious corticate Chara species are diploid while dioecious ones are haploid.
