# Teddington Weir

## Summary
Teddington Weir is a structure on the Thames River where nitrate enters the Thames Estuary. According to Gameson and Preddy's estimates cited in this study, nitrate entering over this weir provides approximately 56 tons of oxygen per day to the estuary through bacterial reduction processes.
