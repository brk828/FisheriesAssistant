# Orchard Mesa Power Plant

## Summary
The Orchard Mesa Power Plant was identified as a potential facility to supplement flows in the 15-mile reach of the Colorado River when discharge falls below approximately 622 cfs. This was part of efforts to deliver and protect Ruedi Reservoir releases through the 15-mile reach for endangered fish habitat management.
