# Tolypella

## Summary
Tolypella is a genus in tribe Nitelleae with 13 species, distinguished by monopodial branchlets, more than two stem branches per node, terete oospores, and lateral antheridia. This study found Tolypella species (T. intertexta and T. prolifera) have chromosome numbers of N=12.
