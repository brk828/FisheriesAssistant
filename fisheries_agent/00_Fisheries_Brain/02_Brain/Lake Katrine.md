# Lake Katrine

## Summary
Lake Katrine was a 35-acre Wisconsin lake with pH 8.2 and temperature 56°F where antimycin A was tested in alkaline water. An application of 7.5 ppb achieved complete kill of common carp and fathead minnow, and a spawning bay treatment with 4 ppb achieved complete mortality of carp eggs while control areas hatched normally.
