# Salt Cedar

## Summary
Salt Cedar (Tamarix ramosissima) is an introduced riparian plant species that competes with native vegetation along the Yampa River. This source notes that high spring flows help restrict Salt Cedar growth, which is important because this invasive plant alters critical fish habitat through shoreline stabilization and displacement of native riparian communities.
