# Fish Production

## Summary
Fish production refers to the total biomass or yield of fish that can be sustainably generated within an aquatic ecosystem or managed system, determined by the interaction of environmental factors such as water quality, light availability, nutrient cycling, and primary productivity. In conservation and fisheries contexts, fish production serves as a key metric for assessing ecosystem health and supporting human uses like recreational fishing and aquaculture, with factors such as water quality standards, pollutant concentrations, and watershed conditions directly influencing the capacity of waters to support viable fish populations. Understanding fish production dynamics is essential for establishing science-based management practices and water quality criteria that protect both wild fisheries and farmed fish operations.
