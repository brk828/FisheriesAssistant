# Greenside Darter

## Summary
The Greenside Darter is a small benthic percid fish species that inhabits flowing streams and rivers across eastern North America, where it occupies ecological niches alongside dominant darter species such as Rainbow and Fantail Darters. As documented in mid-twentieth century ichthyological surveys, this species represents an important component of stream fish communities, particularly in riffle and run habitats where it contributes to the overall diversity and productivity of lotic ecosystems. Its presence in freshwater assemblages reflects the complex community structure of temperate stream fish faunas and its role as an associated species within darter-dominated fish communities.
