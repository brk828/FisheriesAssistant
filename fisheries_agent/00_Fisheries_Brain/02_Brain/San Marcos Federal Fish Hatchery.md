# San Marcos Federal Fish Hatchery

## Summary
The San Marcos Federal Fish Hatchery (later called the Aquatic Station) is the location where the larvae of Haideoporus texanus were collected. The facility has an artesian well drilled in 1895 that penetrates to a 1.5 m cavern at 59.5 m depth in the Edwards Aquifer, and nets were placed in the water distribution chamber to collect specimens.
