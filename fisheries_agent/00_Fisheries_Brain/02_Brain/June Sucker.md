# June Sucker

## Summary
The June Sucker is an endangered catostomid fish endemic to the Virgin River in Utah, a warm, turbid desert stream tributary of the Colorado River Basin. This species has been the subject of intensive conservation efforts documented in permits from the 1980s-1990s, including targeted young-of-year sampling and recruitment monitoring to assess population viability and genetic diversity. The June Sucker exemplifies conservation challenges in arid Southwestern aquatic ecosystems where habitat degradation and flow alterations threaten endemic fish species adapted to specific riverine conditions.
