# Simmons and Szedlmayer 2013

## Summary
Simmons and Szedlmayer 2013 provided empirical data on Gray Triggerfish size at birth that was used in this study. Their estimate of mean size at birth (2.2 mm with SD = 0.04 mm) was incorporated as a strongly informative prior for the L₀ parameter, which was shared across all ageing protocols and sexes in the growth models.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed:** Accurate estimation of [[Gray Triggerfish]] growth parameters while accounting for ageing error from multiple readers and comparing three ageing protocols (whole otoliths, new dorsal spine protocol, old dorsal spine protocol).
