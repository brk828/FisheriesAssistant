# Phillips

## Summary
Phillips (1965) documented patterns of species composition and abundance that varied markedly with season, especially at upstream stations below effluent outfalls in Skeleton Creek. This earlier work suggested that effluents were limiting factors for fish populations during certain or all seasons, providing context for the morphological variations examined in this dissertation.

## Context

> This doctoral dissertation (Oklahoma State University, 1966) investigated how domestic sewage and oil refinery effluents affected the morphological characteristics and population structure of three cyprinid fish species in [[Skeleton Creek]], Oklahoma.
