# Quail Creek Reservoir

## Summary
Quail Creek Reservoir is an impoundment constructed on the Virgin River system in southwestern Utah that significantly altered downstream aquatic habitat and flow regimes in this desert riverine ecosystem. The reservoir's construction and associated water management infrastructure increased mainstem Virgin River flows by approximately 14-17 cubic feet per second on average, representing a substantial hydrological modification relevant to conservation of native fish species adapted to the river's natural flow patterns. As a key feature of the Virgin River water management system, the reservoir has been the subject of scientific scrutiny regarding its impacts on habitat suitability and population dynamics of endemic desert fishes downstream from the impoundment.
