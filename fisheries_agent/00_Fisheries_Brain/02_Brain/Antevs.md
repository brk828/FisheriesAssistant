# Antevs

## Summary
Antevs (1952) estimated that creating pluvial Lake Russell would have required a temperature depression of 7-9°F (2.5-3.0°C) plus a precipitation increase of 29 inches. This was one of several competing models for the climatic changes necessary to produce pluvial conditions.
