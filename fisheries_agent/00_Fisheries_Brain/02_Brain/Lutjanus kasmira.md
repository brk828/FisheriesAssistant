# Lutjanus kasmira

## Summary
Lutjanus kasmira is the scientific name for the Bluestriped Snapper. This source identifies it as potentially the only valuable species among Hawaii's marine fish introductions, in contrast to several other established species that are rated as mistakes due to poisoning risks.
