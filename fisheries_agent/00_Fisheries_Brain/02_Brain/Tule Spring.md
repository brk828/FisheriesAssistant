# Tule Spring

## Summary
Tule Spring is a spring within San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge with water temperature of 16°C where a population of Yaqui Topminnow was documented in April 1989. The topminnows at this location showed more pigment and darker lateral line stripes compared to those from Black Draw, with 24 specimens collected and returned alive.

## Context

> This field report documents fish collecting activities conducted by S.P. Vives and five students across multiple sites in southeastern Arizona during April 1-2, 1989. The survey targeted native and introduced fish species at [[San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge]] and surrounding creek systems....
