# East Clear Creek

## Summary
East Clear Creek is a tributary stream system within the Little Colorado River watershed in Arizona that serves as important habitat for native fish species, particularly the Little Colorado River Spinedace and other endemic cyprinids. As a riparian and aquatic ecosystem located within the Coconino National Forest, East Clear Creek has been a focus of fisheries surveys and conservation monitoring efforts to track the status and distribution of native fish communities in relation to invasive species like Speckled Dace and non-native trout. The creek's fish assemblage composition and relative abundance patterns have been documented through scientific surveys to inform management and conservation strategies for endemic species in the Little Colorado River drainage.
