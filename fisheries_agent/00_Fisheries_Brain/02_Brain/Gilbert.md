# Gilbert

## Summary
Gilbert (Charles Henry Gilbert) was a researcher who reported tropical pelagic animals occurring over subarctic bottom fauna during the 1906 Albatross cruise in the Sea of Japan. Gilbert and Hubbs together established the Philippine Subregion as a distinct zoogeographic unit separate from the East Indian region.
