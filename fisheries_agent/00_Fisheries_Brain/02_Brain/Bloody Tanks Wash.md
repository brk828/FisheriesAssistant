# Bloody Tanks Wash

## Summary
Bloody Tanks Wash is a drainage feature where process solution losses from the Ranchers Bluebird facility cause surface water degradation and a contamination plume in wash alluvium. Additional contaminant enters the wash alluvium from the north within Miami town limits, creating a plume slightly larger than the Bluebird plume.
