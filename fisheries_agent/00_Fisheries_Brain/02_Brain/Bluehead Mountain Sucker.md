# Bluehead Mountain Sucker

## Summary
The Bluehead Mountain Sucker is a native catostomid (sucker family) species endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly found in tributaries of the Colorado River system and associated drainages in Arizona. As a large-bodied benthic fish, it represents an important component of native fish assemblages in perennial stream habitats and serves as an indicator species for ecosystem health in desert aquatic systems. Conservation of this species has been a focus of southwestern fisheries management programs due to habitat loss, water diversions, and competition from non-native species in its historically restricted range.
