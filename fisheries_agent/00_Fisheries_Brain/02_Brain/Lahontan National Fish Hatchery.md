# Lahontan National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Lahontan National Fish Hatchery is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility established in 1965 in Gardnerville, Nevada, under the Bureau of Reclamation's Washoe Project, dedicated to the conservation and propagation of two endemic species of significant conservation concern in the Lahontan Basin: the cui-ui sucker and Lahontan cutthroat trout. The hatchery operates as part of a broader network of facilities including the Marble Bluff Fish Facilities, serving the California and Nevada region and supporting critical fish passage and spawning run management efforts for these imperiled native fish populations. Its primary mission centers on facilitating reproductive success and population recovery of these species, which face threats from habitat alteration, environmental changes, and population decline in the Lahontan aquatic ecosystem.
