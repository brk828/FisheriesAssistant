# Plankton Ecology

## Summary
Plankton Ecology was the focus of the Advanced Limnology course proposed for the Sierra Ancha summer program. The five-week course emphasized the taxonomy, ecology, and evolutionary biology of freshwater plankton, with particular attention to phytoplankton and crustacean zooplankton through field sampling and experimental approaches.
