# Granby Dam

## Summary
Granby Dam regulates flow in the Colorado River below the reservoir, maintaining flows between 20 and 75 cubic feet per second during the study period. A fish trap operated below the dam captured 491 suckers (586 pounds) and was used to tag upstream-migrating game fish, including 74 rainbow trout (mainly April-June) and 56 brown trout (mainly August-October).
