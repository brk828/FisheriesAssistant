# Lake Texoma

## Summary
Lake Texoma is a large reservoir on the Red River formed by the Denison Dam, serving as an important study site for aquatic ecology and fisheries research in the south-central United States. The lake's diverse habitats, including mussel populations and fish communities comprising species like striped bass and rainbow smelt, make it a valuable system for investigating trophic dynamics, species introductions, and the ecological impacts of impoundment on freshwater ecosystems. Its use in multiple fisheries science studies demonstrates its significance as a model system for understanding reservoir ecology and conservation management in North American inland waters.
