# Riparian Areas

## Summary
Riparian Areas are the zones along streams and rivers that have been significantly impacted by cattle grazing in Arizona. This study identifies cattle grazing in riparian zones as one of the primary threats to Arizona's native fish since the late 1800s, fundamentally altering stream and river ecosystems. The survey revealed that the majority of respondents did not recognize cattle grazing as a contributing factor to native fish decline, though support for cattle grazing restrictions increased substantially after respondents learned of its impact on native fish habitat.
