# Queen Creek

## Summary
Queen Creek is a stream in the Gila River basin in Arizona that serves as the receiving water for Arnett Creek. According to this source, Queen Creek contained exotic fish species (Green Sunfish and Mosquitofish) that could move upstream into Arnett Creek during high flow periods, necessitating the construction of a barrier to prevent their entry during the 1996-1997 native fish restoration project.
