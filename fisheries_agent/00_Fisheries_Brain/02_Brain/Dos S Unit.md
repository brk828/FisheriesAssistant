# Dos S Unit

## Summary
The Dos S Unit is an approximately 80,000-acre portion of the Sunflower Allotment straddling Sycamore Creek northeast of Phoenix, with elevations ranging from 1,540 to 6,240 feet. The unit contains diverse vegetation types including Sonoran desertscrub, mesquite bosque, mixed broadleaf deciduous riparian, interior chaparral, and desert grassland, and is the location of the Mud Springs Gila topminnow population.
