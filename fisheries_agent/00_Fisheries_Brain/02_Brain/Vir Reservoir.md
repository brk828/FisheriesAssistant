# Vir Reservoir

## Summary
The Vir Reservoir is an impoundment on the Svratka River that demonstrated significant downstream and fish community effects. The reservoir increased oligothermic fish populations (particularly brown trout), decreased Chondrostoma nasus populations, and altered organic drift composition with 76.2% planktonic organisms below the dam.
