# Pahranagat River

## Summary
The Pahranagat River is a thermal spring-fed river system in Pahranagat Valley, Nevada. This source indicates that the entire remaining population of Pahranagat Roundtail Chub (37-45 adults) is confined to approximately 2,300 meters of unlined channel on the Burns Ranch portion of this river, with habitat alteration through concrete channelization for irrigation having eliminated most historic habitat.
