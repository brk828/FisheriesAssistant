# Encinitas

## Summary
Encinitas is a location in San Diego County, California where the first eastern Pacific specimen of oilfish was caught on August 13, 1942. The specimen was caught at considerable depth, likely on a set-line between 50 and 400 fathoms, and brought to market with rockfish.
