# Blythe, California

## Summary
Blythe, California is a location in the Lower Colorado River Basin where specimens tentatively identified as Tilapia rendalli were collected. This source reports these specimens from near Blythe as part of the documentation of multiple Tilapia species present in California and Arizona portions of the basin.
