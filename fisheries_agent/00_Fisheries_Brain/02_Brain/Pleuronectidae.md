# Pleuronectidae

## Summary
Pleuronectidae (righteye flounders) is one of 29 fish families covered in this bibliography of larval rearing attempts. Multiple studies on pleuronectid species are included, with some achieving successful rearing through metamorphosis.

## Context

> This 1971 NOAA technical report by [[Robert C. May]] compiles an annotated bibliography of scientific papers describing attempts to rear marine fish larvae in laboratory settings from 1878 through 1969.
