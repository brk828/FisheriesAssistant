# Centipede Grass

## Summary
Centipede grass is a ground cover species recommended for establishing vegetation on pond dams and bare banks to prevent erosion. This source identifies it as one of two grass options (along with Bermuda grass) for stabilizing soil and reducing sediment runoff into fishponds.
