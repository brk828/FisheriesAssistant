# Island Lake Complex

## Summary
Island Lake Complex is one of the specific locations within the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge targeted for restoration in this project. The source identifies it as part of approximately 2,000 acres of wetlands and backwaters planned for enhancement through channel construction, backwater reconnection, and water control structures.
