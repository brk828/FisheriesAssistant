# Bonytail Chubs

## Summary
The Bonytail Chub (Gila elegans) is an endangered desert fish species studied for age determination and conservation. Research verified daily increment formation in otoliths of hatchery populations, and conservation efforts included introducing 6,617 individuals into Lake Mohave at Davis Cove in October 1992.
