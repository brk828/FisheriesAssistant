# Six Mile Cove

## Summary
Six Mile Cove is a known spawning concentration area for razorback suckers in Lake Mohave. It is one of several shallow areas with gravel or cobble substrates where endangered razorback suckers aggregate to spawn during their December to June spawning season, with peak activity occurring from January through March.
