# Diodora inaequalis

## Summary
Diodora inaequalis is a keyhole limpet (gastropod mollusk) found in the Puerto Peñasco region. This field guide documents it as part of the mollusk fauna of the Gulf of California intertidal zone, identified using Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other references.
