# Ouray Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Ouray Wildlife Refuge is a wildlife management area containing multiple interconnected wetland units (L-2 through L-10) with existing infrastructure including dikes, canals, and water control structures. The refuge was the site of a 1994 wetland restoration project designed to implement semi-natural flood inflows across multiple units, with water surface elevations maintained between 4660-4666 feet to support wildlife habitat management objectives.
