# Blue-gill

## Summary
Blue-gill (Lepomis macrochirus) is a sunfish species with a dainty appearance and relatively small mouth that can reach ten inches in length. In New York State, notable populations exist in Black Lake and Lake Champlain's South Bay, though individuals typically grow smaller than in midwestern states, and the species commonly hybridizes with pumpkinseeds in certain waters like Chautauqua Lake.
