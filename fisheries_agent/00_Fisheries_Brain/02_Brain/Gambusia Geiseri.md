# Gambusia Geiseri

## Summary
Gambusia geiseri is a species of livebearing mosquitofish in the family Poeciliidae, documented in taxonomic surveys of Texas freshwater ichthyofauna and the Pecos River system. As a member of the tribe Gambusiini, this species exhibits sexually dimorphic morphological characteristics, particularly modified pectoral fin rays in males that are functionally significant for copulatory behavior in livebearing reproduction. The species represents part of Texas's diverse freshwater fish fauna, occupying ecological niches within river systems of the southwestern United States.
