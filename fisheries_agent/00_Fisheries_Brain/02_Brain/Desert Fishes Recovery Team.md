# Desert Fishes Recovery Team

## Summary
The Desert Fishes Recovery Team is an interagency collaborative group established to coordinate the conservation and recovery of native fish species endemic to southwestern desert aquatic ecosystems, particularly threatened species facing decline from hydrological alterations such as water diversion, groundwater depletion, and dam construction. The team, operating under U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service leadership, brings together federal and state agencies, academic consultants, and resource managers to develop recovery strategies, manage captive populations, and address taxonomic and management controversies affecting imperiled desert fishes. Through systematic consultation and coordination, the team works to mitigate the systemic watershed threats that collectively jeopardize multiple desert fish species across the American Southwest.
