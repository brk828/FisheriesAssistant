# Davis Dam

## Summary
Davis Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the Colorado River located between Lake Mead and Lake Mohave that creates a critical boundary in river management and fish conservation efforts. The dam and its associated tailwater reach below the structure represent a distinct ecological zone important for managing both native fish species (such as humpback chub and bonytail) and introduced sport fish populations in the Lower Colorado River system. Davis Dam's operations, including water releases and temperature regulation, significantly influence downstream fish habitat and have been central to cooperative fisheries management programs between Arizona and Nevada since at least the 1960s.
