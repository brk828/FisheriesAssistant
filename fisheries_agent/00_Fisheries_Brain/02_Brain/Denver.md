# Denver

## Summary
Denver is a major city in Colorado located approximately 50 road miles northeast of the study area on the North Fork of South Platte River. The source indicates the study stream's proximity to this population center contributed to the extreme fishing pressure documented in the research (up to 7,175 fisherman-hours per stream mile in 1948).

## Context

> This two-year fisheries management study (1947-1948) on the [[North Fork of South Platte River]] in [[Colorado]] evaluated the effectiveness of stocking legal-size [[Rainbow Trout]] and [[Brook Trout]] (referred to as "lochs") in a heavily fished mountain stream.
