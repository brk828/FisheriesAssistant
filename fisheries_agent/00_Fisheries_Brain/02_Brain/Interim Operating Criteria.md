# Interim Operating Criteria

## Summary
Interim Operating Criteria are temporary operational guidelines for Glen Canyon Dam that were implemented on November 1, 1991, to reduce adverse impacts on downstream resources in Glen Canyon and Grand Canyon while a comprehensive Environmental Impact Statement was being prepared. These criteria were developed jointly by cooperating agencies with input from scientific researchers and reviewed under National Environmental Policy Act requirements, and were designed to remain in effect until a final Record of Decision was issued.
