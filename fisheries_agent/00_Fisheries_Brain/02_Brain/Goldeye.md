# Goldeye

## Summary
Goldeye (*Hiodon alosoides*) is a small, silvery freshwater fish species native to North American lakes and rivers that has historically served as both a food fish and indicator species in fisheries surveys and ecological studies. Characterized by large eyes adapted for low-light vision in deep or turbid waters, goldeye are typically found in oligotrophic and mesotrophic lakes where they occupy mid-water niches and feed on small aquatic organisms. In the context of mid-20th century fisheries research, goldeye populations were monitored alongside other native species to assess lake productivity, ecosystem health, and the effects of environmental changes on freshwater fish communities.
