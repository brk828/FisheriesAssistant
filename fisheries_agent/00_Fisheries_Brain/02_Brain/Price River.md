# Price River

## Summary
The Price River is a tributary of the Colorado River system where Colorado Squawfish populations persist as of the late 1990s. According to the source, this population has reproduced within the past 30 years, making it one of the remaining locations where the species maintains a presence after being extirpated from the lower Colorado River.
