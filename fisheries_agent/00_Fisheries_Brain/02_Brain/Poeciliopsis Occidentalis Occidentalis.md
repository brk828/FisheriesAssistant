# Poeciliopsis Occidentalis Occidentalis

## Summary
Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis, commonly known as the Gila Topminnow, is an endangered native Arizona fish species that inhabits spring-fed aquatic systems and represents a critical subject of translocation and transplant efforts documented in endangered species management records from the 1980s and 1990s. This subspecies co-occurs with other native species like the Desert Pupfish in fragmented habitats such as Yerba Mansa Spring and Cienega Creek, where it faces threats from habitat loss and competition with introduced species. Conservation efforts for P. o. occidentalis have involved careful monitoring, population surveys, and managed relocations as part of broader endangered species recovery programs in the southwestern United States.
