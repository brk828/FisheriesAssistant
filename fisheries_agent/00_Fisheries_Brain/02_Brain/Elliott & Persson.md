# Elliott & Persson

## Summary
Elliott & Persson (1978) developed an exponential model for describing gut evacuation rates in fish. In this study, the gut evacuation of Longfin Dace conformed to the Elliott & Persson exponential model, with three different calculation methods yielding instantaneous evacuation rates ranging from 0.496 to 0.758 h⁻¹, and the evacuation rate appeared unaffected by body size or food type.
