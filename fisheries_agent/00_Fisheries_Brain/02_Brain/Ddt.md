# Ddt

## Summary
DDT is a synthetic chlorinated hydrocarbon pesticide that has significant impacts on aquatic ecosystems and fish populations through bioaccumulation and persistence in aquatic food webs. In fish science and conservation biology, DDT is studied for its toxic effects on fish physiology, reproduction, and survival, as well as its capacity to accumulate in fish tissues and shellfish at levels that may exceed environmental concentrations. Research on DDT residues in commercially important fish and shellfish species has been critical for understanding pesticide contamination in marine and estuarine ecosystems and informing fisheries management and environmental protection policies.
