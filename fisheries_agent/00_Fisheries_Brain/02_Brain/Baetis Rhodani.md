# Baetis Rhodani

## Summary
Baetis rhodani is a small mayfly (Ephemeroptera, Baetidae) commonly used as a bioindicator organism in European stream and river ecology studies due to its sensitivity to environmental disturbances and water quality conditions. This benthic macroinvertebrate is particularly valuable for assessing the impacts of hydrological events, such as floods, on aquatic community structure, as its population dynamics and substrate preferences make it vulnerable to streambed disturbance and substrate mobilization. Its presence and abundance in freshwater habitats serve as important metrics for monitoring stream health and ecosystem resilience in response to physical and chemical environmental changes.
