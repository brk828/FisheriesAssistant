# Pozos de Arvizu

## Summary
Pozos de Arvizu is a location south of San Luis, Sonora where approximately 60 Cocopa individuals lived as of 1986. This represents one of three main population centers for the remaining Cocopa people, along with Yuma, Arizona and Baja California near the Hardy River.
