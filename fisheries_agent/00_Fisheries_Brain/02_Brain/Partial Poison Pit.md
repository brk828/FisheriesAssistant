# Partial Poison Pit

## Summary
Partial Poison Pit was a strip-pit lake that received partial fish population reduction rather than complete eradication. The site demonstrated exceptional channel catfish growth, with 10-inch fish growing to 18.5 inches in 12 months, and produced a notable 6-pound channel catfish catch.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
