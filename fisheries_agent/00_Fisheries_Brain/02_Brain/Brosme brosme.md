# Brosme brosme

## Summary
Brosme brosme (Cusk) is characterized by an elongate, narrow cerebellum that is tightly applied to the medulla oblongata throughout its length. This brain structure typifies less mobile benthic species and contrasts sharply with the elevated cerebellum of mobile coastal forms.
