# Rocky Mountain Research Station

## Summary
The Rocky Mountain Research Station is a USDA Forest Service research entity that conducts field surveys and biological assessments of fish communities and aquatic habitats in western river systems, particularly in the southwestern United States. Operating in cooperation with state wildlife agencies such as the Arizona Game and Fish Department, the station carries out seasonal sampling and monitoring studies to document fish populations and environmental conditions in streams and rivers like the Upper Verde River. This research provides critical baseline data for fisheries management and conservation planning on National Forest lands.
