# Defenders of Wildlife Trust

## Summary
The Defenders of Wildlife Trust was the sole funding organization for this comprehensive mammal survey of the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve. They worked in cooperation with the Bureau of Land Management and the Arizona Natural Heritage Program to support 187 days of field work from fall 1979 through October 1982.

## Context

> This is the fourth annual progress report (1979-1982) on a comprehensive mammal survey of the [[George Whittell Wildlife Preserve]] in the [[Aravaipa Creek]] watershed, Arizona.
