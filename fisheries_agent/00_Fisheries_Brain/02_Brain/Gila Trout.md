# Gila Trout

## Summary
The Gila Trout is an endangered native salmonid endemic to the Gila River drainage in southwestern New Mexico and Arizona, representing a distinct evolutionary lineage of significant conservation concern. This species has experienced severe habitat decline and population fragmentation due to water diversion, introduced non-native fish species, and degradation of riparian ecosystems, necessitating intensive recovery efforts coordinated by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and partner agencies. Conservation strategies for the Gila Trout focus on habitat restoration, genetic management of captive and wild populations, and reestablishment in protected stream segments to prevent extinction of this regionally unique trout species.
