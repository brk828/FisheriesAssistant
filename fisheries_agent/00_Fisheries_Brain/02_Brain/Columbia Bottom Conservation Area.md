# Columbia Bottom Conservation Area

## Summary
Columbia Bottom Conservation Area is a 4,318-acre property located at the confluence of the Missouri and Mississippi Rivers near Saint Louis, Missouri, acquired by the Missouri Department of Conservation in 1997. The site includes 800 acres of forest, a 110-acre island, and former agricultural land, and represents one of the last remaining significant wildlife habitats along the Missouri River in Missouri, where over 90 percent of floodplain forests, prairies, and wetlands had been converted to agriculture by 1980.
