# Verde River Aquatic Ecology

## Summary
The Verde River drainage supports several introduced bass species, with Smallmouth Bass abundant in Beaver Creek by 1956, Spotted Bass established in large numbers in West Creek tributary by 1949, and Rock Bass occurring in lower Oak Creek. Spotted Bass were introduced to the Verde River drainage by 1942.
