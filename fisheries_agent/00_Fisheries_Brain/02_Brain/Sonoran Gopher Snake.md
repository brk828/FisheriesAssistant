# Sonoran Gopher Snake

## Summary
The Sonoran Gopher Snake (Pituophis melanoleucus) is a native Arizona reptile species in the family Colubridae that was proposed for inclusion in a 1994 captive propagation permit program. Under the proposed regulations, captive-bred offspring could be sold once they reached 18 inches in length, with breeding stock required to be PIT-tagged.
