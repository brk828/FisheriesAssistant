# Virgin River Corridor Area of Critical Environmental Concern

## Summary
The Virgin River Corridor Area of Critical Environmental Concern is a Bureau of Land Management designation that encompasses public lands downstream of the Beaver Dam Wash project area. This source indicates that the area also includes a Special Recreation Management Area and that the confluence reach is eligible for wild and scenic river designation under recreational classification.
