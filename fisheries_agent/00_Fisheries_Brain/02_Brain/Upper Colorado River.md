# Upper Colorado River

## Summary
The Upper Colorado River refers to the section of the Colorado River system above Lake Powell that serves as critical habitat for endangered fish species requiring coordinated management of hydrology, spawning areas, and floodplain restoration. This reach, particularly the Grand Valley section and documented spawning zones, is the focus of multi-agency recovery programs that monitor diverse habitat types including main channels, backwaters, and bottomlands across established study transects. Conservation efforts in this region integrate water management, habitat restoration, and long-term ecological monitoring to support the survival and recovery of native fish populations adapted to the river's specific flow regimes and geomorphological characteristics.
