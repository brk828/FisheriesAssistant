# White Mountain Apache Tribe

## Summary
The White Mountain Apache Tribe is a Native American sovereign nation that plays an active role in freshwater fish conservation and management, particularly regarding native fish species in their Arizona territories. The Tribe engages in fisheries science initiatives including fish hatchery operations, rearing programs, and participation in regional management discussions related to aquatic resource stewardship. Their involvement in correspondence and panel discussions regarding fish management reflects their commitment to both the conservation of indigenous aquatic species and the application of scientific approaches to maintain fishery resources within their lands.
