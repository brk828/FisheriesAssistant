# Lirceus

## Summary
Lirceus is a genus of freshwater isopod crustaceans that was formally reinstated as a valid taxonomic classification for North American species previously misclassified under the genus Mancasellus, following a 1949 taxonomic revision by Hubricht and Mackin. As a member of the family Asellidae, Lirceus species are benthic aquatic organisms important to freshwater ecosystems, serving as indicators of water quality and food sources for fish and other aquatic fauna. The genus represents a significant component of North American freshwater isopod diversity and has been extensively studied in systematic and biogeographic surveys of continental freshwater crustacean fauna.
