# Devils Hole Pupfish

## Summary
The Devils Hole Pupfish (*Cyprinodon diabolis*) is an extremely endangered cyprinodontid fish endemic to a single geothermal pool in Nevada's Devils Hole cave system, making it one of the most critically threatened fish species with the smallest known habitat range of any vertebrate. This tiny, isolated population represents an extreme case of genetic bottlenecking and conservation concern, requiring intensive management efforts including captive breeding programs and habitat monitoring to prevent extinction. The species exemplifies the vulnerability of desert pupfishes to environmental perturbations and serves as a flagship conservation case study for aquatic biodiversity preservation in the American Southwest.
