# Craig Lake

## Summary
Craig Lake is a 122-acre warm-water lake in Branch County, Michigan, connected to the St. Joseph River drainage. During the experimental study, it was opened to year-round fishing and showed the highest pan fish productivity at 327 fish per acre per year, with 77% bluegill in the catch composition and exceptionally high winter catch rates (1.8-2.8 fish per hour) due to concentrated bluegill fishing.
