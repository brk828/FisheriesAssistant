# Tijuana Estuary

## Summary
The Tijuana Estuary is the receiving water body for the Tijuana River at the US-Mexico border that experiences significant pollutant loading from urban runoff and non-point sources. According to this proposal, the estuary receives anthropogenic pollutants including lead, cadmium, chromium, copper, nickel, and zinc, as well as conventional contaminants like nitrate, ammonia, phosphate, and suspended solids from the watershed. The research program planned to directly measure mass emissions of these contaminants into the estuary using automated sampling equipment during storm events and dry-weather conditions.
