# Lower Colorado River Pupfish

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Pupfish (Cyprinodon macularius macularius) is a subspecies of Desert Pupfish that was historically found in the Gila River drainage but was eliminated from Arizona prior to the 1950s. By 1991, three introduced wild populations existed in Arizona at Howard Well, Yerba Mansa, and Cold Springs, representing reintroduction efforts for this extirpated form.
