# Panuco

## Summary
The Rio Panuco is a major Atlantic slope river basin in Mexico. This source identifies it as a center of endemic fish diversity with multiple endemic Dionda species, Ictalurus mexicanus, and Ictiobus labiosus, and notes that Ameiurus melas has been introduced beyond its native range into this system.
