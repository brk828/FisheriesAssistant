# Pycnopsyche

## Summary
Pycnopsyche is a genus of caddisfly whose larvae are aquatic insects. This source includes Pycnopsyche larvae in its compilation of aquatic macroinvertebrate calorific values, reporting values ranging from 5195.9 to 6375.0 cal/g ash-free dry weight for caddisfly larvae.
