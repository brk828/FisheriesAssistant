# Flow Regime Alteration

## Summary
Flow regime alteration is a major threat to Shoal Bass, particularly below hydroelectric dams operated as hydropeaking facilities. This source reports that spawning is triggered by relatively high, murky flows at ~20°C water temperature, and that altered flows affect spawning behavior and reproductive success, while low flows during droughts force fish into deep pool refugia.
