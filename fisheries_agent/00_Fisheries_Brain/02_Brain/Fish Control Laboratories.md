# Fish Control Laboratories

## Summary
The Fish Control Laboratories was a research facility located in La Crosse, Wisconsin, operating under the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife. This source originated from this laboratory, where Joseph B. Hunn compiled his comprehensive bibliography on fish blood chemistry in 1967.

## Context

> This comprehensive bibliography compiled by Joseph B. Hunn for the [[Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife]] in 1967 assembles more than 850 references on fish blood chemistry.
