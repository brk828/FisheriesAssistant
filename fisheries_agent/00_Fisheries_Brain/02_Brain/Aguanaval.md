# Aguanaval

## Summary
The Rio Aguanaval is part of an endorheic (closed) basin system in northern Mexico that harbors unique endemic fish fauna. Along with the Rio Nazas, this closed basin system contains species found nowhere else due to its isolation from other drainages.
