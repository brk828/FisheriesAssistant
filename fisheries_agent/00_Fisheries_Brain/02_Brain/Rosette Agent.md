# Rosette Agent

## Summary
Rosette Agent is a systemic protist parasite recently identified in salmonid populations, described as a eukaryotic, obligate intracellular parasite whose taxonomic classification has not yet been established but which has been successfully cultured. This source reports it was detected in captive winter-run Chinook salmon broodstock at Bodega Marine Laboratory in 1994 and in returning late fall-run salmon in the Sacramento River in 1995, with disease progression being slow but resulting in higher mortality rates. The source describes planned research to determine the resistance of winter-run and fall-run Chinook salmon to this parasite and investigate whether MHC genetic variation affects resistance.
