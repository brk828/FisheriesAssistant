# Dendroica striata

## Summary
Dendroica striata, the blackpoll warbler, is a migratory bird species. This source reports an exceptionally high calorific value of 8190 cal/g ash-free dry weight for very fat fall migrants, demonstrating how pre-migration fat accumulation dramatically increases energy content compared to non-migrants (4720-6360 cal/g).
