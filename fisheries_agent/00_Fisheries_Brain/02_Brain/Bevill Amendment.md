# Bevill Amendment

## Summary
The Bevill Amendment is a 1980 provision that suspended Subtitle C hazardous waste regulation for mining wastes pending comprehensive studies under RCRA Sections 8002(f) and 8002(p). This source explains that EPA's 1985 Report to Congress subsequently addressed extraction and beneficiation wastes from metallic ores, uranium overburden, and non-metals, with EPA's 1986 regulatory determination concluding that Subtitle C regulation was inappropriate for mining wastes and proposing instead a Subtitle D program specifically designed for mining waste characteristics.
