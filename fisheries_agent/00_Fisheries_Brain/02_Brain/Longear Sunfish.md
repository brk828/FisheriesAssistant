# Longear Sunfish

## Summary
The Longear Sunfish (*Lepomis megalotis*) is a small North American sunfish species characterized by distinctive wavy blue lines on its cheeks and gill covers, which are particularly prominent in breeding males. As a member of the Centrarchidae family, it inhabits clear, rocky streams and spring-fed waters where it feeds on small invertebrates and plays an important ecological role as both predator of aquatic insects and prey for larger fish species. The species is commonly used in ichthyological surveys and aquatic community assessments as an indicator of good water quality and healthy stream conditions, making it valuable for conservation biology and fisheries management studies.
