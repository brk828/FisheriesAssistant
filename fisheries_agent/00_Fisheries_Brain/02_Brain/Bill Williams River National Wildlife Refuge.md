# Bill Williams River National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Bill Williams River National Wildlife Refuge is a protected aquatic ecosystem in Arizona that serves as a critical habitat and reintroduction site for endangered Colorado River native fish species, particularly the Bonytail (Gila elegans) and other endemic cyprinids. The refuge functions as both a conservation area for maintaining native fish populations and as a recipient site for hatchery-reared fish stocking programs coordinated by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to restore depleted populations of federally endangered species. Management activities at the refuge include regular monitoring of fish populations and coordination with hatchery facilities to support the recovery of species threatened by habitat alteration and non-native fish invasions in the Colorado River Basin.
