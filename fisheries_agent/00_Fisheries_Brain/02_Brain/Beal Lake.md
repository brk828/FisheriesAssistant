# Beal Lake

## Summary
Beal Lake is a water body in Arizona being considered as a potential protected habitat site for endangered native fish. The management plan lists Beal Lake, along with Mittry Lake and Imperial Reservoir, as one of the potential sites under consideration in Arizona for establishing isolated backwater habitats for bonytail chub and razorback sucker.
