# Black Bullhead Catfish

## Summary
Black Bullhead Catfish (Ictalurus melas) is a catfish species in the family Ictaluridae that was present in Lake Pauline, Texas. This species showed notable resistance to rotenone treatment during the 1963 eradication project, with eleven specimens captured in gill nets just days after treatment, though its numbers were greatly reduced overall.
