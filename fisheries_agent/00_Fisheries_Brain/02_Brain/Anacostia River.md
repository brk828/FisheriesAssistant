# Anacostia River

## Summary
The Anacostia River is an urban river in Washington, D.C. that is home to the Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens restoration site. This source uses it as an example of how urban rivers, despite not being natural environments for most wildlife, can provide important habitat corridors linking species with larger river systems upstream and downstream when properly restored.
