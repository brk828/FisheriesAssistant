# Pawnee Montane Skipper

## Summary
Pawnee Montane Skipper is a proposed threatened or endangered butterfly species found in Douglas County, Colorado. The conference report recommended a 1/4-mile buffer excluding aerial ultra-low-volume pesticide spraying around the skipper's habitat to prevent direct mortality and protect its food plants and larval host plants from contamination.
