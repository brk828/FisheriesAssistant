# Buttes Dam

## Summary
Buttes Dam is an authorized CAP project feature on the Gila River between Coolidge Dam and the CAP canal crossing that was never constructed. The dam is listed as one of the authorized geographic locations where CAP fish and wildlife enhancement activities could potentially occur under the project's legal framework.
