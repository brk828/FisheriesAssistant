# Sacramento-San Joaquin Estuary

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin Estuary is a major estuarine system in California that has experienced dramatic declines in native fish populations due to water diversions, altered hydrology, and habitat conversion. Moyle documented declines of Delta Smelt, Splittail, and other native species in this system, and advocated for higher outflows to support native fish populations while serving on the Delta Native Fishes Recovery Team.
