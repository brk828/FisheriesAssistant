# Central Arizona Water Control Study

## Summary
The Central Arizona Water Control Study is a water management project that included biological assessments and conservation recommendations. This source indicates that the fish barrier construction on Tule Creek was required to comply with conservation recommendation number 6 in the Plan 6 Central Arizona Water Control Study Biological Opinion dated March 8, 1983.
