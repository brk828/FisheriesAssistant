# Sheepy Lake

## Summary
Sheepy Lake was the western extension of Lower Klamath Lake in California that historically supported Lost River Sucker populations and spawning runs in its tributary Sheepy Creek. This source documents that the lake was drained for farmland reclamation before 1924, causing heavy fish mortality including large suckers, though a few large fish were observed in Sheepy Creek as late as spring 1960, suggesting a possible remnant spawning population.

## Context

> This 1965 report documents the first confirmed occurrences of [[Lost River Sucker]] (*Deltistes luxatus*) and [[Shortnose Sucker]] (*Chasmistes brevirostris*) in California waters, adding both species to the state's freshwater fish fauna.
