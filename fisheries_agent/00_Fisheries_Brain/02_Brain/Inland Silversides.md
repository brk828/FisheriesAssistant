# Inland Silversides

## Summary
Inland Silversides (Menidia beryllina) are a forage fish species. In Lake Texoma (Texas-Oklahoma), they are part of a diverse forage complex that coexists with Rainbow Smelt and supports a balanced, trophy-sized striped bass fishery that has been sustained for over 20 years.
