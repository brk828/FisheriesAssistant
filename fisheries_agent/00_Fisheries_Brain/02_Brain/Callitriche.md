# Callitriche

## Summary
Callitriche is a rooted aquatic macrophyte that grows in English chalk streams like the River Ivel. Laboratory studies showed that Callitriche exhibits oxygen-dependent respiration between 1.5 and 16.0 ppm dissolved oxygen, following the relation R' = aC^b where respiration rate varies with oxygen concentration.
