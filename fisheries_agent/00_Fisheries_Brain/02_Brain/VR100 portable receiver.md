# VR100 portable receiver

## Summary
The VR100 portable receiver is a portable acoustic receiver with directional hydrophone used for manual tracking of acoustically tagged fish. In this study, it was used to detect signals from V9 coded tags on Northern Pike through 84-100 cm of ice, with detection range testing showing reliable signal detection at 750 m and some signals detected over 2.5 km distance.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary**: [[Northern Pike]] (*Esox lucius*) is an important target species for ice angling in northern regions.
