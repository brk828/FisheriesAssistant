# Coriolis Effect

## Summary
The Coriolis Effect is the deflection of moving air masses caused by Earth's rotation. The lecture notes explain that this effect produces distinct global climate zones including equatorial hot-wet regions, warm-dry doldrums at 23-35 degrees latitude, cool-wet frontal zones at 45-75 degrees, and cold-dry polar regions.
