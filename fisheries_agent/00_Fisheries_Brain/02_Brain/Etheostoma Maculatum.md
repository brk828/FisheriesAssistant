# Etheostoma Maculatum

## Summary
Etheostoma maculatum, commonly known as the spotted darter, is a small freshwater percid fish belonging to the diverse genus Etheostoma, which comprises one of the largest groups of North American darters endemic to various riverine and stream habitats. This species is relevant to ichthyological research and conservation efforts as representatives of the genus have been extensively documented in taxonomic and reproductive biology studies, with museum specimens providing critical provenance data for understanding population distributions and ecological relationships across different geographic ranges. The species exemplifies the importance of careful specimen curation and locality documentation in darter research, as accurate provenance information is essential for assessing conservation status, population genetics, and potential hybridization events within this taxonomically complex genus.
