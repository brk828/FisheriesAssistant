# 32 Pit

## Summary
32 Pit was a reconditioned strip-pit lake that demonstrated rapid bass growth and reproduction following restocking. Stocked largemouth bass reached 1 pound 3 ounces to 1.5 pounds and spawned successfully within one year, and the site produced notable catches including a 6 pound 2 ounce bass in February 1956 and a 2 pound 14 ounce crappie.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
