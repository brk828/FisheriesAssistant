# Primates

## Summary
Primates are covered by one of 31 mammal-focused Specialist Groups within SSC's taxonomic organizational structure. The Primate Specialist Group is part of the broader network of approximately 102 Specialist Groups that form the core operational units of the Commission. This group would engage in typical specialist group activities including Action Planning, species assessments, and conservation interventions.
