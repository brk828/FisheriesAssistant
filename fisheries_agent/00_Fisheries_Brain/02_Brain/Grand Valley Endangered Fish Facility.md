# Grand Valley Endangered Fish Facility

## Summary
Grand Valley Endangered Fish Facility is a propagation facility that raises endangered fish species from the Upper Colorado River Basin. The FY 2000 Recovery Program allocated $220,000 for operation and maintenance of this facility as part of the propagation and genetics recovery element.
