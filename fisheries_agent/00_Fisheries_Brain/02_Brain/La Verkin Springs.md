# La Verkin Springs

## Summary
La Verkin Springs (also known as Pah Tempe Springs) is a significant thermal spring system in the Virgin River watershed of the southwestern United States that contributes substantial inputs of high total dissolved solids (TDS) and other chemical constituents to the mainstream river. As a major geothermal feature within this desert riverine ecosystem, La Verkin Springs influences water chemistry and habitat conditions in ways that are relevant to native fish population monitoring and conservation efforts, particularly regarding species like the woundfin that depend on specific environmental conditions. The spring's chemical contributions have been documented as an important consideration in fish population studies and taxonomic assessments conducted throughout the Virgin River system.
