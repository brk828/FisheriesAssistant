# Pleistocene Epoch

## Summary
The Pleistocene was a geologically recent epoch lasting between 300,000 to 2-3 million years, characterized by repeated glacial-interglacial cycles with four major periods. This source describes it as marked by dramatic climatic oscillations, extensive glaciation, pluvial lake formation, and sea-level fluctuations of 70-100 meters that profoundly influenced freshwater fish dispersal and speciation through land bridge formation, pluvial lake systems, and climatic zone shifts of up to 800 km.
