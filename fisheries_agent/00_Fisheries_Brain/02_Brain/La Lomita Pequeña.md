# La Lomita Pequeña

## Summary
La Lomita Pequeña is a Santa Cruz/Sacaton Phase hamlet in the Salt River Valley, Arizona, occupied during the Hohokam Sedentary period (approximately A.D. 990-1050). Excavations at this site yielded 283 faunal specimens including 255 unmodified bone fragments and 28 bone artifacts, with the assemblage dominated by lagomorphs (74% of identified specimens) and showing faunal patterns remarkably similar to contemporaneous Hohokam sites.
