# Squaliformes

## Summary
Squaliformes is an order of cartilaginous fishes (sharks) within the subclass Elasmobranchii. This source documents that the order contains 9 families of sharks in the Gulf of California, including families such as Heterodontidae, Orectolobidae, Cetorhinidae, Rhincodontidae, Scyliorhinidae, Triakidae, Carcharhinidae, Sphyrnidae, and Squatinidae.
