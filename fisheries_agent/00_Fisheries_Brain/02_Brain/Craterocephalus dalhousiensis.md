# Craterocephalus dalhousiensis

## Summary
Craterocephalus dalhousiensis is an endemic hardyhead fish species described by Ivantsoff and Glover in 1974. This species demonstrates the remarkable endemism at the species level found in Great Artesian Basin springs and represents ancient lineages isolated for thousands to millions of years.
