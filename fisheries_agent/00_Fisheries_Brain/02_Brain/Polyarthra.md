# Polyarthra

## Summary
Polyarthra is a genus of rotifers (zooplankton) found in Lake Powell during this study. It was one of the most frequently occurring zooplankton genera, appearing in 94 of the 116 plankton samples collected, and was part of the zooplankton community that dominated the reservoir's plankton along with copepods and cladocerans.
