# Sonoran Mountain King Snake

## Summary
The Sonoran Mountain King Snake (Lampropeltis pyromelana) is a native Arizona subspecies proposed for captive propagation with a 14-inch minimum size limit for sale of captive-bred offspring. It was one of several Lampropeltis subspecies included in the proposed regulatory framework.
