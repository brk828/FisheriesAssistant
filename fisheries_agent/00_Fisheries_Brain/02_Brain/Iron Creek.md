# Iron Creek

## Summary
Iron Creek is a headwater stream in the Gila River system that was one of five streams where Gila Trout persisted by the 1960s. In 1981, the Iron Creek population was secured through removal of Brown Trout and construction of a waterfall barrier as part of recovery efforts.
