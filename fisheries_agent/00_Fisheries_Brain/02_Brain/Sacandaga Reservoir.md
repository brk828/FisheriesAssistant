# Sacandaga Reservoir

## Summary
Sacandaga Reservoir is a water body in New York State that serves as an example of fish productivity in relation to aquatic vegetation. The source notes that this reservoir produced fish abundantly in its early years before visible weed beds developed, with the fish population initially supported by microscopic plants like diatoms and algae rather than larger aquatic vegetation.
