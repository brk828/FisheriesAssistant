# Red-bellied Sunfish

## Summary
Red-bellied sunfish (Lepomis auritus), also called "long-eared sunfish," is a sunfish species with a large mouth capable of capturing good-sized lures and is often confused with blue-gill. Native and numerous in eastern and southern New York, it prefers stream conditions but adapts equally well to still waters, including swift water habitats like the Delaware River.
