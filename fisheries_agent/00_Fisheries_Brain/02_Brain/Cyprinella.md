# Cyprinella

## Summary
Cyprinella is a genus of small cyprinid fishes found primarily in North American freshwater systems, characterized by their often brightly colored males that display distinctive breeding coloration such as intense tangerine orange hues during spawning season. Members of this genus, including species like Cyprinella formosa, have undergone multiple taxonomic reclassifications throughout ichthyological history, reflecting evolving understanding of cyprinid systematics and phylogenetic relationships. These fishes are ecologically and conservationally significant components of their native drainages, with populations monitored for both their intrinsic biodiversity value and as indicators of freshwater ecosystem health in southwestern and other North American regions.
