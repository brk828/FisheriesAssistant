# Gunlock Reservoir

## Summary
Gunlock Reservoir is an impoundment on the Santa Clara River that has inundated historic Virgin spinedace habitat. According to this conservation strategy, the reservoir is one of three that have collectively inundated approximately 6.8 km of the species' historic range, and the Santa Clara River below the reservoir has been impacted by livestock and recreational use.

## Context

> This November 1994 draft conservation strategy addresses the [[Virgin Spinedace]] (*Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis*), a threatened cyprinid endemic to the [[Virgin River]] basin spanning southwestern Utah, northwestern Arizona, and southeastern Nevada.
