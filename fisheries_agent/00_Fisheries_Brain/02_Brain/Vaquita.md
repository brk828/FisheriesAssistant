# Vaquita

## Summary
The vaquita (*Phocoena sinus*) is a critically endangered porpoise endemic to the Upper Gulf of California, representing a key indicator species for the health of the Colorado River Delta-Gulf of California ecosystem. Though technically a marine mammal rather than a fish, the vaquita appears in fisheries and conservation literature alongside aquatic species like the totoaba because its survival is intimately linked to the same freshwater inflow, estuarine habitat quality, and binational water management decisions that affect fish populations in the region. Its decline reflects broader ecosystem degradation caused by damming and reduced freshwater flow into the Gulf, making it a symbolic focal point for conservation efforts that integrate fish, wildlife, and habitat restoration across the Colorado River system.
