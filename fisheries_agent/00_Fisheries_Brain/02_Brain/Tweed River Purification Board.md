# Tweed River Purification Board

## Summary
The Tweed River Purification Board is the organization that documented and monitored the 1956 fish mortality event in the River Tweed. The Board recorded diurnal pH fluctuations on June 26, 1956, showing pH values reaching approximately 10.0 during daylight hours when photosynthetic activity was most intense.
