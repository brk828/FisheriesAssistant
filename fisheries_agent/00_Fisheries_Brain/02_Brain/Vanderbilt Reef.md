# Vanderbilt Reef

## Summary
Vanderbilt Reef is a study site in Lynn Canal featuring a sunken vessel at 13-28 m depth where rockfish aggregations were observed. No dives were conducted at this site during the November-April cold period.

## Context

> This study documents seasonal behavioral patterns in mixed aggregations of [[Yellowtail Rockfish]] (*Sebastes flavidus*) and [[Dusky Rockfish]] (*S. ciliatus*) observed by divers in [[Lynn Canal]], southeastern [[Alaska]] from 1965-75.
