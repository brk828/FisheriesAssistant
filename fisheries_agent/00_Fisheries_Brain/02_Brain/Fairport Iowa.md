# Fairport Iowa

## Summary
Fairport, Iowa was the location of a U.S. Biological Station where buffalo fish aquaculture research and operations were conducted. At this facility, both razorback and bigmouth buffalo were successfully reared in ponds as small as 3,845 square feet, and fish reached sexual maturity at 4 years old under pond conditions.
