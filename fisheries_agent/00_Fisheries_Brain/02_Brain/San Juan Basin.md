# San Juan Basin

## Summary
The San Juan Basin is a Pacific slope drainage system in Colombia that was among the localities sampled during the 1912-1913 gymnotid collections. It was one of several Pacific drainage river systems surveyed to document the distribution of electric knifefishes in the trans-Andean region.
