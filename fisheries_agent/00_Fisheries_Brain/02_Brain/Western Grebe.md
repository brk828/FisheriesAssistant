# Western Grebe

## Summary
Western Grebe is a fish-eating waterbird that suffered chronic poisoning at Clear Lake from DDD (TDE) used for gnat control. This source reports that dead grebes contained up to 1,600 ppm DDD in tissues due to bioaccumulation through the food chain, with the toxicant concentrating in plankton-eating fish and further magnifying in carnivorous fish that grebes consumed.
