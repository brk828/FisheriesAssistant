# Steelhead

## Summary
Steelhead are the anadromous (sea-run) life-history form of *Oncorhynchus mykiss*, characterized by migratory behavior between freshwater spawning habitats and marine environments, in contrast to the resident rainbow trout form of the same species. These fish typically exhibit larger body size and enhanced silvery coloration as physiological adaptations to their oceanic phase. In fisheries management and conservation contexts, steelhead are significant targets for hatchery production and restoration programs, particularly in Pacific Northwest river systems like the Quinault River in Washington State, where they represent important cultural and ecological resources.
