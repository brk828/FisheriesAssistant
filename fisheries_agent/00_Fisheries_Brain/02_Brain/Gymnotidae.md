# Gymnotidae

## Summary
Gymnotidae is a family of South American freshwater fishes characterized by their ability to generate and detect electric fields, with the electric eel (Electrophorus) being the most notable member. These weakly electric fishes use bioelectrogenesis for navigation, communication, and prey detection in the murky freshwater habitats of the Amazon and other tropical river systems, and they typically inhabit slightly acidic waters. The family is of significant interest in neurobiology and comparative physiology research due to their specialized electric organs and sensory systems, and they represent an important component of Neotropical freshwater fish biodiversity.
