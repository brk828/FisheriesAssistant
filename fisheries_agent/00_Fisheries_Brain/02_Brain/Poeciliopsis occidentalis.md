# Poeciliopsis Occidentalis

## Summary
Poeciliopsis occidentalis, commonly known as the Gila topminnow, is a small native live-bearing poeciliid fish endemic to the Gila River basin and Rio Yaqui drainage in the southwestern United States and Mexico. This species is of significant conservation concern due to its restricted range and declining populations, often found in low numbers and increasingly restricted to upper tributary streams and effluent habitats where it remains isolated from invasive exotic fish species that dominate main channel environments. As a desert fish species, P. occidentalis serves as an important focal species for aquatic conservation efforts and represents a key target in native fish recovery programs throughout the American Southwest.
