# Life History Variation

## Summary
Life history variation describes differences in behavioral strategies among individuals within a population. This source found that 58% of tracked Razorback Suckers moved between reservoir and riverine habitats while 42% remained entirely within the reservoir, suggesting potential adfluvial life history strategy in a significant proportion of the population and intrinsic variation in movement strategies that may reflect natural individual variation or responses to habitat alteration.
