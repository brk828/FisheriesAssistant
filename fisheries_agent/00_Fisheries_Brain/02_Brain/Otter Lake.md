# Otter Lake

## Summary
Otter Lake is a Wisconsin lake that recorded the highest oxygen concentration in the study: 35.7 mg/L at 4.5 meters (416% saturation, 156% absolute saturation). The lake is classified as having a Type IIIa diatom maximum dominated by Fragilaria and other diatoms. Its thermocline was positioned 2.1 meters below the center of gravity (19.6% of maximum depth), differing from the optimal positioning seen in Indiana Oscillatoria lakes.
