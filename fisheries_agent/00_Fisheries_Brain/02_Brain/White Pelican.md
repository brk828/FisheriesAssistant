# White Pelican

## Summary
White Pelican showed much greater susceptibility to toxaphene than DDT in feeding studies. A diet containing 50 ppm toxaphene caused tremors, convulsions, and death in 4-6 weeks, while 50 ppm DDT diet eliminated parasites but caused no mortality.
