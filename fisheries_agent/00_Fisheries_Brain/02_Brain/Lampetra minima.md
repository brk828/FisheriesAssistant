# Lampetra minima

## Summary
Lampetra minima is a lamprey species that went extinct due to improper use of rotenone (a fish toxicant). This source cites this extinction as an example, mentioned by Carl Bond at the conference, of how negligent application of chemical treatments has caused irreversible losses of native aquatic species.
