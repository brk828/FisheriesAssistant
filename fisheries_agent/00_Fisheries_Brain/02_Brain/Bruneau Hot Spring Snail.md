# Bruneau Hot Spring Snail

## Summary
Bruneau Hot Spring Snail is a proposed threatened or endangered aquatic invertebrate species found in Hot Creek, Owyhee County, Idaho. The conference report established a 1/4-mile buffer prohibiting aerial pesticide application around the snail's habitat in specific township and range sections to protect it from pesticide contamination of its aquatic environment.
