# Leptoceridae

## Summary
Leptoceridae is a family of caddisflies (Trichoptera) found in the Red Cedar River study. Along with three other caddisfly families, Leptoceridae was present at Stations IV and VI, and its absence from Station III indicated pollution sensitivity.
