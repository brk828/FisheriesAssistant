# Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act

## Summary
The Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act (SMCRA) is an existing federal law that regulates coal mining wastes. This source identifies SMCRA as providing separate regulatory authority for coal mining wastes, which were therefore excluded from EPA's 1985 Report to Congress on mining waste regulation under RCRA.
