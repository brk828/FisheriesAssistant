# Belowod-See

## Summary
Belowod-See is a lake in Russia that was one of 54 lakes worldwide documented to exhibit metalimnetic oxygen maxima exceeding 12.7 mg/L. It represents one of the single examples from regions outside the main study areas of the United States, Alpine region, and Japan, though no specific data about its characteristics are provided in the source.
