# Grand Wash Fontelicella

## Summary
Grand Wash Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species endemic to Whiskey Spring in the Grand Wash area of Arizona. This hydrobioid snail was classified in 1981 as rare and of special concern due to its extremely limited distribution to a single spring system.
