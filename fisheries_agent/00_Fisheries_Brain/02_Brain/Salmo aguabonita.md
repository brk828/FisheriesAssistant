# Salmo aguabonita

## Summary
Salmo aguabonita, commonly known as Golden Trout, is one of several trout species that was introduced to the Owens River drainage prior to the creation of Crowley Lake. The source notes it was among the game fish introductions to the historically fishless drainage, though it does not indicate that Golden Trout were present in significant numbers when the lake filled or that they contribute substantially to the current fishery.
