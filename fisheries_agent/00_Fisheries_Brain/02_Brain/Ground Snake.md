# Ground Snake

## Summary
The Ground Snake (Sonora semiannulata) is a small snake species inhabiting desert ecosystems. It was confirmed present at Papago Park Military Reservation in Phoenix, Arizona, during the July 1994 biological assessment as one of eight reptile species identified at the site.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
