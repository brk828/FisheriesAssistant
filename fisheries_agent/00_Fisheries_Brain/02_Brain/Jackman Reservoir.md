# Jackman Reservoir

## Summary
Jackman Reservoir is a water body in southern New Hampshire examined in this survey. One largemouth bass from Jackman Reservoir showed very heavy bass tapeworm infection, which was exceptional compared to the typically light to moderate infections observed in this species.
