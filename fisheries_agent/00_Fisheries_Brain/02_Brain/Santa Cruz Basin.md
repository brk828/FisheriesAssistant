# Santa Cruz Basin

## Summary
The Santa Cruz Basin is the region containing the Santa Cruz River in Arizona, which was never a deep river historically and therefore unsuitable habitat for large fish like Colorado River Squawfish that require depths greater than one meter. The Yaqui Catfish was not introduced into the Santa Cruz Basin until 1899, making its presence in earlier Spanish colonial deposits at Tubac anomalous.
