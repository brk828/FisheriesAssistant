# San Xavier District

## Summary
The San Xavier District of the Tohono O'odham Nation was involved in discussions about recharge projects incorporating riparian habitat restoration along the Santa Cruz River. Mark Myers described preliminary design work on a recharge project that would provide riparian habitat restoration desired by the San Xavier District.
