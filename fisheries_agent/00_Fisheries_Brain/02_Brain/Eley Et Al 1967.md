# Eley Et Al 1967

## Summary
Eley et al. 1967 conducted work on reservoir fish species that established 2.0 mg/L as the lower limit of dissolved oxygen for fish distribution. This study adopted this threshold when analyzing vertical fish distributions in Roosevelt and Apache reservoirs, contrasting it with Ellis 1937's higher 5.0 mg/L threshold for riverine habitats.

## Context

> This master's thesis investigated the vertical distribution of fishes in [[Roosevelt Lake]] and [[Apache Lake]], two contiguous reservoirs on the [[Salt River]] in central Arizona, during 1970-1971.
