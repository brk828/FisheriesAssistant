# Dam Impact Studies

## Summary
This study documented impacts of Flaming Gorge Dam on razorback sucker reproduction and habitat. Dam closure altered historic flow and temperature regimens, with spawning now occurring at 9-17°C versus the 20°C optimal for egg hatching, and curtailment of spring flooding resulted in loss of inundated bottomlands that serve as nursery habitat, contributing to recruitment failure and population decline.
