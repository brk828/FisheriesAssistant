# Lymnaeidae

## Summary
Lymnaeidae, or pond snails, are mollusks that were present at all sampling stations except Station VI (6.6 miles downstream from pollution sources). This family was one of several mollusk groups documented in the benthic invertebrate community of the Red Cedar River.
