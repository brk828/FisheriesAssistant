# Virgin River Basin

## Summary
The Virgin River Basin is a tri-state aquatic ecosystem spanning Arizona, Utah, and Nevada that serves as a critical conservation unit for endemic and native fish species, particularly the Virgin Spinedace. Within ichthyology and conservation biology, the basin is studied and managed as an integrated system where fish habitat reaches are classified as either "existing" (currently occupied) or "potential/restoration" areas, with management priorities focused on maintaining habitat integrity against threats such as water rights acquisition and other anthropogenic pressures. The basin represents a key focus area for aquatic resource conservation planning and monitoring efforts in the southwestern United States.
