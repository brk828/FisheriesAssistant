# Fillmore Hatchery

## Summary
Fillmore Hatchery was a California fish production facility that was closed during the 1966-67 fiscal year for modernization work intended to increase its capacity for the following year (1967-68). Despite its closure and the loss of Kern River Hatchery, the state achieved record production by weight during this period.
