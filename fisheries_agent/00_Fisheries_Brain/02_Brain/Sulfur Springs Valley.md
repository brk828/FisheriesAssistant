# Sulfur Springs Valley

## Summary
Sulfur Springs Valley is an immense valley in southeastern Arizona whose floor was dominated by mesquite and sacaton grass during Blumer's observations. Blumer documented that the long, smooth incline descending from the Gleeson foothills toward this valley floor supported Upper Sonoran grassland, representing an unusual altitudinal zone pattern.

## Context

> This historical account documents the botanical work of Swiss-born botanist [[Jacob Corwin Blumer]] (1872-1947) in southeastern [[Arizona Territory]] between August 1906 and October 1907.
