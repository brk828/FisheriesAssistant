# Baker And Schmitz 1971

## Summary
Baker and Schmitz 1971 documented threadfin shad as pelagic plankton feeders, contributing to understanding of the trophic relationship between primary production and forage fish. This work, along with Haskell 1959, supported the analysis of food-chain dynamics linking phytoplankton abundance to threadfin shad distribution in the Arizona reservoirs.

## Context

> This master's thesis investigated the vertical distribution of fishes in [[Roosevelt Lake]] and [[Apache Lake]], two contiguous reservoirs on the [[Salt River]] in central Arizona, during 1970-1971.
