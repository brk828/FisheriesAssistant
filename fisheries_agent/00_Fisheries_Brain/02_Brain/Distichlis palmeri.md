# Distichlis palmeri

## Summary
Distichlis palmeri, known as nipa, was the staple grain of the Cocopa Indians in the Colorado River Delta. This source reports that the Cocopa last harvested this crop in the early 1950s, after which the loss of freshwater flows to the delta made its cultivation impossible.
