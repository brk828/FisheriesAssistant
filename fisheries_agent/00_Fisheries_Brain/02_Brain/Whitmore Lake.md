# Whitmore Lake

## Summary
Whitmore Lake is a 675-acre warm-water lake located in Livingston and Washtenaw counties, Michigan, connected to the Huron River drainage. In this experimental study (1946-1950), it was one of six lakes opened to year-round fishing for species not protected by closed seasons, and over the five-year period, the catch composition was 62.3% bluegill, 14.4% yellow perch, 9.1% crappie, and 5.3% pumpkinseed.
