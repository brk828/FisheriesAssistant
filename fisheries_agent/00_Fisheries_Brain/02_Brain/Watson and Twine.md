# Watson and Twine

## Summary
Watson and Twine were researchers who observed no nitrate reduction until dissolved oxygen reached zero in sewage-effluent mixtures. Their findings suggested complete oxygen inhibition of denitrification, contrasting with this study's results showing denitrification at low but measurable oxygen levels.
