# Pahranagat Spinedace

## Summary
The Pahranagat Spinedace (Lepidomeda altivelis) was one of six native fish species historically found in Pahranagat Valley. This source reports that this species went extinct by the 1950s, representing one of the dramatic declines experienced by all native fish species in the valley.
