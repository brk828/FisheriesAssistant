# Upper Basin Recovery Program

## Summary
The Upper Basin Recovery Program is a cooperative federal-state recovery initiative for endangered Colorado River Basin fishes that was formally initiated in January 1988 after development from 1983-1988. According to this source, the program represents a comprehensive approach that balances instream flow protection with habitat restoration, fish passage construction, non-native fish control, genetic conservation/restocking, and coordinated monitoring/research, and is funded at approximately $11 million annually (FY 96). The program serves as both a reasonable and prudent alternative in Section 7 consultations and provides for recovery by comprehensively addressing habitat needs and limiting factors for the four endangered species.
