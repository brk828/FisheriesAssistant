# Tempe Butte

## Summary
Tempe Butte is a geographic feature that marked a location of subsurface groundwater outflow in the predevelopment hydrologic system. The groundwater flow model estimated approximately 800 acre-feet per year of subsurface outflow occurred at Tempe Butte, representing a small portion of the total 7,300 acre-feet per year of subsurface outflow from the study area.
