# Bradley Brook Reservoir

## Summary
Bradley Brook Reservoir is a water body in New York State with favorable conditions for Yellow Perch growth. Six-year-old perch from this reservoir weighed nearly six times as much as same-age fish from Gilbert Lake, illustrating the dramatic variation in growth rates depending on environmental conditions.
