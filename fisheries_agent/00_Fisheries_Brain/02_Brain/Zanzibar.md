# Zanzibar

## Summary
Zanzibar is an island off the coast of East Africa and the native habitat of the Zanzibari subspecies of Tilapia mossambica. The Zanzibari subspecies is indigenous to the brackish swamps of this region and was one of the two subspecies used in the hybrid production experiments.

## Context

> This 1966 progress report documents the [[California Department of Fish and Game]]'s experimental culture of all-male [[Tilapia mossambica]] hybrids produced by crossing Zanzibari males with Javanese females.
