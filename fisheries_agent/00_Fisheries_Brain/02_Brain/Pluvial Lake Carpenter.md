# Pluvial Lake Carpenter

## Summary
Pluvial Lake Carpenter was an ancient lake system that existed 10,000 to 40,000 years ago in present-day Lake Valley, Nevada. As this lake system dried, fish species including Big Spring Spinedace ancestors became restricted to remnant habitats with permanent water in what is now Meadow Valley Wash, which was formerly drained by the pluvial Carpenter River that joined the pluvial White River to flow into the Colorado River.
