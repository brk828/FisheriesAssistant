# Research Techniques in Animal Behavior

## Summary
Research Techniques in Animal Behavior (Zol 431) was a field class proposed for inclusion in the Sierra Ancha summer program. It was among the courses immediately available for offering, designed to accommodate 12 students in field-based research methods.
