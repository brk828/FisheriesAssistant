# Separation Canyon

## Summary
Separation Canyon is a tributary to the Colorado River located at river mile 239.5 in the Lake Mead inflow area of Grand Canyon. This source identifies it as one of the seasonally-warmed tributaries in the downstream-most section of the study area and notes that large cobble and gravel bars suitable for razorback sucker spawning occur at this location.
