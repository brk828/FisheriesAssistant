# Canelo Pass

## Summary
Canelo Pass is a location in Santa Cruz County, Arizona that serves as the starting point for a proposed segment of the Arizona Trail. The biological opinion evaluates a 12-mile trail segment beginning at Forest Development Road 799 near Canelo Pass and descending through Redrock Canyon.
