# Dingell-Johnson

## Summary
Dingell-Johnson refers to federal funding that was considered for the Leslie Canyon Dam project. The study noted that such federal funds would be precluded from the project due to the presence of endangered Yaqui Topminnow at the site, as federal law prohibits funding projects that jeopardize endangered species under the Endangered Species Act of 1973.
