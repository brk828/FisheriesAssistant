# Freshwater Fish Conservation

## Summary
Freshwater fish conservation encompasses the scientific and management strategies aimed at protecting native fish species and their aquatic ecosystems from extinction, habitat degradation, and other anthropogenic threats. This interdisciplinary field integrates ichthyology, ecology, and regulatory frameworks to preserve biodiversity, maintain ecosystem function, and ensure the long-term survival of freshwater fish populations through habitat restoration, species monitoring, permit-based management, and enforcement of conservation regulations. Freshwater fish conservation is particularly critical given the disproportionately high extinction rates and endemism found in freshwater ecosystems compared to marine or terrestrial environments.
