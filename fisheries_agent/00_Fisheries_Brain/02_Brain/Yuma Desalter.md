# Yuma Desalter

## Summary
The Yuma Desalter is a facility discussed at the workshop as potentially providing better quality water for portions of Mexico, though concerns exist about resulting loss of water supply for the Cienega de Santa Clara. Developing options for the Yuma Desalter to assure continuous water supply for the Cienega was identified as a short-term action item.
