# Loricariidae

## Summary
Loricariidae is a large and diverse family of South American freshwater catfishes characterized by bony plated armor covering their bodies and a specialized sucker-like mouth adapted for grazing on algae and detritus from rock surfaces. As a dominant component of tropical and subtropical stream ecosystems, particularly in Neotropical rivers, they play important ecological roles as primary consumers and occupy similar functional niches to temperate salmonids and galaxiids in their respective biogeographic regions. The family exhibits considerable taxonomic complexity and species diversity, making precise identification challenging, but their abundance and ecological significance make them central to understanding Neotropical aquatic community structure and conservation priorities.
