# Pine Creek Pasture

## Summary
Pine Creek Pasture is one of three north pastures in the Dos S Unit that would be managed under a Santa Rita Grazing System, providing six months of use followed by twelve months of rest. This pasture contains suitable Mexican Spotted Owl habitat overlapping a management territory at its edge, plus additional suitable habitat north of Pine Mountain, and at least two water improvements are planned within one mile of suitable habitat.
