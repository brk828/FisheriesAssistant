# S. Chetverikov

## Summary
S. Chetverikov was one of the four principal scientists who established the foundations of population genetics during 1920-1930. According to this source, his work alongside R. A. Fisher, J. B. S. Haldane, and Sewall Wright provided the theoretical groundwork that enabled the later integration of genetics with evolutionary biology during the Evolutionary Synthesis.
