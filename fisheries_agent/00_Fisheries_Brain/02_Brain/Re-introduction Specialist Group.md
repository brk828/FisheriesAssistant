# Re-introduction Specialist Group

## Summary
The Re-introduction Specialist Group is one of five disciplinary groups within SSC that addresses cross-cutting thematic issues rather than specific taxonomic groups. Its mandate is to develop expertise on reintroductions and translocations of species. The group's work on reintroductions is considered a potentially controversial conservation issue requiring careful review by SSC leadership before interventions with implementing agencies.
