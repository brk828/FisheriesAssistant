# Amargosa Basin

## Summary
The Amargosa Basin is a region in and near Death Valley containing isolated habitats where Cyprinodon nevadensis subspecies occur. This source reports that electrophoretic studies of pupfishes in this basin revealed most of thirty gene loci were homozygous within populations, with only four polymorphic in one population, exemplifying the low within-population genetic diversity characteristic of the Death Valley Model.
