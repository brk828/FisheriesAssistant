# Federal Land Policy and Management Act

## Summary
The Federal Land Policy and Management Act (FLPMA) of October 21, 1976 (43 U.S.C. 1701) is one of the statutory authorities under which BLM Manual 6840 operates for special status species management. This source identifies FLPMA as providing legal authority for BLM's conservation programs alongside the Endangered Species Act and Sikes Act Title II.
