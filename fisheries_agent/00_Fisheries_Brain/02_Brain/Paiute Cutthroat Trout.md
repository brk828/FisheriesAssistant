# Paiute Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
Paiute Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarki seleniris) is found in streams of the Great Basin Province. It represents one of the endemic trout subspecies characteristic of this interior drainage system east of the Sierra Nevada.
