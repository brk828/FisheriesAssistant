# Lee Ferry, Arizona

## Summary
Lee Ferry, Arizona is the geographic point that divides the Colorado River basin into upper and lower basins under the Colorado River Compact of 1922, with each basin allocated 7.5 million acre-feet of water annually. This document notes that water temperatures have been substantially depressed in the 220-mile reach between Lee Ferry and Lake Mead headwaters due to cold-water releases from dams, making this essentially unmodified riparian habitat unsuitable for endemic fish reproduction.
