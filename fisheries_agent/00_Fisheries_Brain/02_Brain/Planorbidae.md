# Planorbidae

## Summary
Planorbidae is a family of freshwater gastropod mollusks (snails) characterized by their planispiral shell coiling pattern and belonging to the subclass Basommatophora, distinguished by the absence of an operculum and possession of monopectinate ctenidia. These snails are ecologically significant in aquatic systems as they serve as important food items for fish and waterfowl, and their presence in fish stomach content analyses provides valuable information about fish feeding ecology and habitat utilization. In conservation and fisheries contexts, Planorbidae distribution and abundance can indicate water quality conditions and are considered key components of benthic macroinvertebrate communities that support aquatic food webs.
