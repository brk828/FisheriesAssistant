# Piscicide

## Summary
A piscicide is a chemical substance used to selectively kill fish, commonly employed in fisheries management and conservation biology for purposes such as removing invasive species, controlling populations, or clearing waters before restocking with native species. Rotenone is the most widely used piscicide in the United States, valued for its effectiveness and relatively rapid degradation in aquatic environments. Piscicides are an important management tool in aquatic ecology, though their use requires careful consideration of environmental persistence, non-target species impacts, and ecological consequences.
