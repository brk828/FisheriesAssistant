# Lernaeoceridae

## Summary
Lernaeoceridae is a family of parasitic copepods commonly found on fishes worldwide but notably scarce in Australian waters. This source describes Impexus hamondi as the third lernaeocerid species recorded from Australia and provides a comprehensive taxonomic key to all 15 known genera in the family based on body shape, holdfast structure, and egg-sac morphology (coiled, axial rod, or straight types).
