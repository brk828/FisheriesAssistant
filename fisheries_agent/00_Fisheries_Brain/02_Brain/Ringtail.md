# Ringtail

## Summary
The Ringtail (Bassariscus astutus) is a strictly nocturnal carnivore that specializes in rocky cliff habitats. This survey found it to be common in the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve but rarely observed due to its nocturnal habits.
