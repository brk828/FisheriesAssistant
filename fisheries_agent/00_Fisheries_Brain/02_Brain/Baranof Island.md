# Baranof Island

## Summary
Baranof Island is located in Southeastern Alaska and is the location of Sashin Creek, where this pink salmon egg decomposition study was conducted from 1961-1963. The island's streams provide spawning habitat for pink salmon and other Pacific salmon species.
