# Coregonus lavaretus

## Summary
Coregonus lavaretus (whitefish) is a salmonid species that was the dominant component of the Lake Mjømane fish community in this survey. It comprised 23.38% of the total catch (3.40 specimens recorded), with individuals measured ranging from approximately 185mm to 260mm in length, and stomach content analysis revealed Chironomidae larvae as prey items.
