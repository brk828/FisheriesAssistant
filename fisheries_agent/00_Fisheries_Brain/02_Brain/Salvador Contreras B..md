# Salvador Contreras B.

## Summary
Salvador Contreras B. was a Mexican ichthyologist who collaborated on the Cuatro Ciénegas Biosphere Reserve management planning effort in 1988. He provided expertise on the fish fauna of the basin as part of the international scientific expert network.
