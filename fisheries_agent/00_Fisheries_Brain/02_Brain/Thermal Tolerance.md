# Thermal Tolerance

## Summary
Thermal tolerance refers to the range of water temperatures within which a fish species can survive and maintain essential physiological functions, encompassing both the upper and lower lethal temperature limits as well as the temperatures at which optimal performance occurs. This concept is fundamental to understanding fish ecology and conservation, as it determines species distribution, habitat suitability, and vulnerability to environmental changes such as climate warming and thermal pollution. Thermal tolerance varies among species and can be influenced by factors such as acclimation history, life stage, and evolutionary adaptation to specific thermal environments.
