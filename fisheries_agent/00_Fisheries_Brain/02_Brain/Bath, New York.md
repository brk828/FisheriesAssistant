# Bath, New York

## Summary
Bath, New York is the location of a New York State Fish Hatchery that provided yearling lake trout (5.4 to 7.5 inches total length) used as one of four sample groups in this fish measurement study. The yearling lake trout from this facility showed statistically significant reductions in measurement error when sedated, though the practical value was minimal.

## Context

> This 1958 study by Wilbur L. Hartman investigated whether sedating fish with [[Urethane]] reduced measurement error and improved accuracy when recording fork and total lengths.
