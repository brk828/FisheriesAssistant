# Cyprus Bagdad Copper Company

## Summary
The Cyprus Bagdad Copper Company operates a pumping station on Francis Creek in the Burro Creek drainage of west-central Arizona that withdraws water under rights for 2,319.36 acre-feet annually, which represented 42.3% of the total 1981 measured discharge. The study recommends voluntary curtailment of pumping during June-August low-flow periods to benefit aquatic resources and black hawk prey availability, and suggests installing a continuous recording flow meter on the company's pipeline to determine actual withdrawal volumes and seasonal patterns.
