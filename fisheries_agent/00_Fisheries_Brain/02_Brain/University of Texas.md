# University of Texas

## Summary
The University of Texas is the institutional affiliation of Dr. Dean Hendrickson, who was scheduled to assist with sampling Mexican Gila populations in five northern river systems during spring 1993. This collaboration was part of the effort to comprehensively sample all Gila populations across their range, including those in northern Mexico.
