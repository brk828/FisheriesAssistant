# Bylas Fontelicella

## Summary
Bylas Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species found in eight springs near Bylas, Graham County, Arizona, monitored since 1969. The species occupies hard substrates in moderate current and co-occurs with Gila Topminnow, with habitat deterioration observed in two springs from cattle trampling, salt cedar, and human activity, and recent Gambusia affinis introductions threatening both species.
