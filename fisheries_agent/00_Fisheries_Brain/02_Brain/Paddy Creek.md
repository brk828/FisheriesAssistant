# Paddy Creek

## Summary
Paddy Creek is a stream in the White Mountains where 12 Apache Trout were collected during the 1988 survey. The population exhibited a strongly male-biased sex ratio of 5.0:1 (10 males to 2 females), contributing to concerns about reproductive dynamics in declining Apache Trout populations.
