# Rana esculenta

## Summary
Rana esculenta is a hybridogenetic frog (family Ranidae) that arose from crosses between Rana ridibunda and Rana lessonae. Reconstruction experiments showed a strong geographical basis for unisexuality, with some ridibunda × lessonae hybrids being hybridogenetic while others had normal meiosis, demonstrating that opportunities for unisexual origins may be restricted to specific progenitor populations. This species also shows evidence of potentially deleterious mutation accumulation consistent with Muller's ratchet operating in asexual lineages.
