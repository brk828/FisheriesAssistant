# Sebastes mystinus

## Summary
Sebastes mystinus (Blue Rockfish) is a Pacific rockfish species mentioned for comparative context. Miller and Geibel (1973) concluded that Blue Rockfish overwintered in different habitat from their summer kelp beds to avoid heavy winter swell, representing another example of seasonal habitat shifts in Pacific rockfishes.
