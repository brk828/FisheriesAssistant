# Brown Bullhead

## Summary
The Brown Bullhead is a freshwater catfish species that appears in ichthyological surveys and fish community assessments, though it is not typically classified among the primary game fish or panfish guilds that are intensively managed in many North American water bodies. This species is documented in comprehensive freshwater fish inventories and taxonomic reviews, where it is described using standard ichthyological methods including morphological characteristics expressed as proportions of total length and documented distribution patterns. As a benthic feeders and habitat generalist, the Brown Bullhead plays an ecological role in freshwater ecosystems and represents an important component of fish assemblage diversity in many temperate lakes and streams.
