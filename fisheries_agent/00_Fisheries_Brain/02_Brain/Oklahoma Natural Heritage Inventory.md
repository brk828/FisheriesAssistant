# Oklahoma Natural Heritage Inventory

## Summary
The Oklahoma Natural Heritage Inventory is an organization that conducted status surveys of the Ouachita Rock Pocketbook in 1989. This survey was one of several scientific assessments cited in the Federal Register document that provided information supporting the endangered species listing decision for this freshwater mussel.
