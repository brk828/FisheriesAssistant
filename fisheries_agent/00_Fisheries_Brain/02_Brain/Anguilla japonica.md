# Anguilla japonica

## Summary
Anguilla japonica is the Japanese Eel, a species of fresh-water eel found in Japan. Like other Anguilla species, it is catadromous, growing in freshwater habitats but breeding in the ocean, and is highly esteemed for food in Japan where eels are reared for market in ponds.
