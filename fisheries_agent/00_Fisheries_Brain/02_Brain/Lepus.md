# Lepus

## Summary
Lepus is a genus of large lagomorphs (hares and jackrabbits) that was the most abundant identified taxon at La Lomita Pequeña. This source reports 18 specimens of Lepus californicus (black-tailed jackrabbits) representing 43% of identified mammals, with 61% of these specimens showing evidence of burning; black-tailed jackrabbits are distributed throughout Arizona below 1800 meters elevation, and species-level identification was not attempted for all Lepus specimens due to small sample size and fragmentary condition.
