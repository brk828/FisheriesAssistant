# St. Croix River

## Summary
The St. Croix River is one of the major New England rivers targeted for Atlantic Salmon restoration efforts. This source reports that it received Atlantic Salmon stocking as part of the restoration program during the decade prior to 1992, contributing to the over 32 million Atlantic Salmon distributed across New England rivers during that period.
