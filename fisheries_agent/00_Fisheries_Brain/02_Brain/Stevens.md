# Stevens

## Summary
Stevens is a researcher who in 1938 predicted future changes to the lower Colorado River system following dam construction. Stevens' work, along with Dill's, provided early documentation of the ecological transformations expected in the river.

## Context

> This 1953 report documents severe fishery losses resulting from [[Bureau of Reclamation]] channelization of the lower [[Colorado River]] between [[Needles]] and [[Topock]], California.
