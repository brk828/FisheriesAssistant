# Peter Bennett

## Summary
Peter Bennett is the author of this 1994 historical account who retraced Jacob Corwin Blumer's travels in southeastern Arizona. Bennett recovered a previously overlooked manuscript by Blumer documenting three horseback trips, and has prepared a draft manuscript about Blumer's work including a complete transcription of Blumer's 1907 manuscript on desert vegetation observations.
