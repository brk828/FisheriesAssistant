# Organpipe National Monument

## Summary
Organpipe National Monument is a protected area relevant to Desert Pupfish conservation, specifically for the Quitobaquito form of the species. The recovery team noted that availability of suitable sites for this subspecific form is very restricted, and habitats outside but within the vicinity of Organpipe National Monument need to be located for reintroduction efforts.
