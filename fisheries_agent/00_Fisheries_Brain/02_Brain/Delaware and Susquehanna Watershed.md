# Delaware and Susquehanna Watershed

## Summary
The Delaware and Susquehanna Watershed is a region in New York State where biological surveys were conducted on Smallmouth Bass populations. Survey data from this watershed documented the reproductive capacity of bass, finding that a 9.8-inch female contained 5,148 eggs and a 15.4-inch specimen contained 11,428 eggs, with conservative estimates suggesting a moderate-sized bass produces approximately 4,000 eggs.
