# Orangethroat Darter

## Summary
The Orangethroat Darter is a small benthic fish species belonging to the family Percidae that inhabits clear, rocky streams and riffles in North American freshwater ecosystems. As a component of diverse darter assemblages in stream communities, it occupies ecological niches alongside dominant species such as Rainbow and Fantail Darters, contributing to the overall biodiversity and trophic structure of lotic habitats. This species is relevant to conservation and fisheries research as an indicator of stream health and water quality conditions, particularly in regions where darter communities are studied for their sensitivity to environmental disturbances and habitat degradation.
