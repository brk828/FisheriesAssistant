# Coleman Creek

## Summary
Coleman Creek is a tributary on the northeastern boundary of the Isabelle Timber Sale area that supports a small self-sustaining population of Apache Trout originating from a 1981 stocking. The upper 1.9 miles of the creek are in unsatisfactory condition for riparian and aquatic values, with concerns about streambank erosion and sediment inflow, though portions had recently been fenced to exclude livestock grazing. Despite poor habitat conditions, the population was considered important to Apache Trout recovery efforts, and steep streamside topography was expected to prevent harvest or burning immediately adjacent to the creek, likely ameliorating most adverse effects from the timber sale.
