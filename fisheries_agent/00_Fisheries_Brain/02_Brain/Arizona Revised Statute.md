# Arizona Revised Statute

## Summary
Arizona Revised Statute 17-306 was cited as the legal authority allowing the Arizona Game and Fish Commission to authorize the sale of live wildlife. The petitioners referenced this statute, along with regulation R12-4-402, as the statutory basis supporting their proposed rule to permit commercial captive propagation of native reptiles and amphibians.
