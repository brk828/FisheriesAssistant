# Turkey Pen Creek

## Summary
Turkey Pen Creek is a tributary or associated waterway of Turkey Creek in the Chiricahua Mountains of Cochise County, Arizona. During the 1997 fisheries survey, sampling was conducted 0.5 miles above the EC Ranch road on this creek as part of the broader assessment of fish populations in the Turkey Creek drainage system.

## Context

> A fisheries survey conducted November 14-15, 1997 along [[Turkey Creek]] on the west side of the [[Chiricahua Mountains]], [[Cochise County]], Arizona documented four fish species.
