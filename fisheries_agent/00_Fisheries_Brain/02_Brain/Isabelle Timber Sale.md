# Isabelle Timber Sale

## Summary
The Isabelle Timber Sale was a proposed timber harvest project covering approximately 7,796 acres with harvest on 1,995 acres yielding approximately 5.66 million board feet of sawtimber using tractor logging in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests. The project included 1 mile of new road construction, 22.3 miles of road reconstruction, mechanical scarification, and prescribed burning, with cutting units abutting Campbell Blue Creek and Castle Creek but not immediately adjacent to Coleman Creek where Apache Trout occurred. Unlike the Campbell sale, no specific buffer zones along drainages were specified and no monitoring of stream flow, sediment, or fish populations was proposed.
