# Quartzite Falls

## Summary
Quartzite Falls is a waterfall barrier on the uppermost Salt River in Arizona. This recovery plan indicates that Quartzite Falls may have historically prevented the Razorback Sucker from colonizing the uppermost Salt River by serving as a barrier to fish passage.

## Context

> This recovery plan addresses the alarming decline of the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*), a monotypic genus endemic to the [[Colorado River]] basin.
