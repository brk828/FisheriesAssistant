# Scale Aging Methods

## Summary
Scale aging methods are techniques used in ichthyology to determine the age of fish by examining the growth rings (circuli) and annuli present on their scales, similar to reading tree rings. These methods are fundamental to fisheries management and conservation biology, enabling researchers to construct age-structure data, growth rates, and population dynamics that inform stock assessments and sustainable harvest strategies. By analyzing scale morphology under magnification, scientists can reconstruct individual fish life histories and cohort patterns essential for understanding population health and developing evidence-based management decisions.
