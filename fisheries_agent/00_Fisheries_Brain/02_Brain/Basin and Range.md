# Basin and Range

## Summary
The Basin and Range refers to a geological province in western North America characterized by crustal extension and collapse. According to this source, Miocene Basin and Range crustal collapse (23-5.3 Ma) created multiple internally-drained basins south and west of the Colorado Plateau, hydrologically disconnecting drainages and allowing ancestral Gila intermedia populations to undergo significant genetic and morphological differentiation in isolation within proto-Gila subbasins.
