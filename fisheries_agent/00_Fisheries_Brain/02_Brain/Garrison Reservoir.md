# Garrison Reservoir

## Summary
Garrison Reservoir in North Dakota was the focus of extensive research on Goldeye, with six completed Master's theses documenting various aspects including depth distribution, life history, lateral distribution relative to ecological factors, and food habits. Research also covered Channel Catfish population dynamics as the population opened to commercial fishing in 1971, plus studies on age and growth of Walleye, Sauger, Yellow Perch, and River Carpsucker.
