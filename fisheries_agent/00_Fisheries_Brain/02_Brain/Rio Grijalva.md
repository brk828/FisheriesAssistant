# Rio Grijalva

## Summary
The Rio Grijalva is a major river system in southern Mexico draining to the Atlantic slope/Gulf of Mexico. This source includes it among the major river systems used as the geographic framework for cataloging Mexican fish distributions in the tropical lowlands.
