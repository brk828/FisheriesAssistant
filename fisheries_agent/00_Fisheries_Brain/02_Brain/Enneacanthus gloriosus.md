# Enneacanthus gloriosus

## Summary
Enneacanthus gloriosus is a sunfish species in the family Centrarchidae found in Alabama. This source indicates it can be identified by having 16-18 scales around the caudal peduncle, fewer than the 19-22 scales found in the related species Enneacanthus obesus.
