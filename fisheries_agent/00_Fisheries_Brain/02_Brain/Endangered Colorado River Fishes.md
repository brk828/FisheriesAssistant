# Endangered Colorado River Fishes

## Summary
Endangered Colorado River fishes refer to native fish species in the Colorado River basin that have experienced severe population declines and habitat loss, primarily due to dam construction, water diversion, altered flow regimes, and introduction of non-native species, making them at risk of extinction. These species, including the Colorado pikeminnow, razorback sucker, humpback chub, and bonytail chub, are subject to intensive conservation and management efforts coordinated among federal, state, and tribal agencies to prevent their complete loss through captive breeding programs, habitat restoration, and recovery planning. Understanding the biology, aging, and population dynamics of these species through scientific research has been critical to developing effective recovery strategies and monitoring their responses to management interventions.
