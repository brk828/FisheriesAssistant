# Achii Hanyo Native Fish Rearing Facility

## Summary
Achii Hanyo Native Fish Rearing Facility is one of the protective rearing facilities used in the razorback sucker repatriation program to raise wild-collected larvae to a size large enough for release into Lake Mohave. This facility, along with others including Willow Beach National Fish Hatchery and Lake Mead Fish Hatchery, provides off-site rearing capacity to produce fish for stocking, with 44 of the 84 repatriates captured during routine monitoring having been reared in such off-site facilities.
