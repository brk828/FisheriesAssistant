# River Test

## Summary
The River Test is a hard-water stream in southern England where Edwards measured macrophyte productivity. Net productivity of macrophytes in the Test ranged from 1.44-2.30 g organic carbon/m²/day, equivalent to 3.8-6.1 g oxygen/m²/day net release.
