# Coati

## Summary
The Coati (Nasua nasua) is a diurnal carnivore that travels in troops and has a confirmed breeding population in the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve. Troops of 15-25 individuals were observed, and the area represents the northern limit of documented breeding for this species, making it biogeographically significant.
