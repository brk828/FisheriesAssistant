# Arizona Department of Health Services

## Summary
The Arizona Department of Health Services is a state agency that was involved in the interagency coordination for the fish reintroduction project. This source indicates that ADHS made a formal request on April 29, 1983, asking the BLM Phoenix District Office to evaluate potential introduction sites as mitigation for the loss of the Cocio Wash topminnow population.
