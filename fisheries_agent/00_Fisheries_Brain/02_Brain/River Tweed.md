# River Tweed

## Summary
The River Tweed is a freshwater river where a significant fish mortality event occurred in 1956 affecting salmon and trout populations. According to this study, the mortalities were caused by high pH values (reaching approximately 10.0) resulting from intense algal photosynthesis during bright sunny weather, with diurnal pH fluctuations documented on June 26, 1956.
