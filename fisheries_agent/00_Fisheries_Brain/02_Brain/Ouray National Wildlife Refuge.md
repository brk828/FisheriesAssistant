# Ouray National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Ouray National Wildlife Refuge is a protected area located along the Green River in the Upper Colorado River Basin that serves as critical habitat for native and non-native fish species, including populations of endangered cyprinids like bonytail and sites where invasive species such as brook stickleback have been documented. The refuge encompasses important backwater and tributary habitats within the regulated flow ecosystem downstream of Flaming Gorge Dam, making it a key focal area for fish recovery efforts and monitoring of aquatic faunal composition in the Upper Colorado River system. As a managed refuge, it represents a significant conservation zone for implementing fisheries management protocols and tracking changes in fish community structure in response to dam regulation and biological invasions.
