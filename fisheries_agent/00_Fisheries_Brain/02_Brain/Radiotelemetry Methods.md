# Radiotelemetry Methods

## Summary
Radiotelemetry was used to track movements of 14 tagged razorback suckers in the Green River basin. The technique revealed that 80% of tracked fish were sedentary, while migratory individuals exhibited movements ranging 52.8-190.4 km, including one fish that moved round-trip 156 km between the Jensen site and Old Charley Wash over three months.
