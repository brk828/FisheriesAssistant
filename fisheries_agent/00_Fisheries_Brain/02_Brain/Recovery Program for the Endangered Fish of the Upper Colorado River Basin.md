# Recovery Program for the Endangered Fish of the Upper Colorado River Basin

## Summary
The Recovery Program for the Endangered Fish of the Upper Colorado River Basin is a collaborative conservation initiative formed in 1987 under a Memorandum of Understanding signed by the Secretary of Interior, governors of Colorado, Wyoming, and Utah, and the Western Area Power Administration administrator. The program invested $60 million over 15 years (with a FY94 budget of $7.7 million) and implemented a five-point recovery strategy including flow management, habitat development, captive breeding, non-native fish restrictions, and research and monitoring. Members include USFWS, USBR, Western Area Power Administration, three state governments, environmental groups, water users, and the Colorado River Energy Distributors Association.
