# Aquaculture

## Summary
Aquaculture refers to the controlled cultivation and rearing of fish and other aquatic organisms in artificial environments such as hatcheries, tanks, and pond systems, serving purposes ranging from commercial production to scientific research and species conservation. In the context of these documents, aquaculture encompasses the technical infrastructure and husbandry practices—including filtration systems and disease management—necessary to maintain healthy populations of cultured fish for breeding programs, experimental work, and food production. This practice bridges applied fisheries management with conservation efforts, enabling both the sustainable harvest of aquatic resources and the preservation of species through controlled breeding and rearing protocols.
