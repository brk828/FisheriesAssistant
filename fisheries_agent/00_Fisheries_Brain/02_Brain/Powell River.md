# Powell River

## Summary
Powell River in Tennessee is one of the localities where newly collected specimens of Noturus flavipinnis were obtained during this study. This source identifies it as part of the Tennessee River drainage where the rediscovered yellowfin madtom populations were documented, along with Copper Creek, Virginia.

## Context

> This paper documents the rediscovery of [[Noturus flavipinnis]] (yellowfin madtom), an [[Ictaluridae]] catfish previously known only from 13 faded specimens collected between 1884 and 1893 from three widely separated localities in the [[Tennessee River]] drainage.
