# Babocomari River

## Summary
The Babocomari River is a historically perennial waterway in the southwestern United States that has experienced significant hydrological degradation, representing a critical example of the widespread loss of permanent flow and riparian wetland systems (cienegas) documented across the region since the 19th century. As evidenced in fisheries and conservation literature, the river's transformation from a reliably flowing aquatic system to an intermittent or degraded state has had substantial impacts on native fish populations and aquatic habitats, making it an important reference point in discussions of desert fish conservation and habitat restoration in the American Southwest.
