# Chydorus

## Summary
Chydorus is a genus of planktonic crustaceans that serves as prey for mosquitofish. This source reports that introduced Gambusia affinis populations completely eliminated Chydorus from experimental ponds, demonstrating the species' destructive impact on native zooplankton communities.
