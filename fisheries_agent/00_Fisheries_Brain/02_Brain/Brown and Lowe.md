# Brown and Lowe

## Summary
Brown and Lowe (1980) is the classification system used in this management plan to identify four biotic communities occurring in the Sierra San Luis area. These communities include Madrean Montane Conifer Forest, Madrean Evergreen Forest, Plains Grasslands, and Gran Bolson (Chihuahua Desert) Grasslands.
