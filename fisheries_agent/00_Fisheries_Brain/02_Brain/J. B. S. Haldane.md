# J. B. S. Haldane

## Summary
J. B. S. Haldane was one of the foundational figures in population genetics. According to this source, he was among the scientists who established the foundations of population genetics during the period 1920-1930, providing the theoretical framework that would later contribute to the Evolutionary Synthesis.
