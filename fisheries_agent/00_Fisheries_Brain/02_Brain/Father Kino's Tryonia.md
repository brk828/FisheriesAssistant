# Father Kino's Tryonia

## Summary
Father Kino's Tryonia is an undescribed Tryonia species endemic to Quitobaquito Springs in Organ Pipe National Monument, Pima County, Arizona. This Priority Category 2 species co-occurs with the Quitobaquito Pupfish and requires inclusion in pupfish recovery planning, with two to three nearby springs remaining uninventoried.
