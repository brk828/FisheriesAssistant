# EC Directive on Conservation of Natural and Semi-Natural Habitats

## Summary
The EC Directive on Conservation of Natural and Semi-Natural Habitats is an important international legal framework mentioned as part of the conservation legislation protecting fish species. It represents one of the key regulatory instruments for fish conservation in Europe.
