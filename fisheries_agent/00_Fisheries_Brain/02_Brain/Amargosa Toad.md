# Amargosa Toad

## Summary
The Amargosa Toad (Bufo nelsoni) is an amphibian species that was petitioned for endangered species listing in 1991. At the time of this document, it was known only from riparian areas along 0.5 mile of the Amargosa River near Beatty, Nevada, and Crystal Spring approximately 1 mile north of Beatty, representing an extremely restricted range.
