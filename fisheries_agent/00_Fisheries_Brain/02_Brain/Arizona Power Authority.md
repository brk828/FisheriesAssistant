# Arizona Power Authority

## Summary
The Arizona Power Authority is a water and power entity participating in the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program (LCR MSCP). They are one of numerous water districts and power authorities involved in this conservation partnership that aims to balance water resource use with species conservation.
