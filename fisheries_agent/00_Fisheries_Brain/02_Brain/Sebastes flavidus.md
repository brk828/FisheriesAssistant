# Sebastes flavidus

## Summary
Sebastes flavidus (Yellowtail Rockfish) is one of two primary species forming the mixed aggregations studied in Lynn Canal. Previous research cited in this study demonstrated that Yellowtail Rockfish from Point Lena exhibited strong site fidelity, returning to home sites even when displaced by 22 km, indicating very restricted home ranges.
