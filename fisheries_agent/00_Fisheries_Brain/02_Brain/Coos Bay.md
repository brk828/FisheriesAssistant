# Coos Bay

## Summary
Coos Bay, Oregon is a Pacific Coast location where striped bass reproductive biology and migration patterns were studied. According to this source, spawning occurs from mid-May to end of June with peak activity around June 10, females mature later than in Atlantic populations (only 18.2% of third-year and 67.9% of fourth-year females were mature), and the population exhibits two distinct migrations: a spring spawning migration upstream and an autumn migration into sloughs.
