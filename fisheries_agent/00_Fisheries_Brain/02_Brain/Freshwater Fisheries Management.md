# Freshwater Fisheries Management

## Summary
Freshwater fisheries management encompasses the science-based practices and policies used to sustain fish populations and aquatic ecosystems in inland waters, including lakes, rivers, and streams. It integrates population monitoring, habitat conservation, regulatory frameworks such as harvest restrictions, and sometimes artificial propagation techniques to balance the ecological integrity of freshwater systems with human recreational and commercial fishing interests. Effective freshwater fisheries management requires understanding fish biology, water quality dynamics, and ecosystem interactions to ensure long-term productivity and biodiversity of inland aquatic resources.
