# Ayerst Laboratories

## Summary
Ayerst Laboratories was the company that obtained registration for the Fintrol-5 formulation of antimycin A. This formulation consisted of antimycin coated on 40-mesh sand designed to release the toxicant evenly within the first 5 feet of water depth.
