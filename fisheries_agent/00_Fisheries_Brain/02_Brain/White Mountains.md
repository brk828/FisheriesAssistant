# White Mountains

## Summary
The White Mountains refers to a mountain range in east-central Arizona that serves as a critical refuge for endemic and native aquatic species, most notably the Apache Trout, and encompasses isolated headwater creek systems that represent discrete populations valuable for conservation and fisheries management. This high-elevation region functions as a "sky island" ecosystem characterized by isolated aquatic habitats that support unique freshwater fauna, including both fish and mollusk communities adapted to coldwater conditions. The geographic and hydrologic isolation of White Mountains creek systems has made the region a focal point for native fish conservation efforts and population restoration projects in the American Southwest.
