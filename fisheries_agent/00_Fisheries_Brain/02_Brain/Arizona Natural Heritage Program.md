# Arizona Natural Heritage Program

## Summary
The Arizona Natural Heritage Program was one of the cooperating organizations in this mammal survey project. They worked alongside the Defenders of Wildlife Trust and the Bureau of Land Management to facilitate the comprehensive documentation of mammals in the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve.

## Context

> This is the fourth annual progress report (1979-1982) on a comprehensive mammal survey of the [[George Whittell Wildlife Preserve]] in the [[Aravaipa Creek]] watershed, Arizona.
