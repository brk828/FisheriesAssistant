# Rio Grande Killifish

## Summary
The Rio Grande Killifish (*Fundulus zebrinus*) is a small cyprinodontid fish native to southwestern North America that has become established as an exotic, invasive species in various aquatic systems outside its native range, including the Colorado River basin. This euryhaline and eurythermal species is notable for its ability to reproduce successfully in introduced environments and its capacity to thrive in diverse habitat conditions, making it a significant concern in conservation biology for its potential impacts on native fish communities. Its presence in multiple river systems documented across ichthyological surveys reflects both its ecological adaptability and the broader problem of unintended fish introductions affecting native aquatic biodiversity.
