# Creek Chub

## Summary
The Creek Chub (*Semotilus atromaculatus*) is a cyprinid minnow that functions as a dominant headwater specialist in small stream ecosystems, characteristically occupying the extreme upper reaches of tributaries where it often excludes other fish species through competitive dominance. As a key indicator species for headwater habitat quality and stream integrity, the Creek Chub's presence and abundance reflect conditions in the smallest, most ecologically sensitive portions of lotic systems, making it an important reference species in ichthyological surveys and aquatic community assessments. Its ecological role as a headwater pioneer and competitor has significant implications for understanding fish community assembly and habitat partitioning in cold-water stream ecosystems.
