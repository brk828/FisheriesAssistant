# Lost River

## Summary
Lost River is a river system in Modoc County where Pumpkinseed were documented in the tributary Antelope Creek in 1942. The Lost River connected to the Klamath River system via a canal system, which may have facilitated fish movement between drainages, though the origin of the Pumpkinseed population remained unknown.
