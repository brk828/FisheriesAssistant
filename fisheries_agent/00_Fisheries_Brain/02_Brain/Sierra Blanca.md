# Sierra Blanca

## Summary
Sierra Blanca is a geographic landmark in eastern Arizona used to define the historical collection location of Lepidomeda vittata. The species was first described in 1874 from specimens collected from the Little Colorado River between the mouth of the Zuni River and Sierra Blanca.

## Context

> The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service issued a final rule on September 16, 1987, determining [[Lepidomeda vittata]] ([[Little Colorado Spinedace]]) to be a threatened species under the [[Endangered Species Act]] of 1973.
