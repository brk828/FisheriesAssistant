# Black Grouse

## Summary
Black Grouse (Lyrurus tetrix) is a bird species in Finland where snow-darkening agents have been applied for wildlife management. According to a 1971 report cited in this study, Finland applied soot and forest fertilizer to 180 hectares to improve nesting conditions for Black Grouse, representing the only other documented large-scale wildlife management application of snow-darkening agents besides the mule deer study.
