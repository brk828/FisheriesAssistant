# Dry Beaver Creek

## Summary
Dry Beaver Creek is a major tributary of the Verde River in Arizona. According to this study plan, it is one of ten major tributaries included in the Advanced Identification assessment, with the study area extending one mile upstream from its confluence with the Verde River to evaluate riparian habitat and aquatic ecosystem functions.
