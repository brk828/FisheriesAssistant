# The Johns Hopkins University

## Summary
The Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore, Maryland is where Cypretta kawatai was maintained and studied in laboratory aquaria. The species was named in honor of Dr. K. Kawata from this institution, and the type locality is listed as aquaria at this university.

## Context

> This paper formally validates the new species **Cypretta kawatai** Sohn and Kornicker, 1972, a freshwater ostracod (Crustacea) from Brazil.
