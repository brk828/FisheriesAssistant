# chalk stream

## Summary
A chalk stream is a type of freshwater habitat found in southern England, characterized by water flowing through chalk geology. The chalk stream near Romsey, Hampshire provided the natural environment where Simulium ornatum var. nitidifrons larvae were collected for this developmental study.
