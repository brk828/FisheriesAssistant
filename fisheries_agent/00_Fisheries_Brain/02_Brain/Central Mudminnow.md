# Central Mudminnow

## Summary
The Central Mudminnow (Umbra limi) is a small freshwater fish species native to North America that inhabits shallow wetland and swamp environments, particularly in drainage systems like the Reelfoot Lake area and associated tributaries. This species is significant in ichthyological and conservation research as an indicator of wetland habitat quality and a sensitive species vulnerable to drainage, agricultural conversion, and pollution from coal mining and resource extraction activities. The Central Mudminnow is often studied in community ecology research examining multi-species assemblages in freshwater ecosystems and has been identified as a conservation priority in regional assessments of threatened and endangered fish fauna.
