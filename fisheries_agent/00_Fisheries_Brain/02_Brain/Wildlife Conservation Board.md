# Wildlife Conservation Board

## Summary
The Wildlife Conservation Board is a California state agency that provided over $4 million for hatchery renovation and expansion following World War II. This funding enabled a fourfold increase in catchable-sized trout production to approximately 7 million fish annually, dramatically expanding California's recreational trout fishing program.
