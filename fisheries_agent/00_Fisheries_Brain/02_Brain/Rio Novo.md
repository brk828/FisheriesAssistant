# Rio Novo

## Summary
Rio Novo is a tributary of the Rio Tocantins in the Lower Amazon basin of Brazil where Bryconamericus novae was collected. The specimens were found below the Cachoeira da Velha waterfall near Piabana, collected by J.D. Haseman.
