# Coregonus Clupeaformis

## Summary
Coregonus clupeaformis, commonly known as lake whitefish, is a freshwater salmonid species native to North American lakes and river systems, particularly in northern regions. This species is ecologically significant as both a commercial and subsistence fishery resource, and it serves as an important indicator organism for studying the effects of environmental conditions and genetic variation on fish populations in aquatic ecosystems. Lake whitefish populations have been subjects of intensive ichthyological research, particularly regarding morphological differentiation between wild and hatchery-reared individuals, as well as investigations into how environmental and genetic factors influence phenotypic traits and population dynamics.
