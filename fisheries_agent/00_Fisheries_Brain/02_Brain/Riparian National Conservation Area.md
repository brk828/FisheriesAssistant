# Riparian National Conservation Area

## Summary
A Riparian National Conservation Area is a federally protected area managed by the Bureau of Land Management to conserve riparian (riverside) ecosystems and their associated resources. The San Pedro River Riparian National Conservation Area in Arizona was the site of a proposed 1995 beaver reintroduction project, with the target area being the section between Hereford bridge and Highway 90 bridge, which had recovered sufficient willow and cottonwood habitat to potentially support a self-sustaining beaver population.
