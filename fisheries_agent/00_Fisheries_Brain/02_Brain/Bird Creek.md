# Bird Creek

## Summary
Bird Creek is a tributary of the Verdigris River that introduces additional organic wastes into the main channel. Darters were rare below the point where Bird Creek enters the Verdigris, indicating that pollution from this tributary significantly impacts fish distribution in the system.
