# Cottonwood-Willow Gallery Forest

## Summary
The Cottonwood-Willow Gallery Forest is described as the rarest forest type in North America, with the San Pedro River hosting the most extensive surviving expanse of this broadleaf riparian association forest. This forest type depends on consistent groundwater levels and surface flow, making it vulnerable to excessive water pumping that lowers water tables and threatens the riparian forest structure that provides critical habitat for diverse wildlife in the arid Southwest.
