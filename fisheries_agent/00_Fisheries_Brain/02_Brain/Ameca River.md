# Ameca River

## Summary
The Ameca River is one of several rivers cutting into the Mesa Central plateau. This source notes that Goodeidae occur in the Ameca River and in smaller drainages between the Ameca and Mezquital rivers, representing part of the distribution pattern of endemic Mesa Central fishes.
