# Taos Plateau

## Summary
The Taos Plateau is a geographic feature in northern New Mexico through which the Rio Grande flows in deeply incised canyons approximately 1,000 feet deep. This source indicates that during middle to late Pliocene time, the Rio Grande flowed south from the San Juan Mountains across the Taos Plateau before continuing southward through New Mexico.
