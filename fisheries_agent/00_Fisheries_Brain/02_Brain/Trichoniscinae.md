# Trichoniscinae

## Summary
Trichoniscinae is the most primitive tribe within Trichoniscidae, to which Xilitloniscus belongs according to Vandel's classification system. This placement is supported by the fundamental characteristics of male pleopod 1 morphology and represents the first and most primitive division of the family Trichoniscidae.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
