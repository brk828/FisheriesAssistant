# Richland Creek

## Summary
Richland Creek is the main watercourse on which Navarro Mills Reservoir was impounded by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers in Navarro County, Texas. It is one of the major tributaries entering the lake basin and had minimal flow during the March 1963 chemical treatment.

## Context

> This job completion report documents a preimpoundment chemical treatment of [[Navarro Mills Reservoir]] in western [[Navarro County]], Texas, conducted March 18-19, 1963.
