# Arizona Alligator Lizard

## Summary
The Arizona Alligator Lizard is hypothetically present at the Orme Reservoir site far below its normal elevational range. This source identifies it as a secretive species that is difficult to detect during surveys, and notes it as an example of higher-elevation species extending downward along flowing streams.
