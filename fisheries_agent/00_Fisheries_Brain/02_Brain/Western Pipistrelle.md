# Western Pipistrelle

## Summary
The Western Pipistrelle (Pipistrellus hesperus) is a small bat species native to desert regions. This assessment confirmed its presence at Papago Park Military Reservation, though bat sampling was noted as unsuccessful and requiring additional survey effort.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
