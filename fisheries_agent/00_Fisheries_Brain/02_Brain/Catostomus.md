# Catostomus

## Summary
Catostomus is a genus of freshwater suckers (family Catostomidae) native to North American aquatic systems, several species of which have become subjects of conservation concern due to habitat loss and environmental degradation in arid regions like Arizona. Representatives within this genus, including undescribed or poorly characterized forms such as the Little Colorado sucker, have been evaluated for federal protection under the Endangered Species Act, reflecting the conservation challenges facing these benthic cypriniform fishes. The spawning ecology and habitat requirements of Catostomus species are particularly important for understanding their population dynamics and informing recovery strategies in threatened desert aquatic ecosystems.
