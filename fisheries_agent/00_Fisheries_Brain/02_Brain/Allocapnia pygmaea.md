# Allocapnia pygmaea

## Summary
Allocapnia pygmaea is a stonefly in Caldwell Hollow that survives dry periods as very small nymphs (2 mm) in subsurface seepage with arrested summer growth. This species was found in subsurface seepage samples but was notably absent from moist soil samples with only water-saturated air, indicating it requires free water for survival.
