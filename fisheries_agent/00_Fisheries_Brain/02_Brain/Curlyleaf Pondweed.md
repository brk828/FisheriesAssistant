# Curlyleaf Pondweed

## Summary
Curlyleaf Pondweed (Potamogeton crispus) is an aquatic plant species managed in fisheries programs. According to this manual, it can be controlled with relatively low herbicide concentrations, requiring only 0.5 ppm of silvex, making it one of the more easily controlled aquatic vegetation species discussed in the document.

## Context

> This 1968 manual from the [[Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife]] provides comprehensive guidance on herbicide use for vegetation control in fish and wildlife management programs.
