# G. C. Williams

## Summary
G. C. Williams was an evolutionary biologist whose 1966 book 'Adaptation and Natural Selection' provided important critiques of group selection theory. According to this source, his work refined thinking about levels of selection and encouraged ecologists to seek traits responsible for population regulation at the organismic level rather than assuming selection operated primarily at group or population levels.
