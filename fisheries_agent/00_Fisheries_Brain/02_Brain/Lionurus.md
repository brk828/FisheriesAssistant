# Lionurus

## Summary
Lionurus is a genus of deep-sea macrourid fishes with multiple species in the East Indies region. This source notes that while L. decimalis is endemic to the Philippine Subregion, several other Lionurus species (L. infranudis, L. evides, L. vittatus, L. richardii, L. parvipes) known from the East Indies were absent from the Sulu Sea collections.
