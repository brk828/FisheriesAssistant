# A.E. Wood State Fish Hatchery

## Summary
A.E. Wood State Fish Hatchery in Texas performed the electrophoretic analysis of tissue and blood samples from 106 Morone specimens collected from Devils Lake. The laboratory analyzed three diagnostic loci (PGI, SORD, and EST) using isoelectric focusing on polyacrylamide gels, finding banding patterns consistent with pure white bass and no evidence of hybridization with striped bass.

## Context

> This study assessed the risk of [[Striped Bass]] (Morone saxatilis) transfer from [[Devils Lake]] to Canadian waters via a proposed outlet to the [[Sheyenne River]].
