# Arroyo Sequit Shellmound

## Summary
The Arroyo Sequit Shellmound (LAn-52) is an archaeological site occupied between approximately 1000 B.C. and 1500 A.D., located on a bluff on the east side of Arroyo Sequit creek in western Los Angeles County. The site yielded 459 fish remains representing approximately 19 species from 14 families, with the greatest species diversity and specimen abundance occurring at depths of 18-30 inches.
