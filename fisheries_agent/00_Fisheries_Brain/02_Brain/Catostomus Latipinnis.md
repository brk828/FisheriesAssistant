# Catostomus Latipinnis

## Summary
Catostomus latipinnis, commonly known as the flannelmouth sucker, is a native cypriniform fish species endemic to the Colorado River basin in the southwestern United States, where it has been documented as experiencing population decline and was recommended for Category 2 candidate status under the Endangered Species Act due to habitat degradation and flow alteration in its lower Colorado River basin populations. This benthic-feeding sucker species is ecologically significant as an indicator of riverine health and serves as an important component of native fish communities in arid southwestern aquatic ecosystems. Conservation efforts for this species reflect broader concerns about maintaining native fish diversity in highly regulated river systems facing threats from water extraction, dam operations, and invasive species competition.
