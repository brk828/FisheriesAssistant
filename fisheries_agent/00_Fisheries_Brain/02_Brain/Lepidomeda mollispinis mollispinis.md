# Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis

## Summary
Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis (Virgin River spinedace) is another spinedace species that has been displaced by the exotic redside shiner (Richardsonius balteatus) in the Virgin River. This displacement is cited as evidence of the competitive threat that exotic shiners pose to spinedace species, supporting concerns about similar threats to the Little Colorado spinedace.

## Context
