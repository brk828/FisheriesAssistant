# Forest Guardians

## Summary
Forest Guardians is a conservation organization based in Santa Fe, New Mexico with approximately 1,000 members who use southwestern public lands for wildlife observation and recreation. The organization works through administrative appeals and litigation to uphold Endangered Species Act provisions and partnered with Consejo Asesor Sierra Madre on cross-border species protection efforts in the Sierra Madre.
