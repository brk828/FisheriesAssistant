# Fish Culture

## Summary
Fish culture refers to the artificial propagation and husbandry of fish in controlled environments such as hatcheries, encompassing the techniques and infrastructure used to breed, rear, and manage fish populations for conservation, restoration, or food production purposes. This practice involves controlled spawning, rearing in artificial systems like raceways and ponds, and the application of scientific methods to improve fish populations and genetic quality. Fish culture has been a fundamental tool in fisheries management since the mid-19th century, though the field has evolved from purely routine propagation methods toward more sophisticated physiological and genetic approaches.
