# Lochsa River

## Summary
The Lochsa River is a tributary of the Clearwater River in Idaho that was included in this 1966 statewide harvest survey. It was part of the Clearwater River drainage system, which contributed significantly to Idaho's anadromous fish harvest during the study period.
