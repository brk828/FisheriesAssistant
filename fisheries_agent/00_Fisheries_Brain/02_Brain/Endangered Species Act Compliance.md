# Endangered Species Act Compliance

## Summary
Endangered Species Act (ESA) Compliance in fish science refers to the legal and biological obligations of resource management agencies to protect and recover fish species listed as threatened or endangered under the Endangered Species Act of 1973, including habitat restoration, monitoring programs, and interagency coordination to prevent extinction. For aquatic systems like the Colorado River and associated native fish populations, ESA compliance encompasses the design and operation of fish rearing facilities, barrier modifications, and backwater habitat restoration projects that support recovery goals for protected species. These compliance efforts require coordination among multiple agencies and often involve cost-sharing agreements and specialized management of candidate species and species of conservation concern to maintain viable populations and ecosystem integrity.
