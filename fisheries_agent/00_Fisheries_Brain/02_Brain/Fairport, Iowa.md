# Fairport, Iowa

## Summary
Fairport, Iowa was the location of a U.S. Fisheries Biological Station where H.L. Canfield developed successful methods for buffalo-fish egg collection and hatching. The station utilized cooperation with local commercial fishermen and fish markets to collect eggs from spawning buffalo-fish in the Mississippi River region.
