# Yellowbelly Sunfish

## Summary
Yellowbelly Sunfish (Lepomis auritus) is one of three sunfish species used in the experimental stocking combinations in this study. Three ponds were stocked at a 1:5 bass to yellowbelly ratio as part of the comparison of different stocking techniques for farm pond management.
