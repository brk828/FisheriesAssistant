# Desolation Gray Canyons

## Summary
Desolation Gray Canyons is a canyon-bound region on the Green River in Utah. According to this source, it is one of six known wild populations of the endangered Humpback Chub, which is restricted to deep, swift, canyon-bound reaches of the Colorado River system.
