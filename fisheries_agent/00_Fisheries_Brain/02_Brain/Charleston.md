# Charleston

## Summary
Charleston is a location on the San Pedro River in Arizona where aquatic surveys were conducted in 1973. This site was one of multiple sampling points along the San Pedro River system where water quality parameters and habitat characteristics were documented as part of the broader Gila River Basin survey.
