# Empetrichthys Latos

## Summary
Empetrichthys latos, commonly known as the Pahrump poolfish, is a small endemic cyprinodontid fish species native to isolated spring systems in the Pahrump Valley of Nevada. This species represents a critical conservation concern as an example of highly specialized desert fish adapted to specific geothermal spring environments, and it is frequently discussed alongside other endangered Nevada endemics in the context of habitat preservation and extinction risk management. The species is of particular interest to ichthyologists studying reproduction, growth patterns, and the ecological dynamics of isolated aquatic populations facing anthropogenic threats.
