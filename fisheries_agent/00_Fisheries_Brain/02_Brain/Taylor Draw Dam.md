# Taylor Draw Dam

## Summary
Taylor Draw Dam is a water management structure relevant to Colorado River fish conservation efforts, particularly regarding the habitat and population dynamics of endangered native fishes such as the Colorado Squawfish (*Ptychocheilus lucius*). The dam's impacts on river hydrology, fish passage, and habitat connectivity are central to understanding the fragmentation and decline of native fish populations in the Colorado River basin, making it an important consideration in recovery planning and management decisions for threatened species. As part of the broader context of dam construction and water diversions affecting the Colorado River ecosystem, Taylor Draw Dam represents one of multiple anthropogenic barriers that have disrupted natural flow regimes and spawning habitats critical to the survival of endemic fish species.
