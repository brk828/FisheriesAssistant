# Lernaea

## Summary
Lernaea is a parasitic copepod genus that infects fish, causing significant disease and mortality in both wild and captive fish populations. In the context of these fisheries and conservation studies from the 1970s-1990s, Lernaea represents an important pathogenic concern for native fish species in aquatic ecosystems like the Rio Yaqui basin, where it may have influenced population dynamics and habitat utilization patterns among native and exotic fish communities. Understanding Lernaea infection rates and distribution is relevant to assessing the overall health status and ecological integrity of native fish populations in conservation-focused ichthyological surveys.
