# Pot Creek

## Summary
Pot Creek is a tributary of the Green River within Lodore Canyon in the Colorado River Basin. This source recommends extending critical habitat for Colorado Squawfish into Lodore Canyon to the confluence with Pot Creek (approximately river mile 481), based on historic habitat use and recent adult use confirmed by radio telemetry studies.
