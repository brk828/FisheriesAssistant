# Gordon R. Smith

## Summary
Gordon R. Smith was one of the major collectors associated with the University of Michigan Museum of Zoology fish collections from Arizona documented in this source. He was among the leading ichthyologists who conducted systematic surveys of Arizona's freshwater fish fauna and made taxonomic identifications of specimens collected primarily between the 1930s and 1970s.
