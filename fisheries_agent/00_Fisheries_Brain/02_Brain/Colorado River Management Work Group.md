# Colorado River Management Work Group

## Summary
The Colorado River Management Work Group is a consultative body through which the Secretary of the Interior determines water supply conditions (surplus, normal, or shortage years) on the Colorado River. The consultation process includes the Seven Basin States and other parties such as tribes, National Park Service, Fish and Wildlife Service, water districts, environmental groups, and power contractors.
