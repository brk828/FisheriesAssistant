# Otter

## Summary
The Otter (Lutra canadensis) is a large semi-aquatic member of the weasel family with a long lithe body, short legs, webbed feet, and a long muscular tail covered in rich glossy brown fur. According to this Michigan study, otters are not abundant but are usually found along wilder waterways in the northern part of the state, range over large areas but never far from water, and create familiar "otter slides" down banks into water. They mate in February with 1-5 young born in late April, and young stay with parents most of the year.
