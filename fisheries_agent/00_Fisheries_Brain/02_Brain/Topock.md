# Topock

## Summary
Topock is a location on the lower Colorado River at the California-Arizona border. The river section between Needles and Topock was channelized between 1946-1951, eliminating approximately 4,300 surface acres of productive marsh, lake, and slough habitat that had supported diverse fish populations.
