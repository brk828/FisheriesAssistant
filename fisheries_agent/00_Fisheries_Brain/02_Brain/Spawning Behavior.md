# Spawning Behavior

## Summary
Smallmouth bass spawning occurs when water temperatures reach 15-18°C (March-May in Arizona), with males driving females to completed nests through nudging and biting. During spawning, the pair positions side by side with the male slightly behind and above, and the female becomes rigid before rapidly vibrating during egg release, with the male simultaneously releasing sperm in repeated cycles until the female is depleted.
