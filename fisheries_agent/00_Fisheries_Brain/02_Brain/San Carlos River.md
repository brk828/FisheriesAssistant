# San Carlos River

## Summary
The San Carlos River is an Arizona stream where the invasive Green Sunfish has demonstrated its ability to eliminate native fish species. This river is cited as an example of the threat that Green Sunfish pose to native fishes, similar to concerns about its spread in Aravaipa Creek.
