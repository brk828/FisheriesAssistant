# Otero County

## Summary
Otero County is a county in Colorado located in the Arkansas River Valley region. This study examined Holbrook Lake Reservoir, an irrigation impoundment located approximately two miles northeast of Swink in Otero County, over an eight-year period from 1947 to 1955.
