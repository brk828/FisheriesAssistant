# Glen Canyon National Recreation Area

## Summary
Glen Canyon National Recreation Area encompasses the Colorado River ecosystem downstream of Glen Canyon Dam in Arizona, which has become a critical focus for native fish conservation and management due to the dam's profound effects on river hydrology, temperature, and habitat. The area is particularly significant for studies and recovery efforts involving endangered species such as the razorback sucker and humpback chub, whose populations have been monitored and managed through coordinated research initiatives. Management of this region involves balancing recreation, hydroelectric operations, and aquatic ecosystem restoration, making it a key site for understanding dam impacts on riverine fish communities and testing conservation strategies.
