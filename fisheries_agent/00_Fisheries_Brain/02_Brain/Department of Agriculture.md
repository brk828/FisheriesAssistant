# Department of Agriculture

## Summary
The Department of Agriculture is identified in this source as the proposed federal funding source for the Fishable Waters Act's voluntary watershed restoration program. Under the proposed Act, the Department of Agriculture would provide federal funding while states would administer locally-controlled Watershed Councils.
