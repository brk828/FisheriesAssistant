# Yaqui Sucker

## Summary
The Yaqui Sucker (*Catostomus bernardini*) is a catostomid fish native to the Yaqui River drainage of southeastern Arizona and northern Mexico, classified as a peripheral species with a highly restricted range and limited habitat availability. This species has experienced significant population declines and local extirpations, as documented by failed reintroduction attempts in isolated water sources like Leslie Canyon spring, reflecting broader conservation challenges faced by endemic fishes in arid southwestern drainages. Its disappearance from historically occupied areas underscores the vulnerability of specialized suckers to habitat fragmentation, water scarcity, and the difficulties of establishing viable populations in marginal or artificially managed aquatic systems.
