# Clearwater River

## Summary
The Clearwater River is a major tributary system in north-central Idaho that serves as a critical habitat and management focal point for anadromous fish species, particularly salmon and steelhead populations. The river system, which includes the North Fork Clearwater River, has been significantly impacted by dam construction (notably Dworshak Dam) and is the location of important federal fish hatcheries designed to support fisheries recovery and conservation efforts. In the context of fisheries science, the Clearwater River represents a key study and management area for understanding the ecological and operational challenges of maintaining viable fish populations in regulated river systems.
