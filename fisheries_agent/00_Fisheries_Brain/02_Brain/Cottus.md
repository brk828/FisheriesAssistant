# Cottus

## Summary
Cottus is a genus of benthic freshwater sculpins found across multiple North American drainage systems, particularly in the Atlantic Slope and Ohio River watershed regions. This genus is characterized by significant taxonomic complexity, with multiple recognized species that often exhibit ambiguous and non-intergrading morphological variation across different populations, making species identification and delineation diagnostically challenging for ichthyologists. Sculpins of the genus Cottus are ecologically important as small benthic fish inhabiting rocky substrates in cool freshwater habitats, and their population diversity across isolated river drainages makes them relevant subjects for conservation biology and biogeographic studies.
