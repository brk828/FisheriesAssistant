# Eurycea rathbuni

## Summary
Eurycea rathbuni is the synonym for Typhlomolge rathbuni, the Texas Blind Salamander. The artesian well where Haideoporus texanus was collected is the type-locality for this blind, depigmented salamander that inhabits the Edwards Aquifer.

## Context

> This study describes the larva of *[[Haideoporus texanus]]*, the first hypogeous (subterranean) dytiscid beetle discovered in North America.
