# Lake Krugloe

## Summary
Lake Krugloe is a freshwater lake in Russia that hosted a Fisheries Station where systematic gill-net experiments were conducted in 1923-1924. These experiments on roach, perch, and pike established fundamental mesh-size relationships and provided data showing that fish were captured in meshes whose perimeter was approximately 60% of body length, with the relationship between greatest body perimeter and optimal mesh perimeter averaging 1.25.
