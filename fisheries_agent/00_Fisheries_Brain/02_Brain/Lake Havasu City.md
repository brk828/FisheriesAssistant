# Lake Havasu City

## Summary
Lake Havasu City is located at the upstream extent of Lake Havasu, a main stem Colorado River reservoir that extends approximately 45 km downstream to Parker Dam. The reservoir serves as one of few release locations for endangered bonytail, with approximately 218,662 fish stocked since 1981.
