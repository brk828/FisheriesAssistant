# Southwestern Research Station

## Summary
The Southwestern Research Station at Portal, Arizona, operated by the American Museum of Natural History, was cited as a comparable model for field research facilities. The proposal suggested that ASU's planned Arizona Museum of Natural History and Culture could develop similar capabilities with the Sierra Ancha Field Station.
