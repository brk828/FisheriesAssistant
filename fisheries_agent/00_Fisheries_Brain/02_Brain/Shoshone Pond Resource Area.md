# Shoshone Pond Resource Area

## Summary
Shoshone Pond Resource Area in White Pine County, Nevada was the site of an emergency refugium established in July 1987 where 75 Big Spring Spinedace were released. The success of this population establishment effort was uncertain at the time of the 1993 recovery plan, representing an early attempt to establish additional populations beyond Condor Canyon.
