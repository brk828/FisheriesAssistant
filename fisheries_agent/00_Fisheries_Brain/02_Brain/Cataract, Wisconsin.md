# Cataract, Wisconsin

## Summary
Cataract, Wisconsin is the location of Rathbone Creek and Cataract Pond, where a field application of antimycin A demonstrated effective detoxification procedures. The site was used to show that potassium permanganate could quickly and completely neutralize antimycin-laden water flowing from treated upstream areas.
