# Chewacla Creek

## Summary
Chewacla Creek is a tributary of the Tallapoosa River in Alabama that served as one of the collection sites for adult threadfin shad used in this study. Six threadfin shad collected from Chewacla Creek on April 25, 1965 (measuring 6.6-7.0 inches total length) were used for fecundity analysis and contained an estimated mean of 22,314 eggs per fish.
