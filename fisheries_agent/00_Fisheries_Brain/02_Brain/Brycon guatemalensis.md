# Brycon guatemalensis

## Summary
Brycon guatemalensis is a characid fish species for which this source provides the apparently first record from British Honduras. The study examined 13 juvenile specimens (largest about 40 mm standard length) from Punta Gorda, documenting their meristics (including approximately 60 longitudinal scales and 31 anal rays), dentition patterns with premaxillary teeth in 4 rows, and distinctive salmon-colored unpaired fins including the adipose fin.
