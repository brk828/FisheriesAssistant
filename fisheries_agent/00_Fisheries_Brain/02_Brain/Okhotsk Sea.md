# Okhotsk Sea

## Summary
The Okhotsk Sea is a body of water adjacent to the Sea of Japan where macrouroid fishes were found to be abundant. This contrasts sharply with the complete absence of Macrouridae in the Sea of Japan itself, demonstrating the isolating effect of the Sea of Japan's shallow rim barriers.
