# Central Mosquito Fish

## Summary
The Central Mosquito Fish (Gambusia affinis) is a non-native species that has been introduced to the Gila River drainage and is identified as a major factor in the decline of the Gila Topminnow. Two of the four remaining natural Gila Topminnow populations (Bylas Spring and Sharp Spring) have been impacted by mosquito fish, which introduce competitors, predators, and potentially non-native parasites and pathogens, creating substantially different selection pressures compared to the uninfested populations at Cienega Creek and Monkey Spring.
