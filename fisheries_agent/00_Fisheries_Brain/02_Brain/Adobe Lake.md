# Adobe Lake

## Summary
Adobe Lake is a specific restoration site within the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge. This source identifies it as one of the locations targeted for restoration and enhancement through channel construction, backwater reconnection, and water control structures as part of the broader wetland restoration effort.
