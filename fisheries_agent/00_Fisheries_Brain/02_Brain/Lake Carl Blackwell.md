# Lake Carl Blackwell

## Summary
Lake Carl Blackwell is a 1,486-hectare turbid reservoir located 11 km west of Stillwater in north-central Oklahoma, USA. During this study (1967-1968), the reservoir was characterized by high turbidity (20-180 JTU), low transparency (Secchi disc typically <50 cm), average depth of 4.0 m, and water levels reduced to 70% of spillway capacity due to seven consecutive years of drought. The reservoir supported a flathead catfish population with spawning sites at 2-5 m depths near rock outcroppings and submerged timber, with water temperatures reaching 24-29°C during the spawning season in late July through August.
