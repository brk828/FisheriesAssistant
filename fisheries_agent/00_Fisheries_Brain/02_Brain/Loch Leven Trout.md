# Loch Leven Trout

## Summary
Loch Leven Trout is an alternative name used in this source for Brook Trout (Salvelinus fontinalis), also referred to as "lochs" in the study. These fish were stocked at legal size (averaging 8.5 inches) alongside rainbow trout and showed 52.4% first-season recovery rates in 1948, which was 16% lower than rainbow trout returns, though they exhibited stronger upstream migration tendencies (59% moved upstream versus 34% for rainbows).

## Context

> This two-year fisheries management study (1947-1948) on the [[North Fork of South Platte River]] in [[Colorado]] evaluated the effectiveness of stocking legal-size [[Rainbow Trout]] and [[Brook Trout]] (referred to as "lochs") in a heavily fished mountain stream.
