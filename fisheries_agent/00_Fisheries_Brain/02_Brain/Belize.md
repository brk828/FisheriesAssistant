# Belize

## Summary
Belize is the modern name for British Honduras, a Central American nation where fish specimens were collected for this study. The vicinity of Belize yielded specimens from brackish water rivers, including two species of Dorosoma (gizzard shads) occurring sympatrically, as well as specimens of Astyanax fasciatus aeneus.
