# Green River Sub-Basin

## Summary
The Green River Sub-Basin is a major drainage area within the Colorado River Basin that was a focus of extensive endangered fish research and recovery efforts. This 1989 meeting agenda indicates research in this sub-basin covered fish interactions, Yampa River humpback chub populations, electrofishing methodology evaluation, sediment transport and channel stability, backwater habitat mapping, trophic dynamics, winter habitat studies for Colorado squawfish and razorback sucker, and use of the Little Snake River by endangered species.
