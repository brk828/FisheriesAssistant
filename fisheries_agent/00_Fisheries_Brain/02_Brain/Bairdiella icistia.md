# Bairdiella icistia

## Summary
Bairdiella icistia is the Bairdiella, a marine fish species from the Gulf of California. It was introduced to the Salton Sea between 1948 and 1956 and became one of three marine species to successfully establish, contributing to the creation of a viable sport fishery in the otherwise unproductive saline waters.
