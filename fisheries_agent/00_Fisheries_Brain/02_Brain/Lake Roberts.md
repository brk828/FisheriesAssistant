# Lake Roberts

## Summary
Lake Roberts is an off-mainstream reservoir in the Gila River basin in New Mexico where the state Department of Game and Fish conducts both trout and warmwater gamefish stocking programs. According to this 1988 U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service correspondence, the reservoir is located upstream from Spikedace and Loach Minnow populations, and while warmwater stocking there presents potential threats to these endangered species, no significant adverse effects had been documented at that time.
