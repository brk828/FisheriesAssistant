# Speckled Pavon

## Summary
Speckled Pavon (Cichla temensis) is a predatory cichlid introduced to Florida's Dade County canals beginning in 1984, marketed as "butterfly cichlid." It was introduced alongside Peacock Cichlid with the assumption these predators would control previously introduced cichlids, but they may instead impact native fish species, and the taxonomy of genus Cichla remains poorly studied with probable undescribed species.
