# Black Bar

## Summary
Black Bar is the primary spawning aggregation site for razorback suckers in the River Zone of Lake Mohave, showing the highest contact rates from November through April with peak activity January through March. Remote PIT scanning at this site confirmed its critical importance as a spawning location, with contacts declining after the spawning season as fish dispersed to other areas.
