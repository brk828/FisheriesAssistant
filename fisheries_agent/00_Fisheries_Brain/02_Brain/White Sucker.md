# White Sucker

## Summary
The White Sucker (Catostomus commersoni) is a widespread North American cypriniform fish in the family Catostomidae, commonly found in streams and rivers across diverse freshwater habitats where it occupies pools and slower-current zones. In ichthyological surveys and taxonomic studies, White Suckers serve as reference specimens for comparative anatomy, particularly in larval identification where diagnostic characters such as interneural bone morphology and fontanelle structure are used to distinguish them from co-occurring sucker species. As a bottom-feeding generalist with relatively high environmental tolerance, the White Sucker is an important indicator species in stream ecology assessments and fish community analysis, often representing a significant component of regional fish faunas in North American aquatic systems.
