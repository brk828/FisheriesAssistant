# Ameiurus Natalis

## Summary
Ameiurus natalis, commonly known as the yellow bullhead, is a small catfish species native to eastern North America that has become established as a non-native invasive species in southwestern aquatic systems including Arizona's desert streams. This opportunistic predator and competitor is documented in fish community surveys where it comprises a notable proportion of the fish assemblage, particularly among larger individuals, and represents a conservation concern due to its potential to prey upon and outcompete native fishes in these sensitive desert aquatic ecosystems. The species' presence in these historical monitoring datasets reflects the broader problem of non-native fish introductions in the Colorado River basin and associated tributaries.
