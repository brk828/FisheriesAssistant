# El Desemboque

## Summary
El Desemboque is a location in Sonora, Mexico (coordinates 30°34'N, 113°01'W) situated between Santo Tomas and Cabo Tepoca in the upper Gulf of California. This source reports that two specimens of the Red Pike-Conger were captured near El Desemboque in January 1984 at 33 m depth on sandy bottom using commercial shrimp nets, marking the first record of this species in the upper Gulf of California and the northernmost occurrence of the species.
