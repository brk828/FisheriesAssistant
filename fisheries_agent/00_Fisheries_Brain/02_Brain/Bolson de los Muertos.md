# Bolson de los Muertos

## Summary
Bolson de los Muertos is an endorheic (closed) basin in the desert regions of northern Mexico. This source lists it among the desert spring and endorheic basin habitats in Chihuahua and Coahuila that represent specialized environments harboring unique fish faunas isolated from other drainage systems.
