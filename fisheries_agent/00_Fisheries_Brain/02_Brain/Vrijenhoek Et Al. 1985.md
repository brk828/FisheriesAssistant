# Vrijenhoek Et Al. 1985

## Summary
Vrijenhoek et al. 1985 established foundational methodologies for using molecular genetic markers, particularly allozyme electrophoresis and DNA-based techniques, to assess genetic variation and population structure in fish species. This work became instrumental in conservation biology for identifying genetically distinct populations and management units, enabling scientists to evaluate the genetic health and evolutionary potential of threatened fish populations through molecular analysis. The approach has been widely applied in studies examining genetic polymorphism across fish populations and informing conservation strategies for species management.
