# Pahranagat Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Pahranagat Speckled Dace (Rhinichthys osculus velifer) is a fish subspecies found in Pahranagat Valley. Surveys in 1990 and 1991 documented extirpation of several species from multiple springs in the Pahranagat and White River valleys, indicating serious conservation concerns for this subspecies.
