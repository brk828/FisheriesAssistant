# Phalloptychus

## Summary
Phalloptychus is a new genus proposed by Eigenmann with Girardinus januarius Hensel as the type species. It is distinguished by an intromittent organ where the third, fourth, and fifth rays form a slender tube at the tips, with no claspers and the organ ending in a simple point, plus very strong retrorse hooks on the fourth ray and an intestine approximately twice the length of the fish.
