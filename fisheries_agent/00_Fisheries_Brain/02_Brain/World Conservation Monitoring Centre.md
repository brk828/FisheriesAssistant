# World Conservation Monitoring Centre

## Summary
The World Conservation Monitoring Centre (WCMC) is a key partner organization that manages global biodiversity information and collaborates with SSC. WCMC cooperates in producing IUCN Red Lists and provides data services, database development assistance, and geographic information systems support to Specialist Groups. The Centre plays a crucial role in SSC's information management infrastructure, supporting the Commission's work in species data standardization and distribution.
