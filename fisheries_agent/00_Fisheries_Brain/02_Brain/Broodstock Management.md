# Broodstock Management

## Summary
Broodstock management refers to the systematic care, selection, and breeding protocols applied to captive fish populations maintained for conservation or aquaculture purposes, with particular emphasis on preserving genetic diversity and fitness in threatened or endangered species. This practice involves deliberate decisions about which individuals to breed, population size maintenance, and breeding schedules to prevent inbreeding depression and maintain the evolutionary potential of the species. In the context of conservation biology, broodstock management is critical for species recovery programs, ensuring that captive-bred populations remain genetically representative of wild populations and suitable for potential reintroduction efforts.
