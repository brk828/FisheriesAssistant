# Red Wash Launch

## Summary
Red Wash Launch (approximately RMI 298.5-298.0) contains backwater habitats identified as one of the six primary sites recommended for concentrated razorback sucker larval monitoring. It is located in the Jensen Reach of the middle Green River.
