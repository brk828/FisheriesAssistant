# Amblyopsidae

## Summary
Amblyopsidae is a family of small freshwater fishes endemic to North America that are characterized by their troglobitic (obligate cave-dwelling) adaptations, including eye loss and pigmentation reduction or albinism. Members of this family, such as *Typhlichthys subterraneus* (Southern Cavefish), are important model organisms in conservation biology and aquatic ecology for understanding how cave-dwelling species maintain relatively extensive geographic ranges despite their specialized habitat requirements and reduced sensory capabilities. The family represents a significant component of North American cave fauna and provides insights into the ecological and evolutionary consequences of subterranean life history strategies.
