# Powell Canyon

## Summary
Powell Canyon is a geographic feature along the Little Colorado River in Arizona that was initially considered as a base camp location for Humpback Chub research operations. The original camp site at the mouth of Powell Canyon (around river kilometer 3.0) was abandoned in response to a request from the Hopi Nation, and researchers relocated their base camp upstream from this location on the north side of the river.
