# Anderson Bottom

## Summary
Anderson Bottom (RMI 31.5-31.0) is a location in the lower Green River recommended for consideration in larval monitoring efforts. During 1993-1995, 170 razorback sucker larvae were collected from the lower Green River, primarily from nearby Millard Canyon.
