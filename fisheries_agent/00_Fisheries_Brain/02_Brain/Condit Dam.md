# Condit Dam

## Summary
Condit Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the White Salmon River in Washington State that became a focal point for dam removal and fish restoration efforts in the late 1990s. The dam was targeted for removal due to its severe impacts on anadromous fish populations, particularly salmon and steelhead, which were blocked from accessing critical spawning habitat upstream. The removal of Condit Dam represented a landmark agreement among stakeholders—including utilities, conservation groups, and government agencies—demonstrating the feasibility of dam removal as a conservation strategy to restore riverine connectivity and fish populations.
