## Summary
The Colorado River Wildlife Council appears to be an interagency coordination body that brought together federal, state, and regional stakeholders to address wildlife management and conservation issues affecting the Colorado River basin and its associated aquatic ecosystems. Based on its involvement with fish health facilities and National Fish Hatcheries in the southwestern United States, the Council likely served as a forum for collaborative decision-making regarding fish disease management, hatchery operations, and the conservation of native aquatic species across multiple state jurisdictions.
