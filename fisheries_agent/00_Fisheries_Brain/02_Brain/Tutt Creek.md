# Tutt Creek

## Summary
Tutt Creek was one of the proposed forest management projects submitted by the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest for Section 7 consultation. Like Milligan Burn, the Forest Service withdrew this project from the consultation package before the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service completed its review.
