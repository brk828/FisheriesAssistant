# Greater Gila

## Summary
The Greater Gila is a region in the southwestern United States where the Greater Gila Biodiversity Project works to preserve biodiversity. The organization's staff has surveyed and sought protection for threatened species in this region under ESA Section 4, efforts that were directly thwarted by the Hutchison Rider moratorium on species listings.
