# Ichthyology

## Summary
Ichthyology is the scientific study of fish, encompassing their taxonomy, physiology, behavior, ecology, and evolutionary biology across freshwater and marine environments. As a discipline within zoology and aquatic sciences, ichthyology provides fundamental knowledge for understanding fish populations, species diversity, and ecosystem dynamics, which directly informs conservation strategies and sustainable fisheries management. The field integrates observational studies, experimental research, and systematic data collection to address both theoretical questions about fish biology and practical applications related to environmental monitoring and resource stewardship.
