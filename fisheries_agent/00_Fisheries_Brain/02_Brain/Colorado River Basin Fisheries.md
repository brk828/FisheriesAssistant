# Colorado River Basin Fisheries

## Summary
The Colorado River Basin in Arizona supports multiple introduced bass species, with Largemouth Bass occurring in the main river channel and associated waters. Striped Bass were stocked below the U.S. Highway 60 bridge near Blythe in 1959, Smallmouth Bass were first recorded in 1953 and stocked above Parker Dam in 1950, and Warmouth Bass were reported from the lower river.
