# DDMU

## Summary
DDMU is a homolog (structurally related compound) of DDE, which is itself a metabolite of DDT. According to this source, DDMU was tested in metabolic studies with deer and rats, but administration of DDMU did not produce the unidentified "Compound X" degradation product that appeared when DDT itself was administered, suggesting DDMU follows a different metabolic pathway than DDT.
