# Arkansas River Valley

## Summary
The Arkansas River Valley is the location in central Colorado where Twin Lakes are situated in its upper reaches. The valley provides the geographic setting for these high-elevation (2802 m) alpine lakes that were studied for primary productivity from 1973-81.
