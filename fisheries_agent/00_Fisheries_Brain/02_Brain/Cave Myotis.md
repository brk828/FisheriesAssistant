# Cave Myotis

## Summary
The Cave Myotis (Myotis velifer) is a bat species that was netted at both ends of the canyon and exhibits colonial roosting behavior. It was one of ten bat species documented during the survey, contributing to the overall mammal diversity of the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve.
