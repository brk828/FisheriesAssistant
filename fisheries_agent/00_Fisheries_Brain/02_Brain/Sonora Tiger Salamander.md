# Sonora Tiger Salamander

## Summary
The Sonora Tiger Salamander is an endangered amphibian species endemic to southwestern aquatic ecosystems, particularly in Arizona's Santa Cruz River Basin and associated riparian habitats, whose conservation status is governed under the Endangered Species Act. As an aquatic and semi-aquatic salamander dependent on perennial and seasonal water flows, its persistence is directly threatened by hydrological alterations including aquifer overdraft, water diversions such as those from the Central Arizona Project, and the loss of riparian habitat connectivity. Its protection exemplifies the intersection of water resource management and endangered species conservation, requiring coordinated management of both surface water flows and groundwater conditions to maintain viable breeding and rearing habitat in the arid Southwest.
