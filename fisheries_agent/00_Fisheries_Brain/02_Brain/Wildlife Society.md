# Wildlife Society

## Summary
The Wildlife Society is a major professional organization for wildlife management and research. It contributed multiple publication series to the OSU collection including Wildlife Monographs and Bulletin, representing approximately 30% of the library's wildlife and terrestrial ecology titles.
