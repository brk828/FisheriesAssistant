# Blue Tilapia

## Summary
Blue Tilapia (Oreochromis aureus) is a non-native cichlid species that became established in Arizona aquatic systems, particularly in research ponds and canal-connected lakes, primarily through aquarium releases and unauthorized stocking rather than natural dispersal. As an invasive species, it represents a significant ecological concern in these systems due to its competitive interactions with native fish fauna and its ability to establish self-sustaining populations in warm-water habitats. The species frequently appears in ichthyological surveys of southwestern United States water bodies as part of broader assessments of community composition changes and exotic species impacts on native fish assemblages.
