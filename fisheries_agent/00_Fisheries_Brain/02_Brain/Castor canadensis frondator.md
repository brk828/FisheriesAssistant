# Castor canadensis frondator

## Summary
Castor canadensis frondator is the beaver subspecies proposed for reintroduction to the San Pedro River in 1995. This source raises concerns that beaver dam construction during early riparian recovery could negatively impact native fish by creating ponded conditions that favor exotic species, increase water loss through evaporation, and damage young cottonwood/willow vegetation critical for endangered bird species.
