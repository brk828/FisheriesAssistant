# Rio Metzquital

## Summary
Rio Metzquital is a Mexican river system that represents the southern limit of Gila distribution (along with Rio Panuco). Gila from this system have very slender caudal peduncles, intermediate-sized scales (60-70 lateral line scales), no or very rarely faint basal radii, and modal vertebrae counts of either 42 or 43, with one tributary originating near Rio Nazas suggesting historical connections.
