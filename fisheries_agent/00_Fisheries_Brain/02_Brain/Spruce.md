# Spruce

## Summary
Spruce is a coniferous tree genus found in high-elevation forests. The lecture notes describe Spruce-Fir forests as occurring in Arizona's coniferous forest zone with closed canopy in semi-humid to humid, temperate to cold climates, positioned above Yellow Pine forests on mountain slopes.
