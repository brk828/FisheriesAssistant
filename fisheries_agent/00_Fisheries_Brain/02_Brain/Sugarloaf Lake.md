# Sugarloaf Lake

## Summary
Sugarloaf Lake is a 180-acre warm-water lake in Washtenaw County, Michigan, connected to the Huron River drainage. It was opened to year-round fishing for bluegills and sunfish, with a catch composition of 49.3% bluegill and 39.6% yellow perch, and seining surveys showed stable to improved young-of-year abundance between 1946 and 1950.
