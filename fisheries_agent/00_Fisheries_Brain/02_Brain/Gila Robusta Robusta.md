# Gila Robusta Robusta

## Summary
Gila robusta robusta is a subspecies of the roundtail chub endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly the Gila River and Little Colorado River systems in Arizona, characterized as a habitat specialist with a restricted and threatened distribution. This freshwater cyprinid represents an important component of native fish assemblages in the American Southwest and has been a focus of conservation and taxonomic research due to its ecological significance and vulnerability to habitat degradation and introduced species competition. The subspecific classification reflects efforts to understand phylogenetic relationships and conservation status within North American cyprinid fishes, with particular emphasis on distinguishing populations across different river drainages.
