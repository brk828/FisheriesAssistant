# Lasker et al.

## Summary
Lasker and colleagues conducted research on anchovy (Engraulis mordax) larval rearing in 1970. Their study achieved 47% survival at 19 days by feeding larvae a combination of the dinoflagellate Gymnodinium splendens plus mollusk veligers, representing one of the more successful early attempts at rearing this commercially important species.
