# Littlefield

## Summary
Littlefield is a location in Mohave County, Arizona, near which five springs support populations of Littlefield Springs Fontelicella. Three springs are on highway right-of-way and two on public land, with the springs co-occurring with unique Speckled Dace and facing potential threats from proposed desalinization projects.
