# Lower Truckee River

## Summary
The Lower Truckee River refers to the downstream reach of the Truckee River in western Nevada, which serves as a critical spawning and migration corridor for the endangered cui-ui, a native fish species dependent on this system for reproductive success. This riverine section is integral to the conservation of cui-ui populations, as documented in management plans and correspondence regarding the Marble Bluff Fish Facility, which was constructed to facilitate fish passage and monitor spawning activities in this ecologically sensitive area. The Lower Truckee River represents a focal point for fisheries management efforts aimed at sustaining endangered fish populations through habitat maintenance, passage infrastructure, and monitoring protocols.
