# Centro Ecológico De Sonora

## Summary
The Centro Ecológico de Sonora is a Mexican governmental institution based in Sonora that serves as a key collaborative partner in the binational conservation efforts for desert fishes and aquatic ecosystems spanning the southwestern United States and northern Mexico. As an active participant in the Desert Fishes Recovery Team framework under the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, the centro contributes Mexican institutional expertise and oversight to the recovery and management of endangered endemic fish species in arid-land aquatic habitats of the region.
