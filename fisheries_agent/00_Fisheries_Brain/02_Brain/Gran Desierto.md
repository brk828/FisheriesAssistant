# Gran Desierto

## Summary
Gran Desierto is a region in Sonora, Mexico that represents the southern extent of the geographic coverage in this Death Valley System bibliography. The bibliography's cross-border coverage extends to this Mexican desert region as part of documenting the broader distribution and conservation context of desert fishes in the southwestern North American arid lands.
