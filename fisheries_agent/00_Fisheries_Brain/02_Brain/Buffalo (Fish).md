# Buffalo (Fish)

## Summary
Buffalo fish are a group of large cyprinid fishes in the genus *Ictiobus* that include three primary species: Bigmouth Buffalo (*Megastomatobus cyprinella*), Black Buffalo (*Ictiobus niger*), and Smallmouth Buffalo (*Ictiobus bubalus*). These are classified as rough or coarse fish that dominate riverine fish communities, particularly in large main-stem rivers and impoundments where they constitute significant portions of total fish biomass and are commonly captured in commercial and survey fishing operations. Buffalo fish are ecologically important as benthic foragers in freshwater ecosystems and represent a substantial component of fish populations in North American river systems, though they are generally not valued as sport fish.
