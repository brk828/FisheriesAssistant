# Tepehuan

## Summary
The Tepehuan are an indigenous community in the Sierra Madre Occidental of Chihuahua, Mexico to whom Consejo Asesor Sierra Madre provides technical and legal services. Like the Tarahumara, the Tepehuan communities are served by conservation efforts that include protection of cross-border species affected by U.S. environmental law enforcement.
