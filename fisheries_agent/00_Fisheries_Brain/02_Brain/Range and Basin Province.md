# Range and Basin Province

## Summary
The Range and Basin Province (also called Great Basin) is an interior region that provides physiographic evidence for past pluvial conditions. During the Pluvial Period accompanying the Wisconsin glaciation, this province contained extensive lakes and full-flowing rivers with vastly greater precipitation than present, followed by rapid desiccation in Postglacial time.
