# Craig

## Summary
Craig is a town in Colorado located near river kilometer 224 on the Yampa River. Adult Colorado Squawfish distribution in the Yampa River extends from the river's mouth upstream to Craig, and the study area was extended to this location to encompass the full range of fish habitat use.
