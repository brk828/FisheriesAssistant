# Cumulative Distribution Function

## Summary
The Cumulative Distribution Function was used in the Population Viability Analysis to model extinction risk for the razorback sucker population. It helped predict the probability and timing of the population reaching various extinction thresholds (50, 100, and 500 individuals), with results showing median times to extinction ranging from approximately 6 to 15 years depending on the threshold, and indicating that 5% probability of persistence would be attained in 9-20 years.
