# Lampetra Aepyptera

## Summary
Lampetra aepyptera, commonly known as the least brook lamprey, is a small non-parasitic lamprey species found in eastern North American freshwater systems, distinguished from closely related brook lamprey species by its characteristic trunk myomere count of 50–59 and smaller body size. As a benthic larval stream dweller, this species plays an ecological role in freshwater ecosystems and serves as an indicator organism for stream health and water quality assessments in ichthyological surveys. Conservation interest in L. aepyptera relates to its dependence on clean, flowing streams and its vulnerability to habitat degradation, making it relevant to broader freshwater fish biodiversity and ecosystem monitoring efforts.
