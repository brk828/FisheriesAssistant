# GeoResearch Inc.

## Summary
GeoResearch Inc. is a software company based in Billings, Montana that developed GPS mapping systems. In 1994, they donated their complete Geolink Mapping System software suite to the University of Arizona to support field data collection and mapping for natural resource management applications.
