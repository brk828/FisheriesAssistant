# Freshwater Fish

## Summary
Freshwater fish are aquatic vertebrates that complete their life cycles in inland water systems including streams, rivers, lakes, and wetlands with low salinity levels. These organisms occupy diverse ecological niches across freshwater habitats ranging from cold-water lotic systems (flowing streams and rivers) to lentic environments (still water bodies), and they play critical roles as both predators and prey in freshwater food webs while serving as important indicators of ecosystem health and water quality. Freshwater fish represent a significant proportion of global fish biodiversity and are essential subjects for conservation efforts due to their vulnerability to habitat degradation, pollution, and other anthropogenic pressures affecting inland aquatic ecosystems.
