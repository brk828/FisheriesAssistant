# Baltic Sea

## Summary
The Baltic Sea is a brackish marine environment where Northern Pike populations have experienced drastic declines since the 1970s-1980s, with commercial catches decreasing considerably throughout the region including Danish straits. The western Baltic Sea, particularly transitional waters like Stege Lagoon, provides brackish spawning habitat for pike populations adapted to higher salinity levels than typical freshwater populations. The reasons for Baltic-wide pike population declines are not well determined but may include poor recruitment, habitat deterioration, or eutrophication.
