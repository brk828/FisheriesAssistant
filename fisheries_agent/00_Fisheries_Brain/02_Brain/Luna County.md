# Luna County

## Summary
Luna County is a county in New Mexico, USA, where the Mimbres River flows. Historical museum specimens of Cyprinella formosa (Beautiful Shiner) were collected from the Mimbres River in Luna County at locations including near Columbus and 5 miles below Fayewood (Dwyer) between 1949 and 1950.
