# Benthic Macroinvertebrates

## Summary
Benthic macroinvertebrates are large invertebrate organisms that inhabit the bottom substrates of aquatic ecosystems, primarily consisting of aquatic insects and other arthropods that serve as fundamental indicators of stream health and ecological productivity. These organisms are essential components of aquatic food webs, functioning as primary consumers and prey for fish, while their taxonomic composition and abundance provide valuable data for assessing water quality, community structure, and trophic dynamics in freshwater systems. Sampling benthic macroinvertebrate communities through standardized methods, such as artificial substrate samplers, enables researchers to estimate aquatic productivity and monitor the biological integrity of stream ecosystems.
