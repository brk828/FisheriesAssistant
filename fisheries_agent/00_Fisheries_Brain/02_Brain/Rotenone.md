# Rotenone

## Summary
Rotenone is a naturally derived piscicide extracted from legume roots that has been the most widely used fish toxicant in the United States since its registration in 1962, particularly for removing non-native fish species during aquatic habitat restoration projects. The compound, traditionally used by indigenous South American peoples for fish harvesting, is favored in fisheries management because it breaks down within a few days in aquatic environments, minimizing long-term ecological persistence. Rotenone's application—whether by broadcast treatment or hand application in complex habitats—makes it a standard tool in conservation biology for reclaiming native fish communities, though its use has generated ongoing scientific debate regarding its environmental impacts.
