# Characeae

## Summary
Characeae are complex freshwater aquatic plants (stoneworts) with worldwide distribution, characterized by distinctive stem nodes and internodes, whorled branchlets, and elaborate reproductive structures. This study establishes baseline chromosome numbers across multiple genera and species, documenting three distinct polyploid series (14:28 in corticate Chara, uniformly 14 in ecorticate Chara, and 6-9-12-18 in Nitelleae) and proposes major taxonomic revisions based on correlations between ploidy levels and reproductive strategies.
