# Alexander Burnes

## Summary
Alexander Burnes led a mission to Kabul from 1836-1838 during which fish specimens were collected from Afghanistan. His expedition contributed to the early documentation of Afghanistan's ichthyofauna as part of the historical collecting efforts in the region.
