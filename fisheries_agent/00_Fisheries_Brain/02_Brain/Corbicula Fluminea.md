# Corbicula Fluminea

## Summary
Corbicula fluminea, commonly known as the Asian clam, is a small freshwater bivalve native to Asia that has become an invasive species in North American aquatic ecosystems. In the context of these studies on reservoir mussel ecology, C. fluminea represents a significant ecological threat to native unionid mussels, as its rapid proliferation following impoundment and habitat alteration can outcompete native species for food and space while potentially altering sediment dynamics and water quality. Its presence in post-impoundment surveys indicates how anthropogenic disturbance facilitates invasive species establishment, contributing to the documented decline of native Unioninae fauna in reservoirs like Kentucky Lake.
