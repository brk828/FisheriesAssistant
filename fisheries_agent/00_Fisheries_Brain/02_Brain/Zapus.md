# Zapus

## Summary
Zapus sp. (jumping mice) are small mammals that possibly occur in the upper reaches of Cibeque Creek, Canyon Creek, Carizzo Creek, and Leonard Canyon. These jumping mice represent one of the aquatic or semi-aquatic mammal species that may be present in the higher elevation streams of the study area.
