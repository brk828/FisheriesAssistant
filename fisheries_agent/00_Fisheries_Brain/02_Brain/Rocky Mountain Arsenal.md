# Rocky Mountain Arsenal

## Summary
Rocky Mountain Arsenal is an industrial site where three lakes polluted with aldrin from an insecticide plant caused chronic waterfowl mortality. This source reports an estimated 2,000 duck deaths annually during winter and early spring, with lake sediments containing up to 480 ppm aldrin and dieldrin, living snails containing up to 88 ppm dieldrin, and dead ducks containing 30-64 ppm dieldrin in their tissues.
