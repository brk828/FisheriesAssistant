# Percina Maculata

## Summary
Percina maculata, commonly known as the blackside darter, is a small benthic fish species found in clear, fast-flowing streams and rivers of eastern North America, particularly in systems like the Allegheny River drainage and its tributaries. This species inhabits rocky substrates in moderate to high-gradient streams and serves as an important indicator of stream health and water quality in lotic ecosystems. As documented in studies of darter ecology and distribution, Percina maculata is significant for understanding fish community structure in Appalachian aquatic systems and is relevant to conservation assessments of headwater and tributary habitats.
