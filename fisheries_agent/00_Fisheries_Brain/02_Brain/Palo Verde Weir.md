# Palo Verde Weir

## Summary
Palo Verde Weir is a structure on the Colorado River that served as a boundary marker for fisheries management activities. In this 1963 report, it marked the downstream limit of a Channel Catfish stocking project, where 404,000 fingerlings were planted between Parker Dam and Palo Verde Weir as part of a cooperative investigation.
