# Paguristes

## Summary
Paguristes is a genus of hermit crabs documented in the Puerto Peñasco intertidal zone. This field guide includes it among the anomuran crabs (hermit crabs and porcelain crabs) found in the Gulf of California region, with identifications made by Clinton Westervelt in consultation with Dr. Janet Haig.
