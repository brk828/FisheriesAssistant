# Abramis brama

## Summary
Abramis brama is a European cyprinid fish used as one of the leuciscin outgroup taxa in this phylogenetic study. It was included along with Notemigonus crysoleucas to help root the phylogenetic tree and establish relationships among the major North American cyprinid clades.

## Context

> This molecular phylogenetic study examined the evolutionary relationships of the tribe [[Plagopterini]], a group of spiny-rayed cyprinid fishes endemic to southwestern [[North America]].
