# Victory Lake

## Summary
Victory Lake was a 26-acre lake at Fort Benning, Georgia where antimycin A was used to selectively remove scale fish from a catfish population. Treatment with 3.6 ppb in the lake and 10 ppb in tributaries/springs killed 5,200 pounds of scale fish (including chub suckers, golden shiners, bluegills, warmouth, and largemouth bass) while killing only one channel catfish and one bullhead, demonstrating catfish resistance to the toxicant.
