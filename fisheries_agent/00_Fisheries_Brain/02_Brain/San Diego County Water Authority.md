# San Diego County Water Authority

## Summary
The San Diego County Water Authority entered into a water transfer agreement with the Imperial Irrigation District for up to 200,000 acre-feet per year, focusing on on-farm efficiency improvements. This represented one of the major water conservation and transfer initiatives in the Colorado River basin discussed at the 1998 workshop.
