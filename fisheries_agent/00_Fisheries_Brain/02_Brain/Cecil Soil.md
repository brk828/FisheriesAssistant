# Cecil Soil

## Summary
Cecil Soil is a local reddish-brown soil of schistic origin found in the Auburn, Alabama area. In this study, when applied as a 4-inch layer in experimental pools, it significantly reduced total and dissolved organic carbon levels (to approximately 55% of pools without soil) but did not significantly affect fish production or nitrogen concentrations.
