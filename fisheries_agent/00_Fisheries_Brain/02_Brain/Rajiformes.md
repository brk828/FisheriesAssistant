# Rajiformes

## Summary
Rajiformes is an order of cartilaginous fishes within the subclass Elasmobranchii that includes rays and skates. This source documents that the order contains 7 families in the Gulf of California, including benthic species such as Rajidae and Stingrays (4 genera, 9 species) and Electric Rays (2 genera, 3 species).
