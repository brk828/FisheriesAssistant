# W. L. Minckley

## Summary
W. L. Minckley was a prominent ichthyologist and conservation biologist who conducted foundational research on native fish species in the American Southwest, particularly in desert aquatic ecosystems. He was instrumental in advocating for the protection and management of endemic and threatened fish species, serving as a key figure in the Desert Fishes Council and contributing to landmark conservation efforts such as the book "Battle Against Extinction: Native Fish Management in the American West." Minckley's work bridged academic ichthyology with applied conservation biology, establishing him as a leading expert on desert fish ecology and the impacts of habitat alteration on Southwestern aquatic fauna.
