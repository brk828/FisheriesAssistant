# Oyster Toadfish

## Summary
The oyster toadfish is a marine fish species that serves as the host for the newly described parasite Argulus chesapeakensis in Chesapeake Bay waters. The host-parasite relationship was documented by Dutcher and Schwartz in 1962, though the parasite was initially misidentified as a different species.
