# Paige Springs Hatchery

## Summary
Paige Springs Hatchery is an Arizona fish hatchery facility that provides grow-out facilities for razorback suckers as part of the state's recovery efforts. The hatchery works in conjunction with Dexter National Fish Hatchery to rear razorback suckers for the 10-year reintroduction program initiated in 1981 by the Arizona Game and Fish Department and U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.
