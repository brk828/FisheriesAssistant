# Pacific Rockfish

## Summary
Pacific Rockfish refers to rockfish species in the North Pacific, including those studied here. The study notes that similar seasonal behavioral patterns (moving into shelter during cold months) have been documented in other Pacific Rockfish species including Puget Sound Rockfish, Copper Rockfish, and Blue Rockfish.

## Context

> This study documents seasonal behavioral patterns in mixed aggregations of [[Yellowtail Rockfish]] (*Sebastes flavidus*) and [[Dusky Rockfish]] (*S. ciliatus*) observed by divers in [[Lynn Canal]], southeastern [[Alaska]] from 1965-75.
