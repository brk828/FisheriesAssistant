# Pittsburg and Midway Coal Mining Company

## Summary
Pittsburg and Midway Coal Mining Company was a coal operator in southeastern Kansas that was preparing a deed for approximately 70 acres of strip-land. This pending donation would have added to the public fishing areas being developed through the strip-pit management program.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
