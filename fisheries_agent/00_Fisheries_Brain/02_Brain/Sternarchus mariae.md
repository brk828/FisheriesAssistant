# Sternarchus mariae

## Summary
Sternarchus mariae is a new species of electric knifefish described in this study from the Magdalena Basin. The type specimen (201 mm) was collected at Girardot and paratype (273 mm) at Apulo, characterized by 173-176 anal rays and a distinctive uninterrupted whitish streak along the median dorsal line from snout to caudal base, and named for Brother Apolinar Maria.
