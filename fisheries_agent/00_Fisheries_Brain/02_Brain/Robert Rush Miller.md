# Robert Rush Miller

## Summary
Robert Rush Miller was a prominent ichthyologist who collaborated with Carl L. Hubbs in documenting and mapping the fish faunas of isolated aquatic systems in now-desiccated pluvial lake basins of the American West, continuing foundational work begun by John Otterbein Snyder. His research was instrumental in understanding the biogeographic patterns and evolutionary history of fish species adapted to relict water systems, particularly in the Great Basin and other regions where ancient lakes had disappeared, contributing significantly to both systematic ichthyology and conservation biology of endemic fish populations in arid regions.
