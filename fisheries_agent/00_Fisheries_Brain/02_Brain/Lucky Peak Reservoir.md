# Lucky Peak Reservoir

## Summary
Lucky Peak Reservoir is a managed impoundment on the Boise River system that receives inflow from Arrowrock Reservoir outflow and Mores Creek. This study found it maintained a stable fish population from 1964-1967, remains nearly homothermal year-round due to high inflow and outflow, and served as a refuge for fish migrating from Arrowrock during the 1966 drawdown.
