# Threatened Species

## Summary
A threatened species in fish science refers to any fish population or taxon that is at risk of becoming endangered due to declining numbers, habitat loss, restricted range, or other conservation concerns, but which has not yet reached critically low populations requiring immediate emergency intervention. These species represent an intermediate conservation status requiring active management and monitoring to prevent further decline and potential extinction. In aquatic ecology and conservation biology, threatened species serve as important indicators of ecosystem health and are often prioritized for research, habitat restoration, and regulatory protection to ensure long-term population viability.
