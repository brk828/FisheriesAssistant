# Ridgenosed Rattlesnake

## Summary
Ridgenosed Rattlesnake (Crotalus willardi obscurus) is a reptile species with restricted distribution in the mountains of the Sierra San Luis area. It is one of 22 protected reptile species in the reserve, which contains 51 reptile species representing 38.93% of the state's total reptile diversity.
