# Grass Carp

## Summary
The grass carp (Ctenopharyngodon idella) is a large Asian cyprinid fish introduced to North America for aquatic vegetation control in managed water bodies, including constructed lentic habitats such as golf course ponds and reservoirs. As an introduced species, grass carp represents a significant concern in conservation biology and aquatic ecology due to its potential to escape into native ecosystems, compete with or prey upon native fish species, and alter aquatic plant communities through herbivory. The species appears in scientific literature examining introduced species dynamics, genetic risks of escaped or hybridized populations, and ecosystem impacts in sensitive habitats such as the Colorado River Basin where native fishes are threatened or endangered.
