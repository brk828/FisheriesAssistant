# Little Tennessee River

## Summary
The Little Tennessee River is a significant freshwater system in the southeastern United States, particularly within the Blue Ridge Province and Piedmont Upland regions, that serves as an important study site for ichthyological and aquatic ecological research. As a tributary system in Tennessee, it has been the focus of field surveys documenting fish diversity and associated aquatic fauna such as dragonflies, making it valuable for understanding regional biodiversity patterns and species distributions across Appalachian watersheds. The river's ecological importance in conservation biology relates to its role as habitat for both common and endemic freshwater species in a biogeographically significant region.
