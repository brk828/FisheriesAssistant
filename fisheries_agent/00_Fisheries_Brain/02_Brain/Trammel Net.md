# Trammel Net

## Summary
A trammel net is a passive fishing gear consisting of three layers of netting designed to capture fish of various sizes by entangling them in the mesh as they attempt to pass through. Commonly used in fisheries sampling and ecological surveys, trammel nets are effective for collecting representative populations of fish species in lakes and rivers with minimal damage to specimens, making them valuable tools for monitoring fish populations and assessing aquatic community composition in conservation and research contexts.
