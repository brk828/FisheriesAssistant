# Price-Stubb Diversion

## Summary
The Price-Stubb Diversion is a water diversion structure that presents a barrier to fish passage in the Upper Colorado River Basin. The FY 1997 work plan allocated $575,000 for fish passage construction or planning at this site as part of habitat restoration efforts to improve connectivity for endangered fish species.
