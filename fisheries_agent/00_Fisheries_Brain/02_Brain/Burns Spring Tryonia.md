# Burns Spring Tryonia

## Summary
An undescribed species of hydrobiid snail (Tryonia n. sp.) endemic to Burns Spring in Mohave County, Arizona, with an elongate-conic shell. This source documents a critically endangered marginal population of approximately 30 individuals that by 1980 was found only in a pool inside an old tunnel rather than the previous 3-meter habitat, with immediate endangered listing and habitat modification recommended.
