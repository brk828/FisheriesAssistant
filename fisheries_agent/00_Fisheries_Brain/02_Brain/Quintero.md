# Quintero

## Summary
Quintero is a small laguna (10 × 20 meters) in the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin of Coahuila, México, fed by subsurface springs and representing the most isolated of three populations of Xiphophorus gordoni studied. This source's histocompatibility experiments revealed that the Quintero population has reduced genetic diversity compared to the other two lagunas, with fish from Quintero rejecting transplanted fins from Los Fresnos and Santa Tecla much faster (MST = 11-14 days) than the reverse direction (MST = 25-27 days), indicating Quintero lacks many histocompatibility genes present in the other populations—likely due to founder effects, population bottlenecks, or its smaller size and greater isolation.
