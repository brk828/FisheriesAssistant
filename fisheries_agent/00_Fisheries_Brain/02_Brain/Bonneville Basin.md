# Bonneville Basin

## Summary
The Bonneville Basin is a large inland drainage system in the western United States, historically centered in Utah, that has served as an important biogeographic region for studying fish evolution, endemism, and conservation. The basin's fish fauna, particularly its endemic species and subspecies, represents significant evolutionary significance for understanding how fish populations have adapted to isolated aquatic systems and diversified in response to historical geological and hydrological changes. Management of Bonneville Basin fish species has been a focus of conservation efforts due to the unique evolutionary lineages and distinctive populations found within this region.
