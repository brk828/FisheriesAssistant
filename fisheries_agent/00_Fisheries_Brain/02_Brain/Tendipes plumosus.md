# Tendipes plumosus

## Summary
Tendipes plumosus is a filter-feeding midge fly larva (family Tendipedidae/Chironomidae) that was one of three dominant species in the oil refinery holding-ponds. This study found it had an average length of 8.3 mm, dry weight of 0.501 mg, a 32-day laboratory life cycle, and predominated in spring populations; it transferred approximately 14,130 kilocalories per year through adult emergence and demonstrated significant ability to reduce effluent turbidity through filter-feeding.
