# George N. Greene

## Summary
George N. Greene was a researcher at Auburn University who conducted experimental studies on pond fertilization and aquaculture. In 1967-1968, he investigated how different nitrogen sources and soil presence affected fish production and water chemistry in plastic-lined pools stocked with Fathead Minnow.

## Context

> This experimental study by [[George N. Greene]] at [[Auburn University]] (1967-1968) investigated the effects of three nitrogen sources (sodium nitrate, ammonium carbonate, and cottonseed meal) and the presence of [[Cecil Soil]] on fish production and water chemistry in fertilized plastic pools....
