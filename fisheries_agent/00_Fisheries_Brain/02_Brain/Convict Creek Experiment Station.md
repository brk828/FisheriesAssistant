# Convict Creek Experiment Station

## Summary
The Convict Creek Experiment Station was a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility located in Mono County, California, where this immunogenetic research on trout blood antigens was conducted from July through November 1962. It served as one of two primary research locations for the study.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
