# John Otterbein Snyder

## Summary
John Otterbein Snyder was a prominent ichthyologist and fisheries scientist who conducted pioneering biogeographical surveys of western North American fish faunas, most notably a 1915 expedition examining the ancient Lake Bonneville basin (now the Great Salt Lake region). His work on isolated fish populations in now-desiccated basins established foundational methods for reconstructing historical fish distributions and understanding speciation patterns in western aquatic systems. Snyder's contributions were instrumental in early 20th-century ichthyological research on conservation and evolutionary biology, influencing subsequent generations of fish scientists working on relict and endemic fish populations.
