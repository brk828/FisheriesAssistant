# Dwarf Bear-Poppy

## Summary
Dwarf Bear-Poppy is a threatened or endangered insect-pollinated plant species. This biological opinion found that aerial pesticide application for grasshopper control posed jeopardy to the species through destruction of its pollinator populations, and established a 3-mile exclusion zone for aerial spraying around its habitat or mandated use of alternative control methods.
