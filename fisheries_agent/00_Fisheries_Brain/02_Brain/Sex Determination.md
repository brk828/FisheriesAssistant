# Sex Determination

## Summary
Sex determination is conducted on captured Northern Pike to track sex ratios and target reproductive females for removal. The 2022 suppression catch showed a 50% female sex ratio, with females averaging 627 mm total length and 1,800 g compared to males at 587 mm and 1,430 g, allowing managers to focus removal effort on mature pre-spawn females to reduce reproductive potential.
