# Upper Gulf Of California

## Summary
The Upper Gulf of California refers to the northernmost marine section of the Gulf of California (Sea of Cortez) that forms the terminus of the Colorado River Delta, representing a critical transition zone between freshwater riverine, estuarine, and marine ecosystems. In fish science and conservation contexts, this region is recognized as an integrated binational ecosystem of high ecological and fisheries significance, where Colorado River flows historically supported distinct fish communities and supported important commercial and subsistence fisheries. The Upper Gulf of California has become a focal point for interdisciplinary conservation efforts addressing the ecological consequences of river flow alterations and seeking to restore ecosystem health across the freshwater-to-marine continuum.
