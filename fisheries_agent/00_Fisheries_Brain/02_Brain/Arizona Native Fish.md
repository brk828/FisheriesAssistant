# Arizona Native Fish

## Summary
Arizona Native Fish refers to over 30 endemic fish species that evolved specialized adaptations to survive in Arizona's isolated streams, rivers, and small pools under severe environmental conditions since the Pleistocene. According to this 1995 survey study, 24 of Arizona's 30 native fish species are listed as endangered at state and/or federal levels, with populations facing dramatic declines since the late 1800s due to habitat alterations from cattle grazing, dams, irrigation, declining water tables, mining, and introduction of non-native species. The study found that only 33.8% of non-anglers were aware Arizona has native fish species, and these fish once served as important food sources for pioneers and Native Americans.
