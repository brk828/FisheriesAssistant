# Lake Ashtabula

## Summary
Lake Ashtabula in North Dakota was the site of innovative nutrient budget research investigating fish harvest as a biological nutrient removal mechanism. The project aimed to remove approximately half of the annual phosphate input (10-11 metric tons) and nitrogen input (7-8 metric tons) through commercial harvest of Yellow Perch, though panel discussions questioned the efficiency of this approach.
