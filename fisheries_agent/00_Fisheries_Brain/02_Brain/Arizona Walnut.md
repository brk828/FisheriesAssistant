# Arizona Walnut

## Summary
Arizona Walnut (Juglans major) is one of the canopy layer broadleaf species in Lower Bonita Creek. It became more prominent on the four uppermost transects along the elevational gradient, and even its seedlings (considered among the least palatable) were affected by severe grazing pressure severe enough to prevent regeneration.
