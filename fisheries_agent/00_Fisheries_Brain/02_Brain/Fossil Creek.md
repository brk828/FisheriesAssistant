# Fossil Creek

## Summary
Fossil Creek, located in Arizona, is a significant aquatic ecosystem that has been the focus of intensive fisheries management and conservation efforts, particularly in relation to the anticipated decommissioning of the Childs/Irving Hydroelectric dams operated by Arizona Public Service. The creek has been subject to systematic biological surveys, including trammel net sampling studies, and serves as a case study for reestablishment and restoration of fish populations in a system previously impacted by hydroelectric development. Interagency collaboration on Fossil Creek's management reflects broader conservation biology concerns about rehabilitating aquatic habitats and native fish communities affected by water infrastructure modifications.
