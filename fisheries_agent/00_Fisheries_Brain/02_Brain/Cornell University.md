# Cornell University

## Summary
Cornell University was one of three cooperating institutions (along with the New York State Conservation Department and the federal Bureau of Fisheries) that conducted the trout nutrition research from 1927-1934. Dr. C. M. McCay, the author of this comprehensive report on trout nutrition, was affiliated with Cornell University.
