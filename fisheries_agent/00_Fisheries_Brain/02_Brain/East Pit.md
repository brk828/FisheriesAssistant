# East Pit

## Summary
East Pit was a strip-pit lake at Miner's State Park that exemplified successful fisheries management through reconditioning. Before treatment it contained 7,700 fish per surface acre with only 30 keeper-size individuals, but after rotenone treatment and restocking it produced channel catfish growing from 3 inches to 12 inches in 15 months, black crappie reaching 6.5 inches in one year, and averaged 1.0 fish per hour catch rate.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
