# Lee'S Phenomenon

## Summary
Lee's Phenomenon is a systematic bias in back-calculated fish growth measurements that occurs when the relationship between body size and scale size changes non-proportionally during a fish's life, causing inaccuracies in estimating historical growth rates from scale annuli. This phenomenon can distort age and growth analyses, particularly when researchers use direct-proportion calculations without accounting for allometric growth changes, though in many practical applications the effect may be too minor to substantially affect population-level growth estimates. Recognition of Lee's Phenomenon is important for ensuring the reliability of back-calculated growth data used in fisheries management and conservation assessments.
