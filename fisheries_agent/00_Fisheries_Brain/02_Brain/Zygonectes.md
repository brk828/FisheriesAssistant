# Zygonectes

## Summary
Zygonectes is an American killifish genus from which both Oryzias and Fundulichthys are distinguished. Oryzias differs from Zygonectes by having a very long anal fin, while Fundulichthys differs in having a small dorsal inserted wholly anterior to an equally small anal fin.

## Context

> This 1906 taxonomic review by [[Jordan]] and [[Snyder]] documents two species of killifishes (family [[Poeciliidae]]) from Japan: [[Fundulichthys virescens]] and [[Oryzias latipes]]. Both are small fishes inhabiting rice swamps, pools, and ditches in southern Japan, likely feeding on mosquitoes....
