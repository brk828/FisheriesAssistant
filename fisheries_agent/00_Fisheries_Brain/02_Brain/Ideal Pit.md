# Ideal Pit

## Summary
Ideal Pit was one of three Linn County strip-pits used in an experimental fertilization study conducted by Hume-Sinclair Coal Mining Company with department supervision. Regular limnological tests documented fertilization effects on food organism abundance at this site.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
