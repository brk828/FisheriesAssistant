# Black Sagebrush

## Summary
Black Sagebrush (Artemisia nova) is a shrub species found in the Pinyon-Juniper Woodland community of the Mono Basin. It occurs at variable elevations between 4,000 and 8,000+ feet alongside Big Sagebrush, Single-leaf Pinyon, and Utah Juniper.
