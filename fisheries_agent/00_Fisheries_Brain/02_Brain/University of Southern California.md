# University of Southern California

## Summary
The University of Southern California's Allan Hancock Foundation contributed taxonomic expertise to this field guide through Dr. Gerald J. Bakus (sponges) and Dr. John Garth (brachyuran crabs). This institution was one of several that provided specialist identifications for the Puerto Peñasco invertebrate survey.
