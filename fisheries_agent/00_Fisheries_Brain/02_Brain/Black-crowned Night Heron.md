# Black-crowned Night Heron

## Summary
Black-crowned Night Heron (Nycticorax nycticorax) is a wading bird that serves as a definitive host for Clinostomum marginatum. Adult trematodes reside in the mouth and esophagus of these birds, which are among the several heron species that complete the parasite's life cycle.
