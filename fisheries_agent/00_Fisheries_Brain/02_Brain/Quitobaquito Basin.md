# Quitobaquito Basin

## Summary
Quitobaquito Basin is an area within Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument containing an important water source. This source reports an extinct cienega was identified in this basin, surrounded by Paleoindian tools and potentially containing mammoth remains, and notes that Andrew Dorsey established canals and orchards there in the 1860s.
