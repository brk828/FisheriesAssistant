# Lago de Patzcuaro

## Summary
Lago de Patzcuaro is a highland lake in central Mexico representing important freshwater habitat diversity. This source lists it among the major highland lakes that contribute to the extraordinary ecological variety of Mexican freshwater habitats documented in the checklist.
