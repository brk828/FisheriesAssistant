# Central Arizona Phoenix Long-Term Ecological Research Project

## Summary
CAP LTER is a research initiative focused on studying the effects of urban development on the Sonoran Desert ecosystem. Student research activities through this program include monitoring nutrients and minerals in stream water and groundwater, collecting and identifying insects, monitoring plant growth and respiration, studying the limnology of urban lakes including water quality testing and microscopic organisms, and conducting GIS investigations using satellite imagery and remote sensing.
