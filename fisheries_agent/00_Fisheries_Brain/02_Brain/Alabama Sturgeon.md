# Alabama Sturgeon

## Summary
The Alabama Sturgeon (*Scaphirhynchus suttkusi*) is a critically endangered, endemic freshwater sturgeon of the Mobile River System in Alabama and Mississippi that has declined from historical abundance to near or complete extirpation due to habitat degradation and overfishing. As a member of the genus *Scaphirhynchus*, it represents an important conservation concern for freshwater sturgeon biodiversity and has been the subject of statistical modeling studies to assess extinction probability in river systems with sparse population data. Its listing under the Endangered Species Act reflects the urgent need for habitat protection and recovery strategies to prevent complete loss of this endemic species.
