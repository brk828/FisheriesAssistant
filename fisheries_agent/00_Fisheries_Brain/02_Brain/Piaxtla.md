# Piaxtla

## Summary
The Rio Piaxtla is a Pacific slope river system containing Catostomus plebeius populations that likely represent an undescribed taxon. This river is one of three Pacific drainages (with Rio Fuerte and Rio Mezquital) where these taxonomically uncertain sucker populations occur.
