# Tilapia Nilotica

## Summary
Tilapia nilotica, commonly known as the Nile tilapia, is a highly adaptable cichlid fish native to Africa that has become one of the most important aquaculture species globally due to its rapid growth rates, hardy nature, and efficient feed conversion. In ecological and conservation contexts, this species is frequently studied for its impacts as an introduced species in non-native aquatic ecosystems, where it can outcompete native fish populations and alter community structure through its omnivorous feeding habits and reproductive success. Its widespread use in comparative aquaculture studies and fisheries research makes it a model organism for understanding tilapiine biology, growth dynamics, and the ecological consequences of fish introductions in freshwater systems.
