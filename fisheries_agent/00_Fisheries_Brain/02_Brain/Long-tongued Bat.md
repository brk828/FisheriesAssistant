# Long-tongued Bat

## Summary
The Long-tongued Bat (Choeronycteris mexicana) is a nectar and pollen-feeding bat that was newly documented for the Aravaipa Creek watershed during this survey. Three individuals were found in an adobe barn in September 1981 and five in September 1982, indicating it is a summer resident in the area.
