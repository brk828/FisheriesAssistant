# Ernst Mayr

## Summary
Ernst Mayr was a leader in the Evolutionary Synthesis and made important conceptual contributions to evolutionary ecology. This source notes that his 1961 distinction between 'proximate' (mechanistic) and 'ultimate' (evolutionary) explanations helped structure ecological research, particularly regarding questions of population regulation, and influenced how ecologists approached their research questions.
