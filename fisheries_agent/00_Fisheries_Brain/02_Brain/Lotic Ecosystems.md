# Lotic Ecosystems

## Summary
Lotic ecosystems are flowing water systems such as rivers and streams that are characterized by unidirectional water movement, which creates distinct physical and biological conditions compared to still-water environments. These dynamic systems are essential habitats for specialized fish species and macroinvertebrate communities adapted to current-driven conditions, making them important subjects for aquatic ecology research, conservation efforts, and water quality monitoring. Understanding lotic ecosystems is critical for fisheries management, as their flowing nature fundamentally influences nutrient cycling, oxygen distribution, and the ecological processes that support fish populations and biodiversity.
