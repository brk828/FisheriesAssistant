# Colorado River Implementation Committee

## Summary
The Colorado River Implementation Committee is the governing body that oversees the Colorado River Recovery Program and makes decisions about project funding and work plans. According to this 1989 memorandum, the Implementation Committee mandated that all 44 funded projects submit annual reports by December 15, 1989, and these reports would directly influence FY-91 funding allocations, with the Technical Group evaluating the reports and providing recommendations to the Implementation Committee at their winter meeting.
