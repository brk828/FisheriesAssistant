# Conservation Policy

## Summary
Conservation policy refers to the strategies and approaches used by government agencies to protect and recover endangered species. This 1987 letter critiques a shift in U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service policy toward what the author calls the "Johnny Applefish" approach—spreading rare species across multiple non-native locations and relying on hatcheries and aquarium facilities rather than protecting natural habitats. The author argues this policy change prioritized avoiding controversy and listing requirements over the congressionally mandated purpose of species conservation.
