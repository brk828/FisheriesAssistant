# Quitobaquito Ecosystem

## Summary
The Quitobaquito Ecosystem is a fragile desert water-dependent ecosystem at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. This source addresses research questions about whether the present pond is a remnant of a larger cienega, whether spring locations shifted over time, and whether changes were related to groundwater, climate, or human use, noting that understanding past conditions helps manage this ecosystem.
