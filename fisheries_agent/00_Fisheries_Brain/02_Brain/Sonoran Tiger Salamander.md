# Sonoran Tiger Salamander

## Summary
The Sonoran Tiger Salamander is an amphibian species of conservation concern in southeastern Arizona's Fort Huachuca and Huachuca Mountains region, where it inhabits native freshwater stream systems within the highly biodiverse San Pedro River drainage. While not a fish species itself, it is relevant to aquatic ecology and conservation biology as it shares freshwater habitat with candidate fish species and reflects the broader conservation needs of this region's imperiled aquatic fauna. Its presence as part of the candidate species assessment underscores the interconnected conservation challenges facing multiple taxa in Arizona's most biologically diverse mountain aquatic systems.
