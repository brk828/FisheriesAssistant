# Rhoadsia minor

## Summary
Rhoadsia minor is a new species of characid fish described from high-elevation mountain streams (4,000 feet) in Mindo, Ecuador, in the Rio Esmeraldas system. It is distinguished from its relative R. altipinna by lesser body depth, relatively feeble dentition, more forward dorsal fin position, and smaller mature size (95mm versus 140mm), and exhibits ontogenetic dental modifications where young lack maxillary teeth entirely while mature specimens develop elongate maxillaries with 13 conical spike-like incisors plus two tricuspid incisors.
