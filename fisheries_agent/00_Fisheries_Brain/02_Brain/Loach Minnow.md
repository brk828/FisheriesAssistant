# Loach Minnow

## Summary
The Loach Minnow (*Tiaroga cobitis*) is a small, federally endangered cyprinid fish endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly found in the Gila River drainage of Arizona and New Mexico. This species is of significant conservation concern due to its limited distribution, sensitivity to habitat degradation, and vulnerability to competition and predation from introduced species, making it a focal species for aquatic ecosystem management and recovery efforts in arid region watersheds. The Loach Minnow's ecological role as an indicator of healthy stream conditions and its use in fish inventory surveys make it an important component of southwestern desert fish conservation programs and regulatory compliance assessments.
