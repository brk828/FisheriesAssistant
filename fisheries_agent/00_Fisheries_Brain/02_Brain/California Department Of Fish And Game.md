# California Department Of Fish And Game

## Summary
The California Department of Fish and Game (now the California Department of Fish and Wildlife) is the state regulatory agency responsible for managing California's aquatic resources, including fish populations and their habitats. In the context of fisheries science and conservation, this agency develops policies, conducts research, and implements programs related to fish species management, monitoring, and protection across the state's diverse aquatic ecosystems. The department's involvement in historical fish science projects, such as those documented in the 1970s-1980s records, reflects its primary role in overseeing fisheries management decisions and conservation initiatives throughout California.
