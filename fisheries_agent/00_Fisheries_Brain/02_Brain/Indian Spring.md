# Indian Spring

## Summary
Indian Spring is one of approximately seven spring systems within 1 km of School Spring that supports a population of Warm Springs Pupfish in Ash Meadows, Nevada. The population shows strong seasonal variation, ranging from 39-164 individuals with numbers typically doubling from winter minimum to late summer maximum due to year-round reproduction in the warm spring waters.
