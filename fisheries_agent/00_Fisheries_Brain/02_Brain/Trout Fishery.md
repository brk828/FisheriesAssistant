# Trout Fishery

## Summary
A trout fishery is a managed aquatic system, typically in tailwater areas below dams or in cool-water environments, that supports populations of trout species through either natural reproduction or regular stocking for recreational and economic purposes. These fisheries are often dependent on specific thermal and water quality conditions, such as cold hypolimnetic discharge from impoundments, which create suitable habitat for coldwater species while potentially altering native warmwater fish communities. Trout fisheries represent an important trade-off in aquatic ecosystem management, providing significant recreational and economic value but sometimes at the expense of native species conservation and ecosystem integrity.
