# Showlow Creek

## Summary
Showlow Creek is a cool tributary rising on the northern slopes of the White Mountains in Arizona that flows into the Little Colorado River. According to Miller (1961), the Little Colorado River Spinedace was historically abundant in this creek prior to 1939, but by 1960 the species had been extirpated from Showlow Creek along with most of its known range.
