# International Commission for the Northwest Atlantic Fisheries

## Summary
The International Commission for the Northwest Atlantic Fisheries is a specialized transboundary organization managing Atlantic fisheries resources that maintains regular publication series. It was among the international commissions represented in the OSU library collection.
