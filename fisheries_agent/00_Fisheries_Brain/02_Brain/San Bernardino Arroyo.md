# San Bernardino Arroyo

## Summary
San Bernardino Arroyo is a watercourse that drains westward from the Sierra San Luis area as part of the Rio Yaqui basin (Hydrologic Region 9). It is one of the drainage systems, along with Cajon Bonito, that carries water from this critical water collection zone in the northern Sierra Madre Occidental.
