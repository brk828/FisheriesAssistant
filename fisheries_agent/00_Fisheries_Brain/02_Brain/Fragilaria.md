# Fragilaria

## Summary
Fragilaria is a diatom genus that was one of the most prevalent phytoplankton organisms in Lake Powell. It occurred in 84 of the 160 plankton samples collected, making it one of the dominant primary producers in the reservoir's plankton assemblage.
