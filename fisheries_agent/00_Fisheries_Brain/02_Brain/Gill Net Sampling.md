# Gill Net Sampling

## Summary
Gill net sampling used 46-m × 1.8-m sinking nets with five ascending mesh panels set perpendicular to shore in shallow vegetated habitats, fished overnight (~23 hours soak time) during March-June. All mesh sizes captured wide size ranges of Northern Pike, with nets set at maximum depths <2 m capturing twice as many fish as nets >4 m depth, and the method proved highly effective at targeting spawning aggregations while maintaining >90% bycatch survival rates.
