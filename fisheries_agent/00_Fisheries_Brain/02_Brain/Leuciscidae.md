# Leuciscidae

## Summary
Leuciscidae is a family of freshwater cypriniform fishes that was established through modern reclassification of many taxa historically grouped under the broader family Cyprinidae. Members of this family are typically characterized by small body size, cycloid scales, and ecological adaptations to variable and often harsh freshwater environments, including systems with fluctuating flow regimes. The family includes numerous species of conservation interest in aquatic ecology, particularly in specialized or threatened freshwater habitats.
