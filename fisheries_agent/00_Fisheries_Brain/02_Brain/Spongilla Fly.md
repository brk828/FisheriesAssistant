# Spongilla Fly

## Summary
Spongilla flies are aquatic insects whose immature stages consume freshwater sponges, including their glassy spicules which pass through their digestive systems intact. These insects emerge as adults at the water surface where they become available as fish food, providing indirect value of sponges to fish ponds.
