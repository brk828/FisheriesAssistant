# Cottidae

## Summary
Cottidae is a family of benthic freshwater and marine fishes commonly known as sculpins, characterized by their bottom-dwelling habits, flattened bodies, and large heads with spiny gill covers. Members of this family are important components of cool- and coldwater aquatic ecosystems across North America and Eurasia, serving as indicator species for habitat quality and as prey for larger fish species. Several cottid species, including endemic forms found in isolated habitats, are of conservation concern due to habitat loss and invasive species competition, making them subjects of particular interest in ichthyological surveys and freshwater ecosystem monitoring.
