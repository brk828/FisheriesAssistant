# Inland Fisheries Branch

## Summary
The Inland Fisheries Branch was a division of the California Department of Fish and Game responsible for managing freshwater fish production and hatchery operations. This report represents the fourteenth annual hatchery cost and production summary produced by the Branch, documenting operations across multiple state and mitigation hatcheries during fiscal year 1966-67.
