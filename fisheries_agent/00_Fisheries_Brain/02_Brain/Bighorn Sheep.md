# Bighorn Sheep

## Summary
Bighorn Sheep (Ovis canadensis) are mammals that inhabit the terrestrial areas along the Colorado River corridor between Glen Canyon Dam and Lake Mead. This source identifies them as one of the mammal species present in the study area affected by dam operations.
