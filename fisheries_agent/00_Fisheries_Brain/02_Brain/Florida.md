# Florida

Florida represents a critical geographic region in ichthyological and conservation research due to its role as both a major center of fish biodiversity and a focal point for aquatic species introductions and invasions. The state's diverse freshwater and estuarine ecosystems, including its spring systems, lakes, and coastal habitats, have historically supported numerous endemic and native fish species while simultaneously serving as a gateway for non-native species establishment, making it a key study area for understanding fish distribution patterns, conservation challenges, and the ecological impacts of species introductions in North American aquatic systems.
