# Sierra San Luis

## Summary
Sierra San Luis is a mountain range in northern Mexico along the U.S. border, forming part of the extreme northern Sierra Madre Occidental. This management plan proposes establishing a 59,000-hectare Wild and Aquatic Flora and Fauna Protection Area there, protecting 395 vertebrate species (70 under federal protection) across diverse habitats ranging from mixed conifer forest at elevations up to 2,520 meters to natural grasslands and critical riparian corridors in lower reaches.
