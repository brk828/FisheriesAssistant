# Campostoma Anomalum

## Summary
Campostoma anomalum, commonly known as the central stoneroller, is a small cyprinid fish native to North American freshwater systems that inhabits upper-reach and headwater streams, where it is recognized as a habitat specialist in systems like the Pecos River. As a benthic forager and detritivore, this species plays an ecological role in stream ecosystems through its feeding behavior and presence, which can influence other aquatic organisms including invertebrates that respond to fish chemical cues. The species is documented across multiple biogeographic surveys and checklists spanning from Canadian drainage systems to southwestern U.S. river systems, reflecting its widespread distribution and importance in freshwater fish inventories and ecological studies.
