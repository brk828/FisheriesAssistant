# Warmwater Fishes

## Summary
Warmwater fishes are species adapted to relatively high water temperatures. This source notes that Razorback Sucker have optimal adult temperatures of 22.9-24.8°C and that reservoirs may provide warmer temperatures for longer periods relative to regulated rivers, potentially benefiting warmwater fishes like this endangered species.
