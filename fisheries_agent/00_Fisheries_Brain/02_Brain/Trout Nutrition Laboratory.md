# Trout Nutrition Laboratory

## Summary
The Trout Nutrition Laboratory was a research facility located at Cortland, New York that conducted systematic trout nutrition research beginning in 1932. This source indicates it was operated cooperatively by the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife, New York State Conservation Department, and Cornell University, and was the institutional home for the authors of this comprehensive feeding guidance circular.

## Context

> This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service circular (Circular 159, October 1963, with April 1964 correction) provides comprehensive guidance on feeding protocols and nutritional management for hatchery-reared trout.
