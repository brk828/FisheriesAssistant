# Jeff Davis County

## Summary
Jeff Davis County is a Texas county where Gammarus hyalelloides occurs at Phantom Lake Spring. This source documents both the small G. hyalelloides and a larger undescribed population (C) inhabiting the canal system fed by this spring.
