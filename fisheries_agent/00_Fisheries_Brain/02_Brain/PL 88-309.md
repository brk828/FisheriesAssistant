# PL 88-309

## Summary
PL 88-309 was federal legislation that provided funding for fisheries research. This research proposal was funded under PL 88-309 from 1966-1972 for studies on Salt River reservoirs in Arizona, though the proposal notes that PL 88-309 was set to terminate in fiscal year 1972-73 with anticipated reinstatement.
