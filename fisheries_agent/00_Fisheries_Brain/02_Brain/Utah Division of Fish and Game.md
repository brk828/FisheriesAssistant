# Utah Division of Fish and Game

## Summary
The Utah Division of Fish and Game is a state agency that, along with the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, maintains 21 water quality sampling stations in Lake Powell's main stem plus additional stations in bays and tributaries. The agency conducts regular monitoring including quarterly sampling of water chemistry parameters and monthly temperature and dissolved oxygen measurements to track reservoir conditions.
