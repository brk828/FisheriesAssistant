# Le Roux Creek

## Summary
Le Roux Creek is a water source associated with Hotchkiss National Fish Hatchery in Colorado, for which the facility holds water rights of 3143 gpm or 5100 acre-feet per year. However, this water source remains unused due to problems with siltation and temperature.
