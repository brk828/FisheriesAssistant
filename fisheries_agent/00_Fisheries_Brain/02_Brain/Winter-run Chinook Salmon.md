# Winter-run Chinook Salmon

## Summary
Winter-run Chinook Salmon is a distinct spawning run of chinook salmon that has been newly listed as endangered. This source identifies it as one of the species in critical decline, representing part of the broader collapse of California's salmon populations that began with peak commercial catches of 600,000 fish in 1882 and have declined ever since.
