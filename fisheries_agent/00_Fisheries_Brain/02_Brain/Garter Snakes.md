# Garter Snakes

## Summary
Garter Snakes are among the riparian species documented at the reservoir sites. This source predicts they could potentially benefit post-impoundment only if stable water levels are maintained and lakeside riparian vegetation develops along the new reservoirs, though immediate impoundment effects will displace most individuals.
