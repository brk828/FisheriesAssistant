# Southern Pacific Railroad

## Summary
The Southern Pacific Railroad played a crucial role in ending the catastrophic 1905-1907 Colorado River flood. The railroad finally closed the gap in the levee on February 10, 1907, by dumping boulders from a thousand rail cars, stopping the river from flowing into the Imperial Valley and Salton Sea.
