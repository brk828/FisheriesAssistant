# Lake Claremore

## Summary
Lake Claremore is a reservoir in the Verdigris River drainage system in Oklahoma. Jenkins (1949) reported 16 fish species from this reservoir in early fisheries studies prior to the comprehensive 1956-1957 survey.

## Context

> This master's thesis documents a comprehensive ichthyological survey of the [[Verdigris River]] drainage in Oklahoma conducted during summer 1956 and spring 1957.
