# Tahoe Dam

## Summary
Tahoe Dam is a structure on the Truckee River below Lake Tahoe. This source reports that spawning Kokanee appeared in the Truckee River below Tahoe Dam in winter 1947-1948, presumed to be migrants from Donner Lake.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
