# Sharp Springs

## Summary
Sharp Springs is a natural Gila Topminnow population site that maintains the highest genetic heterogeneity among natural populations, which correlates with fitness indices according to mtDNA analysis. The site shows variable mosquitofish presence, with topminnows coexisting with mosquitofish in lower pools while upper pools remain mosquitofish-free, and post-1985 management efforts attempted to use Sharp Springs stock for introductions, though only one successful wild population (Heron Spring) resulted from this stock.
