# Cypretta

## Summary
Cypretta is a genus of freshwater ostracod crustaceans established by Vavra in 1895, with Cypridopsis (Cypretta) tenuicauda as its type species. Cypretta kawatai is distinguished from other species in this genus by its more pointed carapace ends, characteristically short dorsal furca seta, and smaller sense club on the second antenna.

## Context
