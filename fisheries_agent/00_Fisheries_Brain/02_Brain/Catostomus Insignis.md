# Catostomus Insignis

## Summary
Catostomus insignis, commonly known as the Sonora sucker, is a native North American cypriniform fish belonging to the sucker family (Catostomidae) that inhabits small to moderate-sized streams and impoundments in the southwestern United States, particularly in Arizona and northern Mexico. This benthic-feeding species, characterized by its ventral sucker-mouth adapted for foraging on algae and organic detritus, represents an important component of native desert stream fish assemblages and has been documented in multiple monitoring surveys of Arizona riparian ecosystems including Aravaipa Creek and Eagle Creek systems. As a native species increasingly sympatric with non-native competitors and predators, C. insignis serves as a conservation indicator of native fish community integrity in threatened southwestern aquatic ecosystems.
