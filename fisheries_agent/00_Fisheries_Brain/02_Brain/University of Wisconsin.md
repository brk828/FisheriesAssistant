# University of Wisconsin

## Summary
The University of Wisconsin is a research institution where antimycin was originally discovered in 1945 by the Department of Plant Pathology. The university continued to be involved in antimycin research, including investigations into the edibility of antimycin-killed fish in collaboration with Ayerst Laboratories and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.
