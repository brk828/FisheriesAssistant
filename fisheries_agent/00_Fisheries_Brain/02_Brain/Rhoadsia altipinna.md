# Rhoadsia altipinna

## Summary
Rhoadsia altipinna is a related characid species from the Chimbo-Chan Chan basin of the Guayas system that occurs at lower elevations (700-800 feet) than R. minor. It differs from R. minor by having greater body depth, stronger dentition, more posterior dorsal fin position, and larger mature size (140mm versus 95mm), though the two species show similar scale counts and ontogenetic patterns of maxillary development.
