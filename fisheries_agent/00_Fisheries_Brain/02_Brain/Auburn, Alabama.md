# Auburn, Alabama

## Summary
Auburn, Alabama is the location of Auburn University Agricultural Experiment Station where this white catfish research was conducted. The spawning season for white catfish at this location occurred from May 11 to June 16, with optimal spawning temperatures between 68°F and 80°F.
