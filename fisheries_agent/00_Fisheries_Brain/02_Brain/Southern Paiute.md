# Southern Paiute

## Summary
The Southern Paiute are a Native American tribal group with cultural and historical connections to the Grand Canyon region. According to this source, the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Phase II program included a Southern Paiute ethnographic inventory conducted by Stoffle et al. (study series Q4) as part of the cultural resources research examining tribal connections to areas affected by Glen Canyon Dam operations.
