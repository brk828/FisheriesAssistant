# Fort Randall Reservoir

## Summary
Fort Randall Reservoir in South Dakota successfully controlled carp populations through water level manipulation rather than chemical treatment. By lowering water levels more than 2 feet within one week of egg deposition, spawning shallows were exposed and carp reproduction was prevented, demonstrating an alternative to chemical piscicides.
