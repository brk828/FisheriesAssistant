# Centrarchid Ecology

## Summary
Centrarchidae is the family to which Shoal Bass belongs, along with other black basses. This source provides detailed ecological information showing Shoal Bass are habitat specialists occupying riverine shoal areas with swift currents and rocky substrates, feeding primarily on insects, crayfish, and fish, with ontogenetic shifts in both habitat use and diet.
