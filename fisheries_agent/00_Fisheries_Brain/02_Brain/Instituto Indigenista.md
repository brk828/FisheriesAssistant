# Instituto Indigenista

## Summary
The Instituto Indigenista is a Mexican government institution that provided credits to the Cocopa community at El Mayor for fishing equipment including nets and motors. This support enabled the Cocopa to form a fishing cooperative (unidad comunal de produccion pesquera).
