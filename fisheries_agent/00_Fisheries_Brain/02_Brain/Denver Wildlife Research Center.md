# Denver Wildlife Research Center

## Summary
The Denver Wildlife Research Center was a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility that conducted pesticide-wildlife research. In 1964, the Center shifted its focus from general surveillance to intensive investigation of new pesticides as potential substitutes for persistent chlorinated organic insecticides, establishing a cooperative agreement with the U.S. Forest Service to evaluate nonpersistent insecticides and developing advanced analytical methods including gas chromatography with electron capture detection.
