# Greenback Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
The Greenback Cutthroat Trout is a native North American salmonid species that appears in fisheries management and conservation planning documents as a focal species for endangered fish recovery and habitat restoration efforts. As referenced in USFWS fisheries action plans, this species represents a conservation priority requiring integrated watershed-level management and inter-agency partnerships to address population declines resulting from habitat degradation, competition with non-native species, and altered water conditions. The species exemplifies the broader shift in aquatic resource management toward holistic ecosystem approaches rather than single-species management strategies.
