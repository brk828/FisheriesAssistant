# Pechacek

## Summary
Pechacek's 1956 research on cold-water fish in Wyoming found that jaw tags adversely affected growth rates, supporting this study's findings. At Marion County Lake, tagged bass grew only 0.5 inches compared to 1.2 inches for untagged bass over the same period, suggesting monel strap jaw tags reduced growth rates in largemouth bass.
