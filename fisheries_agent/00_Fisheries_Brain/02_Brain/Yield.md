# Yield

## Summary
Yield is the total weight of fish harvested from a population over a specified time period. At current fishing effort, northern pike yield was 2.1 kg/ha, but minimum length limits of 508 mm and 610 mm reduced yield to 0.9 kg/ha and 0.1 kg/ha respectively, demonstrating trade-offs between fish size and harvest quantity.
