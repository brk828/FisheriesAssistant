# Desert Nesting Bald Eagle

## Summary
The Desert Nesting Bald Eagle is a federally listed endangered species in Arizona. According to this source, it was one of seven federally listed species at risk from the Central Arizona Project's introduction of exotic species through its feeder canals and inadequate protective systems in the Gila River basin.
