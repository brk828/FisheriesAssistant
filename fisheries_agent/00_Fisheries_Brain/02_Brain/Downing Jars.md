# Downing Jars

## Summary
Downing Jars were specialized hatching vessels used in the artificial propagation process at the Fairport Station. Buffalo-fish eggs were placed in these jars on battery shelves with running water, where they would eye in 3-4 days and hatch in 9-10 days at average temperatures of 62°F.

> This document presents methods developed at the [[U.S. Fisheries Biological Station]] in [[Fairport, Iowa]] for successfully collecting and hatching eggs of buffalo-fishes (genus [[Ictiobus]]), addressing previous failures in artificial propagation of these commercially important food fishes.
