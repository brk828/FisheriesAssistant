# Crago

## Summary
Crago is a genus of shrimp that serves as a major food source for striped bass in San Francisco Bay and the San Joaquin River Delta. This study found Crago shrimp were particularly important during summer/fall, occurring in 35% of striped bass stomachs and representing 53% of identifiable stomach contents by volume, though their populations had declined significantly from approximately 2,500,000 pounds in the 1930s to 500,000-1,000,000 pounds by 1947-1948.
