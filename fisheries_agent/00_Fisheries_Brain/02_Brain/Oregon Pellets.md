# Oregon Pellets

## Summary
Oregon Pellets are a commercial moist refrigerated fish feed that had proven highly successful for trout and salmon culture by 1973. In this study, Oregon Pellets served as the control diet for comparison with experimental soybean-based feeds, containing 55% protein and achieving a mean feed conversion ratio of 0.81 with Channel Catfish.

## Context

> This 1973 study evaluated two experimental fish diets combining roasted soybeans with fresh [[Gizzard Shad]] (Dorosoma cepedianum) as alternatives to commercial [[Oregon Pellets]] for feeding yearling [[Channel Catfish]] (Ictalurus punctatus).
