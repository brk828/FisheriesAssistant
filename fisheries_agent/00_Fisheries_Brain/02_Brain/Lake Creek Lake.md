# Lake Creek Lake

## Summary
Lake Creek Lake was a 1.8-acre spring-fed lake near Saratoga, Wyoming used to demonstrate antimycin A's non-repellency to fish. Clear water allowed direct observation showing that brook trout and northern creek chubs made no escape attempts into spring flows when exposed to 10 ppb, with complete kill achieved and the toxicant flushing from the basin within 48 hours.
