# Canyon Refugia

## Summary
Canyon reaches with deep pools, eddies, and spring inputs provided critical refugia for native fishes during drought periods by maintaining perennial flow. During drought, intermittency in valley reaches forced fish to concentrate in canyon pools fed by groundwater seepage through alluvial reservoirs, making these refugia essential for population persistence.
