# Labridae

## Summary
Labridae is the wrasse family, represented at this site by the California sheephead (Pimelometopon pulchrum). Sixteen bones from California sheephead were identified in the assemblage, including distinctive pharyngeal bones, making it one of the more abundant species recovered.
