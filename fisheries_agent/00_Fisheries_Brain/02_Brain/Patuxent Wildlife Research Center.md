# Patuxent Wildlife Research Center

## Summary
Patuxent Wildlife Research Center is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service research facility that conducted wildlife pesticide studies as part of this 1964 report. The center established experimental colonies for reproduction studies, including a sparrow hawk colony of 42 pairs, and conducted extensive toxicity testing and analytical methodology development for pesticides like DDT and dieldrin.
