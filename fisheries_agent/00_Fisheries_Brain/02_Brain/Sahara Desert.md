# Sahara Desert

## Summary
The Sahara Desert is a major African arid region that during Pleistocene pluvial periods supported long permanent streams including the Saora, Ighorghor, and Tafasset rivers. This source reports that tropical fish Chromys and catfish Clarias lazera currently inhabit Sahara water holes, and Zambezian fauna reached the Atlas Mountains, indicating dramatically wetter past conditions.
