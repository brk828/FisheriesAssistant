# River Yare

## Summary
The River Yare is a hard-water stream in southern England included in Edwards' comparative study of macrophyte productivity. Net productivity measurements in the Yare fell within the range of 1.44-2.30 g organic carbon/m²/day observed across the four rivers studied.
