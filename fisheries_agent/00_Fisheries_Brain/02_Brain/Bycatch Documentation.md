# Bycatch Documentation

## Summary
Bycatch documentation tracks non-target species captured during Northern Pike suppression to ensure minimal impacts on native fish communities. The 2022 suppression captured 5,799 individuals of 19 species including Yellow Perch (46.56%), invasive Tench (19.68%), Pumpkinseed (11.33%), and native salmonids, with bycatch survival rates exceeding 90%.
