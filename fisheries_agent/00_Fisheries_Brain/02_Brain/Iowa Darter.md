# Iowa Darter

## Summary
The Iowa Darter is a small freshwater fish species documented in mid-20th century ichthyological studies as a component of lake fish communities, particularly in standardized population surveys and fish control experiments. Based on its appearance in historical fisheries research examining community composition and fish toxicant applications, it represents one of the minor constituent species in temperate freshwater lake ecosystems, typically occurring alongside dominant species like bluegill sunfish in structured aquatic habitats. The species' presence in these scientific studies reflects its ecological significance as an indicator organism in comprehensive fish population assessments and management practices.
