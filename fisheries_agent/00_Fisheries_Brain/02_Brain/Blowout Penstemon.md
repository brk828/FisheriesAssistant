# Blowout Penstemon

## Summary
Blowout Penstemon is a proposed threatened or endangered plant species that depends on insect pollination. The conference report established a 3-mile buffer zone excluding aerial ultra-low-volume pesticide application around populations to protect the plant's pollinators, or alternatively required use of carbaryl bait or Nosema biological control methods.
