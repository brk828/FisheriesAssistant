# Biotechnical Group

## Summary
The Biotechnical Group (BTG) is a component of the Colorado River endangered fish recovery program that developed and implements a research prioritization process to evaluate and direct research needs for endangered fishes during recovery implementation. According to this source, the BTG's process solicits project ideas and outlines from field biologists and researchers, then rates submissions using specific criteria to produce numerical rankings, though in its second year of use (1989) there was confusion among contributors about the appropriate level of detail required and concerns about limited feedback regarding how the prioritization influenced ongoing and new research in the work plan.
