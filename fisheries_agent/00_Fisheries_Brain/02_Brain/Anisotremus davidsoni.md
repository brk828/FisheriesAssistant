# Anisotremus davidsoni

## Summary
Anisotremus davidsoni is the Sargo, a marine fish species. It was one of 33 marine fish species introduced to the Salton Sea between 1948 and 1956, and became one of only three species that successfully established populations in this saline inland body of water.
