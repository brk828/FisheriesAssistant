# Scott And Crossman 1973

## Summary
Scott and Crossman 1973 is a foundational taxonomic and ecological reference work on North American freshwater fishes that provides comprehensive species descriptions, identification keys, and distributional information widely used in fisheries science and aquatic ecology research. This seminal publication serves as a standard taxonomic authority for fish species identification and classification in studies examining fish populations, community structure, and ecological interactions across North American aquatic systems. Researchers frequently cite this work when establishing baseline biological and distributional data for fish species involved in abundance surveys, competitive dynamics studies, and conservation assessments.
