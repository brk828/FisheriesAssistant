# Salmo Gairdneri

## Summary
Salmo gairdneri, commonly known as the rainbow trout, is a coldwater fish species native to North American Pacific coastal streams and rivers that has become one of the most economically important and widely cultured salmonid species globally. In fisheries science and conservation contexts, this species is significant both as a wild population of ecological importance in its native range and as a heavily stocked species in aquaculture and recreational fisheries worldwide. Its use in assimilation efficiency studies and ecological research has made it a model organism for understanding salmonid nutrition, energy dynamics, and habitat requirements in coldwater aquatic ecosystems.
