# Cheumatopsyche

## Summary
Cheumatopsyche is a genus of caddisflies in the family Hydropsychidae that forms a diverse and abundant element of southeastern United States freshwater fauna. This source describes two new species from the region: C. enigma, which exhibits unusual morphological variability in male genitalia possibly indicating a hybrid zone between formerly isolated populations, and C. petersi, which is closely related to C. analis but distinguished by differences in clasper structure and tenth tergite morphology.
