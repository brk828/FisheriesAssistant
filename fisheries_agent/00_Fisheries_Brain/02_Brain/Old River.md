# Old River

## Summary
Old River is a 4,100-acre oxbow lake in Louisiana that experiences annual flooding from the Mississippi River for an average of 300 days per year when river stages exceed 13.8 feet msl, with an average depth of 15 feet. This study found that largemouth bass in Old River grew significantly faster and heavier than those in nearby False River, with superior growth attributed to nutrient replenishment from flooding, food concentration during water level fluctuations, and higher overall productivity (533.8 pounds per acre standing crop). The annual flood-drawdown cycle creates pulses of invertebrate food production and concentrates prey during drawdown periods, enhancing predation efficiency.
