# Gleason Flat

## Summary
Gleason Flat is a location on the Upper Salt River in Arizona, approximately 34 river miles upstream from Roosevelt Diversion Dam. According to this assessment, recent electrofishing surveys indicated that native fish species were almost completely displaced by non-native species below Gleason Flat, with large biomass of carp and catfish replacing native fishes in downstream reaches.

## Context

> This biological assessment evaluates potential impacts of removing the [[Roosevelt Diversion Dam]] on the [[Salt River]], Arizona.
