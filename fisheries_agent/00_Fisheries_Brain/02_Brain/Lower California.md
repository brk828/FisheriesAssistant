# Lower California

## Summary
Lower California (Baja California) is the location where Lucania browni was discovered, specifically in a hot spring on the eastern margin of a salt lake between the Coast Range and Cocopah Range in the northeastern part of the region. The collection site was approximately 40 miles south of the international border and below sea level.
