# Wickenburg, Arizona

## Summary
Wickenburg, Arizona is the location of the Hassayampa River Preserve and Palm Lake, where this 1993 management meeting took place. The area was the focus of collaborative habitat management efforts between the Arizona Game and Fish Department, The Nature Conservancy, and U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service for native fish conservation and water quality monitoring.
