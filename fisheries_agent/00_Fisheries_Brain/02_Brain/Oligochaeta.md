# Oligochaeta

## Summary
Oligochaeta are a group of sedentary aquatic invertebrates, particularly oligochaete worms, that serve as valuable bioindicators for water quality assessment and pollution monitoring in freshwater ecosystems. Unlike mobile organisms such as fish, oligochaetes are relatively immobile and persistent in their habitat, making them more reliable indicators of local environmental conditions and contaminant exposure. Their presence, absence, or community composition in sediments and water bodies provides continuous, meaningful information about ecosystem health and the effects of pollution that complements or supersedes traditional fish-based toxicity testing approaches.
