# Cyanophyta

## Summary
Cyanophyta, also known as blue-green algae or cyanobacteria, are photosynthetic microorganisms in the phytoplankton community. In Utah Lake, Cyanophyta densities have not declined despite Common Carp biomass reduction, and cyanobacteria blooms (sometimes toxic) continue to occur, indicating persistent water quality challenges for June Sucker recovery.
