# Broeker and Orr

## Summary
Broeker and Orr (1958) estimated that pluvial conditions in the Mono Basin would have required an 8-inch rainfall increase combined with a 9°F temperature drop. Their model represented one approach to calculating the climatic changes needed for Lake Russell's expansion.
