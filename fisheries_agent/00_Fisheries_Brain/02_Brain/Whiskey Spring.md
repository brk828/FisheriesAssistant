# Whiskey Spring

## Summary
Whiskey Spring is a desert spring in the Grand Wash area of Arizona that serves as the sole known habitat for Grand Wash Fontelicella, an endemic hydrobioid snail. The spring was documented in the 1981 mollusk status assessment as supporting a rare species.
