# Adobe Creek

## Summary
Adobe Creek is a site in the Gunnison River system being studied for selenium tolerance. Along with Walter Walker, selenium tolerance studies were being conducted at Adobe Creek to assess whether the Gunnison River has restoration potential given concerns about selenium contamination in the system.
