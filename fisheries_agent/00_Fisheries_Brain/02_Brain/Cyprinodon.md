# Cyprinodon

## Summary
Cyprinodon is a genus of small, euryhaline cyprinodont fishes (pupfishes) widely distributed across arid and semi-arid regions of North America, particularly in isolated desert springs, salt lakes, and hypersaline environments where they exhibit remarkable physiological adaptations to extreme salinity and temperature fluctuations. These fish are of significant conservation concern due to their restricted geographic ranges and vulnerability to habitat loss, making them important model organisms for studying genetic variation, population dynamics, and evolutionary adaptation in isolated aquatic systems. Cyprinodon species are frequently used in ichthyological research to understand ecosystem responses to environmental stress and to inform recovery strategies for endangered desert fish populations.
