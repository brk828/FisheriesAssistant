# Chrysophyta

## Summary
Chrysophyta are diatoms, a group of algae that serve as aquatic primary producers. This source reports that Chrysophyta have a mean calorific value of 4923 cal/g ash-free dry weight, which is higher than other algal groups like green algae (4321 cal/g) and blue-green algae (4600 cal/g).
