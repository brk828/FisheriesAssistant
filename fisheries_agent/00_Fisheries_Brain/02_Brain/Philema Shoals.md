# Philema Shoals

## Summary
Philema Shoals is a 2.5-river-kilometer spawning aggregation area in the lower Flint River, Georgia. This source reports that the majority of telemetered Shoal Bass occupied this site during spawning season (late March to early May), with population estimates ranging from 87 to 181 adults across three sampling periods in 2011.
