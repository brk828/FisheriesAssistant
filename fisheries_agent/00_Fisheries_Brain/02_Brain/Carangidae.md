# Carangidae

## Summary
Carangidae is a family of marine and estuarine fishes commonly known as jacks and scads, characterized by their typically streamlined bodies, forked tails, and pelagic habits. Members of this family are notable for their obligate schooling behavior, as exemplified by species such as the bar jack (Caranx ruber) and bigeye scad (Selar crumenophthalmus), which have been studied to understand the sensory-motor mechanisms underlying fish aggregation and coordinated swimming. As economically and ecologically important forage and game fishes, Carangidae species are significant components of marine food webs and have relevance for both fisheries management and behavioral ecology research.
