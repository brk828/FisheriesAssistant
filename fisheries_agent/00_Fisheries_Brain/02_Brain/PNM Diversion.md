# PNM Diversion

## Summary
PNM Diversion is a location in the San Juan River system located 307 km upstream from the point of translocation above the Piute Farms Waterfall. One translocated razorback sucker from the 2016 cohort was detected at this site, representing the maximum upstream movement distance recorded for translocated fish in that year. This detection demonstrates the potential for extensive upstream movements by razorback suckers when barriers are bypassed through translocation.
