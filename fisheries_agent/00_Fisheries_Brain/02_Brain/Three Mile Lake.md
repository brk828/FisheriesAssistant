# Three Mile Lake

## Summary
Three Mile Lake is an existing water body at Imperial National Wildlife Refuge in Arizona being considered as protected habitat for endangered fish. The management plan lists this 20-acre lake as one of the potential sites under consideration in Arizona for establishing isolated backwater habitats to support bonytail chub and razorback sucker populations.
