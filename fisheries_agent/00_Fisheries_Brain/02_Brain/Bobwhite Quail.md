# Bobwhite Quail

## Summary
Bobwhite Quail is a ground-dwelling game bird extensively studied for pesticide effects in this source. The report documents immediate mortality and population declines following heptachlor and dieldrin treatments at 2 lb/acre in Alabama, with populations requiring two years to return to normal levels, and laboratory studies showing that kepone at 50 ppm completely inhibited reproduction while 1-25 ppm sharply reduced it with many chicks crippled or defective at hatching.
