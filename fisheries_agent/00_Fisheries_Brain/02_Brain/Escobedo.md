# Escobedo

## Summary
Escobedo is a locality in the Cuatro Cienegas basin where unconfirmed reports suggested Tilapia presence in 1988. Unlike other introduction sites, Escobedo is hydrologically connected via canal to Rio Puente Colorado, creating significant concern because this connectivity could facilitate basin-wide invasion of the introduced species into major river systems.
