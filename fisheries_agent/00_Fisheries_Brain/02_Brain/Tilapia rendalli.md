# Tilapia rendalli

## Summary
Tilapia rendalli is an African cichlid species tentatively identified in the Lower Colorado River Basin, distinguished from the similar Tilapia zilli by its unicolored dorsal and caudal fins with dark margins. This source reports that specimens tentatively identified as T. rendalli were collected near Blythe, California, representing one of the additional Tilapia species present in the region beyond the dominant hybridizing complexes.
