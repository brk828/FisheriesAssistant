# Oxnard

## Summary
Oxnard, California was the site of the Daryl Arnold Ranch where in 1953, five threespine sticklebacks emerged from a 1,300-foot deep well that had a flow rate of 1,150 gallons per minute. This was one of the deepest wells documented in the study of fish emergences from California's subterranean waters.
