# Lagunogammarus

## Summary
Lagunogammarus is a proposed genus for which Gammarus zaddachi is the type species, characterized by narrow oostegites. This source discusses whether the Gammarus-Pecos Complex might belong to this genus due to their exceptionally narrow, strap-like oostegites, but notes the complex differs from G. zaddachi in mandibular palp setation and other features, warranting possible future assignment to a separate subgenus or genus.
