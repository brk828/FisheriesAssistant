# Tilapia Aurea

## Summary
Tilapia aurea, commonly known as the blue tilapia, is a cichlid species native to Africa that has become an invasive species in various aquatic ecosystems worldwide, including the southwestern United States. This generalist species is particularly problematic in simple, species-poor ecosystems like those found in the Chihuahuan Desert, where it can outcompete native fauna and fundamentally alter trophic structures through its omnivorous feeding habits and competitive dominance. Its establishment in artificial pond systems and canal networks, such as those at the ASU Research Park linked to the Salt River Project, exemplifies how non-native fish species can rapidly colonize and spread through human-modified aquatic infrastructure.
