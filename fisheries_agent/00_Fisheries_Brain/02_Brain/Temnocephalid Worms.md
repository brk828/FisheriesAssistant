# Temnocephalid Worms

## Summary
Temnocephalid worms are commensal organisms found on crayfish. During 1967 field collections in New Zealand, virtually every crayfish examined was heavily infested with these worms while no ostracods were found, suggesting possible competitive exclusion between the two commensal organisms.
