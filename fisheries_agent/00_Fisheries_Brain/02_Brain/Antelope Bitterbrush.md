# Antelope Bitterbrush

## Summary
Antelope Bitterbrush (Purshia tridentata) is a plant species found in the Jeffrey Pine Forest community of the Mono Basin at elevations between 7,000 and 9,500 feet. It grows alongside Jeffrey Pine, Mountain Big Sagebrush, White Fir, and Quaking Aspen in this montane forest zone.
