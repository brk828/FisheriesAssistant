# Wayne N. Aspinall Unit

## Summary
The Wayne N. Aspinall Unit is one of four major storage units authorized under the Colorado River Storage Project Act of 1956, located on the Gunnison River in the upper Colorado River basin. This document identifies it as part of the upper basin development that contributed to habitat alterations affecting endangered native fishes, with nine major dams now lining the upper mainstem and tributaries.
