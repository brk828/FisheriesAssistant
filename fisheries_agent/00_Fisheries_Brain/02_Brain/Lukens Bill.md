# Lukens Bill

## Summary
The Lukens Bill (H.R. 3735) is a proposed RCRA reauthorization bill that would mandate state-implemented permit programs with separate regulations for industrial wastes, excluding inactive/abandoned facilities and activities regulated under other environmental statutes. This source identifies this bill as an alternative legislative approach to the Baucus Bill, with greater emphasis on state rather than federal implementation.
