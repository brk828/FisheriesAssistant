# Armería River

## Summary
The Armería River is one of several rivers that have cut back into the Mesa Central plateau in Mexico. This source identifies it as part of the range where Goodeidae occur, contributing to the biogeographic distribution of the endemic fish fauna outside the main Lerma-Santiago drainage.
