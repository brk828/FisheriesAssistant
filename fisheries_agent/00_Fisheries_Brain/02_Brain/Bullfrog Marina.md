# Bullfrog Marina

## Summary
Bullfrog Marina is one of four major access points on Lake Powell where creel census was conducted during this study. During the 1968-1969 study period, Bullfrog Marina accounted for 16% of total fishing pressure (5,180 angler trips producing 8,656 fish), representing a 73% increase from the previous year due to improved access roads and completion of marina facilities.
