# San Pedro Martir Trout

## Summary
The San Pedro Martir Trout is a remarkable fish subspecies endemic to the Rio San Rafael in the San Pedro Martir region of Baja California, Mexico. As of 1990, it was the subject of planned Ph.D. research by Gorgonio Ruiz under Salvador Contreras at la Universidad Autonoma de Nuevo León, and was featured in a field trip during the Desert Fishes Council's November 1990 symposium in Ensenada.
