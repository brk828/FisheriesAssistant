# American Merganser

## Summary
The American Merganser (Mergus merganser) is a fish-eating duck with distinctive sexual dimorphism—males have long white bodies with black backs and greenish-black heads, while females are gray with crested brownish-red heads. This Michigan study reports that mergansers breed in northern North America, remain throughout winter on open water in Michigan, nest in hollow trees or stumps near water, and females incubate 6-17 eggs for 28 days. The source notes they are normally found on Great Lakes bays and river-mouth lakes, being forced onto trout streams only during extreme freeze-ups.
