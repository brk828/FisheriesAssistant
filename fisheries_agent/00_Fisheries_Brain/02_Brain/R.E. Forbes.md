# R.E. Forbes

## Summary
R.E. Forbes was a professor who conducted the chemical analysis of the hot spring water where Lucania browni was found. His analysis revealed that the water contained 25% soluble salts total, including 2% common salt (NaCl), 0.5% calcium chloride, and trace amounts of lithium.
