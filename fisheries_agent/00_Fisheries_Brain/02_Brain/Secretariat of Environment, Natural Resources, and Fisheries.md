# Secretariat of Environment, Natural Resources, and Fisheries

## Summary
The Secretariat of Environment, Natural Resources, and Fisheries (SEMARNAP) is the Mexican government agency that signed a Letter of Intent with the U.S. Department of Interior on May 5, 1997. The agreement focused on binational cooperation and data collection for managing protected natural areas in the border region, with the Delta-Upper Gulf Biosphere Reserve as a pilot project.
