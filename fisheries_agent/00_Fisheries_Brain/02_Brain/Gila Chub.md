# Gila Chub

## Summary
The Gila Chub (*Gila intermedia*) is a small cyprinid endemic to Arizona that serves as a focal species in desert fish conservation and recovery efforts coordinated by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team. As an endangered species listed under the Endangered Species Act, the Gila Chub represents a critical conservation concern for maintaining native fish biodiversity in the arid southwestern United States, with recovery planning efforts focused on habitat restoration and population management in its native river systems. The species exemplifies the challenges of conserving small-bodied desert fishes adapted to intermittent, temperature-variable streams that face threats from water diversion, habitat degradation, and introduced species competition.
