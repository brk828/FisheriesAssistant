# Oklahoma Fishery Research Laboratory

## Summary
The Oklahoma Fishery Research Laboratory was a state research institution that conducted fisheries surveys and management studies. This source documents their survey of commercial fish harvest in Oklahoma waters from 1958-1960, which tracked catch data from licensed commercial fishermen through monthly reports and personal interviews to determine the extent of commercially harvested fish in the state.
