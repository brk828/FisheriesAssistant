# Aminotriazole

## Summary
Aminotriazole is one of twelve herbicide active ingredients reviewed in this report for use in aquatic plant management. It was among the herbicides evaluated for toxicity and safety protocols in freshwater fishery management applications.

## Context

> This 1961 special report by Charles L. Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
