# San Joaquin River

## Summary
The San Joaquin River is one of California's major rivers that was fundamentally altered by modern water development. This source specifically describes how the closure of Friant Dam in 1945 deliberately extirpated spring-run chinook salmon from this river, with 50,000 fish present in 1946 and the last fish attempting migration in 1950, and notes it is now too modified for traditional conservation approaches.
