# Grand Canyon Trust

## Summary
The Grand Canyon Trust is an environmental advocacy organization that participated in stakeholder discussions about Glen Canyon Dam operations. At this 1989 meeting, the Trust advocated for immediate initiation of the NEPA process and questioned the legitimacy of conducting environmental studies without an Environmental Impact Statement framework already in place.
