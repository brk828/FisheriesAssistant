# San Bernardino Creek

## Summary
San Bernardino Creek is a small tributary stream within the Rio Yaqui drainage basin located in extreme southeastern Arizona and northern Sonora, Mexico, that serves as an important reference system for documenting changes in native fish fauna and freshwater habitat in arid southwestern North America. This creek represents one of the key study sites in ichthyological surveys examining the historical distribution and current status of endemic and native fish species across the Rio Yaqui basin, particularly regarding habitat loss and hydrological changes such as the decline of perennial flow in desert aquatic ecosystems. Its conservation significance lies in its role as a focal point for understanding biodiversity patterns and threats to native fish assemblages in one of the most ecologically sensitive and water-limited regions of the southwestern United States and Mexico.
