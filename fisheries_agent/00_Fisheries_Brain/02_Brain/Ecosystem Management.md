# Ecosystem Management

## Summary
Ecosystem Management in fish science and aquatic conservation refers to the integrated approach of managing fisheries and wildlife resources by balancing multiple, often competing objectives including ecological diversity maintenance, endangered species protection, and sustainable consumptive uses such as fishing and hunting. This concept emphasizes the need for coordinated decision-making across jurisdictions and agencies—particularly between federal and state authorities—to maintain healthy aquatic ecosystems while accommodating diverse stakeholder interests and resource utilization practices.
