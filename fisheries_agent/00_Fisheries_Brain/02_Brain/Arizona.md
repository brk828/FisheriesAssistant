# Arizona

## Summary
Arizona represents a critical geographic region within USFWS Region 2 for freshwater and riverine fisheries management, encompassing both native warmwater cyprinid systems and altered coldwater habitats created by major dams like Hoover Dam. The state's fisheries science and conservation efforts address the tension between managing introduced coldwater species like Rainbow Trout in tailwater environments and protecting endemic native fish species such as the Virgin Chub and other Gila River basin endemics that face extinction. Arizona's network of federal fisheries field stations and mitigation hatcheries reflect the complex management challenges of maintaining viable sport fisheries while conserving the region's unique aquatic biodiversity in the face of substantial hydrological alterations.
