# Sri Lanka

## Summary
Sri Lanka is a location where Moyle conducted comparative studies on rainforest stream fish assemblages during the 1980s. His research documented resource partitioning among stream fishes and identified conservation priorities for Sri Lankan freshwater fishes facing habitat loss and introduced species threats.
