# Santee Pond

## Summary
Santee Pond was a location where hybrid tilapia were stocked and later examined for stomach contents. Analysis of hybrids from this pond revealed consumption of dragonfly and damselfly larvae, water boatmen adults, mayfly larvae, amphipods, and other insect parts, demonstrating their opportunistic invertivory.
