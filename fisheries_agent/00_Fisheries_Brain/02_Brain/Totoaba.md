# Totoaba

## Summary
The Totoaba is an endangered large fish species native to the Gulf of California and Colorado River Delta region that has become a focal point in conservation efforts for southwestern North American aquatic ecosystems. Historically found in estuarine and freshwater habitats at the mouth of the Colorado River, the species has experienced severe population declines due to habitat loss, water management practices, and other anthropogenic pressures. As a "big-river" specialist fish, the totoaba is often discussed alongside other critically endangered Colorado River endemic fishes in biological assessments and interagency conservation planning regarding lower Colorado River management and restoration.
