# Bass

## Summary
Bass refers to a group of freshwater game fish species, including largemouth bass, smallmouth bass, rock bass, and striped bass, that are ecologically and economically important in North American aquatic systems and commonly targeted in fisheries management and restoration programs. In the context of conservation biology and fisheries science, bass populations are managed through restocking initiatives, habitat assessment, and population monitoring protocols, often grouped within "game fish guilds" that receive targeted management attention alongside other sport fish species. These species serve as key indicators of ecosystem health and are central to both recreational fisheries and aquatic community restoration efforts in freshwater and estuarine environments.
