# Riverside County

## Summary
Riverside County is a county in southern California where the first known California record of Pumpkinseed was documented in 1939 from an artesian-fed reservoir on the Wise Ranch near Mecca at the northeast corner of the Salton Sea. The original population at this site was believed extirpated by 1948, and several secondary plantings from this source failed to establish permanent populations.
