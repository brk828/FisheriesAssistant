# Roosevelt National Forest

## Summary
The Roosevelt National Forest is a U.S. Forest Service managed area in Colorado where water projects required special use permit renewals. Along with projects in Arapaho National Forest, these permit renewals in the 1990s triggered ESA Section 7 consultation requirements that helped drive the development of the Central Platte River Recovery Program.
