# Inland Fisheries Informational Leaflets

## Summary
The Inland Fisheries Informational Leaflets are reference materials published by the California Department of Fish and Game that provide regulatory information for fish breeders and bait fish operators. According to this source, Leaflets Numbers 7 and 8 specifically cover regulations governing the capture, possession, transportation, and sale of live freshwater bait fish, and are available from regional offices.

## Context

> This 1956 administrative report (revised 1960) by the [[California Department of Fish and Game]] provides comprehensive guidance on commercial bait minnow culture in California.
