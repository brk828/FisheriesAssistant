# Lake Almanor State Fish Hatchery

## Summary
The Lake Almanor State Fish Hatchery is a fish hatchery facility located on Clear Creek in Lassen County, California. In this study, it served as the location where approximately 20,000 artificially fertilized Tui Chub eggs from Eagle Lake were transported and maintained for developmental observation, with eggs arriving at the facility around noon on June 24, 1949, about four hours after collection.
