# Lake St. Marys

## Summary
Lake St. Marys is a body of water in Ohio that served as both the location and water source for experimental creek chub propagation ponds. The experimental ponds were constructed from old barrow pits along the lake's east bank, and lake water was supplied to the ponds through a gravity pipe system for the 1941-1942 studies at State Fish Farm No. 1.
