# Spawning Habitat

## Summary
Northern Pike spawning habitat in Box Canyon Reservoir consists of shallow (<2 m depth) flooded areas with aquatic macrophytes and seasonally inundated terrestrial vegetation in sloughs, bays, and backwaters. These slough habitats warm up to 5°C above main-stem temperatures in April, reaching optimal spawning temperatures of 8-12°C, with spawning occurring March-early May and five productive slough sites (4.7-71.0 ha) accounting for 61% of total Northern Pike catch.
