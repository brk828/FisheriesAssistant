# Low Impact Hydropower

## Summary
Low Impact Hydropower is a certification program developed collaboratively by American Rivers and Green Mountain Energy Resources over 18 months to identify environmentally preferable hydropower operations in restructured electricity markets. According to this source, the certification establishes six criteria (fish protection, satisfactory flows, water quality preservation, flooded habitat mitigation, cultural resource protection, and recreational opportunity) that defer primarily to Resource Agency recommendations, with testing on 61 facilities showing 69% would pass without land mitigation requirements. The certification is valid for one year with annual renewal and does not represent a ceiling for mitigation efforts in relicensing proceedings.
