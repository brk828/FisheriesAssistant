# Coordinated Land Management Plan

## Summary
The Coordinated Land Management Plan is a management document completed in 1993 for El Coronado Ranch that covers both private deeded lands and Forest Service allotment lands. This plan guides livestock grazing and land use practices across the 6,153-hectare ranch property in the West Turkey Creek watershed.
