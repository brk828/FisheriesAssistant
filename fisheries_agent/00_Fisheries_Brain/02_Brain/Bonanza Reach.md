# Bonanza Reach

## Summary
Bonanza Reach is a study site at river mile 45 on the White River with diverse substrate representing upper White River cobble-gravel reaches, located within 5 miles of a suspected spawning site. This 1982 study analyzed flows of 75-400 cfs and found relatively diverse substrate resulted in significant habitat for all life stages, with YOY habitat quite high above 100 cfs peaking at 200-300 cfs, adult habitat slightly higher than YOY and increasing with flow, and spawning habitat higher than any other White River site, increasing to a very high percentage of total surface area at 400 cfs.
