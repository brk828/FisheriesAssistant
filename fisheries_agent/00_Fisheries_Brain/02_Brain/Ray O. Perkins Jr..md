# Ray O. Perkins Jr.

## Summary
Ray O. Perkins Jr. was the Engineering Branch Supervisor at the Arizona Department of Water Resources who authored the December 4, 1984 memorandum concluding the Leslie Canyon Dam project. He stated that construction would not be feasible from a hydrologic standpoint because the watershed yield would not support a viable, permanent pool for recreational purposes.
