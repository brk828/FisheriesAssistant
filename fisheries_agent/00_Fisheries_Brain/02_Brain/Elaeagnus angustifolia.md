# Elaeagnus angustifolia

## Summary
Elaeagnus angustifolia, commonly known as Russian olive, is an invasive riparian plant species. In the San Juan River, this species along with Tamarix (salt cedar) has confined, narrowed, and reduced channel braiding, altering the river's physical habitat structure.
