# Pyramid Lake Tribe

## Summary
The Pyramid Lake Tribe is a Native American tribe associated with Pyramid Lake, Nevada, where the endangered cui-ui is endemic. According to this 1990 recovery team meeting document, the Tribe's attorney Bob Pelcyger and consultant Ali Shahroody were scheduled to participate in discussions about Endangered Species Act provisions related to the cui-ui recovery plan, indicating the Tribe's direct involvement in conservation efforts for this culturally and ecologically important species.

## Context

> This document is correspondence from Tom Strekal, Team Leader, scheduling a [[Cui-ui]] Recovery Team meeting for November 29, 1990, in Reno, Nevada.
