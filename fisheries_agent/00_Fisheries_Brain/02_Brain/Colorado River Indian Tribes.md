# Colorado River Indian Tribes

## Summary
The Colorado River Indian Tribes refers to a sovereign Native American nation with treaty rights to Colorado River water and aquatic resources, whose Department of Fish and Game plays an active role in the management and conservation of endangered fish species in the Lower Colorado River Basin. As a stakeholder in federal recovery programs for species such as razorback suckers and bonytail chubs, the Tribes participate in habitat restoration, fish propagation, and species reintroduction efforts within their reservation boundaries and the broader Lower Colorado River ecosystem. Their involvement reflects both their legal authority over tribal waters and their commitment to restoring native fish populations historically affected by dam operations and habitat modification.
