# Lepisosteus platyrhynchus

## Summary
Lepisosteus platyrhynchus, commonly known as Florida gar, is a freshwater fish species that serves as the host for the newly described parasite Argulus meehani. Specimens parasitized by A. meehani were collected from Royal Palm Pond in Everglades National Park, Florida, and the parasite's effects on this host were documented in detail by Kolipinski in 1969.
