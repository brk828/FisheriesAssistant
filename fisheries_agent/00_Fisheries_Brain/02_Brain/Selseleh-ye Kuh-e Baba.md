# Selseleh-ye Kuh-e Baba

## Summary
Selseleh-ye Kuh-e Baba is one of the central mountain ranges in Afghanistan from which surface water originates through precipitation and snowmelt. It is part of the mountainous system that shapes the country's drainage patterns and fish fauna.
