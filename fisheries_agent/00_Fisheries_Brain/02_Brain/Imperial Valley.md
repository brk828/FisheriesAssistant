# Imperial Valley

## Summary
The Imperial Valley is an agricultural region in California that was developed through Colorado River irrigation beginning in 1900, with 10,000 settlers moving onto 100,000 acres by 1904. Under the salinity agreement negotiated through the IBWC, Mexico's water was guaranteed to have the same salinity as water delivered to the Imperial Valley.
