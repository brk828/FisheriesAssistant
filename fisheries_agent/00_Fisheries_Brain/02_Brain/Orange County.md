# Orange County

## Summary
Orange County is a county in southern California where Pumpkinseed were reportedly planted in Irvine Lake (Santiago Reservoir) on November 15, 1948, as part of a mixed warmwater fish shipment from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility in Dexter, New Mexico. The identification was based on field observation only with no specimens retained, and no subsequent records were documented.
