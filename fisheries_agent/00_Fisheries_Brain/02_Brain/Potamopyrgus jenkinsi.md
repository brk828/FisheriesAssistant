# Potamopyrgus jenkinsi

## Summary
Potamopyrgus jenkinsi is a freshwater snail that affects oxygen dynamics through its feeding activities. At densities of 50,000/m², these snails produce up to 140 g dry faeces/m²/day, altering decomposition processes and sediment characteristics in the streams where they occur.
