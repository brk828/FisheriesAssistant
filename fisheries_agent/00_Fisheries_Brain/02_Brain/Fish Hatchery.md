# Fish Hatchery

## Summary
A fish hatchery is a specialized facility designed for the artificial propagation and rearing of fish species, typically operated by government agencies or conservation organizations to support fisheries management, species restoration, or conservation objectives. These facilities control breeding conditions, incubation, and early rearing of fish in captive environments before releasing juveniles into natural waters to supplement wild populations, enhance commercial fisheries, or restore depleted stocks. Fish hatcheries serve as critical tools in aquatic resource management, enabling scientists and managers to increase fish populations and maintain genetic diversity in species facing natural recruitment limitations or environmental pressures.
