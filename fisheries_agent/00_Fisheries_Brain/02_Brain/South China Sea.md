# South China Sea

## Summary
The South China Sea is one of the open waters surrounding the Sulu Sea complex, connected to it by straits 350-550 meters deep. This source reports that unlike the Sulu Sea, the South China Sea shows continued decline in bottom temperature with increasing depth, dropping below 7°C at depths greater than 950 meters.
