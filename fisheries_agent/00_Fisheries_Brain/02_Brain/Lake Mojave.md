# Lake Mojave

## Summary
Lake Mojave is a major reservoir on the lower Colorado River that serves as the current primary stronghold for endangered native fish species, particularly the bonytail chub (Gila elegans), one of the most critically endangered fishes in the Colorado River Basin. As a managed impoundment, Lake Mojave represents a key refuge population for conservation efforts aimed at preventing the extinction of endemic Colorado River fishes, though its artificial nature and management constraints present both opportunities and challenges for fish recovery programs in the lower basin.
