# Warm Springs

## Summary
Warm Springs is one of three current refugia for the Owens Pupfish, consisting of a small pond formed by a single dam and fed by a thermal spring, located on Department of Water and Power property north of Big Pine adjacent to the White Mountains. While it has been free from introduced fish predators, crayfish and mosquitofish have required control measures, with mosquitofish successfully eradicated in 1979 but sporadically reappearing through illegal introductions.
