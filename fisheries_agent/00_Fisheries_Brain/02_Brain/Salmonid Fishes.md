# Salmonid Fishes

## Summary
Salmonid fishes are a group of coldwater fish species including salmon and trout that are susceptible to a cold-water form of columnaris disease. This source indicates that the cold-water form of columnaris may be present in salmon and trout hatcheries year-round, and historical research documented systemic infections in several species of Pacific salmon, rainbow trout, and steelhead trout.
