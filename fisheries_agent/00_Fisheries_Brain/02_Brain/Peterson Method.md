# Peterson Method

## Summary
The Peterson Method is a mark-recapture technique for estimating animal population size based on the proportion of marked individuals recaptured in subsequent sampling. This study used the Chapman-modified Peterson method to calculate annual population estimates for razorback suckers in Lake Mohave, though the authors noted that the condition (M + C) > N* was not met in any year, resulting in negative bias and underestimation of actual population size.
