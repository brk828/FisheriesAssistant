# Dieldrin

## Summary
Dieldrin is a persistent organochlorine pesticide that bioaccumulates in aquatic food webs and poses significant toxicological risks to fish and other aquatic organisms. As a chlorinated hydrocarbon compound related to DDT, dieldrin is highly lipophilic and resistant to degradation, resulting in its accumulation in fish tissues over time and biomagnification through successive trophic levels. Its presence in freshwater and marine environments has been a major concern in fish conservation and aquatic ecology, particularly regarding impacts on fish populations and ecosystem health.
