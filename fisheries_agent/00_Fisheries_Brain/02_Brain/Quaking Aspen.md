# Quaking Aspen

## Summary
Quaking Aspen (Populus tremuloides) is a deciduous tree species found in the Jeffrey Pine Forest community of the Mono Basin at elevations of 7,000 to 9,500 feet. It grows in association with Jeffrey Pine, Mountain Big Sagebrush, Antelope Bitterbrush, and White Fir.
