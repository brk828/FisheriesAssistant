# Cibola National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Cibola National Wildlife Refuge is a protected area along the Lower Colorado River that serves as critical habitat and refugia for endangered native fish species, particularly the Bonytail Chub and Razorback Sucker, providing essential backwater and marsh ecosystems for both juvenile and adult fish recovery. The refuge has been utilized by fisheries management agencies as a site for hatchery-reared fish stocking and monitoring programs as part of broader conservation efforts to restore populations of Colorado River Basin endemics. Its isolated habitats and slow-water conditions make it valuable for both research on fish growth and survival and for maintaining genetic diversity of endangered species through managed propagation and reintroduction initiatives.
