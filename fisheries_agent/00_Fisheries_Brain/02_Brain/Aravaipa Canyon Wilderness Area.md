# Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness Area

## Summary
Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness Area is a protected perennial desert stream ecosystem in the Gila River Basin of Arizona that serves as a critical conservation site for native fish community monitoring and aquatic habitat assessment. The wilderness area and its associated Aravaipa Creek have been the subject of systematic ichthyological surveys since the 1990s to track fish populations and environmental conditions across longitudinal gradients, providing essential baseline data for understanding desert stream ecology and evaluating anthropogenic impacts on native species. This designation and monitoring program reflect the area's significance as a relatively intact southwestern riparian system worthy of long-term scientific study and resource management coordination among agencies including the Arizona Game and Fish Department, Bureau of Land Management, and The Nature Conservancy.
