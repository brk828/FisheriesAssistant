# Pinacate

## Summary
The Pinacate is a volcanic area in the Sonoran Desert region that holds particular cultural significance as sacred land to the Tohono O'odham Nation and was historically part of their homeland. A 1988 symposium on the Pinacate area, co-sponsored by Friends of PRONATURA and the Sonora-Arizona Commission, led to the formation of partnerships that eventually created the International Sonoran Desert Alliance.
