# Pluvial Period

## Summary
The Pluvial Period refers to a time of greatly increased precipitation that accompanied the last glaciation (Wisconsin time), characterized by extensive lakes in the Great Basin, full-flowing rivers, and vastly greater precipitation with much reduced evaporation compared to present conditions. Evidence includes pluvial lake shorelines, establishment of fish populations requiring permanent water, and physiographic features indicating abundant moisture, with rapid desiccation occurring in Postglacial time.
