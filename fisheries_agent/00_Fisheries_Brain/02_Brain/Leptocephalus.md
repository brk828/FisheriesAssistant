# Leptocephalus

## Summary
Leptocephalus is a transparent, elongate larval stage through which all eels pass during their development in marine waters. These larvae have compressed, glassy-clear bodies and commonly reach lengths exceeding six inches, with one documented five-foot-long larva thought to represent a gigantic eel species.
