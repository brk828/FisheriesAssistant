# Commission on National Parks and Protected Areas

## Summary
The Commission on National Parks and Protected Areas (CNPPA) is one of the six voluntary networks within IUCN - The World Conservation Union, operating alongside the Species Survival Commission. During the 1994-1996 triennium, SSC launched a major initiative to develop National Biodiversity Groups in association with CNPPA, bringing together members from both commissions and other IUCN networks to strengthen conservation coordination at the national level.
