# Gila Cypha

## Summary
Gila cypha, commonly known as the humpback chub, is an endemic cyprinid fish of the Colorado River basin that has become a focal species in western aquatic conservation and recovery efforts. This species is recognized for its distinctive morphological features, including a pronounced hump behind its head and specialized gill raker adaptations, and it has been the subject of intensive management, propagation, and population monitoring programs due to its endangered status. As a key indicator species for Colorado River ecosystem health, Gila cypha research has informed broader understanding of how large-scale water management, river rehabilitation projects, and species recovery protocols affect native desert fish communities.
