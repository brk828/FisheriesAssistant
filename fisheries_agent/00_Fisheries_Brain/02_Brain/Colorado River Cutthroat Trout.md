# Colorado River Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
Colorado River Cutthroat Trout is a native salmonid species historically endemic to the Colorado River drainage basin, including tributaries such as those in the Strawberry Reservoir system in Utah, where it represents an important component of the region's native fish assemblage. This species has become a significant focus of conservation biology and fisheries management efforts, with federal hatcheries like Saratoga National Fish Hatchery involved in egg production and broodstock maintenance to support restoration and reintroduction programs. The species exemplifies tensions in aquatic ecosystem management between preserving native fish communities and pursuing recreational fisheries development, as evidenced by scientific controversy over restoration projects that prioritize introduced species over the recovery of native cutthroat populations.
