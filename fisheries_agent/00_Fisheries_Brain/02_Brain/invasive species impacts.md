# Invasive Species Impacts

## Summary
Invasive species impacts refer to the ecological and biological consequences resulting from the introduction of non-native fish and aquatic organisms into freshwater ecosystems, where they compete with, prey upon, or otherwise displace native species, disrupt ecological balance, and degrade habitat quality and ecosystem health. These impacts are a major conservation threat to native freshwater fish populations, particularly in isolated or endemic species with limited ranges, and they interact synergistically with other stressors such as habitat degradation to compound declines in biodiversity and ecosystem function. Understanding and managing invasive species impacts is critical for protecting threatened freshwater fish communities and maintaining the integrity of aquatic ecosystems.
