# Warm Springs Rapid

## Summary
Warm Springs Rapid is a location in Yampa Canyon at river kilometer 6.56 that marks the downstream boundary of the primary Colorado Squawfish spawning area. The lower 51.2 kilometers of the Yampa River, particularly from Warm Springs Rapid upstream to the vicinity of Harding Hole, serves as critical spawning habitat where fish deposit and fertilize eggs on cobble bars.
