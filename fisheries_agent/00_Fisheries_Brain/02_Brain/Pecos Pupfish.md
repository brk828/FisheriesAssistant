# Pecos Pupfish

## Summary
The Pecos Pupfish (*Cyprinodon pecosensis*) is a small cyprinodont endemic to the Pecos River drainage in New Mexico and Texas that was proposed for federal listing under the Endangered Species Act due to severe habitat loss, fragmentation, and threats from water management practices and invasive species. As a pupfish species, it represents a group of highly specialized desert fishes adapted to isolated spring and river systems, making populations particularly vulnerable to environmental disturbance and extinction. Conservation efforts have focused on habitat protection and population management across multiple isolated populations to prevent the species' extinction, reflecting broader conservation strategies for endemic freshwater fish in arid southwestern aquatic ecosystems.
