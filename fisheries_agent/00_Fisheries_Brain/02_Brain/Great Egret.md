# Great Egret

## Summary
Great Egrets were among the predatory bird species observed at Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge that contributed to heavy predation pressure on fish populations. Along with American White Pelicans, Great Blue Herons, and various duck species, these birds were identified as one of the factors causing high mortality in stocked fish.
