# Eleotridae

## Summary
Eleotridae is a family of small to medium-sized goby-like fishes found primarily in freshwater and brackish aquatic systems, though some members exhibit euryhaline tolerance and can inhabit saline environments. Members of this family are distributed across various freshwater habitats including riverine systems, tributaries, and isolated spring ecosystems, with numerous species showing restricted endemic ranges in specialized habitats. In conservation and ecological contexts, Eleotridae are significant as indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and are often studied in drainage-specific biodiversity surveys due to their habitat specificity and vulnerability to environmental degradation.
