# Three Fords Canyon

## Summary
Three Fords Canyon is a location on the Green River in the Colorado River Basin. In this genetic study of endangered Colorado Squawfish, it was one of the sampling sites where fish were collected from the Green River system (river miles 33.7 to 260.5) for biochemical genetic analysis.

## Context

> This draft final report presents a comprehensive biochemical genetic analysis of [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), a large river cyprinid listed as endangered in 1967. The study examined 833 individuals (151 wild adults, 64 juveniles, 478 young-of-year, and 140 hatchery-reared fish)...
