# San Bernardino Valley

## Summary
San Bernardino Valley is located south of San Simon Valley, with San Simon Valley extending northward from the head of San Bernardino Valley starting about 20 miles north of the Mexican boundary. This source uses it as a geographic reference point for defining the southern extent of the study area.
