# Single-leaf Pinyon

## Summary
Single-leaf Pinyon (Pinus monophylla) is a pine species characteristic of the Pinyon-Juniper Woodland community in the Mono Basin. It grows at variable elevations between 4,000 and 8,000+ feet alongside Utah Juniper, Big Sagebrush, and Black Sagebrush, and its nuts were an important food resource for the Northern Paiutes.
