# Stygofauna

## Summary
Stygofauna refers to the specialized fauna inhabiting groundwater and subterranean aquatic environments, including caves, phreatic zones, and interstitial waters, characterized by adaptations to darkness, stable temperatures, and limited food resources. In fish science and conservation biology, stygofauna represents an important but often overlooked component of aquatic biodiversity, encompassing not only fish but also crustaceans, copepods, and other organisms that serve as ecological indicators of groundwater quality and ecosystem health. The study of stygofauna is crucial for understanding subterranean ecosystems and conserving these isolated, vulnerable communities that are increasingly threatened by groundwater contamination and habitat modification.
