# Arroyo Chub

## Summary
The Arroyo Chub (Gila orcuttii) is a small cyprinid fish endemic to southern California coastal drainages, particularly in the Los Angeles and San Diego regions, where it inhabits small streams, arroyos, and springs in fragmented habitats. As a species of special concern in California, the Arroyo Chub exemplifies the conservation challenges facing endemic freshwater fishes in the state, facing threats from habitat degradation, water diversions, non-native species competition, and the isolation of its naturally fragmented populations. Its ecological significance and listing status reflect broader patterns of freshwater fish endemism and vulnerability in California, where geographic isolation of drainage basins has produced high species diversity but also high extinction risk.
