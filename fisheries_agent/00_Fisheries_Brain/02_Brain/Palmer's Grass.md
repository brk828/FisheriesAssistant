# Palmer's Grass

## Summary
Palmer's Grass (Distichlis palmeri) is a plant species that was traditionally harvested by the Oax Pa (Cocopah) people in the Colorado River Delta region. The loss of this resource due to wetland degradation contributed to the cultural disruption and population decline of the indigenous Oax Pa people.
