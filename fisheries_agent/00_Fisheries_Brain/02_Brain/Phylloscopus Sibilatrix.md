# Phylloscopus Sibilatrix

## Context
> - **[[Phylloscopus sibilatrix]] (Wood Warbler):** Associated with a substrate of dead leaves or needles covering the ground uniformly, and scattered/sparse shrubs; indifferent to canopy density. Frequency increases with denser pole-stage stands and greater bracken fern (*fougère aigle*) cover.
