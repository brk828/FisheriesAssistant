# Biological Opinion

## Summary
A Biological Opinion is a formal regulatory assessment issued by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service that evaluates whether a proposed federal action (such as water management, dam operations, or resource development) will jeopardize the continued existence of threatened or endangered fish species and their critical habitats. This document serves as a legally binding determination under the Endangered Species Act and typically includes findings of jeopardy or no jeopardy, as well as "Reasonable and Prudent Alternatives" that agencies must implement to mitigate adverse effects on protected aquatic species. Biological Opinions are central to fish conservation and aquatic resource management, as they establish mandatory compliance requirements for federal activities affecting sensitive fish populations.
