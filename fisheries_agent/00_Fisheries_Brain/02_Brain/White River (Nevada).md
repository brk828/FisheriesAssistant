# White River (Nevada)

## Summary
The White River in Nevada is a significant desert stream system within the southwestern United States that serves as critical habitat for endemic and threatened fish species, particularly the Desert Sucker (*Catostomus clarki*) and other native fishes adapted to arid aquatic environments. This river drainage represents an important component of regional fish biodiversity surveys and conservation documentation efforts, as evidenced by its inclusion in comprehensive ichthyological inventories of southwestern fish distributions and taxonomic assessments. Understanding the White River's fish fauna and ecological conditions is essential for conservation planning in desert aquatic ecosystems where water scarcity and anthropogenic impacts threaten the persistence of native fish populations.
