# Colorado State University

## Summary
Colorado State University houses the Larval Fish Laboratory, whose biologists participated in the ad hoc working group that convened in summer 1992 to set priorities for Phase II electrofishing impact studies. The working group helped establish research priorities focusing on four endangered big-river fishes of the Colorado River system.
