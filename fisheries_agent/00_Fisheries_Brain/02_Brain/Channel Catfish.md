# Channel Catfish

## Summary
Channel Catfish (*Ictalurus punctatus*) is a warmwater fish species widely used in aquaculture and recreational fisheries throughout North America, commonly stocked in reservoirs and lakes as a secondary sport fish component. In conservation ecology contexts, it is frequently referenced as a non-native species in western aquatic ecosystems where it may compete with or prey upon native fishes, and it demonstrates moderate resistance to certain fish toxicants used in management operations. The species' tolerance for degraded water conditions and its establishment in diverse aquatic habitats make it an important consideration in both fisheries management and native species conservation planning.
