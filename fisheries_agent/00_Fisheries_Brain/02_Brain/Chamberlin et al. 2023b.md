# Chamberlin et al. 2023b

## Summary
Chamberlin et al. 2023b conducted prior work on Bayesian hierarchical growth models that this study builds upon. The current study extends their methodological approach to develop a sex-specific Bayesian hierarchical formulation that can simultaneously compare growth parameters across multiple ageing protocols while accounting for between-reader variability.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed:** Accurate estimation of [[Gray Triggerfish]] growth parameters while accounting for ageing error from multiple readers and comparing three ageing protocols (whole otoliths, new dorsal spine protocol, old dorsal spine protocol).
