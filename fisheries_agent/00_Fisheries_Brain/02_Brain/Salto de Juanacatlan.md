# Salto de Juanacatlan

## Summary
Salto de Juanacatlan is a waterfall or rapids that marks the boundary between the upper Lerma Basin and the lower Santiago portion of the Rio Lerma-Santiago system. It serves as a significant biogeographic barrier, with the area above it showing 66% endemism compared to 58% for the entire basin.
