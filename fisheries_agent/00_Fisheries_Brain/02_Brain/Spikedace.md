# Spikedace

## Summary
Spikedace (Meda fulgida) is a small endemic cyprinid fish native to the southwestern United States, particularly the Gila River basin in New Mexico and Arizona, that has become a focal species in desert fish conservation due to its threatened status and specialized habitat requirements in clear, flowing stream sections. The species is protected under the Endangered Species Act, with designated critical habitat areas established to ensure the preservation of its limited populations threatened by water extraction, habitat degradation, and competition from non-native species. Its presence and population status serve as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health in arid southwestern watersheds, making it central to regional fisheries management and desert aquatic recovery efforts.
