# Lily Park Reach

## Summary
Lily Park Reach is a study site at river mile 50 on the Yampa River known for highly diverse substrate with riffles, pools, backwaters, and boulders, and numerous historical adult squawfish captures. This 1982 study found that YOY habitat was much higher than at Maybell due to high substrate diversity with highest values at low flows, adult habitat was dramatically higher than Maybell comprising a high percentage of total surface area at 100-2,000 cfs, and spawning habitat increased 10-fold between 150-1,200 cfs and continued increasing even to 12,000 cfs, though total spawning habitat was less than Maybell at all flows due to less gravel-cobble substrate.
