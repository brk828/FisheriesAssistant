# Section 7 Consultation

## Summary
Section 7 Consultation refers to the mandatory process under the Endangered Species Act requiring federal agencies to consult with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service before authorizing, funding, or carrying out actions that may affect threatened or endangered species and their habitats. In the context of aquatic conservation and fisheries management, particularly on systems like the Lower Colorado River, Section 7 Consultation ensures that water operations, habitat modifications, and species management activities are evaluated for their impacts on protected fish species and that appropriate mitigation measures are implemented to avoid jeopardizing species recovery. This consultation framework has been central to balancing competing demands such as hydropower generation and water delivery with the conservation needs of endemic and endangered fish species in regulated river systems.
