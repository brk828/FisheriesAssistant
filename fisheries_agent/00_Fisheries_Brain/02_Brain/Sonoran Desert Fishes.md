# Sonoran Desert Fishes

## Summary
Sonoran Desert Fishes refers to the native fish fauna endemic to and characteristic of aquatic ecosystems within the Sonoran Desert region, including species such as the Bonytail Chub, Razorback Sucker, and Desert Pupfish that inhabit warmwater streams and rivers like the Gila River Basin and Verde River. These fishes are of significant conservation concern due to habitat degradation and declining populations, making them subjects of intensive monitoring, research, and management efforts by agencies such as the Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge and academic institutions. The study of Sonoran Desert Fishes encompasses ichthyological research on adaptation to harsh desert aquatic conditions, population dynamics, and ecosystem restoration strategies critical to their survival and the health of desert aquatic ecosystems.
