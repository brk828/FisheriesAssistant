# Lake Pauline

## Summary
Lake Pauline is a lake in Texas, United States, that underwent a complete fish population eradication project in 1963. The lake had an unbalanced fish community with rough fish exceeding 90 percent of the population, dominated by River Carpsucker, and was treated with rotenone along with its tributary streams and watershed ponds at a rate of approximately 4 pounds of 5 percent rotenone powder per acre-foot.
