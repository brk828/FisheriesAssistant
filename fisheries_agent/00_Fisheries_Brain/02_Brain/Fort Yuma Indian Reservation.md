# Fort Yuma Indian Reservation

## Summary
The Fort Yuma Indian Reservation is a location in the lower Colorado River region where rainbow trout stocking occurs as part of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's fisheries program. According to this biological opinion, the reservation receives 8,500 rainbow trout annually, stocked into two canals during November and January as part of the winter-only stocking schedule in areas below the main reservoir system.
