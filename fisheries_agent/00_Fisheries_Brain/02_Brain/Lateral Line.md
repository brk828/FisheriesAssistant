# Lateral Line

## Summary
The Lateral Line is a sensory organ in bass consisting of nerve-endings in jelly-like substance exposed through openings from gill-cover to tail. It functions as part of the fish's hearing system, specifically being sensitive to low-pitched sounds, while the inner ear detects high-pitched sounds.
