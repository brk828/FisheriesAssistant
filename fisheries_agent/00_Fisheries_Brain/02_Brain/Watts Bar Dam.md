# Watts Bar Dam

## Summary
Watts Bar Dam is the downstream boundary of the study area on the Clinch River. The river develops rich plankton from Gallaher Bridge downstream to Watts Bar Dam, representing the lower extent of the plankton-rich zone that develops after the river warms and receives nutrient enrichment in the Oak Ridge area.

## Context

> This 1956 study by J.B. Lackey documented the microscopic aquatic biota of the [[Clinch River]] system and adjacent waters in the [[Oak Ridge]] area, focusing on the relationship between suspended microorganisms and radioactivity from [[Oak Ridge National Laboratory]] (ORNL) operations.
