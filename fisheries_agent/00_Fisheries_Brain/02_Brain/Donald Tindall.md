# Donald Tindall

## Summary
Donald Tindall was a graduate student working under Arland Hotchkiss on the Characeae research project. He conducted a detailed thesis study on the life history of Nitella flexilis in Doe Run, Kentucky, and collected extensive environmental data on habitat conditions including temperature, alkalinity, chloride content, and pH across various Characeae collection sites.
