# Redfield Creek

## Summary
Redfield Creek is a stream near the Muleshoe Preserve that serves as a comparison site for Gila Chub populations. This source reports that Redfield Creek had substantially higher Gila Chub densities (20.3 fish per 100 m²) compared to Bass Creek (2.5 fish per 100 m²), suggesting that Bass Creek may have habitat limitations for this species.
