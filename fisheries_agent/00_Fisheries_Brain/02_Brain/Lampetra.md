# Lampetra

## Summary
Lampetra is a genus of parasitic lampreys in the family Petromyzontidae, characterized by their jawless, circular mouths with keratinous teeth adapted for rasping flesh and feeding on fish blood and body fluids. These agnathans occupy important ecological niches as both predators and prey in freshwater and marine ecosystems, though their taxonomy has been subject to considerable revision, with some species experiencing taxonomic reassignment to related genera such as Lethenteron based on morphological studies of dentition and other features. Lampetra species have been of particular interest to ichthyologists and conservation biologists studying declines in native fish faunas, as their predatory impacts and presence serve as indicators of aquatic ecosystem health and species composition changes.
