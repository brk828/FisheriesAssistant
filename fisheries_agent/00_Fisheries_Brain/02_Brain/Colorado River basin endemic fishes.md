# Colorado River Basin Endemic Fishes

## Summary
Colorado River Basin Endemic Fishes are fish species found exclusively or predominantly within the Colorado River system and its tributaries, characterized by morphological and physiological adaptations that reflect their evolution in response to the river's distinctive historical conditions of high flow velocity, turbidity, and sediment load. These endemic species represent unique evolutionary responses to the basin's unique aquatic environment and are therefore of significant conservation concern given anthropogenic alterations to river hydrology and habitat. Understanding these fish and their specific adaptations is central to restoration ecology and conservation biology efforts aimed at maintaining the ecological integrity of this distinct freshwater system.

## Context
> These traits are interpreted as convergent adaptations among Colorado River Basin fishes to a historically fast, turbid, high-sediment river system (see [[Colorado River Basin Endemic Fishes]]).
