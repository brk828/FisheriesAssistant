# Brown (1951)

## Summary
Brown (1951) demonstrated that channel catfish yield more pounds of edible fish per acre than any other species commonly stocked in Texas ponds. This finding helped explain the surging demand for channel catfish fingerlings that motivated the spawning behavior research.
