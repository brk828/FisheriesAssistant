# Yuma Area Office

## Summary
The Yuma Area Office is a regional office of the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation. This source identifies it as the lead agency that prepared the environmental assessment for restoration and enhancement actions along the Lower Colorado River from Imperial Dam through the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge, coordinating with multiple state and federal agencies.

## Context
