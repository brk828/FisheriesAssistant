# American Beaver

## Summary
The American Beaver (Castor canadensis) is a semi-aquatic rodent in the family Castoridae that builds dams creating freshwater impoundments. This source documents that beaver populations increased significantly across New Hampshire in the 1940s, and their impoundments consistently produced waterfowl broods but also caused damage to roads, cultivated lands, and timber through excessive water levels, leading to the development of water control techniques using perforated drain pipes to manage impoundment levels without removing the beavers.
