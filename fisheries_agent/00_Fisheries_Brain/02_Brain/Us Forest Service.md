# Us Forest Service

## Summary
The US Forest Service is a federal agency responsible for managing public lands and waters within the National Forest System, playing a critical role in fish conservation and aquatic ecosystem management through habitat protection, species recovery programs, and monitoring protocols. In the context of Gila Trout conservation, the Forest Service has been instrumental in implementing captive breeding and translocation programs, establishing and maintaining refuge populations in isolated streams on Forest Service lands, and conducting population monitoring to assess the success of recovery efforts for this federally protected Southwest endemic species. Their collaborative involvement in developing monitoring protocols and managing introduced populations across multiple watersheds reflects their central function as stewards of aquatic biodiversity on federal forest lands.
