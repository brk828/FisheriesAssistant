# San Bernardino Ranch Fontelicella

## Summary
San Bernardino Ranch Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species found at San Bernardino Ranch in Cochise County, Arizona, in springs near the headquarters and feeding the main pond. Population declines were noted in two domestic water springs over 12 years, and the report recommends eliminating or lowering the main ranch pond to increase habitat from 10-15 meters to a larger cienega toward the Mexican border.
