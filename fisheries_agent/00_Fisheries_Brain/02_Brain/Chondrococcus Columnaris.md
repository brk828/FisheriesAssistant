# Chondrococcus Columnaris

## Summary
Chondrococcus columnaris is a myxobacterium that serves as the causative agent of columnaris disease, a significant bacterial pathogen affecting both cold- and warmwater fish species with considerable economic impact on aquaculture operations. This gram-negative rod-shaped bacterium is environmentally persistent across waters of varying chemical quality and causes notable mortality in cultured fish populations, making it a critical concern for fisheries management and fish health. The pathogen's susceptibility to various antimicrobial agents has been systematically studied to develop effective therapeutic and control strategies in aquaculture settings.
