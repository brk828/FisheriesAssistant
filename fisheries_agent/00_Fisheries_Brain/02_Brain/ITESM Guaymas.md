# ITESM Guaymas

## Summary
ITESM Guaymas is an academic institution whose researcher Carlos Valdes Casillas presented at the 1997 workshop on restoration activities in the Rio Hardy region. The presentation reported that 60,000 hectares of wetlands currently exist in the delta, with 200,000 hectares remaining undeveloped.
