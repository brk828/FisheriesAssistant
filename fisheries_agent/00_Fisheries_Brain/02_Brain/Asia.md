# Asia

## Summary
Asia is a continent with high station density in the GHCN database, reflecting historical patterns of human settlement and development in industrialized regions. However, northern Asia and the Tibetan plateau represent major data gaps in the network's spatial coverage, with significantly fewer climate monitoring stations in these remote areas.

## Context

> This document describes the Global Historical Climatology Network (GHCN) Version 1.0, a comprehensive global baseline dataset of monthly mean temperature, precipitation, sea level pressure, and station pressure compiled by Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the National Climatic Data Center....
