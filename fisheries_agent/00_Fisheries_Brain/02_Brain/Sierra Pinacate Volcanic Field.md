# Sierra Pinacate Volcanic Field

## Summary
The Sierra Pinacate Volcanic Field is a volcanic region whose lava bed eruptions diverted the flow of Rio Sonoyta southward to the Gulf of California, away from its previous westward course toward the Colorado River Delta. This geological event, which likely occurred within the last 100,000 years, is believed to have isolated the ancestral Rio Sonoyta/Quitobaquito pupfish populations from the remainder of the species' range, contributing to their evolutionary divergence into what is now recognized as Cyprinodon eremus.
