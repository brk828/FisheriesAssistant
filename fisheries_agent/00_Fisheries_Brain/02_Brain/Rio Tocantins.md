# Rio Tocantins

## Summary
The Rio Tocantins is a river system in the Lower Amazon basin of Brazil where Bryconamericus novae was collected. The new species was found in the Rio Novo, a tributary of the Rio Tocantins, specifically below the Cachoeira da Velha waterfall near Piabana.
