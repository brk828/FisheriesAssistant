# Odonata

## Summary
Odonata is the taxonomic order comprising dragonflies and damselflies, which are aquatic insects that play important ecological roles in freshwater systems as both predators and prey for fish populations. In ichthyology and fisheries science, Odonata are significant components of fish diets and serve as indicators of aquatic ecosystem health and water quality conditions. Understanding Odonata communities and their distributions is essential for assessing the trophic dynamics and environmental conditions of aquatic habitats that support fish populations.
