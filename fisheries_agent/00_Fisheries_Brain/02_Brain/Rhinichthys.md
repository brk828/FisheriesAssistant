# Rhinichthys

## Summary
Rhinichthys is a genus of small freshwater cyprinid fishes commonly known as dace, distributed across North America with species adapted to cool, clear streams and rivers. These benthic-oriented fish are important components of lotic ecosystems and have been subjects of taxonomic revision and conservation concern, particularly regarding their distribution status and ecological relationships with other native fish species in their respective drainage systems.
