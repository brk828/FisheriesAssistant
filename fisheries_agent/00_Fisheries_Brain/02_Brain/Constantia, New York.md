# Constantia, New York

## Summary
Constantia, New York is the location of a New York State Fish Hatchery where adult pikeperch (walleye) from Oneida Lake were held for this study. The pikeperch from this facility (15.7 to 16.8 inches total length) were one of four groups tested and showed no significant reduction in measurement error when sedated.

## Context

> This 1958 study by Wilbur L. Hartman investigated whether sedating fish with [[Urethane]] reduced measurement error and improved accuracy when recording fork and total lengths.
