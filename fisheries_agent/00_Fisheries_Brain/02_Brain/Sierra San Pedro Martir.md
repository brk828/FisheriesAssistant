# Sierra San Pedro Martir

## Summary
Sierra San Pedro Martir is a mountain range in Baja California, Mexico, that supports desert aquatic habitats including Arroyo San Rafael. According to this source, it is home to Nelson's Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss nelsoni), an important native fish species that was the subject of multiple research presentations at the 1990 symposium, including studies on home range, sexual maturity, and parasites.
