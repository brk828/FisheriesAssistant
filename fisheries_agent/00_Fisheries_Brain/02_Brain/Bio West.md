# Bio/West

## Summary
Bio/West appears to be a consulting or research firm specializing in aquatic ecology and native fish conservation, with staff scientists like Rich Valdez contributing expertise to collaborative efforts on fish management and conservation in western U.S. river systems. The organization was involved in interdisciplinary research projects during the early 1990s, working alongside federal agencies like the Bureau of Reclamation and academic institutions on issues related to endangered fish species such as the Humpback Chub and Razorback Sucker in regulated river systems.
