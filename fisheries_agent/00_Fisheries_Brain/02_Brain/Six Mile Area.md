# Six Mile Area

## Summary
Six Mile Area is one of three primary spawning areas for razorback suckers in Lake Mohave. Adult razorback suckers concentrate at this location along with Arizona Bay and Hammerhead Cove for spawning activities over coarse gravel or rubble substrates in shallow water.
