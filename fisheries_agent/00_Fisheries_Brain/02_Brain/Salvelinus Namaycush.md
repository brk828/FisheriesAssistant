# Salvelinus Namaycush

## Summary
Salvelinus namaycush, commonly known as lake trout, is a coldwater salmonid species that inhabits deep lakes and oligotrophic (nutrient-poor) water bodies across northern North America. As a specialized coldwater lake fish, it serves as an important indicator species for aquatic ecosystem health and is a key component of both commercial and recreational fisheries in northern temperate and boreal regions. Lake trout are significant predators in lacustrine food webs and their presence and population dynamics provide valuable information about water quality, thermal conditions, and the overall ecological status of deep freshwater lake systems.
