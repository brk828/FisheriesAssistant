# Ditrema laeve

## Summary
Ditrema laeve is a previously described Japanese species that this source considers a synonym of Ditrema temminckii. This taxonomic revision consolidates multiple nominal species into a single valid species.

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
