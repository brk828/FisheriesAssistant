# Thomas Bowman

## Summary
Thomas Bowman is the author of this 1965 taxonomic revision who examined male pleopod morphology that was unavailable to Rioja. Based on this examination, Bowman demonstrated that the species belongs to superfamily Trichoniscoidea rather than Styloniscoidea, necessitating the creation of the new genus Xilitloniscus to properly classify this Mexican cave isopod.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
