# Perch Lake

## Summary
Perch Lake was a Wisconsin lake with soft, acid, warm water where antimycin A was used for selective yellow perch removal. An application of 0.5 ppb killed all stunted perch while causing no harm to northern pike, bluntnose minnows, bullheads, largemouth bass, or smallmouth bass.
