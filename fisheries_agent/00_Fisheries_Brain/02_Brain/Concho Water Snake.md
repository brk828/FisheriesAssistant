# Concho Water Snake

## Summary
The Concho Water Snake is a species in Texas that has been subject to exemption tactics under the Endangered Species Act. This source cites it as an example of how exemption strategies first used in the Snail Darter/Tellico Dam controversy have been repeatedly applied to undermine protections for other endangered species.
