# Reservoir Fish Community Dynamics

## Summary
The source shows how reservoir operations dramatically affected fish communities, with Roosevelt Lake's emptying in 1940 leading to complete extirpation of Yellow Bass that had previously been very abundant. Mass mortality events from heavy silting following floods (circa 1934) also impacted Yellow Bass populations in Roosevelt Lake.
