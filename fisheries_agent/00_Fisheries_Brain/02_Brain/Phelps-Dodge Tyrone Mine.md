# Phelps-Dodge Tyrone Mine

## Summary
The Phelps-Dodge Tyrone Mine is a mining operation with tailings ponds located near Mangas Creek that poses an unresolved water quality concern for the proposed Mangas Creek Reservoir. The document notes that tailings pond spills from this mine have occurred in the past and could potentially impact water quality in the proposed reservoir.
