# Lo-Lo-Mai Spring

## Summary
Lo-Lo-Mai Spring is a desert spring location in Arizona that was surveyed in 1981 for endemic mollusk populations. It represents one of the isolated aquatic refugia supporting unique hydrobioid snail species in the southwestern United States.
