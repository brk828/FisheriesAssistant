# Lake Havasu Fisheries Improvement Program

## Summary
The Lake Havasu Fisheries Improvement Program was a coordinated management initiative implemented by U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service refuges along the Lower Colorado River to enhance fish habitat and support the recovery of native species, particularly threatened taxa like razorback suckers. The program involved the placement of artificial habitat structures and brush piles in Lake Havasu to improve spawning and rearing conditions for native fishes while supporting broader conservation objectives for the Colorado River ecosystem. This program exemplified an active management approach to fisheries restoration, combining habitat enhancement strategies with monitoring efforts such as telemetry studies to assess population responses and recruitment success.
