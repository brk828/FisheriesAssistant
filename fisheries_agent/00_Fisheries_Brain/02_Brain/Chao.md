# Chao

## Summary
The Chao estimator is a two-visit mark-recapture method calculated as N̂ = (M+n-2m)²/(4m) + M + n - m. This study finds that the Chao estimator performs poorly compared to Chapman and recommends discontinuing its use in favor of the Chapman estimator for two-visit population estimation studies.
