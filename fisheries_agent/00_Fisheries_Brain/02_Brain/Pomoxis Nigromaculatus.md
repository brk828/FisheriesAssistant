# Pomoxis Nigromaculatus

## Summary
Pomoxis nigromaculatus, commonly known as black crappie, is a freshwater centrarchid (sunfish family) species that functions as an important predatory component in North American aquatic ecosystems and sport fisheries. This species exhibits significant ecological plasticity, inhabiting diverse freshwater environments including lakes, reservoirs, and rivers where it occupies a piscivorous niche and contributes substantially to fish community structure. Black crappie are frequently encountered in fish catch surveys and represent key indicators of ecosystem health and fishery productivity in temperate North American waters.
