# Semotilus

## Summary
Semotilus is a genus of North American cyprinid fishes (freshwater chubs) comprising four species, with three widely distributed species (*S. atromaculatus*, *S. corporalis*, and *S. margarita*) found across diverse freshwater drainages from the American Southwest to Midwestern waterways. These fish are notable for their reproductive behavior involving the construction of pebble mound nests and their varying degrees of habitat specialization, ranging from generalist species to highly restricted endemics with conservation concerns. The genus represents an important group for studying freshwater fish ecology, phylogenetics, and spawning behavior in North American stream ecosystems.
