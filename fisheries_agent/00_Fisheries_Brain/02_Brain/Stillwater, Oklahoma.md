# Stillwater, Oklahoma

## Summary
Stillwater, Oklahoma is the location of the oil refinery effluent holding-pond system studied in this research. The site consisted of ten holding-ponds covering 634,556 square feet that were placed in operation in July 1960, receiving pretreated effluent from refinery operations including distillation, catalytic cracking, and reforming processes.

## Context

> This 1963 doctoral dissertation by Richard Arnold Tubb examines the population dynamics of bottom-dwelling midge fly larvae (family [[Tendipedidae]], also known as [[Chironomidae]]) in a series of ten oil refinery effluent holding-ponds in [[Stillwater, Oklahoma]].
