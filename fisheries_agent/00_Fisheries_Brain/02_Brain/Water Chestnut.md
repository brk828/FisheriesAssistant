# Water Chestnut

## Summary
Water Chestnut is a surface-floating aquatic plant that became invasive in Massachusetts waters. According to this 1950s bulletin, Massachusetts achieved some success controlling Water Chestnut by spraying it with a mixture of 2,4-D herbicide and No. 2 fuel oil, though hand pulling was also required, and the reproductive nut-like bodies may have been unaffected by the treatment.
