# Osage River

## Summary
Osage River, also called Marais des Cygnes, is a major Kansas waterway where Blue Cat was rare, Short-nosed Gar was found, Shovelnose Sturgeon entered, and Paddlefish were apparently restricted. It is one of the large streams mentioned in the distribution of various Kansas fish species.
