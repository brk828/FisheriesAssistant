# Peter G. Sanchez

## Summary
Peter G. Sanchez was one of three compilers of the 1977 Death Valley System aquatic resources bibliography, working with Robert Rush Miller and David L. Soltz for the National Park Service. He helped assemble the comprehensive reference work documenting literature on Death Valley's aquatic fauna, particularly pupfishes and their extreme desert habitats.
