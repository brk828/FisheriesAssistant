# Coffelt CPS

## Summary
The Coffelt CPS is an electrofishing equipment model that produces a pulse train of three 240-Hz, 1.6-ms pulses every 15th of a second with a 7.2% duty cycle. This study proposes testing this waveform alongside three other current types to evaluate effects on juvenile chubs and razorback sucker embryos/larvae, and it was used in the pilot experiments to expose treatment groups to predetermined voltage gradients.
