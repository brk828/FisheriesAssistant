# Percidae

## Summary
Percidae is a diverse family of freshwater fishes that includes perches, darters, and walleyes, characterized by spiny-rayed fins and generally elongate body forms adapted to benthic and mid-water habitats in temperate freshwater ecosystems. Members of this family are ecologically important as both predators and prey in fish communities, with considerable significance in fisheries management and conservation, particularly regarding species diversity assessments and habitat quality evaluations in North American drainages. The family's abundance in systematic and faunal surveys reflects its prominence in regional fish inventories and its utility as an indicator group for evaluating freshwater ecosystem integrity.
