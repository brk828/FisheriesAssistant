# Gila Fish and Gun Club

## Summary
The Gila Fish and Gun Club is an organization that corresponded with Arizona State University researchers in 1987 regarding the conservation status of native fish species. According to this source, the club received information from a zoology professor about the declining populations of Spikedace and Loach Minnow in relation to the invasive Red Shiner, demonstrating the club's interest in native fish conservation issues in the Gila River system and surrounding Arizona waters.
