# Eudorina

## Summary
Eudorina is a genus of phytoplankton from the Green Algae (Chlorophyta) phylum found in Lake Powell. It was one of the dominant genera during a moderate phytoplankton bloom that occurred in October 1967 in the upper reservoir, along with Fragilaria and Staurastrum.
