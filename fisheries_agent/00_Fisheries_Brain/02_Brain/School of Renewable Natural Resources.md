# School of Renewable Natural Resources

## Summary
School of Renewable Natural Resources is an academic unit at the University of Arizona focused on natural resource management, research, and education. The school was listed in the 1987 directory as one of the educational and research institutions providing critical research and education capacity for environmental and aquatic resource management.
