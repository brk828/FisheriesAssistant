# San Pedro River Management Area

## Summary
The San Pedro River Management Area is a designated study area in Arizona where a comprehensive wildlife and vegetation inventory was conducted across multiple habitat types including broadleaf riparian, mesquite, mesquite/grassland, and grassland. The study design called for 12 transects to sample these different habitat types, with the vegetation inventory covering approximately 30 miles of river.
