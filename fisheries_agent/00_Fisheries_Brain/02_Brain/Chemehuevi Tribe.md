# Chemehuevi Tribe

## Summary
The Chemehuevi Tribe is a tribal nation participating in the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program (LCR MSCP). They are one of six tribal partners involved in this 50-year coordinated conservation approach for the Lower Colorado River basin.
