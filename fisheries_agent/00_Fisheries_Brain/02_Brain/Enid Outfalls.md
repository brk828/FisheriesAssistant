# Enid Outfalls

## Summary
The Enid Outfalls refer to the combined discharge points where an oil refinery and the Enid Municipal Sewage Plant release effluents into Boggy Creek approximately one mile above its junction with Skeleton Creek. Fish populations below these outfalls exhibited marked seasonal variation with maximum abundance in November 1963 and minimum in February, showing strong evidence of seasonal migration patterns, while populations above the outfalls were more stable and showed no migratory movements, likely prevented by negative reactions to the heavily polluted waters downstream.
