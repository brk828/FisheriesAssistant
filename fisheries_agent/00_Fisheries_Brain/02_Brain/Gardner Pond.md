# Gardner Pond

## Summary
Gardner Pond is a gravel-pit pond located in the Grand Valley near Grand Junction, Colorado, that was used as a field study site for testing PIT tags on endangered fish species. The pond experienced critically low dissolved oxygen levels during winter monitoring (3 mg/L on February 9, 1989), and fish recovery rates from this pond were extremely poor, with only 7 of 75 released roundtail chub recovered and zero razorback suckers recovered across multiple sampling events.
