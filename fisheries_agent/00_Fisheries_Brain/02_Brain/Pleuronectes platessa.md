# Pleuronectes platessa

## Summary
Pleuronectes platessa (plaice) was the subject of multiple successful rearing studies. Molander and Molander-Swedmark (1957) achieved 18.6-64.2% metamorphosis on Artemia, while Shelbourne (1964) obtained maximum 66% survival from eggs to metamorphosis.
