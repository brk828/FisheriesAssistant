# Chosen Strait

## Summary
Chosen Strait is a strait nowhere deeper than 180 meters through which the warm Kuro Shiwo current enters the Sea of Japan from the south. This source indicates it is one of the shallow connections that help isolate the Sea of Japan's deep waters and prevent colonization by macrourid fishes.
