# Lake Martin

## Summary
Lake Martin is a 40,000-acre reservoir in Alabama where threadfin shad were successfully introduced. Following the introduction of 575 three-inch threadfin shad in August 1957, population studies 18 months later estimated the population had expanded dramatically to 248 million fish weighing 1.784 million pounds.
