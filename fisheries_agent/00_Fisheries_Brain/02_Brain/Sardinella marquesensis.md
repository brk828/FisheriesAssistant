# Sardinella marquesensis

## Summary
Sardinella marquesensis is the scientific name for the Marquesan Sardine. This source identifies it as one of the problematic marine introductions to Hawaii, noting it was misidentified at introduction and may cause clupeid poisoning, with one human death recorded.
