# Cnemidophorus

## Summary
Cnemidophorus is a genus of whiptail lizards (family Teiidae) that includes unisexual parthenogenetic species. The unisexual forms arose from interspecific hybridization between sexual progenitor species with relatively high genetic distance (Nei's D = 0.73), representing one of the few vertebrate groups capable of true parthenogenesis without requiring sperm. These reptilian parthenogens are cited as examples of unisexual vertebrates that face genetic and ecological constraints on their origins and long-term evolutionary persistence.
