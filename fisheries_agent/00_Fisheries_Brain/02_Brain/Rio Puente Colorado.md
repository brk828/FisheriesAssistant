# Rio Puente Colorado

## Summary
Rio Puente Colorado is a river in the Cuatro Cienegas basin that serves as the type locality for the endemic pupfish Cyprinodon bifasciatus. This river connects via canal to Escobedo and during high water enters the Rio Mesquites system near Puente Orosco, creating a potential dispersal corridor for invasive Tilapia to spread throughout the basin.
