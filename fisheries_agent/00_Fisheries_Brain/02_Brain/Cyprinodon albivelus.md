# Cyprinodon albivelus

## Summary
Cyprinodon albivelus is a pupfish species from the Guzman Basin. This source documents it as one of three Guzman Basin pupfish species being analyzed in a collaborative taxonomic study, with 210 specimen cases entered by September 1981 from 10 localities in the Guzman Basin and surrounding areas.
