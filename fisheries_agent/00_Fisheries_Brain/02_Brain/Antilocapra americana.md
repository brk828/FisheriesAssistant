# Antilocapra americana

## Summary
Antilocapra americana (pronghorn) is represented by one very robust atlas at La Lomita Pequeña, possibly from a large male individual. This specimen was burned (100% of pronghorn remains showed burning), and pronghorn were historically distributed in grasslands throughout Arizona though their range has been greatly reduced since the historic period.
