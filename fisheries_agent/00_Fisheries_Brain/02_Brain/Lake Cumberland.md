# Lake Cumberland

## Summary
Lake Cumberland is a freshwater lake in Kentucky that served as one of the collection sites for spotted black bass (Micropterus punctulatus) specimens in this 1959 morphological study examining the reliability of tongue tooth patches as a diagnostic character.
