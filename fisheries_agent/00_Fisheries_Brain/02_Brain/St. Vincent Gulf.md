# St. Vincent Gulf

## Summary
St. Vincent Gulf in South Australia is the location where one of only two previously known lernaeocerid species from Australia was recorded. Lernaeenicus hemirhamphi was found parasitizing the eye of Hemirhamphus intermedius in this gulf.
