# Rio San Rafael

## Summary
The Rio San Rafael is a river in the San Pedro Martir region of Baja California, Mexico that serves as the habitat for the endemic San Pedro Martir Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss nelsoni). A field trip to this location was planned for November 17, 1990 as part of the Desert Fishes Council symposium activities.
