# European Carp

## Summary
European Carp (Cyprinus carpio) is an introduced fish species present in the lower Colorado River study area. In the diet analysis of striped bass, European Carp appeared only once as prey, representing 1.8% frequency and 0.06 kg of food volume, indicating minimal importance as a food source for striped bass.
