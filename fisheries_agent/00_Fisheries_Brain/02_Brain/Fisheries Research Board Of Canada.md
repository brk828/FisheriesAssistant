# Fisheries Research Board Of Canada

## Summary
The Fisheries Research Board of Canada was a federal scientific institution responsible for conducting research on Canadian fish species and fisheries management during the mid-to-late 20th century. The Board sponsored comprehensive scientific symposia and published peer-reviewed studies covering diverse topics in ichthyology and aquatic ecology, including fish systematics, evolution, distribution, biology, and population dynamics. Through its funding and organizational role, the Board was instrumental in advancing fundamental knowledge of Canadian freshwater and marine fish species while supporting applied research relevant to sustainable fisheries management.
