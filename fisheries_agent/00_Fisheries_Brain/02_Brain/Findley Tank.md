# Findley Tank

## Summary
Findley Tank is a water body located at the Research Ranch in Elgin, Arizona, within the San Pedro River drainage. This source reports it has harbored a Desert Pupfish population of undocumented origin for approximately 20 years (as of 1986), which was never critically examined morphologically or electrophoretically, and was reportedly the source of pupfish transferred to Fort Huachuca, with ranch personnel resisting eradication efforts due to concerns about preserving the population and protecting Mexican Garter Snakes.
