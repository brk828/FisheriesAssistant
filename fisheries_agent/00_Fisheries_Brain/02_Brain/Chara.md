# Chara

## Summary
Chara is an aquatic macrophyte (stonewort) that serves as critical habitat structure in freshwater ecosystems, providing spawning substrate, shelter, and foraging opportunities for fish populations. In the context of these fisheries studies, Chara growth along shorelines and shallow areas is identified as an important component of fish habitat quality that supports recruitment and species-specific niche utilization patterns in lakes and river systems.
