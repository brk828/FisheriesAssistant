# Twin Lakes

## Summary
Twin Lakes is a pair of connected montane glacial lakes in Colorado at 2,802 meters elevation in the Upper Arkansas River Valley. The system consists of an upper lake (196 hectares, 28m maximum depth) and lower lake (589 hectares, 27m maximum depth) that are dimictic, chemically soft water systems supporting a self-reproducing Lake Trout population and stocked Rainbow Trout, with the lower lake containing approximately 85-90% of the fish population.
