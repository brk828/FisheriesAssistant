# Upper Salt River

## Summary
The Upper Salt River is a section of the Salt River in Arizona that contains both native fish populations and reintroduced or experimental populations of endangered fish species. According to this 1988 environmental assessment, the Upper Salt River was identified as one of the key areas of concern in evaluating the potential removal of Roosevelt Diversion Dam, particularly regarding adverse effects on these endangered fish populations and existing sport fisheries.
