# Razorback Sucker Recovery Program

## Summary
The Razorback Sucker Recovery Program is a comprehensive endangered-species management initiative focused on the propagation, health monitoring, and translocation of razorback suckers (Xyrauchen texanus) to restore populations in western U.S. river systems. The program implements standardized protocols including broodstock collection and maintenance, parasite treatment and health diagnostics, genetic tracking through tagging, and strategic release of captive-bred or wild-caught individuals into suitable habitat to counteract the species' decline from habitat degradation and competition with non-native fish species. Operating under the Endangered Species Act, this multi-faceted approach combines ex situ breeding efforts with in situ reintroduction to achieve long-term population recovery and species persistence.
