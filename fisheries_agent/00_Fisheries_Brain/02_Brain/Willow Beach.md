# Willow Beach

## Summary
Willow Beach is a critical location on Lake Mohave in the lower Colorado River system where native fish conservation programs, particularly for endangered razorback suckers, conduct larval collection, rearing, and translocation operations. The site and its associated ephemeral backwaters serve as essential habitat for capturing wild-caught larvae and monitoring stocked fish populations through mark-recapture studies. Willow Beach represents a key focal point for Colorado River native fish recovery efforts, including cutthroat trout introductions and bonytail chub management initiatives documented throughout the 1990s.
