# Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness

## Summary
Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness is a protected area in Arizona centered on Aravaipa Creek, which supports the largest assemblage of native fish species remaining in the state and has served as a critical conservation focus for aquatic ecology and invasive species management. The wilderness area and its associated creek watershed have been subject to management interventions, including exotic fish eradication programs and fishing restrictions, to preserve native fish populations and aquatic habitat integrity. As a designated wilderness with co-managed conservation lands, Aravaipa Canyon represents an important case study in balancing native fish conservation, invasive species control, and recreational use in arid-region aquatic ecosystems.
