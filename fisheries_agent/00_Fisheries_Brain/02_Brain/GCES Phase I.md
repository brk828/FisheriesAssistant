# GCES Phase I

## Summary
GCES Phase I refers to the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Phase I program conducted in the early 1980s. This source indicates that GCES Phase I established classification systems for alluvial deposits and recirculation zones in the Colorado River through Grand Canyon, providing foundational frameworks for understanding sediment dynamics and habitat features affected by Glen Canyon Dam operations.

## Context

> This proposal, submitted 21 August 1995 by researchers from [[Northern Arizona University]], requests $201,816 to continue transitional monitoring of [[Colorado River]] sand bars and backwaters in [[Grand Canyon National Park]].
