# Protrichoniscus

## Summary
Protrichoniscus is the genus most closely related to Xilitloniscus, being the only other trichoniscid genus with a greatly reduced or absent endopod on male pleopod 1. Xilitloniscus is distinguished from Protrichoniscus by the presence of a terminal plumose seta on the exopod of male pleopod 1, and of the six known Protrichoniscus species, five occur only in Mexican caves.
