# Redhorse

## Summary
Redhorse (genus *Moxostoma*) are benthic-feeding cyprinid fishes characterized by their specialized sucking mouths and are commonly found in freshwater river systems where they represent an important component of native fish communities. These bottom-feeding fish are significant for understanding pre-impoundment fish assemblages and community structure in lotic ecosystems, as they were historically abundant before anthropogenic habitat modifications. Redhorse species serve as ecological indicators of riverine health and are relevant to fisheries assessments and conservation studies focused on native fish restoration in modified aquatic systems.
