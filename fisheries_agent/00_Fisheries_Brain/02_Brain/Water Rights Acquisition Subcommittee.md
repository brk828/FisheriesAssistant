# Water Rights Acquisition Subcommittee

## Summary
The Water Rights Acquisition Subcommittee develops strategies for securing water rights to maintain instream flows for endangered fish recovery. In February 1990, the committee approved the subcommittee's report recommending hiring a water rights engineer and appraiser, evaluating conditional water rights on the Little Snake River, and conducting water availability studies for the 15-Mile Reach.
