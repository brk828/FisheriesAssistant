# Eye Flukes

## Summary
Eye Flukes (Diplostomum sp.) are trematode parasites that encyst in fish eye lenses, causing serious pathology including lens opacity, complete blindness, dark coloration, and emaciation in heavily infested fish. This parasite caused significant problems at the Hackettstown, New Jersey state fish hatchery in Rainbow Trout.
