# Cottonwood Artesian

## Summary
Cottonwood Artesian (Site #77) is a reintroduction site for Gila Topminnow that supported a population for 11 years from 1982 to 1992. Conservation pools were constructed at the site in December 1992, and the report recommends it should be restocked with topminnows.
