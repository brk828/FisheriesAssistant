# Thailand

## Summary
Thailand is a Southeast Asian country that is home to endangered catfish species. This 1974 U.S. endangered species list includes two Thai catfish species: the Giant Catfish (Pangasianodon gigas) and Pangasius sanitwongsei, demonstrating the international scope of the Endangered Species Act of 1973.
