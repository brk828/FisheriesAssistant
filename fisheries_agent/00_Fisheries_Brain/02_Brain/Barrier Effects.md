# Barrier Effects

## Summary
Barrier effects refer to how physical obstacles fragment habitat and restrict fish movement. This source documents the Piute Farms Waterfall, a 6-m barrier formed through sediment deposition approximately 30 km upstream from Lake Powell, which has blocked Razorback Sucker access to upstream riverine habitat since 2001, though 58% of tracked fish still moved upstream and were detected at this barrier in all seasons except winter.
