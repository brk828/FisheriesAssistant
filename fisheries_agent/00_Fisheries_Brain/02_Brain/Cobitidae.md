# Cobitidae

## Summary
Cobitidae is a family of small, benthic freshwater fishes commonly known as loaches, characterized by their elongated bodies, small scales, and barbels around the mouth, which they use to forage in sediments and rocky substrates. Members of this family are distributed primarily across Eurasia and are considered primary freshwater fishes, meaning they have limited physiological tolerance for saltwater and depend on freshwater habitats for their life cycles. Cobitidae species play important ecological roles as bottom-dwelling detritivores and are of both scientific interest for taxonomic study and practical concern for conservation, particularly in regions like the Middle East and Eastern Asia where habitat degradation threatens endemic species.
