# Vendace

## Summary
The Vendace (Coregonus albula) is a threatened fish species that was formerly found in 2 Scottish and 2 English lochs. It has gone extinct in one Scottish loch from sewage pollution and is apparently extinct in another, making it a subject of conservation concern and translocation efforts.
