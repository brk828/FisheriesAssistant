# Allan Hancock Foundation

## Summary
The Allan Hancock Foundation at the University of Southern California provided taxonomic expertise for this field guide. Dr. Gerald J. Bakus from this institution identified the sponge specimens, and Dr. John Garth identified the brachyuran crabs.
