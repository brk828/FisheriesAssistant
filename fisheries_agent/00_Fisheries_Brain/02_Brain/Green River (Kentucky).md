# Green River (Kentucky)

## Summary
The Green River in Kentucky is an important freshwater aquatic system studied for its diverse fish fauna and ecological significance in the central United States. This river system has been the subject of systematic ichthyological surveys and species inventories, serving as a reference location for documenting fish diversity, including lamprey species and other native fauna relevant to regional fisheries science and conservation biology. The Green River's ichthyofauna represents important baseline data for understanding fish communities in the interior United States and assessing changes in aquatic biodiversity.
