# Amistad Gambusia

## Summary
The Amistad Gambusia is an endangered livebearing fish endemic to Devil's River in Texas that exemplifies the critical conservation challenges addressed in standardized fish translocation and introduction protocols developed by the American Fisheries Society. This species represents a case study within broader endangered fish recovery efforts, where translocation and introduction strategies are employed as primary recovery tools, though such practices require careful standardization to avoid unintended ecological consequences and genetic complications. The Amistad Gambusia's conservation status and recovery management illustrate the practical application of guidelines governing threatened and endangered fish introductions, particularly regarding genetic integrity and population restoration in fragmented aquatic ecosystems.
