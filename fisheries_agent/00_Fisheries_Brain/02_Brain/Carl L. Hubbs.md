# Carl L. Hubbs

## Summary
Carl L. Hubbs was a pioneering American ichthyologist and evolutionary biologist whose work fundamentally advanced our understanding of fish systematics, speciation, and adaptation in aquatic environments during the twentieth century. His research encompassed taxonomic descriptions of new fish taxa, investigations into mechanisms of speciation in fishes, and broad contributions to conservation biology through detailed studies of fish populations and their ecological relationships. Hubbs's prolific career and extensive correspondence with colleagues established him as a central figure in mid-century ichthyological science and a mentor whose influence shaped the discipline for subsequent generations of fish scientists.
