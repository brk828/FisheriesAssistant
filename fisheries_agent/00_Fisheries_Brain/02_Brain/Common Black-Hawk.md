# Common Black-Hawk

## Summary
Common Black-Hawk (Buteogallus anthracinus) is a state-threatened species that occurs in Bonita Creek. It is one of five state-listed or federally proposed threatened species found in the area, all of which are obligate riparian species that have suffered dramatic declines elsewhere in the Southwest.
