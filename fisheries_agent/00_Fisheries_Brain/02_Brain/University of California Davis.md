# University of California Davis

## Summary
The University of California Davis was identified as one of two potential facilities (along with Montana State University) where Dr. Peter Brussard could conduct electrophoretic analysis of Cui-ui samples to determine hybridization levels. This analysis was critical for understanding the genetic threat to the endangered Cui-ui population.
