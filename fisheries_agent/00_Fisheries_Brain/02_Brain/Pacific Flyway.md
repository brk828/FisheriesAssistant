# Pacific Flyway

## Summary
The Pacific Flyway is a major migratory bird route along North America's Pacific coast. According to the 1999 conference, with the loss of wetlands throughout the Central Valley and Pacific coast, the Salton Sea has become crucial habitat along the Pacific Flyway for migratory birds, highlighting the ecological importance of remaining wetland areas in the region.
