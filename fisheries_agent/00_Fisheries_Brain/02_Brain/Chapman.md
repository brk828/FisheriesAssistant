# Chapman

## Summary
The Chapman estimator is a bias-corrected version of the Lincoln-Petersen estimator for two-visit mark-recapture studies, calculated as N̂ = [(M+1)(n+1)/(m+1)] - 1 based on the hypergeometric distribution. This study identifies Chapman as the superior two-visit method, providing approximately unbiased estimates (< 2% bias) when √(Mn) ≥ 2√N, which translates to requiring at least 63 individuals both marked and recaptured for a population of 1,000.
