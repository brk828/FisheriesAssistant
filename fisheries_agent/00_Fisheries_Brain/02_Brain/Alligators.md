# Alligators

## Summary
Alligators are mentioned as an example of a regulatory gap in California's exotic species list. Despite presenting similar threats to crocodiles (which are prohibited), alligators were not prohibited at the time, demonstrating inconsistencies in the state's approach to regulating potentially dangerous exotic species.
