# Bylas Tryonia

## Summary
Bylas Tryonia is an undescribed Tryonia species found in eight springs near Bylas, Graham County, Arizona, monitored since 1969. The species occupies soft sediment in slack water (different niche from co-occurring Bylas Fontelicella) and faces threats from cattle trampling, salt cedar, human activity, and recent Gambusia affinis introductions that also threaten the co-occurring Gila Topminnow.
