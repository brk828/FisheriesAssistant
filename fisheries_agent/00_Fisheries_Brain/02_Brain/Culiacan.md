# Culiacan

## Summary
Culiacan (Rio Culiacan) is a Mexican river flowing into the Gulf of California that represents part of the southern distribution limit of Gila robusta in Mexico. Populations in this system have 9,9-9,9 fin ray counts and apparently dispersed from the Colorado River through the Gulf of California when freshwater flow was much greater than present.
