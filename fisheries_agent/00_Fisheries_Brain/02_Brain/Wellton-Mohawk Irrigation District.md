# Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation District

## Summary
The Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation District is an agricultural area in Arizona whose drainage water has been critical to sustaining the Ciénega de Santa Clara wetland. This source reports that since 1977, agricultural drainage from this district has been channeled through the M.O.D.E. (Main Outlet Drain Extension) drain, providing approximately 100,000-130,000 acre-feet per year to the wetland, though this supply is considered tenuous and could be threatened if the Yuma desalination plant operates.
