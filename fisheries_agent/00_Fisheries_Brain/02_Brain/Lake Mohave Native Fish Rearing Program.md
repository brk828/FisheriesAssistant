# Lake Mohave Native Fish Rearing Program

## Summary
The Lake Mohave Native Fish Rearing Program was a conservation initiative undertaken by the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation's Native Fish Work Group to restore and maintain populations of native fish species in Lake Mohave through controlled breeding and rearing of fish in captivity. The program, documented throughout 1993-1994, represented a systematic approach to counteracting the decline of endemic fish species in this Colorado River reservoir ecosystem, which had been impacted by non-native species introductions and habitat alterations. This effort exemplified applied conservation biology methods aimed at preserving native ichthyofauna and supporting aquatic ecosystem recovery in the Lower Colorado River system.
