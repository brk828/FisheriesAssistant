# Spencer Creek

## Summary
Spencer Creek is a tributary inlet to the Colorado River in the lower reaches of Lake Mead, downstream of Glen Canyon Dam, where it serves as a sampling location in the Hualapai Aquatic Resources Study. This creek and its confluence with the mainstem Colorado River are monitored to assess fish community responses to interim flow regimes released from the dam, with researchers documenting catch rates and species composition in both the tributary inflow and adjacent mainstem habitat. The creek's discharge and water clarity conditions significantly influence fish sampling effectiveness and community structure, making it an important site for understanding how tributary inputs affect aquatic resources in the regulated Colorado River system.
