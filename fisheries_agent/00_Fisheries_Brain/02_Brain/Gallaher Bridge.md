# Gallaher Bridge

## Summary
Gallaher Bridge is located at mile 14 of the Clinch River and marks a transition point in the river's ecological character. The river warms approximately 5°C and begins developing rich plankton populations from Gallaher Bridge downstream to Watts Bar Dam, contrasting with the cold, plankton-poor conditions upstream from Norris Dam.
