# Posthodiplostomum minimum

## Summary
Posthodiplostomum minimum is a trematode parasite found as Neascus larvae forming cysts on fish livers in Imperial Reservoir. The study documented infection rates of 43.3% in bluegill, 31.1% in largemouth bass, and 18.2% in warmouth, though parasites showed no apparent effect on fish condition.

## Context

> This Master's thesis (1971) investigated the ecology of juvenile fish (10-100 mm) in [[Imperial Reservoir]], a 2955-hectare impoundment on the [[Lower Colorado River]] north of Yuma, Arizona. The study was motivated by planned channelization that could alter critical spawning and rearing habitats....
