# Salmon

## Summary
Salmon are anadromous fish species belonging to the family Salmonidae that migrate between freshwater and marine environments, making them keystone species in both riverine and coastal ecosystems. In the context of historical fisheries management and conservation biology, salmon have been central subjects of artificial propagation and restocking programs dating back to the late 19th century, reflecting their economic importance and vulnerability to overfishing and habitat degradation. Understanding salmon population dynamics, life history patterns, and the effectiveness of hatchery interventions remains fundamental to aquatic conservation and fisheries science, particularly regarding the balance between artificial propagation and natural reproductive success in wild populations.
