# Cyprinodontiform

## Summary
Cyprinodontiform refers to an order of fishes that includes the family Poeciliidae. According to this source, the Poeciliidae family (which contains Gambusia affinis) is a New World group of Cyprinodontiform fishes that likely originated in Central America.
