# 2,4-D

## Summary
2,4-D is one of twelve herbicide active ingredients reviewed for controlling nuisance aquatic vegetation in fishery management. It was among the trichlorophenoxy compounds evaluated for toxicity and safe application protocols in lakes, ponds, and streams.

## Context

> This 1961 special report by Charles L. Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
