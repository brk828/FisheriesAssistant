# Gila Ditaenia

## Summary
Gila ditaenia, commonly known as the Yaqui chub, is a small cyprinid fish endemic to the Rio Yaqui drainage in Mexico and historically present in Arizona, that serves as a conservation priority species in southwestern U.S. fisheries management due to its restricted range and threatened habitat. This species exemplifies the challenges faced by desert fish conservation programs, particularly regarding the development and implementation of recovery plans for imperiled native fishes in arid regions where water management and habitat degradation pose significant threats to persistence.
