# East Fork White River

## Summary
The East Fork White River is a tributary system within the White River drainage that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly loach minnows and other endemic fishes adapted to swift, cool-water conditions. This river segment is characterized by large rubble substrates, clear low-flow conditions during certain periods, and riparian vegetation dominated by willows and cottonwoods that provide essential structural complexity and thermal regulation for aquatic communities. The East Fork White River represents an important study site for monitoring fish abundance, distribution, and habitat conditions as part of broader conservation efforts for threatened and endangered species in the southwestern United States.
