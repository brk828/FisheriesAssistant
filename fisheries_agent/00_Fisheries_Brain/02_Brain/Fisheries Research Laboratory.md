# Fisheries Research Laboratory

## Summary
The Fisheries Research Laboratory at Southern Illinois University was the facility where this study was conducted. The laboratory maintained nine experimental tanks, each containing 200 liters of continuously aerated water under 24-hour lighting, for testing the experimental fish diets on Channel Catfish.

## Context

> This 1973 study evaluated two experimental fish diets combining roasted soybeans with fresh [[Gizzard Shad]] (Dorosoma cepedianum) as alternatives to commercial [[Oregon Pellets]] for feeding yearling [[Channel Catfish]] (Ictalurus punctatus).
