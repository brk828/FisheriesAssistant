# Carcharhinidae

## Summary
Carcharhinidae is a family of sharks commonly known as requiem sharks, characterized by their streamlined bodies, specialized gill slits, and nictitating membranes (protective eyelids), though they are primarily marine species and not typically found in the freshwater systems documented in these ichthyological checklists and identification keys of North American inland waters. While the family may be referenced in comprehensive taxonomic frameworks or marine-freshwater connectivity discussions, it does not represent a significant component of the freshwater fish fauna of Texas, the Pecos River, or Mexican inland drainages that are the focus of these source documents.
