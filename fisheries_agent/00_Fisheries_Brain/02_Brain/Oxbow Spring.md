# Oxbow Spring

## Summary
Oxbow Spring (Site #56) was a reintroduction site where Gila Topminnow failed by 1985-1987. Cold temperatures in the steep, wooded canyon were suspected as the cause of failure, and the site met extirpation criteria prior to 1995.
