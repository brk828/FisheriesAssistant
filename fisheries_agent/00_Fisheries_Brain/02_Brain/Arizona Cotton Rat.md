# Arizona Cotton Rat

## Summary
The Arizona Cotton Rat (Sigmodon arizonae) is a nocturnal rodent that showed highly variable population densities (1.2-33.9 individuals/hectare) in Riparian Forest habitat. This species shows rapid population response to favorable conditions and may breed year-round starting at 6 weeks of age, contributing significantly to the high biomass values recorded in riparian habitats.
