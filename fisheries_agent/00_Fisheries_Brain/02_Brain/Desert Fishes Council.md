# Desert Fishes Council

## Summary
The Desert Fishes Council is a professional organization of ichthyologists, conservation biologists, and resource managers dedicated to the study and conservation of native fishes in arid and semi-arid environments, particularly in the southwestern United States. The Council serves as a collaborative forum for addressing the complex ecological and management challenges facing endemic and endangered desert fish species that inhabit isolated aquatic systems such as springs, spring-fed pools, and fragmented stream reaches. Through meetings, scientific discourse, and formal advocacy, the organization works to promote research-based conservation strategies and coordinate management efforts among academic institutions, government agencies, and environmental organizations to prevent extinction and restore populations of these highly vulnerable species adapted to extreme desert habitats.
