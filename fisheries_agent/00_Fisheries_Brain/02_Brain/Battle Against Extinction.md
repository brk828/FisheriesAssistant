# Battle Against Extinction

## Summary
"Battle Against Extinction" refers to a comprehensive conservation initiative and documentary work addressing the critical threats facing desert fish species in the American Southwest, particularly those species facing extinction due to habitat loss, water management conflicts, and environmental degradation. The concept encompasses both the scientific and policy efforts undertaken by interagency organizations and the Desert Fishes Council to protect endemic and endangered aquatic species through coordinated research, advocacy, and publication of findings that inform conservation strategy. This work represents a pivotal moment in aquatic conservation biology where scientific expertise, institutional collaboration, and public awareness were mobilized to combat the accelerating loss of unique desert fish biodiversity in the region.
