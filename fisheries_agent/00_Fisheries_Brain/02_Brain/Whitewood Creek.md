# Whitewood Creek

## Summary
Whitewood Creek in South Dakota was heavily polluted with rock powder from mining operations, reaching 22% settleable solids by volume and turbidity above 720 APHA units. This pollution completely eliminated all fish species from the creek, with fish populations only reappearing when pollution was reduced to 3% by volume.
