# Bates Well

## Summary
Bates Well is a tank (artificial water impoundment) located in Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument, Arizona, that was used as a refugium site for Desert Pupfish conservation. It was one of several backup populations established to protect the species following the pesticide-related population crash at Quitobaquito in 1976.
