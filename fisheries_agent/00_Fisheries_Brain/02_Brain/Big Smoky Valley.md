# Big Smoky Valley

## Summary
Big Smoky Valley is a Nevada location that supports Rhinichthys osculus lariversi and two undescribed Gila bicolor subspecies. This source identifies it as one of the valleys where endemic fish populations would be directly influenced by the MX Missile System construction and groundwater removal.
