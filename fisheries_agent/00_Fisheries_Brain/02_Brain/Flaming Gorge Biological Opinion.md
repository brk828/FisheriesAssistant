# Flaming Gorge Biological Opinion

## Summary
The Flaming Gorge Biological Opinion addresses how Flaming Gorge reservoir operations impact downstream endangered fish habitat in the Upper Colorado River Basin. As of April 1990, the draft opinion had been extended to approximately August 1, 1990, due to concurrent consultation on the Animas-La Plata project, and the Bureau of Reclamation was considering recommending the opinion be held in abeyance for further study at an estimated cost of $100,000.
