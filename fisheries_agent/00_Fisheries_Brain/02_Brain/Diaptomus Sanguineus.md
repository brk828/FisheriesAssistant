# Diaptomus Sanguineus

## Summary
Diaptomus sanguineus is a calanoid copepod species that represents a smaller-bodied member of the diaptomid fauna in North American freshwater ecosystems. This species occurs across various pond and lentic habitats and is notable for its comparative size relationships within the genus, being distinctly smaller than related species such as D. clavipes. As a zooplankton component of freshwater communities, D. sanguineus plays an important ecological role in aquatic food webs and serves as a useful indicator organism for characterizing copepod assemblage composition across different water body types and geographic regions.
