# Exotic Species

## Summary
Exotic species in fish science refers to fish or other aquatic organisms that have been introduced to a region from outside their native geographic range, typically through human action. These species are distinguished by having their entire native distribution outside the country or region where they are now found, and they pose significant threats to native aquatic ecosystems, wildlife populations, and human interests including agriculture, health, and safety. The introduction of exotic fish species has become a major conservation concern in aquatic ecology due to their potential to outcompete native species, alter habitat structure, and disrupt ecological processes in isolated or sensitive aquatic systems.
