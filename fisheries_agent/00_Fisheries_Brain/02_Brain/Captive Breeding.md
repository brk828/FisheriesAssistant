# Captive Breeding

## Summary
The Captive Breeding Specialist Group is one of five disciplinary groups within SSC that addresses thematic conservation issues across taxa. Its specific mandate is to focus on establishing viable populations through captive propagation and intensive management. The group has its own office in Minneapolis, USA, and works on cross-cutting captive breeding issues rather than being organized by taxonomy.
