# Fouquieria

## Summary
Fouquieria (ocotillo) is a characteristic desert shrub that Blumer used as an indicator species for tracking vegetation zones. He noted the reappearance of Fouquieria at the mouth of Leslie Canyon in association with limestone formations, marking the return of desert vegetation after its absence across the valley floor.
