# Papago Park Military Reservation

## Summary
Papago Park Military Reservation (PPMR) is a military facility located in Phoenix, Arizona, characterized by weathered buttes, bajadas, and desert terraces within the Sonoran Desertscrub ecosystem. A 1994 biological assessment documented 36 native bird species, 13 native mammal species, 8 reptile species, and extensive native flora, with buttes and bajadas ranked as the most sensitive habitats supporting the greatest species diversity.
