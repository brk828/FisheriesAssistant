# Floy Tags

## Summary
Floy Tags are external spaghetti-style identification tags that were used in previous years to mark razorback suckers in Lake Mohave. By 1991, approximately 2,250 of the estimated 65,000 adult razorbacks in the lake had been tagged primarily with these external tags, and three recaptured fish in the 1991 study bore Floy tags from the previous year (1990).
