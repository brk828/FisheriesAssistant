# Quitobaquito Snail

## Summary
Quitobaquito Snail (Tryonia quitobaquitae) is a species found at Quitobaquito Springs in Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. According to this 1996 comment letter, the snail faces potentially dire consequences from human visitation impacts, with its continued existence as a species potentially jeopardized by current and proposed management practices that lack visitor use restrictions or carrying capacity analysis.
