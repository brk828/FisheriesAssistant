# Texas Softshell

## Summary
The Texas Softshell is an introduced turtle species documented at the reservoir sites in Arizona-New Mexico. Along with the Common Snapping Turtle, this source identifies it as one of only two species predicted to benefit from reservoir construction, indicating it would adapt well to impounded waters.
