# Abernathy Creek

## Summary
Abernathy Creek is the water source for the Abernathy Channel, supplying water through a quarter-mile pipeline to the incubation facility. This source reports that natural spawning in Abernathy Creek itself had only 0.58% survival (estimated 16,700 migrants from 2,880,000 eggs), far lower than the 75-78.5% survival achieved in the artificial channel using the same water source.
