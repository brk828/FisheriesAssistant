# Surber Sampler

## Summary
The Surber Sampler is a quantitative sampling device used to collect benthic invertebrates from stream substrates. This study used Surber samplers to conduct systematic bottom fauna surveys at six stations along the Red Cedar River from April through June 1955, collecting samples from comparable gravel and rock substrates at 12-18 inches depth to assess pollution impacts on invertebrate communities.
