# Amiidae

## Summary
Amiidae is a family of primitive holostean fishes represented by the single living genus *Amia* (bowfin, *Amia calva*), which represents an archaic lineage that has persisted in North American freshwaters while becoming extinct in Palearctic regions. The bowfin is taxonomically significant as a living fossil demonstrating morphological and physiological characteristics intermediate between early ray-finned fishes and modern teleosts, including the retention of an air bladder used for respiration and a heterocercal tail. In conservation and ecological contexts, *Amia calva* serves as an indicator species for freshwater ecosystem health and represents an important component of North American ichthyofauna for understanding fish evolution and biogeographic distribution patterns.
