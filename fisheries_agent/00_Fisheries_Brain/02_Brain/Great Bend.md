# Great Bend

## Summary
Great Bend is a canyon-bound habitat section within the Lake Powell inflow area of the San Juan River. This source describes it as having homogeneous shorelines with sand and silt substrates, and notes that acoustic detection efficiency there was 0.82 ± 0.05, lower than in the adjacent Neskahi Bay area.
