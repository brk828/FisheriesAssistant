# Cottonwood Landing

## Summary
Cottonwood Landing is a location in Nevada on Lake Mohave where the last documented collection of young bonytail chub occurred on June 15, 1950. This site represents the most recent evidence of successful bonytail chub reproduction in the Lake Mohave system.
