# Great Basin Complex

## Summary
The Great Basin Complex in Reno is a facility where 2,903 Cui-ui larvae collected during the 1987 emigration survey were stored. This represents the repository for specimens gathered during the spawning run monitoring at Marble Bluff Fish Facility.

## Context

> This report documents the 1987 spawning runs of the endangered [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) and threatened [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]] (*Salmo clarki henshawi*) at [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]], [[Nixon, Nevada]].
