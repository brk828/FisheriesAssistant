# West Virginia

## Summary
West Virginia is the state where this pond management research was conducted during 1955-1956 under Project F-3-R-4 by the West Virginia Conservation Commission. The study examined various bass-sunfish stocking ratios across multiple counties, with water chemistry showing alkaline conditions typical of the region (pH 7.2-8.2, alkalinity 25-35 ppm), and concluded that rainbow trout stocking was not recommended for small West Virginia impoundments due to high summer temperatures.
