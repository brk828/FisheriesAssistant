# Asteromeyenia radiospiculata

## Summary
Asteromeyenia radiospiculata is one of three freshwater sponge species identified in Alabama fish ponds, specifically found at the U.S. Fish Cultural Station in Marion, Alabama. It is a member of the family Spongillidae that inhabits freshwater ponds.
