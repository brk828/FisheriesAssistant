# Yaqui

## Summary
The Rio Yaqui is a major river system in Mexico draining to the Pacific Ocean. This source documents it as habitat for numerous native fish species and notes introductions of Ameiurus melas, Ictalurus furcatus, and possibly Pylodictis olivaris, as well as containing Oncorhynchus species of uncertain taxonomic status.
