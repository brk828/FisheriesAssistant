# Stewart Lake Waterfowl Management Area

## Summary
Stewart Lake Waterfowl Management Area is a wetland area in the Upper Colorado River Basin that was surveyed for contaminants as part of the endangered fish recovery program. The 1986-1989 reconnaissance surveys found moderate selenium levels in American coots and highly elevated selenium concentrations in aquatic plants and fish at this location.
