# Wilderness Designation

## Summary
Wilderness designation is a federal land classification that provides the highest level of protection for natural areas. This option was considered but not analyzed in detail for the San Pedro River area because the lands failed to meet wilderness requirements.

## Context

> The [[Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) released a Draft San Pedro River Management Plan and Environmental Impact Statement for public review in 1988. The plan addresses 47,668 acres along a 36-mile stretch of the upper [[San Pedro River]] near [[Sierra Vista]] in [[Cochise County]], [[Arizona]]....
