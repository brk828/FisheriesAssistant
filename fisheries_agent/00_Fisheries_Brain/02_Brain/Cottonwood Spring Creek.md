# Cottonwood Spring Creek

## Summary
Cottonwood Spring Creek (Site #55) is a reintroduction site where Gila Topminnow failed in 1985 for unknown reasons. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service completed Section 7 consultation in 1990 but never restocked the site, and conditions were found suitable for reintroduction in both 1994 and 1996.
