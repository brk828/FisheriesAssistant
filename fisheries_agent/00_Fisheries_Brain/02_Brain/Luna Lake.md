# Luna Lake

## Summary
Luna Lake is a high-elevation (7,880 ft) montane reservoir in Alpine Valley, Arizona, that served as a representative study site for Arizona Game and Fish Department fisheries management research during the 1950s-1960s. This shallow, well-mixed reservoir supported a productive put-and-take rainbow trout fishery but was subject to seasonal environmental stressors, particularly winter winterkill events caused by extremely low dissolved oxygen levels beneath ice cover. The lake was used as a case study for evaluating various trout management strategies, including comparative stocking protocols and responses to irrigation-driven water level fluctuations typical of managed reservoirs in the region.
