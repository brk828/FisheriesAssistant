# Ecuador

## Summary
Ecuador is the country where Rhoadsia minor was collected, specifically from mountain streams at 4,000 feet elevation in Mindo, Province of Pichincha, in the Rio Esmeraldas drainage system. The species was also reported from the Chota valley in northern Ecuador at approximately 4,900 feet elevation.
