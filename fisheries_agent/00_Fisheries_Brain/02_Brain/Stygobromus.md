# Stygobromus

## Summary
Stygobromus is a genus of small, blind or reduced-eyed amphipod crustaceans endemic to subterranean aquatic environments, particularly cave systems and groundwater habitats in limestone and dolomite regions. These organisms are highly specialized stygobites (cave-obligate species) with restricted geographic distributions tied to specific geological formations, making them important indicators of groundwater ecology and conservation concerns related to cave ecosystem integrity. Their presence and diversity patterns reflect habitat specialization and sensitivity to environmental changes in subsurface aquatic systems, rendering them valuable subjects for studying subterranean biodiversity and assessing the impacts of human activities on cave ecosystems.
