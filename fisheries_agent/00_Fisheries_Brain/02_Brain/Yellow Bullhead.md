# Yellow Bullhead

## Summary
The Yellow Bullhead (*Ameiurus natalis*) is a small catfish species (family Ictaluridae) native to eastern North America that has become established in western aquatic systems, particularly in the Colorado River Basin and associated reservoirs. In ecological contexts, it is documented as a non-native species in constructed and natural lentic habitats where it exhibits high tolerance to chemical stressors, including resistance to fish toxicants used in eradication efforts targeting invasive species. Its presence in western water bodies is of conservation concern because it may compete with or predate upon native fish species, particularly in managed refuge systems designed for endangered endemic fishes.
