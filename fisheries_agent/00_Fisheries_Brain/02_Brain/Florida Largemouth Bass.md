# Florida Largemouth Bass

## Summary
The Florida Largemouth Bass (*Micropterus salmoides floridanus*) is a subspecies of largemouth bass endemic to Florida that is managed as a genetically distinct stock separate from the Northern Largemouth Bass due to documented morphological and genetic differences. This subspecies is of significant conservation and fisheries management importance because historical hatchery stocking practices have created intergrade populations and genetic introgression in various regions, making genetic marker identification and broodstock selection critical tools for maintaining population integrity and managing wild stocks. The Florida Largemouth Bass serves as a model organism in ichthyology research for studying subspecific differentiation, the effects of human-mediated genetic mixing on wild populations, and the application of genetic techniques to fish conservation and management.
