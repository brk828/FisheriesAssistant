# Australasia

## Summary
Australasia was a geographic region from which Dr. Richard Wood provided Characeae specimens for the cytotaxonomic study. These materials contributed to the international scope of the research through collaborative specimen exchange.

## Context

> This progress report covers research conducted under NSF Research Grant G-19449 (continuation of NSF-G13261) from January 1, 1962, documenting cytotaxonomic investigations of the [[Characeae]] (stoneworts) at the [[University of Louisville]].
