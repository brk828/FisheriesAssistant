# Caroga Lake

## Summary
Caroga Lake is a New York State lake where red salmon (sockeye salmon, Oncorhynchus nerka) were experimentally stocked. By 1953, immature red salmon were netted there, indicating survival but not yet reproductive maturity, and managers desired further angler exploration to assess the population.
