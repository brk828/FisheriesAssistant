# Lake St. Clair

## Summary
Lake St. Clair is a shallow freshwater lake located between Lake Huron and Lake Erie in the Great Lakes system, serving as a critical habitat and fishery for warmwater species including muskellunge and other economically important fish. As a transitional water body connecting the upper and lower Great Lakes, it has been a focal point for ichthyological research and fisheries management, particularly regarding fish distribution, mercury bioaccumulation, and conservation of iconic species. The lake's ecological significance in the Great Lakes region makes it an important study site for understanding aquatic contaminant dynamics and fish population dynamics in North American freshwater systems.
