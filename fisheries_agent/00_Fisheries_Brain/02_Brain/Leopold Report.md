# Leopold Report

## Summary
The Leopold Report was an influential document that profoundly impacted natural resource management in the National Park System. According to this 1988 memorandum, the newly established NPCA Commission on Research and Resource Management Policy was undertaking a comprehensive effort that aimed to produce recommendations of equal or greater importance than the original Leopold Report.
