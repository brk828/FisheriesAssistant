# Invasive Fish Species Management

## Summary
Invasive Fish Species Management encompasses the scientific and practical strategies employed to control, monitor, and mitigate the ecological impacts of non-native fish species that establish themselves in aquatic ecosystems where they lack natural predators or competition. This management approach leverages knowledge of invasive species' habitat plasticity, reproductive capabilities, and competitive advantages—such as broad thermal tolerance and dietary flexibility—to implement interventions ranging from physical barriers and chemical controls to population monitoring programs. Effective invasive fish species management is critical for protecting native fish communities and maintaining ecosystem integrity in freshwater environments across diverse habitat types.
