# East Indian

## Summary
The East Indian region is a zoogeographic area distinct from the Philippine Subregion. This source notes that despite intensive dredging, the Albatross expedition failed to find many East Indies macrourid species in the Philippine Subregion, while the Philippine waters contained numerous endemic forms not found in the East Indies, supporting the recognition of these as separate zoogeographic units.
