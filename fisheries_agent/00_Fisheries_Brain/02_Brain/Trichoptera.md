# Trichoptera

## Summary
Trichoptera, commonly known as caddisflies, are an order of freshwater aquatic insects whose larval and pupal stages are crucial components of benthic macroinvertebrate communities in streams and rivers. These organisms are significant indicators of water quality and serve as important food sources for freshwater fish species, making them essential links in aquatic food webs and key organisms in ecological assessments of lotic environments. The presence and abundance of Trichoptera in macroinvertebrate surveys provides valuable information for evaluating stream health and understanding fish feeding ecology and resource availability.
