# Puget Sound

## Summary
Puget Sound is a large estuarine system in Washington State that serves as critical nursery habitat and migration corridor for Pacific salmon species, particularly Chinook (king) salmon, where complex bathymetry, tidal dynamics, and tributary systems like Kennedy Creek and Totten Inlet influence smolt rearing and emigration patterns. As a major component of the North Pacific drainage ecosystem, Puget Sound is central to understanding salmon stock dynamics, hatchery operations, and conservation efforts aimed at sustaining both wild and hatchery-origin populations in the region.
