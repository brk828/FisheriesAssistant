# Idaho Department of Fish and Game

## Summary
The Idaho Department of Fish and Game is a state wildlife management agency that provided kokanee salmon eggs for the Lake Tahoe introduction program. These eggs were used to raise fingerlings at hatcheries in California and Nevada for the 1950 stocking effort of 613,475 fish.
