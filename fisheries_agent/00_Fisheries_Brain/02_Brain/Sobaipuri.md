# Sobaipuri

## Summary
The Sobaipuri were agricultural communities that occupied the Aravaipa Creek area during the Spanish contact period. They represent one of several waves of human occupation spanning over 9,000 years that likely influenced mammal populations through habitat modification and resource use.
