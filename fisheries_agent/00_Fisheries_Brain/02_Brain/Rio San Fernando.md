# Rio San Fernando

## Summary
The Rio San Fernando is a Mexican river system where Ictalurus lupus occurs, first documented by Meek in 1904. This coastal drainage population shows relatively high vertebral counts (mean 46.4-46.9) compared to upstream Rio Grande basin populations, fitting a geographic pattern of increasing vertebral counts in lower tributaries and coastal drainages.
