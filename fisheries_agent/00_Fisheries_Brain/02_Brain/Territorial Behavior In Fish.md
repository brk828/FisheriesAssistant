# Territorial Behavior In Fish

## Summary
Territorial behavior in fish refers to the aggressive defense and active maintenance of specific aquatic spaces by individual fish or groups, typically to secure access to resources such as food, breeding sites, or shelter. This behavior is particularly pronounced during reproductive periods and involves ritualized displays, combat, or exclusion of conspecifics from defined areas. Understanding territorial behavior is essential for fisheries management and conservation, as it influences population dynamics, breeding success, and spatial distribution patterns within aquatic ecosystems.
