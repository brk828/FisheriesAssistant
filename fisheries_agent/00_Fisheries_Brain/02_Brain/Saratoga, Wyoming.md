# Saratoga, Wyoming

## Summary
Saratoga, Wyoming was the location of Lake Creek Lake, a 1.8-acre spring-fed lake used in antimycin A trials. The site's clear water conditions allowed researchers to directly observe fish behavior and confirm that fish showed no alarm or escape attempts when exposed to the toxicant.
