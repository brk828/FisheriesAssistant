# Petersen Disk Tags

## Summary
Petersen Disk Tags are external fish tags consisting of two disks connected by a wire that passes through the fish's body. In this study, they were made of cellulose nitrate disks one-half inch in diameter and 0.045 inch thick, attached with 0.032 inch diameter wire. These tags were attached to the caudal peduncle of striped bass and demonstrated good retention rates, with some tags remaining attached for up to 1,005 days.
