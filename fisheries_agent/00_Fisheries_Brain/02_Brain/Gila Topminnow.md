# Gila Topminnow

## Summary
The Gila Topminnow (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*) is a small, federally endangered livebearing fish endemic to the Gila River drainage of the southwestern United States, notable for its ecological role in desert aquatic systems and its conservation significance as a species at high risk of extinction. As a native desert fish species, it has become a focal point for recovery efforts coordinated by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team and is subject to intensive management under the Endangered Species Act, including research programs and habitat restoration initiatives. The species represents a critical conservation challenge in southwestern aquatic ecology due to its restricted range, sensitivity to habitat degradation, and vulnerability to competition and predation from introduced species.
