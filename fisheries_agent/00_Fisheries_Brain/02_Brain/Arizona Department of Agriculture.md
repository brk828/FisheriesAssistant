# Arizona Department of Agriculture

## Summary
The Arizona Department of Agriculture is a state agency participating in the multi-agency consortium studying the endangered Pima pineapple cactus. Specifically, its Native Plant Program is working alongside the US Fish and Wildlife Service, National Biological Survey, University of Arizona, and other researchers to conduct ecological research supporting conservation and management of this rare species.

## Context

> The [[Pima Pineapple Cactus]] (*Coryphantha scheeri* var. *robustispina*) was listed as endangered by the [[US Fish and Wildlife Service]] in October 1993.
