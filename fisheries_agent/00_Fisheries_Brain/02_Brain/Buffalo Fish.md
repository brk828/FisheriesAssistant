# Buffalo Fish

## Summary
Buffalo fish (Ictiobus spp.) are large, commercially valuable freshwater cyprinids native to North American river systems that have historically served as important species in commercial fisheries and aquaculture operations. These bottom-feeding filter feeders are ecologically significant as they can reach substantial sizes and occupy important niches in riverine and lacustrine ecosystems, though their commercial harvest and management have been subject to regulatory frameworks governing native species exploitation. In the context of Arizona's wildlife regulations, buffalo fish represent a category of commercial aquatic fauna whose harvest and trade have been historically grandfathered into permitted activities under evolving conservation and fisheries management policies.
