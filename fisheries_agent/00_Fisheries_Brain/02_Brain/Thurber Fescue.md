# Thurber Fescue

## Summary
Thurber Fescue is a herbaceous vegetation species found in well-protected areas of western Colorado. This source notes it grows in oak brushlands where ground surfaces are covered by humus and litter, and where infiltration rates are nearly 5 times higher than nearby sagebrush sites where gullies are abundant.
