# Rome Hatchery

## Summary
Rome Hatchery is a fish hatchery facility in New York State that served as a source of brown trout fingerlings for this toxicity study. The hatchery's springwater, characterized by alkalinity of approximately 100 p.p.m. and pH ranging from 7.5 to 8.28, was used as one of two test waters in the experiments comparing cyanide toxicity under different water chemistry conditions.
