# Orestiidae

## Summary
Orestiidae is a fish family designation that was proposed by Eigenmann in 1920 as a placement for the genus Empetrichthys. This taxonomic classification was part of the historical debate about where to place the Pahrump Killifish, though the species is now classified in family Goodeidae.
