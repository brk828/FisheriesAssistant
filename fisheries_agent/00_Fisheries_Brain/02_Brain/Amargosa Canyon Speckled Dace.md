# Amargosa Canyon Speckled Dace

## Summary
Amargosa Canyon Speckled Dace is a subspecies of speckled dace found in California's desert basins. This source recommends it for endangered species listing as part of the Desert basins regional cluster, representing one of the small freshwater dispersant fishes in isolated desert habitats that require conservation protection.
