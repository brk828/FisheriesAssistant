# Endemism

## Summary
Endemism in fish science refers to the occurrence of fish species or populations that are found exclusively within a limited geographic area, such as an isolated basin or water system, and nowhere else in the world. This concept is particularly significant in conservation biology because endemic fish populations, especially those restricted to small areas like isolated desert springs or intermontane valleys, are inherently vulnerable to extinction due to their limited geographic range and restricted habitat. Understanding and protecting endemic fish species is a critical priority for aquatic conservation, as these organisms often represent unique evolutionary lineages with high conservation value.
