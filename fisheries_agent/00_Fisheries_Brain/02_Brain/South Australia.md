# South Australia

## Summary
South Australia is the Australian state containing St. Vincent Gulf, where Lernaeenicus hemirhamphi, one of only two lernaeocerid copepods previously recorded from Australia, was found. This highlights the scarcity of lernaeocerid records from Australian waters prior to the description of Impexus hamondi.
