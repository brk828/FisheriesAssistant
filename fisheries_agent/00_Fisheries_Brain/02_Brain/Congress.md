# Congress

## Summary
Congress is the legislative branch of the United States government that enacted the Endangered Species Act (ESA). According to this legal brief, Congress recognized habitat destruction as "the major cause for the extinction of species worldwide" when creating the ESA and intended that endangered species receive "the highest of priorities" under the law.
