# Surprise Creek

## Summary
Surprise Creek is a tributary inflow to the Lower Colorado River downstream of Glen Canyon Dam that was monitored during the Hualapai Aquatic Resources Study as part of research examining fish community responses to interim flow regimes from the dam. This small tributary system served as a sampling location where researchers documented fish captures and aquatic resource conditions, with particular attention to native fish species like Flannelmouth Sucker whose populations showed notable responses to variations in tributary discharge and mainstem water conditions.
