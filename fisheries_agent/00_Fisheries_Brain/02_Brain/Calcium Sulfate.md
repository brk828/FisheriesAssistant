# Calcium Sulfate

## Summary
Calcium sulfate, commonly known as gypsum, is a flocculating agent used to clear muddy fishponds by settling suspended clay particles. This source recommends applying 15 pounds per 1,000 cubic feet of water in chronically turbid ponds, with the treatment remaining effective for one year or longer under stable conditions.
