# Creel Census Methodology

## Summary
Creel census methodology is a systematic sampling technique used to estimate fish harvest and angler effort by surveying fishermen and their catches at access points or during fishing trips. This approach provides quantitative data on species composition, size distribution, and total removal of fish populations, making it essential for monitoring recreational fishery impacts and informing management decisions regarding catch regulations and conservation strategies.
