# Bluegill Sunfish

## Summary
Bluegill sunfish (*Lepomis macrochirus*) is a small freshwater centrarchid species widely used as a model organism in aquatic ecology and fisheries research due to its abundance, ease of capture, and responsiveness to environmental conditions. As a primary consumer of aquatic macroinvertebrates, bluegill serves as an important indicator species for assessing water quality, food web dynamics, and the productivity of lentic ecosystems. Their population structure, growth rates, and production levels are frequently monitored in experimental and natural systems to evaluate the effects of habitat modifications, nutrient enrichment, and management practices on fish community health.
