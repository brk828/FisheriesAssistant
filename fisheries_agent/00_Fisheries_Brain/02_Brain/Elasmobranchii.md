# Elasmobranchii

## Summary
Elasmobranchii, also known as Selachii, represents the major taxonomic group comprising sharks, rays, and skates, distinguished from bony fishes (Teleostomi/Osteichthyes) by their cartilaginous skeletons, placoid scales, and lack of an operculum and air bladder. This group is ecologically and evolutionarily significant in aquatic ecosystems, occupying diverse marine and some freshwater niches as apex and mesopredators, though they are increasingly subject to conservation concern due to their slow reproductive rates and vulnerability to overfishing. Understanding Elasmobranchii's anatomical, physiological, and ecological characteristics is fundamental to comparative ichthyology and essential for developing effective conservation and fisheries management strategies for these long-lived organisms.
