# Sonoran Mud Turtles

## Summary
Sonoran Mud Turtles (Kinosternon sonoriense) are strongly aquatic southwestern turtles, with the Quitobaquito population representing an endemic subspecies (K. s. longifemorale) called the Rio Sonoyta Mud Turtle. This population declined from hundreds in the 1950s to approximately 100 by the early 1980s due to loss of shallow juvenile habitat and severe nutritional stress (mean body fat only 2.81%), but showed recovery to ~130 individuals by 1993 following habitat restoration that created shallow, vegetated channels.
