# Columbia River Basin Management

## Summary
Columbia River Basin management concerns regarding Northern Pike include threats to all fisheries in the basin, significant investments in fish passage and habitat restoration, and risks of further range expansion. The Box Canyon Reservoir invasion originated from upstream Montana/Idaho sources or illegal introductions, with continued illegal stocking and natural expansion remaining ongoing threats that could spread impacts throughout the basin.
