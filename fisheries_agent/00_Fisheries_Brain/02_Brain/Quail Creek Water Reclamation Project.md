# Quail Creek Water Reclamation Project

## Summary
The Quail Creek Water Reclamation Project was a water infrastructure project under construction as of 1986 that would divert flood flows from the Virgin River for storage. The project was designed to ensure year-round minimum releases of 86 cubic feet per second, and was identified as one of the existing projects causing cumulative impacts on Virgin River Roundtail Chub habitat through water withdrawal, discharge pattern changes, pollution, sedimentation, and channel modification.
