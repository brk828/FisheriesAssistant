# Cooper and Benson

## Summary
Cooper and Benson's 1951 study found minimal growth effects from tagging in hatchery trout, contrasting with this study's findings. While Cooper and Benson observed little impact, this Kentucky study found that jaw-tagged largemouth bass grew only 0.5 inches versus 1.2 inches for untagged fish, suggesting species-specific or tag-type differences in growth impacts.
