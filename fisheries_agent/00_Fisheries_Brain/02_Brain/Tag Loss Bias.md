# Tag Loss Bias

## Summary
Tag loss bias is mentioned in the context of aging methods for Shoal Bass. This source notes that scales may underestimate age in older fish due to regeneration and that false annuli occur, though mechanisms are unclear, suggesting potential biases in age-based population assessments.
