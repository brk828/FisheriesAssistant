# Ocoee River

## Summary
The Ocoee River is a stream system in Tennessee where introgression between Redeye Bass and Smallmouth Bass is highly probable. This assessment is based on sampling conducted 40+ years after initial Redeye Bass stocking events in the region, with hybridization detected through electrophoretic and meristic analysis.
