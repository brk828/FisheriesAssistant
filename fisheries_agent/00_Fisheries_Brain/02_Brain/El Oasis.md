# El Oasis

## Summary
El Oasis is a location near the northeast end of Laguna Salada in the Colorado River delta region of Mexico. During the November 1990 survey, the area near El Oasis was completely dry with no water visible anywhere in the laguna, and windrows of dead fish from a 1987 drying event were still evident on the ground.

## Context

> This trip report documents a November 10-12, 1990 field survey of known [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius macularius*) populations in the [[Colorado River Delta]] region of Sonora and Baja California, Mexico.
