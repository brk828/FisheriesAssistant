# West Fork San Luis Rey River

## Summary
The West Fork San Luis Rey River (WFSLRR) is a second-order headwater stream in San Diego County, California, with a bank-full width generally less than 4 meters that becomes intermittent in summer in high-gradient sections. This source describes it as the location of a successful Black Bullhead removal project where the invasive catfish concentrated in 16 large perennial pools during the 2012-2016 California drought, and where a 1.5-meter upstream dam likely prevented downstream movement of Black Bullhead from a source stock pond. High-flow events in 2017 and 2019 helped flush remaining invasive fish and improved spawning substrate for the native Coastal Rainbow Trout population by removing accumulated fine sediments.
