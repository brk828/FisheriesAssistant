# Cataract Pond

## Summary
Cataract Pond is a small impoundment in Cataract, Wisconsin located downstream of Rathbone Creek that served as a detoxification demonstration site. The pond was drained to low level and refilled 18 hours before antimycin-laden water from upstream treatments reached its spillway, then treated with 2 ppm potassium permanganate to achieve quick and complete detoxification that protected rainbow trout in live cages downstream.
