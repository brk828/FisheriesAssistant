# Des Moines River

## Summary
The Des Moines River is a warmwater riverine system in Boone County, Iowa, that has served as an important field study location for fisheries research, particularly regarding fish movement patterns, angler harvest rates, and population dynamics across multiple species. The river is characterized by the presence of lowhead dams that restrict upstream fish passage, making it a relevant system for studying how physical barriers affect fish distribution and movement ecology. As a focal study area for the Iowa Cooperative Fisheries Research Unit, the Des Moines River has provided valuable empirical data on fish behavior, catchability, and population responses to fishing pressure in managed warmwater river systems.
