# Black Willow

## Summary
Black Willow (Salix nigra) is a tree species that forms part of the forested riparian habitat around Old River. It is one of three dominant tree species mentioned (along with bald cypress and black gum) that characterize the flooded forest environment of this oxbow lake system.
