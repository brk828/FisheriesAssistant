# Arrowhead Cove

## Summary
Arrowhead Cove is a location on the Arizona side of Lake Mohave, approximately 2.25 river miles above Katherine Landing. This source describes it as the site of Camp Five during a March 1988 sampling survey, selected for its accessibility via dirt road, protection from wave action, limited visitor traffic, and documented presence of razorback suckers. The cove was observed to have bow fishing activity and very few carp, raising concerns that razorback suckers might become targets for bow fishermen during daylight hours.
