# Field Museum of Natural History

## Summary
Field Museum of Natural History (FMNH) is one of the institutional collections that provided museum specimens of Gila nigrescens examined in this taxonomic study. The study drew on specimens from multiple museums to analyze geographic variation and taxonomic relationships within the Chihuahua Chub.
