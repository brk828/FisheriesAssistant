# Brazos River

## Summary
The Brazos River is a river system in Texas whose ancestral headwaters were captured by the Pecos River during the middle Pleistocene. This study suggests that this ancient drainage capture event likely brought Gambusia affinis into contact with Gambusia nobilis populations for the first time, potentially contributing to the geographic isolation and evolution of various species in the Gambusia nobilis group through competitive exclusion.
