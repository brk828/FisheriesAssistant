# Chiricahua Frog

## Summary
Chiricahua Frog (Rana chiricahuensis) is a threatened amphibian species found in the Sierra San Luis area that has experienced dramatic population declines and extirpations throughout its U.S. range over the last 15 years. The management plan identifies this species as requiring immediate status evaluation and recovery actions, as amphibian populations have declined throughout Arizona, California, and New Mexico, with many species considered extirpated from native localities.
