# Arnoldson Spring

## Summary
Arnoldson Spring is one of seven historic spring habitats in White Pine County, Nevada, that once supported White River Spinedace. This cool spring system with typical spinedace habitat characteristics (clear water, gravel/sand substrate, emergent vegetation) had its spinedace population extirpated by 1985, likely due to habitat manipulation and nonnative fish introductions that began in the mid-1800s.
