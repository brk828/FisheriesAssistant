# Travertine Canyon

## Summary
Travertine Canyon is a major tributary to the Colorado River in Stratum A of the study area (RM 226-239). This source identifies it as one of the tributaries in the steep, narrow canyon section characterized by swift deep runs, vertical cliffs and talus, and boulder-cobble-gravel substrate.
