# Lake Lowell

## Summary
Lake Lowell is a 10,000-acre off-river reservoir of the Boise River near Nampa in southwestern Idaho, operated by the Bureau of Reclamation for irrigation and managed as a national wildlife refuge supporting over one million overwintering waterfowl annually. The reservoir exhibits extreme eutrophication with phosphate concentrations reaching 1.45 ppm, dissolved solids averaging 310 ppm (versus 40-88 ppm in source water), prodigious blue-green algae blooms, and oxygen depletion in deeper waters, with its fishery deteriorating from excellent in 1960 to poor by 1965.
