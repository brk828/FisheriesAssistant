# Lake Herring

## Summary
Lake herring (*Coregonus artedii*), also known as cisco, is a small coregonine salmonid species characterized by a slender, silvery body with a translucent greenish back and deeply forked tail fin, commonly found in North American freshwater lakes. This species has historically been important for both commercial and ecological studies in aquatic systems, serving as a focal point for research on population dynamics, growth patterns, and the effects of environmental factors such as turbidity on fish communities. Lake herring populations have been extensively monitored and exploited, making them a valuable model organism for understanding changes in exploited fish populations and the broader ecological relationships within lacustrine ecosystems.
