# Jones Hole Creek

## Summary
Jones Hole Creek is a tributary to the Green River in Dinosaur National Monument. According to this 1987 recovery plan for the endangered Bonytail Chub, the Green River below Jones Hole Creek was identified as one of the recommended reintroduction sites for establishing restored populations of this critically declining native cyprinid species.

## Context

> This 1987 recovery plan addresses the critically endangered [[Bonytail Chub]] (*Gila elegans*), a large-river cyprinid native to the [[Colorado River Basin]]. Listed as endangered on April 23, 1980, the species appears close to extinction with no evidence of successful reproduction in recent years....
