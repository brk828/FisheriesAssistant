# Carrying Capacity

## Summary
Carrying capacity in fish science refers to the maximum population size of a fish species that a given aquatic habitat can sustainably support based on available resources such as food, spawning habitat, water quality, and space. This concept is essential for understanding population dynamics and setting realistic management goals, particularly in conservation contexts where natural carrying capacity may be limited by habitat constraints like suitable spawning substrate or inlet/outlet stream requirements. By estimating a waterbody's carrying capacity, fisheries managers can determine appropriate stocking levels and conservation strategies to maintain healthy, sustainable fish populations without exceeding the habitat's ability to support them.
