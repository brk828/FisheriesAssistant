# Crenichthys Baileyi

## Summary
Crenichthys baileyi, commonly known as the White River spinedace, is a small endemic cyprinid fish native to the White River drainage system in Nevada and Utah, making it a species of significant conservation concern due to its highly restricted range and vulnerability to habitat degradation. This species has been the subject of intensive research regarding endangered fish management, artificial hybridization studies for potential conservation breeding programs, and parasitic disease interactions including susceptibility to Asian fish tapeworms. As a threatened aquatic species, C. baileyi serves as an important model organism for understanding the ecological and biological challenges facing small, isolated fish populations in arid western North American watersheds.
