# Colorado River Wildlife Management Committee

## Summary
The Colorado River Wildlife Management Committee is a multi-agency cooperative body that coordinates fisheries and wildlife management across the Colorado River system. According to this 1963 meeting, its Technical Committee included representatives from Arizona, Nevada, California, Utah, Wyoming, the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife, and the National Park Service, who met to discuss cooperative fisheries investigations, stocking programs, and management activities across the Colorado River basin.
