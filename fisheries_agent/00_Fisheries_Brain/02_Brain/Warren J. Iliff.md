# Warren J. Iliff

## Summary
Warren J. Iliff served as Executive Director of The Phoenix Zoo in 1995. He led the preparation of the zoo's 1995-1997 strategic plan, which transformed the institution from a traditional animal exhibition facility into a mission-driven conservation communication organization with the new mission statement: 'We inspire people to live in ways that promote the well-being of the natural world.'
