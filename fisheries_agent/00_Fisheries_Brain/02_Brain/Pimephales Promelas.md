# Pimephales Promelas

## Summary
Pimephales promelas, commonly known as the fathead minnow, is a small cyprinid fish species widely distributed across North American freshwater systems that serves as an important component of aquatic food webs and is frequently encountered in ecological surveys of streams, creeks, and ponds. This species is notably used as both a bioindicator of aquatic community health and a model organism in ecotoxicological research due to its sensitivity to environmental changes and its role as a forage fish for larger predators. Its presence in diverse freshwater habitats, from desert streams to temperate pond systems, makes it a valuable subject for understanding larval fish ecology, predator-prey interactions, and the structure of aquatic communities in varying environmental conditions.
