# Quagga mussel

## Summary
Quagga mussel (Dreissena bugensis) is an invasive mollusk that established in Lake Mohave around 2006, though it was first recorded in Lake Mead in 2007 and likely established in the lower Colorado River basin by 2003. This source notes that the quagga mussel invasion coincided with a final shift in the fish community composition around 2006, though its causal role remains uncertain; mussels can take up to 12 years post-invasion to reach maximum population size when their largest direct ecological effects occur.
