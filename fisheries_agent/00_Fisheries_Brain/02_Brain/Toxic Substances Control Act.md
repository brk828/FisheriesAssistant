# Toxic Substances Control Act

## Summary
TSCA is a federal law regulating chemical substances and their risks. This source mentions that EPA determined two of the twenty conditionally-retained mineral processing wastes (phosphogypsum and phosphoric acid process wastewater) should be addressed under TSCA rather than under RCRA Subtitle D mining waste regulations.
