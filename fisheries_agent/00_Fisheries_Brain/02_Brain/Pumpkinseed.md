# Pumpkinseed

## Summary
The pumpkinseed (*Lepomis gibbosus*) is a centrarchid sunfish species commonly encountered in freshwater lakes and ponds across North America, classified as a food fish in fisheries management contexts. In mid-20th century fisheries research, pumpkinseeds were studied as representative panfish populations to understand mortality dynamics, including natural mortality rates and fishing pressure impacts on population sustainability. This species served as an important indicator organism in ichthyological investigations of pesticide impacts and general fish population ecology in temperate aquatic systems.
