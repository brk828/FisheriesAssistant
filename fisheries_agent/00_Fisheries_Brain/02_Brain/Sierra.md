# Sierra

## Summary
Sierra refers to mackerel species (Scomber spp.) that appear seasonally in the El Golfo de Santa Clara area. This source identifies sierra as one of only three scale fish species (along with shark and mullet) that are relatively common in this part of the Gulf of California, though scale fish overall are rare in the region.
