# Phalloceros

## Summary
Phalloceros is a new genus proposed by Eigenmann with Girardinus caudomaculatus Hensel as the type species. It is distinguished by antler-like claspers at the tip of the second prolonged anal ray, an alimentary canal more than twice entire length, and the fourth anal ray ending in an antrorse point with two large antler-like structures.
