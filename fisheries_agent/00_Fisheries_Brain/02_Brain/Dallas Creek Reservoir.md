# Dallas Creek Reservoir

## Summary
Dallas Creek Reservoir is located on the Uncompahgre River in the Upper Colorado River Basin. Contaminant surveys found elevated levels of selenium, zinc, and copper in fish upstream of this reservoir, with the heavy metal contamination attributed to mining operations in the area.
