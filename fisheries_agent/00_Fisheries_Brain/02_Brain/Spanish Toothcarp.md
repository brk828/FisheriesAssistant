# Spanish Toothcarp

## Summary
Spanish Toothcarp (Aphanius iberus) is an endangered endemic cyprinodontid species from the Iberian Peninsula that is sexually dimorphic with females larger than males. This study found that in the presence of introduced mosquitofish, Spanish Toothcarp received significantly more aggression (especially in July), though feeding rates were not significantly affected in adults, likely because this species occupies lower water column positions (mean 9.4 cm from bottom) reducing encounter rates with mosquitofish and can tolerate higher salinities that provide refuge from mosquitofish.
