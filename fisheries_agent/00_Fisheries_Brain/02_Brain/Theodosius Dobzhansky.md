# Theodosius Dobzhansky

## Summary
Theodosius Dobzhansky was one of the leaders of the Evolutionary Synthesis of the 1930s-1940s. According to this source, he was among the scientists who demonstrated that natural selection of small variations could direct evolutionary change, helping to integrate population genetics theory with evolutionary biology and ecology.
