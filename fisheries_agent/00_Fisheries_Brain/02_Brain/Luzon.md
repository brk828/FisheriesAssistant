# Luzon

## Summary
Luzon is a Philippine island that forms part of the boundary of the Sulu Sea complex and contains Ragay Gulf on its southern coast. This source notes that the northern China Sea off southern Luzon shows normal temperature decline with depth and shares few endemic species with the southern Philippine Subregion, containing mainly certain Coelorhynchus species.
