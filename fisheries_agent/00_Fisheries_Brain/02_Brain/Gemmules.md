# Gemmules

## Summary
Gemmules are asexual reproductive bodies produced by freshwater sponges that can resist unfavorable conditions including freezing and drying. When favorable conditions return, new sponges develop from gemmules, and they may be dispersed by birds and other animals, facilitating the slow colonization of new ponds by these immobile organisms.
