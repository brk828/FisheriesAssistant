# Walter Walker Wildlife Area

## Summary
The Walter Walker Wildlife Area is a location near Grand Junction, Colorado in the upper Colorado River. According to this 1980 proposal, it contains gravel spawning areas that may be similar in characteristics to those documented for razorback suckers in Senator Wash Reservoir and Lake Mohave.
