# Rivulidae

## Summary
Rivulidae is a family of small, primarily tropical and subtropical killifishes characterized by their diminutive size, complex reproductive strategies, and adaptation to ephemeral freshwater habitats such as temporary pools and small streams. Members of this family are of particular conservation concern due to their restricted geographic ranges and vulnerability to habitat loss, making them important indicators of aquatic ecosystem health in their native regions. The family's ecological significance lies in their role as prey species for larger fishes and their dependence on specific microhabitat conditions that make them sensitive to environmental disturbance.
