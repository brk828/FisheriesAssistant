# Cibeque Creek

## Summary
Cibeque Creek is a tributary in the Gila River system. This source recommends extending critical habitat for Razorback Sucker into Cibeque Creek as part of a broader recommendation for the Gila River system, recognizing the potential for reintroduction of the species in the Salt River and Verde River systems.
