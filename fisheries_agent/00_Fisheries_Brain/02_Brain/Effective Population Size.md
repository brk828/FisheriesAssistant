# Effective Population Size

## Summary
Effective population size (Ne) is the number of individuals in an idealized population that would experience the same amount of genetic drift as the actual population being studied, and it is typically smaller than the census population size because not all individuals contribute equally to reproduction. In fish conservation and management, Ne is a critical metric for assessing the viability of endangered species populations and determining whether a population maintains sufficient genetic diversity to persist long-term and adapt to environmental changes. Understanding effective population size helps guide breeding programs, translocation efforts, and recovery strategies for threatened fish species like the razorback sucker and other aquatic organisms facing population declines.
