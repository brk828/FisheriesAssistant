# Witjira National Park

## Summary
Witjira National Park is a protected area in South Australia containing important Great Artesian Basin spring complexes. The bibliography documents multiple conservation assessments, heritage studies, and management recommendations from the 1980s-1990s specifically related to this park and its spring ecosystems.
