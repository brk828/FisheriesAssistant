# San Bernardino County

## Summary
San Bernardino County was the third-ranked trout county in California in 1960, with 3,829 fish reported from 133 reports. It is located in the southern California region where trout fishing increased significantly after 1948 due to extensive stocking programs with catchable-sized trout.
