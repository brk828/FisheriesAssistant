# Rio Cosala

## Summary
Rio Cosala is a clear, cool freshwater stream on the Puebla Plateau in Mexico that drains from the eastern slopes of the volcano Iztaccihuatl. At 2,300 meters elevation near San Martin Texmelucan, it flows approximately 0.33 cubic meters per second through habitat with aquatic vegetation, sand, mud, gravel bottoms, and detritus deposits, where the new isopod species Asellus puebla was collected in December 1966.
