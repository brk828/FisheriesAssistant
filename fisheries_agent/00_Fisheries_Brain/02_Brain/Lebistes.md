# Lebistes

## Summary
Lebistes is a genus of poeciliid fish. Males from this genus were able to produce offspring with M. formosa but at extremely low rates (0.05 broods and 0.13 young per 100 days), with one brood of 27 individuals produced over 2,077 days, and all offspring remained identical to the mother.
