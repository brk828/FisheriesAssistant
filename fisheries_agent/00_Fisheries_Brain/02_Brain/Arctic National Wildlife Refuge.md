# Arctic National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
The Arctic National Wildlife Refuge is a protected area in Alaska that contains what this source describes as 'the last unimpeded mammalian [migration route] left on Earth.' The Maricopa Audubon Society letter criticizes provisions in proposed 1995 endangered species legislation (H.R. 2364) that would affect protections for the refuge, arguing against weakening safeguards for 'a few years of oil production.'
