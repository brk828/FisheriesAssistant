# Guayaquil

## Summary
Guayaquil is a locality in western Ecuador where extensive collections of Sternopygus macrurus were made at the market. Thirty-one specimens measuring 282-700 mm were collected from this location, representing the only gymnotid species found in western Ecuador during the survey.
