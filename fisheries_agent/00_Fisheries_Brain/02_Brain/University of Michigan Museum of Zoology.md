# University Of Michigan Museum Of Zoology

## Summary
The University of Michigan Museum of Zoology is a major institutional repository of ichthyological specimens and collections that serves as an important resource for fish systematics, distribution studies, and historical biodiversity research. Its extensive holdings of preserved fish specimens from the late 20th century and earlier periods enable scientists to document temporal changes in fish distributions, validate contemporary survey findings, and contribute to our understanding of regional fish fauna composition and biogeography. For aquatic ecology and conservation biology, such museum collections provide crucial baseline data for assessing long-term changes in species ranges and ecosystem conditions.
