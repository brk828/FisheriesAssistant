# Cuatrociénegas

## Summary
Cuatrociénegas is a biogeologically significant desert basin in Coahuila, Mexico, renowned for its exceptional concentration of endemic fish species and relict aquatic ecosystems that represent unique evolutionary laboratories for understanding adaptation to extreme desert environments. As a primary research and conservation focus of the Desert Fishes Council, Cuatrociénegas has served as a critical site for binational ichthyological studies and field investigations aimed at preserving its distinctive freshwater fauna threatened by arid climate conditions and anthropogenic pressures. The basin exemplifies the scientific importance of desert aquatic systems for conservation biology, particularly regarding the protection of geographically isolated fish populations with limited ranges and high endemism.
