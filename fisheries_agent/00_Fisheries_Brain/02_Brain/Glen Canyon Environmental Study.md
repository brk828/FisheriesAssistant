# Glen Canyon Environmental Study

## Summary
The Glen Canyon Environmental Study produced data regarding a U.S. Bureau of Reclamation recommendation for a multi-level outlet on Glen Canyon Dam. The Desert Fishes Recovery Team found the study's data insufficient to support this recommendation, with their greatest concern being that elevating water temperatures could eliminate the barrier to upstream movement by non-native predators from Lake Mead, potentially threatening the bonytail population at the mouth of the Little Colorado River.
