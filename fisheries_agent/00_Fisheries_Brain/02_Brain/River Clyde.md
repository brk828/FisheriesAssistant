# River Clyde

## Summary
The River Clyde is a Scottish river that became completely fishless in its lower reaches during peak industrial periods due to pollution. Following pollution control measures, the river is now recovering with a diverse fish community, serving as an example of successful habitat restoration for fish conservation.
