# Lower Klamath

## Summary
Lower Klamath is a National Wildlife Refuge where water quality monitoring was conducted in 1964. Average residues in water (suspended material) were 7.3 ppm and sediment residues were 0.9 ppm, demonstrating pesticide contamination patterns in refuge aquatic systems.
