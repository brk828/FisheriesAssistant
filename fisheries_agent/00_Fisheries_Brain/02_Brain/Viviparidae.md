# Viviparidae

## Summary
Viviparidae is a family of freshwater gastropod mollusks (snails) characterized by their operculed shells and ovoviviparous reproduction, making them distinct from many other aquatic snail families in freshwater and brackish ecosystems. These snails are important components of benthic communities in aquatic habitats, serving as indicators of water quality and playing roles in nutrient cycling and food webs. In conservation and ecological contexts, Viviparidae distributions and populations are monitored to assess freshwater ecosystem health and biodiversity.
