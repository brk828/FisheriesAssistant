# Saratoga Springs

## Summary
Saratoga Springs is an isolated, spring-fed oasis located in the southern portion of Death Valley National Monument that serves as a critical study system for understanding endemic and endangered aquatic species in extreme desert environments. The site, comprising a 3-acre permanent marsh fed by one main spring and three smaller sources, has been extensively documented for its unique ecological characteristics including species distribution, population dynamics, and reproductive biology of both fish and associated fauna. As a geographically isolated aquatic system, Saratoga Springs represents an important conservation focus for threatened endemic fish species and provides insights into population viability and ecosystem management in arid regions.
