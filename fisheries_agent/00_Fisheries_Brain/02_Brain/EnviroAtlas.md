# EnviroAtlas

## Summary
EnviroAtlas is a geospatial database that provides ecosystem service and environmental data at watershed scales across the United States. This study used EnviroAtlas biodiversity indices, which are based on IUCN species range maps reallocated to HUC12 watersheds, to help assess conservation priorities and ecological value across the Colorado River Basin in conjunction with the threat assessment framework.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed**: Developing spatially explicit threat indices to assess ecological integrity across the entire [[Colorado River Basin]] (>1,067,700 km of river network) in the absence of comprehensive field-based monitoring.
