# Last Chance Creek

## Summary
Last Chance Creek was a productive fishing 'hot spot' in Lake Powell that received 13.6% of angler effort but produced 17.5% of the catch at 0.71 fish per hour. It was also the site (along with Kane Creek) of a massive Anacystis bloom in October 1965.
