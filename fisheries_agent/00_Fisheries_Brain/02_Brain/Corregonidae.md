# Corregonidae

## Summary
Corregonidae is the whitefish family, with 5 species documented in Utah according to this source. The family includes endemic Bonneville Basin species such as Bear Lake Whitefish, Bonneville Whitefish, and Lake Bonneville Cisco, as well as native Rocky Mountain Whitefish and introduced Great Lakes Whitefish.
