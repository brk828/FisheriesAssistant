# Cryptochironomus

## Summary
Cryptochironomus is a genus of chironomid midge larvae (9-14 mm in size) detected in Hartlaub Lake. It was found at low density (1.5 larvae per square foot) on August 17, about one month after the toxaphene treatment.

## Context
