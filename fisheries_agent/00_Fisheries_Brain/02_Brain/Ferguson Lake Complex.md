# Ferguson Lake Complex

## Summary
Ferguson Lake Complex is a specific restoration site within the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge. This source lists it among the locations where restoration objectives target enhancement of wetlands and backwaters through channel construction, backwater reconnection, and water control structures as part of the approximately 2,000-acre restoration effort.
