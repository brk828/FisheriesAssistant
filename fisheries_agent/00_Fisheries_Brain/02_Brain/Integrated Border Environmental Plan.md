# Integrated Border Environmental Plan

## Summary
The Integrated Border Environmental Plan (IBEP) was prepared by EPA and Mexico's SEDESOL but is criticized as a non-integrated "wish list" that ignores long-term water supply and lacks state/local participation. The plan fails to guide Congressional appropriations effectively, overemphasizes urban areas while neglecting rural and natural areas, and has received no commitment for cleanup of existing environmental problems including some in Mexico linked to U.S. investment.
