# Lunsemfwa River

## Summary
The Lunsemfwa River is a tributary in the Zambesi River system where seven whole specimens plus one head of Anguilla nebulosa labiata were collected above Power Station Falls. Specimens were captured by a grid at a hydro-electric station intake, and young eels reaching this location would have had to surmount cascades totaling 250 feet in height.
