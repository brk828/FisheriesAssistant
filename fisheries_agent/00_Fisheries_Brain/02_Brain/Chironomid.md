# Chironomid

## Summary
Chironomids (midge larvae) are aquatic insects that form part of the diet of young Colorado River Squawfish less than 50 mm in length. Along with cladocerans and copepods, chironomid larvae are consumed before the fish transition to eating larger insects and eventually become exclusively piscivorous.
