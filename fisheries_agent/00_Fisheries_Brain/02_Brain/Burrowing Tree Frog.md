# Burrowing Tree Frog

## Summary
The Burrowing Tree Frog (Pternohyla fodiens) is an arid-land amphibian that occurs in the United States or Arizona only in the vicinity of Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. This species should be present in the refuge area according to the survey's list of potential native amphibians.
