# Universidad Autonoma de Nuevo Leon

## Summary
Universidad Autonoma de Nuevo Leon is a Mexican university that served as the institutional home for Salvador Contreras-Balderas, one of the principal investigators on this 1993 borderlands aquatic biodiversity proposal. The proposal planned to establish a permanent literature repository at this institution to support conservation efforts and make historical and current data on biologically sensitive aquatic areas accessible to stakeholders in the Mexico-United States borderlands region.
