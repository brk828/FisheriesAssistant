# Foothill Paloverde

## Summary
Foothill Paloverde (Cercidium microphyllum) is a predominant tree species in the Sonoran Desertscrub vegetation community at PPMR. This species exhibited heavy browsing damage by rabbits and rodents during the 1994 survey.
