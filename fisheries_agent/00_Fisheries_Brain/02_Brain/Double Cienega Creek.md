# Double Cienega Creek

## Summary
Double Cienega Creek is a stream in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that required hiking to access for the 1988 survey. The Apache Trout population showed one of the most balanced sex ratios at 1.13:1 (9 males to 8 females), with 17 total specimens collected for genetic analysis.
