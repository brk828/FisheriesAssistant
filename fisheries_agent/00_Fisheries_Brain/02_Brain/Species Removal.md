# Species Removal

## Summary
Species removal refers to the active elimination of non-native or invasive fish species from aquatic systems as a conservation management strategy. This source demonstrates that removal of smallmouth bass (Micropterus dolomieui) from Fossil Creek, Arizona, resulted in unprecedented recruitment of native fishes in recent history, providing evidence that targeted removal of predatory non-native species can be an effective approach for native fish recovery in southwestern river systems.
