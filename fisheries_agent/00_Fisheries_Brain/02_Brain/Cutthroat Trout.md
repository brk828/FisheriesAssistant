# Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
Cutthroat trout are a native North American salmonid species (*Salmo clarki*) with multiple subspecies that occupy cold-water riverine and lacustrine environments, including the Colorado River cutthroat trout (*Salmo clarki pleuriticus*) and Bear Lake cutthroat trout, which have become significant focal species in freshwater conservation biology due to habitat loss, competition from introduced species, and threats to genetic integrity. These fish are managed through a combination of hatchery propagation, habitat restoration, and reintroduction programs, particularly under federal mitigation frameworks like the Colorado River Storage Project Act, though their conservation faces ongoing challenges related to non-native species interactions and the genetic consequences of hatchery management practices.
