# Gila Mountains

## Summary
The Gila Mountains are a mountain range in Arizona. According to this source, an intense and localized storm hit the Gila Mountains on August 21, 1990, causing severe regional flooding that damaged the Big Spring Gila topminnow reintroduction site and resulted in one fatality near Safford.
