# Coryphaenoides

## Summary
Coryphaenoides is a genus of macrourid fishes with multiple species in the Philippine Subregion. Three species (C. paradoxus, C. dubius, C. camurus) are known only from type specimens in the Sulu Sea, while several East Indian species of this genus were notably absent despite intensive dredging by the Albatross.
