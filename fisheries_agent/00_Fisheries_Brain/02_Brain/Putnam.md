# Putnam

## Summary
Putnam described four sub-provinces of the Mono Basin in 1950: the Sierra Nevada, morainal belt, lake plains, and Mono-Inyo Craters. In 1949, he identified an indistinct terrace at 7,170-7,180 feet elevation representing maximum Lake Russell and saw evidence of overflow through the divide north of Cowtrack Mountain through Adobe Meadows at 7,150 feet during Tahoe maximum.
