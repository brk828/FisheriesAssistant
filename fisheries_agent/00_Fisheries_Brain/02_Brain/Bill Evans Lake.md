# Bill Evans Lake

## Summary
Bill Evans Lake is an off-mainstream reservoir in the Gila River basin where the New Mexico Department of Game and Fish stocks both trout and warmwater gamefish, including crappie which have never successfully established in riverine habitat. The reservoir is located upstream from Spikedace and Loach Minnow populations, and while the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service identified it as presenting potential threats through warmwater stocking, no significant adverse effects had been documented as of 1988, and no current solutions existed for reducing potential impacts from this upstream reservoir.
