# Santa Fe River

## Summary
The Santa Fe River is one of three major tributaries entering the Middle Rio Grande system, flowing in below Cochiti Reservoir from the west. These tributary systems on U.S. Forest Service lands at upper elevations (2,000-2,600 m) historically provided coldwater habitat for native species including Rio Grande Cutthroat Trout, Rio Grande Chub, and Rio Grande Sucker.
