# Gleeson

## Summary
Gleeson is a location in southeastern Arizona at approximately 5,200 feet elevation where Blumer documented unusual vegetation patterns. The foothills just east of Gleeson were covered by strictly desert species, representing the upper end of a peculiar inversion of altitudinal zones in the region.
