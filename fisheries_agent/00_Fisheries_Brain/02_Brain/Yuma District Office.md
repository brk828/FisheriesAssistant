# Yuma District Office

## Summary
The Yuma District Office is a Bureau of Land Management office that requested Section 7 consultation for the Lake Havasu Fisheries Improvement Partnership Program. This office was the federal action agency responsible for the proposed project involving habitat enhancement and endangered fish recovery efforts on Lake Havasu.
