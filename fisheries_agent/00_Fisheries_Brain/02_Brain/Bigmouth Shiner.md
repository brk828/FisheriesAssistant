# Bigmouth Shiner

## Summary
The Bigmouth Shiner (*Notropis dorsalis*) is a small cyprinid fish species notable for exhibiting greater intra-specific morphological and meristic variation compared to closely related congeners like the Common Shiner, with this variation manifesting both annually and across different stream populations. This species demonstrates less restrictive habitat specificity than some congeners, occupying a broader range of microhabitats and stream conditions, making it an important comparative model for understanding how habitat flexibility influences phenotypic plasticity in North American minnows. Its variable population structure and adaptive responses to different environmental conditions have made it significant in fisheries research examining the relationship between morphological diversity and ecological niche partitioning among cyprinid fishes.
