# Colorado River Endangered Fishes

## Summary
The Colorado River Endangered Fishes refer to a group of endemic fish species native to the Colorado River Basin that have been listed as endangered under the Endangered Species Act and are the focus of coordinated recovery and management efforts across multiple states. These species face threats from habitat modification, water management practices, competition with non-native fish species, and predation dynamics within the altered river ecosystem, requiring integrated conservation strategies that address both direct biological factors and broader river management policies. Research on these species often employs indirect assessment methods, such as monitoring invertebrate community composition, to evaluate competitive and predatory interactions in large, complex river systems where direct observation of species interactions is difficult.
