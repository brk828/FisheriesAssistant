# Bylas Springs

## Summary
Bylas Springs refers to an isolated desert spring-fed habitat system located in the Gila River drainage of southeastern Arizona, significant in ichthyology and conservation biology as a critical refuge for endemic fish species, particularly the Gila topminnow (Poeciliopsis occidentalis). This spring system has been a key research and management site for monitoring population dynamics and species interactions, with documented populations discovered in 1968 showing a long history of fluctuations between topminnow dominance and invasive mosquitofish colonization. Bylas Springs exemplifies the challenges and conservation priorities associated with maintaining fragile desert aquatic ecosystems and their endemic fauna in the American Southwest.
