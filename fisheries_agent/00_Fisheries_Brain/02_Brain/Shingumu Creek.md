# Shingumu Creek

## Summary
Shingumu Creek is a freshwater stream habitat where a Gila specimen was collected on August 1, 1975 using a 12' x 6' x 1/8" mesh seine. The specimen collected from this creek was tentatively identified as Gila cypha (humpback chub), though definitive identification required additional morphological data beyond what photographs could provide.
