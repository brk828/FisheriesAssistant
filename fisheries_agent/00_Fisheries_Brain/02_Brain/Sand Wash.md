# Sand Wash

## Summary
Sand Wash is a location on the Green River that served as the dividing point between the upper and lower study reaches. The study area was divided into an upper reach extending from Echo Park to Sand Wash, and a lower reach extending from Sand Wash downstream to Turks Head.
