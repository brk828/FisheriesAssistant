# John Martin Reservoir

## Summary
John Martin Reservoir is a warm water fishery in Colorado that received experimental stocking in 1955 as part of species diversification efforts. The reservoir was one of three waters (along with Two Buttes Reservoir and Sloan's Lake) that received 715 Drum (freshwater sheepshead) obtained from Kansas, representing Colorado's efforts to introduce new warm water species.
