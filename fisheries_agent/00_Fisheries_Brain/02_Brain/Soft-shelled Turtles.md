# Soft-shelled Turtles

## Summary
Soft-shelled turtles are reptiles that were legally harvested by Arizona anglers. The 1980 survey found that resident anglers harvested 6,700 soft-shelled turtles statewide, representing 0.1% of the total harvest, making them the least commonly harvested species or taxa reported in the survey.

## Context

> This report presents results from the first year (1980) of a three-year mail survey program designed to estimate statewide angling effort and fish harvest by Arizona's licensed resident anglers.
