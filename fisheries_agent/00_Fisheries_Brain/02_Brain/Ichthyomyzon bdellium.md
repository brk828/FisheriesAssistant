# Ichthyomyzon bdellium

## Summary
Ichthyomyzon bdellium is a lamprey species collected from lower Copper Creek during this study. This source identifies it as a significant new record for the upper Clinch River system in Virginia, indicating it was found among the 44 fish species documented at the N. flavipinnis collection locality.
