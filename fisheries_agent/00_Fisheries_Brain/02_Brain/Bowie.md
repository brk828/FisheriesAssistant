# Bowie

## Summary
Bowie is a settlement in San Simon Valley at 3,747 feet elevation with long-term precipitation records (1867-1908) showing mean annual precipitation of 13.51 inches. This source notes the striking difference between Bowie's precipitation and nearby San Simon's much lower 9.24 inches, despite only 16 miles separation, demonstrating strong orographic effects of the Dos Cabezas Mountains.
