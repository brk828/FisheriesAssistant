# Lake Erie

## Summary
Lake Erie is one of the five Great Lakes and serves as a critical freshwater ecosystem that has been central to fisheries management and conservation biology efforts, particularly for the restoration of native fish populations such as lake trout that were historically depleted due to overfishing, invasive species predation, and habitat degradation. As a shallow, productive lake system, Lake Erie represents an important case study in aquatic ecology and conservation, where federal hatchery programs and management initiatives have been implemented to restore depleted native fish communities and maintain ecosystem integrity. The lake's fish fauna and ecological conditions have made it a focal point for North American fisheries science research and species documentation.
