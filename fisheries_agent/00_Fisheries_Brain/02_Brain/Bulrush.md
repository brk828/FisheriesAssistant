# Bulrush

## Summary
Bulrush (Scirpus sp.) is emergent vegetation that provides cover habitat utilized by bonytail in Lake Havasu. At Regional Park Moabi and Laughlin Lagoon, 15-22% of active tracking contacts occurred in or near bulrush habitat, with multiple fish observed in shallow water near bulrush patches and some tracked several meters into bulrush stands (up to 10 m).
