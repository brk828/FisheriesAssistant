# Union River

## Summary
The Union River is one of the major New England rivers where Atlantic Salmon were historically abundant and where restoration efforts have been focused. According to this source, it received Atlantic Salmon stocking as part of the restoration program during the decade prior to 1992, though current adult returns across all New England rivers (3,000-7,000 annually) remain far below historical levels of 300,000.
