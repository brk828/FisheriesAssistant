# BONY

## Summary
BONY is the abbreviation for Bonytail (Gila elegans), a native cyprinid fish species targeted for conservation in the Lower Colorado River. This source describes removing BONY individuals from Pond 1 during pre-renovation sampling in spring and autumn 2014, recording their PIT tag numbers, lengths, and stocking locations before the ponds underwent chemical treatment to eliminate nonnative fish and create suitable habitat for reintroducing recruiting populations of this species.
