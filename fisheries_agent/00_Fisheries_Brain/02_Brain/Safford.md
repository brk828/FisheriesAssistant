# Safford

## Summary
Safford is a location in Arizona near the Big Spring topminnow reintroduction site. The source reports that the August 21, 1990 storm that damaged Big Spring also caused flooding near Safford that resulted in one fatality, and the Big Spring site was located within the Safford District managed by the Bureau of Land Management.
