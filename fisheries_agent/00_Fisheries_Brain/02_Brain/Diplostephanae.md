# Diplostephanae

## Summary
Diplostephanae is one of two primary sections in the classical division of genus Chara, distinguished by having a double whorl of stipulodes. The research questioned the validity of this traditional dichotomy with Haplostephanae, suggesting that the existence of parallel morphological groups in both sections combined with cytological evidence indicates this classification may not reflect natural relationships.
