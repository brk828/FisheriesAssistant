# Pahsimeroi River

## Summary
Pahsimeroi River is a summer-run Chinook salmon spawning stream in the Salmon River drainage, Idaho. Redd counts increased dramatically from 16 in 1965 to 113 in 1966, reflecting the overall increase in summer chinook spawning escapement.
