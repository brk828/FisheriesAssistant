# Watts Bar Reservoir

## Summary
Watts Bar Reservoir is a Tennessee water body that served as a control site for smallmouth bass in this study. This source collected 36 control smallmouth bass specimens from Watts Bar Reservoir to establish baseline meristic and biochemical genetic characteristics for comparison with the potentially hybridized Roaring River population.
