# Ash Meadows Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Ash Meadows Speckled Dace (*Rhinichthys osculus nevadensis*) is a subspecies of speckled dace endemic to the Ash Meadows spring complex in Nevada, representing one of several highly range-restricted fish species found in this isolated geothermal ecosystem. As a cyprinid species inhabiting a limited spring system, it exemplifies the conservation challenges facing endemic desert fishes that face threats from habitat modification, invasive species, and anthropogenic disturbance to their hydrogeological systems. This taxon serves as an important indicator species for the ecological integrity of the Ash Meadows aquatic community and is typically managed alongside other endemic species like the Ash Meadows Pupfish in coordinated desert spring conservation efforts.
