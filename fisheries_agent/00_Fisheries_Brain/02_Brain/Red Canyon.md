# Red Canyon

## Summary
Red Canyon was a fishing location near Bullfrog Marina on Lake Powell that showed the highest catch rate in that area. Anglers fishing Red Canyon achieved a success rate of 0.63 fish per hour during the 1967-1968 study period.
