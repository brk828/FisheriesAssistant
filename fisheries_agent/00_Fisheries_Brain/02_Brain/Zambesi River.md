# Zambesi River

## Summary
The Zambesi River is a major African river system in the Indian Ocean basin where eels are widely distributed, though no definite records exist above Victoria Falls. This source documents the first records of Anguilla nebulosa labiata from the Zambesi River system in Northern Rhodesia, with specimens collected from the Lunsemfwa River and lower Zambesi River at Chipepo.
