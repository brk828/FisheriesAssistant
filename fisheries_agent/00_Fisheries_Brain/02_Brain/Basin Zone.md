# Basin Zone

## Summary
Basin Zone is the main reservoir section of Lake Mohave, including areas like Carp Cove, Yuma Cove, and Cottonwood Cove, characterized by lentic (still water) habitat. This zone supported an estimated 1,963 repatriated razorback suckers in 2018, with approximately 70% of fish released here remaining in the zone, and shore-based PIT scanners documented continuous spawning activity at multiple sites including Tequila Cove, Yuma Cove, and Half-Way Wash.
