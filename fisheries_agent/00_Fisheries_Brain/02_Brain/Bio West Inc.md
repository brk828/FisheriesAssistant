# Bio/West Inc.

## Summary
Bio/West Inc. is a consulting firm that specializes in aquatic biology and fisheries research, particularly focused on native fish conservation and ecosystem studies in the southwestern United States. The company, represented in Glen Canyon environmental research projects by ichthyologist Dr. Richard Valdez, contributed technical expertise to interdisciplinary teams studying aquatic ecosystems and fish populations in relation to dam operations and environmental management. Bio/West Inc. served as a key partner in collaborative research initiatives examining the impacts of water management practices on native fish species and river ecology in the Colorado River system.
