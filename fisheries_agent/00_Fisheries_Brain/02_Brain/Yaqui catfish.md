# Yaqui Catfish

## Summary
The Yaqui Catfish is an endangered fish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui basin, a transboundary watershed spanning Sonora, Mexico and the Arizona-Mexico border region, making it a subject of international conservation concern and recovery planning efforts by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team. As a native catfish of this arid-region river system, it faces threats from habitat degradation, water extraction, and competition from non-native species, necessitating coordinated monitoring and management protocols within the framework of U.S. Endangered Species Act recovery initiatives. The species represents an important component of the Rio Yaqui's endemic fish fauna, alongside other at-risk taxa such as the Yaqui Chub and Beautiful Shiner, and requires dedicated conservation attention due to its restricted geographic range and declining population status.
