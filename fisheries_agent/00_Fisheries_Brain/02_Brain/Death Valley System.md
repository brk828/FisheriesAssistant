# Death Valley System

## Summary
The Death Valley System refers to a cluster of isolated desert spring ecosystems in the Mojave Desert region of Nevada and California that harbor a unique radiation of endemic pupfish and poolfish species. This interconnected system of geographically distinct but biogeographically related springs and spring-fed impoundments represents a critical conservation focus for desert fish species adapted to specific thermal and hydrogeochemical conditions. The Death Valley System Committee, established under the Desert Fishes Council, coordinates management and research efforts to address threats to these highly specialized and vulnerable endemic fish populations.
