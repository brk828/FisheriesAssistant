# Federal Committee on Pest Control

## Summary
The Federal Committee on Pest Control was a federal oversight body that required prior approval for all pesticide use in Federal programs or programs supported by Federal funds. According to this manual, no pesticides were exempt from this approval requirement, establishing a mandatory review process for all chemical vegetation control activities in fish and wildlife management programs.

## Context

> This 1968 manual from the [[Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife]] provides comprehensive guidance on herbicide use for vegetation control in fish and wildlife management programs.
