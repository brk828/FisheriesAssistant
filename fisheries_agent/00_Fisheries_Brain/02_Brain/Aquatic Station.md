# Aquatic Station

## Summary
The Aquatic Station (formerly San Marcos Federal Fish Hatchery) at Southwest Texas State University is where the Haideoporus texanus larvae were collected. The station has an artesian well drilled in 1895 that draws water from a 1.5 m cavern at 59.5 m depth in the Edwards Aquifer, and regular netting for specimens began in December 1973.
