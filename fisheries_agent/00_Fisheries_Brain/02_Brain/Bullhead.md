# Bullhead

## Summary
Bullhead refers to a group of catfish species (genus Ictalurus, particularly black bullhead and yellow bullhead) that are small to medium-sized freshwater fish commonly found in North American lakes and streams. In fisheries management and conservation contexts, bullheads are typically classified as "rough fish" due to their bottom-feeding behavior and mud-rooting habits, which can contribute to water turbidity and are often viewed as competitors with or predators of more desirable sport fish species. These hardy, adaptable fish were frequently targeted for removal or control in mid-20th century fisheries management programs using methods such as rotenone application to restore or maintain populations of preferred game fish species.
