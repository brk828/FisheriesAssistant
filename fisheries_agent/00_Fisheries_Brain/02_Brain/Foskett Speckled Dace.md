# Foskett Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Foskett Speckled Dace is an endangered fish species with highly restricted range that faces jeopardy from malathion application due to extreme aquatic toxicity concerns. The Service mandates a 1-mile malathion exclusion zone around occupied habitat, though shorter 1/4-mile buffers are adequate for carbaryl and acephate.
