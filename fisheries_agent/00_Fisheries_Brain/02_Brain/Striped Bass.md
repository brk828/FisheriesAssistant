# Striped Bass

## Summary
Striped bass (*Morone saxatilis*) is a non-native piscivorous fish species introduced into Colorado River Basin reservoirs such as Lake Powell, Lake Mohave, and Lake Havasu, where it serves as a popular sport fish but presents significant ecological concerns as a predator of endangered native species including razorback suckers and bonytail chub. As an introduced apex predator, striped bass populations in these systems have become subjects of intensive monitoring and management in relation to conservation efforts for threatened and endangered native fishes, with their trophic role and population dynamics representing a key tension between recreational fisheries objectives and native species recovery in aquatic ecosystems throughout the Lower Colorado River Basin.
