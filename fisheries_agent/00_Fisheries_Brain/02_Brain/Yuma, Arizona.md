# Yuma, Arizona

## Summary
Yuma, Arizona is a location on the lower Colorado River where summer water temperatures commonly approach or exceed 35°C and may drop below 10°C in winter. This document identifies the thermal regime near Yuma as representative of conditions that Colorado Squawfish historically tolerated in the lower basin.
