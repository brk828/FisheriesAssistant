# Southwestern Archaic

## Summary
The Southwestern Archaic is an archaeological period dating from 8500 B.C. to A.D. 300, representing post-Pleistocene hunting-gathering adaptation. This source describes sites at Organ Pipe consisting of chipped stone tools, manufacturing debris, roasting features, and sleeping circles from seasonally mobile groups exploiting wild plants and small game, with artifacts embedded in terrace deposits along Alamo Wash confirming mid-Holocene stabilization dates.
