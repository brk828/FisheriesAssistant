# Conservation Aquaculture

## Summary
Conservation aquaculture refers to the controlled breeding and rearing of fish species in captivity, primarily for the purpose of species preservation, population recovery, or reintroduction into wild habitats. This approach is particularly applied to endangered or threatened fish species where wild populations have declined to critically low levels, and involves maintaining genetically diverse captive populations that can serve as insurance against extinction and sources for restocking efforts. Conservation aquaculture integrates principles of both aquaculture technology and conservation biology to ensure the long-term viability of imperiled fish species while maintaining ecological and genetic integrity.
