# Acid Phosphatase

## Summary
Acid Phosphatase is an enzyme examined at 2 genetic loci in this electrophoretic study. It was monomorphic (showed no variation) in both Rio Grande and Cuatro Cienegas cichlid populations, though the enzyme showed mobility differences between the two geographic forms.
