# Polygonacea

## Summary
Polygonacea is a family of higher aquatic vegetation (likely smartweeds or knotweeds) that was present in Holbrook Lake Reservoir. This vegetation was common in the reservoir from 1946-1948 but had disappeared by 1952-1953, with the loss attributed to drastic water level fluctuations and rough fish activity.
