# Residence Zones

## Summary
Residence Zones are geographic units based on where anglers live, used to analyze spatial patterns in fishing behavior and demand. Arizona was divided into 22 Residence Zones based on population centers, political boundaries, and zip codes, revealing that proximity to fisheries strongly influenced angling patterns—for example, residents near trout waters took shorter trips (1.1 days) for trout but longer trips (1.8 days) to reach warm-water fisheries.

## Context

> This report presents results from the first year (1980) of a three-year mail survey program designed to estimate statewide angling effort and fish harvest by Arizona's licensed resident anglers.
