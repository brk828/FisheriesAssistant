# Distichlis

## Summary
Distichlis is a grass species, referred to as "wild wheat," that was cultivated prehistorically by the Cocopa people and gathered as part of their traditional subsistence. There is renewed interest among the Cocopa at El Mayor in cultivating this plant because it can be irrigated with saline water.
