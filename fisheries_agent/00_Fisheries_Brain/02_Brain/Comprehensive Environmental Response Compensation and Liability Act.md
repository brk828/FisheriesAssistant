# Comprehensive Environmental Response Compensation and Liability Act

## Summary
CERCLA is a federal environmental law that establishes liability for cleanup of hazardous substance releases and contaminated sites. This source explains that CERCLA was significantly expanded to cover mining operations through the Eagle-Picher decision, which ruled that mining wastes containing any listed substances (including thirteen naturally occurring inorganics like copper, lead, and arsenic) qualified as 'hazardous substances' subject to CERCLA liability, even when those same wastes were excluded from hazardous waste regulation under RCRA's Bevill Amendment.
