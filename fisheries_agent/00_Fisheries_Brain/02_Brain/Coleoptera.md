# Coleoptera

## Summary
Coleoptera, the order of beetles, represents an important terrestrial food source for aquatic fish species, particularly larger predatory fish such as trout in freshwater ecosystems. In the diet studies referenced across these ichthyological investigations, Coleoptera comprised a significant proportion of terrestrial insect prey, complementing the dominant aquatic insect orders like Ephemeroptera, Trichoptera, and Diptera that constitute primary dietary components for stream-dwelling fish. The presence of Coleoptera in fish stomach contents indicates foraging behavior at the water-land interface and highlights the nutritional importance of terrestrial arthropod inputs to fish populations in lotic environments.
