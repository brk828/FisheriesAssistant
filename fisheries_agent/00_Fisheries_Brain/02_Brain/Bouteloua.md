# Bouteloua

## Summary
Bouteloua is a grass genus that dominates the grassland vegetation communities in the Sierra San Luis protected area. These grasses occur alongside Prosopis species, Yucca baccata, and Nolina microcarpa in the natural grassland habitats of the reserve.
