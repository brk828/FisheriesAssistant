# British Isles

## Summary
The British Isles have 42 native fish species, of which 10 are under threat including 2 now extinct. Of 235 National Nature Reserves in Great Britain, 75% contain fresh waters but only 20% of these have fish, and only 3 of the 10 most threatened species occur in National Nature Reserves, indicating inadequate protection coverage.
