# Tiemeier

## Summary
Tiemeier was a researcher who collaborated with Deyoe on feeding studies at Kansas State University. His 1969 work found that a 2.5% feeding rate was more efficient than 3.0%, though the higher rate was more economical due to increased yield, and his 1968 work with Deyoe demonstrated benefits of split daily feeding.

## Context

> This 1969 review synthesizes the first decade of research on commercial [[Channel Catfish]] nutrition in the United States. Despite only four research stations intensively studying catfish feeding, excellent practical rations have been developed yielding 1 pound of gain per 1.5-2.0 pounds of feed....
