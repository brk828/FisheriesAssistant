# Wahweap Creek

## Summary
Wahweap Creek is a highly productive fishing area near Wahweap Marina on Lake Powell that received 43.5% of angler trips from that access point. It had the highest success rate at 0.67 fish per hour and was the primary location for rainbow trout, with 97% of trout taken at Wahweap and 94% specifically from Wahweap Creek, where night anglers achieved rates exceeding 10 fish per hour.
