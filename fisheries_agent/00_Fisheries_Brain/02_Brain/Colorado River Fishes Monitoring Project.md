# Colorado River Fishes Monitoring Project

## Summary
The Colorado River Fishes Monitoring Project (CRFMP) was a research program conducted from 1982-1985 by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to study endangered fish species in the Green River Basin. This project expanded upon earlier baseline studies by employing radiotelemetry and flow-recruitment investigations to document distribution, spawning behavior, migration patterns, and habitat requirements of federally endangered species including Colorado squawfish, razorback sucker, and humpback chub.
