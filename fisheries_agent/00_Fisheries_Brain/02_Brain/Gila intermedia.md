# Gila Intermedia

## Summary
Gila intermedia, commonly known as the Gila chub, is a small native cyprinid fish species endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly found in Arizona and New Mexico river systems. This species is significant in ichthyological and conservation research as a taxonomically distinct member of the Gila genus, distinguishable from closely related species like Gila robusta through morphological characteristics including larval pigmentation patterns and myomere counts. As a native fish of arid-region aquatic ecosystems, Gila intermedia holds importance in conservation biology studies focused on protecting endemic freshwater faunas threatened by habitat loss and competition from introduced species in the American Southwest.
