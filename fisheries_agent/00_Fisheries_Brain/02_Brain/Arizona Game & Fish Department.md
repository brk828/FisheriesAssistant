# Arizona Game & Fish Department

## Summary
The Arizona Game & Fish Department is the state wildlife agency responsible for managing fish populations and aquatic resources within Arizona, including the implementation of conservation breeding programs and species recovery efforts for threatened and endangered fish species. In the context of these documents, the Department served as a critical operational partner in the recovery of the endangered razorback sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*), receiving and managing captive-reared individuals from federal hatcheries for reintroduction and monitoring in Colorado River Basin habitats. As a state-level conservation authority, the Department coordinates with federal agencies like the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to execute recovery initiatives, track PIT-tagged individuals, and maintain genetic and population records essential to endangered species restoration programs.
