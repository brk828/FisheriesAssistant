# Campbell Timber Sale

## Summary
The Campbell Timber Sale was a proposed timber harvest project covering approximately 5,416 acres in the Campbell Blue Creek basin of Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests, Arizona, with harvest planned on 2,793 acres yielding approximately 3.298 million board feet of sawtimber. The project included tractor logging, road construction and closure, prescribed burning, and mandatory 132-foot buffer zones along all watercourses to protect threatened Loach Minnow and Apache Trout habitat. The sale was expected to increase watershed disturbance from 4.02% to 10.25% and result in elevated sedimentation, 6.1% increases in peak flows, and reduced base flows in Campbell Blue Creek.
