# Cueva de la Selva

## Summary
Cueva de la Selva is a cave located 8 km west of Xilitla where specimens of Xilitloniscus laevis were collected in November 1963 and November 1964. Collections from this cave yielded both male and female specimens, including the males that allowed Bowman to examine the diagnostic pleopod morphology.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
