# Technicon Autoanalyzer

## Summary
The Technicon Autoanalyzer is an automated analytical instrument used for water quality testing. This source reports it was used for laboratory analyses of water samples from the destratification study, with some modifications made to Standard Methods procedures to accommodate the instrument's capabilities.

## Context

> This 1967 research report from the [[Federal Water Pollution Control Administration]] describes engineering methods to overcome water quality deterioration in stratified impoundments through artificial destratification.
