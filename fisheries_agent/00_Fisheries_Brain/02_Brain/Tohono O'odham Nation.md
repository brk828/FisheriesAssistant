# Tohono O'odham Nation

## Summary
The Tohono O'odham Nation, through its San Xavier District, was involved in discussions about recharge projects that would incorporate riparian habitat restoration along the Santa Cruz River. The Nation had expressed desire for riparian habitat restoration as part of preliminary design work on recharge projects in the area.
