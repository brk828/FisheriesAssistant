# Snail Bullhead

## Summary
Snail Bullhead (Ictalurus brunneus) is a catfish species that prefers slower current areas over sandy/gravel bottoms around submerged logs and debris in water >1 m deep. This survey found snail bullheads were the dominant ictalurid above both dams, comprising 377 of 415 bullheads collected on the Yadkin River and 53 of 66 on the South Yadkin River, with well-distributed size ranges indicating healthy populations.
