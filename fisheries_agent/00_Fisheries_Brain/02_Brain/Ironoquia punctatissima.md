# Ironoquia punctatissima

## Summary
Ironoquia punctatissima is a caddisfly species in Caldwell Hollow that shows exceptional adaptation to intermittent flow by laying eggs in October on the dry stream bed, with larvae appearing in January. Prepupae aestivate in terrestrial leaf litter during dry periods, and this species was among those found in moist leaf litter serving as temporary refuge during drought.
