# Crangonyx forbesi

## Summary
Crangonyx forbesi is an aquatic amphipod that dominates the fauna of Caldwell Hollow, consistently comprising 80-90% of organisms along with Lirceus fontinalis. This species exhibits obligate univoltine reproduction with ovigerous females present April-May, each producing one brood of approximately 90 eggs before dying (males die even earlier), and a sex ratio of 2.2 females to 1 male during peak reproduction. The new generation shows minimal summer growth and survives dry periods as small animals (averaging 3 mm in August) in subsurface seepage, demonstrating remarkable humidity tolerance with 50% survival at 6 days in water-saturated air compared to less than 1 day for permanent stream relatives like Gammarus minus.
