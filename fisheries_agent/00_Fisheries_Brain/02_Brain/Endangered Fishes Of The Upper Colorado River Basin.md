# Endangered Fishes Of The Upper Colorado River Basin

## Summary
Endangered Fishes of the Upper Colorado River Basin refers to native fish species whose populations have declined to critically low levels due to habitat alteration, primarily from dam construction and water management practices in the Upper Colorado River system. These species, including the razorback sucker and other endemic fishes, have become the focus of comprehensive recovery programs that integrate instream flow assessments, habitat restoration, and policy synthesis to prevent extinction and restore viable populations. The conservation effort represents a multi-disciplinary approach combining ichthyological research, water resource management, and ecosystem ecology to address the complex interactions between hydrological modification and fish survival in this unique aquatic ecosystem.
