# Great Salt Plains Reservoir

## Summary
Great Salt Plains Reservoir is a water body in Oklahoma that was used to study white bass tolerance to salinity. White bass survived in this reservoir until chloride concentrations reached 6,000 ppm, demonstrating the species' ability to tolerate highly saline conditions and wide-ranging limnological environments.
