# Cichlasoma Cyanoguttatum

## Summary
Cichlasoma cyanoguttatum, commonly known as the Texas cichlid, is a native freshwater fish species found in south Texas and northeastern Mexico that has become increasingly significant in conservation and ecological studies of Texas aquatic systems. As documented in regional ichthyological surveys, this cichlid represents an important component of native fish fauna in systems like the Pecos River and San Antonio River, though its populations and ecological role must be understood in context with introduced exotic species that have altered native fish community composition. The species is of particular interest in conservation biology due to habitat pressures and the need to distinguish native cichlid populations from introduced aquarium species in Texas waterways.
