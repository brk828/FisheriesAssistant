# Salmo clarkii henshawi

## Summary
Salmo clarkii henshawi, the Lahontan cutthroat trout, is a subspecies native to Lake Tahoe and its tributaries that is distinguished by the distribution, size, and shape of its dark spots and characteristically high gill raker counts. This source establishes that Salmo evermanni is actually a synonym of this subspecies, as morphological comparison revealed no significant differences between evermanni type specimens and henshawi specimens from Lake Tahoe.
