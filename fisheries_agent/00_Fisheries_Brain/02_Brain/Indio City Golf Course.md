# Indio City Golf Course

## Summary
Indio City Golf Course had a pond used for field observations of hybrid tilapia temperature tolerance. Hybrids at this location survived brief exposure to 42°F on November 15, 1964, but experienced complete mortality when temperatures stabilized at 50-55°F for extended periods through December 23.
