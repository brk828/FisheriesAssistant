# Sacramento Hitch

## Summary
Sacramento Hitch (Lavinia exilicauda exilicauda) is a native California minnow in the family Cyprinidae. This source documents an extreme example of range expansion through bait transport, with a specimen collected in 1954 from Ramer Lake in Imperial County, approximately 320 road miles south of its regular range.
