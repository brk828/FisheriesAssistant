# Lake Missoula

## Summary
Lake Missoula was an ice-dammed glacial lake that produced catastrophic floods affecting the Columbia River basin. These floods impacted the distribution of Westslope Cutthroat Trout and influenced their dispersal patterns, including their movement across the Continental Divide into the Saskatchewan and Upper Missouri drainages.
