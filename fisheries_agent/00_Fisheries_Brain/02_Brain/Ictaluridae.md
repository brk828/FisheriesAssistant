# Ictaluridae

## Summary
Ictaluridae is a family of North American catfishes that comprises numerous freshwater species distributed across diverse aquatic ecosystems from the United States through Mexico, including notable genera such as Ictalurus (channel catfishes and related species). These benthic foragers are important components of freshwater fish assemblages and are frequently the subjects of ecological surveys, food-habits studies, and conservation assessments, particularly in regions where species endemism occurs or where non-native introductions may impact native populations. The family exhibits considerable taxonomic diversity and morphological variation, making it a significant focus for systematic studies, anomaly documentation, and biogeographic research in ichthyological literature.
