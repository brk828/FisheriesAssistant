# Sycamore Canyon

## Summary
Sycamore Canyon is a perennial desert stream drainage located in the Atascosa Mountains within the Nogales Ranger District of the Coronado National Forest in Arizona that serves as critical habitat for the federally protected Sonora chub (Gila ditaenia). As part of the species' 1992 recovery plan, Sycamore Canyon was identified as a priority site for interagency habitat conservation efforts, including watershed management and grazing allotment planning to protect and restore this aquatic ecosystem. The canyon represents a key example of collaborative conservation work among federal and state agencies aimed at preserving endemic fish populations and their desert stream habitats in the southwestern United States.
