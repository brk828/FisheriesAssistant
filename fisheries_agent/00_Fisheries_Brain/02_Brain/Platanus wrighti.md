# Platanus wrighti

## Summary
Platanus wrighti is a sycamore species that forms part of the Deciduous Riparian Woodland vegetation in the southwestern United States. According to this study of the Gila River Basin in Arizona, it is one of the dominant riparian tree species, along with cottonwood and willows, that characterizes intermittent and perennial streams at lower elevations.
