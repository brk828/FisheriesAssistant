# Tennessee Valley Authority

## Summary
The Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) is a major federal agency responsible for managing hydroelectric dams, water resources, and fisheries programs across the Tennessee River basin and southeastern United States, playing a critical role in both aquatic habitat alteration and fish conservation efforts. As a large-scale water management system, TVA operations have significantly impacted riverine ecosystems through dam construction and water regulation, while simultaneously establishing hatchery facilities and restoration programs to mitigate species declines and propagate both native and anadromous fishes for population recovery. TVA's dual role—as both a driver of habitat modification and an institution actively engaged in fisheries restoration—makes it central to understanding modern freshwater conservation challenges and management strategies in the southeastern United States.
