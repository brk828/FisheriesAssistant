# Clear Lake Splittail

## Summary
Clear Lake Splittail (Pogonichthys ciscoides) was an endemic fish species found in Clear Lake within California's Sacramento-San Joaquin Province. This source identifies it as an extinct species that was unique to this large natural lake system.
