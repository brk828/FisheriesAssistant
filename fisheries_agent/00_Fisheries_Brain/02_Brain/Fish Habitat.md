# Fish Habitat

## Summary
Fish habitat encompasses the physical, chemical, and biological characteristics of aquatic environments necessary to support fish populations and their life processes, including water flow dynamics, substrate composition, water quality parameters, and food availability. Instream flow—the natural movement of water through river channels—represents a critical habitat component that maintains suitable conditions for fish survival and reproduction while supporting broader ecosystem functions. Fish habitat quality is fundamentally determined by measurable environmental variables such as dissolved oxygen, nutrient concentrations, sediment composition, and hydrological conditions, all of which influence both fish community structure and the availability of food resources.
