# Pearce Ferry

## Summary
Pearce Ferry is located at river mile 280 on the Colorado River, marking the downstream end of the Grand Canyon study area where it meets the upper reaches of Lake Mead. The 70-kilometer stretch from Bridge Canyon to Pearce Ferry represents the Lake Mead inflow area, which this source identifies as potentially the only suitable habitat in Grand Canyon for completing all razorback sucker life stages due to its braided channel system, flooded bottomlands, seasonally-warmed tributaries, and spawning substrate, though it also contains high densities of non-native predatory fish.
