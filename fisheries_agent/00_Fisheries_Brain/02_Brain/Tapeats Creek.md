# Tapeats Creek

## Summary
Tapeats Creek is a tributary of the Colorado River within the Grand Canyon that serves as an important sampling locality in ichthyological surveys and a critical habitat for both native and introduced fish species. The creek's cooler waters support populations of native species such as speckled dace (Rhinichthys osculus) and introduced rainbow trout (Salmo gairdneri), making it a key site for monitoring fish communities and assessing the ecological impacts of species introductions in the Grand Canyon ecosystem. Its inclusion in multiple regional fish surveys underscores its significance for understanding tributary-mainstem fish habitat associations and conservation status in this unique aquatic system.
