# College Farm Pit

## Summary
College Farm Pit was a managed strip-pit lake that produced quality panfish growth. The site yielded bluegills measuring 5.5-7 inches in June 1953, black crappie reaching 7.75 inches, and a notable 2-pound crappie catch in May 1954.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
