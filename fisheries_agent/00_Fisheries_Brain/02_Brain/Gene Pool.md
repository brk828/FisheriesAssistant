# Gene Pool

## Summary
A gene pool refers to the total genetic diversity and hereditary material present within a population or species. According to this 1977 legal opinion, protecting hybrids under the Endangered Species Act could harm conservation efforts by diluting or eliminating the original gene pool of endangered species through interbreeding, as hybrids may interbreed with remaining purebreds and disrupt the genetic integrity that enables species to reproduce themselves rather than producing other species.
