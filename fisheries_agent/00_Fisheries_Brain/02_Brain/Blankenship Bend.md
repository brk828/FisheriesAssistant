# Blankenship Bend

## Summary
Blankenship Bend is a reservoir-type study site in Lake Havasu where bonytail post-stocking survival was monitored. At this site, 55% of telemetry-tagged fish (N=22) were either lost or confirmed dead, and remote PIT scanning contacted 27% of stocked fish (N=509), with 96% of contacts occurring in the first week post-release.

## Context

> This multi-year study (2013-2016) documented post-stocking survival, distribution, and habitat use of endangered [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) in [[Lake Havasu]], [[Colorado River]].
