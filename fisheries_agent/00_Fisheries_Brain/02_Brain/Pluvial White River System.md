# Pluvial White River System

## Summary
The Pluvial White River System is a network of desert spring systems in Nevada that supports endemic fish species and unique invertebrate communities. This research proposal seeks to conduct comprehensive limnological surveys of this system, which may connect with the White River Valley System, to document baseline water chemistry and ecological conditions before further development and water table lowering alter these rapidly disappearing habitats.
