# Cottonball Marsh Pupfish

## Summary
The Cottonball Marsh Pupfish is a critically endangered endemic species of the Cyprinodontidae family inhabiting the isolated spring systems of the Death Valley region, representing one of many highly range-restricted desert pupfish species facing extinction due to habitat loss, groundwater depletion, and invasive species competition. As a specialist inhabitant of ephemeral or marginal aquatic habitats in the arid Great Basin, this pupfish exemplifies the conservation challenges facing desert aquatic ecosystems where multiple endemic species compete for limited water resources and face threats from human water management practices. The species' survival depends on active conservation interventions including habitat protection, population monitoring, and potentially captive breeding programs coordinated among Death Valley area management agencies.
