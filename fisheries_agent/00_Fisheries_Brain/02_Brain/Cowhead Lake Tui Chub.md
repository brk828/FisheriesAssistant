# Cowhead Lake Tui Chub

## Summary
Cowhead Lake Tui Chub is a subspecies of Tui Chub endemic to California's interior drainages. This source recommends it for endangered species listing as part of the Interior drainages regional cluster, representing one of the small freshwater dispersant fishes in isolated habitats that are threatened with extinction.
