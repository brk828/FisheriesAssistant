# Dyar's Rule

## Summary
Dyar's Rule (1890) predicts that sclerotized structures in insect larvae increase by a consistent geometric ratio between successive instars. This study found that while the first three instars of Simulium ornatum var. nitidifrons showed approximate geometric size increases in frons-clypeus width, this pattern was not maintained in later instars, indicating the species does not follow Dyar's Rule.
