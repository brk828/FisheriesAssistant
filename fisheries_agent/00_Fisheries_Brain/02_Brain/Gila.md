# Gila

## Summary
Gila is a genus of cyprinid fishes native to the southwestern United States, particularly the Colorado River basin and associated drainages, with several species and subspecies of significant conservation concern due to habitat loss and environmental degradation. Members of this genus, such as Gila cypha (humpback chub), are important subjects of ichthyological research and conservation biology efforts focused on understanding taxonomy, population dynamics, and recovery strategies for endemic fish species in arid aquatic ecosystems. The genus serves as a key indicator of freshwater ecosystem health in the American Southwest and has been central to debates over water management, species protection, and the ecological impacts of river modification in the region.
