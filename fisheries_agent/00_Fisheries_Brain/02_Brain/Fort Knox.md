# Fort Knox

## Summary
Fort Knox area in Kentucky contains a small lake where Chara contraria populations show cytological radiomimetic effects with aberrant chromosome numbers (ranging from rare 14s up to 42s alongside normal 28s). The lake is treated annually with sodium arsenate for aquatic vegetation control, suggesting environmental chemical influences on cytology, and the Fort Knox Radiation Laboratory was used for preliminary X-radiation experiments on Nitella flexilis.
