# Lepomis humilis

## Summary
Lepomis humilis (orangespotted sunfish) is a centrarchid fish found from North Dakota to Texas in sluggish pools. This study rated it as a good bioassay animal due to its prolonged spawning period providing continuous specimen supply, small size that seldom outgrows test requirements, successful transport and holding for over one month, and calm behavior during tests, though it required live food and could tolerate low oxygen levels. It showed a 96-hour median tolerance limit of 17.19% effluent concentration, making it 77% as resistant as the reference guppy.
