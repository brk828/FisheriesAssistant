# Rotenone Treatment

## Summary
Rotenone treatment is a fish management technique that uses rotenone, a naturally-derived piscicide, to selectively eliminate unwanted fish populations in lakes or streams to restore aquatic ecosystems or improve conditions for target species. This chemical approach is commonly employed in conservation efforts to remove non-native or competing fish species that may prey on or outcompete desirable species such as native trout and salmonids. Rotenone application allows resource managers to reset fish communities in isolated water bodies, facilitating the subsequent restocking of preferred species and the restoration of ecological balance in freshwater systems.
