# Columnaris Disease

## Summary
Columnaris Disease is a bacterial infection in fish caused by the myxobacterium *Cytophaga columnaris* (also known as *Chondrococcus columnaris*), which can trigger significant fish-kill epidemics in both wild and cultured populations. This disease has considerable economic importance in aquaculture and fisheries, with environmental factors such as water chemistry and physical stress conditions influencing both the persistence of the pathogen and susceptibility of fish hosts. The disease has been documented as a major cause of mortality in various fish species, including channel catfish, making it an important concern in conservation biology and aquatic ecosystem management.
