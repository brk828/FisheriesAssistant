# San Bernardino Divide

## Summary
The San Bernardino Divide is the southern topographic boundary of San Simon Valley at its highest elevation point of approximately 4,700 feet above sea level. This source identifies it as the location where lava beds (grayish-black or reddish-brown vesicular basalt) occur as part of the valley's Quaternary geology.
