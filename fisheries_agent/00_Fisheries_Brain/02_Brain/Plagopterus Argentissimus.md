# Plagopterus Argentissimus

Plagopterus argentissimus, commonly known as the Colorado River squawfish or pikeminnow, is a large cyprinid fish species native to the Colorado River basin that has become a focal point for conservation efforts due to its endangered status and ecological significance as a apex predator in riverine ecosystems. The species was historically abundant throughout western North American river systems but experienced dramatic population declines due to habitat fragmentation from dam construction, water diversions, and competition from non-native fish species. Its recovery is considered an important indicator of overall ecosystem health in the Colorado River system and has been central to various management and restoration programs aimed at preserving native fish communities in the American Southwest.
