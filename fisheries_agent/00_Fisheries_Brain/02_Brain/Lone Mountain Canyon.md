# Lone Mountain Canyon

## Summary
Lone Mountain Canyon is a tributary of Bear Canyon in the Huachuca Mountains of Arizona at 5,520 feet elevation where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva occurs. This is one of the current Arizona population sites in a mid-elevation cienega habitat.

## Context
