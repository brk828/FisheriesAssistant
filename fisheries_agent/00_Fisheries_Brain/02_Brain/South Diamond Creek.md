# South Diamond Creek

## Summary
South Diamond Creek is a headwater stream in the Gila River system that historically supported one of only five remaining Gila Trout populations by the 1960s. In July 1989, wildfire and subsequent floods reduced this population by more than 95%, demonstrating the vulnerability of small isolated populations; it was used as a temporary holding area (accessed by helicopter through the Aldo Leopold Wilderness) for Gila Trout during the Mogollon Creek restoration project.
