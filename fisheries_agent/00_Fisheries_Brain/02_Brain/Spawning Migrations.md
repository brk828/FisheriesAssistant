# Spawning Migrations

## Summary
Colorado squawfish exhibit directed spawning migrations of 200+ km from their home ranges (5 km reaches) to one of two widely separated spawning grounds, showing unerring site fidelity across years with no switching between sites, suggesting olfactory homing. Post-spawn adults return over days to weeks, while in the lower basin, historical spawning likely occurred March-July in canyon reaches with the delta serving as a major nursery area.
