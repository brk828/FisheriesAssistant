# Gila Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Gila Roundtail Chub (*Gila robusta grahami*) is a cyprinid endemic to the southwestern United States, representing a subspecific designation of *Gila robusta* that inhabits smaller perennial streams and headwater habitats within the Gila River basin and adjacent drainages. This native fish species has become a focus of conservation and recovery efforts due to habitat degradation, water management impacts, and competition from non-native species, reflecting the broader challenges facing endemic desert fishes in the American Southwest. As a generalized cyprinid adapted to warm-water stream ecosystems, the Gila Roundtail Chub serves as an important indicator species for assessing the health and integrity of arid-region freshwater ecosystems and represents the fine-scaled, geographically and taxonomically specific approach required for effective desert fish recovery programs.
