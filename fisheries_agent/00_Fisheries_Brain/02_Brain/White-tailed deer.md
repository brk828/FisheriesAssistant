# White-tailed deer

## Summary
White-tailed deer (Odocoileus virginianus) is a medium-sized ungulate that inhabits grasslands and riparian environments. This source notes that white-tailed deer occur in grasslands and riparian environments in the Salt River Valley region, though deer remains from La Lomita Pequeña were only identified to genus level (Odocoileus sp.) and could represent either white-tailed or mule deer.
