# Bullfrog Bay

## Summary
Bullfrog Bay is one of the key sampling locations identified in this Lake Powell monitoring proposal. It is listed among the 14 stations along the reservoir mainstem where CTD profiling and water chemistry samples would be collected during the three annual lake-wide sampling trips.
