# Catostomus discobolus yarrowi

## Summary
Catostomus discobolus yarrowi is a subspecies of sucker fish found in the southwestern United States. The Desert Fishes Recovery Team identified it as a potential future candidate for listing under the Endangered Species Act, and team members were asked to familiarize themselves with this taxon along with several other native fish species during the December 1985 meeting.
