# Calima River

## Summary
The Calima River is a Pacific slope tributary where important gymnotid specimens were collected, including one specimen of Sternarchus leptorhynchus (130 mm) from a creek near its mouth. The river also yielded specimens of Gymnotus carapo and Hypopomus brevirostris during the survey.
