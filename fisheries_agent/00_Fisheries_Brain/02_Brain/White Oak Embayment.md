# White Oak Embayment

## Summary
White Oak Embayment is a location in the Clinch River system where silt was found to trap radioactivity effectively. Centrifuged silt from this embayment showed 2 × 10⁴ counts/min/gram while the supernatant water was near background levels (August 31, 1956), demonstrating how suspended particles in the Clinch River water capture and transport radioactive materials.
