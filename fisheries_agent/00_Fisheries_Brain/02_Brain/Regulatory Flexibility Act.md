# Regulatory Flexibility Act

## Summary
The Regulatory Flexibility Act is a federal law requiring agencies to assess whether regulations will have a significant economic effect on a substantial number of small entities. In this critical habitat designation for spikedace, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service determined there would be no significant economic effect on small entities, with maximum identifiable added costs of only $150,000 shared across two species' critical habitats.
