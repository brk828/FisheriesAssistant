# San Juan Arm

## Summary
The San Juan Arm is the second major branch of Lake Powell's Y-shaped configuration, extending 64 miles upstream from the Colorado-San Juan confluence at full pool. This arm receives inflows from the San Juan River characterized by much lower salinities (361 ppm average) than the Colorado sources, though salinity increased 52% during the study period; these less-saline waters appear to float in a large surface patch in mid-reservoir rather than being entrained in deeper density currents.
