# Umbridae

## Summary
Umbridae is a family of small freshwater fishes commonly known as mudminnows, classified as a primary freshwater family with limited salinity tolerance and restricted geographic distribution primarily in the Northern Hemisphere. These fish are characterized by their small size, primitive morphological features, and preference for sluggish, vegetated freshwater habitats, making them important indicators of specific freshwater biogeographic regions and paleozoogeographic patterns. Members of this family, such as *Novumbra hubbsi*, are significant in conservation biology and ichthyogeographic studies due to their relict distributions that reflect historical continental patterns and limited dispersal capabilities.
