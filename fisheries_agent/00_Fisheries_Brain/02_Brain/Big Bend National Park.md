# Big Bend National Park

## Summary
Big Bend National Park, located in Texas along the Rio Grande, is a critical conservation area for endemic and threatened fish species in the Chihuahuan Desert region, particularly supporting populations of native cyprinids like Campostoma ornatum in its tributary streams such as Tornillo Creek and Terlingua Creek. The park represents a key protected habitat within the larger Rio Grande basin recovery efforts for desert fishes and serves as an important reference site for monitoring aquatic biodiversity across the middle Rio Grande ecosystem. As part of the binational Chihuahuan Desert/Rio Grande Complex, Big Bend National Park contributes significantly to fish conservation and research initiatives spanning both the United States and Mexico.
