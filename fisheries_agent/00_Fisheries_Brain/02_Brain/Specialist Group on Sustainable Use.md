# Specialist Group on Sustainable Use

## Summary
The Specialist Group on Sustainable Use is one of five disciplinary groups within SSC that addresses thematic conservation issues across taxa. Its specific mandate is to promote sustainable use as a conservation tool, reflecting one of the three core principles from the World Conservation Strategy. The group's work addresses sustainable utilization, which is identified as a potentially controversial issue requiring review by SSC leadership before formal interventions are made.
