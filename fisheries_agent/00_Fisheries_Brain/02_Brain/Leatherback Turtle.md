# Leatherback Turtle

## Summary
The Leatherback Turtle is a marine reptile species documented as endemic and endangered in the Upper Gulf of California bioregion. It is one of several threatened marine species in the region that the International Sonoran Desert Alliance aims to protect through comprehensive, binational ecosystem management.
