# Shad

## Summary
Shad refers to a group of anadromous and semi-anadromous fish species in the family Clupeidae, characterized by their migratory life history and ecological importance in freshwater and estuarine systems. These silvery fish are significant both commercially and ecologically, serving as forage species for predatory fish populations and as indicator species for water quality and habitat connectivity. In conservation and fisheries management contexts, shad populations are monitored to assess the health of riverine ecosystems and the effectiveness of habitat restoration efforts, particularly regarding dam removal and fish passage initiatives.
