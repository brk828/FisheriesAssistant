Xiphophorus gordoni is an endemic platyfish (family Poeciliidae) described by Miller and Minckley in 1963 from the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin in Coahuila, Mexico, representing part of this region's unusually high concentration of endemic fish taxa. This species has been of particular scientific interest for genetic and histocompatibility research, serving as a model organism for studying tissue transplantation and genetic differentiation among isolated populations in this unique desert aquatic ecosystem. Its discovery and subsequent study exemplify the conservation and evolutionary significance of the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin as a biodiversity hotspot for freshwater ichthyofauna in northern Mexico.

## Xyrauchen

### Summary
Xyrauchen is a genus of suckers in the family Catostomidae, most notably represented by the razorback sucker (Xyrauchen texanus), a large North American catostomid fish characterized by a distinctive humped back and compressed body form. This genus has significant conservation importance as the razorback sucker is endemic to the Colorado River basin and is listed as endangered due to habitat degradation, river regulation, and competition from introduced species. Xyrauchen species are bottom feeders adapted to large river systems and represent a key component of native ichthyofaunal communities in western North American aquatic ecosystems.

## Xyrauchen texanus
<!-- fm: auto_enriched: true -->

### Summary
Xyrauchen texanus, commonly known as the razorback sucker, is an endangered freshwater fish species native to the Colorado River basin characterized by a distinctive nuchal hump (pronounced hump behind the head) and highly specialized habitat requirements. This large sucker species has become a focal point of conservation efforts throughout the southwestern United States, with management programs including hatchery propagation, PIT tagging for population monitoring, and strategic stocking in designated river reaches and reservoirs to recover depleted wild populations. The species serves as a key indicator of Colorado River ecosystem health and represents a critical target for both fisheries management and endangered species recovery initiatives in the Lower Basin region.
