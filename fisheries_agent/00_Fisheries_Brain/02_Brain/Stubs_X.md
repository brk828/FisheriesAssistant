## Xiphophorus
<!-- fm: auto_enriched: true -->

### Summary
Xiphophorus is a genus of small livebearing fish (family Poeciliidae) native to Mexico and Central America that has become significant in both aquatic ecology and genetic research due to its natural occurrence in isolated desert spring systems like the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin and its utility as a model organism for studying genetic markers and population differentiation. The genus encompasses species well-adapted to specific aquatic habitats, making it valuable for understanding fish conservation in restricted endemic ranges and for investigating the genetic basis of traits through comparative studies across populations.
