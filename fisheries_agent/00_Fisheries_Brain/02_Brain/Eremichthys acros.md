# Eremichthys acros

## Summary
Eremichthys acros is the desert dace, a cyprinid fish species. This source identifies it as a species in the Black Rock Desert that would be secondarily influenced by the proposed MX Missile System.

## Context

> This December 1979 urgent letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]] to NANFA members warns of catastrophic threats to desert aquatic ecosystems in [[Nevada]] and [[Utah]] from the proposed [[MX Missile System]].
