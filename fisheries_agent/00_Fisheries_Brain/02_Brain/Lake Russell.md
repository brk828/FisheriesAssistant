# Lake Russell

## Summary
Lake Russell was a large pluvial lake that occupied the Mono Basin during the Pleistocene, reaching an estimated maximum surface area of 267 square miles. The lake expanded during glacial maxima, with the highest shoreline at 7,170-7,180 feet elevation during the Tahoe glaciation, and may have overflowed through Adobe Meadows into Adobe Valley at 7,150 feet, potentially connecting to a chain of pluvial lakes extending to Death Valley.
