# Gambusia Amistadensis

## Summary
Gambusia amistadensis is a small livebearing fish endemic to the Amistad Reservoir region in Texas, representing one of the native freshwater fish species documented in comprehensive surveys of Texas aquatic fauna. As a member of the Gambusiidae family (mosquitofish), this species is of particular interest in conservation biology due to its restricted geographic range and potential vulnerability to habitat alteration, water management practices, and competition from introduced species. The species exemplifies the importance of biodiversity inventories in identifying and monitoring rare or localized fish populations within complex aquatic ecosystems.
