# Typhlichthys Subterraneus

## Summary
Typhlichthys subterraneus, commonly known as the Southern Cavefish, is a relatively large-bodied troglobitic fish belonging to the family Amblyopsidae that inhabits cave systems primarily in the Ohio and Mississippi River basin region. This species is notable in conservation and ecological research as an example of a troglobite with an extensive geographic range despite its specialized cave habitat, demonstrating that range extent in subterranean fishes correlates more strongly with mobility and niche breadth than with body size alone. Its presence in multiple cave systems across the central United States makes it an important indicator species for understanding how aquatic organisms adapt to and persist in isolated subterranean environments.
