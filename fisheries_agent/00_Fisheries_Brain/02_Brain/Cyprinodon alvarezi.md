# Cyprinodon alvarezi

## Summary
Cyprinodon alvarezi is a pupfish species suspected to be related to Cyprinodon macrolepis and Cyprinodon fontinalis. This source discusses it as part of ongoing taxonomic work on Guzman Basin pupfishes, with researchers proposing it forms a relationship group separate from Cyprinodon artatus.
