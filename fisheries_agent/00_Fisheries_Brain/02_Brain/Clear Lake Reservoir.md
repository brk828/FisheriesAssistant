# Clear Lake Reservoir

## Summary
Clear Lake Reservoir is a water body in California that receives Boles Creek as a tributary and serves as the origin point of the Lost River. This source indicates that Lost River flows north from Clear Lake Reservoir into Oregon, then re-enters California before discharging into Tule Lake, forming part of the habitat system for Lost River Sucker and Shortnose Sucker.

## Context

> This 1965 report documents the first confirmed occurrences of [[Lost River Sucker]] (*Deltistes luxatus*) and [[Shortnose Sucker]] (*Chasmistes brevirostris*) in California waters, adding both species to the state's freshwater fish fauna.
