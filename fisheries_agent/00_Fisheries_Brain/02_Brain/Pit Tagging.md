# Pit Tagging

## Summary
Pit tagging is a non-lethal fish identification and tracking method that involves implanting passive integrated transponder (PIT) tags—small electronic microchips—into individual fish to enable long-term monitoring of their movements, survival, and population dynamics. This technique is particularly valuable for endangered species recovery programs and multi-agency conservation efforts, as it allows researchers to re-identify and track the same individual fish across multiple sampling events and locations without causing significant harm. PIT tagging systems form a critical component of standardized monitoring protocols in aquatic ecology, supporting data collection and interagency data-sharing initiatives that inform evidence-based management decisions.
