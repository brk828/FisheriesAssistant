# Rock Pocket Mouse

## Summary
The Rock Pocket Mouse is a rodent species for which the George Whittell Wildlife Preserve lies at the eastern edge of its range. This positioning contributes to the area's biogeographic significance as a transition zone where Sonoran, Great Plains, Chihuahuan, and Madrean faunal elements converge.
