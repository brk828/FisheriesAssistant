# Deerlodge Park

## Summary
Deerlodge Park is a location on the Yampa River used as a reference point for flow measurements. According to this source, typical peak flows at Deerlodge Park during late May to early June range from 7,748 to 20,476 cfs (representing 75-25% exceedance values), which are critical for supporting endangered fish species.
