# Phytoplankton

## Summary
Phytoplankton are microscopic photosynthetic organisms that form the foundation of aquatic food webs and represent the principal primary producers in most freshwater and marine ecosystems. In fish science and aquatic ecology, phytoplankton productivity is a critical measure of ecosystem health and carrying capacity, directly influencing the availability of food for zooplankton and ultimately determining fish growth, survival, and yield. The abundance and composition of phytoplankton communities serve as key indicators of water quality, nutrient status, and the overall energy flow through aquatic food chains supporting fish populations.
