# Acipenser stellatus

## Summary
Acipenser stellatus is the stellate sturgeon, a species mentioned as ecologically associated with pickerel (Sander) in the Caspian Sea region. Combined gill nets with different mesh sizes on common side-lines were designed to capture both species simultaneously, with appropriately-sized fish wedging in the first net encountered while smaller fish pass through to finer mesh sections.
