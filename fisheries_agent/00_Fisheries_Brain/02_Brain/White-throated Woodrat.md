# White-throated Woodrat

## Summary
The White-throated Woodrat (Neotoma albigula) is a rodent that builds conspicuous stick dens and is usually observed or common at lower elevations. This survey found individuals with a mean weight of 171.9 grams in the Aravaipa Creek area.
