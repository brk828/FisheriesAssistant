# Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences

## Summary
The Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences operated the Institute of Vertebrate Zoology in Brno, which included Ichthyological and Ornithological Departments. This institute was one of the major participating research organizations in the coordinated aquatic research program.
