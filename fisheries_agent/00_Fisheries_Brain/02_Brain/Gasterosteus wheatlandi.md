# Gasterosteus wheatlandi

## Summary
Gasterosteus wheatlandi is the Twospine Stickleback, representing a significant taxonomic clarification in Canadian waters. It was reported from Quebec (McAllister 1960) and Newfoundland (Scott and Crossman 1964), having been previously confused with G. aculeatus.
