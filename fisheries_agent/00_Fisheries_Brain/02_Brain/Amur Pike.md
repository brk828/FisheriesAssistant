# Amur Pike

## Summary
Amur Pike (Esox reicherti) is an Asian pike species closely related to the native Northern Pike. It was introduced to Pennsylvania and has already produced hybrids with Northern Pike, demonstrating the genetic contamination risks that occur when introducing sister species that can interbreed with native congeners.
