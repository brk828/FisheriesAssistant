# Couch's Spadefoot Toad

## Summary
Couch's Spadefoot Toad (Scaphiopus couchii) is an amphibian expected to occur at Papago Park based on its distribution and habitat requirements. The assessment noted that its occurrence would be dependent on the presence of standing water after summer rains, though no amphibians were observed during the survey period.
