# Wyoming Toad

## Summary
The Wyoming Toad is an endangered amphibian threatened by all three pesticides (carbaryl, malathion, and acephate) which can affect both aquatic larvae and terrestrial adults that feed on arthropods. The reasonable and prudent alternative requires a 1/4-mile buffer on each side of the Little Laramie River in Albany County, Wyoming, to protect this species from jeopardy.
