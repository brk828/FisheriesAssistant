# Yavapai-Prescott Indian Community

## Summary
The Yavapai-Prescott Indian Community proposed to divert 500 acre-feet of water annually (equivalent to approximately 1 cfs) from the upper Verde River as part of the Central Arizona Water Exchange Project. The assessment found that if only this community proceeded with its smaller diversion, overall impacts to spikedace would be minimal.

## Context

> This biological assessment evaluates potential impacts to the threatened [[Spikedace]] (*Meda fulgida*) from the [[Central Arizona Project]] (CAP) Water Exchange Project, specifically water diversions from the upper [[Verde River]] system.
