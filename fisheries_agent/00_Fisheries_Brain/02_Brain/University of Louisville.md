# University of Louisville

## Summary
The University of Louisville Department of Biology is the institutional home of Principal Investigator Arland Hotchkiss, who conducted this NSF-funded research on Characeae cytology and taxonomy. The university supported the research through its facilities and graduate student Donald Tindall's work on Nitella flexilis.
