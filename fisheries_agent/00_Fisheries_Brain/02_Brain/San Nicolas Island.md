# San Nicolas Island

## Summary
San Nicolas Island is one of the California Channel Islands where CPSU/UA staff conducted restoration and monitoring projects in 1994. The island was part of broader Channel Islands research efforts that were discussed at The Fourth California Islands Symposium.
