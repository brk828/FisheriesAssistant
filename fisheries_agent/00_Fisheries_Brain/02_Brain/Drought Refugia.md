# Drought Refugia

## Summary
Drought periods are identified as the most dangerous natural condition for native fishes, forcing concentration in canyon pools fed by groundwater seepage when valley reaches become intermittent. Whitewater canyons with deep pools, eddies, and spring inputs maintaining perennial flow served as critical drought refugia, allowing populations to persist through low-flow periods.
