# Lepomis Marginatus

## Summary
Lepomis marginatus, commonly known as the dollar sunfish, is a small freshwater sunfish species native to southeastern North America that inhabits lentic and swamp environments, often occurring in association with aquatic vegetation and detrital habitats. This species appears in regional fish surveys and conservation documentation as part of efforts to document fish faunal composition and biodiversity in drainage systems, particularly in Kentucky and surrounding regions where it may represent range extensions or important components of local ichthyofaunal assemblages. Its presence in survey records contributes to understanding the distribution patterns and ecological associations of sunfish species across different drainage basins and habitat types in eastern North American freshwater systems.
