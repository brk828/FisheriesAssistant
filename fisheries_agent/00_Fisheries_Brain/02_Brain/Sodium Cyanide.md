# Sodium Cyanide

## Summary
Sodium cyanide (NaCN) is an extremely poisonous piscicide used in fishery management. The estimated lethal oral dose in humans is 50-60 mg per person, and it releases toxic hydrocyanic gas in solution; chronic exposure causes fatigue, dermatitis, and thyroid enlargement, requiring safety precautions including rubber gloves and carrying amyl nitrite inhalants.
