# Water Chemistry

## Summary
Water chemistry in fish science and aquatic ecology refers to the physical and chemical properties of aquatic environments, including dissolved gases (such as oxygen and carbon dioxide), pH, alkalinity, and mineral composition, all of which directly influence fish physiology, survival, and ecosystem function. These chemical parameters are critical for understanding fish habitat quality, metabolic processes, and the impacts of pollution, algal activity, and industrial effluents on aquatic organisms and their communities. Monitoring and analyzing water chemistry is essential for fish conservation, aquaculture management, and assessing the health and suitability of freshwater and marine ecosystems.
