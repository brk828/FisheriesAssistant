# Gillnets

## Summary
Gillnets are vertical mesh nets that catch fish by their gills when they attempt to swim through. This source indicates that gillnets and trammel nets were the only practical gear types for commercial fishing in Oklahoma during 1958-1960, as state regulations required three-inch square mesh minimum, nets set at least 100 yards from shoreline, and operation at least four feet below the surface.
