# Partners for Wildlife

## Summary
Partners for Wildlife is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service program that supports habitat conservation projects in partnership with landowners. According to this 1996 notice, approximately 15% of the program's 1995 projects directly benefitted listed species, candidates, or species of concern, with this proportion reaching 85% in western states, demonstrating its role in voluntary conservation efforts for at-risk species.
