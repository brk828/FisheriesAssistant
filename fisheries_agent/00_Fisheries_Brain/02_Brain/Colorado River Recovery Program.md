# Colorado River Recovery Program

## Summary
The Colorado River Recovery Program is a comprehensive, federally-coordinated conservation initiative led by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Region 6, Denver) designed to protect and restore endangered fish species native to the Colorado River basin, particularly the Colorado squawfish, through integrated habitat management, population monitoring, and research on ecological factors affecting species recovery. The program encompasses multiple coordinated projects involving mark-recapture studies, fish predation assessments, backwater habitat protection, and data management systems to track population dynamics and guide adaptive management decisions for these imperiled aquatic species.
