# Conrad P. Straub

## Summary
Conrad P. Straub was an author affiliated with the Robert A. Taft Sanitary Engineering Center who co-authored this 1959 paper on radioactive waste disposal in stream environments. He contributed to developing approaches for calculating permissible discharge levels of radioactive materials and emphasized the need for comprehensive environmental monitoring programs.
