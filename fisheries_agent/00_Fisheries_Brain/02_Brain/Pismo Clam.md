# Pismo Clam

## Summary
Pismo Clam is a mollusk species whose size distribution in archaeological middens provides evidence for past temperature conditions. Midden sites along the Baja California coast dated to approximately 300 years B.P. contain abundant small Pismo Clams, which are associated with a warm period that required greater surface water availability.
