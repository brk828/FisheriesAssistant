# Green Sunfish

## Summary
The Green Sunfish (*Lepomis cyanellus*) is a small centrarchid (sunfish family) native to eastern North America that has become established in western aquatic ecosystems, including the Colorado River Basin and Arizona waterways, where it functions as an introduced species competing with and potentially preying upon native fishes. As a generalist predator and omnivore tolerant of diverse habitat conditions, it is commonly encountered in both natural riverine systems and constructed urban lentic environments such as golf course ponds. In conservation contexts, the Green Sunfish represents a significant concern for native fish assemblages, particularly federally listed and threatened species like those found in the Verde River and Colorado River systems.
