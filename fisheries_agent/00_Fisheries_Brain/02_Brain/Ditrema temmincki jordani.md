# Ditrema temmincki jordani

## Summary
Ditrema temmincki jordani is a previously described Japanese species that this source considers a synonym of Ditrema temminckii. This taxonomic revision reduces the number of recognized species within the Japanese Ditrema genus.

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
