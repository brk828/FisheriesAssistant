# Serendipity Ranch

## Summary
Serendipity Ranch contains three large ponds in southern Arizona that were evaluated during the 1991 survey. All three ponds were found to be fishless despite probable recent presence of goldfish and forage fish, with aquatic invertebrates being extremely rare and vegetation choking the ponds.
