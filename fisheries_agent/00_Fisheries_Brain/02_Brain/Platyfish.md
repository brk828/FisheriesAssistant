# Platyfish

## Summary
Platyfish (Poecilia sp.) is an exotic fish species that was introduced to School Spring but was successfully eliminated from this Warm Springs Pupfish habitat. The removal of this exotic species was part of habitat management efforts to reduce competition and protect the endangered pupfish population.
