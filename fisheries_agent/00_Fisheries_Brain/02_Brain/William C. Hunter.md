# William C. Hunter

## Summary
William C. Hunter is a researcher from Arizona State University who conducted this 1987 baseline study documenting vegetation and breeding bird communities along the cattle exclosure on Lower Bonita Creek for the U.S. Bureau of Land Management. The study established baseline data from 1985-1986 to assess the effects of cattle exclusion on riparian regeneration and avifauna.
