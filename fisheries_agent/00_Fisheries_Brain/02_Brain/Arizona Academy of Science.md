# Arizona Academy of Science

## Summary
The Arizona Academy of Science proposed the Leslie Creek area as a Natural Area under the Arizona State Parks system. This proposal was based on the quality of the area's riparian ecosystem and its unique aquatic fauna, including endangered fish species.
