# Bluefish

## Summary
Bluefish (Pomatomus saltatrix) is a piscivorous fish that co-occurs with striped bass in Atlantic coastal waters. This study found that bluefish can consume prey up to 63% of their body length due to their shearing dentition, compared to only ~40% for striped bass, and that much smaller bluefish (80-155 mm) exploit larger absolute prey sizes (optimal ~71 mm at 118 mm predator length) than larger striped bass (optimal ~41 mm at 340 mm predator length), suggesting potential competition for small prey and that striped bass may be more vulnerable to prey shortages.
