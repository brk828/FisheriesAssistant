# Binghamton Swamp

## Summary
Binghamton Swamp is a water area in the San Pedro River basin owned by The Nature Conservancy. According to this source, it is one of several protected waters (along with Muleshoe Ranch and Canelo Cienega) that substantially adds to the importance of Nature Conservancy properties for endangered fish conservation, providing additional protection for native species including Gila Chub, Speckled Dace, Longfin Dace, Desert Sucker, and Sonora Sucker.
