# Bitter Lake NWR

## Summary
Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge is located in Chaves County, New Mexico, and consists of six interconnected shallow impoundments (Units 3, 5, 6, 7, 15, 16) totaling approximately 586 surface acres, constructed primarily as waterfowl habitat with water supplied from springs. Despite decades of stocking warmwater species like Channel Catfish and Largemouth Bass, the refuge experienced persistent failures in establishing self-sustaining sport fish populations due to extreme salinity (reaching 35 ppt in some units), critically low dissolved oxygen from dense Chara beds, very limited deep-water habitat, and heavy predation by waterfowl.
