# Granby Reservoir

## Summary
Granby Reservoir is the largest and deepest of three connected water bodies in Colorado's Colorado River system, sampled to depths of 100 feet in this study. In 1953, it yielded 598 trout in 4,169 fishing hours (0.14 catch-per-man-hour), with Native Trout dominating catches at 40%, followed by Rainbow Trout at 35%. The reservoir showed entomostracan plankton counts of 2.9-38.3 organisms per liter and surface temperatures of 43-66°F with thermoclines at 18-27 feet during summer months.
