# University of California at Davis

## Summary
University of California at Davis was one of two alternative facilities that Dr. Peter Brussard from University of Nevada - Reno planned to use for conducting electrophoretic analysis of Cui-ui samples. This alternative was necessary because his own laboratory at UNR would not be ready in time to complete the hybrid analysis by the December 1 deadline.
