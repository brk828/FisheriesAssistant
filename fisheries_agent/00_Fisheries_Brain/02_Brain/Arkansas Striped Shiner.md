# Arkansas Striped Shiner

## Summary
The Arkansas Striped Shiner (Notropis zonata pilsbryi) is a subspecies of minnow found in Kansas. This source documents it as part of the Arkansas River drainage fauna in southern Kansas, representing one of the many geographic variants among the state's 48 cyprinid species.
