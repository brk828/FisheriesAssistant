# Don Pedro Reservoir

## Summary
Don Pedro Reservoir is located in Tuolumne County, California. As of 1955, it was being used as an experimental site for transplanting native freshwater smelt from the lower Sacramento and San Joaquin Rivers, and freshwater shrimp from the delta area were introduced there in 1954 to evaluate their forage potential.

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
