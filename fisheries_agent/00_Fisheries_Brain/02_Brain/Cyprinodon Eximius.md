# Cyprinodon Eximius

## Summary
Cyprinodon eximius, commonly known as the Cuatro Cienegas pupfish, is a small cyprinodont fish endemic to the Cuatro Cienegas region of Mexico and historically or marginally present in the United States along creek and spring systems. This species is typically listed as endangered or threatened due to its severely restricted geographic range, habitat fragmentation, and vulnerability to environmental changes such as water extraction and habitat degradation. As a range-restricted taxon of conservation concern, C. eximius serves as an important indicator species for assessing the health of desert spring and pupfish ecosystems in the southwestern North American aquatic fauna.
