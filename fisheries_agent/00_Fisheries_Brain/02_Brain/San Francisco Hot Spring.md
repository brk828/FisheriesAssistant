# San Francisco Hot Spring

## Summary
San Francisco Hot Spring is a location in New Mexico that represents the eastern extent of the historical range of the Gila Topminnow (Poeciliopsis occidentalis). This source indicates that the Gila Topminnow was formerly distributed throughout the Gila River basin from near Yuma, Arizona to San Francisco Hot Spring in New Mexico before experiencing dramatic range contractions.
