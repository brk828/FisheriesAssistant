# Aquatic Ecology

## Summary
Aquatic ecology is the scientific study of the physical, chemical, and biological interactions within freshwater and aquatic environments, with particular emphasis on understanding how these factors influence fish populations and community structures. In the context of fish conservation and management, aquatic ecology provides the foundational knowledge needed to assess habitat quality, predict responses to environmental changes, and develop effective strategies for protecting native fish species and maintaining ecosystem integrity. This discipline integrates multiple scientific approaches including geomorphology, hydrology, and biology to comprehensively understand the conditions necessary for sustainable fish populations and healthy aquatic systems.
