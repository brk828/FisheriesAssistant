# Littlefield Spring

## Summary
Littlefield Spring is a natural spring located below the Virgin River Narrows in Arizona that contributes highly saline water to the Virgin River. This spring, along with La Verkin Springs, helps create the extreme environmental conditions (high salinity, temperature, and turbidity) that historically served as barriers to exotic fish invasion while favoring native fauna like the Virgin River Roundtail Chub that are adapted to these harsh conditions.
