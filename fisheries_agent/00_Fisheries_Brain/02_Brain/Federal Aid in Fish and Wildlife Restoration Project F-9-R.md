# Federal Aid in Fish and Wildlife Restoration Project F-9-R

## Summary
Federal Aid in Fish and Wildlife Restoration Project F-9-R was a broader fisheries management and conservation research program that supported the temperature-controlled aquarium facility at Livingston Manor. The laboratory's experimental capabilities contributed to this federal aid project's research objectives.
