# District IV

## Summary
District IV is an administrative region of the Arizona Game and Fish Department that encompasses the Lower Colorado River area, including the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge. This 1961 survey documented that mean annual river flows in District IV declined steadily from approximately 20,000 cubic feet per second in 1952 to 9,818 c.f.s. in 1960, and angler use in the district increased from 1.8 anglers per surface acre per year (1954-55) to 6.7 anglers per surface acre per year (1960).
