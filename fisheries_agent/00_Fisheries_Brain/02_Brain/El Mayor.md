# El Mayor

## Summary
El Mayor, located at Kilometer 55 of the Mexicali-San Felipe Highway, is a Cocopa community that depends mainly on fall and winter tourism, with residents functioning as guides to sport fishermen and duck hunters. The community holds a 143,000-hectare communal ejido with 43 legal member families and is forming a fishing cooperative with credits from the Instituto Indigenista.
