# Potassium Permanganate

## Summary
Potassium permanganate is a chemical oxidizing agent used in aquaculture and fisheries management as a therapeutic treatment for ectoparasitic infections in cultured fish populations. However, its application in fish science presents a critical safety challenge, as the concentrations required for effective parasite control (2–8 ppm) often exceed the acute toxicity threshold (96-hour LC50) for certain species like striped bass, making standard therapeutic dosing potentially lethal to the target fish. This narrow margin between therapeutic and toxic doses has made potassium permanganate a problematic treatment option in fish hatchery and conservation operations, requiring careful species-specific evaluation before use.
