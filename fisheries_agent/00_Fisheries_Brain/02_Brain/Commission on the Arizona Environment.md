# Commission on the Arizona Environment

## Summary
The Commission on the Arizona Environment was a governmental or quasi-governmental body in Arizona that compiled and published the 1987 Arizona Environmental & Resource Conservation Directory. The Commission worked with assistance from Jackie Rich and Julie Hodgson to catalog over 200 businesses, organizations, and agencies concerned with environmental resources in Arizona.
