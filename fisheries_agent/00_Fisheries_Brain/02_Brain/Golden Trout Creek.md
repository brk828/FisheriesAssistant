# Golden Trout Creek

## Summary
Golden Trout Creek is a location in Tulare County, California, from which Golden Trout specimens were collected for this study. Fish from Golden Trout Creek failed to react or reacted only slightly to certain immune sera, showing antigenic patterns similar to Laurel Lakes fish rather than Cottonwood Lakes fish.
