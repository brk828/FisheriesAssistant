# Cui-Ui Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Cui-ui Recovery Plan is a comprehensive, interagency conservation strategy designed to restore populations of the cui-ui (*Chasmistes cujus*), an endangered endemic fish species in Nevada's Pyramid Lake, through coordinated management of spawning habitat, water flows, and fish passage facilities. The plan involves multiple stakeholder agencies collaborating to monitor and enhance recruitment success by ensuring adequate stream conditions for this critically endangered cui-ui to successfully spawn and recruit juveniles, with documented success evidenced by population increases from approximately 150,000 individuals in the early 1980s to over 1 million by 1993. Key components include operational guidelines for facilities like the Marble Bluff Fish Facility and management protocols that optimize hydrological conditions during spawning seasons when high water flows are essential for reproductive success.
