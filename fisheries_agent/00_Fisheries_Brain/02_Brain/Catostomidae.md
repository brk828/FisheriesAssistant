# Catostomidae

## Summary
Catostomidae is a family of freshwater cypriniform fishes commonly known as suckers, characterized by their specialized bottom-feeding morphology and native distribution across North American river systems. Members of this family are ecologically important components of native fish assemblages and are frequently subjects of conservation concern, particularly in western U.S. aquatic ecosystems where habitat degradation and competition from non-native species threaten their persistence. The family includes several imperiled taxa that are monitored and managed under aquatic recovery programs due to their sensitivity to water development impacts and stream habitat alteration.
