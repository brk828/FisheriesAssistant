# Elves Chasm

## Summary
Elves Chasm is a location in Grand Canyon between 131-172 miles below Glen Canyon Dam, in the reach extending to Havasu Creek. This area has been identified as containing downstream aggregations of Humpback Chub and is targeted as a potential site for mainstem spawning under the proposed thermal augmentation scenario. The proposed release of 15°C water in June-September would warm this reach to optimal spawning temperatures of 16-22°C during June-July.
