# Grant Creek

## Summary
Grant Creek is a White Mountains stream where phenotypic observations of a 30-fish sample revealed genetic heterogeneity within the population, with some individuals resembling pure East Fork White River Apache Trout while others were obvious hybrids. According to Dr. Tom Dowling's interpretation, such populations with mixed pure and hybrid individuals will not become genetically swamped and likely trend toward pure Apache Trout over time in the absence of Rainbow Trout stocking.
