# Yampa River Basin Recovery and Water Management Plan

## Summary
The Yampa River Basin Recovery and Water Management Plan is a planning effort to balance endangered fish recovery needs with existing and future water development in the Yampa River Basin. The FY 1998 work plan allocated $398,000 for development of this plan, recognizing the Yampa River as the most important river in the Upper Colorado River Basin for maintenance and recovery of endangered fishes.
