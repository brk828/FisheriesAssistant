# Fontana Lake

## Summary
Fontana Lake is an impoundment constructed in 1944 on the Little Tennessee River bordering Great Smoky Mountains National Park. The lake's creation has influenced fishery resources in tributary waters to an unknown extent, though specific impacts had not been quantified as of 1960.
