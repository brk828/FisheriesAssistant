# San Simon Area

## Summary
The San Simon Area is one of the principal agricultural regions in San Simon Valley, developed for irrigation farming using artesian water following the discovery of flowing wells in late 1910. This source documents it as the center of the flowing-well area where all irrigation comes from the lower confined artesian aquifer rather than the upper unconfined aquifer.
