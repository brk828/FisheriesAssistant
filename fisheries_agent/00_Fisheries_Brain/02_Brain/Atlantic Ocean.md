# Atlantic Ocean

## Summary
The Atlantic Ocean in fish science represents a major biogeographic region and conservation focus characterized by diverse marine and diadromous fish populations spanning coastal, estuarine, and oceanic habitats from tropical to temperate zones. This basin includes ecologically significant species such as shads, cod, haddock, American eel, and various marine fishes that have been subject to extensive historical management through hatchery stocking and restocking programs due to their economic and ecological importance. The Atlantic Ocean's fish communities are defined by complex connectivity patterns between marine and freshwater systems, making it a critical area for understanding fish migration, population dynamics, and the effects of human intervention on wild stocks.
