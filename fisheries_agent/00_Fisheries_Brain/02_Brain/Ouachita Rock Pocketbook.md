# Ouachita Rock Pocketbook

## Summary
The Ouachita Rock Pocketbook (Arkansia wheeleri) is a freshwater mussel in the family Unionidae that inhabits the Kiamichi River in Oklahoma and historically occurred in Arkansas. This Federal Register document lists the species as endangered in Oklahoma and Arkansas under the Endangered Species Act of 1973. The species was first described in 1912 and has been the subject of status surveys examining its distribution and population status, with archaeological remains documenting its historical presence in Oklahoma.
