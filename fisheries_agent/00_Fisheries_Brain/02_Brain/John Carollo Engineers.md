# John Carollo Engineers

## Summary
John Carollo Engineers was a private engineering firm that provided design support for the 1987 Arizona Environmental & Resource Conservation Directory. The company contributed professional services to help produce this comprehensive catalog of environmental organizations in Arizona.

## Context

> This comprehensive directory, published by the Commission on the Arizona Environment in 1987, catalogs businesses, organizations, and agencies concerned with environmental resources in Arizona. The document provides contact information and descriptions for over 200 entities across multiple sectors...
