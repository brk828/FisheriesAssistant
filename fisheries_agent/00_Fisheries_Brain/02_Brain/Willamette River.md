# Willamette River

## Summary
The Willamette River is a tributary of the Columbia River in Oregon that supports populations of Chinook salmon with both yearling and subyearling life histories. This study focused on hatchery-produced yearling spring Chinook salmon from three primary upper-Willamette populations (McKenzie River, North Fork Santiam River, and South Fork Santiam River), which were reared at Willamette Hatchery before being transported to Blind Slough for release into the Columbia River estuary.
