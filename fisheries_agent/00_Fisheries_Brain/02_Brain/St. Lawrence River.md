# St. Lawrence River

## Summary
The St. Lawrence River represents a critical freshwater and estuarine system in eastern Canada that serves as a primary habitat and migratory corridor for numerous fish species, including anadromous and catadromous fishes such as Atlantic Sturgeon. As a major river system connecting the Great Lakes to the Atlantic Ocean, it has been a focal point for fisheries research and conservation studies, including extensive fish tagging programs that have tracked movement patterns and population dynamics across both the river proper and its estuary. The system's ecological and commercial importance has made it a key subject for understanding fish behavior, distribution, and the impacts of human activities on aquatic biodiversity in northeastern North America.
