# Prosopium

## Summary
Prosopium is a genus within the Salmonidae family, specifically belonging to the subfamily Coregoninae, and comprises freshwater whitefish species found in North American and Eurasian freshwater systems. Members of this genus are characterized by their adaptation to lacustrine and riverine environments and are significant components of Holarctic freshwater fish assemblages, making them important for understanding coregonid phylogenetics, biogeography, and the evolutionary history of salmonid fishes. The genus has fossil representation in Pliocene lacustrine deposits, providing paleontological evidence for understanding historical distribution patterns and environmental changes in North American aquatic ecosystems.
