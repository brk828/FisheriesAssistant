# Carassius Auratus

## Summary
Carassius auratus, commonly known as the goldfish, is a domesticated cyprinid fish originally native to East Asia that has become one of the most widespread non-native fish species globally due to its popularity in aquaculture and ornamental trade. In ecological contexts, feral and escaped goldfish populations are recognized as invasive species with significant impacts on native aquatic communities, as they are generalist omnivores capable of outcompeting native cyprinids and altering benthic habitats through their foraging behavior. Its presence in wild ecosystems represents an important case study in aquatic invasion biology and the conservation challenges posed by the intersection of ornamental aquaculture and environmental integrity.
