# Von Bertalanffy Growth Model

## Summary
The Von Bertalanffy Growth Model is a mathematical model describing fish growth over time using parameters for asymptotic length (L∞) and growth coefficient (K). This source calculated razorback sucker growth parameters as L∞ = 108.2 cm and K = 0.0818, with approximately linear growth for three years post-stocking (6 cm in year 1, 5 cm in year 2), and noted the K value was much lower than Lake Mohave populations (K=0.86), possibly due to shorter time-at-large in the lower river.
