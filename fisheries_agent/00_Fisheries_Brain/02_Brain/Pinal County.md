# Pinal County

## Summary
Pinal County is a county in Arizona that contains part of Aravaipa Creek. The 1974 fish survey covered portions of Aravaipa Creek located in both Pinal County and Graham County.

## Context

> A fish survey of [[Aravaipa Creek]] in [[Arizona]] was conducted November 9-11, 1974, by Arizona State University's Ichthyology class under supervision of Dr. W. L. Minckley.
