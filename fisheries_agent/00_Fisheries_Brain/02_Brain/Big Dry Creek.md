# Big Dry Creek

## Summary
Big Dry Creek received a replicated population of Gila Trout from Spruce Creek in 1985 as part of recovery efforts. The 1985 antimycin treatment of Big Dry Creek provided reference data showing that macroinvertebrate communities recovered within one year following piscicide application.
