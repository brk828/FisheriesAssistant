# Svratka River

## Summary
The Svratka River is a stream in Czechoslovakia that was dammed to create the Vir Reservoir. Below the dam, organic drift was dominated by planktonic individuals (76.2%) swept from the reservoir, with plankton remaining viable 55 km downstream.
