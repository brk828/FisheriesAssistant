# Lilaeopsis

## Summary
Lilaeopsis is a genus containing 13 species of perennial rhizomatous herbs inhabiting temperate and alpine regions of North and South America and Australasia. Lilaeopsis schaffneriana is one of 4 strictly freshwater species in the genus, occurring in southeastern Arizona, central and northern Mexico, and northwestern South America.

## Context

> This status report documents [[Lilaeopsis schaffneriana]] subspecies recurva, a rare semi-aquatic perennial plant endemic to cienega wetlands in southeastern Arizona and adjacent Sonora, Mexico.
