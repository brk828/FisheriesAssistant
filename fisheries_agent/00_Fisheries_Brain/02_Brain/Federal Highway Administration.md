# Federal Highway Administration

## Summary
The Federal Highway Administration is a federal agency that may be involved in multi-agency consultations under the Endangered Species Act. This source identifies the Federal Highway Administration as one of several federal agencies (along with Corps of Engineers, Bureau of Reclamation, Federal Emergency Management Agency, and Natural Resources Conservation Service) that could be involved in consultations alongside the Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management, raising questions about which consultation regulations would apply in such multi-agency scenarios.
