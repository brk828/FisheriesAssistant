# Two Buttes Reservoir

## Summary
Two Buttes Reservoir is a warm water fishery located in Baca County, Colorado, that was monitored as one of nine representative waters in the 1955 creel census. The reservoir had relatively poor fishing success with a catch-per-man-hour of only 0.24 (389 fishermen caught 221 fish), and it received stocking of 715 Drum (freshwater sheepshead) from Kansas along with John Martin Reservoir and Sloan's Lake.
