# Astacidae

## Summary
Astacidae is a family of freshwater crustaceans commonly known as crayfish or crawfish, characterized by their resemblance to small lobsters with paired claws and segmented bodies. As keystone species in freshwater ecosystems, Astacidae members play important roles in nutrient cycling, substrate disturbance, and food webs, making them significant subjects for conservation biology and aquatic ecology research, particularly regarding population dynamics, habitat requirements, and responses to environmental degradation. The family has been a major focus of systematic and taxonomic studies, with considerable attention to species identification, distribution patterns, and biogeographic variation across different freshwater regions.
