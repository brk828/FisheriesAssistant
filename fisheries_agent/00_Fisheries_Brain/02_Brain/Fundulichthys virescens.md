# Fundulichthys virescens

## Summary
Fundulichthys virescens (common names: Omedaka, Akitabira) is a small killifish species from Japan known only from Schlegel's 1846 description based on Bürger's drawing. It was reported as very common in brooks and rivers flowing into Nagasaki Bay but was not collected by Jordan and Snyder, and no descriptions have been published since Schlegel's original account.

## Context

> This 1906 taxonomic review by [[Jordan]] and [[Snyder]] documents two species of killifishes (family [[Poeciliidae]]) from Japan: [[Fundulichthys virescens]] and [[Oryzias latipes]].
