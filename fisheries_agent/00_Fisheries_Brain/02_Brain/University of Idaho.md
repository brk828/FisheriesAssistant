# University of Idaho

## Summary
The University of Idaho is a research institution that received subcontracts under the Colorado River Fisheries Project. According to this source, the university conducted research on spawning biology and early life history of Northern Squawfish (with inferences to Colorado squawfish), culture and spawning technique development, histopathological effects of toxicants, and acute toxicity bioassays of 16 selected chemicals (8 organic compounds including DDT and PCB, and 8 inorganic compounds including selenium and mercury).
