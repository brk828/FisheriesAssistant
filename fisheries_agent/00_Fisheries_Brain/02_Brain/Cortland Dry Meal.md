# Cortland Dry Meal

## Summary
Cortland Dry Meal is a pelleted dry food formula developed for feeding hatchery trout. This study used formula No. 6, which contained 24% wheat flour middlings, 24% whitefish meal, 16% cottonseed meal, 7% dry skim milk, 10% dry brewers yeast, 17% distillers solubles, and 3% cod liver oil as the basis for an all-pellet experimental diet that proved economically superior to traditional meat-supplemented diets.
