# Salvelinus Fontinalis

## Summary
Salvelinus fontinalis, commonly known as the brook trout, is a freshwater salmonid species native to eastern North America that inhabits cool, well-oxygenated streams and rivers across multiple major drainage systems. As a cold-water specialist and indicator species of high-quality aquatic ecosystems, brook trout are significant for both conservation biology and fisheries management, requiring specific habitat conditions including adequate dissolved oxygen levels and clean substrates for spawning. The species' presence and population status serve as important benchmarks for assessing watershed health and the impacts of environmental degradation on freshwater fish communities.
