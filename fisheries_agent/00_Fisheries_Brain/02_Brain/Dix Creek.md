# Dix Creek

## Summary
Dix Creek is a tributary of the San Francisco River where sampling of a 20-meter section yielded 37 Desert Sucker, 9 Sonora Sucker, 28 Speckled Dace, and 13 Longfin Dace. The creek was also notable for wildlife observations including a Zone-tailed hawk (Buteo albonotatus).
