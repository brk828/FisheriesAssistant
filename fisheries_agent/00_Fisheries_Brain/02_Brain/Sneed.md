# Sneed

## Summary
Sneed was a collaborator with Dupree on channel catfish nutrition research in 1966. Their joint work examined protein requirements at different temperatures, determined optimal corn oil levels (12.5-14.0%), and found that 21% cellulose was optimal as nonnutritive bulk in purified diets.

## Context

> This 1969 review synthesizes the first decade of research on commercial [[Channel Catfish]] nutrition in the United States. Despite only four research stations intensively studying catfish feeding, excellent practical rations have been developed yielding 1 pound of gain per 1.5-2.0 pounds of feed....
