# Lower Gila River Bird Habitat Management Area

## Summary
The Lower Gila River Bird Habitat Management Area is a U.S. Forest Service management designation that covers a portion of the upper Gila River in New Mexico. This area is part of the land ownership mosaic in the Gila River system where spikedace persist, representing one of the three primary remaining locations for the species.
