# South Fork Boise River

## Summary
The South Fork Boise River is one of the primary tributaries feeding Arrowrock Reservoir and serves as a major spawning area for reservoir fish. During the 1967 drawdown, gill nets in the upper South Fork arm of the reservoir caught predominantly suckers, while nets near the dam caught mostly salmonids.
