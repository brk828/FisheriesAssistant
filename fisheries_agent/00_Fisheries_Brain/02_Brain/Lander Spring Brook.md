# Lander Spring Brook

## Summary
Lander Spring Brook was a spring and brook in Chaves County, New Mexico, that once supported an abundant amphipod population. This source indicates it had salinity of 3.87‰ and was reported by Noel (1954) to contain "G. fasciatus" as the most abundant metazoan, but the population is now extinct as the spring and brook were dry by summer 1967, possibly representing a sixth species in the complex.
