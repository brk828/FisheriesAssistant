# Shorthead Redhorse

## Summary
The Shorthead Redhorse (*Moxostoma macrolepidotum*) is a freshwater sucker species in the family Catostomidae that inhabits North American river systems, including the Susquehanna River. This species is of interest to ichthyologists and fisheries managers because of its spawning behavior, larval development patterns, and ecological role in lotic ecosystems, with research documenting its reproductive biology and early life history stages to better understand population dynamics and conservation status. The Shorthead Redhorse serves as an indicator species for river health and has been the subject of spawning studies and developmental research aimed at improving understanding of catostomid fish ecology and aquatic system productivity.
