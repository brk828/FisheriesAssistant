# Bubbling Springs

## Summary
Bubbling Springs is a hatchery facility that was designated to receive and rear Humpback Chub eggs collected from the Lower Colorado River in 1993. The propagation study plan proposed comparing Bubbling Springs water versus Lower Colorado River water for egg incubation, but the reviewer strongly criticized this approach, arguing that the native LCR water should be used exclusively and that using 90% Bubbling Springs water could kill most of the spawn if the water proved incompatible with the eggs.
