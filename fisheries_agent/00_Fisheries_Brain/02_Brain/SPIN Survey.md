# SPIN Survey

## Summary
SPIN (Spring Pike Index Netting) is a standardized gill net survey method used to monitor Northern Pike populations in Box Canyon Reservoir. The survey uses 46-m × 1.8-m sinking gill nets with five ascending mesh panels (2.5-3.2-3.8-4.4-5.1 cm bar measure) set overnight in shallow spawning habitats during March-April, with CPUE (catch per unit effort) serving as the primary population index that declined from 13.2 fish/net presuppression to 0.1 fish/net by 2017.
