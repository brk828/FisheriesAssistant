# Colorado River Delta-Upper Gulf of California Biosphere Reserve

## Summary
The Colorado River Delta-Upper Gulf of California Biosphere Reserve is a protected area in Mexico that encompasses six critical wetland areas: El Doctor, Rio Hardy, Cienega de Santa Clara, Laguna Salada, the riparian corridor of the mainstem Colorado River, and the inter-tidal zone. By 1998, these areas represented remnant habitat from what was historically one million acres of wetlands, though significant quality habitat remained including approximately 10,000 hectares of riparian and wetland areas.
