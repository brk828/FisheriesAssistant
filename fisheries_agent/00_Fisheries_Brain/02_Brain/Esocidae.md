# Esocidae

## Summary
Esocidae is a family of freshwater and estuarine fishes commonly known as pikes, which includes popular game fish species such as muskellunge, northern pike, and pickerel. Members of this family are characterized as apex predators with elongated bodies, large mouths, and aggressive feeding behavior, making them ecologically significant in their native aquatic systems and important targets for recreational fisheries across North America and Eurasia. Their classification as game fish reflects their economic and cultural value to sport fishing communities, while their predatory role influences the structure and function of aquatic ecosystems they inhabit.
