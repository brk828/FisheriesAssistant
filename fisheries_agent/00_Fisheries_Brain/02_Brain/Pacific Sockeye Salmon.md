# Pacific Sockeye Salmon

## Summary
Pacific Sockeye Salmon (Oncorhynchus nerka) is the anadromous form of salmon from which Kokanee is derived as a landlocked subspecies. This source describes Kokanee as a landlocked form of Pacific Sockeye Salmon (also called red salmon) that was introduced to California waters beginning in 1941.

## Context
