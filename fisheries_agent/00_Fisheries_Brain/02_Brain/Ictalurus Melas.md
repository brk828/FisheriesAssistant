# Ictalurus Melas

## Summary
Ictalurus melas, commonly known as the black bullhead, is a freshwater catfish species native to North America that has been widely studied in ichthyological research regarding parasitic infections, predation dynamics, and physiological responses to environmental stressors. This species serves as an important subject in fisheries science and aquatic ecology due to its ecological role as both a predator and prey organism in freshwater ecosystems, as well as its utility as a model organism for understanding how fish populations respond to various chemical and biological influences in their aquatic habitats.
