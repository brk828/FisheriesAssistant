# Pringsheim

## Summary
Pringsheim is a researcher cited in this study regarding the taxonomic relationships of blue-green algae. In 1963, Pringsheim considered Oscillatoria to be closely related to Beggiatoa (colorless blue-green algae), which supports the hypothesis that some Oscillatoria species may possess chemosynthetic metabolic pathways.
