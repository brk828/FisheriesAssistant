# Coastal Rainbow Trout

## Summary
Coastal Rainbow Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss irideus) is a native salmonid subspecies that represents the last remaining native trout population in San Diego County, California. This source documents how the population was threatened by invasive Black Bullhead through predation on juveniles and redds, competition for resources, and habitat degradation, but rebounded dramatically from 72 observed individuals in 2016 to 446 in 2017 (primarily young-of-year) following successful removal of the invasive species. The population inhabits a short section of headwater stream with intermittent summer flows bounded by perennial pools in the West Fork San Luis Rey River.
