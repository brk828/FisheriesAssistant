# Lepomis Macrochirus

## Summary
*Lepomis macrochirus*, commonly known as bluegill, is a small freshwater sunfish species native to North America that serves as an important game fish and forage species in recreational fisheries and aquatic ecosystems. Valued for sport fishing due to its abundance, accessibility to anglers, and willingness to take baits and lures, bluegill also functions as a key prey species for larger predatory fish like largemouth bass and plays a significant role in aquatic food webs. As a prolific breeder adapted to diverse freshwater habitats including lakes, ponds, and reservoirs, bluegill populations are frequently used as indicators of ecosystem health and are often studied in fisheries management and ecological research.
