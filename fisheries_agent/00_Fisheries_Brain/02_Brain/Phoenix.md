# Phoenix

## Summary
Phoenix is a major city in central Arizona where urban fishery opportunities were identified as potential enhancement projects. The Agua Fria River/Lake Pleasant/urban fishing/Phoenix area recharge was identified as one of five priority enhancement categories for CAP fish and wildlife enhancement.
