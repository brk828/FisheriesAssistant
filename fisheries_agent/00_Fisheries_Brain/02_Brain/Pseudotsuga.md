# Pseudotsuga

## Summary
Pseudotsuga (Douglas-fir) is a gymnosperm genus included in the terrestrial vegetation samples. This conifer contributed to the compilation's data on terrestrial primary producers, which showed a grand mean of 4600 cal/g ash-free.
