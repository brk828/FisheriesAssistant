# Owens Speckled Dace

## Summary
Owens Speckled Dace is a subspecies of speckled dace found in California's desert basins. This source recommends it for endangered species listing as part of the Desert basins regional cluster, indicating it is among the small freshwater dispersant fishes in isolated habitats threatened with extinction.
