# Shark

## Summary
Shark is one of the scale fish species that appears seasonally in the El Golfo de Santa Clara area. This source reports that shark catches by the Cooperativa Venustiano Carranza showed an 80% decline in summer catches compared to the previous year, indicating significant population changes in the Gulf of California.
