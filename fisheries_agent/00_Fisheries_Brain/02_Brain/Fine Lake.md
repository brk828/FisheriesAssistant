# Fine Lake

## Summary
Fine Lake is a 320-acre warm-water lake in Barry County, Michigan, connected to the Grand River drainage. During the experimental study, it was opened to year-round fishing and showed the highest bluegill dominance among study lakes at 86.8% of the catch, with 4.0% yellow perch and 3.2% largemouth bass comprising most of the remaining catch.
