# Asellus Aquaticus

## Summary
Asellus aquaticus is a freshwater isopod crustacean commonly used as a bioindicator species and model organism in aquatic ecology and ecotoxicology studies. This benthic detritivore plays an important role in freshwater food webs and sediment processing, making it valuable for assessing water quality, pollution impacts, and community composition changes in streams and rivers. Its sensitivity to environmental stressors and widespread distribution across European freshwater systems makes it particularly useful for monitoring biological community responses to organic pollution and other anthropogenic disturbances.
