# Chione

## Summary
Chione is a genus of bivalve mollusks documented at Puerto Peñasco in the Gulf of California. This source includes Chione species among the molluscs identified in the intertidal zone, with identifications based on Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other references.
