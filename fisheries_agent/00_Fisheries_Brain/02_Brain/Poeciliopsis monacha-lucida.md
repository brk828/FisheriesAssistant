# Poeciliopsis monacha-lucida

## Summary
Poeciliopsis monacha-lucida is a hybridogenetic fish that was the first unisexual vertebrate successfully synthesized in the laboratory by Schultz in 1973, produced through crosses of P. monacha females with P. lucida males. This synthetic unisexual demonstrates that interspecific hybridization can experimentally reproduce the conditions leading to unisexual reproduction. The successful laboratory synthesis of this form provided crucial evidence for understanding how natural unisexual vertebrates originate through hybridization-induced disruption of normal meiosis.
