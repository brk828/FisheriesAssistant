# Paoha Island

## Summary
Paoha Island is an island in Mono Lake covered with 200-300 feet of white lacustrine sediments and featuring a wave-cut terrace 12 feet below the 1889 lake level. The island has a Recent lava flow on its northern shore without pumice veneer and hosts high-temperature springs associated with tufa towers, indicating continuing tectonic activity.
