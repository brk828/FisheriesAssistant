# Colorado Game and Fish Department

## Summary
The Colorado Game and Fish Department was the state agency that conducted this two-year fisheries management study (1947-1948) under the direction of Ray H. Hess. The department's Fisheries Management Division used this research to evaluate stocking programs and develop quantitative data to optimize trout stocking for equitable angler distribution and maximum return on hatchery investment.

## Context

> This two-year fisheries management study (1947-1948) on the [[North Fork of South Platte River]] in [[Colorado]] evaluated the effectiveness of stocking legal-size [[Rainbow Trout]] and [[Brook Trout]] (referred to as "lochs") in a heavily fished mountain stream.
