# Bureau Of Sport Fisheries And Wildlife

## Summary
The Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife was a federal agency responsible for overseeing fish and wildlife management programs, conservation initiatives, and environmental impact assessments related to aquatic and terrestrial resources. Operating under the U.S. Department of the Interior, the bureau coordinated with state fish and game departments on fisheries management projects, supervised fish population control operations, and conducted feasibility studies on proposed water development projects to evaluate their ecological impacts on fish and wildlife. This agency played a critical role in mid-20th century fisheries science by establishing standards for environmental review and managing large-scale aquatic management programs across western U.S. river systems.
