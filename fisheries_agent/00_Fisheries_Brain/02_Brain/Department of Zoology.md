# Department of Zoology

## Summary
The Department of Zoology is an academic unit at Arizona State University in Tempe, Arizona. According to this 1986 memorandum, it housed the Desert Fishes Recovery Team, which was responsible for developing and reviewing recovery plans for multiple endangered desert fish species in the southwestern United States.
