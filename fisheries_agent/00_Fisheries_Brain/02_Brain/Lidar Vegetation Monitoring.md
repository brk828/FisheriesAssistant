# Lidar Vegetation Monitoring

## Summary
Lidar (light detection and ranging) vegetation monitoring was conducted in FY18 at Imperial Ponds to measure vegetation structure, identify structural diversity, and assess successional growth stages. Preliminary analyses suggest airborne lidar may not provide necessary detail for marsh habitat evaluation, and a procedure to analyze and provide vegetation structure metrics will be developed for future reports.
