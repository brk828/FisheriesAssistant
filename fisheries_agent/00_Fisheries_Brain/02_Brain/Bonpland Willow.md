# Bonpland Willow

## Summary
Bonpland Willow (Salix bonplandiana) is one of the canopy layer broadleaf species in Lower Bonita Creek. It became more prominent on the four uppermost transects (BC-6, 7, 8, 9) along with Arizona Walnut, Netleaf Hackberry, and Velvet Ash, where the three dominant lower-elevation species comprised only 37-48% of broadleaf composition.
