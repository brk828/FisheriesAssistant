# Stocking Success Assessment

## Summary
The document provides evidence of varying stocking success, with Largemouth Bass becoming widely established in virtually all suitable warm-water habitats throughout Arizona. Smallmouth Bass became abundant in Beaver Creek by 1956 following circa 1942 plantings, while Spotted Bass established large populations in West Creek by 1949 after Verde River drainage introductions.
