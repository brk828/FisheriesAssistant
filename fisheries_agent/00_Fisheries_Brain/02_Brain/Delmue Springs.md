# Delmue Springs

## Summary
Delmue Springs is the primary water source for Condor Canyon, providing base flow of approximately 200 gallons per minute to the stream section that supports the only extant Big Spring Spinedace population. The springs maintain permanent flowing water essential for the species' survival in this desert environment.
