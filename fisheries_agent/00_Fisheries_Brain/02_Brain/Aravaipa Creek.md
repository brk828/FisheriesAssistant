# Aravaipa Creek

## Summary
Aravaipa Creek is a relatively pristine desert stream in Arizona (spanning approximately 24 km across Graham and Pinal Counties) that represents a critical refuge for native southwestern fish species and has been a focal point for conservation efforts, particularly regarding the protection of endangered endemic species like the Gila topminnow and desert pupfish from invasive species such as the red shiner. The creek's intact native fish community and relatively unaltered hydrological conditions make it a high-priority site for desert fish recovery programs and aquatic ecosystem management within the Gila River Basin.
