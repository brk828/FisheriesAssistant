# Upper Verde River

## Summary
The Upper Verde River is a segment of the Verde River system in Arizona that serves as an important reference area for studying native fish assemblages and habitat conditions in the Sonoran Desert region. This reach has been subject to scientific surveys and monitoring efforts, particularly to document the presence and distribution of native fish species such as longfin dace, speckled dace, desert sucker, and Sonora sucker, which represent the standardized core native assemblage found across Gila River basin tributaries. The Upper Verde River's ecological status and fish communities have been evaluated through collaborative research between federal agencies and state wildlife management to inform conservation decisions and assess impacts of human activities on aquatic ecosystems.
