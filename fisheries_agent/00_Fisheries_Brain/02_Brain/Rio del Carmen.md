# Rio del Carmen

## Summary
Rio del Carmen is one of five major drainage systems in Chihuahua, Mexico inhabited by the Chihuahua Chub (Gila nigrescens). Specimens from this drainage were included in the taxonomic and morphometric analyses of the species, showing morphological characteristics that group with Rio Santa Maria populations rather than Rio Casas Grandes or Mimbres River fish.
