# Q. oblongifolia

## Summary
Q. oblongifolia (Quercus oblongifolia) is one of the dominant oak species in the Sierra San Luis reserve. It is a key component of the oak forest community at elevations of 1,800-2,100 meters, which covers more than 70% of the reserve.
