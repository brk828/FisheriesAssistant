# Recovery Plan

## Summary
A Recovery Plan is a comprehensive, science-based strategy document developed for threatened or endangered fish species that outlines specific conservation actions, management objectives, and recovery criteria necessary to restore populations to sustainable levels. These plans typically prioritize in-situ (in-place) conservation measures and habitat restoration over propagation or captive breeding, which are considered last-resort tools to be employed only when natural recovery mechanisms have failed or are insufficient. Recovery Plans serve as the authoritative framework guiding interagency coordination and management decisions under the Endangered Species Act, ensuring that all recovery efforts are grounded in current scientific understanding and established conservation priorities.
