# Black Rocks

## Summary
Black Rocks is a geomorphological section of the Upper Colorado River located between Debeque Canyon and Westwater Canyon in the mainstem Colorado River system. In fisheries monitoring and management contexts, this canyon reach serves as a defined reference site or sampling location for standardized ichthyological surveys and research related to native fish populations and aquatic habitat assessment in the Colorado River basin. The designation of Black Rocks as a named reach reflects its importance as a discrete geographic unit for organizing long-term fish monitoring data and conservation studies across the fragmented Colorado River system.
