# Fundulus Zebrinus

## Summary
Fundulus zebrinus, commonly known as the plains killifish, is a small cyprinodontid fish native to the central United States that inhabits warm, shallow freshwater systems including tributaries of the Colorado River basin. This species is notable in aquatic ecology and conservation contexts for its tolerance of variable thermal and chemical conditions, making it an indicator of habitat quality in warmwater tributary streams, though it has become increasingly rare in some portions of its historical range due to water management practices and habitat alteration. Its presence in Colorado River system surveys, particularly in warmer tributary reaches like Kanab Creek, reflects its ecological role as a native fish species adapted to the distinct thermal and flow regimes of these systems.
