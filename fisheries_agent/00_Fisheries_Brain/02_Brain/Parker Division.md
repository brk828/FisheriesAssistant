# Parker Division

## Summary
The Parker Division (also referred to as Parker II) is a reach of the Colorado River identified as one of three priority areas for annual razorback sucker stocking under the 1986-1995 management plan. The plan specified that fry and 10-15 cm fingerlings would be stocked annually in this division behind sandbars and in small backwaters to establish populations and enhance survival of the endangered species.
