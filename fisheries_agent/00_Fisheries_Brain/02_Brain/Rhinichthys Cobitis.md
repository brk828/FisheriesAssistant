# Rhinichthys Cobitis

## Summary
Rhinichthys cobitis, commonly known as the loach minnow, is a small cyprinid fish endemic to the southwestern United States that has become a focal point for conservation efforts due to its severely restricted range and declining populations. This species inhabits small, clear streams and spring systems in Arizona and New Mexico, where it faces threats from habitat alteration, water diversion, and competition from introduced species. The loach minnow's specialized ecological requirements and limited distribution make it a significant indicator species for aquatic ecosystem health in arid western watersheds and have prompted research into its taxonomy, population dynamics, and recovery strategies.
