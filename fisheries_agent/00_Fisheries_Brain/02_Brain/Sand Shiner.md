# Sand Shiner

## Summary
The Sand Shiner is a nonnative cyprinid fish species that has become established in southwestern aquatic systems, particularly in the Colorado River basin. As documented in monitoring studies, this invasive species thrives in zero-velocity nursery habitats such as backwaters and shoreline embayments, where it uses these areas as significant refuge and breeding grounds. The Sand Shiner's ecological success in these habitats represents a notable concern for native species conservation, as its dominance in nursery zones can compete with and potentially impact the survival and recruitment of endemic species like the Humpback Chub.
