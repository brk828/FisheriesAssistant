# Arizona Ecological Services Office

## Summary
The Arizona Ecological Services Office is a regional field office of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service responsible for implementing federal environmental compliance and species conservation efforts in Arizona and surrounding areas, including the review and issuance of Biological Opinions on projects that may affect threatened and endangered aquatic species. This office plays a critical role in evaluating impacts to native fish populations and their habitats in western river systems, particularly in the Colorado River Basin, and coordinates with other agencies and stakeholders on fish management and conservation initiatives.
