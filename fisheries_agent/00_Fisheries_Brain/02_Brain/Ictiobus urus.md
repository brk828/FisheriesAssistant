# Ictiobus urus

## Summary
Ictiobus urus, commonly called mongrel, sheep's-nose, or blue-rooter buffalo, is one of three buffalo fish species in the United States. It has an elliptical robust body with a turned-down mouth and predominates in southern lakes while ranking third in rivers. This species is found throughout the Mississippi Drainage system.
