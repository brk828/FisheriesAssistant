# Bighead Carp

## Summary
Bighead carp (Hypophthalmichthys nobilis) is a large Asian cyprinid fish that has become a significant concern in fisheries science and conservation biology due to its invasive potential in non-native ecosystems. As a planktivorous filter-feeder, it competes directly with native fish species for zooplankton resources and can achieve rapid population growth in suitable environments, making it a documented invasive species across multiple continents. The ecological impacts of bighead carp escapes from aquaculture facilities and intentional introductions have prompted extensive research into its establishment dynamics and effects on native fish communities and aquatic food webs.
