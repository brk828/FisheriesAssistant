# South Platte River

## Summary
The South Platte River is a major tributary of the Platte River system that is hydrologically connected to the 59-mile critical habitat reach in central Nebraska. Water use activities on the South Platte many miles upstream can affect downstream critical habitat, making these activities subject to ESA Section 7 scrutiny under the Central Platte River Recovery Program.
