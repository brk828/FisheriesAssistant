# Whitewater Lake, Nebraska

## Summary
Whitewater Lake, Nebraska was a 490-acre lake with highly alkaline water (pH 9.3) and temperature of 62°F where antimycin A was tested. At this high pH, 7.3 ppb degraded before achieving complete fish kill, and bioassays indicated 12-15 ppb would be needed, demonstrating that antimycin degrades rapidly at pH above 8.5.
