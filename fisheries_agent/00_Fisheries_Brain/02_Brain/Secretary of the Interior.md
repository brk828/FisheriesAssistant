# Secretary of the Interior

## Summary
The Secretary of the Interior is the federal official responsible for administering the Endangered Species Act listing process under Section 4. According to the ESA, the Secretary must make an initial determination within 90 days of receiving a listing petition, a second determination within 12 months, and finalize warranted actions within 18 months, though the Hutchison Rider prevented these determinations from being finalized during the moratorium period.
