# Sternopygus macrurus

## Summary
Sternopygus macrurus is one of the four most widely distributed gymnotid species and proved to be the only gymnotid found in western Ecuador during this study. The survey collected 89+ specimens (29-700 mm) from 17 localities, with 31 specimens collected extensively at Guayaquil market measuring 282-700 mm.
