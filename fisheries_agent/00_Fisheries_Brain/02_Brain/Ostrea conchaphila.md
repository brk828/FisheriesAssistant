# Ostrea conchaphila

## Summary
Ostrea conchaphila is a bivalve species (oyster) found at Puerto Peñasco, Sonora, Mexico. This field guide documents it as part of the molluscan fauna inhabiting the intertidal zone of the Gulf of California at this location.
