# Saguaro National Monument

## Summary
Saguaro National Monument was one of the park units that received GIS technical support from CPSU/UA staff in 1994. The monument was also the subject of research on saguaro cactus flower bud suppression by roosting vultures, documented in a paper by Bennett and Kunzmann.
