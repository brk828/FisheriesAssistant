# Longnose Dace

## Summary
The Longnose Dace (*Rhinichthys cataractae*) is a small cyprinid minnow commonly found in cool, fast-flowing headwater streams and is frequently used as an indicator species for assessing aquatic ecosystem health and water quality conditions. As a benthic invertebrate feeder and forage fish, it plays an important ecological role in supporting populations of larger predatory fish such as trout while remaining sensitive to habitat degradation, making it valuable for monitoring genetic diversity and population structure in conservation studies. Its presence and heterozygosity levels are often examined in fisheries research to understand the impacts of environmental stress and habitat fragmentation on native fish communities in headwater systems.
