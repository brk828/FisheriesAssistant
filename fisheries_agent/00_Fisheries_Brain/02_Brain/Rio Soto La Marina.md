# Rio Soto La Marina

## Summary
Rio Soto La Marina is one of the Mexican river systems where Dr. Contreras Balderas conducted ichthyofaunal surveys and research. His work in this system was part of his broader studies on fish communities and zoogeography in northern Mexican aquatic ecosystems.
