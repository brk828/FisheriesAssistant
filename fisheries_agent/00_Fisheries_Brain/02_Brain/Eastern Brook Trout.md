# Eastern Brook Trout

## Summary
Eastern Brook Trout (*Salvelinus fontinalis*) is a coldwater salmonid species native to eastern North America that occurs as a minor component in many recreational fisheries, as documented in creel census and population studies. In ichthyological research, this species is typically sampled alongside dominant trout species using standardized anatomical protocols, such as scale sampling from consistent body locations posterior to the pectoral fin, to enable comparative analysis of age, growth, and population dynamics. The species' presence in aquatic ecosystems and catch compositions provides important baseline data for assessing fish community structure, health status (including parasite loads), and the relative success of different trout species in shared habitats.
