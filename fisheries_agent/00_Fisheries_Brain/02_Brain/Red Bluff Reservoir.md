# Red Bluff Reservoir

## Summary
Red Bluff Reservoir is a saline inland water body in the Pecos River System of western Texas with highly variable salinity levels, ranging from 140 ppm to 7,000 ppm chlorides. This study documents its use as an experimental site for marine fish introductions from 1957-1971, where 1,022 red drum and other marine species were stocked to establish sport fisheries in waters too saline for traditional freshwater game fish.
