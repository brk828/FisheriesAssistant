# Yale School of Forestry and Environmental Studies

## Summary
The Yale School of Forestry and Environmental Studies is an academic institution whose dean, Dr. John C. Gordon, served as a member of the National Parks and Conservation Association's Commission on Research and Resource Management Policy in 1988-1989. Dr. Gordon was one of several experts from diverse disciplines recruited to examine policy issues related to research and resource management across the National Park System.
