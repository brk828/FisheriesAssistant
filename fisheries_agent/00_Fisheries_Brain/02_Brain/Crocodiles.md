# Crocodiles

## Summary
Crocodiles are cited in this document as an example of inconsistencies in California's prohibited species regulations. The Department noted that crocodiles are prohibited while alligators and caimans are not, despite all three groups presenting similar threats, illustrating how the prohibited species list was not comprehensive.
