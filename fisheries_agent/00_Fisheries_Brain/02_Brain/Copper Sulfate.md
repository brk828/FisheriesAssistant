# Copper Sulfate

## Summary
Copper sulfate is a chemical algaecide commonly used in aquatic systems to control algal growth, particularly effective against unbranched filamentous green algae at concentrations around 0.5 ppm. However, its toxicity to aquatic organisms is highly dependent on water hardness, making it potentially hazardous to fish and other fauna in soft-water environments at relatively low concentrations. As a water quality management tool, copper sulfate presents a significant trade-off in aquatic ecology between achieving algal control and minimizing toxic effects on non-target aquatic life.
