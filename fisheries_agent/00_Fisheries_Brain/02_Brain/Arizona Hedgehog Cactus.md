# Arizona Hedgehog Cactus

## Summary
Arizona Hedgehog Cactus (Echinocereus triglochidiatus arizonicus) is a special-interest plant species evaluated during the Spur Cross Ranch survey. None were observed during field surveys, and the habitat was not similar to known occupied sites, making it extremely unlikely to occur or be adversely impacted by the planned development.
