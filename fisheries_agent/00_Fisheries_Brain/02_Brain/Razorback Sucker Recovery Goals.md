# Razorback Sucker Recovery Goals

## Summary
Razorback Sucker Recovery Goals are conservation targets established for the endangered species. This source states that revised recovery goals call for stable populations of 5,000 individuals, but found that present stocking locations in the Lower Colorado River do not support this objective, with more than 2.5 million fish stocked over three decades (1974-2008) but <1% ever recaptured and long-term survival (>1.5 years) extremely rare.
