# Gila County

## Summary
Gila County is a county in Arizona, USA, that contains Campaign Creek and Upper Horrell Springs within the historic range of the Gila topminnow. This biological opinion addresses road repair activities in Gila County that could affect a reintroduced population of endangered Gila topminnow established in 1983.
