# Fish Springs National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Fish Springs National Wildlife Refuge is a wildlife refuge in Utah evaluated as a secondary candidate site for a Colorado River endangered fish hatchery. The refuge has nine springs providing approximately 40 cubic feet per second of flow with year-round warm water temperatures of 77-83°F (ideal for accelerated fish growth), though the water has high salinity (chloride 640-1,280 ppm, TDS 1,878-5,640 ppm) that may affect eggs and fry. Major drawbacks include its remote location (6-7 hours from Upper Basin stocking sites), limited infrastructure, and concerns that constant warm water may prevent proper broodstock maturation for spawning.
