# Whitewater Canyons

## Summary
Whitewater canyons are turbulent river reaches characterized by deep pools, eddies, and spring inputs that maintain perennial flow. In the Colorado River system, these canyon reaches provided critical refugia for native fishes during drought periods, with adult humpback chubs specifically occupying deep swirling eddies in turbulent canyon reaches near boulders.
