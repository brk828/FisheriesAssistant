# San Rafael Allotment

## Summary
The San Rafael Allotment is a grazing permit area on the Coronado National Forest in southern Arizona that includes the Bog Hole wetland site. The allotment permittee (Bob Hudson) was involved in the 1989 management planning discussions, with concerns about potential impacts to livestock capacity from proposed habitat development projects and a proposal to use an enclosure for bulls 30-40 days in the fall.
