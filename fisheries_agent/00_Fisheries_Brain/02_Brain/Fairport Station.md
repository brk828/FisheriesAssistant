# Fairport Station

## Summary
Fairport Station refers to the U.S. Fisheries Biological Station at Fairport, Iowa where experimental work on buffalo-fish propagation was conducted. Prior to the work at this station, efforts to collect and hatch buffalo-fish eggs had resulted in absolute failure or minimal success.

## Context

> This document presents methods developed at the [[U.S. Fisheries Biological Station]] in [[Fairport, Iowa]] for successfully collecting and hatching eggs of buffalo-fishes (genus [[Ictiobus]]), addressing previous failures in artificial propagation of these commercially important food fishes.
