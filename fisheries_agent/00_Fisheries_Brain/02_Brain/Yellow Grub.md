# Yellow Grub

## Summary
Yellow Grub (Clinostomum sp.) is a trematode parasite that appears as yellowish-white spots under the skin and deeply embedded in the flesh of freshwater fish. When the cyst wall breaks during fish dressing, the grub can be observed moving on the host's flesh, making it one of the more visible parasites encountered by anglers.
