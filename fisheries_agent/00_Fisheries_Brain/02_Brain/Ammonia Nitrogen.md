# Ammonia Nitrogen

## Summary
Ammonia nitrogen is a form of dissolved nitrogen measured in water chemistry analyses. In this study, ammonia nitrogen concentrations ranged from 0.068 ppm in unfertilized pools to 0.202 ppm in nitrate-fertilized pools, with fertilized treatments showing significantly higher levels than unfertilized controls.

## Context

> This experimental study by [[George N. Greene]] at [[Auburn University]] (1967-1968) investigated the effects of three nitrogen sources (sodium nitrate, ammonium carbonate, and cottonseed meal) and the presence of [[Cecil Soil]] on fish production and water chemistry in fertilized plastic pools....
