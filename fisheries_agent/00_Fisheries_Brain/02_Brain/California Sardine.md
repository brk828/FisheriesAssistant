# California Sardine

## Summary
The California sardine is a pelagic marine fish species (Sardinops cerulea) found in the Pacific Ocean off California. This source establishes it as the type species of the newly created genus Sardinops, distinguished from European sardines by features including complex fimbriate gillraker processes, regular scale arrangement, weaker ventral scutes, and typically a row of dark spots behind the head.

## Context

> This 1929 taxonomic revision by [[Carl L Hubbs]] establishes the new genus *Sardinops* for the [[California Sardine]] and related Pacific species, distinguishing them from the European sardines (genus *Sardina*).
