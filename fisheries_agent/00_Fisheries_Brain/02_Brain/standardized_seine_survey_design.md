# standardized_seine_survey_design

## Summary
This is a spatially stratified annual monitoring protocol conducted in Utah Lake each August from 2012-2018, using a 184-meter by 3-meter commercial beach seine with 2-inch square mesh. The surveys involved 27-43 hauls per year at sites distributed around the lake circumference (varying due to accessibility constraints during low water periods), with each haul recording total carp count and subsampling 30 individuals for length and weight measurements to generate density-at-age estimates.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed**: Evaluate the population-level consequences of a decade-long [[Common Carp]] removal effort in [[Utah Lake]] (>12,000 tons harvested 2009-2018) and determine whether current mechanical removal strategies can maintain carp biomass below management targets (75% reduction)...
