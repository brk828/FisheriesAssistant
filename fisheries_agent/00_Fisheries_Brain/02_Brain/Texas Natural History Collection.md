# Texas Natural History Collection

## Summary
Texas Natural History Collection (TNHC) is one of the institutional collections that provided museum specimens of Gila nigrescens examined in this taxonomic study. Multiple institutional collections were used to obtain adequate sample sizes across the species' five major drainage systems.
