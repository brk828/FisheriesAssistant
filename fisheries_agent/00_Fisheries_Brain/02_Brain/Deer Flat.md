# Deer Flat

## Summary
Deer Flat is a National Wildlife Refuge that showed anomalously high pesticide contamination in 1964. Sediment residues averaged 40.0 ppm (much higher than other refuges), correlated with scarcity of aquatic invertebrates, and Black-crowned night heron mortality in 1963 showed residues up to 25 ppm DDT, 4 ppm dieldrin, and 5 ppm toxaphene.
