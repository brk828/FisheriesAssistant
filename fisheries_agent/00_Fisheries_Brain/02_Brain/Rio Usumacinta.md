# Rio Usumacinta

## Summary
The Rio Usumacinta is one of the major river systems in southern Mexico draining to the Atlantic slope/Gulf of Mexico. This source lists it as part of the geographic framework for Mexican fish distributions, representing tropical lowland habitat in the southeastern region of the country.
