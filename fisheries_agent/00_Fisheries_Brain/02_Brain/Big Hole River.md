# Big Hole River

## Summary
The Big Hole River is a river in Montana that represents one of the last refuges for Montana Arctic Grayling. Stream-resident populations of this native salmonid are now found only in the upper Big Hole River and its tributaries, representing less than 8% of the species' historic native range in the upper Missouri River drainage.
