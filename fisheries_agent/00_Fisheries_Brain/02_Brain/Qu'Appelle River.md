# Qu'Appelle River

## Summary
The Qu'Appelle River is a river system in southern Saskatchewan, Canada, that includes Buffalo Pound Lake where this Northern Pike movement study was conducted. The study tracked pike movements both upstream (west) and downstream (east) within Buffalo Pound Lake, which is part of this regulated river system.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary**: [[Northern Pike]] (*Esox lucius*) is an important target species for ice angling in northern regions.
