# Arbuckle Group

## Summary
The Arbuckle Group is a Cambrian-Ordovician geological formation consisting of limestones and dolomites up to 1,980 meters thick. In the Blue River study area, this formation created stream bottoms containing eroded limestone boulders and numerous springs, supporting heterotrophic metabolism where respiration exceeded photosynthesis.
