# White River Speckled Dace

## Summary
The White River Speckled Dace (Rhinichthys osculus velifer) is one of six native fish species in Pahranagat Valley that historically coexisted with the Pahranagat Roundtail Chub. This source identifies it as one of the species that has experienced dramatic declines and notes that research is needed on interspecific interactions between this dace and the roundtail chub.
