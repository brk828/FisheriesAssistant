# White Catfish

## Summary
White catfish (Ameiurus catus) are a North American freshwater species belonging to the family Ictaluridae that have become established in various aquatic ecosystems, particularly in coastal and inland waterways where they are studied for their growth rates, population dynamics, and ecological interactions. As both a subject of aquaculture research and an invasive species in some regions, white catfish are important to fisheries management and conservation biology due to their ecological impacts on native fish communities and their role as hosts for parasites and pathogens in aquatic environments. Their ability to adapt to diverse habitats and reproduce successfully makes them significant for understanding invasion dynamics and competition in modified freshwater ecosystems.
