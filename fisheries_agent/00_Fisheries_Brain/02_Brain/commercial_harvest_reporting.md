# commercial_harvest_reporting

## Summary
This refers to the systematic collection of removal data from the large-scale Common Carp control program in Utah Lake, including monthly harvest biomass records (2009-2018) and fishing effort measured as number of seine hauls (2013-2018). The data were provided by the June Sucker Recovery Implementation Program and Utah Division of Wildlife Resources, documenting over 12,000 tons of carp removed during the study period, though effort data for 2009-2012 had to be imputed as the mean of 2013-2015 levels.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed**: Evaluate the population-level consequences of a decade-long [[Common Carp]] removal effort in [[Utah Lake]] (>12,000 tons harvested 2009-2018) and determine whether current mechanical removal strategies can maintain carp biomass below management targets (75% reduction)...
