# Lake Ontario

## Summary
Lake Ontario is one of the five Great Lakes and represents a critical conservation focus in ichthyology due to the historical extirpation and ongoing restoration efforts of native fish populations, particularly lake trout (*Salvelinus namaycush*), which were decimated by overfishing, sea lamprey predation, and habitat degradation. Federal hatchery programs, such as the Allegheny National Fish Hatchery, have been established specifically to restore lake trout and other native species to Lake Ontario's deepwater ecosystems. The lake serves as a key case study in Great Lakes fish conservation biology and the application of artificial propagation and stocking programs for ecosystem recovery.
