# Thermal Pollution

## Summary
Thermal pollution refers to the degradation of aquatic ecosystems caused by the discharge of heated water from industrial sources, particularly power plants and other industrial facilities, which elevates water temperatures beyond the tolerance ranges of native fish species and disrupts their physiological processes. This form of water quality degradation alters thermal gradients that fish depend on for migration, reproduction, and metabolic regulation, and often compounds other stressors such as dissolved oxygen depletion, making it a significant concern in fisheries conservation and aquatic ecosystem management. Thermal pollution is typically assessed using standardized laboratory methods that simulate realistic thermal conditions to understand fish behavioral responses and establish water quality criteria for the protection of aquatic life.
