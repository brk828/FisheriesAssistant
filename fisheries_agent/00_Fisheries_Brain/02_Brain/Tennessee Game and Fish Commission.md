# Tennessee Game and Fish Commission

## Summary
The Tennessee Game and Fish Commission was a state agency whose representative, Mr. Eugene Whitney, contributed to fish surveys in Great Smoky Mountains National Park in 1957. The commission was one of several federal and state agencies involved in documenting the park's fish fauna.
