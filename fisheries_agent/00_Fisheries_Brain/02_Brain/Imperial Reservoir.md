# Imperial Reservoir

## Summary
Imperial Reservoir is a 2955-hectare impoundment on the Lower Colorado River north of Yuma, Arizona, completed in 1938 for water storage. This study found it supports an excellent warm-water sport fishery with shallow lakes containing dense beds of Najas marina and cattails that provide critical spawning and rearing habitat for juvenile fish, with water temperatures ranging from 7.8°C in winter to 32.2°C in summer and an 11-month growing season.
