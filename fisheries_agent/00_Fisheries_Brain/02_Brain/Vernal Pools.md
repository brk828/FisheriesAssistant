# Vernal Pools

## Summary
Vernal Pools are ephemeral standing waters within the Sacramento-San Joaquin Province that include northern claypan pools and northeast volcanic pools. They are also found in the Southern California Coastal Province on the Santa Rosa Plateau and San Diego Mesa, representing important seasonal aquatic habitats.
