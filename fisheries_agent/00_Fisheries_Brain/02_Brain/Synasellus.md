# Synasellus

## Summary
Synasellus is a genus of freshwater isopod crustaceans belonging to the family Stenasellidae, characterized by distinctive morphological features including specialized male pleopod structures. These small aquatic invertebrates inhabit subterranean and groundwater environments, serving as important indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and cave biodiversity. The genus is notable in systematic and biogeographic studies of stenasellid isopods, with species exhibiting unique anatomical adaptations such as reduced appendages and modified reproductive structures that reflect their specialized cave or interstitial habitat preferences.
