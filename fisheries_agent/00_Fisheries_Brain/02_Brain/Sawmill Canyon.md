# Sawmill Canyon

## Summary
Sawmill Canyon contains Mono Basin Till (Illinoian equivalent) with laterals at oblique angles to younger Tahoe forms. Tahoe Till in this location overrides basalt, providing dating evidence that places Tahoe glaciation at 60,000-90,000 BP.
