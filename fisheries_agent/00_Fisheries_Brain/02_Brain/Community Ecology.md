# Community Ecology

## Summary
Community ecology is the study of how species distributions and abundances are structured in ecological communities. This source examines a fundamental methodological controversy in community ecology about whether patterns like species-to-genus ratios on islands result from biological interactions (particularly interspecific competition) or from random colonization processes.
