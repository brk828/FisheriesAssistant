# Cienega

## Summary
A cienega is a rare, spring-fed wetland or marsh ecosystem characteristic of arid Southwestern North America, typically fed by underground aquifers and supporting unique riparian vegetation and aquatic fauna. These critical desert water sources, along with their associated springs and streams, provide essential habitat for endemic and threatened aquatic species adapted to the region's water-scarce landscape. Cienegas have become increasingly threatened by water extraction, livestock impacts, and habitat degradation, making them priority ecosystems for conservation efforts in the American Southwest.
