# Red Bluff Research Pumping Plant

## Summary
The Red Bluff Research Pumping Plant is a facility near the Red Bluff Diversion Dam where the US Fish and Wildlife Service conducted studies on Sacramento squawfish and striped bass populations. Research objectives at this location included determining predator population size, analyzing food habits, measuring growth rates, assessing age structure, and evaluating spawning condition to better understand Sacramento River fish community dynamics.
