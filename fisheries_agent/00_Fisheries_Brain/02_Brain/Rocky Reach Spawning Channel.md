# Rocky Reach Spawning Channel

## Summary
Rocky Reach Spawning Channel is another spawning channel facility used for comparative analysis. This source reports survival rates of 11.8-42.3% at Rocky Reach, considerably lower than the 68-95.5% achieved at Abernathy Channel.
