# Acrolein

## Summary
Acrolein is a chemical piscicide that was evaluated as an alternative to rotenone for rough fish control in California. According to this 1963 report, acrolein requires a concentration of 3.0 ppm for complete eradication of carp, goldfish, and bullhead, making it more expensive than rotenone, though treated water remains toxic for only a few days and the concentrated form is highly toxic to humans.
