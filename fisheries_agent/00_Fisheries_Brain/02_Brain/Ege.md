# Ege

## Summary
Ege conducted a classic 1939 taxonomic revision of Anguilla species that established the identification methods used in this study. The revision employed multiple diagnostic characters including coloration, dentition, body proportions, and particularly the "D" value (distance between verticals through anus and origin of dorsal fin as percentage of total length).
