# Formosa

## Summary
Formosa (Taiwan) is mentioned as part of the range of Hymenocephalus striatissimus striatissimus, along with Japan and eastern Luzon. This indicates biogeographic connections between Formosa and the broader Western Pacific region for some deep-sea fish species.
