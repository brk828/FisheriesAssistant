# Tavasci Spring

## Summary
A spring in Yavapai County, Arizona, owned by Phelps Dodge Corporation and involved in a land trade to become part of Tuzigoot National Monument. This source reports the spring's habitat was reduced to 2 meters and its endemic Fontelicella population declined from viable in 1976 to possibly extinct by 1980 due to artificial pooling and canal construction.
