# Caenidae

## Summary
Caenidae is a family of small mayflies (order Ephemeroptera) that represents one of twelve major families of mayflies found in freshwater aquatic ecosystems. These insects are characterized by their diminutive size and are ecologically important as benthic macroinvertebrates that serve as food sources for fish and other aquatic predators, making them significant indicators of water quality and stream health in biomonitoring studies. The family is distributed across various freshwater habitats and is commonly referenced in taxonomic surveys and faunal inventories of mayfly diversity.
