# Bonytail

## Summary
The bonytail (*Gila elegans*) is a large cyprinid fish native to the Colorado River basin and a member of the "big-river" endemic fish guild, characterized by morphological adaptations to lotic environments. This species is now critically endangered throughout its range, with populations severely depleted by habitat modification, water management practices, and competition from introduced fishes, making it a priority for conservation and recovery efforts in the southwestern United States. Taxonomic identification of juvenile bonytail relies on morphometric analyses and comparative morphology, particularly to distinguish it from closely related endemic chubs such as the humpback chub and roundtail chub.
