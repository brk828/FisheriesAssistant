# Trout Fisheries

## Summary
The source does not provide specific information about trout fisheries management, though it lists rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) and brown trout (Salmo trutta) among the nonnative species present in the system. The cold hypolimnetic releases from Glen Canyon Dam (7-10°C) that prevent native warmwater fish reproduction would create suitable conditions for coldwater trout species.
