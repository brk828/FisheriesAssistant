# Beal Slough

## Summary
Beal Slough is one of only two backwater habitats that remained on the California side after the Needles-Topock channelization project. Together with the old river channel, these remnant habitats totaled just 76 surface acres of fishable water, compared to 4,300 acres pre-channelization.
