# Black Rocks Canyon

## Summary
Black Rocks Canyon is a section of the Colorado River that supports one of the major remaining concentrations of Humpback Chub (Gila cypha) as of the early 1980s. This recovery plan identifies it as one of the critical locations for establishing self-sustaining populations, with ripe fish collected there in June 1980 and three females stripped of approximately 18,000 eggs total.
