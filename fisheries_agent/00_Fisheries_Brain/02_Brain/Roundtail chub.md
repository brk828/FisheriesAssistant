# Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Roundtail Chub (*Gila robusta*, family Cyprinidae) is a native cyprinid minnow of the Colorado River basin that serves as an important ecological indicator and conservation concern in southwestern aquatic systems. As a medium to large-bodied fish species, it is distinguished from related chubs like the Humpback Chub and Bonytail through morphometric analyses of body shape and proportions, and its presence or absence in fish assemblages reflects broader ecosystem health in regulated river systems altered by dams and nonnative species invasions. The species' ecological role, taxonomic relationships, and vulnerability to anthropogenic disturbances make it relevant to both basic ichthyological research and federal endangered species management in the Colorado River.
