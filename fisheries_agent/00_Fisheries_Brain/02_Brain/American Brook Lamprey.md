# American Brook Lamprey

## Summary
The American Brook Lamprey (Lampetra appendix) is a small, jawless fish native to North American freshwater ecosystems that plays an important ecological role as both a detritivore and prey species for larger fish and wildlife. As documented in Kentucky drainage records and conservation assessments, this species has experienced population declines and shifting distributions due to habitat degradation and water quality changes, leading to its designation as a species of conservation concern in several states. Understanding its presence and status is critical for comprehensive freshwater biodiversity monitoring and the assessment of overall aquatic ecosystem health in eastern North American drainages.
