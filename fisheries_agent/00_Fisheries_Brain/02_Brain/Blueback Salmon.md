# Blueback Salmon

## Summary
Blueback salmon refers to a Pacific Northwest anadromous salmon species that serves as a host for columnaris disease, a bacterial infection caused by *Chondrococcus* species. In the context of these ichthyology studies, blueback salmon are documented as susceptible to the pathogenic bacterium alongside other salmon species like Chinook salmon, indicating the disease's relevance to wild and cultured salmon populations in western North America. The species' inclusion in disease host-range studies highlights its ecological and economic importance in Pacific fisheries and the need for understanding pathogen transmission in salmon ecosystems.
