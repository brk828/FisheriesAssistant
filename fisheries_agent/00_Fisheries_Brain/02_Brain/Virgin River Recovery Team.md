# Virgin River Recovery Team

## Summary
The Virgin River Recovery Team is an interagency collaborative effort established to address the conservation and recovery of native fish species and aquatic ecosystems within the Virgin River system, which spans Utah, Nevada, and Arizona. This team coordinates monitoring, research, and management activities across multiple jurisdictions to support endangered species recovery, particularly for native fishes adapted to the Virgin River's unique hydrological and ecological conditions. The team's work integrates scientific data collection with adaptive management strategies to balance water resource demands with ecosystem restoration objectives in this sensitive desert aquatic system.
