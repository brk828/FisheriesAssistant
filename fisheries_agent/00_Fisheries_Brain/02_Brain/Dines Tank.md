# Dines Tank

## Summary
Dines Tank is a water body located approximately 7 kilometers downstream from Knoll Lake in Leonard Canyon. This source indicates that Little Colorado Spinedace are consistently found in Dines Tank while rainbow trout are absent, demonstrating the inverse distribution pattern between these two species that occurs throughout the Little Colorado River drainage.
