# Utah Sucker

## Summary
The Utah Sucker (*Catostomus ardens*) is a native cyprinid species of the Colorado River basin and associated water systems that has become established in various aquatic habitats including reservoirs and mid-river reaches. As a benthic-feeding sucker, it occupies an ecological niche distinct from piscivorous predators, though its population dynamics and distribution have been influenced by habitat alterations and interactions with both native and nonnative fish species. The species is documented in conservation assessments alongside discussions of native fish recovery and the ecological impacts of nonnative fish communities, making it relevant to understanding Colorado River basin aquatic ecosystem composition and management.
