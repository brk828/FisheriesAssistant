# Cold Springs

## Summary
Cold Springs is one of three sites that successfully maintained an introduced population of Lower Colorado River Pupfish as of 1991. This site, along with Howard Well and Yerba Mansa, represents efforts to reestablish this subspecies in Arizona after it was eliminated from the state prior to the 1950s.
