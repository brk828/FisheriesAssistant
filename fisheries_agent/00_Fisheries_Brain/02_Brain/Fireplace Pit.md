# Fireplace Pit

## Summary
Fireplace Pit was part of the interconnected Miner's State Park complex and experienced problems with excessive 'skunk moss' growth that retarded fish growth and reduced fishing success. The pit supported channel catfish that grew over three years from 10-11 inches to 14 pounds, with a 14-pound channel catfish caught from the complex in April 1956.
