# CEDO

## Summary
CEDO is the organization where Peggy Turk-Boyer conducted a survey of local fishermen in the Upper Gulf region. The survey revealed that 90% of San Felipe fishermen agreed sustainability is viable, and that release of freshwater into the gulf was viewed as very important, especially by San Felipe and el Golfo fishermen.
