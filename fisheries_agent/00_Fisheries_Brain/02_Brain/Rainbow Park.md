# Rainbow Park

## Summary
Rainbow Park is a location along the Green River in Utah within the study area used for monitoring reintroduced Bonytail Chub. According to this source, it was part of the habitat range where radio-tagged fish were tracked, situated between Whirlpool Canyon and Split Mountain Canyon, and one of the surviving fish (Freq. #635) remained in the Island/Rainbow Parks area for 20 days after release before relocating downstream.
