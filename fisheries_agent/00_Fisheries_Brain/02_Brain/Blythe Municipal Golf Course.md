# Blythe Municipal Golf Course

## Summary
Blythe Municipal Golf Course has a pond that was identified in the 1985 management plan as one of the available grow-out habitats for rearing razorback sucker fry to minimum stocking size. The plan proposed utilizing this pond, along with other facilities, to rear young razorbacks from 4-6 inches before stocking them in the Colorado River system.

## Context
