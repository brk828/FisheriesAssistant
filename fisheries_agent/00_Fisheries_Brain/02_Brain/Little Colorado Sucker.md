# Little Colorado Sucker

## Summary
The Little Colorado Sucker is a suckerfish (family Catostomidae) endemic to the Little Colorado River drainage in the southwestern United States, representing a taxonomically unresolved species within the genus Catostomus that was recommended for federal candidate species status in 1993 due to conservation concerns. Like other southwestern sucker species, this fish faces threats from habitat degradation, water management practices, and competition from non-native species, making it a focal point for desert aquatic ecosystem recovery efforts. The unresolved taxonomic designation of this species reflects the historical difficulty in characterizing its distinct evolutionary lineage and ecological role within Colorado River basin fish communities.
