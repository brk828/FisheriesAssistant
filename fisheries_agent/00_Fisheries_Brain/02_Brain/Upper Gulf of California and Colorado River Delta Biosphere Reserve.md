# Upper Gulf of California and Colorado River Delta Biosphere Reserve

## Summary
The Upper Gulf of California and Colorado River Delta Biosphere Reserve was established by Mexican presidential decree on June 10, 1993, in response to concerns from fishermen about declining shrimp and fish catches. The Reserve aims to regulate fishing activities and promote ecosystem restoration through research and management, with current restoration activities focusing on the Rio Hardy wetlands and the nucleus zone of the Reserve.
