# Watershed Management

## Summary
Watershed Management refers to the integrated planning and implementation of practices designed to protect and restore aquatic ecosystems by addressing land-use activities and hydrological processes across entire drainage basins. In the context of fish science and conservation, watershed management encompasses strategies to mitigate anthropogenic disturbances such as logging, water development, and channel modification that degrade fish habitat and stream integrity. This holistic approach recognizes that fish populations and their supporting ecosystems depend on maintaining proper watershed-scale conditions including sediment transport dynamics, riparian vegetation, stream channel morphology, and overall hydrological function.
