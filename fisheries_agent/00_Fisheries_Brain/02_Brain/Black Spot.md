# Black Spot

## Summary
Black Spot is a parasitic condition caused by Neascus sp. that was observed in some northern common shiner specimens collected from Flint Creek. Fish showing noticeable parasitism were excluded from the lethal oxygen testing to avoid confounding the experimental results.
