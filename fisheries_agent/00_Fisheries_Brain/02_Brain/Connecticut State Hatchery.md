# Connecticut State Hatchery

## Summary
The Connecticut State Hatchery at Burlington was one of two facilities where experimental trout nutrition research was conducted from 1927-1934. This hatchery served as a research site for the cooperative work between the New York State Conservation Department, the federal Bureau of Fisheries, and Cornell University to improve feeding methods in fish hatcheries.
