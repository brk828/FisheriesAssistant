# Yellow-nosed Cotton Rat

## Summary
The Yellow-nosed Cotton Rat (Sigmodon ochrognathus) is a rodent species that was newly documented for the Aravaipa Creek watershed during this 1979-1982 survey. The George Whittell Wildlife Preserve represents the northern edge of this species' range, making it biogeographically significant as part of the area's position at the convergence of Sonoran, Great Plains, Chihuahuan, and Madrean faunal elements.
