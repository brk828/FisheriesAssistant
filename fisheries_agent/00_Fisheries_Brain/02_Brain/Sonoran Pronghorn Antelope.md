# Sonoran Pronghorn Antelope

## Summary
Sonoran Pronghorn Antelope is a threatened or endangered species identified as a potential management target for Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. However, the document notes that the historic presence of this species on the refuge is under review, and managing for it would require grassland habitat that conflicts with habitat needs of other species like ocelot.
