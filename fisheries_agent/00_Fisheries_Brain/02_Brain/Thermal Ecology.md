# Thermal Ecology

## Summary
Thermal ecology examines how temperature influences species distribution, behavior, and physiology. This source demonstrates that Razorback Sucker movements were strongly driven by temperature (P < 0.001) rather than flow, with fish spawning at 8-17°C, preferring adult temperatures of 22.9-24.8°C, and using deeper reservoir water during summer when river temperatures peaked at 26.2°C, suggesting temperature may be a more influential movement cue than flow in inflow areas.
