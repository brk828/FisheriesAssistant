# Cladocerans

## Summary
Cladocerans are small aquatic crustaceans that serve as prey for young Colorado River Squawfish. Fish smaller than 50 mm in length feed primarily on cladocerans along with copepods and chironomid larvae before transitioning to larger prey items as they grow.
