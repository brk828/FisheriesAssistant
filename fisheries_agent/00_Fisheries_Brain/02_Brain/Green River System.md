# Green River System

## Context
> This 1973 study by Stalnaker and Holden (Utah Cooperative Fishery Unit, Utah State University) documents the historical and post-impoundment distribution of seven indigenous large-river fish species endemic to the upper Colorado River Basin, with a focus on the [[Green River System]].
