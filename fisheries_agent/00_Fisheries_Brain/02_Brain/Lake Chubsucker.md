# Lake Chubsucker

## Summary
The Lake Chubsucker (*Erimyzon sucetta*) is a freshwater cyprinid species commonly associated with warm-water, vegetation-dense aquatic ecosystems and frequently co-occurs with other pan fish and small predatory species in managed fisheries systems. As a benthic feeder and member of the pan fish community, it plays a minor ecological role in warm-water fish assemblages but is typically not a primary target for management or conservation efforts, serving instead as an indicator species for certain habitat types and aquatic vegetation conditions. Its presence in ichthyological surveys and ecological studies provides valuable information about community composition and the relative health of vegetated warm-water lake and pond environments.
