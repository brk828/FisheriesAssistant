# Chara vulgaris

## Summary
Chara vulgaris is a monoecious species of stonewort in the corticate group of the genus Chara. This study found it has a diploid chromosome number of N=28 and noted that its cytological appearance differs markedly from species like Chara braunii, supporting taxonomic distinctions within the genus.
