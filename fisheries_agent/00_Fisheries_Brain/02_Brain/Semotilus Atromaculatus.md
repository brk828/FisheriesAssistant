# Semotilus Atromaculatus

## Summary
Semotilus atromaculatus, commonly known as creek chub, is a small freshwater cyprinid species that inhabits cool, clear headwater streams and upper reaches of river systems, particularly in North American drainage basins like the Pecos River. This species is characteristic of swift-water, rocky habitat preferences and serves as an important ecological indicator of high-quality, well-oxygenated aquatic environments. Creek chub populations are significant for understanding stream ecosystem health and community structure in upland lotic systems, as their presence and abundance reflect suitable thermal and habitat conditions in coldwater stream networks.
