# Q. hypoleucoides

## Summary
Q. hypoleucoides (Quercus hypoleucoides) is one of the dominant oak species in the Sierra San Luis reserve. It occurs in the oak forest community at elevations of 1,800-2,100 meters that dominates more than 70% of the reserve's surface.
