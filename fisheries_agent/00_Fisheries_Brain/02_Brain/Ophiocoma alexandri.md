# Ophiocoma alexandri

## Summary
Ophiocoma alexandri is a brittle star species found in the Puerto Peñasco intertidal zone. It has medium-length arm spines, a gray disc, and arms banded in gray and white, with thinner and longer arms compared to the similar species O. aethiops.
