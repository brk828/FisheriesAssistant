# Ute Pipeline Project

## Summary
The Ute Pipeline Project was a water development project in the Upper Colorado River Basin that received a draft biological opinion in the mid-1990s. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service suggested additional project-specific measures beyond the standard $10 per acre foot depletion fee, even for existing depletions, which water users viewed as a significant departure from the programmatic approach principles of the Recovery Program.
