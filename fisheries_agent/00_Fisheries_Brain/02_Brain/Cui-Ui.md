# Cui-Ui

## Summary
The cui-ui (*Chasmistics cujus*) is an endangered catostomid fish (sucker family) endemic to Pyramid Lake, Nevada, that has been the focus of intensive conservation efforts documented through multiple recovery team meetings and management plans since the late 1980s. As a large, long-lived lake sucker species facing population decline, the cui-ui represents a critical case study in aquatic conservation biology, with management strategies centered on fish passage operations and population monitoring to prevent extinction. The species exemplifies the interagency collaboration required for endangered fish recovery, involving coordination between U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, state agencies, and recovery teams to address habitat fragmentation and population viability in endemic freshwater systems.
