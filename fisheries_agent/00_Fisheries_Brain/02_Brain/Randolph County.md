# Randolph County

## Summary
Randolph County is a West Virginia county where two rainbow trout ponds were established at high elevations (2,000 and 3,000 feet). Despite the elevation, summer temperatures likely exceeded the 73°F tolerance threshold for trout, contributing to poor survival and growth rates that led researchers to conclude trout stocking was not suitable for small West Virginia impoundments.
