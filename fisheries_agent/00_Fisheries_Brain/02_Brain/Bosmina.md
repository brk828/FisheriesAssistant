# Bosmina

## Summary
Bosmina is a genus of small cladoceran zooplankton. In Lake Mohave, Bosmina was one of the dominant zooplankton groups during the razorback sucker larval period and served as a primary food source for small (<12 mm) razorback sucker larvae, though larvae selected Bosmina individuals smaller than the average size available in the plankton.
