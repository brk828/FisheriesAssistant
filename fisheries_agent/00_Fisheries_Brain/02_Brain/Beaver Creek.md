# Beaver Creek

## Summary
Beaver Creek represents a significant study site in Kentucky ichthyological research, featured prominently in mid-20th century investigations of fish growth rates, population dynamics, and community structure across regional water bodies. The creek served as a reference or comparative location in studies examining how mining activities and water chemistry changes (particularly acid mine drainage) affected fish populations and species composition in Appalachian streams. Its inclusion in multiple foundational fisheries surveys underscores its importance as a baseline system for understanding natural fish assemblage patterns and the impacts of anthropogenic disturbance on aquatic ecosystems in the eastern United States.
