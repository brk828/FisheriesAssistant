# Sonoran Chub

## Summary
The Sonoran Chub is an imperiled native fish species endemic to the southwestern United States and Mexico, particularly the Gila River drainage and associated tributaries in Arizona and Sonora. This species has become a focal point for desert fish recovery efforts due to its vulnerability to habitat degradation and competition from introduced exotic warmwater species, particularly green sunfish in localized populations such as those in Sycamore Canyon. As a component of the broader Southwestern desert fish recovery program managed by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team, conservation strategies for the Sonoran Chub have emphasized habitat protection and exotic species control to ensure the species' persistence in its native range.
