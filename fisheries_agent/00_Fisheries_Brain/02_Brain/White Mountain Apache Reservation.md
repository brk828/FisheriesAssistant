# White Mountain Apache Reservation

## Summary
The White Mountain Apache Reservation contains four Gila populations in Salt River tributaries that were targeted for sampling in the taxonomy project. However, these populations were not accessible to researchers due to permit denial, representing a gap in the comprehensive sampling effort across the Colorado River drainage.
