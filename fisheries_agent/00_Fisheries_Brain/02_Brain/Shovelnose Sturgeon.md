# Shovelnose Sturgeon

## Summary
The Shovelnose Sturgeon (*Scaphirhynchus platorynchus*) is a small sturgeon species native to large North American river systems, particularly the Mississippi River, characterized by its distinctive flattened, shovel-like snout adapted for benthic feeding. As a benthic omnivore and historically significant food fish, this species occupies an important ecological role in river ecosystems and has been documented across multiple states including Kentucky as part of native sturgeon faunas. The Shovelnose Sturgeon represents an important subject for conservation and fisheries management due to its sensitivity to riverine habitat alterations and its presence in regions affected by human development and dam construction.
