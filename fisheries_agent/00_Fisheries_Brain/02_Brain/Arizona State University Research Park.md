# Arizona State University Research Park

## Summary
Arizona State University Research Park is a research facility in Arizona that maintains ponds for fish conservation and research purposes. In July 1992, this facility received 18 endangered Bonytail Chub that were successfully acclimated and released into a pond at the site, where they were observed swimming normally to deep water after release.
