# Yuma Clapper Rails

## Summary
Yuma Clapper Rails are an endangered bird species dependent on the lower Colorado River ecosystem. According to this source, the Ciénega de Santa Clara may contain the largest remaining populations of this endangered species, making the wetland's protection a conservation priority.
