# Indus River

## Summary
The Indus River is a major river system that receives flow from three Afghan drainage basins including the Kabul River, Chamkani-Kurram River, and Zhob-Gowmal River. It serves as the source of Oriental fish species that have colonized the Kabul River basin, making that basin the most diverse in Afghanistan despite its small size.
