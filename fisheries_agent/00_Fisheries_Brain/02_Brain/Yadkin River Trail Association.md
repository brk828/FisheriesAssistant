# Yadkin River Trail Association

## Summary
The Yadkin River Trail Association is an organization that improved fishing access by adding 13 sites on the Yadkin River and 2 on the South Yadkin River. This increased access led to greater fishing pressure, which factored into management decisions to preserve the abundant bullhead fishery above Idols Dam rather than introducing flathead catfish that could eliminate bullheads.
