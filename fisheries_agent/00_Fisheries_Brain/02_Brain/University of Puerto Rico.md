# University of Puerto Rico

## Summary
The University of Puerto Rico's Institute of Marine Biology contributed to this field guide through Mr. Charles E. Cutress, who provided taxonomic identifications of the anemone specimens. This was one of several institutions that provided specialist expertise for the invertebrate survey.
