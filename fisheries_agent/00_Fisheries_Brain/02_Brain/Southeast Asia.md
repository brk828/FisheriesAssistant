# Southeast Asia

## Summary
Southeast Asia is a geographic region where, according to this 1977 legal opinion, large-scale crossbreeding of endangered crocodilian species was already occurring at the time. This commercial breeding activity was cited as a practical example of how hybrid production could circumvent Endangered Species Act protections if hybrids were not covered by the law.
