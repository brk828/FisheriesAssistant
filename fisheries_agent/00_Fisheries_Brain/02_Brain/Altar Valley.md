# Altar Valley

## Summary
The Altar Valley is a region in southeastern Arizona that encompasses Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. The valley's ecosystem has been significantly altered from its historical Sonoran Savanna Grassland state, and the refuge management plan seeks to restore it through experimental habitat management approaches.
