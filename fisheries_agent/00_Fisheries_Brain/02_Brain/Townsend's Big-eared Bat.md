# Townsend's Big-eared Bat

## Summary
Townsend's Big-eared Bat (Plecotus townsendii) is a bat species that roosts colonially and is easily disturbed by human activity. This survey documented a maternity colony in Oak Grove Canyon at 3,550 feet elevation in 1958 within the Aravaipa Creek watershed.
