# Price-San Rafael Project

## Summary
The Price-San Rafael Project is a water development project in Utah that was undergoing Section 7 consultation as of February 1990. The project would result in a depletion of 25,623 acre-feet of water and generate $238,910 in depletion charges at the rate of $10 per acre-foot to support endangered fish recovery efforts.
