# Brook Trout

## Summary
Brook Trout (Salvelinus fontinalis) is a coldwater salmonid species native to eastern North America that serves as both an important indicator organism for aquatic ecosystem health and a keystone species in conservation and fisheries management programs. As a highly sensitive indicator of water quality and temperature conditions, Brook Trout is frequently used in hatchery propagation efforts for species restoration, mitigation of habitat loss from dam construction, and recreational fishery enhancement across North American river systems. The species' ecological role in maintaining stream ecosystem structure, combined with its vulnerability to environmental degradation and competition from non-native salmonids, makes it a central focus of conservation biology and coldwater stream restoration initiatives.
