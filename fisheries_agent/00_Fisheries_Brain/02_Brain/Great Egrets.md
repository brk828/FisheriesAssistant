# Great Egrets

## Summary
Great Egrets (Ardea alba) are piscivorous birds present in Lake Havasu and identified as one of the abundant avian predator species in the system. They are mentioned as part of the suite of bird species that pose predation threats to stocked bonytail, though no specific predation events by Great Egrets were documented in this study.
