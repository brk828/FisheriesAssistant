# Brushiest Allotment

## Summary
The Brushiest Allotment is a livestock grazing area on the Globe Ranger District that includes part of West Fork of Pinto Creek, a previously approved Gila Topminnow reintroduction site. This source indicates the allotment borders the Superstition Wilderness and was subject to new consultation for riparian pasture fencing to improve livestock control.
