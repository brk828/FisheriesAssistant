# Sacramento Sucker

## Summary
The Sacramento Sucker (*Catostomus occidentalis*) is a native cyprinid fish species historically distributed throughout California's Sierra Nevada foothill streams and San Joaquin Valley drainage systems. As a benthic-feeding sucker species, it plays an important ecological role in native fish communities, but has experienced substantial range contraction and population decline due to habitat alteration, water management, and competition from introduced species. This species serves as a key indicator organism for assessing the integrity of California's native fish fauna and the ecological impacts of human modification on foothill stream ecosystems.
