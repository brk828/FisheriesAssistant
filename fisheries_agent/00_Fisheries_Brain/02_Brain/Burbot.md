# Burbot

## Summary
The burbot (*Lota lota*) is a freshwater gadiform fish species and the only true cod found in inland waters of the Northern Hemisphere, characterized by its benthic lifestyle and preference for cold, well-oxygenated environments in lakes and rivers. As a piscivorous predator and important indicator species for cold-water ecosystem health, burbot populations have declined significantly across much of their historical range due to habitat degradation, warming water temperatures, and altered thermal regimes, making them a conservation concern in many regions. The species plays an ecological role as both predator and prey in northern aquatic food webs while serving as a valuable food fish in some northern communities and regions.
