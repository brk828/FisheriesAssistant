# Black Range

## Summary
The Black Range is a mountain range in southwestern New Mexico where the Mimbres River headwaters originate on its western slope. This source identifies the Black Range as the source area for the Mimbres drainage, an isolated fluvial system of the endorheic Guzman Basin.
