# Atlantic Silverside

## Summary
Atlantic Silverside (Menidia menidia) is a natural prey species of striped bass in Chesapeake Bay and Atlantic coastal waters. The study notes that Atlantic silversides are morphologically similar to the emerald and striped shiners used in laboratory trials, and bluefish have been documented consuming Atlantic silversides up to 63% of their body length.
