# Stewart Lake Drain

## Summary
Stewart Lake Drain is a flooded lowland area in the Green River basin that serves as a resting-feeding area for razorback suckers during the breeding season. This study found that 18% of ripe razorback suckers were captured in low-velocity backwater habitats like Stewart Lake Drain, which has sand-silt and vegetated substrates, and the area is considered important for species recovery due to annual movements of fish into these flooded areas.
