# Uchida and King

## Summary
Uchida and King were researchers who in 1962 demonstrated that tilapia showed significantly superior growth on high-quality trout feeds compared to livestock feed (white wheat middlings). They suggested that prolonged use of trout feed might impair spawning potential, though the California study found no such impairment after 18 months.
