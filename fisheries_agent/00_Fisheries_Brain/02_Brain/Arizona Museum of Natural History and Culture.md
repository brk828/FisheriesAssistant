# Arizona Museum of Natural History and Culture

## Summary
The Arizona Museum of Natural History and Culture was a planned institution mentioned in the proposal as part of ASU's strategic alignment. The proposal noted that the Sierra Ancha field program would be compatible with plans for this museum, which was envisioned as comparable to the American Museum of Natural History's Southwestern Research Station.
