# Rivulus

## Summary
Rivulus is a genus of fish found in tropical America that can access dense aquatic vegetation where Gambusia cannot penetrate. The source identifies Rivulus species as potential alternatives to Gambusia for mosquito control that could complement other species by reaching areas of thick vegetation.
