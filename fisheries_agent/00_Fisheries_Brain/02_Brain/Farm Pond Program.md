# Farm Pond Program

## Summary
The Farm Pond Program refers to a fisheries management initiative that focuses on stocking and managing small impoundments, ponds, and lentic (still-water) systems on private agricultural or rural lands to support recreational fishing and fish populations. This program typically involves hatcheries distributing fish stock to farm ponds and similar small water bodies coded as Lake/Pond (LK) systems, representing an important component of broader fisheries distribution efforts that also encompass reservoirs, streams, and other aquatic habitats. The program serves both conservation and recreational objectives by extending fish management practices beyond traditional public waters to private landowner properties.
