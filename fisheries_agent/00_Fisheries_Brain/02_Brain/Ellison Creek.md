# Ellison Creek

## Summary
Ellison Creek is a tributary of the White River in Nevada where the creek's confluence with the White River represented one of seven historic habitats for White River Spinedace. This riverine habitat, unlike the six spring-based habitats, supported spinedace in the 1930s when 429 individuals were collected from the White River, but the population was extirpated by 1985.
