# Figtree Springs

## Summary
Figtree Springs is one of the springs at Slaughter Ranch with a water rights priority date of January 1, 1884. Combined with Cattail Spring, it produces approximately 26,000,000 gallons per annum (49 gallons per minute) as part of the ranch's artesian aquifer system.
