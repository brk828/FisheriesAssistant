# Lake Valley

## Summary
Lake Valley, Nevada is the present-day location of the former Pluvial Lake Carpenter system. This area was the site of the ancient lake that existed 10,000 to 40,000 years ago, whose drying led to the restriction of Big Spring Spinedace to remnant habitats in Meadow Valley Wash.
