# Lake LeConte

## Summary
Lake LeConte was a pluvial lake that occupied the Salton Sink during wetter periods, with shore bars at 45 feet above sea level showing minimal erosion over the past approximately 300 years. Radiocarbon evidence indicates the lake persisted until approximately 300 years ago, and recession lines spaced about 5 feet apart suggest an evaporation rate about double the present rate, providing evidence for extreme aridity in recent centuries.
