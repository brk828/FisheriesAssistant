# Aravaipa Wilderness

## Summary
Aravaipa Wilderness is a special use area that provides stream protection within the proposed critical habitat. It is located in an area where the Bureau of Land Management is the primary manager, with Aravaipa Creek being one of the few remaining locations where both Spikedace and Loach Minnow are still common.
