# Wayne E. Kirch Wildlife Management Area

## Summary
Wayne E. Kirch Wildlife Management Area is a State of Nevada property in Nye County that encompasses the Flag Springs complex, the last remaining habitat for White River Spinedace. The state's ownership of both the springs and associated water rights provides some protection for the species, and the Nevada Department of Wildlife installed habitat improvement structures and removed excess watercress from the springs in 1992 as part of conservation efforts.
