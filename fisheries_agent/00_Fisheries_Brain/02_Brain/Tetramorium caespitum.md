# Tetramorium caespitum

## Summary
Tetramorium caespitum, commonly known as the pavement ant, is an omnivorous ant species approximately 4 mm in length that feeds primarily on insects. This study documents their use of sand grains as tools during coordinated attacks on bee nests, including dropping grains as weapons, plugging nest entrances, and employing specialized guard ants to prevent bee escape—representing a sophisticated predatory strategy involving multiple behavioral components.
