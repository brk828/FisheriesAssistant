# Rio Grande Sucker

## Summary
Rio Grande Sucker (Pantosteus plebeius) is a catostomid fish native to the Rio Grande basin from southern Colorado to Chihuahua, Mexico, historically occupying multiple drainages including the San Luis Valley Closed Basin and Mimbres River. This study found the species at 37% of surveyed sites (41 of 112), with occupancy positively associated with warmer water temperatures (occupancy increasing from 0.15 at 7.8°C to 0.78 at 21.0°C) and negatively associated with riffle-dominated habitats, preferring small, warm streams in lower Strahler orders. The species has experienced significant declines due to habitat degradation, nonnative species, water use, and climate change, with 5,732 km (27%) of analyzed stream length predicted as suitable habitat, concentrated primarily in the Rio Grande/Elephant Butte basin of north-central New Mexico.
