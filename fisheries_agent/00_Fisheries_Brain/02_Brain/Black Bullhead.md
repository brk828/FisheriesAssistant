# Black Bullhead

## Summary
The Black Bullhead (*Ameiurus melas*) is a small ictalurid catfish species found in North American freshwater systems that is notable for its ecological role as a benthic omnivore and its demonstrated high toxicant resistance, making it a useful indicator species in aquatic toxicology and fish population surveys. As a warmwater species commonly found in littoral zones and near-shore habitats of lakes and ponds, the Black Bullhead is an important component of multi-species fish assemblages and is frequently employed in fisheries research and biotic sampling protocols to assess aquatic ecosystem health and community composition.
