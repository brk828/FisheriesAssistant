# Colorado River Fishes Recovery Program

## Summary
The Colorado River Fishes Recovery Program is a comprehensive federal conservation initiative aimed at restoring and recovering native fish species, particularly endemic and endangered taxa like bonytail chub and other Colorado River endemics, that have been depleted or extirpated from their native ranges due to dam construction, water management, and habitat alteration. The program coordinates multi-agency efforts across river basin recovery teams to implement species reintroduction plans, captive breeding and hatchery operations, and habitat restoration strategies throughout both the Upper and Lower Colorado River Basins. This represents a major long-term commitment to reversing the ecological decline of native fishes in one of North America's most heavily managed aquatic ecosystems.
