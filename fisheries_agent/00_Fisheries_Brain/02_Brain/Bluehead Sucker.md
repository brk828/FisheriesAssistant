# Bluehead Sucker

## Summary
The Bluehead Sucker (Pantosteus delphinus) is a native catostomid fish species found in the Colorado River system, characterized by morphological features including distinctive interneural bones and frontoparietal fontanelle structures that allow diagnostic separation from related sucker larvae and juveniles. This species is commonly encountered in ecological surveys of western riverine systems and serves as an important indicator of native fish community composition in conservation assessments of endangered aquatic ecosystems. Research on Bluehead Suckers contributes to understanding the population dynamics and habitat requirements of native fishes in the Colorado River basin, where they coexist with other native species and non-native competitors.
