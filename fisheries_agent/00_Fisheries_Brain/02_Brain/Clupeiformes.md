# Clupeiformes

## Summary
Clupeiformes is an order of bony fishes (Osteichthyes) that includes important forage fish families. This source documents that the order in the Gulf of California includes Engraulidae (5 genera, 16 species) and Clupeidae (8 genera, 8 species).
