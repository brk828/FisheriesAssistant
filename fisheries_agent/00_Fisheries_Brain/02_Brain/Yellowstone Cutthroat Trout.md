# Yellowstone Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
The Yellowstone Cutthroat Trout is a native salmonid species historically endemic to the Yellowstone Lake ecosystem and upper Snake River drainage that has served as a critical subject in fisheries management and conservation studies, particularly regarding species introductions, ecological interactions, and restoration efforts in western North American aquatic systems. This subspecies has been extensively studied in the context of native fish conservation, habitat management, and the ecological consequences of fish stocking and translocation programs in alpine and montane lake environments.
