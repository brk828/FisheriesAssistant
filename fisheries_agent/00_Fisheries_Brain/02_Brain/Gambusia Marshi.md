# Gambusia Marshi

## Summary
Gambusia marshi is a poeciliid (livebearing fish) species endemic to the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin in Coahuila, Mexico, formally described by Minckley in 1962. This species represents an important component of the basin's highly endemic and evolutionarily significant fish fauna, which has been the subject of long-term research into the origin and evolution of isolated aquatic ecosystems. As a small livebearing fish inhabiting thermally extreme environments within the basin, G. marshi serves as a valuable model for understanding adaptation, speciation, and conservation of endemic fishes in geographically isolated spring systems.
