# American Eel

## Summary
The American Eel is a catadromous fish species native to Atlantic coastal waters of North America that exhibits complex long-distance migratory behavior, spawning in the Sargasso Sea and returning to freshwater and estuarine habitats including systems like the St. Lawrence River for growth and development. In fisheries research and tagging studies, American Eels serve as important indicator species for monitoring aquatic connectivity and ecosystem health, with tag recovery rates typically ranging from 3-11% depending on tagging methodology, providing valuable data on movement patterns and population dynamics across the Northwest Atlantic region.
