# Etheostoma Fonticola

## Summary
Etheostoma fonticola, commonly known as the Fountain Darter, is a small freshwater darter species endemic to Texas that represents an important subject for conservation biology and ichthyological taxonomy studies. As a member of the diverse genus Etheostoma, this species is significant for understanding darter systematics, reproductive biology, and hybridization patterns within the Percidae family, as demonstrated by its use in experimental crossing studies with other darter species. The Fountain Darter's restricted range and ecological specificity make it a key indicator species for aquatic habitat quality and conservation efforts in Texas spring ecosystems.
