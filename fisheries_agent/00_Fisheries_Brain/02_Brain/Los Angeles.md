# Los Angeles

## Summary
Los Angeles is a west coast market city where depth interviews with major buyers in July-August 1968 revealed that most were unaware Montana had commercial fisheries and characterized the fish species as inferior products for ethnic markets. One Los Angeles wholesaler specifically noted that Montana fish quality was inferior to locally-sourced fish and could not command comparable prices in the market.
