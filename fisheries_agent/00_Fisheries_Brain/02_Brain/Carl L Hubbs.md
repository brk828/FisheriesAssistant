# Carl L Hubbs

## Summary
Carl L. Hubbs was the ichthyologist who authored this 1929 taxonomic revision establishing the new genus Sardinops for Pacific sardines. He verified morphological observations by Thompson, added critical new characters distinguishing Pacific from European sardines, and designated Maletta cerulea Girard, 1854 as the type species of Sardinops.

## Context

> This 1929 taxonomic revision by [[Carl L Hubbs]] establishes the new genus *Sardinops* for the [[California Sardine]] and related Pacific species, distinguishing them from the European sardines (genus *Sardina*).
