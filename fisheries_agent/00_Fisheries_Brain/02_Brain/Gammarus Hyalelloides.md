# Gammarus Hyalelloides

## Summary
Gammarus hyalelloides is a small amphipod species from Phantom Lake Spring in Jeff Davis County, Texas, with mean male length of 7.07 mm. This source indicates it is characterized by long first antennae relative to body length, reduced setation, and low accessory flagellum article counts (3.60). Statistical analysis suggests a small form from 3.5 miles west of Toyahvale (population M) is likely conspecific with G. hyalelloides.
