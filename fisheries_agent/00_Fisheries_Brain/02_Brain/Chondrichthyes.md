# Chondrichthyes

## Summary
Chondrichthyes is the taxonomic class comprising cartilaginous fishes, characterized by skeletons composed entirely of cartilage rather than bone, and includes sharks, rays, skates, and chimaeras. This group is fundamental to ichthyological classification and represents a major lineage in aquatic ecosystems, though members are predominantly marine with relatively few freshwater representatives. Understanding Chondrichthyes taxonomy and identification is essential for fish science, as these species occupy important ecological niches and require distinct conservation approaches compared to osteichthyean (bony) fishes.
