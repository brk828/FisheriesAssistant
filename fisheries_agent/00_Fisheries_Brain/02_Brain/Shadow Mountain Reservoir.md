# Shadow Mountain Reservoir

## Summary
Shadow Mountain Reservoir is a mid-sized water body connecting Granby Reservoir to Grand Lake, characterized by high turbidity (Secchi disc 3-6 feet) and heavy phytoplankton blooms reaching 1.7 cubic centimeters per liter in August 1953. The reservoir produced 942 fish in 3,317 hours (0.28 catch-per-man-hour), with Rainbow Trout comprising 87% of catches. It was the focus of intensive rough fish control operations including seining that removed 7,175 pounds of suckers and tagging studies showing 12.0% first-year returns for rainbow trout and 8.7% for brown trout.
