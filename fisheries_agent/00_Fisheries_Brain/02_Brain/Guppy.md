# Guppy

## Summary
The guppy (Poecilia reticulata) is a small live-bearing fish of the family Poeciliidae, native to South America but widely distributed globally through the aquarium trade and intentional introductions. In fisheries and conservation contexts, guppies serve as important model organisms for studying population genetics, adaptation to novel environments, and the ecological impacts of non-native species introductions, particularly in isolated aquatic systems where they may compete with or prey upon native species. Their rapid reproduction, phenotypic plasticity, and capacity to establish populations in diverse habitats make them significant subjects for research on evolutionary biology and conservation challenges in fragmented ecosystems.
