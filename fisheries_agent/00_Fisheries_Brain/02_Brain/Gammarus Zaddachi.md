# Gammarus Zaddachi

## Summary
Gammarus zaddachi is a brackish-water amphipod commonly found in European estuaries and tidal streams that exhibits endogenous circatidal activity rhythms synchronized with tidal cycles. This species is ecologically significant as a model organism for studying behavioral responses to environmental salinity gradients and tidal influences, with a documented chlorinity tolerance range exceeding 2,000 mg/L, making it an important indicator of estuarine ecosystem health and a subject of research on anadromous fish migration patterns in coastal aquatic systems.
