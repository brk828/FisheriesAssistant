# Santa Clara Slough

## Summary
Santa Clara Slough is an aquatic habitat system in the southwestern United States that has served as an important site for monitoring and studying fish populations, particularly endemic or threatened species such as pupfish. The slough represents a critical freshwater ecosystem where conservation efforts have focused on population assessment, habitat management, and species supplementation to maintain viable populations in the face of environmental pressures and water management challenges.
