# Susquehanna River

## Summary
The Susquehanna River is a major river drainage system in the Central Appalachian region that serves as an important watershed for ichthyological surveys and fish faunal inventories in Pennsylvania and surrounding areas. As a significant freshwater river system, it supports diverse fish communities and has been the subject of multiple scientific studies examining fish distribution, species composition, and parasite infection dynamics. The Susquehanna River drainage represents a distinct biogeographic unit for organizing and cataloguing regional fish fauna in comparative ecological and conservation studies.
