# South Peoples Spring

## Summary
South Peoples Spring is a desert spring located in the Phoenix District of Arizona (T12N R10W SE SW Sec.14) on public lands managed by the Bureau of Land Management. This source reports that it was one of the initial sites selected for reintroduction of endangered native fish species, with desert pupfish successfully introduced there in August 1982, and it was also proposed as one of the sites for Gila topminnow introduction in the 1983 supplemental environmental assessment.

## Context

> This supplemental environmental assessment, prepared by the [[U.S. Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) Phoenix District Office in December 1983, authorizes the introduction of [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis*) and [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius macularius*)...
