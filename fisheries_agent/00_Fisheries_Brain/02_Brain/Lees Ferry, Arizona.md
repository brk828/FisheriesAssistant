# Lees Ferry, Arizona

## Summary
Lees Ferry, Arizona is the geographic dividing point that separates the Colorado River Basin into upper basin states (Colorado, New Mexico, Utah, Wyoming) and lower basin states (Arizona, California, Nevada). This division is relevant to understanding the historical distribution and current status of endangered fish species like the bonytail chub and razorback sucker throughout the basin.
