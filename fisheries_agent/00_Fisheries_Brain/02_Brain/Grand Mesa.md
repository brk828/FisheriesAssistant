# Grand Mesa

## Summary
Grand Mesa is a lava-capped tableland in Colorado at approximately 10,000 feet elevation where Forest Lake is located. The study notes that the lake's pH (averaging 7.5) reflects basic chemistry from surrounding basaltic lava formations, and recommends that the stocking schedule developed for Forest Lake be applied to similar "type" lakes on Grand Mesa.
