# Udall Center for Studies in Public Policy

## Summary
The Udall Center for Studies in Public Policy worked with the EPA Border Environmental Plan Public Advisory Committee to provide grassroots perspectives on transboundary environmental problems in the U.S.-Mexico border region. The center contributed to documenting critical environmental and public health concerns that had been historically neglected in the border area.
