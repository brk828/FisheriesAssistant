# Cichla temensis

## Summary
Cichla temensis is the scientific name for Speckled Pavon, marketed as "butterfly cichlid." This source discusses its introduction to Florida beginning in 1984 as an example of problematic management, noting that the genus Cichla has poorly studied taxonomy with at least one known misidentification and probable undescribed species, raising questions about what was actually introduced.
