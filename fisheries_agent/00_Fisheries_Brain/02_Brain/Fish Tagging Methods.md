# Fish Tagging Methods

## Summary
Fish tagging methods are scientific techniques used to mark individual fish with external or internal identifiers—such as physical tags, dyes, or fin clips—to track their movements, survival, and population dynamics in natural and cultured environments. These methods are essential tools in fisheries science and conservation biology, enabling researchers to monitor fish behavior, migration patterns, homing ability, and responses to environmental conditions, while also providing critical data for stock assessment and management decisions. The choice of tagging method depends on fish species, size, study objectives, and the specific ecological or aquacultural context being investigated.
