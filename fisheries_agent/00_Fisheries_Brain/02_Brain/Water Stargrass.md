# Water Stargrass

## Summary
Water Stargrass (Heteranthera dubia) is an aquatic plant species that can be controlled with herbicides in fisheries management contexts. This manual recommends using iso-octyl ester of 2,4-D at 20 lbs. acid equivalent per acre during periods of vigorous growth for effective control of this species.
