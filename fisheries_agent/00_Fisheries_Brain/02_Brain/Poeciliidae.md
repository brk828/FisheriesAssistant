# Poeciliidae

## Summary
Poeciliidae is a family of small freshwater and brackish-water fishes commonly known as livebearers, characterized by internal fertilization and the production of live young rather than eggs. The family includes ecologically and evolutionarily significant genera such as Gambusia (mosquitofishes) and Fundulus relatives, many of which are endemic to limited geographic ranges and have become subjects of intensive conservation efforts due to their vulnerability to habitat loss and extinction. Poeciliids are particularly important in aquatic ecology as indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and have served as model organisms for studying adaptation, sexual selection, and the evolutionary consequences of small population sizes in isolated desert spring systems.
