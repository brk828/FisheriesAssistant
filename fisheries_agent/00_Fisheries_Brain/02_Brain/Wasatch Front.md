# Wasatch Front

## Summary
The Wasatch Front is a geological feature that isolated the Bonneville Basin in the north more than 30 million years ago. This tectonic event contributed to the drainage disruptions that the study proposes led to the divergence of plagopterin fish lineages in western North America.

## Context

> This study used allozyme electrophoresis to assess phylogenetic relationships within the cyprinid tribe [[Plagopterini]] of western North America.
