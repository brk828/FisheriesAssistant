# Eocene

## Summary
The Eocene was a geological epoch occurring approximately 40 million years ago. This source identifies it as the beginning point of a secular cooling trend that eventually culminated in Pleistocene glaciations, with West German fish genera indicating Eocene temperatures of 22°C compared to progressively cooler subsequent periods.
