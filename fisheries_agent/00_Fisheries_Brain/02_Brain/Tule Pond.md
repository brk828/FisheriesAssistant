# Tule Pond

## Summary
Tule Pond is one of 16 existing water sources at San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge in Arizona. It was designated for Phase II development (Years 6-10) under the 1986 Master Plan to create habitat for reintroducing rare Rio Yaqui fish species.
