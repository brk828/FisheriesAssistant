# United States

## Summary
The United States is the party alleged in this petition to be failing to effectively enforce Section 4 of the Endangered Species Act due to the 'Hutchison Rider' budgetary amendment that prohibited the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service from making final determinations for species listings or critical habitat designations for the remainder of Fiscal Year 1995. The petition argues this suspension was motivated by economic considerations related to trade, farming, ranching, logging, and fishing activities rather than legitimate resource allocation.
