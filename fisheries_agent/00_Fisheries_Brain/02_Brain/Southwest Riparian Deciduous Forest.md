# Southwest Riparian Deciduous Forest

## Summary
Southwest Riparian Deciduous Forest is a natural community type characterized by cottonwood-willow series vegetation. This habitat type was identified as one of the conservation targets in the Sonoran Desert ecoregional assessment, representing important riparian corridors within the desert landscape.
