# Smallmouth Bass

## Summary
Smallmouth bass (*Micropterus dolomieu*) is a centrarchid (sunfish family) species native to North American freshwater systems, particularly cool to warm streams and reservoirs of the Mississippi River drainage and Great Lakes regions. In conservation and ecological contexts, smallmouth bass function as both a recreationally important gamefish and, in some aquatic ecosystems, a potentially invasive predator that can compete with or prey upon native fish species, particularly in western river systems where they have been introduced outside their native range. Their ecological role, habitat requirements, and interactions with native fish assemblages make them a relevant species in fisheries management, restoration planning, and biogeographic studies of fish communities across diverse aquatic systems.
