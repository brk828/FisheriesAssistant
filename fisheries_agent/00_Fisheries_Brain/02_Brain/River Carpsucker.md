# River Carpsucker

## Summary
The river carpsucker is a freshwater cyprinid fish species found in North American river systems that has been studied in ichthyological research for its life history characteristics, including age structure, size distribution, and reproductive biology. As a benthic forager and filter-feeder, this species is relevant to aquatic ecology studies examining fish community dynamics and the ecological role of cyprinids in fluvial ecosystems. The river carpsucker has served as a model organism in fisheries science research investigating population demographics and reproductive fecundity in native North American fish assemblages.
