# Least Bitterns

## Summary
Least Bitterns are among the bird species documented at the Ciénega de Santa Clara wetland. This source identifies them as one of the species using this important migratory bird habitat in the Colorado River Delta.

## Context

> This article examines the ecological degradation of the [[Colorado River Delta]] and proposes strategies for reallocating water to restore and protect this critical aquatic ecosystem.
