# Erskine Creek

## Summary
Erskine Creek is a stream where catch distribution patterns were documented, demonstrating the highly unequal harvest typical of catchable trout fisheries. At this location, just 6% of anglers caught 50% of the total harvest, with the most successful anglers being local residents familiar with planting schedules.
