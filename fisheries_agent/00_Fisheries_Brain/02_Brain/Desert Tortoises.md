# Desert Tortoises

## Summary
Desert Tortoises (Gopherus agassizii) are a protected species found in the Aravaipa Creek area of Arizona. During the road repair and replacement project, a biologist was required to be on-site during construction specifically to protect Desert Tortoises from harm, indicating their presence in the project area and the need for protective measures during ground-disturbing activities.
