# Empetrichthys

## Summary
Empetrichthys is a genus of small cyprinodont fish endemic to the Death Valley region of California that represents a critically endangered lineage with extremely limited geographic distribution. The genus nearly went extinct in the late 1960s but was saved through coordinated conservation efforts by multiple agencies that recognized the urgent need to preserve these unique species found nowhere else in the world. As a geographically isolated and ecologically specialized group, Empetrichthys serves as an important case study in fish conservation biology, demonstrating both the vulnerability of desert aquatic fauna to environmental changes and the potential for successful intervention through collaborative management and artificial propagation techniques.
