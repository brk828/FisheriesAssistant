# Wisconsin Conservation Commission

## Summary
The Wisconsin Conservation Commission is the state agency that entered into a $60,000 contract for comprehensive field testing of antimycin during 1966-1967. The commission's testing program focused particularly on common carp control in Wisconsin lakes and streams, including trials of under-ice applications and spawning congregation treatments.
