# Instream Flow Requirements

## Summary
Instream Flow Requirements refer to the minimum or optimal water discharge levels that must be maintained in riverine systems to support fish populations and their essential habitats, including spawning, feeding, and refuge areas. These flow thresholds are scientifically determined based on species-specific dependencies and are used as regulatory mechanisms to protect aquatic ecosystems, particularly for sensitive or endangered species in regulated river systems like the Upper Colorado River. By establishing and maintaining appropriate flow regimes, instream flow requirements serve as a critical conservation tool to ensure adequate habitat quality and reproductive success for fish communities dependent on natural hydrological conditions.
