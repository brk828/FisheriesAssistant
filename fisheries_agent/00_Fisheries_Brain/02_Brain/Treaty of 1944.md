# Treaty of 1944

## Summary
The Treaty of 1944 between the United States and Mexico divided waters of the Colorado and Rio Grande rivers, guaranteeing Mexico 1,500,000 acre-feet of Colorado River water annually. The treaty established the International Boundary and Water Commission (IBWC) to manage water deliveries and resolve future issues between the two nations.
