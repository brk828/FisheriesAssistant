# Emlen and Oring

## Summary
Emlen and Oring (1977) developed a framework for classifying and analyzing mating systems in animals. This source proposes to use their framework to describe the mating systems of two pupfish species, including characteristics such as manner of mate acquisition, number of mates, pair bond characteristics, and parental care patterns.
