# Delta-Upper Gulf Biosphere Reserve

## Summary
The Delta-Upper Gulf Biosphere Reserve is the protected area identified as a pilot project under the May 5, 1997 Letter of Intent between the Department of Interior and SEMARNAP. An implementation committee of agencies and NGOs was established to coordinate management activities in this reserve.
