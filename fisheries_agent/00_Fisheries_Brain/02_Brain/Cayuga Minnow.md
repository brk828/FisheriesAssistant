## Summary
The Cayuga Minnow (Notropis bifrenatus) is a fish species found in Dwyer's Pond near the study area. It inhabits deeper, quieter water environments where Black-nosed Dace are absent, representing a different fish community composition than the rapid-flowing stream habitat preferred by Black-nosed Dace.
