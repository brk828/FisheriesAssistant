# Parental Care

## Summary
Male smallmouth bass provide exclusive parental care, assuming all responsibilities after driving females away from nests. Males guard eggs for 3-5 days until hatching, then herd and vigorously defend the schooling fry for several weeks until young reach 30-40 mm total length, after which juveniles become solitary or form small groups.
