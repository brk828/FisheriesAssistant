# U.S.-Mexico Border Region

## Summary
The U.S.-Mexico Border Region encompasses 28 counties (21 designated as distressed) with 14 pairs of twin cities sharing common airsheds and transnational river basins. As of 1990, the region had 9.2 million people (projected to double in 22 years) with 56.9% ethnic minority population and 20.7% poverty rate, facing severe environmental degradation from rapid industrialization and urbanization without adequate infrastructure investment. The region supports 460 endangered, threatened, proposed, or candidate species and includes shared ecosystems in the Gulf of Mexico and Gulf of California.
