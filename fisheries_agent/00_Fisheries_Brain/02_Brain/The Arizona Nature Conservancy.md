# The Arizona Nature Conservancy

## Summary
The Arizona Nature Conservancy is a conservation organization that, as of the 1980s, played a central role in documenting and addressing the crisis facing Arizona's native freshwater fish fauna, which had declined from 31 pre-settlement species to a state where one was extinct and four were extirpated. The organization developed and implemented conservation strategies focused on habitat preservation and maintenance of stable stream flows to combat the primary threats to these species: exotic fish introductions and loss of perennial aquatic ecosystems. Through projects such as lake and stream renovations, the Arizona Nature Conservancy worked to restore native fish populations and protect remaining populations in Arizona's freshwater systems.
