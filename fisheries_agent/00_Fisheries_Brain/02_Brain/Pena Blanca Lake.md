# Pena Blanca Lake

## Summary
Pena Blanca Lake is a warmwater Arizona reservoir that served as a study site for the Arizona Cooperative Fishery Unit's investigations of fish community structure, visitor-use patterns, and fisheries management during the 1960s. The lake was utilized to examine centrarchid species assemblages (including largemouth bass, bluegill, green sunfish, and black crappie) and ictalurid populations as part of broader research on small-impoundment fish ecology and production dynamics in desert aquatic systems.
