# Buffalofishes

## Summary
Buffalofishes (genus Ictiobus) are primary consumer fish that feed on plankton and organic material in reservoir food webs. In the Salt River reservoir system, buffalofishes are part of the introduced species assemblage and are classified as mobile species that show uniform growth throughout individual lakes, presumably moving to optimize feeding conditions.
