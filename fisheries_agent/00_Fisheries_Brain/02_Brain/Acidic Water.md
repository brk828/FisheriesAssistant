# Acidic Water

## Summary
Acidic water has low pH and alkalinity, characteristic of bog lake environments. Camerton Lake had pH 6.1 and very low total alkalinity (3 mg CaCO₃/L), creating acidic conditions typical of bog lakes in north-central Minnesota.
