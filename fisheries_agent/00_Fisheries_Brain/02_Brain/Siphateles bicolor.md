# Siphateles bicolor

## Summary
Siphateles bicolor is a fish species from the Lahontan system that exhibits subspecies with similar taxonomic challenges to those seen in Xystrosus and Algansea. This source uses it as a comparative example, noting that the difficulty in distinguishing young Xystrosus from Algansea rubescens without gill raker counts parallels situations found in subspecies of Siphateles bicolor.
