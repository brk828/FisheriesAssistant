# Dunaliella

## Summary
Dunaliella is a phytoplankton genus tested in larval rearing experiments. Fabre-Domergue and Bietrix (1905) used it along with larval fishes as prey to successfully rear sole to 55-76 mm over 8 months, though phytoplankton alone rarely extended survival beyond starved controls.
