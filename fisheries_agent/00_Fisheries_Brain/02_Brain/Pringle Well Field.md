# Pringle Well Field

## Summary
The Pringle Well Field is a groundwater pumping location in the Pinal Creek Basin where total dissolved solids increased threefold over 40 years, from 800 mg/L in 1942 to 2,500-2,900 mg/L in 1982-1983. This increase reflects long-term seepage of acidic mining and milling process solutions, with present water being predominantly calcium sulfate type indicating sulfuric acid leach solution impacts.
