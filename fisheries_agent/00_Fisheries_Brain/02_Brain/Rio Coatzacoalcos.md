# Rio Coatzacoalcos

## Summary
The Rio Coatzacoalcos is one of the major river systems in southern Mexico draining to the Atlantic slope/Gulf of Mexico. This source lists it as part of the geographic framework for documenting Mexican fish distributions in the tropical southeastern region.
