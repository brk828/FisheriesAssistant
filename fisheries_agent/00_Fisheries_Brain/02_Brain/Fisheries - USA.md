# Fisheries - USA

## Summary
Fisheries - USA is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service policy that guides the agency's fisheries management programs. This source indicates it was implemented through the "Action for Angling" plan signed on November 25, 1991, working in conjunction with the National Recreational Fisheries Policy to direct field operations and support recreational fishing activities.
