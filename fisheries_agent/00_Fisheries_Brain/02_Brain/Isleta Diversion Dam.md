# Isleta Diversion Dam

## Summary
Isleta Diversion Dam is one of six mainstream dams on the middle Rio Grande that has modified the river's natural flow regime. According to this source, during below-average water years, the river channel may be completely dry from this dam downstream for approximately 179 km (111 mi) for two months or more due to irrigation diversions, representing a major threat to the endangered Rio Grande Silvery Minnow through habitat dewatering.
