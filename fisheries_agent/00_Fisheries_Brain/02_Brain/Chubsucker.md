# Chubsucker

## Summary
Chubsucker is a fish species found in Lawrence Brook, Massachusetts prior to the 1955 rotenone reclamation. This source lists it among the large numbers of warm-water species present in the pre-treatment fish community but provides no additional details about its abundance or characteristics.
