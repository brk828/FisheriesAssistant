# Ft. Huachuca

## Summary
Ft. Huachuca is a U.S. military base in Arizona whose operations have been documented over twenty years to cause devastating effects on the San Pedro River through excessive water pumping. The base proposed an expansion that would bring nearly 5,000 additional military personnel and dependents (contributing to a total regional population increase of approximately 15,000 people), which was challenged in a lawsuit for failing to adequately evaluate and mitigate environmental impacts under the National Environmental Policy Act.
