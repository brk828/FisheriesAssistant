# Pine, Colorado

## Summary
Pine, Colorado is a location near the upper boundary of the 1947 study section on the North Fork of South Platte River at approximately 6,700 feet elevation. The source uses it as a geographic reference point for defining the upstream extent of the research area.

## Context

> This two-year fisheries management study (1947-1948) on the [[North Fork of South Platte River]] in [[Colorado]] evaluated the effectiveness of stocking legal-size [[Rainbow Trout]] and [[Brook Trout]] (referred to as "lochs") in a heavily fished mountain stream.
