# Squawfish

## Summary
Squawfish (genus *Ptychocheilus*, family Cyprinidae) are native North American cyprinids that have become a focal point in fisheries management and conservation biology, particularly in western river systems. These fish have been widely implicated as predators and competitors of economically and ecologically valuable salmonids, though scientific evidence for their impact has been subject to considerable debate and critical review. Squawfish ecology and their interactions with native and introduced fish species remain important considerations in the management of threatened aquatic ecosystems and native fish conservation efforts.
