# Spotted Gar

## Summary
The Spotted Gar (*Lepisosteus oculatus*) is a longnose gar species that inhabits shallow freshwater and estuarine wetlands, particularly those with dense aquatic vegetation such as lizard's tail and spatterdock beds, where its deep-bodied, streamlined morphology allows it to evade capture by seines and navigate complex vegetated habitats. As a documented component of estuarine and wetland fish faunas across the southeastern and central United States, including Louisiana and Illinois, this species serves as an ecological indicator of well-vegetated shallow-water systems and is relevant to understanding fish distributional patterns and habitat requirements in both coastal and inland aquatic environments.
