# Ciénega de Santa Clara

## Summary
The Ciénega de Santa Clara is the largest remaining wetland in the Colorado River Delta, covering approximately 20,250 hectares in the eastern delta region. According to this source, it serves as a major stopover point for migratory birds along the Pacific Flyway and may contain the largest remaining populations of endangered Yuma Clapper Rails and Desert Pupfish. The wetland expanded dramatically after 1977 when agricultural drainage from the Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation District in Arizona began flowing through the M.O.D.E. drain, currently providing 100,000-130,000 acre-feet per year, though this water supply is considered tenuous.
