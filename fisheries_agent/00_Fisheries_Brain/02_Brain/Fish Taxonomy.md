# Fish Taxonomy

## Summary
Fish Taxonomy is the scientific classification and systematic organization of fish species based on morphological, anatomical, and diagnostic characteristics such as meristic counts (fin rays, vertebrae, scale numbers), morphometric measurements, coloration patterns, and osteological features. This foundational discipline enables ichthyologists to identify, describe, and distinguish fish populations, subspecies, and species while establishing evolutionary relationships within fish groups. Fish Taxonomy provides the essential framework for comparative studies, species identification, and informed conservation and fisheries management decisions.
