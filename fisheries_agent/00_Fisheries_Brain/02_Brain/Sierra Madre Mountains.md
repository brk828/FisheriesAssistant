# Sierra Madre Mountains

## Summary
The Sierra Madre Mountains are a geographic feature that influenced the distribution of Gila species through stream capture events. According to Meek, portions of western rivers east of the Sierra Madre Mountains were formerly upper tributaries of eastward-flowing streams before western streams cut back and captured their headwaters along with their fish fauna.
