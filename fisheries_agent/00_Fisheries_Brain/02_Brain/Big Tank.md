# Big Tank

## Summary
Big Tank is the lowermost pond on El Coronado Ranch in West Turkey Creek, Arizona. Under the habitat conservation plan, this pond may be stocked with non-native, non-reproducing game fish for limited private recreational fishing, which could result in incidental take of endangered Yaqui chub and other native species.
