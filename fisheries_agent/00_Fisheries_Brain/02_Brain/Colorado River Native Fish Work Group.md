# Colorado River Native Fish Work Group

## Summary
The Colorado River Native Fish Work Group is a multi-agency collaborative body comprising federal and state agencies that coordinates native fish recovery efforts in the Colorado River system. This source identifies the Work Group as the coordinating entity that developed the razorback sucker rearing facilities around Lake Mohave and oversees the backwater-rearing program as a pilot project and prototype for other potential native fish recovery efforts along the lower Colorado River Basin.
