# Dry Skim Milk

## Summary
Dry skim milk is a feed ingredient containing 35.4% protein, 1.7% fat, 48.7% lactose, 8.1% ash, and 3.9% water that proved highly effective for trout nutrition when combined with fresh meat (especially liver). While it cannot be fed alone indefinitely, mixtures of one-third dry skim milk and two-thirds raw liver with yeast and cod liver oil produced 10% better growth than straight liver for fry, though the chief challenge was its very soluble physical composition unless bound with meat juices or produced in special granular forms.
