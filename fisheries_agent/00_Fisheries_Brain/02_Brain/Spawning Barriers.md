# Spawning Barriers

## Summary
Spawning barriers were recommended as physical barriers in tributary streams during spring thaw to prevent northern pike from spawning and establishing stream populations. Block nets were also recommended at spawning areas during spring thaw to trap spawning pike for elimination, targeting the critical period following ice-out when adult pike move into shallow spawning areas.
