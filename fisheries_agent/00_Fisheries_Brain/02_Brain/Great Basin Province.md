# Great Basin Province

## Summary
The Great Basin Province includes interior drainages east of the Sierra Nevada in California. Key habitats include Lake Tahoe (a large, deep, extraordinarily clear alpine lake), Eagle Lake (containing endemic Eagle Lake Rainbow Trout), Mono Lake (a distinctive alkaline lake), desert springs with endemic pupfishes, and streams supporting Lahontan Cutthroat Trout, Paiute Cutthroat Trout, and other native species.
