# Southerly International Boundary

## Summary
The Southerly International Boundary refers to the international border between the United States and Mexico along the Colorado River. This source indicates it marks the southern extent of the action area evaluated in the 1997 Biological Opinion on Lower Colorado River Operations, with the action area extending from Lake Mead downstream to this boundary.
