# Sociedad Ictiologica Mexicana

## Summary
Sociedad Ictiologica Mexicana (Mexican Ichthyological Society) is a professional scientific organization that Dr. Salvador Contreras Balderas founded and served as its first President from 1986 to 1990. This founding role demonstrates his leadership in establishing professional infrastructure for ichthyological research in Mexico.
