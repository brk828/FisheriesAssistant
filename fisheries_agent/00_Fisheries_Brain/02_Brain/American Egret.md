# American Egret

## Summary
American Egret is a fish-eating bird that was among the species killed by toxaphene poisoning at Tule Lake and Lower Klamath Refuges in 1960. This source reports that dead egrets contained 3.0 ppm toxaphene in whole bird samples, within the range known to cause fatal poisoning.

> This comprehensive 1962 report by the [[Fish and Wildlife Service]] (Circular No. 143) summarizes research findings from 1960 on pesticide effects across wildlife, sport fisheries, and commercial fisheries.
