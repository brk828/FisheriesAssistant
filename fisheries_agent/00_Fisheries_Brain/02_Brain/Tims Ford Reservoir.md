# Tims Ford Reservoir

## Summary
Tims Ford Reservoir is an impoundment on the upper Elk River in Tennessee that has significantly impacted the Boulder Darter population. The reservoir has altered the thermal regime of the tailwaters below the dam from warm to cold water, and water level fluctuations of 2-3 feet caused by power generation operations at the reservoir create unstable habitat conditions that may strand nests or reduce reproductive success by altering the depth and velocity characteristics of nest cavities.

## Context

> The [[Boulder Darter]] (*Etheostoma wapiti*) is a small endangered member of the perch family (Percidae) listed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in 1988. This bottom-dwelling darter survives in only approximately 63 miles (101 kilometers) of the lower [[Elk River]] system in southern Tennessee...
