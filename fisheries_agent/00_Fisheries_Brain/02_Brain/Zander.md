# Zander

## Summary
Zander (Stizostedion lucioperca) is a European fish species closely related to the native North American Walleye. Its proposed introduction to Lake Sakakawea, North Dakota raised serious concerns about hybridization and genetic swamping of native Walleye populations, and would create interjurisdictional problems affecting downstream states including South Dakota, Nebraska, Iowa, Kansas, and Missouri.
