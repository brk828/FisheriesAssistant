# International Commission On Zoological Nomenclature

## Summary
The International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN) is the primary governing body responsible for establishing and maintaining standardized scientific names for animals, including fish species. In ichthyology and fisheries science, the ICZN resolves taxonomic disputes through its plenary powers—such as validating preferred genus names or suppressing older, unused names—to ensure nomenclatural stability and consistency in scientific communication. This regulatory authority is essential for fish conservation and research, as it prevents taxonomic confusion and establishes which scientific names should be used in formal biological literature and management decisions.
