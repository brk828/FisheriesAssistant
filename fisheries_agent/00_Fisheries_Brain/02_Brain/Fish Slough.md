# Fish Slough

## Summary
A fish slough refers to a shallow, marshy waterbody or drainage channel in arid or semi-arid regions that serves as critical habitat for endemic and specialized fish species. These wetland features, often characterized by slow-moving water and dense vegetation, are particularly important in desert ecosystems where they represent isolated aquatic refugia supporting unique assemblages of fish adapted to specific environmental conditions. Fish sloughs have been a focus of conservation efforts by organizations like the Desert Fishes Council due to their high biodiversity value and vulnerability to water depletion and regional development pressures.
