# Bonita Creek

## Summary
Bonita Creek is a 15-mile tributary within the Gila Box Riparian National Conservation Area in southeastern Arizona that serves as critical riparian habitat for fish and wildlife conservation. As part of the broader Gila River system evaluated for designation under the National Wild and Scenic Rivers System, Bonita Creek represents a free-flowing desert lotic ecosystem with significant ecological value for endangered species management and aquatic biodiversity. The creek's riparian corridor and associated aquatic habitats were recognized as warranting federal protection due to their importance for supporting native fish populations and maintaining ecosystem integrity in the arid Southwest.
