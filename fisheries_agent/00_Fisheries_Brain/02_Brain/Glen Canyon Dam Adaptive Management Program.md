# Glen Canyon Dam Adaptive Management Program

## Summary
The Glen Canyon Dam Adaptive Management Program is a collaborative scientific initiative designed to evaluate and optimize dam operations on the Colorado River to benefit endangered fish species, particularly the humpback chub, while maintaining ecosystem integrity. The program employs an adaptive management approach that uses controlled experimental flows and peer-reviewed technical assessments to understand how dam operations—including flow regimes, temperature, and sediment dynamics—affect fish populations and their food bases throughout the river system. By integrating findings from multiple studies and stakeholder input, the program aims to develop scientifically defensible management strategies that balance hydroelectric operations with the conservation needs of native aquatic communities.
