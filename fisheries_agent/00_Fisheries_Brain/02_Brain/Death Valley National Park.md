# Death Valley National Park

## Summary
Death Valley National Park is a conservation area in the western United States that was included as one of the planned site visit destinations for Mexican participants in this wetland management training program. The park represents an example of an operating preserve where trainees would gain hands-on experience in conservation and management practices during 8-12 day visits.
