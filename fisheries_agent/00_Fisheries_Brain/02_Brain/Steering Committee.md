# Steering Committee

## Summary
The Steering Committee is the primary decision-making body for the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program. According to this source, it meets 12 times per year in Las Vegas, with meetings typically running from 8:30/9:30 AM to 4:00/4:30 PM, and its travel costs are covered by in-kind contributions rather than the program budget.
