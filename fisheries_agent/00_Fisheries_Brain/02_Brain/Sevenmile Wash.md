# Sevenmile Wash

## Summary
Sevenmile Wash is a stream system in the Winters and Sedow Allotments on the Globe Ranger District that contains multiple proposed Gila Topminnow reintroduction sites with natural water supplies. This source identifies two high-quality locations in the Winters Allotment and a third location in the Sedow Allotment between Highway 60 and Winters Fence, with planned management including riparian pasture fencing and possible pipeline/drinker installation from an existing windmill.
