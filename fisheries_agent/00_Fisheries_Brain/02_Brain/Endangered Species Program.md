# Endangered Species Program

## Summary
The Endangered Species Program refers to coordinated federal conservation initiatives, primarily administered by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, designed to protect fish species and aquatic populations at risk of extinction through regulatory oversight, habitat management, and interagency consultation. Within the context of fisheries science and aquatic ecology, this program encompasses monitoring declining fish populations, implementing recovery plans, and ensuring compliance with the Endangered Species Act to prevent species loss and maintain ecosystem integrity. These programs typically involve collaboration among government agencies, scientific experts, and stakeholders to develop evidence-based strategies for species recovery and long-term population viability in aquatic systems.
