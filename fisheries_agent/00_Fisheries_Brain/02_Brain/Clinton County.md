# Clinton County

## Summary
An Adirondack county in New York where legislative bait restrictions prohibiting use of minnows or fish as bait in reclaimed and natural brook trout ponds became effective on January 1, 1952. This county was included in the regulatory framework supporting the trout restoration efforts.
