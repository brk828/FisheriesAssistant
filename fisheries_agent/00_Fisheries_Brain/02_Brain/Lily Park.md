# Lily Park

## Summary
Lily Park is an area on the Yampa River in the Upper Colorado River Basin. This study documented the presence of endangered razorback suckers in the Lily Park area for two consecutive years (1979-1980), indicating it serves as habitat for this federally listed endangered species.
