# Paluxy Sand

## Summary
Paluxy Sand is a Cretaceous Trinity Group formation consisting of 120-215 meters of conglomerate, fine sand, and clay. In the Blue River, this formation created shifting sand substrates that prevented establishment of substantial benthic producer communities, resulting in the lowest productivity (1.1 kg O₂/m²/year) and strongly heterotrophic metabolism where the community produced only 39% of what it consumed.
