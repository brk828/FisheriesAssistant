# Rathbone Creek

## Summary
Rathbone Creek is a stream in Cataract, Wisconsin that was treated with 7.5 ppb antimycin A for at least 8 hours over several miles to eliminate minnows and suckers. The treatment demonstrated successful detoxification when the antimycin-laden water reached Cataract Pond downstream, where potassium permanganate was used to neutralize the toxicant and protect rainbow trout in live cages 500 feet below the spillway.
