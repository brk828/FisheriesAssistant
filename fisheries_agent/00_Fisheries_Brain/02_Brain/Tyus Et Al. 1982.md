# Tyus Et Al. 1982

## Summary
Tyus et al. 1982 is a foundational ichthyological study that documented the reproductive failure and functional extirpation of wild Bonytail Chub populations in the Lower Colorado River Basin, establishing critical baseline data on the conservation status of this endemic fish species. This work provided essential evidence that remaining reservoir populations of Bonytail Chub were no longer reproducing in the wild, highlighting the severe ecological impacts of dam construction and habitat alteration on native Colorado River fishes. The study's findings became a cornerstone reference for understanding population decline mechanisms in endangered endemic fish species and informing recovery strategies for Colorado River basin conservation.
