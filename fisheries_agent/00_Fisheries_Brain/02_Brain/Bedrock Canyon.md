# Bedrock Canyon

## Summary
Bedrock canyon reaches with whitewater, deep pools, and eddies provided critical refugia during drought periods and important adult habitat for species like humpback chub. Historical spawning in the lower basin likely occurred in canyon reaches such as Salt River Canyon during March-July, demonstrating the importance of these geomorphic features for native fish life cycles.
