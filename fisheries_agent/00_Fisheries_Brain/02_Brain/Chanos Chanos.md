# Chanos Chanos

## Summary
Chanos chanos, commonly known as milkfish, is an economically important euryhaline teleost fish species capable of thriving in both freshwater and brackish environments, including estuarine systems and coastal aquaculture settings. The species exhibits moderate growth rates of approximately 1-1.36 kg per annum and demonstrates a strong ecological preference for calm, vegetated habitats with minimal water current, making it well-suited for extensive aquaculture systems in still-water environments. As a widely cultivated food fish across tropical and subtropical regions, milkfish represents a significant resource in global fisheries and aquaculture, particularly in areas where its habitat requirements and growth characteristics align with local environmental conditions.
