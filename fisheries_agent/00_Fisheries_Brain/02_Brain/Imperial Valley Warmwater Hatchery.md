# Imperial Valley Warmwater Hatchery

## Summary
Imperial Valley Warmwater Hatchery is a California Department of Fish and Game facility located at Niland where rearing ponds were constructed specifically for growing out razorback suckers to appropriate stocking size. The facility is identified in the management plan as one of the available grow-out habitats to rear razorback fry to a minimum stocking size of 10-15 cm (4-6 inches) before release into the wild.
