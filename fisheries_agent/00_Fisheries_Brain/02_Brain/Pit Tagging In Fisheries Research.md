# Pit Tagging In Fisheries Research

## Summary
Pit tagging in fisheries research refers to the subcutaneous implantation of Passive Integrated Transponder (PIT) tags into fish for individual identification and long-term tracking purposes. This non-invasive marking technique allows researchers to monitor fish movement, survival, growth, and population dynamics without recapture, making it particularly valuable for conservation studies of endangered or threatened species. PIT tags are commonly employed in aquatic ecology and fisheries management to gather detailed behavioral and ecological data that inform management decisions and species recovery efforts.
