# Ramsey Canyon Leopard Frog

## Summary
The Ramsey Canyon Leopard Frog (Rana subaquavocalis) is a newly described species (1993) that exhibits unique underwater calling behavior and inhabits montane streams and artificial impoundments in southeastern Arizona's Huachuca and Swisshelm Mountains. This source documents that the species exists at only two artificial impoundments in Ramsey and Brown canyons with a total adult population of fewer than 100 individuals, having been recently extirpated from a third locality, and was recommended for Federal Candidate Category 2 status in 1994 due to critical extinction danger from its extremely small population size, limited distribution to just two sites, and vulnerability in dynamic streamside habitats.
