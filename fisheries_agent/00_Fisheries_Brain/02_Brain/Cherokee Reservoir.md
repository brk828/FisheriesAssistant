# Cherokee Reservoir

## Summary
Cherokee Reservoir is a Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) storage reservoir characterized by significant water level fluctuations that substantially influence fish habitat conditions and distribution patterns. As a mainstream impoundment in the TVA system, Cherokee Reservoir experiences dynamic thermal stratification and dissolved oxygen dynamics that directly affect the spatial distribution and movement patterns of fish species, particularly largemouth bass, making it an important study site for understanding how reservoir management and limnological conditions shape fish populations and conservation outcomes in regulated river systems.
