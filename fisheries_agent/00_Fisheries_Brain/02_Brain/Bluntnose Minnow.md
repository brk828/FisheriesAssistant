# Bluntnose Minnow

## Summary
The Bluntnose Minnow is a small freshwater cyprinid fish commonly found in North American streams and headwater ecosystems, often serving as an important forage species and indicator organism in aquatic ecological studies. This species is frequently documented in ichthyological surveys and life history research, including parasitological studies, due to its abundance in diverse freshwater habitats and its role in food webs. As a hardy, adaptable minnow species, it has been utilized in both field investigations of stream community structure and captive research settings to understand fish physiology, behavior, and ecological relationships in freshwater systems.
