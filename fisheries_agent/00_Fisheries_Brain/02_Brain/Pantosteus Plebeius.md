# Pantosteus Plebeius

## Summary
Pantosteus plebeius, commonly known as the bluehead sucker, is a North American freshwater sucker species that serves as a morphological reference point in catostomid taxonomy and systematics. This species is significant in ichthyological research for comparative anatomical studies, as it exhibits distinctive characteristics in body form, caudal peduncle morphology, and lip structure that help distinguish it from congeners and related genera. P. plebeius has been documented across various drainage systems in western North America and remains important for understanding catostomid distribution patterns and evolutionary relationships within the sucker family.
