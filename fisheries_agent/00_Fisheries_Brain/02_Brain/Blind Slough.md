# Blind Slough

## Summary
Blind Slough is a release site in Oregon located approximately 40 km upstream from the Columbia River mouth where hatchery yearling Chinook salmon were acclimated for one week before release. The study examined 527,340 hatchery yearlings released from this location during 2002-2005, with juveniles migrating rapidly through the estuary (estimated transit time <2 days) to reach the ocean.
