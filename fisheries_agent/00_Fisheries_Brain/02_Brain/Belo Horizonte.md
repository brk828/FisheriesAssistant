# Belo Horizonte

## Summary
Belo Horizonte is a city in Brazil where Cypretta kawatai is presumed to have originated from freshwater ponds and ditches. This location represents the known natural geographic distribution of the species.

## Context

> This paper formally validates the new species **Cypretta kawatai** Sohn and Kornicker, 1972, a freshwater ostracod (Crustacea) from Brazil.
