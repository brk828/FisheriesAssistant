# Grand Junction

## Summary
Grand Junction is a geographic sampling location on the Colorado River mainstem in western Colorado that served as a key study site for monitoring wild Razorback Sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*) populations during the 1990s mitochondrial DNA research program. The area, which includes associated lentic habitats such as Highline Lake and Etter Pond, represented the upper mainstem Colorado River section within the broader longitudinal and altitudinal gradient of population sampling across the Colorado River Basin. This location was strategically important for characterizing the genetic diversity and population structure of Razorback Sucker in both riverine and reservoir environments as part of recovery and conservation efforts for this endangered species.
