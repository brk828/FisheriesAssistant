# Sonoran Desert Aquatic Ecosystems

## Summary
Sonoran Desert Aquatic Ecosystems refers to the interconnected freshwater systems found within the Sonoran Desert region, primarily centered around rivers such as the Gila River and their associated riparian habitats, which support specialized and often endemic fish communities adapted to arid conditions. These ecosystems are characterized by extreme environmental variability, including seasonal flow patterns, temperature fluctuations, and limited water availability, creating unique conservation challenges for native fish species that have evolved in isolation within these desert waterways. The protection and restoration of these aquatic systems have been central concerns in southwestern United States fisheries management, particularly regarding threatened and endangered species endemic to the Sonoran Desert region.
