# American Coots

## Summary
American Coots (Fulica americana) are waterbirds that have been documented as prey items for large adult Colorado Squawfish. Historical observations include Colorado Squawfish consuming newly hatched American Coots, demonstrating that large adults are not limited to fish in their diet.
