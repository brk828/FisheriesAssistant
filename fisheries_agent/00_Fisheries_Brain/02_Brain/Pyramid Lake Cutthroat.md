# Pyramid Lake Cutthroat

## Summary
Pyramid Lake Cutthroat is an introduced cutthroat trout subspecies in Utah. This source lists it among the introduced salmonids, distinguishing it from the three native cutthroat subspecies (Lake Bonneville, Colorado River, and Yellowstone cutthroats) found in Utah.
