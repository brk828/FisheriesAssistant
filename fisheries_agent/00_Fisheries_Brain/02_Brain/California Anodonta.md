# California Anodonta

## Summary
Anodonta californiensis, a large freshwater mussel (approximately 80 mm) with a thin shell that requires native fish as hosts for its glochidial larvae. This source indicates the species was extirpated from most Arizona drainages due to elimination of native fish hosts (likely Gila species), demonstrating the critical ecological relationship between the mussel and native fish populations.
