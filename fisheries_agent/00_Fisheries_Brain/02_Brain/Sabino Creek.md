# Sabino Creek

## Summary
Sabino Creek is a stream system in the Santa Catalina Mountains of southeastern Arizona that serves as an important habitat for native fish species, particularly the imperiled Gila Chub, making it a focal point for regional conservation efforts coordinated by interagency working groups. The creek's ecological significance lies in its role as a refugium for native cyprinids, though its populations face threats from invasive species such as Green Sunfish that compete with and prey upon native fish. Management of Sabino Creek exemplifies the collaborative approach to aquatic conservation in the arid Southwest, where limited water resources and multiple land management agencies require coordinated native fish restoration planning.
