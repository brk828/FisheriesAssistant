# Palythoa ignota

## Summary
Palythoa ignota is a soft coral (zoanthid) that characterizes Zone IV (0 to -3 feet) of the Puerto Peñasco intertidal, along with the brown sponge Geodia mesotriaena. It is light to dark brown with very short tentacles that fold when exposed, forms colonies in tide pools, and covers large areas of the lower intertidal sandstone reef; it also extends into higher tide pools with permanent water.
