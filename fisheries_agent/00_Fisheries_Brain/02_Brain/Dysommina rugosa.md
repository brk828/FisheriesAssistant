# Dysommina rugosa

## Summary
Dysommina rugosa is a deep-sea eel species from the North Atlantic, first collected in 1886 off southern Georgia at 352 fathoms depth. This elongate, compressed fish has distinctive features including a long snout, 4-5 large widely-spaced vomerine teeth, skin-covered eyes, and the anus positioned far forward of mid-length, with uniform light tan coloration in preserved specimens.
