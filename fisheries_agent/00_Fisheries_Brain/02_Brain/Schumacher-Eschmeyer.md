# Schumacher-Eschmeyer

## Summary
The Schumacher-Eschmeyer estimator is a regression-based multi-visit mark-recapture method calculated as N̂ = Σ(Mₖ²nₖ)/Σ(Mₖmₖ). This study develops a novel bias-adjusted version N̂ = Σ[(Mₖ+1)²(nₖ+1)]/Σ[Mₖ(mₖ+1)] - 2 that exhibits lower variability at small sample sizes and greater robustness to assumption violations compared to adjusted Schnabel, making it particularly valuable for low-recapture scenarios common in threatened species studies like Atlantic Sturgeon.
