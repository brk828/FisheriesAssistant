# Iron Blue Dun

## Summary
Iron Blue Dun is the common name for Paraleptophlebia adoptiva, derived from the cloudy slate blue color of the wings in the dun (first winged) stage. This source describes the dun stage as having a dull grayish-mahogany colored body about 3/8-inch long with three slender, smoky-amber tails and slate blue wings. The dun stage lasts 24 hours in warm, dry weather but may persist for several days in cool, rainy conditions before molting into the spinner stage.
