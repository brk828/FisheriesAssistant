# O'Shaughnessy and Hewitt

## Summary
O'Shaughnessy and Hewitt were researchers who reported that nitrification begins at approximately 7% oxygen saturation in activated sludge effluent. This study found similar results, with the border between nitrification and denitrification occurring around 7% saturation, supporting their earlier findings.
