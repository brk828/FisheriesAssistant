# Poeciliopsis

## Summary
Poeciliopsis is a genus of small livebearing fish native to Mexico and the southwestern United States, particularly important in conservation biology due to several species being endemic to isolated desert spring systems that face significant threats from habitat degradation and water depletion. These fish have been the subject of recovery and management efforts, as documented in fisheries conservation consultation records, and their isolation in fragmented aquatic ecosystems makes them valuable models for studying evolutionary adaptation and the impacts of habitat fragmentation on biodiversity. The genus has also been utilized in molecular and genetic research to understand fish population dynamics and evolutionary relationships within the Poeciliidae family.
