# George Whittell Wildlife Preserve

## Summary
The George Whittell Wildlife Preserve is a protected area owned by Defenders of Wildlife that encompasses most of the perennial stream sections of Aravaipa Creek above and below the Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness. This preserve provides important habitat protection for spikedace, helping to maintain one of only three remaining population centers for the species, though the area is still affected by upstream groundwater pumping.
