# Thomomys bottae

## Summary
Thomomys bottae (Botta's pocket gopher) is represented by one burned mandible at La Lomita Pequeña, suggesting consumption though pocket gophers were likely agricultural pests. This species is common along riparian habitats in desert regions and grasslands.
