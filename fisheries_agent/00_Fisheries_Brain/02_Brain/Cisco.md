# Cisco

## Summary
Cisco (*Coregonus artedii*) is a commercially and recreationally important freshwater whitefish species native to North American lakes, particularly the Great Lakes and associated water bodies, that has experienced significant population declines due to overharvesting, invasive species competition, and habitat degradation. As a deepwater coldwater specialist, the cisco serves as an indicator species for ecosystem health and was historically exploited through gill-net fisheries, making it a valuable subject for population dynamics and fisheries management studies. The species' vulnerability to exploitation and its ecological role as both a predator and prey in lacustrine food webs make it central to understanding freshwater fish conservation biology and the impacts of human-induced environmental change in Great Lakes ecosystems.
