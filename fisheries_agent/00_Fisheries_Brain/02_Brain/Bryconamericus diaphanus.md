# Bryconamericus diaphanus

## Summary
Bryconamericus diaphanus is a previously described characid fish species originally named by Cope. The authors note that their newly described species Bryconamericus novae from the Rio Tocantins basin may possibly be identical to B. diaphanus, though they maintain them as separate pending further study.
