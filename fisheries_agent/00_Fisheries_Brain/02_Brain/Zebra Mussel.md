# Zebra Mussel

## Summary
The zebra mussel (Dreissena polymorpha) is an invasive freshwater bivalve that poses significant threats to aquatic ecosystems and fisheries management in North America, particularly in western reservoirs and river systems. As a prolific colonizer of hard substrates, zebra mussels can form dense populations that clog water intake structures, alter native benthic communities, and compete with native organisms for food and space, thereby affecting habitat quality for economically and ecologically important fish species. Their presence in critical fish management areas like the Lower Colorado River reservoirs necessitates monitoring and control efforts as part of integrated ecosystem management strategies for native fish conservation.
