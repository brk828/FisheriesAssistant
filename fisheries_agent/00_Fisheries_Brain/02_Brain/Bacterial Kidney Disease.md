# Bacterial Kidney Disease

## Summary
Bacterial Kidney Disease (BKD) is an infectious disease in fish caused by the bacterium Chondrococcus columnaris, characterized by systemic infection that affects kidney tissue and overall fish health, particularly impacting cultured and wild salmon populations. The disease is of significant concern in aquaculture and fisheries management, as infected fish experience reduced survival rates and production losses, making understanding bacterial persistence in various water conditions critical for hatchery operations and conservation efforts. Research on BKD transmission and bacterial survival in different water environments has been essential for developing management protocols at fish culture facilities aimed at protecting both hatchery stocks and naturally reproducing populations.
