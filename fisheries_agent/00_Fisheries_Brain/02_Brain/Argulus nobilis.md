# Argulus nobilis

## Summary
Argulus nobilis is a previously described species of parasitic crustacean that shares similar respiratory area shape with the newly described Argulus meehani. It is distinguished from A. meehani by having more than 10 sclerotized rod elements in its suckers, compared to fewer than 10 in A. meehani.
