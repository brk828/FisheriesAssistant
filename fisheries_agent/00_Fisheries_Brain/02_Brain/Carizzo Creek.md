# Carizzo Creek

## Summary
Carizzo Creek is a stream in east-central Arizona with a mean flow of approximately 60 m³/min based on 12 years of USGS records. The upper reaches may support jumping mice (Zapus sp.) and beaver populations that could be especially disturbed by construction activities.
