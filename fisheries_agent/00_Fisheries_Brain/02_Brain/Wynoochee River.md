# Wynoochee River

## Summary
Wynoochee River in Washington experienced elimination of trout food and cover due to silt from gravel washing operations. Even at relatively low turbidities of 91-102 ppm, the siltation was sufficient to destroy the habitat quality needed to support trout populations.
