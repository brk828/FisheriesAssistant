# Lerma Basin

## Summary
The Lerma Basin is the upper portion of the Rio Lerma-Santiago system, located above Salto de Juanacatlan. When considered separately from the larger basin, it shows an exceptional 66% endemism rate, making it the most important center of fish endemism in México, with its isolation in the tropical highland Mesa Central being particularly important for diversification.
