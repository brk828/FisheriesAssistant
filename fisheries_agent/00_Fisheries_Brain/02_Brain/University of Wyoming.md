# University of Wyoming

## Summary
The University of Wyoming in Laramie hosted the Inland Commercial Fisheries Workshop on April 15-16, 1971. This workshop brought together fisheries researchers, state game and fish personnel, and National Marine Fisheries Service representatives from across the western United States to discuss commercial fishery research and development in inland reservoirs.
