# San Pedro River Conservation Area

## Summary
San Pedro River Conservation Area is a Bureau of Land Management area in the planning stage (as of 1986). This source identifies it as a location at risk from the spread of alien Desert Pupfish populations through flooding from upstream sites like Fort Huachuca, which could jeopardize recovery efforts for the native subspecies.
