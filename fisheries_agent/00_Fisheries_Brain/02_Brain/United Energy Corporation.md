# United Energy Corporation

## Summary
United Energy Corporation (UEC) was a company that requested permits to import and possess eight exotic fish species (three Chinese carp species and five tilapia species) for aquaculture and research purposes at a facility near Yermo, California in 1984. The California Department of Fish and Game supported their proposal conditionally based on the facility's remote location and stringent security measures, though they emphasized this approval should not set precedent for general aquaculture use of these species.
