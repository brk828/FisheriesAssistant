# Ojo Caliente Springs

## Summary
Ojo Caliente Springs is a spring system in Socorro County, New Mexico, that supports populations of Fort Harmony Tryonia. The springs are located on private land adjacent to state land and have been suggested as a potential transplant site for the endangered Socorro Isopod due to their excellent habitat conditions.
