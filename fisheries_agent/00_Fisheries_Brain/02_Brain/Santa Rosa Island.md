# Santa Rosa Island

## Summary
Santa Rosa Island is a California Channel Island that provides important zooarchaeological evidence for temperature changes through midden analysis. Indian middens dated to about 7,000 years B.P. contained primarily Red Abalone, a cold-water indicator suggesting persistence of glacial cooling, while later middens show a shift to warm-water Black Abalone, documenting postglacial warming.
