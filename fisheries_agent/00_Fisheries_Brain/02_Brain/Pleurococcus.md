# Pleurococcus

## Summary
Pleurococcus is a green alga that was part of the characteristic community in highly radioactive seepage from Disposal Pit IV, occurring at densities of 180,000/ml. This species, along with Chlorella, Oscillatoria, and Lyngbya, formed an assemblage common in organic waste degradation that appeared resistant to both high radiation (250-400 mr/hr) and high salt concentrations.
