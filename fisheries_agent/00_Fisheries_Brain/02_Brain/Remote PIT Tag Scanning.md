# Remote PIT Tag Scanning

## Summary
Remote PIT tag scanning involves permanent automated scanners (2-3 per pond) that continuously detect tagged fish movements in the Imperial Ponds backwaters. Weirs with attached antennas were installed in Ponds 4, 5, and 6, with data downloaded every 2-4 weeks to monitor razorback sucker and bonytail populations.
