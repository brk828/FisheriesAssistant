# Juniper-Cross Mountain

## Summary
Juniper-Cross Mountain refers to water rights on the Yampa River that were the focus of acquisition efforts by the Colorado River Recovery Implementation Program to protect instream flows for endangered fish. A public hearing in Craig, Colorado in January 1989 revealed divided local opinion, with the City of Craig supporting the Recovery Program but requesting construction of a 100,000 acre-foot reservoir on a Yampa tributary and preferring long-term lease rather than outright purchase of the Juniper-Cross water rights for instream flow purposes.
