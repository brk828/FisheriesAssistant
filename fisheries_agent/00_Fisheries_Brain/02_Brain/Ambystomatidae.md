# Ambystomatidae

## Summary
Ambystomatidae is the family of mole salamanders, one of the amphibian families represented in the herpetofauna of the four Arizona-New Mexico reservoir sites. This source includes members of this family in its comprehensive assessment of 87 species across the study areas.
