# Sailfin Molly

## Summary
The Sailfin Molly (*Mollienesia latipinna*) is a livebearing fish native to South Texas and Mexico that occupies a detritivorous ecological niche in freshwater and brackish aquatic systems, feeding primarily on fibrous plant detritus and organic materials. In the context of introduced species ecology, this molly is notable as a sympatric competitor in invaded habitats where it can establish populations alongside native fishes, and its dietary habits and reproductive capacity make it a significant component of trophic guild structure in modified aquatic ecosystems. As both a common aquarium fish and an invasive species in some regions, understanding its trophic role and ecological interactions is relevant to conservation efforts and fisheries management in systems it colonizes.
