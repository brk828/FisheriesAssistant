# Woods Canyon Lake

## Summary
Woods Canyon Lake is a lentic fishery in Arizona's Region I that was subject to creel census monitoring by the Arizona Game and Fish Department during the 1960s to assess recreational fishing productivity and trout population dynamics. The lake supported rainbow trout populations with documented catch rates of approximately 0.31 fish per man-hour and average trout lengths around 10.0 inches, making it part of a suite of monitored lakes used to evaluate factors influencing trout production and the effectiveness of fisheries management practices. As one of several Arizona lakes affected by anthropogenic stressors such as water drawdown, Woods Canyon Lake represented a system where researchers investigated the relationship between habitat conditions, fish growth, and angler success to inform conservation and management strategies.
