# Gila Robusta

## Summary
Gila robusta, commonly known as the roundtail chub, is a native cyprinid fish species of southwestern North American rivers, particularly the Colorado River basin, that has experienced significant population declines and was recommended for endangered species candidate status due to habitat loss and competition from non-native species. This species is an important component of native fish assemblages in the region and has been the subject of conservation monitoring and management efforts throughout its range. Gila robusta represents a key indicator of ecosystem health in arid riverine systems and remains a priority for fisheries conservation in the American Southwest.
