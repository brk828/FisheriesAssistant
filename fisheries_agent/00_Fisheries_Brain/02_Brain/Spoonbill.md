# Spoonbill

## Summary
Spoonbill (paddlefish) is a large filter-feeding fish that was captured incidentally during the 11-month gill net and trammel net sampling period in Fort Gibson Reservoir. The total catch included 60 pounds of spoonbill, representing a minor component of the overall catch compared to more abundant species like carp, catfish, and buffalo.
