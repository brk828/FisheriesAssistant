# Eagle-Picher Industries v. EPA

## Summary
Eagle-Picher Industries v. EPA (759 F.2d 905, D.C. Cir. 1985) was a landmark court decision that dramatically expanded CERCLA liability for the mining industry. The court ruled that mining wastes containing any substances listed under referenced environmental statutes qualified as 'hazardous substances' under CERCLA, rejecting the industry's argument that the RCRA Bevill Amendment exclusion should apply to CERCLA's hazardous substance definition, thereby exposing mining operations to liability for releases of naturally occurring materials present in virtually all mining wastes.
