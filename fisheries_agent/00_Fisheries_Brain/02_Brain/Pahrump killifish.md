# Pahrump Killifish

## Summary
The Pahrump Killifish (*Empetrichthys latos*) is a small livebearing fish endemic to isolated spring systems in Pahrump Valley, Nevada, and represents a critically endangered species of significant conservation concern due to its extremely restricted range and vulnerability to habitat loss and groundwater depletion. As a member of the Goodeidae family, it is distinct from the more well-known pupfishes in the region and serves as an important indicator species for the ecological health of the unique desert spring ecosystems of the Great Basin. Its survival depends on protecting the specialized aquatic habitats that sustain it, making it a focal point for desert aquatic conservation efforts in the southwestern United States.
