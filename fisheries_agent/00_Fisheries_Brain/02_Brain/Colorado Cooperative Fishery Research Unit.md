# Colorado Cooperative Fishery Research Unit

## Summary
Colorado Cooperative Fishery Research Unit conducted gill net sampling in Twin Lakes that provided species composition verification for the hydroacoustic survey. Their sampling confirmed that 95% of fish caught below the thermocline were Lake Trout, with 100% Lake Trout in midwater sets, validating the acoustic population estimates.
