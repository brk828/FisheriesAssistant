# Argulus laticauda

## Summary
Argulus laticauda is a previously described species of parasitic crustacean that was initially confused with the newly described Argulus chesapeakensis. It is distinguished from A. chesapeakensis by having cuspidate (rather than non-cuspidate) spines on the basal plate of the second maxilla and spatulate (rather than digitate) antennal spines.
