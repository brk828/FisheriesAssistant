# Ictiobus Cyprinellus

## Summary
Ictiobus cyprinellus, commonly known as the bigmouth buffalo, is a large cyprinid fish native to North American freshwater systems that has been the subject of aquacultural and nutritional studies due to its potential as a food fish and its ecological role in river ecosystems. This species is of interest to fisheries scientists and conservation biologists because of its presence in various aquatic habitats, including introduced populations in non-native regions, and its nutritional value as a cultivated food source. Research on bigmouth buffalo has examined both its husbandry in pond-rearing operations and its ecological implications in altered river systems like the Colorado River.
