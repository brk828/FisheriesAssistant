# Tina J. Ayers

## Summary
Tina J. Ayers is a researcher affiliated with Northern Arizona University who co-authored this 1995 proposal for continuing riparian and wetland plant assemblage monitoring in the Colorado River corridor downstream of Glen Canyon Dam. She collaborated with Kearsley on developing aerial photography mapping and ground-truthing methods during prior Interim Flows monitoring.
