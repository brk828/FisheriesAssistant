# WWF

## Summary
WWF (World Wide Fund for Nature) provided support for SSC's Action Planning Programme, which was launched in 1986 with backing from WWF and the Sultanate of Oman. The Action Planning programme represents one of SSC's most important activities, with 26 Action Plans published by March 1994. WWF's support enabled this major initiative to review conservation needs, identify priorities, and recommend actions for species' long-term survival.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium.
