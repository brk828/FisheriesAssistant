# Robertson Creek Channel

## Summary
Robertson Creek Channel is another spawning channel used for operational comparison in this study. This source notes that Robertson Creek operated at 100 c.f.s., substantially higher than the Abernathy Channel's 1.2 c.f.s. average discharge.
