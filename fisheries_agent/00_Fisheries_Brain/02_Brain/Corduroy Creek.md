# Corduroy Creek

## Summary
Corduroy Creek is a stream in the White Mountains where 26 Apache Trout were collected in 1988. The population exhibited a relatively moderate male-biased sex ratio of 1.6:1 (16 males to 10 females), which was closer to balanced compared to many other surveyed streams.
