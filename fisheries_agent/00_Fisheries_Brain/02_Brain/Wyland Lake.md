# Wyland Lake

## Summary
Wyland Lake is an example of a Type I pseudo-metalimnetic oxygen maximum lake, where a temporary oxygen maximum of 19.3 mg/L developed at 2 meters depth on July 11, 1955. This type of maximum forms during June-July calm periods under secondary thermoclines in small, protected lakes but disappears when wind-induced circulation incorporates the layer into the epilimnion.
