# Folsom Reservoir

## Summary
Folsom Reservoir is a California water body that was not chemically treated prior to impoundment but still produced outstanding largemouth bass fishing in its early years. This example was cited in the report to question the necessity of pre-impoundment chemical treatments of drainages above new reservoirs.
