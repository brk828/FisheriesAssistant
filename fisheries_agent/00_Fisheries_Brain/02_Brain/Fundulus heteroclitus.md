# Fundulus Heteroclitus

## Summary
Fundulus heteroclitus, commonly known as the mummichog or killifish, is a small euryhaline fish species native to Atlantic coastal regions of North America that has become a model organism in fish physiology and ecology research. This species is valued in scientific studies for its exceptional osmoregulatory capabilities, allowing it to thrive across a wide salinity range from freshwater to hypersaline environments, making it ideal for investigating physiological adaptations to environmental stress. Its widespread use in laboratory and field research has contributed significantly to understanding fish immunity, seasonal physiological changes, and disease resistance mechanisms in aquatic systems.
