# Argulus meehani

## Summary
Argulus meehani is a newly described species of parasitic crustacean (branchiuran) found on Florida gar in freshwater habitats of Everglades National Park, Florida. It is distinguished from related species by its distinctive respiratory area shape, the absence of scales on the basal plate of the second maxilla, fewer than 10 sclerotized rod elements in its suckers, and the presence of hyaline areas on the second antenna. The species was described from 92 specimens collected by M. C. Kolipinski from Royal Palm Pond.
