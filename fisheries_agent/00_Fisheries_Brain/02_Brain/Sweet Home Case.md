# Sweet Home Case

## Summary
The Sweet Home Case is a legal case that upheld the interpretation that "taking" under the Endangered Species Act includes significant habitat modification that injures species by impairing essential behaviors. This source cites this case as legal precedent supporting the broad definition of "take" under ESA Section 7.
