# Tunnel Creek

## Summary
Tunnel Creek is a stream entering Lake Tahoe where Piute sculpin spawning areas have been documented. Like Griff Creek, the mouth of Tunnel Creek serves as one of the identified spawning locations for sculpins in the lake.
