# Doudoroff Method

## Summary
The Doudoroff Method refers to standardized bioassay protocols used in this study for testing fish toxicity to oil-refinery effluents. The method involved holding fish for a minimum of 10 days before testing when practical, conducting exploratory bioassays to determine concentration ranges, then running full-scale bioassays with 10 fish per concentration in two replications, with survival counts recorded at 1, 6, 12, 24, 48, and 96 hours.
