# Adirondack

## Summary
The Adirondack region refers to an upland area in New York State where Yellow Perch has become problematic in ponds and streams following introduction. In these Adirondack waters, perch compete with native Trout populations and are considered a nuisance species.

## Context

> The [[Yellow Perch]] (*Perca flavescens*) is one of the most widely distributed food and game fishes in [[New York State]], eliciting mixed reactions from anglers. While valued as a pan fish for its palatability and ease of capture, it can become a nuisance species, particularly in [[Adirondack]]...
