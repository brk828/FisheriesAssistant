# Central Valley Project

## Summary
The Central Valley Project is a major water development infrastructure system constructed between 1941-1982 that fundamentally altered flow regimes in California rivers. This source identifies it, along with the State Water Project, as one of the massive water development projects that placed dams and reservoirs on every major stream, contributing significantly to the decline of native fish populations.
