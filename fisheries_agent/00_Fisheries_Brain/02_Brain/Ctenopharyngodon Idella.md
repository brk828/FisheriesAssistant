# Ctenopharyngodon Idella

## Summary
Ctenopharyngodon idella, commonly known as the grass carp, is a large cyprinid fish native to East Asian freshwater systems that has been widely introduced globally for aquatic vegetation control in fisheries and aquaculture applications. As an herbivorous species with high plant consumption rates, it plays a significant ecological role in both native and introduced ranges, though its invasive potential in non-native ecosystems poses considerable conservation concerns. The species is also notable as a host and vector for parasitic infections, including Asian fish tapeworms, which can affect both the fish itself and human consumers in regions where grass carp is cultured for food.
