# Fowler (1917)

## Summary
Fowler (1917) documented spawning behavior for several related ictalurid species including brown bullhead (Ameiurus nebulosus), white catfish (A. catus), yellow bullhead (A. natalis), and madtom (Schilbeodes insignis). This comparative work on related catfish species provided context for understanding channel catfish reproductive biology.
