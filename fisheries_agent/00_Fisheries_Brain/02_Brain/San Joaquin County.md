# San Joaquin County

## Summary
San Joaquin County was the top-ranked catfish county in California in 1960, with 6,333 fish reported from 82 reports. It is located in a region where catfish fishing was particularly productive, contributing to the nearly 50% of catfish caught in Region 2.
