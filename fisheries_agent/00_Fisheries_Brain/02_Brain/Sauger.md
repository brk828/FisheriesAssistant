# Sauger

## Summary
Sauger (*Stizostedion canadense*) is a percid fish species native to North American freshwater systems, particularly large river systems like the Mississippi River, that serves as an important food fish for both commercial and recreational fisheries. Ecologically, sauger occupy similar niches to walleye in larger, more turbid river environments and are characterized by their predatory behavior on smaller fish species. Research on sauger populations, including studies on their age, growth, and length-weight relationships, has been essential for understanding fish community dynamics and informing management strategies in large river ecosystems.
