# Lahontan Basin

## Summary
The Lahontan Basin is a hydrographic region in the Great Basin of northwestern Nevada and northeastern California that encompasses the historical watershed of Lake Lahontan and its associated river systems, including the Truckee and Carson Rivers. This basin is significant in fish science and conservation as the native habitat for endemic and sensitive fish species, most notably the cui-ui (Chasmistes cujus) and Lahontan cutthroat trout, making it a focal area for fisheries management and species recovery programs conducted through facilities like the Lahontan National Fish Hatchery. The basin's altered hydrology due to water diversions and damming has necessitated intensive conservation and hatchery-based recovery efforts to sustain populations of these species of concern.
