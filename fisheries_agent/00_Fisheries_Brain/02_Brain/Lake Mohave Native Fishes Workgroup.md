# Lake Mohave Native Fishes Workgroup

## Summary
The Lake Mohave Native Fishes Workgroup is one of the management jurisdictions overseeing the razorback sucker repatriation program in Lake Mohave on the Lower Colorado River. This workgroup is part of the collaborative management structure that includes the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program, and the Bureau of Reclamation, working together to implement adaptive management strategies for the endangered razorback sucker population.
