# Put-And-Take Fishery

## Summary
A put-and-take fishery is a management strategy in which hatchery-reared fish, typically fingerlings or larger stock, are regularly stocked into water bodies to provide immediate recreational fishing opportunities rather than relying on natural reproduction or long-term population establishment. This approach prioritizes short-term angler satisfaction and catch rates by supplementing populations with cultured fish that are expected to be harvested within a season, making it particularly useful for waters with limited natural recruitment or in heavily fished systems where wild populations cannot sustain harvest pressure. Put-and-take fisheries are commonly evaluated through creel censuses and catch-per-unit-effort metrics to assess stocking effectiveness and optimize management protocols.
