# Escalante

## Summary
Escalante is a flooded bottomland habitat site in the Upper Colorado River Basin identified as important for endangered fish recovery. The recovery plan calls for excluding nonnative fishes from razorback sucker habitat at this location between 1994-1996, recognizing its value as potential habitat for razorback sucker and Colorado squawfish.
