# Fort Benning, Georgia

## Summary
Fort Benning, Georgia was the location of Victory Lake, a 26-acre water body where antimycin A was successfully used for selective fish control. The treatment removed scale fish while preserving channel catfish and bullhead populations, with post-treatment netting confirming healthy catfish up to 4.5 pounds.
