# Red River

## Summary
The Red River refers to a major river drainage system in North America that serves as an important geographic reference point for fish distribution, conservation, and management in ichthyology and fisheries science. The Red River drainage spans multiple states and regions, historically supporting diverse native fish fauna including species like Notropis blennius (blenny shiner), and represents a significant watershed for both baseline biodiversity documentation and conservation priorities under frameworks like the Endangered Species Act. As an interjurisdictional river system managed by multiple agencies including the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, the Red River drainage is relevant to regional fisheries management strategies, species recovery programs, and the identification of refugial populations for threatened and endangered aquatic species.
