# Trout Creek

## Summary
Trout Creek refers to a small montane stream system, exemplified by a third-order stream in central Colorado, that serves as a model study site for investigating how environmental disturbances—particularly fluctuating water discharge and drought conditions—affect fish populations and aquatic macroinvertebrate communities. As a representative headwater ecosystem, Trout Creek provides important insights into stream ecology, fish habitat requirements, and the resilience of cold-water fish species to hydrological stress, making it a valuable reference system for understanding trout production dynamics and conservation needs in montane watersheds.
