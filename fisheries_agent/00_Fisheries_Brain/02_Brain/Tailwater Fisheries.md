# Tailwater Fisheries

## Summary
Glen Canyon Dam creates hypolimnetic releases of 7-10°C water that rarely warm to 16°C even 400+ km downstream at Lake Mead. These cold tailwater conditions render 300+ km of Marble and Grand canyons unsuitable for native warmwater species reproduction, though the source does not specifically discuss tailwater fisheries management for sport species.
