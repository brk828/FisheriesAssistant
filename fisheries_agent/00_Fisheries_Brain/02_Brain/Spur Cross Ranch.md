# Spur Cross Ranch

## Summary
Spur Cross Ranch is an approximately 2,154-acre property in Maricopa County, Arizona, adjacent to the southern boundary of the Tonto National Forest. A 1996 biological evaluation found that the site contains diverse habitats including upland desertscrub, xeroriparian areas, riparian woodland, and wetlands, with a planned development expected to directly impact about 726 acres (34%) while preserving 1,145 acres (53%) as natural open space.
