# Redmond Flat

## Summary
Redmond Flat is a location in Arizona where a Bald Eagle nest was documented upstream of Roosevelt Diversion Dam. Skulls of large Flathead Catfish were found in this eagle nest, providing evidence that eagles were foraging on these large fish from the river system.
