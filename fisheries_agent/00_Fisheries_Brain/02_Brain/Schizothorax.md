# Schizothorax

## Summary
Schizothorax is a genus of cyprinid fishes (snow trout) well-represented in Afghanistan with 13 species documented. This source notes these cold-adapted fish dominate upper reaches of rivers, and mentions that some endemic species may prove synonymous with more widespread species pending systematic revision, with specific examples from the Sistan basin.
