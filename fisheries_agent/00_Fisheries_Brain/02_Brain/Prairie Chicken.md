# Prairie Chicken

## Summary
The Greater Prairie Chicken is a grouse species that was part of artificial propagation studies at the Denver Wildlife Research Center in 1964. The source reports that from 41 fertile eggs, 24 chicks hatched and 17 were successfully reared to maturity, with these captive-bred birds being used for pesticide toxicity testing as part of research into effects of insecticides on wildlife.
