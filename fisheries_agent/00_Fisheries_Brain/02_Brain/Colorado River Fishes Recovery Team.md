# Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team

## Summary
The Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team is an inter-agency coordination body established to develop and implement recovery plans for endangered and endemic fish species of the Colorado River Basin, particularly focusing on Lower Basin populations of species such as razorback suckers, Colorado squawfish, bonytail chub, and humpback chub. Comprising representatives from state and federal wildlife agencies, the team prioritizes conservation tasks and consolidates recovery strategies across multiple species and geographic regions through collaborative planning meetings and administrative oversight. The team's work represents a multi-jurisdictional approach to managing the recovery of native "big river" fishes threatened by habitat alteration and other anthropogenic pressures in the Colorado River system.
