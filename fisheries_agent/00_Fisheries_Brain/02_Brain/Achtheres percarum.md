# Achtheres percarum

## Summary
Achtheres percarum is a parasitic copepod that typically infests perch (Perca fluviatilis), usually attaching in the buccal cavity where it can cause considerable inflammation. This source documents new Yorkshire distribution records from three localities (Doe Park Reservoir, Bretton Park Lake, and River Ouse) and reports considerable morphological variation, particularly in maxillary arm length and body proportions, with adult females ranging from 2.5-5 mm in length.
