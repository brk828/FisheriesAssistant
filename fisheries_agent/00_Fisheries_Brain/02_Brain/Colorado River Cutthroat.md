# Colorado River Cutthroat

## Summary
Colorado River Cutthroat (Salmo clarkii pleuriticus) is a native cutthroat trout subspecies found in Utah. This source lists it as one of three native cutthroat subspecies in the state, adapted to the Colorado River drainage fauna.
