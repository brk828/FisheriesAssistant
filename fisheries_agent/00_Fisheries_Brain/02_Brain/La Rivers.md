# La Rivers

## Summary
La Rivers is a researcher who conducted work on Nevada spring systems and published information in 1962 on the waters of Ash Meadows, Moapa Valley, and White River Valley. Despite this previous work, the current proposal notes that comprehensive limnological data for these spring systems remains incomplete and insufficient for meaningful comparisons among springs.

## Context
