# Gold Bluff Mine

## Summary
The Gold Bluff Mine was discovered by Maxwell's father ('The Argonaut') during the California Gold Rush, following an Indian attack and robbery. Maxwell uses this detailed historical narrative extensively in the document to establish credibility and draw parallels between gold mining and water development as sources of wealth creation, describing how the mine yielded gold from prehistoric river deposits.

## Context

> This 1935 advance print document by George Hebard Maxwell presents an ambitious proposal for water conservation and land reclamation in Arizona and California through comprehensive development of the [[Colorado River]].
