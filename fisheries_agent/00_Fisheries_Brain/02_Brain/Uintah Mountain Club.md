# Uintah Mountain Club

## Summary
The Uintah Mountain Club filed a notice of intent to sue the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service as of January 1989, though the specific grounds for the lawsuit are not detailed in this meeting summary. The Service indicated it might request support from other Recovery Program participants if the litigation materialized.
