# Re-introductions

## Summary
Re-introductions is one of the thematic conservation issues addressed by SSC's disciplinary groups rather than taxonomic specialist groups. The Re-introduction Specialist Group's mandate is to develop expertise on reintroductions and translocations of species. Re-introduction activities are considered potentially controversial issues that require review by the SSC Chair's Office, Programme Office, and appropriate disciplinary groups before submission to implementing agencies.
