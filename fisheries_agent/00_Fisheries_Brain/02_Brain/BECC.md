# BECC

## Summary
BECC (Border Environment Cooperation Commission) is the agency expected to approve the proposed Mexicali II wastewater treatment plant facility. Workshop participants identified participating in the BECC process for the Mexicali wastewater treatment plant as a short-term action item, as effluent from these facilities represents a potential water source for delta restoration.

## Context

> This document summarizes a binational workshop held October 30-31, 1997, in Mexicali, Baja California, Mexico, focused on the ecological and social restoration of the [[Colorado River Delta]] and [[Upper Gulf of California]].
