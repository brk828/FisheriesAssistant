# Sustainable Use

## Summary
Sustainable use is both a core conservation principle and a thematic focus area within SSC, derived from the World Conservation Strategy's emphasis on sustainable use of living natural resources. The Specialist Group on Sustainable Use is one of five disciplinary groups with a mandate to promote sustainable use as a conservation tool. Sustainable utilization is identified as a potentially controversial issue requiring careful review before SSC interventions with governments or other parties.
