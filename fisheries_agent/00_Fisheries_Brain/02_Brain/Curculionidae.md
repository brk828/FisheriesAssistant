# Curculionidae

## Summary
Curculionidae are weevils, a family of beetles. In this warm-water stream study, Curculionidae were found only at Stations I (upstream control) and Station III (immediately below sewage outfalls), suggesting they can tolerate some pollution conditions but were not present in the transition or recovery zones downstream.
