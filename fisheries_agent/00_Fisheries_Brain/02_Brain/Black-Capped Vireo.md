# Black-Capped Vireo

## Summary
Black-Capped Vireo is a proposed threatened or endangered bird species. The conference report recommended excluding aerial ultra-low-volume pesticide application from the vireo's foraging and nesting habitat, and required coordination with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Tulsa office and a university researcher to identify nesting areas in Oklahoma before any grasshopper control activities.
