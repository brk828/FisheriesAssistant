# Paul Marsh

## Summary
Paul Marsh is an ichthyologist and researcher affiliated with Arizona State University who has been a significant contributor to the study and conservation of endemic fish species in the southwestern United States, particularly in isolated desert aquatic ecosystems such as the Cuatrociénegas Basin in Mexico. His work has involved collaborative research on endangered fish species, including genetic analysis and morphological studies of bonytail chubs and other Colorado River fishes, often conducted through inter-institutional protocols coordinated with government agencies managing these threatened populations. Marsh has been instrumental in organizing scientific symposia and coordinating research efforts across academic institutions and natural resource management agencies focused on desert fish conservation.
