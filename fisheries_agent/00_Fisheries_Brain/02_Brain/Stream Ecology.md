# Stream Ecology

## Summary
Stream ecology is the scientific study of the physical, chemical, and biological processes that structure freshwater stream ecosystems, including the interactions between aquatic organisms and their environment. In fish science and conservation contexts, stream ecology encompasses understanding how factors such as water flow, habitat complexity, benthic invertebrate communities, and riparian conditions directly influence fish populations, feeding ecology, and species recolonization dynamics. This integrative approach is essential for developing effective conservation and management strategies that maintain healthy fish communities and ecosystem function in lotic (flowing water) environments.
