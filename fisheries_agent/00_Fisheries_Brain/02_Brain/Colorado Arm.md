# Colorado Arm

## Summary
The Colorado Arm is one of two major branches of Lake Powell's Y-shaped configuration, extending 163 miles upstream from the Colorado-San Juan confluence at full pool. This arm receives inflows from the Colorado River and Green River, with flow-weighted average dissolved solids of 427-547 ppm during 1965-1969, and serves as a pathway for density currents that transport cold, high-oxygen water downstream toward the dam.
