# Coregoninae

## Summary
Coregoninae is a subfamily within the Salmonidae family that includes freshwater whitefish and cisco species, characterized by their specialized morphological adaptations to lacustrine and riverine environments. These coregonids are ecologically significant components of northern freshwater ecosystems, with considerable phenotypic diversity both within and among species that results from complex interactions between environmental and genetic factors. The subfamily's phylogenetic position within Salmonidae and its adaptive radiation, particularly in large lakes, make Coregoninae important for understanding fish evolution, population differentiation, and the conservation of specialized freshwater fish communities.
