# Leslie Canyon Dam

## Summary
Leslie Canyon Dam was a proposed dam site on Leslie Creek in Cochise County, Arizona, originally planned in the 1960s-1970s to create a 350-acre recreational lake. The 1984 feasibility study concluded the project was not viable due to insufficient watershed yield (643 vs. originally estimated 1,900 acre-feet annually), downstream water rights claims exceeding 1,000 acre-feet, and the presence of endangered Yaqui Topminnow at the site, which created insurmountable legal barriers under the Endangered Species Act.
