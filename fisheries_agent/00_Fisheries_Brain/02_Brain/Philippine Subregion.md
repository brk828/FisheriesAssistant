# Philippine Subregion

## Summary
The Philippine Subregion is a distinct zoogeographic unit established by Gilbert and Hubbs, encompassing waters around the central Philippine Islands north of the Tawi Tawi group and south of west-central Luzon, excluding the Pacific Ocean along Luzon's east coast. This source demonstrates its coordinate status with the East Indian region based on its numerous endemic deep-sea fish species, particularly macrourids, which likely evolved within this region due to its unusual environmental conditions of warm temperatures at great depths and partial hydrographic isolation.
