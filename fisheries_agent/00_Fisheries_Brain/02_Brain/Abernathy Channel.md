# Abernathy Channel

## Summary
The Abernathy Channel is a 2,000-foot long by 10-foot wide artificial spawning and incubation channel constructed in 1959 near Longview, Washington, designed specifically for egg planting and incubation rather than adult spawning. This source reports that it successfully incubated Fall Chinook and Chum Salmon eggs at survival rates of 68-95.5% from eyed egg to migrant stage, substantially higher than other channels, operating at very low water flow (1.2 c.f.s.) and high egg densities (up to 435 eggs per square foot).
