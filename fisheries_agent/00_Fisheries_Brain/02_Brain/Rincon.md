# Rincon

## Summary
Rincon is a plankton sampling station on Lake Powell that showed high zooplankton abundance as part of the fertility gradient increasing from the dam toward the inflow. Peak zooplankton densities reached 40,470 organisms per 100-foot sample in June, with an average of 32.38 organisms per liter during peak periods.

## Context

> This report documents the fourth year of a six-year cooperative fisheries investigation on [[Lake Powell]], the reservoir created by [[Glen Canyon Dam]].
