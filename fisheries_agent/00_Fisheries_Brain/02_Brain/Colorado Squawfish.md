# Colorado Squawfish

## Summary
The Colorado Squawfish (*Ptychocheilus lucius*) is a large cyprinid native to the Colorado River basin that has become a focal species in endangered fish recovery efforts due to its severe population decline and extirpation from much of its historic range. As a piscivorous big-river fish requiring warm water and specific habitat conditions, it serves as an indicator of ecosystem health in the Colorado River and its tributaries, particularly in critical nursery areas like Green River backwaters. Conservation monitoring and management of this species involves population estimation, predation ecology studies, and habitat restoration as part of the broader Colorado River Recovery Program aimed at preventing complete extinction.
