# Godfrey Sykes

## Summary
Godfrey Sykes was an engineer who measured the water temperature of the hot spring where Lucania browni was found, using a laboratory instrument. He recorded the temperature as 128°F (53°C) in the presence of collector Herbert Brown and Dr. D.T. MacDougal.

## Context

> This 1907 publication by [[David Starr Jordan]] and [[Robert Earl Richardson]] describes a new species of killifish, *[[Lucania browni]]*, collected from a hot spring in northeastern [[Lower California]] (Baja California).
