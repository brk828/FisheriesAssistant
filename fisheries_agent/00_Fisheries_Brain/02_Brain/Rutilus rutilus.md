# Rutilus rutilus

## Summary
Rutilus rutilus is a fish species that occurs in both the Amu Darya basin of Afghanistan and the Caspian Sea basin. Its presence in both systems demonstrates the zoogeographic affinities between these basins, reflecting past hydrological connections.
