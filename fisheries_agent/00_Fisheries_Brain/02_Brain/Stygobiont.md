# Stygobiont

## Summary
A stygobiont is an aquatic organism, particularly fish and crustaceans, that is obligately adapted to live exclusively in subterranean groundwater environments such as cave systems and phreatic zones, characterized by the absence of light and typically displaying troglomorphic features like eye reduction or loss, depigmentation, and elongated appendages. These organisms represent specialized fauna of inland karstic and phreatic habitats where they persist in conditions of stable temperature, low oxygen, and minimal food availability. Stygobionts are important indicators of groundwater ecosystem health and present significant conservation concerns due to their restricted habitat ranges and vulnerability to contamination or habitat disturbance.
