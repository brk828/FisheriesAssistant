# Rodeo Area

## Summary
The Rodeo Area is one of the principal agricultural regions in San Simon Valley characterized by dry farming supplemented by flood water irrigation rather than well-based irrigation. This source notes the younger stream deposits reach 300-400 feet thick near Rodeo, and the underlying lake bed sediments have been penetrated to 1,000 feet depth in wells.
