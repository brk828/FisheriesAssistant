# BirdLife International

## Summary
BirdLife International is identified as a key partner organization that SSC collaborates closely with for bird conservation work. Rather than establishing separate SSC bird specialist groups, the Commission works with BirdLife International, along with the International Waterfowl and Wetlands Research Bureau (IWRB) and World Pheasant Association, to address avian conservation issues. This partnership extends SSC's capacity to address conservation challenges across diverse bird taxa and habitats.
