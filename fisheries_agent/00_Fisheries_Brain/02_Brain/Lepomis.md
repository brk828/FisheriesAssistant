# Lepomis

## Summary
Lepomis is a genus of centrarchid fishes commonly known as sunfish, which are small to medium-sized freshwater predators that inhabit streams and lakes across North America. In aquatic ecology and conservation contexts, Lepomis species (particularly L. cyanellus, the green sunfish) are significant as apex predators that influence lower trophic levels through both direct predation and indirect effects via chemical signaling through skin mucus. These fish are ecologically important as model organisms for studying predator-prey interactions and the cascading effects of predation risk on invertebrate behavior and ecosystem processes such as leaf litter processing in freshwater systems.
