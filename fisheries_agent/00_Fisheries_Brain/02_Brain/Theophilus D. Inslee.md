# Theophilus D. Inslee

## Summary
Theophilus D. Inslee was a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service researcher who conducted a 1983 study investigating persistent failures in establishing self-sustaining sport fish populations at Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge in New Mexico. His study monitored water quality parameters and fish populations, revealing that extreme salinity, low dissolved oxygen, limited habitat, and heavy predation made the refuge's impoundments fundamentally unsuitable for sustaining quality sport fisheries.
