# Surigao Sea

## Summary
The Surigao Sea is one of the associated inland waters included in the Sulu Sea complex. This source indicates that it maintains a nearly constant bottom temperature at depths greater than 550 meters, averaging approximately 11.5°C, almost 2°C warmer than the Sulu Sea proper.
