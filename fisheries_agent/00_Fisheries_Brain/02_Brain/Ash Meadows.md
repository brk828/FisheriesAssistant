# Ash Meadows

## Summary
Ash Meadows is a spring-fed complex of isolated wetland habitats in the Death Valley region that represents a critical refugium for endemic fish and aquatic species found nowhere else in the world. These highly restricted spring systems and marshes have become iconic examples of relictual desert aquatic ecosystems where geographic and hydrographic isolation has generated exceptional biodiversity and endemism comparable to other globally significant aquatic centers. Conservation of Ash Meadows has been a priority for the Desert Fishes Council due to the extreme vulnerability of its species to hydrological disturbance, groundwater extraction, and other anthropogenic threats that could drive multiple endemic taxa to extinction.
