# Gila River Bird Habitat Management Unit

## Summary
The Gila River Bird Habitat Management Unit is a designated area along the Gila River that would be partially inundated by the proposed Conner Dam - 1 alternative. This unit is part of the Resource Category 1 riparian and active floodplain habitats along the Gila River, which are considered unique, rare, and valuable to fish and wildlife with a management goal of no loss of habitat values.
