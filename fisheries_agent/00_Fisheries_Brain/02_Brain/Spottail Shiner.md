# Spottail Shiner

## Summary
The Spottail Shiner is a small cyprinid forage fish commonly found in freshwater lakes and rivers throughout North America, where it serves as an important prey species for piscivorous fishes such as walleye, pike, and perch. In ichthyological and fisheries research, this species is frequently examined in diet studies and food web analyses due to its ecological role as a key link between zooplankton and larger predatory fish in lentic and lotic systems. The Spottail Shiner's abundance and availability make it a significant component of the diet of both commercial and recreational fish species, and understanding its distribution and population dynamics is relevant for fisheries management and aquatic ecosystem assessments.
