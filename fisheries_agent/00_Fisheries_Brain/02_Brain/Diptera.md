# Diptera

## Summary
Diptera is an order of insects that includes flies and midges, which are ecologically significant as aquatic macroinvertebrates in freshwater ecosystems where they serve as important food sources for fish species like rainbow trout and other game fish. In ichthyology and fisheries research, Diptera larvae and adults are commonly documented components of fish diets and benthic community surveys, making them valuable indicators of stream habitat quality and trophic dynamics. Understanding Diptera communities is essential for assessing aquatic ecosystem health and predicting food availability for fish populations in conservation and management contexts.
