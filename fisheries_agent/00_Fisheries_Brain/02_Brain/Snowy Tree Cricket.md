# Snowy Tree Cricket

## Summary
The Snowy Tree Cricket (Oecanthus niveus) is a cricket species whose chirping behavior was studied for evidence of synchronism. This source identifies it as providing the strongest documented case of potential rhythmic synchronism in animals, based on Professor A.F. Shull's 1907 observations of two individuals maintaining synchronized chirping patterns, though the author notes that temperature effects on chirping rate could alternatively explain the apparent synchronization and the phenomenon remains unresolved.
