# Sedillo Springs

## Summary
Springs in Socorro County, New Mexico, that serve as the type locality for the Socorro Hydrobioid (Amnicola neomexicana). This source reports the species was presumed extirpated from this location, and the springs are used for Socorro city domestic water supply.
