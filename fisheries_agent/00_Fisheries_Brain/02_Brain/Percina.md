# Percina

## Summary
Percina is a genus of small benthic and semi-pelagic freshwater fish belonging to the family Percidae, commonly known as darters, that are native to river drainages of southeastern North America. These fish occupy diverse microhabitats including riffles, pools, and sand raceways, with different species exhibiting varying habitat preferences and degrees of morphological specialization. Percina species are significant subjects of systematic and taxonomic study, serving as important indicators of freshwater ecosystem health and biodiversity in stream and river conservation efforts.
