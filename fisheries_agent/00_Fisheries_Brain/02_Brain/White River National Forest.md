# White River National Forest

## Summary
White River National Forest is a national forest in northwestern Colorado that contains Colorado River Cutthroat Trout populations. A 1992 study of 50 streams in this forest found that the subspecies had disappeared from 8 streams and was not expected to remain long in 11 others, illustrating the ongoing decline of this native trout.
