# Deer Creek

## Summary
Deer Creek is a relatively unmodified tributary of the Sacramento River in Tehama County, California, that has served as an important study site for examining native fish community ecology and microhabitat use patterns in Mediterranean-climate stream systems. Research conducted in Deer Creek, particularly a seminal 1985 study on microhabitat utilization by native California stream fishes, has provided critical baseline data on how fish species with different life histories and ecological requirements partition habitat resources in intact tributaries. This stream system is valued in conservation biology as a reference condition site for understanding the habitat structure and fish assemblage composition of relatively undisturbed Sacramento River tributaries prior to extensive anthropogenic modification.
