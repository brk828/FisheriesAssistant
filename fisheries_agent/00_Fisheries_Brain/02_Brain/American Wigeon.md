# American Wigeon

## Summary
American Wigeon (Anas americana) are migratory waterfowl that utilize the wetland and backwater habitats created along the Colorado River following Glen Canyon Dam closure in 1963. These habitats became important for American Wigeon and other waterfowl species, though the majority of marshes were eliminated during flooding events between 1983-1986.
