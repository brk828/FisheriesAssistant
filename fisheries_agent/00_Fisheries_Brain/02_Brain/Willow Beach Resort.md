# Willow Beach Resort

## Summary
Willow Beach is a location approximately 12 miles downstream from Hoover Dam in Black Canyon where daily water level fluctuations were measured at 0.5 feet under existing conditions and would increase to 2 feet under the proposed powerplant modification. The last bonytail chubs collected in Black Canyon were taken at Willow Beach 20 years prior to this 1981 assessment, and the area above Willow Beach has extremely limited eddy and pool habitat preferred by bonytails.
