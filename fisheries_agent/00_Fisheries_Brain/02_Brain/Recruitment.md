# Recruitment

## Summary
Recruitment is the addition of young fish to a population or fishery. The northern pike population was dominated by young fish with most individuals age-3 or younger, and age-2 and age-3 fish accounted for 87% of total secondary production, indicating strong recruitment of young age classes.
