# Elephant Butte Lake

## Summary
Elephant Butte Lake in New Mexico was the site of Project 6-11-R investigating the biological potential of rough fish species for commercial harvest. Research showed that removal of 68% of the Smallmouth Buffalo standing crop resulted in population shifts favoring River Carpsucker and Gizzard Shad, while growth rates of remaining buffalo increased, reducing time to marketable size from 9 to 7 years. Rotenone estimates indicated total fish biomass of 400-600 pounds per acre.
