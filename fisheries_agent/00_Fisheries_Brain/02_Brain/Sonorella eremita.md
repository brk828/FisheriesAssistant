# Sonorella eremita

## Summary
A large terrestrial snail with a light brown band and wide toothless aperture, restricted to the northwest end of San Xavier Hill near Tucson in Pima County, Arizona. This source reports it is threatened by urban development from Tucson expansion and recommends listing as threatened with consideration of transplanting to nearby suitable hills.
