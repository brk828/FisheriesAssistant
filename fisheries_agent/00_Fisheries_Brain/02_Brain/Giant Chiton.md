# Giant Chiton

## Summary
Giant Chiton (Cryptochiton stelleri) is a cold-water mollusk species now confined to waters north of Point Conception that serves as evidence for past cool periods. Abundant Giant Chiton remains in Indian middens along the Baja California coast dated to approximately 900 years B.P. indicate a notably cool period when this northern species extended far south of its present range, possibly correlating with the "green" period in Greenland.
