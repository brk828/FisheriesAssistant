# Geodia mesotriaena

## Summary
Geodia mesotriaena is a brown to black demosponge that co-characterizes Zone IV (0 to -3 feet) of the Puerto Peñasco intertidal along with the soft coral Palythoa ignota. It forms large colonies in tide pools, has readily felt spicules, and extends into higher tide pools with permanent water bodies.
