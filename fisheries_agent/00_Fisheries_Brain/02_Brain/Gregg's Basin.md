# Gregg's Basin

## Summary
Gregg's Basin is one of the four major sections of Lake Mead, with South Cove serving as the single sampling station in this section. During June, this basin acted as a natural eutrophic lake (25-75 g C/m²/growing season), and it consistently showed the highest primary productivity values that decreased downstream toward Temple Bar across all five surveys.
