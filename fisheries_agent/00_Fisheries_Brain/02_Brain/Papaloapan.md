# Papaloapan

## Summary
Papaloapan refers to a river basin in Mexico with notable cave systems. This source documents cave systems in the Papaloapan basin as habitat for specialized troglobitic (cave-dwelling) fish species including Rhamdia reddelli (Papaloapan blindcat), representing unique subterranean aquatic biodiversity.
