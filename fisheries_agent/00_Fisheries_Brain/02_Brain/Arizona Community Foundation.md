# Arizona Community Foundation

## Summary
The Arizona Community Foundation is the funding organization to which this 1997 proposal was submitted. SCENE requested support for a three-year program to engage 120 high school students annually in hands-on scientific research experiences, with emphasis on aquatic ecology and endangered species conservation.
