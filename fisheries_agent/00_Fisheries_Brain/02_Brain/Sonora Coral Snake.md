# Sonora Coral Snake

## Summary
Sonora Coral Snake (Micruroides euryxanthus) is an endangered snake species found in the Sierra San Luis area. It is among the 22 protected reptile species in the reserve, which supports exceptional reptile diversity with 51 species from 11 families.
