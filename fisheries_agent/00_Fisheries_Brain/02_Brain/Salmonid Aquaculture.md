# Salmonid Aquaculture

## Summary
Salmonid aquaculture refers to the commercial and conservation-based breeding, rearing, and cultivation of salmon and trout species in controlled environments such as hatcheries, raceways, and ponds. In the context of fisheries management and conservation, salmonid aquaculture serves critical functions including mitigation of population declines caused by habitat loss or dam construction, maintenance of recreational fisheries, and restoration of wild populations through strategic hatchery programs. The practice combines fish culture technology with ecological management objectives to balance human resource needs with conservation goals in freshwater aquatic systems.
