# Seattle National Fishery Research Center

## Summary
The Seattle National Fishery Research Center conducted research on temperature tolerance for Cui-ui egg incubation. Their preliminary results confirmed that 17°C represents the upper limit for suitable incubation temperatures, which helped explain the lower-than-expected larvae survival in 1987 when temperatures reached 20°C.

## Context

> This report documents the 1987 spawning runs of the endangered [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) and threatened [[Lahontan Cutthroat Trout]] (*Salmo clarki henshawi*) at [[Marble Bluff Fish Facility]], [[Nixon, Nevada]].
