# Aquatic Diversity Management Areas

## Summary
Aquatic Diversity Management Areas (ADMAs) are priority conservation areas identified using a structured framework. This classification system for California's inland waters was specifically designed to support the establishment of ADMAs by providing a hierarchical structure to maximize protection of aquatic biodiversity with limited resources.
