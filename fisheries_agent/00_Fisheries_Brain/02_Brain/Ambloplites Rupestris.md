# Ambloplites Rupestris

## Summary
*Ambloplites rupestris*, commonly known as the rock bass, is a freshwater sunfish species native to North America that inhabits rocky and gravelly stream and lake environments, particularly in eastern river systems. This species is notable in ichthyological research for its ecological role as a benthic predator and its use in studies examining habitat preferences, age-growth dynamics, and resistance to environmental stressors in lotic and lentic ecosystems. The rock bass has served as an important model organism for understanding fish community composition, subspecific variation, and the effects of stream substrate type on population dynamics across different geographic regions.
