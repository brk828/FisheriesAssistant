# Tanon Strait

## Summary
Tanon Strait is a waterway between Negros and Cebu that is part of the Sulu Sea complex. This source reports that it has much higher bottom temperatures than the Sulu Sea proper, maintaining approximately 17°C at depths between 400-700 meters.
