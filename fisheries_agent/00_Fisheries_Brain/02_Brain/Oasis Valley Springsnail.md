# Oasis Valley Springsnail

## Summary
The Oasis Valley Springsnail (Pyrgulopsis micrococcus) is a hydrobiid snail found in Oasis Valley and also occurs in the Moapa River area. It was one of 30 aquatic invertebrate and vertebrate species petitioned for endangered species listing in 1991.
