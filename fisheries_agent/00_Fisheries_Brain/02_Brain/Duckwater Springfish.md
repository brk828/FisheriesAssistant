# Duckwater Springfish

## Summary
Duckwater Springfish is an endemic fish species found in Railroad Valley, Nevada. This source identifies it as one of the species directly threatened by the proposed MX Missile System, along with Locke's Ranch springfish and eight undescribed Gila bicolor subspecies in the same valley.
