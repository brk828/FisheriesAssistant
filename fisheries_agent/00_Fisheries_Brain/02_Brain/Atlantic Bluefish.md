# Atlantic Bluefish

## Summary
Atlantic Bluefish (Pomatomus saltatrix) is a commercial fishery species found to be at low abundance and over-exploited according to the 23rd Northeast Regional Stock Assessment Workshop in January 1997. The source reports that the stock's spawning biomass declined 63% from 293,006 metric tons in 1986 to a historic low of 110,000 metric tons in 1995, while fishing mortality increased to twice the overfishing reference point. A control date of May 29, 1997 was established to discourage speculative entry into the commercial fishery while management measures are developed, with recommendations to reduce fishing mortality to halt the spawning stock biomass decline.
