# Bolsón de los Muertos

## Summary
Bolsón de los Muertos is a desert basin located in Chihuahua, México, that contains endemic aquatic fauna. At the 1981 symposium, research was presented documenting the endemic fauna found in desert spring habitats of this region, highlighting it as an important area for desert fish biodiversity in the Chihuahuan Desert.
