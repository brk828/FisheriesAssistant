# Orchard Mesa Check

## Summary
Orchard Mesa Check is a water management structure whose operations were identified as a critical assumption that needed to be defined before conducting a water availability study for the 15-Mile Reach. The Water Rights Acquisition Subcommittee recommended that this study be conducted after resolving issues related to the Orchard Mesa Check and conditional water rights.
