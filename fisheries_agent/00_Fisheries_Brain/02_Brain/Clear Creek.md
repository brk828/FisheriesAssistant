# Clear Creek

## Summary
Clear Creek is a drainage system in Illinois that historically supported populations of fish species including the Cypress Minnow (*Hybognathus hayi*) during the 1930s–1940s, but has since experienced confirmed extirpation of this and other species. As a reference site in ichthyological surveys, Clear Creek drainage represents an example of historical fish habitat loss in the region, with no specimens of previously documented species recovered during modern status reassessment efforts. The creek's inclusion in distribution and abundance studies highlights the importance of tracking drainage-level changes in fish communities for conservation and restoration planning.
