# Imperial East Mesa

## Summary
The Imperial East Mesa is a geographic feature in southeastern California adjacent to the Sand Hills (Alagodones Dunes) and the western bajada of the Chocolate Mountains. The uppermost 78 km of the Coachella Canal runs along this mesa through the Colorado Desert region.
