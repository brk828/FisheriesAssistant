# Lepidomeda

## Summary
Lepidomeda is a genus of small cyprinid fishes endemic to the southwestern United States, particularly the Colorado River basin and associated drainage systems. Members of this genus, commonly known as spinedaces, are characterized by their small size, anterior dorsal fin spines, and specialized adaptations to arid-region stream ecosystems. These fishes are significant subjects of conservation concern due to habitat loss and competition from introduced species, making them important indicators of aquatic ecosystem health in the American Southwest.
