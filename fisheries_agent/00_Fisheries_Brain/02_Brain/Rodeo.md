# Rodeo

## Summary
Rodeo is a settlement in the San Simon Valley at approximately 4,300 feet elevation, located in the southern portion of the valley. According to this source, Rodeo had a mean annual precipitation of 13.60 inches (1910-1915 records) and was one of the principal agricultural areas, characterized by dry farming supplemented by flood water irrigation rather than artesian well irrigation.
