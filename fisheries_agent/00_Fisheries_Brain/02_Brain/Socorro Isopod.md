# Socorro Isopod

## Summary
The Socorro Isopod (Thermosphaeroma thermophilus) is a crustacean species that was listed as endangered or threatened under the Endangered Species Act. This source indicates it was one of the aquatic taxa included in the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's five-year review of species listed in 1978, and after evaluation, no substantial evidence was found to warrant changing its protected status.
