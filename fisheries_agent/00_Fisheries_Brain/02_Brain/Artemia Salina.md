# Artemia Salina

## Summary
Artemia salina is a small crustacean brine shrimp commonly used in aquatic ecology and fisheries research as both a model organism for limnological studies and a forage food source for cultured and wild fish populations. Its presence in saline aquatic systems makes it particularly valuable for investigating the physical and biological characteristics of salt pond ecosystems and for understanding food web dynamics in brackish and marine environments. Due to its hardy nature, rapid reproduction, and ease of cultivation, A. salina is frequently employed in laboratory studies examining fish nutrition, ecotoxicology, and broader aquatic community structure.
