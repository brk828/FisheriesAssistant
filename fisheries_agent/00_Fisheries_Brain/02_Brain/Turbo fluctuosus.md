# Turbo fluctuosus

## Summary
Turbo fluctuosus is a gastropod mollusk species documented at Puerto Peñasco, Sonora, Mexico. This source includes it among the intertidal mollusks of the Gulf of California, with identifications made using Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other references.
