# Larval Development

## Summary
Larval Colorado squawfish drift downstream after hatching, with juveniles concentrating in nursery embayments 100-150 km below spawning sites. Humpback chub eggs and larvae require temperatures of at least 15°C for development, with optimal conditions at 19-22°C, which are now only available in the Little Colorado River in the lower basin.
