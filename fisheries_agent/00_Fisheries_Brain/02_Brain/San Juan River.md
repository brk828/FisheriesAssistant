# San Juan River

## Summary
The San Juan River is a major tributary of the Colorado River Basin that serves as critical habitat for large-river endemic fish species, particularly endangered Colorado River native fishes that have experienced significant population declines due to habitat alterations from dam construction and water management. As designated critical habitat under the Endangered Species Act, the San Juan River functions as an essential refuge and spawning area for species recovery efforts managed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, alongside other key river segments including the Yampa, Colorado, and Green Rivers. The river's ecological role in maintaining populations of native fishes and supporting interjurisdictional conservation initiatives makes it a priority focus for fisheries management and endangered species recovery programs in the southwestern United States.
