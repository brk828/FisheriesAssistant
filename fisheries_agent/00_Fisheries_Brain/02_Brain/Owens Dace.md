# Owens Dace

## Summary
The Owens Dace (*Rhinichthys evermanni*) is an endemic cyprinid fish historically found in the Owens River drainage of California that has become a critical species in conservation biology due to its severely restricted range and vulnerability to habitat modification, water diversions, and introduced species competition. As part of the Death Valley area's diverse endemic fish fauna, the Owens Dace represents one of several highly specialized aquatic species dependent on maintaining specific spring and stream habitats in the Great Basin region. Its conservation status and inclusion in regional management discussions reflects broader concerns about preserving endemic fish diversity in arid western ecosystems threatened by water resource development and anthropogenic disturbance.
