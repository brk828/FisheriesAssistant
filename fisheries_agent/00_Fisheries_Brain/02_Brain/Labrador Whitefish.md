# Labrador Whitefish

## Summary
The Labrador Whitefish is another name for the Adirondack Whitefish (Coregonus clupeaformis), representing the most commonly distributed and caught form of whitefish in the region. It is a bottom-feeder that spawns in fall over stony shoal areas and has been the subject of artificial propagation efforts in New York since 1890. The species is highly valued as a food fish and supports both commercial and recreational fisheries.
