# Philippine Islands

## Summary
The Philippine Islands form the boundaries and core of the Philippine Subregion, a distinct zoogeographic unit established by Gilbert and Hubbs. The Philippine Subregion includes waters around the central Philippine Islands, north of the Tawi Tawi group and south of west-central Luzon, excluding the Pacific Ocean along the east coast of Luzon.
