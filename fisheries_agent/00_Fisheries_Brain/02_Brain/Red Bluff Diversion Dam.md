# Red Bluff Diversion Dam

## Summary
The Red Bluff Diversion Dam is a structure on the Sacramento River that differs from Columbia River dams in having operable gates that can be opened during the non-irrigation season. According to this source, opening these gates is identified as the "single most important step for enhancing the fish community" because it mimics natural system conditions, allows free fish passage, and enables predator-prey balance to reestablish between Sacramento squawfish and salmon.
