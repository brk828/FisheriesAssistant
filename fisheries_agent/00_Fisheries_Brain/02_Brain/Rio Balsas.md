# Rio Balsas

## Summary
The Rio Balsas is a major Pacific slope river basin in Mexico. This source documents it as habitat for various fish species and notes the presence of unresolved Ictalurus species resembling I. dugesi in Balsas tributaries in Morelos, and that the introduced black bullhead (Ameiurus melas) has been established in this system beyond its native range.
