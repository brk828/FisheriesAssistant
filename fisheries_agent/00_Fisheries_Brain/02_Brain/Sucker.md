# Sucker

## Summary
Suckers are freshwater fish of the family Catostomidae, commonly represented by species such as *Catostomus macrocheilus*, that are frequently surveyed in ichthyological studies and fish population assessments as indicator organisms for aquatic ecosystem health and water quality. These benthic feeders are notable for their high phosphate content in bone and scales, making them bioaccumulators of radioactive isotopes like ³²P and ⁶⁵Zn, and their sensitivity to environmental stressors such as oxygen depletion from algal blooms, making them valuable sentinels in toxicological and ecological monitoring studies. Suckers are widely distributed across North American freshwater systems and have been subject to extensive population surveys and ecological research due to their abundance and ecological significance in lake and river communities.
