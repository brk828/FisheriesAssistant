# Trout Stocking

## Summary
Trout stocking is the deliberate introduction of hatchery-reared trout into natural or altered aquatic systems to supplement or establish populations for recreational fishing and conservation purposes. This management practice is particularly valuable in environments with degraded habitat, altered hydrology, or insufficient natural reproduction capacity, where wild populations cannot sustain themselves through spawning and recruitment alone. The efficacy and appropriate application of trout stocking programs depend on factors such as water quality, spawning habitat availability, and the specific ecological context of each system, making it a nuanced tool that complements rather than replaces wild trout conservation efforts.
