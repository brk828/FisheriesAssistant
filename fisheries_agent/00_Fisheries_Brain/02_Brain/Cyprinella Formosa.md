# Cyprinella Formosa

## Summary
Cyprinella formosa, commonly known as the beautiful shiner, is a cyprinid fish species endemic to the Mimbres River system in Luna County, New Mexico, though it remains extant in portions of Mexico. This small minnow is of significant conservation concern as it has been extirpated from its native New Mexico range and represents one of several imperiled cyprinid taxa requiring recovery efforts by fisheries management and conservation teams. The species exemplifies the broader crisis affecting endemic southwestern fishes, particularly those dependent on increasingly fragmented and altered desert river systems.
