# Western Governors Association

## Summary
The Western Governors Association (WGA) developed a December 1989 draft proposal for mining waste regulation that prioritized state primacy, continuation of existing state programs, and greater flexibility in approval mechanisms compared to EPA's approach. This source notes that WGA's proposal differed from EPA's by covering all metallic ore/mineral industries regardless of Report to Congress coverage, regulating active leaching operations, proposing soil criteria, and requiring corrective action after every exceedance study.
