# Colorado River (Texas)

## Summary
The Colorado River in Texas is a major freshwater aquatic system in central Texas that serves as critical habitat for diverse fish species and supports important conservation efforts related to endemic and threatened aquatic fauna. This river system is particularly significant for studies of fish distribution, population dynamics, and ecological responses to dam construction and water management practices, as exemplified by research at major impoundments like Inks Dam. The Colorado River represents an important focal point for understanding how hydrological modifications and human water use affect fish communities and ecosystem health in Texas freshwater ecosystems.
