# University of Utah

## Summary
The University of Utah is the institutional affiliation of Mr. Mark Rosenfeld, who falsely claimed co-authorship on a cutthroat trout research proposal. According to the correspondence, Rosenfeld had no part in the proposal's planning, designing, or writing, and his only contribution was teaching basic tissue culture techniques to Dr. Shiozawa in winter 1987.
