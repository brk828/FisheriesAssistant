# Sipsey River

## Summary
The Sipsey River is a tributary of the Tombigbee River in Alabama. The study area for this blue catfish research extended to one mile below the junction where the Sipsey River meets the Tombigbee River in Greene County, Alabama.

## Context

> This study examines the growth, age composition, and mortality rates of [[Blue Catfish]] (*Ictalurus furcatus*) in the upper [[Tombigbee River]] of western Alabama using data collected August 5, 1965.
