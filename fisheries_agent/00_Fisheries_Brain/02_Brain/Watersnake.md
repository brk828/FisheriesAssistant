# Watersnake

## Summary
The Common Watersnake (Natrix sipedon) is a non-poisonous aquatic snake found throughout Michigan near any lake, pond, or stream. According to this source, it has mottled brown coloration with dark brown saddle-shaped bands on a gray-brown background, bears 20-25 young alive in late summer, and commonly basks in the sun in trees or bushes 6-8 feet above stream banks. It hibernates on land during cold months.
