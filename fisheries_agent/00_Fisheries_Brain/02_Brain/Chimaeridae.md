# Chimaeridae

## Summary
Chimaeridae is a family of cartilaginous fishes within the subclass Holocephali, representing chimaeras. This source documents it as the sole family representing Holocephali in the Gulf of California, contributing to the region's total of 18 cartilaginous fish families.
