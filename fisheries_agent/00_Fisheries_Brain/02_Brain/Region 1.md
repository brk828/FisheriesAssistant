# Region 1

## Summary
Region 1 encompasses the Pacific coast states (Washington, Idaho, Oregon, California, Nevada, and Hawaii) and experienced the most ESA consultation conflicts during FY 1987-1991, issuing 79 jeopardy opinions with 8 confirmed blocked projects (all BLM timber sales). This high conflict rate reflects the region's combination of high species diversity and intense development pressure.
