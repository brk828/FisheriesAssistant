# Department of Chesapeake Bay Affairs

## Summary
The Department of Chesapeake Bay Affairs was a government agency that produced staff reports on environmental issues affecting the Chesapeake Bay region. This 1964 literature review on turbidity and siltation effects was published as one of their comprehensive staff reports synthesizing scientific findings on aquatic ecosystem impacts.
