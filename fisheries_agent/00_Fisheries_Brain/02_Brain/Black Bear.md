# Black Bear

## Summary
Black Bear (Ursus americanus) is an endangered species with verified presence in the Sierra San Luis area in 1991 and 1993. The species is one of 70 vertebrates under federal protection in the reserve and is subject to illegal hunting, making it a priority for conservation management and protection measures.
