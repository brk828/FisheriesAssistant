# Wilkin's Dam

## Summary
Wilkin's Dam is a proposed Bureau of Reclamation project that would be located at the confluence of Clear Creek and East Clear Creek as part of the larger Mogollon Mesa Project. The dam would inundate approximately 8 miles of Little Colorado spinedace habitat and significantly decrease downstream flows; while the project was inactive as of 1985, pressure existed to reactivate it, making it a potential future threat to the species.
