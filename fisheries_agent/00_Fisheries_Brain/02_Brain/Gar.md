# Gar

## Summary
Gar are a complex of primitive, elongated predatory fishes in the genus Lepisosteus, including species such as Alligator, Longnose, Shortnose, and Spotted gar, that have historically comprised a significant portion of commercial and rough fish catches in inland waters of North America. These organisms function as apex predators in their ecosystems and are often captured as bycatch in commercial fishing operations, where they represented approximately 20% of catch weight in some studies. Gar populations in small inland lakes are typically present in low abundances and have been subject to fishing pressure, making them important indicators of predator community composition and ecosystem health in freshwater systems.
