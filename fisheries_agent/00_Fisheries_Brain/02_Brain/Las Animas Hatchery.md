# Las Animas Hatchery

## Summary
Las Animas Hatchery is a Colorado fish production facility that participated in the state's warm water stocking efforts in 1955. Along with Wray Hatchery and the Fish Control and Salvage Unit, it produced Walleye fry and fingerlings as part of the 6,060,713 Walleye stocked across Colorado waters that year.
