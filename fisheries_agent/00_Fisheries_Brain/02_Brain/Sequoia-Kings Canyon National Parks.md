# Sequoia-Kings Canyon National Parks

## Summary
Sequoia-Kings Canyon National Parks were scheduled as one of the future meeting locations for the commission examining research and resource management policy in the National Park System. The selection of these parks as a meeting site indicates they were considered important locations for the commission's work in evaluating policy issues across diverse park units.
