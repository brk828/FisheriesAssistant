# Santa Clara

## Summary
Santa Clara is a location in the lower Colorado River region of Mexico that supported a Desert Pupfish population of over 20,000 individuals as of 1978. Despite facing threats from desalinization plant discharge and introduced species, this population remained relatively robust compared to other sites in the region.
