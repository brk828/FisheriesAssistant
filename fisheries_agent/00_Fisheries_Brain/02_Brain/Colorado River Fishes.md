# Colorado River Fishes

## Summary
Colorado River Fishes refers to the native fish fauna endemic or native to the Colorado River basin, including species such as the Colorado pikeminnow that have evolved specifically within this river system and are dependent on its unique hydrological and ecological conditions. These fishes are of significant conservation concern due to habitat degradation from dam construction, water diversions, and invasions by non-native species, making their recovery and management critical objectives in Colorado River aquatic ecology. The concept encompasses both the ecological relationships these species maintain with specific habitats like backwater nurseries and the broader conservation biology efforts required to sustain their populations across the river system.
