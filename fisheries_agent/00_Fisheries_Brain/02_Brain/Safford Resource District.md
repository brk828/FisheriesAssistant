# Safford Resource District

## Summary
The Safford Resource District is a U.S. Bureau of Land Management administrative unit that commissioned this baseline study on Lower Bonita Creek. The district manages the area where the cattle exclosure was established in 1984 to assess riparian restoration potential.

## Context

> This 1987 baseline report documents vegetation and breeding bird communities along a 3 km cattle exclosure established in 1984 on [[Lower Bonita Creek]], [[Graham County]], [[Arizona]].
