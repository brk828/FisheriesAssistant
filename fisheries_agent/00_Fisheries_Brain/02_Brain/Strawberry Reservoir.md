# Strawberry Reservoir

## Summary
Strawberry Reservoir is a water body in Utah's Colorado River drainage basin that was the subject of significant fisheries management and conservation debate during the 1980s regarding proposed restoration efforts. The reservoir and its tributary streams historically supported native fish assemblages, but management decisions and environmental assessments related to the Strawberry Valley Fishery Restoration project raised concerns among ichthyological experts about inadequate consideration of the system's native fish history and ecology. As a focal point for fisheries restoration initiatives, Strawberry Reservoir represents a case study in balancing aquatic ecosystem conservation with resource management objectives in complex riverine systems.
