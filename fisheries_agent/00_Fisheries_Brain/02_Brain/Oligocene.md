# Oligocene

## Summary
The Oligocene is a geological epoch during which extensive paleorivers drained westward from the western North American landscape, entering the Pacific Ocean through what is now the Los Angeles Basin. This study proposes that the plagopterin progenitor likely occupied these Oligocene paleoriver watersheds, with genera and species diverging later due to drainage disruptions from regional tectonism.
