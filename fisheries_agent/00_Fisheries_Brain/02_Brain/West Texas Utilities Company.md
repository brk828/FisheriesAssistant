# West Texas Utilities Company

## Summary
West Texas Utilities Company operated an electric plant with an intake canal at Lake Pauline. During the 1963 rotenone treatment, two screens were constructed and installed at the plant's intake canal to prevent dead fish from entering and potentially overloading the rotating screens.
