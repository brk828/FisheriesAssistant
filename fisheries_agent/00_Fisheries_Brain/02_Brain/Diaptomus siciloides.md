# Diaptomus Siciloides

## Summary
Diaptomus siciloides is a species of calanoid copepod, a small crustacean zooplankton organism found in freshwater aquatic systems. As a member of the genus Diaptomus, it plays an important role in aquatic food webs as a primary consumer, feeding on phytoplankton and serving as a crucial food source for larval and juvenile fish. This species contributes to the overall species composition and seasonal dynamics of limnetic copepod communities in freshwater lakes and ponds across North America.
