# Teruya Uyeno

## Summary
Teruya Uyeno is a co-author of this systematic revision of the genus Gila in North America. He worked alongside Robert Rush Miller to undertake taxonomic clarification of this cyprinid fish genus that had experienced considerable nomenclatural confusion.
