# Upper Waterton Lake

## Summary
Upper Waterton Lake is a lake in Alberta, Canada that served as one of two source populations for the Mysis relicta introduction program to western U.S. lakes. Between 1963 and 1964, opossum shrimp were collected from Upper Waterton Lake at depths of 40 to 140 feet using specialized tow nets, with the majority of the approximately 182,300 shrimp introduced to Lake Tahoe and other California/Nevada lakes originating from this population.
