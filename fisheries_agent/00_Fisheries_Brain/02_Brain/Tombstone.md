# Tombstone

## Summary
Tombstone is one of the ten Arizona development projects proposed in Maxwell's 1935 water conservation plan. The Tombstone Project would draw water from the San Pedro River and subterranean sources beneath Tombstone, replenished from the Dragoon Mountains watershed, and Maxwell suggested that historic Tombstone mines could be reopened below the 500-foot level using pumping powered by electricity from the proposed Glen Canyon Dam.
