# Water Milfoil

## Summary
Water Milfoil (Myriophyllum sp.) is an aquatic plant found in small amounts in Section B of the Coachella Canal. This submergent vegetation grew on the sandy bottom along with small amounts of Sago Pondweed in the straight canal reach.
