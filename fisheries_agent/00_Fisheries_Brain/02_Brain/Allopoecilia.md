# Allopoecilia

## Summary
Allopoecilia is a genus of poeciliid fish. Males from this genus were among the few non-Mollienisia males that successfully produced offspring with M. formosa, though at much lower rates (0.18 broods and 1.78 young per 100 days) compared to Mollienisia males, and all offspring remained matroclinous.
