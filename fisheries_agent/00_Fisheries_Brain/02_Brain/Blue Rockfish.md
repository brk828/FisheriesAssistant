# Blue Rockfish

## Summary
Blue Rockfish (Sebastes mystinus) is a Pacific rockfish species that exhibits seasonal habitat shifts. Miller and Geibel (1973) concluded that Blue Rockfish move from summer kelp bed habitats to different overwintering locations to avoid heavy winter swell.
