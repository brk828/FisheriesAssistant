# SW Arizona Bald Eagle Recovery Team

## Summary
The SW Arizona Bald Eagle Recovery Team (Southwest Arizona Bald Eagle Recovery Team) is a species recovery group that was included on the contact list for the 1989 Bog Hole management planning. Their inclusion reflects concerns about assessing development impacts on Bald Eagles that use the Bog Hole and San Rafael Valley area.
