# South Fork Snake River

## Summary
The South Fork Snake River is mentioned in the source as part of the Snake River system. It is relevant to the distribution of Yellowstone Cutthroat Trout, which were historically isolated above Shoshone Falls in the Snake River drainage of southcentral Idaho.
