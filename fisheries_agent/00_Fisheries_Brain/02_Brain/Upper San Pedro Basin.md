# Upper San Pedro Basin

## Summary
The Upper San Pedro Basin is a 6,500 square kilometer transborder watershed with 72% in the United States, designated by The Nature Conservancy as one of 12 'Last Great Places of the Western Hemisphere' due to its extraordinary ecological diversity including over 300 bird species. This proposal describes it as supporting diverse ecosystems ranging from Chihuahuan Desertscrub to various conifer forests across elevations from 1,100 to 2,900 meters, with the San Pedro River being among the few Southwest rivers with unregulated flow. The research program aims to study land use-ecological relationships, particularly how groundwater and surface water decline from agricultural, municipal, and mining activities threaten riparian ecosystems.
