# Ciliata mustela

## Summary
Ciliata mustela (Five-bearded Rockling) is a Lotinae species characterized by small optic lobes and small eyes, indicating minimal reliance on visual cues for feeding. Its brain structure exemplifies the reduced visual dependence typical of members of this subfamily.
