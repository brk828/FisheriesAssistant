# Daphnia Magna

## Summary
Daphnia magna is a small freshwater crustacean commonly used as a live food source in aquarium fish culture and laboratory settings. In fish science and aquaculture, this cladoceran is valued for its high nutritional content and ease of mass culture, making it an ideal food organism for rearing larval and juvenile fish species. Its presence in aquatic ecology research also makes it useful as a model organism for studying impacts on aquatic food webs and fish nutrition in controlled experimental conditions.
