# San Joaquin Basin

## Summary
The San Joaquin Basin is a major California drainage system separated from the Mohave River by high mountains. It is one of the systems where Siphateles species occur, and the Mohave River shows no geological evidence of previous connection to this basin, making the presence of Siphateles in the isolated Mohave River biogeographically puzzling.
