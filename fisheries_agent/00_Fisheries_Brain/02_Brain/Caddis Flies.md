# Caddis Flies

## Summary
Caddis flies are aquatic insects, some species of which have immature stages that consume freshwater sponges including their glassy spicules. Like spongilla flies, adult caddis flies emerge at the water surface and serve as fish food, representing an indirect ecological benefit of sponges in fish pond ecosystems.
