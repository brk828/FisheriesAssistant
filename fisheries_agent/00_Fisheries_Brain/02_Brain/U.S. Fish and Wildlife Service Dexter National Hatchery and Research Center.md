# U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Dexter National Hatchery and Research Center

## Summary
The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Dexter National Hatchery and Research Center in New Mexico is a federal facility that produces endangered fish species. This source indicates that bonytail stocked at the Arizona Nature Conservancy Hassayampa Preserve in 1986 (879 juveniles) and 1987 (4,600 young) originated from this hatchery.
