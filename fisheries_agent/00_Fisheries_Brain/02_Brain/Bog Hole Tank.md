# Bog Hole Tank

## Summary
Bog Hole Tank is a constructed habitat in the Santa Cruz River drainage created by the U.S. Forest Service and Arizona Game and Fish Department for Mexican Duck. According to this source, it was the site of the first discovered unauthorized introduction of Quitobaquito Desert Pupfish in 1976, confirmed through electrophoretic analysis, with the population growing large enough to disperse up to 1 kilometer downstream into the upper Santa Cruz River before being depleted by introduced predators.
