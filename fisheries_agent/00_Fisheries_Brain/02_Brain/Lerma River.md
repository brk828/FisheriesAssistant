# Lerma River

## Summary
The Lerma River is a major westward-flowing river that, together with the Santiago River, forms the principal drainage system of Mexico's Mesa Central region. This source reports that parts of the Lerma River have been diverted for cities and agriculture, reducing aquatic habitat and threatening the highly endemic fish fauna that inhabits the Lerma-Santiago system.
