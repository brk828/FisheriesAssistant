# Plantago cordata

## Summary
Plantago cordata is a plant species from Illinois streams used as a comparative model for understanding the habitat requirements of Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva. The flooding pattern affecting Lilaeopsis—requiring intermediate flooding frequency to manage competition but being extirpated when floods are too frequent and intense—matches the model developed by Bowles and Apfelbaum (1989) for Plantago cordata.

> This status report documents [[Lilaeopsis schaffneriana]] subspecies recurva, a rare semi-aquatic perennial plant endemic to cienega wetlands in southeastern Arizona and adjacent Sonora, Mexico.
