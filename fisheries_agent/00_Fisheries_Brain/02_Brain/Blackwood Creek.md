# Blackwood Creek

## Summary
Blackwood Creek is a tributary to Lake Tahoe that served as one of the source locations for cutthroat trout spawn collected by Sisson Hatchery during 1895-1896. The cutthroat trout fry reared from this and Taylor Creek spawns were distributed throughout California mountain streams, including the Santa Ana River where they later became the basis for the Salmo evermanni description.
