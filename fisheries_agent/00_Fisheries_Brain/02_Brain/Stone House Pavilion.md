# Stone House Pavilion

## Summary
Stone House Pavilion was a major capital project completed in Year 1 (1994-95) of The Phoenix Zoo's strategic plan with a budget of $1,230,914. It was the largest single capital investment in the first year, representing part of the zoo's transformation into a mission-driven conservation communication organization.
