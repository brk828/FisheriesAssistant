# Cortland Hatchery

## Summary
Cortland Hatchery is a research facility in New York, USA that operates under a cooperative agreement between the New York Conservation Department, Fish and Wildlife Service, and Cornell University to advance salmonid nutrition and management science. In 1954, the hatchery conducted comprehensive experimental studies on brook trout and brown trout, investigating calcium and cobalt absorption from water, metabolic rate reduction for high-density transport, vitamin requirements, and dietary components, all using rigorous factorial experimental designs at controlled temperatures (36-51°F).
