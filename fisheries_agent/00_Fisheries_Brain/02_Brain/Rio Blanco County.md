# Rio Blanco County

## Summary
Rio Blanco County is a Colorado county containing part of the White River study area. The source identifies a 16-kilometer stretch of the White River between Rio Blanco Lake and the County Road 65 bridge in Rio Blanco County as one of five trend zones monitored for endangered Colorado squawfish and humpback chub.
