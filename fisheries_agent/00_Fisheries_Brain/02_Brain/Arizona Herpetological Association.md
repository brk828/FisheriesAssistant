# Arizona Herpetological Association

## Summary
The Arizona Herpetological Association (AHA) is a non-profit organization that advocates for herpetological interests and wildlife conservation policy in Arizona, as evidenced by its role in petitioning the Arizona Game and Fish Department to revise state wildlife regulations. While primarily focused on reptiles and amphibians rather than fish, the AHA's regulatory advocacy efforts intersect with broader conservation biology and wildlife management frameworks that govern Arizona's native aquatic and terrestrial fauna. The organization represents stakeholder interests in captive propagation and wildlife management regulations that fall under the purview of ichthyological and fisheries management agencies.
