# Sucker Family

## Summary
The Sucker Family (Catostomidae) is almost entirely North American with only two Asiatic species. This source documents that several sucker species formerly common in the upper Ohio Basin have been extirpated or greatly reduced, including the Highfin Sucker, River Carpsucker, Blue Sucker, and Sturgeon Sucker.
