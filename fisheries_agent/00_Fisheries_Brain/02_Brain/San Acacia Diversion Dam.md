# San Acacia Diversion Dam

## Summary
San Acacia Diversion Dam is one of three diversion structures in the Middle Rio Grande that fragment the river system. Under low-flow conditions, these diversions can completely dewater extensive river sections, representing a primary threat to mainstream fish fauna by causing habitat loss and concentrating populations below diversions.
