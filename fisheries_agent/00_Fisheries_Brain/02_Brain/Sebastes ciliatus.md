# Sebastes ciliatus

## Summary
Sebastes ciliatus (Dusky Rockfish) is one of two primary species forming the mixed aggregations studied in Lynn Canal. Along with Yellowtail Rockfish, Dusky Rockfish exhibited seasonal behavioral shifts between active feeding above substrate in warm months and inactive retreat into shelter during cold months.
