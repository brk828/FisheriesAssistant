# Cross Mountain

## Summary
Cross Mountain is a location on the Yampa River where The Nature Conservancy was negotiating water rights acquisition as of February 1990. The Water Rights Acquisition Subcommittee's report included evaluation of conditional water rights in this area as part of the strategy to secure instream flows for endangered fish recovery.
