# Sonoran Savanna Grassland

## Summary
Sonoran Savanna Grassland is the historical habitat type of the Altar Valley, characterized by scattered brush cover and native grasses. The Buenos Aires refuge management plan aims to restore this ecosystem through cessation of grazing impacts, brush management, native grass seeding, and water retention measures to support Masked Bobwhite Quail recovery.
