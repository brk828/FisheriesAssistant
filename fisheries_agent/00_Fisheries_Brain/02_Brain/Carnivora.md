# Carnivora

## Summary
Carnivora (carnivores) represent only 2% of identified mammals at La Lomita Pequeña, with a single third phalanx from an unidentified carnivore larger than a coyote. This specimen was burned (100% of carnivore remains showed burning), and carnivores represent 0-2% of fauna across comparable Hohokam Sedentary period sites.
