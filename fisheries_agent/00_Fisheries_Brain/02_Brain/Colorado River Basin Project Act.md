# Colorado River Basin Project Act

## Summary
The Colorado River Basin Project Act (P.L. 90-537) is the primary legal authority for the Central Arizona Project, with Section 301(a) authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to construct, operate, and maintain CAP for multiple purposes including conservation and development of fish and wildlife resources and enhancement of recreation opportunities. The Act's Sections 102 and 301(a) provide the authority for CAP fish and wildlife enhancement, though the current interpretation requires expenditure of CAP appropriations to be associated with authorized project features.
