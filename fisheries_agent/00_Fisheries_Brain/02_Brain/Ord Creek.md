# Ord Creek

## Summary
Ord Creek is a small mountain stream in Apache County, Arizona, within the Gila River Basin that originates at Bull Cienega and flows northward into the North Fork of the White River through mixed riparian and forest habitats. The creek gained prominence in ichthyology as the site of a 1977–1978 piscicide treatment using Antimycin A to remove introduced salmonids and facilitate the reintroduction of the native Arizona Trout (*Salmo apache*), making it a significant case study for examining both fish restoration methods and the non-target ecological effects of chemical fish eradication. Ord Creek has also served as an important reference population for genetic studies assessing the genetic purity and population structure of native southwestern fish, particularly in understanding connectivity between connected waterbodies and source populations.
