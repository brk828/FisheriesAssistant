# Analysis Of Variance In Creel Census Data

## Context

Murphy (Idaho Department of Fish and Game) presents a two-year (1952–1953) creel census of the lower 24 miles of the [[Little Salmon River]], Idaho, conducted in response to increasing angling pressure, an extended fishing season, and reports of declining catch success....
