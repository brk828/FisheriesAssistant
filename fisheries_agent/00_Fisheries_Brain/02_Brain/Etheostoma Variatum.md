# Etheostoma Variatum

## Summary
Etheostoma variatum, commonly known as the Variegate Darter, is a small freshwater fish species endemic to the Ohio River drainage system, with populations distributed from south-central Indiana through the Allegheny and Monongahela river systems. This benthic darter exhibits habitat preferences for lower, larger stream reaches and typically dominates in downstream sampling stations, often co-occurring with related darter species such as E. zonale and being replaced upstream by E. caeruleum in smaller tributaries. The species is of ecological and conservation interest as an indicator of stream health and habitat condition in Appalachian freshwater systems, particularly regarding the effects of mining activities and stream fragmentation on small-stream fish communities.
