# Trichomycteridae

## Summary
Trichomycteridae is a family of small catfishes found primarily in South American freshwater systems, particularly in Andean and lowland river basins, characterized by their diminutive size and often cryptic benthic habits. Members of this family are of interest to ichthyologists and conservation biologists due to their diversity in geographically isolated river systems, their roles in aquatic food webs, and their sensitivity to habitat degradation and water pollution. The family's ecological and taxonomic importance has made it a subject of systematic study and species description efforts throughout the twentieth century.
