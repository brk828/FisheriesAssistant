# U.S. Army Corps Of Engineers

## Summary
The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers is a federal agency responsible for managing water resources and infrastructure projects across the United States, including the construction and operation of dams, reservoirs, and water control systems that significantly impact aquatic ecosystems and fish populations. In the context of conservation biology and fisheries science, the Corps' management decisions directly affect critical aquatic habitats, particularly in western desert ecosystems where endemic fish species depend on specific hydrological conditions maintained by these engineered systems. The agency's regulatory authority and operational control over water resources make it a central player in determining the survival prospects of endangered and endemic fish species, particularly in isolated spring and stream ecosystems vulnerable to habitat alteration or water diversion.
