# Clear Lake Oaks

## Summary
Clear Lake Oaks is a shoreline location on Clear Lake in Lake County, California. This study documented Sacramento hitch spawning at this site on May 3, 1957, where detailed observations showed spawning beginning at 7 p.m. in shallow water (1-5 inches depth) over gravel at 64°F water temperature, and where hundreds of eggs in various developmental stages were successfully collected and hatched.
