# Sonoita Creek

## Summary
Sonoita Creek is a small permanent tributary of the Santa Cruz River in southern Arizona that serves as an important study site for aquatic ecology and fish conservation research. As a relatively pristine aquatic ecosystem in an arid region, the creek has been the subject of scientific investigations examining the effects of anthropogenic disturbances, particularly municipal wastewater discharge, on fish populations and stream health. The creek's ecological significance lies in its role as a stable aquatic habitat in the semi-arid Southwest, making it valuable for understanding how freshwater fish communities respond to environmental degradation and conservation challenges in water-limited landscapes.
