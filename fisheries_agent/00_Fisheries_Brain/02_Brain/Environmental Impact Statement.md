# Environmental Impact Statement

## Summary
An Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) is a formal environmental assessment document required for major federal actions. The Bureau of Reclamation announced plans for EIS scoping sessions scheduled for January-February 1990 to assess Glen Canyon Dam operations, though concerns were raised about the limited time for preparation and whether a 'quick-fix' approach would produce an adequate document.
