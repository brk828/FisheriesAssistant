# Snyder and Langbein

## Summary
Snyder and Langbein (1962) proposed that pluvial conditions could be achieved through decreased evaporation from 44 to 31 inches while maintaining modern temperatures. This model emphasized evaporation reduction rather than major temperature or precipitation changes.
