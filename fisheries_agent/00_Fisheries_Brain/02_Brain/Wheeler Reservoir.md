# Wheeler Reservoir

## Summary
Wheeler Reservoir is a mainstream impoundment on the Tennessee River in northern Alabama created by the Tennessee Valley Authority that fundamentally altered the region's aquatic ecosystem and fisheries by converting a free-flowing riverine system into a stable, stratified reservoir environment. Since its construction in the early 20th century, the reservoir has served as a critical study site for ichthyological research investigating how dam impoundment affects fish community composition, species abundance, habitat utilization patterns, and both commercial and recreational fishery dynamics. The reservoir's relatively stable water levels and distinct habitat zones have made it particularly valuable for long-term fisheries management studies, including pioneering research on largemouth bass population dynamics and exploitation rates through tag-and-recapture methods.
