# Point Lena

## Summary
Point Lena is a study site in Lynn Canal featuring a sunken vessel at 13-28 m depth where rockfish aggregations were observed. During cold months, fish at Point Lena moved progressively deeper into the vessel wreckage both laterally and vertically, with no rockfishes found during normal diving excursions from November-April.
