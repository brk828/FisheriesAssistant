# White River

## Summary
The White River refers to a river system in the south-central United States (primarily Arkansas) that has been extensively studied in ichthyological research due to dramatic ecological transformations resulting from dam construction and reservoir development. Originally a warmwater riverine system, the upper White River was fundamentally altered by the sequential construction of four multipurpose dams (Beaver Dam, Table Rock Lake, Lake Taneycomb, and Bull Shoals Reservoir), which converted it into a series of cold-water impoundments and affected both fish communities and hatchery operations. The river system has been a focus of fish conservation and distribution studies, including work on species recovery programs and the monitoring of native fish populations in the context of habitat modification.
