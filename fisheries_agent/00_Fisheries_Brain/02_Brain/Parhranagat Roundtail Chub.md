# Parhranagat Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Parhranagat Roundtail Chub is an endangered fish with restricted habitat range that would be jeopardized by malathion use due to the pesticide's high aquatic toxicity. A 1-mile malathion exclusion buffer is required around occupied waters, while 1/4-mile buffers suffice for carbaryl and acephate applications.
