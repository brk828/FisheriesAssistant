# Idaho

## Summary
Idaho is a neighboring state that served as a market for Montana Carp and Suckers, with trout farms purchasing these species at 2¢/pound. Idaho was also part of the regional market area (along with Montana, Wyoming, Utah, North Dakota, and South Dakota) projected to reach 52 million pounds of fish consumption by 1980.
