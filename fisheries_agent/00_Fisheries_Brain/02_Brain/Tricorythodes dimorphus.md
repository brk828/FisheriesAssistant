# Tricorythodes dimorphus

## Summary
Tricorythodes dimorphus is a species of mayfly found in desert stream ecosystems. Along with Baetis quilleri, this species was among the dominant mayflies in Sycamore Creek during May 1983, contributing to the high macroinvertebrate biomass that made up >90% of the invertebrate community (along with chironomids) and serving as preferred prey for Longfin Dace.
