# Arrowrock Reservoir

## Summary
Arrowrock Reservoir is a managed impoundment on the Boise River system in Idaho, fed by the Middle Fork and South Fork Boise River. This study found that its fish population remained stable in 1965-1966 but changed drastically in 1967 following an extreme drawdown to 940 acre-feet in fall 1966, which caused oxygen deficiency, fish mortality, and a drop in catch rates from 6.8 to 1.8 fish per net-hour, though the percentage of game fish increased due to continued stocking.
