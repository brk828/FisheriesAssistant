# Soleidae

## Summary
Soleidae is a family of flatfishes (order Pleuronectiformes) characterized by their asymmetrical body morphology, with both eyes positioned on one side of the head, making them benthic specialists adapted to life on the seafloor. While primarily marine, some members of this family have evolved euryhaline capabilities, allowing them to tolerate and inhabit brackish and freshwater environments, particularly in regions like Mexico where secondary freshwater colonization has occurred. The family's presence in freshwater systems represents an example of how certain marine lineages have successfully adapted to inland aquatic ecosystems, contributing to regional ichthyofaunal diversity in transitional zones between marine and continental waters.
