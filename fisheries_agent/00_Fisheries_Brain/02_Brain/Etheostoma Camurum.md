# Etheostoma Camurum

## Summary
Etheostoma camurum, commonly known as the bluebreast darter, is a small freshwater fish species endemic to the Ohio River drainage system, particularly documented in the Allegheny River watershed and its tributaries such as French Creek in Pennsylvania and New York. This species has been the subject of reproductive biology, ecological density, and conservation studies, with research indicating its presence across specific drainage records and occasional natural hybridization with related darter species. As a benthic stream inhabitant, E. camurum represents an important indicator species for assessing aquatic ecosystem health and water quality in Appalachian river systems.
