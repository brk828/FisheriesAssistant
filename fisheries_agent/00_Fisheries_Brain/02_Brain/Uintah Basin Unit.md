# Uintah Basin Unit

## Summary
The Uintah Basin Unit is a water development project in the Colorado River Basin. According to this 1989 meeting summary, the Price-San Rafael Biological Opinion included provisions for crediting water accretions (natural water gains) from the Uintah Basin Unit as part of endangered fish recovery efforts.
