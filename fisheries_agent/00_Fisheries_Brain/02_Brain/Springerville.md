# Springerville

## Summary
Springerville is identified as one of the ten Arizona development projects in Maxwell's 1935 water conservation and land reclamation proposal. Each project was to be centered on local water sources and allocated 500,000 acres for development.

## Context

> This 1935 advance print document by George Hebard Maxwell presents an ambitious proposal for water conservation and land reclamation in Arizona and California through comprehensive development of the [[Colorado River]].
