# Oak Ridge

## Summary
Oak Ridge is a location in Tennessee that houses the Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), which discharged radioactive wastes into the Clinch River system via White Oak Creek. This 1956 study found that the Clinch River enters the Oak Ridge area virtually plankton-free from Norris Dam but develops considerable plankton downstream due to warming, nutrient enrichment, and fertilization from ORNL operations.
