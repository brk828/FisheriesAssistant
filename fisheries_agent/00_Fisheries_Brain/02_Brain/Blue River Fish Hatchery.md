# Blue River Fish Hatchery

## Summary
The Blue River Fish Hatchery is an aquaculture facility located near the project area on the Blue River. The road realignment project includes installing a culvert to handle drainage from the hatchery's settling pond, and unauthorized work prior to proper permitting resulted in direct hatchery discharge into the river (bypassing the settling pond), which carried sediment, excess nutrients, and other potentially harmful materials directly into the river.
