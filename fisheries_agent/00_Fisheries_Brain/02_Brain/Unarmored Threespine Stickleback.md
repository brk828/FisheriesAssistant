# Unarmored Threespine Stickleback

## Summary
The Unarmored Threespine Stickleback (*Gasterosteus aculeatus williamsoni*) is an endangered subspecies of the threespine stickleback endemic to the headwaters of the Santa Clara River in northern Los Angeles County, California, distinguished by the absence of the bony lateral plates characteristic of other stickleback populations. This critically rare fish is restricted to small, intermittent shallow stream habitats and represents a unique evolutionary lineage of conservation concern, with studies examining its thermal and respiratory tolerances to understand its specific environmental requirements and aid in recovery efforts. As the only known native population of this subspecies and a component of the Santa Clara River system's unusually high fish diversity for a southern California coastal stream, the Unarmored Threespine Stickleback serves as an important indicator species for freshwater ecosystem health in the region.
