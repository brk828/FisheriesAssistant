# Lepomis Gibbosus

## Summary
Lepomis gibbosus, commonly known as the pumpkinseed sunfish, is a small freshwater centrarchid native to eastern North America that inhabits ponds and shallow lake environments. This species is notable in ecological studies for its well-documented nesting behavior, wherein males construct substrate-specific nests in shallow, sun-exposed waters, demonstrating clear microhabitat preferences and reproductive strategies. As a abundant component of many temperate freshwater fish communities, L. gibbosus serves as an important model organism for understanding sunfish population dynamics, movement patterns, and habitat utilization in lentic ecosystems.
