# Amino Peptidase

## Summary
Amino Peptidase is an enzyme examined at 1 genetic locus in this study. It showed a single band pattern in both Rio Grande and Cuatro Cienegas populations, with the Rio Grande C. cyanoguttatum form displaying greater mobility than the endemic Cuatro Cienegas forms.
