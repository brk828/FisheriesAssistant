# Rabbit Valley Rapids

## Summary
Rabbit Valley Rapids is a location on the Green River in the Colorado River Basin. It served as one of the sampling sites in this study where Colorado Squawfish specimens were collected from the Green River system for genetic analysis using starch gel protein electrophoresis.

## Context

> This draft final report presents a comprehensive biochemical genetic analysis of [[Colorado Squawfish]] (*Ptychocheilus lucius*), a large river cyprinid listed as endangered in 1967. The study examined 833 individuals (151 wild adults, 64 juveniles, 478 young-of-year, and 140 hatchery-reared fish)...
