# West Texas Permian Basin

## Summary
The West Texas Permian Basin is a geological region including the Toyah Basin where several Gammarus-Pecos Complex populations occur. This source indicates it represents the western portion of areas once covered by Permian seas, with Permian marine sediments partially eroded by the Pecos River.
