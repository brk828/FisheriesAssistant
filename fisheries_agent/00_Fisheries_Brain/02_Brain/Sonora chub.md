# Sonora Chub

## Summary
The Sonora Chub (*Gila ditaenia*) is a small endemic cyprinid fish native to the Rio Yaqui basin along the Arizona-Sonora border that has been designated as a federally endangered species requiring active recovery management. As a habitat specialist adapted to specific stream conditions in the southwestern United States and Mexico, the Sonora Chub represents a key conservation priority for desert aquatic ecosystems and has been the subject of coordinated recovery planning efforts by the Desert Fishes Recovery Team and U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. Its protection involves habitat restoration initiatives, population monitoring, and interagency coordination to prevent extinction and restore viable populations in its native range.
