# EDTA Titration

## Summary
EDTA Titration is a chemical analysis method used to measure water hardness by determining calcium and total hardness concentrations. In this study, EDTA Titration was performed four times at monthly intervals throughout the experiment to monitor water chemistry in the experimental pools, allowing researchers to verify that hardness treatments (using calcium carbonate, calcium sulfate, or magnesium carbonate additions) achieved their target levels and to track calcium hardness (ranging 16.7-157 ppm as CaCO₃) and total hardness in the pools.
