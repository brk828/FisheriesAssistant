# John Wesley Powell Centennial

## Summary
The John Wesley Powell Centennial was a commemorative program funded through the U.S. Geological Survey via Senator Goldwater that served as a precedent for the proposed C. Hart Merriam Centennial. This earlier centennial demonstrated the feasibility of obtaining federal legislative funding for large-scale, extended commemorative scientific programs.
