# Post Office Canyon Wells

## Summary
Post Office Canyon Wells are two existing alluvial wells that would remain in continuous operation under the proposed project to supply water to 83 homes. These wells currently supply 35,000,000 gallons per year against a demand of 13,000,000 gallons per year.
