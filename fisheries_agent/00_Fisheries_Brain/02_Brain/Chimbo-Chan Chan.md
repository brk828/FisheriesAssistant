# Chimbo-Chan Chan

## Summary
Chimbo-Chan Chan is a river basin that is part of the larger Guayas drainage system in Ecuador. This basin occurs at lower elevations (700-800 feet) and is the known habitat of Rhoadsia altipinna, a species related to but distinct from the high-elevation R. minor.
