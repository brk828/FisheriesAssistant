# Yuma Dunes

## Summary
The Yuma Dunes are an extremely arid sand dune habitat located south and east of Yuma, Arizona, that supports populations of the Colorado Desert Fringe-Toed Lizard. These dunes are mostly encompassed by military lands where monitoring procedures are being developed to track lizard population trends.
