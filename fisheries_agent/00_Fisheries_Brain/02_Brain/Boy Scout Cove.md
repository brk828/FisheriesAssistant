# Boy Scout Cove

## Summary
Boy Scout Cove is the second-largest spawning aggregation site for razorback suckers in the River Zone of Lake Mohave, identified through remote PIT scanning data. This site shows elevated contact rates during the November through April spawning season, serving as an important secondary spawning location after Black Bar.
