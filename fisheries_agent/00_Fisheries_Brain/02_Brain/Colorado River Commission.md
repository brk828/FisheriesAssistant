# Colorado River Commission

## Summary
The Colorado River Commission is a Nevada state agency that represents Nevada's water and power interests in Colorado River matters. According to this source, the Commission participated in the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program Steering Committee since 1993 and arranged funding from Nevada stakeholders for development of a long-term, consensus-based conservation program intended to provide regulatory certainty while protecting listed species.
