# Drainage Endemism

## Summary
Drainage endemism refers to species restricted to single river drainage systems. This source documents strict drainage endemism in the M. coosae species group, with each of the five recognized species restricted to a single drainage system (Coosa, Cahaba, Tallapoosae, Black Warrior, or Chattahoochee rivers), with no intermediate forms observed between species and distributions reflecting physiographic barriers and drainage divides that have isolated populations.
