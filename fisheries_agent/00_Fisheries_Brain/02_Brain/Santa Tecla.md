# Santa Tecla

## Summary
Santa Tecla is the largest of three lagunas studied (approximately 30 × 70 meters), located about 27 km south-southeast of Cuatro Ciénegas, México, and representing the type locality for Xiphophorus gordoni. This source's transplantation experiments showed that Santa Tecla's population exhibits high intra-population graft acceptance (MST = 34 days) suggesting significant inbreeding, shares similar histocompatibility characteristics with Los Fresnos (reciprocal MST = 19 days), and shows greater genetic differentiation from Quintero, with Quintero hosts rapidly rejecting Santa Tecla fins (MST = 14 days) compared to the reverse direction (MST = 25 days).
