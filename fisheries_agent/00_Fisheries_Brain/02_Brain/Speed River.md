# Speed River

## Summary
The Speed River is a small, meandering agricultural stream in Ontario, Canada that serves as a model study system for investigating organic carbon cycling and nutrient dynamics in temperate freshwater ecosystems. As a slow-flowing, silty lotic system with characteristic substrate composition and seasonal leaf-litter inputs, it has been instrumental in research examining how allochthonous organic matter (particularly dissolved organic carbon from autumn leaf leachate) is processed and utilized by aquatic communities in nutrient-limited stream environments. Its use in controlled experiments simulating natural stream conditions has contributed significantly to understanding energy flow pathways and biogeochemical processes in temperate hard-water streams influenced by agricultural land use.
