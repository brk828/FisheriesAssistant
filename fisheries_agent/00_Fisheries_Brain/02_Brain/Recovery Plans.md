# Recovery Plans

## Summary
Recovery Plans are formal, science-based documents developed by fisheries and wildlife agencies that outline specific strategies, timelines, and resource requirements for restoring threatened or endangered fish species to sustainable population levels. These plans are typically created following species listing decisions and undergo peer review by independent experts to ensure they address critical biological needs such as habitat restoration, population management, and threat mitigation. Recovery Plans serve as the operational framework guiding conservation efforts and inter-agency coordination to achieve species delisting or long-term persistence in their native ecosystems.
