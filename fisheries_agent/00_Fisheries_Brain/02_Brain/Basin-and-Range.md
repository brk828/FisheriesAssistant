# Basin-and-Range

## Summary
The Basin-and-Range Province is a physiographic region characterized by parallel mountain ranges separated by deeply alluviated basins, further subdivided into the endorheic Bonneville Basin and an exorheic zone where the Colorado River enters the Gulf of California. Meda and Plagopterus are restricted to the southern Basin-and-Range Province (lower Colorado River basin), where they were isolated more than 20 million years ago south of the Colorado Plateau as its margin differentiated.
