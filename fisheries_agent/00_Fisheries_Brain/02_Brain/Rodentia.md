# Rodentia

## Summary
Rodentia (rodents) represent 5% of identified mammals at La Lomita Pequeña, including Botta's pocket gopher and hispid cotton rat. Many rodent remains likely represent natural introduction through burrowing rather than cultural deposition, as evidenced by only 7% of unidentified small mammals from burned pithouses showing burning.
