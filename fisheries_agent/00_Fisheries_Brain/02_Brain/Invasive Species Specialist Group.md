# Invasive Species Specialist Group

## Summary
The Invasive Species Specialist Group is one of five disciplinary groups within SSC that addresses thematic conservation issues across taxa rather than focusing on specific taxonomic groups. Its specific mandate is to work to reduce threats from invasive species to biodiversity. Alien species eradication is identified as a potentially controversial issue that requires review by the SSC Chair's Office, Programme Office, and appropriate disciplinary groups before interventions are made.
