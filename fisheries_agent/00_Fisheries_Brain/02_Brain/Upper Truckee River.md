# Upper Truckee River

## Summary
The Upper Truckee River enters Lake Tahoe at the south end, where its mouth features extensive marshes. These marsh habitats may contribute to the dramatically higher parasite infection rates (24% strigeoid trematode metacercaria) observed in south shore Piute sculpin populations compared to north shore fish (0.2%).
