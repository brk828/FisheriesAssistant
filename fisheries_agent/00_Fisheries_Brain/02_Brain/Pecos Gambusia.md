# Pecos Gambusia

## Summary
The Pecos Gambusia (*Gambusia nobilis*) is an extremely rare, critically endangered livebearing fish endemic to a single spring system in the Pecos River drainage of Texas, making it one of the most restricted fish species in North America. As a small poeciliid fish adapted to specific thermal and chemical conditions of desert springs, it represents a conservation priority for maintaining genetic diversity and understanding specialized adaptations to isolated aquatic ecosystems. The species' survival depends on habitat protection and careful management of its limited populations, which have been subject to intensive study and conservation efforts to prevent extinction.
