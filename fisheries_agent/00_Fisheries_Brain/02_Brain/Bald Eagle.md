# Bald Eagle

## Summary
The Bald Eagle (*Haliaeetus leucocephalus*) in aquatic ecology contexts refers to a top predator species that depends on healthy riverine and riparian ecosystems, particularly for nesting habitat and fish prey availability in watersheds like the Gila River Basin. In conservation biology and Endangered Species Act compliance, the Bald Eagle serves as an indicator species for ecosystem integrity, with its presence and reproductive success reflecting the overall health of aquatic habitats and the persistence of native fish communities that comprise its diet. Management considerations for aquatic systems must account for Bald Eagle habitat requirements, including cliff-nesting sites and riparian forest structure that supports both the eagle and the fish populations upon which it depends.
