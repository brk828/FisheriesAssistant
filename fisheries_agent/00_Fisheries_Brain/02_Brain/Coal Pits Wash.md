# Coal Pits Wash

## Summary
Coal Pits Wash is one of eleven tributaries or sub-tributaries in the Virgin River basin where current populations of Virgin spinedace exist. This conservation strategy identifies it as part of the species' current distribution.

## Context

> This November 1994 draft conservation strategy addresses the [[Virgin Spinedace]] (*Lepidomeda mollispinis mollispinis*), a threatened cyprinid endemic to the [[Virgin River]] basin spanning southwestern Utah, northwestern Arizona, and southeastern Nevada.
