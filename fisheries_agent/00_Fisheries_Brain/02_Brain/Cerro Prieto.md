# Cerro Prieto

## Summary
Cerro Prieto is a location in the Lower Colorado River Delta region where a desert pupfish population was sampled in this study. The Cerro Prieto population deviated from other delta samples in its secondary haplotype complement, showing some genetic distinctiveness within the broader Colorado River Delta group of Cyprinodon macularius.
