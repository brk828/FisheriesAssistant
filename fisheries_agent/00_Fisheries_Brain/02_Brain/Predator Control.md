# Predator Control

## Summary
Predator control in Box Canyon Reservoir involved intensive mechanical suppression of invasive Northern Pike through targeted gill netting during spawning aggregations. The program removed 17,464 Northern Pike over 2012-2018, achieving 82.6-99% CPUE reductions and removing an estimated 83-95% of the population in the first year, though a 2018 rebound when effort dropped below 267 net sets demonstrated the need for sustained control efforts.
