# Oligocephalus

## Summary
Oligocephalus is a subgenus within the darter genus Etheostoma (family Percidae) that groups morphologically and ecologically similar species of small freshwater fishes. The subgenus was established to classify darters that share distinctive characteristics and ecological requirements, facilitating systematic organization and understanding of relationships among closely related darter species. This taxonomic designation is important for conservation and management efforts targeting specific darter lineages in North American freshwater ecosystems.
