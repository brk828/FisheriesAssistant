# Cooperative Fish and Wildlife Research Unit

## Summary
The Cooperative Fish and Wildlife Research Unit is a research organization at Colorado State University. In this study, the Unit was conducting concurrent research on electrofishing impacts on trout, and the researchers coordinated with them to share methodological approaches and findings relevant to understanding electrofishing effects on fish.

## Context

> This research proposal, submitted by [[Colorado State University]] Larval Fish Laboratory to the [[U.S. Bureau of Reclamation]], outlines a Phase II controlled experimental study to evaluate the acute and latent effects of electrofishing on endangered [[Colorado River]] fishes.
