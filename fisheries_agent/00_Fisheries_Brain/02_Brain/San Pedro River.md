# San Pedro River

## Summary
The San Pedro River is a critical riparian ecosystem in southeastern Arizona that serves as important habitat for native fish species and other aquatic organisms, though it faces significant conservation challenges from groundwater overdraft, non-native fish invasions, and military operations at nearby Fort Huachuca. As a potential candidate for designation under the National Wild and Scenic Rivers System, the river has been the focus of numerous conservation efforts and legal actions aimed at protecting its endangered and endemic fish species, including the Gila chub, from habitat degradation and extinction. The San Pedro River exemplifies the complex management issues facing southwestern U.S. river systems where water availability, species conservation, and human land use demands frequently conflict.
