# Gate Spring

## Summary
Gate Spring is one of three perennial habitat areas in Redrock Canyon supporting Gila topminnow and is identified as the most vulnerable concentration area. This small area with a closely confined channel is the only surface water in central Redrock Canyon, making it highly attractive to trail users, and a nearby rock art site would draw additional visitors, raising concerns about recreational impacts.
