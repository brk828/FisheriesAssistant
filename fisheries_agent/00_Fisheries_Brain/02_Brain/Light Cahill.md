# Light Cahill

## Summary
Light Cahill is a fly-fishing pattern that most closely imitates Stenonema rubrum and related species among existing patterns. For better imitation, the source recommends experimental patterns using transparent white quill body, white and ginger hackle mixture, and white tails on a No. 12 hook, with pale olive materials as substitutes for closely allied Stenonema species.
