# Canelo Hills Cienega

## Summary
Canelo Hills Cienega is a spring complex in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, covering less than 5 acres that supports high-density populations of Canelo Cienega Fontelicella. The Nature Conservancy purchased the land and applied for water rights to protect this habitat, which also supports Gila intermedia.
