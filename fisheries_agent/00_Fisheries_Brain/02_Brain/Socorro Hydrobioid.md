# Socorro Hydrobioid

## Summary
Amnicola neomexicana, a minute hydrobiid snail (less than 1.6 mm) with a globose shell and eroded nuclear whorls, originally from Sedillo Springs in Socorro County, New Mexico. This source reports it was presumed extirpated from its type locality, though a similar snail discovered at Torreon Springs in 1980 raised hope the species survives, and it co-occurs with the endangered isopod Thermospheroma thermophilum.
