# Water Distribution

## Summary
Water Distribution refers to the allocation and delivery of water across multiple connected aquatic units. This source describes a system using gravity flow canals, multiple culverts, and controlled water surface elevations across ten pond units, with improvements planned to optimize water delivery through inlet redesign, canal maintenance, and extension of the gravity feed canal to Pond #5.
