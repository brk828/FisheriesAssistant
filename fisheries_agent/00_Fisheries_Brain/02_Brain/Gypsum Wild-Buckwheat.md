# Gypsum Wild-Buckwheat

## Summary
Gypsum Wild-Buckwheat is a threatened or endangered plant species that depends on insect pollinators for reproduction. The Service determined that grasshopper control pesticides would likely jeopardize this species by killing its pollinators, and required a 3-mile buffer excluding aerial ultra-low-volume spraying around populations or use of carbaryl bait or biological control instead.
