# Us Fish And Wildlife Service

## Summary
The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service is the primary federal agency responsible for managing and conserving fish species, including those designated as threatened or endangered under the Endangered Species Act, and serves as the lead authority for implementing recovery plans and coordinating conservation efforts with state and local entities. In the context of aquatic ecology and fisheries management, the USFWS develops and enforces policies for species protection, manages habitat restoration projects, and oversees scientific research and monitoring programs related to fish conservation. The agency frequently coordinates with state wildlife agencies, local governments, and conservation organizations to balance resource management objectives with the protection of vulnerable fish populations and their aquatic ecosystems.
