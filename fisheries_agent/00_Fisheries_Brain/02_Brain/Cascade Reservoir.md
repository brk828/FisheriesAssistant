# Cascade Reservoir

## Summary
Cascade Reservoir is an impoundment of the North Fork Payette River in central Idaho, formed in a wide, shallow basin with sloping shorelines. Despite receiving water from naturally oligotrophic sources with low dissolved solids (18-20 ppm) and hardness (8 ppm), the reservoir exhibits high biological productivity and eutrophic conditions characterized by extensive blue-green algae blooms and summer oxygen deficits in deeper waters, attributed to nutrient additions from agricultural return flows and raw sewage.
