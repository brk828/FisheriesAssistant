# Leslie Creek

## Summary
Leslie Creek is a tributary of Whitewater Draw within the Rio Yaqui drainage system of southeastern Arizona that has served as a significant study site for documenting and monitoring native fish fauna distributions and habitat conditions in the region. As part of the broader Whitewater Draw watershed system, Leslie Creek represents an important microhabitat for endemic and threatened fish species, particularly in the context of assessing preferred ecological conditions such as shallow warm waters and sandy substrates. The creek's fish communities and ecological characteristics have been documented in multiple recovery and conservation assessments aimed at understanding the status and habitat requirements of native Yaqui drainage fishes.
