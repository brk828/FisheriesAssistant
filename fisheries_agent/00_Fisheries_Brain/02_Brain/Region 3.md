# Region 3

## Summary
Region 3 encompasses the Midwest and had minimal ESA consultation conflicts during FY 1987-1991, issuing only 3 jeopardy opinions with 1 blocked activity over the five-year period. This was one of five regions that collectively issued only 26 jeopardy opinions across three-fourths of U.S. land area.
