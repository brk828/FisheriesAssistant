# Arizona Native Plant Law

## Summary
The Arizona Native Plant Law (revised 1981) is state legislation protecting native plants. It protects all cactus family members and several other native plant species on Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge, including the Santa Cruz Striped Agave and other plants under consideration for federal protection.
