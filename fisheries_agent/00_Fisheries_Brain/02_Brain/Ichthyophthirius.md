# Ichthyophthirius

## Summary
Ichthyophthirius is a parasitic ciliate protozoan that causes a common and economically significant disease in cultured and wild fish populations, characterized by white spots on the fish's body and fins. This pathogen was a notable concern in early fish culture operations, including warm-water hatcheries culturing non-native species like channel catfish, where infection could threaten the success of propagation efforts. Chemical control methods were developed to manage Ichthyophthirius infections in aquaculture settings, making it a key target for disease management in fisheries and conservation programs.
