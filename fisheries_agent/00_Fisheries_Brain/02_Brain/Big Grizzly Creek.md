# Big Grizzly Creek

## Summary
Big Grizzly Creek is one of three main tributaries feeding Lake Davis reservoir. It was identified as a potential spawning area for northern pike, with physical barriers recommended during spring thaw to prevent pike from spawning and establishing stream populations.
