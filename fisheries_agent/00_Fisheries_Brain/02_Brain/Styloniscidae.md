# Styloniscidae

## Summary
Styloniscidae is a family to which Mulaik incorrectly moved Protrichoniscus in 1960, based on the presence of molar setae while ignoring fundamental differences in pleopod morphology. This revision supports Vandel's original placement of Protrichoniscus (and by extension the related Xilitloniscus) in Trichoniscidae rather than Styloniscidae.

## Context
