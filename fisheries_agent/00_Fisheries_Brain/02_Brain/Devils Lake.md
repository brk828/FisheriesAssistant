# Devils Lake

## Summary
Devils Lake is a saline, hypereutrophic natural lake in northeast North Dakota covering 67,235 surface acres with a maximum depth of 32 feet and average depth of 16 feet. The lake experiences dramatic water level fluctuations and serves as the terminus of a 3,814 square mile watershed. Approximately 13,000 striped bass fingerlings were stocked there in 1977, but extensive sampling (over 90,000 netting hours) captured only one striped bass, and genetic analysis of 106 Morone specimens found no evidence of striped bass reproduction or hybridization with white bass.
