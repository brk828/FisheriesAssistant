# Riparian Ecosystems

## Summary
Riparian ecosystems are the transitional zones of vegetation and habitat along streams, rivers, and other water bodies that serve as critical interfaces between aquatic and terrestrial environments. In fish science and conservation, these ecosystems are essential for maintaining water quality, temperature regulation, bank stability, and providing food sources and refuge for fish populations, making them fundamental to the health and sustainability of aquatic communities. The management and protection of riparian areas is therefore central to fisheries conservation and aquatic ecology, as degradation of these zones directly impacts fish habitat quality and ecosystem function.
