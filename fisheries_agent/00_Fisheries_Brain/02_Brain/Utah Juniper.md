# Utah Juniper

## Summary
Utah Juniper (Juniperus osteosperma) is a conifer species found in the Pinyon-Juniper Woodland community of the Mono Basin. It occurs at variable elevations between 4,000 and 8,000+ feet in association with Single-leaf Pinyon, Big Sagebrush, and Black Sagebrush.
