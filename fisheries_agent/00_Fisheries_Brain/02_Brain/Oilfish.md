# Oilfish

## Summary
Oilfish (Ruvettus pretiosus) is a deep-water fish species from the family Gempylidae that typically inhabits depths between 50 and 400 fathoms. This source documents the first known occurrence of oilfish in the eastern Pacific Ocean, with a 475 mm specimen caught off Encinitas, California in 1942, and notes that its flesh contains oil with strong purgative properties that could have potential commercial value.
