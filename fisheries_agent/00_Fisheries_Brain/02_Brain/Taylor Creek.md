# Taylor Creek

## Summary
Taylor Creek is a stream at the south end of Lake Tahoe near documented Piute sculpin spawning areas. The extensive marshes at the mouth of Taylor Creek may contribute to higher parasite infection rates (24% strigeoid trematode metacercaria) observed in south shore sculpin populations compared to north shore fish.
