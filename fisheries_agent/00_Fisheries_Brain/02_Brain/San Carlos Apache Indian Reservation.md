# San Carlos Apache Indian Reservation

## Summary
The San Carlos Apache Indian Reservation near Bylas contains three hot springs that were surveyed for native fish populations. Middle Spring had dried completely the previous year with no fish present, while Bylas Springs and Salt Creek both contained Gila Topminnow (comprising 6.7% and 3.6% of fish fauna respectively) along with mosquitofish, though a barrier at Bylas Springs continued to leak water near a salt cedar stump and through its west side.
