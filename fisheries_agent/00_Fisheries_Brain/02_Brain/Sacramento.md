# Sacramento

## Summary
Sacramento is both a residence area for categorizing angler populations and an administrative region (Region 2) in California's inland fisheries system. In 1960, this region contributed 21.8% of the state's trout catch (6,200,000 fish) and dominated catfish production with nearly 50% of the state total (4,490,000 fish), and was one of two major striped bass regions.
