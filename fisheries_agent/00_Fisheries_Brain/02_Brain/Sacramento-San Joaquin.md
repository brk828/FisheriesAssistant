# Sacramento-San Joaquin

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin River system, including its Delta and San Pablo Bay, is the spawning grounds for the striped bass population that migrates to Tomales Bay. Fifteen tagged fish (23% of returns) were recaptured in this system during April-May spawning migrations, and four fish originally tagged in this system were later caught in Tomales Bay, confirming that Tomales Bay striped bass are part of this population and that factors controlling migrations from this system are the most important determinant of Tomales Bay striped bass abundance.

## Context

> This tagging study (1963-1966) investigated the migration patterns of [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*) in [[Tomales Bay]], California, located 40 miles northwest of [[San Francisco]]. A total of 207 striped bass were tagged using disk-dangler tags, with 65 tag returns received by July 1968....
