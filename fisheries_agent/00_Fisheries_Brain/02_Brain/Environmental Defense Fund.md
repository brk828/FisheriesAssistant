# Environmental Defense Fund

## Summary
The Environmental Defense Fund is one of the environmental organization participants in the Upper Colorado River Recovery Program. This conservation group was part of the 1988 cooperative agreement that brought together federal agencies, states, water users, and environmental organizations to recover endangered fish while allowing water development to continue.
