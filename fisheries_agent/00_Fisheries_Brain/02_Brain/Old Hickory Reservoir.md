# Old Hickory Reservoir

## Summary
Old Hickory Reservoir is a major impoundment on the Cumberland River in Tennessee that serves as a comparative case study in aquatic ecology and fish disease research, particularly regarding reservoir epizootics and the impacts of dam construction on native mussel and fish communities. The reservoir is notable in ichthyological literature for hosting documented outbreaks of fish diseases and serving as a reference site for understanding how impoundment alters native freshwater fauna, including the failure of unionid mussels to successfully colonize post-impoundment habitats despite the presence of suitable fish hosts. Its ecological significance lies in demonstrating the complex relationships between reservoir hydrology, fish population dynamics, disease prevalence, and the collapse of historically dominant benthic communities following dam construction.
