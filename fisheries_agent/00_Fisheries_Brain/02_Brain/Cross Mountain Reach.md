# Cross Mountain Reach

## Summary
Cross Mountain Reach is a unique cliff-bound gorge habitat at river mile 53 on the Yampa River where numerous adult squawfish have been captured, characterized by deep, swift conditions. This 1982 study found that YOY habitat was limited and available primarily at low flows due to the deep, swift nature, adult habitat was very extensive across a broad flow range with approximately half of maximum habitat retained even at 10,000 cfs (where CRFP captured the most adult squawfish), but no spawning habitat was demonstrated at any flow due to lack of gravel-cobble substrate within the gorge.
