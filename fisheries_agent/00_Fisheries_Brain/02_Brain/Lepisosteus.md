# Lepisosteus

## Summary
Lepisosteus is a genus of primitive freshwater and euryhaline fishes commonly known as gars, characterized by elongated bodies, specialized musculature for predatory feeding, and the ability to inhabit diverse hydrological conditions across Atlantic Slope drainages and other North American aquatic systems. These highly adaptable fishes are notable for their diadromous or euryhaline life history strategies, allowing them to persist across multiple drainage systems and maintain ecological connectivity in freshwater ecosystems. As anatomically distinct bony fishes with unique muscular architecture, gars serve as important functional predators in stream and pond communities and represent a significant lineage for comparative ichthyological study.
