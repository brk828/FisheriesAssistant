# Forest Shreve

## Summary
Forest Shreve was a researcher who contributed to the development of ecological concepts in the Southwest through his treatment of the Sonoran Desert published in 1951. His work was part of the broader development of ecological understanding that built upon and extended Merriam's original life-zone concept during the mid-20th century.
