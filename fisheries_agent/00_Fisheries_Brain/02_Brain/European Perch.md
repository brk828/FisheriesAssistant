# European Perch

## Summary
The European perch (Perca fluviatilis) is a freshwater fish species native to European waters that serves as an important model organism in fisheries science and aquatic ecology research. It is commonly studied for population dynamics, growth patterns, and gillnet selectivity due to its ecological significance in freshwater ecosystems and its value in both commercial and recreational fisheries. The species has been utilized in various morphometric and theoretical studies to understand fish capture efficiency and size-dependent vulnerability to different fishing gear types.
