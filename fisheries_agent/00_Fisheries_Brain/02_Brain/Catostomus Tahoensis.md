# Catostomus Tahoensis

## Summary
Catostomus tahoensis, commonly known as the Tahoe sucker, is a species of freshwater sucker endemic to the Lake Tahoe basin and associated drainages in the Sierra Nevada region of California and Nevada. This catostomid fish is morphologically distinguished by specific characteristics of its caudal peduncle and body proportions, which differentiate it from related sucker species such as C. rimiculus, C. microps, and C. griseus. As a native fish species in the Lahontan ichthyofauna, C. tahoensis represents an important component of high-elevation lacustrine ecosystems and has conservation significance in the context of introduced species impacts and habitat alteration in the Lake Tahoe system.
