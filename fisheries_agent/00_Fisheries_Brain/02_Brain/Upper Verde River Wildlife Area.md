# Upper Verde River Wildlife Area

## Summary
Upper Verde River Wildlife Area is a location in Arizona (34.867749°N, 112.401625°W) where Redeye Bass were found during surveys. This source reports that dietary analysis of fish from this area (previously misidentified as Smallmouth Bass) showed lower piscivory rates and preference for invertebrate prey consistent with Redeye Bass, though gut contents did include some native fish species.
