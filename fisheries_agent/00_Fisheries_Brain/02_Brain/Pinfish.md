# Pinfish

## Summary
Pinfish (*Lagodon rhomboides*) is a small estuarine fish species commonly found in coastal waters and saltwater systems of the southeastern United States, particularly in Louisiana and other Gulf Coast regions. As a seasonal immigrant that spawns offshore with juveniles dominating estuarine populations during summer months, pinfish play an important ecological role in estuary food webs and are frequently documented in ichthyological surveys of coastal fish communities. The species is notable for its salinity tolerance and presence in highly saline environments, making it a key indicator organism for understanding estuarine fish assemblages and their responses to environmental conditions.
