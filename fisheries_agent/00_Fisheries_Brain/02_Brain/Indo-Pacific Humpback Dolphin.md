# Indo-Pacific Humpback Dolphin

## Summary
The Indo-Pacific humpback dolphin is a marine mammal species for which mark-recapture population estimation methods are applicable. This study cites dolphin abundance surveys as one of the contemporary fisheries and wildlife applications (among >1,650 articles since 2016) where the evaluated closed population mark-recapture estimators, particularly the bias-adjusted methods, can be appropriately applied.
