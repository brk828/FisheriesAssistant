# Lewis and Clark Reservoir

## Summary
Lewis and Clark Reservoir is a reservoir in South Dakota where Smallmouth Buffalo populations have been studied. Research at this reservoir found that Smallmouth Buffalo reach maturity at a much later age (age VII-X) compared to populations in warmer southern waters like Arkansas ponds (age II), demonstrating how environmental conditions affect the species' life history.
