# Sediment Subcommittee

## Summary
The Sediment Subcommittee is a working group within the Recovery Implementation Program that addresses sediment-related research priorities. By February 1990, the subcommittee had finalized a discussion paper prioritizing sediment research, and investigations on the Yampa River showed minimal sediment impact to date, prompting cost-benefit evaluation of future sediment research.
