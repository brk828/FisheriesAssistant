# Zannichellia

## Summary
Zannichellia is a submergent aquatic plant that appeared in Apache Lake by 1975 as part of a fundamental ecosystem transformation. This vegetation establishment occurred following stabilization of downstream reservoirs for pump-generation operations, representing a total change from 1973 when Apache Lake had no true riparian or aquatic plants along hundreds of meters of shoreline.
