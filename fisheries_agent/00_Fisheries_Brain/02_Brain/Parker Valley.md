# Parker Valley

## Summary
Parker Valley is a location in the lower Colorado River system containing a canal system. In early 1980, two young razorback suckers (12-15 inches) were captured in Parker Valley's canal system during annual canal drawdowns, likely originating from the lower Colorado River between Parker Dam and Headgate Rock Dam.
