# University of São Paulo

## Summary
The University of São Paulo in Brazil contributed taxonomic expertise through Dr. and Mrs. Ernest Marcus, who identified the opisthobranch specimens (sea slugs and nudibranchs) for this field guide. This institution was one of several international collaborators on the Puerto Peñasco invertebrate survey.
