# Salmo

## Summary
Salmo is a genus of freshwater and anadromous fish in the family Salmonidae that includes Atlantic salmon and brown trout, characterized by their predatory behavior, cold-water habitat requirements, and cultural and economic importance to fisheries. The taxonomic classification of Salmo has been subject to scientific debate, particularly regarding the proper placement of related groups like Parasalmo as either distinct genera or subgenera, with ichthyologists disagreeing on whether splitting these taxa better reflects evolutionary relationships and aids in fisheries management and conservation efforts.
