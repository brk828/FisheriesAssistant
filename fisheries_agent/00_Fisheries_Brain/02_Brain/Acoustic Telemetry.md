# Acoustic Telemetry

## Summary
Acoustic telemetry is a tracking method using sound-emitting tags and underwater receivers to monitor fish movements. This source used acoustic tags (80-86 mm long, 12-19 g in water) programmed to emit signals approximately every minute with 3-year battery life, achieving detection ranges of 700-900 m and detection efficiency of 0.78-0.99 across receiver gates to track 69 Razorback Suckers over 18 km of river-reservoir habitat.
