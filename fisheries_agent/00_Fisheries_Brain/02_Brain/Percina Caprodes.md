# Percina Caprodes

## Summary
Percina caprodes, commonly known as the logperch, is a benthic darter species native to North American freshwater systems that exhibits notable geographic variation in reproductive characteristics such as egg complement size and fecundity. As a small cyprinodont fish, it serves as an important subject in comparative studies of darter biology, morphology, and life history traits relevant to understanding freshwater fish diversity and ecological adaptation. The species has been documented in scientific collections and natural history surveys, contributing to our knowledge of regional fish fauna and the conservation status of small benthic fishes in riverine ecosystems.
