# Xeroriparian Mixed Scrub

## Summary
Xeroriparian Mixed Scrub is a riparian habitat type associated with ephemeral water supply found throughout most drainages at Spur Cross Ranch, including Cottonwood Creek. This habitat is dominated by mesquite, catclaw, desert broom, and burrobush, and would be among the habitats directly impacted by the planned development.
