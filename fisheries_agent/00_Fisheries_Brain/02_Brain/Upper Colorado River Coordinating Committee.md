# Upper Colorado River Coordinating Committee

## Summary
The Upper Colorado River Coordinating Committee was formed in 1984 by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service along with the Bureau of Reclamation and representatives from Colorado, Wyoming, and New Mexico after FWS withdrew an initial draft conservation plan. According to this source, this committee was instrumental in developing the cooperative approach that led to the Upper Basin Recovery Program, with the core concept that the program would serve as a reasonable and prudent alternative in Section 7 consultations while comprehensively addressing habitat needs.
