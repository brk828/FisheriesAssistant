# Van Dorn Bottle

## Summary
A Van Dorn Bottle is a water sampling device used to collect water samples at specific depths in lakes and reservoirs. In this Lake Powell study, it was used to collect water samples for dissolved oxygen analysis, which were then tested using the Winkler Method to document oxygen depletion patterns throughout the reservoir.
