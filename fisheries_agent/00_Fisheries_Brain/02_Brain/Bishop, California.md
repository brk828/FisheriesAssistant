# Bishop, California

## Summary
Bishop, California became the permanent headquarters location for the Desert Fishes Council as of February 1990. The new office was constructed at P.O. Box 337, Bishop, California 93514, with telephone number (619) 872-8751, following the retirement of Executive Secretary E.P. Pister from the California Department of Fish and Game.
