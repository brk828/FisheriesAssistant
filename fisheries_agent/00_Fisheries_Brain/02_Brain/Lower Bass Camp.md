# Lower Bass Camp

## Summary
Lower Bass Camp is a location on the Colorado River approximately 1 kilometer upstream of Shinumo Creek inflow. This source documents that one adult razorback sucker was captured near Lower Bass Camp in April 1984 using electrofishing, and the fish was marked with a Floy tag; an unverified sighting of another razorback sucker was also reported at Bass Camp near Shinumo Creek in 1989.
