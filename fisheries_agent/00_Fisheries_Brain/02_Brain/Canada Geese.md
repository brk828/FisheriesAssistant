# Canada Geese

## Summary
Canada Geese (Branta canadensis) are waterfowl that utilize wetland habitats created along the Colorado River following Glen Canyon Dam closure in 1963. These wetland and backwater habitats became important for Canada Geese and other migratory waterfowl, though approximately 95% of marshes were eliminated between 1983-1986 due to flooding events.
