# Lower Sonoran Life Zone

## Summary
The Lower Sonoran Life Zone is the ecological classification for the lower Colorado River basin where this study took place. It is characterized by hot, dry climate with annual precipitation of 7.5-12.6 cm, maximum air temperatures of 46-52°C, and sparse vegetation including palo verde, ironwood, mesquite, and saltcedar.
