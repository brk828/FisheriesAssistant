# Utah Lake

## Summary
Utah Lake is a large, shallow freshwater lake in Utah, USA, characterized by dynamic habitat conditions including significant fluctuations in surface area (ranging from approximately 28,000 to 38,000 hectares). This source describes it as having littoral and pelagic zones that support both invasive Common Carp populations and the endangered June Sucker, with lake area fluctuations strongly influencing carp recruitment dynamics through a positive environmental covariate effect (γ = 1.37) in the stock-recruitment relationship.
