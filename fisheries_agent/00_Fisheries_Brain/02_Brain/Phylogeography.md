# Phylogeography

## Summary
Phylogeography examines the geographic distribution of genetic lineages within and among species. This source used mitochondrial DNA (ND2 gene) analysis across 182 specimens to reveal phylogeographic patterns supporting recognition of four new species within the Redeye Bass complex, with genetic divergence ranging from 0.00-11.52% and patterns suggesting historical headwater drainage connections or past widespread distributions explaining relationships among species from different river systems.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary:** This study revises the [[Redeye Bass]] (*Micropterus coosae*) species complex, originally described by Hubbs and Bailey (1940) from the Mobile River basin, Chattahoochee, and Savannah rivers.
