# Ulothrix

## Summary
Ulothrix is a filamentous alga found in the highly radioactive seepage from Pit IV (water: 3,103 counts/min/ml) that concentrated radioactivity 45× in its ash (14 × 10⁴ counts/min/gram from a 2.9 mg sample). The ash represented only 17% of dry algal weight, indicating most radioactivity was retained in non-volatile cellular components.
