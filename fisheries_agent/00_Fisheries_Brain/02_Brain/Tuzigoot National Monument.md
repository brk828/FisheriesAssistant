# Tuzigoot National Monument

## Summary
Tuzigoot National Monument is a protected area located within the Verde Valley, Arizona. According to this 1990-1991 survey, it is one of multiple protected areas in the Verde River corridor region that encompasses approximately 60 river miles from above Clarkdale to below Camp Verde.
