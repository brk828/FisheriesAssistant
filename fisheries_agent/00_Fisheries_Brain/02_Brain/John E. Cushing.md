# John E. Cushing

## Summary
John E. Cushing was a researcher affiliated with the University of California Santa Barbara who co-authored this 1964 study on trout erythrocyte antigens. His prior work with Sprague (1952) on salmonid erythrocyte antigens was cited as foundational research that this study built upon.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
