# Tomales Bay

## Summary
Tomales Bay is a narrow estuarine bay extending 10 miles southwest from the Pacific Coast, located 40 miles northwest of San Francisco, California. This study found it serves as a non-spawning habitat for mature striped bass from the Sacramento-San Joaquin River system, with fish showing seasonal absence during April-June spawning periods and returning in fall and winter, though the scarcity of small bass indicates no local spawning occurs there.

## Context

> This tagging study (1963-1966) investigated the migration patterns of [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*) in [[Tomales Bay]], California, located 40 miles northwest of [[San Francisco]]. A total of 207 striped bass were tagged using disk-dangler tags, with 65 tag returns received by July 1968....
