# San Marcos, Texas

## Summary
San Marcos, Texas (in Hays County) is the location where Haideoporus texanus was discovered in the Edwards Aquifer. The collection site is an artesian well at the Aquatic Station, located approximately 670 m from San Marcos Springs, and this locality is the type-locality for numerous subterranean organisms including the Texas Blind Salamander.

## Context

> This study describes the larva of *[[Haideoporus texanus]]*, the first hypogeous (subterranean) dytiscid beetle discovered in North America.
