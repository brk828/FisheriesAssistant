# Top Minnow

## Summary
Top Minnow (Fundulus diaphanus) is a small freshwater fish species found in New York State waters. According to this source, it serves as a second intermediate host in the Bass Tapeworm life cycle, acquiring larval tapeworms by feeding on infected copepods, with the larvae then encysting in its body cavity.
