# Pahranagat Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Pahranagat Roundtail Chub is a cyprinid (minnow family) fish species endemic to the Pahranagat Valley in Nevada, representing a geographically isolated population of conservation interest within the Great Basin region. As a native desert fish species, it serves as an important indicator of aquatic ecosystem health in arid and semi-arid environments, and its population dynamics are monitored in fisheries and conservation surveys examining native fish distributions and abundances. The species exemplifies the management challenges faced by resource agencies in protecting endemic fishes within restricted ranges that are vulnerable to habitat loss, water diversions, and introduced species competition.
