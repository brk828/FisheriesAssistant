# Division of Environmental Management

## Summary
The Division of Environmental Management conducts quarterly water quality monitoring in North Carolina rivers. Their monitoring indicated the Yadkin River had a good Water Quality Index rating, though the Biological Index based on benthic macroinvertebrates rated "fair" for the Yadkin River, while the South Yadkin River showed poor benthic diversity due to heavy silt load.
