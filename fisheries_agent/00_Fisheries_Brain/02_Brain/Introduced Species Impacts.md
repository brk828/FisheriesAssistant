# Introduced Species Impacts

## Summary
Introduced species impacts refer to the ecological effects of non-native species on native populations and ecosystems. This study demonstrates that introduced white bass and other non-native predators (walleye, largemouth bass, channel catfish, green sunfish) have contributed to the decline of June sucker from millions to fewer than 1000 individuals, with predation by young white bass identified as a major cause of recruitment failure in the now-endangered native sucker population.
