# Rio Panuco

## Summary
The Rio Panuco is a major Atlantic slope river basin in Mexico that drains to the Gulf of Mexico. This source documents it as home to multiple endemic fish species including several Dionda species, Ictalurus mexicanus, and Ictiobus labiosus, and notes that cave systems in the Papaloapan basin (which may refer to related drainages) contain specialized troglobitic fauna.
