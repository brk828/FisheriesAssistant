# Lucania browni

## Summary
Lucania browni (Brown's Killifish) is a small killifish species of the family Fundulidae described in 1907 from a hot spring in northeastern Lower California, Mexico. This species demonstrates remarkable adaptation to extreme conditions, living in water at 128°F (53°C) with 25% soluble salts, and is distinguished from other Lucania species by its shorter ventral fins and distinctive coloration patterns including darker blotches forming a broken lateral band.
