# Sonoran Chub Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Sonoran Chub Recovery Plan is a recovery planning document for the Sonoran chub, a southwestern desert fish species. According to the December 1985 Desert Fishes Recovery Team meeting, work on this plan was scheduled to begin following the species' listing and completion of the Yaqui Fishes Recovery Plan, with a tentative target date of December 1986 for the draft plan. The team planned a field trip to Sycamore Canyon in summer 1986 to visit Sonoran chub habitat as part of the planning process.
