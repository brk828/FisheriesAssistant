# Sacramento-San Joaquin River Delta

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin River Delta is a major estuarine system in California that serves as key habitat for white sturgeon. This source identifies it as one of the primary locations where white sturgeon are found in inland bays, sloughs, and larger rivers north of Monterey Bay.

## Context

> This 1954 California Department of Fish and Game document announces the reopening of recreational sturgeon fishing after a 35-year closure.
