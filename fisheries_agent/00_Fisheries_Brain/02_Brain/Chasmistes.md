# Chasmistes

## Summary
Chasmistes is a genus of large cyprinid fishes endemic to western North American freshwater systems, characterized by distinctive meristic features and specialized morphological adaptations. Members of this genus, which include species such as the cui-ui, are of significant conservation concern due to their restricted distributions and vulnerability to habitat degradation, making them important subjects of systematic study and recovery efforts. The genus has been examined through meristic analysis and comparative morphology to clarify taxonomic relationships and distinguish genuine species from potential hybrids within lacustrine and riverine environments.
