# Invasive Species Management

## Summary
Invasive Species Management in fish science encompasses the coordinated strategies and actions designed to prevent, control, or eradicate non-native fish species that have become established in aquatic ecosystems and threaten native biodiversity, ecosystem functions, or fisheries resources. This management approach integrates monitoring of range expansion, habitat protection, population control measures, and restoration of impacted native species, while working across jurisdictional boundaries including international borders where invasive species have spread. Effective invasive species management requires collaboration among resource agencies, scientific understanding of invasion pathways and ecological impacts, and sometimes difficult decisions about maintaining refugial populations of native species while addressing the threat posed by established invaders.
