# International Boundary And Water Commission

## Summary
The International Boundary and Water Commission (IBWC) is a binational U.S.-Mexico treaty organization responsible for managing shared water resources and environmental conditions in transboundary river systems, particularly the Colorado River and Rio Grande, which directly impacts fish habitat, species conservation, and aquatic ecosystem management in the Lower Colorado River Basin. In the context of fish science and conservation, the IBWC coordinates with agencies like the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation and academic institutions to address fish population recovery, habitat restoration, and water quality issues affecting native aquatic species in internationally shared waters. The commission's role is essential for implementing cross-border conservation strategies and ensuring compliance with environmental regulations that protect fish communities in sensitive ecosystems such as the Cienega de Santa Clara and lower basin reaches.
