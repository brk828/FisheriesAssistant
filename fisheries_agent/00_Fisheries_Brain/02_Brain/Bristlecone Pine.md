# Bristlecone Pine

## Summary
Bristlecone Pine is a tree species adapted to extreme high-elevation conditions. The lecture notes mention it as an example of trees at timberline that show special adaptations to severe wind, cold, and water scarcity, exhibiting gnarled and twisted growth forms.
