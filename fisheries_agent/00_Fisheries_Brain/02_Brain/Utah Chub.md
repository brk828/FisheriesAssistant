# Utah Chub

## Summary
The Utah Chub (Gila atraria) is a cyprinid fish species native to the Great Basin region of western North America that has become significant in fisheries science and conservation biology contexts. As a native cyprinid, it is often referenced alongside other species in discussions of aquatic ecosystem management, particularly regarding habitat interactions and the effects of invasive species introductions. The species appears in scientific literature examining surrogate species selection and response protocols for managing fish communities in western aquatic systems.
