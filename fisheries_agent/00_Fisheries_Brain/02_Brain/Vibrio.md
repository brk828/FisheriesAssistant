# Vibrio

## Summary
Vibrio is a bacterial infection that affected tilapia in the Salton Sea and played a key role in the 1996 Avian Botulism outbreak. The Vibrio infections weakened the tilapia fish, allowing botulism spores to penetrate their tissues, which then led to the poisoning of birds that consumed the infected fish.
