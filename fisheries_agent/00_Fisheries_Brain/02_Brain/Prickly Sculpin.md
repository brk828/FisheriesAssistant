# Prickly Sculpin

## Summary
The Prickly Sculpin (*Cottus asper*) is a small benthic freshwater fish native to coastal drainages of the Pacific Northwest that serves as an important indicator species for stream health and habitat quality in aquatic ecology studies. As a bottom-dwelling predator sensitive to water quality degradation and habitat loss, this sculpins presence or absence in surveys reflects the integrity of cool, rocky stream ecosystems and can be used to assess the impacts of anthropogenic disturbance on native fish communities. The species is documented in historical fish fauna surveys as part of efforts to monitor the decline and conservation status of native fish assemblages in California river systems during the mid-to-late 20th century.
