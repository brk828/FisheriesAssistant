# James E. Deacon

## Summary
James E. Deacon is an ichthyologist and conservation biologist who has been instrumental in advancing desert fish conservation in the American Southwest, most notably as co-editor of the seminal work *Battle Against Extinction: Native Fish Management in the American West*. His contributions to the field encompass both scientific research and advocacy for native fish species threatened by habitat loss and extinction in arid regions. Deacon's work has helped establish desert fish conservation as a collaborative, interdisciplinary field that integrates scientific, historical, and ethical perspectives on aquatic ecosystem management.
