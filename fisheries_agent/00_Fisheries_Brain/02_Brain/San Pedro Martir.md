# San Pedro Martir

## Summary
San Pedro Martir is a region in Baja California, Mexico that contains the Rio San Rafael, home to the endemic San Pedro Martir Trout. This area was the destination for a field trip during the November 1990 Desert Fishes Council symposium held in Ensenada, Mexico.
