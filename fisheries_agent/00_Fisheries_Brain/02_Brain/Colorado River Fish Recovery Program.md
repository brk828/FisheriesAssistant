# Colorado River Fish Recovery Program

## Summary
The Colorado River Fish Recovery Program is a comprehensive conservation initiative designed to restore and protect endangered native fish species in the Colorado River Basin through a combination of habitat management, captive breeding, and population monitoring strategies. The program focuses on species such as razorback suckers and bonytail chubs, employing techniques including broodstock collection, refugium maintenance in isolated lentic habitats, and removal of non-native fish species that compete with or prey upon native populations. This multifaceted approach represents a collaborative effort to counteract the ecological impacts of river modification and species decline while maintaining viable populations of Colorado River Basin endemics.
