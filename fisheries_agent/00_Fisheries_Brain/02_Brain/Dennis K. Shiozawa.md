# Dennis K. Shiozawa

## Summary
Dr. Dennis K. Shiozawa was the co-author with Dr. Williams of the cutthroat trout research proposal developed beginning in late fall 1986. Dr. Shiozawa learned basic tissue culture techniques from Mr. Rosenfeld in winter 1987 but subsequently modified these techniques for work with trout cells and chromosomes independently, and he structured his laboratory specifically to conduct the analyses outlined in the research proposal.
