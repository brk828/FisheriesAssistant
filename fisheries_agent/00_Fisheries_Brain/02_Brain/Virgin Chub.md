# Virgin Chub

## Summary
The Virgin Chub is an endemic fish species found exclusively in the Virgin River, a tributary system within the Colorado River Basin, and represents a significant conservation concern due to its limited geographic range and specialized habitat requirements. Like other Virgin River endemics such as the Virgin Spinedace and Woundfin, the Virgin Chub exhibits specific adaptations to the river's historical water chemistry and flow conditions, including tolerance for elevated total dissolved solids (TDS). Its vulnerability to habitat modification, water management practices, and potential introgression from non-native chub species makes it a focal species for conservation planning efforts in the Virgin River ecosystem.
