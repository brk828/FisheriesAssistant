# Contracaecum brachyurum

## Summary
Contracaecum brachyurum is a nematode parasite found near the esophagus and in viscera of fish in Imperial Reservoir. The study found these nematodes were less common than trematodes in most species except green sunfish, and they showed no apparent effect on fish condition.

## Context

> This Master's thesis (1971) investigated the ecology of juvenile fish (10-100 mm) in [[Imperial Reservoir]], a 2955-hectare impoundment on the [[Lower Colorado River]] north of Yuma, Arizona. The study was motivated by planned channelization that could alter critical spawning and rearing habitats....
