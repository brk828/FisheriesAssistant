# Gatun Lake

## Summary
Gatun Lake is a body of water in Panama where Peacock Cichlid (Cichla ocellaris) "effectively eliminated six of the eight common fish species and drastically reduced a seventh." This case is cited as evidence of the potential ecological damage that Cichla introductions could cause in Florida, including possible increases in mosquito populations due to elimination of fish that feed on mosquito larvae.
