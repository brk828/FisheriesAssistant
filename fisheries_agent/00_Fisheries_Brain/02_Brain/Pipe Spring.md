# Pipe Spring

## Summary
Pipe Spring is one of five named springs at San Bernardino Ranch in the Rio Yaqui drainage system. This management plan indicates that Pipe Spring historically fed a cienega area that has since been converted to cultivated fields, and its water should be released to help restore cienega wetland habitat.
