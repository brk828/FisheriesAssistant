# Pinacate Lava Flows

## Summary
The Pinacate Lava Flows are geological formations that altered the drainage pattern of the Rio Sonoyta in the desert region between Arizona and Sonora, Mexico. According to this source, these lava flows diverted the Rio Sonoyta southward, causing the river to now sink into sand on the east side of the flow rather than reaching the Gulf of California as it did historically, which isolated the Desert Pupfish population and allowed it to diverge morphologically over thousands of years.
