# Rock Bass

## Summary
Rock bass (Ambloplites rupestris) is a small to medium-sized centrarchid (sunfish family) predator that inhabits freshwater streams and lakes, characteristically occupying deep pool habitats where it preys on smaller fishes and aquatic invertebrates. In mid-20th century ichthyological surveys of Kentucky and similar temperate regions, rock bass served as an important indicator species for pool-dependent habitat conditions and contributed to the trophic structure of warm-water fish communities alongside other centrarchids like smallmouth bass. The species' distribution and relative abundance in stream ecosystems reflect local hydrology and substrate complexity, making it a relevant subject for fisheries management assessments and aquatic community ecology studies.
