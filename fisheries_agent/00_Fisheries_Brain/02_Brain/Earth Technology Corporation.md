# Earth Technology Corporation

## Summary
Earth Technology Corporation was a consulting firm contracted by the Arizona Department of Water Resources in 1984 to conduct a feasibility reassessment of the Leslie Canyon Dam site. Their hydrologic reanalysis determined that the watershed's actual average annual runoff of 643 acre-feet was only about one-third of the original 1,900 acre-feet estimate, leading to the conclusion that the site was not feasible for a viable recreational reservoir.
