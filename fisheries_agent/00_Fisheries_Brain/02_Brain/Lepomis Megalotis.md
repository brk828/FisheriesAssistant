# Lepomis Megalotis

## Summary
*Lepomis megalotis*, commonly known as the longear sunfish, is a small freshwater centrarchid native to North America that plays an important ecological role as both a forage fish for larger predators and an insectivore controlling aquatic invertebrate populations in stream and river ecosystems. This species is significant in community ecology studies due to its interactions with other fish species, including predation on eggs and larvae of other fishes and vulnerability to predation by larger species, making it a key component in understanding food web dynamics in warmwater fisheries. As a relatively abundant and widespread sunfish, *L. megalotis* is frequently encountered in ecological surveys and serves as an indicator species for assessing the health and structure of fish communities in freshwater aquatic systems.
