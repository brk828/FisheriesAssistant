# Brush Mouse

## Summary
The Brush Mouse (Peromyscus boylii) is a nocturnal rodent that occurs at higher elevations in brushier and more mesic habitats than the Cactus Mouse. In Riparian Forest East habitat, it occurred at densities of 7.3-18.2 individuals per hectare and was one of the dominant rodent species in that vegetation association.
