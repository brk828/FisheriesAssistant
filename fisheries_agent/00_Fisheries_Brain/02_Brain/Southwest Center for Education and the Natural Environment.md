# Southwest Center for Education and the Natural Environment

## Summary
SCENE is a non-profit organization with five years of experience in K-12 science education programs. In this 1997 proposal, SCENE serves as the coordinating organization for a three-year program to involve high school students in authentic scientific research at Arizona State University, bringing educational expertise and established programs like Science Connections (serving over 1,000 students annually) and Super Science Saturday (attracting 4,000-5,000 visitors).
