# International Association Of Fish And Wildlife Agencies

## Summary
The International Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies (IAFWA) is a coalition organization representing state fish and wildlife agencies across the United States that serves as a collective voice in advocating for state interests in natural resource management and conservation policy. As demonstrated in 1994 documents, IAFWA and its member states (such as the 14 Midwestern states represented through AMFWA) engage with federal agencies like the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, USDA Forest Service, and others to coordinate ecosystem management approaches and ensure consistent implementation of conservation practices across regions. The organization functions as a critical institutional link between state-level fisheries and wildlife management authorities and federal resource management agencies in developing cooperative agreements and memoranda of understanding on conservation initiatives.
