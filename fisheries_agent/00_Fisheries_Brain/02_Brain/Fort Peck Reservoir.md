# Fort Peck Reservoir

## Summary
Fort Peck Reservoir is a 245,000-acre man-made reservoir in Montana that represents the state's largest commercial fishing water. Montana Fish and Game Department biologists estimated it could sustain annual yields of 6-9 million pounds of commercial fish species (Buffalofish, Carp, Goldeye, and Suckers), though water level management practices eliminated spawning seasons for extended periods, creating boom-and-bust cycles in fish availability.
