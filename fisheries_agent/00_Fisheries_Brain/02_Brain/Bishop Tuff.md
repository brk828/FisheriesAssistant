# Bishop Tuff

## Summary
The Bishop Tuff is a volcanic deposit dated at 700,000 years that overlies Sherwin Till northwest of Bishop. This dating provides a minimum age constraint for the Sherwin glaciation (Kansan equivalent), establishing it as >700,000 years old.
