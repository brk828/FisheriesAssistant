# Boulder City

## Summary
Boulder City is a city in Nevada that served as the meeting location for the Native Fish Work Group on August 19, 1991. The work group convened there to review progress on native fish restoration efforts at nearby Lake Mead and plan future conservation actions for endangered species like the Razorback Sucker.

## Context

> The [[Native Fish Work Group]] convened on August 19, 1991, in [[Boulder City]], Nevada, to review progress on native fish restoration efforts at [[Lake Mead]] and plan future actions. The meeting focused on the [[Yuma Cove]] backwater project involving [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*)...
