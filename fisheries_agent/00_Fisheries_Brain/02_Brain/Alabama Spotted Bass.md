# Alabama Spotted Bass

## Summary
The Alabama Spotted Bass (Micropterus henshalli) is a black bass species that occurs sympatrically with members of the Redeye Bass species group in several drainages. This source documents morphologically intermediate hybrids between M. henshalli and various Redeye Bass species at multiple localities, with hybridization particularly common in the Chattahoochee River where M. henshalli has been introduced, posing a conservation threat through genetic swamping of native endemic species.
