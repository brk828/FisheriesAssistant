# Lepidomeda Mollispinis

## Summary
Lepidomeda mollispinis, commonly known as the Virgin River spinedace, is a small endemic cyprinid fish found exclusively in the Virgin River drainage of Nevada and Utah. This species is significant in conservation biology as it has been listed as endangered due to habitat loss, water diversion, and competition from introduced species, making it a focal point for aquatic ecosystem restoration efforts in the southwestern United States. The species' limited range and specialized habitat requirements make it a valuable indicator of the health of Virgin River aquatic ecosystems.
