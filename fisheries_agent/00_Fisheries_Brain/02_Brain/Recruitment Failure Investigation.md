# Recruitment Failure Investigation

## Summary
Recruitment failure investigation revealed that despite 55% of captured adults being in breeding condition, razorback sucker populations showed minimal or nonexistent recruitment to adult stage. The study documented that stocks consist of aging cohorts with no widespread successful recruitment, attributed to altered flow/temperature regimens, loss of nursery habitat, predation by non-native fishes, and potentially hybridization, with the low number of reproducing adults considered limiting to species survival.
