# Wellton-Mohawk Canal

## Summary
The Wellton-Mohawk Canal was constructed as part of the solution to the Colorado River salinity crisis to carry hypersaline discharge water to the Santa Clara Slough. This infrastructure was designed by the IBWC to prevent saline water from the Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation Project from entering the main Colorado River flow to Mexico.
