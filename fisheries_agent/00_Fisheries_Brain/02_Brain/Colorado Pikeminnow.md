# Colorado Pikeminnow

## Summary
The Colorado Pikeminnow (Ptychocheilus lucius), formerly known as the Colorado Squawfish, is a large cyprinid fish species endemic to the Colorado River basin that has become a focal species in endangered fish conservation and aquatic ecology due to its severe population decline caused by dam construction, water diversion, and habitat alteration. As one of the largest native fishes in the Colorado River system, it represents a key indicator of riverine ecosystem health and has been central to multispecies recovery programs that examine the impacts of flow regime manipulation, reservoir formation, and habitat restoration on native fish communities in the American Southwest. Its conservation status and ecological role make it an important model organism for understanding how anthropogenic changes to river systems affect endemic fish fauna and for evaluating management strategies to restore native fish populations.
