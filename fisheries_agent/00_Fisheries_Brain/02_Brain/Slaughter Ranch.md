# Slaughter Ranch

## Summary
Slaughter Ranch is a property in Douglas, Arizona that contains an extensive artesian aquifer system originating from an 1887 earthquake-induced fault. The ranch has water rights claims for six springs and ten artesian wells with priority dates ranging from 1882 to 1943, and the water is of excellent quality with low total dissolved solids that was historically bottled and sold commercially until the 1930s.
