# Lake Whitefish

## Summary
Lake Whitefish (Coregonus clupeaformis) is a commercially and ecologically important native freshwater fish species endemic to North American Great Lakes and other cold-water systems that has experienced significant population declines and extirpation in many regions due to overfishing, habitat degradation, and competition from invasive species. As a benthic-feeding coregonid, lake whitefish play a crucial role in deepwater lake ecosystems and their abundance serves as an indicator of ecosystem health and the integrity of cold-water fish communities. The species has been a key focus of fisheries management and conservation efforts, particularly in the Great Lakes where regulatory frameworks attempt to balance commercial exploitation with population recovery objectives.
