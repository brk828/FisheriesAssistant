# Forrest Shreve

## Summary
Forrest Shreve was a botanist whose papers, archived at the University of Arizona herbarium, contained a previously undiscovered manuscript by Jacob Corwin Blumer. This manuscript, titled 'Observations on Desert Vegetation Made for Dr. Volney Spalding,' recorded Blumer's observations from three horseback trips in 1906 and 1907.
