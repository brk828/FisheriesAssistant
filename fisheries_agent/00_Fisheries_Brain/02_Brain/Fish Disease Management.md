# Fish Disease Management

## Summary
Fish Disease Management encompasses the integrated approach to preventing, diagnosing, and treating pathogenic and parasitic infections in fish populations, particularly in controlled environments such as commercial aquaculture and hatchery systems. This includes both chemical treatments (such as copper chloride and furan-based compounds) and mechanical interventions applied at specific dosages to control external parasites and ectoparasites while minimizing secondary complications like scale loss and bacterial infections. Effective disease management is critical for maintaining fish health and survival rates in production systems, balancing therapeutic efficacy against potential adverse effects on the host organism.
