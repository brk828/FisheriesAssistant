# Etter Pond

## Summary
Etter Pond is an off-channel floodplain habitat in the Upper Colorado River Basin that serves as a nursery and refuge area for stocked razorback suckers (Xyrauchen texanus) and other endangered native fish species. As a seasonally-flooded riverside wetland distinct from the main river channel, Etter Pond provides critical rearing habitat for larval and juvenile fishes while reducing their exposure to flow regulation impacts and hybridization risks in the mainstem Colorado River. Genetic surveys of Etter Pond populations were used by the Colorado River Basin Genetics Panel to evaluate the success of reintroduction and stocking programs as part of endangered species recovery efforts.
