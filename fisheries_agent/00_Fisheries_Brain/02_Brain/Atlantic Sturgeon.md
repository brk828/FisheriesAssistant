# Atlantic Sturgeon

## Summary
The Atlantic sturgeon (Acipenser oxyrhynchus) is a large, long-lived anadromous fish species in the family Acipenseridae found throughout the Northwest Atlantic coastal zone and major river systems such as the St. Lawrence River. This species has been a subject of fisheries research and conservation attention due to its ecological significance and vulnerability to overfishing, with historical studies employing fish tagging methods to document migratory behavior and population dynamics across coastal and riverine habitats. As a primitive, cartilaginous-skeletoned fish, the Atlantic sturgeon presents unique challenges for research methodologies, including specialized tag placement techniques that account for its distinctive dermal anatomy.
