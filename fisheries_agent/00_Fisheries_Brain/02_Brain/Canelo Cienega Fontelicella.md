# Canelo Cienega Fontelicella

## Summary
Canelo Cienega Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species discovered in 1973 at Canelo Hills Cienega in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, and monitored through 1980. The spring complex covers less than 5 acres but supports high population densities, and The Nature Conservancy purchased the land and applied for water rights to protect this Priority Category 2 species that co-occurs with Gila intermedia.
