# Lutjanidae

## Summary
Lutjanidae is a family of perciform fishes commonly known as snappers, characterized by their robust body structure, terminal mouths with sharp teeth, and typically reddish coloration. These are economically important marine and estuarine fishes found in tropical and subtropical waters worldwide, serving as key species in both commercial fisheries and reef ecosystems. Members of Lutjanidae are of significant conservation concern due to their vulnerability to overfishing and often form predictable spawning aggregations that make them susceptible to targeted exploitation.
