# Paradise

## Summary
Paradise is a weather station location in the mountainous terrain surrounding San Simon Valley at 5,400 feet elevation. This source reports that Paradise received substantially higher precipitation than valley floor locations, with a mean annual precipitation of 20.76 inches (1908-1915 records), demonstrating the strong orographic influence of the mountains on local precipitation patterns.
