# Ophiothrix spiculata

## Summary
Ophiothrix spiculata is a brittle star characterized by very long arm spines and a disc that tends toward pentagonal shape. It occurs in various colors (usually orange or brown) with arms banded in orange and white in the Puerto Peñasco intertidal zone.
