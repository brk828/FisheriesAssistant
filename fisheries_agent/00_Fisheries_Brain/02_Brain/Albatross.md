# Albatross

## Summary
The Albatross was a U.S. Fish Commission Steamer that collected the two known specimens of Dysommina rugosa in 1886. The specimens were taken at Albatross Station 2669 using a large beam trawl at 352 fathoms depth in the Gulf Stream off southern Georgia.
