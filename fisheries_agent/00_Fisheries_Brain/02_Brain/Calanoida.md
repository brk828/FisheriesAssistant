# Calanoida

## Summary
Calanoida are a group of copepod zooplankton found in Lake Mead. They showed maxima from February to June at various stations and displayed more even distribution throughout the lake system with similar counts at different sites, suggesting they are not good water quality indicators; their peak period in Lake Mead occurred later than typical boreal lake patterns.
