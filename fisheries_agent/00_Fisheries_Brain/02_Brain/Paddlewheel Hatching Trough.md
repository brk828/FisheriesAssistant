# Paddlewheel Hatching Trough

## Summary
Paddlewheel Hatching Trough is a type of hatching equipment used in the white catfish spawning experiments. White catfish eggs were successfully hatched in this device as an alternative to allowing them to hatch in situ in the spawning boxes.
