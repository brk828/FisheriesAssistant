# Cortland Experimental Hatchery

## Summary
The Cortland Experimental Hatchery in New York was established in summer 1932 as a purely experimental station designed to improve feeding methods across American hatcheries. The facility was fully equipped to conduct experiments with varying scales of trout populations, from very few individuals to thousands, allowing investigators to work unhampered by the restrictions of production hatcheries.
