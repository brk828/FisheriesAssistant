# Laguna de Terminos

## Summary
Laguna de Terminos is a coastal lagoon in southern Mexico. This source lists it among the coastal lagoon habitats that contribute to the extraordinary ecological variety of Mexican aquatic environments, representing brackish water habitats where freshwater and marine faunas interact.
