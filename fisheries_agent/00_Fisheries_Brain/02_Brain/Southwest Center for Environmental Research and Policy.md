# Southwest Center for Environmental Research and Policy

## Summary
The Southwest Center for Environmental Research and Policy (SCERP) received Congressional funding before significant research questions were identified and without thorough search for appropriate expertise. The Committee recommends that research should be coupled more closely to physical and administrative problems, undertaken only when aimed at meeting locally identified needs, and subject to open professional peer review processes.
