# Invasive Species Impacts On Native Fish

## Summary
Invasive Species Impacts on Native Fish refers to the ecological disruptions caused when non-native fish and aquatic organisms establish populations in native fish habitats, competing for resources, predating on native species, and altering ecosystem conditions that native communities depend upon for survival. In systems like Aravaipa Creek, invasive species present a significant threat to endemic and sensitive native fishes such as Spikedace, Loach Minnow, and Roundtail Chub, potentially reducing population sizes and genetic diversity of these species. Understanding and managing invasive species impacts is critical for conservation efforts aimed at protecting declining native fish communities and maintaining aquatic ecosystem integrity.
