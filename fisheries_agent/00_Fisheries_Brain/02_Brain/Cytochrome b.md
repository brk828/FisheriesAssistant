# Cytochrome b

## Summary
Cytochrome b is a mitochondrial gene commonly used in molecular phylogenetic studies. This study used cytochrome b gene sequences to examine evolutionary relationships among plagopterin minnows, applying a divergence rate of 0.5% per million years for North American cyprinids to estimate divergence times ranging from less than 1 million to over 40 million years ago.
