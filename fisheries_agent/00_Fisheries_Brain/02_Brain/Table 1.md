# Table 1

## Summary
In fish science literature, "Table 1" typically refers to a foundational data table that presents diagnostic morphometric and meristic characters (measurements and counts of fin rays, scales, vertebrae, etc.) used to distinguish between fish taxa or populations. These tables serve as essential reference documents for species identification and taxonomic classification, often providing population-level summary statistics that researchers use to differentiate closely related species or evaluate intraspecific variation. The inclusion of Table 1 data is standard practice in ichthyological studies, enabling readers to verify taxonomic assignments and compare morphological characters across populations or sampling sites.
