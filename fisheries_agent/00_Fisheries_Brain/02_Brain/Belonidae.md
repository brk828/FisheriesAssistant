# Belonidae

## Summary
Belonidae is a family of elongated, predatory fishes commonly known as needlefishes, characterized by their distinctive long, slender bodies and prominent beaks lined with sharp teeth. While primarily marine and coastal species, some belonids inhabit freshwater systems including rivers like the Río Bravo, where they function as piscivorous predators within riverine food webs. The family has received taxonomic attention in ichthyological literature regarding the standardization of scientific nomenclature for various species across different geographic regions.
