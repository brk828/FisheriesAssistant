# Wolf Fry Traps

## Summary
Wolf Fry Traps are inclined-plane traps installed at the lower end of the Abernathy Channel to collect migrating juvenile salmon. This source indicates that two such traps were used to capture and count the migrants produced from the channel incubation experiments.

## Context
