# Fort Desoto, Florida

## Summary
Fort Desoto, Florida was the location of a tidal basin trial where antimycin A was tested on marine species. The trial exposed 38 marine species to the toxicant, with a six-hour exposure to 7 ppb killing sensitive species like anchovies, triggerfish, flounders, and rays, while catfishes, common jack, and redfish proved more resistant.
