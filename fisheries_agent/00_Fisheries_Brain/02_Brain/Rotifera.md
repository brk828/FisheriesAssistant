# Rotifera

## Summary
Rotifera (rotifers) are microscopic zooplankton organisms. In Lake Mohave's open waters, rotifers were rare during the razorback sucker larval period, but they were the predominant zooplankton group in the Arizona Bay backwater habitat and served as one of the food items consumed by small razorback sucker larvae.
