# Julian M. Humphries

## Summary
Julian M. Humphries is the lead author of this 1982 taxonomic study on Ictalurus lupus, conducted at the Museum of Zoology, University of Michigan. He and co-author Robert Rush Miller used multivariate morphometric and meristic analyses to establish the taxonomic validity of the headwater catfish as a distinct species from the channel catfish.
