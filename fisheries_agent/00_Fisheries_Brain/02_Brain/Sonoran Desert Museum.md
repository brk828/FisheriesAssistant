# Sonoran Desert Museum

## Summary
The Sonoran Desert Museum maintained captive populations of desert pupfish for conservation purposes. However, genetic analysis revealed that both of their captive populations showed signs of introgressive hybridization, indicating genetic contamination that compromised the integrity of these conservation breeding stocks.
