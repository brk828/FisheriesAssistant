# Six and Nine Mile

## Summary
Six and Nine Mile area is one of the documented spawning aggregation sites for razorback suckers in Lake Mohave. The study identified this location as part of the network of spawning sites that razorback suckers visit during the November-March spawning period, with individuals showing fidelity to specific aggregations including this area across multiple spawning seasons.
