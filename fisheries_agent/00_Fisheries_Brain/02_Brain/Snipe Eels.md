# Snipe Eels

## Summary
Snipe Eels (family Nemichthyidae) are bizarre deep-sea eels inhabiting cold black mid-depths, characterized by threadlike jaws rough with tiny teeth for entangling prey and an extremely short body cavity. Their excessively elongate tail region extends into a long filament.
