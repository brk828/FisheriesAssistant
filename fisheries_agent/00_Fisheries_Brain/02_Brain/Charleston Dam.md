# Charleston Dam

## Summary
Charleston Dam is an authorized CAP project feature on the San Pedro River near Sierra Vista that was never constructed. The Charleston Dam area was identified as one of the authorized project feature areas (along with Hooker Dam) representing potential fish and wildlife enhancement opportunities, and discussions included using CAP water delivery to Sierra Vista to replenish the groundwater table and arrest the increasing cone of depression affecting the San Pedro River.
