# Gameson and Preddy

## Summary
Gameson and Preddy were researchers who estimated that nitrate entering the Thames Estuary over Teddington Weir provides approximately 56 tons of oxygen per day to the estuary through reduction processes. Their work quantified the oxygen contribution from nitrate reduction in the Thames.
