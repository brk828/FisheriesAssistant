# Box Elder Campground

## Summary
Box Elder Campground is a study site at river mile 2 on the lower Yampa River with sand bars, deep pools, cobble riffles, and backwaters where larval squawfish have been documented. This 1982 study found it provided the highest available habitat of all Yampa sites for all life stages, with YOY habitat showing two peaks (100-400 cfs and 1,000-1,200 cfs) due to complex channel features, adult habitat highest of all Yampa reaches at 100-1,700 cfs with significant adult concentrations documented during spawning season, and spawning habitat highest at 400-4,000 cfs with a significant peak at 700-2,000 cfs.
