# Amphistichinae

## Summary
Amphistichinae is a new subfamily established in this source comprising several surf fishes of California. Males have the same anal fin ray count as females but with rays crowded anteriorly and one ray modified into a large triangular plate, and the subfamily has bluntly conic teeth in two series (except lower jaw of Hyperprosopon argenteus).

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
