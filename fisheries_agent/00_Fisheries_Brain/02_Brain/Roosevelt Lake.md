# Roosevelt Lake

## Summary
Roosevelt Lake is a major reservoir on the Salt River in the Gila River drainage system of Arizona that serves as an important ecological reference point for studying native freshwater fish assemblages and the impacts of dam construction on aquatic ecosystems. As the uppermost impoundment in the Salt River system, Roosevelt Lake represents a critical habitat for endemic and native fish species of the Colorado River basin, including species of conservation concern such as the razorback sucker. The lake's creation through damming fundamentally altered the hydrology and thermal structure of the historic Gila River aquatic system, making it a focal point for understanding how reservoir construction affects native fish populations and freshwater biodiversity in arid southwestern aquatic ecosystems.
