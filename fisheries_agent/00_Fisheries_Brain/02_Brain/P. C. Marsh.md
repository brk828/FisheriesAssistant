# P. C. Marsh

## Summary
P. C. Marsh was an Associate Professor and co-investigator on this 1990 Arizona State University proposal for native fish taxonomy research in the Colorado River Basin. He was designated as key personnel essential to the project and was part of the research team that proposed field sampling across multiple river systems in the southwestern United States and Mexico.
