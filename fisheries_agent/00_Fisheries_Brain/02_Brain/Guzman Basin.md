# Guzman Basin

## Summary
The Guzman Basin is a geographic region in the Rio Yaqui watershed system that serves as a critical habitat area and collection site for native desert fish species, particularly in conservation and restocking efforts. Located in the southwestern United States near the U.S.-Mexico border, the Guzman Basin (which includes areas such as Palomas) has been utilized as a source population for obtaining native fish species to support recovery programs and reintroduction initiatives at facilities like the San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge. This basin has been central to binational conservation translocation efforts involving multiple agencies across the United States and Mexico working to preserve and restore endangered desert fish populations.
