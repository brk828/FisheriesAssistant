# Redlands Power Diversion

## Summary
The Redlands Power Diversion is a structure built in 1907 that spans the entire Gunnison River 1.5 miles above its mouth. It is considered an effective fish migration barrier except during brief maintenance shutdowns on April 1 and November 1, and despite this barrier, a Colorado Squawfish population persisted above it until at least 1971.
