# Bathygadus filamentosus

## Summary
Bathygadus filamentosus is a deep-sea macrourid fish species known from the East Indies. This source notes that despite intensive dredging over months in the Philippine Subregion, the Albatross expedition failed to collect this species, highlighting the faunal distinctness of the isolated Sulu Sea.
