# Virgin Roundtail Chub

## Summary
The Virgin Roundtail Chub (*Gila robusta seminuda*) is a small cyprinid endemic to the Virgin River system in the southwestern United States, representing a distinct subspecies adapted to warm, turbid desert stream conditions. As a native fish species occurring in woodland and grassland stream habitats at intermediate elevations, it serves as an important indicator of riverine ecosystem health and is a focus of conservation efforts aimed at protecting native fish assemblages in the Colorado River basin from competition with introduced species and habitat degradation. This species is typically included in ichthyological surveys and endangered species management programs designed to maintain the integrity of remaining native fish communities in the arid Southwest.
