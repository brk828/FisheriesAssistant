# Goldroad Well Spring

## Summary
Goldroad Well Spring is a spring in the Black Mountains of Arizona that serves as the sole habitat for Goldroad Fontelicella, an endemic hydrobioid snail. This spring was documented in the 1981 status assessment as supporting a rare species with extremely limited distribution.
