# Orangespotted Sunfish

## Summary
The orangespotted sunfish (Lepomis humilis) is a small centrarchid species commonly found in freshwater stream and river ecosystems across North America, where it serves as an important forage fish and indicator species for aquatic habitat quality in ecological surveys. This sunfish occupies shallow, vegetated habitats in flowing and still waters and is frequently enumerated in fish population assessments to evaluate community composition and ecosystem health. Its presence and relative abundance in stream surveys, such as those conducted in Kansas river systems, provides valuable baseline data for monitoring changes in fish assemblages and assessing the impacts of environmental disturbances on native freshwater fish communities.
