# Colonia Juarez

## Summary
Colonia Juarez is a locality in the Rio Casas Grandes drainage identified as a source for potential restocking of the Mimbres River. If the Mimbres River stock becomes extinct, chubs from Rio Casas Grandes in the vicinity of Casas Grandes or Colonia Juarez are recommended for restocking as the most phenetically similar populations.
