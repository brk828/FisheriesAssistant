# Cuatro Ciénegas

## Summary
Cuatro Ciénegas is a globally significant desert spring ecosystem in the Chihuahuan Desert of northern Mexico that serves as a biodiversity hotspot of extraordinary importance to ichthyology and conservation biology, harboring exceptionally high levels of freshwater fish and mollusk endemism. The site is particularly notable as the type locality for endemic cichlid species such as *Cichlasoma minckleyi* and numerous other fish taxa found nowhere else in the world, making it a critical natural laboratory for understanding speciation, adaptive radiation, and ecosystem dynamics in isolated aquatic systems. Its conservation significance stems from the extreme vulnerability of its endemic fauna to habitat degradation, introduced species, and water extraction pressures, positioning Cuatro Ciénegas as a priority for aquatic conservation efforts comparable in ecological importance to other globally recognized freshwater biodiversity centers.
