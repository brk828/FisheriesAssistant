# Owl Point

## Summary
Owl Point is a location in the upper portion of Lake Mohave that marked the upstream boundary of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's sampling area during the March 1991 survey. The USFWS worked from Arizona Bay up-lake to Owl Point as part of the cooperative monitoring effort for endangered razorback suckers and bonytail.
