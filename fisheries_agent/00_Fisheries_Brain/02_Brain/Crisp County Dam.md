# Crisp County Dam

## Summary
Crisp County Dam is a hydroelectric facility operated as a hydropeaking facility on the Flint River. This source notes its operation influences flow throughout the 50-river-kilometer study area and raises concerns that alterations of natural flow regimes affect Shoal Bass spawning behavior and reproductive success.
