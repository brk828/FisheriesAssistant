# Fishery-Oceanography Center

## Summary
The Fishery-Oceanography Center in La Jolla was a Bureau of Commercial Fisheries (later National Marine Fisheries Service) facility where extensive larval fish research programs were developing. This bibliography was prepared specifically to support researchers at this center.

## Context

> This 1971 NOAA technical report by [[Robert C. May]] compiles an annotated bibliography of scientific papers describing attempts to rear marine fish larvae in laboratory settings from 1878 through 1969.
