# Synechococcus

## Summary
Synechococcus is a blue-green algae genus for which a photosynthetic quotient (PQ) of 1.08 has been reported. This study used a PQ of 1.1 for blue-green algae-dominated systems, closely matching the Synechococcus value, to convert oxygen production data to carbon equivalents in Oscillatoria-dominated lakes.
