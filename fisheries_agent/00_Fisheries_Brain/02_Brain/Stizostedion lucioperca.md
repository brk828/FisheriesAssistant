# Stizostedion lucioperca

## Summary
Stizostedion lucioperca is the scientific name for Zander, a European fish species. This source discusses its proposed introduction to Lake Sakakawea as an example of the genetic risks posed when introducing closely related or sister species that could hybridize with native congeners like Walleye.
