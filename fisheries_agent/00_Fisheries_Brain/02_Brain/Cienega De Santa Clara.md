# Cienega De Santa Clara

## Summary
Cienega de Santa Clara is a wetland preserve located in the Colorado River Delta region near the U.S.–Mexico border that serves as a critical habitat for fish species and other aquatic fauna dependent on Colorado River water resources. This ecologically significant site has been central to ichthyological research and conservation efforts, particularly regarding the impacts of upstream water management practices on delta ecosystems and native fish populations in the upper Gulf of California. The wetland's ecological integrity and fish biodiversity are directly threatened by water allocation policies and hydrological alterations in the Colorado River system, making it a focal point for coordinated conservation and restoration initiatives between U.S. and Mexican research institutions.
