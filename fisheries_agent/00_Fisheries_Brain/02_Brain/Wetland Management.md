# Wetland Management

## Summary
Wetland Management refers to the active control and manipulation of wetland systems through infrastructure and engineering interventions. This source describes a comprehensive management system for ten interconnected wetland pond units (L-1 through L-10) at Ouray Wildlife Refuge, involving controlled water levels ranging from 4660 to 4686 feet elevation, canal networks, culverts, and dikes to optimize water flow distribution and habitat quality.
