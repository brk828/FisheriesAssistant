# Rio Bravo

## Summary
Rio Bravo (Rio Grande) is a major river system in the Mexico-United States borderlands that has experienced severe ecological degradation due to salinization. In the lower 400 km of the river, salinization caused the replacement of 32 native freshwater or slightly brackish species by 75 species, mainly marine or salt-tolerant forms, representing a dramatic shift in the aquatic community composition.
