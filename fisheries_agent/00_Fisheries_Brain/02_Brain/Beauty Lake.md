# Beauty Lake

## Summary
Beauty Lake was a 44-acre lake in Quebec with maximum depth of 42 feet where 5 ppb of Fintrol-30 was applied to eradicate northern pike, minnows, suckers, pumpkinseeds, and yellow perch to restore native brook trout. The treatment revealed a previously undetected American smelt population in deep cold water and achieved complete success on 13 target species, with only brown bullheads surviving.
