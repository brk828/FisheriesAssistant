# Little Diamond Creek

## Summary
Little Diamond Creek is a stream in the White Mountains where introduced Brown Trout heavily outnumbered native Apache Trout during the 1988 survey. The stream contained 111 Brown Trout compared to only 26 Apache Trout, demonstrating the severe competitive pressure from non-native salmonids on the threatened Apache Trout population.
