# Atlas Mountains

## Summary
The Atlas Mountains are a North African mountain range that received Zambezian fauna during Pleistocene pluvial periods. This source cites the presence of these tropical African fish forms in the Atlas Mountains as evidence of dramatically different hydrological conditions when the Sahara supported permanent streams.
