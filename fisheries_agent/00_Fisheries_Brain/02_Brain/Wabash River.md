# Wabash River

## Summary
The Wabash River is a major tributary of the Ohio River in the midwestern United States that serves as an important aquatic system for ichthyological research and fish conservation, supporting diverse lotic and lentic habitats including main-stem river channels, tributary creeks, and associated wetlands. This river system and its tributaries have been extensively studied as sampling locations for documenting fish species distributions, habitat associations, and population dynamics across various substrate types and stream gradients. The Wabash River drainage represents a significant biogeographic region for understanding fish communities and documenting range extensions or new distributional records of freshwater species in the Ohio River basin.
