# San Xavier Hill

## Summary
San Xavier Hill is a location in Pima County, Arizona, near Tucson that serves as the restricted habitat for the terrestrial snail Sonorella eremita. The species is found only at the northwest end of the hill and is threatened by urban development from Tucson's expansion.
