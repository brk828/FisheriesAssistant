# Cambaridae

## Summary
Cambaridae, the crayfish family, is mentioned as an example of regulatory inconsistency in California's exotic species list. The entire family was prohibited, yet the Australian Marron (from a different family) was not prohibited despite being considered a serious threat, illustrating gaps in the state's approach to regulating invasive aquatic species.
