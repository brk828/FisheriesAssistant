# Work Group

## Summary
The Work Group provides technical staff support to the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program, with participation ranging from 20-40 members. According to this source, Work Group members, along with Steering Committee members, form an 18-member evaluation committee for reviewing proposals, and the group holds meetings in addition to the Steering Committee's 12 annual meetings.
