# Pacific Slope

## Summary
The Pacific Slope refers to the Pacific drainage systems of Colombia and Ecuador that were sampled during this study, including the Dagua River, San Juan Basin, Calima River, Patia Basin, and western Ecuador. These Pacific slope localities yielded gymnotid collections including the range extension of Sternarchus leptorhynchus.
