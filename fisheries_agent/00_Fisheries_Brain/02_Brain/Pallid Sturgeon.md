# Pallid Sturgeon

## Summary
The Pallid Sturgeon (*Scaphirhynchus albus*) is a critically endangered species of bottom-feeding sturgeon native to the Mississippi River and its major tributaries, representing one of the rarest fish species in North America. This primitive, cartilaginous fish is of significant conservation concern due to habitat loss from dam construction and river modification, making it a focal species for fisheries management and restoration efforts at hatcheries and research stations throughout the Mississippi River system. Understanding the pallid sturgeon's distribution, ecology, and population dynamics is essential for designing effective recovery programs and maintaining genetic viability in both wild and captive populations.
