# Pinna rugosa

## Summary
Pinna rugosa is a bivalve mollusk documented at Puerto Peñasco in the Gulf of California. This source includes it among the molluscs identified in the intertidal zone, with identifications made using Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other taxonomic references.
