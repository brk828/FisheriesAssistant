# UNLV

## Summary
UNLV (University of Nevada, Las Vegas) operates facilities for holding and researching Virgin River native fish species as part of the recovery effort. The source indicates UNLV was solicited for research proposals focusing on the top 5 Recovery Plan priorities, including parasite questions, community interactions relevant to Red Shiner eradication, and life stage studies.
