# Gulf Breeze, Florida

## Summary
Gulf Breeze, Florida is the location of a marine research facility where acute toxicity studies tested pesticide effects on marine organisms in 1964. Studies at this site examined impacts on phytoplankton productivity, oyster shell growth, adult shrimp survival and equilibrium, and juvenile fish mortality, establishing EC₅₀ values for multiple pesticides across these marine species.
