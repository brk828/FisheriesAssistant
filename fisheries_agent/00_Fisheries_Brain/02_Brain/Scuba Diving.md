# Scuba Diving

## Summary
Scuba diving is an underwater observation technique that emerged as a fisheries management tool in the mid-1950s. According to this 1957 survey, 14 U.S. states were actively using scuba diving for direct observation of fish behavior, habitat assessment, and verification of management interventions, though its effectiveness was limited by water turbidity, particularly in inland waters.

## Context
