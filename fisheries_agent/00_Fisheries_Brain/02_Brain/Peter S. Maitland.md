# Peter S. Maitland

## Summary
Peter S. Maitland is the author of this comprehensive 1993 review on global fish conservation. His work examines approximately 30,000 fish species worldwide and presents case studies from multiple continents, proposing conservation strategies including legislation, habitat restoration, translocation, and captive breeding.
