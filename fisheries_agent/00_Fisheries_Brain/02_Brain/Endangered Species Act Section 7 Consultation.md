# Endangered Species Act Section 7 Consultation

## Summary
Section 7 Consultation under the Endangered Species Act is a mandatory interagency review process required when federal agencies propose actions that may affect threatened or endangered species or their critical habitats. During consultation, federal agencies must work with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service or National Marine Fisheries Service to assess potential impacts and develop mitigation measures to avoid jeopardy to listed species. This process ensures that federally-funded or permitted projects—such as dam operations, timber management, or erosion control activities—are evaluated for their effects on fish populations and aquatic ecosystems before implementation.
