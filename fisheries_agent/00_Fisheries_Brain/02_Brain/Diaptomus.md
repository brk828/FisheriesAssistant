# Diaptomus

## Summary
Diaptomus is a genus of calanoid copepods that serves as an important component of freshwater zooplankton communities and a key food resource for planktivorous fish species. These small crustaceans are widely distributed across diverse aquatic systems ranging from temporary to permanent water bodies, and their species composition and abundance are used as ecological indicators of water body type and environmental conditions. In aquatic ecology and fisheries science, Diaptomus populations are monitored to assess plankton community structure, understand trophic dynamics, and evaluate the productivity and health of freshwater ecosystems.
