# Lamnidae

## Summary
Lamnidae is the family of mackerel sharks represented at the Arroyo Sequit site by 7 teeth from two species: salmon shark and mako. These large pelagic sharks, weighing 100-200 pounds, were much more probably caught from boats than from shore by aboriginal fishermen.
