# Cuatro Cienegas Basin

## Summary
The Cuatro Ciénegas Basin is a unique hydrogeological system in Mexico characterized by numerous interconnected springs and wetlands that support exceptional aquatic biodiversity and endemic species found nowhere else on Earth. This isolated basin has served as a critical natural laboratory for understanding spring ecology and aquatic evolution, though it faces significant conservation challenges from habitat degradation and anthropogenic disturbance to its spring systems. The basin's springs, such as those at Mojarral, are recognized as ecologically outstanding freshwater ecosystems of global conservation importance for fish and other aquatic organisms.
