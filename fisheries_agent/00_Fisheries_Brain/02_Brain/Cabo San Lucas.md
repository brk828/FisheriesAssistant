# Cabo San Lucas

## Summary
Cabo San Lucas, Baja California Sur, Mexico was part of the previously documented distribution range of the Red Pike-Conger. This source notes that the species was known from Cabo San Lucas south to Colombia before the new record from El Desemboque in the upper Gulf of California extended the range significantly northward.
