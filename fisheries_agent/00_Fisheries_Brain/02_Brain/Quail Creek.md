# Quail Creek

## Summary
Quail Creek is a tributary confluence point and critical sampling location on the Virgin River system in southwestern Utah that represents a key monitoring station for assessing habitat conditions and population dynamics of endangered fish species, particularly the Virgin spinedace. Within the context of Virgin River conservation efforts, Quail Creek marks an important section of the mainstem reach between Pah Tempe Springs and the Washington Fields Diversion where flow regimes and physical habitat characteristics are measured to evaluate the effectiveness of water management and species recovery plans. The creek's location along the longitudinal gradient of the Virgin River system makes it essential for understanding habitat suitability across the critical range of endangered aquatic species in the Colorado River basin.
