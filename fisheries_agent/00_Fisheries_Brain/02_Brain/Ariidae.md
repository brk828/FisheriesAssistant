# Ariidae

## Summary
Ariidae is a family of catfishes commonly known as sea catfishes, characterized by the possession of venomous spines at the base of their pectoral and dorsal fins that can inflict painful puncture wounds. Members of this family are primarily euryhaline or marine species, though some can tolerate freshwater environments, making them subjects of both ecological concern and management interest in aquatic systems. The family appears in freshwater fish inventories and conservation discussions primarily in the context of invasive species risk assessment, as illustrated by historical proposals to introduce ariid species into inland water bodies such as the Salton Sea.
