# Lake Sturgeon

## Summary
Lake Sturgeon (*Acipenser fulvescens*) is a large, long-lived anadromous fish species native to North American river systems, particularly the Mississippi River drainage and associated freshwater habitats, that has been an important subject of fish tagging and migration studies since the mid-20th century. As a primitive sturgeon species with relatively low tag return rates compared to other anadromous fishes, Lake Sturgeon populations have historically supported commercial and subsistence fisheries while facing conservation challenges related to habitat degradation, damming, and overexploitation. The species' presence in multiple river drainages from the Mississippi to northeastern Atlantic systems makes it a key indicator of aquatic ecosystem health and an important focus for fisheries management and restoration efforts.
