# Big Sandy River

## Summary
The Big Sandy River is a major drainage system within the Ohio River basin that serves as an important ichthyological region for documenting and understanding freshwater fish biodiversity in central Appalachia. As a significant tributary system, it represents one of the key aquatic drainages studied in regional fish surveys and conservation assessments, particularly for cataloging native fish species distributions and habitat characteristics across West Virginia and adjacent areas. The river's ecological importance lies in its role as a distinct biogeographic unit for fish populations, making it a critical focus area for fisheries science and freshwater conservation efforts in the region.
