# Cicadellidae

## Summary
Cicadellidae is the family of leafhoppers, a group of insects in the order Homoptera. This source describes five new species of deltocephaline leafhoppers within this family from North America, all distinguished by unique genitalic characters, particularly aedeagal morphology.
