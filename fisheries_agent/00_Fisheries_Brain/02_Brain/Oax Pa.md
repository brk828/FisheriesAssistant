# Oax Pa

## Summary
The Oax Pa (Cocopah) are indigenous people of the Rio Hardy area in the Colorado River Delta region. They experienced drastic population decline and cultural disruption due to loss of traditional fishing and harvesting of Palmer's Grass (Distichlis palmeri) resulting from the degradation of the Colorado River Delta wetlands.
