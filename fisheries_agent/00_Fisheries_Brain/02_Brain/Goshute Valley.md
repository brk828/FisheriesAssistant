# Goshute Valley

## Summary
Goshute Valley is a valley in Nevada that would be directly affected by the proposed MX Missile System. This source identifies it as habitat for Relictus solitarius (relict dace).

## Context

> This December 1979 urgent letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]] to NANFA members warns of catastrophic threats to desert aquatic ecosystems in [[Nevada]] and [[Utah]] from the proposed [[MX Missile System]].
