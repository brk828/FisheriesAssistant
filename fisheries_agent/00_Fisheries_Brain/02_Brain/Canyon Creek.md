# Canyon Creek

## Summary
Canyon Creek is a stream in east-central Arizona with a mean flow of approximately 60 m³/min. The upper reaches of Canyon Creek are identified as a critical concern location because the proposed power line route passes over or very near a newly established Arizona Game and Fish hatchery, and the area supports aquatic mammals including beaver and possibly jumping mice.
