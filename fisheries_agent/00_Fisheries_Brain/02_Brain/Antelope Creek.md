# Antelope Creek

## Summary
Antelope Creek is a tributary of Lost River in Modoc County where three small Pumpkinseed specimens (largest 42 mm standard length) were collected on September 7, 1942. This represented one of the scattered distribution records for this rare introduced species in California.
