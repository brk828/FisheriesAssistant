# Gramineae

## Summary
Gramineae (grasses) is an extensive angiosperm family included in the terrestrial vegetation samples. Grass seeds showed consistently higher calorific values (4600 cal/g ash-free) compared to vegetative plant parts (4414 cal/g ash-free).
