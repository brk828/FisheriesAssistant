# Sebastes emphaeus

## Summary
Sebastes emphaeus (Puget Sound Rockfish) is a Pacific rockfish species mentioned for comparative context. Moulton (1975) documented similar seasonal patterns in this species in Puget Sound, noting a dramatic decline in sightings in fall with fish huddled in caves by November (based on 61 observations over 12 months).
