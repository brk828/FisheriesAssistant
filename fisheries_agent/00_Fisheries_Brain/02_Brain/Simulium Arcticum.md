# Simulium Arcticum

## Summary
Simulium arcticum is a blackfly species (Diptera: Simuliidae) that serves as a dominant benthic macroinvertebrate in cold, clear riverine habitats, particularly in regulated river systems downstream of dams. As a key component of aquatic food webs in oligotrophic streams, this filter-feeding organism is sensitive to changes in water clarity, flow regimes, and temperature, making it an important indicator taxon for assessing the ecological impacts of dam operations and thermal modifications on lotic ecosystems. Its abundance and distribution patterns are commonly used to monitor benthic community responses to hydrological and limnological changes in cold-water river reaches.
