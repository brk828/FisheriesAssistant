# Steamboat Lake

## Summary
Steamboat Lake is a water storage facility in the Upper Colorado River Basin identified as a source for flow augmentation to benefit endangered fish. The recovery plan calls for leasing 3,000 acre-feet of storage water from Steamboat Lake by August 1993 as a Priority 1-2 action to help provide appropriate seasonal flows for endangered fish recovery.
