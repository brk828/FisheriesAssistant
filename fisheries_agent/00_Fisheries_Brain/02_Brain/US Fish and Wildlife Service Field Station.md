# US Fish and Wildlife Service Field Station

## Summary
The US Fish and Wildlife Service Field Station in Fort Collins housed fish collections from the Upper Colorado River Basin. In FY 1993, this facility and the National Ecology Research Center transferred to the National Biological Survey, with collections relocating to the Albuquerque Field Station and becoming associated with the Museum of Southwestern Biology.

## Context

> This FY 1993 annual report documents the second year of a three-year project (1992-1994) to reorganize, catalogue, and manage the massive holdings of preserved fish specimens from the Upper Colorado River Basin housed at the Larval Fish Laboratory, Colorado State University.
