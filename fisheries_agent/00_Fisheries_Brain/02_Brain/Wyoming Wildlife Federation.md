# Wyoming Wildlife Federation

## Summary
The Wyoming Wildlife Federation is one of the participating organizations in the Upper Colorado River Recovery Program. This wildlife conservation group was part of the 1988 cooperative agreement that established the collaborative framework for recovering endangered fish species while accommodating water development needs.
