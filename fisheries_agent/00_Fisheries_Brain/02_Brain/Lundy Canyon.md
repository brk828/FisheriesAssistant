# Lundy Canyon

## Summary
Lundy Canyon is a location in the Mono Basin where evidence of continuing tectonic activity is visible in the form of a 50-foot fault scarp cutting across Tahoe moraine. This fresh scarp indicates ongoing geological activity in the region.
