# U.S. Atomic Energy Commission

## Summary
The U.S. Atomic Energy Commission was the federal agency that established discharge limits for radioactive materials at one-tenth of the National Committee on Radiation Protection and Measurements maximum permissible concentrations (MPC). This regulatory framework was designed to protect public health from radioactive contamination in stream environments receiving discharges from nuclear facilities.
