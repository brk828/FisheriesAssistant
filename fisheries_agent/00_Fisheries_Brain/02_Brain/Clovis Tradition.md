# Clovis Tradition

## Summary
The Clovis Tradition is a Paleoindian archaeological culture dating to approximately 10,000-8500 B.C. This source reports the recovery of a single Clovis projectile point from Growler Valley at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument.
