# Strawn And Hubbs 1956

## Summary
Strawn and Hubbs 1956 refers to pioneering laboratory work on artificial hybridization techniques in darter fishes, establishing foundational methods for manually stripping gametes from wild-caught and laboratory-reared specimens to produce interspecific hybrids. This work was instrumental in advancing understanding of reproductive compatibility among darter species and provided experimental protocols that influenced subsequent hybridization studies in ichthyology, particularly research on the Etheostomatinae subfamily conducted throughout the late 1950s.
