# Temperature-Dependent Behavior

## Summary
Temperature-dependent behavior describes how fish activity and habitat use respond to thermal conditions. This source found that monthly Razorback Sucker activity increased significantly with mean monthly river temperature (P < 0.001; R² = 0.59) but showed no relationship with river discharge, suggesting temperature alone can drive movements and that fish used deeper water during summer months when temperatures peaked at 26.2°C, possibly tracking optimal temperatures.
