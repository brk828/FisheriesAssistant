# Dead Horse Ranch State Park

## Summary
Dead Horse Ranch State Park is a protected area located within the Verde Valley, Arizona. According to this 1990-1991 survey, it is one of several state and national protected areas in the Verde River corridor region, and survey respondents favored using public funds to purchase land adjacent to such parks as buffer zones (63.8% of residents, 75.0% of leaders).
