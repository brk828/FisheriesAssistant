# Verhoeven

## Summary
Verhoeven was a researcher who demonstrated a two-stage reduction process in denitrification: first nitrate reduces to nitrite plus nitrous oxide, then these compounds reduce to nitrogen gas. This work helped clarify the intermediate steps in bacterial denitrification.
