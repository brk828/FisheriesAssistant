# Tendipes attenuatus

## Summary
Tendipes attenuatus is a midge larva species (probable identification) that dominated the Tendipes population in Hartlaub Lake following toxaphene treatment. The population of this species increased dramatically from about 10 to 602 larvae per square foot by November 1962.

## Context

> This study examined the impact of toxaphene treatment for fish eradication on the benthic invertebrate community of [[Hartlaub Lake]], Wisconsin.
