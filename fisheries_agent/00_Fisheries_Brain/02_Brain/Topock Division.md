# Topock Division

## Summary
The Topock Division is a reach of the Colorado River within Havasu National Wildlife Refuge that was designated as one of three priority river reaches for annual stocking of razorback sucker fry and fingerlings. The management plan called for stocking appropriate numbers of fry and 10-15 cm fingerlings in this division, with fish to be released behind sandbars and in small backwaters as far upstream as possible to enhance survival.
