# Region 2

## Summary
Region 2 covers the Southwest United States and issued only 8 jeopardy opinions during FY 1987-1991, with 3 blocked activities (averaging less than 1 per year). This low conflict rate occurred despite the region covering a substantial portion of U.S. land area.
