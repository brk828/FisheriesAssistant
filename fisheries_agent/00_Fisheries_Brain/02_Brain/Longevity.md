# Longevity

## Summary
Colorado River native fishes exhibit remarkable longevity, with razorback suckers averaging approximately 35 years old in Lake Mohave, Colorado squawfish potentially exceeding 50-75 years to reach 1.8 m length, and bonytails averaging approximately 40 years at 45-55 cm. Flannelmouth suckers can reach approximately 35 years, bluehead suckers live 20+ years, while speckled dace live only 2-3 years.
