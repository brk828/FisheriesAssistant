# Boulder City Municipal Golf Course

## Summary
Boulder City Municipal Golf Course is a facility in Boulder City that contains an artificial pond used as an off-site rearing location for juvenile razorback suckers in the repatriation program. Ten of the 55 recaptured repatriated fish originated from this pond, making it one of the successful rearing sites for juveniles before their release into Lake Mohave.
