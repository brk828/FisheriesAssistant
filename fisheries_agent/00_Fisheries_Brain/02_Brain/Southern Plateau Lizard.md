# Southern Plateau Lizard

## Summary
The Southern Plateau Lizard is a species hypothetically present at the Orme Reservoir site. This source notes it as an example of mesic or higher-elevation species that can occur far below typical altitudinal ranges when flowing streams provide suitable habitat, appearing at Orme far below its normal Upper Sonoran or Transition zone distribution.
