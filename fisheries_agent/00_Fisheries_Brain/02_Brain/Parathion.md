# Parathion

## Summary
Parathion is an insecticide for which toxicity data was tabulated in this report. While seldom used directly by fishery biologists, it required evaluation for potential effects on fish when applied by other agencies.

## Context

> This 1961 special report by Charles L. Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
