# Tallapoosa Bass

## Summary
Tallapoosa Bass (Micropterus tallapoosae) is one of the species within the Redeye Bass complex that resulted from genetic revision separating the original Redeye Bass into several distinct species, each restricted to disjunct upland river systems in the Southeastern United States. This source lists it as a related species to Redeye Bass but provides no additional specific information about it.
