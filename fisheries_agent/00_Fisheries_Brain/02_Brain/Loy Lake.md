# Loy Lake

## Summary
Loy Lake is a 42-acre freshwater impoundment (230 acre-feet) located near Denison in Grayson County, Texas. In 1963, it underwent complete fish eradication using rotenone treatment due to an undesirable fish community dominated by Gizzard Shad (58% of population) and stunted Bluegill (18%), achieving approximately 95% mortality before planned restocking with Largemouth Bass, Channel Catfish, and Redear Sunfish.
