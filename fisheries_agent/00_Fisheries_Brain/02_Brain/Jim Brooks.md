# Jim Brooks

## Summary
Jim Brooks was personnel from Dexter National Fish Hatchery who was proposed to participate in the 1987 Rio Yaqui fish collection expedition. This source indicates he would operate the tank truck from Dexter that would be used to transport the collected fish specimens.
