# Dissolved Oxygen

## Summary
Dissolved oxygen (DO) is the concentration of oxygen gas dissolved in water that is essential for the respiration and survival of fish and other aquatic organisms. In fisheries and aquatic ecology, DO depletion is recognized as a critical stressor resulting from organic pollution, eutrophication, and sediment decomposition, with insufficient oxygen levels causing direct fish mortality or creating unsuitable habitat conditions. Monitoring and maintaining adequate dissolved oxygen concentrations is therefore fundamental to fish conservation, water quality assessment, and understanding how anthropogenic pollution impacts aquatic ecosystems.
