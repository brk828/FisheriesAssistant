# Pilon River

## Summary
The Pilon River is a waterway where Channel Catfish populations were studied for hematological differences related to water quality. Research showed significant differences in hemoglobin levels (7.84 vs. 6.22 mg/100ml) and hematocrit (31.75% vs. 33.04%) between populations, potentially related to varying magnesium concentrations (20 ppm increasing to 80 ppm) in the river.
