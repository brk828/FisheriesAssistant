# Lake Havasu Fisheries Improvement Project

## Summary
The Lake Havasu Fisheries Improvement Project was a Bureau of Land Management proposal to enhance non-native sport fish habitat across 42 coves and bays in Lake Havasu, a Colorado River reservoir on the California-Arizona border, by designating and improving backwater and lentic (still-water) areas as critical habitat zones. The project was controversial among conservation biologists and fisheries managers because it prioritized non-native species enhancement in a system where native fish recovery was also a management objective, raising ecological concerns about predation pressure on threatened and endangered species and the potential conflicts between sport fishery development and native species conservation goals in the lower Colorado River basin.
