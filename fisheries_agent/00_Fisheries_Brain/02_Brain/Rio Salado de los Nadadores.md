# Rio Salado de los Nadadores

## Summary
The Rio Salado de los Nadadores is a river near Sacramento, Coahuila, Mexico. The research proposal planned to sample this location specifically to assess potential gene flow from the Cuatro Cienegas endemic cichlids into Cichlasoma cyanoguttatum populations, as part of the genetic studies component of the collaborative research project.
