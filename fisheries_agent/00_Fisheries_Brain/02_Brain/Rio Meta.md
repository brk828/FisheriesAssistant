# Rio Meta

## Summary
The Rio Meta is a river system in Colombia where two new species were collected: Hemigrammus barrigonae and Hyphessobrycon metae. Both species were collected at Barrigona by Manuel Gonzales, representing additions to the Orinoco basin ichthyofauna.
