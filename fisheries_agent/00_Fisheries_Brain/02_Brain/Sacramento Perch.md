# Sacramento Perch

## Summary
The Sacramento Perch (*Archoplites interruptus*) is a centrarchid (sunfish family) endemic to central California freshwater systems that represents a significant conservation concern due to its restricted native range and vulnerability to habitat loss and introduced species competition. As one of California's native freshwater fishes, it exemplifies the state's high rate of fish endemism and the challenges facing isolated relict populations in modified aquatic ecosystems. Its presence in fragmented habitats and potential listing as a species of special concern underscore the broader conservation issues affecting California's endemic fish fauna.
