# Pecos River System

## Summary
The Pecos River System is a major part of the distribution range of Ictalurus lupus in the United States. The 1982 field surveys found healthy populations throughout all but the extreme headwaters of the Pecos River, though evidence of hybridization with I. punctatus was detected in the lower Pecos River near Independence Creek.
