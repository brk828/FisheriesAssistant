# Amargosa Tryonia Snail

## Summary
The Amargosa Tryonia Snail (Tryonia variegata) is a hydrobiid snail species found in the Amargosa River and adjacent habitats in Nevada and California. It was among the 20 aquatic invertebrate species, primarily springsnails and aquatic beetles, petitioned for endangered species listing in 1991.
