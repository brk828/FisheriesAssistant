# propagule pressure

## Summary
Propagule pressure refers to the risk of nonnative fish species being entrained through Glen Canyon Dam and establishing populations downstream in the Grand Canyon ecosystem. This study found that entrainment risk varies seasonally with reservoir stratification and water level, with highest risk during stratified conditions (May-December) when fish concentrate in the epilimnion and historic low water levels position dam intakes within or near this layer, potentially allowing nonnative species to pass through and threaten recovering native fish populations downstream.
