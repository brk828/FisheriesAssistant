# University of Pittsburgh

## Summary
The University of Pittsburgh is the academic institution that housed the Pymatuning Laboratory of Ecology where this calorific equivalents reference compilation was developed. The university supported Kenneth W. Cummins' work in compiling standardized energy values for ecological studies.

## Context
