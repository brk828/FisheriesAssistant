# Albert G. Friend

## Summary
Albert G. Friend was an author affiliated with the Robert A. Taft Sanitary Engineering Center who co-authored this 1959 paper on radioactive waste disposal in streams. He contributed to evaluating multiple stream uses and developing frameworks for calculating permissible radioactive discharge levels based on downstream population exposures.
