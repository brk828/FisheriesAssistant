# Sacramento Mountains

## Summary
The Sacramento Mountains are a major mountain range in south-central New Mexico that played a critical role in drainage evolution. This source documents that uplift of the Sacramento Mountains during late Tertiary to middle Pleistocene time "beheaded" the ancestral Pecos River in the area of Sierra Blanca, fundamentally altering the drainage pattern.
