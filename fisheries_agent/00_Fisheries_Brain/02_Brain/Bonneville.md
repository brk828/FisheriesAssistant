# Bonneville

## Summary
The Bonneville Basin is an endorheic subdivision of the Basin-and-Range Province that was isolated in the north by the Wasatch Front more than 30 million years ago. This study suggests that the divergence of Lepidomeda and Gila copei was attributed to the separation of Colorado Plateau and Bonneville Basin drainages during the region's tectonic history.
