# Schizothorax anjac

## Summary
Schizothorax anjac is a snow trout species (family Cyprinidae, tribe Schizothoracini) recorded from the Sistan basin of Afghanistan. Some authors regard this species as a synonym of other Schizothorax species, indicating uncertain taxonomic status pending systematic revision.
