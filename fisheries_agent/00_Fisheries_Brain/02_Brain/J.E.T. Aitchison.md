# J.E.T. Aitchison

## Summary
J.E.T. Aitchison participated in the Afghan Delimitation Commission and collected fish specimens that yielded 7 species, including 3 new species. His collections were an important contribution to the early knowledge of Afghanistan's ichthyofauna.
