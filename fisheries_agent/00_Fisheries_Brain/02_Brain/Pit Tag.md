# Pit Tag

## Summary
A Pit Tag, or Passive Integrated Transponder tag, is a microchip implant used to permanently and uniquely identify individual fish for long-term monitoring and tracking studies in fisheries science and conservation biology. These small electronic tags are surgically implanted into fish bodies and can be read remotely without recapture, allowing researchers to track movement patterns, survival rates, and population dynamics of aquatic organisms over extended periods. Pit tags are particularly valuable for endangered species management and behavioral studies where repeated identification of the same individuals is necessary for understanding population ecology and conservation outcomes.
