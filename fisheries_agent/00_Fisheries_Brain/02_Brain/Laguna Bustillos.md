# Laguna Bustillos

## Summary
Laguna Bustillos is an endorheic (closed) basin in northern Mexico that contains unique endemic fish fauna. As an isolated water body with no outlet to the ocean, it has developed a distinctive fish assemblage separate from surrounding drainages.
