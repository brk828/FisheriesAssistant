# Serranidae

## Summary
Serranidae is a fish family commonly recognized in fisheries management and sport fishing contexts as temperate basses, which includes economically and recreationally important species such as white bass. These perciform fishes are typically classified as game and pan fish in North American freshwater and brackish ecosystems, making them significant subjects in conservation biology and fisheries science due to their popularity in recreational fishing and their ecological roles in aquatic communities.
