# Arcto-Tertiary Geoflora

## Summary
The Arcto-Tertiary Geoflora refers to a paleobotanical assemblage of plant species adapted to cooler, moister conditions that characterized northern regions during the Tertiary Period. Hubbs uses paleobotanical data from this flora as evidence for reconstructing long-term climatic trends along the Pacific Coast, showing a general cooling from warm Tertiary conditions through the Quaternary.
