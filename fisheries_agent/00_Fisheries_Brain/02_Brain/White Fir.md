# White Fir

## Summary
White Fir (Abies concolor) is a conifer species that occurs in the Jeffrey Pine Forest community of the Mono Basin. It grows at elevations between 7,000 and 9,500 feet alongside Jeffrey Pine, Mountain Big Sagebrush, Antelope Bitterbrush, and Quaking Aspen.
