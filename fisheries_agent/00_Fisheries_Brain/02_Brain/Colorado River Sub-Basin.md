# Colorado River Sub-Basin

## Summary
The Colorado River Sub-Basin is a major drainage area within the Colorado River Basin where endangered fish recovery research was conducted. According to this 1989 meeting agenda, research in this sub-basin included studies of endangered fishes in Cataract Canyon, streamflow recommendations, instream flow habitat simulations, hydrology and temperature modeling, and distribution of non-salmonid sportfish in northwestern Colorado.
