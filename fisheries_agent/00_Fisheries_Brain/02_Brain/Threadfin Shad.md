# Threadfin Shad

## Summary
Threadfin Shad (*Dorosoma petenense*) is a small, planktivorous forage fish in the herring family (Clupeidae) that plays an important ecological role as a primary food source for predatory fish species in freshwater reservoirs and lakes throughout North America. Native to the southeastern United States but widely introduced into western reservoirs including those of the Colorado River Basin, threadfin shad support recreational and commercial fisheries by sustaining populations of larger gamefish such as largemouth bass and stripers. Their rapid reproduction, schooling behavior, and ability to thrive in warm-water impoundments make them a key species in fisheries management strategies, though their introduction into non-native ecosystems can have complex ecological consequences for native species assemblages.
