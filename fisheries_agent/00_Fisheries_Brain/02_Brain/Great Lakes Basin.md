# Great Lakes Basin

## Summary
The Great Lakes Basin is a geographic and administrative region encompassing the northeastern United States and upper Midwest that serves as a critical focal area for fish health management, conservation, and aquaculture operations across multiple jurisdictional boundaries. As referenced in these fisheries documents, the Basin represents an 8-state service region where federal, state, tribal, and private entities coordinate fish disease diagnosis and control, hatchery operations, and ecosystem management through cooperative frameworks and integrated regulatory approaches. This region is significant in aquatic ecology and fisheries science both for its substantial freshwater fishery resources and as a model system for multi-jurisdictional fish health and conservation partnerships.
