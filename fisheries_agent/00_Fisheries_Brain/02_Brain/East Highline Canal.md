# East Highline Canal

## Summary
The East Highline Canal is a water conveyance structure in the Imperial County area of California. According to this 1985 management plan, young razorback suckers were collected in 1973-74 from ponds that were filled with water from this canal near Niland, providing evidence of razorback reproduction in the area during that period.
