# dorsal_spine_aging_protocol

## Summary
This is a method for determining age of Common Carp using dorsal spine structures, following procedures recommended by Watkins et al. 2015. In this study, 166 individuals (ranging 55-793 mm total length, ages 0-19) were aged from spines collected in 2016-2017, with two independent readers achieving 77% overall agreement and 89% agreement for fish aged 6 years or younger, providing the age-length data needed to parameterize the von Bertalanffy growth model.
