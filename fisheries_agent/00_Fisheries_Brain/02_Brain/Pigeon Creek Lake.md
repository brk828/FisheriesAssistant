# Pigeon Creek Lake

## Summary
Pigeon Creek Lake is a proposed dam and reservoir project on Pigeon Creek, a tributary of the Blue River in Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests, Arizona. The project, estimated to cost $5-9 million, was intended to provide water-based recreation to assist the local Greenlee County economy, but the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service raised significant concerns about its impacts to threatened and endangered species, native fish communities, and riparian habitat, recommending a full Environmental Impact Statement be prepared.
