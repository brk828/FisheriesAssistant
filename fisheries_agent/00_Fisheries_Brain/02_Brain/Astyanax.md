# Astyanax

## Summary
Astyanax is a genus of characin fishes (family Characidae) representing Neotropical faunal elements in Mexico. This source documents the genus as part of the Mexican ichthyofauna, with at least one species (Astyanax aeneus) showing trans-isthmian distribution occurring on both Pacific and Atlantic slopes in southern Mexico and Central America.
