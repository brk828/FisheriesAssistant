# Bright Angel Creek

## Summary
Bright Angel Creek is a significant tributary of the Colorado River within Grand Canyon National Park that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly the endemic humpback chub (Gila cypha), whose type locality is associated with the creek's mouth. This tributary system has been extensively surveyed and monitored as part of Grand Canyon fish conservation efforts, providing refuge for threatened native cyprinids and serving as an important focal area for understanding population dynamics and habitat use patterns of endangered species in the Colorado River Basin. Bright Angel Creek represents a key reference site for assessing the ecological health and recovery potential of native fish communities in the Grand Canyon ecosystem.
