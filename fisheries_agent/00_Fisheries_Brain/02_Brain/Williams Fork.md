# Williams Fork

## Summary
Williams Fork is a tributary or landmark on the Yampa River in Colorado, located near river kilometer 198.4. The study area for habitat use of endangered fishes initially extended from Echo Park upstream to near Williams Fork, and was later extended further upstream to Craig at river kilometer 224.
