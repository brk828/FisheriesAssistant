# Serviceberry

## Summary
Serviceberry (Amelanchier alnifolia) is a shrub species that grows in the study area in Colorado. In this research, dense serviceberry stands provided natural windbreaks that helped minimize windblown snow effects on the experimental plots, and tall serviceberry shrubs created small shaded patches that prevented complete snow melt even on treated areas.
