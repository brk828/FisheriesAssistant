# Utah Division Of Wildlife Resources

## Summary
The Utah Division of Wildlife Resources is the state agency responsible for managing fish populations and aquatic ecosystems within Utah, including oversight of fishery restoration projects and environmental assessments for activities affecting fish communities and their habitats. As evidenced by its involvement in major initiatives like the Strawberry Valley Fishery Restoration project and monitoring programs for sensitive species such as Colorado squawfish, the division serves as the primary governmental body coordinating fish conservation, species management, and aquatic resource protection throughout the state. The agency works collaboratively with scientific committees and conducts assessments to balance fishery management objectives with conservation biology principles.
