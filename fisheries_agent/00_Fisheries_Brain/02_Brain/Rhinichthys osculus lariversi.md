# Rhinichthys osculus lariversi

## Summary
Rhinichthys osculus lariversi is the Big Smoky Valley speckled dace, an endemic subspecies of speckled dace. This source identifies it as a species directly threatened by the proposed MX Missile System in Big Smoky Valley, Nevada.

## Context

> This December 1979 urgent letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]] to NANFA members warns of catastrophic threats to desert aquatic ecosystems in [[Nevada]] and [[Utah]] from the proposed [[MX Missile System]].
