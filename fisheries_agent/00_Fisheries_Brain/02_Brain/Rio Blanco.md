# Rio Blanco

## Summary
Rio Blanco is a river in the Rio Esmeraldas system of Ecuador where Rhoadsia minor was collected. It is located at 4,000 feet elevation in Mindo, Province of Pichincha, representing a high-elevation mountain stream habitat.
