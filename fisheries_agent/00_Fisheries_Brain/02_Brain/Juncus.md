# Juncus

## Summary
Juncus (rushes) are obligate wetland species that occur in wet fluvial marshes in the Colorado River corridor. This source reports that Juncus species were among the wet marsh species that experienced riverward displacement under the Interim Flows regime, and by 1991 they were part of the 30% of marsh vegetation classified as wet marsh dominated by cattails, rushes, and bulrushes.
