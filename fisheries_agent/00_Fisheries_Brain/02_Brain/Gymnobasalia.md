# Gymnobasalia

## Summary
Gymnobasalia is a series within the genus Chara that includes the Chara zeylanica complex. This group demonstrated unprecedented ploidy variation ranging from n=14 to n=70 (2x to 10x the base of 7), with the extreme variation suggesting the complex encompasses multiple cryptic species distinguishable primarily by chromosome number.
