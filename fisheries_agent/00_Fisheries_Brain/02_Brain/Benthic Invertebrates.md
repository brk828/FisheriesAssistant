# Benthic Invertebrates

## Summary
Benthic invertebrates are organisms living on or in the substrate of aquatic ecosystems, including insects, crustaceans, mollusks, and annelids that collectively form the foundation of aquatic food webs and serve as primary food sources for fish populations. These organisms are critical indicators of water quality and ecosystem health, as their taxonomic composition and abundance reflect environmental conditions such as temperature, dissolved oxygen, and organic matter inputs. In stream ecosystems, benthic invertebrates process organic materials like leaf litter and facilitate energy transfer from primary production to higher trophic levels including fish communities.
