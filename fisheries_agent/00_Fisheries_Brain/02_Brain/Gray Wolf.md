# Gray Wolf

## Summary
The Gray Wolf is a species listed as endangered or threatened under the Endangered Species Act. This legal opinion uses the Gray Wolf as an example in discussing hybrid offspring, specifically noting that crosses between Gray Wolf and Red Wolf produce hybrids that do not conserve the pure genetic heritage of either parent species and therefore are not protected under the ESA.
