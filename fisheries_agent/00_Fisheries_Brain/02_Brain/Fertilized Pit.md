# Fertilized Pit

## Summary
Fertilized Pit was one of three Linn County strip-pits used in an experimental fertilization study conducted by Hume-Sinclair Coal Mining Company with department supervision. The site produced largemouth bass reaching 9-12.5 inches (up to 1.5 pounds) in the first year and documented successful crappie reproduction in 1955, which was unusual for clear strip-pits with Secchi depths exceeding 6 feet.
