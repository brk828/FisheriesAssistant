# Gambusia yucatana

## Summary
Gambusia yucatana is a species of mosquitofish that originates from marine or brackish water habitats. This source notes that marine-origin G. yucatana can survive direct transfer from seawater to freshwater, demonstrating greater salinity tolerance than freshwater forms of Gambusia affinis, which cannot tolerate abrupt transfer to seawater.
