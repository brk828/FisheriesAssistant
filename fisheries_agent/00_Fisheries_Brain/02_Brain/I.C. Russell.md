# I.C. Russell

## Summary
I.C. Russell authored the definitive 1889 monograph describing Pleistocene glaciation and the synchronous expansion of Lake Russell during pluvial times in the Mono Basin. He initially suggested pre-pluvial discharge through Aurora Valley into the East Walker River but calculated the highest shoreline to be 100 feet below the divide.
