# Asterionella

## Summary
Asterionella is a genus of pennate diatom that serves as a key indicator organism in phytoplankton community studies of freshwater lakes and rivers, valued for its quantitative tracking in succession analyses and its sensitivity to environmental conditions such as light availability and water stratification. As a dominant phytoplankton genus, Asterionella populations exhibit characteristic changes in chlorophyll composition and degradation patterns that reflect in-situ physiological responses to depth, light climate, and seasonal dynamics in aquatic ecosystems. Its presence and abundance are routinely monitored in comparative limnological and fluvial ecology research across diverse systems ranging from small chalk streams to major river basins, making it an important taxa for understanding phytoplankton community structure and ecosystem health.
