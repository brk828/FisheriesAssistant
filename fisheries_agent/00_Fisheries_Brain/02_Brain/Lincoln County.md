# Lincoln County

## Summary
Lincoln County is a county in Nevada, USA, that contains Pahranagat Valley. This source describes it as the location where the critically endangered Pahranagat Roundtail Chub is restricted to a single population of 37-45 adults in approximately 2,300 meters of the Pahranagat River on the Burns Ranch.
