# Gunnison River

## Summary
The Gunnison River is a major tributary of the Colorado River system that plays a critical role in the conservation biology and recovery of endangered fish species, particularly the Colorado pikeminnow (Colorado squawfish). As a significant source of inflow to the Colorado River mainstem, the Gunnison River's flow characteristics and management directly influence habitat availability, water quality, and population dynamics in downstream critical habitat reaches, making flow manipulation studies and coordination essential components of the Endangered Fish Recovery Program. The river's confluence with the Colorado River and the nursery habitats it supports are key considerations in comprehensive aquatic ecosystem management and endangered species recovery efforts throughout the Upper Colorado River Basin.
