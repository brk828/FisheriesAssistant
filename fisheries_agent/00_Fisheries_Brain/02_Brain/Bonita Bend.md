# Bonita Bend

## Summary
Bonita Bend (RMI 31.5-31.0) is a location in the lower Green River recommended for consideration in larval monitoring efforts, listed together with Anderson Bottom. It is part of the lower Green River system where 170 larvae were collected during 1993-1995.
