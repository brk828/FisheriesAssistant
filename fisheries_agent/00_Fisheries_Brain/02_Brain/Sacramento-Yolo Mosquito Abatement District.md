# Sacramento-Yolo Mosquito Abatement District

## Summary
The Sacramento-Yolo Mosquito Abatement District is a California pest control organization that served as the source of mosquitofish for Montana's experimental introduction. In 1969, this district provided 2,000 mosquitofish to the Cascade County Mosquito Abatement District for use in Montana's mosquito control efforts.
