# Prehistoric Ceramic Period

## Summary
The Prehistoric Ceramic Period dates from A.D. 300-1450 and was dominated by Hohokam Culture in the Papagueria region. This source documents three main phases at Organ Pipe: Colonial period (A.D. 775-975) with red-on-buff ceramics along Sea of Cortez trails, Sedentary period (A.D. 975-1150) with shell manufacturing expansion, and Classic period (A.D. 1150-1400) showing radical reorganization with Tucson Basin emerging as a second core area.
