# Blue Spring Fontelicella

## Summary
Blue Spring Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species found at Blue Spring and Castle Spring in Eddy County, New Mexico. Populations have been monitored from 1968-1980, showing stable conditions at Blue Spring with densities up to 2 individuals per square centimeter, and the species co-occurs with native darters and livebearers while facing threats from ongoing oil exploration.
