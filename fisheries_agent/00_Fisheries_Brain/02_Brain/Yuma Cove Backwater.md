# Yuma Cove Backwater

## Summary
Yuma Cove Backwater is a 2.6-acre natural lakeside cove on Lake Mohave used as off-channel habitat for razorback sucker conservation. This source reports that 200 razorback suckers stocked there in 2013 grew to over 400 adults producing hundreds to thousands of young-of-year within a few years, though apparent overabundance led to removal of 80 adults and 2,877 young-of-year in 2019, and remote PIT-tag scanning achieved 90-100% detection of fish later harvested.
