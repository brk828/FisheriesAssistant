# Serpulorbis

## Summary
Serpulorbis is a genus of gastropod mollusks found in the Puerto Peñasco region. This field guide documents it among the mollusks of the Gulf of California intertidal zone, identified using Keen's "Sea Shells of Tropical West America" and other references.
