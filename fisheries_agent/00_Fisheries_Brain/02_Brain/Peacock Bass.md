# Peacock Bass

## Summary
Peacock Bass refers to a group of aggressive, colorful cichlid species native to South American river systems that have been experimentally introduced into North American waters, particularly in Texas, as an alternative sport fish for specialized aquatic environments such as highly saline inland lakes and low-diversity freshwater impoundments where native gamefish cannot thrive. These fish are valued in fisheries management for their predatory efficiency in controlling rough fish populations and establishing novel recreational fisheries in marginal habitats, though their introduction represents an example of non-native species stocking practices documented in mid-to-late 20th century conservation and fisheries policy. The peacock bass introductions reflect broader historical efforts by wildlife agencies to maximize productive use of diverse aquatic systems through species translocation, though such practices now raise contemporary concerns regarding ecological impacts on native fish communities.
