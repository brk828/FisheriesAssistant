# Gymnodinium splendens

## Summary
Gymnodinium splendens is a dinoflagellate (single-celled phytoplankton) that was used as a food source in larval fish rearing experiments. In Lasker et al.'s 1970 study of anchovy larvae, it was fed in combination with mollusk veligers and contributed to achieving 47% survival at 19 days, demonstrating its value as part of a mixed diet for first-feeding larvae.
