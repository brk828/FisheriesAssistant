# San Simon

## Summary
San Simon is a key settlement in San Simon Valley at 3,594 feet elevation where artesian water was discovered in late 1910, triggering rapid agricultural development. This source documents extensive groundwater data from the area, including precipitation records (1881-1915, mean 9.24 inches annually), water table depths of 60-70 feet, and artesian wells penetrating lake bed sediments to reported depths of 1,230 feet.
