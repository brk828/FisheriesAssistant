# Tarpon atlanticus

## Summary
Tarpon atlanticus is a euryhaline species that regularly enters tropical rivers from coastal waters. As a marine/estuarine fish that penetrates freshwater systems, it represents one of several species that move between saltwater and freshwater habitats in Mexico.
