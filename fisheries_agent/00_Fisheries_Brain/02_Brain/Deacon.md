# Deacon

## Summary
James Everett Deacon is the principal investigator of this research proposal and an Associate Professor at Nevada Southern University with a Ph.D. in Vertebrate Zoology/Ichthyology from the University of Kansas (1960). He has conducted previous work on Nevada spring systems and has published on Kansas fish biology, Flathead Catfish, and southwestern fish distributions, and holds memberships in the American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists, American Fisheries Society, and Ecological Society of America.
