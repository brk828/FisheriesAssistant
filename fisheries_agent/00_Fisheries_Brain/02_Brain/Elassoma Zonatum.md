# Elassoma Zonatum

## Summary
Elassoma zonatum, commonly known as the banded pygmy sunfish, is a small freshwater fish species native to the southeastern United States that inhabits lentic and swamp environments, particularly in heavily vegetated areas with soft substrates. This species is notable in ichthyological surveys as a specialist of low-gradient, acidic waters and is often documented in association with other lentic fauna in regional fish faunal assessments and biodiversity inventories. As a small-bodied endemic with specific habitat requirements, E. zonatum serves as an indicator species for pristine swamp and blackwater ecosystems in conservation and ecological monitoring studies.
