# San Diego

## Summary
San Diego is a coastal city in southern California that serves as an important location for historical climate records and biogeographic observations in this study. Weather data from San Diego beginning in 1849 document a cooling trend through the second half of the 19th century, and the Pacific Railroad Survey of 1853-57 found warm-water fish species there (including a seahorse) that resembled assemblages from Turtle Bay and Magdalena Bay, indicating warmer conditions about a century before the study.
