# Paraleptophlebia adoptiva

## Summary
Paraleptophlebia adoptiva is an early-season mayfly species found in Michigan trout streams north of Town Line 16. This source describes it as a cold-hardy species that emerges from mid-April through early June (peak in early May), often during harsh weather conditions at trout season opening, making it a critical food source for trout when few other aquatic insects are active. The nymphs are approximately 3/8-inch long at maturity with dark brown to almost black coloration, and they develop through late summer, fall, and winter before emerging as winged adults.
