# Xystrosus

## Summary
Xystrosus is a monotypic cyprinid genus containing only Xystrosus popoche, found in Lake Chapala and the Santiago River above falls. This source notes that young specimens are indistinguishable from Algansea rubescens without counting gill rakers (41-67 total on first arch in Xystrosus), suggesting possible synonymy and questioning the genus's validity.
