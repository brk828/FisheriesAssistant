# Arizona Game And Fish Commission

## Summary
The Arizona Game and Fish Commission is the state regulatory agency responsible for managing and conserving Arizona's fish and wildlife resources, including oversight of aquatic species, habitat protection, and fisheries management within the state. In the context of the referenced documents, the Commission played a key role in multi-species conservation efforts, particularly regarding the Lower Colorado River ecosystem, where it coordinated with federal agencies to balance conservation objectives with water and power operations. As a state fish and wildlife management authority, the Commission establishes regulations, issues permits for species-related activities, and implements conservation programs to protect both aquatic and terrestrial fauna.
