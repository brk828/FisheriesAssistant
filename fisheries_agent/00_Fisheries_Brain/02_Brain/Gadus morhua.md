# Gadus morhua

## Summary
Gadus morhua (Atlantic cod) is a member of subfamily Gadinae examined in this study, with a large specimen (511 mm body length) having a brain length of 25.8 mm. As a representative of Gadinae, it possesses well-developed optic lobes larger than the forebrain, reflecting the visual capabilities of this commercially important species.
