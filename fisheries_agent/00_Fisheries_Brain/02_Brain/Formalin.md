# Formalin

## Summary
Formalin is a chemical disinfectant and therapeutic agent widely used in aquaculture and fish disease management to treat parasitic and bacterial infections in both pond and hatchery settings. In ichthyology research and aquatic ecology, formalin serves dual purposes as both a fish specimen preservative for morphological studies and as an in vivo treatment, though its application requires careful dosage consideration as fish species exhibit varying tolerance levels, with some species like striped bass being particularly sensitive to prolonged low-concentration exposures despite tolerating brief high-concentration treatments. Understanding formalin's toxicity profiles across different fish species is essential for optimizing therapeutic efficacy while minimizing harmful ecological impacts in managed aquatic systems.
