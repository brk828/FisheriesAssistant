# Ammodytes tobianus

## Summary
Ammodytes tobianus is used as a comparative example of a shallow pelagic species that, despite having small eyes, possesses large optic lobes. This demonstrates that optic lobe development correlates with habitat depth and visual dependence rather than simply eye size.
