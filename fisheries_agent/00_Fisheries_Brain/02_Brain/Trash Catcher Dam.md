# Trash Catcher Dam

## Summary
A Trash Catcher Dam is a low-profile habitat improvement structure (maximum 20 inches high) designed to create pools and slow water flow in shallow, unproductive stream sections. The dam consists of steel fence posts spaced about 2 feet apart supporting heavy-duty hogwire fencing on the upstream side, with boulders placed on the upstream apron for stability and natural stream debris (leaves, twigs, aquatic plants) accumulating to impound water. The structure must have a level top across the channel with ends extending into both banks to prevent erosion, and functions best when positioned to deepen holes around natural features like stumps and boulders in shaded areas.
