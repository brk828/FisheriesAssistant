# Water Pollution Research Laboratory

## Summary
A Water Pollution Research Laboratory is a specialized facility dedicated to investigating the effects of various contaminants and environmental stressors on aquatic organisms, particularly fish species. These laboratories conduct experimental research to quantify toxicity thresholds, survival rates, and physiological responses of fish to pollutants such as ammonia, salinity changes, and other water quality parameters. By combining analytical chemistry techniques with controlled biological assessments, such facilities provide critical data to inform water quality standards, conservation strategies, and pollution mitigation efforts in aquatic ecosystems.
