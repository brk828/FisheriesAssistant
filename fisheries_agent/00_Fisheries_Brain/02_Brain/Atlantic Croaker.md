# Atlantic Croaker

## Summary
The Atlantic Croaker (*Micropogonias undulatus*) is a sciaenid fish species commonly found in estuarine and coastal environments, particularly along the Atlantic and Gulf coasts, where it functions as an important component of estuarine fish communities. As a seasonal immigrant that spawns offshore with juveniles dominating summer estuarine populations, it plays a significant ecological role in brackish and saline estuarine habitats. The species is relevant to both commercial fisheries and conservation efforts due to its abundance in productive estuary systems and its ecological interactions with other sympatric sciaenid species in these dynamic aquatic ecosystems.
