# Cedar Creek Hatchery

## Summary
Cedar Creek Hatchery was a California Department of Fish and Game facility located on the Eel River that was completely destroyed in the December 1964 flood, resulting in the loss of all fish on hand. The destruction of this hatchery contributed to a substantial decrease in steelhead production, which dropped from 2,772,032 fish (101,635 pounds) in 1963-64 to 2,121,102 fish (30,516 pounds) in 1964-65.
