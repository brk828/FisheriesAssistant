# Salt Creek

## Summary
Salt Creek is an isolated, spring-fed desert stream in Riverside County, California, that serves as a critical wild population site for endemic fish species, particularly the desert pupfish and related taxa adapted to closed-basin arid ecosystems. As one of the few remaining naturally occurring populations in highly restricted California desert habitats, Salt Creek represents a significant conservation concern due to its geographic isolation, limited genetic diversity, and vulnerability to anthropogenic disturbance, making it a key reference site for monitoring wild population status and for comparison with refugium populations maintained for species recovery purposes.
