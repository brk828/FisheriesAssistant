# Ash Meadows Pupfish

## Summary
The Ash Meadows Pupfish (*Cyprinodon nevadensis*) is a small cyprinodontid fish endemic to the Ash Meadows spring complex in Nevada, representing one of numerous highly specialized desert pupfish species adapted to isolated geothermal spring systems. As a critically endangered taxon with extremely limited geographic range, this species exemplifies the conservation challenges facing endemic desert aquatic fauna and serves as a focal point for efforts to protect unique desert spring ecosystems and their dependent biota. The species' survival depends on maintaining habitat integrity within its spring complex, making it a key indicator of broader desert spring conservation success in the Great Basin region.
