# Fisheries Science

## Summary
Fisheries science is an integrative discipline that combines ecological research, quantitative population studies, and conservation-focused investigation to understand fish populations and their aquatic environments, particularly in applied contexts such as managing endangered species and sustaining commercial or recreational fisheries. Drawing from biology, ecology, and environmental science, it encompasses systematic field studies, population monitoring, and policy-relevant research that extends beyond basic taxonomic or descriptive work to provide evidence-based management strategies for fish conservation and sustainable resource use. The field has historically emphasized the need for comprehensive, long-term ecological investigations across different aquatic systems to address complex conservation challenges facing fish populations.
