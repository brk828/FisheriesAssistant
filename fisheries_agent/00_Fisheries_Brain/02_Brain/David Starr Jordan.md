# David Starr Jordan

## Summary
David Starr Jordan was a pioneering American ichthyologist and naturalist of the late 19th and early 20th centuries who made significant contributions to fish taxonomy and species description, establishing important conventions for designating type species that influenced subsequent ichthyological nomenclature. His work, often conducted in collaboration with colleagues such as Evermann and Gilbert, set standardized practices for formal scientific naming and classification of fish species that became foundational to modern ichthyology. Jordan's systematic approach to fish taxonomy and his prolific publications established him as a central figure in the development of rigorous fish science during a period of rapid species discovery and systematic organization.
