# Six-Mile Cove

## Summary
Six-Mile Cove is a location on the Nevada side of Cottonwood Basin in Lake Mohave, along the Nevada-Arizona border. The area contains a spawning shoal approximately 5 acres in size in about 5 meters of water that has been consistently used by razorback suckers for spawning and has been sampled for population monitoring over multiple years (1988-1991).
