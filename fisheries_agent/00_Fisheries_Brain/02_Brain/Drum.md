# Drum

## Summary
Drum (freshwater drum) is a rough fish species that showed dramatic population increases in Fort Gibson Reservoir between 1958 and 1959. Adult drum populations increased from 90 fish per acre in 1958 to 152 fish per acre in 1959, while young-of-the-year densities surged from 14 fish weighing 0.2 pounds per acre in 1958 to 385 fish weighing 3.2 pounds per acre in 1959, indicating highly successful reproduction and establishing drum as a significant component of the reservoir's fish community.
