# Dexter Creek National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Dexter Creek National Fish Hatchery is a fish propagation facility that received endangered fish for broodstock development. This source reports that approximately 270 future broodstock Colorado squawfish (from lot 4DYAWB) were distributed to this hatchery in 1980 as part of the propagation efforts for endangered Colorado River fishes.
