# Barbatia reeveana

## Summary
Barbatia reeveana is a bivalve mollusk in the family Arcidae documented at Puerto Peñasco. This source includes it among the molluscs identified in the intertidal zone of the Gulf of California, with identifications based on Keen's reference works and other taxonomic literature.
