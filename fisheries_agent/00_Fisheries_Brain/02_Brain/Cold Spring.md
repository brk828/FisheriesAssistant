# Cold Spring

## Summary
Cold Spring is one of seven historic spring habitats that once supported White River Spinedace populations in the White River Valley of Nevada. Like other historic spinedace habitats, it featured cool (65°-71°F) clear water with gravel and sand substrate and emergent vegetation, but the spinedace population was extirpated by 1985 due to habitat destruction and nonnative species introductions.
