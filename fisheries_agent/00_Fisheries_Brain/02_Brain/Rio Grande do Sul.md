# Rio Grande do Sul

## Summary
Rio Grande do Sul is a region in southeastern Brazil that shares its Poeciliid fish fauna with the La Plata Basin. All Poeciliid species recorded from Rio Grande do Sul also occur in the La Plata basin, and the Poeciliid fauna effectively distinguishes this region as a distinct biogeographic province from the Amazonian Basin to the north.
