# San Manuel High School

## Summary
San Manuel High School was the location where the Bureau of Land Management held a public meeting on July 22, 1970, at 1:00 p.m. in its Cafetorium. This meeting was organized to solicit public comments on vehicular use and management considerations for the Aravaipa Canyon Primitive Area.
