# Fathead Minnows

## Summary
Fathead minnows (Pimephales promelas) were stocked as supplemental forage in predation experiments with threadfin shad and largemouth bass. In both experiments where they were used (at 1,000 minnows per acre), fathead minnows were nearly eliminated by predation, with only 0.30-0.64 pounds per acre recovered at harvest, demonstrating they were heavily consumed by predators alongside or instead of threadfin shad.
