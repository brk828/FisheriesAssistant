# Blue Range Primitive Area

## Summary
The Blue Range Primitive Area is a protected area near Pigeon Creek that was under consideration for reintroduction of the endangered Mexican Wolf (Canis lupus baileyi) at the time of this 1991 assessment. Its proximity to the proposed Pigeon Creek Lake project raised concerns about potential impacts to this endangered species recovery effort.
