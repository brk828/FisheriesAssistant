# Southwest Center For Biological Diversity

## Summary
The Southwest Center for Biological Diversity is a nonprofit conservation organization that engages in legal advocacy and policy work to protect endangered aquatic species and their habitats, particularly in the southwestern United States. In the context of fish science and conservation, the organization has been instrumental in filing lawsuits and pursuing critical habitat designations for imperiled fish species such as the Gila Topminnow, Desert Pupfish, Spikedace, Loach Minnow, and Razorback Sucker, while challenging federal agencies and private entities whose activities threaten these species. The organization's work exemplifies conservation biology's intersection with environmental law, using litigation and regulatory mechanisms to enforce protections for federally listed fish species and their aquatic ecosystems.
