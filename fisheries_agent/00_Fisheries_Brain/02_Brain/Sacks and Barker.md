# Sacks and Barker

## Summary
Sacks and Barker were researchers who found that oxygen suppresses enzyme formation and interferes with enzyme action in denitrification. Their work showed that 4% oxygen in the gas phase reduced denitrification rate to 50% or less of the anaerobic rate.
