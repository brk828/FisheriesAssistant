# Bairdiella

## Summary
Bairdiella is a genus of small croaker fish (family Sciaenidae) found in coastal and estuarine waters, particularly in warm temperate and subtropical regions of the western Atlantic and eastern Pacific oceans. In the context of fisheries and conservation biology, Bairdiella species are notable as subjects of fish introduction and establishment studies, including experimental plantings in non-native waters such as the Salton Sea, where their survival and ecological impacts were monitored as part of broader research on exotic fish introductions in North America. These fish typically inhabit brackish and marine environments with moderate to high salinity preferences, making them relevant to understanding how marine species adapt to novel aquatic ecosystems and the potential ecological consequences of deliberate fish stocking programs.
