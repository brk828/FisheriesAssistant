# Santiago Atitlan

## Summary
Santiago Atitlan is a community of 14,000 people located on Lake Atitlan where fishing was vital to the local economy. Native commercial fishermen were earning less than 50 cents per day using primitive worm-and-line methods, catching only 2-3 pounds per day, though the study demonstrated that modern techniques could increase harvest to 100-150 pounds per night and an agricultural cooperative there purchased a generator to begin experimental electrofishing operations.
