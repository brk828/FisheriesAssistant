# Canadian River Basin

## Summary
The Canadian River Basin is a major drainage system in northeastern New Mexico comprising about 12,500 square miles with mountainous, plateau, and plains regions. This source documents that the Canadian River originates on the east slope of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains and flows approximately 312 river miles in New Mexico before joining the Arkansas River, with perennial tributaries including the Vermejo, Cimarron, Mora, and Ocate Creek.
