# Little Outfit Tank

## Summary
Little Outfit Tank is a water body in southern Arizona that was surveyed in 1991 and found to contain only goldfish and bullfrogs. The tank had never been stocked with topminnow, and a nearby Forest Service tank was noted to periodically dry.
