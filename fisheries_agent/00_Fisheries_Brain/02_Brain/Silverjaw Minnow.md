# Silverjaw Minnow

## Summary
The Silverjaw Minnow (*Ericymba buccata*) is a small cyprinid fish species inhabiting North American stream systems, studied primarily for its age and growth characteristics using scale annuli analysis methods in stream ecology research. This species is relevant to fisheries and conservation studies as a representative minnow component of freshwater fish communities, where its population dynamics and life history patterns provide insights into stream ecosystem health and cyprinid recruitment in tributary systems. Research on this species contributes to understanding the ecological roles and temporal abundance patterns of smaller forage fish within integrated fish assemblage surveys.
