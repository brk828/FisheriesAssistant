# White Crappie

## Summary
White Crappie (*Pomoxis annularis*) is a freshwater sunfish species in the family Centrarchidae that serves as an important food and game fish in North American inland waters. This species is commonly stocked in lakes and reservoirs for recreational fishing and is often managed alongside other panfish species like Black Crappie and Bluegill as part of integrated fisheries management programs. White Crappie populations are valued both for their ecological role in controlling smaller fish populations and their significance to sport and subsistence fishing communities.
