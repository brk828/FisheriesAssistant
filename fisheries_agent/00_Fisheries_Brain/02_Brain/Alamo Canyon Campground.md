# Alamo Canyon Campground

## Summary
Alamo Canyon Campground is a camping facility at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument that is located near a mine adit hosting the largest known maternity colony of Lesser Long-nosed Bat in the United States. The 1996 management plan proposed expanding the campground by four new drive-in campsites plus day-use parking for six additional vehicles, which could result in twice as many campers present during dawn and dusk when bats return to roost and owls actively forage, though the plan concluded this would have "no adverse effect" without providing scientific basis for that determination.
