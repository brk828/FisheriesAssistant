# Tallapoosa River

## Summary
The Tallapoosa River in Alabama was one of the collection sites for adult threadfin shad used in the experimental pond studies. Adult threadfin shad were collected from the river near Tallassee, along with collections from its tributary Chewacla Creek.

## Context

> This study examined the production efficiency of [[Threadfin Shad]] (*Dorosoma petenense*) in experimental ponds at varying stocking densities (100–1,600 adults per acre) to determine optimal rates for maximum forage production.
