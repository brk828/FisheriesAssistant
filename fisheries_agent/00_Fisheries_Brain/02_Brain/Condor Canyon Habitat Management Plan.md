# Condor Canyon Habitat Management Plan

## Summary
The Condor Canyon Habitat Management Plan is a Bureau of Land Management plan published in 1990 that outlines management actions for Big Spring Spinedace habitat. The plan includes measures to exclude livestock from March 15 to November 15, limit vehicle use to the railroad bed, file for spring water rights, and install flow gauging stations, with implementation being a priority recovery action estimated to cost $29,000.
