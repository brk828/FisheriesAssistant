# Pima County Board of Supervisors

## Summary
The Pima County Board of Supervisors is the governing body that passed Resolution No. 1998-250 establishing the Sonoran Desert Conservation Plan and committing the county to upholding the Endangered Species Act through regional conservation planning. The Board initiated a public stakeholder process in October 1998 and invited participation from Native American Tribes, federal and state land managers, and municipalities within Pima County.
