# LDH-B

## Summary
LDH-B (Lactate Dehydrogenase-B) is an enzyme system examined through allozyme electrophoresis in biochemical genetic studies. This source reports that LDH-B is duplicated in Cui-ui (Chasmistes cujus), with both duplicated loci sharing the same alleles between the two species examined, and that previous reports of "polymorphic LDH" in these fish likely represent mis-scoring of these duplicated loci.
