# Tule

## Summary
Tule (Scirpus americanus) is an emergent aquatic sedge plant that forms dense stands in shallow water. At Quitobaquito Springs, Tule provides critical habitat structure for juvenile Sonoran Mud Turtles, with trapping success 3.3-fold higher in Tule-vegetated areas, and the turtles also feed heavily on young Tule shoots when animal prey is scarce.
