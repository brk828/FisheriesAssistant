# Slender Madtom

## Summary
The Slender Madtom (Noturus exilis) is a small catfish species endemic to specific freshwater systems in the central United States that has become a conservation concern due to its limited range and declining populations. As a benthic insectivore inhabiting small to medium-sized streams with specific habitat requirements, this species serves as an indicator of stream health and ecosystem integrity in ichthyological surveys. The Slender Madtom's status as a state-listed species of conservation concern reflects broader patterns of fish biodiversity loss in North American freshwater ecosystems and highlights the vulnerability of specialized stream-dwelling taxa to habitat degradation and fragmentation.
