# Gammarus-Pecos Complex

## Summary
The Gammarus-Pecos Complex is a group of seven freshwater-to-miohaline amphipod populations inhabiting springs and streams in Texas and New Mexico, all within 90 km of the Pecos River. This source describes three named species (G. pecos, G. hyalelloides, G. desperatus) and four undescribed populations that share diagnostic features including absence of calceoli, C-setae on mandibular palps, and exceptionally narrow oostegites. Statistical analysis reveals at least two additional undescribed species within the complex, representing the most southerly inland Gammarus species in North America.
