# Railroad Valley Springfish

## Summary
The Railroad Valley Springfish (*Crenichthys nevadae*) is a critically endangered endemic fish species native to isolated hot springs in Nevada's Railroad Valley, representing a conservation priority in ichthyology due to its extremely limited natural habitat and high extinction risk from environmental degradation. As documented in refuge management studies, this species has been the subject of captive breeding and translocation programs, such as the established population at Chimney Hot Springs, which serve as insurance populations against the loss of native spring localities. The species exemplifies the conservation challenges faced by highly specialized, geographically restricted aquatic fauna in the Great Basin region and demonstrates the importance of ex-situ management strategies for preventing extinction of endemic springfish taxa.
