# Alamo Wash

## Summary
Alamo Wash was a natural drainage channel whose water rights were purchased by the California Development Company to divert Colorado River water for irrigation. During the catastrophic 1905-1907 flood, the entire Colorado River flowed through Alamo Wash and New River, filling the Imperial Valley and creating the Salton Sea while destroying much of Mexicali's infrastructure.
