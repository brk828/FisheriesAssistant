# Coral Reef Fish

## Summary
Coral Reef Fish are covered by a dedicated Specialist Group within SSC's fish conservation program. The Coral Reef Fish Specialist Group is one of 6 fish-focused groups within the Commission's approximately 102 total Specialist Groups. This taxonomic group would conduct typical specialist group activities including species assessments and conservation interventions.
