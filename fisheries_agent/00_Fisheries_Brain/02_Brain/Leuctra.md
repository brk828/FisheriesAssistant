# Leuctra

## Summary
Leuctra is a genus of stoneflies (order Plecoptera) whose nymphs are important organisms in aquatic ecology and fish diet studies, but present significant taxonomic challenges because nymphal forms cannot be reliably distinguished to species level using morphological characters alone, whereas adult forms are readily identifiable. These small aquatic insects, which typically hatch at 0.5–1.0 mm in length, serve as key bioindicators of stream health and food sources for fish populations, making their accurate identification and growth documentation essential for understanding freshwater ecosystem dynamics and fish nutrition.
