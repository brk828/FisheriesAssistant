# Cnesterodon decemmaculatus

## Summary
Cnesterodon decemmaculatus is a Poeciliid species with a geographic distribution from Rio Grande do Sul to Maldonado and the Bolivian Chaco. It is one of the species documented in this taxonomic revision of the La Plata Basin fauna.
