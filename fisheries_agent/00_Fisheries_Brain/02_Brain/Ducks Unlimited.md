# Ducks Unlimited

## Summary
Ducks Unlimited is a waterfowl conservation organization that was included on the contact list for the Bog Hole management planning process. This inclusion reflects the site's primary management objective of waterfowl habitat, as the existing impoundment was created for waterfowl management.
