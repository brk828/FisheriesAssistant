# Clupea harengus

## Summary
Clupea harengus (herring) was the subject of the earliest successful larval rearing (Meyer, 1878) and multiple subsequent studies. Blaxter (1968) achieved 182 days survival using wild plankton plus Balanus nauplii, with 1-4% surviving through metamorphosis, and the species is part of the most-studied family (Clupeidae) with 9 species across 23 studies.
