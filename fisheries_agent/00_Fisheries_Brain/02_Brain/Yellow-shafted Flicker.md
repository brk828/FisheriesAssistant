# Yellow-shafted Flicker

## Summary
Yellow-shafted Flicker is a woodpecker species that suffered mortality from DDT applications for Dutch Elm Disease control. This source lists flickers among the songbird species affected in Wisconsin communities where DDT was sprayed for elm disease, though specific mortality data for this species were not provided.
