# Wendell L. Minckley

## Summary
Wendell L. Minckley was a prominent ichthyologist and aquatic ecologist whose work examined the impacts of human resource exploitation on freshwater ecosystems, particularly in the American Southwest. His conceptual frameworks and research on fish conservation and habitat degradation, especially regarding desert aquatic systems, significantly influenced modern conservation biology and fisheries management approaches. Minckley's scholarly contributions bridged theoretical ecology with practical conservation concerns, establishing important precedents for understanding anthropogenic effects on aquatic biodiversity.
