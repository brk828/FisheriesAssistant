# Rio San Bernardino

## Summary
Rio San Bernardino is a location in Sonora, Mexico near Mexico Highway 2 where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva was found at a low elevation site (subtropical marsh). Two small patches totaling less than 1 m² each were discovered in May 1988, but both were destroyed by flooding in August 1988.
