# Papagueria

## Summary
The Papagueria is a desert region in southwestern Arizona where Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument is located. This source describes it as a peripheral region of Hohokam Culture occupation, with continued expansion documented into the western Papagueria during the Classic period (A.D. 1150-1400).
