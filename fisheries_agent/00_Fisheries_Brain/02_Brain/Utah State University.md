# Utah State University

## Summary
Utah State University, particularly through its Institute for Natural Systems Engineering under researchers like Dr. Thomas B. Hardy, has served as a key academic institution contributing to endangered species conservation and aquatic ecosystem management in the Colorado River basin and related southwestern river systems. The university's work has focused on scientific research and permitting related to native fish conservation, habitat assessment, and flow management studies necessary for protecting threatened and endangered aquatic species. USU has collaborated with government agencies and other research organizations on critical projects involving river monitoring, geomorphological analysis, and the development of science-based management strategies for western aquatic ecosystems.
