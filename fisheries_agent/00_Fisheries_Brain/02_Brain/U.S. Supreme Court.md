# U.S. Supreme Court

## Summary
The U.S. Supreme Court described the Endangered Species Act in Tennessee Valley Authority v. Hill (437 U.S. 153, 180, 1978) as 'the most comprehensive legislation for the preservation of endangered species ever enacted by any nation.' This characterization is cited in the petition to emphasize the importance of the ESA's enforcement mechanisms that were suspended by the Hutchison Rider.
