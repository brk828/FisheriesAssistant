# Red Butte Reservoir

## Summary
Red Butte Reservoir is a lentic refuge site in Salt Lake County, Utah, used for June sucker conservation. This source reports that June sucker have successfully spawned and recruited in this reservoir, and as of the study period, it produced the largest numbers of returning stocked fish that were later recaptured during spawning runs in the Provo River, demonstrating its effectiveness as a refuge population site.
