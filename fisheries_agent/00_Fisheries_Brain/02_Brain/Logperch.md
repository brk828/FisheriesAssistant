# Logperch

## Summary
Logperch refers to small freshwater darter fish of the genus Percina, characterized by their elongated bodies and distinctive vertical bar patterns, commonly found in clear streams and rivers with rocky or gravelly substrates across North America. These benthic insectivores play an important ecological role in stream ecosystems as prey for larger fish species and as indicators of water quality and habitat integrity. Logperch have been subjects of scientific study regarding reproductive biology, geographic distribution, and conservation status, particularly in relation to stream habitat degradation and species management.
