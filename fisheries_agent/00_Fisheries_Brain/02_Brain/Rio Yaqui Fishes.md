# Rio Yaqui Fishes

## Summary
Rio Yaqui Fishes refers to the endemic fish fauna of the Río Yaqui drainage system in northwestern Mexico and the southwestern United States, which includes several threatened and endangered species such as the Yaqui chub (Gila ditaenia), spikedace (Meda fulgida), and loach minnow (Tiaroga cobitis). This fish assemblage became a significant focus of conservation and recovery planning efforts during the 1980s, with multiple species requiring formal recovery plans to address habitat degradation and population decline in this arid-region aquatic ecosystem. The Rio Yaqui fishes represent an important case study in conservation biology for endemic freshwater fish communities facing threats from water development and environmental change in southwestern North America.
