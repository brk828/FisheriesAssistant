# Fecundity

## Summary
Fecundity refers to the reproductive capacity measured by egg production in female striped bass. In the Colorado River population, mature ova ranged from 149,142 to 308,086 per kg body weight (mean 231,643 per kg), which was considerably greater than other populations such as Oregon (220,000/kg) and North Carolina (176,000/kg).
