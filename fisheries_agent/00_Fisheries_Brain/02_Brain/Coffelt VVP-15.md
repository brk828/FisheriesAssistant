# Coffelt VVP-15

## Summary
The Coffelt VVP-15 is electrofishing equipment used in the pilot experiments to expose fathead minnow eggs (and later razorback sucker eggs) to electrical fields. The equipment was used with waveforms and field intensities commonly employed to sample Razorback Sucker over spawning bars in the Flaming Gorge Flow Investigations program.
