# San Luis Rio Colorado

## Summary
San Luis Rio Colorado is mentioned as one of the border communities where the Nogales Health Coalition conducted surveys and community health assessments. The city represents one of the twin-city pairs in the border region facing environmental health challenges from transboundary pollution and inadequate infrastructure.
