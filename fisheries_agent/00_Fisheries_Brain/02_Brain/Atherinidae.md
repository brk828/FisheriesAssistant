# Atherinidae

## Summary
Atherinidae is a family of small, primarily euryhaline fishes commonly known as silversides, characterized by their slender bodies, terminal mouths, and a distinctive silvery stripe along their lateral line. Members of this family are distributed across freshwater, estuarine, and marine environments in the Americas and other regions, playing important ecological roles as forage fish and serving as indicators of environmental conditions in coastal and transitional aquatic systems. In the context of North American ichthyology, atherinids are notable components of estuarine fish assemblages and are frequently documented in surveys of fish distribution and anomalies, particularly in regions spanning from the eastern United States to Mexico.
