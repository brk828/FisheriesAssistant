# Tucson Basin

## Summary
The Tucson Basin is a region in southern Arizona that emerged as a second Hohokam core area. This source reports that during the Classic period (A.D. 1150-1400), there was a shift from Phoenix basin to Tucson Basin exchange networks at Organ Pipe, indicated by ceramic assemblages, and notes protohistoric sites with ceramics similar to the Tucson basin.
