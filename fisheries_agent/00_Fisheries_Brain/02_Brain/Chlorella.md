# Chlorella

## Summary
Chlorella is a green alga that formed blooms in the ORNL settling basin and demonstrated varying radioactivity accumulation depending on location. In the settling basin bloom, it accumulated 1.13 × 10⁵ counts/min/gram, while downstream at 8 miles it showed lower concentrations, illustrating the pattern of diminishing radioactivity with distance from the source.
