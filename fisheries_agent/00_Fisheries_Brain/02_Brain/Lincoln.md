# Lincoln

## Summary
Lincoln, California was the location of the Delbert Dowd Ranch where in 1950, five Sacramento squawfish (Ptychocheilus grandis) emerged from a 543-foot deep well. Specimens from this emergence were preserved at the California Academy of Sciences as collection #20,504.
