# Bridge Canyon

## Summary
Bridge Canyon is a location in the lower Grand Canyon at river mile 234.9 in Stratum A. This source reports that river stage monitoring at Bridge Canyon documented a maximum fluctuation of 36 cm over 20.7 hours, a temporary bench mark was established there for long-term monitoring, and red shiners were first encountered at this location (RM 235.2) during the study.
