# Desert Pupfish

## Summary
The Desert Pupfish (Cyprinodon maculatus) is a small, highly adaptable cyprinodontid fish endemic to the southwestern United States that has become a focal species in conservation biology due to its extreme vulnerability to habitat loss and water management changes in arid regions. This species exemplifies the conservation challenges facing desert aquatic fauna, as it persists in fragmented populations across isolated springs, creeks, artificial ponds, and agricultural drainage systems that are subject to rapid environmental fluctuations and anthropogenic alterations. Its recovery is coordinated through federal initiatives such as the Desert Fishes Recovery Team, making it a key indicator of desert ecosystem health and a priority for endangered species management in the American Southwest.
