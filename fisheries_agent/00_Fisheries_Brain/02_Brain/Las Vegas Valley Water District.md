# Las Vegas Valley Water District

## Summary
Las Vegas Valley Water District is a water agency planning a major surface water diversion project on the Virgin River, with the diversion point moved to approximately 1 mile north of Halfway Wash. The source indicates the project includes a storage reservoir at Halfway Wash, a diversion structure spanning the entire river width, and requires NEPA process and ESA consultation, with target review by Nevada State Engineer in July 1993/1994.
