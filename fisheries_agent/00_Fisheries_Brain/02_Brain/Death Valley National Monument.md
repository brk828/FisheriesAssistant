# Death Valley National Monument

## Summary
Death Valley National Monument serves as a critical research site and conservation focal point for studying isolated desert fish populations and their endemic species, particularly through the work of the Desert Fishes Council and affiliated researchers. The monument's geographically isolated spring systems, such as Saratoga Springs, provide unique natural laboratories for understanding the ecology, population dynamics, and conservation needs of endangered endemic fishes adapted to extreme desert aquatic environments. Meetings and symposia held at the monument have facilitated collaborative research and conservation planning for threatened species and their fragile habitats in the face of environmental threats and habitat degradation.
