# Cholame Ranch

## Summary
Cholame Ranch was the site of a 1952 fish emergence event in California where approximately 50 threespine sticklebacks (Gasterosteus aculeatus microcephalus) emerged from a 600-foot deep well with a 1,200 gallon per minute artesian flow. Multiple witnesses observed the sticklebacks swimming within the well shaft itself, and the high artesian velocity would have prevented fish from swimming up from surface sources, making this one of the most compelling cases for possible true subterranean fish emergence, though inadvertent introduction via 36,000 gallons of drilling water from roadside pools remains an alternative explanation.
