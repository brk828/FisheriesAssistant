# Wendell Minckley

## Summary
Wendell Minckley was a prominent ichthyologist and desert fish biologist whose expertise on Sonoran Desert fishes and aquatic ecosystems made him a key scientific figure in Colorado River Basin fish conservation efforts during the late 20th century. His work contributed to the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies program and informed recovery initiatives for endangered native fishes, particularly species like the Humpback Chub, through participation in collaborative research coordination and flow management discussions. Minckley's involvement in interdisciplinary roundtables and recovery programs exemplified the integration of basic ichthyological research with applied fisheries management for threatened desert aquatic systems.
