# Shallow Lake

## Summary
A shallow lake is a waterbody where wind can mix the entire water column, creating uniform temperatures with depth. Camerton Lake's maximum depth of 3.0 m allowed complete mixing, making water temperature more responsive to air temperature than in deeper lakes.
