# Sonoran Desertscrub Biome

## Summary
The Sonoran Desertscrub Biome is one of the major biotic communities in the study area, represented by Lower Desertscrub vegetation on easterly-facing hillslopes at around 2,920 feet elevation. This habitat type supported the highest total nocturnal rodent densities (up to 62.2 individuals/hectare) and was characterized by typical Sonoran Desert vegetation with high plant diversity and structural complexity.
