# Steelhead Trout

## Summary
Steelhead trout are anadromous salmonids that migrate between freshwater rivers and the ocean, returning to natal streams to spawn, making them a keystone species for both ecological integrity and fisheries management in Pacific Northwest river systems. These highly migratory fish are sensitive to habitat fragmentation caused by dams and other riverine barriers, necessitating extensive hatchery programs and dam removal efforts to maintain viable populations and genetic diversity across distinct regional runs. Conservation of steelhead trout serves as a critical indicator of overall watershed health and ecosystem function in North American river basins.
