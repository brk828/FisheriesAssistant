# Frenchman Reservoir

## Summary
Frenchman Reservoir is a water body in California where northern pike were first confirmed in the state in 1988 when a 7-pound individual was captured. The reservoir was chemically treated in 1991 with 15,000 gallons of Nusyn-noxfish to eradicate pike, and the treatment was considered successful as subsequent sampling failed to capture any northern pike.
