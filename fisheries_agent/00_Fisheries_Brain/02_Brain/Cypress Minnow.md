# Cypress Minnow

## Summary
The Cypress Minnow (*Hybognathus hayi*) is a rare cyprinid fish endemic to the southeastern United States, historically known from isolated wetland and oxbow habitats in states including Illinois, where it was long presumed extirpated. This species inhabits shallow, vegetated freshwater systems typically 0.3 to 2.3 meters deep and represents a conservation priority due to its restricted range, low abundance, and vulnerability to wetland habitat loss. Rediscovery efforts in the late 1980s and early 1990s documented its continued persistence in relict populations and expanded its known distribution, providing important insights into its ecology and status.
