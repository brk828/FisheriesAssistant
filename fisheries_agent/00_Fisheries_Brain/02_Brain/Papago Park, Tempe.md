# Papago Park, Tempe

## Summary
Papago Park, Tempe is the location of an Arizona Game and Fish Department hatchery where the first documented introduction of Tilapia to the Lower Colorado River Basin occurred. This source reports that fish identified as Tilapia mossambica were imported from Hawaii to this facility in March 1961, marking the beginning of Tilapia establishment in Arizona waters.
