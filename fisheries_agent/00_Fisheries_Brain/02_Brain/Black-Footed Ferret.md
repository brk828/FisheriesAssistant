# Black-Footed Ferret

## Summary
The black-footed ferret is not a fish species but rather serves as a comparative reference point in ichthyological and conservation literature to illustrate the severity of endangerment faced by certain fish species like the bonytail chub (*Gila elegans*) in the Colorado River system. By equating the conservation status of critically imperiled fish with that of the black-footed ferret—one of North America's most endangered mammals—fisheries biologists emphasize that some aquatic species face extinction risks comparable to the most dire terrestrial conservation crises.
