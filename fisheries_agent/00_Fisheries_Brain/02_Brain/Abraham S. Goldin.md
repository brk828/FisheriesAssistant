# Abraham S. Goldin

## Summary
Abraham S. Goldin was an author affiliated with the Robert A. Taft Sanitary Engineering Center who co-authored this 1959 paper on radioactive waste disposal. He contributed to the analysis of radioactive contamination pathways and the development of methods for assessing environmental and public health implications of nuclear facility discharges.
