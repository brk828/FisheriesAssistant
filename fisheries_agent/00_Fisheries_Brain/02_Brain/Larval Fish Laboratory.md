# Larval Fish Laboratory

## Summary
The Larval Fish Laboratory is a specialized research facility, housed at Colorado State University as part of the Colorado River Recovery Program, dedicated to the identification, analysis, and archival study of larval fish specimens from the Colorado River system. This laboratory conducts comparative analyses of historical and contemporary larval fish collections to determine the presence and distribution of native fish species, particularly endangered species such as the Razorback Sucker and Humpback Chub, in support of conservation and recovery efforts. The facility plays a critical role in understanding recruitment patterns, habitat utilization, and population dynamics of native fishes through systematic examination of ichthyoplankton samples.
