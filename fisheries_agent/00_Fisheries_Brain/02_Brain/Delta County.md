# Delta County

## Summary
Delta County is the Colorado county where Forest Lake is located on Grand Mesa (R. 94 W., T. 12 S.). The study notes that water stored in Forest Lake is released via the outlet stream for irrigation of fruit orchards in the valley below the mesa.

## Context
