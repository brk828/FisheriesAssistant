# Vaucheria

## Summary
Vaucheria is a filamentous alga found in seepage streams that demonstrated significant radioactivity accumulation. A mat sample accumulated 22,000 counts/min/gram dry weight and maintained this activity for 14+ days with virtually no decay, indicating stable retention of radioactive materials in the algal tissue.
