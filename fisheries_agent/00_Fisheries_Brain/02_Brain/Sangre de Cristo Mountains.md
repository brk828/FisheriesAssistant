# Sangre de Cristo Mountains

## Summary
The Sangre de Cristo Mountains are a major mountain range in northern New Mexico that serves as the headwaters for both the Pecos River and Canadian River. This source documents that uplift of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains during late Pliocene time deprived the ancestral Pecos River of much of its headwaters, which were diverted into the newly established south-flowing Rio Grande.
