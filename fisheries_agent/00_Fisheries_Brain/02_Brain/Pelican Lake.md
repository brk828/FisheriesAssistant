# Pelican Lake

## Summary
Pelican Lake is a water body near the Ouray National Wildlife Refuge in Utah that serves as a potential water source for endangered fish hatchery operations. The study notes that natural water temperatures in Pelican Lake range from 32° to 81°F annually, with growing season temperatures of 68-81°F, making it suitable for warm-water fish culture.
