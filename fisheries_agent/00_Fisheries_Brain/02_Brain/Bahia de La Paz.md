# Bahia de La Paz

## Summary
Bahia de La Paz is a coastal system in Baja California Sur, Mexico, where Dr. Contreras Balderas conducted ichthyological research. This work was part of his broader studies on Mexican aquatic ecosystems, which included both interior and coastal systems.
