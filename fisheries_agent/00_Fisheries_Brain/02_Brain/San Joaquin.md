# San Joaquin

## Summary
San Joaquin was one of the top three fish-producing hatcheries by weight in California during 1964-65, producing 174,185 pounds. When Nimbus Hatchery exceeded its king salmon egg capacity, eggs were transferred to San Joaquin (along with Moccasin hatchery), which produced nearly 3,000,000 king salmon fingerlings from these transferred eggs.
