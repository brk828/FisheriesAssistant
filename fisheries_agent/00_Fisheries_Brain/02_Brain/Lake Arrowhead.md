# Lake Arrowhead

## Summary
Lake Arrowhead is one of seven lakes studied in this evaluation of California's catchable trout program. It was used as a representative water body to assess harvest rates, angling effort, and catchability of stocked rainbow trout through creel census methods.

## Context

> This comprehensive study evaluates California's catchable-sized trout stocking program from 1954-1958, during which approximately seven million 7-to-8-inch [[Rainbow Trout]] (Salmo gairdnerii) were stocked annually at a cost of $1,366,000.
