# Sedgwick-Rafter Counting Chamber

## Summary
The Sedgwick-Rafter Counting Chamber was the quantitative methodology used to analyze plankton samples collected from Lake Powell. This technique, following P.S. Welch's limnological methods, was used to count zooplankton organisms in triplicate subsamples under binocular microscope at 3.5x to 20x magnification.
