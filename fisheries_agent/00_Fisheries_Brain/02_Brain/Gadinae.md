# Gadinae

## Summary
Gadinae is a subfamily of Gadidae characterized by larger, more developed optic lobes and variable cerebellar structure that correlates strongly with ecology—elevated cerebellum in mobile coastal species versus cerebellum applied to medulla in deep-water species. This subfamily includes both highly mobile coastal/anadromous species and deep-water forms, representing the greatest ecological diversity among gadid subfamilies.
