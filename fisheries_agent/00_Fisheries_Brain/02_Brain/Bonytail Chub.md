# Bonytail Chub

## Summary
The Bonytail Chub (Gila elegans) is a native cyprinid fish endemic to the Colorado River Basin and is considered one of the most critically endangered fish species in Arizona and the western United States. This species has experienced severe population declines due to habitat loss, water management practices, dam construction, and competition from non-native fish species, leading to its status as a federally protected species requiring active conservation management. Recovery efforts have focused on habitat restoration, captive breeding and reintroduction programs, and strategic stocking in suitable refuge waters to prevent extinction and restore viable populations in its historical range.
