# Clear Lake

## Summary
Clear Lake, Iowa is a shallow, turbid freshwater lake characterized by limited water clarity (Secchi-disc readings typically less than 24 inches) and diverse habitat types including heavy aquatic vegetation, rocky substrates, and dock structures that support varied fish populations. As a study site in ichthyology and fisheries research, Clear Lake has served as an important model system for developing and testing fish sampling methodologies, particularly electrofishing techniques for population surveys in vegetated freshwater systems. The lake's physical characteristics—shallow depth, high turbidity, and complex habitat structure—make it ecologically representative of many productive temperate lakes and relevant for understanding fish community dynamics and conservation management in comparable aquatic ecosystems.
