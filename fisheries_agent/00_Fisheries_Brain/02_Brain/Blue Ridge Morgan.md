# Blue Ridge Morgan

## Summary
Blue Ridge Morgan was a proposed forest management project on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that designated 973 acres for management toward threshold conditions for Mexican Spotted Owl habitat. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service concurred with the Forest Service's determination that this project may affect but is not likely to adversely affect listed species or critical habitat.
