# Gastropoda

## Summary
Gastropoda refers to the class of mollusks that includes snails and slugs, which serve as important bioindicators and study subjects in aquatic ecology and water quality assessment. In freshwater ecosystems, gastropods are valued for their sedentary nature and sensitivity to environmental conditions, making them more reliable indicators of water quality and pollution than highly mobile organisms like fish. These organisms are frequently surveyed as part of malacological investigations to understand aquatic community composition, biodiversity, and the ecological health of freshwater systems.
