# Sonoran Sucker

## Summary
The Sonoran Sucker (Catostomus insignis) is a native cyprinid fish species endemic to the Sonoran Desert region of Arizona, including streams like Aravaipa Creek, where it represents a key component of warmwater desert stream fish communities adapted to flashy, low-gradient hydrology. As a benthic sucker, it fills an important ecological role in native desert stream guilds alongside species like Longfin Dace and Roundtail Chub, but has become increasingly vulnerable to habitat degradation, water extraction, and competition from introduced species. Its presence and status serve as an indicator of stream ecosystem health in the southwestern United States, making it a species of conservation concern in regional biodiversity assessments.
