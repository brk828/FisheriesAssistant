# Philipson Bomb

## Summary
The Philipson Bomb is a modified bomb calorimeter manufactured by Gentry-Wiegert Instruments, representing one of three common analytical procedures for measuring calorific values. The compilation acknowledges that methodological differences among calorimetry techniques can affect reported values.
