# Iris Spring

## Summary
Iris Spring was a proposed project on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that involved treatments in Mexican Spotted Owl critical habitat. The project was designed to affect only a small portion (1,000 acres) of a much larger critical habitat unit (81,873 acres), and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service concurred with the Forest Service's determination that it was not likely to adversely affect listed species or critical habitat.
