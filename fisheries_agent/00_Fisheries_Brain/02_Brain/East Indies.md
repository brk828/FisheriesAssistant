# East Indies

## Summary
The East Indies is a zoogeographic region from which the Philippine Subregion is distinguished as a separate, coordinate unit. Despite intensive dredging, the Albatross failed to obtain numerous East Indies macrourid species in the Philippine Subregion, demonstrating the faunal distinctness created by the region's unusual warm deep-water temperatures.
