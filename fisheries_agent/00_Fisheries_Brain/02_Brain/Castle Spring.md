# Castle Spring

## Summary
Castle Spring is a spring in Eddy County, New Mexico, that supports populations of Blue Spring Fontelicella. The population at this location fluctuated from rare in 1968 to common during 1971-1980, and the report recommends acquiring water rights and establishing a 0.8 km natural sanctuary from the source to a cement canal.
