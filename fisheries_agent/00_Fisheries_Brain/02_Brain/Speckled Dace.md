# Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Speckled Dace (*Rhinichthys osculus*) is a small-bodied cyprinid minnow native to southwestern North American aquatic systems, particularly in the Colorado River basin and associated desert drainages. This species is of conservation interest because it exhibits multiple distinct evolutionary units or populations with significant genetic differentiation, raising questions about appropriate management and protection strategies for different lineages. The Speckled Dace serves as an important indicator species in desert aquatic ecosystems and is frequently documented in surveys of native fish communities, though specific conservation status varies by population.
