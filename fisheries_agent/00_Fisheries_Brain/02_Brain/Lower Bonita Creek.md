# Lower Bonita Creek

## Summary
Lower Bonita Creek is a riparian corridor in Graham County, Arizona, located 37 km northeast of Safford, flowing at elevations between 975-1128 m before joining the Gila River. It supports among the highest breeding bird species richness found in southwestern riparian systems (62 species recorded in 1985-1986), though the system has been degraded by over 100 years of cattle grazing and severe floods in 1978-1979 and 1983 that removed much of the mature canopy vegetation. A 3 km cattle exclosure was established in 1984 to assess whether excluding cattle would allow successful regeneration of broadleaf riparian trees and improve wildlife habitat.
