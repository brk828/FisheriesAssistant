# Rio Chama

## Summary
The Rio Chama is one of three major tributaries draining upper elevation forested lands into the Middle Rio Grande, confluencing near Espanola. These tributary systems, primarily on U.S. Forest Service lands at elevations of 2,000-2,600 m, historically provided coldwater habitat for native trout and associated cyprinids and catostomids.
