# Baetid Mayflies

## Summary
Baetid mayflies are aquatic insects in the family Baetidae that serve as important prey items in freshwater ecosystems. In Sycamore Creek, baetid mayflies (including Baetis quilleri and Tricorythodes dimorphus) were the preferred insect prey of Longfin Dace when abundant, comprising a major portion of macroinvertebrate biomass (>90% with chironomids) in May 1983, though their relative abundance declined to only 9% of macroinvertebrate biomass by November 1983.
