# Scomber japonicus diego

## Summary
Scomber japonicus diego (Pacific mackerel) is the most abundant identified species at the site with 23 specimens. This offshore species ordinarily occurs offshore but is not strictly pelagic, was probably taken most frequently from boats, and two vertebrae were partly charred suggesting cooking or disposal in fire.
