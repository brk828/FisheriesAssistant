# Sashin Creek

## Summary
Sashin Creek is a pink salmon spawning stream on Baranof Island in Southeastern Alaska that has been a Bureau of Commercial Fisheries research site since 1934. The study area encompasses approximately 13,600 square meters of spawning ground with stream bottom composed of 48% particles >25mm, 44% between 1-25mm, and 8% <1mm, supporting escapements of nearly 50,000 female pink salmon with potential deposition of approximately 100 million eggs.
