# John Hamill

## Summary
John Hamill was involved in recovery planning for endangered Colorado River fishes in the upper basin. According to this source, he outlined the upper basin recovery plan at the 1987 meeting and made the controversial statement that Bonytail Chub should be considered "functionally extinct" and would not be considered when reviewing proposed water projects, a declaration that was met with silence and minimal apparent support from meeting attendees.
