# Lincoln-Petersen

## Summary
The Lincoln-Petersen estimator is a two-visit mark-recapture method that calculates population size as N̂ = Mn/m, where M is the number marked, n is the total captured on the second visit, and m is the number of marked individuals recaptured. This study finds that the Lincoln-Petersen estimator exhibits substantial negative bias at small sample sizes and recommends discontinuing its use in favor of the bias-corrected Chapman estimator for two-visit studies.
