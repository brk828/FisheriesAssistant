# Chara excelsa

## Summary
Chara excelsa is a tentatively identified species from Mexican collections (Tindall, 1960-1961) that was found to have N=28 chromosomes. The uncertain identification (indicated by a question mark in the proposal) reflects the taxonomic challenges being addressed through this cytotaxonomic study.
