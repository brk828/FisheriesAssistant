# Forage Fish

## Summary
Forage fish are small fish species that serve as a primary food source for larger predatory fish and other aquatic fauna within their ecosystems. These species, which include various minnows, shiners, and small herrings, occupy lower trophic levels and are characterized by their abundance, rapid reproduction, and availability in diverse aquatic habitats ranging from streams to lakes. The productivity and population dynamics of forage fish are critical factors influencing the growth, survival, and overall health of sport and commercial fish populations, making them essential components of aquatic food webs and fisheries management strategies.
