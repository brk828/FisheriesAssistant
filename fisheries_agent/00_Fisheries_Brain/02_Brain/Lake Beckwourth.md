# Lake Beckwourth

## Context
> - **Sierra Nevada spillover event**: Adams et al. (Abstract 51959) describe a probable Middle Pleistocene spillover of pluvial [[Lake Beckwourth]] (Feather River/Pacific drainage) into the [[Lake Lahontan]] basin, potentially transferring fish between Sierra Nevada west-slope and Lahontan drainages.
