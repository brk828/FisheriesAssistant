# Roaring River

## Summary
Roaring River is an upland stream system in middle Tennessee where introgressive hybridization between redeye bass and smallmouth bass has been documented. This source reports that by 1987, redeye bass (introduced in the 1950s) had expanded downstream from headwater reaches into middle and lower reaches, creating overlapping distributions with endemic smallmouth bass populations, and biochemical genetic analysis revealed heterozygous individuals throughout the river system indicating widespread hybridization across multiple generations.
