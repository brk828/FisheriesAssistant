# Tims Ford Dam

## Summary
Tims Ford Dam created the impoundment on the upper Elk River and is responsible for thermal alteration of the downstream tailwaters, changing them from warm to cold water habitat. The dam's power generation operations cause water level fluctuations of 2-3 feet that significantly impact Boulder Darter reproductive habitat by altering the specific depth and current velocity conditions that males select for spawning sites.

## Context

> The [[Boulder Darter]] (*Etheostoma wapiti*) is a small endangered member of the perch family (Percidae) listed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in 1988. This bottom-dwelling darter survives in only approximately 63 miles (101 kilometers) of the lower [[Elk River]] system in southern Tennessee...
