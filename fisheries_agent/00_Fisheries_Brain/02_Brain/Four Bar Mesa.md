# Four Bar Mesa

## Summary
Four Bar Mesa is a location near Highway 666 in the Blue River area where a proposed highway joining Highway 666 already existed. This highway proposal was identified as a potential cumulative impact concern, as increased recreational traffic from the Pigeon Creek Lake project might necessitate construction of a new access road along the San Francisco River and Blue River.
