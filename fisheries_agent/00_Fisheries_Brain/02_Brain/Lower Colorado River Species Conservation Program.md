# Lower Colorado River Species Conservation Program

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Species Conservation Program was established through a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) executed in August 1995 by federal agencies including the U.S. Department of the Interior, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, and Bureau of Reclamation. According to this source, the program was developed to accommodate current water diversions and power production while working toward recovery of endangered species, but was challenged for violating the Endangered Species Act by reversing the legal requirement that federal agencies give endangered species priority over their primary missions. The MOA was alleged to improperly predetermine reasonable and prudent alternatives for biological opinions, delegate recovery authority to a multi-party Steering Committee rather than FWS, and was executed without public notice, NEPA compliance, or proper ESA Section 7 consultation.
