# Western Longnose Sucker

## Summary
Western Longnose Sucker (Catostomus catostomus) was one of three sucker species targeted in rough fish control operations across the study waters. A total of 10,253 pounds of suckers (all three species combined) were removed through trapping, seining, gill-netting, and rotenone treatment during 1953.
