# Invasive Fish Species

## Summary
Invasive fish species are non-native fish populations that establish themselves in aquatic ecosystems outside their natural geographic range, typically introduced through human activities such as aquaculture, aquarium releases, or bait bucket transfers. These species often outcompete native fish for resources, prey upon indigenous species, and alter habitat conditions, thereby threatening biodiversity and disrupting ecological balance in their new environments. Their successful establishment and spread can have significant economic and ecological consequences for native fisheries and aquatic community structure.
