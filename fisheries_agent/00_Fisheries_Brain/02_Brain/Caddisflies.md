# Caddisflies

## Summary
Caddisflies are aquatic insects (genus Hydropsyche and Goera identified in the study) that were abundant in Cascadilla Creek but formed only a very small percentage of Black-nosed Dace diet. Despite being as abundant and presumably as easy to catch as mayflies, caddisflies were largely avoided by the dace, demonstrating selective feeding preferences rather than indiscriminate consumption of available prey.
