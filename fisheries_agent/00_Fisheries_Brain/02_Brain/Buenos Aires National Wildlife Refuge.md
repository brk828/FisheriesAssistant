# Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility located near Sasabe, Arizona that served as a critical propagation and reintroduction site for endangered native fish species, particularly the razorback sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*), during the 1980s recovery efforts. The refuge's isolated stock-watering ponds were repurposed as growout and refugium habitat where hatchery-reared fish could be reared to larger sizes before reintroduction into southwestern aquatic systems, functioning as an extension of the Dexter National Fish Hatchery operations. This refuge exemplifies the application of wildlife management lands for species recovery and conservation breeding programs in arid-region aquatic ecosystems.
