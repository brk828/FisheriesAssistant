# Enneacanthus obesus

## Summary
Enneacanthus obesus is a sunfish species in the family Centrarchidae occurring in Alabama waters. This source reports it has 19-22 scales around the caudal peduncle, which distinguishes it from the related Enneacanthus gloriosus that has 16-18 scales in this region.
