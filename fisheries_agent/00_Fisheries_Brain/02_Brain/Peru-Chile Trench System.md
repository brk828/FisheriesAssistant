# Peru-Chile Trench System

## Summary
The Peru-Chile Trench System is a deep-sea region in the Pacific Ocean located at approximately 07°53'S, 80°30'W. This source reports the collection of a new ostracod species from this trench system at 520 m depth, where water conditions include extremely low dissolved oxygen (0.15 ml/l), temperature of 7.74°C, and salinity of 34.637 parts per thousand, making it a habitat for specialized deep-sea benthonic organisms.

## Context

> This paper describes *Bathyconchoecia deeveyae*, a new species of deep-sea benthic ostracod collected from the [[Peru-Chile Trench System]] at 520 m depth during Cruise 11 of the R/V Anton Bruun in October 1965.
