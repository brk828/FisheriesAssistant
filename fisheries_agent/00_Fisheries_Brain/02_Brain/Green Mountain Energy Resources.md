# Green Mountain Energy Resources

## Summary
Green Mountain Energy Resources is an organization that collaborated with American Rivers over 18 months to develop the Low Impact Hydropower certification criteria. According to this source, this partnership resulted in a certification program establishing six environmental criteria for identifying environmentally preferable hydropower operations in restructured electricity markets.
