# South Spring

## Summary
A spring in the Roswell area of Chaves County, New Mexico, that formerly supported the Roswell Fontelicella but dried due to groundwater pumping. This source lists it as one of several Roswell-area springs that were eliminated, leaving only Roswell Country Club Spring with surviving populations.
