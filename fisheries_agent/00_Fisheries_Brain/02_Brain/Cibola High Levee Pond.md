# Cibola High Levee Pond

## Summary
Cibola High Levee Pond is a 4.9-acre cutoff oxbow used as off-channel habitat for endangered native fishes in the Lower Colorado River. This source reports that bonytail tracked at this site showed site fidelity, repeatedly returning to specific riprap crevices for cover, demonstrating successful use of the habitat features provided.

## Context

> **Taxonomic & Life-History Summary**: [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*, Cyprinidae) and [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*, Catostomidae) are endemic large-river fishes of the [[Colorado River Basin]]. Razorback sucker can live >40 years; a single female produces >200,000 ova per season....
