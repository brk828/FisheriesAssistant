# Barnstable County

## Summary
Barnstable County is the Cape Cod county in Massachusetts where Cliff Pond is located. Despite the pond's location in this county, only 12.6% of the angling pressure came from local Barnstable County residents, with most anglers traveling from elsewhere in Massachusetts and beyond.
