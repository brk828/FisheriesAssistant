# Sacramento Squawfish

## Summary
The Sacramento Squawfish (*Ptychocheilus grandis*) is a large native cyprinid endemic to California's major river systems, particularly the Sacramento-San Joaquin drainage, and represents one of the largest freshwater cyprinids native to North America. This species has experienced substantial range contraction and population decline documented in surveys from the 1970s-1980s, making it an important indicator of habitat degradation and ecosystem change in Sierra Nevada foothill streams and associated valley systems. As an apex predator within cyprinid communities, the Sacramento Squawfish has ecological significance for understanding predator-prey dynamics and the impacts of environmental perturbation on native fish assemblages in altered California aquatic ecosystems.
