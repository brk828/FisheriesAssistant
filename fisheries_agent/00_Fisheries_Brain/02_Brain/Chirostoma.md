# Chirostoma

## Summary
Chirostoma is a genus of freshwater silverside fishes (family Atherinopsidae) endemic to central Mexican lakes, particularly Lake Chapala, that have historically supported important subsistence and commercial fisheries in the region. These fishes represent a key component of Mexico's endemic lacustrine fauna and have been subjects of taxonomic revision and molecular phylogenetic study to understand their evolutionary relationships and biogeographic patterns within the Mesa Central's unique freshwater fish assemblage. Their conservation and management are significant concerns given their ecological importance to Mexican lake ecosystems and their vulnerability as endemic species with restricted geographic ranges.
