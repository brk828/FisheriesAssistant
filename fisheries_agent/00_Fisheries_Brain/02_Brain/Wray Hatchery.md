# Wray Hatchery

## Summary
Wray Hatchery is a Colorado fish production facility that contributed to the state's warm water stocking program in 1955. The hatchery was one of three sources (along with Las Animas Hatchery and the Fish Control and Salvage Unit) that produced Walleye fry and fingerlings, contributing to the 6,060,713 Walleye stocked statewide that year.
