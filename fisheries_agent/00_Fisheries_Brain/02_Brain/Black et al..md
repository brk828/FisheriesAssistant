# Black et al.

## Summary
Black et al. (1954) conducted previous research on lethal oxygen concentrations that provided validation for this study's findings. Their mean lethal oxygen value at 66.4°F under low carbon dioxide tension (0.73 p.p.m.) was only 0.05 p.p.m. lower than the curve value from this study, suggesting good agreement between methodologies.
