# Walleye

## Summary
The walleye (Sander vitreum) is a freshwater percid fish species native to North America that serves as an economically and recreationally important food fish in lakes and rivers throughout the northern United States and Canada. Characterized by their large reflective eyes adapted for low-light vision and predatory behavior, walleyes are sought-after game fish that support significant commercial and recreational fisheries. In conservation and aquaculture contexts, walleye populations are actively managed through hatchery propagation programs and stocking initiatives to maintain viable populations in both natural waters and managed fisheries.
