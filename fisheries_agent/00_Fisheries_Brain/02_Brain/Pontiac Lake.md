# Pontiac Lake

## Summary
Pontiac Lake is a 585-acre warm-water lake in Oakland County, Michigan, connected to the Clinton River drainage and maintained by a dam. During the experimental study, it was opened to year-round fishing and showed a catch composition of 79.6% bluegill, 7.3% largemouth bass, 5.5% yellow perch, and 4.8% crappie over the five-year period.
