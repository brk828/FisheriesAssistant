# Railroad Valley Tui Chub

## Summary
The Railroad Valley Tui Chub (Gila bicolor ssp.) is a cyprinid fish subspecies found in Bull Creek headwater springs, Duckwater Creek, Blue Eagle Spring, Butterfield Spring, and several artesian wells in Railroad Valley, northeastern Nye County, Nevada. Its status had not been recently checked at the time of this document, with threats including groundwater pumping for alfalfa irrigation and oil exploration activities.
