# Cetonurus robustus

## Summary
Cetonurus robustus is a deep-sea macrourid fish species known from the East Indies. This source reports it was absent from the Philippine Subregion collections, contributing to evidence of the faunal distinctness of the isolated Sulu Sea complex.
