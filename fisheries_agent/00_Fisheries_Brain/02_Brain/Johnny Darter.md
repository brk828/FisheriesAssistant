# Johnny Darter

## Summary
The Johnny Darter (*Etheostoma nigrum*) is a small benthic darter species commonly found in freshwater streams and rivers across North America, particularly in cool to moderate-temperature headwater and mid-reach habitats. As a member of the darter family (Percidae), it serves as an important indicator species for stream health and ecological assessments due to its sensitivity to water quality and habitat conditions. Its presence in ichthyological surveys and fish community studies reflects the structural complexity and stability of stream substrates, making it a valuable component of aquatic ecosystem monitoring and conservation biology.
