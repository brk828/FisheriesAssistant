# Gila Elegans

## Summary
Gila elegans, commonly known as the roundtail chub, is a native cyprinid fish species endemic to the Colorado River basin that has become a critical focus of conservation efforts due to its significant decline from habitat alteration, water management practices, and competition from introduced non-native species. As a medium-sized benthic forager, this species historically occupied diverse riverine habitats throughout the lower Colorado River system but now persists in fragmented populations, making it an important indicator species for the ecological health of southwestern aquatic ecosystems. Research on Gila elegans, including population monitoring, captive propagation, and genetic analysis, has been essential for understanding the conservation status of native Colorado River fishes and informing recovery strategies for this endangered species.
