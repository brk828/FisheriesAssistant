# Flat Bullhead

## Summary
Flat Bullhead (Ictalurus platycephalus) is a catfish species that prefers rocky riffle areas with rubble substrate. This survey collected 37 flat bullheads on the Yadkin River and 13 on the South Yadkin River above the dams, and notably found 23 flat bullheads in catfish basket traps on the Yadkin River, demonstrating their presence in diverse habitats alongside snail bullheads.
