# Red Bank Well

## Summary
Red Bank Well marks one end of a valley bottom closure area in Redrock Canyon. The biological opinion specifies that the valley bottom from Red Bank Well to Redrock Well would be closed to motorized vehicles and mountain bikes as a protective measure.
