# Splake

## Summary
A splake is a hybrid fish resulting from the cross between a female brook trout and a male lake trout, combining desirable traits from both parent species for aquaculture and fisheries management purposes. This interspecific hybrid has been utilized in fish stocking programs and conservation efforts, particularly in eastern North American freshwater systems, to enhance recreational fishing opportunities and adapt to changing environmental conditions. Splakes are valued for their growth rates and ability to thrive in various water conditions that may be marginal for either parent species alone.
