# Lake Oahe

## Summary
Lake Oahe is a reservoir in the Missouri River system where rainbow smelt were successfully introduced and established in 1971. In this lake, smelt occupied waters with temperatures of 6-14°C, suspending near the bottom during the day and moving upward to the thermocline at night, demonstrating their characteristic thermal partitioning behavior.
