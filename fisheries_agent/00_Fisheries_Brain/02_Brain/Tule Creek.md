# Tule Creek

## Summary
Tule Creek is a lotic (flowing water) system in Arizona that has served as a study site for monitoring populations of native aquatic species, particularly the Lowland Leopard Frog (*Rana yavapaiensis*), using mark-recapture methods such as Jolly-Seber models to assess population dynamics and fluctuations over time. The creek represents an important riparian habitat within the lower elevation range preferred by this species and has been the subject of conservation assessments and biological opinions regarding fish and amphibian management in the southwestern United States.
