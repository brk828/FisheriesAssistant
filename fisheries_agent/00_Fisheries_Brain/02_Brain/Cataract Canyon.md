# Cataract Canyon

## Summary
Cataract Canyon is a reach of the Upper Colorado River located in Utah that represents a significant geographic section within the native range of Colorado River fish species, particularly endemic chubs and other native fishes of conservation interest. This canyon segment, along with other Upper mainstem Colorado River reaches like Westwater Canyon and Black Rocks, serves as an important sampling locality for ichthyological surveys and museum specimen collection efforts aimed at documenting population variation and genetic diversity in native fish species. The canyon's fish communities have been studied as part of broader conservation and taxonomic investigations to understand species distribution, morphological characteristics, and the effects of river management on native aquatic fauna.
