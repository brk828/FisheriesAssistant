# Stephens Ranch

## Summary
Stephens Ranch was one of several long-term rainfall monitoring stations used in the hydrologic analysis of the Leslie Canyon watershed. It was included among the regional stations (along with Rucker Canyon, Douglas, Willcox, and Tucson) used to establish the hydrologic base period of 1951-1982 for estimating average annual runoff.
