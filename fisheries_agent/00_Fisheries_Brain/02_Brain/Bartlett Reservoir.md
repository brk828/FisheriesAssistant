# Bartlett Reservoir

## Summary
Bartlett Reservoir is a reservoir on the Verde River in Arizona, part of the Gila River basin. In March 1985, intensive monitoring using gill nets, trammel nets, and electrofishing was conducted to detect reintroduced Razorback Suckers, but none were found; the fish community was dominated by non-native species including Threadfin Shad (25.0% of net catch), Common Carp (22.9%), Largemouth Bass (66.1% of electrofishing catch), and Black Crappie (17.8% of net catch).
