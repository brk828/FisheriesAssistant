# Eldorado Canyon

## Summary
Eldorado Canyon is a geographical location within the Lake Mead reservoir system in the Arizona-Nevada region that served as a significant study site for fisheries research and fish movement investigations during the mid-20th century. The canyon area was utilized by ichthyologists conducting cooperative studies on fish distribution, population dynamics, and angling success across the Lake Mead warmwater fishery, which was characterized by diverse species composition including Largemouth Bass, Channel Catfish, and Black Crappie. This location provided important data for understanding fish behavior and habitat utilization patterns in large impoundment ecosystems of the southwestern United States.
