# Lawrence Brook

## Summary
Lawrence Brook is a 7.75-mile marginal trout stream in Royalston, Massachusetts, originating from a warm-water impoundment in New Hampshire and experiencing summer temperatures in the high 70s to low 80s. This source reports that following 1955 rotenone treatment and fingerling stocking, the stream showed modest fingerling survival (21 fish recovered from 19,000 stocked) but exceptional growth rates and a three-fold increase in trout numbers compared to pre-treatment, with anglers reporting the best fishing quality in memory despite complications from hurricane flooding and subsequent recolonization from the upstream pond.
