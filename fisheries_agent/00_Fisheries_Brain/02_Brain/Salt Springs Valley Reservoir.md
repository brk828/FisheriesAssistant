# Salt Springs Valley Reservoir

## Summary
Salt Springs Valley Reservoir is located in Calaveras County, California. It was one of the waters where the California Department of Fish and Game introduced Fathead Minnow, which had been imported from New Mexico in 1951 and 1953, as part of their forage fish stocking program.

## Context

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
