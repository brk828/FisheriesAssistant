# Little Timber Sale

## Summary
Little Timber Sale was a proposed timber thinning project on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that included commercial and precommercial thinning operations. The project designated 181 acres for management toward threshold conditions for Mexican Spotted Owl habitat and aimed to reduce open road density from 4.4 to 1.9 miles per section while improving forest health and reducing dwarf-mistletoe infection.
