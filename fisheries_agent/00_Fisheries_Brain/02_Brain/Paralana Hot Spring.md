# Paralana Hot Spring

## Summary
Paralana Hot Spring is one of the key spring complexes in the Great Artesian Basin documented in this bibliography. It is among the numerous spring locations throughout South Australia, Queensland, and New South Wales covered in the geological, hydrological, and biological literature compiled here.
