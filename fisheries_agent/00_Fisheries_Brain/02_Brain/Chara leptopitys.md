# Chara leptopitys

## Summary
Chara leptopitys is a species of stonewort that exhibits endopolyploidy, where different cells within the same organism have different chromosome numbers. This study documented that larger antheridia contained cells with 35 and 42+ chromosomes in paired groups alongside normal 14-chromosome cells.
