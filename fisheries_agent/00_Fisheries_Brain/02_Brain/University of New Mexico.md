# University of New Mexico

## Summary
The University of New Mexico is the institutional affiliation of Steven P. Platania, who coordinated the Desert Fishes Council Fish Count Program from this institution. The university served as an organizational hub for this collaborative monitoring initiative involving participants from six southwestern states.
