# Upper Sonoran

## Summary
Upper Sonoran refers to a biogeographic life zone characterized by grassland vegetation in this region. Blumer documented that Upper Sonoran grassland with Yucca Elata dominated the several-mile incline descending from the Gleeson foothills toward Sulfur Springs Valley, occurring at lower elevations than desert vegetation in an unusual inversion of typical altitudinal patterns.

> This historical account documents the botanical work of Swiss-born botanist [[Jacob Corwin Blumer]] (1872-1947) in southeastern [[Arizona Territory]] between August 1906 and October 1907.
