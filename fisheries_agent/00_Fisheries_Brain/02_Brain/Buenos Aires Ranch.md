# Buenos Aires Ranch

## Summary
Buenos Aires Ranch is the historic ranch property that became Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. The 112,500-acre refuge was established on former ranch lands in the Altar Valley of southeastern Arizona, primarily to protect and restore populations of the endangered Masked Bobwhite.
