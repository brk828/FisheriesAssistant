# Ranchers Exploration and Development Corporation

## Summary
Ranchers Exploration and Development Corporation was one of three active mining companies operating during the study period. The company's Bluebird facility experienced process solution losses that caused surface water degradation in Bloody Tanks Wash and a contamination plume in wash alluvium, though reduction of operations resulted in marked water quality improvements.
