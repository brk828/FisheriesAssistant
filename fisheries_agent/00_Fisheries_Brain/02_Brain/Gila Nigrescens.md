# Gila Nigrescens

## Summary
Gila nigrescens, commonly known as the Chihuahua chub, is a native cyprinid fish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui drainage in northern Mexico (Sonora and Chihuahua) that has been a focus of binational conservation translocation efforts. This species was targeted in multi-agency capture-and-transport expeditions involving U.S. and Mexican wildlife agencies in the 1980s as part of broader efforts to survey and potentially establish refuge populations of Rio Yaqui native fishes, reflecting early cooperative conservation initiatives for threatened desert fish taxa.
