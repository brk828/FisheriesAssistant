# Arizona Projects Office

## Summary
The Arizona Projects Office is a Bureau of Reclamation office that convened the July 19, 1993 meeting to discuss fish and wildlife enhancement opportunities associated with the Central Arizona Project. The office, through its Chief of Environmental Division Bruce Ellis, organized the meeting in response to discussions with environmental resource organizations and the Governor's Advisory Committee on CAP Environmental Working Group.
