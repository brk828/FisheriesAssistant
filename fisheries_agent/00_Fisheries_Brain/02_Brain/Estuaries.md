# Estuaries

## Summary
Estuaries are transitional aquatic ecosystems where rivers meet and mix with ocean water, creating unique environments characterized by fluctuating salinity, temperature, and nutrient levels that are critical nursery habitats for numerous fish species and other aquatic organisms. These dynamic coastal zones support complex ecological processes essential for fisheries productivity and biodiversity, making them particularly vulnerable to anthropogenic disturbances such as thermal pollution from industrial facilities and other water quality degradation. Understanding estuarine ecology and protecting these systems is fundamental to maintaining healthy fish populations and sustaining the fisheries resources that depend on them during various life stages.
