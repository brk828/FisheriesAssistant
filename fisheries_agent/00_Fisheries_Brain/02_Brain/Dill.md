# Dill

## Summary
Dill is a researcher who in 1944 characterized the river-reservoir system of the lower Colorado River following dam construction in the late 1930s. Dill predicted future ecological changes to the system resulting from these modifications.

## Context

> This 1953 report documents severe fishery losses resulting from [[Bureau of Reclamation]] channelization of the lower [[Colorado River]] between [[Needles]] and [[Topock]], California.
