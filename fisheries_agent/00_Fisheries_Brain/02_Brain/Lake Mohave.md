# Lake Mohave

## Summary
Lake Mohave is a large reservoir on the Colorado River between Arizona and Nevada, formed by Davis Dam, that serves as critical habitat for endangered native fish species including razorback suckers and bonytail chub. This impoundment and its associated riverine reaches below the dam have been the focus of extensive fisheries research and recovery efforts since the 1960s, with cooperative management programs between Arizona and Nevada aimed at monitoring and restoring populations of threatened endemic fish species. Lake Mohave's unique ecological conditions and fish communities make it a key study site for understanding the impacts of dam-induced habitat fragmentation and for evaluating conservation strategies in the Lower Colorado River Basin.
