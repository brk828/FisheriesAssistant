# Lilaeopsis schaffneriana

## Summary
Lilaeopsis schaffneriana is a rhizomatous herbaceous perennial plant in the parsley family with two subspecies separated by the continental divide in Mexico. Subspecies recurva, endemic to southeastern Arizona and northern Sonora, is a rare semi-aquatic plant found in cienega wetlands that produces slender cylindrical hollow leaves 3-20 cm tall from creeping underground rhizomes and tiny flowers on short peduncles. The species has experienced significant population losses at historic sites due to water diversions, groundwater pumping, and flood-related habitat destruction, with only about 15 locations documented in surveys from 1988-1990.
