# Chara hydropitys

## Summary
Chara hydropitys is a Characeae species collected during the Texas to Mexico expedition in September 1961. Chromosome counts revealed n=14, consistent with the pattern of chromosome numbers occurring in multiples of 7 found in the Chareae tribe.
