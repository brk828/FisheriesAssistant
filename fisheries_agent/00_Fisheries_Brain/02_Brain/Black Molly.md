# Black Molly

## Summary
Black Molly is a small fish species that never exceeds four inches in length. According to this 1963 report, California found Black Mollies by the millions in the Salton Sea, where fishermen could use dipnets to collect them as bait for Corvina, though California recommended no action on legalizing them for river bait until more information was available about potential impacts on the river fishery.
