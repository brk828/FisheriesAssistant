# Poecilia Mexicana

## Summary
Poecilia mexicana is a livebearer fish species native to Mexico that inhabits freshwater ecosystems, particularly in springs, streams, and coastal drainages from the Atlantic slope. As a member of the family Poeciliidae, this species is ecologically significant in its native range and has conservation relevance due to habitat loss and potential impacts from introduced species competition in Mexican aquatic systems. The species also has scientific importance as a model organism in ecological and evolutionary studies, particularly regarding adaptation to extreme environments and interspecific interactions in freshwater communities.
