# San Marcos River

## Summary
The San Marcos River is a spring-fed freshwater ecosystem in Texas notable for its exceptional biodiversity and endemism, particularly regarding its specialized fish fauna including species like the fountain darter (Etheostoma fonticola). As a karst-associated river system with relatively constant temperature and flow characteristics, it serves as a critical habitat for numerous aquatic organisms adapted to its unique hydrogeological conditions, making it an important subject of conservation efforts and ecological research focused on preserving endemic species and their specialized habitats.
