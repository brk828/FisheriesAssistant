# Elkhorn Creek

## Summary
Elkhorn Creek is a Kentucky waterway that has served as an important study site for ichthyological research, particularly for investigating fish growth rates and population composition across multiple species. As one of several Kentucky water bodies sampled in foundational mid-20th century fisheries surveys, Elkhorn Creek has contributed valuable baseline data on fish population dynamics and community structure relevant to the state's fisheries management and conservation efforts. The creek represents a typical Kentucky stream ecosystem used to understand fish productivity and inform management practices for both game and non-game fish species.
