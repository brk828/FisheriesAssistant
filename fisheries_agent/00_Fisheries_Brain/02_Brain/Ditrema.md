# Ditrema

## Summary
Ditrema is one of two Japanese genera within the Embiotocidae family, placed in the primitive Embiotocinae subfamily. This source considers multiple previously described species as synonyms, recognizing Ditrema temminckii as the valid species name.

## Context

> This 1918 systematic revision by Carl L. Hubbs reorganizes the family [[Embiotocidae]] (viviparous perches or surf-fishes) of [[California]], establishing three subfamilies based on anal fin structure and dentition.
