# Aurora Trout

## Summary
Aurora Trout (Salvelinus timagamiensis) was synonymized with Brook Trout (S. fontinalis) by Sale in 1967. This taxonomic change, documented in the checklist, eliminated Aurora Trout as a distinct species.
