# Fish Passage Barriers

## Summary
Fish passage barriers are physical or hydrological obstacles in aquatic systems that impede or prevent the movement of fish between habitats, including dams, culverts, waterfalls, and other structures that fragment riverine and coastal ecosystems. These barriers disrupt critical fish migration patterns necessary for spawning, feeding, and life cycle completion, leading to population isolation, reduced genetic diversity, and declining fisheries productivity. Understanding and mitigating fish passage barriers is essential for conserving migratory fish species and maintaining healthy aquatic ecosystems.
