# Gila copei

## Summary
Gila copei is a cyprinid fish species that shows striking morphological similarities to Lepidomeda but lacks the ossified, spine-like fin rays characteristic of traditional Plagopterini. This study found that G. copei's phylogenetic placement relative to other genera was poorly resolved despite allozymic similarity to Lepidomeda, with parsimony analysis placing it as sister to the Gila + Plagopterus + Meda clade rather than to Lepidomeda. The most parsimonious explanation is that G. copei may be a member of Plagopterini that lost the spiny-rayed characters present in the ancestral plagopterin.
