# Chemical Waste Management Inc. v. EPA

## Summary
Chemical Waste Management Inc. v. EPA (869 F.2d 1526, D.C. Cir. 1989) was a court decision that upheld EPA's expansive interpretation of hazardous waste rules. The court upheld extension of the 'derived-from' rule to leachate from wastes not considered hazardous when disposed and application of the 'mixture' rule to contaminated soil and groundwater under the 'contained-in rule,' thereby expanding the scope of materials classified as hazardous waste.
