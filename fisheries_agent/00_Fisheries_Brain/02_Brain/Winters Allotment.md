# Winters Allotment

## Summary
The Winters Allotment is a livestock grazing area on the Globe Ranger District in Arizona where rest rotation grazing systems implemented in the early 1980s have improved riparian and aquatic conditions. This source identifies it as containing high-quality Gila Topminnow reintroduction sites in Sevenmile Wash, demonstrating that compatible management for threatened fish species and livestock operations is achievable.
