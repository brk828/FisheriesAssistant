# Central Arizona Project Canal

## Summary
The Central Arizona Project Canal (CAP Canal) is a major water conveyance system in Arizona that was monitored at seven pump plant stations during this 1999 survey. This canal contained zero native fish species, with all 12 taxa captured being non-native, dominated by Bluegill (53% of catch) and Common Carp (22%), making it the only water system surveyed with complete absence of native species.
