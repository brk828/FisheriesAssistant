# Rana yavapaiensis

## Summary
Rana yavapaiensis is the lowland leopard frog, classified as rare. This source reports that this species has experienced dramatic population declines and extirpations from many localities in Arizona, California, and New Mexico over the 15 years prior to 1994, and is found in the Sierra San Luis protected area.
