# Ligia

## Summary
Ligia is a genus of isopods found in the upper intertidal zone at Puerto Peñasco. These organisms are the primary scavengers in the upper subzone of Zone I (+15 to +19 feet), where they inhabit bare granite rocks in the splash zone, and are replaced by beach isopods (Tylos) and amphipods in sandy beach areas.
