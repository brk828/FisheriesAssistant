# University Arboretum Center

## Summary
The University Arboretum Center near 'A' Butte at Arizona State University features a 50-yard recycling aquatic ecosystem designed for endangered fish conservation, with pools, riparian vegetation, and marshland. This facility provides semi-natural conditions between field and laboratory settings where students conduct water quality analysis, biological monitoring, and life-history studies of endangered fishes using non-sacrificial techniques, in collaboration with Phoenix Zoo, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, and Arizona Game and Fish Department.
