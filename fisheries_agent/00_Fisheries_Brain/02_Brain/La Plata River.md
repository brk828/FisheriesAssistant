# La Plata River

## Summary
The La Plata River is a tributary of the San Juan River in New Mexico. According to this source, the proposed Animas-La Plata irrigation project would pump water from the Animas River in Colorado to an offstream reservoir, with releases going to the La Plata River for domestic and irrigation use.
