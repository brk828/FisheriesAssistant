# Thunnus thynnus

## Summary
Thunnus thynnus (bluefin tuna) is represented at the site by 3 vertebrae, with the largest from a fish weighing perhaps 50 pounds. This strictly pelagic species provides clear evidence of boat use by aboriginal fishermen, as it could not have been caught from shore.
