# Pecos River

## Summary
The Pecos River is a major tributary of the Rio Grande in the southwestern United States that spans approximately 650 kilometers from Roswell, New Mexico, to its confluence with the Rio Grande in Texas, encompassing diverse aquatic habitats including saline springs, gypsum sinkholes, and desert streams. This river system is particularly significant in fish conservation and aquatic ecology due to its unique hydrogeochemistry and endemic fish fauna, making it a critical focus area for understanding cyprinid distributions and managing threatened species in arid freshwater ecosystems. The Pecos River has been central to debates regarding introduced versus native fish species and represents an important case study in southwestern aquatic conservation biology.
