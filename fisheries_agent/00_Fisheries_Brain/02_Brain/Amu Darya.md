# Amu Darya

## Summary
The Amu Darya is a 2500 km river forming Afghanistan's northern boundary with the USSR, flowing from the Pamirs to the Aral Sea. Its fish fauna (20.4% of Afghanistan's species) shows strong affinities with the Caspian Sea basin due to historical connections via the Uzboi Valley, and includes endemic genera like Aspiolucius and Pseudoscaphirhynchus, though the upper reaches are impoverished compared to lower sections.
