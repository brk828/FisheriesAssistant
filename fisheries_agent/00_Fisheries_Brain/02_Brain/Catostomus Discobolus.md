# Catostomus Discobolus

## Summary
Catostomus discobolus, commonly known as the bluehead sucker, is a native cypriniform fish species endemic to the Colorado River basin and its tributaries, including systems like Kanab Creek in the Grand Canyon region. This species is ecologically significant for understanding habitat partitioning and spawning dynamics in high-gradient desert streams, where its distribution is heavily constrained by physical barriers such as bedrock reaches and boulder jams that limit upstream migration and reproduction. The species' presence and reproductive success in tributary systems serve as important indicators of native fish community health and habitat connectivity in southwestern aquatic ecosystems facing ongoing conservation challenges.
