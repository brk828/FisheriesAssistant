# Santa Rita Grazing System

## Summary
The Santa Rita Grazing System is a rest-rotation grazing management approach that would be implemented in the proposed action. Two separate Santa Rita systems would manage the north pastures (Maverick, Log Corral, Pine Creek) and south pastures (Otero, Picadilla, Adams), providing six months of use followed by twelve months of rest.
