# Grand Valley Irrigation Company

## Summary
Grand Valley Irrigation Company operates a diversion structure on the Colorado River that historically blocked fish passage. The FY 1998 work plan allocated $370,000 for completion of a fish passage structure at this diversion, which would restore access to 3 additional miles of Colorado River habitat for endangered fish species.
