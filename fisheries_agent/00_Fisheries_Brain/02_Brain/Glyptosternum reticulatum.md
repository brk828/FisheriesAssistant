# Glyptosternum reticulatum

## Summary
Glyptosternum reticulatum is a sisorid catfish found in Afghanistan. This source identifies it as one of only two Siluriformes species in the Kabul River basin that are not Oriental elements, suggesting it has different zoogeographic origins than most catfish in that drainage.
