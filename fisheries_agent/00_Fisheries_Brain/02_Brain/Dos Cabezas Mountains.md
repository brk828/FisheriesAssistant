# Dos Cabezas Mountains

## Summary
The Dos Cabezas Mountains are a 20-mile-long range on the western boundary of San Simon Valley, named for prominent twin peaks that rise to approximately 8,000 feet elevation. This source describes them as nearly bare mountains with no important streamways contributing to San Simon Valley, and notes their strong orographic influence on local precipitation patterns.
