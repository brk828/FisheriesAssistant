# San Simon Cienaga

## Summary
San Simon Cienaga is a 1,200-acre marsh located in San Simon Valley with black organic-rich soil from accumulated vegetation and a water table at the surface. This source identifies it as the origin point of San Simon Creek and notes it represents the shallowest groundwater conditions in the valley.
