# Lake Helena

## Summary
Lake Helena is a 2,100-acre man-made reservoir in Montana that serves as one of four reservoirs supporting the state's commercial fisheries operations. It is located east of the Continental Divide and contains species with commercial value including Carp, Buffalofish, Goldeye, and Suckers.
