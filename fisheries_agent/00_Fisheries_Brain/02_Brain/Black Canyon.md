# Black Canyon

## Summary
Black Canyon refers to the riverine reach of the Colorado River immediately below Hoover Dam, which represents critical habitat for endangered fish species including the razorback sucker and bonytail chub. This geomorphologically distinct canyon section maintains riverine conditions distinct from the reservoir environments upstream, providing essential spawning and rearing habitat for native fish populations that depend on flowing water conditions. As a conservation priority area, Black Canyon has been central to recovery efforts and monitoring programs for Colorado River endemic fishes since at least the late 1970s.
