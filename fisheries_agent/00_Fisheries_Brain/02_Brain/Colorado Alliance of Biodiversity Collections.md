# Colorado Alliance of Biodiversity Collections

## Summary
The Colorado Alliance of Biodiversity Collections (CABC) is a newly emerged organization that functionally unites natural history collections in Colorado. It provides a potential framework for ensuring the permanency and online access of the Upper Colorado River Basin fish collections housed at Colorado State University.

## Context

> This FY 1993 annual report documents the second year of a three-year project (1992-1994) to reorganize, catalogue, and manage the massive holdings of preserved fish specimens from the Upper Colorado River Basin housed at the Larval Fish Laboratory, Colorado State University.
