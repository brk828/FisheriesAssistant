# The Nature Conservancy

## Summary
The Nature Conservancy, as referenced in desert fish conservation literature, is a major non-governmental organization that has collaborated with ichthyologists and the Desert Fishes Council to support research, habitat protection, and species recovery programs for endemic and threatened fish populations in southwestern aquatic ecosystems. The organization has played a key funding and advocacy role in conservation initiatives targeting critically endangered species in isolated desert springs and riparian corridors, particularly in the American West where numerous single-locality fish species face extinction threats. Through partnerships with academic institutions and government agencies, The Nature Conservancy has contributed to habitat management planning, biological opinion development, and long-term monitoring protocols essential for the survival of desert fish fauna.
