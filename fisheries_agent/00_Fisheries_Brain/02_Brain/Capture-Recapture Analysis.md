# Capture-Recapture Analysis

## Summary
Capture-recapture methods were used to document razorback sucker movements and spawning site fidelity, with 68 fish being recaptured during the study. The analysis revealed that 91% of recaptured fish remained in the Jensen spawning site, 31 fish showed fidelity to specific spawning reaches across years, and one male was recaptured at the exact same Yampa River location seven years after initial capture.
