# Freshwater Wetland

## Summary
Freshwater Wetland refers to inland aquatic habitats characterized by shallow water and emergent vegetation. This source documents a managed freshwater wetland pond system consisting of ten interconnected units served by canals, with infrastructure modifications planned to address ecological succession, vegetation changes, and habitat quality for aquatic organisms over time.
