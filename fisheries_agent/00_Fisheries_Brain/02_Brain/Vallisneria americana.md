# Vallisneria americana

## Summary
Vallisneria americana, commonly known as wild celery, is an aquatic plant that grew luxuriantly in some areas of the Red Cedar River during this study. Its photosynthetic oxygen production contributed to maintaining dissolved oxygen levels above 90% saturation despite elevated biochemical oxygen demand from sewage pollution.
