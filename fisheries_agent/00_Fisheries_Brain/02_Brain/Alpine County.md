# Alpine County

## Summary
Alpine County is a California county where Heenan Lake is located, which served as the collection site for Lahontan Cutthroat Trout specimens used in this immunogenetic study. The Lahontan Cutthroat Trout from this location were among five trout species examined for erythrocyte antigens.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
