# Caspian Sea

## Summary
The Caspian Sea basin is a major zoogeographic source region whose fish fauna shares many species with Afghanistan's Amu Darya basin due to historical transgressions linking these basins, including at the end of the Pleistocene via the Uzboi Valley. Shared species include Acipenser nudiventris, Salmo trutta, Barbus brachycephalus, and several others.
