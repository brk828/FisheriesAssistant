# Bartlett Dam

## Summary
Bartlett Dam is a reservoir structure on the Verde River in Arizona, completed in 1939. It is one of six major dams constructed in the Salt-Verde drainage system that together created combined storage capacity exceeding 2 million acre-feet, fundamentally altering the predevelopment hydrology of the region.
