# Angling Mortality

## Summary
Angling mortality is identified as one of three major factors contributing to the continual decline of Shoal Bass throughout their range. This source notes that angling pressure, along with dams and non-native black basses, threatens populations, though specific mortality rates are not quantified in the excerpted text.
