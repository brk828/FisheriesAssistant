# Lake Cibola

## Summary
Lake Cibola is an area on the lower Colorado River identified as a location for potential future channelization projects. The report mentions it as one of several sections where additional channelization was being planned, which would likely result in similar fishery impacts as observed at Needles-Topock.

## Context

> This 1953 report documents severe fishery losses resulting from [[Bureau of Reclamation]] channelization of the lower [[Colorado River]] between [[Needles]] and [[Topock]], California.
