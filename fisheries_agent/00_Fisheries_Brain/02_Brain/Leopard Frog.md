# Leopard Frog

## Summary
The Leopard Frog, likely referring to the native *Rana pipiens* or related ranid species, appears in aquatic survey documentation as an incidental herpetological observation in southwestern desert aquatic systems. While not a fish species itself, its presence in fisheries and aquatic ecology surveys indicates the broader biodiversity and ecosystem health of freshwater habitats being studied for conservation purposes. The frog's occurrence in these riparian and spring-fed systems serves as a supplementary indicator of aquatic habitat quality alongside ichthyological surveys of native fish populations.
