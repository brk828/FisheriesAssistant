# EPA

## Summary
The Environmental Protection Agency's pesticide program accounted for 169 of the 350 jeopardy opinions issued during FY 1987-1991 (covering 113 chemicals and 169 uses), representing an abnormal consultation pattern that skewed overall statistics. Despite receiving jeopardy opinions, all 169 EPA pesticide activities proceeded after adopting reasonable and prudent alternative labeling requirements.
