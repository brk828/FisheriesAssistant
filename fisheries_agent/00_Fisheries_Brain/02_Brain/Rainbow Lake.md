# Rainbow Lake

## Summary
Rainbow Lake is a lentic fishery in Arizona's Game and Fish Department Region I that was subject to creel census monitoring in the 1960s to assess recreational fishing productivity and trout populations. The lake supported a rainbow trout fishery with documented catch rates of 0.44 fish per man-hour and average trout lengths of 9.6 inches, making it a moderate-productivity recreational fishery compared to nearby Arizona lakes. Rainbow Lake, like other District I waters, faced significant management challenges from irrigation-driven water drawdown, which degraded aquatic habitat quality and represented a primary conservation concern for fisheries management during this period.
