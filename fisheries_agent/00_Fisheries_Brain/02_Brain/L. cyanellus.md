# L. cyanellus

## Summary
L. cyanellus is the Green Sunfish, a small sunfish species. At Havasu Landing pond, some Green Sunfish were present among the approximately 25 small sunfish per square foot that apparently controlled the expansion of the introduced shrimp population through predation.
