# Genetic Bottleneck

## Summary
A genetic bottleneck in fish populations occurs when a sudden reduction in population size dramatically decreases genetic diversity, resulting in a smaller pool of alleles available for future generations. This phenomenon is particularly critical in conservation biology for endangered fish species, as it can lead to reduced fitness, increased inbreeding depression, and diminished adaptive potential to environmental changes. Understanding genetic bottlenecks is essential for developing effective management strategies to maintain viable populations and preserve the evolutionary potential of threatened aquatic species.
