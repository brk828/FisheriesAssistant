# Western Yellow-billed Cuckoo

## Summary
The Western Yellow-billed Cuckoo is one of six bird species targeted for conservation in the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program. This species is associated with riparian habitats along the Lower Colorado River, which historically supported large populations but has been severely reduced due to loss of cottonwood-willow habitat over the past century. The conservation strategy proposes adding 12,000-16,000 acres of riparian vegetation to support this and other riparian bird species, though the peer review panel noted this represents a relatively small quantity compared to the hundreds of thousands of acres removed, suggesting recovery is likely to be modest.
