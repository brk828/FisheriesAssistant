# Shasta River

## Summary
The Shasta River is a tributary of the Klamath River in California where steelhead migrate from the ocean to spawn. This 1932-1933 study documented 8,525 steelhead entering the Shasta River between September and December, finding that water temperature did not determine why fish selected this particular tributary over other available options, and that steelhead began spawning in the Shasta River earlier than in other Klamath River tributaries.
