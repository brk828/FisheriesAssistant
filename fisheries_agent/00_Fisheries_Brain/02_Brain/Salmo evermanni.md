# Salmo evermanni

## Summary
Salmo evermanni is a fish species described by Jordan and Grinnell in 1908 from specimens collected in the upper Santa Ana River in the San Bernardino Mountains at 7,500 feet elevation. This source demonstrates that it is actually a junior synonym of Salmo clarkii henshawi (Lahontan cutthroat trout), as the specimens were derived from Lake Tahoe cutthroat trout planted in the Santa Ana River in 1895-1896, eleven years before collection. The population is now extinct, likely replaced by subsequently stocked rainbow and steelhead trout.
