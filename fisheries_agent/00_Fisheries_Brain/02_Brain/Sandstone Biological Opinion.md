# Sandstone Biological Opinion

## Summary
The Sandstone Biological Opinion is a Section 7 consultation under the Endangered Species Act for a water development project in the Upper Colorado River Basin. As of April 1990, it was progressing with expected conservation measures similar to the Muddy Creek Opinion, and a final draft was anticipated in May-June 1990.
