# Striped Shiner

## Summary
Striped Shiner (Notropis chrysocephalus) is a freshwater fish used as live prey in the laboratory foraging experiments with striped bass. In trials where striped shiners were offered at a prey-to-predator ratio of 0.559 (56% of predator length), striped bass could not consume any prey despite making attacks, demonstrating the upper size limit for striped bass prey consumption.
