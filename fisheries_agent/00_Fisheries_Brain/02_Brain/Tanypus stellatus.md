# Tanypus stellatus

## Summary
Tanypus stellatus is a small omnivorous midge fly larva (6-8 mm length, 0.131 mg dry weight) that feeds on decaying algae and does not build cases. This study found it was most abundant in fall and winter, had a life cycle of approximately 60 days in spring/summer (60-80 days for overwintering populations), and was the most resistant to effluent toxicity, maintaining residual populations during toxic surges that eliminated other species.
