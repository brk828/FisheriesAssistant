# A. B. Wheatland

## Summary
A. B. Wheatland was a researcher at the Water Pollution Research Laboratory and lead author of this 1959 study on denitrification in rivers and estuaries. The research investigated how bacterial processes reduce nitrate in polluted waters, particularly in the Thames Estuary.
