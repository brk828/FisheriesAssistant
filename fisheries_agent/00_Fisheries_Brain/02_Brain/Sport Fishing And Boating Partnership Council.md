# Sport Fishing And Boating Partnership Council

## Summary
The Sport Fishing and Boating Partnership Council is a collaborative advisory body established to coordinate between the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and recreational fishing and boating stakeholders, with the primary objective of developing comprehensive policies and programs that support sustainable recreational fisheries across the United States. Formed in response to Congressional direction in the mid-1990s, the Council facilitates stakeholder engagement and provides recommendations regarding the Service's role in managing fish hatcheries, maintaining fish populations, and promoting recreational fishing opportunities while balancing conservation objectives. Rather than conducting original ichthyological research, the Council functions as a policy-coordinating entity that bridges resource management agencies, recreational users, and conservation interests to ensure recreational fisheries are sustainable and accessible.
