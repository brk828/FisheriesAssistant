# White River Valley System

## Summary
The White River Valley System is a desert spring system in Nevada that may connect with the Pluvial White River System. It is one of the areas where La Rivers provided water chemistry information in 1962, though comprehensive limnological data remains incomplete, and it is included as a survey location in this research proposal.

## Context

> This research proposal by James Everett Deacon (Associate Professor, Nevada Southern University) seeks funding to conduct comprehensive limnological surveys of spring systems in Nevada, particularly the [[Pluvial White River System]], [[Moapa Valley]], and [[Ash Meadows]].
