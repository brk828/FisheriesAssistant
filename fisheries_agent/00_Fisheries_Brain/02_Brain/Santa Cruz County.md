# Santa Cruz County

## Summary
Santa Cruz County is a county in southern Arizona that is part of the native range of the Gila Topminnow. According to this source, Monkey Springs in Santa Cruz County was the original collection site for the genetic stock used in the topminnow reintroduction program, with fish from this location being maintained at Boyce Thompson Arboretum and later used for stocking efforts across four National Forests.
