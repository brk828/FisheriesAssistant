# Chemical Eradication

## Summary
Chemical eradication using rotenone was attempted at Lake Davis in October 1997 with 64,000 pounds powdered rotenone and 16,000 gallons Nusyn-noxfish targeting 50 µg/L concentration across 50,000 acre-feet. The treatment initially appeared successful with approximately 20 tons of dead fish removed, but northern pike were rediscovered 17 months later in May 1999, and formulated rotenone was subsequently removed as a control option due to concerns about Proposition 65 chemicals.
