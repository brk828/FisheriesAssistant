# Ruby Canyon

## Summary
Ruby Canyon is a section of the Colorado River in the Upper Colorado River Basin that was identified as important habitat for endangered fish species. This source documents that Ruby Canyon, particularly the Black Rocks area, is the only location within the study area where both Colorado squawfish and humpback chub were found living together (sympatric populations), and that humpback chub populations are distributed throughout Ruby Canyon rather than concentrated in just one area.
