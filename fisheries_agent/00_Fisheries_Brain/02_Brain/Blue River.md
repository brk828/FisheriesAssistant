# Blue River

## Summary
The Blue River is a tributary of the Gila River located in Greenlee County, Arizona, that serves as critical habitat for endangered endemic fish species such as the loach minnow. This ~78 km river system and its watershed have become a focal point for conservation efforts due to threats from cumulative hydrologic alterations, including multiple small reservoir developments on its tributaries like Pigeon Creek, which affect native fish populations and riparian ecosystems. Management of the Blue River requires careful coordination across the entire watershed to balance water development with the preservation of aquatic biodiversity in this arid-region river system.
