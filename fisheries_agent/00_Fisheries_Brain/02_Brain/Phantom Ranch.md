# Phantom Ranch

## Summary
Phantom Ranch is a location in Grand Canyon approximately 103 miles downstream from Glen Canyon Dam. Historical pre-dam temperature data from this site show that water temperatures ranged from 2°C in winter to 23°C in summer, with suitable spawning temperatures (16-22°C) occurring from late May through early July. This indicates that native fishes historically spawned 1-2 months later than the current March-May spawning period observed in the Little Colorado River tributary.
