# Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program

## Summary
The Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program is a comprehensive conservation initiative designed to restore and maintain populations of native fish species that have been depleted or threatened by dam construction, water diversions, and habitat alterations in the Colorado River system. The program coordinates research, monitoring, and management strategies across multiple stakeholders to recover endemic species such as bonytail chub and other endangered fishes through habitat restoration, captive breeding, reintroduction efforts, and adaptive management practices. This multi-agency effort represents a major commitment to aquatic ecosystem recovery in one of North America's most heavily modified river systems.
