# Paraleptophlebia

## Summary
Paraleptophlebia is a genus of mayflies in the family Leptophlebiidae found in Michigan trout streams. This source describes four species in the genus (P. adoptiva, P. mollis, P. praepedita, and P. debilis) that emerge at different times from spring through fall, and notes that all species are valuable as fish food, with P. adoptiva being particularly important to fly-fishermen due to its early-season emergence when few other stream insects are active.
