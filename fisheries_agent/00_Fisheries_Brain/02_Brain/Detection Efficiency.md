# Detection Efficiency

## Summary
Detection efficiency measures the probability that tagged fish are successfully recorded when passing acoustic receivers. This source achieved high detection efficiency across receiver gates (0.78-0.99), with higher efficiency in Neskahi Bay (0.93 ± 0.06) than Great Bend (0.82 ± 0.05), and notes that detection probability at the waterfall PIT antenna was previously estimated at 0.6-0.9.
