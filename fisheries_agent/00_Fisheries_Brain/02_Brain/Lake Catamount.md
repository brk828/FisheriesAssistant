# Lake Catamount

## Summary
Lake Catamount is an off-channel reservoir in the Yampa River basin that serves as a major propagule source for invasive Northern Pike. Over 5,500 Northern Pike were removed from Lake Catamount during 2008-2010 alone, twice the number removed from the three main Yampa River study reaches during the same period, and approximately 6% of multiply-captured Northern Pike in the river had escaped from off-channel translocation sites including this reservoir.
