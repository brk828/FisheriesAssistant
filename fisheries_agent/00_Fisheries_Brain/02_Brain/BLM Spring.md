# BLM Spring

## Summary
BLM Spring is one of three current refugia for the endangered Owens Pupfish, located in the southeast portion of Fish Slough. In October 1981, a dam was rebuilt there and two shallow pools were created on either side of the stream channel upstream of the dam to provide pupfish habitat.

## Context

> This recovery plan addresses the [[Owens Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon radiosus*), an endangered species endemic to [[Owens Valley]], California.
