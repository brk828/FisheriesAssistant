# Cyprinodon Elegans

## Summary
Cyprinodon elegans is a cyprinodontid fish species belonging to the genus Cyprinodon, a group of small, euryhaline killifishes that inhabit isolated aquatic systems in arid regions of North America and Mexico. This species appears in taxonomic and ecological literature examining the diversity and conservation status of Pecos River ichthyofauna, where related Cyprinodon species like C. pecosensis have undergone formal taxonomic revision and represent important indicators of localized aquatic endemism. The genus Cyprinodon as a whole is significant in conservation biology due to the vulnerability of many species to habitat loss, water management alterations, and environmental perturbations in their restricted ranges.
