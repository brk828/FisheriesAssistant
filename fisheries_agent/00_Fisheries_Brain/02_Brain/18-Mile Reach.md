# 18-Mile Reach

## Summary
The 18-Mile Reach is a section of the Colorado River in the Upper Colorado River Basin recognized as a critical spawning and nursery area for endangered fish species including Colorado Squawfish. Aerial photography analysis from 1937-1986 indicates this reach has experienced reduction in overall width and channel complexity due to flow regime alterations from dam operations. The reach is a focus area for studying how flow manipulations from the Aspinall Unit affect habitat availability and endangered fish recovery.
