# Phenacobius Mirabilis

## Summary
Phenacobius mirabilis, commonly known as the suckermouth minnow, is a small cyprinid fish native to midwestern North American stream systems that exhibits specialized habitat preferences for small to medium-sized streams with sandy or silty substrates and minimal water flow. This species represents a rare-specialist classification in ichthyofaunal assemblages, occupying a restricted environmental niche with limited geographic distribution compared to generalist stream fishes, making it a potentially important indicator species for assessing stream habitat quality and conservation status in affected drainage systems.
