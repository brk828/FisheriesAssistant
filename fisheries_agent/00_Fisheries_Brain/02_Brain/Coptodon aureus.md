# Coptodon aureus

## Summary
Coptodon aureus (formerly Tilapia aurea), commonly known as Blue Tilapia, was one of five tilapia species requested for import by UEC and was not confirmed present in California at the time. The Department considered it a greater threat than some other tilapia species because of its cold tolerance, which would increase its survival and spread potential if it escaped into California waters.
