# Conklin Creek

## Summary
Conklin Creek is a stream in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that required hiking to access during the 1988 survey. The Apache Trout population there showed a male-biased sex ratio of 4.2:1 (21 males to 5 females), with 26 total specimens collected for genetic analysis.
