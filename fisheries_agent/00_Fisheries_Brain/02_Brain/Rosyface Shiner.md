# Rosyface Shiner

## Summary
The Rosyface Shiner (*Notropis rubellus*) is a small cyprinid minnow found in North American freshwater systems, particularly in the Arkansas River Basin and headwater regions, where it co-occurs with other shiners and benthic fishes in cool to moderate stream habitats. As a forage fish species, it plays an important ecological role in aquatic food webs, serving as prey for larger predatory fish including pike and trout, making it relevant to both natural ecosystem dynamics and the diet composition studies of game fish. The species is typically documented as part of multi-species assemblage surveys examining stream fish communities and their responses to environmental conditions and biotic interactions.
