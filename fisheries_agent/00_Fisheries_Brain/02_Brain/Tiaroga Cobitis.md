# Tiaroga Cobitis

## Summary
Tiaroga cobitis, commonly known as the loach minnow, is a small cyprinid fish native to the southwestern United States that was recognized as a distinct genus separate from Rhinichthys based on morphological and systematic reassessment. This species is of significant conservation concern and has been the subject of multiple endangered species recovery planning efforts, reflecting its imperiled status in desert aquatic ecosystems of the American Southwest. The taxonomic reinstatement of Tiaroga as a genus distinct from other minnow groups represents an important refinement in the systematics of North American desert fishes and has implications for its conservation management and recovery strategies.
