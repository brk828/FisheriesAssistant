# Department of Interior

## Summary
The Department of Interior (DOI) is the U.S. government agency that signed a Letter of Intent with SEMARNAP on May 5, 1997, focusing on binational cooperation and data collection for managing protected natural areas in the border region. The Delta-Upper Gulf Biosphere Reserve was identified as a pilot project area under this agreement.
