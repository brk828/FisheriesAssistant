# Jordan And Evermann

## Summary
Jordan and Evermann refers to the landmark ichthyological works produced by David Starr Jordan and Barton Warren Evermann in the late 19th and early 20th centuries (particularly 1896 and 1896–1900), which provided the first comprehensive scientific treatment and identification guide for North American freshwater fishes. Their foundational publications established critical taxonomic designations and type-species assignments for numerous fish genera, serving as the authoritative reference for fish identification and nomenclature in the United States until subsequent regional identification manuals were developed in the mid-20th century. The validity and influence of their taxonomic work remained subjects of formal scientific discussion and nomenclatural review well into the modern era of ichthyology.
