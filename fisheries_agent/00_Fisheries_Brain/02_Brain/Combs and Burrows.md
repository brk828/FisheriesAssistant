# Combs and Burrows

## Summary
Combs and Burrows are researchers who conducted a 1957 study on temperature effects on salmon egg survival. This source cites their work demonstrating that mortalities increase in chinook and sockeye eggs at constant 60°F temperatures, which helped explain the poor 14.6% survival observed during the unusually warm 1965-66 season.
