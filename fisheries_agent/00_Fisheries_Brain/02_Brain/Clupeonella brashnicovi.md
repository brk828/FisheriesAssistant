# Clupeonella brashnicovi

## Summary
Clupeonella brashnicovi is the Caspian herring, a marine species studied during Caspian Sea expeditions that provided key data on mesh selectivity. Baranov's analysis established that for this species the optimal mesh bar to fish length relationship is a = 0.125L, with selectivity coefficients K₁ = 0.12 and K₂ = 5.8, and the relationship between greatest body perimeter and optimal mesh perimeter averaged 1.25.
