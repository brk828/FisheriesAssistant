# Siskiyou County

## Summary
Siskiyou County is a county in northern California where Pumpkinseed populations were found to be quite common in mining dredge ponds and quieter stretches of the upper Klamath River by the early 1950s. Multiple collections from 1951-1953 documented hundreds of specimens ranging from 2.6 inches to approximately 7 inches in various locations along the Klamath River system.
