# Little Colorado Canyon

## Summary
Little Colorado Canyon is the canyon system formed by the Little Colorado River in Arizona, where it flows to its confluence with the Colorado River. This 1991 study established three sampling reaches within this canyon system (Confluence, Powell, and Salt) to monitor endangered humpback chub populations, capturing 4,793 individual chub across these reaches during July-August field work.
