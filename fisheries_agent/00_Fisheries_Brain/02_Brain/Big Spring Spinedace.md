# Big Spring Spinedace

## Summary
The Big Spring Spinedace (Lepidomeda mollispinis pratensis) is a threatened subspecies of cyprinid fish endemic to Meadow Valley Wash in Lincoln County, Nevada. It is distinguished by a higher, more pointed dorsal fin, longer pelvic fins, and smaller, more oblique mouth compared to other spinedace subspecies, with bright silver coloration and specimens ranging from 48 to over 110 millimeters total length. The only extant population occupies a 5-mile section through Condor Canyon, making it extremely vulnerable to threats including nonnative species, habitat modification, and catastrophic events.
