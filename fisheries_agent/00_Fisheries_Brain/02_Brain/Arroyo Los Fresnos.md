# Arroyo Los Fresnos

## Summary
Arroyo Los Fresnos is a location in Sonora, Mexico where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva occurs at two sites: Los Fresnos cienega (which has an extensive sub-irrigated meadow) and Rancho Casa Blanca downstream (where scattered spots occur along an eroded stream). These are mid-elevation cienega habitats supporting the rare plant.

## Context

> This status report documents [[Lilaeopsis schaffneriana]] subspecies recurva, a rare semi-aquatic perennial plant endemic to cienega wetlands in southeastern Arizona and adjacent Sonora, Mexico.
