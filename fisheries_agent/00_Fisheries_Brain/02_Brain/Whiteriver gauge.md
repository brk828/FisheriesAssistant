# Whiteriver gauge

## Summary
Whiteriver gauge is a stream flow measurement station on the White River used to assess cumulative water withdrawal impacts on Loach Minnow habitat. Total existing diversions within the Loach Minnow range (28.4 cubic feet per second) represent 45% of the mean median July discharge measured at this gauge.
