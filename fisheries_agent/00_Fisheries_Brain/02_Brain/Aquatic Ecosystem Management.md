# Aquatic Ecosystem Management

## Summary
Aquatic Ecosystem Management refers to the systematic planning and implementation of strategies to maintain the health, biodiversity, and productivity of freshwater and marine environments, with particular emphasis on sustaining fish populations and their habitats. This management approach integrates scientific understanding of fish ecology and population dynamics with administrative and policy frameworks to balance conservation objectives with resource utilization and human needs. As evidenced in these governance and planning documents, aquatic ecosystem management serves as a foundational concept linking academic training in aquatic sciences to practical employment in natural resource agencies responsible for environmental decision-making and regulatory oversight.
