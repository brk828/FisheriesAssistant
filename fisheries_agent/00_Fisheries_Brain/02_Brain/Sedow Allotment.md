# Sedow Allotment

## Summary
The Sedow Allotment is a livestock grazing area on the Globe Ranger District where changes in livestock management through rest rotation grazing have improved riparian conditions since the early 1980s. This source identifies it as containing proposed Gila Topminnow reintroduction sites in both Sevenmile Wash and Hess Canyon, with the latter being a marginal location in the JU Pasture that will be divided into four smaller pastures.
