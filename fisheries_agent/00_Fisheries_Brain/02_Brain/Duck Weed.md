# Duck Weed

## Summary
Duck Weed (*Lemna* sp.) is an aquatic plant that forms dense surface mats on water bodies. At Mesquite Spring, complete coverage by Duck Weed was a major factor in habitat degradation and pupfish mortality, as the dense mat prevented atmospheric oxygen exchange with the water and contributed to critically low dissolved oxygen levels (0.60-1.0 mg/l) that proved lethal to stocked Desert Pupfish despite the species' known tolerance for hypoxic conditions.
