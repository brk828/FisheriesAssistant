# Scotia Canyon

## Summary
Scotia Canyon is a high-elevation site (5,700-6,000 feet) in the Huachuca Mountains of Arizona where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva occurs in two sections separated by a dry reach. The upper section has dense populations while the lower section has medium-to-low density; permanent monitoring transects established in 1989 showed the upper section covered 43.6% of available habitat with 75.3% at high density.
