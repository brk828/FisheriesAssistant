# Rio Grande de Santiago

## Summary
The Rio Grande de Santiago is part of the Rio Lerma-Rio Grande de Santiago basin system in central Mexico. This source identifies this basin as a center of Goodeidae (splitfin) diversity with numerous endemic species, and notes that the introduced black bullhead has been established in the Lerma-Santiago system.
