# Upland Streams

## Summary
Upland streams are elevated watercourses typically characterized by rocky substrates and flowing water above the fall line. This source indicates all M. coosae species group members occupy upland stream habitats with rocky pools in the Piedmont region, with physiographic restriction to these habitats limiting gene flow among populations, and notes that M. warriorensis is scarce in areas with flat, shallow streambeds lacking deep pools that serve as refugia during low water or drought.
