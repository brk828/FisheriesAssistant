# Bluespotted Grouper

## Summary
Bluespotted Grouper (Epinephelus argus) is a marine fish deliberately introduced to Hawaii. It became established but is rated as a mistake because it causes ciguatera poisoning, representing one of four problematic species among the 13 marine fish that became established in Hawaiian waters through deliberate and unplanned introductions.
