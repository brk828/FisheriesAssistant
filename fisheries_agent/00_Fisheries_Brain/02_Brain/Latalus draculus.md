# Latalus draculus

## Summary
Latalus draculus is a new species of deltocephaline leafhopper measuring 2.8 mm (male) to 3.4-3.6 mm (female), related to L. uncinatus but distinguished by its broad pygofer and wide lateral membranes. The holotype and paratypes were collected from Oak Creek Canyon in Yavapai County, Arizona.
