# Cultus Lake

## Summary
Cultus Lake is a freshwater lake system that has served as an important study site for ichthyological and ecological research, particularly regarding fish populations, aquatic insect communities, and their numerical relationships. The lake has been utilized in investigations examining fish migrations, benthic invertebrate fauna including stoneflies, and broader community dynamics within lacustrine ecosystems. Its role in fisheries science appears to center on understanding the interactions between fish populations and their aquatic food sources in a controlled or well-documented freshwater environment.
