# Peregrine Falcon

## Summary
The Peregrine Falcon appears in these fisheries and conservation documents as a key indicator species for ecosystem health and watershed integrity, particularly in the context of the Gila River Basin and Blue River watershed. As an apex predator sensitive to environmental degradation and contaminants like DDT, the falcon's presence or recovery signals that aquatic ecosystems and their food webs remain relatively intact and unimpaired. The species serves as a conservation priority that often correlates with broader efforts to protect native fish communities and maintain pristine hydrological conditions in southwestern river systems.
