# Sediment Deposition

## Summary
Sediment deposition refers to the accumulation of sediment particles in aquatic systems. This source describes a managed sediment deposition system that includes a dedicated sediment settling pond along a levee road, where sediment is allowed to settle out of the water before being removed and returned to the river. This design feature is incorporated into the nursery habitat system to maintain water quality and prevent excessive sediment accumulation in the pond units.
