# Spring Creek NFH

## Summary
Spring Creek NFH (National Fish Hatchery) is a facility located in Washington State that was one of the top producing hatcheries in the National Fish Hatchery System. In Fiscal Year 1992, it distributed 15,140,710 fish and produced 8,796,637 eggs, making it the second-highest fish producer and a major contributor to Pacific Salmon and Steelhead restoration efforts in the Pacific Northwest.
