# Tennessee River

## Summary
The Tennessee River is a major freshwater drainage system in the southeastern United States that has served as an important study area for ichthyological research and fish conservation efforts. As a significant tributary of the Ohio River, it supports diverse cyprinid and other fish communities that have been subjects of systematic taxonomic and ecological investigations. The river's aquatic ecosystem, including its mussel fauna like Corbicula species, represents a critical region for understanding fish biodiversity, range distributions, and freshwater conservation in eastern North America.
