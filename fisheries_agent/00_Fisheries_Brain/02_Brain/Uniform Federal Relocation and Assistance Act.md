# Uniform Federal Relocation and Assistance Act

## Summary
The Uniform Federal Relocation and Assistance Act is federal legislation that would govern the relocation process for residents in the Mangas Creek Reservoir area if any of the alternatives requiring that reservoir were implemented. The document identifies this as the legal framework that would apply to displaced residents, though specific numbers of affected residents are not provided.
