# Eastern New Mexico University

## Summary
Eastern New Mexico University (ENMU) is one of the institutional collections that provided museum specimens of Gila nigrescens examined in this taxonomic study. The study analyzed 202+ specimens from multiple institutions to conduct comprehensive morphometric, meristic, and reproductive assessments of the Chihuahua Chub.
