# Colorado Water Congress

## Summary
The Colorado Water Congress is one of the participating organizations in the Upper Colorado River Recovery Program. This organization was part of the cooperative agreement created in 1988 between federal agencies, state governors, and various stakeholder groups to recover endangered fish species while allowing water development to continue in the Upper Colorado River Basin.
