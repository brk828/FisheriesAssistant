# Iron Gate Hatchery

## Summary
Iron Gate Hatchery was one of California's "mitigation" hatcheries operated by the Department of Fish and Game but financed by water development agencies rather than state funds. It was among the facilities contributing to the state's fish production system during the 1966-67 fiscal year.
