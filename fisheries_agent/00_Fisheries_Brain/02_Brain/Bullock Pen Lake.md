# Bullock Pen Lake

## Summary
Bullock Pen Lake is a 178-acre state-owned impoundment in north central Kentucky, approximately 30 miles south of the Cincinnati-Covington-Newport urban area. This study found that during opening week in 1955, the lake yielded 535 largemouth bass with a tag return rate of 27.3%, and it was the only study lake with a viable bluegill fishery (332 caught) as well as an unauthorized population of black bullhead that had been illegally introduced.
