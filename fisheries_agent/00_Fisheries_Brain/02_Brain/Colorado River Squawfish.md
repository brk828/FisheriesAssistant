# Colorado River Squawfish

## Summary
The Colorado River Squawfish (*Ptychocheips lucius*) is an endangered native cyprinid species endemic to the Colorado River Basin that has become a focal point for conservation efforts and captive breeding programs to prevent extinction. As a large predatory fish historically adapted to warm-water, free-flowing riverine conditions, the species exhibits specific thermal preferences and habitat requirements that have made its recovery and management challenging in the context of regulated river systems and hatchery maintenance. Its inclusion in coordinated recovery efforts alongside other endangered Colorado River fishes reflects the species' ecological significance as a key component of the native aquatic ecosystem and its vulnerability to anthropogenic environmental modifications.
