# Bathygadus sulcatus

## Summary
Bathygadus sulcatus is a species of macrourid fish that is endemic or distinctive to the Philippine Subregion. This species is one of numerous macrourids found in the isolated Sulu Sea complex that maintains unusually warm bottom temperatures (approximately 10°C at depths greater than 550 meters).
