# Veterinary Specialist Group

## Summary
The Veterinary Specialist Group is one of five disciplinary groups within the IUCN Species Survival Commission that addresses cross-cutting conservation themes rather than specific taxonomic groups. According to this 1994-1996 member's guide, the group's mandate is to ensure that specialist veterinary advice is available for wild species populations, supporting conservation efforts that require veterinary expertise such as disease control and wildlife health management.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium.
