# Redlands Diversion Dam

## Summary
The Redlands Diversion Dam is a water infrastructure structure on the Colorado River system that exemplifies how dams and water diversions have fundamentally altered the natural flow regimes critical to native fish species recovery. The dam's operations disrupt the historical hydrological patterns—particularly the timing and magnitude of spring floods and summer low flows—that are essential for maintaining suitable habitat conditions, including backwater nursery areas for larval fish development. Understanding and managing the Redlands Diversion Dam's operational parameters is therefore central to recovery efforts for endangered Colorado River fish species, as restoring appropriate flow synchronization between connected river systems depends on modifying how such diversions are controlled.
