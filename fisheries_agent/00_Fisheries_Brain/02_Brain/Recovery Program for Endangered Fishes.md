# Recovery Program for Endangered Fishes

## Summary
The Recovery Program for Endangered Fishes was the overarching initiative that developed the Flooded Bottom Land Restoration Program to address recruitment failure in razorback suckers and potentially other endangered species like Colorado Squawfish and Bonytail. The program initiated systematic inventory of flood plain habitats throughout 870 river miles, scoring and ranking sites based on proximity to spawning areas, floodability, and land ownership, with priority sites in the Green River located downstream from the last known spawning area.
