# Chara australis

## Summary
Chara australis is an ecorticate (non-corticated) species of stonewort in the genus Chara. This study found it has a chromosome number of N=14, consistent with all ecorticate Chara species examined, which uniformly showed 14 chromosomes regardless of whether they were monoecious or dioecious.
