# Roseate Tern

## Summary
The Roseate Tern (Sterna dougallii) is a colonial seabird studied using multistate capture-recapture models to estimate dispersal and recruitment patterns across breeding sites in the Mid-Atlantic region (Falkner Island Connecticut, Chesapeake, and Carolinas). This source reports that Roseate Terns show site-specific philopatry rates, with Site A having the lowest natal philopatry (0.582) and breeding philopatry (0.885), while Sites B and C showed higher fidelity (>0.89 natal, >0.97 breeding); natal dispersal intensity was greater than breeding dispersal, and full recruitment to the breeding population occurred at age 5 years. The lower philopatry at Site A coincided with population decline, suggesting emigration in response to local conditions.
