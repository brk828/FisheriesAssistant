# Lower Neches Valley Authority

## Summary
Lower Neches Valley Authority was the water management agency that controlled Dam B Reservoir. The Authority denied a winter 1962-63 drawdown request needed for water hyacinth control due to water supply reserve concerns, but indicated willingness to reconsider drawdowns after McGee Bend Reservoir completion upstream.
