# Pachycheles setimanus

## Summary
Pachycheles setimanus is a porcelain crab species found in the Puerto Peñasco intertidal zone. This field guide includes it among the anomuran crabs documented for the Gulf of California region, with identification by Clinton Westervelt consulting with Dr. Janet Haig.
