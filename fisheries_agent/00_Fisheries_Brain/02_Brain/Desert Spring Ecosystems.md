# Desert Spring Ecosystems

## Summary
Desert spring ecosystems are isolated aquatic habitats found in arid regions, particularly the Southwestern United States, that support endemic fish species adapted to extreme environmental conditions such as limited water flow, high temperatures, and restricted geographic ranges. These spring-fed systems, including small pools and remnant streams, are critical refugia for specialized desert fish species that exist nowhere else in nature, making them of paramount importance for conservation efforts aimed at preventing extinction. The fragility and isolation of these ecosystems make them vulnerable to water extraction, habitat alteration, and introduced species, requiring targeted management and species reintroduction programs to maintain biodiversity and ecosystem integrity.
