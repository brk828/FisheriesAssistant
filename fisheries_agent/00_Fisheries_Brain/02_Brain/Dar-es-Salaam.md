# Dar-es-Salaam

## Summary
Dar-es-Salaam is a location in Tanganyika where two elvers of Anguilla nebulosa labiata were found in Mzinga Creek, 200 yards above high water mark. This finding contributed important evidence for the catadromous life history of this eel species.
