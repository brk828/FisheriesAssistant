# Anguilla

## Summary
Anguilla refers to a genus of catadromous eels found in freshwater and estuarine systems across tropical and subtropical regions, characterized by their amphidromous life history wherein adults migrate to the ocean to spawn while juveniles (elvers) return to freshwater habitats to mature. These eels are significant components of freshwater fish communities and have been documented in diverse geographic locations including African rivers and drainage systems, where they occupy important ecological niches as both predators and prey. Their presence in freshwater ecosystems serves as an indicator of connectivity between riverine and marine environments, making them important subjects for conservation and hydrological studies examining fish passage and habitat integrity.
