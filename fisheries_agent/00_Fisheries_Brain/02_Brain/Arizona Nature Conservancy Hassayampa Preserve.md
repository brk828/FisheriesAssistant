# Arizona Nature Conservancy Hassayampa Preserve

## Summary
The Arizona Nature Conservancy Hassayampa Preserve is a conservation site that includes Palm Lake. According to this source, 17 adult bonytail were salvaged from Palm Lake at this preserve in April 1992 and subsequently stocked at ASU Research Park in July 1992; these bonytail originated from earlier stockings at the preserve in 1986 and 1987.
