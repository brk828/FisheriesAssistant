# Eriocaulon carsonii

## Summary
Eriocaulon carsonii is a rare aquatic or semi-aquatic plant species found in Great Artesian Basin springs. The bibliography documents botanical research on this and other rare vegetation species associated with the isolated wetland oases created by mound springs in the Australian desert.
