# C. atrorus

## Summary
C. atrorus (Cyprinodon atrorus) is the blackfin pupfish, an endemic species of the Cuatro Ciénegas basin. This species naturally hybridizes with C. bifasciatus in contact zones, shows strong morphological resemblance to the Cyprinodon eximius complex of the Mexican Plateau, and inhabits marshes and isolated pools.
