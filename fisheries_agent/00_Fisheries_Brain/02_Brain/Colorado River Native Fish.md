# Colorado River Native Fish

## Summary
Colorado River Native Fish refers to the endemic and historically indigenous fish species of the Colorado River basin that have become imperiled due to habitat alteration, flow regulation, and invasion of non-native species, necessitating coordinated recovery efforts through hatchery propagation, habitat restoration, and reintroduction programs. Key species within this assemblage include the humpback chub and razorback sucker, which are the focus of interagency conservation initiatives involving backwater habitat creation, native fish rearing facilities, and long-term population monitoring to ensure survival and reproductive success in this heavily modified riverine ecosystem.
