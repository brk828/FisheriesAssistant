# Aqua Fria River

## Summary
The Aqua Fria River is one of the rivers in the Gila River Basin where spikedace were historically abundant throughout suitable habitats. The species has been eliminated from approximately 95% of its former range, including the Aqua Fria River, due to factors such as exotic fish introductions, damming, diversions, overgrazing, and groundwater pumping.
