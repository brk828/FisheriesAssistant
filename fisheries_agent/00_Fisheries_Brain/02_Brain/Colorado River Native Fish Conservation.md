# Colorado River Native Fish Conservation

## Summary
Colorado River Native Fish Conservation refers to the coordinated scientific and management efforts to protect, restore, and maintain populations of fish species indigenous to the Colorado River ecosystem, particularly in downstream reservoirs like Lake Mohave. This conservation approach integrates field sampling, population monitoring, and research protocols to track the status of native species while addressing threats from habitat alteration, non-native species competition, and water management practices. The concept emphasizes the application of rigorous ichthyological research methods to inform evidence-based conservation strategies that preserve the ecological integrity and biodiversity of this critical aquatic system.
