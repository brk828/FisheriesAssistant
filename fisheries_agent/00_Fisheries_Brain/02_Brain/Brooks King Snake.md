# Brooks King Snake

## Summary
The Brooks King Snake is a king snake variety cited in the petition as an example of captive-bred reptiles available in the commercial market at prices of $75-$100. It was used to demonstrate how captive breeding programs had made various king snake morphs accessible to hobbyists.
