# Chara aspera

## Summary
Chara aspera is a corticate Chara species found to be haploid with N=14 chromosomes. As a dioecious species with 14 chromosomes, it fits the strong correlation observed in corticate Chara where dioecious species are haploid (N=14) and monoecious species are diploid (N=28).
