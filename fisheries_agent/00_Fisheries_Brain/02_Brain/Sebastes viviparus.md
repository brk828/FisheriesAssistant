# Sebastes viviparus

## Summary
Sebastes viviparus is cited as a comparative example demonstrating that optic lobe development correlates with depth rather than eye size alone. Despite having large eyes, this deeper-dwelling species exhibits small optic lobes, contrasting with shallow pelagic species that may have small eyes but large optic lobes.
