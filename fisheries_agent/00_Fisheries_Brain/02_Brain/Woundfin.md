# Woundfin

## Summary
Woundfin (Plagopterus argentissimus) is a small, endemic cyprinid fish native to the Virgin River drainage in the southwestern United States that has become a species of significant conservation concern due to severe population declines and habitat loss. The species is threatened by water diversions, habitat degradation, competition from introduced fishes, and predation, making it the subject of ongoing recovery efforts including habitat restoration and management initiatives. As a endemic desert stream fish with specialized ecological requirements, the woundfin represents a critical indicator of Virgin River ecosystem health and has been central to debates over water management and native fish conservation in the American Southwest.
