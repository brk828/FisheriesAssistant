# Etheostoma Blennioides

## Summary
Etheostoma blennioides, commonly known as the greenside darter, is a small freshwater percid fish native to North American streams and rivers, particularly in the central and eastern United States. This species is frequently referenced in systematic ichthyological studies and museum specimen documentation as an important model organism for understanding darter taxonomy, distribution patterns, and conservation status across different geographic populations. The species' inclusion in comprehensive fish checklists and systematic revisions reflects its significance in regional freshwater ecology and its value for tracking biodiversity and specimen provenance in ichthyological collections.
