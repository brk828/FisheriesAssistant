# Plains Stoneroller

## Summary
The Plains Stoneroller (Campostoma anomalum plumbeum) is a subspecies of minnow found in Kansas. This source documents it as one of the numerous plains-adapted subspecies in the state's fish fauna, representing a geographic variant of the widespread stoneroller species.
