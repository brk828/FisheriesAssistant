# Balsas

## Summary
The Rio Balsas is a major Pacific slope river basin in Mexico. This source documents it as habitat for diverse fish fauna, notes the presence of unresolved Ictalurus species resembling I. dugesi in tributaries in Morelos, and indicates that Ameiurus melas has been introduced into this system.
