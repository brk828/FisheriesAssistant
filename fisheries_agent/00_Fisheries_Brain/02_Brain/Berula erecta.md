# Berula erecta

## Summary
Berula erecta is an emergent aquatic plant found in the River Ivel. About 7% of the Berula crop grows above water, which means photosynthetic oxygen from these emergent portions is lost directly to the atmosphere rather than contributing to dissolved oxygen in the stream.
