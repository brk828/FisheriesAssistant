# Arizona Cooperative Fisheries Research Unit

## Summary
The Arizona Cooperative Fisheries Research Unit (ACFRU) is a research facility in Tucson that conducted early experimentation with Tilapia hybridization in the region. This source documents that ACFRU began working with the "Malacca hybrid" (T. mossambica × T. hornorum cross) in 1962, using T. mossambica stock from Tishomingo National Fish Hatchery and T. hornorum from Malaysia.
