# South Yadkin River

## Summary
The South Yadkin River is a waterway in North Carolina that joins the Yadkin River system and supports catfish populations. This survey found the river above Cooleemee Dam unsuitable for flathead catfish stocking due to its small size (width <22.7 m), shallow depth (<1 m average), predominantly sandy bottom, and poor benthic diversity, though bullhead populations were present at moderate densities (CPUE 0.68 fish/minute).
