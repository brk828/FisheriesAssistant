# Ouray Endangered Fish Hatchery

## Summary
The Ouray Endangered Fish Hatchery is a propagation facility for endangered fish species in the Upper Colorado River Basin. The FY 1998 work plan allocated $296,000 for its operation and maintenance, plus $1.83 million in capital funding for facility expansion as part of the program's propagation and genetics management efforts.
