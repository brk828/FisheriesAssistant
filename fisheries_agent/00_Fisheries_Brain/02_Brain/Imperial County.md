# Imperial County

## Summary
Imperial County is a location in southern California where a Sacramento Hitch was collected in 1954 from Ramer Lake. This collection site was approximately 320 road miles south of the species' regular range, illustrating how native minnows had been widely transported as bait and occurred well outside their original distributions.
