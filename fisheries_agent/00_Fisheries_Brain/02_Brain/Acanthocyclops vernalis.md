# Acanthocyclops vernalis

## Summary
Acanthocyclops vernalis is a copepod that underwent mass reproduction in carp ponds after carp fry consumed large Daphnia populations. This mass reproduction occurred within 2-3 weeks of Daphnia consumption, along with nanoplankton increases.
