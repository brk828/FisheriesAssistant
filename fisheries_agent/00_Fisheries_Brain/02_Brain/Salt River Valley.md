# Salt River Valley

## Summary
The Salt River Valley in Arizona supported diverse habitats during the Hohokam Sedentary period, including riparian corridors, desert scrubland, and grasslands. Fish remains from La Lomita Pequeña indicate utilization of aquatic resources from the prehistoric canal system in this valley.
