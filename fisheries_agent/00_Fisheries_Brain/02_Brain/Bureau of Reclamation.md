# Bureau Of Reclamation

## Summary
The Bureau of Reclamation is the primary U.S. federal agency responsible for managing water resources and dam operations across the American West, with significant authority over aquatic ecosystems through control of reservoir releases, flow regimes, and water allocation. In the context of fish science and conservation, the Bureau plays a critical regulatory role in determining habitat conditions for native fish species—particularly imperiled endemic fishes in desert aquatic systems—through its management of dams, water diversions, and downstream flow patterns that directly affect water temperature, sediment dynamics, and spawning habitat. As a major stakeholder in endangered species recovery efforts, the Bureau coordinates with agencies like the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service on biological opinions, adaptive management protocols, and research initiatives designed to balance water resource demands with aquatic ecosystem conservation objectives.
