# Stowe Gulch

## Summary
Stowe Gulch is located 2.1 miles downstream from the Klondyke Tailings on Aravaipa Creek and marks the beginning of perennial flow and critical native fish habitat containing seven threatened or endangered native fish species. This source reports that water quality sampling at Stowe Gulch in March-April 1993 showed no contamination from the upstream tailings, with all surface water quality standards met and no toxic trace metals detected except boron. The location represents where bedrock forces subsurface water to the surface, creating the start of the perennial stream reach that flows into the Aravaipa Canyon Wilderness.
