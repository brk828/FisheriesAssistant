# Biological Species Concept

## Summary
The Biological Species Concept defines species based on reproductive isolation, where organisms that can interbreed and produce fertile offspring belong to the same species, while reproductive barriers separate different species. In fish science and aquatic ecology, this concept is particularly challenging to apply, especially in cases like deepwater lake fish that are morphologically similar with overlapping spawning seasons and limited opportunities for natural interbreeding, making it difficult to empirically determine reproductive compatibility. This limitation has led some researchers to consider alternative species concepts, such as phylogenetic approaches, when assessing species boundaries in aquatic organisms where reproductive isolation cannot be easily demonstrated or observed.
