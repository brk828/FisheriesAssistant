# Acroneuria

## Summary
Acroneuria is a genus of stoneflies that requires 2 or more years to complete its life cycle. This study identifies Acroneuria as an example of invertebrates that cannot survive in intermittent streams like Caldwell Hollow, where the annual drying period prevents animals with multi-year life cycles from successfully completing their development.
