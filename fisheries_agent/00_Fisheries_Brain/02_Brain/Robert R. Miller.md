# Robert R. Miller

## Summary
Robert R. Miller was a pioneering ichthyologist and conservation biologist who made significant contributions to the study of freshwater fish fauna, particularly in Mexico and the southwestern United States, with extensive work on species distribution, abundance, and taxonomy in systems like the Rio Papigochic and Rio Conchos drainages. His research at institutions including the Scripps Institution of Oceanography established foundational knowledge about rare and endemic fish species in the Sierra Madre Occidental region, work that remains central to understanding and preserving North American freshwater fish biodiversity. Miller's correspondence and field studies documented historical ichthyological collections and collection localities that continue to inform modern conservation efforts for threatened aquatic ecosystems.
