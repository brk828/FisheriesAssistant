# Picadilla Pasture

## Summary
Picadilla Pasture is one of three south pastures in the Dos S Unit that would be managed under a Santa Rita Grazing System, providing six months of use followed by twelve months of rest. The biological opinion requires that pothole construction and water system replacement at Mud Springs must be completed before implementing grazing management in this pasture.
