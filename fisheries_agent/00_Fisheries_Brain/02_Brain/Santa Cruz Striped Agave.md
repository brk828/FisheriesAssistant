# Santa Cruz Striped Agave

## Summary
Santa Cruz Striped Agave (Agave parviflora) is a rare plant species that occurs on Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge. As a member of the cactus family, it is protected by the Arizona Native Plant Law (revised 1981).
