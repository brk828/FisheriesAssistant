# Dinobryon

## Summary
Dinobryon is a chrysophyte (golden-brown algae) that dominates the phytoplankton community in Twin Lakes along with diatoms. This species is characteristic of phosphorus-limited oligotrophic waters and is adapted to low phosphorus conditions (<20 µg/L).
