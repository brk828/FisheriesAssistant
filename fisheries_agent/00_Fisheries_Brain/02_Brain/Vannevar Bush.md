# Vannevar Bush

## Summary
Vannevar Bush articulated an important principle about the relationship between basic and applied research that this source uses to support continued funding for evolutionary genetics. The source quotes his 1945 statement that 'Under the pressure for immediate results...applied research invariably drives out pure,' arguing that maintaining support for basic research in evolutionary genetics is essential even as applied challenges like genetically engineered organism release emerge.
