# Ring-necked Pheasant

## Summary
The Ring-necked Pheasant is a game bird species permitted for commercial captive propagation in Arizona under existing regulation R12-4-413. The petition cited this as part of the precedent for allowing commercial breeding of native reptiles, noting that the proposed reptile regulations would actually be more restrictive than the existing bird propagation rules.
