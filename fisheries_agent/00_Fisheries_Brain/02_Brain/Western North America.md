# Western North America

## Summary
Western North America refers to the arid and semi-arid regions of the western United States and northwestern Mexico, characterized by unique aquatic habitats including large rivers, marshlands, cienegas (wetland meadows), and springs. This source focuses on this region as the native range of several endangered fish species, particularly in the Colorado River Basin and surrounding areas in Arizona, New Mexico, and California, where extensive habitat modification through dam construction and water management since 1900 has threatened native fish populations.
