# Salmon National Forest

## Summary
Salmon National Forest in Idaho was the site of a 1964 study on DDT persistence following spruce budworm control application at 1 lb/acre in fuel oil. Forage plants showed high initial residues (wheatgrass 384.1 ppm, balsamroot 312.9 ppm) declining over 3 months, and deer rumen contents collected 1 month post-spray contained 27.0-185.3 ppm total DDT residues.
