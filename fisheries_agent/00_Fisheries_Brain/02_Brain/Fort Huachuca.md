# Fort Huachuca

## Summary
Fort Huachuca is a U.S. Army installation in Arizona whose military operations, particularly groundwater pumping and associated hydrological impacts, have been documented as major threats to the San Pedro River ecosystem and its dependent aquatic fauna, including five federally listed fish species such as the Gila Topminnow, Desert Pupfish, and Spikedace. The base became a focal point for conservation litigation in the 1990s when environmental organizations sued over alleged violations of the Endangered Species Act related to habitat destruction and aquifer overdraft affecting this ecologically significant riparian zone in the Southwest.
