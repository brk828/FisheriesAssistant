# Tres Alamos

## Summary
Tres Alamos was a site where Gila Topminnow had been introduced as part of recovery efforts. According to this 1989 document, Tres Alamos was among at least 14 introduced topminnow sites that were lost (populations extirpated) between 1987 and 1989.

## Context

> This 1989 correspondence from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Ecological Services office documents a proposal to reclassify the [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis*) from endangered to threatened status.
