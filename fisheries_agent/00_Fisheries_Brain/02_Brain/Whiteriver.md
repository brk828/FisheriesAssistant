# Whiteriver

## Summary
Whiteriver is a town on the White Mountain Apache Reservation in Arizona that is the intended recipient of water from the proposed diversion project. The project aims to supply 480,000,000 gallons per year to meet the town's projected water demand through 2022, with municipal and industrial demand expected to increase from 319,000,000 gallons per year at Phase I start to 480,000,000 gallons per year during Phase II.
