# Washington Fields Diversion

## Summary
The Washington Fields Diversion is a water extraction structure on the Virgin River in Washington County, Utah that marks a critical boundary in the river system's aquatic habitat, particularly for endangered species like the woundfin minnow. This diversion defines the downstream extent of historically occupied woundfin habitat and serves as a key reference point for delineating river reaches of conservation concern in fisheries management and habitat restoration planning. The diversion's location and operation are central to debates regarding flow management, habitat suitability modeling, and the effectiveness of recovery strategies for endemic desert fish species in the Virgin River Basin.
