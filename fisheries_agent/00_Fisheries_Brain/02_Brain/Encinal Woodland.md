# Encinal Woodland

## Summary
Encinal Woodland is a type of oak woodland community in Arizona. The lecture notes list it as one of the woodland types in Arizona's altitudinal zonation, characterized by open canopy structure in semi-arid to semi-humid, warm or cold conditions.
