# Lower Colorado River Basin

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Basin refers to the downstream portion of the Colorado River system, extending from the Grand Canyon to the Mexican border, which serves as the primary habitat for endemic "big-river" fish species including razorback suckers, bonytail chubs, and other native fishes dependent on mainstem channel flows and associated backwater habitats. This region has been the focus of intensive fish conservation and recovery efforts, including monitoring programs, captive breeding initiatives, and habitat management through facilities in impoundments like Lake Mohave. The basin represents a critical management zone for multi-species conservation programs addressing the ecological impacts of dams, flow regulation, and habitat fragmentation on native fish populations.
