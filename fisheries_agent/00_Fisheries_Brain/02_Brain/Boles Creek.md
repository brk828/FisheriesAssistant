# Boles Creek

## Summary
Boles Creek is an intermittent tributary stream of Willow Creek in Modoc County, California, that enters Clear Lake Reservoir. This source documents it as the location where Shortnose Sucker were first collected in California on May 15, 1955, with mature fish observed in apparent spawning migration in May 1960.

## Context

> This 1965 report documents the first confirmed occurrences of [[Lost River Sucker]] (*Deltistes luxatus*) and [[Shortnose Sucker]] (*Chasmistes brevirostris*) in California waters, adding both species to the state's freshwater fish fauna.
