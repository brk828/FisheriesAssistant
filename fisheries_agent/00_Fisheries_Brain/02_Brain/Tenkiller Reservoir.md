# Tenkiller Reservoir

## Summary
Tenkiller Reservoir is an Oklahoma impoundment notable in fisheries science as a dimictic lake that develops strong summer thermal stratification with severe dissolved oxygen depletion in its hypolimnion, conditions that significantly influenced downstream fishery management when hydroelectric discharge released anoxic water into the Illinois River tailwater below the dam. The reservoir's limnological characteristics, particularly the cold, oxygen-poor hypolimnetic outflows, became a focal point for mid-20th century research examining whether tailwater conditions could support coldwater fish species like rainbow trout through put-and-take fishery approaches and what impacts reservoir operations had on downstream aquatic communities.
