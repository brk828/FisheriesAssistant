# Salmincola gordoni

## Summary
Salmincola gordoni is a parasitic copepod typically found on salmonid hosts (brown trout, grayling, salmon) and can be distinguished from similar species by its distinctly triangular cephalothorax and absence of clear hindbody segmentation. This source reports an unusual host record of an egg-bearing female found on perch at Bretton Park Lake, and notes the species appears markedly euryhaline, occurring both in freshwater far from the sea and on sea trout in marine environments.
