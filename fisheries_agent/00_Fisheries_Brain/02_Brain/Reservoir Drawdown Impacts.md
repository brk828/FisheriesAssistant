# Reservoir Drawdown Impacts

## Summary
Roosevelt Lake was emptied in 1940, which resulted in the complete extirpation of the previously abundant Yellow Bass population. No Yellow Bass were observed in the reservoir from 1940 through at least 1953, demonstrating the severe impact of complete reservoir drawdown on established fish populations.
