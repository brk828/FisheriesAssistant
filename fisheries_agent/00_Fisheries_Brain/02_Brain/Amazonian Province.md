# Amazonian Province

## Summary
The Amazonian Province is a distinct biogeographic region that is separated from the Rio Grande do Sul-La Plata province by differences in Poeciliid fauna. The Poeciliids serve as effective biogeographic indicators distinguishing these two provinces, representing one of the clearest faunal boundaries among South American freshwater fishes.
