# Virgin Islands

## Summary
The Virgin Islands are a U.S. territory included in the geographic coverage of this Federal Register notice reviewing candidate species for endangered and threatened status. This notice explicitly states that its taxonomic review spans all U.S. states, territories, and possessions including the Virgin Islands, indicating that animal taxa native to this territory were considered for possible addition to the List of Endangered and Threatened Wildlife under the Endangered Species Act.
