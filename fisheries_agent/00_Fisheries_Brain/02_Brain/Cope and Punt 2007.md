# Cope and Punt 2007

## Summary
Cope and Punt 2007 developed prior work on Bayesian hierarchical growth models that this study extends. The current study adapted their approach to simultaneously compare growth parameters across multiple ageing protocols and sexes while incorporating between-reader variability as a random effect in the von Bertalanffy growth model framework.

## Context

> **Core Problem Addressed:** Accurate estimation of [[Gray Triggerfish]] growth parameters while accounting for ageing error from multiple readers and comparing three ageing protocols (whole otoliths, new dorsal spine protocol, old dorsal spine protocol).
