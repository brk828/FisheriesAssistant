# Little Colorado River Sucker

## Summary
The Little Colorado River Sucker is an undescribed species of catostomid (sucker family) endemic to tributaries of the Little Colorado River in Arizona that was under genetic and taxonomic study as of the early 1990s. This taxon was identified as a conservation priority and recommended for Category 2 candidate status under the Endangered Species Act due to its limited range and potential threats to its habitat in the Little Colorado River drainage system. As an endemic species with unclear taxonomic status, it represents the type of cryptic or poorly documented native fish fauna of the Colorado River system requiring both systematic clarification and management attention for long-term conservation.
