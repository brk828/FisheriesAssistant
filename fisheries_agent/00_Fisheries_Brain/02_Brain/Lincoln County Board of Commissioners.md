# Lincoln County Board of Commissioners

## Summary
The Lincoln County Board of Commissioners is identified as a key stakeholder that should be continuously involved in Big Spring Spinedace recovery efforts. The recovery plan emphasizes the need for ongoing communication with this local government body as part of the public information program to ensure support for conservation measures.
