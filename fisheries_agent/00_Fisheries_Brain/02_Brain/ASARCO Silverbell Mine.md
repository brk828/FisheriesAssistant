# ASARCO Silverbell Mine

## Summary
The ASARCO Silverbell Mine is a copper mining operation located northwest of Tucson, Arizona. This source documents that recurrent copper mine spills from this facility in August and September 1981 completely extirpated the Gila topminnow population in Cocio Wash on public lands, an event that precipitated the need for mitigation through reintroduction of topminnows and pupfish at alternative sites.

## Context

> This supplemental environmental assessment, prepared by the [[U.S. Bureau of Land Management]] (BLM) Phoenix District Office in December 1983, authorizes the introduction of [[Gila Topminnow]] (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis*) and [[Desert Pupfish]] (*Cyprinodon macularius macularius*)...
