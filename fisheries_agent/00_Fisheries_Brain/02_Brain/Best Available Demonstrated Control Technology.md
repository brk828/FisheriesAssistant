# Best Available Demonstrated Control Technology

## Summary
BADCT is a technology-based standard required under Arizona's aquifer protection permit program for demonstrating compliance with groundwater quality standards. This source indicates that mining operations in Arizona must demonstrate BADCT and achieve compliance with drinking water standards at designated points of compliance as part of the state's Environmental Quality Act requirements for protecting groundwater quality.
