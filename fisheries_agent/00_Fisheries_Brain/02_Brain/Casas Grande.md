# Casas Grande

## Summary
Casas Grande is a location in the former Rio Grande tributary system where Gila populations have distinctive characteristics including 9,9-9,8 fin ray counts, modal vertebrae count of 43, 68-80 lateral line scales (fine scales), and no basal radii on scales. These populations show morphological characters intermediate between Colorado River and Rio Grande faunas.
