# Cauca River

## Summary
The Cauca River is a tributary system within the Atlantic drainage of Colombia where gymnotid collections were made during this 1912-1913 survey. It was one of the major river systems sampled as part of documenting the distribution of Gymnotidae in trans-Andean Colombia and Ecuador.
