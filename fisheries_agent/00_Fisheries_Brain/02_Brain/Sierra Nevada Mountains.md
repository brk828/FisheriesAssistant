# Sierra Nevada Mountains

## Summary
The Sierra Nevada Mountains are a mountain range in California that is the native range of Rainbow Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss). According to this source, Rainbow Trout from the Sierra Nevada Mountains were introduced to the southwestern United States in the late 1800s, where they competed with and hybridized with native trout species.
