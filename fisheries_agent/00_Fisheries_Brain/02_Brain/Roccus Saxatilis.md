# Roccus Saxatilis

## Summary
Roccus saxatilis, commonly known as the striped bass, is a euryhaline teleost fish species capable of inhabiting both freshwater and estuarine environments, making it an important indicator species for studying thermal tolerance and habitat utilization across varying salinity and temperature gradients. This species demonstrates significant ecological relevance in fisheries management and conservation biology due to its commercial and recreational importance, as well as its sensitivity to water temperature fluctuations and habitat degradation in riverine and coastal systems. Research on R. saxatilis populations provides valuable data on fish growth rates, thermal preference thresholds, and population dynamics in response to environmental stressors such as water temperature changes and habitat modification.
