# Sonoran Mud Turtle

## Summary
The Sonoran Mud Turtle (Kinosternon sonoriense) is a small, semi-aquatic chelonian native to the southwestern United States and northwestern Mexico that inhabits shallow freshwater systems including springs, marshes, and desert pools. While not a fish species, it is relevant to aquatic ecology studies as a sympatric inhabitant of isolated desert spring ecosystems and as a potential indicator species for assessing the health and integrity of these specialized aquatic habitats. Its presence in surveys alongside endemic fish species like Poeciliopsis occidentalis reflects its ecological importance in understanding complete community dynamics of threatened desert spring ecosystems in regions like the Quitobaquito Springs.
