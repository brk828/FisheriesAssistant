# Coconino National Forest

## Summary
The Coconino National Forest represents a critical management jurisdiction encompassing key aquatic habitats within the Little Colorado River watershed in Arizona, including the East Clear Creek system and its tributaries that support endemic fish species of conservation concern. As a U.S. Forest Service-administered landscape, the Coconino National Forest has served as an important site for implementing conservation measures and monitoring programs for sensitive and federally protected fish species, including funding habitat restoration and population assessment initiatives. The forest's riparian and stream ecosystems are significant for maintaining biodiversity in the southwestern United States and have been focal points for research on stream temperature dynamics, species distribution, and conservation status evaluations.
