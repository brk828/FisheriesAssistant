# Instituto de Investigaciones Oceanologicas

## Summary
The Instituto de Investigaciones Oceanologicas is a research institution at UABC whose researcher Salvador Galindo-Bect presented findings demonstrating correlations between Colorado River flows and commercial fishing success in the upper gulf. His research focused on breeding areas within the Biosphere Reserve and the formation of salinity barriers that historically protected breeding zones from predators.
