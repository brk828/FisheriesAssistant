# Owens Pupfish

## Summary
The Owens Pupfish (Cyprinodon radiosus) is an endangered endemic species native to the Owens Valley in California, representing a critical case study in desert aquatic conservation biology. This small cyprinodontid fish historically inhabited isolated spring and stream systems in an arid region, making it highly vulnerable to habitat loss from water extraction and environmental degradation. The species exemplifies the conservation challenges faced by desert pupfishes, which require specialized protection strategies and habitat restoration efforts to prevent extinction, and has been a focal point for discussions among conservation biologists and fisheries managers regarding the preservation of California's unique desert ichthyofauna.
