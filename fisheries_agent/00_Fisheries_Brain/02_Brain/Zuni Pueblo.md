# Zuni Pueblo

## Summary
Zuni Pueblo is a Native American tribe with ethnohistorical connections to the Grand Canyon corridor. This source documents that the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies Phase II program included a Zuni Pueblo ethnohistorical evaluation conducted by Anyon et al. (study series U) as part of the comprehensive tribal consultation and cultural resources studies examining impacts of dam operations on culturally significant areas.
