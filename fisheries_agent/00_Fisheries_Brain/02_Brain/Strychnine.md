# Strychnine

## Summary
Strychnine is a rodenticide for which toxicity data was included in this report. Though not directly applied by fishery biologists, it was evaluated for potential effects on fish populations when used by other agencies.

## Context

> This 1961 special report by Charles L. Sowards of the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife addresses safety protocols for fishery biologists using chemical compounds and electrical equipment in field operations.
