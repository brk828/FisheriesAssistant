# Lake Clementine

## Summary
Lake Clementine is a reservoir in Placer County, California. It served as an experimental transplant site for native freshwater smelt from the lower Sacramento and San Joaquin Rivers, and freshwater shrimp from the delta area were introduced there in 1954 as part of forage fish trials.

## Context

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
