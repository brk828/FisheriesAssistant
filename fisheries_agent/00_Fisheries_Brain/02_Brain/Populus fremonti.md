# Populus fremonti

## Summary
Populus fremonti is a cottonwood species that is a dominant component of Deciduous Riparian Woodlands in the southwestern United States. According to this study of the Gila River Basin in Arizona, it characterizes intermittent and perennial streams at lower elevations along with willows and sycamore.
