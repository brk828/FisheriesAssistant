# Executive Review Committee

## Summary
The Executive Review Committee (ERC) is an administrative body that oversees the Glen Canyon Environmental Studies program. According to this November 1989 briefing document, the ERC held a meeting on October 24, 1989 to discuss ongoing environmental studies related to Glen Canyon Dam operations, including review of a low flow test and plans for an upcoming Environmental Impact Statement scoping process.
