# Solea solea

## Summary
Solea solea (sole) was successfully reared in multiple studies documented in the bibliography. Fluchter (1965) achieved 80% metamorphosis using Artemia nauplii, and Fabre-Domergue and Bietrix (1905) reared sole to 55-76 mm over 8 months using Dunaliella and larval fishes as prey.
