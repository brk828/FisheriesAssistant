# Upper Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program

## Summary
The Upper Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program is a collaborative federal initiative established to protect and restore populations of native fish species threatened with extinction in the Upper Colorado River Basin, including species such as the Colorado pikeminnow, humpback chub, razorback sucker, and bonytail chub. The program integrates scientific research, habitat management, and water operations to address threats posed by dam construction, flow alterations, and non-native species introduction while balancing competing water use demands across multiple states. This comprehensive approach combines field recovery efforts, captive breeding programs, and population monitoring to achieve species recovery goals through coordinated management across state and federal jurisdictions.
