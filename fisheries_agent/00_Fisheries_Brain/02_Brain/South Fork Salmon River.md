# South Fork Salmon River

## Summary
The South Fork Salmon River is a tributary within Idaho's Salmon River drainage system that was used as a validation site for harvest estimation methods. This study found that permit-based estimates for this river (242 steelhead in 1966, 75 in 1965, 726 in 1964) consistently matched check station counts, while questionnaire estimates were 3-4 times higher, demonstrating the accuracy of the permit method.
