# Redbelly Tilapia

## Summary
Redbelly Tilapia is a cichlid species that appears in aquatic monitoring studies of managed research park ecosystems, likely representing either an introduced or naturalized population within artificial pond systems. As a non-native fish species, it exemplifies the broader conservation concern of exotic species introduction through aquaculture escapes, unauthorized stocking, or canal systems, which can alter native fish community composition and ecosystem dynamics in freshwater environments. Its presence in these survey datasets reflects the importance of monitoring non-native species distributions in controlled research settings to understand invasion dynamics and impacts on native fish assemblages.
