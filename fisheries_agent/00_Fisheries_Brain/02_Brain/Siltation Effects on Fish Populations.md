# Siltation Effects on Fish Populations

## Summary
Heavy silting following a flood around 1934 caused mass mortality of Yellow Bass in Roosevelt Lake. This siltation event, combined with subsequent reservoir emptying in 1940, contributed to the complete extirpation of Yellow Bass from Roosevelt Lake by 1940, with no observations through 1953.
