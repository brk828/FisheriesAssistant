# Robin

## Summary
Robin was one of the songbird species monitored for DDT residues following forest application at Knox Mountain, California in 1964. Unlike other species, Robin residues decreased below pre-treatment levels by 3 months post-application, showing a different pattern of pesticide accumulation or metabolism.
