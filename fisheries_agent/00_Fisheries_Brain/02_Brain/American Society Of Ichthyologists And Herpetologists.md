# American Society Of Ichthyologists And Herpetologists

## Summary
The American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists (ASIH) is a professional scientific organization dedicated to the study and conservation of fish and amphibian species, operating through specialized committees such as the Environmental Quality Committee that provide expert commentary on habitat conservation plans and environmental management issues. As evidenced by their engagement with regional conservation initiatives like the Virgin River Habitat Conservation Plan and collaborative efforts in desert fish research, ASIH serves as a authoritative voice in ichthyology and fisheries science, promoting interdisciplinary dialogue between academic researchers and resource management agencies. The society facilitates the advancement of fish conservation biology through its regional chapters, annual meetings, and technical publications that address critical issues in aquatic ecology and species preservation.
