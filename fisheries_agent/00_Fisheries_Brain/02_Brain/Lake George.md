# Lake George

## Summary
Lake George is a New York State lake that produces the largest landlocked salmon (Atlantic salmon, Salmo salar) of any lake in New York. By 1953, it showed particularly strong population growth with an upturn in abundance especially pronounced during fall spawning operations, contributing significantly to the record catch of 309 landlocked salmon that year.
