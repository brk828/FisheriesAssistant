# Predator-Prey Models

## Summary
Predator-prey models describe the interactions and dynamics between predators and their prey populations. This study develops a size-structured predator-prey model using a parabolic consumption function c(x,y) = 4(x/y)(1 – x/y) that integrates body depth distributions of June sucker prey with gape size distributions of white bass predators to calculate relative vulnerability over time, revealing that June suckers experience 19 days with vulnerability >0.5 under mean growth scenarios.
