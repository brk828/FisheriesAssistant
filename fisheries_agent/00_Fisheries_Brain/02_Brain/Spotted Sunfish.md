# Spotted Sunfish

## Summary
The Spotted Sunfish refers to a small freshwater sunfish species in the family Centrarchidae, characterized by distinctive spot patterns on its body and fins. As a member of the sunfish genus, it occupies ecological niches in warm-water habitats and is relevant to fish surveys and distribution studies that track the presence and expansion of species ranges, particularly in regions like Illinois where faunal documentation efforts have established baseline records for comparative conservation assessments. These diminutive centrarchids are important indicators of aquatic ecosystem health and are frequently documented in ichthyological surveys examining biodiversity and distributional changes in freshwater systems.
