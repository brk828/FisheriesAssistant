# Desert Fishes

## Summary
Desert fishes are native fish species endemic to arid and semi-arid aquatic habitats, primarily in the southwestern United States and northern Mexico, that have evolved specialized adaptations to thrive in isolated water bodies such as springs, rivers, and lakes with extreme environmental conditions. These species represent a disproportionately high proportion of aquatic biodiversity and conservation concern in arid regions, facing severe threats from habitat destruction due to water development, groundwater extraction, and competition or predation from introduced non-native species. The term encompasses a diverse taxonomic group that requires coordinated, landscape-level management approaches through recovery teams and councils dedicated to preserving these ecologically and evolutionarily significant fauna.
