# Eleginus navaga

## Summary
Eleginus navaga (Navaga) is a mobile coastal Gadinae species characterized by an elevated cerebellum that does not rest on the medulla oblongata and often projects dorsally above the optic lobes. This species shares morphological features with other mobile coastal forms including widely spaced dorsal and anal fins, shortened posterior first anal fin, interrupted lateral line, and pit organs on the head.
