# Sandstone Project

## Summary
The Sandstone Project was a water development project undergoing Section 7 consultation under the Endangered Species Act, with its Biological Opinion delayed as of January 1989 over sediment impact issues. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service committed to consulting with the Management Group and Implementation Committee before making unilateral decisions about sediment handling for this project.
