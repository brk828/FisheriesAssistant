# Painted Rock Reservoir

## Summary
Painted Rock Reservoir is one of the low-elevation water bodies in the Lower Colorado River Basin where introduced Tilapia species became established. This source identifies it as one of the primary establishment sites for Tilapia populations in the region, along with other reservoirs, canals, and river mainstems.
