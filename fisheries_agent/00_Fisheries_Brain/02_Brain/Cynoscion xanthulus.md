# Cynoscion xanthulus

## Summary
Cynoscion xanthulus is the Orangemouth Corvina, a marine fish species. It was successfully introduced to the Salton Sea from the Gulf of California (1948-1956) as one of three established species, and Texas later attempted copycat introductions, even hybridizing it with the native Spotted Seatrout and releasing both hybrids and parent stocks into reservoirs, raising concerns about genetic dilution of an important Gulf of Mexico food fish.
