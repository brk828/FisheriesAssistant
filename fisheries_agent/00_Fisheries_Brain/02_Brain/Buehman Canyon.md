# Buehman Canyon

## Summary
Buehman Canyon (site 133) is a stream location in southern Arizona where Desert Pupfish were stocked in July 1989. The February 1991 survey found no Desert Pupfish from this stocking effort, with Longfin Dace being extremely abundant and moderate numbers of leopard frog tadpoles present.
