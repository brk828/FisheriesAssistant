# Utah Prairie Dog

## Summary
The Utah Prairie Dog is an endangered mammal in Iron and Millard Counties, Utah, that faces jeopardy from malathion and acephate exposure through consumption of contaminated cicadas and plants. The reasonable and prudent alternative prohibits malathion and acephate use within 1/4 mile of prairie dog towns to avoid jeopardizing the species.
