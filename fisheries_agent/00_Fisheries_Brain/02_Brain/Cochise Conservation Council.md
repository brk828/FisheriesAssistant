# Cochise Conservation Council

## Summary
The Cochise Conservation Council is a conservation organization that participated in the 1989 Bog Hole management work session. Jerry Pratt represented both this organization and the Arizona Wildlife Federation at the meeting to discuss management of the wetland area and its native fish populations.
