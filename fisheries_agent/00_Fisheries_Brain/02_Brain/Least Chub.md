# Least Chub

## Summary
Least Chub (Iotichthys phlegethontis) is a small desert fish species found in western Utah spring-marsh complexes. Larry Crist presented research on its distribution and food habits in these specialized aquatic habitats at the 1980 Desert Fishes Council symposium.
