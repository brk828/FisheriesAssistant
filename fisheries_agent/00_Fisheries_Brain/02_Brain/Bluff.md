# Bluff

## Summary
Bluff is a town that served as a geographic reference point in the Upper Colorado River Basin during the 1992-1995 larval razorback sucker survey. It was used to help identify sampling locations, particularly in relation to the San Juan River system.

## Context

> This map documents the spatial distribution and abundance of larval [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*) captures throughout the [[Upper Colorado River Basin]] during the period 1992-1995.
