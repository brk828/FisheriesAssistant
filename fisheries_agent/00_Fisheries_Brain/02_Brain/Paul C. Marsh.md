# Paul C. Marsh

## Summary
Paul C. Marsh is a prominent ichthyologist and principal investigator who has conducted extensive research on fish populations and ecology in southwestern aquatic systems, particularly in the Colorado River basin and associated reservoirs. His work has involved monitoring native and non-native fish species, obtaining regulatory permits for scientific collection and research, and contributing to conservation biology efforts for threatened fish populations in arid region ecosystems. Marsh's research has been foundational in understanding fish community dynamics and informing management decisions for aquatic conservation in the southwestern United States.
