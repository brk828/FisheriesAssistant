# Wall-eyed Pike

## Summary
Wall-eyed pike is a predatory fish species that helps control sunfish populations through predation. Waters with sufficient numbers and sizes of wall-eyed pike and similar fish-eaters do not experience the sunfish overpopulation problems common in waters lacking adequate predatory fish.
