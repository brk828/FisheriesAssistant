# Creel Census

## Summary
A creel census is a survey method used to monitor and assess recreational fishing activity by sampling the catch of anglers, typically through direct interviews at fishing access points or through angler-returned creel cards. This technique provides fisheries managers with essential data on catch composition, fishing effort, and harvest rates that inform stock assessment, population management decisions, and the evaluation of stocking programs. Creel census data are fundamental to understanding the sustainability of fisheries and the effectiveness of conservation measures in both river and reservoir systems.
