# Salmo Gairdnerii

## Summary
Salmo gairdnerii, commonly known as the rainbow trout, is a freshwater salmonid species native to western North America that has become a model organism in ichthyological research due to its significance in both aquaculture and wild populations. This species exhibits well-documented physiological cycles, including seasonal thyroid activity patterns and reproductive behaviors, making it valuable for studying fish development, endocrinology, and the effects of environmental conditions on early life stages. As an important species in fisheries management and conservation, S. gairdnerii has been extensively studied in hatchery settings to optimize egg incubation protocols and improve survival rates for both artificial propagation and wild population restoration efforts.
