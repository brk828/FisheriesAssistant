# Culicoides

## Summary
Culicoides is a genus of biting midges that appeared in Hartlaub Lake following toxaphene treatment. It was detected in low numbers (1-20 larvae per square foot) in the profundal zone post-treatment, where it had not been recorded before.

## Context

> This study examined the impact of toxaphene treatment for fish eradication on the benthic invertebrate community of [[Hartlaub Lake]], Wisconsin.
