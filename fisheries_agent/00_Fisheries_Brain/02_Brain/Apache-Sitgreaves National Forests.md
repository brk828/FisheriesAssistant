# Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests

## Summary
The Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests represent a critical landscape for the conservation of endemic and endangered aquatic species in the southwestern United States, particularly serving as habitat for the federally endangered Apache trout and loach minnow within its riparian systems and streams. From a fisheries management perspective, this forest complex requires careful coordination under the Endangered Species Act Section 7 consultation process, as activities such as road maintenance, timber operations, and in-channel material extraction pose direct threats to aquatic ecosystems through substrate degradation and riparian buffer disruption. The forests exemplify the intersection of terrestrial resource management and aquatic conservation biology, where upland activities must be evaluated for their cascading effects on downstream fish populations and water quality.
