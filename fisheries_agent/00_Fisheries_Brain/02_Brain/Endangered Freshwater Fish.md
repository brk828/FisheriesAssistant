# Endangered Freshwater Fish

## Summary
Endangered freshwater fish are aquatic species inhabiting inland water systems that face significant risk of extinction due to habitat degradation, water pollution, hydrological alterations, or other anthropogenic stressors that reduce their populations below sustainable levels. These species require targeted conservation interventions addressing their specific habitat requirements, physiological vulnerabilities, and ecological needs to prevent population collapse and maintain biodiversity in freshwater ecosystems. Understanding the distribution, habitat use, growth patterns, and biochemical responses of endangered freshwater fish species is essential for developing effective recovery and management strategies.
