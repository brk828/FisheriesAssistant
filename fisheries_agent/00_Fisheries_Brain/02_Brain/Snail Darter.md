# Snail Darter

## Summary
The Snail Darter (*Percina tanasi*) is a small benthic percid fish endemic to the Tellico River drainage in Tennessee that became a landmark species in conservation biology and endangered species law during the 1970s. This cryptic riverine specialist, inhabiting freshwater habitats with flowing water and rocky substrates, serves as an important ecological indicator of stream health and aquatic ecosystem integrity. The species gained prominence in legal and policy discussions as a case study exemplifying the conflicts between environmental conservation and large-scale water resource development projects, highlighting the vulnerability of small, habitat-specialized fishes to anthropogenic landscape alterations.
