# San Bernadino National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
San Bernadino National Wildlife Refuge is a protected area in Arizona near the Mexican border that has been affected by transboundary air pollution. The source reports that emissions from Mexican copper smelters contribute to acid precipitation (pH 4.7-5.0) near the refuge. This acid precipitation is suspected as a factor in the extirpation of the Tarahumara Frog from Santa Cruz River tributaries in the region.
