# Arizona Fish and Game Department

## Summary
The Arizona Fish and Game Department is a state wildlife management agency. Along with the Nevada Fish and Game Department, it documented fish mortality from the June 1962 Lake Mead test explosions, assisted by the National Park Service, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, and sportsmen.
