# Granite Reef Dam

## Summary
Granite Reef Dam is a water management structure on the Salt River in Arizona that has significant implications for fish populations and aquatic ecosystems in the region. As a barrier to fish movement and habitat connectivity, the dam affects native fish species distribution, migration patterns, and genetic structure of populations in the lower Salt River system. The dam's operation and potential modifications have been central to fisheries management discussions and conservation efforts aimed at restoring native fish communities in the Salt River drainage.
