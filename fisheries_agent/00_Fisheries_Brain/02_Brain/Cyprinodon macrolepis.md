# Cyprinodon macrolepis

## Summary
Cyprinodon macrolepis is a pupfish species that researchers suspect may be related to Cyprinodon fontinalis and Cyprinodon alvarezi. This source mentions it in the context of examining relationships among Guzman Basin pupfishes, suggesting these three species may form a group distinct from Cyprinodon artatus.
