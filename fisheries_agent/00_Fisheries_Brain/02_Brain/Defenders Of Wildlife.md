# Defenders Of Wildlife

## Summary
Defenders of Wildlife is a prominent conservation organization that has actively engaged in aquatic ecosystem restoration and native fish conservation efforts, particularly in the Colorado River system and desert aquatic habitats of the Southwest. As evidenced in late 1990s initiatives, the organization collaborated with other conservation groups to advocate for and propose comprehensive plans to restore native fisheries and restore critical freshwater and estuarine habitats, demonstrating its role as a key stakeholder in freshwater conservation biology and the protection of imperiled fish species in arid and semi-arid regions.
