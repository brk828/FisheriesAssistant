# Campbell Flat Spring

## Summary
Campbell Flat Spring (Site #25) was a reintroduction site where Gila Topminnow persisted for 4-6 years. The site has perennial water with thick vegetation and was recommended for re-evaluation and restocking rather than being declared extirpated.
