# Six-Mile

## Summary
Six-Mile is a specific sampling location in Lake Mohave that was monitored by the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation during the March 1991 cooperative fish survey. This area was important for razorback sucker captures and recaptures, with multiple fish showing little to no movement when recaptured in the same vicinity after 12-24 months, and one individual recaptured there nine years after initial tagging in the same area.
