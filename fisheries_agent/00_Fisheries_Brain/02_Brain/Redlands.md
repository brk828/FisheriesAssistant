# Redlands

## Summary
Redlands is a site in the Upper Colorado River Basin where fish passage evaluation was ongoing. The FY 1997 work plan allocated $111,800 to continue evaluating fish passage options at this location as part of habitat restoration efforts for endangered fish species.
