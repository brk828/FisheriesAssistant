# Brier Creek

## Summary
Brier Creek is a stream locality in Kentucky that has served as a sampling site in multiple ichthyological and aquatic ecological studies, particularly those examining fish community structure, faunal patterns, and the relationship between stream characteristics and fish assemblages. The creek appears to represent an important reference site within Kentucky's physiographic regions, likely the Piedmont Upland or Blue Ridge Province, where researchers have collected baseline data on fish fauna and associated aquatic organisms to understand community composition and ecological stability. Its inclusion across multiple studies suggests Brier Creek holds scientific value as a documented study location for comparative analysis of stream fish communities and their environmental relationships.
