# Chevelon Creek

## Summary
Chevelon Creek is a tributary of the Little Colorado River in Arizona that serves as an important sampling site for monitoring native fish populations, particularly the endemic and federally protected Little Colorado River spinedace, roundtail chub, and Little Colorado River sucker. The creek has been extensively surveyed using multiple sampling methods (seining, electrofishing, gill netting) as part of conservation efforts to assess the status and genetic diversity of native fish assemblages in the Little Colorado River basin. As a relatively fish-diverse location with established populations of multiple native species across various size classes, Chevelon Creek represents a significant refuge habitat for native fishes in the region.
