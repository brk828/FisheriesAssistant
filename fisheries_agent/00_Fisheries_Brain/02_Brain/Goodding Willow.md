# Goodding Willow

## Summary
Goodding Willow (Salix gooddingii) is one of the canopy layer broadleaf species in Lower Bonita Creek's riparian vegetation. On the three lowermost transects, it comprised (along with Fremont Cottonwood and Arizona Sycamore) 65-93% of broadleaf species, but natural regeneration of seedlings was prevented by cattle trampling and browsing between 1979 and 1983.
