# Dexter National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Dexter National Fish Hatchery is a federal aquaculture facility that serves as a primary breeding and rearing center for endangered Colorado River basin fishes, particularly razorback suckers and bonytail, producing specimens for reintroduction programs throughout the Southwest. Operating as part of the National Fish Hatchery System, Dexter maintains long-term broodstock populations that supply fish for restoration efforts aimed at recovering species depleted by river habitat alteration and fragmentation. The hatchery's production complements other facilities like Arizona Game and Fish Department's Bubbling Ponds Hatchery, forming a collaborative foundation for recovery initiatives across the Colorado River basin.
