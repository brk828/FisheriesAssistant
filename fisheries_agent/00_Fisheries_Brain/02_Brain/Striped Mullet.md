# Striped Mullet

## Summary
Striped Mullet (*Mugil cephalus*) is a euryhaline fish species of significant commercial importance in coastal and estuarine fisheries, characterized by its migratory behavior between marine and freshwater environments. This species exhibits seasonal movements from offshore Gulf waters into tidal streams and bays during fall and winter months, forming large schools that support substantial commercial harvest operations, historically representing over two-thirds of total catch weight in some fisheries. As a detritivorous feeder adapted to variable salinity and temperature conditions, Striped Mullet serves as an important ecological link in estuarine food webs while remaining a primary target of commercial fishing pressure in coastal regions.
