# Tule Lake

## Summary
Tule Lake is a National Wildlife Refuge where pesticide contamination was monitored in 1964. Water samples showed suspended material averaged 20.3 ppm DDT in incoming agricultural water, decreasing to 6.0 ppm in marsh areas and 1.5 ppm in sediments, and fish-eating birds died following exposure to toxaphene in waste agricultural water, with fish containing up to 8 ppm toxaphene and dead birds containing 3-650 ppm in tissues.
