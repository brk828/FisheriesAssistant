# Biochemical Oxygen Demand

## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a chemical measure of the amount of oxygen consumed by microorganisms as they decompose organic matter in water, serving as a standard indicator of organic pollution and water quality in aquatic ecosystems. In fish science and conservation, elevated BOD levels indicate excessive organic loading (from sewage, agricultural runoff, or other sources) that can deplete dissolved oxygen and create hypoxic conditions lethal to fish and other aerobic aquatic organisms. However, as historical critiques note, relying solely on BOD and other chemical parameters can be misleading, as biological communities themselves provide more integrated and ecologically relevant assessments of actual water conditions and ecosystem health.
