# Pimelometopon pulchrum

## Summary
Pimelometopon pulchrum is the California sheephead, a wrasse that commonly enters very shallow water. Sixteen bones from this species were recovered from the shellmound, including distinctive pharyngeal bones, and it could have been caught from either shore or boat.
