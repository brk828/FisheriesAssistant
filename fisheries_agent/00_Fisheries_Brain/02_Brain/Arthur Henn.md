# Arthur Henn

## Summary
Arthur Henn is co-author of this 1914 taxonomic paper describing four new characid fish species from South America. He worked alongside Carl H. Eigenmann at the Zoological Laboratory of Indiana University to provide detailed morphological descriptions and comparative analyses of these new species.
