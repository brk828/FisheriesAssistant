# Grayrocks Dam Project

## Summary
The Grayrocks Dam Project in Wyoming was a water development project whose construction was enjoined in the late 1970s due to Section 7 ESA concerns. The project was ultimately allowed to proceed through the ESA exemption process with the requirement of establishing a $7 million trust fund for habitat acquisition, representing an early example of ESA impacts on Platte River Basin water development.
