# Death Valley Agabus Diving Beetle

## Summary
The Death Valley Agabus Diving Beetle (Agabus rumpii) is an aquatic beetle species known only from Carson Slough in Inyo County, California, though it may also occur in nearby Ash Meadows, Nevada. It was one of 30 species petitioned for endangered species listing in 1991 and was designated as a Category 2 candidate species.
