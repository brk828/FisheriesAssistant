# Sunfish Family

## Summary
The Sunfish Family (Centrarchidae) is the taxonomic family to which Smallmouth Bass belongs. According to this source, members of this family are more evolutionarily advanced than soft-finned species like trout and salmon, possessing true spines and complicated scale structures.
