# Sewall Wright

## Summary
Sewall Wright was one of the principal founders of population genetics theory. This source identifies him as one of four key scientists (along with R. A. Fisher, J. B. S. Haldane, and S. Chetverikov) who established the foundations of population genetics between 1920 and 1930, creating the mathematical and conceptual basis for understanding how genetic principles apply to populations.
