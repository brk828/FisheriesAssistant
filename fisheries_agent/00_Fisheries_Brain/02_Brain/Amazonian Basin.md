# Amazonian Basin

## Summary
The Amazonian Basin is a biogeographic region to the north of Rio Grande do Sul and the La Plata Basin. The Poeciliid fishes serve as effective biogeographic indicators that distinguish the Rio Grande do Sul-La Plata province from the Amazonian Province, representing one of the clearest faunal boundaries among South American freshwater fishes.
