# Crenichthys

## Summary
Crenichthys is a genus of small cyprinodontid fishes commonly known as springfish, exemplified by *Crenichthys baileyi*, that are notable for their reproductive isolation and resistance to hybridization compared to related genera like Fundulus. These fishes are endemic to isolated spring and groundwater habitats, primarily in the Great Basin region, and are of significant conservation interest due to their restricted ranges and specialized ecological requirements. Their genetic distinctiveness and difficulty in hybridizing with other cyprinodont genera make them valuable subjects for studying reproductive barriers and population genetics in endangered aquatic species.
