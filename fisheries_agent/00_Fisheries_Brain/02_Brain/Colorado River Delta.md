# Colorado River Delta

## Summary
The Colorado River Delta refers to the ecologically complex wetland and estuarine ecosystem at the terminus of the Colorado River where it historically met the Gulf of California, representing a region of significant binational (U.S.-Mexico) conservation importance for fisheries and aquatic biodiversity. This delta system, including key wetland sites like the Ciénega de Santa Clara, serves as critical habitat for endemic and migratory fish species and supports multidisciplinary research efforts focused on understanding hydrological, water quality, and biological processes essential for sustaining aquatic communities in an increasingly water-stressed region. The Colorado River Delta exemplifies the intersection of fisheries conservation, ecological restoration, and international water management in arid-zone aquatic ecosystems.
