# Australian Marron

## Summary
Australian Marron is cited as an example of a regulatory gap in California's exotic species regulations. Despite being considered a serious threat, it was not prohibited because it belongs to a different family than Cambaridae (the prohibited crayfish family), illustrating how the prohibited species list was not comprehensive in addressing all similar threats.
