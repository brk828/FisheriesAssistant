# T. E. Dowling

## Summary
T. E. Dowling was a Professor and co-investigator on this 1990 Arizona State University proposal for taxonomic and genetic study of native fish in the Colorado River Basin. He was designated as key personnel essential to the project and was involved in the research team that proposed integrated morphological, protein electrophoresis, and DNA analysis approaches.
