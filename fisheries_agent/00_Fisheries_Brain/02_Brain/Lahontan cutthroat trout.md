# Lahontan Cutthroat Trout

## Summary
The Lahontan Cutthroat Trout is a threatened native salmonid species endemic to the endorheic (closed-basin) lake and river systems of the Great Basin in Nevada, particularly Pyramid Lake and Walker Lake, where it historically served as a keystone species in desert aquatic ecosystems. This species has been the focus of intensive conservation and recovery efforts involving federal hatcheries, including the Lahontan National Fish Hatchery, which was established in 1965 specifically to support breeding programs and reintroduction initiatives aimed at restoring populations diminished by habitat loss, water diversion, and introduced species competition.
