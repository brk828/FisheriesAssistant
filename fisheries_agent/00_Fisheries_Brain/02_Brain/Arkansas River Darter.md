# Arkansas River Darter

## Summary
The Arkansas River Darter (Etheostoma cragini) is a darter species documented in Kansas. This source lists it among 19 percid species in the state and identifies it as part of the Arkansas River drainage fauna in southern Kansas, noting it is now recognized as an imperiled species of conservation concern.
