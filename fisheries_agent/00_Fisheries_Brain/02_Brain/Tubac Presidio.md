# Tubac Presidio

## Summary
The Tubac Presidio was a Spanish colonial site in Arizona where archaeological excavations recovered 60 fish bones, 32 of which were identified by Dr. W. L. Minckley. The fish assemblage from this site includes exotic species not native to the local Santa Cruz River, providing evidence of extensive trade connections and resource procurement patterns during the Spanish colonial period, with specimens distributed across Early, Middle, and Late occupation periods.
