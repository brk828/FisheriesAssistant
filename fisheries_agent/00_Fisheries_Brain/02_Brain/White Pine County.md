# White Pine County

## Summary
White Pine County, Nevada is the location of Shoshone Ponds, one of three current refugia for the Pahrump Killifish. The site, located approximately 50 km southeast of Ely on Bureau of Land Management land, has maintained reproducing populations in 3 of 5 ponds since transplantation efforts began in 1972.
