# Russell

## Summary
I.C. Russell authored the definitive 1889 monograph describing Pleistocene glaciation and synchronous expansion of pluvial Lake Russell in the Mono Basin. Russell initially suggested pre-pluvial discharge through Aurora Valley into East Walker River but calculated the highest shoreline to be 100 feet below the divide, and in 1885 estimated that a 20-inch rainfall increase with same temperature would be needed to create pluvial conditions.
