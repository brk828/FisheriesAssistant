# Golenkinia radiata

## Summary
Golenkinia radiata is an alga that formed blooms downstream from the ORNL discharge point and showed radioactivity accumulation of 5.8 × 10³ counts/min/gram at 8 miles downstream. This represented lower radioactivity concentration compared to settling basin species like Chlorella, consistent with the pattern of diminishing radioactivity with distance from White Oak Creek.

Lackey documented the microscopic aquatic biota of the [[Clinch River]] system and adjacent waters in the [[Oak Ridge]] area, focusing on the relationship between suspended microorganisms and radioactivity from [[Oak Ridge National Laboratory]] (ORNL) operations.
