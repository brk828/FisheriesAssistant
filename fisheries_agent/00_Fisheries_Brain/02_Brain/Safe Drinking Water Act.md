# Safe Drinking Water Act

## Summary
The Safe Drinking Water Act establishes drinking water quality standards that are incorporated into RCRA mining waste regulations for groundwater protection. This source explains that current Part 257 criteria prohibit contamination of underground drinking water sources beyond facility boundaries, with 'contaminate' defined as exceeding Safe Drinking Water Act standards for arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, nitrate, selenium, silver, fluoride, and six pesticides.
