# Rhizome Fleabane

## Summary
Rhizome Fleabane is an endangered plant that relies on insect pollinators for reproduction. Aerial pesticide application would likely jeopardize this species through pollinator mortality, requiring a 3-mile no-aerial-spray buffer around occupied habitat or alternative use of carbaryl bait or biological control methods.
