# Characidae

## Summary
Characidae is a large and diverse family of freshwater fishes native primarily to South America and Africa, characterized by the presence of an adipose fin and teeth-bearing jaws, making them important subjects for freshwater fish identification and taxonomic studies. Members of this family have been extensively documented in ichthyological keys and identification manuals since the early 20th century, serving as significant components of freshwater ecosystems and aquarium trade. The family's prominence in scientific literature reflects its ecological importance and the ongoing need for accurate species-level identification tools in both research and conservation contexts.
