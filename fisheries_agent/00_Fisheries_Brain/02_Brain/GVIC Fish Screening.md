# GVIC Fish Screening

## Summary
GVIC Fish Screening is a new project proposed for FY 2000 to install fish screens at a water diversion facility in the Upper Colorado River Basin. The Recovery Program allocated $700,000 for FY 2000 and projected $1.15 million for FY 2001 to prevent endangered fish from being entrained in irrigation diversions.
