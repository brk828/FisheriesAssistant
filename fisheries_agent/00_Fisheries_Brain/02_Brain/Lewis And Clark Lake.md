# Lewis And Clark Lake

## Summary
Lewis and Clark Lake is a relatively young reservoir on the Missouri River in South Dakota that serves as a reference ecosystem for comparing fish growth rates and biological productivity across different aquatic systems. In ichthyological studies, it is notably characterized by faster fish growth rates compared to older, more established river systems like the Mississippi River pools, reflecting the higher productivity and favorable conditions typical of newly formed impoundments. Its inclusion in comparative studies of water quality and fish condition makes it a valuable model system for understanding how reservoir age and ecosystem maturation influence aquatic biota and fisheries productivity.
