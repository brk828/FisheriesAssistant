# Bellview-Gibson Mine

## Summary
The Bellview-Gibson Mine is an abandoned mine site whose surface runoff from past heap-leach attempts causes Arizona Water Quality Standard violations in Pinto Creek. The Bellview-Gibson area represents the most significant impact from inactive mine facilities and is responsible for current stream standard violations in Pinto Creek Basin.
