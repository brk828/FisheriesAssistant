# Oak Creek

## Summary
Oak Creek is a small montane stream system in Arizona that served as a significant case study in mid-20th century fisheries management, particularly regarding the sustainability of hatchery-dependent Rainbow Trout populations under intense recreational fishing pressure. With less than 40 surface acres of publicly accessible trout water in Oak Creek Canyon, the stream became a focal point for examining the challenges of managing put-and-take fisheries in limited habitat subjected to rapidly increasing human recreational demand. The repeated creel census studies and management assessments of Oak Creek during the early 1960s documented the ecological and administrative difficulties of maintaining viable sport fisheries in small, heavily exploited aquatic systems.
