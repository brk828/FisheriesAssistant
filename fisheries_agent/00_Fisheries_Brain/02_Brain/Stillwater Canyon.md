# Stillwater Canyon

## Summary
Stillwater Canyon is a reach of the Colorado River in the Upper Colorado River Basin identified as potential spawning habitat for endangered fish. The recovery plan designates identifying additional spawning sites in the Labyrinth Canyon/Stillwater Canyon area as a Priority 3 research action to be conducted between 1994-1996.
