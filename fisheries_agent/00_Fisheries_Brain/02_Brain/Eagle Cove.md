# Eagle Cove

## Summary
Eagle Cove is a named sampling location along Lake Mohave reservoir on the Colorado River. This 1986 survey included Eagle Cove as one of several sites where experimental gill netting and electrofishing were conducted to sample Razorback Sucker populations.

## Context

> This document records experimental gill netting and electrofishing surveys conducted on [[Lake Mohave]] in May 1986 targeting [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*).
