# DRC-1339

## Summary
DRC-1339 is an avicide (bird control chemical) for which new analytical methods were developed during the 1964 research. Three detection methods were established: volumetric (±0.02% accuracy), gravimetric (±0.1% accuracy), and paper chromatographic (±10% at 0.1-0.2 ppm levels), and metabolism studies in rats identified N-acetyl derivative and carboxy acid metabolite products.
