# Upper Peninsula

## Summary
The Upper Peninsula refers to the northern of Michigan's two main landmasses. This study notes that Bullfrogs are less common in the Upper Peninsula compared to the Lower Peninsula, indicating regional differences in species distribution across Michigan's geography.
