# Ecological Niche

## Summary
An ecological niche in fish science refers to the specific role, habitat, and resource utilization patterns that a fish species occupies within its aquatic ecosystem, encompassing factors such as feeding preferences, spatial distribution, depth preferences, and interactions with other species. Understanding the ecological niche of fish populations is critical for conservation biology and fisheries management, as it helps identify habitat requirements, predict responses to environmental change, and assess potential competition or resource overlap between species. The niche concept is particularly important in aquatic ecology when evaluating how artificial propagation, translocation, or population management strategies might affect both target species and their ecological communities.
