# San Miguel Island

## Summary
San Miguel Island is located off the Pacific Coast and represents a cold-water environment. This source notes that western San Miguel Island maintains cold-water conditions with summer temperatures approximately 10°C cooler than southern Santa Cruz Island, and this temperature gradient has been maintained in recent millennia.
