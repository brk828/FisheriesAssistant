# Cascade County Mosquito Abatement District

## Summary
The Cascade County Mosquito Abatement District is a Montana pest control organization that experimentally introduced mosquitofish to the state in 1969. Its Director Entomologist, John Toenyes, imported 2,000 mosquitofish from California for both planting in local waters and maintaining as brood stock for mosquito control purposes.
