# Sacramento Splittail

## Summary
Sacramento Splittail is a large, long-lived freshwater dispersant fish in the family Cyprinidae native to California. This source identifies it as one of the species recommended for endangered species listing as part of the Central California regional cluster, indicating it is among the taxa in critical decline in the state's aquatic ecosystems.
