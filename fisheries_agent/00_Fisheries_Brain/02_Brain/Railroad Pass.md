# Railroad Pass

## Summary
Railroad Pass is a geographic location in San Simon Valley where older Quaternary stream deposits are exposed in gravel hills. According to this source, these exposed deposits consist of gray-colored, coarse well-assorted gravel with mature erosional topography, representing the oldest of the valley fill sediments and reaching at least 750 feet in thickness.
