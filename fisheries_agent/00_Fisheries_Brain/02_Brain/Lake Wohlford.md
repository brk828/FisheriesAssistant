# Lake Wohlford

## Summary
Lake Wohlford is a 222-acre reservoir located 7 miles northeast of Escondido in San Diego County, California, owned by the Escondido Mutual Water Company with a maximum volume of 7,000 acre-feet. This source reports that a compressed air destratification system was installed in 1961-1962 to eliminate thermal stratification, which successfully improved water quality by reducing chlorine demand from 14 ppm to 7 ppm (saving $6,000 annually), eliminating hydrogen sulfide and taste/odor complaints, and achieving complete destratification in approximately 2 hours of operation. The system also reduced evaporation by approximately 4% yearly and appeared to dampen phytoplankton bloom magnitude, though a 1959 fish kill from rapid drawdown and subsequent restocking prevented full evaluation of destratification effects on the fishery.
