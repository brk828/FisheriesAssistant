# Whitewater Creek

## Summary
Whitewater Creek is a tributary of the San Francisco River in Catron County, New Mexico. According to this 1985 proposal, the loach minnow (Tiaroga cobitis) was found in the lower 1.5 kilometers of Whitewater Creek, representing one of the few remaining stream segments where this threatened species still persisted after an 85% reduction in its historical range.
