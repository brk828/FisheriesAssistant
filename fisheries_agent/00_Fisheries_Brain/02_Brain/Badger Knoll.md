# Badger Knoll

## Summary
Badger Knoll was a proposed prescribed fire project on the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that involved burning in Mexican Spotted Owl critical habitat. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service deferred consultation on this project pending completion and review of a detailed burn plan, making it one of three projects for which consultation was not concluded during this review.
