# Sacaton Grasslands

## Summary
Sacaton Grasslands are a dominant plant community type in the Upper San Pedro Basin riparian corridor that the proposal identifies as requiring study of relationships between ecosystem integrity and groundwater/surface flow regimes. The research program planned field studies at 10 sacaton grassland sites to assess biotic integrity through measurements of water potential, height, cover, vegetation volume, and reproductive output (seed weight, seeds per inflorescence, inflorescences per plant) in relation to hydrologic parameters. These assessments would help elucidate hydrologic requirements for restoring degraded stands and maintaining high-quality grasslands.
