# Council on Environmental Quality

## Summary
The Council on Environmental Quality is a federal body that assessed environmental threats to the San Pedro basin. In 1981, it warned that groundwater overdraft was the major desertification force in the area and could exhaust the aquifer by 2020, potentially causing the San Pedro River to run dry like the Santa Cruz River.
