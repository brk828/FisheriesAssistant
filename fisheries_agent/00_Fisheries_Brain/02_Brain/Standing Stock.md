# Standing Stock

## Summary
Standing stock is the total biomass of fish present in a population at a given time. Northern pike standing stock was 29.0 kg/ha under no regulation and increased to 31.9 kg/ha under complete catch-and-release, with mean biomass (B̄) used in production calculations showing turnover ratio of 0.41.
