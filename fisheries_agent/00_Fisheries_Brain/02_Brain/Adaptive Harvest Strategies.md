# Adaptive Harvest Strategies

## Summary
Adaptive harvest strategies for Northern Pike suppression involved transitioning from intensive suppression (2012-2014) to a reduced-effort control phase (2015-2018), with annual effort reductions of up to 50% once CPUE targets were met. The program demonstrated that angler exploitation alone (20% in 2010) and harvest derbies were insufficient for population control, requiring sustained mechanical removal with annual monitoring to detect recruitment rebounds, as evidenced by the 2017-2018 population increase when effort dropped below 267 net sets.
