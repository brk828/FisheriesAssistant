# Channel Islands National Park

## Summary
Channel Islands National Park is represented on the NPS coordination group through Gary E. Davis, a Research Marine Biologist who serves as the expert for Coastal/Marine Environments. Davis brings research marine biology expertise to inform Commission deliberations on marine and coastal resource management policy within the National Park System.
