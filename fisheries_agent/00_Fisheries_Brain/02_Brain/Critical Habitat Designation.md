# Critical Habitat Designation

## Summary
Critical Habitat Designation, in the context of fish conservation, is the formal identification and legal protection of specific geographic areas essential to the survival and recovery of threatened or endangered aquatic species, as mandated under the Endangered Species Act. These designations delineate the physical and biological features—such as particular river systems, flow regimes, water quality parameters, and habitat characteristics—that a species requires for feeding, breeding, and long-term population viability. For example, critical habitat for Colorado River Basin fish species includes specific river segments and their associated ecological conditions necessary to support populations and prevent further decline or extinction.
