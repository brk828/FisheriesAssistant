# Shore Seining

## Summary
Shore seining is a fish sampling method that involves pulling a net through nearshore shallow water habitats. In the 1987 Lake Mohave survey, shore seining was conducted at 24 sites with varying effort levels ranging from 76 to 2,745 square meters, documenting species composition in nearshore areas and finding Largemouth Bass present at 17 of the 24 sites sampled.
