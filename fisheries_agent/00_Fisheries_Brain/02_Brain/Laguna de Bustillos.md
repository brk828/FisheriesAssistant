# Laguna de Bustillos

## Summary
Laguna de Bustillos is an endorheic (closed) basin in Chihuahua, Mexico that contains a distinctive population of Chihuahua Chub referred to as Gila cf. nigrescens. This population is probably taxonomically distinct from other G. nigrescens populations based on unique allometric growth patterns, consistently lower lateral-line scale counts (mean 64.6 vs. 69.7), fewer head pores, and complete separation from other populations in multivariate analyses.
