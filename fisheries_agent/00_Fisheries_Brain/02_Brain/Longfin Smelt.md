# Longfin Smelt

## Summary
Longfin Smelt is a native fish species that has been petitioned for endangered species listing by the American Fisheries Society. This source recommends it for listing in both the Central California and North Coast regional clusters, indicating it is among the species experiencing critical population declines in California's aquatic ecosystems.
