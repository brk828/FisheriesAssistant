# Partners in Flight

## Summary
Partners in Flight is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service conservation program that works to benefit bird species. According to this 1996 Federal Register notice, it is one of the existing Service programs through which candidate species conservation is emphasized, alongside refuges, fisheries, and migratory bird programs.
