# Riparian Systems

## Summary
Riparian Systems in the arid Southwest are critical corridors that support disproportionately high biodiversity relative to their limited extent. These systems have been heavily impacted by multiple stressors including livestock grazing, altered hydrology, and periodic catastrophic flooding, with Bonita Creek representing a typical example of such degraded but potentially recoverable habitat.
