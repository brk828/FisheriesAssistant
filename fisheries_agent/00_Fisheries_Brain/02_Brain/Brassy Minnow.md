# Brassy Minnow

## Summary
The Brassy Minnow (Hypsilepisbrachiata) is a small cyprinid species inhabiting sluggish, sediment-rich backwater environments including stream oxbows, swamps, and lakes with soft substrates and abundant woody debris but minimal aquatic vegetation. This species is commonly documented as a co-occurring component of diverse fish assemblages in North American freshwater systems and serves as an indicator organism for understanding community structure and habitat associations in low-energy aquatic ecosystems. Its presence in historical ichthyological surveys reflects broader patterns of fish distribution across specific microhabitat conditions characterized by low water velocity and detrital-dominated bottom conditions.
