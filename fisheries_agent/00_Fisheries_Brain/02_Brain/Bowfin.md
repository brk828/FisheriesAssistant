# Bowfin

## Summary
The Bowfin (Amia calva) is a primitive freshwater fish species of North American origin that demonstrates moderate resistance to ichthyocidal toxicants, making it an important indicator species in fisheries management and fish control studies. As a hardy, adaptable species native to slow-moving waters and swamps, the Bowfin's physiological tolerance to chemical stressors provides valuable baseline data for understanding how different fish taxa respond to management techniques and environmental perturbations. Its presence and abundance in aquatic ecosystems serve as an ecological indicator of water quality and habitat conditions relevant to broader conservation and fisheries assessment efforts.
