# Gila River System

## Summary
The Gila River System encompasses rivers in Arizona and New Mexico and is part of the native range of the Desert Sucker (Catostomus clarkii). The Desert Sucker is found throughout this system as well as in the Colorado River drainage below the Grand Canyon and the Virgin and White Rivers of Utah and Nevada.
