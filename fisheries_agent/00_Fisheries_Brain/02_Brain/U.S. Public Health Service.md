# U.S. Public Health Service

## Summary
The U.S. Public Health Service is a federal agency that conducted companion studies related to the Columbia River radioecology research. According to this source, their studies were initiated in July 1951 and coordinated with the ongoing aquatic organism radioactivity investigations at Hanford.
