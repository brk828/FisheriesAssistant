# Etheostoma Swaini

## Summary
Etheostoma swaini is a darter species in the percid family that was historically used as a taxonomic classification for certain small freshwater fishes in southeastern North American drainages, though subsequent ichthyological work demonstrated that some specimens initially assigned to this species, particularly those from the Okaloosa region of Florida, represented a distinct valid species that was re-established as Etheostoma okaloosae. The species exemplifies the importance of rigorous taxonomic revision in fish systematics, where expanded specimen collections and morphological analysis can clarify species boundaries that were obscured by limited type material, with implications for accurate biodiversity assessment and conservation management of localized darter populations.
