# Rain Creek Allotment

## Summary
Rain Creek Allotment is a cattle grazing allotment that includes approximately 0.5 mile of stream zone within the Mogollon Creek renovation area. The allotment is grazed with 151 head of cattle from March 1-31 and additional cattle at other times, with grazing excluded May 1-August 15; current management was considered not inconsistent with Gila Trout recovery objectives and no changes to permitted numbers or management were proposed.
