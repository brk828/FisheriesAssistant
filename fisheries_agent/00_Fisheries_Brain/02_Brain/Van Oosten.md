# Van Oosten

## Summary
Van Oosten is a researcher who described methods for age determination in fishes using scales in 1928. This source notes that Van Oosten's scale method has been used extensively for determining growth rates of fishes in natural waters, though considerable doubt has been expressed about the accuracy of age determinations, especially for fish from southern waters.

## Context

> This study evaluated the accuracy of the scale method for age determination in [[Largemouth Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*) and [[Bluegill]] (*Lepomis macrochirus*) from Alabama waters using fish of known ages from experimental ponds.
