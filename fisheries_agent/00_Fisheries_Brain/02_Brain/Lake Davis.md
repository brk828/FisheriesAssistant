# Lake Davis

## Summary
Lake Davis is a 4,026-acre reservoir in the Feather River drainage at 5,775 feet elevation in Plumas National Forest, California, with extensive macrophyte beds covering up to 40% of the lake area. Northern pike were first confirmed there in August 1994, rapidly expanded through 1997, were chemically treated in October 1997, but were rediscovered in May 1999 with evidence of successful reproduction, indicating treatment failure.
