# Steven P. Platania

## Summary
Steven P. Platania is a researcher affiliated with the University of New Mexico who served as an organizer of the Desert Fishes Council Fish Count Program. In 1988, he coordinated program modifications and protocols based on participant feedback from multiple southwestern states, establishing standardized monitoring procedures for desert fish populations.
