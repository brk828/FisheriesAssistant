# Santa Cruz Island

## Summary
Santa Cruz Island is located off the Pacific Coast, with its southern portion representing relatively warm-water conditions. This source notes that southern Santa Cruz Island has summer temperatures approximately 10°C warmer than western San Miguel Island, and this temperature gradient has persisted in recent millennia despite overall climate fluctuations.
