# Boulder Creek

## Summary
Boulder Creek is a tributary entering Cascade Reservoir from the east side in central Idaho, flowing through cattle ranches and irrigated farmland. Monthly water samples were collected from this creek from April through October 1965 to assess nutrient loading from agricultural return flows.
