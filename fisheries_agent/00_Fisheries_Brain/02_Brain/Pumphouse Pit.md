# Pumphouse Pit

## Summary
Pumphouse Pit served as the control site in the three-pit Linn County fertilization experiment. The site produced black bullheads reaching 11.25 inches and 9 ounces, demonstrating growth without experimental fertilization treatment.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
