# Owens Valley Refuge

## Summary
Owens Valley Refuge is a conservation facility in the western United States that was included in the planned itinerary for Mexican trainees participating in this wetland management program. It was selected as one of several operating refuges and conservation units where participants would receive hands-on management training and observe established conservation practices.
