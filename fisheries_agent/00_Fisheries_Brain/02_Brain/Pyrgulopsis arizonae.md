# Pyrgulopsis arizonae

## Summary
Pyrgulopsis arizonae, known as the Bylas Springsnail, is an aquatic snail ranked G2 (imperiled globally) and S2 (imperiled in state). This springsnail was identified as a federal candidate species and conservation target for aquatic invertebrates during the Sonoran Desert ecoregional assessment.
