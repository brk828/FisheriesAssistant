# Gammarus Desperatus

## Summary
Gammarus desperatus is an amphipod species from North Spring in Roswell, New Mexico, with mean male length of 12.98 mm. This source characterizes it as unique within the complex, with the shortest antenna-to-body-length ratio (0.474), highest coxal plate and pereopod base setation, and unusual mandibular palp features including C-setae clustering in couplets or triplets. It inhabits miohaline waters with salinity of 5.02‰.
