# Dalrymple

## Summary
Dalrymple provided radiometric dating in 1967 showing that the Mono-Inyo Craters complex exhibits Recent volcanic activity dating to 6,000-10,000 years ago. This dating helped establish the timeline of volcanic activity in relation to lake level fluctuations.
