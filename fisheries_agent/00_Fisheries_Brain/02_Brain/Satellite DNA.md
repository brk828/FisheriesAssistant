# Satellite DNA

## Summary
Satellite DNA is an emerging molecular genomic feature that the research laboratory was acquiring capabilities to survey at the population scale. This technique, along with mitochondrial DNA analysis, was expected to yield substantial new data for desert pupfish conservation genetics beyond what traditional allozyme analysis could provide.
