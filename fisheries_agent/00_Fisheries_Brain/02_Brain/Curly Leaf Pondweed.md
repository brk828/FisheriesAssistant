# Curly Leaf Pondweed

## Summary
Curly Leaf Pondweed (Potamogeton crispus) is an aquatic plant species. This plant formed dense stands in shallow regions of the Arizona Bay backwater habitat alongside Eurasian watermilfoil, creating vegetated areas that provided foraging habitat for razorback sucker larvae seeking food resources among macrophytic vegetation.
