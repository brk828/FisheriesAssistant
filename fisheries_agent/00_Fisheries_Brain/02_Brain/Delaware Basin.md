# Delaware Basin

## Summary
The Delaware Basin is a geological region where Gammarus-Pecos Complex populations occur. This source identifies it as one of the areas once covered by Permian seas and notes that the Permian Basin was connected to open sea through the Delaware and Marfa basins via a geosyncline crossing northern Mexico.
