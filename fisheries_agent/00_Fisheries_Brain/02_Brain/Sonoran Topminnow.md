# Sonoran Topminnow

## Summary
The Sonoran Topminnow (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*) is a small-bodied, live-bearing freshwater fish endemic to the Gila River Basin of Arizona and Sonora, Mexico, belonging to the family Poeciliidae and inhabiting desert springs, cienegas, and low-order streams. This species has become a significant focus of conservation and recovery efforts due to its ecological vulnerability and documented replacement by the invasive Mosquitofish (*Gambusia affinis*), making it a key model organism for understanding competitive displacement and species replacement dynamics in desert aquatic ecosystems. Its imperiled status has warranted inclusion in formal U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service recovery planning for southwestern desert fishes since at least the 1980s.
