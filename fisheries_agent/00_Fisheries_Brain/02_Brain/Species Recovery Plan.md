# Species Recovery Plan

## Summary
A Species Recovery Plan is a comprehensive, science-based management strategy developed by government agencies and stakeholders to restore populations of threatened or endangered fish species to sustainable levels. These plans typically outline specific conservation actions, habitat restoration objectives, monitoring protocols, and recovery goals with defined timelines and responsible parties, serving as the primary framework for coordinating interagency efforts to prevent species extinction and facilitate their return to ecological viability.
