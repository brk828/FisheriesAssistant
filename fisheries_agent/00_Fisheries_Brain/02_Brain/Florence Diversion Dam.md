# Florence Diversion Dam

## Summary
Florence Diversion Dam is a structure on the Gila River in Arizona that marks the downstream boundary of the fish collection area for this restoration project. The source indicates that native Longfin Dace and Desert Sucker were to be captured from the Gila River between Coolidge Dam and Florence Diversion Dam for reintroduction into Arnett Creek and Telegraph Canyon during 1997.

## Context
