# Walter Walker

## Summary
Walter Walker is a site in the Gunnison River system being studied for selenium tolerance. The Recovery Program was conducting selenium tolerance studies at this location to determine the potential for habitat restoration in the Gunnison River, as selenium contamination affects the suitability of restoration sites.
