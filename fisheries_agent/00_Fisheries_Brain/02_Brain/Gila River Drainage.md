# Gila River Drainage

## Summary
The Gila River Drainage is a major hydrological basin in the southwestern United States that encompasses multiple perennial montane and desert stream systems, including tributaries such as the Blue River, Aravaipa Creek, and Eagle Creek, which serve as important sampling units in ichthyological and conservation research. In fish science and conservation biology, this drainage system is significant for its endemic and native fish populations and is frequently compared with other regional drainages (such as the Salt River Drainage) to study patterns of genetic variation, species distribution, and habitat utilization across distinct aquatic ecosystems. The Gila River Drainage represents a critical geographic and ecological unit for understanding fish population structure and conservation priorities in arid southwestern North American aquatic systems.
