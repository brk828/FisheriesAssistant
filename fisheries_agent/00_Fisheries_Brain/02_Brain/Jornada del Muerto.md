# Jornada del Muerto

## Summary
The Jornada del Muerto is a geographic region in south-central New Mexico through which the ancestral Rio Grande flowed. This source documents that during middle to late Pliocene time, the Rio Grande flowed along the east side of the Socorro Valley through the Jornada del Muerto into south central New Mexico before eventually reaching the Rio Conchos at Presidio.
