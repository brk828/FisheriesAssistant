# Pozo Caliente

## Summary
Pozo Caliente is a location in the lower Colorado River region of Mexico that maintained a large Desert Pupfish population of approximately 30,000 or more individuals as of 1978. Despite threats from channelization, mosquitofish (Gambusia affinis) introduction, and potential commercial salt drying pool development, this population remained one of the largest documented in the region.
