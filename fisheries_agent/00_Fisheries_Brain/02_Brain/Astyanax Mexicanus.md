# Astyanax Mexicanus

## Summary
Astyanax mexicanus is a small characin fish native to Mexico that has become a significant model organism and invasive species in ichthyological research, particularly notable for its presence in North American aquatic ecosystems where it competes with native species in systems like the San Antonio River. As a generalist species, it exemplifies the ecological threat posed by exotic fish introductions to native freshwater communities, especially in species-depauperate ecosystems such as those found in the Chihuahuan Desert where low trophic complexity makes native fauna vulnerable to competitive displacement. The species' ability to establish populations in diverse aquatic habitats, from spring-fed rivers to reservoirs, demonstrates the invasiveness potential of non-native characins in altered freshwater environments.
