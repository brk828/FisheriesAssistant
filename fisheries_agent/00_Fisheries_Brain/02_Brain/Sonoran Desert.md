# Sonoran Desert

## Summary
The Sonoran Desert, as it relates to fish science and conservation, refers to an arid biogeographic region in northwestern Mexico and southwestern United States characterized by unique and often endemic freshwater fish communities that face significant conservation challenges due to water scarcity and habitat alteration. This region has been a focal point for ichthyological research and international conservation efforts, particularly through binational initiatives like the Desert Fishes Recovery Team, which coordinates protection and restoration strategies for native fish species adapted to the desert's limited aquatic ecosystems. The Sonoran Desert's aquatic fauna represents a critical conservation priority due to the high diversity of endemic species and the region's vulnerability to anthropogenic water management and environmental change.
