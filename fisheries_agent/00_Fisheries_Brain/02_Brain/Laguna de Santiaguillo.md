# Laguna de Santiaguillo

## Summary
Laguna de Santiaguillo is a Mexican water body where Gila populations have 8,9-9,8 fin ray counts, modal vertebrae count of 42, 60-70 lateral line scales (intermediate scale size), and no basal radii on scales. One tributary of Rio Nazas originates near this location, and populations show morphological similarities suggesting historical connections.
