# Colorado Desert

## Summary
The Colorado Desert is an extremely arid region in southeastern California where the Coachella Canal is located. This desert environment naturally lacks surface water, making the canal an important artificial aquatic corridor that extends fish distributions far beyond their natural limits in this region.
