# Lower Klamath Lake

## Summary
Lower Klamath Lake was a large lake in California that historically supported abundant populations of Lost River Sucker and Shortnose Sucker, sustaining indigenous and settler fisheries until the 1920s. This source documents that the lake (including its western extension Sheepy Lake) was drained and converted to farmland before 1924, causing heavy fish mortality and apparent extirpation of these sucker species, though the area was later reflooded after farming efforts failed.

## Context
