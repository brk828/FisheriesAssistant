# Permian Red Beds

## Summary
The Permian Red Beds are geological formations (Upper and Lower Enid Formations) consisting of red shales and sandstones from the Permian period that underlie the Skeleton Creek drainage basin. These formations contribute naturally high turbidity and alkalinity to the stream, creating baseline environmental conditions that fish populations must cope with in addition to anthropogenic pollution sources.
