# Craterocephalus gloveri

## Summary
Craterocephalus gloveri is an endemic hardyhead fish species described by Crowley and Ivantsoff in 1990. This species represents one of the biological discoveries documented in the bibliography's coverage of the unique endemic fish fauna that evolved in isolated Great Artesian Basin spring habitats.
