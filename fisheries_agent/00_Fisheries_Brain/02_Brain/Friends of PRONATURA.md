# Friends of PRONATURA

## Summary
Friends of PRONATURA is a U.S.-based conservation organization founded in 1986 that is affiliated with the Mexican conservation group PRONATURA A.C. The organization co-sponsored a 1988 symposium on the Pinacate volcanic area and became a founding partner of the International Sonoran Desert Alliance, working to develop binational approaches to natural resource management in the Sonoran Desert and Upper Gulf of California region.
