# University of Nevada Reno

## Summary
The University of Nevada Reno is a research institution where Dr. Peter Brussard was conducting electrophoretic analysis to determine the extent of hybridization between Cui-ui and Lake Tahoe Suckers. At the time of the 1989 meeting, this analysis was incomplete, and Dr. Brussard was considering using facilities at other universities to complete the genetic work.
