# ESA Exemption Committee

## Summary
The ESA Exemption Committee is the body from which projects can seek exemptions from Endangered Species Act requirements. Of 52 BLM timber sales that received jeopardy opinions in FY 1991, 44 sought exemptions from this committee after 8 were dropped due to ESA concerns.
