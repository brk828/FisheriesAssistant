# Peebles Navajo Cactus

## Summary
Peebles Navajo Cactus is an endangered cactus species that relies on insect pollinators for reproduction. The Service determined that aerial pesticide spraying would likely jeopardize this species through mortality of halicitid bee pollinators, requiring a 3-mile exclusion buffer for aerial applications or use of alternative control methods.
