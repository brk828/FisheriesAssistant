# Yankee Fork

## Summary
Yankee Fork is a tributary in the Salmon River drainage where spring-run Chinook salmon spawn in its upper reaches. Redd counts increased from 233 in 1965 to 454 in 1966, reflecting the overall increase in spring chinook spawning escapement.
