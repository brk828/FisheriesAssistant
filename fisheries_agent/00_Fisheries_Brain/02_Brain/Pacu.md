# Pacu

## Summary
Pacu (Colossoma sp.) is a non-native fish from the family Characidae. This source documents the capture of an adult pacu (42.5 cm TL, 1.485 kg) at ASU Research Park in November 1992, representing an aquarium release problem, with anglers reporting catching four such fish total and observing numerous others up to 3 kg in the system.
