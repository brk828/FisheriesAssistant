# Tennessee Valley Authority v. Hill

## Summary
Tennessee Valley Authority v. Hill is a 1978 U.S. Supreme Court case (437 U.S. 153, 184) that established the legal precedent that endangered species must receive "the highest of priorities" in federal decision-making. This legal notice cites this case to emphasize the duty of the Secretary of the Interior and Bureau of Reclamation to prioritize protection of the razorback sucker, particularly regarding federal actions like the Animas-La Plata Project and Navajo Dam reoperation that could destroy or adversely modify the species' habitat.
