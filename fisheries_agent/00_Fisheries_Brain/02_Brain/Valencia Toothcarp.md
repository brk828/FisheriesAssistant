# Valencia Toothcarp

## Summary
Valencia Toothcarp (Valencia hispanica) is an endangered endemic species from the family Valenciidae restricted to freshwater habitats in the Iberian Peninsula. This study found that Valencia Toothcarp showed more pronounced negative responses to mosquitofish than Spanish Toothcarp, including significantly reduced feeding rates, increased aggression received (peaking in April), and disrupted courtship behavior, attributed to greater vertical habitat overlap with mosquitofish (both occupying upper water column at ~13-14 cm from bottom) and restriction to freshwater environments where mosquitofish thrive.
