# Big Bend Gambusia

## Summary
The Big Bend Gambusia is a small cyprinodontid fish species endemic to the Big Bend region of Texas that has become critically endangered due to habitat loss, water extraction, and competition from introduced species, making it a focal species for conservation efforts in arid southwestern aquatic ecosystems. This species represents an example of highly localized freshwater fish biodiversity at extreme risk of extinction, with populations restricted to isolated spring systems that require intensive management and protection to ensure survival. As a small live-bearer inhabiting limited groundwater habitats, the Big Bend Gambusia exemplifies the vulnerability of endemic desert fishes and the importance of preserving unique aquatic microhabitats in arid regions.
