# Arizona Game And Fish Department

## Summary
The Arizona Game and Fish Department (AGFD) is the state wildlife agency responsible for managing fish populations and aquatic resources throughout Arizona, including conservation and regulatory oversight of native and non-native fish species in the state's diverse aquatic ecosystems. As a primary stakeholder in southwestern fisheries management, AGFD collaborates with federal agencies such as the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and Bureau of Reclamation on conservation initiatives, endangered species recovery programs, and ecological monitoring efforts, particularly for imperiled native fishes in the Colorado River basin and desert aquatic habitats. The department's mandate encompasses fisheries science, population assessment, habitat management, and implementation of regulations to balance recreational fishing interests with biodiversity conservation goals in arid-region aquatic environments.
