# Conservation Modeling

## Summary
Conservation modeling uses quantitative approaches to inform management decisions for threatened species. This study develops a vulnerability model integrating growth trajectories, size distributions, and gape limitation to identify the critical first 30 days post-hatch as the highest-risk period for June suckers, and recommends specific management actions including flow manipulation to advance spawning timing, careful evaluation of vegetation additions, and design of predator-free grow-out areas based on size-specific vulnerability estimates.
