# Boyce Thompson

## Summary
Boyce Thompson refers to a location maintaining a refugium population of Desert Pupfish (Cyprinodon macularius). The 1986 recovery team meeting notes indicated that the Desert Pupfish population at Boyce Thompson was doing well, serving as one of the important captive populations for this endangered species.
