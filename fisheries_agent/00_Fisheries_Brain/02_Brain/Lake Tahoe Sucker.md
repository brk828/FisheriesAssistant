# Lake Tahoe Sucker

## Summary
The Lake Tahoe Sucker is a fish species in the Catostomidae family that poses a critical hybridization threat to the endangered Cui-ui. Physical characteristics of many Cui-ui individuals suggested hybrid presence between these two species, though electrophoretic analysis had not yet confirmed this at the time of the 1989 meeting. The Recovery Team recommended preventing purported hybrids from spawning and culling them at the Marble Bluff Fish Facility fishway entrance to protect the genetic purity of the Cui-ui population.
