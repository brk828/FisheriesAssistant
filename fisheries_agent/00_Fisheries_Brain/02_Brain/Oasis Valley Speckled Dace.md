# Oasis Valley Speckled Dace

## Summary
The Oasis Valley Speckled Dace is a fish subspecies found in the Amargosa River and adjacent habitats in Nevada and California. It was one of the species petitioned for endangered species listing due to threats from habitat modification including channelization and recreational vehicle use in the Amargosa River channel.
