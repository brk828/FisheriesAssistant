# Southland Paper Mill

## Summary
Southland Paper Mill was an industrial facility near Lufkin, Texas that discharged effluent into the Angelina River, creating the biggest pollution problem in Region 3-B during this period. The mill's black water effluent was expected to pollute the planned McGee Bend Reservoir, causing aesthetic damage and destroying spawning grounds through bottom deposits.
