# Grand Valley Water Management

## Summary
Grand Valley Water Management is a water conservation project in the Upper Colorado River Basin designed to provide water for endangered fish recovery. According to the FY 1998 work plan, this project received $1.78 million in funding for canal improvements aimed at providing up to 30,000 acre-feet of water for endangered fish through reduced demand and storage in Green Mountain Reservoir, though the project was scheduled for mid-year review due to continuing negotiations and schedule delays.
