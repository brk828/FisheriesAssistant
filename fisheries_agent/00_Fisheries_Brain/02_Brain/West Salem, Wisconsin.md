# West Salem, Wisconsin

## Summary
West Salem, Wisconsin is the location of Veterans Memorial Park, where a successful 1965 field trial demonstrated antimycin's effectiveness in eliminating common carp and other undesirable fish species. The trial showed that antimycin degraded completely within 6 days and caused no harm to semi-tame mallard ducks that consumed dead minnows.
