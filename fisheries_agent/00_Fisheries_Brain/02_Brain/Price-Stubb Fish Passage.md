# Price-Stubb Fish Passage

## Summary
Price-Stubb Fish Passage is a habitat restoration project focused on restoring fish passage at the Price-Stubb Diversion Dam in the Upper Colorado River Basin. The FY 2000 Recovery Program allocated $1.3 million for this ongoing project to allow endangered fish species to move through this barrier.
