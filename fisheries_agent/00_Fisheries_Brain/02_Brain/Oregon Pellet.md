# Oregon Pellet

## Summary
Oregon Pellet is a commercial moist refrigerated fish feed that had proven highly successful for trout and salmon culture by 1973. The experimental diets in this study were designed to incorporate moisture from fresh fish directly into pellets similar to Oregon Pellet methodology, but with simpler formulation and less sophisticated equipment requirements.

## Context

> This 1973 study evaluated two experimental fish diets combining roasted soybeans with fresh [[Gizzard Shad]] (Dorosoma cepedianum) as alternatives to commercial [[Oregon Pellets]] for feeding yearling [[Channel Catfish]] (Ictalurus punctatus).
