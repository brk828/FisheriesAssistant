# Index Medicus

## Summary
Index Medicus is a major medical and biological literature indexing service that was used as one of the standard reference sources for this bibliography. The compilation followed Index Medicus conventions for journal title abbreviations and used it as a source for locating relevant fish blood chemistry references.

## Context

> This comprehensive bibliography compiled by Joseph B. Hunn for the [[Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife]] in 1967 assembles more than 850 references on fish blood chemistry.
