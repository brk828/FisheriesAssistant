# Tempe Union High School District

## Summary
The Tempe Union High School District serves approximately 12,500 students in grades 9-12, with 32% from minority backgrounds (19% Hispanic, 6% African-American, 5% Asian, 3% Native American). The district is a partner organization in this proposal, providing student and adult liaison recruitment, scheduling coordination, and classroom integration support for the research program.
