# Bitter Lakes National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Bitter Lakes National Wildlife Refuge is a conservation facility in the western United States that was included in the planned site visit itinerary for this wetland management training program. It was selected as one of several operating refuges where Mexican participants would receive hands-on training and observe established conservation and management practices.
