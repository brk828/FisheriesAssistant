# Contracaecum spiculigerum

## Summary
Contracaecum spiculigerum is a nematode parasite found near the esophagus and in viscera of fish in Imperial Reservoir. The study documented this species along with C. brachyurum as being less common than trematode infections in most fish species except green sunfish, with no apparent effect on fish condition.

## Context

> This Master's thesis (1971) investigated the ecology of juvenile fish (10-100 mm) in [[Imperial Reservoir]], a 2955-hectare impoundment on the [[Lower Colorado River]] north of Yuma, Arizona. The study was motivated by planned channelization that could alter critical spawning and rearing habitats....
