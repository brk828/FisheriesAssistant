# Redear Sunfish

## Summary
The redear sunfish (Lepomis microlophus), commonly known as shellcracker, is a freshwater sunfish species belonging to the family Centrarchidae that is widely distributed across North American aquatic systems and frequently utilized in fisheries sampling and management studies. This species is characterized by a maximum size threshold of approximately 9 inches and is valued both as a forage fish supporting larger predatory species and as a component of subsistence and recreational fisheries. Redear sunfish are typically encountered in ecological surveys using electrofishing and netting methods to assess community composition and population dynamics in lakes, reservoirs, and hatchery facilities.
