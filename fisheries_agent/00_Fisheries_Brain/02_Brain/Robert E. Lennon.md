# Robert E. Lennon

## Summary
Robert E. Lennon was a researcher with the Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife who co-authored this 1970 report on antimycin A field applications. He worked with Bernard L. Berger to document over 50 field trials of this fish toxicant across North America.
