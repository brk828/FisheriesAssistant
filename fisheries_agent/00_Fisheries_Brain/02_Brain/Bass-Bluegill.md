# Bass-Bluegill

## Summary
Bass-Bluegill refers to a traditional polyculture pond management system combining largemouth bass and bluegill sunfish. According to this source, Bass-Bluegill ponds require similar physical conditions to catfish ponds (satisfactory water supply, adequate clay subsoil, ample watershed) but are less expensive to manage than intensive catfish production systems and can be successfully maintained in ponds typically too small (0.5-0.75 acres) for bass-bluegill production.
