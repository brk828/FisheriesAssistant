# Jeffrey Pine

## Summary
Jeffrey Pine (Pinus jeffreyi) is the dominant conifer of the Jeffrey Pine Forest community at 7,000-9,500 feet elevation in the Mono Basin. This forest type is one of the major plant communities in the mid-elevation zones of the basin.
