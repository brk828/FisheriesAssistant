# Loch Lomond

## Summary
Loch Lomond is a Scottish lake where the non-native Ruffe appeared in 1982 and became established as one of the commonest fish species. It is also the source location for Powan translocation efforts, where adults are netted from spawning grounds, stripped for eggs, hatched indoors, and released into selected lochs as part of conservation breeding programs.
