# Owens Chub

## Summary
The Owens Chub (Gila bigelovii) is an endemic cyprinid fish historically found in the Owens River system of California that has become a significant conservation focus due to its restricted range and vulnerability to habitat degradation, water diversion, and competition from invasive species. As part of the broader endemic fish fauna of the Great Basin and Mojave Desert regions, it represents the type of range-restricted freshwater fish species requiring intensive management and recovery efforts similar to other critically endangered desert endemics discussed in Death Valley Area Committee conservation initiatives. Its status exemplifies the conservation challenges facing desert aquatic ecosystems where multiple endemic species with extremely limited distributions face compounded threats from water resource development and ecosystem disruption.
