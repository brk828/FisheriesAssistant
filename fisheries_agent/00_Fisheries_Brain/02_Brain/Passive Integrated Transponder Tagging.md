# Passive Integrated Transponder Tagging

## Summary
Passive Integrated Transponder (PIT) tagging is a fish marking method used to track individual razorback suckers and bonytail at Imperial Ponds. Tagged fish were monitored using remote scanners to track movements and estimate population sizes, with data downloaded every 2-4 weeks throughout 2018.
