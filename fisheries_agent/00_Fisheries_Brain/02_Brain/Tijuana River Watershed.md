# Tijuana River Watershed

## Summary
The Tijuana River Watershed is a 4,500 square kilometer transborder basin spanning the US-Mexico border, with two-thirds located in Mexico. According to this 1998 research proposal, the watershed has experienced decades of raw sewage flows from Tijuana and Tecate, making it the single greatest source of lead and other anthropogenic pollutants to the coastal ocean of southern California and Baja California. The proposal outlines research to identify pollutant sources, measure contaminant loading to the estuary, and model relationships between land use patterns and water quality using GIS-based approaches.
