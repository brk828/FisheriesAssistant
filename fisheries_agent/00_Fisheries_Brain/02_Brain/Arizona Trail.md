# Arizona Trail

## Summary
The Arizona Trail is a recreational trail system in Arizona for which a new segment was proposed through Redrock Canyon. The proposed 12-mile Canelo Pass to Patagonia segment would accommodate hikers, horses/packstock, and non-motorized mountain bikes, with projected use of 300-1,728 people per year, and was evaluated for its potential impacts on endangered Gila topminnow habitat.
