# Alkali Bulrush

## Summary
Alkali Bulrush (Bolboschoenus maritimus) is a native emergent aquatic plant species. In Utah Lake, it is found in nearshore habitats as part of the native emergent plant community, which also includes hardstem bulrush and cattail, though invasive Phragmites often dominates these areas.
