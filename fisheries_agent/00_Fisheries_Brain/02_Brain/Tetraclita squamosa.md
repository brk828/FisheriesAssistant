# Tetraclita squamosa

## Summary
Tetraclita squamosa is a large barnacle species that dominates Zone II (+11 to +7.5 feet) of the Puerto Peñasco intertidal. This zone, characterized by these large barnacles, extends to approximately mean sea level.
