# Procambarus clarki

## Summary
Procambarus clarki is a crayfish species that serves as prey for striped bass in the lower Colorado River. In the diet study, crayfish appeared in 4 stomachs (7.3% frequency) with 10 individuals totaling 0.32 kg, representing 6.5% of total food volume by weight.
