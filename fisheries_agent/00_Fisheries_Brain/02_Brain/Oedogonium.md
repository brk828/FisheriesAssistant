# Oedogonium

## Summary
Oedogonium is a filamentous green alga that grew richly in the ORNL settling basin and demonstrated exceptional radioactivity accumulation capabilities. Samples accumulated radioactivity at concentration factors exceeding 10,000× the surrounding water (37 × 10⁴ counts/min/gram dry weight vs. 35 counts/min/ml water), with older mats showing even higher concentrations (15.5 × 10⁵ counts/min/gram ash, approximately 21,000× water concentration), suggesting potential for controlled harvesting as a decontamination strategy.
