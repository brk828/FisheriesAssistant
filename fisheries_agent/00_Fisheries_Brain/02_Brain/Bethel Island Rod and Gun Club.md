# Bethel Island Rod and Gun Club

## Summary
The Bethel Island Rod and Gun Club was an angling organization that participated in the striped bass tagging study by conducting publicity programs to encourage tag returns from recreational fishermen. Along with the San Francisco Striped Bass Club, they offered prizes and awards to promote angler participation in reporting tagged fish recoveries.
