# Campostoma Ornatum

## Summary
Campostoma Ornatum is a cyprinid fish species endemic to the southwestern United States, with known populations restricted to Tornillo Creek and Terlingua Creek within Big Bend National Park, tributaries of the Rio Grande. As a native North American fish of conservation concern, this species represents an important component of the desert aquatic fauna that has become increasingly threatened by habitat degradation and competition from exotic species introductions. Its conservation status and limited geographic range make it a focal species for recovery efforts aimed at protecting native desert fish communities in the American Southwest.
