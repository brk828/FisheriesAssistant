# Rudd

## Summary
Rudd (*Scardinus erythrophthalmus*) is a European cyprinid fish that has been introduced into North American freshwater systems, where it presents a significant taxonomic and management challenge in baitfish aquaculture due to the difficulty of distinguishing it from the native Golden Shiner (*Notemigonus crysoleucas*). As an invasive species, rudd can establish in wild populations and alter native fish community structure, making morphological and meristic characterization essential for regulatory monitoring and prevention of further spread through baitfish industries.
