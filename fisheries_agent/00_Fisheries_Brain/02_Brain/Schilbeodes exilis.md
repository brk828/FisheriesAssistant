# Schilbeodes exilis

## Summary
Schilbeodes exilis is a species of madtom catfish. This source notes it as one of five species recorded from the Kansas portion of the Verdigris River drainage that was not collected during the 1956-1957 Oklahoma survey, suggesting it may be present in the system but was not encountered in the Oklahoma sampling locations.
