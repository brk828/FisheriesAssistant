# Carlin-Dangler Tags

## Summary
Carlin-Dangler Tags are external fish marking devices that were used extensively for about 10 years on Colorado River endangered fishes prior to this 1990 study. The study notes concerns about this tagging method, including unsatisfactory tag retention rates and potential for secondary infections from the needle punctures required for attachment, which prompted investigation of alternative marking methods like PIT tags.
