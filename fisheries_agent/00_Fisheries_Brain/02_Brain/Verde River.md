# Verde River

## Summary
The Verde River is a major tributary of the Salt River in Arizona that serves as critical habitat for native fish species in the Lower Colorado River Basin and Gila River Basin, supporting populations of federally listed and sensitive species including razorback suckers, roundtail chub, spikedace, and various native suckers. As a riverine system with relatively intact reaches and mixed fish assemblages, the Verde River represents an important conservation focus for desert aquatic ecology and native fish recovery efforts. The river's hydrological integrity and tributary connectivity make it significant for maintaining genetic diversity and ecological function in the arid Southwest's increasingly fragmented aquatic ecosystems.
