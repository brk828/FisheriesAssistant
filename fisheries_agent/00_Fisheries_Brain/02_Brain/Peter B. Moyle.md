# Peter B. Moyle

## Summary
Peter B. Moyle is a pioneering ichthyologist and aquatic ecologist who has made significant contributions to understanding fish ecology, particularly regarding microhabitat preferences of native California stream fishes and their conservation needs in altered aquatic environments. His research, including foundational studies on fish assemblages in relatively unmodified streams, has been instrumental in developing ecological principles for fish habitat management and conservation biology in western North American rivers and streams. Through his scientific work and correspondence on critical issues like Colorado River management, Moyle has established himself as a key figure in applying ecological research to practical fisheries conservation and restoration.
