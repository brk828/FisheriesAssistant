# Coregonus lavaretus maraena

## Summary
Coregonus lavaretus maraena is a coregonid fish species studied for aquaculture potential in Czechoslovakia. This source reports research confirming the importance of large Cladocera in the summer diet of this species, and documenting fat content differences between juvenile/adult and male/female fish, as well as assessing its nutritive value and food industry utilization potential.
