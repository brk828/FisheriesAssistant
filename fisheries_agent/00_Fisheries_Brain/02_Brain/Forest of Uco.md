# Forest of Uco

## Summary
The Forest of Uco was a major capital project planned for construction in Year 2 (1995-96) of The Phoenix Zoo's strategic plan, with a budget of $1,600,000. This exhibit was designed to interpret tropical rain forest, cloud forest, and elfin forest ecosystems as part of the zoo's Tropics Trail, conveying the message 'I depend on others.'
