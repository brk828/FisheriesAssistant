# Sycamore

## Summary
Sycamore (*Platanus* sp.) is a riparian tree species that forms a key component of the Gila River and Verde River riparian corridor in Arizona, functioning as critical habitat within the aquatic-terrestrial ecotone alongside cottonwoods and willows. In the context of desert fish conservation and research, sycamore-dominated riparian zones provide essential shade, bank stability, and organic matter inputs that support native Sonoran Desert fish communities and their ecological requirements in these warmwater stream systems. The presence and health of sycamore stands serve as an indicator of riparian integrity and ecosystem function in arid southwestern river systems.
