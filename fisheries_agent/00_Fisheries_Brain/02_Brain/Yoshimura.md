# Yoshimura

## Summary
Yoshimura is a researcher who studied Japanese lakes and their trophic classification. In 1938, Yoshimura classified 8 of 12 Japanese plus-heterograde lakes (those with metalimnetic oxygen maxima) as mesotrophic, supporting this study's finding that most plus-heterograde lakes fall into the mesotrophic category.
