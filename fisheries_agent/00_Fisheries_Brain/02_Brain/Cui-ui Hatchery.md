# Cui-ui Hatchery

## Summary
The Cui-ui Hatchery is a facility at Pyramid Lake where conservation breeding of the endangered cui-ui occurs. In May 1989, Mark Coleman was conducting experiments there on the effect of female age on egg viability, and larvae were observed to be non-phototactic (not attracted to light), with most remaining on the bottom of stream tanks.

## Context

> Field survey conducted May 4-5, 1989 at [[Pyramid Lake]], Nevada to assess hybridization between endangered [[Cui-ui]] (*Chasmistes cujus*) and [[Tahoe Sucker]] (*Catostomus tahoensis*).
