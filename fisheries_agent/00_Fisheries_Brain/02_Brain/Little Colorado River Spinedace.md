# Little Colorado River Spinedace

## Summary
The Little Colorado River Spinedace is a small cyprinid fish endemic to the Little Colorado River drainage in Arizona, a tributary system of the Colorado River. This species represents one of several imperiled desert fish taxa subject to recovery planning and field surveys by conservation teams including the Desert Fishes Recovery Team and Arizona Game and Fish Department, particularly in tributaries such as East Clear Creek, Chevelon Creek, and Barbershop Creek within the Coconino National Forest. Like other Colorado River basin endemics, the Little Colorado River Spinedace faces threats from habitat degradation and represents a focus species for southwestern aquatic conservation efforts during the 1980s-1990s recovery planning period.
