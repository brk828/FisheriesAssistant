# Fundulus Olivaceus

## Summary
Fundulus olivaceus, commonly known as the blackspotted topminnow, is a small freshwater killifish species native to the southeastern United States that inhabits shallow, often vegetated habitats in coastal plain streams and marshes. This species is of ecological and parasitological interest as it serves as a host for various fish parasites, including monogenean ectoparasites such as Gyrodactylus species, making it relevant to studies of host-parasite relationships and fish health in natural populations. As a relatively small cyprinodontid, F. olivaceus plays a role in freshwater ecosystem dynamics and is monitored in conservation and ecological surveys across its range in the Gulf Coast drainage systems.
