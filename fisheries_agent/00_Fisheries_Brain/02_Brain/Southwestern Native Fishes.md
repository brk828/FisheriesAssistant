# Southwestern Native Fishes

## Summary
Southwestern Native Fishes refers to indigenous fish species naturally occurring in the arid and semi-arid aquatic ecosystems of the southwestern United States, many of which have experienced significant population declines and habitat loss due to water development, species introductions, and environmental degradation. These fishes are typically adapted to ephemeral and variable desert aquatic habitats such as intermittent streams, springs, and shallow water bodies characterized by thermal fluctuations and periodic drying, making them of critical importance in conservation biology and restoration efforts throughout the region. Management and recovery programs for southwestern native fishes often focus on maintaining and restocking populations in artificial or semi-artificial systems that recreate the historical conditions of their native desert habitats.
