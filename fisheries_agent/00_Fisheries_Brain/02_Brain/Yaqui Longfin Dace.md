# Yaqui Longfin Dace

## Summary
The Yaqui Longfin Dace (Agosia chrysogaster, "Yaqui form") is a native cyprinid fish endemic to the Rio Yaqui drainage system in Arizona, historically occurring in streams such as West Turkey Creek. Though not federally listed as endangered, this distinct form held Candidate 2 conservation status as of 1994 and shares critical habitat with the federally endangered Yaqui Chub, making it an important co-occurring species in regional conservation and habitat management efforts. Its presence in the same aquatic ecosystems as other endemic, at-risk species underscores the conservation significance of protecting intact riparian habitats in the Yaqui drainage basin.
