# American Lobster

## Summary
American lobster is a commercially important crustacean species studied using mark-recapture methods in marine protected areas and no-take reserves. This study identifies lobster population assessment as one of the contemporary fisheries applications where the evaluated closed population mark-recapture estimators are widely used, with the bias-adjusted Chapman and Schumacher-Eschmeyer methods recommended for improved accuracy.
