# Centerfire Creek

## Summary
Centerfire Creek is a stream in the White Mountains where 18 Apache Trout were sampled in 1988. Notably, this stream showed a balanced sex ratio of 1.0:1 (9 males to 9 females), which was unusual compared to the male-biased ratios observed in most other surveyed streams.
