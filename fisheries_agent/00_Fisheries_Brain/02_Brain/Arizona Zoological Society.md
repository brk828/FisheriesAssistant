# Arizona Zoological Society

## Summary
The Arizona Zoological Society is the organization that operates The Phoenix Zoo. According to the 1995-1997 strategic plan, the zoo maintained independence from government support and operated as a self-sustaining enterprise under this society, serving approximately 921,000 visitors annually in Maricopa County, Arizona.
