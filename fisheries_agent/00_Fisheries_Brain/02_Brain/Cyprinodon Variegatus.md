# Cyprinodon Variegatus

## Summary
Cyprinodon variegatus, commonly known as the sheepshead minnow, is a small cyprinodontid fish species notable for its ecological adaptability and presence as an introduced species in inland freshwater systems. This euryhaline species, naturally occurring in coastal brackish and marine environments, demonstrates the capacity to colonize and breed in diverse aquatic habitats including thermally-altered reservoirs and spring-fed river systems, where it prefers shallow waters with soft substrates and minimal vegetation. Its occurrence in multiple Texas water bodies documents the broader phenomenon of exotic fish introductions and their integration into local ichthyofaunal communities, making it relevant to both conservation and invasion ecology studies.
