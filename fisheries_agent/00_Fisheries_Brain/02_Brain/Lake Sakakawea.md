# Lake Sakakawea

## Summary
Lake Sakakawea is a reservoir in North Dakota that was the proposed site for Zander introduction. The proposal exemplified poor interjurisdictional consultation, as the introduction would affect multiple downstream states (South Dakota, Nebraska, Iowa, Kansas, and Missouri) without their input, and posed genetic contamination risks to native Walleye populations.
