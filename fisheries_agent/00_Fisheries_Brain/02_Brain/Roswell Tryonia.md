# Roswell Tryonia

## Summary
An undescribed species of hydrobiid snail (Tryonia n. sp.) endemic to the Roswell area of Chaves County, New Mexico, now restricted to Roswell Country Club Spring. This source documents the population was rare in 1966-1971, became abundant by 1973 (1 individual per square centimeter), then declined after springhead modification in 1973-74, with transfer to Dexter National Fish Hatchery recommended as a conservation measure.
