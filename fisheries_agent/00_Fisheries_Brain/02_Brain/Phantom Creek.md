# Phantom Creek

## Summary
Phantom Creek is a tributary of the Colorado River in Grand Canyon where a single specimen of Bonytail Chub was taken by an angler in either 1942 or 1944. This represents one of the few modern records of this now extremely rare or extinct species in the Grand Canyon.
