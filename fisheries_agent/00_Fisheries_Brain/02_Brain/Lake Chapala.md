# Lake Chapala

## Summary
Lake Chapala is Mexico's largest freshwater lake and a historically significant aquatic ecosystem of ichthyological importance, particularly for its endemic and commercially valuable fish populations such as Chirostoma species (silversides) that have supported local subsistence and commercial fisheries. As a freshwater system in central Mexico, Lake Chapala has been a subject of scientific research and conservation concern regarding its native fish fauna, fisheries management, and ecological conditions. The lake represents an important case study in Mexican freshwater fish conservation and fisheries science, given its endemic species diversity and socioeconomic dependence on aquatic resources.
