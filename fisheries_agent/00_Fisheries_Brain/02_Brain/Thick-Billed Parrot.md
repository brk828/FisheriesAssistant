# Thick-Billed Parrot

## Summary
The Thick-Billed Parrot was the subject of a reintroduction program featured in The Phoenix Zoo's Arizona Woodlands exhibit. This program was identified as a priority conservation initiative in the zoo's 1995-1997 strategic plan, demonstrating direct links between human behavior and species preservation with emphasis on arid land ecology.
