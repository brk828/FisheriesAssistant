# Sport Fishing Institute

## Summary
The Sport Fishing Institute was an influential organization that shaped recreational fisheries management practices during the mid-to-late 20th century, evolving from a harvest-focused philosophy centered on maximizing catch rates to a quality-oriented approach emphasizing ecological integrity and the angler experience. Based on historical records, the Institute supported large-scale fisheries management interventions, including chemical treatments like rotenone applications to restore sport fish populations, while simultaneously transitioning toward conservation principles that prioritized sustainable recreational fishing and environmental quality over simple catch maximization. This organizational shift reflected broader changes in fisheries science from utilitarian maximum sustained yield models toward ecosystem-based management and the recognition that fishing quality depends fundamentally on maintaining healthy aquatic ecosystems.
