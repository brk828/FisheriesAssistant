# Pond Weeds

## Summary
Pond weeds are aquatic plants that can grow in farm fish ponds and compete with desirable microscopic algae for nutrients and space. According to this source, pond weeds are more likely to establish when nitrogen fixation proceeds slowly in cold water (February-March), allowing water to remain clear enough for weed growth; systematic fertilization with complete 8-8-2 fertilizer can eliminate pond weeds over time, after which ponds can transition to phosphate-only fertilization without risk of weed re-establishment.
