# Duchesne River

## Summary
The Duchesne River is a tributary of the Green River in northeastern Utah that serves as an important study site in Colorado River fish conservation, particularly at its confluence with the Green River where research has focused on the impacts of nonnative predatory fish on endangered native species. This river system represents a critical habitat area for threatened Colorado River endemic fishes and has been the subject of intensive ichthyological surveys and recovery program investigations examining early life-history stages and recruitment dynamics of native fish populations.
