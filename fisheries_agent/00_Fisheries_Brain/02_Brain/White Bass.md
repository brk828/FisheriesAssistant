# White Bass

## Summary
White Bass (*Morone chrysops*) is a freshwater fish species of significant importance in North American fisheries and aquatic ecology, particularly in large river systems and reservoirs where it serves as both a valued sport fish and an ecological indicator species. The species is characterized by its migratory behavior and variable growth rates depending on habitat conditions, as documented through age and growth studies in systems like the Mississippi River and reservoir environments. White Bass populations are commonly monitored through creel census surveys and environmental assessments to evaluate harvest sustainability and ecosystem health, especially in relation to human modifications such as dredging operations and reservoir management practices.
