# Sacramento-San Joaquin Rivers

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin Rivers watershed includes the Feather River drainage where Lake Davis is located. Unsubstantiated reports of northern pike in the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta indicated potential risk of spread to this major California watershed system.
