# Parapetenia

## Summary
Parapetenia is a subgenus of Cichlasoma cichlids. In the Cuatro Ciénegas basin, four endemic species of Cichlasoma (Parapetenia) form a remarkable species-flock exhibiting trophic specialization (detritivore, molluscivore, and two piscivores) with diagnostic pharyngeal dentition, wide sympatry, and closer relationships to southern forms than to coastal Rio Grande cichlids.
