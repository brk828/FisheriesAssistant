# Lourdes Juarez

## Summary
Lourdes Juarez is a Mexican biologist affiliated with Centro Ecologico who served as a key collaborator for binational fish conservation efforts. This source indicates she was responsible for coordinating Mexican collecting permits through SEDUE (Sonora office) and would participate in the 1987 Rio Yaqui fish collection expedition along with an assistant named Alejandro, with funding needed for their vehicle mileage and per diem.
