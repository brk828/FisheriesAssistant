# Eulachon

## Summary
Eulachon (Thaleichthys pacificus) is an anadromous fish species found in the Klamath and North Coast Province. It is part of the complex anadromous fish assemblage that also includes Chinook Salmon, Coho Salmon, Steelhead, and Coastal Cutthroat Trout.
