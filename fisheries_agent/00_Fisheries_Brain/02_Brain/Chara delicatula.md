# Chara delicatula

## Summary
Chara delicatula is a monoecious species of stonewort in the corticate group of the genus Chara. This study found it has a diploid chromosome number of N=28, fitting the pattern observed in corticate Chara where monoecious species are diploid and dioecious species are haploid (N=14).
