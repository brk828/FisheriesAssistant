# Algansea rubescens

## Summary
Algansea rubescens is a predominantly lacustrine Algansea species from Lake Chapala with 19-28 gill rakers. This source notes that young specimens are indistinguishable from Xystrosus popoche without counting gill rakers, and that aberrant counts occasionally reaching 32 in Santiago River specimens suggest possible hybridization with Xystrosus.
