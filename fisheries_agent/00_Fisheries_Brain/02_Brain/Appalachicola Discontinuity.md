# Appalachicola Discontinuity

## Summary
The Appalachicola Discontinuity is a biogeographic pattern recognized for many southeastern fishes involving phylogeographic breaks in this region. This source notes that the drainage endemism patterns observed in the M. coosae species group are consistent with the Appalachicola Discontinuity, representing a broader pattern of aquatic species diversification in the southeastern United States driven by drainage isolation.
