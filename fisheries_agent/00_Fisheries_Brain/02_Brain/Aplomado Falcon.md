# Aplomado Falcon

## Summary
The Aplomado Falcon is an extirpated species being considered for reintroduction at Buenos Aires refuge. The plan indicates reintroduction is possible without extensive habitat alterations, noting that wild quail populations are adapted to raptor predation.
