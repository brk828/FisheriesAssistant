# Sonora Sucker

## Summary
The Sonora Sucker (*Catostomus insignis*) is a southwestern North American cyprinid fish species belonging to the family Catostomidae, characterized by the subterminal, cartilaginous-edged mouth typical of benthic algae-grazing suckers. As a endemic species of desert aquatic systems in the American Southwest, it has been identified as a conservation priority and was recommended for addition to the federal Animal Candidate Review List due to threats from habitat degradation, water management practices, and potential competition from non-native species. The species serves as an important indicator of native fish community health in arid region riverine ecosystems and is central to southwestern aquatic recovery and reintroduction efforts.
