# Bunting Spring

## Summary
Bunting Spring is one of 16 existing water sources at San Bernardino National Wildlife Refuge in Arizona. It was designated for Phase I development (Years 1-5) under the 1986 Master Plan to create habitat for reintroducing rare Rio Yaqui fish species.
