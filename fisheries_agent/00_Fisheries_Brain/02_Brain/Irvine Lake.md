# Irvine Lake

## Summary
Irvine Lake (also known as Santiago Reservoir) in Orange County received a mixed warmwater fish shipment on November 15, 1948 that reportedly included Pumpkinseed from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service facility in Dexter, New Mexico. The identification was based only on field observation with no specimens retained, and no subsequent records confirmed establishment.
