# True Frogs

## Summary
True Frogs (family Ranidae) are among the 29 species associated with riparian and aquatic habitats at the reservoir sites. This source predicts they could potentially be enhanced post-impoundment only if stable water levels are maintained and lakeside riparian vegetation develops, though most species will be decimated by flooding.
