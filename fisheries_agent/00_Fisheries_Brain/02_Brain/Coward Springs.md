# Coward Springs

## Summary
Coward Springs is a district in South Australia characterized by mound springs and artesian bores located south and west of Lake Eyre, which serve as isolated aquatic "island" ecosystems in an arid environment. These naturally occurring permanent water sources are of significant ecological importance as they provide critical habitat for specialized aquatic and semi-aquatic fauna in an otherwise water-limited landscape. The springs are characterized by elevated mound structures composed of various sediments and have been the focus of biodiversity surveys investigating fish, herpetofauna, and other organisms dependent on these rare freshwater habitats.
