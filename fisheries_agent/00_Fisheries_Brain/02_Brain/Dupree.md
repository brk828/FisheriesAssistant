# Dupree

## Summary
Dupree was a prominent researcher who conducted multiple studies on channel catfish nutrition in the 1960s. His work demonstrated that weight gain increased linearly with protein levels up to 40% (casein/gluten) or 35% (soybean) at 76°F, determined the 10 essential amino acids required by channel catfish, showed that partially hydrogenated corn oil was most effective at sparing protein (0.73 g protein per 1 g oil), and found optimal growth occurred with 12.5-14.0% corn oil in the diet.

## Context

> This 1969 review synthesizes the first decade of research on commercial [[Channel Catfish]] nutrition in the United States. Despite only four research stations intensively studying catfish feeding, excellent practical rations have been developed yielding 1 pound of gain per 1.5-2.0 pounds of feed....
