# Gulf of Tomini

## Summary
The Gulf of Tomini is one of the surrounding open waters adjacent to the Sulu Sea complex. Unlike the isolated Sulu Sea which maintains warm bottom temperatures above 10°C, the Gulf of Tomini shows continued decline in bottom temperature with increasing depth, dropping below 7°C at depths greater than 950 meters.
