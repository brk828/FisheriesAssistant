# Unit L-3

## Summary
Unit L-3 is one of the management units in the interconnected Pond L-7 system at Ouray Wildlife Refuge. It maintains a water surface elevation of 4664 feet and is separated from other units by dikes with elevations between 4663 and 4667 feet.
