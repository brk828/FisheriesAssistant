# Common Sucker

## Summary
The Common Sucker refers to a group of freshwater fish species belonging to the family Catostomidae that are typically classified as "rough fish" in fisheries management due to their perceived competition with more commercially valuable species. These bottom-feeding cyprinids are ecologically important as both forage for predatory fish species and as indicators of aquatic ecosystem conditions, though they have historically received less management attention than game fish. Common suckers play a functional role in aquatic food webs and were subjects of various fisheries studies examining age, growth, and population dynamics in relation to introduced species and habitat management.
