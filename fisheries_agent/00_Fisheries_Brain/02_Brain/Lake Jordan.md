# Lake Jordan

## Summary
Lake Jordan is a reservoir on the Coosa River in Alabama. Adult threadfin shad used in this study were collected from the Coosa River below Lake Jordan dam, which was one of three collection sites for experimental broodstock.

## Context

> This study examined the production efficiency of [[Threadfin Shad]] (*Dorosoma petenense*) in experimental ponds at varying stocking densities (100–1,600 adults per acre) to determine optimal rates for maximum forage production.
