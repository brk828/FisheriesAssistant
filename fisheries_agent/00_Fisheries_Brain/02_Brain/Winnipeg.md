# Winnipeg

## Summary
Winnipeg is a Canadian city that represented the primary market for Montana Goldeye, where the species commanded premium prices ($1.50-$1.95/pound retail) as a smoked delicacy served in restaurants and railroad dining cars. However, by September 1967, 400,000 pounds sat in cold storage there, causing prices to fishermen to collapse from 45-50¢/pound to as low as 17¢/pound, and Montana fishermen reported being cheated on weight and price by wholesalers in this market.
