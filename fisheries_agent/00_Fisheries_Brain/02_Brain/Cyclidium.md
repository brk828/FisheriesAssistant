# Cyclidium

## Summary
Cyclidium is a ciliate protozoan that was part of the characteristic waste-treatment community thriving in highly radioactive seepage areas (250-400 mr/hr) from Disposal Pit IV. This organism, along with other ciliates and algae like Oscillatoria, Lyngbya, and Chlorella, showed resistance to both high radiation and high salt concentrations typical of organic waste degradation environments.
