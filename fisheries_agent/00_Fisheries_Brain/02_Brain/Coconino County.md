# Coconino County

## Summary
Coconino County is a county in northern Arizona that was the focus of fish collections documented in this source from 1934-1978. Collections from this county included specimens from the Colorado River through Grand Canyon, East Clear Creek, Little Colorado River, and various tributaries, documenting native species like Gila cypha (humpback chub), Lepidomeda vittata (Little Colorado spinedace), and multiple Catostomus species, as well as evidence of hybridization between species.
