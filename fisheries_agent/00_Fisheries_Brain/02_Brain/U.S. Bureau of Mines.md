# U.S. Bureau of Mines

## Summary
The U.S. Bureau of Mines was a federal agency that contributed $125,100 in funding to the Globe-Miami National Prototype Groundwater Study. This collaborative investigation examined water quality impacts from copper mining operations in eastern Arizona.
