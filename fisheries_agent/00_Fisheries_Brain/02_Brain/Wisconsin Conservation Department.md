# Wisconsin Conservation Department

## Summary
The Wisconsin Conservation Department routinely treated lakes with toxaphene to remove undesirable fish populations. Personnel observed large numbers of dead Tendipes larvae on lake surfaces following treatments, which prompted this investigation of recovery dynamics and faunal changes.

## Context

> This study examined the impact of toxaphene treatment for fish eradication on the benthic invertebrate community of [[Hartlaub Lake]], Wisconsin.
