# Chota

## Summary
The Chota is a hot, dry valley in northern Ecuador located at approximately 4,900 feet elevation near the irrigated sugar plantation "Cabuyal." Rhoadsia minor was reported from this location, demonstrating the species' occurrence in contrasting habitat conditions compared to the humid sub-tropical forest streams where it was originally collected.
