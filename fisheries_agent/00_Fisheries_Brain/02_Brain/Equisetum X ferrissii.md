# Equisetum X ferrissii

## Summary
Equisetum X ferrissii (Ferris' horsetail) is a plant species found in dry marshes of the Colorado River corridor. This source reports it occurred along with Phragmites australis in dry marshes, which comprised approximately 70% of the total marsh vegetation (about 25 hectares total) documented in the park corridor by 1991.
