# Fort Harmony Tryonia

## Summary
Fort Harmony Tryonia is an undescribed Tryonia species found at Ojo Caliente Springs and Warm Springs in Socorro County, New Mexico. This Priority Category 2 species has abundant populations in excellent habitat on private land adjacent to state land, and the springs have been suggested as a transplant site for the endangered Socorro Isopod.
