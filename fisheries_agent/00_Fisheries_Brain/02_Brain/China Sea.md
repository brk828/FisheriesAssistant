# China Sea

## Summary
The China Sea (specifically the northern China Sea off southern Luzon) is a marine region adjacent to the Philippine Subregion. This source notes it shows normal temperature decline with depth, contrasting with the Sulu Sea's warm bottom temperatures, and shares few endemic species with the southern Philippine Subregion.
