# Cichlasoma Minckleyi

## Summary
Cichlasoma minckleyi is a highly polymorphic endemic cichlid fish species found exclusively in the Cuatro Ciénegas desert spring ecosystem in northern Mexico, making it a key subject for understanding freshwater fish endemism and conservation in isolated aquatic systems. As a prominent member of this globally significant but threatened ecosystem, the species serves as an important model organism for studying adaptive radiation, trophic ecology, and the impacts of environmental change on specialized freshwater fish communities in arid regions.
