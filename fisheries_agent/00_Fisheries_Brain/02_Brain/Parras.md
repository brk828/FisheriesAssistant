# Parras

## Summary
Parras is a Mexican location where Gila populations have distinctive characteristics including very slender caudal peduncles (head length into least depth 2.7-3.4), very fine scales (68-80 lateral line scales), but a relatively low modal vertebrae count of 41. Populations have some strong basal radii on scales and 68-80 lateral line scales, demonstrating that scale number and vertebrae number are not always correlated.
