# Great Smoky Mountains National Park

## Summary
Great Smoky Mountains National Park is a protected area located on the Tennessee/North Carolina border that contains freshwater streams, rivers, headwaters, and tributaries. As of 1960, the park supported 72 documented fish species across 12 families, representing a biogeographically complex transition zone where northern-southern and Atlantic-Mississippi Valley fish forms intergrade.
