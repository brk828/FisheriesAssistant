# Lucania Parva

## Summary
Lucania parva is a small cyprinodontid (killifish) species that inhabits freshwater systems, particularly in the southeastern United States. As a member of the killifish family, it represents an important component of freshwater fish biodiversity and has been studied in limnological and ecological surveys examining fish community composition and distribution patterns in American freshwater ecosystems. This species is relevant to conservation biology discussions concerning endemic and specialist freshwater fish fauna, particularly in regions like Florida where cyprinodontid diversity is notably high.
