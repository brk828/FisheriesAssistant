# Gap Creek

## Summary
Gap Creek in Arizona received Gila Trout stockings in 1972 as part of recovery efforts. However, the Gap Creek population was lost to drought between 1987 and 1990, illustrating the vulnerability of small isolated populations in headwater streams.
