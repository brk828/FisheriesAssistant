# Randolph Hatchery

## Summary
Randolph Hatchery is a New York State Conservation Department fish rearing facility located in New York. This study used the hatchery to conduct a controlled experiment on brown trout fingerlings from September 1956 to September 1957, examining the effects of size-sorting on sex ratios and growth rates under standardized rearing conditions.

## Context
