# Willows

## Summary
Willows are woody plants that can establish along pond margins. According to this farm pond construction guidance, willows should be prevented from growing in shallow pond margins (1.5-3 feet depth) because their establishment, along with other emergent vegetation like cattails and bulrush, impairs fishing success, promotes pond scum formation, and creates mosquito breeding habitat that fish cannot adequately control.
