# Red Pike-Conger

## Summary
The Red Pike-Conger (Cynoponticus coniceps) is a benthic, piscivorous fish in the family Muraenesocidae that reaches 1 meter in length and inhabits warm, shallow coastal waters at depths of 10-100 m on sand and mud bottoms. This source documents the first occurrence of the species in the upper Gulf of California near El Desemboque, Sonora, Mexico in January 1984, representing a significant northward range extension of approximately 1,055 km from its previously known distribution from Mazatlan and Cabo San Lucas south to Colombia. The species is characterized by a robust, moderately elongate body with a large mouth, distinctive vomerine teeth, and coppery brown coloration darker on the dorsum.
