# Logan Fisheries Experiment Station

## Summary
Logan Fisheries Experiment Station is a Utah Division of Wildlife Resources research facility located on a Logan River/Bear River tributary with approximately 42 concrete raceways and water from ~15 artesian wells producing 1800-2700 gpm at 50-63°F. During 1980-81, the facility modified units for recirculation and successfully reared squawfish and humpback chub, though the water temperature of ~68°F yielded suboptimal growth rates (0.25 mm/day versus the optimal 0.4 mm/day at 77°F), and its location outside the Colorado River system poses risks of disease and non-endemic fish transfer.
