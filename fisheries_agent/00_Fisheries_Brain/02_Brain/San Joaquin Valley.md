# San Joaquin Valley

## Summary
The San Joaquin Valley is a region in California's Central Valley where Moyle documented ecological impacts of introduced species. His research showed that introduced Bullfrogs prey on native frogs in this valley, and he documented dramatic declines of native fish fauna in the San Joaquin River system.
