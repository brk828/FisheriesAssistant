# Old Charlie Inlet

## Summary
Old Charlie Inlet (RMI 251.8) is identified as one of the six primary sites recommended for concentrated razorback sucker larval monitoring efforts. It is located in the Ouray Reach near the mouth of Old Charlie Wash, which serves as a pre-spawning staging and post-spawning recovery area for adults.
