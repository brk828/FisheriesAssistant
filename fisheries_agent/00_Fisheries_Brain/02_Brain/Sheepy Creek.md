# Sheepy Creek

## Summary
Sheepy Creek is a short, spring-fed stream that was a tributary to Sheepy Lake where Lost River Sucker historically spawned. This source documents that local residents harvested large numbers of suckers from a location called "Sucker Hole" using traps and snag gear until before 1924, and a few large fish were observed in the creek in spring 1960, suggesting possible persistence of a remnant spawning population despite the lake's drainage.

## Context
