# Clinostomidae

## Summary
Clinostomidae is the taxonomic family to which Clinostomum marginatum (yellow grub) belongs. This source identifies C. marginatum as a member of this family and notes taxonomic discussions regarding synonymy with C. complanatum within the Clinostomidae.
