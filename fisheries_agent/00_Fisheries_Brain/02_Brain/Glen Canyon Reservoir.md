# Glen Canyon Reservoir

## Summary
Glen Canyon Reservoir is another name for Lake Powell, formed by Glen Canyon Dam at Page, Arizona under the Colorado River Storage Project. During this 1965-1966 study period, the reservoir stood at approximately 3,540 feet elevation and when filled would reach 3,700 feet, extending approximately 185 miles upstream.
