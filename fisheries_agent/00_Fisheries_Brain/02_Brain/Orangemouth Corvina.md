# Orangemouth Corvina

## Summary
Orangemouth Corvina is a marine fish species that has been documented in the context of exotic fish introductions and establishment studies in the United States, particularly in evaluations of proposed or actual introductions to inland aquatic systems like the Salton Sea. As a potentially invasive species, it appears in fisheries science literature examining the ecological and management implications of non-native fish establishments, contributing to broader understanding of how exotic species colonize and persist in novel aquatic environments. The species represents a case study in conservation biology regarding the risks and outcomes of deliberate or accidental fish introductions into isolated water bodies.
