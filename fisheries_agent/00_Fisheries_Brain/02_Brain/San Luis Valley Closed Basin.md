# San Luis Valley Closed Basin

## Summary
The San Luis Valley Closed Basin is a hydrologically isolated drainage in Colorado that historically supported populations of both Rio Grande Chub and Rio Grande Sucker. This basin represents part of the historical range of these imperiled species before their significant declines across the broader Rio Grande region.
