# Gammaridae

## Summary
Gammaridae is a family of small freshwater and marine amphipods that includes important members of aquatic food webs and serves as a key indicator group for assessing habitat quality and biodiversity in aquatic ecosystems. Within this family, subterranean amphipods like the genus Stygonectes represent specialized evolutionary lineages adapted to cave and groundwater environments, making them valuable subjects for studying speciation, adaptation, and the conservation of unique subterranean fauna. The family's ecological significance and diversity across different habitat types, including cave systems, make Gammaridae relevant to both basic evolutionary research and applied conservation biology of groundwater and freshwater ecosystems.
