# Lyman Lake

## Summary
Lyman Lake is an Arizona reservoir that was included in a 1965–1966 visitor-use and fisheries survey conducted by the Arizona Game and Fish Department to assess recreational fishing pressure and fish population dynamics across multiple state impoundments. As part of broader Central Arizona Project investigations during the 1960s, Lyman Lake represented one of several study sites used to evaluate fishery management challenges, particularly those related to water-level fluctuations from irrigation demands and their effects on fish habitat and angler catch rates. The lake exemplifies the conservation and management concerns facing Arizona's reservoir fisheries during a period of competing demands between water resource development and recreational fish population sustainability.
