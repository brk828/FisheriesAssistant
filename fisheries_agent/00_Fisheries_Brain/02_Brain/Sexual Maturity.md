# Sexual Maturity

## Summary
Sexual maturity in the Colorado River striped bass population occurred remarkably early compared to other populations. Fifty percent of age-3 females had mature ovaries and were ready to spawn, and almost all age-4 females were mature, contrasting sharply with other populations where only 18.2-67.9% of age-4 females reach maturity.
