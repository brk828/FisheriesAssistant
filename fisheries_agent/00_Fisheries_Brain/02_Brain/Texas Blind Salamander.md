# Texas Blind Salamander

## Summary
The Texas Blind Salamander (Typhlomolge rathbuni, synonym Eurycea rathbuni) is one of numerous subterranean organisms for which the artesian well site is the type-locality. This blind, depigmented salamander shares the same Edwards Aquifer habitat with Haideoporus texanus.

## Context

> This study describes the larva of *[[Haideoporus texanus]]*, the first hypogeous (subterranean) dytiscid beetle discovered in North America.
