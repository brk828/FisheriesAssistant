# Roswell Fontelicella

## Summary
An undescribed species of hydrobiid snail (Fontelicella n. sp.) formerly occurring in South Spring and other Roswell-area springs in Chaves County, New Mexico, now dried from groundwater pumping. This source reports the single remaining population at Roswell Country Club Spring was significantly reduced after 1973-74 springhead modification but had stabilized at reduced levels by 1980, with threatened status recommended.
