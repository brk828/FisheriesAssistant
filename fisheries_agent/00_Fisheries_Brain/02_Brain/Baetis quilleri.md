# Baetis quilleri

## Summary
Baetis quilleri is a species of mayfly in the family Baetidae found in desert streams. This species was one of the dominant macroinvertebrates in Sycamore Creek during May 1983, contributing to the >90% of macroinvertebrate biomass represented by mayflies and chironomids, and serving as a preferred prey item for Longfin Dace when abundant.
