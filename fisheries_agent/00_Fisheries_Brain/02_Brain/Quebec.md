# Quebec

## Summary
Quebec is a Canadian province supporting 103 freshwater fish species according to this checklist. The document reports the Twospine Stickleback from Quebec (McAllister 1960) and notes that Quebec red trout (Salvelinus marstoni) was placed in synonymy with S. alpinus by McPhail in 1961.
