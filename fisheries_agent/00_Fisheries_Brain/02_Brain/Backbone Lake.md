# Backbone Lake

## Summary
Backbone Lake was a lake in Iowa where antimycin A was applied at 50 ppb to carp spawning sites ranging from 0.25 to 5 acres. Although a storm caused dilution of the treatment, the trial demonstrated the non-repellency characteristic of antimycin A to fish.
