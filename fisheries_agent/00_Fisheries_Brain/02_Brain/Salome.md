# Salome

## Summary
Salome is identified as one of the ten Arizona development projects in Maxwell's 1935 water conservation plan. Each project was designed to have its own 'Golden River' (local water source) and 'Treasure Valley' (irrigable land).

## Context

> This 1935 advance print document by George Hebard Maxwell presents an ambitious proposal for water conservation and land reclamation in Arizona and California through comprehensive development of the [[Colorado River]].
