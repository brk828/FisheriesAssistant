# Yellow Bass

## Summary
Yellow Bass (*Morone mississippiensis*) is a non-native percichthyid fish species in the family Moronidae that has invaded western aquatic ecosystems, particularly in Arizona's Salt River Project canal system and associated constructed lentic habitats. In the context of conservation biology for the Colorado River Basin, Yellow Bass represents an invasive predator threat to native endangered species such as Bonytail and Colorado Squawfish, as it competes for resources and preys upon native fauna in both natural and ex-situ refuge settings. Its presence in fish survey collections indicates successful establishment in atypical habitats far from its native eastern North American range, where it contributes to the ecological degradation of native fish communities.
