# Gempylidae

## Summary
Gempylidae is the fish family known as "escolars" that includes deep-water species like the oilfish. Prior to the 1942 oilfish record, this family was known in California only from a 1932 record of Xenogramma carinatum found at Long Beach, making oilfish only the second gempylid species recorded from California waters.
