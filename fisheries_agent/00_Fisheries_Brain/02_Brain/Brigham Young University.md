# Brigham Young University

## Summary
Brigham Young University is one of the collaborating institutions in the Gila Taxonomy Project. Researchers at this university conducted DNA analyses on Gila specimens, including RAPD (Random Amplified Polymorphic DNA) techniques and cytochrome-B mitochondrial DNA sequencing, with DNA extracted from both fresh and preserved museum material using cloning techniques combined with polymerase chain reaction.
