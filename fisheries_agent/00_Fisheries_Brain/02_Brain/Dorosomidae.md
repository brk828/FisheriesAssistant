# Dorosomidae

## Summary
Dorosomidae is a small family of mostly marine fishes closely related to herrings (Clupeidae), commonly known as gizzard shads. This source describes their distinguishing features including a specialized stomach structure, absence of lateral line, small inferior toothless mouth, strongly compressed belly with bony serrae forming a keel, and a filiform last dorsal fin ray similar to tarpons.
