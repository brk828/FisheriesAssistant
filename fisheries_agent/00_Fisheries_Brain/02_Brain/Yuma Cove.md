# Yuma Cove

## Summary
Yuma Cove is a managed backwater habitat within Lake Mohave in the Lower Colorado River system that has been specifically engineered and manipulated for larval and juvenile fish production, particularly for native fish species conservation efforts. The cove's management has involved physical modifications such as berm fortification and liner placement, vegetation management through macrophyte introduction, and periodic reclamation using rotenone treatment to control predatory and competing species, especially odonate larvae. Light trap sampling and monitoring protocols have been employed to track reproductive success and evaluate the cove's effectiveness as a controlled propagation environment for endangered or threatened fish species in the regulated Colorado River ecosystem.
