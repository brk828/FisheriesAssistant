# Rural Settlements System

## Summary
The Rural Settlements System was Maxwell's proposed model for agricultural settlement featuring small family farms of 5-10 acres for intensive diversified cultivation. Modeled after Mormon colonies and California's 'Littlelands Movement,' the system would create 'Forest Farm Estates' (20-acre forest tract plus 5-acre garden farm) and 'Homecroft Orchard Estates' (10-acre tracts with 9 acres orchard, 1 acre for family food), emphasizing 'Individual Independent Homes on the Land' for economic security and non-competitive tree crops.
