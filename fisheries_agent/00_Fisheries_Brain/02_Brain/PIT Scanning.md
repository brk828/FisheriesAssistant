# PIT Scanning

## Summary
PIT Scanning refers to the use of remote detection systems to monitor fish implanted with Passive Integrated Transponder (PIT) tags. In this study, five remote PIT scanning deployments were used to track stocked bonytail, with contact rates ranging from 5-68% of released fish depending on site, and most contacts occurring within the first 2 weeks post-release. PIT scanners were also deployed under bird roosting sites where they detected 14 bonytail tags, providing evidence of avian predation.
