# Carmen

## Summary
Carmen is a location in the Rio Carmen system, a former Rio Grande tributary, where Gila populations have 9,9-9,8 fin ray counts, modal vertebrae count of 42, and 68-80 lateral line scales. The populations show intermediate morphological characteristics between Colorado River and Rio Grande faunas.
