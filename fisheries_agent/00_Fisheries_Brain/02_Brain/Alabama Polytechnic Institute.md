# Alabama Polytechnic Institute

## Summary
Alabama Polytechnic Institute operated an Agricultural Experiment Station where preliminary experiments on winter feeding of fathead minnows were conducted. The research addressed production challenges in commercial baitfish aquaculture, specifically the problem of undersized fish failing to reach marketable size within one year.
