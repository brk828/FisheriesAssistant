# Yaqui Shiner

## Summary
The Yaqui Shiner is a small native fish species endemic to the Rio Yaqui drainage in southeastern Arizona, historically occurring in warm, shallow stream habitats with moderate current and sandy substrates. This species represents an important component of the native fish fauna in the Yaqui basin and has been subject to conservation assessment and recovery planning efforts alongside other endemic southwestern fishes threatened by habitat alteration and non-native species invasion. The Yaqui Shiner's ecological role and status within its limited range make it significant for understanding and managing the biodiversity of this arid region's aquatic ecosystems.
