# Sistan Lakes

## Summary
The Sistan Lakes are a system of variable-extent freshwater lakes located mostly in Iran. This source indicates they receive drainage from the Helmand River, which drains about 40% of Afghanistan, with the Hamun-e Helmand being the largest lake in the system.
