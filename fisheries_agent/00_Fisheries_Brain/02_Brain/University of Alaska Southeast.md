# University of Alaska Southeast

## Summary
The University of Alaska Southeast is an academic institution that collaborated on this research project. The university worked with other institutions to examine TDS effects on salmonid species and develop ion-specific regulatory approaches for protecting Alaska's freshwater fisheries from mining effluent impacts.
