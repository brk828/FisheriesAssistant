# Sanborn's Long-nosed Bat

## Summary
Sanborn's Long-nosed Bat is an endangered bat species found in the southwestern United States. According to this monitoring plan, it is one of approximately 24 bat species that occur in the San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area in Arizona.
