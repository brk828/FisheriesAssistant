# Chromis punctipinnis

## Summary
Chromis punctipinnis is the blacksmith, a member of the damselfish family (Pomacentridae) that commonly enters very shallow water. One vertebra from a blacksmith approximately 6 inches long was recovered from the site, representing the smallest individual fish in the entire assemblage.
