# Pine Mountain

## Summary
Pine Mountain is a geographic feature in the Dos S Unit where additional suitable Mexican Spotted Owl habitat exists north of the mountain. This habitat is located within or near Pine Creek Pasture, which is part of the proposed grazing management plan.
