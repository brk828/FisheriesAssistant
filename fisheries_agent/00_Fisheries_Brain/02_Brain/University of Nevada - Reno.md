# University of Nevada - Reno

## Summary
University of Nevada - Reno is the institution where Dr. Peter Brussard worked and planned to conduct electrophoretic analysis of Cui-ui samples to determine the presence of hybrids. However, his laboratory would not be re-assembled in time to complete the analysis by December 1, 1989, so he planned to use facilities at other universities instead.
