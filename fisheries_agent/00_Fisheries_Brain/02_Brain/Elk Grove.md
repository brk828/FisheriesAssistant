# Elk Grove

## Summary
Elk Grove is the location of the California Department of Fish and Game's Central Valley Hatchery. This hatchery supplied 3,200 fingerling smallmouth bass that were stocked in the Colorado River four miles downstream from Parker Dam in August 1950.
