# Antimycin A

## Summary
Antimycin A is an antibiotic-derived piscicide used in fisheries management to selectively remove non-native fish species from aquatic ecosystems, particularly in conservation efforts for endangered fish populations. As a chemical fish toxicant, it works by disrupting cellular respiration in fish while breaking down relatively quickly in the environment, making it a practical tool for habitat restoration projects like those undertaken to protect endangered species such as the Gila Topminnow in isolated spring and creek populations. Its use is carefully regulated and implemented as part of integrated management protocols to control invasive species while minimizing impacts on target native fish communities.
