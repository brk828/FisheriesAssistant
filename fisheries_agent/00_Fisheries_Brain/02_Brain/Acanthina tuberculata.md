# Acanthina tuberculata

## Summary
Acanthina tuberculata is a gastropod species found at Puerto Peñasco, Sonora, Mexico. This source documents it as part of the invertebrate fauna in the Gulf of California intertidal habitats, including rocky shores and tide pools.
