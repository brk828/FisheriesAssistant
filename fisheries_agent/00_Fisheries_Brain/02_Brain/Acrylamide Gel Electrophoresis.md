# Acrylamide Gel Electrophoresis

## Summary
Acrylamide Gel Electrophoresis is one of the advanced molecular techniques used in this study to analyze allozyme variation in desert pupfish populations. This method, along with starch gel electrophoresis and isoelectric focusing, enabled the survey of approximately 55 allozyme loci and revealed significant genetic polymorphism that earlier studies had missed.
