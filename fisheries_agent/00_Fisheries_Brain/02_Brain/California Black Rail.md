# California Black Rail

## Summary
The California Black Rail is a bird species managed by personnel at Cibola National Wildlife Refuge in Blythe, California. According to this administrative directory, it is one of the Colorado River desert bird species under the responsibility of Ronald McKinstry at the Phoenix Ecological Services Office.
