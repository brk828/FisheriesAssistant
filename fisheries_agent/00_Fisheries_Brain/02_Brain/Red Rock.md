# Red Rock

## Summary
Red Rock is a tributary of the Sonoita River in Arizona that supports populations of Gila Topminnow (Poeciliopsis occidentalis). It is one of several nearby water bodies in the Santa Cruz River region documented to contain this federally listed native fish species, providing regional context for the species' distribution in southern Arizona.
