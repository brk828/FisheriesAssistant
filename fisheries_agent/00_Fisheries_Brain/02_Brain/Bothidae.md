# Bothidae

## Summary
Bothidae is a family of flatfishes (order Pleuronectiformes) characterized by having both eyes located on the same side of the head, though typically on the left side, distinguishing them from the related Pleuronectidae (right-eyed flounders). Members of this family are primarily marine and estuarine inhabitants found in coastal waters worldwide, where they inhabit soft substrates and exhibit the typical flatfish morphology and benthic lifestyle. Bothidae represents an important component of coastal ichthyofaunal assemblages and fisheries resources, with several species of commercial and ecological significance in marine and brackish water ecosystems.
