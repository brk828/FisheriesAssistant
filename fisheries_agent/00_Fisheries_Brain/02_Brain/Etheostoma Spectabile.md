# Etheostoma Spectabile

## Summary
Etheostoma spectabile, commonly known as the Orangespotted Darter, is a small North American percid fish species that has served as an important model organism in comparative ichthyological studies examining geographic variation in reproductive traits and interspecific hybridization capacity among darters. Research on this species and related Etheostoma taxa has documented patterns in fecundity variation across populations and thermal environments, as well as demonstrated the widespread ability of darter species to produce viable hybrid offspring despite significant phylogenetic distances. The species' use in these fundamental studies has contributed significantly to understanding population-level adaptations, reproductive strategies, and taxonomic relationships within the diverse darter family.
