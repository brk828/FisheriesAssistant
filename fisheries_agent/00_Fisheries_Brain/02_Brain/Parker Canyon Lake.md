# Parker Canyon Lake

## Summary
Parker Canyon Lake is an Arizona reservoir constructed in 1963 that functions as a coldwater to coolwater transitional fishery system, historically managed as a put-and-take trout fishery but significantly altered by unauthorized warmwater species introductions such as bass that created competitive and predatory pressures on the native cold-water fish community. The lake has been a focal point for Arizona Game and Fish Department limnological surveys and visitor-use fisheries investigations since reaching spillway level in the mid-1960s, serving as an important case study for understanding human-mediated shifts in reservoir fish community composition and the management challenges of maintaining coldwater fisheries in the face of invasive species establishment.
