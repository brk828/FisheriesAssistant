# Wahweap Marina

## Summary
Wahweap Marina was the primary creel census location in the lower Lake Powell reservoir, providing the only access point to that area during the study period. The marina received 44.7% of all angler trips, with census operations documenting 5,804 trips producing 17,529 fish in 30,531 hours at an average success rate of 0.57 fish per hour.
