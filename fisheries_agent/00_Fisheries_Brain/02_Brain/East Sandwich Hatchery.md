# East Sandwich Hatchery

## Summary
East Sandwich Hatchery was the source of trout that showed dramatically poor survival when stocked early in the season, with only 1.7% recovery compared to 7.7% from other state hatcheries in comparative trials. The study found that early-stocked fish from this hatchery originated from semi-hibernating broodstock in low winter water temperatures, resulting in weakened condition and high mortality from handling stress when planted in February versus April.
