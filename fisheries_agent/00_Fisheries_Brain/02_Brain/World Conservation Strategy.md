# World Conservation Strategy

## Summary
The World Conservation Strategy (1980) is a foundational document that provides part of the guiding philosophy for SSC's work. It established three key principles that guide the Commission: conservation of biological diversity, preservation of life-support systems, and sustainable use of living natural resources. This strategy was succeeded by the document 'Caring for the Earth' (1991), whose principles SSC aims to implement.

## Context

> This document is a comprehensive member's guide for the [[IUCN]] [[Species Survival Commission]] (SSC) covering the 1994-1996 triennium. The SSC is described as the largest global network of volunteer experts working for species conservation, comprising approximately 5,000 members in 170 countries....
