# Imperial Dam

## Summary
Imperial Dam is a water control structure on the Colorado River that creates a reservoir and regulates water flow, serving as a critical reference point in fisheries management and conservation efforts for native and sport fish species in the lower Colorado River system. The dam's operations, including water level fluctuations and flow management, significantly influence fish habitat conditions, species distribution, and population dynamics downstream, making it a key consideration in conservation strategies for endangered species such as razorback suckers and in the management of sportfish stocking programs in the region.
