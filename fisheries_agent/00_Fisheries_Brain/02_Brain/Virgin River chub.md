# Virgin River Chub

## Summary
The Virgin River Chub is a small, native cyprinid fish endemic to the Virgin River in the southwestern United States that has become a focal species in conservation and recovery efforts due to significant population declines resulting from habitat alteration, dam construction, water diversion, and competition from introduced nonnative fishes. As one of the primary endangered native fishes of the Lower Colorado River system, the Virgin River Chub has been subject to various management and restoration initiatives, including habitat conservation planning and potential captive propagation programs. Its recovery represents a key conservation priority in Sonoran Desert aquatic ecosystems and is typically addressed alongside other endemic Virgin River species such as the Woundfin in comprehensive river management and endangered species recovery plans.
