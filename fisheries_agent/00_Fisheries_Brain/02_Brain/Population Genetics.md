# Population Genetics

## Summary
Population genetics in fish science examines the genetic variation and evolutionary processes within and between fish populations, including allele frequencies, genetic diversity, and gene flow. This field is critical for understanding population structure, evolutionary adaptation, and the impacts of isolation or connectivity on wild fish stocks. In conservation and fisheries management, population genetic analyses inform strategies for maintaining genetic diversity, assessing population viability, and making decisions about species reintroduction, hatchery breeding programs, and the designation of distinct management units.
