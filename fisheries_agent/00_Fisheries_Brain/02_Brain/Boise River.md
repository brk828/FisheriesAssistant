# Boise River

## Summary
The Boise River is a river in southwestern Idaho that serves as the water source for Lake Lowell reservoir. Water from the Boise River, with dissolved solids of 40-88 ppm, is transported to Lake Lowell via the concrete-lined New York Canal, where dissolved solids increase to an average of 310 ppm in the reservoir.
