# Fort Verde State Historic Park

## Summary
Fort Verde State Historic Park is a protected historic site located within the Verde Valley, Arizona. According to this 1990-1991 survey, it is one of multiple protected areas in the Verde River corridor region, and the survey found strong support for protecting significant historic and archaeological resources in the valley (78.7% of residents, 58.4% of leaders).
