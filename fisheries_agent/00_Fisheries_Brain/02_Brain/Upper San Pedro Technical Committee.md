# Upper San Pedro Technical Committee

## Summary
The Upper San Pedro Technical Committee is a group of hydrological experts that met in Sierra Vista on October 28, 1993. At this meeting, the committee reached three consensus points about groundwater pumping's negative impacts on the San Pedro River, including that the river would not survive current pumping levels without aggressive conservation measures and that Arizona water law does not reflect hydrological reality.
