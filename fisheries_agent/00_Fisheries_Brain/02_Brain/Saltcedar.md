# Saltcedar

## Summary
Saltcedar (Tamarix ramossissima) is a riparian plant species that has become well-established along the Colorado River below Glen Canyon Dam. Following dam closure in 1963, saltcedar thrived in areas where it previously existed in small numbers, apparently responding to optimal daily inundation during critical seedling periods, though the 1983 flood eliminated approximately 50% of riparian vegetation including saltcedar.
