# Lake Manly

## Summary
Lake Manly was a large Pleistocene lake system that existed in the Death Valley region during wetter pluvial periods. This ancient interconnected lake system facilitated the dispersal of native fishes throughout the region, and its subsequent drying and fragmentation during aridification isolated fish populations in remnant springs and streams, driving speciation and local adaptation that shaped current fish distributions.
