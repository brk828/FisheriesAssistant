# Great Deserts of the World

## Summary
Great Deserts of the World (GDOW) was a major multi-year capital initiative in The Phoenix Zoo's strategic plan to create comprehensive desert ecology presentations. The project included a preorganizer phase ($350,000 in Year 3, 1996-97) followed by construction and opening in Years 4-5 (1997-1999) with approximately $2,000,000 investment, as part of the Desert Trail conveying the message 'I can adapt.'
