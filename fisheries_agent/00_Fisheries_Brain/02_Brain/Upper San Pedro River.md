# Upper San Pedro River

## Summary
The Upper San Pedro River is a desert aquatic habitat in Arizona that was the focus of conservation and survey efforts. In 1986, an ichthyology class conducted field surveys of fishes in this area, and it was mentioned in connection with a BLM exchange area relevant to desert fish recovery planning.
