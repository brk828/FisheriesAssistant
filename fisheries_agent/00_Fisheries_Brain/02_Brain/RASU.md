# RASU

## Summary
RASU is the abbreviation for Razorback Sucker (Xyrauchen texanus), a native catostomid fish species targeted for conservation in the Lower Colorado River. This source describes removing RASU individuals from Pond 1 during pre-renovation sampling in spring and autumn 2014, with population estimates generated between sampling periods, before the ponds underwent rotenone treatment to eliminate nonnative fish and prepare the habitat for reintroducing recruiting populations of this species.
