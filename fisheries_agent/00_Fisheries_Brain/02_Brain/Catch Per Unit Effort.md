# Catch Per Unit Effort

## Summary
Catch Per Unit Effort (CPUE) is a standardized fisheries metric that quantifies the number or biomass of fish captured per unit of fishing activity, such as per hour of angling, per net-night, or per trap deployment. This measure serves as a practical index of fish population abundance and availability, enabling researchers and managers to assess population trends, evaluate fishing pressure, and compare fishing success across different times, locations, or fishing methods without requiring absolute population counts. CPUE is particularly valuable in mark-recapture studies and creel surveys as it provides consistent data for estimating population parameters and monitoring the effectiveness of fishery management actions like fingerling stocking programs.
