# San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area

## Summary
The San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area encompasses the San Pedro River and its riparian zone in southeastern Arizona near Fort Huachuca, recognized as one of the Southwest's few remaining relatively intact aquatic ecosystems and designated as critical habitat requiring specialized management and monitoring. This conservation area was established with particular focus on protecting native fish populations and aquatic biodiversity threatened by groundwater pumping and other anthropogenic stressors, with management guided by comprehensive riparian management plans and oversight by technical committees of fisheries and conservation experts. The area represents a key example of federal efforts to preserve southwestern riparian corridors and their associated ichthyofauna through coordinated biological resource management between multiple agencies including the BLM, USFWS, and Department of Defense.
