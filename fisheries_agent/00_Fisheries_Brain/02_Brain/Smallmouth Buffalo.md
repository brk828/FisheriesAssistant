# Smallmouth Buffalo

## Summary
Smallmouth buffalo (Ictiobus bubalus) is a large, native North American cyprinid fish species that inhabits freshwater systems and plays an important ecological role as a benthic filter-feeder consuming zooplankton and detritus. This species is of interest in fisheries science and conservation contexts, particularly regarding its interactions with introduced species, reproductive dynamics in complex aquatic communities, and its potential status as both a native resource and competitor in altered ecosystems. Understanding smallmouth buffalo ecology and population dynamics is relevant to broader questions about native species persistence and community restructuring in freshwater habitats experiencing biological invasions.
