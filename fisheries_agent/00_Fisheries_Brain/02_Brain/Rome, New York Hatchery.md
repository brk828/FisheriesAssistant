# Rome, New York Hatchery

## Summary
Rome, New York Hatchery was a facility where brown trout carrying capacity experiments were conducted. This source uses data from this hatchery as an example, noting that 1-inch brown trout at 53°F were carried at 1.75 lb/ft³ in troughs and fed at 7.6% body weight.

## Context

> This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service circular (Circular 159, October 1963, with April 1964 correction) provides comprehensive guidance on feeding protocols and nutritional management for hatchery-reared trout.
