# Endangered Freshwater Fish Conservation

## Summary
Endangered Freshwater Fish Conservation encompasses the scientific and management strategies employed to prevent the extinction of threatened fish species inhabiting inland aquatic ecosystems through habitat restoration, captive breeding programs, population monitoring, and regulatory protection. This interdisciplinary approach integrates ichthyological research, ecological assessment, and resource management to address the specific threats facing vulnerable freshwater species, particularly endemic fish populations like those in the Colorado River basin and isolated desert systems. The field combines basic biological research with applied conservation practices to maintain genetic diversity, restore suitable habitats, and establish viable wild populations of species at risk of disappearing.
