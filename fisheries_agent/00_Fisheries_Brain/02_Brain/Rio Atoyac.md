# Rio Atoyac

## Summary
Rio Atoyac is a river that the Rio Cosala enters downstream from the collection site of Asellus puebla. The Rio Atoyac flows through the Puebla Plateau and eventually bends south and west to join the Rio Balsas, which drains to the Pacific Ocean.
