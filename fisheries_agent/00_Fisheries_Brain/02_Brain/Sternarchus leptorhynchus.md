# Sternarchus leptorhynchus

## Summary
Sternarchus leptorhynchus is an electric knifefish previously known only from the Essequibo Basin of British Guiana, for which this study documents a significant range extension to Pacific slope Colombia. Two specimens were collected: one (130 mm) from a creek near the mouth of the Calima River and one (209 mm) from Cordova in the Dagua River.
