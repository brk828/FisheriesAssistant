# Upper Horrell Springs

## Summary
Upper Horrell Springs consists of several small hillside seeps and two moderate-sized springs (modified with springboxes for domestic water supply) located on the east side of Campaign Creek in Gila County, Arizona. These springs provide the primary water source for the 1-2 mile perennial section of Campaign Creek and were stocked with Gila topminnow in 1983 as part of species recovery efforts.
