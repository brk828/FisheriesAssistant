# UNLV Living Stream

## Summary
UNLV Living Stream is a university-based facility for holding and researching Virgin River native fish species. The source reports it was rehabilitated and operational for 2 months as of September 1992, contained no native species at that time, and 50 Woundfin were requested from salvage operations to stock the facility.
