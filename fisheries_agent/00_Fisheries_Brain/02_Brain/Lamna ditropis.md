# Lamna ditropis

## Summary
Lamna ditropis (salmon shark) is a mackerel shark represented at the site by 3 teeth. This species is commonly pelagic but occasionally enters shallow water, and individuals at the site were estimated at 100-200 pounds and were much more probably caught from boats than from shore.
