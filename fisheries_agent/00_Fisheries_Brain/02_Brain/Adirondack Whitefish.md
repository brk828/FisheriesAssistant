# Adirondack Whitefish

## Summary
The Adirondack Whitefish is a form of Coregonus clupeaformis that represents the most commonly distributed and caught whitefish in New York waters. It is a bottom-feeding fall spawner found in deep, well-aerated lakes, valued as a food fish with an international reputation for flavor. This whitefish has been successfully propagated artificially since 1858 and occurs in at least 48 New York lakes including Lake Erie and Lake Ontario.
