# Independence Strain

## Summary
The Independence Strain is a hatchery strain of Lahontan Cutthroat Trout raised at Lahontan National Fish Hatchery. In the 1987 spawning run, 12 of 19 sacrificed tagged cutthroat trout were identified as Independence Strain fish that had been stocked in Pyramid Lake during 1982-1983.
