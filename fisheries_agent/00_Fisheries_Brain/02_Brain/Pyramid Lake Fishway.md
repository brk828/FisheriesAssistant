# Pyramid Lake Fishway

## Summary
The Pyramid Lake Fishway is an infrastructure facility designed to facilitate the spawning migration of cui-ui (Chaoborus cuspidatus), an endemic fish species in Nevada's Pyramid Lake, by enabling movement between the lake and spawning habitat in the lower Truckee River. As a critical conservation tool for this potamodromous species, the fishway supports the cui-ui's life history requirement to migrate from their adult lake habitat into riverine conditions for successful reproduction. The implementation and maintenance of this fishway has been integral to the species' recovery efforts, contributing to the dramatic population increases observed in the early 1990s by enabling access to spawning grounds during periods of adequate river flow.
