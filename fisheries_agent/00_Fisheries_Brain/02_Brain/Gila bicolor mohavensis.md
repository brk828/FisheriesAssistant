# Gila bicolor mohavensis

## Summary
Gila bicolor mohavensis, commonly known as the Mojave chub, is a desert fish species that was among the taxonomic groups discussed at the 1981 Desert Fishes Council symposium. This subspecies was included in presentations addressing the status, ecology, and management of desert fishes across the southwestern United States.
