# Short-nosed Gar

## Summary
Short-nosed Gar (family Lepisosteidae) is found mostly in the Kaw and Osage rivers in Kansas, with a beak approximately 5 times as long as wide. Like the long-nosed gar, it has sharp-edged scales and beaks with sharp teeth that make it destructive to game fishes and fishing gear.
