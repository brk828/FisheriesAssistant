# Public Trust Doctrine

## Summary
The Public Trust Doctrine is a legal principle requiring government to manage public resources (including rivers, fish, and wildlife) for the greatest public good. This source identifies it as one of the available legal tools for fish conservation in California and notes it was successfully applied in the preservation of Mono Lake, though overall legal tools remain insufficient to address the biodiversity crisis.
