# Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia

## Summary
The Academy of Natural Sciences of Philadelphia is a scientific institution that received paratype specimens of Laccocythere aotearoa. Additional type material including the holotype and allotype were deposited at the Smithsonian Institution.
