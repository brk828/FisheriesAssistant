# Dorosoma

## Summary
Dorosoma is a genus of gizzard shads (family Dorosomidae) for which this source documents two species occurring sympatrically near Belize. The study describes one potentially undescribed subspecies distinguished from other Dorosoma by having 36 anal rays (versus 30-33 in Dorosoma cepedianum), a longer body (depth 2.9 in standard length), and longer head (4 in standard length), with possible affinities to Dorosoma cepedianum exile from Texas.
