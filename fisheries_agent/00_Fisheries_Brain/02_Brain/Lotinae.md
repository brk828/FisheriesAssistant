# Lotinae

## Summary
Lotinae is a subfamily of Gadidae (codfishes) characterized by relatively small optic lobes (often smaller than the corpora striata), cerebellum typically resting on the medulla oblongata, and less divided unpaired fins. Members predominantly inhabit deeper waters or lead sedentary coastal lives, with brain structure reflecting reduced visual dependence and limited mobility compared to other gadid subfamilies.
