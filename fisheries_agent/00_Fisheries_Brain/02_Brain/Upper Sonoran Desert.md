# Upper Sonoran Desert

## Summary
The Upper Sonoran Desert is a biological community type found at lower elevations (less than 3,000 feet) in the Sierra Ancha region of Arizona. This habitat type was one of several diverse ecosystems accessible within 10 miles of the Sierra Ancha Field Station, providing students opportunities to study ecological gradients from desert to montane environments.
