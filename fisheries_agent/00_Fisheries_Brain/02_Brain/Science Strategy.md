# Science Strategy

## Summary
The Science Strategy is a component of the Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program's adaptive management framework. At Imperial Ponds, it guides post-development monitoring over multiple years, with analyses informing potential site improvements and future project design decisions.
