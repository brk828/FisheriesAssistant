# Spring Pike Index Netting

## Summary
Spring Pike Index Netting (SPIN) is an annual monitoring survey conducted in May to assess Northern Pike population status relative to abundance targets in Box Canyon Reservoir. The 2022 SPIN survey deployed 63 nets and yielded catch rates of 0.10 pike/net-night north of Riverbend (target ≤0.50) and 0.26 pike/net-night south of Riverbend (target ≤1.73), with both areas meeting management targets.
