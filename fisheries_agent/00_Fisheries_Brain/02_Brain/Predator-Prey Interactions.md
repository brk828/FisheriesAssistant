# Predator-Prey Interactions

## Summary
Smallmouth bass in Arizona's upper Salt River system prey heavily on native minnows and suckers (Gila, Pantosteus, Catostomus), with juvenile bass observed repeatedly attacking feeding aggregations of young native fish. This predation on newly-hatched native fish larvae has totally suppressed reproductive success of local native species, with bass stomachs found distended with tiny fish larvae even as they continued feeding.
