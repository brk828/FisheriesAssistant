# Bass Creek

## Summary
Bass Creek is a perennial desert stream in the Gila River drainage of Arizona, containing approximately 6.1 km of surface water with a well-developed riparian zone featuring large trees, cobble and small boulder substrate, and pools formed by deflection around riparian vegetation. This source documents that Bass Creek supports five native fish species (Longfin Dace, Speckled Dace, Gila Chub, Sonoran Sucker, and Desert Sucker), with Gila Chub restricted to a 6 km section from the pipeline crossing upstream to Pine Creek, and fish access to the upper watershed blocked by a 1 km stretch of deep alluvium that only flows during flash floods.
