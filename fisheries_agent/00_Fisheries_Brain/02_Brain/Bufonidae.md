# Bufonidae

## Summary
Bufonidae (toads) is one of two possible families to which three amphibian specimens (long bones lacking epiphyses) recovered from the Tubac Presidio may belong, the other being Ranidae (frogs). These specimens were distributed across Middle and Late periods and may be intrusive rather than cultural deposits.
