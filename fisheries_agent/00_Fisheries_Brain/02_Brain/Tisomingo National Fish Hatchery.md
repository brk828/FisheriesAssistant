# Tisomingo National Fish Hatchery

## Summary
Tisomingo National Fish Hatchery is a federal fish hatchery located in Oklahoma that was the source of fish shipments to Topock Marsh in 1977. The hatchery inadvertently included at least seven non-target fish species, bullfrog tadpoles, and crayfish in shipments intended for Channel Catfish and hybrid sunfish, with hatchery workers acknowledging they were "hurried in the capturing, holding, and loading of the requested fish." Following the contamination incidents, the hatchery manager was contacted about the error and other managers were instructed to ensure only requested fish leave the facility.
