# Coachella Valley Fringe-Toed Lizard

## Summary
The Coachella Valley Fringe-Toed Lizard (Uma inornata) is a desert-dune endemic species found in the southwestern United States with specialized morphological adaptations including toe fringes that function like snowshoes for locomotion on loose sand. This species is listed as both federally threatened and California threatened due to significant habitat loss from threats including cropland conversion, pesticides, urbanization, and off-road vehicles.
