# Austria

## Summary
Austria is part of the Alpine region where 14 lakes (combined with Switzerland) were examined for metalimnetic oxygen maxima. Austrian Alpine lakes, like Swiss lakes, showed highest oxygen values in the largest lakes (contrary to US patterns) and exhibited greater variability in relative depth (coefficient of variation 75%) compared to US lakes. The lakes' oxygen maxima were more influenced by edaphic stability than morphometric characteristics.
