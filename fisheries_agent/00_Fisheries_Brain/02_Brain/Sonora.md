# Sonora

## Summary
Sonora refers to a Mexican state located in northwestern Mexico along the Pacific coast and the U.S.-Mexico border, recognized as an important region for ichthyological research and aquatic biodiversity conservation. Within fish science and conservation biology, Sonora is particularly significant for its diverse freshwater fish fauna, including endemic species found in its arid and semi-arid thorn forest ecosystems, as well as its coastal and estuarine environments that support economically important fisheries. The state's institutions, such as IMADES, have been instrumental in conducting systematic inventories, species identification, and conservation efforts aimed at protecting both living fish fauna and understanding paleontological records of extinct freshwater fish species from the region.
