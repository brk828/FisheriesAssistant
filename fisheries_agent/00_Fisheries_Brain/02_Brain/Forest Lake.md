# Forest Lake

## Summary
Forest Lake is a 32.5-acre high-elevation reservoir lake located on Grand Mesa in Delta County, Colorado, at approximately 10,000 feet elevation with a maximum depth of 55 feet. This study (1952-1954) found it supported populations of Rainbow Trout, Brook Trout, and Native Trout, with an overall catch-per-man-hour of 0.78 from 1,310 fisherman trips yielding 3,793 fish. The lake exhibited thermal stratification in summer and evidence of periodic winter oxygen depletion (1.95 ppm in March 1954), with limited spawning habitat and a bottom-type outlet used for irrigation that typically causes significant drawdown by late August.
