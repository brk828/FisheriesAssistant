# Salt Trail Canyon

## Summary
Salt Trail Canyon is a tributary canyon that enters the Little Colorado River approximately 10 km upstream from its confluence with the mainstem Colorado River in Grand Canyon, Arizona. This source reports that one of two research base camps was established at the mouth of Salt Trail Canyon during the 1989 humpback chub monitoring study, and that two tagged humpback chub moved downstream from this location to the confluence area (10 km distance) in 5 and 7 days respectively.

## Context

> This report presents the third year of monitoring the endangered [[Humpback Chub]] (*Gila cypha*) population in the [[Little Colorado River]], Arizona, during May 1989.
