# Amador County

## Summary
Amador County is a California county containing Salt Springs Reservoir. This source identifies Amador County as the location of Salt Springs Reservoir, which received the first experimental Kokanee planting in California in 1941.

## Context

> This 1948 report documents the introduction and establishment of [[Kokanee]] (Oncorhynchus nerka kennerlyi), a landlocked form of [[Pacific Sockeye Salmon]], into California waters beginning in 1941.
