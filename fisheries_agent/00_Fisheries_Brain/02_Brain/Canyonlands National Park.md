# Canyonlands National Park

## Summary
Canyonlands National Park is a protected terrestrial landscape in Utah that encompasses critical aquatic ecosystems, including the Colorado River and its tributaries, which support native fish species and serve as important study areas for understanding riverine ecology and fish conservation in arid southwestern environments. The park's designation and management have been relevant to discussions regarding fish introductions, species management, and the balance between recreational access and the preservation of native aquatic biodiversity within these canyon riverine systems.
