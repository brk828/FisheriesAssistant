# Caddo Lake

## Summary
Caddo Lake is a natural freshwater ecosystem located on the Texas-Louisiana border that represents an important aquatic habitat for fish species research and conservation, particularly notable for studies of cyprinid fishes including minnows and shiners endemic to or characteristic of East Texas blackwater systems. As a cypress swamp-dominated lake with unique hydrological and vegetational characteristics, Caddo Lake serves as a significant study site for documenting fish biodiversity, species distributions, and the ecological relationships within acidic, tannin-stained waters typical of southeastern U.S. wetland ecosystems. The lake's ecological significance makes it relevant for understanding fish community composition and conservation strategies for native aquatic fauna in this region.
