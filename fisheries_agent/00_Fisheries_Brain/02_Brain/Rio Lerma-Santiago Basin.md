# Rio Lerma-Santiago Basin

## Summary
The Rio Lerma-Santiago Basin is the most significant center of endemism for freshwater fishes in México, with 58% overall endemism that increases to 66% when considering only the Lerma Basin above Salto de Juanacatlan. This basin is located in the isolated tropical highland Mesa Central region, and its geographic isolation has been particularly important for fish diversification.
