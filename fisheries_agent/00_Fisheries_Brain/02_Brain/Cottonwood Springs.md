# Cottonwood Springs

## Summary
Cottonwood Springs is a spring in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, that supports populations of Monkey Spring Fontelicella along with Monkey Spring. The springs are on private land with protective owners and have been fenced, maintaining stable mollusk populations since 1969.
