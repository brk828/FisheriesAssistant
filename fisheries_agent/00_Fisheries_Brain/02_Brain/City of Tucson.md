# City of Tucson

## Summary
The City of Tucson was identified by Grant Ward of the Central Arizona Water Conservation District as one of several entities involved in recharge projects with potential fish and wildlife enhancement opportunities. The city was part of discussions about recharge projects in the Tucson area that could incorporate riparian and wetland components.
