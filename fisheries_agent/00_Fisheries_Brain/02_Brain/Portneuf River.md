# Portneuf River

## Summary
The Portneuf River is a river in Idaho whose water chemistry is comparable to Snake River water used in fluoride toxicity bioassays. Laboratory tests using water with chemistry similar to the Portneuf River at 55°F determined that the 96-hour median tolerance limit for rainbow trout fingerlings exposed to sodium fluoride was 160-220 ppm.
