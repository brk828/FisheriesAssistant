# Arland Hotchkiss

## Summary
Arland Hotchkiss was the Principal Investigator of NSF Research Grant G-19449 conducting cytotaxonomic investigations of the Characeae (stoneworts) at the University of Louisville. He led extensive field collections across the United States and Mexico and supervised graduate students in chromosome counting and ecological studies of freshwater algae.
