# Flood Tolerance

## Summary
Native Colorado River fishes are adapted to extreme discharge variability (0.5 to 7,100 m³/s at Yuma pre-dam) by moving to the surface along shore during floods, avoiding channel turbulence and bedload. Flooding rarely harms native populations and instead removes nonnative predators and competitors while natives persist, often with enhanced recruitment following flood events.
