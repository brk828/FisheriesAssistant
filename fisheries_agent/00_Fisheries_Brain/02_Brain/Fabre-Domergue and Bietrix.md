# Fabre-Domergue and Bietrix

## Summary
Fabre-Domergue and Bietrix (1905) conducted an early successful larval rearing study. They reared sole (Solea solea) to 55-76 mm length over 8 months using Dunaliella phytoplankton and larval fishes as prey.
