# 1948 Upper Colorado River Compact

## Summary
The 1948 Upper Colorado River Compact is an interstate water allocation agreement that allocates water among states in the Upper Colorado River Basin. This compact, along with the 1922 Colorado River Compact, creates legal constraints on how water can be managed for endangered species recovery, as recovery programs must work within existing interstate water allocation frameworks.
