# Leonard Canyon

## Summary
Leonard Canyon is a tributary of East Clear Creek in Coconino County, Arizona, and is part of the Little Colorado River drainage system. According to this 1985 proposal, Leonard Canyon at Dines tank was the only tributary of East Clear Creek known to harbor Little Colorado spinedace populations at that time.

## Context
