# Guadalupe Mountains

## Summary
The Guadalupe Mountains are a mountain range in southeastern New Mexico whose uplift significantly altered drainage patterns. This source documents that uplift of the Guadalupe Mountains during late Tertiary to middle Pleistocene time "beheaded" the ancestral Pecos River in the area of Sierra Blanca, fundamentally changing the river's course and fish fauna.
