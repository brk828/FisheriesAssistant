# Silverhill-Montosa Watershed

## Summary
Silverhill-Montosa Watershed is a 26.6 square mile watershed on the Cibola National Forest near Magdalena, New Mexico. This source documents that its gully system expanded dramatically from 16 miles in 1936 to 84 miles in 1968 (a 425% increase over 27 years), with no new gullies forming but existing ones expanding.
