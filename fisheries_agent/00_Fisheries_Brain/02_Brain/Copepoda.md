# Copepoda

## Summary
Copepoda are small crustacean zooplankton that form a crucial component of freshwater and marine ecosystems, serving as a primary food source for planktivorous fish and playing a vital role in aquatic food webs and nutrient cycling. In ichthyology and fisheries science, copepods are significant both as natural prey organisms that support fish populations and as potential vectors for parasitic infections in cultured and wild fish species. Their seasonal abundance and species composition in lakes and reservoirs make them important indicators of aquatic ecosystem health and productivity, with monitoring programs like those conducted in Kansas lakes providing essential data for understanding fish population dynamics and environmental conditions.
