# El Doctor

## Summary
El Doctor is a wetland area named after a small settlement on the northeastern Colorado River Delta, covering approximately 500-700 hectares. This source reports that it is sustained by artesian springs (pozos) with lower salinity levels, which results in greater plant species diversity compared to other delta wetlands. The wetlands have remained fairly stable over two decades, though they are subject to overgrazing by local cattle.
