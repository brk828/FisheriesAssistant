# Golden Trout

## Summary
Golden Trout (Oncorhynchus aguabonita) is a high-elevation salmonid species native to California's Sierra Nevada mountains, characterized by distinctive coloration and relatively slow growth rates compared to other trout species. In fisheries management and conservation contexts, Golden Trout serve as an important indicator species for alpine aquatic ecosystem health and are frequently used in stocking programs alongside Rainbow Trout and other salmonids to maintain populations in their native habitat and high-elevation lakes. Their growth patterns, including delayed scale annuli formation in some populations, have made them subjects of life history studies that inform broader understanding of salmonid biology and adaptive strategies in cold, oligotrophic mountain environments.
