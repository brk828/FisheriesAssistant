# Soil Conservation Service

## Summary
The Soil Conservation Service is a federal agency that, along with FERC, the Bureau of Reclamation, and the Army Corps of Engineers, manages water resources and manipulates natural river flows. According to this source, these federal agencies collectively manage 40% of water resources in the western United States and control the flow of hundreds of rivers in the East.
