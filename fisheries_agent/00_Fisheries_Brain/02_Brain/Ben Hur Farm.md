# Ben Hur Farm

## Summary
Ben Hur Farm is an agricultural research facility located in East Baton Rouge Parish, Louisiana, USA. In this 1967 study, it served as the site for nine experimental earthen ponds (ranging from 0.10 to 0.25 acres) used to test crawfish waste as supplemental feed for channel catfish, with water supplied from a 2,400-foot deep artesian well.
