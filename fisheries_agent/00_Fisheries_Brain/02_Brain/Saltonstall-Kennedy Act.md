# Saltonstall-Kennedy Act

## Summary
The Saltonstall-Kennedy Act was the federal legislation under which this channel catfish spawning research was conducted. It provided funding for fisheries research at the U.S. Fish Cultural Station in Tishomingo, Oklahoma during the 1950s.
