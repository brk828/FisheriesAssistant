# Lepidomeda altivalis

## Summary
Lepidomeda altivalis is the Pahranagat spinedace, a cyprinid fish species that may already be extinct. This source identifies it as a species in Pahranagat Valley, Nevada, directly threatened by the proposed MX Missile System.

## Context

> This December 1979 urgent letter from the [[Desert Fishes Council]] to NANFA members warns of catastrophic threats to desert aquatic ecosystems in [[Nevada]] and [[Utah]] from the proposed [[MX Missile System]].
