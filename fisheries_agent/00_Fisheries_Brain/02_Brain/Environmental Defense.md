# Environmental Defense

## Summary
Environmental Defense is one of the partner organizations in the Upper Colorado River Endangered Fish Recovery Program, which was established in 1988. The program is a collaborative partnership working to recover four endangered fish species while allowing continued water development in the Colorado River Basin.
