# Gila Box Riparian National Conservation Area

## Summary
The Gila Box Riparian National Conservation Area is a 20,000-acre protected riparian corridor managed by the Bureau of Land Management in southeastern Arizona, encompassing 23 miles of the Gila River and 15 miles of Bonita Creek northeast of Safford. Established under the Arizona Desert Wilderness Act of 1990, this conservation area was designed to protect and manage the unique freshwater lotic (flowing-water) habitat and associated riparian ecosystem that support native fish species and aquatic biodiversity in the arid southwestern region. The area's management emphasizes conservation biology principles through advisory committee oversight and frameworks such as Limits of Acceptable Change to balance ecological protection with sustainable use of these critical aquatic and riparian resources.
