# Seriola dorsalis

## Summary
Seriola dorsalis (yellowtail) is represented at the site by 3 vertebrae, with the largest from a fish about 32 inches and perhaps 12 pounds. This offshore species ordinarily occurs offshore but is not strictly pelagic, and was probably taken most frequently from boats but may have been caught occasionally from shore.
