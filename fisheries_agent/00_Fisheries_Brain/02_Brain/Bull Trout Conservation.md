# Bull Trout Conservation

## Summary
Bull Trout (Salvelinus confluentus) conservation in Box Canyon Reservoir was threatened by invasive Northern Pike, though Bull Trout rarely appeared in Northern Pike diets and accounted for <0.1% of bycatch during suppression operations. The Northern Pike invasion posed risks to Bull Trout recovery efforts and the substantial investments in habitat restoration and conservation programs for this native salmonid species.
