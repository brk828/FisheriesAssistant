# Totuava

## Summary
Totuava is a fish species subject to an eight-year fishing ban that was widely ignored according to this source. One fisherman reported that five years prior to 1986, during a particularly good year, truckloads of totuava weighing 15-80 kg each were leaving El Golfo daily, indicating significant illegal harvest despite conservation measures.
