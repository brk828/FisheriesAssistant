# Alpine Ranger District

## Summary
The Alpine Ranger District is an administrative unit of the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forests where the proposed Navopache Power powerline rerouting project is located. The district manages the upper Blue River area where the powerline crossing occurs.

## Context

> This biological opinion, issued March 24, 1997, evaluates the proposed rerouting of an existing [[Navopache Power]] powerline crossing the [[Blue River]] in [[Greenlee County]], [[Arizona]].
