# Reeve M. Bailey

## Summary
Reeve M. Bailey was a prominent ichthyologist at the University of Michigan Museum of Zoology who made significant contributions to North American fish taxonomy and systematic biology during the mid-20th century, particularly through his work on freshwater fish identification and regional faunal surveys. His scholarly reviews and comprehensive monographs, such as the fishes of South Dakota, helped advance the field of systematic ichthyology and served as important reference works for fish identification and regional biodiversity documentation. Bailey's work exemplified the landmark developments in ichthyological science during this period and influenced the study and classification of freshwater fish communities.
