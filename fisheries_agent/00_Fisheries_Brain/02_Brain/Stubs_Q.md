### Summary
Quitobaquito Springs is a critical desert spring ecosystem located in southwestern Arizona that serves as the primary habitat for the endemic pupfish species Cyprinodon maculatus eremus (Quitobaquito pupfish), making it a focal point for conservation efforts and aquatic biodiversity preservation in arid regions. This isolated spring system represents a vulnerable relict habitat where endemic fish populations depend entirely on the maintenance of hydrological conditions and protection from introduced species that threaten their survival. The springs exemplify the conservation challenges inherent to desert aquatic ecosystems where small, geographically isolated populations face extinction risk from environmental disturbance, groundwater depletion, and anthropogenic impacts.
