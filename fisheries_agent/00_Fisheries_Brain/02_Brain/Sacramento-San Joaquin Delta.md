# Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta

## Summary
The Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta is a complex estuarine system in northern California where freshwater from the Sacramento and San Joaquin rivers meets tidal saltwater influences, serving as critical spawning habitat, nursery grounds, and migratory corridors for economically and ecologically important fish species such as striped bass. This region represents a transition zone between riverine and marine environments, making it essential for understanding fish life history patterns, recruitment dynamics, and population connectivity in California's aquatic systems. The Delta's ecological significance has made it a focal point for fisheries research examining fish behavior, movement patterns, and conservation needs in altered estuarine ecosystems.
