# Juniper Springs Canyon

## Summary
Juniper Springs Canyon (also referred to as Juniper Canyon in the source) is an upper canyon area of the Yampa River where Colorado squawfish were found. Four Colorado squawfish ranging from 500-820mm were captured by electrofishing in the upper end of this canyon in October 1978, including one of the largest specimens (820mm, estimated 12 pounds) documented from the Yampa River in years, all captured in a constricted pooled area over sand and boulder substrate.
