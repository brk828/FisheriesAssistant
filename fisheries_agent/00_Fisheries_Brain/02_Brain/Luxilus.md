# Luxilus

## Summary
Luxilus is a subgenus of cyprinid minnows (family Cyprinidae, genus Notropis) historically distinguished by comparative morphological characters, particularly scale structure including a characteristic apical radii range of 19-27 fine ridges radiating from the scale focus. This taxonomic classification has been central to systematic studies of North American minnow diversity and has informed understanding of cyprinid evolution and geographic distribution patterns across freshwater ecosystems. Luxilus represents an important focal group for examining intrageneric variation and for establishing morphological diagnostic criteria used in fish identification and conservation assessments.
