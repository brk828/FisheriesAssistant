# Ondatra zibethicus

## Summary
Ondatra zibethicus (muskrat) is an aquatic mammal identified as occurring in the streams of east-central Arizona examined in this study. It is listed among the aquatic mammals that may be present at the proposed power line crossing points.

## Context

> This 1973 literature review and impact assessment examines the vertebrate and invertebrate fauna of several streams and rivers in east-central Arizona at proposed power line crossing points.
