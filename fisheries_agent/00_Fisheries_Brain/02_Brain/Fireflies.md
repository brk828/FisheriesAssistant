# Fireflies

## Summary
Fireflies (family Lampyridae) are bioluminescent beetles that were reported by multiple observers to flash synchronously. This source critically examines these reports and concludes they likely represent "accidental synchronism" - a statistical illusion where large numbers of individuals (approximately 60 fireflies observed over two acres) flashing at slightly different rates inevitably produce apparent synchronization through chance alone, rather than demonstrating true rhythmic synchronism.
