# Yermo

## Summary
Yermo is a location in San Bernardino County, California where United Energy Corporation constructed an aquaculture and research facility in 1984. The facility's remote location was a key factor in the California Department of Fish and Game's conditional support for allowing exotic fish species at the site, as it reduced the risk of escape into California waters.
