# Pteronarcys

## Summary
Pteronarcys is a genus of large stoneflies with life cycles lasting 2 or more years. According to this study, species with such extended life cycles are eliminated from intermittent stream environments like Caldwell Hollow because they cannot complete their multi-year development when the stream goes completely dry each summer.
