# Tenebrio molitor

## Summary
Tenebrio molitor, the mealworm beetle, is a terrestrial insect used in ecological energetics studies. This source reports that T. molitor shows variation in calorific values by life stage and fat content, ranging from 4858.1 to 6578.9 cal/g ash-free dry weight.
