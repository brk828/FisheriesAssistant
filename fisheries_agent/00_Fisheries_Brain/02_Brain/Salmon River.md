# Salmon River

## Summary
A Salmon River refers to any river system or tributary that supports Pacific salmon populations and serves as critical habitat for their spawning, rearing, and migration cycles. In the context of fisheries science and conservation, Salmon Rivers are characterized by their ecological importance as spawning grounds and nursery areas, often managed through hatchery programs, dam operations, and habitat restoration to sustain both wild and hatchery salmon populations. These river systems are fundamental to understanding salmon life history, population dynamics, and the effectiveness of conservation interventions such as fish passage improvements and water management strategies.
