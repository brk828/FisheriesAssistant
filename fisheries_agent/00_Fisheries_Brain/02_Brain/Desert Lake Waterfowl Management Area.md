# Desert Lake Waterfowl Management Area

## Summary
Desert Lake Waterfowl Management Area is a location where water rights retention was a question being addressed in the pending Price-San Rafael Biological Opinion. The April 1990 meeting noted this water rights issue remained unresolved as part of the Section 7 consultation process.
