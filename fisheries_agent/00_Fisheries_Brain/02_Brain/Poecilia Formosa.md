# Poecilia Formosa

## Summary
Poecilia formosa, commonly known as the Amazon molly, is a freshwater poeciliid fish native to Mexico and Central America that has gained significance in fish science as an introduced species in various aquatic ecosystems, including Texas waters where it comprises a notable portion of exotic fish fauna. This species is particularly important in conservation and ecological studies because its introduction into non-native habitats represents a case of exotic species invasion with potential impacts on native fish communities and local biodiversity. As a member of the poeciliid family, P. formosa is also relevant to broader ichthyological research on fish genetics, distribution patterns, and the ecological consequences of species translocation beyond their native ranges.
