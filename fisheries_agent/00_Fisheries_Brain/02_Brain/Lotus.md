# Lotus

## Summary
Lotus is an emergent flowering aquatic plant found in freshwater habitats. The source lists it alongside water lilies and pickerel weed as examples of flowering plants that grow in New York State waters, representing one category of aquatic vegetation that may require management in certain situations.
