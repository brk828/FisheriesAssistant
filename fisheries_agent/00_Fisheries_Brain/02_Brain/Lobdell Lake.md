# Lobdell Lake

## Summary
Lobdell Lake is a 545-acre warm-water lake in Genesee County, Michigan, connected to the Saginaw River drainage and maintained by a dam. It was one of six lakes opened to year-round fishing for species not protected by closed seasons, with a five-year catch composition of 67.8% bluegill, 14.8% yellow perch, 6.8% pumpkinseed, and 6.2% crappie.
