# Coachwhip

## Summary
The Coachwhip (Masticophis flagellum) is a snake species found in desert habitats. This survey confirmed its presence at Papago Park Military Reservation in Phoenix, Arizona, where it was one of eight reptile species documented during the July 1994 biological assessment.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
