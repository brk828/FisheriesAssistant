# Sportsman's Drain

## Summary
Sportsman's Drain (RMI 296.5) is identified as one of the six primary sites recommended for concentrated razorback sucker larval monitoring efforts. It is located in the middle Green River system where larvae utilize low-velocity nursery habitats.
