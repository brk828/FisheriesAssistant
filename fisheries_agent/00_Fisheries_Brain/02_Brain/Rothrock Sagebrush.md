# Rothrock Sagebrush

## Summary
Rothrock Sagebrush (Artemisia rothrockii) is a plant species found in the Sub-Alpine community at 9,500-10,500 feet elevation in the Mono Basin. It occurs in the zone characterized by patches of conifers with open meadows.
