# Fisheries Policy

## Summary
Fisheries policy regarding northern pike in California designates them as a "detrimental animal" under California Code of Regulations Title 14 Section 671, making it unlawful to import, transport, or possess them alive except under permit. Management policy evolved from chemical eradication attempts to a 2000 plan emphasizing containment, control, public education, and increased enforcement, with modified stocking strategies including discontinuing fingerling trout stocking and planting only larger catchable-sized trout.
