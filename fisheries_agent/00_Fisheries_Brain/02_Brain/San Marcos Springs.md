# San Marcos Springs

## Summary
San Marcos Springs is a large first-order spring located approximately 670 m from the artesian well where Haideoporus texanus was collected. The spring shares the same water source (the Edwards Aquifer) as the well at the Aquatic Station.

## Context

> This study describes the larva of *[[Haideoporus texanus]]*, the first hypogeous (subterranean) dytiscid beetle discovered in North America.
