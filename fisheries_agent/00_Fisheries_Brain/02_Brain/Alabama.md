# Alabama

## Summary
Alabama is the location where this pond management guidance was developed and applied. This source is an Alabama Polytechnic Institute Extension Service publication designed to help Alabama farmers and 4-H Club members improve fish production in freshwater ponds by addressing turbidity problems.
