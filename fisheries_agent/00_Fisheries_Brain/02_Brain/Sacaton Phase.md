# Sacaton Phase

## Summary
Sacaton Phase is a period of Hohokam occupation in Arizona, part of the Sedentary period (approximately A.D. 990-1050 at La Lomita Pequeña). Faunal assemblages from Sacaton phase sites show remarkable consistency across the region, with lagomorphs representing 74-83% of identified fauna and ungulates contributing 7-21%.
