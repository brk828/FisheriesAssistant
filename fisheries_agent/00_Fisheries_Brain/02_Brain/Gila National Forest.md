# Gila National Forest

## Summary
The Gila National Forest is a protected forest area in New Mexico that encompasses critical aquatic habitat for native fish species, particularly along the mainstem Gila River. As a management unit, the forest serves as an important focal point for fish inventory surveys and conservation efforts targeting federally-listed trout species and their freshwater ecosystems. The forest's designation reflects the conservation significance of its riverine habitats and the need for coordinated scientific monitoring and management to protect declining native fish populations within this region.
