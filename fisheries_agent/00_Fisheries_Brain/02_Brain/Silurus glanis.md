# Silurus glanis

## Summary
Silurus glanis is the sheatfish (European catfish), a large freshwater fish species studied for its parasites. This source reports that fry of this species were found to host multiple parasites including Ichthyophthirius multifiliis, Ancylodiscoides siluri, Diplostomum spathaceum, Proteocephalus osculatus, Silurataenia siluri, and Piscicola geometra.
