# Xenopus

## Summary
Xenopus laevis is the African clawed frog, an exotic amphibian species. This source reports that X. laevis was discovered in Agua Dulce Canyon within the stickleback's habitat and poses a severe threat as a potential predator, with eradication efforts ongoing as part of the recovery plan for the endangered Unarmored Threespine Stickleback.
