# Chain Pickerel

## Summary
Chain Pickerel (Esox niger) is a freshwater predatory fish species belonging to the pike family (Esocidae), commonly found in North American lentic and semi-lentic habitats including ponds, marshes, and slow-moving waters. In fisheries science and conservation contexts, Chain Pickerel are significant as both sport fish and ecological indicators, though they are frequently managed through population control measures (such as rotenone application) when their predatory dominance creates trophic imbalances in managed water bodies. The species is taxonomically notable for its ventral pectoral fin positioning, which requires specialized consideration in standardized ichthyological sampling protocols such as scale collection for age and growth analysis.
