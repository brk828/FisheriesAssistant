# UPGMA

## Summary
UPGMA (Unweighted Pair Group Method with Arithmetic Mean) is a clustering analysis method used to examine evolutionary relationships. In this study, UPGMA was employed alongside distance Wagner analyses to examine phenetic relationships among 27 populations of Gila species from the Lower Colorado River Basin based on genetic data from protein electrophoresis at 25 gene loci.
