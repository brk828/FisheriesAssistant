# Acipenseridae

## Summary
Acipenseridae is the family of true sturgeons, ancient anadromous fish characterized by cartilaginous skeletons, elongate bodies, and barbels around their mouths, including commercially and ecologically significant genera such as Acipenser, Scaphirhynchus, and Psephurus. Members of this family are of major conservation concern due to their slow growth rates, late sexual maturity, and high vulnerability to overfishing and habitat degradation, with numerous species listed as threatened or endangered. The family represents a relict lineage of primitive ray-finned fishes with important roles in estuarine and riverine ecosystems, particularly in anadromous populations that migrate between freshwater and marine environments.
