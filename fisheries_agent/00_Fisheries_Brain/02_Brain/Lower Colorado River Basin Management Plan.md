# Lower Colorado River Basin Management Plan

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Basin Management Plan was a proposed management framework that had been shelved prior to 1997. According to this source, the Colorado River Commission criticized the Biological Opinion for effectively implementing this plan without public participation, thereby undermining the collaborative Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program process that was underway.
