# Cope and Yarrow

## Summary
Cope and Yarrow were investigators who contributed to early classification attempts of Gila species in 1875. Their work was hampered by gross errors in locality data, mixing of species from distinct drainage systems, and inadequate material that provided little appreciation of intraspecific variation or zoogeography.
