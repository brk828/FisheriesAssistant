# Pacifica

## Summary
Pacifica is a coastal location near San Francisco where four tagged striped bass (6.3% of tag returns) were recaptured in ocean waters. This demonstrates that striped bass from Tomales Bay also migrate into Pacific coastal waters as part of their movement patterns.

## Context

> This tagging study (1963-1966) investigated the migration patterns of [[Striped Bass]] (*Roccus saxatilis*) in [[Tomales Bay]], California, located 40 miles northwest of [[San Francisco]]. A total of 207 striped bass were tagged using disk-dangler tags, with 65 tag returns received by July 1968....
