# Aquatic Ecosystems

## Summary
Aquatic ecosystems encompass the complex, interconnected biological and physical communities within freshwater and marine water systems, including their watersheds, organisms, and environmental processes that support fish populations and aquatic biodiversity. In fish science and conservation, aquatic ecosystems are understood as integrated wholes requiring holistic, ecosystem-level management approaches that consider multiple species, habitat functions, and water quality parameters rather than isolated single-species management strategies. This systems-based perspective recognizes that fish health and fishery sustainability depend on maintaining the integrity of entire aquatic environments, from headwater streams to estuaries, and their connections to surrounding terrestrial and human-modified landscapes.
