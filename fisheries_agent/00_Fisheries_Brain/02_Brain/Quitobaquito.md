# Quitobaquito

## Summary
Quitobaquito is a desert aquatic habitat supporting a genetically distinct population of desert pupfish (Cyprinodon macularius). This population shows multiple fixed allelic differences from Salton Sea populations and possesses unique alleles not found elsewhere, leading researchers to conclude it likely deserves subspecific recognition.
