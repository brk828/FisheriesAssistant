# Abrams Creek

## Summary
Abrams Creek is a stream within Great Smoky Mountains National Park where multiple fish species were recorded during surveys. The creek was notable as a location where Goldfish (Carassius auratus), an undesirable introduced species from the Orient, was observed, though its establishment status was unknown.
