# Siler Pincushion Cactus

## Summary
Siler Pincushion Cactus is an endangered cactus dependent on halicitid bee pollinators that may forage up to 3 miles from nest sites. Aerial ultra-low-volume pesticide application would likely jeopardize this species through pollinator loss, requiring a 3-mile buffer or alternative use of carbaryl bait or biological control.
