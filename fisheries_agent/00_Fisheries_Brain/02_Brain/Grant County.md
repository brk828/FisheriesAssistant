# Grant County

## Summary
Grant County is a county in New Mexico that includes portions of the upper Gila River watershed. The 1973 field surveys documented aquatic conditions in Grant County as part of the comprehensive Gila River Basin assessment, representing the New Mexico portion of the study area.
