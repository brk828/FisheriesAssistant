# Alabama River

## Summary
The Alabama River is a major riverine system within the Mobile River drainage of the southeastern United States that serves as critical habitat for various aquatic species, particularly supporting freshwater fauna including odonates and other benthic organisms. As a component of extensive river networks spanning thousands of miles of frontage, the Alabama River represents significant aquatic habitat that has been subject to water management modifications including hydroelectric impoundment and irrigation infrastructure development. This river system is ecologically important for fish conservation and serves as a focus for critical habitat designations and species protection efforts within the Gulf of Mexico drainage basin.
