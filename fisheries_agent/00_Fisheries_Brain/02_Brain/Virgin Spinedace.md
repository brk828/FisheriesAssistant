# Virgin Spinedace

## Summary
The Virgin Spinedace (*Lepidomeda mollispinax*) is a small native cyprinid fish endemic to the Virgin River Basin spanning Arizona, Utah, and Nevada that has been listed as an endangered species under the Endangered Species Act due to habitat loss, water diversion, and competition from introduced nonnative fish species. As a desert stream specialist, the Virgin Spinedace inhabits specific stream reaches within the lower Colorado River system where it faces threats from altered flow regimes downstream of Glen Canyon Dam, groundwater extraction, and degraded water quality. Conservation efforts for this species have focused on identifying and protecting existing populations and restoring potential habitat reaches through habitat management and in some cases eradication of competing nonnative fishes.
