# Angling Success

## Summary
Angling success refers to the fishing catch rates and patterns for striped bass in the lower Colorado River. Data from the Annual Bullhead City Striped Bass Derby (1968-1972) showed increasing fishing effort and entries each year, with fish averaging 598-689 mm and 4.0-6.40 kg, and feeding activity peaking during evening hours (57.5% of fish caught between 1800-2400 hours).
