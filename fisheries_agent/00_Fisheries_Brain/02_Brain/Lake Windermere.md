# Lake Windermere

## Summary
Lake Windermere is a major freshwater lake in England managed by the Freshwater Biological Association that serves as an important natural study site for ichthyological and aquatic ecology research. The lake's European perch (*Perca fluviatilis*) populations have been extensively sampled for physiological studies, including research on seasonal reproductive cycles and endocrine function. As a well-characterized freshwater ecosystem with accessible fish populations, Lake Windermere has provided critical baseline data for understanding fish biology and population dynamics in temperate lake systems.
