# Southern Redbelly Dace

## Summary
The Southern Redbelly Dace (*Chrosomus erythrogaster*) is a small cyprinid minnow commonly found in clear, spring-fed streams across the Mississippi River drainage system, where it typically associates with other cool-water species such as creek chubs and white suckers. This species is ecologically significant as an indicator of high-quality aquatic habitats and serves as an important component of small stream fish communities in regions from the upper Midwest through southern Illinois. Its presence in ichthyological surveys and habitat assessments reflects preferences for well-oxygenated, clear waters with moderate current, making it a useful species for monitoring stream health and assessing the integrity of lotic ecosystems.
