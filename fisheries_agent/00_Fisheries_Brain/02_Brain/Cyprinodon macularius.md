# Cyprinodon Macularius

## Summary
Cyprinodon macularius, commonly known as the desert pupfish, is a small-bodied cyprinodont fish endemic to the southwestern United States that has become a focal species in conservation biology and aquatic ecology research. This species is notable for its restricted habitat distribution and has been the subject of numerous translocation and recovery efforts, particularly in Arizona, reflecting its status as an imperiled native fish requiring active management intervention. The desert pupfish serves as an important indicator of desert aquatic ecosystem health and has been extensively studied regarding its breeding behavior, ecology, and response to habitat modification and species introductions.
