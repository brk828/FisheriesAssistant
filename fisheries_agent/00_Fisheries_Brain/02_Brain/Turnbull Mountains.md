# Turnbull Mountains

## Summary
The Turnbull Mountains are one of the mountain ranges surrounding the Aravaipa Creek watershed where the Klondyke Tailings investigation took place. This source identifies the Turnbull Mountains as part of the geographical setting for the study area, listed among other nearby ranges including the Winchester Mountains, Pinaleno Mountains, Galiuro Mountains, and Santa Teresa Mountains.

## Context

> This investigation by the [[Arizona Department of Environmental Quality]] documents water quality impacts from the [[Klondyke Tailings]] along [[Aravaipa Creek]] in [[Graham County]], Arizona.
