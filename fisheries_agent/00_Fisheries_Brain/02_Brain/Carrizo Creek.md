# Carrizo Creek

## Summary
Carrizo Creek is a stream in the Salt River basin that receives water from an interbasin transfer. The source documents that water is transferred from Showlow Lake in the Little Colorado River basin to Carrizo Creek, and this transfer has been circumstantially implicated in faunal transfers between basins. Such interbasin connections create pathways for non-native species movement and can impact native aquatic communities.
