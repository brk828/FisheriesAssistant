# Southern Flounder

## Summary
The Southern Flounder (*Paralichthys lethostigma*) is a euryhaline flatfish species native to coastal and estuarine waters of the southeastern United States that exhibits notable salinity tolerance, making it relevant for fisheries management and experimental introductions into inland aquatic systems. As documented in mid-twentieth century fisheries research, this species was considered for stocking in highly saline inland lakes and impoundments where native freshwater game fish could not survive, reflecting its ecological significance in aquaculture and fisheries conservation efforts. Southern Flounder represent an important commercial and recreational fish resource in coastal fisheries while also serving as a model organism for understanding species translocation and habitat suitability in altered aquatic ecosystems.
