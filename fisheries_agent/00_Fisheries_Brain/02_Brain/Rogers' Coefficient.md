# Rogers' Coefficient

## Summary
Rogers' Coefficient (S) is a measure of genetic similarity used to compare populations based on electrophoretic data. In this study, S = 1.0 between the two morphologically distinct endemic Cuatro Cienegas species (indicating complete genetic identity), while S = 0.582 between Cuatro Cienegas cichlids and Rio Grande C. cyanoguttatum (within the range typical for sibling species pairs).
