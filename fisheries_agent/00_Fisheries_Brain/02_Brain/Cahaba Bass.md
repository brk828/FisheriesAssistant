# Cahaba Bass

## Summary
Cahaba Bass (Micropterus cahabae) is one of several species within the Redeye Bass complex, each restricted to disjunct upland river systems in the Southeastern United States following genetic revision that separated the original Redeye Bass description into multiple species. This source notes that phylogenetic analysis placed Verde River samples in a well-supported clade with both Redeye Bass and Cahaba Bass from their native range, and one individual showed potential minor genomic contribution from Cahaba Bass (Q-value 0.087) in SNP analysis.
