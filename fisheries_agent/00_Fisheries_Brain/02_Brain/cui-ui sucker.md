# Cui-Ui Sucker

## Summary
The Cui-Ui Sucker (*Catostomus cujus*) is an endemic fish species native to the Lahontan Basin of Nevada that has become a focal point for conservation efforts due to its critically endangered status and dependence on specific lacustrine habitats. This specialized catostomid was historically abundant in Pyramid Lake but experienced severe population declines following water management alterations and ecosystem disruptions in the early-to-mid 20th century. The species is now managed through propagation programs at facilities like Lahontan National Fish Hatchery and Marble Bluff Fish Facilities, making it a key indicator species for evaluating the effectiveness of aquatic ecosystem restoration and endangered species recovery initiatives in the Great Basin.
