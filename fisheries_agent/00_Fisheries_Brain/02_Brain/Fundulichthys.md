# Fundulichthys

## Summary
Fundulichthys is a killifish genus that includes the Japanese species F. virescens. The genus is distinguished from the American genus Zygonectes by having a small dorsal fin inserted wholly anterior to an equally small anal fin, and a lunate caudal fin.

## Context

> This 1906 taxonomic review by [[Jordan]] and [[Snyder]] documents two species of killifishes (family [[Poeciliidae]]) from Japan: [[Fundulichthys virescens]] and [[Oryzias latipes]]. Both are small fishes inhabiting rice swamps, pools, and ditches in southern Japan, likely feeding on mosquitoes....
