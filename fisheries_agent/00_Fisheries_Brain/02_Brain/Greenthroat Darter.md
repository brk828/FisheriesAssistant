# Greenthroat Darter

## Summary
The Greenthroat Darter (*Etheostoma lepidum*) is a small percid fish endemic to spring-fed streams of the Edwards Plateau in Texas, notable for its role in ichthyological research examining geographic variation in reproductive traits such as fecundity and egg complement across isolated populations. This species has been a subject of developmental and ecological studies investigating how environmental conditions in distinct spring habitats influence life history characteristics and population dynamics. As a spring-dependent specialist, the Greenthroat Darter represents an important model organism for understanding adaptation and conservation needs in limited freshwater ecosystems vulnerable to hydrological and environmental change.
