# Santiago River

## Summary
The Santiago River is part of the Lerma-Santiago drainage system, with waterfalls near Guadalajara marking a biogeographic boundary. This source notes that the upper Santiago River above these falls harbors endemic species including Xystrosus popoche and Falcularius chapalae, and that aberrant gill raker counts in Algansea rubescens from the Santiago suggest possible hybridization with Xystrosus.
