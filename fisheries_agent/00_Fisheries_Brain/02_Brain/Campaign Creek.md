# Campaign Creek

## Summary
Campaign Creek is a small perennial stream in Gila County, Arizona, with 1-2 miles of perennial flow fed primarily by Upper Horrell Springs. The creek features rocky substrate with a high proportion of pools in steep topography, riparian vegetation including cottonwood and sycamore, and was stocked with Gila topminnow in 1983, though the population has not been observed since 1989 despite being considered potentially extant due to sampling difficulties in complex habitat.
