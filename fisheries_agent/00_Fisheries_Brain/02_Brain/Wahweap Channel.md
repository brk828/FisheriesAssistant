# Wahweap Channel

## Summary
Wahweap Channel was one of three major control points where periodic stratified plankton samples were collected to examine depth zones (below 100 feet, 100-50 feet, 50-25 feet, and 25 feet to surface). This location was used to study vertical zonation patterns in the reservoir's plankton community.
