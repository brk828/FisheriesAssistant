# Lake Trophic Status

## Summary
Lake trophic status refers to the classification of lakes along a nutrient gradient ranging from oligotrophic (nutrient-poor, oxygen-rich) to eutrophic (nutrient-rich, oxygen-depleted), which fundamentally determines the lake's productivity and the structure of its aquatic communities. This classification is based on nutrient concentrations, chlorophyll levels, and dissolved oxygen profiles, particularly in relation to seasonal stratification and redox conditions in deep versus shallow systems. Trophic status is critical for fish conservation and management, as it directly influences habitat quality, species composition, and the suitability of lakes for supporting healthy fish populations and benthic communities.
