# Rio Sabinas System

## Summary
The Rio Sabinas System is a river system in the region near Cuatro Cienegas, Mexico. The research proposal planned to collect samples of Cichlasoma cyanoguttatum from this system to serve as a remote baseline or control population for genetic studies comparing the endemic Cuatro Cienegas cichlids with other Mexican cichlid populations.
