# Val Verde County

## Summary
Val Verde County is a county in Texas, USA, that was home to Goodenough Spring, the only known natural habitat of the Amistad gambusia (Gambusia amistadensis). The spring in Val Verde County was permanently flooded beginning in July 1968 by the Amistad Reservoir, leading to the extinction of this endemic fish species.
