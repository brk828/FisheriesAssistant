# Cushing and Sprague

## Summary
Cushing and Sprague conducted research in 1952 on salmonid erythrocyte antigens that provided foundational work for this 1964 study. Their prior research was cited as part of the extensive body of work on salmonid blood antigens that informed the immunogenetic techniques used to study California trout populations.

## Context

> This 1964 study by [[John R. Calaprice]] and [[John E. Cushing]] investigated erythrocyte (red blood cell) antigens in California trout species to determine if immunogenetic techniques could identify relationships and variation useful for fisheries management.
