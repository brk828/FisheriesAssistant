# Blue Lake

## Summary
Blue Lake is a coldwater lake in California or Nevada that was selected as one of four western lakes to receive introductions of Mysis relicta (opossum shrimp) in 1964. Approximately 2,000 live opossum shrimp were stocked in Blue Lake as part of efforts to establish forage populations for trout and test the species' potential in coldwater environments.
