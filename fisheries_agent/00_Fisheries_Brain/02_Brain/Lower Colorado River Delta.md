# Lower Colorado River Delta

## Summary
The Lower Colorado River Delta is a region in Mexico that historically supported an extensive network of desert pupfish populations. This study found that pupfish populations in this region form part of the species Cyprinodon macularius and show marginally significant genetic divergence from Salton Sea populations, with distinct secondary haplotypes (such as haplotype F occurring in five of six delta samples but absent from Salton Sea samples).
