# Cueva de la Porra

## Summary
Cueva de la Porra is a cave located 10.9 km northeast of Xilitla where specimens of Xilitloniscus laevis were collected. This cave represents the third known location for this troglobitic species, yielding three females and one male specimen.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
