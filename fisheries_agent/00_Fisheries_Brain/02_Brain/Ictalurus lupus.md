# Ictalurus lupus

## Summary
Ictalurus lupus is a catfish species in the family Ictaluridae, commonly known as headwater catfish. In the Cuatro Ciénegas basin, a subspecifically differentiated form exists that shows northern/eastern affinities and may have connections to the Mexican Plateau fauna.
