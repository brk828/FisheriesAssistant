# Lepidomeda albivallis

## Summary
Lepidomeda albivallis is a plagopterin minnow found only in the pluvial White River (a Pleistocene-Holocene tributary to the Virgin River). This study found it forms an unresolved trichotomy with L. mollispinis subspecies, with divergence times of 0.0-1.4 million years ago, likely resulting from disruption of water connections through recent aridification.
