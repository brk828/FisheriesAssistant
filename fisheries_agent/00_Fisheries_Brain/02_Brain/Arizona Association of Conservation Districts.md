# Arizona Association of Conservation Districts

## Summary
The Arizona Association of Conservation Districts is a state-level organization representing local conservation districts. It was one of the five primary collaborating organizations that developed the BIOTA Project proposal in 1993, and had representation on the project's Steering Committee to ensure participation from local conservation perspectives.
