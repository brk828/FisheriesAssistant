# Death Valley System Committee

## Summary
The Death Valley System Committee is a specialized working group of the Desert Fishes Council established to coordinate conservation efforts and monitor the status of endemic fish species within the Death Valley drainage system and associated southwestern desert aquatic ecosystems. Operating since at least 1974, the committee convened annual meetings to compile status reports, assess threats to endemic taxa, and develop management strategies for protecting species facing extinction due to habitat degradation and environmental change in the region. This committee exemplified the Desert Fishes Council's broader mission to address the critical conservation needs of highly specialized and geographically isolated fish populations across the American Southwest.
