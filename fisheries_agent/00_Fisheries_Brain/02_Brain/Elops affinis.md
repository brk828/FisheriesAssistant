# Elops affinis

## Summary
Elops affinis, commonly known as ladyfish, is a fish species found in the lower Colorado River region. According to this 1977 report, populations of ladyfish are doomed to extirpation due to the drying of natural waters resulting from desalinization operations on the lower Colorado River mainstream.
