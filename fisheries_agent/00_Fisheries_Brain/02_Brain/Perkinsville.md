# Perkinsville

## Summary
Perkinsville is a geographic reference point near U.S. Forest Service Road 638 on the upper Verde River in central Arizona that served as a fish research study site for multiple fisheries surveys conducted by the Rocky Mountain Research Station during the 1990s. The location was used to establish baseline data on native Sonoran Desert fish populations and their microhabitats in this perennial warmwater stream system. Perkinsville represents one of several standardized monitoring stations used to assess fish community composition and ecological conditions in the Verde River drainage.
