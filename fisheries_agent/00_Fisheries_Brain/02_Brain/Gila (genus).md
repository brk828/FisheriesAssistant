# Gila (Genus)

## Summary
Gila is a genus of cyprinid fishes endemic to the southwestern United States and Mexico, comprising several species of conservation concern including the bonytail and roundtail chub. The genus has been subject to ongoing taxonomic revision, particularly regarding species boundaries between *Gila intermedia* and *Gila robusta*, with taxonomic uncertainty historically complicating conservation and listing decisions. Members of this genus have been central to desert fish recovery efforts, with subspecies-level distinctions recognized among populations and genetic analysis employed to assess population structure and diversity.
