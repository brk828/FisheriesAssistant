# Stickland

## Summary
Stickland was a researcher who found that 1.65% oxygen in a gas stream (equivalent to 0.32 ppm in solution) reduced denitrification to only 12% of the anaerobic rate with E. coli. This work demonstrated strong oxygen inhibition of denitrification.
