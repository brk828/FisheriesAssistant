# St. David

## Summary
St. David is a location in Arizona along the San Pedro River that marks the northern endpoint of a 30-mile vegetation inventory conducted in May 1986. The survey extended from Hereford to near St. David, specifically to the end of the San Juan de las Boquillas y Nogales Land Grant.
