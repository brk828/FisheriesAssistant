# Sonoyta Mud Turtle

## Summary
The Sonoyta Mud Turtle (family Kinosternidae) is a federal candidate species requiring wetland habitats in the Sonoran Desert region. It was identified as one of five aquatic or semi-aquatic species of conservation concern in Pima County's preliminary species list for the Sonoran Desert Conservation Plan.
