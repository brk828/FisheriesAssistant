# Cocopah Indians

## Summary
The Cocopah Indians are an indigenous tribe living in the Colorado River Delta region. According to this 1999 conference, Cocopah fishermen's livelihoods are directly impacted by the presence or absence of river flows affecting fish larvae production, and the community struggles with lack of river flows, chemical contamination including pesticides and heavy metals, and loss of traditional subsistence practices.
