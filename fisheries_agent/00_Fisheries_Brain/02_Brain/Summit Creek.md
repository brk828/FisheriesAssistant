# Summit Creek

## Summary
Summit Creek is a tributary of the Secesh River where 112 summer chinook salmon were experimentally transferred from Hells Canyon Dam in 1966. By September 4, ten redds were counted (seven above and three below the release site), though dead females examined had immature ovaries suggesting premature mortality.
