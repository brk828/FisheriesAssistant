# University of Washington

## Summary
The University of Washington was the institutional affiliation of Dr. Jerry Franklin, who served as Chairman of the National Parks and Conservation Association's Commission on Research and Resource Management Policy in 1988. Dr. Franklin was one of several academic experts recruited to examine research and resource management policy issues in the National Park System.
