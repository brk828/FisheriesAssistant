# Lake Atitlan

## Summary
Lake Atitlan is a scenic, clear-water freshwater lake in Guatemala characterized by very blue water with high visibility. This 1967 study found the lake supported abundant populations of carnivorous fish including Largemouth Bass and Crappie, with productivity described as far greater than most U.S. lakes, though deeper areas were practically devoid of fish and the fish community structure was imbalanced with insufficient herbivorous species.
