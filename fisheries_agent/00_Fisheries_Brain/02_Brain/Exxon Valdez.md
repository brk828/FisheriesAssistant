# Exxon Valdez

## Summary
The Exxon Valdez oil spill is cited as an example of environmental disasters caused by human activities that have contributed to accelerating species extinction rates in the United States. The report identifies such disasters, along with poorly planned development, unsustainable agriculture and forestry, pollution, and ecosystem mismanagement, as factors that may have increased extinction rates possibly 10-fold above historical levels.
