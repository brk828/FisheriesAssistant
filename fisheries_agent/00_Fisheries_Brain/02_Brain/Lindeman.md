# Lindeman

## Summary
Lindeman published influential work in 1942 on trophic-dynamic ecology that established the foundation for using calories as a common denominator in ecosystem energy studies. This compilation builds on Lindeman's approach by providing standardized calorific data for quantifying energy flow through ecosystems.
