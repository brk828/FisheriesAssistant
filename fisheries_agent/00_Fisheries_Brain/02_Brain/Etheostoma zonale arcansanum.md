# Etheostoma zonale arcansanum

## Summary
Etheostoma zonale arcansanum is a subspecies of banded darter. This source lists it as one of five species documented from the Kansas portion of the Verdigris River that was not collected during the Oklahoma survey, indicating its presence in the upper drainage but absence or rarity in the Oklahoma segment sampled.
