# Otolith

## Summary
Otoliths are calcified structures in fish inner ears used for balance and hearing. In this study, the Arizona Game and Fish Department included otolith studies as part of their research objectives for understanding Humpback Chub biology, likely to determine age, growth patterns, or environmental history of individual fish.
