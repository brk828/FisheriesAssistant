# California Leaf-nosed Bat

## Summary
The California Leaf-nosed Bat (Macrotus californicus) is a C2 candidate species that dwells in caves and mines year-round in desertscrub habitats and has been recorded near Phoenix. The assessment identified potential roost sites in the buttes at Papago Park and recommended no modification of buttes and additional bat surveys to determine presence.
