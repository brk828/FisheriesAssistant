# Cyprinodon Bifasciatus

## Summary
Cyprinodon bifasciatus, commonly known as the Cuatro Ciénegas pupfish, is a small endemic cyprinodontid fish species found exclusively in the isolated aquatic systems of Cuatro Ciénegas, Mexico, where it plays an important role as both a predator of small invertebrates and prey for larger aquatic fauna within the local food web. This species is of significant conservation concern due to its restricted geographic range and dependence on specific hydrogeological conditions in its desert spring habitat, making it vulnerable to environmental changes and water extraction pressures. Its presence and population dynamics serve as important indicators of ecosystem health in this unique and biodiversity-rich region.
