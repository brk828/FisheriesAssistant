# Tetrahymena pyriformis

## Summary
Tetrahymena pyriformis is a protozoan species included among the aquatic macroconsumers analyzed for calorific content. This organism contributed to the aquatic macroconsumer dataset showing a grand mean of 5290 cal/g ash-free.
