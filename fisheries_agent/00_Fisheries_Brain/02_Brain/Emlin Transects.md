# Emlin Transects

## Summary
Emlin Transects are a standardized survey method specified for broadleaf riparian areas in the San Pedro River study, designed to be 400 feet wide and one-half mile long. Only three areas along the surveyed 30-mile reach met these width criteria, requiring modifications to the methodology for narrower riparian zones.
