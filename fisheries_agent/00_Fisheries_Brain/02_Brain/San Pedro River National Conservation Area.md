# San Pedro River National Conservation Area

## Summary
The San Pedro River National Conservation Area encompasses a gaining reach between Palominas and Charleston where groundwater discharges into the stream. This source documents that the area has experienced 67% decrease in low flows from 1943-1992 (to approximately 1 cubic foot per second) and is undergoing early-stage riparian recovery following cattle removal, though it faces threats from groundwater pumping associated with Fort Huachuca and Sierra Vista expansion.
