# Etheostoma Parvipinne

## Summary
Etheostoma parvipinne, commonly known as the smallfin darter, is a small benthic fish species endemic to specific drainage systems in the southeastern United States, characterized by notably reduced fin sizes relative to congeners and typically associated with riffle and gravel substrate habitats in clear, flowing streams. This species has been documented in ichthyological surveys across multiple regions including western Kentucky and other Gulf Slope drainages, making it an important indicator organism for stream habitat quality and biodiversity assessments in freshwater ecosystems. Like other darters in the genus Etheostoma, E. parvipinne represents a specialized niche within lotic communities and has conservation significance due to its limited range and dependence on pristine flowing-water conditions.
