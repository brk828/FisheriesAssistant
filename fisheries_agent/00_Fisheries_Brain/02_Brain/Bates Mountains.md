# Bates Mountains

## Summary
The Bates Mountains are a mountain range at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. This source reports a trail through these mountains has been used since the Early Archaic period (ca. 8500 B.C.) as the main travel corridor between the desert and the Sea of Cortez, and is currently used by hikers and researchers.
