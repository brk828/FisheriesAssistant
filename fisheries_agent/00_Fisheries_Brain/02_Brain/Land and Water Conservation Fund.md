# Land and Water Conservation Fund

## Summary
The Land and Water Conservation Fund is a federal funding mechanism established by Congress to provide long-term conservation and protection of lands with important natural resource values. According to this source, Congress appropriated money from this fund to the BLM during 1991 and 1992 specifically for initial land acquisitions along the San Pedro River, and these funds were used to purchase several scattered parcels of riparian habitat.
