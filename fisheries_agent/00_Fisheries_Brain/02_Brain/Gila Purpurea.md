# Gila Purpurea

## Summary
Gila purpurea, commonly known as the Yaqui chub, is a small endemic cyprinid fish native to the Yaqui River drainage in Arizona and Sonora, Mexico, that has become a focus of conservation efforts due to its restricted range and declining populations in its native habitat. The species has been subject to translocation and stocking programs, including introductions into refugial populations in streams such as West Turkey Creek in the Chiricahua Mountains, as part of strategies to preserve the species and establish insurance populations against extinction. Its management represents an important case study in southwestern U.S. fish conservation, involving field surveys, population monitoring, and the challenges of maintaining genetically viable populations in fragmented aquatic ecosystems.
