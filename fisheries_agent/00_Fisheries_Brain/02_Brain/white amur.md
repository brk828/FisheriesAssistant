# White Amur

## Summary
The White Amur, commonly known as grass carp (*Ctenopharyngodon idella*), is a large Asian cyprinid fish introduced to aquatic systems as a biological control agent for managing aquatic vegetation. In the context of aquatic ecology and conservation, the White Amur represents both a management tool and a potential ecological concern, as its effectiveness in controlling nuisance plant growth must be weighed against potential impacts on native fish communities and aquatic ecosystems. Its use in regulated fisheries and aquatic management systems reflects the tension between intentional species introductions for specific management objectives and broader conservation goals protecting native aquatic biodiversity.
