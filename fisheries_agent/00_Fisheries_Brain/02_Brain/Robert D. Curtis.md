# Robert D. Curtis

## Summary
Robert D. Curtis was the Special Services Division Chief at the Arizona Department of Water Resources who received the December 4, 1984 memorandum from Ray O. Perkins Jr. regarding the Leslie Canyon Dam feasibility conclusions. He was the recipient of the final recommendation that the project was not hydrologically feasible.
