# Pinacate Biosphere Reserve

## Summary
The Pinacate Biosphere Reserve is a recently designated (as of 1994) protected area in Mexico that forms part of the western Sonoran Desert and Upper Gulf of California bioregion. It is one of several protected areas in the region that the International Sonoran Desert Alliance seeks to manage through unified, cross-border strategies.
