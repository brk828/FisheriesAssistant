# Adobe Valley

## Summary
Adobe Valley is the destination of overflow from Lake Russell during the Tahoe glacial maximum, as identified by Putnam (1949). This overflow at 7,150 feet elevation would have connected Lake Russell to a chain of pluvial lakes extending to Lake Manly in Death Valley.
