# Owens Lake

## Summary
Owens Lake is a lake in Inyo County, California, that marked the southern extent of the Owens Pupfish's historical range. The pupfish originally occurred in the Owens River and adjacent springs and sloughs from Fish Slough south to, but not in, Owens Lake, and in springs around the lake.
