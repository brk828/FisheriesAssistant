# Colorado River Basin Native Fishes

## Summary
Colorado River Basin Native Fishes refers to the endemic and naturally occurring fish species and assemblages that inhabit the Colorado River system and its tributaries, including species such as pikeminnows, suckers, and pupfish that evolved in this arid region's unique aquatic environments. These fishes represent important components of the Colorado River ecosystem and have become central to conservation and management discussions due to threats from habitat modification, water extraction, invasive species introductions, and altered flow regimes. The scientific and policy interest in these native faunas, as reflected in the documents referenced, centers on their ecological roles, population monitoring, and the development of management strategies to preserve remaining populations and restore degraded habitats.
