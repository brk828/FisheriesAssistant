# Percina Tanasi

## Summary
Percina tanasi, commonly known as the snail darter, is a small North American freshwater dace that serves as a significant indicator species for stream habitat quality and conservation in the southeastern United States. This species is notable for its restricted geographic range and specific ecological requirements, including clear, flowing waters with rocky substrates, making it an important focal point for aquatic conservation efforts and environmental impact assessments in Appalachian river systems. Morphologically, P. tanasi can be distinguished from related Percina species by the position of its anterior dorsal saddle, which is situated under the spinous dorsal fin origin rather than entirely anterior to it, and its specialized feeding ecology targeting small aquatic invertebrates reflects its niche as a benthic insectivore in riverine ecosystems.
