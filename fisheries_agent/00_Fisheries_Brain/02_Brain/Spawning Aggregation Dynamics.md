# Spawning Aggregation Dynamics

## Summary
Spawning aggregation dynamics in Shoal Bass involve dense concentrations of adults at specific shoal complexes during April to early June. This source reports that fish move in and out of spawning areas in large groups during early spawning season, with apparent survival within the aggregation increasing from 0.15 to 0.71 as spawning progressed, and movement rates decreasing to ~150 m/d during peak spawning.
