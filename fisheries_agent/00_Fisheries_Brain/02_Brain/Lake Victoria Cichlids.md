# Lake Victoria Cichlids

## Summary
Lake Victoria Cichlids refer to an endemic species flock of cichlid fish native to Lake Victoria in East Africa, representing a remarkable example of adaptive radiation and biodiversity within a single lacustrine ecosystem. These cichlids have become a critical focus in conservation biology due to their dramatic decline following the introduction of the predatory Nile Perch (*Lates niloticus*) in the 1980s, which devastated native populations and caused the extinction of numerous species. The Lake Victoria cichlid crisis exemplifies the broader conservation challenges posed by invasive species in freshwater ecosystems and has prompted international efforts to protect remaining endemic species through research, captive breeding programs, and habitat management initiatives.
