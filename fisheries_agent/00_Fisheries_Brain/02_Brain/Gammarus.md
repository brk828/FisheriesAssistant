# Gammarus

## Summary
Gammarus is a genus of freshwater and brackish-water amphipod crustaceans that plays a significant ecological role as a primary food source for fish species and other aquatic predators. These small, shrimp-like organisms are important indicators of water quality and habitat conditions, with different species showing varying distributions across geographic regions and environmental preferences. In fisheries and conservation contexts, Gammarus populations are monitored both as indicators of ecosystem health and as a food web component crucial for sustaining fish populations, particularly in streams and lakes.
