# Vann's Lake

## Summary
Vann's Lake is an oxbow lake in the Verdigris River drainage in Oklahoma. This source reports it as the only location where Yellow Bass (Morone interrupta) was documented in the system, but notes the lake dried completely in 1954-1955, likely resulting in the extirpation of this species from the drainage.
