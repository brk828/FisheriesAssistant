# Algansea lacustris

## Summary
Algansea lacustris is a predominantly lacustrine Algansea species from Lake Patzcuaro. This source's preliminary data place it in the third species group characterized by 19-28 gill rakers, distinguishing it from headwater and river/lake species groups.
