# Los Fresnos

## Summary
Los Fresnos is a laguna approximately 25 meters in diameter located about 1 km northwest of Santa Tecla in the Cuatro Ciénegas Basin, with a heavily vegetated main pool and complex canal systems that have experienced water level reduction from irrigation demands. This source found that the Los Fresnos population of Xiphophorus gordoni showed high intra-population graft acceptance (MST = 34 days) indicating substantial inbreeding, and demonstrated genetic similarity to Santa Tecla (reciprocal transplants both MST = 19 days) but greater genetic differentiation from Quintero, particularly when serving as donor to Quintero hosts (MST = 11 days).
