# Quitobaquito Spring

## Summary
Quitobaquito Spring is a small, isolated desert spring located in southwestern Arizona that serves as a critical freshwater refuge and endemic fish habitat within an otherwise arid region. The spring's unique hydrogeochemistry, characterized by calcium-bicarbonate water chemistry typical of groundwater systems, creates a specialized aquatic microhabitat that supports endemic fish populations adapted to this specific isolated ecosystem. As a rare desert spring system, Quitobaquito has been the subject of conservation concern and scientific study due to its ecological significance as a relict aquatic ecosystem and the vulnerability of its endemic species to anthropogenic disturbance and environmental change.
