# Red-Bellied Dace

## Summary
The Red-Bellied Dace is a small cyprinid fish species found in North American freshwater systems, characterized by its distinctive coloration and preference for cool, clear streams and small lakes with moderate to swift currents. As a forage fish and indicator species, it plays an important ecological role in aquatic food webs while serving as a useful indicator of water quality and habitat integrity due to its sensitivity to pollution and environmental degradation. Its presence and abundance have been documented in historical fish surveys and distribution studies, making it relevant to understanding changes in fish community composition and the impacts of human activities on native ichthyofauna.
