# River Chess

## Summary
The River Chess is a hard-water stream in southern England where Edwards measured macrophyte net productivity. The Chess was one of four unpolluted to moderately polluted rivers where macrophyte productivity ranged from 1.44-2.30 g organic carbon/m²/day.
