# Jones Creek Channel

## Summary
Jones Creek Channel is another spawning channel in the Washington-Oregon-Idaho area used for comparison with the Abernathy Channel. This source reports that Jones Creek operated at much higher water flows (25-30 c.f.s. during spawning, 15 c.f.s. during incubation) compared to Abernathy's successful operation at only 1.2 c.f.s.
