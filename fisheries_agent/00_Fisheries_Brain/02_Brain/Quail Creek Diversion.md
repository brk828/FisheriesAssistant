# Quail Creek Diversion

## Summary
Quail Creek Diversion is a water diversion structure on the Virgin River in Utah that has significantly impacted Virgin spinedace habitat. According to this source, the largest remaining populations of Virgin spinedace occur in the upper mainstem Virgin River above this diversion, and approximately 4.5 km (2.8 mi) of river between the diversion and Pah Tempe Springs has been identified as a priority area for establishing interim fish maintenance flows to help restore the species' habitat connectivity and reduce population fragmentation.
