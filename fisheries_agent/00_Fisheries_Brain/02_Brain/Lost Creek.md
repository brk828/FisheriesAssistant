# Lost Creek

## Summary
Lost Creek appears to be a tributary stream of the lower Colorado River within the study reach of the Hualapai Aquatic Resources Study, situated between Diamond Creek and Pearce Ferry in the Grand Canyon region. As a tributary habitat, Lost Creek was monitored as part of systematic research examining how interim flow releases from Glen Canyon Dam affected fish populations and aquatic community structure in secondary channels and tributary streams. These tributary habitats were important study sites for understanding refugial zones and fish density patterns in response to mainstem flow regime changes.
