# Evolutionary Synthesis

## Summary
The Evolutionary Synthesis was a major scientific development occurring from 1936-1947 that integrated genetics with evolutionary biology. This source describes how it was led by scientists including Theodosius Dobzhansky and Ernst Mayr, and demonstrated that natural selection acting on small variations could direct evolutionary change, resolving earlier conflicts between Mendelian genetics and Darwinian evolution.
