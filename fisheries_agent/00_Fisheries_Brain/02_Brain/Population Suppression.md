# Population Suppression

## Summary
Population suppression of Northern Pike in Box Canyon Reservoir reduced the estimated population from 6,570 fish (>350 mm) in 2012 to achieving target abundances of <1.7 fish/net by 2013-2018 through intensive gill netting. The program removed 92.9% of total catch in the first three years (2012-2014), with effort requirements of 6.2-9.6 nets/ha/season at productive sites initially, declining to <1.0 fish/ha/season by 2016-2018, though a 2017-2018 rebound when effort was reduced demonstrated the need for sustained suppression above minimum thresholds.
