# Owens Sucker

## Summary
The Owens Sucker (*Catostomus* sp.) is a cyprinid fish species endemic to the Owens River basin in California's Great Basin region, representing a significant conservation concern due to habitat loss and water diversion in its historically limited range. As a native sucker species adapted to specific riverine conditions in an arid region, it exemplifies the broader conservation challenges facing Great Basin fishes that depend on increasingly fragmented and altered aquatic ecosystems. Its ecological significance lies in its role as a native component of desert aquatic communities and its use as an indicator species for the health of remaining natural spring and river systems in the Death Valley region.
