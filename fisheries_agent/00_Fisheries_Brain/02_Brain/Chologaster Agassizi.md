# Chologaster Agassizi

## Summary
Chologaster agassizi, commonly known as the spring cavefish, is a small freshwater species inhabiting groundwater and cave systems, notable for its use in behavioral and physiological research related to temperature avoidance and sensory adaptation in aquatic organisms. As a specialized cave-dwelling ichthyofaunal representative, this species demonstrates the ecological diversity of headwater and subterranean habitat specialists within North American freshwater systems and serves as an important model organism for understanding how fishes adapt to extreme environmental conditions with limited light and stable thermal regimes.
