# International Sonoran Desert Alliance

## Summary
The International Sonoran Desert Alliance is a non-governmental organization that co-organized the October 1997 binational workshop on Colorado River Delta and Upper Gulf of California restoration in Mexicali, Mexico. The organization volunteered to serve as a lead NGO in the implementation committee established under the Department of Interior and SEMARNAP Letter of Intent for managing protected natural areas in the border region.
