# Fish Slough Snail

## Summary
Fish Slough Snail (Fontelicella sp.) is an undescribed mollusk species that occurs in the OVNFS spring outflow and Northeast Spring within Fish Slough. It is one of several rare species that contribute to the ecological significance of the Fish Slough wetland area.
