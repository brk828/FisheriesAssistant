# Elephant Butte Dam

## Summary
Elephant Butte Dam was completed in 1916 and forms Elephant Butte Reservoir, which marks the downstream boundary of the Middle Rio Grande reach. This dam is one of two major mainstream reservoirs that fragment the 177 km river section and has contributed to habitat loss and population declines of native fish species.

## Context

> This 1994 status assessment documents the dramatic decline of native fish fauna in the [[Middle Rio Grande]] between Espanola and [[Elephant Butte Reservoir]], New Mexico.
