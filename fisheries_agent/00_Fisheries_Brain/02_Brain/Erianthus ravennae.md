# Erianthus ravennae

## Summary
Erianthus ravennae is an invasive exotic Eurasian bunchgrass that expanded rapidly in the Colorado River corridor during Interim Flows monitoring (1992-1995). This source identifies it as one of several invasive exotics of management concern that were rapidly colonizing riparian habitats, with implications for native plant diversity and wildlife habitat quality.
