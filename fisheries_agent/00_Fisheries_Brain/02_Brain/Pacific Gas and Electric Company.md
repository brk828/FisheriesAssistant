# Pacific Gas and Electric Company

## Summary
Pacific Gas and Electric Company reimbursed the California Department of Fish and Game $38,678 during 1967-68 for fish stocking in power project areas. The reimbursement covered 38,830 pounds of catchable trout and 125,060 Kokanee Salmon fingerlings planted in areas affected by their hydroelectric operations.
