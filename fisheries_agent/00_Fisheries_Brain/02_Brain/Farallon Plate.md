# Farallon Plate

## Summary
The Farallon Plate was an oceanic tectonic plate that was subducted beneath the North American continent. According to this study, the paleo-subduction of the Farallon Plate during the Cretaceous was responsible for the calc-alkaline magmatism that produced the Mesozoic andesites and Laramian granodiorites of the Basal Complex in the Sierra Madre Occidental, with decreased magmatic activity following a later consumption phase of the plate possibly linked to Oligocene ignimbrite emplacement.
