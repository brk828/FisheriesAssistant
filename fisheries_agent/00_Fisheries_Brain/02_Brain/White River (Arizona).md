# White River (Arizona)

## Summary
The White River in Arizona is a significant coldwater aquatic system in the state's east-central region that serves as critical habitat for native and introduced trout populations, particularly in reaches below the confluence of the Snake and Ord Creeks. As documented in mid-20th century fisheries management assessments, the White River was central to proposed water resource development projects and their evaluation for impacts on fish conservation and recreational fisheries. The river's ecological importance made it a focal point for interagency review processes examining how dam construction and water management decisions would affect trout stream productivity and aquatic biodiversity.
