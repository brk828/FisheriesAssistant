# William J. Matthews

## Summary
William J. Matthews is a pioneering ichthyologist and aquatic ecologist whose influential work in the 1980s established foundational frameworks for understanding fish community structure and organization in North American stream systems. His research systematically examined how fish assemblages vary along stream gradients and whether classification systems like stream order effectively predict community composition, thereby contributing essential theoretical and methodological approaches to modern fish ecology and conservation biology. Matthews's studies laid crucial groundwork for subsequent investigations into longitudinal patterns in fish communities and the ecological factors structuring fish assemblages in flowing waters.
