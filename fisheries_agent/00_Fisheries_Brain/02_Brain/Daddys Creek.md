# Daddys Creek

## Summary
Daddys Creek served as one of two control population sites providing pure Smallmouth Bass specimens for this hybridization study. Along with Dale Hollow Reservoir, it was used to establish baseline genetic and meristic characteristics of Smallmouth Bass for comparison with potentially hybridized populations.
