# Richard Wood

## Summary
Richard Wood was a botanist from Australasia who collaborated with the Characeae research project by providing specimens. His contributions helped expand the geographic coverage of the cytotaxonomic study beyond North American collections.

## Context

> This progress report covers research conducted under NSF Research Grant G-19449 (continuation of NSF-G13261) from January 1, 1962, documenting cytotaxonomic investigations of the [[Characeae]] (stoneworts) at the [[University of Louisville]].
