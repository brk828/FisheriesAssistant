# Volvox

## Summary
Volvox is a genus of phytoplankton (colonial green algae) that commonly blooms in Crowley Lake during summer months. These phytoplankton blooms, along with zooplankton blooms, occasionally create oxygen depletion in shoal areas of the reservoir.
