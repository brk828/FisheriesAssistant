# Argulus chesapeakensis

## Summary
Argulus chesapeakensis is a newly described species of parasitic crustacean found on oyster toadfish in the marine/estuarine waters of Chesapeake Bay, Maryland. It is distinguished from related species by its respiratory area shape, digitate (not spatulate) antennal spines, and the morphology of spines on the basal plate of the second maxilla. The species was previously misidentified as Argulus laticauda and was described from 91 specimens collected at Chesapeake Biological Laboratory.
