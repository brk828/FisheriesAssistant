# Chlorella pyrenoidosa

## Summary
Chlorella pyrenoidosa is a green alga that was the dominant species in a characteristic community thriving in highly radioactive seepage from Disposal Pit IV (250-400 mr/hr), with densities reaching 3,532,000 cells/ml. A bloom of this species developed rapidly in the ORNL settling basin (from near-zero to 20,000 cells/ml in 3 days), accumulating radioactivity at 1.13 × 10⁵ counts/min/gram, far exceeding what cell division alone would predict.
