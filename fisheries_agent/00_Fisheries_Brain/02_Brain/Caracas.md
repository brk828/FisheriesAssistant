# Caracas

## Summary
Caracas is the Venezuelan location near where Sicydium montanum was collected from mountain brooks. The species inhabits freshwater streams in the coastal mountain range in this region.

## Context

> This 1920 taxonomic description by Carl L. Hubbs establishes [[Sicydium montanum]] as a new species of goby collected from a mountain brook in [[Macuto]], [[Caracas]], [[Venezuela]].
