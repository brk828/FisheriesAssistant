# San Dieguito Tradition

## Summary
The San Dieguito Tradition is a Paleoindian archaeological culture dating to approximately 10,000-8500 B.C. This source identifies it as one of two Paleoindian traditions documented at Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument, alongside the Clovis Tradition.
