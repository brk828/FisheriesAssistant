# 13th-Hole Pond

## Summary
13th-Hole Pond is a golf-course pond at Tiara Rado Golf Course near Grand Junction, Colorado, used as a field study site for PIT tag testing on roundtail chub. The pond yielded better fish recovery than some other study sites, with 39 of the original 60 roundtail chub recovered across multiple sampling events (days 114, 296, and 360), though some mortality occurred before release and no fish were recovered on the final sampling at day 437.
