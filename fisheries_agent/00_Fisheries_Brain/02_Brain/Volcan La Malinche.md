# Volcan La Malinche

## Summary
Volcan La Malinche is a volcano that forms the eastern boundary of the Puebla Plateau in Mexico. Along with Iztaccihuatl and Popocatepetl to the west, it defines the intermontane basin where Asellus puebla inhabits the Rio Cosala drainage system.
