# Lermichthys

## Summary
Lermichthys is a goodeid genus placed in the subfamily Girardinichthyinae. This source's preliminary examination suggests that Lermichthys and Girardinichthys appear identical, indicating the classification may contain more genera than justified and that consolidation is needed.
