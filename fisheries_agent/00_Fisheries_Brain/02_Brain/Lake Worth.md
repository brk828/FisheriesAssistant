# Lake Worth

## Summary
Lake Worth is a reservoir in the lower Flint River system used in a translocation study of Shoal Bass. This source found that 10 of 12 translocated fish returned from Lake Worth to the river after an average of 21 days, with mean daily movement rates peaking at 955 m/d during the river re-entry phase.
