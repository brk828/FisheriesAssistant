# Alabama Agricultural Experiment Station Bulletin 254

## Context
> This circular, published by the Alabama Agricultural Experiment Station, presents eight years of controlled pond experiments (20–100 ponds) investigating the reproductive biology of [[Bluegill]] (*Lepomis macrochirus*) and [[Largemouth Black Bass]] (*Micropterus salmoides*) in managed farm ponds....
