# Snaketown

## Summary
Snaketown is a Hohokam site with Santa Cruz-Sacaton and Sacaton phase occupations. Its faunal assemblage shows remarkable similarity to La Lomita Pequeña, with lagomorphs representing 75% of identified fauna, artiodactyls 21%, and rodents 4%, suggesting stable subsistence strategies across the Hohokam region during the Sedentary period.
