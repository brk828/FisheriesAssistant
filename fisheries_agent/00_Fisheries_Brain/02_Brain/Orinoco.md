# Orinoco

## Summary
The Orinoco is a major South American river basin to which the Rio Meta system belongs. The newly described species Hemigrammus barrigonae and Hyphessobrycon metae, both collected from the Rio Meta in Colombia, represent additions to the ichthyofauna of this basin.
