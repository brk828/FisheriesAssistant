# Food and Drug Administration

## Summary
The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is the U.S. federal agency responsible for establishing residue tolerances for chemicals in food products. According to this 1968 manual, the FDA had not established residue tolerances for fish or shellfish for any herbicide at that time, meaning fish and shellfish from herbicide-treated waters could not be sold for human consumption.

## Context

> This 1968 manual from the [[Bureau of Sport Fisheries and Wildlife]] provides comprehensive guidance on herbicide use for vegetation control in fish and wildlife management programs.
