# Fish Collection Facility

## Summary
A fish collection facility is an engineered structure designed to maintain ecological connectivity and fish passage between water management units. In this engineering scheme for Pond L-7, fish collection facilities are being constructed with inlet culverts from the main canal, with surrounding pond topography being regraded to accommodate the facility, explicitly addressing the need for fish movement during flood management operations.
