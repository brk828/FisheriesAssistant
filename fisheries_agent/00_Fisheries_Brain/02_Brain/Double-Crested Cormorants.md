# Double-Crested Cormorants

## Summary
Double-Crested Cormorants (Phalacrocorax auritus) are piscivorous birds identified as major predators of stocked bonytail in Lake Havasu. Multiple acoustic and PIT tags were recovered under their roosting sites on power lines, and their populations peak in Arizona during March-April and October-November, coinciding with typical bonytail stocking periods.
