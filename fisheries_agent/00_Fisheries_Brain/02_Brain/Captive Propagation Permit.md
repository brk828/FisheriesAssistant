# Captive Propagation Permit

## Summary
The Captive Propagation Permit was a proposed regulatory framework in the 1994 petition that would allow licensed individuals to breed and sell offspring of specific Arizona native reptile and amphibian species under strict conditions. The permit system included requirements for PIT-tagging breeding stock, species-specific size limits for sale, veterinary oversight, detailed record-keeping, facility inspections, and prohibitions on selling wild-caught animals or breeding stock.
