# Rio Esmeraldas

## Summary
The Rio Esmeraldas is a river system in Ecuador that drains the western folds of Mt. Pichincha. Rhoadsia minor was collected from the Rio Blanco, a tributary of this system, at 4,000 feet elevation in extremely humid sub-tropical forests.
