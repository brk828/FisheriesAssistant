# Utah Division of Wildlife

## Summary
The Utah Division of Wildlife is a state wildlife management agency that proposed introducing Rainbow Smelt into Lake Powell in 1990 to provide additional forage for Striped Bass. The proposal acknowledged that smelt would likely spread downstream through Grand Canyon National Park into Lake Mead and Lake Mohave, but concluded that endangered endemic fishes would face minimal interaction and competition with the introduced species.
