# Ataeniobius

## Summary
Ataeniobius is a monotypic goodeid genus containing only Ataeniobius toweri, placed in its own subfamily Ataeniobiinae. This source notes it is considered the most primitive goodeid due to its lack of embryonic nutritive rectal processes (trophotaeniae).
