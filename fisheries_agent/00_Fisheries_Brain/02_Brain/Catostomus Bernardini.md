# Catostomus Bernardini

## Summary
Catostomus bernardini is a native sucker species (Family Catostomidae) endemic to the Rio Yaqui basin in northwestern Mexico, documented in ichthyological surveys of the region during the 1980s as part of conservation assessments of native fish assemblages. This species was among the target taxa for specimen collection efforts in the lower Rio Yaqui, reflecting concerns about the status and distribution of native fishes in a basin increasingly impacted by exotic species introductions and habitat degradation. Like other native Rio Yaqui fishes, C. bernardini likely occupied uppermost tributary and effluent habitats, where native species showed concentrated occurrence patterns distinct from the main channel distributions of introduced exotic species.
