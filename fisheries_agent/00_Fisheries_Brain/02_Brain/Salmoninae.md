# Salmoninae

## Summary
Salmoninae is a subfamily of salmonid fishes that includes species like Rainbow Trout, Brook Trout, and Brown Trout. According to this source, Salmoninae are the most susceptible fish group to electrofishing injuries, with injury incidence rates documented as high as 98%, particularly for spinal damage when exposed to electrical fields.
