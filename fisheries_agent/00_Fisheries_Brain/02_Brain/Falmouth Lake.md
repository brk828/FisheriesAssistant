# Falmouth Lake

## Summary
Falmouth Lake is a 225-acre impoundment in northern Kentucky with a maximum depth of 42 feet and volume of 4,630 acre-feet. This source reports it was used as a test site for diffused air destratification in 1966, requiring five mixing operations and 134 hours to achieve near-uniform temperature, with results showing 86% reduction in sulfides, 27% reduction in iron, and prevention of autumnal blue-green algal blooms.
