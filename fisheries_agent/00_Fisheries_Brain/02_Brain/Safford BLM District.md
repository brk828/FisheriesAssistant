# Safford BLM District

## Summary
The Safford BLM District is a Bureau of Land Management administrative area identified for experimental population introductions of endangered fish. The 1986 recovery team meeting discussed plans for the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to prepare an experimental population rule for stocking Gila Topminnows into approximately 30 sites on Safford BLM District lands.
