# Rio Grande Darter

## Summary
The Rio Grande Darter (Etheostoma grahami) is a small fish species whose early life history was documented at this conference. Studies showed that its eggs average 1.7 mm in diameter, larvae hatch at 5.0 mm total length, and caudal and pectoral fin rays begin development at 6.7 mm.
