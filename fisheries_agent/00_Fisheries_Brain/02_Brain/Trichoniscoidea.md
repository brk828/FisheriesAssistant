# Trichoniscoidea

## Summary
Trichoniscoidea is the superfamily to which Xilitloniscus laevis actually belongs, as determined by examination of male pleopod morphology. This placement corrects the original classification and is based on fundamental differences in the form and musculature of male pleopod 1 compared to members of Styloniscoidea.

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
