# Wallop-Breaux

## Summary
Wallop-Breaux is a federal funding program that supports state fish and wildlife activities. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service noted that Utah's Division of Wildlife Resources receives funding through Wallop-Breaux (along with Section 6 programs), which constitutes federal involvement in their activities and recommended considering withdrawal of such funding if the state would not comply with Endangered Species Act consultation requirements for the proposed rainbow smelt introduction.
