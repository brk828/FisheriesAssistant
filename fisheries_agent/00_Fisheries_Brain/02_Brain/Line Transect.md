# Line Transect

## Summary
Line transect is a distance sampling method where observers travel along predetermined linear paths (transects) and record perpendicular or radial distances to detected animals. This source emphasizes that transects should be randomly placed across the grain of topography and landscape, with laser rangefinders and GPS units used to precisely measure distances and locations of detected animals. The method requires that animals on or very near the centerline are detected with certainty (g(0) = 1), and failure to meet this assumption causes severe negative bias in population estimates.
