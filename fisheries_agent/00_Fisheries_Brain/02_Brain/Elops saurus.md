# Elops saurus

## Summary
Elops saurus is the Atlantic relative of Elops affinis (the ten-pounder) and is considered an equivalent game fish species. The authors reference this species to establish that ten-pounders should provide excellent sport fishing opportunities in the Salton Sea, as both species share similar sporting qualities including taking bait and artificial lures and making long leaps.
