# Canyon Ferry

## Summary
Canyon Ferry is a 35,200-acre man-made reservoir in Montana that serves as one of four reservoirs where the state's commercial fisheries operate. It is located east of the Continental Divide and contains approximately 20 fish species, with commercial value concentrated in Carp, Buffalofish, Goldeye, and Sucker species.
