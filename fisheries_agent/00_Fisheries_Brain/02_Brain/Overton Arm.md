# Overton Arm

## Summary
Overton Arm is one of the four major sections of Lake Mead reservoir. The study established three water-quality sampling stations within this section: Miner's Cove, Echo Bay, and Temple Bar, with Echo Bay showing comparable low primary productivity values to Temple Bar, while Miner's Cove showed increased productivity in each survey.
