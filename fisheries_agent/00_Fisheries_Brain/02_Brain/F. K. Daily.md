# F. K. Daily

## Summary
F. K. Daily was a botanist from Indiana who provided Characeae specimens to support the cytotaxonomic research project. She was one of several collaborators who contributed materials from various locations to expand the study's geographic scope.

## Context

> This progress report covers research conducted under NSF Research Grant G-19449 (continuation of NSF-G13261) from January 1, 1962, documenting cytotaxonomic investigations of the [[Characeae]] (stoneworts) at the [[University of Louisville]].
