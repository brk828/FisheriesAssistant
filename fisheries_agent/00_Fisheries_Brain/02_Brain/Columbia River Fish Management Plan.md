# Columbia River Fish Management Plan

## Summary
The Columbia River Fish Management Plan (CRFMP) is a framework adopted in 1988 for protecting, rebuilding and enhancing salmon runs and allocating in-river harvest activities. This case references the CRFMP as part of the broader habitat and management context for Snake River salmon, building upon decades of state-tribal fishing allocation disputes dating to the late 1960s.
