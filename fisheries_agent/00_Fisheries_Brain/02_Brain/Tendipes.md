# Tendipes

## Summary
Tendipes are midge larvae (family Chironomidae) that are benthic detritivores/filter feeders dwelling in lake bottom sediments. In Hartlaub Lake, the Tendipes population exploded from approximately 10 larvae per square foot to over 600 larvae per square foot four months after toxaphene treatment, likely due to release from predation pressure by both Chaoborus larvae and fish.

## Context
