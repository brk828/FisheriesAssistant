# San Francisco Peaks

## Summary
San Francisco Peaks is a mountain range in northern Arizona that was the site of important ecological research. The lecture notes reference C. Hart Merriam's late 1800s work on San Francisco Peaks, which established the concept of distinct altitudinal life zones that Arizona exhibits from montane tundra to desert.
