# Wood Ducks

## Summary
Wood Ducks (Aix sponsa) are waterfowl that breed in beaver impoundments. According to this source, summer brood censuses in New Hampshire consistently showed that beaver flowages produced Wood Duck broods, making these impoundments valuable waterfowl habitat that wildlife managers sought to preserve through water level control techniques.

## Context

> This 1948-1949 progress report documents experimental techniques developed by the [[New Hampshire Fish and Game Department]] to control water levels in [[American Beaver]] impoundments using perforated drain pipes.
