# Saratoga Springs Pupfish

## Summary
The Saratoga Springs Pupfish is a relict endemic species inhabiting isolated geothermal spring systems within the Death Valley/Amargosa drainage basin, representing a living fossil population adapted to highly specialized alkaline aquatic environments. As a member of the Cyprinodontidae family, this pupfish exemplifies the conservation challenges facing desert spring endemics, which face extinction risks from habitat degradation, groundwater depletion, invasive species, and extremely limited geographic ranges. The species serves as a critical indicator of desert aquatic ecosystem health and a priority for conservation management within the Death Valley region's endemic fish assemblage.
