# Arizona State Land Department

## Summary
The Arizona State Land Department is a state agency responsible for managing state-owned lands. It was included as one of the participating agencies with representation on the BIOTA Project's Steering Committee, ensuring state land management perspectives were incorporated into the biodiversity assessment process.
