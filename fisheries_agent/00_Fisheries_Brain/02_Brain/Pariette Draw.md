# Pariette Draw

## Summary
Pariette Draw is a location in the Upper Colorado River Basin that was included in reconnaissance surveys for contaminants conducted between 1986-1989. The surveys documented elevated selenium levels in this area as part of the broader assessment of contamination threats to endangered fish species throughout the basin.
