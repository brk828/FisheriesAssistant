# Linesville Creek

## Summary
Linesville Creek in Crawford County, Pennsylvania is one of the freshwater systems from which aquatic samples were collected for calorific analysis. The creek provided detritus and aquatic organism samples including substrates like roots, leaves, and bark from stream systems.
