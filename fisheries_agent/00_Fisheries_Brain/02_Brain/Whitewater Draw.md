# Whitewater Draw

## Summary
Whitewater Draw is a tributary drainage system in the Rio Yaqui basin of southeastern Arizona that has served as a focus for ichthyological surveys assessing native fish fauna composition and distribution within the southwestern United States. Beyond its contemporary ichthyological interest, the system is significant to fisheries and conservation science for its paleoenvironmental and paleohydrological records, which document shifts between braided stream conditions during wetter Pleistocene periods and more arid Holocene conditions that would have substantially altered aquatic habitat availability and fish community structure. Understanding the Late Quaternary alluvial history and climate changes recorded in Whitewater Draw's stratigraphy provides essential context for interpreting present-day fish assemblages and informing conservation strategies for native species in this arid-region drainage.
