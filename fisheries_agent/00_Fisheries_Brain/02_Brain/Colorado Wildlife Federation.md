# Colorado Wildlife Federation

## Summary
The Colorado Wildlife Federation is one of the participating organizations in the Upper Colorado River Recovery Program. This wildlife conservation organization joined the 1988 cooperative agreement alongside government agencies, water user groups, and other environmental organizations to implement recovery efforts for endangered fish species in the Upper Colorado River Basin.
