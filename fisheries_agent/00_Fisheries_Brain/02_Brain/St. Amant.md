# St. Amant

## Summary
J. A. St. Amant is the author of this 1966 progress report on the culture of Tilapia mossambica hybrids in southern California. The report documents experimental work conducted by the California Department of Fish and Game on producing and evaluating all-male tilapia hybrids.

## Context

> This 1966 progress report documents the [[California Department of Fish and Game]]'s experimental culture of all-male [[Tilapia mossambica]] hybrids produced by crossing Zanzibari males with Javanese females.
