# David C. Haskell

## Summary
David C. Haskell was a researcher who developed formulas in 1955 and 1959 relating trout carrying capacity to metabolic rate and feeding levels. This source cites his equation for calculating feeding percentages from conversion rates and daily length increase, and his work on determining permissible fish weight per cubic foot based on food consumption rates.

## Context

> This U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service circular (Circular 159, October 1963, with April 1964 correction) provides comprehensive guidance on feeding protocols and nutritional management for hatchery-reared trout.
