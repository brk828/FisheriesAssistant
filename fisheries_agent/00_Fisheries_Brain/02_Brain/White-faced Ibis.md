# White-faced Ibis

## Summary
White-faced Ibis are among the bird species documented at the Ciénega de Santa Clara wetland. This source lists them as one of the species found at this major stopover point for migratory birds in the Colorado River Delta.

## Context

> This article examines the ecological degradation of the [[Colorado River Delta]] and proposes strategies for reallocating water to restore and protect this critical aquatic ecosystem.
