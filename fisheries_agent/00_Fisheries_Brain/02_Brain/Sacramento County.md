# Sacramento County

## Summary
Sacramento County was the second-ranked striped bass county in 1960 with 993 fish reported from 93 reports, and the fourth-ranked black bass county with 872 fish reported from 35 reports. It is part of Region 2, which was one of two major striped bass regions and dominated catfish production in the state.
