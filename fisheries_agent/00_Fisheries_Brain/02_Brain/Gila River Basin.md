# Gila River Basin

## Summary
The Gila River Basin is a critical arid-region watershed in the southwestern United States (primarily Arizona and New Mexico) that supports endemic and threatened native fish species adapted to desert stream conditions, making it a focal area for aquatic conservation efforts. This basin encompasses diverse aquatic habitats including riparian corridors, lotic (flowing water) reaches, and tributaries that harbor specialized species such as spikedace, loach minnows, and Sonoran topminnows, many of which are federally listed as endangered or sensitive. Management and recovery activities in the Gila River Basin are coordinated among multiple agencies and conservation teams to address threats from habitat degradation, invasive species, and hydrological alterations while attempting to maintain or restore native fish populations.
