# United Technologies

## Summary
United Technologies was one of three private sector companies that provided financial support for the publication of the 1987 Arizona Environmental & Resource Conservation Directory. The company's contribution helped fund this comprehensive catalog of environmental and resource conservation organizations in Arizona.

## Context

> This comprehensive directory, published by the Commission on the Arizona Environment in 1987, catalogs businesses, organizations, and agencies concerned with environmental resources in Arizona. The document provides contact information and descriptions for over 200 entities across multiple sectors...
