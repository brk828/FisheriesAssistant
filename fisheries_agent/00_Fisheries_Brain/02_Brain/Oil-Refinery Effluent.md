# Oil-Refinery Effluent

## Summary
Oil-refinery effluent is wastewater discharged from petroleum refining operations, including crude units, coking units, catalytic crackers, catalytic reformers, thermal reformers, and compound houses. In this study, the effluents tested had pH values of 8.9-10.0, contained 65-94 ppm ammonia, 4.2-29 ppm phenol, 0-1.6 ppm sulfide, and had chemical oxygen demand of 260-465 ppm. The effluent's pH initially made test solutions alkaline but shifted toward neutrality during bioassays due to fish respiration and volatilization of compounds.
