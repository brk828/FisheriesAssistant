# Trichoniscidae

## Summary
Trichoniscidae is the family to which the genus Xilitloniscus belongs, specifically within its most primitive division according to Vandel's classification system. The family placement is supported by the form and musculature of male pleopod 1, which shows the characteristic "type trichoniscien" rather than "type styloniscien."

## Context

> This taxonomic revision establishes **Xilitloniscus** as a new genus for the Mexican cave isopod previously described as *Cordioniscus laevis* by [[Enrique Rioja]] in 1956.
