# Cotter River

## Summary
The Cotter River catchment in the Australian Capital Territory, Australia, was the collection location for this study, specifically three tributary streams: Kangaroo, Lees, and Pierces creeks. Mountain Galaxias were collected from these streams using backpack electrofishing on two dates in June 2017 for the subsequent PIT tagging trial conducted in aquarium conditions.
