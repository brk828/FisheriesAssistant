# Amphigonopterus aurora

## Summary
Amphigonopterus aurora is one of two herbivorous species in the specialized Micrometrinae subfamily that inhabits rock-pools of the central California coast. This source establishes Amphigonopterus as a new genus with this species (formerly Abeona aurora) as the genotype.
