# Centrarchid Life History

## Summary
The source documents multiple Centrarchidae (sunfish family) species introduced to Arizona, including Largemouth Bass, Smallmouth Bass, Spotted Bass, Rock Bass, and Warmouth Bass. These species established in various warm-water habitats including lakes, reservoirs, river channels, streams, and ranch ponds, though specific age, maturity, fecundity, and growth parameters were not reported.
