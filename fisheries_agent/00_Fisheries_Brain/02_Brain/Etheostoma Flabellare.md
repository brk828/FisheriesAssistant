# Etheostoma Flabellare

## Summary
Etheostoma flabellare, commonly known as the Fantail Darter, is a small benthic darter species found in flowing freshwater streams and rivers of eastern North America, particularly in the Upper Allegheny River drainage and other Appalachian watersheds. This species is notable for its ecological preference for riffle habitats, where it occupies the lower portions of current-swept areas, and has been extensively studied in comparative ichthyological surveys examining darter community composition, sex ratios, microhabitat selection, and physiological tolerance to water quality variations. As a headwater and mainstream resident, the Fantail Darter serves as an important indicator organism in aquatic ecology research and stream conservation assessments due to its sensitivity to habitat degradation and its role in freshwater fish communities.
