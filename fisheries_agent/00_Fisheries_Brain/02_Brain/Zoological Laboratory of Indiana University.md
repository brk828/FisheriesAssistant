# Zoological Laboratory of Indiana University

## Summary
The Zoological Laboratory of Indiana University is the institutional affiliation of the authors of this 1914 taxonomic study. Carl H. Eigenmann and Arthur Henn conducted their research and species descriptions under the auspices of this laboratory.
