# Jack Salmon

## Summary
Jack Salmon, also known as walleye (family Percidae), was recently introduced into some Kansas state lakes by 1947 with "considerable success," though the guide notes it was too early to make definitive statements about the introduction. The guide suggests it might be more suitable than gar as a predator for controlling seriously overstocked lakes.
