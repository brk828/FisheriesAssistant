# Tabanidae

## Summary
Tabanidae are horse fly larvae that were found in the bottom fauna of Holbrook Lake Reservoir. They comprised only 0.4% of bottom organisms by number, with the bottom fauna being dominated by Chironomus (midge fly) larvae at 89.2%.
