# Tucson Aqueduct

## Summary
The Tucson Aqueduct is one of the authorized geographic locations for CAP fish and wildlife enhancement activities. It is listed among the authorized project features where expenditure of CAP appropriations for enhancement could be associated under the current legal interpretation.
