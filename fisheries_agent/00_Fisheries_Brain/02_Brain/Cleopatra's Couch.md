# Cleopatra's Couch

## Summary
Cleopatra's Couch is a critical spawning reach at river mile 16.5 on the Yampa River where CRFP radiotelemetry and larval collections clearly demonstrated successful Colorado Squawfish spawning in 1981. This 1982 study found spawning habitat was relatively high and increasing with discharge (analyzed for 250-1,200 cfs range), achieving the highest values among either Yampa or White River sites when considering pool interspersion, though YOY habitat was quite limited due to lack of fine sediment with shallow water and the reach was not considered an important resident area for adults post-spawning.
