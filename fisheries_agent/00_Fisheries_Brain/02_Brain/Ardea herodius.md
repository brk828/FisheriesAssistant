# Ardea herodius

## Summary
Ardea herodius is the great blue heron, a large wading bird. Along with other heron species, A. herodius is identified as a piscivorous predator of woundfin (Plagopterus argentissimus) in the Virgin River system, especially during low flow and clear water conditions when fish are more exposed.
