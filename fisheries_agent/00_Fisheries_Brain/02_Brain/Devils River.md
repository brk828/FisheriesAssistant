# Devils River

## Summary
The Devils River is a tributary of the Rio Grande where Ictalurus lupus occurs, but evidence of hybridization with I. punctatus was detected in specimens from this location. The lower Devils River was inundated by Amistad Reservoir, and stocking of I. punctatus in the reservoir likely introduced the hybridization threat; the authors recommended monitoring this population undergoing introgression.
