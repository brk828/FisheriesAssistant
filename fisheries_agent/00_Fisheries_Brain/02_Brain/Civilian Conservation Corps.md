# Civilian Conservation Corps

## Summary
The Civilian Conservation Corps was proposed by Maxwell as the training mechanism for creating 500,000 trained 'forest-farmers and Homecrofters' to implement the Rural Settlements System. Maxwell envisioned 50,000 trained workers per project, coordinating with the U.S. Soil Conservation Service and Forest Service to prepare workers for small-scale intensive farming and forest management.
