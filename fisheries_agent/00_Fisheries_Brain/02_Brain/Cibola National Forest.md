# Cibola National Forest

## Summary
Cibola National Forest is a national forest in New Mexico that contains watersheds studied for gully development. This source examined two watersheds on this forest—the Silverhill-Montosa Watershed and the Monica Watershed—which demonstrated different patterns of active gully expansion during the mid-20th century.
