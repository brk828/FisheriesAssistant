# Arapaho National Forest

## Summary
The Arapaho National Forest is a U.S. Forest Service managed area in Colorado where several cities (Boulder, Fort Collins, Greeley, Loveland) and utilities had water projects requiring special use permit renewals. In the 1990s, these permit renewals triggered ESA Section 7 consultation requirements, contributing to the need for development of the Central Platte River Recovery Program.
