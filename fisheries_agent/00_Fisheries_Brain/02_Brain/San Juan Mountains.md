# San Juan Mountains

## Summary
The San Juan Mountains are a mountain range in southwestern Colorado where the Rio Grande originates. This source documents that during middle to late Pliocene time, the Rio Grande flowed south from the San Juan Mountains across the Taos Plateau, establishing the headwaters of what would become New Mexico's largest drainage system.
