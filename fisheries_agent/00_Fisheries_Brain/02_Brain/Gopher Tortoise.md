# Gopher Tortoise

## Summary
Gopher Tortoise (eastern population) is a reptile that was included as a candidate taxon in the 1991 review for possible listing under the Endangered Species Act. The eastern population of this tortoise species was being evaluated separately from other populations for potential endangered or threatened status.
