# Duchesne Rivers

## Summary
The Duchesne River is a tributary in the Colorado River Basin where razorback suckers have been documented. Radio-telemetry studies found adult razorback suckers utilizing the main channel of the Duchesne River outside the breeding season, selecting habitats with depths of 0.6-3.4 meters over sand and silt substrates with water velocities of 0.1-0.6 meters per second.
