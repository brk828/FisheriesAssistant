# Common Chuckwalla

## Summary
Common Chuckwalla (Sauromalus obesus) is a Candidate Category 2 (C2) reptile species that was confirmed present on PPMR buttes both north and south of McDowell Road. This species primarily occupies rocky structures with cracks and crevices for cover and occasionally moves onto nearby flats to feed, leading to recommendations that buttes not be modified.
