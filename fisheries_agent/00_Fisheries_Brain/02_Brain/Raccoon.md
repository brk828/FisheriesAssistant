# Raccoon

## Summary
The Raccoon (Procyon lotor) is a nocturnal furbearer characterized by its distinctive black mask and bushy ringed tail. This Michigan source reports that raccoons occur throughout the state in wooded, well-watered areas, breed in February after leaving hibernation, and produce 2-6 young about 63 days later. Males help rear the young, which stay with the family for an extended time.
