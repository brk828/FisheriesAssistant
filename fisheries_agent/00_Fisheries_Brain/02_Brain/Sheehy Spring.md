# Sheehy Spring

## Summary
Sheehy Spring is a desert spring locality within the Gila River basin (likely in Arizona) that serves as a significant reference site in Gila Topminnow conservation and monitoring efforts. Based on its appearance in status reports and recovery meeting minutes, it appears to be one of the historical or rediscovered populations of this endangered fish species, contributing important data to understanding the distribution and persistence of Gila Topminnows across their fragmented range in southwestern desert aquatic ecosystems.
