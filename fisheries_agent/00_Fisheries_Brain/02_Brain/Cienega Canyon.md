# Cienega Canyon

## Summary
Cienega Canyon is one of several tributary waterways that drain into the Blue Ridge Dam reservoir on the north side of the Mogollon Rim. It is part of the watershed that feeds into East Clear Creek and contributes runoff to the reservoir created by the dam.
