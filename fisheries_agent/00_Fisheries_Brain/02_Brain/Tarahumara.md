# Tarahumara

## Summary
The Tarahumara are an indigenous community in the Sierra Madre Occidental of Chihuahua, Mexico to whom Consejo Asesor Sierra Madre provides technical and legal services. The organization's work with the Tarahumara includes conservation efforts for species that cross the U.S.-Mexico border and are affected by U.S. Endangered Species Act enforcement.
