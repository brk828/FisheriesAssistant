# Laurel Canyon

## Summary
Laurel Canyon is the location on Aravaipa Creek where the Klondyke Tailings are situated, 2.1 miles upstream of Stowe Gulch and 1.2 miles downstream from the hamlet of Klondyke. This source documents that a stock pond at the confluence of Laurel Canyon and Aravaipa Creek receives runoff from the northwest corner of the lower tailings pile and showed elevated trace metals including a violation of surface water quality standards for dissolved copper (0.029 mg/l exceeding the 0.018 mg/l standard for aquatic and wildlife warm water acute).
