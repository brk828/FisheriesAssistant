# Ecosystems Biology

## Summary
Ecosystems Biology is an NSF program that indirectly supports genetics research through its Long Term Ecological Research (LTER) projects. This source identifies it as one of the programs within the Division of Biotic Systems and Resources that contributes to NSF's overall support for evolutionary and ecological genetics, though it does not provide specific funding figures for this program.
