# Craterocephalus

## Summary
Craterocephalus is a genus of hardyhead fish found in Australian desert springs. This bibliography documents multiple endemic species from the Great Artesian Basin, including species described by Ivantsoff and colleagues in the 1970s-1990s that evolved in isolation in spring habitats.
