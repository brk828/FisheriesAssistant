# Lake Pillsbury

## Summary
Lake Pillsbury is a reservoir where downstream migration of stocked trout was documented during spilling conditions. The study found that 12% of planted trout migrated downstream into the Eel River, demonstrating how water management operations can affect the retention of stocked fish in reservoir systems.
