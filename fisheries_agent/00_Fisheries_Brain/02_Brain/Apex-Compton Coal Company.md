# Apex-Compton Coal Company

## Summary
Apex-Compton Coal Company was one of three coal companies that donated strip-land for public fishing development in southeastern Kansas. The company's land donation contributed to the 600 acres of strip-pit lakes managed for public fishing.

## Context

> This report documents fisheries management activities in reclaimed coal strip-pit lakes across southeastern Kansas (Crawford, Cherokee, and Linn Counties) from approximately 1951 through spring 1956.
