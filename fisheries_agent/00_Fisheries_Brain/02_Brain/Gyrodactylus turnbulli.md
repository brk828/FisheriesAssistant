# Gyrodactylus turnbulli

## Summary
Gyrodactylus turnbulli is a monogenean fluke parasite native to wild guppies in Trinidad that lives on body surfaces, mainly gills, and multiplies asexually. This source reports that guppies have spread worldwide and now persist in several warm springs in Arizona, potentially bringing this parasite into contact with endangered Gila Topminnow populations. The source describes planned research to determine Gila Topminnow resistance to this fluke across four remaining watersheds and investigate whether MHC variation and inbreeding affect resistance, noting that previous research by Lyles found inbreeding generally increased susceptibility to this parasite in guppies.
