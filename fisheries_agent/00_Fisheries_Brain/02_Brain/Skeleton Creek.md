# Skeleton Creek

## Summary
Skeleton Creek is a permanent stream in north-central Oklahoma that originates northeast of Enid and flows approximately 75 miles south-southeasterly through Garfield, Kingfisher, and Logan Counties before entering the Cimarron River. This study examined fish populations along a 60-mile gradient of the creek from 1963-1964, documenting how oil refinery and sewage effluents affected species diversity and abundance, with 18,917 fish representing 24 species collected during the year-long investigation.
