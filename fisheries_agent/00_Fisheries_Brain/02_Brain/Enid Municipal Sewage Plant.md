# Enid Municipal Sewage Plant

## Summary
The Enid Municipal Sewage Plant is a secondary treatment facility that discharges 2-2.5 million gallons per day of treated sewage into Boggy Creek, one mile above its junction with Skeleton Creek. The plant's effluent, combined with oil refinery discharge, created conditions that influenced fish distribution and abundance downstream, though dilute sewage at upstream stations appeared to have fertilizing effects that increased fish populations without seriously affecting dissolved oxygen.
