# Diamond Creek

## Summary
Diamond Creek is a tributary of the Colorado River located at River Mile 225.7 in the Grand Canyon, serving as a significant sampling location and ecological reference point in aquatic studies of the Colorado River ecosystem downstream of Glen Canyon Dam. The creek and its surrounding reach are important habitats for native fish species, particularly tributary-associated species like speckled dace, and represent a key area for monitoring the effects of dam operations on fish communities and aquatic ecosystems in the lower Colorado River system.
