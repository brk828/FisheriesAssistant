# Odocoileus

## Summary
Odocoileus (deer) represents 10% of identified mammals at La Lomita Pequeña, with 4 specimens identified and 50% showing burning. Deer metatarsals were the primary raw material for bone artifact manufacture, particularly long awls, and an antler tine fragment showed slight polish suggesting use as a lithic flaker.
