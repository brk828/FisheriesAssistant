# Coeur d'Alene River

## Summary
The Coeur d'Alene River is a river system in Idaho where biologists have studied Northern Pike predation impacts on trout populations. Research from this system estimated that a population of 600 to 1,000 Northern Pike could consume between 2,500 and 4,200 trout per year, data that was used to project potential impacts at Parker Canyon Lake.
