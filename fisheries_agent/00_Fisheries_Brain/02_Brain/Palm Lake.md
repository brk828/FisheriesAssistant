# Palm Lake

## Summary
Palm Lake is an artificial impounded reservoir located within the Hassayampa River Preserve in Arizona's Sonoran Desert that served as a critical management facility for native freshwater fish conservation in the Colorado River Basin and Gila River drainage. In the context of aquatic conservation biology, Palm Lake functioned as an ex situ refuge and breeding facility for imperiled native fish species facing displacement by non-native competitors, representing a key component of regional native fish restoration efforts during the 1980s.
