# Pahranagat Pebblesnail

## Summary
The Pahranagat Pebblesnail (Fluminicola merriami) is a hydrobiid snail found in Pahranagat Valley. It was among the species affected by documented extirpations from multiple springs in the Pahranagat and White River valleys during surveys conducted in 1990 and 1991.
