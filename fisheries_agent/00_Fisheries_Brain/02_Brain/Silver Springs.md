# Silver Springs

## Summary
Silver Springs is a thermostatic artesian spring system in Florida used for comparative analysis in the Blue River study. Its headwater had annual primary production of 6.8 kg O₂/m²/year with a P/R ratio of 1.06, which was approached but not exceeded by the Blue River's most productive granite reach (7.8 kg O₂/m²/year).
