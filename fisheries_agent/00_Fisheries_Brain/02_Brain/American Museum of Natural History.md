# American Museum of Natural History

## Summary
The American Museum of Natural History was referenced in the proposal as the parent institution of the Southwestern Research Station at Portal, Arizona. This research station served as a model for the type of field facility that ASU's planned Arizona Museum of Natural History and Culture could develop.
