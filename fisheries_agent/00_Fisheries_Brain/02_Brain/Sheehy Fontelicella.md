# Sheehy Fontelicella

## Summary
Sheehy Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species restricted to a 5-meter diameter headspring area at Sheehy Spring in Santa Cruz County, Arizona, with extremely high density (5 individuals per square centimeter). Discovered in 1976 and monitored through 1978-1980, the population showed slight increases and reduced cattle activity, and co-occurs with Poeciliopsis occidentalis occidentalis and Gila intermedia.
