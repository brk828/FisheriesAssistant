# Auburn

## Summary
Auburn, Alabama was the location of the Agricultural Experiment Station where winter feeding experiments on fathead minnows were conducted in fertilized rearing ponds. Previous experiments at Auburn had shown that even with supplemental feeding during fall and winter, approximately 15% of minnows failed to reach salable size within one year.
