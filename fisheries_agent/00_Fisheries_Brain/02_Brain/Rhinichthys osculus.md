# Rhinichthys Osculus

## Summary
Rhinichthys osculus, commonly known as the speckled dace, is a small native cyprinid fish species found in western North American freshwater systems, particularly in the Colorado River drainage. This species typically comprises a minor component of fish communities in its range, often representing less than 5% of small fish populations in survey samples, and serves as an important indicator organism for assessing the structure and composition of native fish assemblages in southwestern aquatic ecosystems. As a native species, R. osculus is of conservation interest in regions where non-native fish introductions have altered community composition and threaten endemic ichthyofauna.
