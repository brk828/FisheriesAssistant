# Desert Minnow

## Summary
Desert Minnow (Cyprinodon macularius) is a small fish native to the Salton Sink that serves as a primary prey species for ten-pounders in the Salton Sea. Stomach content analysis found that ten-pounders consumed 1-34 desert minnows per fish (ranging 1-2 inches in length), though the authors noted that desert minnow populations might not be sufficient to sustain a large ten-pounder population.
