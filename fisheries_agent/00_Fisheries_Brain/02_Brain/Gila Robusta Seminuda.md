# Gila Robusta Seminuda

## Summary
Gila robusta seminuda, commonly known as the Virgin River chub, is a subspecies of the roundtail chub endemic to the Virgin River drainage in the southwestern United States, distinguished by its smaller size and specific morphological characteristics compared to other Gila robusta populations. This taxon has been the subject of significant taxonomic revision and conservation concern, with recovery planning efforts focused on protecting its limited habitat and addressing threats to its continued survival in an increasingly water-stressed region. As a native cyprinid fish, it represents an important component of the Virgin River's aquatic ecosystem and has been a priority for fisheries management and conservation biology research since the 1980s.
