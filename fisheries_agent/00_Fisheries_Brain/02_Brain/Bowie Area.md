# Bowie Area

## Summary
The Bowie Area is one of the principal agricultural regions in San Simon Valley where irrigation relies on pumped wells rather than flowing artesian wells. This source indicates that in this area, artesian water rises under pressure but remains below the surface, requiring pumping for agricultural use.
