# Rough Fish

## Summary
Rough fish is a term used in fisheries management to describe non-game fish species that are considered undesirable or of low commercial and recreational value, often including suckers, carp, and other species targeted for removal from water bodies. These fish were frequently the focus of selective harvesting programs using commercial fishing gear in mid-20th century management efforts aimed at improving conditions for more valued game fish species. The classification reflects the conservation priorities and management philosophies of that era, when removal of rough fish was considered a legitimate strategy to enhance fishery productivity in reservoirs and lakes.
