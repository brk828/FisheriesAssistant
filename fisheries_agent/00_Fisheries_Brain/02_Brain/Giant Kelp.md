# Giant Kelp

## Summary
Giant Kelp refers to kelp species, particularly in the genus Macrocystis, that serve as indicators of cool-water conditions. During the Wisconsin glaciation, Giant Kelp and other cool-water species like the Pacific Sardine were able to disperse across then-cooled tropical waters, connecting temperate regions and providing evidence that sea-surface temperatures dropped approximately 3°C in winter and 8°C in summer during that period.
