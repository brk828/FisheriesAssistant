# Tucson

## Summary
Tucson is a major city in central Arizona served by the Central Arizona Project and identified as having multiple fish and wildlife enhancement opportunities. Recharge projects in the Tucson area were identified as one of five priority enhancement categories, with specific proposals including preliminary design work on recharge projects incorporating riparian habitat restoration, urban fishery opportunities, and improvement of water quality in Lakeside Lake near Tucson.
