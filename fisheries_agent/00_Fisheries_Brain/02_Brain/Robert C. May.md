# Robert C. May

## Summary
Robert C. May is the author of this 1971 NOAA technical report compiling an annotated bibliography of marine fish larval rearing attempts. He conducted an extended literature search covering 91 years of studies (1878-1969) to support researchers at the Bureau of Commercial Fisheries Fishery-Oceanography Center in La Jolla.
