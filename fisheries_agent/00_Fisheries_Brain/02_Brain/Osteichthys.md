# Osteichthys

## Summary
Osteichthys is the class-level taxonomic classification for the Pahrump Killifish, representing bony fishes. It falls between Chordata (phylum) and Cyprinodontiformes (order) in the species' taxonomic hierarchy.

## Context

> The [[Pahrump Killifish]] (*Empetrichthys latos latos* Miller) is an endangered desert spring fish originally endemic to three spring systems in [[Pahrump Valley]], southern [[Nye County]], [[Nevada]].
