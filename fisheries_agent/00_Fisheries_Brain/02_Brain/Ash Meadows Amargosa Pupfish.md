# Ash Meadows Amargosa Pupfish

## Summary
The Ash Meadows Amargosa Pupfish (Cyprinodon nevadensis mionectes) was extirpated from Jackrabbit and Forest springs in Ash Meadows due to direct pumping from these springs during the late 1960s and early 1970s. This extirpation demonstrates the severe impacts of groundwater pumping on endemic desert fish species.
