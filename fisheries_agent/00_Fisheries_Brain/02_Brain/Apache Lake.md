# Apache Lake

## Summary
Apache Lake is a warm, monomictic desert reservoir on the Salt River in central Arizona that serves as an important study system for understanding fish distribution, community composition, and ecological responses to anthropogenic disturbance in arid aquatic ecosystems. The lake supports introduced sport and forage fish species including largemouth bass, black crappie, threadfin shad, and carp, with a resident community dominated by lentic (quiet-water) adapted species that may be vulnerable to environmental alterations such as those induced by pump-storage hydroelectric operations. As a multi-purpose impoundment built under the Reclamation Act of 1902, Apache Lake has been the subject of ichthyological research examining fish distribution patterns and habitat use, making it a significant reference system for desert reservoir ecology and fisheries management in the southwestern United States.
