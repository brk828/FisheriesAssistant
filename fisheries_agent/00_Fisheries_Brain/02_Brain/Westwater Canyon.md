# Westwater Canyon

## Summary
Westwater Canyon is a geomorphologically significant reach of the Upper Colorado River system, located between the Piceance Creek area and Cataract Canyon, that serves as critical habitat for native fish species including the Colorado Pikeminnow and other sympatric fishes in regional monitoring and conservation studies. As a defined study segment within the larger Colorado River basin, Westwater Canyon has been a focal point for interagency standardized monitoring protocols and investigations into how flow regimes, physical habitat structure, and canyon morphology influence native fish population dynamics and early life stage survival. This canyon reach represents an important reference area for understanding river ecosystem responses and evaluating the effectiveness of water management and conservation strategies targeting endangered and threatened fish species across the upper Colorado River system.
