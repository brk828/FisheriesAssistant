# Ants

## Summary
Ants (family Formicidae) were reported by Cox to knock their heads against leaves simultaneously when alarmed. This source dismisses this observation as observer illusion, arguing that true synchronism would require ants to perceive time relations and anticipate sounds, capabilities the author considers unlikely and attributes the apparent synchrony to psychological bias in the observer.
