# Leon Springs Pupfish

## Summary
The Leon Springs Pupfish is a small cyprinodontid fish species endemic to limited freshwater habitats in the southwestern United States, representing a critically endangered or extinct-in-the-wild taxon that has served as both a conservation priority and a subject of ecological research. As a highly specialized inhabitant of isolated spring systems with restricted ranges, this pupfish exemplifies the vulnerability of desert aquatic species to habitat loss and environmental degradation, making it a focal species for conservation efforts and aquatic ecosystem management. The species has been utilized in research protocols examining surrogate species selection and contaminant impacts on non-game fishes, contributing to broader understanding of how endemic species respond to environmental stressors and informing conservation strategies for other imperiled freshwater taxa.
