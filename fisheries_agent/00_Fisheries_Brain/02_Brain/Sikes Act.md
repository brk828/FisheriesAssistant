# Sikes Act

## Summary
The Sikes Act is a foundational federal statute that grants the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service authority to manage fish and wildlife resources and recreational activities on National Wildlife Refuges and other federal lands. As one of the legislative pillars of the Service's fisheries mandate, the Sikes Act establishes the legal framework for developing and implementing fisheries management plans, including recreational fishing management and the restoration and protection of aquatic resources on refuge properties with fishery potential.
