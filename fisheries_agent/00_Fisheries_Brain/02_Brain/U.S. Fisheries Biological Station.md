# U.S. Fisheries Biological Station

## Summary
The U.S. Fisheries Biological Station was a federal facility that conducted fish propagation research and operations. The station at Fairport, Iowa successfully developed methods for collecting and hatching buffalo-fish eggs after previous efforts had resulted in absolute failure or minimal success.

## Context

> This document presents methods developed at the [[U.S. Fisheries Biological Station]] in [[Fairport, Iowa]] for successfully collecting and hatching eggs of buffalo-fishes (genus [[Ictiobus]]), addressing previous failures in artificial propagation of these commercially important food fishes.
