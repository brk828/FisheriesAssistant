# Auburn No. 2 Pellets

## Summary
Auburn No. 2 Pellets is a commercial fish feed used in the white catfish production experiments. The pellets were fed daily except Sundays as supplemental feeding, resulting in feed conversion ratios of 1.7 to 2.0, which were comparable to channel catfish performance.
