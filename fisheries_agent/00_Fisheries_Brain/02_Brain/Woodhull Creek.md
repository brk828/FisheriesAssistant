# Woodhull Creek

## Summary
Woodhull Creek is a water body in New York State whose water was used in cyanide toxicity experiments on brown trout and smallmouth bass. The creek water is characterized by acidic conditions with low alkalinity (6-12 p.p.m.) and pH below neutral, and when compared to high-alkalinity Rome Hatchery springwater, showed no significant difference in cyanide toxicity to brown trout at equivalent concentrations.
