# Socorro County

## Summary
A county in New Mexico containing multiple spring systems with endemic mollusks including Sedillo Springs, Torreon Springs, Ojo Caliente Springs, and Warm Springs. This source documents several threatened and rare hydrobiid species from springs in this county.
