# Fish Ladder

## Summary
A fish ladder is an engineered structure designed to enable anadromous fish, particularly salmon, to migrate upstream past dams and other barriers by providing a series of ascending pools or channels that reduce the water height differential they must overcome. Fish ladders are critical conservation tools in riverine ecosystems, allowing migratory species to access spawning habitat in upstream reaches that would otherwise be blocked by hydroelectric dams and water control infrastructure. These structures are essential for maintaining populations of species dependent on unrestricted migration between freshwater spawning grounds and marine feeding areas, and their presence or absence significantly influences the viability of fisheries and the fulfillment of tribal treaty rights in dam-regulated rivers.
