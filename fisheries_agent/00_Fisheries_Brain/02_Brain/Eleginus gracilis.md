# Eleginus gracilis

## Summary
Eleginus gracilis (Saffron Cod) is a mobile coastal species with an elevated cerebellum characteristic of highly maneuverable gadids. Its brain structure reflects adaptation to active swimming behavior, with the cerebellum positioned above rather than resting on the medulla oblongata.
