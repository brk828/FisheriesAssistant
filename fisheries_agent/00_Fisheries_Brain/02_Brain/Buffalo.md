# Buffalo

## Summary
Buffalo refers to several species of large-bodied suckers in the genus Ictiobus, including Smallmouth Buffalo, Black Buffalo, and Bigmouth Buffalo, though this study often did not differentiate between species. In Fort Gibson Reservoir in 1959, buffalo populations showed minimal successful reproduction with densities of 12 fish per acre weighing 20.3 pounds per acre, grew to average lengths of 5.3 to 14.8 inches over six years, and were captured effectively using whip trammel nets (542 pounds of Smallmouth Buffalo and 4 pounds of Bigmouth Buffalo across 27 sets) and trap nets (25.1 pounds per lift in large nets).
