# University Of Arizona

## Summary
The University of Arizona served as an institutional partner in federally-authorized research programs related to endangered aquatic species conservation, particularly involving humpback chub and razorback sucker populations in the Colorado River system. As a research institution, the university collaborated on studies addressing threats to these species, including investigations into parasitic infections like tapeworms that affected fish health and survival. The university's involvement reflected the role of academic institutions in supporting federal fisheries management agencies with scientific expertise and research capacity for endangered species recovery efforts during the 1980s-1990s.
