# San Vicente Reservoir

## Summary
San Vicente Reservoir is a reservoir in San Diego County, California. According to this 1955 report, it was used as an experimental introduction site for Threadfin Shad, where the species was thriving after being brought from Tennessee in 1953.

## Context

> This October 1955 California Department of Fish and Game bulletin describes an active program to introduce four non-native forage fish species into California's warmwater reservoirs to support predatory gamefish populations.
