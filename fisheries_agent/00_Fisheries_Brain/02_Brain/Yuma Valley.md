# Yuma Valley

## Summary
Yuma Valley refers to canal systems in Arizona that were compared to the Coachella Canal. After a 1974 pesticide spill, Yuma Valley canals showed similar species composition but different community structure, with much higher densities of small forage fish (10.84 fish/m²) and lower game fish biomass (32.8%) compared to Coachella Canal's 84.3% game fish biomass.
