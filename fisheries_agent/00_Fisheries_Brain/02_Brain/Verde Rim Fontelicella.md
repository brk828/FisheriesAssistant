# Verde Rim Fontelicella

## Summary
Verde Rim Fontelicella is an undescribed Fontelicella species endemic to Nelson Place Spring along the Verde Rim in Arizona. This hydrobioid snail was classified in 1981 as rare and of special concern due to its restriction to a single spring location.
