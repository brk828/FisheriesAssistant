# St. George, Utah

## Summary
St. George, Utah was the location of the 18th Annual Desert Fishes Council Meeting in November 1986. At this meeting, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service personnel presented the Recovery Implementation Program for Endangered Fish Species in the Upper Colorado River Basin, and the Desert Fishes Council requested review copies of the program despite the official review period closing on November 15, 1986.
