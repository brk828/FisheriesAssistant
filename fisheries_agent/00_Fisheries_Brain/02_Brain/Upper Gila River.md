# Upper Gila River

## Summary
The Upper Gila River is a critical reach of the Gila River system in the southwestern United States that historically supported one of the richest native fish communities in the entire Gila River Basin, with ecological conditions suitable for species such as Razorback Sucker and Colorado Pikeminnow. This region has been a focal point for fish conservation and research efforts due to its significance as a refuge for native aquatic biodiversity and its relatively less-altered hydrological and habitat conditions compared to other parts of the basin, making it essential for understanding historical fish distributions and maintaining viable populations of endemic and endangered species.
