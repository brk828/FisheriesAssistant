# Arizona State Trust

## Summary
Arizona State Trust lands are state-owned lands managed for revenue generation, primarily through leasing. At Buenos Aires National Wildlife Refuge, 90,178 acres of the 112,500-acre refuge are Arizona State Trust lands leased by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service for grazing rights only, with all other development rights retained by the state, creating significant constraints on management options.
