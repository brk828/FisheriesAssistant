# Rio Papaloapan

## Summary
The Rio Papaloapan is an Atlantic slope river basin in Mexico draining to the Gulf of Mexico. This source documents cave systems in the Papaloapan basin as habitat for specialized troglobitic fauna including Rhamdia reddelli (Papaloapan blindcat), representing subterranean aquatic biodiversity.
