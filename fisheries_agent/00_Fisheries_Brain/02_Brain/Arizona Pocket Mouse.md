# Arizona Pocket Mouse

## Summary
The Arizona Pocket Mouse (Perognathus amplus) is a C2 candidate species that principally inhabits sparse desertscrub areas. Though not detected during the trapping surveys at Papago Park, it may occur in the area, and the assessment recommended restricting disturbance to immediate construction areas and revegetating with native plants at similar densities.

## Context

> This biological assessment was conducted 8–18 July 1994 for the [[Papago Park Military Reservation]] (PPMR) in Phoenix, Arizona, in support of an environmental assessment for proposed construction of a consolidated maintenance facility by the Arizona Army National Guard.
