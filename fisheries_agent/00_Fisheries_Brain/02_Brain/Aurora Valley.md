# Aurora Valley

## Summary
Aurora Valley is a drainage feature that Russell (1889) initially suggested as a route for pre-pluvial discharge from Lake Russell into the East Walker River of the Lahontan drainage system. However, Russell calculated that the highest shoreline was 100 feet below the divide, making this overflow route unlikely.
