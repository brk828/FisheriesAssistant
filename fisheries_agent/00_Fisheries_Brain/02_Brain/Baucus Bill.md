# Baucus Bill

## Summary
The Baucus Bill (S. 1113) is a proposed RCRA reauthorization bill that would establish a federal permit program for mining wastes with 10-year permit terms, numerical performance standards, and 'best available control technology' requirements. This source presents this bill as one of two competing legislative approaches to mining waste regulation being considered during the 1989-1990 reauthorization period.
