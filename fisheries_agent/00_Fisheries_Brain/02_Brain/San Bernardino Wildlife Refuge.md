# San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge is a protected area in southeastern Arizona that supports populations of endangered fish species including the Yaqui Topminnow and Gila Chub. During spring 1989 surveys, the refuge manager reported recent construction of two new ponds and plans to dam Black Draw to recreate ciénega-like conditions, with a temporary dirt dam already in place 15-20 meters upstream from the border fence.

## Context

> This field report documents fish collecting activities conducted by S.P. Vives and five students across multiple sites in southeastern Arizona during April 1-2, 1989. The survey targeted native and introduced fish species at [[San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge]] and surrounding creek systems....
