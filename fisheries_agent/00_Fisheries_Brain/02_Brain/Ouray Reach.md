# Ouray Reach

## Summary
Ouray Reach is a study site at river mile 1 on the White River, characterized as a sand-bed segment with straight channel representing the lower 15 miles to the White-Green confluence. This 1982 study found the substrate was exclusively sand, resulting in predictably high habitat for YOY and adults but no spawning habitat, with YOY habitat the highest of all White River sites at 100-700 cfs and adult habitat high at most flows with a broad peak at 500-1,200 cfs.
