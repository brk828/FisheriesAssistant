# East Verde River

## Summary
The East Verde River is an aquatic habitat in Arizona where multiple sampling sites (sites 17-20) were surveyed during the 1988 October Fish Count. The survey documented threatened species including Gila Chub and Roundtail Chub at these locations.

## Context

> The First Annual 1988 October Fish Count (OFC) was a volunteer-based fish monitoring program organized by the [[Arizona Game and Fish Department]] Nongame Branch.
