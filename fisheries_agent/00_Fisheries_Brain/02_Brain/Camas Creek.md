# Camas Creek

## Summary
Camas Creek is a tributary within the Salmon River drainage in Idaho that supports chinook salmon spawning. In 1967, surveys documented 256 chinook salmon redds in Camas Creek, representing an increase from 212 redds counted in 1966.
