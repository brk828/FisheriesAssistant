# Gambusia Heterochir

## Summary
Gambusia heterochir, commonly known as the Clear Creek gambusia, is a small livebearing freshwater fish endemic to Clear Creek in Texas and classified within the family Poeciliidae. This species is notable for its predatory adaptations, including a comparatively powerful jaw, sturdier teeth, and blunter gill rakers that enable it to feed on other aquatic organisms such as the amphipod Hyalella texana. As a spring endemic with a highly restricted geographic range, G. heterochir represents the type of specialized freshwater fish diversity characteristic of Texas's unique ichthyofauna and serves as an important model for understanding predator-prey interactions and morphological adaptations in isolated aquatic ecosystems.
