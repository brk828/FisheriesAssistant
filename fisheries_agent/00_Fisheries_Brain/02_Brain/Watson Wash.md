# Watson Wash

## Summary
Watson Wash is a thermal spring located on BLM land near Thatcher, Arizona, that supports Gila Topminnow populations but also contains problematic non-native species including guppies and Red Shiners. The site was also noted for the presence of bullfrogs, identified by their croaking behavior, representing another non-native threat to the native fish community.
