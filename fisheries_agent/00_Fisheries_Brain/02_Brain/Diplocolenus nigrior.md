# Diplocolenus nigrior

## Summary
Diplocolenus nigrior is a new species of deltocephaline leafhopper measuring 3.7 mm (male only known), heavily marked with brown coloring and distinguished by truncate plate apices. It represents a primitive branch in the evolutionary line leading to D. configuratus and D. brevior, with intermediate plate morphology forming a phenocline.
