# Garden Canyon

## Summary
Garden Canyon is a high-elevation site (6,150 feet) in the Huachuca Mountains of Arizona where Lilaeopsis schaffneriana subspecies recurva occurs along a half-mile stretch. The population exists at low density mixed with grasses and sedges in this marshy to bog-like alpine meadowland habitat.
