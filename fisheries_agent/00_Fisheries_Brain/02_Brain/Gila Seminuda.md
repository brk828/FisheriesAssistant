# Gila Seminuda

## Summary
Gila seminuda, commonly known as the Gila topminnow, is a small native fish species endemic to the southwestern United States that has become a focal point in conservation biology due to its endangered status and significance as an indicator of aquatic ecosystem health in the Colorado River Basin. The species is of particular evolutionary and ecological importance for understanding adaptation to arid-region aquatic systems and has been the subject of intensive monitoring, recovery programs, and research efforts to prevent its extinction and restore populations in its native habitat.
