# Upper Mainstem Subbasin

## Summary
The Upper Mainstem Subbasin is a geographic subdivision of the Colorado River Basin where 104 of the 919 adult razorback sucker captures (11%) were recorded between 1975 and August 1995. It represents a smaller portion of razorback sucker habitat compared to the Green Subbasin.
