# Spotted Bass

## Summary
Spotted Bass (Micropterus punctulatus) is a centrarchid fish species characterized by broad thermal tolerance and eurythermal habitat preferences, making it adaptable to diverse water temperature conditions in freshwater ecosystems. As a sportfish of recreational and ecological significance, spotted bass populations have been studied in impoundments and rivers to understand their exploitation rates, movement patterns, and thermal habitat selection. This species represents an important component of temperate freshwater fish communities and is relevant to fisheries management, particularly in understanding how centrarchids respond to environmental variation and fishing pressure.
