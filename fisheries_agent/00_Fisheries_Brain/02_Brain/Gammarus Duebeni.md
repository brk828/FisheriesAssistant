# Gammarus Duebeni

## Summary
Gammarus duebeni is a euryhaline amphipod crustacean species inhabiting tide pools and tidal freshwater environments along the North Atlantic coast from southern Labrador to Massachusetts, making it an important model organism for studying osmoregulation and adaptation to variable salinity conditions in aquatic ecosystems. This species is frequently used as a reference taxon in comparative morphological and ecological studies of freshwater and brackish-water amphipod communities, particularly because its geographic and ecological distribution does not overlap with most North American freshwater Gammarus species, allowing researchers to isolate specific biological responses to environmental stressors. Its occurrence in estuarine and coastal habitats makes G. duebeni valuable for investigating the effects of anthropogenic pollutants and environmental contaminants on sensitive aquatic invertebrate populations in transitional marine-freshwater systems.
