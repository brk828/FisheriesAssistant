# Lake Mohave Native Fish Workgroup

## Summary
The Lake Mohave Native Fish Workgroup is an organization that established the razorback sucker repatriation program in Lake Mohave in the early 1990s. This program collects wild-produced razorback sucker larvae, rears them in protective captivity to sizes of 30+ cm total length, and then stocks them back into the reservoir to maintain the critically imperiled population.
