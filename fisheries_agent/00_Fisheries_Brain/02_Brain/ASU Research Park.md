# Asu Research Park

## Summary
The ASU Research Park is an experimental facility in central Arizona that served as a controlled site for native fish research and conservation efforts, particularly involving young-of-year fish studies and experimental stocking programs. The park functioned as a research location where ichthyologists and fisheries scientists conducted surveys and monitoring of native fish populations, likely supporting both baseline ecological research and applied conservation management objectives for Arizona's aquatic fauna. Based on the documented research activities from the 1990s, the facility appears to have been utilized as a field station for testing native fish propagation and restoration techniques relevant to arid-region aquatic ecosystems.
