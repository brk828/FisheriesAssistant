# Tilapia hornorum

## Summary
Tilapia hornorum is an African cichlid species characterized by 16-18 dorsal spines, 28-30 total dorsal spines plus rays, and 18-26 gill rakers, with breeding males displaying black coloration with red dorsal fin margins and silver-white patches. This source documents that T. hornorum was imported from the Tropical Fish Culture Institute in Malacca, Malaysia in 1962 and has largely hybridized with Tilapia mossambica to form a single reproducing complex in the Lower Colorado River Basin, with specimens showing T. hornorum characteristics most common along the mainstream Colorado River.
