# Puerto Peñasco

## Summary
Puerto Peñasco is a coastal location in Sonora, Mexico, on the Gulf of California where the University of Sonora established a Marine Research Station. This 1966 field guide documents the invertebrate fauna of this site, which features an approximately 22-foot vertical intertidal zone with varied habitats including rocky shores, sandy beaches, and tide pools.
