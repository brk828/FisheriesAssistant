# Snake River fall chinook

## Summary
Snake River fall chinook salmon is a threatened species listed under the Endangered Species Act on May 22, 1992. This case found that the 1993 NMFS Biological Opinion estimated only 242-246 individuals would return in 1993, with juveniles experiencing 81-93% mortality and adults experiencing 41% mortality (representing 5-9% reduction from baseline) due to FCRPS operations, yet NMFS produced "counter-intuitive" model results showing 70-99% probability of stabilization that the agency itself questioned.
