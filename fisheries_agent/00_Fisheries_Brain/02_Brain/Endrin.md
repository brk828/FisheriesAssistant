# Endrin

## Summary
Endrin is a highly toxic organochlorine insecticide that poses severe risks to fish populations and aquatic ecosystems through both direct exposure and bioaccumulation. This persistent synthetic pesticide has been documented to cause catastrophic fish kills in aquatic environments when applied to adjacent agricultural areas, particularly through aerial drift, and can create selective evolutionary pressure in fish populations exposed to chronic contamination, as demonstrated in studies of pesticide-resistant strains. Its environmental persistence and lipophilic properties make it a significant concern for aquatic conservation, as it accumulates in fish tissues and can remain bioavailable in aquatic systems long after initial application.
