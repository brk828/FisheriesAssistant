# Wolf Creek

## Summary
Wolf Creek is a freshwater stream in Union County, Georgia, located 3.5 miles north of Neels Gap, where specimens of the newly described caddisfly species Cheumatopsyche enigma were collected. This location served as the type locality for C. enigma (holotype collected August 30, 1945) and was notable for containing specimens that exhibited the species' characteristic extreme morphological variability within a single collection site.
