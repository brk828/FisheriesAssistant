# Superior Allotment

## Summary
The Superior Allotment is a livestock grazing area on the Globe Ranger District containing Arnett Creek, a proposed Gila Topminnow reintroduction site. This source indicates the site was planned as a fish improvement project including construction of a fish barrier, removal of non-native Green Sunfish, native species reintroduction, riparian pasture fence development, and potential water source development outside the riparian pasture.
