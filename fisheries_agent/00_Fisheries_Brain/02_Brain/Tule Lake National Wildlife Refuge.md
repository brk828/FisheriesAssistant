# Tule Lake National Wildlife Refuge

## Summary
Tule Lake National Wildlife Refuge experienced fish-eating bird mortality from toxaphene contamination in waste agricultural water in 1964. Fish contained up to 8 ppm toxaphene while dead birds contained 3-650 ppm in tissues, and water monitoring showed DDT concentrations decreasing from 20.3 ppm in incoming agricultural water to 6.0 ppm in marsh areas.
