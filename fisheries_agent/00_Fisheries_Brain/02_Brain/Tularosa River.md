# Tularosa River

## Summary
The Tularosa River is a tributary stream system in New Mexico that serves as critical habitat for native fish species, particularly within the greater Gila River drainage. As documented in conservation assessments, the Tularosa River supports aquatic communities in higher-gradient mainstem river environments and represents an important component of the southwestern river ecosystem network for endangered and threatened fish species management. Its approximately 24-kilometer documented range highlights its significance as a conservation priority within New Mexico's aquatic biodiversity framework.
