# San Bernardino Mountains

## Summary
The San Bernardino Mountains in southern California were the location where Salmo evermanni specimens were collected from the upper Santa Ana River above falls on Mount San Gorgonio. According to stocking records, cutthroat trout fry from Lake Tahoe tributaries were planted in streams throughout the higher parts of these mountains in 1895-1896, including 23,500 fry in the Santa Ana River system.
