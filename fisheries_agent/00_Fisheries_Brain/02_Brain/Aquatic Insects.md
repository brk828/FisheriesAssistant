# Aquatic Insects

## Summary
Aquatic Insects was a five-week course proposed for the Sierra Ancha summer program covering aquatic habitats, life history, functional morphology, systematics, and ecology of aquatic insects. The course required students to collect and correctly identify 50 specimens (generally to genus level) representing seven primary aquatic insect orders in both larval and adult stages.
