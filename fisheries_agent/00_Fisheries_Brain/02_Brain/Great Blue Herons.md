# Great Blue Herons

## Summary
Great Blue Herons (Ardea herodias) are piscivorous birds present in Lake Havasu that prey on stocked bonytail. Direct observation documented a Great Blue Heron capturing and consuming a bonytail at Regional Park Moabi, and literature indicates these birds can consume fish up to 390-500 mm total length.
