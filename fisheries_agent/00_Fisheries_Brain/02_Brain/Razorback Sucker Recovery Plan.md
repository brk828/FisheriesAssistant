# Razorback Sucker Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Razorback Sucker Recovery Plan is a comprehensive federal conservation initiative focused on protecting and restoring populations of the razorback sucker (*Xyrauchen texanus*), an endangered fish endemic to the Colorado River basin. Developed by the Colorado River Fishes Recovery Team in the early 1990s, the plan addresses critical habitat designation, species-specific management strategies, and coordinated expenditures among federal agencies like the Bureau of Reclamation to reverse the species' decline caused by habitat modification, dam construction, and water management practices. This recovery effort exemplifies multiagency collaboration in applied conservation biology to preserve native aquatic biodiversity in highly altered river systems.
