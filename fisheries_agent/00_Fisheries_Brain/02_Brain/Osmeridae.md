# Osmeridae

## Summary
Osmeridae is a family of small, silvery forage fishes characterized by the presence of an adipose fin, including species such as rainbow smelt (Osmerus mordax). Members of this family are ecologically important as prey fish in freshwater and marine ecosystems, with some species exhibiting anadromous or lacustrine life histories that make them significant components of food webs and fisheries. The family's role in aquatic ecology and their susceptibility to invasive introductions have made them relevant subjects in conservation and fisheries management studies.
