# Fish Disease Control Centers

## Summary
Fish Disease Control Centers are specialized facilities within the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service's Fishery Resources program. As of this review, there were 9 such centers operating alongside 78 hatcheries and 6 Fish Technology Centers as part of the National Fish Hatchery system.
