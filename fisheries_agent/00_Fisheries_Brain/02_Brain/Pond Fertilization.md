# Pond Fertilization

## Summary
Pond fertilization in fish science refers to the practice of adding nutrients or organic matter to pond systems to enhance primary productivity and increase the availability of plankton and other natural food sources for cultured fish species. This management technique is used to improve fish growth rates and overall pond productivity by stimulating the growth of phytoplankton and zooplankton that serve as forage for fish, thereby reducing dependence on supplemental feeds and increasing economic efficiency in aquaculture operations. The effectiveness of pond fertilization can vary based on factors such as the timing and type of fertilizer applied, pond characteristics, and the specific fish species being cultured, as demonstrated by differential growth responses observed in polyculture systems with different fish combinations.
