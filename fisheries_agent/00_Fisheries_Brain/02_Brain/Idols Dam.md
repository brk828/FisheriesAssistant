# Idols Dam

## Summary
Idols Dam is a Duke Power hydroelectric facility near Clemmons, Forsyth County, on the Yadkin River that acts as a barrier preventing upstream migration of flathead catfish from High Rock Lake. The survey confirmed no reproducing flathead catfish populations exist above the dam, while abundant bullhead populations thrive upstream and flathead catfish (including a state record 27.93 kg specimen) are present below.
