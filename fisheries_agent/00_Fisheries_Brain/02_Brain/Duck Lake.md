# Duck Lake

## Summary
Duck Lake is a 630-acre warm-water lake in Calhoun County, Michigan, connected to the Kalamazoo River drainage. It was one of six lakes opened to year-round fishing for species not protected by closed seasons, with a five-year catch composition of 69.0% bluegill, 11.1% yellow perch, 6.5% pumpkinseed, and 4.4% largemouth bass.
