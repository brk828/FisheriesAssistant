# DDNU

## Summary
DDNU is a homolog (structurally related compound) of DDE, the DDT metabolite. This source reports that in metabolic studies, administration of DDNU to deer and rats did not produce the mysterious "Compound X" degradation product that was detected following DDT administration, indicating that DDNU is metabolized differently than the parent DDT compound.
