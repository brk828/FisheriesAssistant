# Lake Emerald Shiner

## Summary
The Lake Emerald Shiner (Notropis atherinoides) is a minnow species that is incredibly abundant in the Great Lakes, where it forms an important dietary component for Lake Trout and other predators. Despite its abundance in large lake systems, this source reports that efforts to establish this species in small inland lakes have been uniformly unsuccessful, indicating it requires specific large-lake conditions that cannot be replicated in smaller water bodies.
