# Cuatro Ciénegas Basin

## Summary
The Cuatro Ciénegas Basin is an isolated, ancient desert wetland system in Coahuila, Mexico, characterized by unique hydrogeological and thermal conditions that have promoted extraordinary aquatic biodiversity and endemism. This globally significant ecosystem encompasses diverse aquatic habitats including springs, pools, and hypersaline lagoons with distinctive physical and chemical properties, creating a natural laboratory for studying adaptation and speciation in isolated aquatic systems. The basin is particularly notable in ichthyology and conservation biology for harboring endemic fish communities and other aquatic fauna that have evolved in this geologically complex, hydrologically isolated environment over extended periods.
