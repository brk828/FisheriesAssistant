# Fish Springs

## Summary
Fish Springs was one of the California Department of Fish and Game hatcheries operating in Region 5 during the 1964-65 fiscal year. It was part of the state's network of facilities producing trout, salmon, and other fish species.

> During the 1964-65 fiscal year, California Department of Fish and Game hatcheries planted a record-breaking **60,129,973 fish** totaling **1,879,769 pounds**, though total weight was 207,290 pounds less than the previous year.
