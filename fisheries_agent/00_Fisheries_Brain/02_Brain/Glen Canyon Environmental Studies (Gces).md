# Glen Canyon Environmental Studies (Gces)

## Summary
The Glen Canyon Environmental Studies (GCES) was a comprehensive research initiative focused on understanding the ecological impacts of Glen Canyon Dam operations on the Colorado River ecosystem and its aquatic communities. As a multi-agency collaborative effort, GCES provided foundational data and coordination for various fish science and conservation biology studies examining how dam operations affected river hydrology, water quality, and native fish populations, particularly in relation to endangered species management. The program served as a coordinating framework that facilitated communication among resource agencies and research institutions investigating aquatic trophic dynamics and fish ecology in the Colorado River system.
