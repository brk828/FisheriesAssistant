# Grout Brook

## Summary
Grout Brook is a tributary stream flowing into Skaneateles Lake in the Finger Lakes Region, with approximately 5 miles of trout fishing habitat studied. During the opening week of fishing season (1955-1957), it experienced moderate fishing pressure and had the highest fishing success rates among the four streams studied (16-37% of anglers caught fish), with opening-week catches typically in the 100-300 fish range.
