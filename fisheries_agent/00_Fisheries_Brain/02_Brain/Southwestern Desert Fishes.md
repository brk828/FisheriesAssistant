# Southwestern Desert Fishes

## Summary
Southwestern Desert Fishes refer to endemic fish species inhabiting the arid regions of Arizona, New Mexico, and northern Mexico, particularly within the Gila River, Little Colorado River, and Yaqui River systems. These fishes are characterized by their adaptation to isolated headwater and mainstem stream habitats in desert environments and represent a distinct conservation concern due to their vulnerability to habitat fragmentation, water resource development, and invasion by non-native species. This taxonomic and ecological grouping is particularly significant in conservation biology, as many of these endemic species face declining populations and have been subject to federal endangered species protections and management initiatives.
