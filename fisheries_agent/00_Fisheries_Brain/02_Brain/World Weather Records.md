# World Weather Records

## Summary
World Weather Records (WWR) is a series of meteorological publications that served as one of the major data sources consolidated into the GHCN database. Sharp increases in GHCN station numbers correspond to inclusion of specific WWR publication periods from 1951, 1961, and 1981.

## Context

> This document describes the Global Historical Climatology Network (GHCN) Version 1.0, a comprehensive global baseline dataset of monthly mean temperature, precipitation, sea level pressure, and station pressure compiled by Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the National Climatic Data Center....
