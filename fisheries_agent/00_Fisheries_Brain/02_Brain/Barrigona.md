# Barrigona

## Summary
Barrigona is a locality on the Rio Meta in Colombia where two new species were collected by Manuel Gonzales: Hemigrammus barrigonae (65 paratypes, 25-43mm) and Hyphessobrycon metae (66 paratypes, 16-35mm). The species names commemorate this collection locality.
