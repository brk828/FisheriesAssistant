# Laughlin Lagoon

## Summary
Laughlin Lagoon is a confined backwater study site in Lake Havasu with dense bulrush stands and culverts utilized by bonytail. Fish stocked here were larger (mean 400+ mm TL) and showed better survival with 83% actively tracked through week 4, slower mortality decline, and minimal dispersal (mean 1.14 km from release), though 54% were ultimately confirmed dead and 46% lost.

## Context

> This multi-year study (2013-2016) documented post-stocking survival, distribution, and habitat use of endangered [[Bonytail]] (*Gila elegans*) in [[Lake Havasu]], [[Colorado River]].
