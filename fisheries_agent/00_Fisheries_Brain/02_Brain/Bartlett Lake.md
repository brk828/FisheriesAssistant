# Bartlett Lake

## Summary
Bartlett Lake is a recreational reservoir in Arizona that served as one of five key study sites in a 1965-1966 Arizona Game and Fish Department visitor-use and fisheries survey conducted under the River Basin Investigations program. The lake was monitored for recreational fishing pressure, angler usage patterns, and catch data as part of broader Central Arizona Project fish and wildlife investigations aimed at understanding how constructed reservoirs supported fisheries and human recreation in the region. These survey efforts provided baseline data on reservoir-dependent fish populations and recreational demand that informed Arizona's fisheries management and conservation planning during the mid-twentieth century.
