# Longnose Sucker

## Summary
The Longnose Sucker (Catostomus catostomus) is a benthic cyprinid fish species commonly found in North American freshwater systems, including montane rivers and streams, where it serves as an important component of fish community structure and as a useful indicator organism for studying age and growth dynamics in riverine ecosystems. This species is characterized by its bottom-feeding ecology and resilience to environmental disturbances, making it valuable for investigations into fish population responses to habitat changes such as flow fluctuations and drought conditions. In fisheries science, longnose suckers are frequently studied alongside salmonids in comparative age and growth analyses to understand species-specific life history patterns and population recovery potential in regulated river systems.
