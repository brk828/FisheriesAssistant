# Sevier River

## Summary
The Sevier River is a sub-basin of the Bonneville Basin system from which all Gila copei material used in this study was collected. Specimens of both G. copei (n=10) and G. atraria (n=18) were sampled from the Sevier River for allozyme analysis.

## Context

> This study used allozyme electrophoresis to assess phylogenetic relationships within the cyprinid tribe [[Plagopterini]] of western North America.
