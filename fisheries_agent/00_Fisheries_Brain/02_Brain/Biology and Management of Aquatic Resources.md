# Biology and Management of Aquatic Resources

## Summary
Biology and Management of Aquatic Resources (Bio 413) was proposed as a lecture/laboratory course with field trips for the Sierra Ancha summer program. It was among the immediately available courses designed to accommodate up to 18 students.
