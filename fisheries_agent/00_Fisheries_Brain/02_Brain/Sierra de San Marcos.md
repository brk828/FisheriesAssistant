# Sierra de San Marcos

## Summary
Sierra de San Marcos is a mountain range in the Cuatro Ciénegas basin. Thermal springs near this sierra are the endemic habitat of Cyprinodon bifasciatus, the thermal endemic pupfish that lives exclusively in waters exceeding 30°C.
