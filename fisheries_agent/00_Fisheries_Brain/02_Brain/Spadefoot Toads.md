# Spadefoot Toads

## Summary
Spadefoot Toads (family Pelobatidae) are among the burrowing amphibians documented at the reservoir sites. This source identifies them as desert and grassland species that can penetrate higher elevations than normal on open, well-drained terraces in the study area.
