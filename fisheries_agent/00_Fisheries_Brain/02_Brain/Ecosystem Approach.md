# Ecosystem Approach

## Summary
The Ecosystem Approach in fish conservation represents a shift from single-species management to a more integrated, multi-species strategy that considers the broader environmental context and interconnected habitats where fish populations exist. Rather than focusing protection efforts on individual endangered species in isolation, this approach recognizes that species share common habitat requirements, environmental stressors, and ecological relationships, allowing resource managers to address conservation needs more efficiently across entire aquatic systems. This perspective emerged in fisheries science as agencies recognized that protecting critical habitat and ecological processes benefits multiple species simultaneously and creates more sustainable, ecosystem-wide conservation outcomes.
