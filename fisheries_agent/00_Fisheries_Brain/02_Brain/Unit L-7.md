# Unit L-7

## Summary
Unit L-7 is one of the management units within the Pond L-7 freshwater pond system at Ouray Wildlife Refuge. It maintains a water surface elevation of 4660 feet and is connected to Unit L-9 (elevation 4661 feet), with the engineering scheme proposing removal of approximately 30% of the pond levee to this unit and construction of a low head weir at elevation 4466 feet.
