# Green Subbasin

## Summary
The Green Subbasin is a geographic subdivision of the Colorado River Basin where 815 of the 919 adult razorback sucker captures (89%) were recorded between 1975 and August 1995. It encompasses the Green River system where the largest extant riverine razorback sucker population occurs.
