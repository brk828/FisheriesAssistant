# Genetics Analyses Incorporated

## Summary
Genetics Analyses Incorporated is a commercial laboratory located in Smithville, Texas that conducted protein electrophoresis analyses for the Gila Taxonomy Project. As of December 1992, they had analyzed 557 of 867 fresh specimens for approximately 55 presumptive loci, with work approximately 53% complete and an anticipated total of about 1,050 specimens to be analyzed.
