# Yampa Basin Feasibility Study

## Summary
The Yampa Basin Feasibility Study was a planned investigation of the Yampa River system in the Colorado River Basin. As of September 1989, the Recovery Implementation Program Management Group was developing the scope of work and contract for this study, which would support endangered fish recovery planning in the Yampa River.
