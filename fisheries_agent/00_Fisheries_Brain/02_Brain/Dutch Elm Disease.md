# Dutch Elm Disease

## Summary
Dutch Elm Disease control programs involved DDT applications that resulted in environmental contamination and wildlife mortality. Studies in control areas showed DDT concentrated in the top inch of soil, but earthworm residues were eight times greater than surface soil levels, and adult robins on sprayed campus areas contained far higher residues than control areas with peak mortality in late April.
