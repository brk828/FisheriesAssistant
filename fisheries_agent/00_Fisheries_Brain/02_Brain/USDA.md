# USDA

## Summary
The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) is recommended in this review to assume generic responsibility for assisting private aquaculture. The review suggests transferring this responsibility from the Fish and Wildlife Service to USDA, with establishment of an interagency group to ensure activities consider prevention of potential negative impacts on indigenous fish populations.
