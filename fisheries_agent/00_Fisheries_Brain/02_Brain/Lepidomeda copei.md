# Lepidomeda copei

## Summary
Lepidomeda copei is the revised taxonomic name for fish from the upper Snake River and Bear River previously called Gila copei or Snyderichthys copei. This study's molecular evidence showed Snake River populations nest within Lepidomeda (diverging 8.5-9.7 Ma from other northern Lepidomeda), requiring transfer from Gila to Lepidomeda, with the type locality at a tributary of Bear River at Evanston, Wyoming.
