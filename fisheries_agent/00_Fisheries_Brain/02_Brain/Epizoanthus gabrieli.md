# Epizoanthus gabrieli

## Summary
Epizoanthus gabrieli is a very small zoanthid (soft coral) with many fine banded tentacles and a column encrusted with sand grains. It is found under rocks and ledges in the lowest intertidal zone at Puerto Peñasco.
