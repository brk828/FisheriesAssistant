# Desert cottontail

## Summary
Desert cottontail (Sylvilagus audubonii) occurs below 1800 m elevation in brushy areas of the Salt River Valley region. Cottontails (Sylvilagus sp.) represent 24% of identified mammalian remains at La Lomita Pequeña, though species-level identification was not attempted due to fragmentary condition.
