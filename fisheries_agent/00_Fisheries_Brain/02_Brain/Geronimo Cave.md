# Geronimo Cave

## Summary
Geronimo Cave is a landmark on the East Fork White River that marks the upstream extent of the currently known Loach Minnow distribution. The species is known to occupy approximately 14 miles from below Geronimo Cave on the East Fork downstream to below Canyon Day on the mainstem White River.
