# Saltillos

## Summary
Saltillos is a locality in Mexico that contains populations of Gila fish. According to this source, Saltillos populations are characterized by larger scales (55-67 lateral line scales), 8 dorsal and anal fin rays with 8 pelvic rays (8,8-8,8 configuration), and faint or weak basal radii on their scales.
