# Gila Topminnow Recovery Plan

## Summary
The Gila Topminnow Recovery Plan is a comprehensive conservation strategy developed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and Arizona Game and Fish Department to address the endangered status of *Poeciliopsis occidentalis*, a native Arizona fish species facing severe population declines. This recovery plan establishes management frameworks and interim extirpation criteria to guide reintroduction efforts, habitat restoration, and viable population establishment for this endangered poeciliid fish. The plan represents a coordinated interagency approach to preventing complete extinction of the species through transplantation programs and conservation prioritization of remaining and reestablished populations.
