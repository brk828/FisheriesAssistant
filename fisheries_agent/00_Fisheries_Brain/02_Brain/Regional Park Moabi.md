# Regional Park Moabi

## Summary
Regional Park Moabi is a confined backwater study site in Lake Havasu featuring dense bulrush stands that provide potential cover for bonytail. This site showed relatively better outcomes with 68% of stocked fish contacted via PIT scanning (N=424) and 15-22% of tracking contacts occurring in or near bulrush habitat, though 29% of telemetry fish were confirmed dead and 71% were lost.

## Context
