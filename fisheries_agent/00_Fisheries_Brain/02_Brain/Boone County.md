# Boone County

## Summary
Boone County is a county in central Iowa, USA, through which the Des Moines River flows. This study examined a 7-mile section of the Des Moines River in Boone County between the Fraser and Boone dams, finding it provided approximately 85,000 man-hours of fishing annually with most anglers (72%) living within a 10-mile radius of the river.
