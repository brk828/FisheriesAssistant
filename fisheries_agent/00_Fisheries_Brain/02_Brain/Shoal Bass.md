# Shoal Bass

## Summary
Shoal bass (Micropterus cataractae) is a black bass species endemic to the Apalachicola River basin, described scientifically only in 1999. This source reports that it prefers moving waters with rocks and shoals, is considerably larger than redeye bass (world record 3.97 kg), and is distinguished by greater lateral line scale counts and dark vertical bars. The species is considered threatened in Florida and of special concern in Alabama and Georgia, having been eliminated from most of the Chattahoochee River in Alabama due to impoundments, and now comprises only about 10% of catch in the Flint River.
