# Odocoileus hemionus

## Summary
Odocoileus hemionus (mule deer) prefer brushy areas and forest margins in the region. While deer (Odocoileus sp.) were identified at La Lomita Pequeña, species-level distinction between mule deer and white-tailed deer was not made, though both species' habitat preferences (brushy areas, forest margins, grasslands, and riparian environments) were consistent with the Salt River Valley environment.
