# Jerome State Historic Park

## Summary
Jerome State Historic Park is a protected historic site located within the Verde Valley, Arizona. According to this 1990-1991 survey, it is one of several protected areas in the Verde River corridor region, and survey respondents showed strong support for protecting significant historic and archaeological resources (78.7% of residents, 58.4% of leaders favored development only with adequate protection of such resources).
