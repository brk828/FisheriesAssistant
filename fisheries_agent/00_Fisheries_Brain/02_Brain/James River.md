# James River

## Summary
The James River is a major Atlantic Slope drainage system in Virginia that serves as an important ichthyological study area for understanding fish diversity and migratory species dynamics along the eastern United States. As an anadromous fish habitat and estuarine system, the James River has been systematically surveyed for fish populations and serves as a key reference site for documenting species distributions across multiple drainages in the Central Atlantic region. The river's ichthyofauna includes significant populations of sturgeon species and represents an important ecological zone for conservation efforts related to Atlantic Slope fish communities.
