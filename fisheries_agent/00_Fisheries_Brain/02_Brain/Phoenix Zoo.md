# Phoenix Zoo

## Summary
The Phoenix Zoo refers to a conservation facility that developed a significant refugial habitat program for endangered Colorado River Basin fishes, most notably converting an existing ornamental lake into a dedicated breeding and holding facility for federally endangered species such as the Razorback Sucker and Bonytail. This facility serves as an important example of ex-situ conservation, wherein zoos and similar institutions partner with fisheries agencies to maintain genetically diverse populations of critically endangered aquatic species outside their native habitats as insurance against extinction. The Phoenix Zoo's involvement in fish conservation demonstrates how non-traditional aquatic facilities can contribute meaningfully to species recovery programs and long-term genetic management of imperiled fish populations.
