# Grand Falls

## Summary
Grand Falls is a waterfall barrier on the Little Colorado River in Arizona. According to this recovery plan, Grand Falls may have historically excluded the Razorback Sucker from the Little Colorado River by acting as a physical barrier to upstream fish movement.

## Context

> This recovery plan addresses the alarming decline of the [[Razorback Sucker]] (*Xyrauchen texanus*), a monotypic genus endemic to the [[Colorado River]] basin.
