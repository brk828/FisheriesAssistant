# Eurasian Watermilfoil

## Summary
Eurasian Watermilfoil (Myriophyllum spicatum) is an aquatic plant species. In the Arizona Bay backwater habitat at Lake Mohave, this plant formed dense stands in shallow regions along with curly leaf pondweed, providing vegetated habitat where razorback sucker larvae could forage for food resources including vegetation-associated invertebrates.
