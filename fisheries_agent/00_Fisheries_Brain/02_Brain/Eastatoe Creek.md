# Eastatoe Creek

## Summary
Eastatoe Creek is a freshwater stream in Pickens County, South Carolina, where paratype specimens of the newly described caddisfly species Cheumatopsyche enigma were collected. It represents one of several collection localities in South Carolina and Georgia where this morphologically variable species was found during limnological studies.
