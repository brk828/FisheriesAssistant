# Swisshelm Mountains

## Summary
The Swisshelm Mountains are a mountain range in southeastern Arizona whose western base marked a transition in vegetation patterns. Blumer noted that typical desert shrubs, which were absent from the Sulfur Springs Valley floor, did not reappear until the surface began rising toward the western base of the Swisshelm Mountains.
