# Peloncillo Mountains

## Summary
The Peloncillo Mountains are a mountain range in the American Southwest where Degenhardt and Christiansen (1974) documented six localities of Kinosternon sonoriense on the eastern slopes. These localities, along with five K. f. flavescens localities in the nearby Playas Basin of Hidalgo County, New Mexico, provide strong evidence of complete allopatry between the two species and demonstrate the effectiveness of the Cochise Filter Barrier in separating their distributions.
