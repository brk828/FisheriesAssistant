# Cimarron River

## Summary
The Cimarron River is the receiving water body for Skeleton Creek, located approximately six miles north of Guthrie, Oklahoma. The study found evidence that fish conducted seasonal spring migrations from the Cimarron River upstream into Skeleton Creek, with migration extending as far as Station 15 (15 miles from the confluence) in April and May when many males showed breeding coloration and females were gravid.
