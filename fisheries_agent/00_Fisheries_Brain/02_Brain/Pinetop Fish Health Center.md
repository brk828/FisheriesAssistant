# Pinetop Fish Health Center

## Summary
The Pinetop Fish Health Center is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service diagnostic laboratory established in 1974 in Pinetop, Arizona, that provides pathological examination and disease analysis services for fish health management across federal hatcheries and conservation programs. Operating from laboratory facilities at Alchesay National Fish Hatchery, the center conducts necropsies and investigates mortality events in culturally and ecologically important fish species, including endangered taxa like razorback suckers and bonytail chubs managed under species recovery initiatives. This facility serves as a critical diagnostic resource for identifying disease, parasites, and other causes of fish mortality in propagation and holding operations throughout the southwestern United States.
