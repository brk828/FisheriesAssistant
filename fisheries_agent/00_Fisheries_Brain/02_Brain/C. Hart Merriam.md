# C. Hart Merriam

## Summary
C. Hart Merriam was a pioneering biologist who published a groundbreaking biological survey in 1890 titled "Results of a Biological Survey of the San Francisco Mountain Region and Desert of the Little Colorado, Arizona." This work introduced the life-zone concept that equated elevational zones with biogeographic realms, revolutionizing habitat classification across North America and remaining influential in Southwest habitat descriptions for over 80 years.
