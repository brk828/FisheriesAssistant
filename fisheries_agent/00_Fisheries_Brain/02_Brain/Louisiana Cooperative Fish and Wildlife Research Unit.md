# Louisiana Cooperative Fish and Wildlife Research Unit

## Summary
The Louisiana Cooperative Fish and Wildlife Research Unit was the institutional affiliation of Dr. C.F. Bryan, who provided critical technical review of the Flooded Bottom Land Restoration Program's umbrella proposal in 1994. Dr. Bryan emphasized the need to understand historical pre-dam river conditions and razorback sucker early life history requirements before implementing large-scale restoration, and proposed three potential management approaches including shoreline rotenone applications, predator-excluded nurseries, and extensive organically enriched rearing areas.
