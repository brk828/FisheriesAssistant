# Draper Lake

## Summary
Draper Lake is one of the specific locations within the Imperial National Wildlife Refuge designated for restoration activities. The source lists it among the sites where restoration objectives target enhancement of wetlands and backwaters through channel construction, backwater reconnection, and water control structures.
