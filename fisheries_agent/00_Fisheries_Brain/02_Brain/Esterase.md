# Esterase

## Summary
Esterase is an enzyme examined at 2-4 genetic loci in this electrophoretic analysis. In Cuatro Cienegas cichlids, two distinct five-banded patterns were observed (Type II at 84% frequency), with no association to species, sex, or size, while Rio Grande C. cyanoguttatum showed a single invariant three-band phenotype.
