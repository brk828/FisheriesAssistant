# Silver Carp

## Summary
Silver Carp (*Hypophthalmichthys molitrix*) is a planktivorous cyprinid fish native to East Asia that has become a significant concern in conservation and fisheries management due to its invasive potential and ecological impacts in non-native waters. As a filter-feeding species that consumes phytoplankton and zooplankton, silver carp can outcompete native fish species for food resources and alter aquatic food webs when introduced into new ecosystems. The species is widely cultured for aquaculture and food production globally, but escapes from farming operations have led to established populations in several regions, making it a focus of invasive species research and aquatic ecosystem management efforts.
