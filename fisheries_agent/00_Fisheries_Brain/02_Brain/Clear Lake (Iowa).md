# Clear Lake (Iowa)

## Summary
Clear Lake (Iowa) is a large (3,643-acre), shallow (maximum depth 20 feet), eutrophic natural lake in north-central Iowa that has served as a major field research site for long-term ichthyological and fisheries studies since the 1950s. The lake's well-documented fish communities, including largemouth bass, walleye, yellow bass, white bass, and sunfish species, combined with its standardized sampling protocols (creel censuses and gillnetting), have made it an important model system for investigating fish population dynamics, movement ecology, angler harvest, and species distribution patterns in shallow, productive freshwater ecosystems.
