# Cienega Creek

## Summary
Cienega Creek is a desert stream system in the BLM Tucson Resource Area of Arizona that serves as critical habitat for endangered fish species, particularly the Gila Topminnow (*Poeciliopsis occidentalis*). As a free-flowing riparian corridor within the Gila River basin, Cienega Creek represents an important refugial population site and target for stream rehabilitation and habitat restoration efforts aimed at conserving native desert fish communities threatened by water depletion, invasive species, and habitat degradation. The creek exemplifies the fragmented, relict aquatic ecosystems characteristic of the southwestern desert landscape that require active management and watershed-scale conservation interventions.
