# Dolly Varden

## Summary
Dolly Varden (*Salvelinus malma*) is a species of char in the Salmonidae family native to Pacific coastal and Arctic regions of North America and Asia, characterized by light spots on a darker body and highly variable life history patterns ranging from resident freshwater populations to anadromous forms. In fisheries management and conservation contexts, Dolly Varden populations are monitored as both a minor component of recreational catch and as an indicator of ecosystem health in northern aquatic systems, with their abundance and distribution reflecting environmental conditions and the impacts of fish introduction programs. The species exhibits ecological plasticity, occupying similar niches to other salmonids and demonstrating sensitivity to habitat degradation, making it relevant to conservation assessments across its range.
