# Coelorhynchus velifer

## Summary
Coelorhynchus velifer is a deep-sea macrourid fish species endemic to the Philippine Subregion. This source identifies it as one of the few endemic species found in the northern district off southern Luzon in the China Sea, as well as in the Sulu Sea complex.
