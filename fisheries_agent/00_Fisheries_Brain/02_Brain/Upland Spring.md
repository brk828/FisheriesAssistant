# Upland Spring

## Summary
Upland Spring is a warm spring (32°C) within San Bernardino Wildlife Refuge that supports a morphologically distinct population of Yaqui Topminnow. Female topminnows at this location exhibited distinctive darker "India-ink-like" perianal markings compared to other refuge populations, with 25 specimens collected and returned alive in April 1989.
