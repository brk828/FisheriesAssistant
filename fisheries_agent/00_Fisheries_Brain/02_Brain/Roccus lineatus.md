# Roccus lineatus

## Summary
Roccus lineatus (Striped Bass) was unsuccessfully introduced to the Salton Sea in 1929 and 1930, representing one of several failed attempts to establish sport fisheries in this water body prior to the arrival of ten-pounders. This species is mentioned as part of the historical context of fish introduction efforts at the Salton Sea.
